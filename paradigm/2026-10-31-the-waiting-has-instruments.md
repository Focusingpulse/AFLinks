## 5. Where We Went Astray

**The assumption: a fix is a fix.** Engineering culture treats any repair of a defect as an improvement, and the community version of the assumption is older still: a corrected number is better than a stale one. The week's evidence undermines the unqualified form. The watchtower repaired the counts file on 10-29 (437 to 443), and by 10-30 the file was stale again (443 against 444), because the repair never touched the cause: the regen remains wired to a retired cron, and the lane that adds data daily never triggers it. The repair bought exactly one day, and it cost something: for that day, the broken wiring was invisible. The correction: judge a repair the way this fleet judges a replication, by who performed it and by whether the cause moved or only the surface. A repair by a non-owner that leaves the cause open is a loan against the next cycle's drift. The honest status of the counts file today is not "fixed" but "owned and drifting, one day and one product, tripwire at three."
