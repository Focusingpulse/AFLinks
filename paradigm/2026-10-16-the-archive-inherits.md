## 2. The Cross-Domain Claim

The growth engine has inverted. A wins on arithmetic that even degraded instruments record: 3,584 plus 120 plus 274 against five. ## 5. Where We Went Astray

The mainstream assumption today's corpus undermines: a growing archive is a growing understanding. A gain of 4,609 documents in a day reads as progress, and the same day's ledger shows the counters unverified, the theme map 44 days old, and the concept tagger dead. Volume is not orientation. An archive that doubles while its instruments stand still becomes harder to know, not easier: every unstaged inheritance buries the live trickle under custodial mass, and every stale index misdescribes what the vault now holds. The community's mirror-image error is the inverse consolation: that because the material is preserved, the work is done. Preservation is custody, and custody without working instruments is a warehouse with a broken catalog. This is the eighth correction in the line: Report 30 located the critic inside the source, Report 31 separated the name from the claim, Report 32 showed the shelves were never one shelf, Report 33 attached verdicts to strands, Report 34 made survival an act of custody, Report 35 priced the access tilt, Report 36 showed that the registry remembers, and today's is that growth is not dialogue: an archive can inherit faster than it can listen.

## 6. What This Means

First, tag custody at intake. A Tesla patent, a throttled ICKAR monograph, and a fresh viXra preprint should not enter the claim pipeline wearing the same evidentiary posture. Three custody classes (estate, mirror, live) stamped at intake would let every downstream report weight a document's voice correctly, and would make the next inversion visible in the feed rather than only in a scout's prose.

Second, repair the instruments before the next harvest. The day's highest-value work items are not documents: a recomputed paradigm_lenses.json over 131,126 entries, a verified counter builder, and an answer to the whole-file writer hazard outrank any available +4,000. Growth priorities should yield one fire to instrumentation; the vault can afford a paused harvest better than a wrong self-description.

Third, re-price the live trickle. When estates arrive by the thousand, each living document carries more signal, not less: the ICCF-27 proceedings, the sbornik-29 volume, RENSIT №6, and the NEXYS October window are now the highest-signal watches in the fleet, because any one of them re-weights the live side of the ledger in a single event. The scout's probe-every-fire discipline on those lanes is exactly right, and the report layer should treat their arrival as front-page news whenever it comes.
