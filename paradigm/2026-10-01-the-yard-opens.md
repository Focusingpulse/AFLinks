## 2. The Cross-Domain Claim

Three languages, three lanes, one morning, one pattern. The archive's first controlled-test cards do not vindicate the traditions and do not refute them. They split the claims. The water evaporates faster: real, and mundanely explained. The rats' blood panel moves: real, mechanism unaddressed. The 3 millisecond rheotome row survives strict statistics: real; the chronaxia doctrine built above it does not. The scalar kit does something at the bench: real resonance; the overunity arithmetic fails the one instrumented measurement. Across all of it, the observation tends to survive the control while the mechanism and the doctrine do not. And the Yard's own instruction, that disagreeing validations should be read together and not blended, is the archive independently arriving at the selection principle this report series runs on (Maryanskyy 2026, arXiv:2603.20324: blending destroys the standout insight, selection preserves it). Two controlled instruments disagreeing about one pyramid is also the diversity argument at the instrument level (Yang et al. 2026, arXiv:2602.03794): different measurement channels see different things, and the disagreement is the information.

## 3. Rival Readings

**Reading A: rehabilitation at the effect level.** On this reading the Yard's opening week is the tradition's best news in decades. The folk observations replicate under controlled conditions: water dries faster in the pyramid, the rats are measurably less stressed, one biomagnetismo measurement survives Bonferroni in two studies. The miscites and the bad mechanisms were dressing; the observations underneath were sound. Evidence for this reading: the 2002 paper replicates the folk observation and says so; the Bhat series moved five blood markers in the same direction across six years with the shape control built in; the 3 millisecond row is consistent across two independent subject groups.

**Reading B: a slow-motion refutation machine.** On this reading the split is a waystation on the road to nulls. Where a controlled test covers the doctrine, the doctrine dies: pyramid drying is heat flow, not a field. Where the test does not cover the doctrine, the doctrine simply has not been tested yet: one unblinded lab's rat panel, one unblinded rheotome thesis, one 45 percent efficiency measurement against a 500 percent claim. The Bhat validation itself concedes that nobody has repeated anyone, that no blinding is described, and that the entire orientation doctrine rests on that single lab's follow-up work. Evidence for this reading: the one fully controlled, fully analyzed result in the set (Ivanitskii and Narimanov) came out mundane; the IGF measurement came out at 45 percent; every doctrine-level claim in today's cards is either refuted or resting on one laboratory.

**Reading C: the split is the finding.** On this reading neither vindication nor refutation is what the controlled stratum produces, because the traditions never packaged claims at the effect level. They packaged bundles: an observation, a mechanism, and a doctrine, sold together. Controls unbundle them. The archive's real product is the unbundling itself, and the correct unit of processing is the row, not the tradition. Evidence for this reading: the Yard's own authorship note refusing to blend its two disagreeing validations; the REOTOMO dossier separating the surviving row from the failed headline claim; dossier 059 holding the vendor's 500 percent, Meyl's 10, and the IGF's 45 percent side by side as three different claims about one apparatus.

## 4. The Selection

I select Reading C, with a criterion attached that decides the individual rows. The criterion, extended from Report 22: where a controlled result exists and covers the doctrine, the doctrine loses (the pyramid field as an explanation of pyramid drying, on the water row; overunity, on the IGF row). Where a controlled result exists but does not cover the doctrine, the row stays open (the rat panel, where thermal geometry has no purchase; the 3 millisecond row, which supports an excitability shift but not the pair doctrine; the Torsind, still awaiting its silk versus nylon control from Report 22). Reading A loses today because it overreads the Bhat series: one laboratory, no described blinding, and the validation's own confidence grade is low-medium on generalization. Reading B loses today because it overreads the water test: a thermal account of evaporation is not a thermal account of blood chemistry, and the validation file itself warns against using one to close the other. Neither loses permanently; both win rows under the criterion. That is the point of selecting rather than blending.

What would change my reading: a second laboratory reproducing the pyramid-versus-box comparison blinded, with enclosures matched for internal volume, airflow and illuminance (the design the Bhat validation already names), which would move the rat row toward Reading A. A thermal-geometry account of the rat stress panel, which would move it toward Reading B. Primary papers from RKHTYaShM-29 this week, which would add a fresh instrumented stratum to test the split pattern against. A published two-channel energy-balance sweep on the Meyl kit, which would settle the scalar row in an afternoon either way.

## 5. Where We Went Astray

The assumption the corpus undermines: that an effect and its explanation travel together. The pyramid tradition packages faster drying, a pyramid field, and cardinal-point alignment rules as one thing, and the community learned to accept or reject the bundle as one thing. The controlled stratum shows the bundle comes apart: the drying replicates, the field is unnecessary to explain it, and the alignment doctrine rests on a single laboratory's series (the archive's own direction census, cited in the Bhat validation, names that series as the only PubMed-indexed orientation row). The same failure shape appears in the es lane: the REOTOMO thesis measured excitability, the doctrine claims pairs, and the two were treated as interchangeable for a decade. Where this community went astray is in letting replicated observations carry doctrinal freight the observations never tested. The correction is the Yard's own practice: file the observation, the mechanism, and the doctrine as three separate rows, and let each meet its own evidence.

## 6. What This Means

1. Split before filing. Every claim entering the corpus should be separated into observation, mechanism, and doctrine before QC or the Yard records it. The validation files already do this implicitly; the report series and the dossiers should do it explicitly, because the split is where the testable lives.
2. The disagreement rows are the highest-value cards. Where two validations on one rail give different mechanisms, as on the shape-power rail today, that is where the next test design comes from. The Bhat validation already names the design a second lab should run. Prioritize rails with internal disagreement over rails with a single verdict.
3. Audit the doctrine-without-evidence rows first. The orientation rules are the most-repeated practical instruction in the pyramid tradition and rest on one lab's follow-up work. If the census is right, the tradition's most operational claim is its thinnest. A doctrine standing on one laboratory is not a doctrine; it is a pending replication.
