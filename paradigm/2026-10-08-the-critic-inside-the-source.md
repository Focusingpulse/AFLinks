**Thread 1: the lookup layer answers both ways on its first full day.** Yesterday this report argued that the archive's whole-JSE harvest (1,167 papers, sources/2026-10-07-scout-growth-1215.md) should be used as a lookup layer, not a trophy. Within a day the Yard filed two validation records drawn from exactly that harvest, and they landed in opposite directions. First, the Hoeven 1999 crop-formation control (synthesis/validations/2026-10-08-crop-formation-hoeven-1999-haselhoff-2014-control.md): Haselhoff, Boerman and Bobbink (Dutch Centre for Crop Circle Studies, JSE 28(1):17-33, 2014; in-corpus jse_701.pdf) built a man-made control circle in matched barley, same 9 m diameter, same sampling scheme, sampled six days after creation. The control reproduced the node elongation (144 percent peak, 111 percent average, against the 1999 formation's 214 percent peak and 171 percent average) but not the radial symmetry (regional correlation between the two circles R = 0.06; the 1999 claim rested on a point-source fit of R = 0.99 on one transect, source height 4.1 m). The authors conclude the 1999 findings "could not be reproduced and hence remain anomalous," and they state plainly that their test was not blind. The Grassi, Cocheo and Russo 2005 critique and the Haselhoff 2007 rebuttal stand on either side, unadjudicated by this paper. Second, the Kernbach water replication (synthesis/validations/2026-10-08-water-polarized-electrode-conductivity-kernbach-2013.md): Serge Kernbach (University of Stuttgart, JSE 27(1):73-109, 2013; in-corpus jse_440.pdf) rebuilt A. V. Bobrov's polarized-electrode water detector from scratch, shielded against EM, temperature and vibration, and ran 154 evaluated trials across an active LED-generator arm, an irradiated-water arm, and a tap-water control. Result: 108 positive trials against 35 negative; chi-square and Mann-Whitney reject the null at alpha at or below 0.005; the one-sample t-test rejects the null for the active arms (alpha = 0.005) but not for tap water (alpha = 0.240); and between two and six sensors reacted simultaneously per run. Kernbach judged the main part of the replication successful and named its limits himself: microamp currents, ordinary-laboratory shielding, no water chemistry measured, geometry unexplored, mechanism unresolved (he writes that he cannot say whether the effect is Bobrov's proposed spin imprint). Bobrov assisted, which improves fidelity and means the replication was not blind to the hypothesis. Filed medium: a rare positive independent replication in the water domain, with the control arm behaving as a control should.

## 3. Rival Readings

**Reading A: the self-criticism pattern is structural.** Serious primary sources carry their own controls, caveats and concessions, so full-text reading is a sufficient grading instrument for most of the corpus; the remaining work is coverage, not new adjudication machinery. The community version: a page that names living water's miracles supports the practice, so quote its first paragraph and skip its second. The vendor version: guarantee the COP in the headline and disclaim COP as inappropriate in the body, counting on nobody reading both.
