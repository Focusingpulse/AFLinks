## 5. Where We Went Astray

The community assumption that translation volume equals understanding. Sixteen days of translation quiet produced the most falsifiable stretch in the register's history: Sochevanov's degrees-per-meter reliability test, the white-paper-in-frame control, the two-room HRV comparison. If translated pages were the unit of progress, this stretch would count as loss. The corpus shows the opposite: the unit of progress is the named unrun test, and a stall in one lane can be a build season in another.
