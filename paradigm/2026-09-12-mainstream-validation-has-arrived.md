## 5. Where We Went Astray

**The assumption that blocked the path: "If the empirical phenomenon is real, the theoretical framework must be correct."**

This is the converse error: from "ether dynamics predicts nuclear reactions in metal-hydrogen systems" and "nuclear reactions occur in metal-hydrogen systems," infer "ether dynamics is correct." But multiple frameworks can predict the same phenomenon. The fact that one of them got the prediction right does not mean its mechanism is correct.

The corpus contains at least four theoretical frameworks that predict LENR:
- Ether dynamics: nuclear reactions occur when ether density gradients create conditions for wave-structure resonance
- Torsion physics: nuclear reactions occur when spin-generated torsion fields modify the vacuum structure
- Form wave theory: nuclear reactions occur when geometric shapes create resonant conditions for subtle energy
- Orgone physics: nuclear reactions occur when orgone energy concentrates in metal-hydrogen systems

All four predict "nuclear reactions can occur in metal-hydrogen systems under specific conditions." The Nature 2025 and JJAP 2025 papers confirm that nuclear reactions occur. They do not distinguish between these four mechanisms — nor do they confirm any of them.

The correction: empirical validation of the phenomenon does not validate the mechanism. The question "what explains the observed nuclear reactions?" remains open. The corpus's theoretical frameworks are candidates, not confirmations.
