## 6. What This Means

Three implications for how this community processes its material:

1. **Instrument the joints, not the output.** The translation clock measured an output (the newest dated work) and by itself could not say whether the stream was dead. A field's recovery announces itself first as a resumption of the ordinary: a newsletter after 18 days, an association page unfreezing, a proceedings volume finally posting. The ordinary is the leading indicator. Treat it as data.
