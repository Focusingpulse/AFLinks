## 2. The Cross-Domain Claim

The shape-power and radiesthesia complex is not a set of isolated national folklores that happen to agree. It is one trans-European research program with documented citation flow, whose branches converge on the same operator-distance doctrine (the witness sample, the photo channel), and whose every branch carries its own cheapest unrun test. The convergence is real. What it is evidence of is the question, and today the corpus says at least part of it is one idea traveling, not many witnesses discovering.

## 3. Rival Readings

**Reading A: Independent convergence is signal.** Two traditions arriving at the photo-witness doctrine independently would be the strongest kind of evidence this archive can hold: the argument from convergent discovery. The Okhatrin patent tier is deeply instrumented (four rigs with named instruments: the ChS-43 counter, the GNU-KS gravimeter, SRP-2 and Anri-01-02 dosimeters, the S-106 oscilloscope), which suggests a live research program rather than folklore copying.

**Reading B: The convergence is citation flow.** On this reading, agreement between traditions is produced by borrowing and by commerce, not by independent discovery. Evidence: the Kovalenko corpus cites Pagot 1978, a documented French-to-Russian transmission inside the very lane where the convergence claim was raised; the French school's key instrument co-author (Lambert) founded the Maison de la Radiesthesie, the era's radiesthesia commercial hub; the Moineau apparatus was named across 18 years but never technically described; and the maisons a cancer sentence was carried verbatim into the modern training economy. Many claims, few sources.

**Reading C: The doctrine is a convenience that migrates to failure.** The photo-witness exists to solve the operator's distance problem, and every tradition that needs remote operation invents or adopts it. Evidence: the Schauberger replica builders reach for Erdstrahlen (earth rays) exactly where the measured effect gets fragile, and the NET-Journal editors stated the self-fulfilling-prophecy epistemology as editorial policy in 2025 (FAL-de-216-1..5); Desbuquoit conceded in 1948 that no protective neutralizer works in all cases; Ighina's 1972 communiqué claimed positive results 13 days after an eyewitness failure report preserved on the same page (FAL-it-217-1). The doctrine moves to wherever the failure is.

## 4. The Selection

Reading B wins today. The documented transmission row (Kovalenko citing Pagot 1978) is direct evidence of citation flow inside the very lane where the convergence claim was raised, and the French citation chain (Desbuquoit cites Cody; Cody's primary unfindable; Besnard and Lambert a single conditional sentence) shows how few actual sources sit under a multi-decade tradition. Reading A stays alive on the Okhatrin row specifically: I have no documented French-to-Russian transmission for the microlepton patent, and its instrumentation tier is genuinely different in kind from the French apparatus tier. Reading C I keep as the mechanism that explains why B keeps happening: commerce and the operator's distance problem generate the same doctrine on demand. What would change my selection: a pre-1978 Russian photo-witness source, or documentation that the Okhatrin biolocation lineage had no exposure to French radiesthesia literature (either promotes A); a direct French citation inside the Okhatrin corpus (closes the row for B).

## 5. Where We Went Astray

The community assumption that a granted patent is a tested device, and more broadly that N traditions claiming something is N witnesses. The Okhatrin patent is granted, and granting certifies novelty on paper, not efficacy on a bench; the Schauberger split shows the same confusion from the other side (the granted patents are water engineering; the famous Repulsine was never granted). And the witness-counting habit fails on its own terms in this corpus: the three French ionisation instruments turned out to be one citation chain. Convergent claims are a question about provenance, not an answer.
