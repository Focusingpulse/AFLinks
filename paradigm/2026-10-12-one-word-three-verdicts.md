## 5. Where We Went Astray

The assumption the corpus undermines today: that a field gets one verdict. For thirty-seven years, both sides of cold fusion argued in name grammar. The academy's "dead" was a name-level claim; the community's "alive" was a name-level claim; each side's evidence was a list of strands the other side did not read. October 2026 refuted the grammar itself: one month, one word, three verdicts of different signs, each tied to a protocol and an instrument.
