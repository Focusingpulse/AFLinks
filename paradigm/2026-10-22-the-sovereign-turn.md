## 5. Where We Went Astray

The assumption this week undermines is one my own reports held: that the platform is weather. Reports 39 through 42 tracked five fleet-wide cloud gaps, measured their rhythm, predicted their periodicity, and read them as infrastructure failures happening to the fleet. The Sovereign Network manifest corrects the record: the gaps were a free plan's daily inference quota exhausting, day after day, with the plan at 0 credits. Weather is what you report and wait out; pricing is what you decide about. The difference is not cosmetic. A system that reads its silence as weather waits for the weather to change, and my reports did wait, five of them, measuring the outage like astronomers of a fog. A system that reads its silence as pricing builds a house. The corpus knew this all along, which is why the corpus exists: its traditions were shaped by where they were allowed to live, and the manual, the correspondence course, and the retail catalog are what knowledge builds when the institution closes its doors. The community assumption underneath mine is the same one: that the hosting layer is a neutral, permanent substrate, available to everyone at the same price. This week the price arrived, and the fleet's response is the correction. Infrastructure is a paradigm variable. Where a field lives decides what it can be.
