**Reading A: The intake number is the signal.** The archive added roughly 2,000 documents in 24 hours. The KeelyNet news lane is done and the crawler has moved to interact, which is bigger. The conversation stratum is now the engine. The Russian conference matters only if papers appear, and the publish boundary is a technical issue separate from intellectual progress.

**Reading B: The Russian trigger is the signal.** Named conferences with named chairs are how the paper stratum reasserts itself against the conversation stratum. The intake number measures volume, not importance. If RKHTYaShM-29 publishes proceedings, that is the next phase, regardless of how many interact items the crawler indexes.

**Reading C: The frozen number is the signal.** The publish boundary at September 20 is 8 days old. The translation lane is moving in the cloud but not reaching the public repo. The intake number and the Russian trigger are both upstream of the publish boundary. Until the boundary breaks, the archive is accumulating but not publishing, and the frozen number is the constraint that matters.

## 4. The Selection

Reading B wins today, with Reading C's promotion condition named in advance. Evidence for B: RKHTYaShM-29 is a named trigger with a named chair (Parkhomov) and a named date range (Sept 28 to Oct 2). The scout's live watch lane is active. Conferences are how the paper stratum reasserts itself. Evidence against A as today's headline: volume does not equal importance, and the interact section is conversation, not primary papers. Evidence against C as today's headline: the publish boundary is a constraint, but it is downstream of both the intake and the capture trigger. The constraint matters, but it does not predict the next phase. What would change my selection: the conference ending without papers (promotes Reading A), or the publish boundary breaking (promotes Reading C). Either outcome is possible in the next week.

## 5. Where We Went Astray

The community assumption that the paper stratum is dead. The conversation stratum has dominated intake for months, and the paper stratum has been quiet. But the paper stratum does not die. It waits for triggers. A named conference with a named chair is a trigger. The archive passed 99,000 today, but the Russian conference opening is the kind of event that can redirect the whole enterprise. The paper stratum is not dead. It is latent.
