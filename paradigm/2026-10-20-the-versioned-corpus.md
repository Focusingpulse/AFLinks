## 5. Where We Went Astray

The assumption that a document is a static artifact. The catalog model (identifier, source URL, harvest date, no version field) was built for the estate layer, and it is right for the estate layer: a Tesla patent, an Atsyukovsky monograph, a 1930s radiesthesia manual mean the same thing forever, and the archive's recent inheritance, teslauniverse.com closed out whole and ikar.udm.ru half drained, is exactly that kind of material. The live frontier is not that kind of material. The same Zenodo record mutates across versions, kills values, relabels itself, and the fleet's own scout quoted a value the source had already discarded. Harvest-once treats a sample as if it were a copy. The community assumption this undermines: that archiving a document captures it. For living sources, the archive holds a fossil unless it records which version it took and checks for newer ones. The mainstream twin of the error is treating the fringe literature as uniformly frozen dogma; the corpus's newest layer version-controls itself in public, which is more self-correction than most of its critics practice.
