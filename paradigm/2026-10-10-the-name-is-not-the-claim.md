## 3. Rival Readings

**Reading A: convergence without contact is the signal.** Three research establishments with no significant contact arrived at the same identification of the carrier. The Japanese groups measured spiral-motion qi effects on water conductivity and photomultiplier dark current before they knew the torsion word. The Chinese program measured named masters against physical instrumentation for thirty years and landed on the spin field. The detector strand already holds one positive independent replication in this corpus (Kernbach's Stuttgart rebuild of the Bobrov water detector, 154 trials, control arm quiet, graded in Report 30). On this reading, independent arrival at one carrier is what a real object looks like, and the program's own kill-tests (the photo-swap, the Faraday-sealed receiver, temperature-controlled dark-current metrology) deserve to be run rather than presumed failed.

**Reading B: the convergence is vocabulary diffusion plus selection, and the hardened verdicts have already landed.** The Japanese node adopted torsion terminology after contact, by its own account. The Beijing laboratory exists because its founder translated the Russian book. Li Si-Chen's identification explicitly compares his results against the Russian line as a foreign body of work. Those are transmission lines, not independence. And when metrology hardens, the claimed effects fall below threshold (PulsePen 2025); the materials strand is a triple negative inside state academies; the family's citation ecology bottoms out in a vendor's shop blog cited as physics in an academy paper. On this reading, this week's map is a map of a rumor's wiring, drawn in admirable detail.

**Reading C: the bundle has no single fate, so unbundle it.** "Torsion" names at least four claim strands, and this week's own filings already grade them separately. Materials: triple negative (Ukraine). Detectors: one non-blind positive (Kernbach on Bobrov) with a known temperature-artifact exposure, since dark current is exquisitely temperature-sensitive. Communication: scheduled distance claims, zero blind third-party replications, and one decisive experiment named and never published. Biology and form: applied claims (varroa, truffle, dowsing) testable at garden scale, with the 1913 digging commission as the historical template and its mixed verdict printed whole. On this reading, A over-reads the convergence because the independence has documented exceptions, and B over-reads the hardened verdicts because each was earned on one specific strand and then stretched over the whole name.

## 4. The Selection

Reading C wins today, and the decisive reason is that the corpus itself has already unbundled: every validation record filed this week carries a strand and a strand-level confidence grade, not a verdict on the name. A's convergence evidence is real but weaker than advertised at exactly the joints where independence matters (renaming after contact, translation as the lab's origin, explicit comparison in the identifying paper). B's null results are real and load-bearing, and each is scoped: a sintered tin-bronze coupon says nothing about water cells, and a water cell says nothing about an 11,000 km photograph channel. Blending A and B into a middle position would destroy exactly the information their disagreement carries (Maryanskyy 2026); the function of this report is to select, not to smooth. ## 5. Where We Went Astray

The assumption the corpus undermines today: that a field's name is a claim. "Torsion is real" and "torsion is pseudoscience" are the same category error in opposite colors. A carrier name gathers a bundle of claims, and then both cultures grade the bundle as one proposition: the academy commission condemns the name, the distance records defend the name, while the actual evidence keeps arriving dressed as one strand at a time (a sintered bronze coupon, a CR-39 chip, a bag of numbered table-tennis balls, a cave plan drawn to the millimeter). The mainstream version grades the name down on one strand's null; the community version grades the name up on one strand's convergence; both skip the strand. The 1913 Academy had the right unit of adjudication a century ago: its members did not debate radiesthesia as a name, they dug where the dowser said to dig, and they printed the dry well in the same report as the hits. The name is a filing cabinet. The claim is the thing you can dig.

## 6. What This Means

1. **File by strand, not by name.** When a source says torsion, aether, scalar, or qi, the first grading question is which strand its evidence actually touches: materials, detectors, communication, or biology. A state-academy null on sintered metal is a verdict on the materials strand and, simultaneously, a finding about that strand's citation ecology (a vendor blog cited as physics); it is not a verdict on water detectors. A Stuttgart water-detector positive is a verdict on the detector strand; it is not a verdict on interstellar communication. The community should refuse both directions of the stretch, because each stretch is the category error of Section 5.

2. **Run the named decisive experiments, cheapest first.** The blind photo-swap needs existing hardware and one afternoon. The Yard's home cards are garden-scale: the coil-geometry water dossier filed today, the Risy varroa and truffle protocols once the seeder writes them, the crop-formation blind radial test next growing season. The 1913 commission is the template: choose the claim you can dig, dig it, and print the dry wells next to the hits. Five blind home results would outrank another year of distance records.
