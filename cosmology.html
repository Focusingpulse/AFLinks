Why twelve sides?

Most people see cosmology from one or two sides — the mainstream story, or the dissent from it. This page exists because the interesting patterns show up when you rotate the object and look from many angles at once: what each side agrees on is often more revealing than what they argue about.

Here you'll find the standard model defended by its best communicators, the strongest critics of its foundations, and the historical researchers who found solar-biological connections the mainstream forgot. Nothing here is endorsed. Nothing here is dismissed. Every position is presented as its holder would state it, with the evidence they stand on.

Honest framing: this vault is 94% viXra/Rex/tuks ecology — a specific alternative community, not a representative sample of "hidden science." The value is in what becomes visible when all these communities are collected side by side, including the mainstream they dissent from.

Featured Collection

Pierre-Marie Robitaille — The Sky Scholar

PhD, chemical spectroscopy · Professor of Radiology, Ohio State (MRI instrumentation) · 124+ research works · YouTube: Sky Scholar

A real scientist with a real publication record — his early career was mainstream: MRI instrumentation, thermal emission physics, IEEE and peer-reviewed journals. Then he turned his spectroscopy expertise on astrophysics and concluded the foundations are broken. His central claims:

1. The Sun is condensed matter, not gas. Gases cannot produce a blackbody spectrum (the stellar opacity problem). The Sun is layered liquid metallic hydrogen with a true surface — evidenced by sunspots, granules, limb darkening, the chromosphere's continuous emission. His "Forty Lines of Evidence for Condensed Matter — The Sun on Trial" is the master argument.

2. Kirchhoff's law of thermal emission is invalid. If Kirchhoff is wrong, the entire edifice of stellar astrophysics built on cavity-radiation arguments wobbles — this is his deepest attack, aimed at the 1860 foundation of astrophysical spectroscopy.

3. The CMB is not cosmic. The "cosmic" microwave background is dominated by Earth-bound and galactic foreground water signals — the satellites measured oceans and hydrogen bonds, not the Big Bang's afterglow.

4. Black hole images are artifacts. The EHT ring is a product of limited uv-coverage and narrow field of view — "indistinguishable from artifact." This is his most credible attack, because he is not alone: Japanese radio astronomers (Miyoshi et al., NAOJ) independently re-analyzed the public EHT data and found a core-knot-jet structure, no ring — published in a mainstream journal. The fight is live, not settled: the EHT Collaboration published a formal rebuttal (2024) arguing Miyoshi's method pre-assumes its own morphology and ignores Sgr A*'s minute-scale variability, and that their pipeline reproduces non-ring structures when present. Neither side has closed the case — this is an open technical dispute between specialists, and the honest status is "contested, unresolved."

Status: A claim is a claim until tested. Robitaille's spectroscopy critiques are technically argued and deserve engagement, not dismissal — but his positions are rejected by mainstream astrophysics. The archive preserves both his papers and the mainstream's responses. Watch his strongest falsifiable thread: if gases truly cannot emit blackbody spectra, laboratory opacity measurements should show it — that's checkable.

The Sun-Biology Bridge

Alexander Chizhevsky — Heliobiology

1897–1964 · Soviet biophysicist · founder of heliobiology & aero-ionization · worked with Tsiolkovsky · invited by Arrhenius & Columbia University

The "Russian solar biology guy." Chizhevsky spent decades documenting that solar activity — the ~11-year sunspot cycle — correlates with biological and social phenomena on Earth: epidemics, tree rings, wheat harvests, and human "mass excitability" (riots, revolutions, wars). He analyzed sunspot records against historical events from 500 BCE to 1922 CE across 71 countries and found major collective events cluster around sunspot maximum.

His work was suppressed at home (he spent years in the gulag-era camps) and dismissed abroad, but the core observation — solar cycles modulating terrestrial biology — has since been partially rehabilitated. Modern research on solar-geomagnetic effects on melatonin, circadian rhythms, and cardiovascular events descends directly from his program. Edward R. Dewey's Foundation for the Study of Cycles published and extended his data.

The connection to this vault: Thomas Joseph Brown's longitudinal-instant-connection view of sunlight (the "real-time connection" between star and viewer) extends Chizhevsky's program into the aether-physics frame — if the Sun's influence isn't carried only by 8-minute photons but by faster-than-light longitudinal waves (Tesla/Dollard LMD), then heliobiology gains a mechanism the mainstream never tested. That's a claim, not a finding — but it's a claim with a lineage, and this archive preserves the whole lineage.

Status: The sunspot-biology correlation is real and replicated in modern studies (solar cycle effects on human physiology are published in mainstream journals). The mechanism is the open question. The superluminal-longitudinal mechanism remains unverified — no replication exists yet. A prime candidate for the Replication Yard.

The Mainstream Anchor

Neil deGrasse Tyson — The Standard Model's Voice

PhD astrophysics, Columbia · Director, Hayden Planetarium · host of Cosmos: A Spacetime Odyssey

Included because you can't see the shape of the disagreement without the strongest statement of the consensus. Tyson represents the position Robitaille, Alfvén, and the archive's ecology argue against: black holes are real and well-tested, dark matter is a real (if undetected) substance, the Big Bang happened, and the CMB is its afterglow.

His core positions: Black holes are "the universe's most fascinating objects" — confirmed by gravitational waves and direct imaging. Dark matter: "we know it's there because gravity tells us" — the evidence is gravitational even though the particle is undetected. The CMB is the best-measured signal in all of science and the strongest evidence for the Big Bang.

Why he's on the dial: the honest 12-side view needs the mainstream stated at its best, not caricatured. Every critic above is arguing against this position — so it belongs in the record. His popularizations (Cosmos, Origins, Astrophysics for People in a Hurry, the StarTalk podcast) are the reference frame most people actually learned cosmology from.

Status: Mainstream consensus, well-supported on black holes (LIGO ~300 mergers, EHT imaging, Sgr A* stellar orbits), weaker on dark matter (zero direct detections in 40+ years — the honest tension this page exists to hold).

🧪 The Lab Bench — where the argument meets the table

the replicable experiments. claims you can check, ranked by how checkable they are

Steinhauer's analog black holes (mainstream, replicated math). You can't build a gravitational black hole in a lab — but Jeff Steinhauer built the math of one in a Bose-Einstein condensate of ultracold rubidium atoms, and Hawking radiation appeared exactly as Hawking's equations predict: thermal spectrum, entangled Hawking/partner pairs straddling the sonic horizon (Nature Physics, 2016). This is the strongest evidence that black hole evaporation is real physics, not just formalism — and it's tabletop-replicable. Any theory that kills Hawking radiation has to explain this bench.

ALPHA-g antimatter gravity (mainstream, decisive). CERN dropped magnetically-trapped antihydrogen, 2023: it fell down, 0.75 ± 0.13 ± 0.16 g. Repulsive antimatter gravity — the pillar of Dirac-Milne cosmology and several aether-adjacent models — is ruled out at better than one in a quadrillion. The paper states it plainly: results "do not support cosmological models relying on repulsive matter–antimatter gravitation." An alternative theory made a falsifiable prediction, the test ran, the theory lost. That is the system working — and it's why the ledger above now says falsified.

SAFIRE plasma chamber (alternative, unreplicated). Montgomery Childs' team built a chamber producing stable spherical plasma double layers around an anode — the exact geometry the Electric Sun model claims the Sun has. Their own reports claim Lyman-alpha photon reservoirs, 110,000 K probe deformation, hints of LENR transmutation, no radioactivity. But: no peer-reviewed publication, no independent replication, and the project went commercial (Aureon) after the foundation mandate ended. The claims are checkable in principle — and nobody outside the team has checked them. Flagged as a Replication Yard candidate: the double-layer geometry itself is reproducible with modest lab equipment.

MOND vs ΛCDM (theory, discriminating). The single cheapest discriminator in this whole fight: MOND predicts galactic rotation curves with one parameter and no particle; ΛCDM needs a particle 40 years of detectors haven't found. The galaxy-scale data increasingly fits MOND better; cluster and CMB data still needs dark matter. Whoever resolves that tension wins the argument.
