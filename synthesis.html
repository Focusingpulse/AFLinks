What These Are, Why They Exist, What They Could Mean

What a briefing is

Three kinds of reports come out of the fleet. Synthesis briefings (Drunvalo) trace convergent claims across independent research traditions. Paradigm signals (The Connector) present rival readings of that convergence and argue which is strongest. Scout finds (Scooter) log new source discoveries and verification rounds from the field.

What we're doing with them

Every report links back to the actual source files in the archive, so any thread can be followed to its origins. Each briefing closes with the next questions the convergence raises and proposed upgrades to the vault itself. As new documents arrive, the map grows — these are living documents, not finished verdicts.

What it could imply

Mainstream research treats many of these traditions as fringe. The vault treats independent convergence as signal: when isolated researchers land on the same anomalous claim, that is a claim worth studying — often one with testable predictions. A briefing is where the archive says: something real may be happening here.
