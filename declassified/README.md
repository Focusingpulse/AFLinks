# Declassified Documents Section

## What This Is

A curated index of declassified, newly-opened, and publicly-released government documents relevant to the Living Library's research domains: torsion physics, ether/aether theories, LENR/cold fusion, consciousness studies, plasma cosmology, water structure, cymatics/geometry, regenerative agriculture, and borderland research.

These are documents that governments have **chosen to make public** — through official declassification, FOIA releases, archival openings, or legislative disclosure. We do not host classified material. We curate metadata and link to official sources.

## Why It Matters

Declassified documents are where suppressed or forgotten research surfaces into the public record. They show what governments knew, when they knew it, and what they chose to hide — and what they eventually chose to release. The patterns across countries and decades reveal the real history of the fields we study.

## Cross-Reference Framework

A document declassified by Country A may reference programs in Country B that Country B has not declassified. This is **expected and legal** — it's Country A's decision to release. We track these cross-references explicitly:

- **origin**: The country that declassified/released the document
- **references**: Other countries whose programs are described in the document
- **cross_ref_note**: What the cross-reference reveals

This lets you see the pattern: French archives referencing German nuclear cooperation, CIA archives documenting Soviet psychotronics programs, Pentagon files on UAP sightings over US nuclear facilities — each country's declassified files illuminate another's still-classified programs.

## Sourcing Rules

1. **Only public sources.** Documents must be already declassified or publicly released by an official government body, or reported by credible journalism citing official sources.
2. **Always record provenance.** Who declassified what, when, through which portal or mechanism.
3. **Link to official sources.** We link to government archives, official publications, and credible news sources. We do not mirror classified files.
4. **Flag fragility.** Sources on fringe sites (earth-chronicles.ru, legitim.ch, etc.) are flagged for archival preservation.
5. **Never self-censor.** If a government chose to declassify it, it's public. We curate it.

## Metadata Format

Each find includes:
- **Title** (original language + English)
- **Source URL**
- **Language** (document language)
- **Origin** (who declassified/released)
- **References** (other countries mentioned)
- **Domain(s)**
- **Work type** (legal decree, intelligence assessment, archival inventory, etc.)
- **Practical applicability**
- **Key entities** (people, organizations, programs)
- **Host stability** (high/medium/low)
- **Verification** (official source, peer-reviewed, needs verification)
- **Release date** (when the document was declassified/opened)
- **Cross-ref note** (what cross-references reveal)
