## Details

Digital "Sicherheitsplattform Strom" under construction since 2024. ~4,300 large consumers (>8 GWh) to be registered. In crisis: 3-day notice to reduce consumption, non-compliance → shutdown. Functional minimal version autumn 2026, full system winter 2026/2027. Amprion warns safety margins may fail by 2028. Gap: 40 GW needed vs 12 GW planned by 2035.
