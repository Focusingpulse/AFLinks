## Details

Pégase (INB n°22) was a 35 MW research reactor at Cadarache, operated 1963-1975 for fuel testing. Converted to radioactive storage in 1980. Dismantling authorized in 5 stages through 2065. Part of France's dual-track nuclear policy: dismantle old while building new EPR2 at Gravelines.
