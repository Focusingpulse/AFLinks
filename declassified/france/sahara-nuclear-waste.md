## Cross-Reference

France buried hundreds of tonnes of contaminated waste in the Algerian Sahara during nuclear tests (1960-1967). The burial locations were not documented in publicly accessible records. Algeria has requested topographic maps since 2021. An IAEA assessment in 1999 was insufficient — sensitive zones remained inaccessible. Four underground tests may not have been fully contained.

**Cross-ref note:** This is France's colonial nuclear legacy. The declassification push is about France releasing its own records about waste it left in a former colony's territory. Algeria cannot find the waste without France's maps.
