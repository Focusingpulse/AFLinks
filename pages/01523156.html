Infinity Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Speaker Glenn Borchardt Date 7 November 2020 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording Infinity was a live session presented by Glenn Borchardt on 7 November 2020 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. Dr. Borchardt has over 50 years of theoretical, experimental, and observational experience as a scientist especially interested in scientific philosophy. Although he has produced over 500 scientific reports, including journal articles, chapters, books, consulting reports, and computer programs, the best by far is his book, "The Scientific Worldview: Beyond Newton and Einstein." It introduced univironmental determinism as the universal mechanism of evolution and the proper basis for scientific philosophy. It challenges the current, wildly popular, though absurd claim that the universe is finite and that it exploded out of nothing. This theme was developed in detail in my recent book, "Infinite Universe Theory." Both books are completely logical from beginning to end in support of infinite universe theory as a replacement for the Big Bang Theory. The switch from the assumption of finity to the assumption infinity will result in the Last Cosmological Revolution. Summary Glenn Borchardt on infinity and the philosophical vocabulary behind it, in conversation with David de Hilster , who introduced him as the philosopher whose work his and his father's particle model stands on. The big bang was deliberately held over to a later session; this one was about the assumptions. Why a scientist needs philosophy Borchardt's opening observation was that he holds a doctorate of philosophy and never took a course in philosophy, and that the reason is that what philosophy departments teach is largely religious, which is of no use in science.
