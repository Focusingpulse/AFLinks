The Earth's history The core of every macro-object in the model is a reactor fuelled by universe-created particles, and all chemical elements, compositions and substances are produced within it by their self-annihilation. Everything else in the talk follows from that. The Earth's core was born, on this account, from the explosive rotational fusion of the Sun's core 4.57 billion years ago, and the Moon's from the Earth's at the same date.
