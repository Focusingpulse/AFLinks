Probably posted it as comment somewhere, but here is for real. Here I uploaded a number of the microfilm prints of Tesla's correspondence with George Scherff and J.P. Morgan. (there are about 2700 pages and I have only uploaded a few hundred) They tell details that can not be found elsewhere in articles, patents or notes. Most of it has more historical value than technical but is still very interesting. Some letters can barely be read and some can not be read at all. Some because of the poor quality of the microfilm process, some because of poor contrast and some because of the handwriting. It would of course be very nice to have these transcribed..... It is an awful lot of work for one person, but there are thousands of us here.... So if you have a couple of minutes to spare and would like to do something to preserve Tesla's legacy, download a letter, and post a transcription here....
