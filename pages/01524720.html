Whatever their origin and purpose, they march on in the face of media indifference and scepticism, continuing to attract new seekers and raising questions about the very nature of reality. for if even a handful of crop formations can be shown to lie beyond the boundaries of human-made art, as many observers believe has already been demonstrated, then the world is clearly not as it has been sold to us by narrow scientism.
