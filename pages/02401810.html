GTM2026-main029 Modeling opinion formation on video-hosting platforms with a feedback loop Ilya A. Sukharev1,2 , Ivan V. Kozitsin1,3 1 V.A. Trapeznikov Institute of Control Problems of the Russian Academy of Sciences. 2 Lomonosov Moscow State University, Moscow, Russia 3 Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (State University) ilia.sukharev@math.msu.ru We introduce an agent-based model that illustrates user behavior on online video-sharing platforms. The model involves two types of actors: agents (information consumers) and channels (information producers). After interacting with channels, agents can update their opinions and upvote the content they are exposed to. In describing these processes, our model integrates both individual psychological biases (such as selective exposure and confirmation bias) and platform-specific mechanics. These include popularity bias (promoting high-popularity content that receives many likes) and individualization (e.g., providing users with opinion-consistent or, conversely, opinion-discordant content). Additionally, our model considers a specific attribute of information known as content quality and incorporates quality-aware mechanisms for information processing. By combining theoretical and computational approaches, we derive a mean-field description of the model, prove its convergence to the model dynamics, identify equilibria, and analyze the asymptotic behavior of the model.
