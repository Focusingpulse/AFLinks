IV. The Three States of Consciousness — GA 10. Initiation and Its Results (1909)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 2,390,731
The life of man is passed in three states, which are as follows: waking, dreaming sleep, and dreamless deep sleep. One may comprehend how to attain to a higher knowledge of the spiritual worlds by forming an idea of the changes in the conditions that have to be undergone by the aspirant to such knowledge. Before a person has passed through the necessary training, his consciousness is continually broken by the periods of rest which accompany sleep.
