Theosophy in the Gospels — An Easter Reflection Article in “Deutschland, Weimarische Landeszeitung”, Second Edition, March 27, 1904 [ 1 ] Theosophical Lecture. On Friday evening, Dr. Rudolf Steiner delivered the sixth of his theosophical lectures on “Theosophy in the Gospels: An Easter Reflection” in the large recreation hall. [ 2 ] The speaker demonstrated the profound meaning that can be found in the legends and myths of various peoples when one seeks to understand them from the perspective of a symbolic view of nature.
