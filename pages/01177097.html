Email: weilinxu@ciac.ac.cn Date: 2022-11-14 Abstract: We propose a new concept of “molecule aging”, in which the molecules are treated by some special methods to produce some different properties but with no apparent structural changes of molecules. Such “aging” is different from the traditional concept of “aging” in which the breaking of chemical bond is involved. We hope the construction of new instrument platform with ultrahigh sensitivity and resolution and new theoretical model describing new aging molecules can induce positive effect in all kinds of fields, such as energy and life process. 1.
