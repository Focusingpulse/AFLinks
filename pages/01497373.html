(courtesy University of Science and Philosophy ) ( click to enlarge ) Dale Pond Years ago, driving in the early morning hours along the New York Thruway (it was quiet almost no traffic) on my way to deliver a dynasphere, I heard these powerful emotional sweet words: "I have given you everything. I have nothing more to give you." I broke down emotionally realizing these words came from God. I weeped all the way to Maine, in gratitude and thankfulness. [ Dale Pond ] As one advances through their spiritual growth there will be times of uncontrollable weeping . This is a release and re-balancing of the physical, mental and spiritual systems. Manly Palmer Hall "The lost key is the mastery of emotion. Through each one of us courses the fiery rays of human emotion, a great seething cauldron of power behind each expression of the dynamic energy of human experience. Through the perversion of human emotions there comes into the world untold sorrow, which through reaction, manifests in the mental and physical bodies. The Great Work of the Master can be called the art of balance. It can be truly said of the Master that through suffering she or he has learned to be glad; through weeping , has learned to smile; and through dying, has learned to live. The Master is not ordained, but is the natural product of cause and effect, and none but those who live the cause can produce the effect."
