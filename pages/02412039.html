What is the SMOT ? This is a very simple Free Energy demonstrator that anyone can build easily. The principle is very simple : a steel ball climbs a magnetic ramp, the magnetic energy is converted into kinetic/potential energy and the "regauging" effect is done by the gravity during the drop...
