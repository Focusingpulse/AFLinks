YouTube - LidMotor Solar CFL Light I used one small CFL bulb as it uses less energy and put out almost as much light as the 13 watt CFL. The small CFL runs at full brightness! I used a plastic container I got from the dollar store (as it was only $1.) The light uses 5 AA batteries in series (2650mah) and lasts for over 9 hours on a full charge. This light would be great for anyone that is camping, fishing or out on a boat. To charge the battery bank I am using a 12 volt solar panel that puts out 12 volts @ 125ma (max) so it should charge in all light conditions but not enough amps (current) to overcharge the battery bank. I am very pleased with this project and will build 2 more just like it to use up the remainder of the cfl drivers that I got. What a neat project!
