"Franklin's Five Universal (omni-sectarian) Principles of all Sound Religion" by Benjamin Franklin "Here is my creed : 1 - I believe in one God , the Creator of the universe. 2 - That he governs it by his providence. 3 - That he ought to be worshipped. 4 - That the most acceptable service we render to him is in doing good to his other children. 5 - That the soul of man is immortal, and will be treated with justice in another life respecting its conduct in this. These I take to be the fundamental points in all sound religion ."
