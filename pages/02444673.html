Judge Mathis on "Entitled to the Success of Others"

· archived # 2,444,673
08/13/13 - Judge Mathis on "Entitled to the Success of Others" Plaintiff wins lotto and parasite brother takes advantage. Judge Mathis takes off on this towards the end of this video to comment on some people who feel they are entitled to a part of others success. This couple wins $225,00 in the lottery and this family member BELIEVES it is part his. I see this and hear this in Mexico all the time. Here, if some native people see you have a vehicle, decent house, spending money, etc...they feel you OWE THEM. One true story: A few years back a married American couple lived in a large, comfortable house, had two cars, nice furnishings, nice clothes, they travelled often and were not ostentatious, just enjoying their retirement years. They employed a married mexican couple to cook, clean and garden for them. This American couple gave them gifts and paid holidays off, vacation time and helped them when they needed it. I don't recall the exact details but something along the lines of the mexican couple asked to borrow several thousand dollars for some luxury item and the American couple denied them, so they quit. The mexican woman went all over town saying how these people were cheap and treated them badly and boldly stating that BECAUSE the American couple WERE RICH, they OWED IT TO THESE MEXICANS TO SHARE IT WITH THEM. Those of us who knew the story and the people were appalled that these miserable people would publicly defame this nice couple who had often gone out of their way to help the mexican couple and were now maliciously saying they EXPECTED it (to be given gifts, money and more) because it was OWED to them since the Americans were successful and RICH. I later found that many here think like that. That 'mi casa es su casa' is total crap ...they take it to mean your house and everything in your possession is part theirs.
