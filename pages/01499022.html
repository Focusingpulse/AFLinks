The original Chinese version of this paper begins on p. 14（本文中文版从第 14 页开始） Review of Mining Model for Scientific Discoveries Hongzhou Zhao (赵红州) & Guohua Jiang (蒋国华) Beijing Research Centre for Science of Science, Beijing, China Translated by Jed Rothwell and Wu-Shou Zhang Abstract The process of scientific discovery resembles mining operations. It advances continuously across different spatial scales and energy levels of matters, with distinct scientific fields becoming “mineable disciplines” in different eras. The historical condition for the shift of mineable disciplines is the discovery of fundamental energy conversion effects inherent in the current mineable level. Before this condition is met, scientific history often witnesses large-scale “back￾mining” phenomena. Both “mineable” and “back-mining” are crucial factors influencing scientific development and effective means for predicting future strategic trends in science.
