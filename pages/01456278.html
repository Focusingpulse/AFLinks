Charles Alderton

· archived # 1,456,278
Long before Coca-Cola or Pepsi hit the shelves, a Texas pharmacist named Charles Alderton was experimenting behind the counter of Morrison’s Old Corner Drug Store in Waco. He loved the smell of his shop, a mix of fruit syrups, spices, and medicine, and wanted to create a drink that tasted the way his store smelled. So, he began blending different syrups until he found the perfect combination of flavors. The result was a drink with 23 mysterious ingredients, fruity, spicy, and completely unique. Customers at the soda fountain loved it so much that Alderton named it Dr Pepper, and by 1885, it became the oldest major soft drink in America.
