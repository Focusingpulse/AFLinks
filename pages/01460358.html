Law of Matter and Force "Coextensive and coeternal with space and duration , there exists an infinite and unchangeable quantity of atomoles , the base of all matter ; these are in a state of constant vibratory motion, infinite in extent, unchangeable in quantity, the initial of all forms of energy ."
