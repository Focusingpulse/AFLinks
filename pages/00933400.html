Anomalous datasets reveal metagenomic fabrication pipeline that further questions the legitimacy of RaTG13 genome and the associated Nature paper John F. Signus ABSTRACT Recently, Daoyu Zhang et. Al [1], Mona Ralker et. Al [2] and Mohit Singla et. al [3] have reported exceptional anomalies associated with the RaTG13 metagenomic dataset which was inconsistent with that of a real fecal sample. Despite extensive search, we are only able to isolate 2 datasets Other than RaTG13 itself, that possessed significant levels of non-adaptor repeat sequences and absence of bacteria in the context of “bat” and “fecal” or “virome”. Furthermore, the analysis of such datasets have revealed an established pipeline of which a viral sequence is “rehosted”—e.g. added to a metagenomic sample that originally did not contain such viral sequences.
