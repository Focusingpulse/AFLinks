The Flood and PATHS

· archived # 2,431,537
We have probably all heard the story of the man who prayed to God to save him from a flood. He kept turning down help from people in boats and finally a helecopter, saying he had prayed to God and trusted God would save him. He died and when he met God he asked him why God did not save him. God replied that he kept sending people to help but the guy turned them all down. One of the ways PATHS works is to draw people, things, and experiences to you to help you with your desired module(s). Do not confuse the situation. If you go on the business productivity module and suddenly get new business, you would probably attribute that to PATHS. But I've had a client on the skin module who's skin was looking better, but the client mentioned that someone gave them some wonderful skin cream shortly after going on PATHS. Is it PATHS or the cream?!?! You can go on and off the modules to prove it to yourself, but please understand that PATHS works on many levels XO Jessica
