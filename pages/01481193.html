law of conservation of energy

· archived # 1,481,193
The law of conservation of energy is an empirical law of physics . It states that the total amount of energy in an isolated system remains constant over time (is said to be conserved over time ). A consequence of this law is that energy can neither be created nor destroyed: it can only be transformed from one state to another.
