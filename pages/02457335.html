KEELY 40laws

· archived # 2,457,335
Preface to Keelys' Laws of Sympathetic Vibration John W. Keely worked out a system detailing the laws which govern the fusion of energy into what we perceive as molecular aggregations. Keelys' Laws of Vibration also describe the resonant relationships which occur between mass aggregations. Publisher and author, William J. Colville was a close friend of Keely. Colville was also a student of metaphysics. His friendship with Keely resulted in the sharing of many of Keelys' research findings, including forty of Keelys' laws. These laws were included in a little-known metaphysical novel, "Dashed Against the Rock", which was published in 1894. "Dashed Against the Rock" is the story of a young establishment scientist who is led through stages to the acceptance of the vibratory laws which govern mental science. Dan A. Davidson, in his book, "A Breakthrough to New Free Energy Sources", gives an excellent condensed history of Keely and his work.
