kimo2026-main211 Smart Cities and AI in BRICS Countries: New Opportunities for Strategic Communication - Panel "BRICS Strategic Communication in Global Governance: Main Directions, Methods, Prospects" Atahualpa P. Blanchet1,1 1 Researcher Ponencia invitada atahclub@gmail.com Paper abstract: Urbanization challenges intersect with technological transformation in the BRICS bloc, driving diverse applications of artificial intelligence (AI) across major cities. With strategic public policy frameworks emerging under the Brazilian BRICS presidency, AI is increasingly embedded in solutions for mobility, energy management, waste logistics, and risk resilience in metropolitan contexts. This study examines how AI innovations in BRICS urban environments are not only reshaping city infrastructures but are also creating new opportunities for strategic communication between governments, citizens, and global partners. Drawing on case examples including AI-driven traffic management systems in São Paulo and integrated urban platforms in Shanghai, the paper analyzes how these technologies influence public narratives, governance legitimacy, and international cooperation mechanisms. Methodologically, the research combines comparative policy analysis, document review (including official BRICS digital publications), and interviews with urban planners and communication strategists. Key findings highlight the role of AI as a communication catalyst in building inclusive public services and enhancing narratives of sustainable development within and among BRICS nations.
