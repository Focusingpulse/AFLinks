Warfield (born 15 February 1947) is an American physician based in Scottsdale, Arizona, who has presented a long series of papers to the Natural Philosophy Alliance on electromagnetic propulsion, the structure of the electron, and an aether cosmology built around the idea of "inflowing space". Research Inflowing space Warfield's foundational proposal, set out in " New Theory of Flowing Space (an Alternative to Einstein's GRT) " (2006) and " Consequences of the Theory of Inflowing Space " (2007), is that gravitation is the motion of space itself toward a massive body
