Preface — Christianity as a Mystical Fact (1961)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 2,386,986
Christianity as a Mystical Fact was the title given to this book by its author when eight years ago he included in it the contents of lectures held in the year 1902. This title was intended to indicate the particular character of the book. It represents an attempt to describe not merely the mystical content of Christianity in its historical form, but how Christianity arose out of mystical conception.
