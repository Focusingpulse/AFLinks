Weber's force between point charges is explored and thoroughly analysed. Amp're's force between current elements is discussed in connection with modern experiments relating to the Amp're versus Grassmann--Biot--Savart controversy. Amp're's force is a central feature of this work, as Maxwell maintained it should always be in the study of electrodynamics, although it is included in few textbooks on electromagnetism. A detailed study of this force is an outstanding feature of this book.
