Qing Zeng Born ( 1958-01-14 ) January 14, 1958 (age 68) Yuechi County, Guang'an, Sichuan, China Residence Wuhan, Hubei, China Nationality Chinese Alma mater University of Electronic Science and Technology of China Known for Criticism of Maxwell curl field theory and relativity Scientific career Fields Physics , Electromagnetism Institutions Air Force Radar Academy (PLA) Zeng Qingping ( Chinese : 曾清平), also rendered Qing Zeng , born January 14, 1958, is a Chinese physicist and a professor at the Air Force Radar Academy of the People's Liberation Army. He is known within the dissident-science community for his critical writings on the Maxwell curl field theory , electromagnetic induction , the constancy of the speed of light, and special relativity . Education Undergraduate study — University of Electronic Science and Technology of China, 1979 to 1983 No record of a postgraduate degree was found for this article. Zeng holds the rank of professor at the Air Force Radar Academy of the People's Liberation Army. Biography Zeng was born in Yuechi County, Guang'an City, Sichuan province, China. From 1979 to 1983 he studied at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China, where he received his college education. He later became a professor at the Air Force Radar Academy of the People's Liberation Army, based in Wuhan, Hubei province. Scientific contributions Zeng states that his questioning of established physical theory began during his university years, prompted by his physics teacher and by discussion among his classmates during China's "Spring of Science" period. From 1998 onward he published a series of papers that questioned the Maxwell curl field theory and identified what he considered to be errors in its origins. His work argues that disputes exist in the physical interpretation of electromagnetic induction , in the measurement of the speed of light , and in conceptions of space and time.
