Good Fortune on Daytona Beach Trip

· archived # 2,431,602
Mary and I just returned yesterday from our trip to Daytona Beach Bike Week. We had a GREAT TIME! How does this relate to the Good Fortune and Inspired Luck Module? ( Besides being fortunate enough to be able to make the trip? ) This year, Daytona Bike week ran from February 29th - March 9th. We live in Northwest Mississippi, and have to ride across Mississippi, Alabama, Georgia and a good part of Florida to get to Daytona Beach. Our schedule did not allow us to be there the entire time, so we chose to leave on the afternoon of the 27th, arrive at noon on the 29th, stay until morning of the 4th, and arrive back here around noon on the 6th. This was all planned out weeks ago...way before there was any weather forecasts. The trip down there was sunny and we did not have a drop of rain. The evening we arrived in Birmingham it had been snowing that very morning. But when we got there it was dry and in the 50's! Our entire stay in Florida was dry and warm, highs in the 80's with blue skies. Within a few hours of leaving Daytona Beach the weather turned nasty and it has been raining there off and on ever since...temperatures dropped, and as I type this there are strong storm warnings for Daytona Beach. Tomorrow the high is forecast to be 64 degrees, raining and windy! When we left out of Daytona Beach we had one rainstorm that lasted a whopping 30 minutes, which we waited out under a canopy at a gas station. When we arrived home yesterday it was in the 60's with blue skies. It is now 23 degrees here and sleet/snow. The roads are iced over! It is amazing how we missed all the bad weather riding there and back, and how the 5 days we were there, we enjoyed the best weather of the 10 day event. Would this have happened if we were not on the Good Fortune Module? Did the Good Fortune Module affect the weather? Did the Good Fortune Module affect our planning weeks ahead of time? There is no way to know....however, we could not have been more Fortunate on this trip! ALSO!!! We go
