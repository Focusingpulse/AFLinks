, Dornach Translated by Steiner Online Library Invitation to All Societies and Groups to the International Delegates' Conference in Dornach Dear Friends, We are taking the liberty of sending you a transcript of Dr. Steiner's extremely important address at the General Assembly of the Goetheanum Association on June 17, together with the invitation to the Assembly of Delegates. 1 See Part II, p. 146. Please address correspondence regarding the delegates' meeting to: Haus Friedwart, 1st floor, Dornach, marked “Delegiertenversammlung”. With best regards, Dr. I. Wegman, Albert Steffen, William Scott
