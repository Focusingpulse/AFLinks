GC2026-SGG042 Earthquake source development scenarios based on data of the tectonic fault structure Kseniya G. Morozova1 1 Sadovsky Institute of Geosphere Dynamics, Russian Academy of Sciences, Russia morozova.kg@idg.ras.ru According to modern concepts, most earthquakes are the result of movement along already formed faults of the earth crust. Variations in the structure of the tectonic fault plane determine the patterns of accumulation and realization of tectonic stresses. However, the way the inhomogeneities of the frictional properties of the sliding plane of the fault zone affect the earthquake has not been sufficiently studied, and this determines the dynamics of the event. Currently, no model has been created that describes the spatiotemporal evolution of the entire spectrum of deformation phenomena associated with fault zones. The features of the formation of strong earthquake and earthquake scenarios should be taken into account when developing methods for monitoring the stress-strain state of large faults and constructing numerical models of their evolution. Depending on the configuration and rheological properties of the sliding plane, earthquakes of varying magnitude and intensity can be realized. Based on the data presented in open sources, an expanded seismic catalog of strong earthquakes (M>5.8) was formed, which contains information about the time, location, magnitude of events, as well as a map of coseismic displacements, the source time function.
