In the interests of peace, Dr. Tesla said he plans to place the ray at the disposal of the Geneva disarmament conference. He announced the invention yesterday, on the occasion of his 75th birthday. The force ray, Dr. Tesla said, would consist of particles, driven electrically and projected in vast curtains, miles high and 100 miles long. The speed of the particles — traveling at velocities of 50,000,000 volts — was described by him as giving the ray its destructive powers. He said projecting plants, set up every 200 miles along a border, each shooting rays 100 miles on each side, would make the border impenetrable.
