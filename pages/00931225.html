The simple deﬁni- tion of bioterrorism is the utilization of pathogenic micro-organism as an instrument of war and terror. Various notorious microbe has been applied as biological weapons, such as Bacillus antracis, Bubonic plague, Cholera, Botulinum, and others. As the weaponiz- ing germs would eventually require sophisticated microbiology laboratory, not many nations can build it. History has shown that this kind of weapon is much easier to prepare than nuclear and chemical ones.
