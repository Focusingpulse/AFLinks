Lifetime Achievement Award: Dr. Glenn Borchardt Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Date 25 February 2023 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording Lifetime Achievement Award: Dr. Glenn Borchardt was a live session on 25 February 2023 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. Dr. Glenn Borchardt is one of the most influential scientists outside of mainstream science with his Ten Assumptions of Science, Neo-mechanics, and the Infinite Universe Theory. Many modern critical thinkers have based their own models and theories on Borchardt's ground-breaking work. A geologist by trade, Borchardt has been a staunch opponent of the Big Bang and relativity for many decades and is known as one of the premier scientific philosophers of our time. Summary The CNPS Lifetime Achievement Award presented to Glenn Borchardt , the second of the year after Nick Percival 's a month earlier. David de Hilster hosted and conducted the conversation, and two of Borchardt's collaborators joined at the end: George Coyne and Steven Bryant . De Hilster opened with the list of previous recipients — Halton Arp , presented in Europe by Peter Marquardt when Arp was already frail; Ron Hatch ; Bill Lucas ; Domina Eberle Spencer , whose doctorate in mathematics was from MIT and who would argue with anyone who raised the ether; James Maxlow ; Wal Thornhill , who had died that month; Gerald Pollack ; and Percival — and his own reason for the programme: mainstream science has not caught up with this work, so the society has a responsibility to recognise these people while they are alive.
