The program began with QMU carrier and receiver ledgers, audited proton Compton scattering, mirror A = 7 magnetic transitions, behind-the-limb solar events, and occulted nuclear-line observations, and then developed a same-event carrier-identification architecture based on independent measurements of the universal QMU scale, transported ligt, recoil, multiplicity, and diffraction. The work produced several durable no-go results: deposited ligt alone cannot determine carrier species; free propagation cannot silently change species; source association does not identify a carrier; and a measured source-to-carrier handedness slope cannot separately determine source packing and transfer efficiencies. A conditional geometric construction yielded the ideal packing eigenvalue µAF = 6π 5 , but the current measured proton-electron mass ratio differs by approximately 18.8249 ppm. No audited observation requires phtnp , and no physical datum currently supports replacing the measured proton mass by the ideal value. The project is therefore closed as an unconfirmed but falsifiable investigation. The mathematical ledgers, negative empirical results, proposed hybrid experiment, and explicit criteria for reopening are preserved as the scientific outcome.
