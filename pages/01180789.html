The model identifies Dama Ale volcano — then a volcanic island in paleo-Lake Abhe Bad — as the Late Upper Palaeolithic archetype that determined both the astronomical timing and the geodetic layout of Giza in 2550 BCE. The Khafre causeway’s great-circle alignment to Dama Ale (2,345 km, lateral deviation <15 m, 0.4 arcsecond precision) provides independent geodetic confirmation of a deliberate Egypt–Afar axis (3).
