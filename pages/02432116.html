This problem is very common and can cause mood swings, low energy, anxiety, digestive problems, skin problems, irritability, headaches, intense cravings, OCD, allergies, P.M.S. and much more. This PATH will help restore the natural, healthy balance of Candida. Once the Candida is under control these symptoms can be eliminated and you can feel healthy and happy once again.
