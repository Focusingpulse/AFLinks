Pari Spolter Born ( 1930-01-30 ) January 30, 1930 Tehran , Iran Residence Granada Hills, California, United States Nationality Iranian-American Alma mater University of Geneva University of Wisconsin–Madison Known for Gravitational Force of the Sun (1993); alternative theory of gravitation Scientific career Fields Biochemistry , gravitation Pari Spolter (born January 30, 1930) is an Iranian-American biochemist, publisher, and author. Trained as a biochemist, she is best known outside her original field for her 1993 book Gravitational Force of the Sun , in which she challenges the accepted theories of gravitation of Isaac Newton and Albert Einstein and proposes an alternative formulation in which gravitational force is independent of mass. Her ideas lie outside the mainstream of physics and astronomy and have been published chiefly through her own imprint, Orb Publishing Company, rather than in the peer-reviewed scientific literature. Biography Education Licence ès sciences chimiques , mention biologiques (a degree in biochemistry), University of Geneva , 1952 M.S., University of Wisconsin–Madison , 1959 Ph.D. in biochemistry, University of Wisconsin–Madison , 1961 Career Spolter was born in Tehran , Iran, on January 30, 1930. She earned a licence ès sciences chimiques , mention biologiques (a degree in biochemistry) from the University of Geneva in Switzerland in 1952, and subsequently a Master of Science (1959) and a Ph.D. in biochemistry (1961) from the University of Wisconsin–Madison. From 1952 to 1957 she taught at the University of Tehran. In 1957 she immigrated to the United States, and about a year later she married the pediatrician Herbert Spolter; the couple had four children. She held academic and research posts as a research associate and instructor at Temple University in Philadelphia (1961–1965) and as a research biochemist at the Public Health Service Hospital in San Francisco (1965–1968).
