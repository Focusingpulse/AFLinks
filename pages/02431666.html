The importance of blogging about it

· archived # 2,431,666
When I first started on PATHS a little over three weeks ago, I made a commitment to blog about my daily progress (using this very forum's blog function, in fact). I am so glad that I did so now, as otherwise I would be failing to notice some small but very significant changes that have been taking place recently. I highly recommend keeping a blog, and faithfully reporting to it daily. It's easy to set up, and it only takes a few minutes a day. You may just be astounded when you go back and read over it later on.
