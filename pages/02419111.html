The Round Robin >> Vol. 02 >> No. 11 [21] - TELEPATHY - (Involuntary transmission) We present data from an amateur telepathic experiment, not planned for scientific purposes - so that the record is poorly put together. Nevertheless, we believe it is unusual and highly suggestive, with respect to the unintentional transmission, along with failure of the message intended. Participants. Mark Probert, in San Diego. Harry Probert, his brother, in Los Angeles (125 mi.) (Mark P. is psychic and mediumistic; Harp P. has no such tendencies.) ____________ Oct. 29, '46. 7.00-7.15 p.m. H.P. receiving: (a) big red rose, (b) large feet, (c) a broken phonograph record, (d) the words "red-red-red", (e) the colour red. Comment by M.P. (sending): I did not try to send any of these items; however - with regard to (b), I visualized Harry as lying on a couch with his feet up, and myself as whispering in his ear. This is a familiar position for him and his feet stick up conspicuously. As to (c), I was in a restaurant while trying to transmit to Harry, and there was a cracked record in the "juke" box which annoyed me. (d), (e), and the red rose do not click except with the fact that there was a pink - colored newspaper on the table, and I was looking at it but not trying to send it. 7.15-7.30. H.P. sending: (a) a beam of light, (b) a large police dog. Comment by M.P. (receiving): A strong impulse to look at a light bulb. I decided to send it as an item.
