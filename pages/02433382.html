Don't make me laugh.........yet!

· archived # 2,433,382
Well, good morning to all you rays of light across the world It is truly wonderful to be here typing my usual gibberish wondering where this note will be read. It is particularly wonderful as my actually went awfully quiet at the end of August. Silent in fact. It was very nearly and meet my face to face, a bit prematurely in my humble opinion!! Anyway thanks to the wonders of modern drugs , surgery and who/whatever is out there looking after me is back thumping merrily Enough of the emoticons! 48 year old conveyancing lawyer in Paignton (Devon, England) was chewing a toffee on Sunday 30 August 9pm and to cut a long story short I felt suddenly unwell and ended up in Hospital a few minutes later. I was rushed to Accident & Emergency and was laying on an assessment bed, with my son watching as they tried to resuscitate after a rather nasty heart attack (is there another kind?) Suffice to say if you are going to have one then in hospital next to a set of jump leads is a realllly good place to be. Dicky ticker was successfully re-plumbed at end of September by a lovely axe wielder at Derriford Hospital and I am back at home sporting what is affectionately know in this neck of the woods a "Derriford Zip" Recovery going ok and I hope to be fit for work in a month or so. In the meantime I have the unaccustomed luxury of a bit of time to think. My thoughts have lead me by a slightly convoluted route to here, contemplating matters spiritual. Having visited the other side personally please accept my apologies but my visit was too short to gain any lasting impression further than flailing arms and legs as my nursey friends put he national grid through my nipples, so this particular journey is still based on faith rather than knowledge. My faith? Hmmm well lets say I had a number of "experiences" whilst undergoing some healing training a couple of years back sufficient to convince me there is rather more to this life than meets the eye.
