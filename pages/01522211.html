Roy Keys Inc.), Montreal Pages 1,914 in five volumes Wilhelm Weber's Main Works on Electrodynamics Translated into English is a five-volume edition, edited and largely translated by Andre K T Assis , of the electrodynamic writings of Wilhelm Eduard Weber (1804–1891). It was published by Apeiron of Montreal between 2021 and 2024, and makes available in English for the first time the greater part of Weber's work, together with related papers by Gauss, Kirchhoff, Fechner, Carl Neumann, Tisserand and others. Weber's Elektrodynamische Maassbestimmungen — the eight Electrodynamic Measurements memoirs published between 1846 and 1894 — are distributed across the series, and each is accompanied by editorial introductions setting out what the paper argues and how it was received.
