A Desalination of Seawater s worldwide fresh water supplies become increasingly stressed from population growth and uncontrolled water use, desalination of seawater has become an increasingly sought-after alternative for new water supply in coastal areas. More than 1,000 seawater desalination plants operate around the world and more than two dozen new seawater projects are in development in the United States. This manual of water supply practice provides technical and planning guidance for drinking water utilities that currently operate, are developing, or are considering desalination facilities. It provides information on seawater treatment technologies, pre- and post-treatment, source-water and finished-water quality, environmental impact, residuals management, construction, operation, and maintenance costs, safety, and more.
