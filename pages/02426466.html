( 2006.01 ) ABSTRACT A method is provided for acquiring and transmitting bio metric data ( e.g. , vital signs) of a user, where the data is analyzed to determine whether the user is suffering from a viral infection , such as COVID - 19 . The method includes using a pulse oximeter to acquire at least pulse and blood oxygen saturation percentage, which is transmitted wire lessly to a smartphone. To ensure that the data is accurate , an accelerometer within the smartphone is used to measure movement of the smartphone and / or the user . Once accurate data is acquired, it is uploaded to the cloud ( or host ) , where the data is used ( alone or together with other vital signs) to determine whether the user is suffering from (or likely to suffer from ) a viral infection , such as COVID - 19 .
