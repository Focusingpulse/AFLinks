VIII. Pitch (ethos and pathos), Note Values, Dynamics, Changes of Tempo - GA 278. Eurythmy as Visible Singing

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 27 February 1924 · archived # 2,441,149
Today, I will try to pass on some things which will bring our studies of tone eurythmy to a provisional conclusion, so that a stimulation for the advance of the substance of tone eurythmy will have been given. The first step in this direction will be to digest those things I have given. Then, a little later on, it will be necessary to hold a further series of lectures, either on tone eurythmy or speech eurythmy, [50] for it is quite clear that a living stream of development has to be maintained.
