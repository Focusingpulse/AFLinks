The book is based on lectures given during the years at the Ben Gurion University, Israel. Spinors are used extensively in physics; it is widely, accepted that they are more fundamental than tensors and the easy way to see this fact is the results obtained in general relativity theory by using spinors, results that could not have been obtained by using tensor methods only.
