Puetz is an American cycle analyst and statistician, Senior Research Scientist at the Progressive Science Institute , and the discoverer of what he calls the Universal Wave Series — a harmonic series of cycles claimed to run through geological, biological, astronomical and financial records alike. Puetz's distinguishing claim is quantitative and unusually specific: that the cycles found across these very different domains are not independent but period-tripled , each successive cycle three times the length of the last, so that a single equation with 32 terms covers periods from 57.3 years to 1.64 billion years . He argues that a harmonic relationship of this kind across unrelated fields implies a common physical source. He is the co-author, with Glenn Borchardt , of Universal Cycle Theory: Neomechanics of the Hierarchically Infinite Universe (2011) and of the Neomechanical Gravitation Theory (2012), in which the universal cycles are attributed to variations in aether density produced by compression waves. Unusually among researchers documented on this wiki, Puetz has taken this work into the mainstream peer-reviewed literature , publishing period-tripling analyses in Chaos, Solitons & Fractals and Mathematical Geosciences . Background Puetz came to the subject from financial markets rather than from academia. Working as a market analyst, he became interested in cyclic analysis and began looking for a plausible physical explanation of cyclic behaviour in market data.
