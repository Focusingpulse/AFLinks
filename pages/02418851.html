, Berlin Translated by Steiner Online Library 15. The Transition from the Atlantean Race to Our Own [ 1 ] Let us take concepts comparatively from the human being itself. When you say something to your sister, you can distinguish three successive stages in this communication. First, a dark feeling that you want to say something, for example something loving; the second is the thought of how you want to communicate this feeling, of which the other person is unaware; third, when you express the thought through the word.
