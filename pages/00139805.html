These phenomena are associated with the geodesic motions of material particles or photons in the immediate vicinity of large masses that can be regarded as point sources of gravity. The limited purposes of this article are to present the underlying physics of the exponential metric of Yilmaz and to demonstrate that it correctly encompasses the observed phenomena.
