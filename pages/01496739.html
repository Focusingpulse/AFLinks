Hughes IF we strike the twelve as written in musical clef , beginning with the lowest A in the bass clef , each key-note , with its trinities , scale , and chords , sounds three harmonics . We may follow with the twelve keys as they rise , and descend by following the keys upwards as written in musical clef , each key falling lower.
