Bilateral Recursive Harmonic Intelligence Loop (BRHIL): A Hybrid Human-AI Coherence Framework

👤 Jonathan Stieve · · archived # 1,231,857

BRHIL leverages harmonic coherence, recursive feedback, and supervised reinforcement learning to enable adaptive intelligence that continuously evolves from the integration of human intuition and machine computation. The framework demonstrates how novel signal integration, decoherence control, and bidirectional feedback can enhance predictive modeling, cross-domain reasoning, and enterprise-scale AI applications. This work provides a theoretical foundation and practical methodology for hybrid intelligence systems, supporting research, simulation, and deployment in both academic and industrial contexts.
