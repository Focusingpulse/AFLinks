nova

· archived # 1,482,007
noun: a star that ejects some of its material in the form of a cloud and become more luminous in the process "A vast mass of the vacuum cold freezes our sun into its density and keeps it from exploding. Some suns generate heat beyond their melting points and explode as novas , but some of them are conquered by cold before they expand beyond the limits of their exhaustion ."
