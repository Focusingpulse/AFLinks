So declares Lord Kelvin . In a speech at Delmonico's Lord Kelvin announced that the myriad electric lights of Broadway made a spot of light so brilliant upon the face of the earth that the Martians could not help but notice it, and that in consequence their frequent signals noticed by astronomers are really but an attempt to get into communication with the Bright Light district. This, of course, is a blow to Nikola Tesla, who has confided to the rest of the world that it was he who had been signaling Mars for years past, and reading the answer in the stars. The theory of the great British scientist that the people of Mars have neglected the signals of Tesla is uncomplimentary, but Tesla will probably not be much disturbed thereby. According to Lord Kelvin, the Martians for years past have been reading the electric lighted devices of Tom O'Rourke and "Lid" McCoy, and have been frantically trying to signal back to earth answers to these signs.
