CLASSICAL VS PATH COUPLING ON A GEOMETRIC STATE SPACE: GLAUBER DYNAMICS FOR q-COLORINGS OF Cn IZ TECUM´ ABSTRACT. We analyze one coupling of single-site Glauber dynamics for proper q-colorings of the cycle Cn in two ways. Drift on the Hamming disagreement count and path coupling on Hamming-adjacent pairs give the one-step contractions E dH (X1,Y1) ≤ αcl(q,n)dH (X0,Y0), E dG(X1,Y1) ≤ αpc(q,n)dG(X0,Y0), with αcl(q,n) = 1−(q−4)/ n(q−2)  and αpc(q,n) = 1−(q−6)/ n(q−2)  , both attained. The thresholds are q ≥ 5 and q ≥ 7, and the constants separate by 2/ n(q−2)  uniformly in n. The gap is geometric. Transposing two colors across an edge produces a pair at Hamming distance 2 that no single recoloring connects, so the path metric dG of the color graph charges a created disagreement +2 rather than +1; such pairs pack ⌊n/2⌋ to a cycle, giving diamdH = n and diamdG = ⌊3n/2⌋ for q ≥ 5, hence closed O(nlogn) bounds for both methods. The loss is attributable to the sparse edge set rather than to path coupling, since the complete edge set recovers αcl.
