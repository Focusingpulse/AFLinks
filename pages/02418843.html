7. The Three Realms of Nature — GA 91. Inner and Outer Evolution

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 91 August 1904 · archived # 2,418,843
, Graal Translated by Steiner Online Library 7. The Three Realms of Nature [ 1 ] We want to submit some more questions in relation to the three kingdoms of nature. We know that every being must pass through these three kingdoms. To each mineral belongs the form; from this formed being of nature the plant is to arise. If it were to evolve in the same direction, without new impetus, it would of course always remain mineral - at most it could become more perfect as a mineral.
