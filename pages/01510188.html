I tested my oil heater at 1500 W just to see how much it would cost me for a month. Results 4 hours 40 min 6.44 KW -------------------- So... per minute. 240 + 40 = 280 min 280 /60 = 4.67 hours So 6.44KW / 4.67 = 1.38KW Per hour. So for a day... 33.12KW * .08 cents or $2.64 per day. or $79.2 per month. ------------------------------------------------------------------ Also I found that my TV plugged turned OFF consumed 5 Watts of power, my fancy speakers consumed 7 watts ( Turned off with the switch ) So putting these on a power strip with a flip switch will save a bunch I did not know I was spending. I thought I would pass this info along for those of you who have electronic items that are plugged in but not used but once a month, well worth the time to either put in a power strip with an on off, or plug them in, that is some free energy, and $ in your pocket.
