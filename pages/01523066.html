Baer (died 25 June 2007) was a German writer on the foundations of physics, working at Dresden, who argued across four books that the physical world-picture of the twentieth century rests on a mistake a Jahrhundertirrtum , a century's error and that relativity is the visible part of it. He published through the Spur-Verlag at Dresden. His e-mail and website have been maintained since his death by his students, who continue to promote his work.
