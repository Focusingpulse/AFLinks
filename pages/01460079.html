Keely | Russell Working Doc This collaborative document is designed to ask questions and to seek the in-depth answers essential to understanding The Wave and Sympathetic Vibratory Physics . The goal in developing this body of knowledge to confirm the concepts of great scientists and inventors ( Keely , Russell , Tesla , Schauberger , etc.) and for replication of their devices and the creation of new applications. Future experiments will be key in validating ideas and will lead to the technological breakthroughs we all desire to see implemented in the world. * The questions below will act as a table of contents and underneath them, questions can be answered. Add any questions you feel would benefit this document. If you edit a current answer to improve upon it, do so by writing underneath the current answer and explain where it needs to be altered and where more information could be added. Also through instant message (the chat feature), you can discuss what answer seems to be the most accurate before changing the original answer. There can be multiple answers for each question, please leave your name on the answer in case someone wants to further discuss the concept. Thank you all for joining this project! NOTE: It is obvious from some of these questions what is lacking is a solid grounding in basic sound and vibration physics.
