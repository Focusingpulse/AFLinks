ISARD-2025-satellite010 Validation of total ozone columns measurements from the Russian Arktika M satellite Alexander V. Polyakov1 , Yurii Timofeyev1 , Vladislav Bloshchinskiy Bloshchinskiy2 , Petr Nerobelov1 , Yuliya Shamilova2 , Anastasiya Boroditskaya2 1 St Petersburg University, St. Petersburg, Russia 2 Far East Center "Scientific Research Center of Space Hydrometeorology "Planeta", Khabarovsk, Russia a.v.polyakov@spbu.ru Due to its unique highly elliptical trajectory, the Arktika-M series of Russian spacecraft plays virtually the same role for the polar regions of the Northern Hemisphere as geostationary satellites do for the equatorial and mid￾latitude regions of the Earth. On board the spacecraft, in addition to other equipment, is installed multispectral instrument MSU-GS, which performs measurements in 10 spectral channels in the visible and infrared spectral regions. One of the spectral channels covers the 9.6 µm ozone band, which makes it possible to determine the total ozone content in the vertical column. Due to high spatial resolution (up to 4 km nadir) and periodicity of measurements (15 minutes), the instrument provides unique information on spatial and temporal variability of the total ozone content in the vertical column of the atmosphere (VCO).
