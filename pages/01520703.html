Title New Energy News (December 2001) Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Patrick G Bailey Keywords New Energy , Cold Fusion, Zero Point Energy, renewable energy policy, Charge Clusters Published 2001 Journal New Energy News Volume 8 Number 12 No. of pages 21 Abstract Overview This is the December 2001 issue of New Energy News (Volume 8, Number 12, ISSN 1075-0045), the monthly newsletter of the Institute for New Energy (INE), edited by Patrick G Bailey and distributed by email in plain ASCII and PDF form. It is not a research paper but a news bulletin: a compilation of edited items, forwarded articles, correspondence and editorial comment gathered by Bailey, Hal Fox and Tom Valone. INE was then based at 3084 E. 3300 South, Salt Lake City, with annual membership at $35 including the newsletter, or $150 including four quarterly issues of the Journal of New Energy . The issue's centre of gravity is the relationship between conventional energy policy and the "new-energy" field. Roughly half the pages are given over to a detailed digest of a U.S. Senate bill and a DARPA propulsion solicitation, the rest to short news items, a substantial book review, letters and meeting announcements. The editorial position running through the issue is explicit and repeated: that the commercialization of new-energy devices — cold fusion , zero point energy extraction, charge-cluster technology — would relieve strategic dependence on imported oil, reduce pollution, and improve economic conditions worldwide. That framing departs from the mainstream energy-policy literature of the period, which treated the renewable/efficiency portfolio as the whole of the alternative-energy question and did not admit the anomalous-energy claims as candidates at all. Contents of the issue INE website and housekeeping Bailey opens with a note that the INE website (established 27 January 1996) is to be upgraded.
