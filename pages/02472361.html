In all these inventions yours was the leading hand. Without you they would never have been patented; without you they would never even have been thought of. And yet you get no credit for some of the most marvelous discoveries of the age?” Mr. Tesla smiled. “That the inventions have materialized, that they benefit mankind, is enough,” he said. “I am not selfish. My name is honored. I live well.
