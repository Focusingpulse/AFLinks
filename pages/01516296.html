The White Rose Society rexresearch.com The Pamphlets of the White Rose Society http://www.historyisaweapon.com/defcon1/whiterose.html The White Rose was a close-knit group of friends in Nazi Germany. They anonymously published a series of six pamphlets, distributing them to scholars, medics, and pub owners. They began to do public grafitti critical of Hitler. Several were caught distributing fliers and they were all rounded up and executed.
