Abstract Two papers in this program independently discovered, and independently mis-stated, the same one-line theorem. Both recorded the correction in an errata table rather than in the body. This paper extracts it, states it in the form under which it cannot be attacked, and fixes its scope by exhaustive computation. The theorem: there is no nonzero homomorphism from an idempotent semigroup into the additive reals. If x composed with x equals x, and f carries composition to addition, then f(x) = 2f(x), hence f(x) = 0. No lattice structure is required, and stating it as a lattice result is what caused both prior withdrawals — lattices do carry valuations, and the first version of this claim denied it.
