Lecture Four — GA 131. From Jesus to Christ (1966)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 8 October 1911 · archived # 2,422,670
, Karlsruhe Translated by Charles Davy Lecture Four [ 1 ] We can perhaps sum up the outcome of the last lecture in the following way. From the Mystery of Golgotha until the coming of the epoch at whose portal we now stand, a man could attain by various exoteric means to an experience of the Christ-Impulse—an experience preceding any actual Initiation. One of these exoteric ways is through the Gospels, through the New Testament.
