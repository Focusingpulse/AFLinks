PRINCIPLES OF A NEW PHASE NUCLEATION DEVELOPMENT FOR THE MULTY - PHASE INDUSTRIAL REAL POLY CRYSTALLINE MATERIALS. PART I: THE PRINCIPLES FOR THE NOMINALLY MONO – PHASE MATERIALS Tkachenko Igor, Miroshchenko Yurii, Tkachenko Olesia, Miroshchenko Victoria, Miroshchenko Sergii, Tkachenko Svitlana Annotation. Based on thermodynamics, dislocation theory together with own theoretical and experimental results, a new phase nucleation development for the mono - phase industrial real poly crystalline materials was analized. At first, the terms “multy-phase” and “mono-phase” materials were specified and reformulated, introducing an addition one3“nominally”. The dislocation substructure role in solute chemical elements segregation and subsequent new phase nucleation was analyzed. It was theoretically shown and experimentally verivied possibilities to obtain super fine and homogeneus structure due to isothermal holding at optimal both temperature and duration for the nominally mono-phase metal materials. It was shown that the possibilities may be expanded on other multiphase materials, particularly, Grey and White Cast Irons. Keywords: a new phase nucleation, dislocation substructure, grain boundaries, solute element segregations, ultra fine structures. 1.1. Evaluation the role of various factors in a formation of microstructures in polycrystalline metallic materials.
