It was shown that the higher harmonics generated by non-centrosymmetric bonded electrons can take on properties of negative index systems. By controlling the nonlinearity, tailor-made optical metamaterials with a defined negative refractive index are possible. This becomes interesting in the context of the fact that matter waves with a refractive index of exactly minus one form the perfect microscope. A suitable set of new characteristic optical response tensors, which are no longer Lorentzartig in view of the newly gained knowledge, were derived for non-centrosymmetric media and their importance for the various nonlinear orbital response functions was shown and discussed.
