Dear All, FIRST OVERUNITY RESULTS ? ( Mechnical Output Power > AVG Electrical Input Power ) Today (11-20-98), I have reconducted new measurements about the mechanical efficiency of my Newman's Energy machine v2.0. I have used a new digital oscilloscope (Tektronix THS720P ), with this instrument, I have been able to measure in real time and accurately the electrical power input, in spite of the strong and short spikes of back current. The mechanical power power ouput has been measured with the Prony Brake method.
