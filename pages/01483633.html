spiritual science

· archived # 1,483,633
Spiritual Science is of the unseen , non-observable , scalar , spirit , quantum , Mind . “It is dangerous to be right in matters on which the established authorities are wrong”. [ Voltaire ] Rudolf Steiner "Men hoped to satisfy their search for knowledge without initiation and so gradually came to believe that only the physical world was their field of research." But what is the physical world really? We won't come to terms with it if we only consider his physical appearance. We only understand the physical world when we are able to apprehend the spirit that informs it. Humanity must regain this knowledge once again, because we are at a crossroads today. The world presents a picture of disruption and growing chaos. Yet we know that in the midst of this chaos, darkening passions that threaten to destroy everything, the intuitive are aware of the presence of spiritual powers that actively strive to awaken in man a new spirituality. And the preparation for anthroposophy is fundamentally listening to this voice of spirit that can still be heard amidst the cry of our materialistic age." [ Rudolf Steiner , Torquay, August 16, 1924.] "It is difficult to describe these things in words because our language has been coined for the external, physical plane, so we have to exert ourselves when applying it to super-sensible conditions. You could say that everything to which we ordinarily apply our understanding lives coarsely, densely in our soul because our brain is always at our disposal
