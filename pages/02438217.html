109 — GA 263. Correspondence with Edith Maryon 1912–1924

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 12 June 1922 · archived # 2,438,217
My dear Edith Maryon! Since your first letter, no news has arrived from the studio. All is well here, except for the ongoing Kolisko affair. But that is below the surface; outwardly, nothing appears to be amiss. We should be glad to be accepted, but we must not harbour any illusions. What we want can only happen slowly, but we must also be mindful of the moment.
