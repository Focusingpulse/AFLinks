GA 319. Polarities in Health, Illness and Therapy

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 28 August 1923 · archived # 2,442,929
, Pennmenmawr Translated by G.F. Karnow, M.D.; edited by Martha Bosch Since I was asked to speak about the therapeutic principles which have developed out of the anthroposophical view of the world, I will gladly meet this request. However, it is difficult to be brief, especially about this kind of subject matter which is so extraordinarily extensive. In one brief lecture, which can only be aphoristic, one can hardly develop correct ideas of what is important. In addition, certain deliberations must be undertaken in such an attempt which are quite removed from what people generally think about.
