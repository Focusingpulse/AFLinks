Percival sets out the problem Smoot faced: the radiation fits a blackbody pattern and is extremely uniform in every direction, which sits well with the Big Bang but leaves unexplained how a highly structured universe of galaxies and superclusters could develop from a uniform beginning. Working strictly from what Smoot himself writes, Percival examines the reasoning and the mindset behind the search for the wrinkles that were expected to supply the missing structure.
