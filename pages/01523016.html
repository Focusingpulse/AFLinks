Turner is an English engineer, author and entrepreneur based in Gloucester, United Kingdom, who is known for the design and manufacture of gyroscopes and for his interest in gyroscopic propulsion, antigravity and magnetic propulsion . He is the founder of the retailer and manufacturer Gyroscope.com and the author of the introductory textbook Gyroscopes: An Introduction for Students, Engineers and Pilots . Education B.Sc. with Honours in computing University of Gloucestershire Biography His final-year dissertation was awarded a prize by the Government Communications Headquarters (GCHQ). He is based in the Abbeymead area of Glouc
