SEPTEMBER 1994 Statement on the Status of "Cold Fusion" Magazine. This statement is being released on 12 September 1994 by the former Editors of "Cold Fusion" Magazine (Eugene F. Mallove, Editor; Stu Norwood, Managing Editor; Contributing Editors: Lawrence Forsely, Jed Rothwell, and Christopher P. Tinsely): Three issues of "Cold Fusion" Magazine have appeared: May, June, and July/August, 1994. In June 1994, internal disagreements between the editorial staff and the publisher (Wayne Green, Inc. of Peterborough, New Hampshire) reached a point at which the editorial staff concluded they could no longer work within the Wayne Green, Inc. organization. The editors wanted to continue with the excellent 80page magazine format, supported by the cold fusion industry and the growing readership of the publication. The publisher wanted to reduce it to a newsletter with a fraction of the information content of the magazine. Consequently, all of the editors severed their relationship with Wayne Green, Inc.
