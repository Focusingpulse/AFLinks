No one, save the few mystics of long ages, has ever known God, or God's ways. The long heralded peace which passeth understanding awaits for science to tear away the veil which has for so long hidden the face of the Creator. Religion can be united as ONE only by dispelling the ignorance which now cloaks the faith-and-belief god of fear which has bred so many intolerant groups of unknowing men.
