This paper analyzes the role of MORENA (National Regeneration Movement) in shaping Mexico's international positioning, focusing on how political parties influence foreign policy beyond government actions. It examines the party's relations with Latin American governments, its interaction with global powers such as the United States, China, and Russia, and its participation in international forums. The aim is to assess how MORENA's ideological commitments are reflected in its diplomatic practices. Although MORENA has emphasized solidarity with left￾leaning governments in Latin America, the administration has also maintained practical and balanced relationships with key global actors, avoiding direct confrontations. This approach demonstrates the party's ability to navigate complex geopolitical dynamics while remaining true to its core principles.
