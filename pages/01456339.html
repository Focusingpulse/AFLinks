Imperator Emeritus Christian Bernard Some of you may have met Christian Bernard at Rosicrucian events or read his writings in AMORC books and magazines. Christian Bernard was born in France on November 30, 1951 and his parents were Rosicrucians. “As a member of the Junior Order of Torchbearers, a Rosicrucian children's group; Christian … yearned to become a full-fledged student of the Rosicrucian teachings. Therefore, he wrote to the Imperator to be granted special permission to become a member of AMORC at the age of 15”. (Rosicrucian Digest, May 1990)… He served as Grand Master of the French speaking jurisdiction from 1977 to 1993. “Christian Bernard proceeded to effect many improvements in his jurisdiction and expanded the French-speaking membership… For ten years he presided over many Rosicrucian conventions, traveled worldwide for the Order, promoted the creation of cultural centers, and gave his support to the successful launching of a private radio station sponsored by the French-speaking members …”. (Rosicrucian Digest, May 1990) In April 1990, the Board elected him to the office of Imperator. AMORC is not a religion, but is interested in the spiritual dimensions of life.
