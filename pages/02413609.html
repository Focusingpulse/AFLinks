12. Knowledge and Immortality — GA 68b. The Circular Flow of Man's Life

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 5 February 1910 · archived # 2,413,609
, Kassel Translated by Steiner Online Library Compare 12. Knowledge and Immortality [ 1 ] Ladies and gentlemen! When a person takes a moment to step back from the ordinary work of the day—the tasks that occupy him from morning to night—those great questions surely touch his soul: those great questions concerning what we might call human destiny, which a person must ask himself when he reflects on the highest goals. [ 2 ] One of these questions is undoubtedly that of the nature of knowledge. Linked to its dignity and nature is the question of what must distinguish it from the things around it.
