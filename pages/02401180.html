iberorus2025-Т1.4.001 “Smoke without fire”? Transformation of Javier Milei's public narrative after 2023 presidential election in Argentina Maria A. Shpak1 1 Primakov National Research Institute of World Economy and International Relations of the Russian Academy of Sciences (IMEMO) m.shpack.new@gmail.com The growing presence of right-wing political actors in Latin America is driven primarily by the peculiarities of national political systems, protest voting, and electoral dynamics, whereby the population refuses to trust incumbent governments and votes for the opposition, rather than by an ideological shift. Against the backdrop of declining voter confidence in traditional parties, the positions of extreme right-wing, anti-system and populist forces are consolidating. Thus, it is relevant to examine the political dynamics in Argentina, where Javier Milei, a libertarian leader of "La Libertad Avanza", holds presidency since 2023. His success was the result of a set of system factors, as well as the candidate's positioning in the digital media space. The paper provides a comparative analysis of Milei's public narrative during the election campaign and the first year of his presidential term. The aim of the study is to identify the features of Milei's narrative transformation after assuming office and his strategy for adapting to the challenges of domestic political and socio-economic development.
