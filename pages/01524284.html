Theoria Primarius Book Author James H Wiborg Language English Subject Force and energy; cosmology ; space and time Published 1992 Publisher J. H. Wiborg (self-published), Tacoma, Washington Pages vi, 249 Theoria Primarius is a 1992 book by James H Wiborg , self-published in Tacoma, Washington. Its author, a businessman who founded the firm that became the chemical distributor Univar, described it as a unified field theory of time and space and regarded it as his magnum opus — the product of a lifetime of private work carried on alongside a career that had nothing to do with physics. Overview The book sets out Wiborg's own foundational — "primary" — theory of physics, an attempt to derive the structure of matter and the physical forces from first principles. It is a large-format illustrated volume of vi + 249 pages, printed at 29 cm, with bibliographical references, and was issued from the author's own address on North 46th Street in Tacoma rather than through any publisher. No copy has been consulted for this article, and none appears to be freely available: the book remains in copyright, and the single digitised copy — scanned by Google from the University of Michigan's holding — is available only as search-only text through HathiTrust. What can be established is therefore its subject, classification and circulation rather than its argument, and these are set out below. Anyone with access to a copy is invited to replace this section. Subject and classification The most informative evidence about the book's content, absent the text, is how professional cataloguers read it. The Library of Congress assigned it three subject headings — Force and energy , Cosmology , and Space and time — and the classification BD632 , which falls not in the physics range but in BD , speculative philosophy, within its cosmology sequence and specifically at space and time. That placement is worth noting rather than glossing over. It indicates a work read by its cataloguers as metaphysics
