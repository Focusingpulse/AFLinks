IV. Definition of the Concept of Experience - GA 2. A Theory of Knowledge (1940)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 1,555,616
Two spheres thus stand over against one another,—our thinking and the objects with which this is occupied. These latter are designated, in so far as they are accessible to our observation, as the content of experience. Whether or not there are other objects of thought outside the field of our observation, and of what sort these may be, we shall for the moment leave undetermined. Our first task shall be to fix sharply the boundaries of the two spheres, experience and thought.
