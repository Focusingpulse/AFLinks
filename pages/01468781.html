VIBRATIONAL INTERFERENCE

· archived # 1,468,781
Keely If a homogenous disk of "proper proportions" of silver , gold and platinum were made the subject of magnetic interference in an alternating field, the molecular antagonistic thirds would cause a very erratic gyroscopic movement . Differentiation of sympathetic flow , (i.e., interference ) as through a homogenous wire, can be equated in the same manner as in the trexar . Two differing molecular densities of the proper ratio will harmoniously adjust chords of that ratio , i.e., a ratio of 1:3 will adjust thirds , 1:4 will adjust fourths , 1:7 will adjust sevenths , etc. Return to Snell Manuscript - the book
