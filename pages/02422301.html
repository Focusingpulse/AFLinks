ERZION MODEL INTERPRETATION OF THE EXPERIMENTS WITH HYDROGEN LOADING OF VARIOUS METALS Yury Bazhutov Terrestrial Magnetism, Ionosphere and Radiowave Propagation Institute (IZMIRAN), Russian Academy of Science, Moscow, Russia, E-mail: erzion@mail.ru Abstract-- The short review of Cold Nuclear Transmutation investigation for 22 years after its discovery is presented. It is shown the main physical results of Rossi-Focardi and our experiment with the hydrogen loading of various metals. It is proposed the Erzion Model of Catalytic Nuclear Transmutation for theoretical explanation of the generation of excess heat, new chemical elements and isotopes, X-ray and neutron bursts radiation in these experiments. Index Terms-- Erzion Model of Catalytic Nuclear Transmutation, hydrogen loading of various metals. I. INTRODUCTION 23 years ago for the world community by Martin Fleischmann, Stanley Pons and Steven Jones was declared the opening of a new nuclear physics phenomenon - cold nuclear fusion (CNF) [1,2], capable in the near future to become the basis for a new simple, safe and most efficient nuclear power. In March 23 at the press conference in the American University of Utah, Martin Fleischmann and Stanley Pons announced receipt of a simple electrolysis experiment with heavy water and excess heat up to 3 watts at 1 watt expended. To understand its mechanism, it is possible that the most significant there were the results obtained in the Bombay Atomic Center by nuclear physicists of the 11-th independent groups, which a year later showed that the excess energy in the high current heavy water electrolysis are observed with very abnormal ratio of the neutron to tritium yield, relatively to a standard mechanism for nuclear fusion [3]. The yield of tritium relative to energy output is 1000 times smaller, and the neutron flux is suppressed, even a million times (3-11 orders of magnitude of suppression in different groups).
