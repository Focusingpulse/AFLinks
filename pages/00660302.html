A Geometric Algebra (GA) Solution to a Multiple-Tangency Problem February 17, 2025 James Smith LinkedIn group “Pre-University Geometric Algebra” https://www.linkedin.com/groups/8278281/ Abstract Using GA’s capacities for formulating reflections, we solve an interesting multiple-tangency problem. The solution is obtained in two ways, the easiest of which transforms the relevant reflections into a single rotation. The solution is validated via a GeoGebra worksheet. Two multivectors A and B are equal if and only if all of their respective parts of equal grade are equal to each other. For example, in the case of two vectors u and v, if u · v + u ∧ v = α + βB, where α and β are scalars, and βB is a bivector, then u · v = α, and u ∧ v = βB. 2. We will use Macdonald’s definitions for the inner and outer product ([3], p. 101) . In the case of vectors, u · v = ⟨u · v⟩0, and u ∧ v = ⟨u · v⟩2. 3.
