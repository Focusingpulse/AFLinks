6. The Mystery of Golgotha — GA 97. The Christian Mystery

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 2 December 1906 · archived # 2,420,046
, Cologne Translated by Steiner Online Library 6. The Mystery of Golgotha [ 1 ] The secret underlying the Mystery of Golgotha is one of the deepest secrets of world development. To understand it, we must use occult wisdom to look back thousands of years into the past of Earth’s evolution. This deeper penetration into the mystery of Golgotha is not contradicted by the fact that what Christ Jesus accomplished is meant to be understandable to every simple mind. That is indeed the case. But a full understanding of this greatest event on Earth must be drawn from the depths of mystery wisdom. [ 2 ]
