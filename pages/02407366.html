50 THINGS TO KNOW BOOK SERIES REVIEWS FROM READERS I recently downloaded a couple of books from this series to read over the weekend thinking I would read just one or two. However, I so loved the books that I read all the six books I had downloaded in one go and ended up downloading a few more today. Written by different authors, the books offer practical advice on how you can perform or achieve certain goals in life, which in this case is how to have a better life. The information is simple to digest and learn from, and is incredibly useful. There are also resources listed at the end of the book that you can use to get more information. 50 Things To Know To Have A Better Life: Self-Improvement Made Easy!
