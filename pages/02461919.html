On the leftmost figure in this slide, we show a plate with balanced opposing forces. The vector summation of all the forces' is zero, so the system has no resultant vector. And any point in one of these second-order hidden vectors is a scalar and composed of a still finer hidden vector substructure, as shown in the lower right figure. This process repeats ad infinitum, and all is structures within structures. At least it can be modeled that way, for it already is treated that way in particle physics.
