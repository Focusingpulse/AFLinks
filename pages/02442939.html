Lecture Ten - GA 320. Light Course (1977)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 3 January 1920 · archived # 2,442,939
, Stuttgart Translated by George Adams Lecture Ten [ 1 ] My dear Friends, [ 2 ] I will now bring these few improvised hours of scientific study to a provisional conclusion. I want to give you a few guiding lines which may help you in developing such thoughts about Nature for yourselves, taking your start from characteristic facts which you can always make visible by experiment. In Science today—and this applies above all to the teacher—it is most important to develop a right way of thinking upon the facts and phenomena presented to us by Nature.
