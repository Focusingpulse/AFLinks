They seem fairly well written but do require someone who has the ability to understand technical things and who can build things without having a parts list and the exact drawing in front of them (shouldn't be a problem here). I did read a complaint about his booklets on the carbs though. J Bruce McBurney ( http://www.inett.com/himac/ ) didn't feel that the methods George was using were the best way to do it, however I think that they can work. George has some interesting ideas and the ones that I have been trying are relitively inexpensive. I guess that it is like anything. Don't get all your info from one source, and remember that everything may not be correct (be optimistic but don't believe without a solid base). as far as ordering goes I had one booklet that came through with pages missing and when I sent it back I got a correct one very promptly, so feel safe sending them your money as you will get your info within a week to a week and a half.
