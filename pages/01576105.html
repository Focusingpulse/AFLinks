The preliminary conclusion is that PMM can explain the mechanism underlying gravitational phenomena through a concise and intuitive flat-spacetime model. In most weak-field and strong-field regimes, its results are consistent with mainstream theories. This work proposes that gravitation is not a direct interaction between mass bodies, but rather the result of an imbalance in external potential pressure caused by mutual interference between their potential fields. Starting from underlying physical principles, it attempts to explain the phenomenon of universal gravitation 1 and to establish a system of equations for deriving and testing representative gravitational phenomena.
