John W. Keely told us in one of our earliest readings the design we are replicating was of a later device he built of which we do not have photos. Our design, as given by him, is one of his later and more advanced designs. The story of building the first Musical Dynasphere , Atlin , is detailed in my book Atlin - Knowing I Am . Since building Atlin I have constructed four others: Symael , Altea , Alcea and Alya . A new one is under construction awaiting a caretaker to step forward. Each was modified somewhat from the preceeding ones but not so much you'd notice just looking at it. These modifications represent ideas and concepts learned along the way about their design and function. The dynasphere project is an evolving one. More Dynasphere pictures are here , here and here . To get an idea of what happens around dynaspheres please read what others have said about their experiences. When we began construction we thought we were building a free energy machine because that is what we thought we were reading about in the original documents and other historical documents . We were later to be surprised to learn of the other unforeseen elements demonstrated by the dynasphere such as Love , healing , personal spiritual development, etc. as witnessed by others . Our horizons quickly expanded into those areas. Working and playing with these marvelous Dynaspheres has taught us many things.
