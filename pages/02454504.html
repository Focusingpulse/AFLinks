In case you aren't familiar with the story, it is one I've been intrigued by now for many years. There is supposed to be a temple in India dedicated to a Sufi Saint, Qamar Ali. As I recall the story there are two large stones in the temple courtyard, one on the order of 9kg, the other 11kg. (that can't be right as 9kb = 24 pounds and 11kg = 29 pounds) Twice daily, a devotee from the temple will collect volunteers, 11 for the large stone, who are to touch the stone with one finger of one hand. They are instructed to chant in a specific fashion, 'Qamar Ali Dervish'. After a couple of minutes the stone is supposed to rise into the air and remain there as long as all 11 people touch it and are chanting. If one finger is removed, the stone instantly regains its weight and falls to the ground. The same applies to the 9kg stone but it requires 9 people. When recounting this story recently to a young Indian engineer from Northern India he knew the story but had never seen it. That was the first person I'd ever met who also knew of this story.
