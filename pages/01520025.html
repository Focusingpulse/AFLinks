Experimental Aetherometry, Vol. 2B: The Discovery of Dark Massfree Energy Book Author Paulo N Correa , Alexandra N Correa Language English Subject Aetherometry; massfree (ambipolar) energy; Tesla waves; orgone; cosmic background radiation Published 2006 Publisher Akronos Publishing, Concord, Ontario Pages 370 ISBN 0968906028 Experimental Aetherometry, Vol. 2B: The Discovery of Dark Massfree Energy is a 2006 book by Paulo and Alexandra N Correa , published by their own imprint, Akronos Publishing. It is the second half of the second volume of Experimental Aetherometry , following volume 2A (2004) and preceding volume 3A . Its central proposal is that there exists a second kind of electricity — massfree or ambipolar — distinct both from ordinary charge flow and from electromagnetic radiation, and that this is what Tesla meant by longitudinal waves and what Reich meant by orgone. What the book argues The Correas' claim is that three things previously treated as separate, or as mythology, are one physical phenomenon: Tesla's longitudinal electricity — what has been variously called Tesla waves, Tesla radiation or Tesla currents Reich's orgone (OR) and deadly orgone (DOR) — and its reported dual thermal and electroscopic effect on orgone accumulators and Faraday cages their own ambipolar radiation , or massfree electricity They argue that Tesla was right that the phenomenon reduces neither to fluxes of monopolar charge nor to electromagnetic radiation, but failed to distinguish it clearly from either; and that Reich failed to give energy and frequency spectra for OR and DOR, or to pin down what his "orgonotic charges" were. The book's claim to originality is that it supplies what both lacked: a spectrum for massfree electric energy, and an account of its interactions.
