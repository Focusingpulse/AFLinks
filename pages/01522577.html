Noyes and Ricardo L Carezani Keywords Autodynamics , special relativity , calorimetry, SLAC, klystron, Bertozzi experiment Published 1984 Journal Physical Review A Volume 29 Pages 2110 "Calorimetric Test of Special Relativity" is the 1984 Physical Review A paper by D. R. Walz, H. P. Noyes and Ricardo L Carezani reporting the only accelerator experiment ever conducted to test Autodynamics against special relativity . It was carried out at the Stanford Linear Accelerator Center, and its result agreed with special relativity. The page of that name at autodynamics.org is Carezani's retrospective account, written some twenty years later, of why he regards the outcome as consistent with his theory rather than against it. Overview Carezani had reached Stanford by correspondence in the 1970s and met the theoretical physicist H. P. Noyes there after moving to California. Noyes wrote the paper; Walz was technically responsible for the experiment; Carezani supplied the theory under test and, by his own account in the endnotes to Muon Decay and Autodynamics , "only participated in writing the short explanation about the theory and discussing the experiment." The measurement compared the kinetic energy of accelerated electrons , determined calorimetrically, against the energy inferred from their velocity. The relativistic expression was confirmed. Noyes's paper states the motivation plainly: "The alternative theory which led to the test presented here was developed by one of us (RLC) in an effort to understand why the energy expected from beta decay did not show up in a calorimeter." The argument Why Carezani regards the result as inapplicable Carezani's position rests on the two-regime rule that governs all of Autodynamics.
