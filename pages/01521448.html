About In 1984, coinciding with the 100th anniversary of Tesla's arrival in the U.S., a group of highly dedicated people organized the first Nikola Tesla Symposium in Colorado Springs. Speakers included Marc Seifer, J. & K. Corum, Oliver Nichelson, Moray B. King, Tom Bearden, and Andrija Puharich.
