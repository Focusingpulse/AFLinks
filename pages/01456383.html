In the antibiotic age, interest in silver may have waned a little. The work is published in Science Translational Medicine. Here is silver 's multi-pronged approach: first, silver sticks very strongly to sulfur , found in parts of proteins. These sulfur groups normally bond to each other in proteins, holding them together and keeping the protein folded up in its correct shape.
