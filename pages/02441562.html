Discussion 3 - GA 295. Discussions with Teachers (1997)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 24 August 1919 · archived # 2,441,562
, Stuttgart Translated by Helen Fox Discussion Three Someone told the story of “Mary’s Child,” first for melancholic and then for sanguine children. RUDOLF STEINER: I think in the future you will have to pay more attention to your articulation. The two versions, as you gave them, were too much alike. The difference must also lie in the articulation. If you bring out these details more emphatically you will not fail to impress the melancholic children. For the sanguines I would introduce more pauses into the story, especially at the beginning, so that the children are compelled to listen to you
