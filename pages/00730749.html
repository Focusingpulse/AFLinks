ON A SPECIFIC FAMILY OF ORTHOGONAL POLYNOMIALS OF BERNSTEIN-SZEGO TYPE ¨ MARTIN NICHOLSON Abstract. We study certain weight functions on [−1, 1] which are particular cases of the general weights considered by Bernstein and Szeg¨o. These weights are numbered by two positive integers and when these integers tend to infinity, these weights approximate weight functions on R considered by Ismail and Valent. We also consider modifications of these weight functions by a continuous variable a > 0. These ideas are then used to find finite analogs of some improper integrals first studied by Glaisher and Ramanujan. 1.
