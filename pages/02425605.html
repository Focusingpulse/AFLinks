Livingston & Penn Data and Findings so Far (and some random reflections) Leif Svalgaard Stanford, July 2011 1 What is Livingston Measuring? From 2001 to 2011 Livingston and Penn have measured field strength and brightness at the darkest position in umbrae of 1843 spots using the Zeeman splitting of the Fe 1564.8 nm line. Most observations are made in the morning [7h MST] when seeing is best.
