GC2026-STP007 Study of velocity variations of electrons in solar type III radio bursts Mariia Y. Bauman1 , Larisa K. Kashapova2 1 Kazan (Volga Region) Federal University 2 Institute of Solar-Terrestrial Physics, Siberian Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences, Irkutsk, Russia baumanmary40@gmail.com Type III radio bursts are generated by electrons accelerated during a solar flare and propagating from the site of primary energy release to the upper layers of the solar corona. Modeling of the generation process of type III radio bursts predicts that the velocity of electrons obtained from measurements at different frequency ranges should differ. This fact can lead to different electron energy estimations obtained from X-Ray and radio ranges. We tested this hypothesis by measuring electron velocities for various frequency ranges using data from the spectropolarimeter SOLARSPEL of the Radioastrophysical Observatory (ISTP SB RAS) in the range 50-3000 MHz. The paper presents the results of a study of eleven type III radio bursts that occurred in 2024. Preliminary results are discussed and can be used to explain the differences in spectral parameters of observations in the X-Ray and radio ranges and to study the topology of the radiation source during the flare. We would like to express our gratitude to the Institute of Solar-Terrestrial Physics SB RAS for the use of the SOLARSPEL spectropolarimeter data. This work was supported by the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of the Russian Federation.
