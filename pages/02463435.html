Thomson III Quantum AetherDynamics Institute david@quantumaetherdynamics.org September 13, 2025 Abstract We present a QMU-native Lagrangian for the conversion of one-dimensional dark mass strings into visible matter through Aether-unit nucleation driven by chronovibrational alignment at a Compton separation. The distributed-charge variable chrg distinguishes APM charge dimensions from single-dimension SI charge. The Aether magnetic charge is ea 2 , while eemax 2 denotes the canonical distributed magnetic-charge unit. A universal mass-to-magnetic-charge ratio γm/chrg is enforced locally, holding for the electron, proton, neutron, their antiparticles, and for the maximum Aether content (ma , ea 2 ). The interaction sector contains a Casimir-like nucleation term that peaks at a species Compton wavelength, enabling two magnetically aligned particles to chronovibrate the intervening space into a new Aether unit. If unbound, the newborn unit expands as a photon; if captured by atomic or nuclear potentials, it yields a new bound electron or proton. Atomic binding enters via a QMU potential VEBE that reproduces electron binding energies; nuclear binding enters via VNuc consistent with the APM nuclear (Gforce) ledger. The model predicts: (i) antineutrinos are liberated mass strings (free, uncharged); (ii) neutron mass and magnetic properties arise from a captured string segment between bound p and e; (iii) slow secular creation of matter and photons in environments rich in aligned pairs at λC ; and (iv) scaling relations for galactic growth consistent with ongoing Singularity split.
