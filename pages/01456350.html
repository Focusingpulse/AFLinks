Chuck Yeager

· archived # 1,456,350
General Charles Elwood “Chuck” Yeager, a legendary aviator whose courage reshaped history. Chuck Yeager passed away peacefully in 2020 at the age of 97. During World War II, Yeager’s bravery was tested long before his name became immortal. After being shot down over Nazi-occupied France, he evaded capture with the help of the French Resistance, crossing the Pyrenees on foot and returning to combat, an act of survival and resolve that defined his character. Years later, on October 14, 1947, Yeager once again faced the unknown. Piloting the experimental Bell X-1, and flying with broken ribs, he became the first human in history to break the sound barrier, proving that fear could be outrun and limits could be shattered. He did not seek fame. He sought the edge of possibility.
