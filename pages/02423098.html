Christmas Celebration II — GA 156. Occult Reading and Occult Hearing

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 27 December 1914 · archived # 2,423,098
, Dornach Translated by Steiner Online Library Christmas Celebration II [ 1 ] The great modern mystic Angelus Silesius once said these beautiful words: Christ is born a thousand times in Bethlehem But not in you; you remain lost forever. [ 2 ] There are two sides to this saying. One is that it serves, as it were, as a declaration: the true, authentic Christmas season must be celebrated in the inner heart of the human being, and all external Christmas celebrations must serve as an inspiration to strive for the inner reality that, on this sacred winter night, draws up from the depths of our soul
