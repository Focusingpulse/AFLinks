World-Universe Cosmology Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Speaker Vladimir S Netchitailo Date 10 May 2025 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording World-Universe Cosmology was a live session presented by Vladimir S Netchitailo on 10 May 2025 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. World-Universe Cosmology (WUC) presents a transformative cosmological framework that challenges and revises the standard Big Bang Model (BBM). By addressing and resolving key paradoxes in contemporary cosmology, WUC offers a fresh perspective on the origins, evolution, and structure of the World. Host by Ian Cowan Summary Having given eight separate presentations on his framework over the previous year, Vladimir S Netchitailo returned to compress twenty-four years of work into a single hour — a review of World-Universe Cosmology, its principal claims, and the historical lineage he reads it as continuing. A different kind of eternal universe WUC rejects the initial singularity. The Universe is eternal and continuously generates new matter, which puts it in the family of steady-state models — Netchitailo quoted Fred Hoyle on obtaining an expanding stationary universe by adding continuous creation to the field equations, without a cosmological constant. But four things distinguish it: a distinct concept of a beginning of the World as opposed to the Universe; a specific mechanism for matter creation; a decreasing energy density; and a four-dimensional geometry. That geometry is the model's organising idea, and it turns on a distinction Netchitailo pressed hard: stretching, not expansion. The World is the hypersphere — the three-dimensional surface — of a four-dimensional nucleus whose radius grows at c . Expansion, he argued, presupposes a centre; a practically infinite universe has none, so it is unclear what is expanding or into what.
