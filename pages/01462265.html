Sympathetic Sympathetic Sympsionics Symbol Pertaining to, involving, depending on, acting or effected by ' sympathy ', or a real or supposed affinity , correspondence , spooky action at a distance , remote viewing , or hidden influence. As one , same as, fellow feeling . The term ' sympathetic ' is an adjective which describes or qualifies the term(s) coming after it. For instance a " sympathetic flow " is a flow that is sympathetic (same as, coincident, harmonious) to something else.
