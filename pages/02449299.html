The bazooka design lends itself to upscaling to a simple but effective vortex cannon....cool... Patent number 2,855,714 has a good description on how the vortex forms......The mechanics of forming smoke rings are well understood and are explained in numerous texts on physics and fluid dynamics. The manner which smoke rings are formed by the present toy gun may be briefly explained as follows. Sudden forward movement of the disc or plunger initially compresses the smoke/air immeadiately in front of the plunger and produces an wave front which moves twoard the exit orifice. Upon the wave front reaching the oriface, the new compressed smoke/air in the casing issues thru the oriface in a forming of a puff. The forward surface seperating the moving mass of compressesd smoke/air behind the wave front from the realitivly still air ahead of the wave front is commonly referred to as the "surface of discontinuity". When this surface of discontinuity reaches the oriface, it is, in effect, rolled up by the circular edge(of the oriface)there,(Coanda Effect),of to form a vortex ring which is propagated outwardly through the oriface to form a smoke ring......
