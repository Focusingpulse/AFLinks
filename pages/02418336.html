PENETRATION INTO UNREACHED Aleksandar D.Vorgu~i} Faculty of Electronic Engineering University of Ni{, Yugoslavia Abstract - The first preliminary results of attempt at objective physical detection (measuring) the geopathogenic zones are given in this paper. The geopathogenic zones are the influencing zones of the underground flowing waters and geological clefts, which have negative effects on living organisms. Because the nature of geopathogenic influences is unknown so far, there are hitherto no instruments for its detection and measuring. Here the first preliminary results objective detection the geopathogenic zones influence on the brain wave frequency are given. So this is the first physical detection of the geopathogenic zones so far. Keywords - Brain waves, Dowsing, Geopathogenic zones, Radiesthesy, Underground water. I. PREFACE I dedicate not only this paper but work and results, to the magnificent achievements of Nikola Tesla, to the man who peered into all pores of man’s life. In the famous lecture “Experiments with Alternate Currents of Very High Frequency and Their Application to Methods of Artificial Illumination” held before the IEEE at Columbia University, New York on may 20. 1891, Tesla explains the path he chose in his research and the reason why he used precisely light and luminous effects in order to enter into the nature of electricity. He explains it by the need for light in man’s enlightenment. He said: “The spark of an induction coil, the glow of an incandescent lamp, the manifestations of the mechanical forces of currents and magnets are no longer beyond our grasp; instead of the incomprehensible, as before, their observation suggest now in our minds a simple mechanism, and although as to its precise nature, all is still conjecture, yet we know that the truth cannot be much longer hidden, and instinctively we feel that the understanding is dawning upon us” [1].
