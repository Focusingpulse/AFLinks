93 — GA 263. Correspondence with Edith Maryon 1912–1924

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 2,438,201
I have read the letter; unfortunately it is over with eurythmy this time, but since it could not have been done properly, it is perhaps better to wait for Oxford; then we will be able to achieve something decent. I am a little sorry. No other news has come. Today we got the Ahriman out and started the preparatory work for transferring the Lucifer – Geck, of course, resisted and first appealed to Sonderegger and then to Aisenpreis, but in vain, because what I ordered was the only right thing to do.
