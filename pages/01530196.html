This work has also involved cross￾linked analysis in order to identify effects due to contaminants that could affect the isotopic shift estimate. Nickel thin films have been sputtered on a polymeric substrate and loaded with hydrogen by electrolysis. Reference and active thin films have been prepared contemporaneously during the same sputtering process to have on both the same deposition and the same impurities composition. Secondary Ion Mass Spectroscopy (SIMS) has been used to analyze the isotopic composition of the electrolyzed and blank substrates. Preliminary results (Violante et al., Proc. 10th Int. Conf.
