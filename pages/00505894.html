1 A Study on the Pattern in the Prime Numbers and Primality Test Minho Baek Abstract The purpose of this paper is to introduce patterns in prime numbers and then primality test from those patterns. Those patterns are the regularity among odd numbers for eliminating the composite number such as Sieve of Eratosthenes. Those patterns shows the regularity among the odd numbers except 2. Those patterns may be used to determine which odd numbers are prime numbers. This pattern may reduce the computation time to find prime numbers in some number range. 1. Pattern in the prime Numbers All odd numbers could be expressed as a list of numbers having the regular pattern, as shown in Figure 1. - A prime number is the number except numbers in square boxes.
