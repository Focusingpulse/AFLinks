This has been called the electrical age, because of the extraordinary developments in applying the subtle current to practical and beneficent purposes. But evidently we are only in the initial stages of electrical progress. That at least is the opinion expressed by those who have devoted their lives to studying and experimenting in the electrical world. No one has attained greater prominence in this line of activity than Nikola Tesla. Hence what he says, even if clothed in what seems to the average reader the language of extravagance, is certain to command attention. Mr. Tesla contributes a remarkable article to The Manufacturers’ Record of Baltimore, in which he makes an extraordinary forecast of electricity’s amazing possibilities. He includes a history of electricity from the time of its discovery to the present time and hints that Moses might have been a skilled electrician and that many in ancient times, including the Romans who kept the vestal fires burning, may have had some knowledge of the subject.
