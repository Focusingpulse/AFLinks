Words for Annie Besant after the Presidential Election My dear Theosophical friends, It is with great satisfaction that I greet you again today, after our long break. Hopefully we will be able to start a very good winter campaign with this evening. It is gratifying to note that we have had to enlarge this room as well by expanding our branch. And above all, it is even more gratifying that we have been able to enlarge it.
