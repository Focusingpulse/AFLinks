GC2026-STP038 Estimation of Parameter Dynamics for a Global Coronal Wave Daria D. Kuziy1 , Elena G. Kupriyanova1,2 1 St Petersburg University 2 Main (Pulkovo) Astronomical Observatory of the Russian Academy of Sciences Alana_K@list.ru Global coronal waves are one of the solar activity manifestations [1, 2]. They are generated as a result of dramatic energy release processes, such as solar flares, coronal mass ejections (CME), and jets, allowing for the diagnosis of these processes. However, the nature of global waves is not yet clear. There are different mechanisms under consideration, such as magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) waves [3], heating processes in the current shells [4], those connected with magnetic lines stretching during CMEs [5], or magnetic reconnection process expansion from a CME epicenter [6]. To identify the mechanism of global waves, it is necessary to estimate the dynamics of their parameters. We present an analysis of the 24 February 2011 global coronal wave using an MHD-approach with SDO/AIA data. The authors are grateful to the SDO/AIA team for their open data policy and to the Python developers for providing the essential tools used in this study. References 1. Nitta, N. Delannée, C. et al., Solar Physics. 247, 123 (2008). 5. Chen, P. F., Fang, C. & Shibata, K., Astrophysical Journal. 622, 1202 (2005). 6. Attrill, G. D. R. et al., Astrophysical Journal Letters. 656, L101 (2007).
