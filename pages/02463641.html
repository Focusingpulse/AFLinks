The weak-field sector is closed entirely by a set of foundational ledger identities. These relations fix all metric potentials without introducing new free constants. We compute the standard PPN expressions for perihelion precession, Shapiro delay, geodetic and Lense–Thirring precession, and light bending. We show that the effective PPN parameters (γ, β) arise as fixed values γ = 1, β = 1 from the ledger identities and are therefore not adjustable. APM successfully reproduces the standard values for perihelion precession, Shapiro delay, and light bending. The gravitomagnetic sector is shown to be consistent with LAGEOS and Gravity Probe B data, with the Lense–Thirring effect correctly derived from a dual-gauge cross-term in the APM Lagrangian. All APM predictions are shown to match observation without free parameters.
