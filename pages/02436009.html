Lecture VIII — GA 235. Karmic Relationships I (1972)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 9 March 1924 · archived # 2,436,009
, Dornach Translated by George Adams. Revised by M. Cotterell, C. Davy and D.S. Osmond Lecture VIII [ 1 ] I said yesterday that although it is a somewhat hazardous venture to speak of individual karmic connections, I intended to do so, and that I would take as examples the personalities of whom I gave you certain biographical details. Later on we shall also be able to study the karma of less representative personalities, but I have chosen, in the first place, examples which show clearly how in the karmic course of repeated phases of existence, the evolution of mankind as a whole goes forward.
