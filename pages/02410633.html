INTRO: During my visit to Airventure 2015, Oshkosh, USA, i noticed the very large amount of nice looking, but expensive airplanes. There were hardly any ultralights. “Where is the airplane for the man of the street?” was already my biggest concern. The lack of ultralights confirmed my fear. The BirdGlider might be the missing link. It can get you airborne. At first as a glider, later as a motorised ultralight. Due to starting up another project, i can no longer work on the BirdGlider. But …it is too good to shelve it. So …i give it to you. Now it is all up to you. Start up a small community to help each other to build it and use it. I will help from a sideline. But i need to concentrate on my new project. WARNING: at the moment of this writing, the BirdGlider is a noncertified, non-tested airplane! If you build it, be very very careful during the test flights. Don’t be the first to fly it without any experience in gliding airplanes. Don’t do any changes to the construction unless you know airplane engineering. Koen Van de Kerckhove The free plans Nest of Dragons If you think i don’t trust the BirdGlider, you are wrong. I am still willing to test-fly the first completed BirdGlider if it is build according to the plans. People, to become a pilot, a good pilot i advise learning to glide and soar. So, don’t put a engine on this airplane from the start. Also … if you intend to install a engine, you need to know that the connection parts are NOT designed to take that extra weight.
