The most natural, because the genetic, way to write the scales is to make the major scales all in sharps , after C, because the major genesis is upward in ratios ascending; and to make the minor scales all in flats , after A, because the minor genesis is downward in ratios descending. Let the young student, however, always keep in mind that the sharps and flats are simply marks to show how Nature , at whatever pitch we are taking the scales , is securely keeping them in the same form as when they are first generated; and in their birthplace no sharps or flats are needed.
