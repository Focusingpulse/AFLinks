But Dr. Morton was thinking entirely of physiology when he discovered how to produce an alternating current of extraordinary capacity, whereas Tesla later had thought only of the physical results of his experiments. There is no controversy between the two men. Tesla recognizes Morton’s discovery and acknowledges it; Morton frankly and most cordially admits the value of Tesla’s demonstrations. In his lecture recently Dr. Morton illustrated his discoveries with some very remarkable experiments, which are certain to be regarded by the medical profession as of the utmost importance. He developed by means of his electrical machine, which does not differ greatly from Tesla’s, an electrical current of such marvelous celerity that the ether itself furnished a medium for its communication instead of a wire. A person was placed upon an insulated stool and connected with one end of the machine. Dr. Morton then took an insulated wand in his hand, the wand being connected with what to laymen may be best understood as the dynamo, and then approached the subject. Waving the wand perhaps two feet away from the subject a shower of electric sparks passed between the subject and the wand, although there was no other contact between them than that furnished, not by the atmosphere, but by the ether.
