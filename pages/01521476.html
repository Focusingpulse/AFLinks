Overview Presented at the 18th Natural Philosophy Alliance conference in College Park in 2011, this paper proposes what Robert Bennett calls the ALFA model: an Absolute Lab Frame and a Flexible Aether . The programme has two halves. The first is destructive, and Bennett opens by claiming that "relativity's credibility can be — and is — disposed of with one page of common sense logic". The second is a re-reading of the classical aether experiments — Arago, Faraday's rotor, Fizeau, Airy, Michelson–Morley, Michelson–Gale, Sagnac and Ruyong Wang's linear extension of it — from which he concludes that the laboratory is the motionless frame and the aether a flexible, dynamic medium.
