"The New Knowledge" is the term used for designating the revelations which came with the study of radium and radio-activity. "The New Knowledge" has taught us that matter — lead, stone, wood, hydrogen, oxygen, everything and anything which we call matter — is nothing but electricity. An atom of hydrogen consists of a thousand "electrons" — mere charges of electricity. An atom of lead consists of some hundreds of thousands of such "electrons'. That is why hydrogen is so light and lead so heavy. Each is drawn to that great mass of matter, the earth, in proportion to the number of electric charges which make up the earth. That is the law of gravitation. It is all a question of electricity. We ourselves are nothing but collections of "electron" of many sorts. The rose in the bosom of beauty, and the bone in the jaws of the Beast, are like the Beauty and the Beast, nothing but greater or lesser, simpler or more complex collection of "electrons" or electric charges. Matter studied closely vanishes in matterless electricity.
