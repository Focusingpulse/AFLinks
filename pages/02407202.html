Bismarck, The Man of Political Success Napoleon the First certainly expressed the creed of most great politicians when he said: "Events must never determine politics, but politics must determine events. To be carried away by every incident is to have no political system at all." Bismarck's greatness is based on the fact that he made exactly the opposite profession of faith to his own.
