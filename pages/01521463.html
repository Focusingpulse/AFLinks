TeslaTech, LLC Organization Founder Steven R Elswick Type Privately held company; conference organizer and publisher Purpose Supporting and encouraging independent inventors through publications and conferences Headquarters Queen Valley, Arizona, United States Publisher and conference organizer Steven R Elswick Publication ExtraOrdinary Technology (later ExtraOrdinary Science & Technology ) Website www.teslatech.info TeslaTech (also written Tesla Tech , and trading as TeslaTech, LLC) is an American organization founded by the electrical engineer Steven R Elswick that is dedicated to the study, publication and demonstration of Tesla technology and related unconventional science. It states its mission as supporting and encouraging independent inventors through its publications and its conferences, and it presents itself as an outlet for research that its participants regard as neglected or actively suppressed by mainstream institutions. Its two principal activities are the annual ExtraOrdinary Technology Conference , held for most of its run in Albuquerque, New Mexico, and the magazine ExtraOrdinary Technology . Background and founding TeslaTech grew directly out of two earlier ventures led by Elswick. The first was the International Tesla Society , established in the early 1980s in Colorado Springs by Dale Pond, Toby Grotz and Elswick. The Society held its first International Tesla Symposium in 1984, marking the centennial of Nikola Tesla 's arrival in the United States, and continued to hold symposia in even-numbered years through 1998; from 1989 it also ran the more informal, engineering-oriented Extraordinary Science conferences in odd-numbered years. Elswick served as an officer and director of the Society from 1984 to 1995, edited the symposium proceedings, and edited and produced its periodicals, including Tesla magazine and Extraordinary Science magazine.
