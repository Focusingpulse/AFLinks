Quark and Particle Isomers 8/10/23 1 Quark and Particle Isomers John Caywood Independent Researcher john_caywood@hotmail.com Abstract The process of finding the least common denominator of quarks has yielded four new unique quarks. Taking this process one step further, particles made of these new quarks can be proposed and investigated. If these new particles are just isomers of existing particles, how can we tell if a particle is composed of the well-known quarks or the new quarks?
