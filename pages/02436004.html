Osmond Lecture III [ 1 ] Karma is best understood by contrasting it with the other impulse in man—that impulse which we describe with the word Freedom . Let us first place the question of karma before us, quite crudely, if I may say so. What does it signify? In human life we have to record the fact of reincarnation, successive earthly lives.
