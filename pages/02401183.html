iberorus2025-G.008 The role of cultural values in creating the political image of Michelle Bachelet Elizaveta O. Grebennikova1 1 Russian Presidential Academy of National Economy and Public Administration, Russia elisaveta.grebennikova@gmail.com The relevance of the influence of cultural values on the formation of political image is due to the beliefs and principles underlying the ideology of politicians. In addition, the analysis helps to understand how these attitudes shape their political behavior, decision-making and communication strategies. The example of the political career of Chilean President Michelle Bachelet demonstrates the important role of cultural values in the formation of the political image of women leaders. The scientific novelty lies in the fact that this study reveals the connection between informal institutions, such as cultural norms and social values, and the formation of the political image of women leaders using the example of Michelle Bachelet. To analyze the political image, the values ​adhered to by the residents of Chile were determined using the approaches of Geert Hofstede and Shalom Schwartz. An analysis of Bachelet's speeches shows that she practiced feminism without repression and provocation, demanding social integration, which is the central axis of her election programs.
