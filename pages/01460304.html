The Law of Assimilation - The Foundation Dynamic of All That Is [Notes, and thoughts on the subject of polarity and its dynamics] Throughout SVP , the svpwiki and Walter Russell's writings is a reoccurring thread that seems to bear on most everything else. One encounters this thread of thought often and in seemingly different forms or areas of application. Bloomfield-Moore wrote in Keely and His Discoveries that this idea, concept, principle, dynamic or law is the Cosmical Law that is the foundation of all other laws. " By this law ( Law of Assimilation ) a far greater number of the phenomena of nature and the laboratory can be explained than have been otherwise explained by scores of laws which are frankly admitted to be empirical. " [ Bloomfield-Moore , Veil Withdrawn , Law of Assimilation ] The law is in three parts or functions not unlike as revealed in Keely 's Law of Force " Energy manifests itself in three forms: Creative , the vibrating aggregate; Transmissive , being the propagation of isochronous waves through the media in which it is immersed; Attractive , being its action upon other aggregates capable of vibrating in unisons or Harmony ." [ Keely , 1894] Male Sex Female Action Transmission Reaction Force Resistance Motion There is the Creator Action/Ideation/Desire the Created.
