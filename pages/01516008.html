Herbs vs Measles -- Traditional Chinese medicine patents rexresearch rexresearch1 Herbs vs Measles Purely Chinese herbal medicine bath packet -- CN111388588 The invention discloses medicinal liquor for preventing measles. The medicinal liquor is characterized by being made from 30 g of safflowers, 20 g of doubleteeth pubescent angelica roots, 30 g of cassia barks, 28 g of Chinese taxillus herbs, 18 g of belvedere fruits, 20 g of honeysuckle buds and flowers, 6 g of Chinese caterpillar fungus, 20 g of Chinese angelica, 8 g of liquorice roots, and 800 ml of rice wine . The medicinal materials are finely ground, the ground materials are put in a container, Chinese liquor is added, the container is sealed, soaking preservation is carried out for 20 days, and residues are removed through filtration to obtain the medicinal liquor. Properties of the medicinal materials supplement each other, so that the medicinal liquor has the efficacy of dispersingwind and diffusing the lung, clearing heat and detoxifying the body, and promoting eruption, and can efficiently cure measles.
