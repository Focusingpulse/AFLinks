Owl nebula

· archived # 1,461,151
"If you now study the ring nebulae of the heavens you can see numerous examples of these dying suns. Many of them are shown in these pages. The Owl nebula is a very interesting example of an "unwinding" sun. It has a big hole clear through it. The Ring nebula in Lyra is also interesting to note because what was left of a sun regenerated at the center to repeat throwing off rings until nothing is left."
