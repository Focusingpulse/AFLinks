Received the following email from Hal Fox about the Minato Motor, thought it might be interesting to all; ================== Date: 21 July 1998 From: Hal Fox <halfox@slkc.uswest.net> To: Jerry Wayne Decker <jdecker@keelynet.com> Subject: Minato Motor Dear Jerry Decker, I have asked for further engineering information from TRICOSMOS on the input/output engineering measurements. I have a verbal that says for the small Minator motor hooked to a squirrel-cage fan, the input power used was 73% less than for the standard (normal fractional HP motor) fan assembly. I believe that we have problems in measuring pulse-driven devices with multimeters. Same for the output power. If the 500 Kg unit puts out 1 KW and if it is over unity, it will not compete with other new-energy devices. One kilowatt from 1,000 pounds will not be competitive. Hard to put in my battery-operated car. Thanks for including me in your frequent notes. Best regards, Hal Fox.
