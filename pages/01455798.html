Arcturus ( click to enlarge ) Q. 6– The sixth problem concerns interplanetary and intersystem dwelling between earthly lives. It was given through this source that the entity Edgar Cayce , after the experience as Uhjltd , went to the system of Arcturus , and then returned to earth. Does this indicate a usual or unusual step in soul evolution ? A. 6– As indicated, or as has been indicated in other sources besides this one as respecting this very problem, Arcturus is that which may be called the center of this universe, through which individuals pass and at which period there comes the choice of the individual as to whether it is to return to complete there – that is, in this planetary system , our sun , the earth sun and its planetary system, or to pass on to others. This was an unusual step, and yet a usual one. [ There is a river ] See Also Astrology Ether - Cayce Stages of Soul Development There is a River
