It proposes that a single tired light mechanism accounts for both the cosmological redshift and the intrinsic redshift of quasars, without any expansion of the universe. Abstract A reasonable explanation of both redshifts: cosmological (without expansion of the universe) and intrinsic, is given using a single tired light mechanism. In the first case, the redshift is produced because the light interacts with microwaves. In the second, the interaction is with radio waves. And all this is compatible with a static universe with a space temperature of 2.7 ºK. The mechanism García Juliá's starting point is that intergalactic space is not empty: it carries the microwave background, which he treats as thermal radiation in equilibrium at 2.7 ºK. Light crossing that space interacts with the microwaves and loses energy exponentially, E ( t ) = E (0) e − t /τ with τ a time constant — a transfer of energy from photon to microwave that he likens to the discharge of a capacitor. Since E = h ν the same law applies to frequency, and the redshift follows as z = e t /τ − 1 For t ≪ τ this reduces to z = t /τ, which is the low-redshift form of Hubble's law provided that τ = 1/ H so the Hubble constant is reinterpreted not as an expansion rate but as the inverse of the photon's energy-loss time constant. Substituting d = ct gives z = e Hd / c − 1 so that on this account redshift grows exponentially with distance rather than linearly — a departure from the Doppler reading that becomes significant only at large z .
