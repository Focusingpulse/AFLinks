Introduction Itis well known that, after anodic oxidation of metals like Al and Ta, the system metalfmetaloxidefelectrolyte shows rectification. However, the origin and seat of this property is unknown. Especially the question whether this rectification is uniform across the surface or is restricted to (many) spots on the surface, is often discussed. It is the aim of the present paper to show that up till now there are no definite indications for "point rectification". In this discussion we have restricted ourselves to Al-oxide layers. 2.
