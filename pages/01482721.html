psychokinesis

· archived # 1,482,721
The term psychokinesis (from the Greek "psyche", meaning mind , soul , heart , or breath ; "kinesis", meaning motion , movement ; literally "mind-movement"), also referred to as telekinesis (Greek , literally "distant-movement") with respect to strictly describing movement of matter , sometimes abbreviated PK and TK respectively, is a term coined by publisher Henry Holt to refer to the direct influence of mind on a physical system that cannot be entirely accounted for by the mediation of any known physical energy . Examples of psychokinesis could include distorting or moving an object, and influencing the output of a random number generator .
