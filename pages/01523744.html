Refutation that Experiments Verify Relativity and a Reasonable Proof of E = mc2 Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Speaker Jorma Jormakka Date 8 November 2025 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording Refutation that Experiments Verify Relativity and a Reasonable Proof of E = mc2 was a live session presented by Jorma Jormakka on 8 November 2025 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. When pointing out very serious mathematical errors in the relativity theory, people try to argue that the theory has been verified by a large number of physical experiments and therefore it must be correct. The presented paper looks at some of the most commonly presented experiments that claim to verify relativity theory and gives alternative explanations to the results of those experiments. Jorma Jormakka presents, Hosted by Ian Cowan. Summary A first appearance by Jorma Jormakka , a Finnish mathematician and engineer — latterly professor of military technology at the National Defence University in Helsinki, before that a full professor of telecommunications at Aalto, with a doctorate in mathematics and an early training in theoretical physics — now based in Poland. Host Ian J Cowan had approached him after Harry Ricker drew his papers to the society's attention. The talk was delivered as a pre-recorded video, and Jormakka's opening remark set the scope wider than the advertised paper: not that the experiments fail to verify relativity, but that the theory is wrong in its entirety. The standing argument The paper's premise is a familiar exchange. Point out a mathematical error in relativity, and the reply is that the theory has been confirmed by thousands of experiments, so one is wasting one's time. Jormakka's response is to go through the confirmations. His objection to the Lorentz transformation is that the time in a moving inertial frame is a local time — a coordinate that depends on position, formally like time-zone time. Flyin
