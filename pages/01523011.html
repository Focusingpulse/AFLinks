"Tony" Robertson is an American aerospace engineer who spent some 23 years in research and development at NASA Marshall Space Flight Center in Huntsville, Alabama, and who has become a central organiser of the field of advanced and "breakthrough" space propulsion . He is the founder and president of the Institute for Advanced Studies in the Space, Propulsion & Energy Sciences (IASSPES) and chaired the Space, Propulsion & Energy Sciences International Forum (SPESIF). On this wiki he is best known as the co-author, with Frank J Znidarsic , of " The Flow of Energy ". From 1974 to 1978 he served in the US Navy as a jet-engine technician on the F-14's TF30 engine. From 1984 to 1987 he was a project engineer at the Naval Weapons Center, China Lake, coordinating solid-rocket-motor testing for the vertically launched ASROC. At NASA Marshall he worked as an aerospace engineer on advanced-concepts and advanced-space-transportation programmes, helping to establish Marshall's Advanced Concept Research Facility.
