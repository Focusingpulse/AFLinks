Einstein's special theory of relativity asserts that no physical object can travel faster than the speed of light. The theory also holds that mass and energy will have different values depending on your frame of reference. The idea that the mass (energy) density in any one frame would always be at least equal to or greater than zero is called the "weak energy condition."
