Astakhova1 , Anastasia Y. Bundel1 , Dmitry Y. Alferov1 , Marina V. Shatunova1 , Inna A. Rozinkina 1 1 Hydrometeorological Research Center of the Russian Federation, Moscow, Russia elena_ast_hmc@mail.ru Ensemble methods are widely used in numerical weather forecasts, allowing for an a priori assessment of their quality and providing users with an extended volume of forecast products, including probabilistic ones. An ensemble system should describe forecast uncertainties associated with both the inaccuracy of the initial and boundary data for numerical models, and the imperfection of the models themselves. In this paper, we consider methods accounting for atmospheric model imperfections in ensemble forecasting and assess the impact of perturbing various parameters of the schemes that describe subgrid-scale processes, including cloud-radiation interaction and subgrid-scale orographic drag, on ensemble mean forecasts, the ensemble spread, and probabilistic characteristics of forecasts. Numerical experiments were performed using the ICON-Ru2-EPS regional high-resolution ensemble system [1], based on the ICON model with a grid step of about 2 km, which made it possible to resolve deep convection explicitly.
