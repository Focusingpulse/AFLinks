I am not convinced that nature needs an >intermediate that lasts only 10^-20 seconds or less. Many of the doubters in the physics community (mostly non-HEP people by that time) were convinced when the intermediate was produced directly in p-pbar collisions and its decays observed. Now that 15 million Z's have been observed and the branching ratios for different decay modes studied in great detail, it is hard to see how anyone could doubt the *existence* of that intermediate and that its properties are exactly those needed to explain what is known at low energies about the weak interaction. When LEP-200 comes on line, the same will be done for the W's. -- James A.
