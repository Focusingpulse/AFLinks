, Munich Translated by Steiner Online Library The First Prayer: “Brothers of Antiquity” Notes by Alice Kinkel, first degree The first prayer distinguishes us from all other such endeavors, which are based on documents or on a traditional wisdom. We do not refer to anything like that, we only tie in with the work that has been done, with what has actually been achieved. In the near future, among other things that will be undertaken against us, our occult movement will also be tried to be suspected and vilified, so we should know this, to which we are joining.
