Part IV.2: Observations about Atmospheric Phenomena — GA 6. Goethe's World View (1985)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 1,585,787
In 1815 Goethe becomes acquainted with Luke Howard's Attempt at a Natural History and Physics of the Clouds . He is stimulated by it to sharpen his reflection about cloud formations and atmospheric conditions. He had in fact already made many earlier observations about these phenomena and recorded them. But he lacked “overview and branches of science to connect with” in order to bring together what he had experienced.
