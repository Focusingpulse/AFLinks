It does not. Something in that list of assumptions must be wrong, and which one is wrong has been a central question of cosmology ever since. The paradox The argument is old. Thomas Digges raised the difficulty in the sixteenth century, Kepler used it against an infinite universe, Edmond Halley and Jean-Philippe de Chéseaux discussed it in the eighteenth, and Heinrich Wilhelm Olbers restated it in 1823, which is how it acquired his name.
