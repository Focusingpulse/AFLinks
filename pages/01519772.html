Background The Electric Universe conferences were organised by the Thunderbolts Project around the Electric Universe model, which holds that electric currents and plasma play a far larger role in astrophysics than gravitational cosmology allows. Two were recorded in the World Science Database: The Human Story in Las Vegas in January 2012 and The Tipping Point in Albuquerque a year later.
