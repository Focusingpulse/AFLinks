, Basel Translated by Steiner Online Library Address at the Cremation of Harald Lille Dear mourners! Our dear friend Harald Lille has left us young. He has covered a part of his life with us in common work, common endeavor, common striving. We got to know him, and in him we got to know what can work so that our thoughts and feelings follow his soul as it now ascends into spiritual worlds. Since your soul left your body, dear friend Lille, all the moments in which I was able to get to know you have once again come to my mind.
