The Threefold Social Order [ 1 ] Following a series of lectures in Stuttgart, Dr. Steiner spoke here last Sunday—at the request of the Esslingen workers—in the Kugelsaal about the threefold social order, and, as everywhere else, he received extremely enthusiastic and heartfelt applause. In his two-hour lecture, he provided a profound analysis of how the divide between the bourgeoisie and the proletariat arose and grew ever deeper.
