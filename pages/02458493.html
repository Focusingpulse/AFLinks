Their failure transformed the group into an association of young researchers with much solidarity, criticizing foreign models and developing a theory-oriented parapsychological cross-disciplin￾ary research in the 1970s and 1980s. The works of François Favre and Pierre Janin, two infl uential members of GERP, are reviewed as examples. A short presentation is offered of some of their theories. GERP although dormant has not been dissolved, thus this history reveals a recent but underestimated pe￾riod of French parapsychology and the lineaments of an original research to be deepened.
