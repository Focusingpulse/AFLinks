Mathematical Analysis and Modeling of Pyramidal Containers, Right Pyramids, and Related Polyhedra 10 Sept 2019 ©2019 H C Rajpoot 1 Mathematical Analysis and Modeling of Pyramidal Containers, Right Pyramids, and Related Polyhedra Harish Chandra Rajpoot 10 Sept 2019 Department of Mechanical Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Delhi, New Delhi 110016, India 1. Introduction: In this paper, we aim to derive the generalized formula to compute all the important parameters like V-cut angle using HCR’s Theorem, edge length of open end, dihedral angle using HCR’s Corollary, surface area, and volume of a pyramidal flat container with a regular polygonal base, right pyramids, and polyhedral [1]. We will apply these generalized formulas to compute important parameters & make paper models of the pyramidal flat containers with square, regular pentagonal, hexagonal, heptagonal & octagonal bases, right pyramids [2,3,4] and non-uniform polyhedra [5,6,7]. The concept of making pyramidal flat containers, right pyramids, and polyhedra (bi-pyramids) is mainly based on the rotation or folding of two co￾planar planes about their intersecting straight edges (as discussed in detail and formulated in HCR’s Theorem). In brief, the procedure of making a pyramidal flat container, right pyramid or polyhedron using a sheet (of desired thickness) of paper, polymer, metal or alloy which can be easily cut, bent & butt-joined at the mating lateral edges, is based on the following steps 1. Making a drawing on a thin sheet of paper (we can also use a sheet of polymer, metal, or alloy) 2. Cutting and removing undesired parts from the sheet drawing to get a blank 3. Bending lateral faces (trapezoidal in case of flat container and triangular in case of a pyramid) about the edges of a regular polygonal base 4. Joining the mating edges of lateral faces (gluing in case of paper, welding in case of polymer, metal or alloy) In the above procedure, the most important step is to make a drawing accurately
