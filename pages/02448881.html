This is not to say that I believe the search for free energy is a waste of time, quite the opposite. Yet it seems that, paradoxically, our society is constantly propelled forward by the very people who would be termed 'heretics'. Society is not logical, it is not creative, nor does it have a conscience. Society exibits all of the hallmarks of a 'lower' animal i.e. instinctive, reflexive, short term survival goals, etc. It is the individual within a society that causes creative change. Society, by its nature, resists change. That resistance to change is something we, on the fringe, have all encountered in some form or another. Perhaps this is as it should be. IMHO, a Papp or Gray or whatever device gets nowhere (in the free energy arena) for one obvious reason. It's not that nobody will listen, I'm (and many others) all ears.
