Giving

· archived # 1,459,635
Russell God gives of Himself to all His universe in an eternity of endless regiving . God 's universe regives of itself to God in an eternity of endless giving . That which God gives is Love . That which is regiven is love . That is the divine story of Creation . It is a story of cause and effect in the giving and regiving of love . [ Atomic Suicide , page 189] Cayce "Keep optimistic as much as possible. Know that as ye give out ye receive, and that alone as ye have given away do ye possess." [ Cayce 1804-1] "Then, your ideal is not what you may acquire by 'Gimmie - Gimmie - Gimmie,' but 'What may I do, what may I give , in my relationships to others to make that association the BEAUTIFUL EXPERIENCE,' for which ye long so always!" [ Cayce 2030-1] "In GIVING does a soul grow, even as a tree, even as a rock, even as a sunset..." [ Cayce 699-1] The Christ Letters GIVE and GIVE abundantly and rejoice that you have gifts to give to those in need because as you GIVE so will your gifts be restored to you, in the way that you most need them. GIVE with happy hearts, GIVE in the faith and knowledge that where there is lack in your lives, so will the ‘Father’ do Its Love Work abundantly in you and for you. Do nothing with a heavy heart because a heavy heart is what you will continue to have. GIVE everything with joyous spirit, that everything in your life may bring to you only joy and spiritual insight.[ The Christ Letters , L2] See Also bequeathal Dynaspheric Force Figure 14.02 - Thoth
