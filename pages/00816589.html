Multi-layer GRPO Fei Ding 1 Abstract The success of DeepSeek-R1 (DeepSeek-AI et al., 2025) has demonstrated the effectiveness of the GRPO algorithm. However, due to the absence of process rewards, GRPO often suffers from in￾efficiencies in exploration, as a single detailed error can result in an entirely incorrect final an￾swer, leading to zero rewards.To address these challenges, we propose MGRPO (Multi-layer GRPO). In the first layer, GRPO operates identi￾cally to the original version, generating an initial response. This response is then fed into a second￾stage GRPO process, which primarily trains the model to correct errors. Experimental results in￾dicate that MGRPO outperforms standard GRPO, achieving superior performance. 1. Introduction Sparse Outcome Rewards provide feedback only at the final step of reasoning, which often leads to inefficiencies in rein￾forcement learning (RL) for large language models (LLMs). A minor mistake in an intermediate step can result in a com￾pletely incorrect final answer, leading to zero rewards and making the exploration process highly inefficient (Uesato et al., 2022; Lightman et al., 2023a; Wang et al., 2023; Yuan et al., 2024a). In contrast, Dense Process Rewards demonstrate significant advantages in terms of reasoning scalability, training effi￾ciency (Sutton & Barto, 2018), and credit assignment (Leike et al., 2018). However, the practical application of dense rewards in RL training remains limited (Setlur et al., 2024), and current state-of-the-art industrial models still primarily rely on verifiable outcome rewards without achieving break￾throughs in dense reward utilization (DeepSeek-AI et al., 2025; Team et al., 2025).
