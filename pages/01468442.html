The Evening Herald November 24-1898

· archived # 1,468,442
THE last has not been heard of the Keely motor . The president of the Keely Motor Co. has publicly stated that Keely leaves a document, covering some 2,000 pages, in which is contained all the information to make clear, the nature of his discovery, and he also states that Mr. Keely had been making steady progress toward perfecting his motor up to the time of his death. It may confidently be expected from what the president of the company says that the world will soon be treated to another chapter of the Keely motor discovery. But the world will remain skeptical until there shall be a practical demonstration of the value of the alleged discovery. Return to Keely Chronology
