Correspondin￾gly, lowest common multiple, or smallest common multiple of three integers p, q and r, usually denoted by lcm(p, q, r), is the smallest positive integer that is divisible by p, q and r, e.g., lcm(3, 6, 9) = 3×6 = 6×3 = 9×2 = 18, etc. We will reduce the problem of finding of the Least Common Multiple of three integer numbers to the following two integer minimization problems, wherein the first one is polynomial-time problem and another one is NP-hard problem (see also [1, 4]). 2.
