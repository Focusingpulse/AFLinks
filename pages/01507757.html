How many fingers do you type with?

· archived # 1,507,757
Just curious, I know technically your supposed to keep all your fingers on the keyboard but I never could get the hang of that..not sure why. I only use my two index fingers to basically type all of my words. My right ring finger, i use to hit backspace and my left thumb to hit space. So I use four fingers total lol...Just curious to see how everyone else types. Im nearly just as fast as typing the "right" way with all fingers. Its actually pretty amazing, with muscle memory I can type most all letters without looking at the keyboad..with no starting point reference. Its almost as if my fingers know where the keys are before my mind can even guess. Almost as if for that moment you are thinking with something that is outside of your brain. really fun stuff. and they say chatting online wont teach you anything lol taught me to type faster lol
