Keely Doubted The latest tests of Mr. Keely's "motors," run by " etheric vapor ," have not been of a character to increase public confidence in the reality of his pretended discoveries. The actual discovery of a new motive power of the capability which Keely claims for his " etheric vapor " would be promptly followed, we may be sure, by its utilizations. Keely seems to be content with experiments and with occasional exhibitions of his apparatus. Keely is undoubtedly a humbug. (The New York Times) 6/8/1885 See Also Chronology Liberator
