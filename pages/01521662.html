Roy Keys Inc.), Montreal Pages 306 ISBN 9781987980103 The Experimental and Historical Foundations of Electricity, Volume 2 is a 2018 book by Andre K T Assis , published by Apeiron of Montreal. It continues The Experimental and Historical Foundations of Electricity (2010), carrying the same method forward from the amber effect and the discovery of conductors and insulators into the phenomena of charged conductors. The method is the distinctive thing about both volumes. Every effect is introduced through an experiment the reader can perform with simple and inexpensive materials — adhesive tape, plastic, aluminium foil — and the concepts and laws are drawn from what the experiments show, rather than stated first and illustrated afterwards. Historical sources are quoted throughout.
