In my definition of atom I do not confine myself to the sixth subdivision , where this luminiferous ether is developed in its crude form, as far as my researches prove. Possibly, in time , a light may fall upon this theory that will bring its simplicity forward for scientific research. At present I can only compare it to some planet in a dark space , where the light of the sun of science has not yet reached it."
