While current metrics focus on productivity gains and syntactical correctness, this pa￾per argues that these metrics are insufficient to capture the long-term systemic risks introduced by AI. We pro￾pose the concept of Epistemic Debt: the divergence between the complexity of a software system and the developer’s cognitive model of that system.
