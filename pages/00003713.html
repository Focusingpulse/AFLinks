Foundations of Physics, Vol. 11, Nos. 1/2, t981 The Quasi-Doppler Experiment According to Absolute Space-Time Theory Stefan Marinov ~ Received November 20, 1979 We find the relation between the frequencies received by two observers placed at a git'en parallel with 180 ° difference in longitude when they observe a distant light (radio) source. This relation depends on the absolute velocity of the Earth; however, because of the occurrence of aberration, the effect cannot be registered in practice.
