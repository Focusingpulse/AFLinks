(Dated: August 23, 2025) Abstract We study the Malay words of A Malay-English Dictionary (Romanised) by R. J. Wilkinson, 1932. We draw the natural logarithm of the number of words, normalised, starting with a letter vs the natural logarithm of the rank of the letter, normalised.
