Site visitors since 01/01/03 10/18/15 - Drinking water from thin air A private firm has developed a machine that condenses the water vapour in the air into clean drinking water. The firm recently donated the machines to the state government, which is planning to use them in the scarcity-hit areas. The scarcity-hit Jalna district received three such machines, which were installed at the civil hospital, Zilla Parishad and collector's office on Saturday. Each machine has the capacity to generate around 500 litres of water a day. State water supply and sanitation minister Babanrao Lonikar inaugurated the air water harvester (Akash Amrit) on Saturday. The company has donated 100 such machines to the state government to tackle water scarcity in the state. Lonikar said the private company has designed the device that offers a green and clean drinking water solution. "The machine harvests clean drinking water from air. The private company has donated 100 such machines to the state government and around 10 machines will be installed in the drought-hit Jalna district," he said. An expert from the company said the technology uses proprietary knowledge of superior condensation engineering techniques to convert atmospheric water vapour into the cleanest form of drinking water. "Unlike water filtration technologies like reverse osmosis, which require a water source, our technology does not require one," the expert said. The device costs around Rs 8 lakh and can function in full capacity when the maximum temperature is between 20 and 45 degrees celsius. The humidity levels in the atmosphere have to be 30%, the expert said. "The technology also offers the advantage of an instantaneous solution for drinking water as it starts producing water within minutes of installation. Depending on the prevailing humidity and temperature conditions, the system is capable of making 500 to over 1,000 litres of clean drinking water per day," the expert said.
