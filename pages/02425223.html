Introduction In some societies, living in dry areas, people sometimes engage in magical rituals in order to make the rain fall. In their view, rain is such a precious resource that it belongs to the gods realm. In our modern societies, it has been a long time since the rain is not a secret anymore. Right after the Second World War, John Von Neumann, Hungarian American scientist, was able to forecast the weather using the very first computers. In the same motion, he started to conceive the possiblity of manipulate the climate. His idea was to spread colorants on the poles in order to modify the albedo effect at the scale of the planet. The first american test happened in 1958 in Japan, and consisted of an operation of cloudseeding. Approximatively at the same time, in USSR, scientists like M.Budyko also tried to seed the clouds with specific chemicals in order to make the rain fall. At that time, the climate and more generally the planet as a system of interelations between natural elements definitely lost its supernatural dimension.
