The Round Robin >> Vol. 03 >> No. 01 [1] THE ENTRANCE INTO LIFE ETERNAL (Selected from Swedenborg's account of the first experiences after Death.) "After they (Angels or spirits) have given the newly awakened person the use of light, they render him every service he can possibly desire, and instruct him concerning all things of the other life, so far as he is able to comprehend them. But if he is not disposed to receive instruction, he separates himself from them, and is received by other good spirits who also render him kind offices. But if his life in the world has been such that he cannot remain in the company of the good, he wishes to leave them also, and these changes continue until at length he associates himself with those who are in accord with his life in the world. With them he finds his own life, and, wonderful to relate, he then leads a life similar to that which he led in the world. Afterward he is led from one state to another, and at last either into heaven or hell; this also I have been permitted to learn by much experience. "I have spoken with some on the third day after their death . . . and I told them that funeral arrangements were being made for burying them, and at this they were struck with astonishment, declaring that they were alive . . . and wondering exceedingly that during their Life in the body they had not believed in such a life after death . . . (such persons) are exceedingly ashamed when they find that they are alive after death: those who are confirmed in this belief are associated together and for the most part attached to some infernal society . . .
