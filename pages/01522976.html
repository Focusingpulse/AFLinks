Gabriel Thierrin Born ( 1921-12-22 ) December 22, 1921 Surpierre, Switzerland Died August 1, 2016 (2016-08-01) (aged 94) Ottawa, Ontario, Canada Residence London, ON, Canada Nationality Swiss-Canadian Known for Semigroup and code theory; the syntactic monoid of codes; a five-dimensional cosmological model Scientific career Fields Algebra ; semigroup theory; formal languages and codes; DNA computing Institutions Université de Montréal; University of Western Ontario Gabriel Thierrin (22 December 1921 – 1 August 2016) was a Swiss-Canadian mathematician, Professor Emeritus of Mathematics at the University of Western Ontario and one of the founders of the computer science programme at the Université de Montréal . He worked for more than half a century on semigroups and rings, then on automata, formal languages and codes, and finally on DNA computing, publishing some 240 items over a career running from 1951 to 2007. He appears on this wiki for a single paper out of that total: "A model of a five-dimensional universe," published in Speculations in Science and Technology in 1990. Cosmology was, in his own description, a lifelong personal interest rather than his profession, and the proportions should be kept in view — he is listed in The Worldwide List of Dissident Scientists on the strength of about one publication in two hundred and forty.
