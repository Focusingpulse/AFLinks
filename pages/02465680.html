According to Spiller's system, both ether and atoms are material. The atoms are indivisible. Chemistry, being based on the correctness of this statement, forces us to accept it". - We are "forced to accept it", only until it is proved by demonstration to be false. Until this stage was reached, it would have been as useless to make Mr. Keely's theories known, as it would be to publish a treatise to prove that two and two make five.
