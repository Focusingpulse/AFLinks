Tags: None Jetijs Gold Member Join Date: Aug 2007 Posts: 2137 Share Tweet #2 03-31-2011, 08:09 PM You can use HV diodes for this. If you can't get them, just use several low cost 1000V diodes in series. Use fast ones. UF5408 are good, they are rated 1000v 3A, just solder 30 of them in series and you will have one monster diode rated for 30kV at 3A, should do just fine. Another way is using different electrode materials at spark gap, they will work like a galvanic cell, just with higher voltages. For example I have tried tungsten and aluminum, I noticed that if I put HV + to tungsten and - to aluminum, I need way bigger voltages to jump the same gap as I need if the polarity is reversed (or maybe it was the other way around) anyway, it works like a diode, but I don't know how effective, diodes are a better way.
