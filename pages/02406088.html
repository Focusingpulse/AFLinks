Early electrostatic generators were simple friction machines, used by Benjamin Franklin and others to uncover many facts about the nature of electricity. When Volta in 1800 gave the world its first source of ample steady current —an invention that led to the telegraph, the Atlantic Cable, electric lights, and all the rest of the electrical world of appliances we know interest in electrostatics began to lag.
