A fairly well know phenomena of Dendrite (sp?) Growth shows up in the manufacturing of printed circuit boards. If the board is slightly contaminated (doesn't take much, oil from fingers for example) you can watch these things grow under a microscope. Shaped liked stair steeped lightning bolts, look like small crystals, and while I was watching them I'd swear they where 'alive' as they wiggled around shorting out traces.
