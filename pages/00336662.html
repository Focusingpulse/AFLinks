This force drives tectonics and volcanism. Jupiter’s volcanic moons are paved in basaltic rock, like the ocean floor of the Earth. Jovian moon Io is the most volcanic celestial body in the solar system. Saturn’s tidally pumped moons have cryovolcanoes. Planetary rings were formed by moons ripped apart by tidal pumping forces.
