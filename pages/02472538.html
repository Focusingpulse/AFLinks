Baltimore, Md., September 11 — A typewriter electrically operated by the human voice, thus doing away with the operator and saving much time and labor in offices, is to be one of the wonders of the near future, says Nikola Tesla, the Inventor, in the Manufacturers Record, reviewing the progress of electricity and forecasting its future. The amazing possibilities of electricity also include wonderful achievements in telautomatics, Tesla says. In the way of devices and implements of warfare, a new electric gun will soon be brought out. Dirigibles and aeroplanes will be equipped with small electric generators of high tension, from which the deadly currents will be conveyed through tin wires to the ground. Battleships and submarines will have electric and magnetic feelers so delicate that the approach of any body under water, or in darkness, will be detected. There will be torpedoes and floating mines that will direct themselves automatically, and without fail get in fatal contact with the object to be destroyed.
