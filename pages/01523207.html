Keene is an American scientist, Director of the Binary Mechanics Lab, which he founded in 1999, and originator of Binary Mechanics — a theoretical framework that treats space, time and energy as fully quantized ("binary") rather than continuous, and that seeks to derive fundamental physical constants from first principles. Keene has served as Editor of the Journal of Binary Mechanics since 2010. Working outside the acad
