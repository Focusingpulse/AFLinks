Customized Personal Profile Help

· archived # 2,433,055
Hi everyone, When you registered with this forum, you filled out some profile questions. But wait! That is just the simple profile that comes with the forum package. You have a very advanced, very powerful tool for free so you can network, promote your business, ideas, links, etc... You have your own free blog, discussion site, etc... In the main menu bar above, there is a My Blog link and that takes you directly to your own private blog once you are registered and signed in. Next to that is Profiles link. Click it and then click on My Profile. You will see what I mean. The entire profile page is able to be edited simply by dragging and dropping the "blocks" around on your page however you want them. You can play around with the menu above where the picture block is. You can add blocks, delete blocks by clicking the X, or you can edit the blocks by clicking the little tool icon. At the top, you can see the Disussion link, Homepage link, Resume Link, Blog Link, etc... Those are all there for you to build your network, get the word out and whatever else you want to do with them. More info will be posted here soon...
