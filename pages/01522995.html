Geoff Haselhurst Residence Australia Nationality Australian Known for Promoting the Wave Structure of Matter; the website On Truth & Reality (spaceandmotion.com) Scientific career Fields Philosophy of science, physics, metaphysics Geoff Haselhurst is an Australian philosopher and independent researcher, and the author of the philosophy-and-physics website On Truth & Reality (spaceandmotion.com). He is the principal advocate of the Wave Structure of Matter (WSM), a foundational theory of physics developed by Milo Wolff , which he argues provides the simplest possible account of physical reality. Wave Structure of Matter Haselhurst's central concern is the foundations of physics. Following Milo Wolff , he rejects the conception of matter as discrete point particles and holds instead that matter is made of spherical standing waves in a continuous, wave-bearing space . On this account what appears as a particle — an electron, say — is the wave-center of a spherical standing wave in the space medium, and the interactions between such wave-structures give rise to the phenomena that conventional physics describes with separate postulates. From this single foundation — in his phrasing, that "one thing exists," space together with its spherical wave motions — Haselhurst argues that the results of quantum theory and relativity, Mach's principle, and features of cosmology can be deduced rather than assumed. He presents the Wave Structure of Matter as the outcome of applying Occam's razor to physics itself: the simplest and most unified starting point, from which (he holds) the rest follows without appeal to opinion. These claims are not accepted by mainstream physics. Beyond physics, his website extends the same emphasis on a unified, physically grounded worldview into philosophy, metaphysics, ethics, ecology and education, drawing on thinkers such as Spinoza.
