" Power over music does not depend solely on the mere agreement of 'how to do it.' The student in song will never learn the perfection of beauty except from the preparation of the heart . To make a real musician, there must be a sense of the ever-presence of the Creator of all beauty . The boy-musician must begin his day with prayer , and end it with praise . This made Handel , Bach , Haydn , and Mozart . Music is neither dram nor sweetmeat , neither sensual nor intellectual . It is made so now; but in this order of music there is neither joy nor love , thankfulness nor reverence ."
