Biological Terrain-Cellular vitality

· archived # 2,431,623
Useful information. A definition of biological terrain (in reference to our human bodies) is the internal environment of our bodies. This covers thousands of factors, hormones levels, cellular vitality, hydration, functioning of all organs, etc. It is unspeakably important that we keep our internal biological terrain clean and balanced, functioning as smoothly as possible to deal with internal and external challenges. There are many different ways that people have devised to get a measurement of the bodies biological terrain. Here are a few: Looking at blood, saliva, urine and sometimes hair and/or fingernails for electron levels, PH, toxicity. The SCIO measures the following (among many other factors)- Voltage- related to adrenals Amperage- related to brain function and serotonin Resistance- related to the ease of flow of energy through the body Hydration- related to the ease of flow of water, and the amount of water PH balance- related to the alkalinity/acidity balance in the body Cellular vitality which is assessed by looking at the hydration and angle (or curvature) of a cell.
