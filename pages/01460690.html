Medu-Neter

· archived # 1,460,690
R. A. Schwaller de Lubicz THE SYMBOL in our modern languages there is no word that designates the exact meaning of Symbol , as it was conceived by the Ancients. This is why I should like to replace the word symbol with the word Medu-Neter , which conveys the "signs that bear the Neters " (" Neter " signifying the Principle or the Idea in the Platonic sense.)
