To the best of the writer's knowledge, this publication was the only one which succeeded in gaining Tesla's consent to publish his autobiography. He also cooperated with the publishers in the undertaking and eventually saw the famed work appear under the title "My Inventions." With the consent and cooperation of Mr. Gernsback, I translated this work into the Croatian language and published it in "Slobodna Misao" in 1926 on the occasion of Dr. Tesla's 70th birthday. The translation proved to be quite a task, but the pleasure I derived from the end result more than repaid me for the hours spent in the translating. Tribute Paid In 1919 In his announcement in "Electrical Experimenter" in January of 1919, which introduced the publication of "My Inventions," Mr. Gernsback said: "Ninety percent of the entire electrical industry pays tribute to his (Tesla's) genius.
