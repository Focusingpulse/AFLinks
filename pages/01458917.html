As degree of harmonicity or enharmonicity is relative (relationship between two or more quantities) there are enharmonic relationships, ratios , chords or intervals . A shortcut (general rule of thumb) to determining enharmonicity as opposed to harmony is if the number is divisible by 2 (with no remainder) it is considered harmonic but on the natural scale . All other divisions are some degree of enharmonic in relation to the harmonic . In SVP or Keely 's jargon, Harmonic , Dominant and Enharmonic refer to, respectively, Syntropic , Dominant and Dispersive or Entropic . Enharmonic may be considered as dispersive or entropic .
