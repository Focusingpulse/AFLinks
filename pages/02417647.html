I read the summary as the **current state of the work**, and I think the paper should be written differently from the way we've been talking about the individual experiments. The experiments are no longer the story. The story is the **geometry of change of the transformation field**: X^{(r)}\subset X^{(r+1)} creates a relative sector. That sector is not best understood as “the states that disappeared when we went backward.” It is a mathematically defined layer of transformations between two admissibility levels. Collapsing the old arena to a cone point exposes its topology. Symmetry then determines which of those transformations remain visible. The resulting invariant residual is a first Betti number. That is a coherent mathematical paper. I would lead the reader into it from the problem of **what it means for a system to change**, rather than opening with token graphs. The strongest conceptual chain is: \boxed{ \text{change of substrate} \rightarrow \text{change of admissible transformations} \rightarrow \text{relative sector} \rightarrow \text{quotient/cone} \rightarrow \text{cycles} \rightarrow \text{residual degrees of freedom} } Then the Nexus interpretation comes afterward.
