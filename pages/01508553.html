Will 2013 be a Turnaround Year for FE?

· archived # 1,508,553
Why are there so many fraudulent and unsubstantiated claims in FE---far more than any other sector? Think about it--has anyone made a fortune selling fake OU devices? It's practically become a fad--YouTube has half a million FE videos. And it's no longer professors who are dismissing FE claims, there are now websites dedicated to debunking them including this one (Farmhand seems to be this site's BS detector). But is FE about to restore its reputation in the public imagination as it goes mainstream? In a surprising move, Sterling Allan who's been reporting on 'exotic energy' for over a decade has just placed Steorn and Andrea Rossi---subjects of intense ridicule in the past--on his 'Top 5 (closest-to-market) List' . There are already a handful of FE devices commercially available now and I'm sure, unlike Magnacoaster's Vorktex, they wouldn't be shipping if they didn't perform. My guess is that once the public sees these devices in operation, reducing energy costs, the technology will go viral. In the end, it's about money. Governments under huge debt burdens can no longer afford to be in the power generating business, particularly nuclear--and the ROI for wind and solar only increases that debt burden while adding little to the grid. They may have no choice but to actively encourage distributed FE generation as they decommission aging nuclear power stations and scale back thermal. Heavily dependent on energy imports, Japan may become the first FE economy, having already de-nuked after its devastating tsunami. And China, even more dependent on imported energy, will want its millions of engineers to be on the forefront of such technology, just as they currently are with solar. And what about the oil-igarchs? Have they been secretly funding some of the many fraudulent FE claims or naysayers on sites such as this and threatening university funding if profs endorse such 'pseudo' science? Even Wikipedia seems under such control, but to their credit, they make it apparent.
