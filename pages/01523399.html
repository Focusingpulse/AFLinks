Edwards Born 1952(age73–74) Residence Toronto, ON, Canada Nationality Canadian Knownfor Static Universe , Pushing Gravity , Expanding Earth Since 1983 he has been at the Gerstein Science Information Centre of the University of Toronto. He has diverse research interests and has been the author of several articles on cosmology and the origin and early evolution of life. Over the years he has made theoretical contributions in four areas: origins of life, geophysics, gravitation physics and cosmology. Education B.Sc. in biology York University, 1977 He studied biology, biochemistry and plant ecology at McMaster University, York University and the University of Saskatchewan. Abstracts 2007 - " Induction of Gravitation in Moving Bodies " 2007 - " Le Sage's Theory of Gravity: the Revival by Kelvin and Some Later Developments " 2007 - " Photon-Graviton Recycling as Cause of Gravitation
