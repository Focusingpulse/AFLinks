Debye frequency

· archived # 1,458,589
The Debye frequency of a crystal is a theoretical maximum frequency of vibration for the atoms that make up the crystal. It was proposed by Peter Debye as part of the Debye model . It is generally not the same as the actual maximum oscillation frequency for the crystal , due to approximations in the model, however it is still a useful quantity. It plays a role in the computation of the specific heat capacity of solids and in theoretical estimates of rates of diffusion .
