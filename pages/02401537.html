GC2026-PERM026 Comparative analysis of the magnetic and geochemical characteristics of bottom sediments from Lake Kandrykul (Cis-Urals) and the Bolshoe/Maloe Miassovo lakes (Southern Urals) Anastasiia Iusupova1 1 Kazan (Volga Region) Federal University yusupovaanast095@gmail.com A comparison was conducted between bottom sediments from Lake Kandrykul (Cis-Urals) and the Bolshoe and Maloe Miassovo lakes (the Southern Urals). It was established that these sites differ in the degree of coherence between geochemical and magnetic proxies, as well as in the applicability of the CIA index. For Lake Kandrykul, CIA was shown not to be a direct climatic proxy: its variations are largely controlled by the mineralogical–lithological characteristics of the incoming terrigenous material and the degree of preservation of primary silicates (as evidenced by the relationship between CIA and WIP and by signs of geochemical immaturity of the sediments). At the same time, the magnetic parameter k_para shows statistically significant positive correlations with lithophile, chalcophile, and some siderophile elements, reflecting the dominance of terrigenous (allogenic) input and depositional conditions; in contrast, redox-sensitive U–Mo–Re and Sr display opposite trends, indicating the contribution of within-basin biogeochemical processes. For the Bolshoe and Maloe Miassovo lakes, no significant correlations were found between magnetic and geochemical parameters, indicating independent variability of magnetic characteristics relative to geochemical associations.
