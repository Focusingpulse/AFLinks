It had been hidden from me. Even if, quite naturally, potency creates many enemies, and more and more frequently now, the enemy murders you while laughing at the police. But where does this potency come from? Perhaps because I was able to write, without any concessions, exactly what I thought of my epoch? Might then that potency be nothing other than that which "the strong souls have over the weak ones", what has often in history gone in the guise of witchcraft? That's what is meant by "pursuing one's infernal course in another world", rather than one's paradisiac career in this one. Having assumed my historical responsibilities, obviously I do not pretend to be innocent. Hegel said that only the stones are innocent. (...) As can be seen, my name is on the proscription lists of my epoch. Everywhere else, besides, my name has been erased - from art, from the history of ideas, from the history of contemporary events. (...) The dominant fakery may well have everyone else's approval; it will have to go without mine."
