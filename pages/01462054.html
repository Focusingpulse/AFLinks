A new physical force has been discovered associated with the mineral shungite . This mineral produces a measurable repulsive force . This force is not electrical or magnetic or gravitational. Fullerenes (c60, for example) are also known by the name " Bucky-balls " after Buckminster Fuller and his " tensegrity " research, because structurally they resemble some of Fuller's tensegrity domes, and because Fuller actually predicted that there should be carbon molecules which resembled his domes. The formal name for this variety of carbon molecules is " Buckminsterfullerene ". On 9 October 1996 The Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences decided to award the Nobel Prize in Chemistry to Professor Robert F. Curl , Jr., Professor Sir Harold W. Kroto and Professor Richard E.Smalley for their discovery of fullerenes. Shungite (aka: shungit, schungit, shieldite) is the common name for the natural mineral containing fullerenes. The atomic components of the mineral shungite are: Carbon - 20 - 95%, Silicon - 5 - 60%, Aluminum - up to 4%, Iron of-3,5%, Magnesium - up to 3,5%, Kaliy - up to 12%, Sulfur - up to 1,2%, Calcium up to 0,58% Phosphorus - up to 0,34%, and many other macroelements and microelements as admixtures. Fullerenes are produced by 2 processes: Combustion of carbon-bearing fuels, and high voltage , high current density, electrical discharges through existing carbon deposits, such as coal.
