I want to tell you a story about a lucky penny I found. I was walking on the dirt road I live on and found a penny.... Oh I thought my Luck module is kicking in! I picked it up and put it in my pocket and kept walking. I came to my meditation rock, sat down and started having a conversation in my head with my boyfriend. I was feeling angry and hurt by something he has said. A bee began buzzing me and stung me on the arm. After the initial shock, there was a realization that the bee was responding to the field I was putting out of anger and hurt. Then I remembered an email I got saying that putting a penny on a bee sting makes the pain go away. So I reached into my pocket whipped out the penny and put it on the sting and indeed the pain went away. So then I got the idea to focus on the gratitude I feel toward my bf and wow! the pain of anger and hurt went away and so did the bees! And the moral you ask? Always pick up that penny you find because you never know how it will serve you. (or something like that!)
