kimo2026-main552 Energy Networks and BRICS Strategic Communication Ehsan Rasolinezhad1 1 University of Tehran, Iran mneplusno@gmail.com This article examines the important issue of BRICS communications and energy networks among the member countries of this organization. One of the most significant dimensions of BRICS countries' interactions is their collective role in global energy markets. Among BRICS members, there are major oil-producing countries such as Russia and Iran, while on the other hand, China and India are two of the world's most important crude oil importers. Therefore, examining the energy network among BRICS members is a crucial factor in enhancing their level of communication. In this article, a feasibility study on increasing energy interactions in downstream and upstream industries (in terms of purchase and sale, investment, and technology transfer) is conducted using expert opinions. For this purpose, the Analytical Hierarchy Process (AHP) method was employed, and the opinions of 20 Iranian energy experts were assessed and analyzed. The results of this study indicate that energy purchase and investment agreements can be the top priority for BRICS countries. In the second rank, the establishment of joint oil fields can strategically serve as an appropriate approach to enhance the level of BRICS communications. Keywords: energy networks, strategic communications, BRICS members, energy investment, energy agreements
