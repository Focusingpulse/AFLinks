· archived # 2,431,906
To help shed some light on the different PATHS Modules, and what they do, we will be posting the different Module descriptions in here so that you can share your experiences and ask any questions you may have about the Module. We look forward to your responses! This Module was produced to support you in all the areas needed to be a great billiards player. It addresses aim, focus, cue speed and impact control, geometric trajectories, as well as thinking of shots in advance and successfully leaving the ball. In addition to this, it can also help you to foster an attitude of confidence that will be of great assistance while practicing and competing.
