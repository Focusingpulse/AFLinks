New York. March 10. — The dreams of industrial magnates, pioneers in aerial and maritime adventure, and scientists who have long sought a scheme for the transmission of energy by wireless are about to come true. Nikola Tesla, veteran inventor in the radio field, broke a silence of years Saturday and announced the perfection of a system whereby he could transmit power without wires. The realization of this dream has been the life aim of the pioneer inventor and for the last several years Professor Tesla has virtually secluded himself from the world, living almost a hermit's life to be able to devote all his energy to the development of his power transmission system. Saturday, Professor Tesla came forth from his hermit-like seclusion and in a voice of ringing triumph proclaimed to the world his system, as comprising an electrical generator, which delivers its energy into the earth and stores it there, whence it can be unlocked by anyone having the proper "magical" electrical key.
