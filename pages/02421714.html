, Hannover Translated by Steiner Online Library 9. Why Must Human Beings Be Reincarnated Again and Again? [ 1 ] Among other things, the body with its organs, for example the heart, must become more and more perfect. Today, man can still have little effect on his soul. When he can move his heart organ at will through ether currents, he will become the independent conqueror of the organism. Thus humanity changes from form to form.
