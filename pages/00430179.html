Merry-go-round and time-dependent symplectic forms Urs Frauenfelder Joa Weber∗ Universit¨at Augsburg UNICAMP January 5, 2026 Abstract In the merry-go-round fictitious forces are acting like centrifugal force and Coriolis force. Like the Lorentz force Coriolis force is velocity de￾pendent and, following Arnol0d [Arn61], can be modeled by twisting the symplectic form. If the merry-go-round is accelerated an additional ficti￾tious force shows up, the Euler force. In this article we explain how one deals symplectically with the Euler force by considering time-dependent symplectic forms. It will turn out that to treat the Euler force one also needs time-dependent primitives of the time-dependent symplectic forms.
