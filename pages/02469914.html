Born in the Austro-Hungarian province of Lika in 1856 and having migrated to America at an early age, Tesla began experimenting in wireless communication some years before the now better known Senator Marconi of Italy entered that field. Here Tesla met with success, both scientifically and financially before concentrating exclusively on the subject of wireless power. His standing in the commercial world was such that when he decided to erect the world’s first wireless tower he received considerable financial backing from the House of Morgan. For the site of the tower, he was induced to choose Shoreham through one James S. Warden, manager and director of the Suffolk County Land Company which contributed a 200-acre plot, quite large enough for the tower, a powerhouse and a broadcasting station. The Land Company, it seems, still owned 1,800 adjacent acres which Manager Warden hoped to develop into a high-class community in which Mr. Tesla’s future employees and their families might obtain convenient residence.
