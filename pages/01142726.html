1 In-depth look at intuition Bertrand Wong Eurotech, S’pore Branch Email: bwong8@singnet.com.sg Abstract This paper emphasizes the importance of intuition, which is apparently frequently given little attention. It appears that logic is equated with intelligence and is regarded as the most important aspect of thinking by many. The author here attempts to raise the status of intuition vis-à-vis logic or reasoning and in doing so he is presenting intuition from a different and perhaps unique perspective. The paper explains the importance of intuition though it may not be perfectly effective in bringing the desired outcomes, a state of affair which applies also to logic. Nevertheless, intuition plays an important part in our intellectual and creative endeavors, apparently aided by logic, which is explained in this paper.
