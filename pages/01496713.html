Naught exists but God . Man exists as ONE with God , but until he is aware of his Oneness he is but a thought-recording image of God 's imagining. Some day he will know, however, for that is God 's intent in creating Man. Know thou, therefore, that motion merely simulates God 's knowing , and God 's qualities, and the purposefulness of His divine drama of Creation . Simulations are not reality , however, nor do they exist. Imaginings come and go. They change and have dimension . God 's imaginings are not God , however. The play cannot be the Playwright."
