Changing gears takes less than 0.001157 seconds with continuous output power! SmeshGear operates practically shock-free. SmeshGear provides a selectable, additional (higher or lower) speed-ratio on a rotating shaft. The original performance remains what it was, virtually 100%. The additional speed-ratio has yields greater than 98%. It makes no difference whether the direction of rotation is left, right, or variable. No matter where the power comes from, where it goes, or even if it varies: SmeshGear processes it flawlessly. SmeshGear is patented worldwide. A gearbox that combines the many benefits of existing transmissions, without the disadvantages! Fail-safe and well proven. With the same (electric) motor: SmeshGear provides these advantages: • A much wider range of speeds • Less energy is used, enhancing the range and/or top speed. Mechanical translation keeps the motor operating in the optimal speed range. • An up to seven times higher starting torque possible. • The rpm range is much larger. The motor remains in its ideal rpm range. • An up to seven times higher breakout force is possible. • It can provide rapid movement in some applications (e.g. it can quickly raise and lower a crane, when it is not loaded, saving time!) • SmeshGear can function as a gear coupling, i.e. start/run without load.
