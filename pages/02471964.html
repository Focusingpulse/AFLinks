Circuit Court for the Northern District of Illinois, which decision upholds the Tesla patent and grants an injunction against the use of the split-phase principle in a wattmeter made by the defendants in the action. The opinion cites the opinions of Judges Archbold, Hazel, Townsend and Lacombe sustaining the patent, and the opinion of the Court of Appeals reversing the Lacombe decision, which latter ruling was followed by Judge Colt in another case. All of the above cases have been reported in these columns.
