An Alternative Model of Probability Theory by D Williams --------------------------------------------------------------------- Abstract: An alternative model of probability is outlined and compared with the standard version. Difficulties in extending the Central Limit Theorem are resolved by adopting a new version of population mean developed using the new model and dx￾less integrals. New types of sample means are proposed with one tested against the standard mean which appear to have faster convergence. Much remains to be done in this area. --------------------------------------------------------------------- There are alternative models of geometry (euclidean, non-euclidean), analysis (standard, non-standard), set theory (ZF with/without AC) and so on.
