It is said that the distributed capacitance should be minimized because it kills the Q of the circuit. That brings me to the question, how can distributed capacitance affect the Q when the Q is represented as L/R. Where is C in that equation? Why not maximise the distributed capacitance? Did not Tesla say that it was useful and did he not in his later experiments use the planar bifilar coil he made famous? Did he not talk about the increased capacity of that coil?
