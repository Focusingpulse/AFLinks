That aged scientist has, in his 73 years of life, had a disturbing habit of making what has seemingly been impossible come true. Absent from active life for numerous years, it has become the habit of the present generation to take his promises and proposals for new discoveries with the traditional "grain of salt." Yet he can look back on an active life of research and invention that has made him one of the modern Titans. His life work was aptly summarized some years ago by another electrical expert, Dr. B. A. Behrend, when he wrote: "Were we to eliminate from our industrial world the results of Mr. Tesla's work, the wheels of industry wouli cease to turn, our electric trains and cars would stop, our town would be dark, our mills dead and idle. So far-reaching is this work, that it has become the warp and woof of industry." That is fulsome praise indeed, but no more than Dr. Tesla merits. Achievements that attract such glowing tribute one would suppose enough for one man's lifetime, but Dr. Tesla is not content. He presses on to new and even greater marvels. A strange and baffling secrecy surrounds him, however.
