Law of Heat " Atoms under the tension of chemical combination oscillate with an amplitude directly as the temperature , inversely as the pressure , and as the square of the specific heat . Diminishing the pitch of oscillation inversely as the square of the distance of the atoms apart, and simultaneously increasing the vibrating pitch of the atom by absorption of overtones and higher harmonics ."
