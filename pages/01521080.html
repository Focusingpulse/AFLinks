Pyramid Power Book Author Patrick Flanagan Language English Subject "Pyramid energy", bioenergetics Published 1973 Publisher Pyramid Publishers / DeVorss & Co, Pages 173 ISBN 0875162096 Pyramid Power is a 1973 book by G. Patrick Flanagan , published by Pyramid Publishers (Glendale, CA) and later by DeVorss & Co. Overview The book presents Flanagan's research into what he calls the bio-energetic properties of pyramid shapes — the idea that a pyramid's geometry concentrates a subtle energy affecting biological and physical processes. He surveys traditional concepts of "life energy" across cultures (prana, kundalini, chi, tumo, bioplasma), reports experiments with model pyramids, and connects the phenomena to a proposed new theory of nature. It became a popular text of the 1970s "pyramidology" movement. About the author Dr. G. Patrick Flanagan (born 1944) is an American inventor and author who has held numerous patents (including the "Neurophone," a device for transmitting sound through the skin) and has worked on projects such as human-to-computer and human-to-dolphin communication. He has written several books on pyramid energy and "subtle energy" science. Publication details Author: G. Patrick Flanagan Publisher: Pyramid Publishers / DeVorss & Co.
