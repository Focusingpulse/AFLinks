Vibraic: Describing an internal, scalar vibratory condition that stores and transmits potential without external motion or oscillation; distinct from kinetic vibration. In plain terms: Vibraic describes vibration as a state, not a movement. It is not shaking, not oscillation, and not wave motion through space.
