While we are still wise to refuse to accept as facts the beliefs of the ancient pre-Polynesians, as now imperfectly understood, no harm can come from checking those beliefs against those presently current in the psycho-religious field. On the contrary, much may be gained. Because this idea is so new to most of us, its full significance does not appear upon reading for the first time the simple statement above.
