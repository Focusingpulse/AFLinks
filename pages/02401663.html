GC2026-SGG062 Identification of Material Constants for Oil-saturated and Extracted Carbonate Rocks Based on the Results of Triaxial Non-proportional Loading Alexey V. Zaitsev1 , Anton A. Fukalov1 , Roman N. Kourov1 1 Perm National Research Polytechnic University, Perm, Russia a-zaitsev@mail.ru Using decomposition of hoop and radial components of displacement vector to the trigonometrical and generalized power series, the new exact analytical solutions to problems on the equilibrium state of thick-walled heavy anisotropic central- and axial-symmetric bodies fixed on their external surfaces and subject to the action of uniform internal lateral pressure, are obtained [1, 2]. The estimation of an initial strength of cylindrical and spherical reinforced concrete monolithic mine working supports for long-term storage of gas and oil is carried out on the basis of a multicriteria approach taking into account real damage mechanisms (i.e. damage from tension or compression in radial, hoop and axial directions, and from transversal and antiplane shear) of anisotropic axial￾and central-symmetric bodies.
