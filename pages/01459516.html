Free Spirit

· archived # 1,459,516
noun: 1) a person with a highly individual or unique lifestyle or imagination : nonconformist. 2) a person who lives according to his or her own wishes and beliefs unconstrained from obligation to authority, society's conventions or others. See Also Spiritual Freedom
