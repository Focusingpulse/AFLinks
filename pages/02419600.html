Journal of Borderland Research >> Vol. 41 >> No. 05 [1] THE "MAGA-GRAVITY" BAND OF THE ONE FORCE By the Yada di Shi'ite With CQC by Meade Layne and Riley Crabb "The markings on the plaster casts of the shoes of the Venusian are a symbolic language. In the center of the 'A' shoe (right foot) are two lines crossing one another and converging on the oval design which is a Disc (Flying Saucer). This represents the fact that if, and only when the magnetic forces of the earth are CROSSED can they be made of sufficient power to be used as a driving force. The earth itself generates only a negligible amount of magnetism. But when these lines are crossed they become aligned with two other planets, Mars and Venus -- which is what the two small circles on the left and right side of the crossed lines represent; the one below being the Earth. Venus, Mars and the Earth drawing their magnetic currents from the Sun. "On the heel of the same shoe is the ancient Swastika, indicating that those of most ancient times knew that the magnetic forces of the Sun, Venus, Mars and Earth moved from west to east between these four bodies." But the Yada did not say which way the Hooked Cross was turning. After Schauberger we would guess that it is turning clock-wise, concentrating a center of force for the work that is to be done. "The circle at the far tip of the shoe with a line running through its center is the Sun. The curved lines on either side of both shoes depict [2] the fact that when the magnetic lines of force are crossed they will create a path in which gravity is completely nul
