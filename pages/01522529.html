The principle His account of the origin was that he had been reading theoretical physics for thirty years and found that with time he understood less rather than more — and that the reason is a body of models with an exclusively mathematical character, whose elements may or may not correspond to anything physical. From the beginning of the twentieth century, on his account, mathematics has overruled physics. His remedy is what he calls bijective research methodology: between the set that is the universe and the set that is the model, every element of the model must correspond to exactly one element of physical reality. Applied to the spacetime model, he holds that no such correspondence exists — physicists have been convinced for a century that time is the fourth dimension of space, without experimental evidence. His argument is about what the fourth coordinate actually is. In x 4 = ict , the product of light speed and time is a distance, so x 4 is a spatial dimension — imaginary, but spatial — and not time.
