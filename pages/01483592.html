spinning sphere

· archived # 1,483,592
"The meaning of the spinning sphere which she saw, indicates that the awareness within has come on the nearness of that proclaimed from the beginning, "Know the Lord thy God is one!" And who is his mother, his brother, his sister? They that do the will of the Father . What is the will ? The law and love expressed in the words which the Master gave, "Love the Lord thy God with all thine heart , thine body, thy soul ; and thy brother as thyself." [ Cayce (587), from "Edgar Cayce's Story of Jesus", page 158]
