Some Facts about Relations and Operations of Algebras SHAO-DAN LEE Abstract Let A be a σ-algebra. Suppose that Θ is a congruence of A. Then Θ is a subalgebra of A × A. If ϕ is an automorphism from A to A, then 〈ϕ, ϕ〉 is an automorphism of A × A. And it is obvious that 〈ϕ, ϕ〉(Θ) is a congruence of A. Let B be a σ-algebra and ψ a homomorphism from A to B. Then B′ := ψ(A) is a subalgebra of B. And 〈ψ, ψ〉(Θ) is a congruence of B′ . If ψ is an epimorphism, then 〈ψ, ψ〉(Θ) is a congruence of B. Suppose that A is a category of all σ-algebras. Let A, B ∈ A and ψ: A → B be a homomorphism. Then the pullback A ⊓B A is isomorphic to a congruence of A. An n-ary relation  of an algebra A is a subset of A n . If  satisfies some conditions, then  is a subalgebra of A n . The set of languages is a lattice. If  is the set of the compositions of the operations in a language σ, then  is an algebra.
