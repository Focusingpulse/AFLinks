In other words, power is free if we don't need any more of it in order to run a load. We just keep retaining the level of power that is in the load, and it never goes away so we never have to supply any more power to run the load if it doesn't spend any power. Energy is what is consumed to power a load. And so, if no more energy is required past an initial amount to power a load, then the load keeps maintaining its empowerment at no further expense of energy.
