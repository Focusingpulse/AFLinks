INE 1998 SYMPOSIUM TENTATIVE SCHEDULE August 14-15, 1998 University of Utah Union Building Salt Lake City, Utah The INE Conference Proceedings is included with the Registration Price: The Registration Fee for the symposium is: US$100.00 for INE members if paid for before August 1, 1998; US$125.00 for non-members, if paid for before August 1, 1998; and US$150.00 after August 1 and at the door. Students attending Universities (with current student identification) will only be charged $10.00 per day for admission. If students wish to obtain the Proceedings, there will be an extra charge. FRIDAY, August 14, 1998 Registration: 8:00 - 8:30 AM Presentations: 8:30 - 10:00 AM Break: 10:00 - 10:30 AM Presentations: 10:30 - Noon Buffet Lunch: Noon - 1:30 PM Presentations: 1:30 - 3:00 PM Break: 3:00 - 3:30 PM Presentations: 3:30 - 5:00 PM Free Networking: 7:00 - 9:00 PM With Selected Authors SATURDAY, August 15, 1998 Registration: 8:00 - 8:30 AM Presentations: 8:30 - 10:00 AM Break: 10:00 - 10:30 AM Presentations: 10:30 - Noon Buffet Lunch: Noon - 1:30 PM Presentations: 1:30 - 3:00 PM Break: 3:00 - 3:30 PM Roundtable With: 3:30 - 5:00 PM Selected Authors Return to the INE Main Page www.padrak.com/ine/NEN_6_2_10.html Jun. 19, 1998.
