unfolding

· archived # 1,497,048
Term used by Walter and Lao Russell to denote spiritual development, awakening , enlightenment . Jesus The only Truth is Love . It has many faces, and sometimes it is confusing when one attempts to discern Love and its expressions in one’s life . Such confusion can be dispelled through the power of the Good Heart . Your Heart is naturally good. The energy which surrounds you, swirling in response to your outlook, emotions , actions , thoughts , dreams and illusions , filters the Love that comes from you and that which comes to you . Your quest for Truth must begin with the Beauty of the Self . That is the Good Heart . The Good Heart is one that sees itself as a Center to and from which blessings flow in abundance . If this picture is desired, the heart will open to accommodate its full power of Love , bringing peace to the Self and others. We stand by to assist your unfolding , and the clarification of your beauty and Light . [ Jesus , Good Heart ]
