"In 2004, the UN Secretary-General Kofi Annan said that as regime change was the prime aim of the Iraq War, it was illegal. With great sadness and anger, I now believe him to be right," Lord Prescott wrote in a Sunday Mirror column, the confession coming shortly after Sir John Chilcot's report into the lead-up to the war was unveiled.
