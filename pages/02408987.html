ISBN 978-81-906221-7-2 paper back Cover design by Devarajan Thangadurai and Shameer Ali Designed and printed in India by Cinnamonteal, Goa Preface World population is growing rapidly and the growth syndrome is likely to accelerate several folds, if proper measures are not taken to meet the requirements of this increase in the population. At present more than 500 million people are suffering from hunger or malnutrition, which can turn into famine if proper measures are not taken to control it. Therefore, there is need to increase food production and design a better food security system such that to increase yield per unit area. The production levels can be increased considerably through the utilisation of modern biotechnology techniques for improved plant production. Grain legumes constitute an important group of plants that have an important share in feeding humans and animals throughout the world. However, this group of plants is given less importance compared to cereal grains research; resultantly the most grain legumes grown throughout the world are low yielding and susceptible to various biotic and abiotic stresses. Various types of stresses, plant diseases, pests, rodents, birds, weeds are all taking heavy toll on the economies and prosperities of nations.
