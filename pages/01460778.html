Mind-imagining

· archived # 1,460,778
Russell "What it has been it still is. No microscope of man can find one electron of that tree within its seed, for if one could find what is really there one would find God . The patterned tree is the image of Mind-imagining . Mind-Light projected that image into space to manifest Mind-imagining . Mind rested between its cycles of imagining and withdrew the image into its equilibrium . Every unit of Creation unfolds and refolds in that life-death manner. Nothing has happened to the Idea of the tree. It eternally exists and will again unfold into action and again become the imaged form of Mind-imagining in repeated sequences when conditions of electric pressures are favorable for its reappearance." [ Atomic Suicide , page 118] "He must learn that his body is but a formed, moving image of his Mind-imaginings ." [ Atomic Suicide , page 234] Sri Nisargadatta Maharaj "Once you realize that the world is your own projection, you are free of it. You need not free yourself of a world that does not exist, except in your own imagination ! However is the picture, beautiful or ugly, you are painting it and you are not bound by it. Realize that there is nobody to force it on you, that it is due to the habit of taking the imaginary to be real. See the imaginary as imaginary and be free of fear ."
