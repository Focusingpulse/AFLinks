You need to know the exact clearances to use for the rod and inner chamber, and the clearances of the inner and outer chambers. The plans are good to have as they also contain information on building an air management system, and they don't cost that much. Plus, then you would have a license to build and use one legally. And you'd be happy to know that GEET donates 25% of all profits back to the communities that have made purchases. So, I whole-heartedly support people in buying their own sets of plans. The length of the rod is also important and it depends on the fuel you are using. A longer rod will tend to work good with "dirty fuels" such as crude oil or soda pop, while the shorter rod would work well with gasoline and diesel fuels. Yes, you can build a chamber for burning such "fuels" such as plastic bottles. I don't remember having heard about wood being used but I wouldn't be surprised.
