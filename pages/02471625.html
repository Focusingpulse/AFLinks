He is claiming so much for his invention that if he can realize his claims he will revolutionize affairs. Three things he says he can do with his machine — annihilate space by sending unlimited electric power to any part of the world without wires, blow up the world’s navies by guiding dynamite boats against them without shore connections, and change electricity into brilliant light without any accompanying heat. The first, it is claimed, will make natural forces do all the world’s work, the second will abolish war by making it too terrible to engage in, and the third will change the existing methods of illumination all over the world. All these achievements depend upon one thing — the changing of an ordinary electric current into one of tremendous voltage or intensity — and this Tesla claims to have actually accomplished by the machine that he calls an “oscillator.” What is this marvelous little machine which will almost go into an ordinary pocket, yet which can hurl the power of Niagara across to Paris and run the machinery of the coming exposition in that city?
