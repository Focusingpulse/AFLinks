Honored to be here

· archived # 2,433,364
I put this on my profile and realized it should be here. I just wanted to say to anyone who might wander into my profile that I feel excited and honored to be among the posters of EnergeticForum. I tend to attract (or possibly seek out) the unusual and not-quite-mainstream. During my ramblings around the vast reaches of the net I have found that the things I encounter that interest me most and "sing in my heart" are the very things that Society views as unworthy of notice and deems fringe psudoscience. I think that the underlying viewpoint of helping humanity for the sake of humanity is what Mainstream Society doesn't promote or understand. I am often shocked by how many people teach and are taught to "look out for number one" because the others won't, can't and shouldn't care for anyone but themselves. I am asamed that my fellow humans have not grasped the fact that we are all interconnected with and through the world and,what is done to the least of us, impacts every other person and thing in the universe. I cannot express how very happy it made me to find this bastion of light, where those few who understand the simple secret, that good done for the sake of good rewards the spirit and balms the soul, gather. I can't be on this forum often due to time constraints but find it soothing to have it in my life now. Thank you all for your work, words and wisdom. I hope to be able to contribute something of worth to this righteous endeavor and will strive to do so. Humbly, Bast333
