Nagel Research Professor Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering The George Washington University 202-994-5293 nagel@gwu.edu Physics Department University of Notre Dame 16 September 2005 New Energy Times Archives Topics to be Covered Reproducibility, Controllability & Optimization Evidence for Nuclear Reactions Evidence for Surface Reactions Recent Theoretical Developments New Energy Times Archives Introduction to Reproducibility The Scientific Method, including reproducibility within and between laboratories, has a large literature, much of it in the Philosophy of Science. There are essentially two kinds of reproducibility: Different things are done, but the results are comparable. The same things are done, but sometimes the results vary. The first is characteristic of old and understood technologies. The second is often the case in new fields when all the relevant variables are not understood or controlled. There are many examples of the first type of reproducibility. LENR is now an example of the second type of reproducibility.
