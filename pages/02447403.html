Abstract NTT Basic Research Laboratories 3-1 Morinosato-W akamiya Atsugi -Shi, Kanagawa 243-01 Japan The cross sections and branching ratios of d+d reactions were measured as a function of deuteron energy by using low-energy deuterium ion bombardment. The branching ratio of d(d,3He)n to d(d,p)t were found to be one to one at energies from 2.5 keY to 20 keY in the eM frame. The reation rate of d(d,p)t at 2.5 keY was four orders of magunitude less than that at 20 keY. These energy dependences were good agreement with those extrapolated from measurements of the d+d reaction which was derived by the high-enegy (mega-electron-volts) deuterium ion bombardments. 2.
