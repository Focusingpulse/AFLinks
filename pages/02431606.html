Choosing Paths modules using muscle/energy testing

· archived # 2,431,606
I thought about writing a post about this topic quite a while ago but hadn't gotten around to it. I've finally decided to share one method that I use as there seems to be more & more requests popping up from people wondering how to select the best Paths modules for themselves. I stress that this is a suggestion only. If it doesn't resonate with you then disregard the information & keep doing whatever you are happy doing. There are a number of ways to muscle test or energy test.There is no right or wrong way & I have written a bit more about it over on the Theta thread. The method that I am going to explain here is simply the method that I find works well for me & is the easiest method for me to explain to people who are unfamiliar with it, so that they can then pick it up quickly to test themselves when I am doing a Theta session with them. This method can equally be applied to this process for choosing the best Paths modules for yourself. So that you can be empowered to make informed choices for yourself should you wish to do so. This is the basic procecedure: 1. First,you need to be hydrated (have a glass of water on hand) & make sure you've drunk some water earlier. If you are dehydrated you can fool your body into thinking it is temporarily hydrated by placing your hands around your back & applying gentle yet firm pressure over your kidneys if necessary when making a statement. If you are not hydrated then your responses will not be correct even though you think they are. 2. Stand with your feet comfortably apart, knees relaxed facing NORTH to test for all statements. Shoes can be on or off, it doesn't matter. (If you don't know where North is & can't work it out by the sun rising in the East & the sun setting in the West. Then try this. Face a direction & say aloud "I am facing North" & rotate & repeat the statement for all four directions.
