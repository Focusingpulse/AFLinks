To what or whom is an attorney's first duty? § 4 ATTORNEY & CLIENT 7 C.J.S. Converted text: His first duty is to the courts and the public not to the client, and wherever the duties to his client conflict with those he owes as an officer of the court in the administration of justice, the former must yield to the latter. 2.
