Live Physics & Cosmology News and Discussion Including Nobel Prize for 2019 Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Date 12 October 2019 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording Live Physics & Cosmology News and Discussion Including Nobel Prize for 2019 was a live session on 12 October 2019 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. Summary A Dissident Science Live broadcast — David de Hilster hosting from his own channel, unannounced, to try the new streaming system. It opened as a memorial and became a two-hour conversation with a viewer in Australia who goes by Axman. Ron Hatch Ron Hatch had died the previous week at eighty, and de Hilster gave the fullest account of him the series contains. His working life was in GPS: over thirty patents, one of the most respected people in the field, a company of his own eventually bought by John Deere for tractor guidance, and a salary he told de Hilster he could not refuse. He received the society's lifetime achievement award in 2016 and gave a talk at the University of Maryland, Relativity in the Light of GPS . His dissident position was Lorentzian. He held that GPS shows the flaws in relativity rather than confirming it, and had corresponded with NASA about picosecond-scale discrepancies which he thought were explained by adding the satellite's speed to the speed of light — c + v and c − v — which would violate special relativity outright. He wrote a book on it, published papers in GPS journals, and was, as he says in de Hilster's film, simply ignored. The personal note de Hilster wanted on record: Hatch was the first investor in Einstein Wrong , giving him the money to buy the camera in December 2004 that started eight years of filming. He had thirteen children and fourteen grandchildren, was caring for his wife Nancy in his last years, and was working on new papers with colleagues the week he died.
