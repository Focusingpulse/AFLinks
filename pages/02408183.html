International Standard Book Number: 0-672-23830-6 PREFACE Many home owners throughout the country have a few simple basic hand tools. These simple tools generally include a hammer, various size screwdrivers, a ruler, a pair of pliers, and some type of saw. Also, somewhere in the corner of the garage or basement, there is a small box of assorted nails and screws. Although these tools are not considered ample for the doit-yourself homeowner, it is a good start in the right direction if they are in good working condition and of good quality. A good rule to follow when purchasing a hand or power tool is to obtain the best quality for the money spent and have fewer tools than to purchase several tools of insufficient quality which will not hold up or are not adequate for the particular job.
