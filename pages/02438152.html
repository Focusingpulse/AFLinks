44 — GA 263. Correspondence with Edith Maryon 1912–1924

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 23 June 1920 · archived # 2,438,152
My dear Edith Maryon! As things have turned out, I cannot be in Dornach before Sunday. I do not like it at all, but there is no other way. Regarding the program proposed by Baron Rosenkrantz, I can only say that I will make every effort to carry it out. Naturally, I am prepared to show the visitors the building and the studio myself and discuss them in detail. Now I can hardly say anything else from here, except that despite everything I will leave a lot of unfinished work here.
