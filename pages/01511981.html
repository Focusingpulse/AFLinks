This design require two relay with 4 parallel contact such as FRM3-4B5 The self oscillating relay tandem part is this: Relay 1 will turn on when relay 2 turn off, and the opposite. Capacitor will make sure the relay will turn on long enough to activate all contact. The circuit is based from Ronald brantts mentioned in "report on 4 battery switch". The circuit: If you spot error, please post: All the switch (S1 to S6) should closed/gated/turned on when the appropriate relay turned on. Relay 1 control S1, S3 and S6.
