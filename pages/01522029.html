Thomas Eugene Bearden (17 December 1930 – 3 February 2022), who wrote as Tom Bearden , was an American retired U.S. Army officer, nuclear engineer and theorist who became one of the most widely read figures in the modern free-energy movement. He argued that the electromagnetic theory taught today is a truncation of Maxwell 's original quaternion theory, and that the missing structure – which he called scalar electromagnetics – permits electrical energy to be drawn from the active vacuum. He was co-inventor of the Motionless Electromagnetic Generator (MEG) , a long-time collaborator of John Bedini , the author of a large body of books and papers on scalar weaponry and vacuum energy, and the proprietor of the website cheniere.org, which for two decades served as a primary reference archive for the free-energy research community. Biography Bearden was born in Huntsville, Alabama, on 17 December 1930. He entered active Army duty as an officer in June 1954 and served twenty years, with postings that included Korea, Vietnam and Canada, retiring with the rank of lieutenant colonel. Alongside his technical work he was a third-dan practitioner of aikido and the author of a manual on the Yoseikan style, and a country-music songwriter and performer. He was married twice; his second wife, Doris, died in 2013 after forty-nine years of marriage. Bearden died in Huntsville on 3 February 2022 after a lengthy illness. (Some movement obituaries and later web sources give the date as 6 February 2022; the funeral home notice and the published obituary both give 3 February.)
