Keely " Vibrophone , which is fashioned after the human ear ," [ Vibratory Sympathetic and Polar Flows ] " In the image of man " Keely constructed his liberator . Not literally, but, as his vibrophone (for collecting the waves of sound and making each wave distinct from the other in tone when the " wave plate " is struck after the sound has died away) is constructed after the human ear so his liberator corresponds in its parts to the human head .
