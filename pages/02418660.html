Basic Principles of Over Unity Electromagnetic Machines A Scientific View into the World of Free Energy from Electric Charges and Magnetic Fields by Jovan Marjanovic, M.Sc. Johann W. Goethe said that the biggest secrets were the open ones. You look at them but you do not see them. The same is true with the electromagnetic field. It is known fact that after switching off an electromagnet, its magnetic energy will be returned back to the circuit, usually as a spark. What was missed is the fact that the magnetic energy performed work by attraction of an iron bar and didn’t consume itself. This work, minus heat losses inside the circuit, is free energy or over unity energy. The goal of this book is to unlock the secrets of over unity and to teach people what is over unity and how it works. The reader will learn basic ideas of making new ultra efficient electric motors and generators and how to improve existing patents which use permanent magnets.
