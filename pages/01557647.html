At this point in the construction, the base had been leveled using water as a guide. Every thing was ready for the first row of casing stones to be set in place. How would YOU bring 16 ton stones from across the river, that are already on barges to the site of the Great Pyramid? Evidence indicates that a series of locks from the Nile to the building site were created to move the stones up form the river to the building site. These series of water locks brought the first level of casing stones up to the site. The locks worked just as the water locks work in the Panama Canal. The subterranean cuttings which are a colossal hydraulic ram supply the needed water for the locks.
