Commencing in 1983, he was trained by Prof. Kyril Stanyukovich (1916-1989), a prominent scientist in gaseous dynamics and General Relativity. He was also trained with Dr. Abraham Zelmanov (1913-1987), the famous cosmologist and researcher in General Relativity. During the 1980?s, he was also trained by Dr. Vitaly Bronshten (1918-2004), the well-known expert in the physics of destruction of bodies in atmosphere. Dmitri Rabounski has published about 30 scientific papers and 6 books on General Relativity, gravitation, physics of meteoroids, and astrophysics.
