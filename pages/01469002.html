Words Terms and Definitions

· archived # 1,469,002
One of the difficulties in reading Keely , Cayce or Russell are understanding their words , terms and definitions . They used words and terms in manners not of the ordinary. To address this issue, at least in part, I've attempted to record herein their words and terms as they used them. This method preserves the original wording of these geniuses in the contexts they used. To pull a word or term out of context nullifies any original intended meaning . Where context was not so important the common definition has been supplied from http://www.onelook.com , https://www.wikipedia.org or equally valued source. In a few cases these discoverers supplied their own definitions. In general it is not possible to understand these people with an ordinary high school or university level education. One is encouraged to go beyond those limited levels of indoctrination if one is to understand the Higher Mind concepts offered by these geniuses. One must unlearn erroneous ideas and concepts to be free to learn and use unlimited awareness subject of SVP . Many times some will attempt to wedge SVP into their own limited understanding. Of course this doesn't work. One can only understand these people by rising to their level in mental faculties. "The basic tool for the manipulation of reality is the manipulation of words. If you can control the meaning of words, you can control the people who must use the words." Philip K. Dick See Also AI Interpretations of SVP 1.22 - Definitions of Vibration 14.
