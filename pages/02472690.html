No 6481. COMPLETE SPECIFICATION. [Communicated from abroad by Nikola Tesla, of the City and State of New York, United States of America, Electrician.] Improvements Relating to the Electrical Transmission of Power and to Apparatus Therefor I, Henry Harris Lake, of the firm of Haseltine, Lake & Co., Patent Agents Southampton Buildings, in the County of Middlesex, do hereby declare the nature of this invention and in what manner the same is to be performed, to be particularly described and ascertained in and by the following statement:- The practical solution of the problem of the electrical conversion and transmission of mechanical energy involves certain requirements which the apparatus and systems heretofore employed have not been capable of fulfilling. Such a solution primarily demands a uniformity of speed in the motor, irrespective of its load within its normal working limits.
