· archived # 1,460,573
Mutations http://www.organstops.org/_apps/Mutations.html The word mutation refers to any single-rank stop that sounds at a pitch other than unison or octaves . The following table shows the relationship between harmonics and mutations . Stop pitches are shown for four different harmonic series . Note that unison and octave sounding pitches are not considered mutations . Independent stops are rarely seen higher than 1' pitch , and pipes are usually not made much smaller than 1/24' (1/2"), the top of a 61-note 1 1/3' rank. For completeness, stop-pitches are shown for all of the first 32 harmonics , even though some of them have never been used.
