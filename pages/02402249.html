MolPhot2026-main006 Reconfigurable Photonic Crystals Enabled by Gas-Discharge Plasma Chengxun Yuan , Jianfei Li1 1 Harbin Institute of Technology Ponencia invitada yuancx@hit.edu.cn Plasma offers a dynamically tunable electromagnetic medium for photonics due to its strong dispersion, controllable effective permittivity, and broadband interaction with electromagnetic radiation. When gas-discharge plasma is integrated with periodic photonic structures, it forms plasma photonic crystals with reconfigurable band structures, modal characteristics, and wave-manipulation capabilities. This review summarizes recent advances in plasma-based photonic crystal platforms, including tunable absorbers, robust transmission systems for extreme electromagnetic environments, and plasma-assisted control of high-Q resonant states. We first discuss plasma￾enabled absorbers, where plasma dispersion and collisional loss provide active control over absorption frequency, bandwidth, and intensity. We then review plasma photonic crystals designed for communication blackout and other negative-permittivity environments, where engineered periodic structures support guided modes, topological waveguiding, and Dirac zero-index states to overcome conventional plasma cutoff limitations. Finally, we highlight plasma-assisted manipulation of bound states in the continuum, in which tunable plasma permittivity serves as a non-geometric control parameter for generating, merging, and reconfiguring BICs and polarization singularities without structural modification.
