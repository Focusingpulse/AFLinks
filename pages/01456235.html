[p. 22] IV Summary New thinking is very difficult. We will make it easier to comprehend by telling it simply and in easy stages. As a first step in this direction you must know that life in a man's body is exactly the same as life in your car battery. As long as its electrical charge exceeds its discharge it lives and grows with increasing vitality . When its discharge exceeds its charge it loses its vitality and slowly dies. This is as true of a flower as of a man, or atom, or flaming sun. We again repeat the fact that all things live and grow because of their ability to generate enough heat to charge their bodies with their normal voltage , and no more . Each living body has a normal temperature and pulsation frequency which must be constant in order to live and grow. Death begins with the slightest [p. 23] upset of that normalcy. Even one degree of extra heat beyond 98.6° will upset the entire metabolism of man. That one degree of fever is one fifth of his way to death . That is equally true of everything. Our planet already has one degree of fever, caused by radioactivity . That one degree has already upset the earth's metabolism enough to cause many fishes in the seas to migrate into colder waters. It is causing ice caps and glaciers to melt. Ten more degrees of radioactive fever will make of this planet a barren waste.
