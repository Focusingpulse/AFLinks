Pon's Procurement Request

· archived # 1,518,898
Professor Stanley Pons University of Utah Department of Chemistry Salt Lake City, Utah 84112 Dear Professor Pons: March 13, 1989 I am pleased to inform you that your proposal, "The Behavior of Electrochemically Compressed Hydrogen and Deuterium," has been recommended for funding. A procurement request authorizing award negotiations has been issued and sent to the Department of Energy's Chicago Operations Office, which will conduct the negotiations. Even though you should be aware that the Department of Energy assumes no obligation until the award has been made, let me take this opportunity to extend to you my congratulations and best wishes for a successful project.
