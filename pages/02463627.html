A finite gate coherence window τg (dimensionless τg∗ := τg Fq ) converts a fixed-quantum photon-front cadence into an integer count N (ν ∗ ) of coherently admissible quanta per emission event. The resulting “surplus law” produces (i) a sharp threshold set by an integer N0 and (ii) a staircase post-threshold energy descriptor which, under ensemble averaging, approaches the familiar quasi-linear dependence on ν ∗ without requiring a continuously variable photon quantum. To keep the physics experimentally decidable, we introduce dual conjugate potential-like scalars φe := enrge /e2 and ψe := enrge /eemax 2 that parameterize the same event energy in electrostatic and magnetic charge primitives identified with the emitted electron kinetic-energy descriptor in the zero-smear limit. Two competing gate laws are then stated: an electrostatic-primary coherence control (τg∗ , N0 governed mainly by E) and a magnetic-primary coherence control (τg∗ , N0 governed mainly by M). The paper closes with discriminating laboratory protocols, expressed entirely in QMU variables, that can determine which channel dominates coherence maintenance and threshold in valence-seat filling.
