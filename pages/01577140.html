X. Philosophy of Spiritual Activity and Monism — GA 4. The Philosophy of Spiritual Activity

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 1,577,140
The naive person, who considers real only what he can see with his eyes and grasp with his hands, also requires for his moral life incentives that are perceptible to the senses. He requires a being who communicates these incentives to him in a way understandable to his senses. He will let these incentives be dictated to him as commandments by a person whom he considers to be wiser and more powerful than himself, or whom, for some other reason, he acknowledges as a power standing over him.
