Trail Tree Newsletter April 2023 This is Volume 60 of the Quarterly Trail Tree Project Newsletter. We hope the topics in this newsletter will be of interest to you. If you want us to report on other things, just let us know and we will be happy to do that. Below is all the latest news. Trail Tree (TT) Database Trail Tree Database We ended 2022 with the database at 3,354 trees. Now, we are just crossing over the 3,400 mark for Marker Trees. The trees in the database now cover 45 states and Canada. We need someone to find trees in North Dakota, Idaho and Nevada to round out the lower 48 states. Trees of Interest For this Newsletter, we have selected trees to show from those submitted in July to November 2022. NC Trees Jill sent in this tree from the travels of her hiking group. They have been hiking the many trails in NC. They have located several Marker Trees all of which are very old trees. TN Trees We have three trees from TN that are of interest for this Newsletter. Matt and his wife just bought property in TN and found this Marker Tree on their property which has been there for a long time but never reported until someone with knowledge of the Marker Trees came along. 1 NC Tree Blake is a new researcher for the Mountain Stewards covering eastern TN. He started finding some remarkable trees in the area of Monteagle, TN. Stacy, a fellow researcher, and Diane and I joined with Blake to visit several trees he had been locating. The tree shown below on the left is one of the more remarkable trees located. This tree is marking the graves of four Catawba Indians who had fled to TN to get away from the encroachment of the colonial settlers. These Catawba unfortunately were murdered by TN locals. TN Tree Diane could not hike the several miles out and back to see Blake’s tree but she found her own tree while waiting for us to return. It is shown to the right.
