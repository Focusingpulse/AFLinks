Mastromatteo STMicroelectronics Via Tolomeo,1 – 20010 Cornaredo (MI) ICCF15 - 2009, Roma 5-9 September 2009 Oriani’s claims Abstract A relatively simple technique using CR39 particle detectors has been developed that in 25 consecutive electrolyses has reproducibly produced charged particle tracks, showing unambiguous evidence of a nuclear reaction during electrolysis of heavy or light water solutions. Nuclear tracks can be produced upon the surface and beyond the 1 mm thickness of the CR39 detectors. Nuclear activity of some sort can persist in the Viton o-rings used in the electrolysis cell so that charged energetic particles can be generated subsequently without electrolysis. U.
