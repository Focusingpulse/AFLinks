I have shown in some past Notes that this value was also determinable from dimensional analysis using the Planck Natural Units. However, it intrigued me to know just what this constant really stood for. I knew that it was related to the 'force' of gravity in terms of Rhysmonic Cosmology, but past attempts to evaluate it always resulted in some extra terms in mass, length, or time.
