Ophanim

· archived # 1,461,112
The Ophanim The Ophanim, commonly known as the Wheels, represent one of the most abstract and complex descriptions of celestial attendants in scriptural traditions, completely devoid of humanoid features. Their primary physical description originates in the prophetic visions of Ezekiel. The text details a massive structure of intersecting wheels, forming a complex mechanism of a wheel within a wheel, capable of multidirectional movement without turning. Their defining visual characteristic is that their entire outer structure, including the rims, is described as being densely covered in eyes. They burn with an intense internal light and manifest the appearance of beryl stone, flashing with sapphire and topaz hues. The manifestation of the Ophanim is intrinsically linked with potent sound and cosmic vibration. When they move, the sound of their wings is famously compared to the noise of great waters, the voice of the Almighty, the voice of speech, and the noise of an immense host. This description suggests a complex and physically overwhelming auditory event, emphasizing structured, resonant power rather than verbal language. As direct servants of the Divine, the Ophanim form the living infrastructure that supports the firmament upon which the throne rests.
