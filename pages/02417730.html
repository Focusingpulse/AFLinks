On Sunday evening, Dr. Rudolph Steiner of Berlin delivered a highly interesting lecture on this topic in the convention hall of the Artists’ Association, which culminated in the following remarks. About 100 years ago, Schiller made the following statement: “Natural science and philosophy must continue to go their separate ways for a long time to come,” and this prophecy has held true to this day.
