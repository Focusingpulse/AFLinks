SOCIETY ATTENDS IN FORCE Bride Daughter of the Late Augustus Whiting of New York, and Groom Son of the Late Theodore A. Havemeyer. Newport, R. I., July 11. — The principal social event of the season here thus far was the wedding of Mr. Harry O. Havemeyer, Jr., and Miss Charlotte Whiting, daughter of the late Mr. and Mrs. Augustus Whiting of New York. The families of both the bride and groom are well known summer residents in Newport. The bride is a granddaughter of Judge Swan of Ohio, and the groom is the youngest son of the late Theodore A. Havemeyer and nephew of Mrs. Charles M. Oelrichs. Miss Lily Oelrichs and Miss Natica Rives, daughter of Mrs. George L. Rives, at whose summer home, Swanhurst, on Bellevue avenue, the ceremony took place, were bridesmaids. Rev. William R. Meehan, pastor of St. Mary’s church, officiated, and the guests included all the prominent members of the Newport summer colony. The bride was given away by her uncle, George L. Rives. She carried a shower bouquet of orange blossoms and lilies of the valley, festooned with white silk ribbon streamers. In her hair was a magnificent tiara of five large diamond suns, the groom’s gift.
