Overview This is a short open letter (dated 22 November 1997, published in New Energy News in January 1998) in which Frank J Znidarsic describes an experiment and asks the cold fusion community for feedback and prior art. It is candid to an unusual degree — Znidarsic states plainly that "the results to date have been less than expected" and, at the end, that "the latest tests showed no anomalous energy." Its interest is chiefly historical . The empirical relationship Znidarsic reports here in rough form is the seed of the theoretical programme he pursued for the next two decades — his claim of a fixed " velocity of the transitional quantum state " underlying cold fusion, antigravity and force unification, developed in his later papers such as " The Control of the Natural Forces " and " Elementary Anti-Gravity ". The experiment and the claim Znidarsic's premise is that cold-fusion electrodes behave as room-temperature superconductors , and that the fusion process is driven by the mechanical vibration of a superconducting structure at a characteristic frequency. He notes that CETI's active beads, about 1,000 ångströms thick, and Talbot and Scott Chubb 's fine-grained palladium, are structures of a particular small size, and proposes that the operating frequency is set by that size.
