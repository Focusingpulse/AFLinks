It is an open secret with electricians that he had solved some very important problems in the conservation of energy, and that he had nearly perfected appliances which would greatly advance the art of electricity. In less than an hour the work of years vanished In smoke. The career of Nikola Tesla, since he came to the United States, has astonished even members of the profession in which he has achieved such magnificent triumphs. The greater part of his preliminary and early experiments had been in the direction of the development of the alternating electric current and its application to practical electrics. Tesla has not been a self-asserting scientist. Great as his discoveries on alternating current lines have been, they have come to the notice of the world almost unheralded. It was only after their utility had been demonstrated by the unfailing test of practical work under load conditions, that these inventions have been exploited.
