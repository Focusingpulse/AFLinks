So last night and this morning a wonderful thing happened to me. First a bit of a back story, back in February my girlfriend at the time broke up with me. This was for me a devastating thing to go through since I had fallen very much in love with this woman, who was also my best friend. Fast forward to now. So I am now living with her as a roommate, we are still good friends. It is her and I and her twin sister and her fiance Josh( my name is Josh also and we have the same birthday, weird...) So last night she went out on a date finally, and the great thing is I was fine with this; this morning I was up when she came in from her date( the fun of the walk of shame, most of us have been there) and the great thing is I was still ok with this. I really thought that I would respond poorly to this, and I think that without some of the help of Paths I still would have. I really think that it has helped me to really love myself so I could find more of a true love for my friend and not just a selfish jealous "love" that I had before.
