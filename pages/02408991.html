Mandal (New Delhi) The Journal of Arid legumes publishes original research, review and theme papers on various aspects on crops i.e.: Clusterbean (Guar), Moth bean (Dew gram), Cowpea (Lobia) and Horsegram (Kulthi). The research articles on these crops receive priority.
