A Generalization of the Riemann Functional Equation Jose Risomar Sousa ORCID: 0009-0002-7779-0808 June 18, 2025 Abstract A generalization of the Riemann functional equation that holds for b > 0 (unlike the one available in the literature, which holds for 0 < ℜ(b) ≤ 1) is introduced. The insight that led to this new relation came from a new formula for the zeta function created herein that implies the Riemann functional equation. A few minor developments that stem from new formulae introduced previously are also discussed.
