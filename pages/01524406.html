Studied medicine in Vienna and Pamplona (Spain). 1969 Graduation. 1970-1977 Fellow in Cancer Research, Tumor Immunology. ca. 20 papers on the topic. 1978 - 2004 exerted profession as physician. Rella's papers relate to the field of philosophy and physics. He promotes the idea of the physical vacuum as a dynamical shattering lattice structure in: "Der Schritt in die Analogie" part I, Wr. Jb. f. Philos. (1984), 129-138; part II, Wr. Jb. f. Philos. (1985), 141-158.
