Walter Rella Born ( 1944-05-31 ) May 31, 1944 (age 82) Residence Payerbach, Austria Nationality Austrian Known for Relativity Scientific career Fields Medical Doctor Walter Rella was born in Feldkirch, Austria. Studied medicine in Vienna and Pamplona (Spain). 1969 Graduation. 1970-1977 Fellow in Cancer Research, Tumor Immunology. ca. 20 papers on the topic. 1978 - 2004 exerted profession as physician. Rella's papers relate to the field of philosophy and physics. He promotes the idea of the physical vacuum as a dynamical shattering lattice structure in: "Der Schritt in die Analogie" part I, Wr. Jb. f. Philos. (1984), 129-138; part II, Wr. Jb. f. Philos. (1985), 141-158. Work as a translator A substantial part of Rella's presence in the World Science Database is as a translator rather than an author, and the distinction matters when reading the entries below: several are English versions of early twentieth-century German papers, in which the argument is the original author's and Rella's contribution is the rendering. The dates in the list are those of the originals , not of the translations. They include Einstein 's 1909 Salzburg lecture on the nature of radiation — the paper containing the fluctuation argument that first stated wave–particle duality — together with papers from 1917, 1924 and 1926 on the propagation of gravity, the behaviour of light from extraterrestrial sources, and a repetition of the Harress–Sagnac experiment. His 2003 entry is a translation of chapter abstracts from a work on general etherodynamics. Making these available in English is a service independent of the position anyone takes on their contents, since several have no widely accessible English version. Readers should note that mathematical expressions in the resulting files have sometimes lost their superscripts in transcription, so formulae are worth checking against the originals.
