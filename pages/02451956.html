This is easy to > prove if you can do, or imagine, ordinary technical drafting. Yes, you're missing something obvious. The satellites aren't all located due south of your home. They're all along the celestial equator (more or less, allowing for parallax, which is why equinox blackouts don't happen exactly at the equinoxes), but the celestial equator passes through the east and west points on your horizon as well as the point due south whose altitude equals the complement of your latitude. So you could have a dish pointing due east or due west, though the signal strength would be pretty poor. These days most commercial TV broadcasts occupy the same one or two satellites, but in fact there are probably satellites at most of the allowed orbital positions. (Only a few degrees seperation are necessary for a large dish.
