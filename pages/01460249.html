Commentary on the Kriya Sutras of Babaji Chapter 3 The Discipline: Sadhanam Literal Translation V1) Patience , self study and self surrender are called Kriya Yoga . V2) To have Realisation , when surrendered to the absolute Self , are called Atmapranidhan . V3) To have Realisation and to remove the obstructions to Realisation , a discipline is necessary. V4) Meditation purifies the desires of the heart . V5) After purification by Yoga practice, pure knowledge of the Self is revealed. V6) With the practice of Kriyas given by the Gurudev , the Yogi gets all energy from within. V7) Always associate with Guru as Self ; keep away from association with bad elements. Watch the respiration as the Guru has instructed. V8) Non- violence, truthfulness, non-stealing, celibacy and not taking help from others; these five are called yama . V9) Purity, contentment and following the instructions of Guru is niyama . V10) Then the impurities are destroyed. V11) Continuing for a long time to sit easily in any posture is Asana . V12) When the continuation of breathing is naturally stopped, Kebala Kumbhaka is held. V13) When Kebala Kumbhaka is held, Pranayama , also, always is held. V14) Making the sense organs inward is Pratyahara . V15) At the blessing of the SELF , divine qualities are revealed in the heart of the Sadhaka .
