Update on Project SQM Replication Project Update 24-08-05. After months of trying to follow the wiring in the video with Mr Sweet and Mr Bearden I now think I have figured out the circuit in the Conditioning setup. Some of this is a estimate and it may not be fully correct so more investigation is needed. This diagram is version 1 and I bet there will be more versions to come. The circuit ties in line with my update on the 15-08-05. Click Here. The idea behind this circuit it to trigger the SCR at the zero crossing and the capacitor would charge off cycle. This would give an approximate 30Hz cycle and a 60 Hz oscillation inside the magnets because the right angle of the pulse to the magnets. There are two small things here I can think of, one is there may be a resistor on the gate of the SCR. The other thing is the pulse may over run the discharge cycle and stay on if the coil and Capacitor (C2) are not tunned properly. I believe this circuit is not enough to make the magnets oscillate
