The Tibetan Book of Living and Dying SOGYAL RINPOCHE Revised and Updated Edited by PATRICK GAFFNEY AND ANDREW HARVEY I WOULD LIKE TO DEDICATE THIS BOOK TO Jamyang Khyentse Chökyi Lodrö, Dudjom Rinpoche, Dilgo Khyentse Rinpoche, Nyoshul Khen Rinpoche, Khyentse Sangyum Khandro Tsering Chödrön, and all my beloved masters, who have been the inspiration of my life. May this book be a guide to liberation, read by the living, and to the dying, and for the dead. May it help all who read it and spur them on their journey to enlightenment!
