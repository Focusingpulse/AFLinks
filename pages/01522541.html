He has won various awards, such as the King of Sweden Medal in 1996 for his achievements in science. His research areas include Plasma Physics, Fusion Research and Extended Electromagnetic Theory. He has published more than 160 papers, and is a member of various national and international societies. Prof. Dr. Bo Lehnert is indeed a protege of Hannes Alfven (google "Bo Lehnert" + "Hannes Alfven". The former is an eminent scientist and internationalist in his own right, a King of Sweden Gold Medallist and Member of the Royal Swedish Academy which awards the physics and chemistry Nobel Prizes.
