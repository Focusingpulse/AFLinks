Metin Arık Born ( 1948-03-21 ) March 21, 1948 (age 78) Residence Istanbul, Turkey Nationality Turkish Alma mater Istanbul University; University of Pittsburgh (PhD, 1974) Known for The Arık–Coon q -deformed oscillator; Fibonacci oscillators; the YARK theory of gravitation Scientific career Fields Theoretical physics; mathematical physics; high energy physics; cosmology Metin Arık (born 21 March 1948) is a Turkish theoretical physicist, Emeritus Professor of Physics at Boğaziçi University in Istanbul. His research spans mathematical physics, high energy physics and cosmology; he is best known in the mainstream literature for the Arık–Coon oscillator , an early deformation of the harmonic-oscillator algebra that anticipated the q -deformed oscillators of quantum-group theory by more than a decade. He appears on this wiki as a co-author of Tolga Yarman on three critiques of the principle of equivalence presented to the Natural Philosophy Alliance , and as the A in the YARK (Yarman–Arık–Kholmetskii) theory of gravitation. Education Ph.D. in physics — University of Pittsburgh, 1974 M.S. in physics — University of Pittsburgh, 1970 B.S. in physics and mathematics — Istanbul University, 1969 He held a NATO scholarship from 1965 to 1974. Career Arık was a research assistant at Istanbul University (1968–1969) before moving to the United States, where he was a teaching and research assistant at the University of Pittsburgh through his graduate years (1969–1974). He then held research associateships in high energy theory at Ohio State University (1974) and Brookhaven National Laboratory (1974–1976), and at Westfield College of the University of London, first in high energy theory (1976–1978) and then in high energy experiment (1978–1979).
