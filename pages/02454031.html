"Jones Prize" free-energy device contest **************************** Contest Rules****************************** 1. To qualify as a "free energy device", the submitted apparatus must meet the following terms and conditions: It must be a free-standing device that requires no external connections of any type; employment of any external energy source such as electricity, radiation (including nuclear,solar, infrared, microwave, or any other type of electromagnetic energy), wind, or water is prohibited. 2. The device must be delivered to my location and set up in a special designated room which is sealed and arranged for remote monitoring by closed-circuit video. 3. The device must produce energy in an extractable, measurable form. 4. The device must operate while unaided and untouched continuously for a period of five thousand (5,000) hours. Jerry D.
