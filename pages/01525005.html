The Herman Branover affair To AKRONOS Main Page < Previous section Table of Contents The Herman Branover affair This is perhaps one fo the most egregious episodes in our search for serious sponsors of our work. When we visited New hampshire in June of 2003, we had extensive conversations about using some of the work we had done in nuclear physics. Gene was familiar with much of this, and had always wanted us to exploit it. We had always resisted it, because of its obvious military applications. In fact, we had scrupulously kept silent about it. By 2003 it had become obvious that we had no real or effective sponsor for our work and its varied technologies, so Gene thought that we should use one more ace card - our work in nuclear fusion. We gave him a limited mandate to do this at first. He was determined that Israel could only benefit from these technologies and that an approach had to be made to a suitable party. Several were then under way, some with Israeli parties. Then ICCF-10 happened. Gene experienced intense ostracism for presenting our work in the booth that he rented, but he kept his head vertical. he was impressed with the work presented by El-Boher, From Energetics Technologies and correctly concluded that the work was promising but was stuck in the usual rut of unrepeatability. So he approached I. Dardik (on Aug. 27, 2003, the day before Gene's poster presentation at ICCF-10) - and they had a long meeting in Gene's hotel room, where he presented segments of our "Pulsed Plasma/Aether Motor" DVD, and alluded to a possibly beneficial collaboration between the two teams. That night near 11 pm, Gene phoned us quite excited to report on the meeting. Next day, Aug. 28th, he again phoned to report that he had another extended talk with Dardik, that this was positive and there could be another fit that related to our biophysical and serum-free medium technologies. On September 2, Gene again met with El-Boher, and out of his own pocket gave him a copy of the DVD.
