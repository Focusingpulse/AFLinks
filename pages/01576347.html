VII. Epistemological Conclusion - GA 3. Truth and Knowledge (1963)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 1,576,347
We have established that the theory of knowledge is a science of significance for all human knowledge. The theory of knowledge alone can explain to us the relationship which the contents of the various branches of knowledge have to the world. Combined with them it enables us to understand the world, to attain a world-view. We acquire positive insight through particular judgments; through the theory of knowledge we learn the value of this insight for reality.
