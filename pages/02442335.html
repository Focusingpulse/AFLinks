06/16/16 - WO2016000075 SYSTEM AND METHOD FOR INCREASING THE LENGTH OF TELOMERES A device, method and system for the microwave resonance therapy of the chromosomes, telomeres and DNA, and extending the length of telomeres, by generating a wide and uniformly distributed spectrum of electromagnetic frequencies and ultrasound vibrations that induce electromagnetic and ultrasonic resonance in chromosomes and telomeres, and electromagnetic resonance in DNA. A plurality of sharp conductive pins mounted on the surface of a board, excited by sharp and short alternating electric pulses, create complex electrostatic fields that excite a mass of piezoelectric crystal grains, having a wide and uniform distribution of size, filling the space around the pins. Also, a wide band electromagnetic oscillator for DNA resonance is disclosed. One embodiment of an applied system is presented, with a plurality of telomeric resonators embedded in a blanket or pad, and the driving apparatus.
