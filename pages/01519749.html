In a metal wire it is carried by conduction electrons; in an electrolyte or a Plasma by ions of both signs; in a vacuum tube by free electrons. The standard account Current is defined as the rate at which charge passes a surface, and is measured in amperes. Since the 2019 revision of the SI the ampere is fixed by defining the elementary charge as exactly 1.602176634 10 19 coulomb; before that it was defined by the force between two parallel current-carrying wires, which is why the magnetic constant used to have an exact value.
