Introduction Models like Quantum Graphity and Loop Quantum Gravity treat smooth differential manifolds not as fundamental entities, but as low-energy thermodynamic limits of discrete, background-independent quantum systems. A persistent issue in these frameworks is maintaining topological stability without introducing unphysical background friction or damping parameters. This work outlines a framework connecting topological gauge protection on a discrete network to local curvature and particle mass generation. Rather than claiming a complete theory of everything, the goal is to show how discrete lattice mismatches naturally produce gravitational curvature, and how topological invariants can condition particle mass scales. 2.
