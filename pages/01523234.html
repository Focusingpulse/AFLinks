"Jim" Bourassa is an American researcher who, with David W. Thomson III , co-founded the Quantum AetherDynamics Institute and co-developed the Aether Physics Model (APM), a quantized-aether "theory of everything" that the two present as an alternative to the Standard Model of particle physics. Aether Physics Model The Aether Physics Model treats the aether not as empty space but as a quantum structure a lattice of "aether units," each conceived as a quantum of rotating magnetic field from which the properties of subatomic particles and the forces between them are said to be derived.
