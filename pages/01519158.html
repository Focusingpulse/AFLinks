A new theory of gravity is presented. It contains 300 years of Newtonian gravitation and effectively converts classical Newtonian gravity into a quantum theory. It mediates the gravitational force via the exchange of spin 0 gravitons and passes several tests including perihelion shift, light bending and Shapiro time delay. Hosted by Ian Cowan. Summary Having spent several previous sessions arguing that the Lorentz transformations are wrong, Stephan Gift here followed the consequence through. If the transformations fail, four-dimensional spacetime goes with them, general relativity goes with that, and something must take its place. What he offered is a single linear wave equation for a scalar gravitational potential. Clearing the ground He began by restating the five tests he holds the Lorentz transformations to fail: the one-way speed of light between fixed points on the Earth's surface, measured with synchronised GPS clocks; the Michelson–Gale experiment and its modern ring-laser successors, for which they predict no fringe shift at all; Wang 's moving-fibre experiment; the GPS clock synchronisation algorithm, which they can produce only in part; and superluminal signals, which they can accommodate only at the cost of causality violation, so that the establishment's energy goes into denying such signals exist. If that is right, then spacetime is not a real structure and general relativity is wrong — and with it the big bang, black holes, Hawking radiation, dark energy and Einsteinian gravitational waves.
