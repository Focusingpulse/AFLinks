We ﬁnd bimodal PA distributions of the ﬁlaments, and long and short populations of radio ﬁlaments. Our PA study shows the evidence for a distinct population of short ﬁlaments with PA close to the Galactic plane. Mainly thermal, short-radio ﬁlaments (<66″) have PAs concentrated close to the Galactic plane within 60° < PA < 120°. Remarkably, the short ﬁlament PAs are radial with respect to the Galactic center at l < 0° and extend in the direction toward Sgr A*.
