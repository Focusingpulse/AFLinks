In Brief How sleep need is sensed, integrated, and conveyed to downstream sleep circuits is poorly understood. Blum et al. find that Ca2+ transients in fly astrocytes correlate with time spent awake and identify molecular and circuit mechanisms underlying the generation, amplification, and transmission of these signals encoding sleep need.
