postulate

· archived # 1,482,506
noun: something taken as self-evident or assumed without proof as a basis for reasoning. Mathematics, Logic. a proposition that requires no proof, being self-evident, or that is for a specific purpose assumed true, and that is used in the proof of other propositions; axiom. a fundamental principle. a necessary condition; prerequisite. Synonyms hypothecate, presuppose, conjecture. hypothesis , theory ; axiom ; assumption, conjecture. [dictionary.com] Robert Hooke "He postuled for the first time the term "cell"..." Wikipedia, Postule "The few complete specimens that have been found have 12 thorax segments. A postule in the middle of each axial ring may be present. Frontal margin perpendicular to the midline, but gradually curling backward further out. Tip blunt in frontal segments, ending in a short backward pointing spine for rear segments."
