RM CORES RM Cores are square-designed cores that offer all the magnetic and mechanical advantages of pot cores, plus the added feature of maximizing magnetic performance while minimizing PC board space. Easy to assemble and adaptable to automation, completed units provide at least 40% savings in mounting area compared to a similar size pot core assembly. Section 8 RM Cores RM cores are available in seven standard sizes. Printed circuit bobbins or plain bobbins are available. Typical applications include differential inductors, power inductors, filter inductors, telecom inductors and broadband transformers.
