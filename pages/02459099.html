After a Bachelor of Science (given with Honors) he proceeded to receive a Master of Science and Doctorate from the University of Sydney, which then kept him on for seven years to teach in the Department of Agricultural Chemistry. After that he taught at the University of Kentucky for another twelve years, and then went to Virginia Tech, where he spent 21 years before retirement in 1999, and where he is still Professor Emeritus. He was Dean of Arts and Sciences at Virginia Tech for eight years, an experience that led him to write (under a pseudonym) a book called To Rise Above Principle. In addition to his regular appointments, he had visiting appointments at the University of Michigan, the University of Southampton, and Rikagaku Kenkyusho in Tokyo.
