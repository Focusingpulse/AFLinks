GC2026-PERM039 Geomagnetic field intensity at the Permian–Triassic boundary based on data from the Kuznetsk basin in the context of the Mesozoic Dipole Low problem Andrey A. Eliseev1,2 , Dmitriy V. Metelkin1,2 1 Novosibirsk National Research State University 2 A.A. Trofimuk Institute of Petroleum Geology and Geophysics, Siberian Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences Eliseevaa@ipgg.sbras.ru The global database of geomagnetic field intensity (http://wwwbrk.adm.yar.ru/palmag/database.html) at the Permian–Triassic boundary (~252 Ma) is predominantly filled with determinations from the most extensively studied Norilsk and Maymecha-Kotuy areas of the Siberian Large Igneous Province [1, 2, 3]. The exceptionally low values of the virtual dipole moment (VDM) discovered there led to the hypothesis that the Mesozoic Dipole Low (MDL) began at the Permian–Triassic boundary [1]. However, the MDL hypothesis has been repeatedly criticized, with arguments based on the necessity of applying strict reliability criteria for data filtering, including shortcomings in the analysis of magnetic properties of the studied samples and the statistical robustness of the results [4]. For instance, single determinations of the absolute ancient geomagnetic field intensity, meeting generally accepted criteria and comparable in magnitude to the present-day field, have been obtained from intrusive rocks of the Siberian LIP [5].
