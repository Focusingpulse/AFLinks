The purpose of this 5th mission is to demonstrate that the QRO-UAV is able to do a close inspection of a 20 meters high railway viaduct. In this test mission the QRO-UAV is placed again in real situation. The 5th test mission of the QRO-UAV is : Railway viaduct inspection... Tests results : During this 5th test mission the QRO software has fully demonstrated that it is able to handle the turbulences produced by the venturi effect wind flowing through the arches of the viaduct and the electromagnetic interferences (EMI) induced by the proximity of the high voltage electric lines. The QRO-UAV is a very userfull tool for such an inspection mission.
