Unzicker observes that the incompatibility of quantum mechanics and gravity is treated as a central problem, that the Planck length combines G and h and is therefore taken to mark where quantum gravitational effects matter and that this is very nearly the whole of what has been established. No theory exists and no observable effect has been found.
