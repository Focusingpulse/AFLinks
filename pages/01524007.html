Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Christian Sutterlin Keywords particle, photon , electromagnetic waves Published 2012 No. of pages 3 Abstract Particle and wave natures of the photon allow for explaining quite simply almost all phenomena related to electromagnetic waves. The purpose of this note is to show that the replacement of the particle photon by a set of particles could make everything simpler.
