ON GENERALIZED LI-YAU INEQUALITIES LI-CHANG HUNG Abstract. We generalize the Li-Yau inequality for second derivatives and we also establish Li-Yau type inequality for fourth derivatives. Our derivation relies on the representation formula for the heat equation. . 1.
