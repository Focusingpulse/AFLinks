University of Technology, Gorakhpur-273010 (UP), India Introduction: We know that the great circle is a circle whose plane passes through the centre of sphere. The part of a great circle on a sphere is known as a great circle arc. The length of minor great circle arc (i.e. less than half great circle) joining any two arbitrary points on a sphere of finite radius is the minimum distance between those points. Here we are interested in finding out the minimum distance or great circle distance between any two arbitrary points on a spherical surface of finite radius (like globe) for the given values of latitudes & longitudes.
