Thiaoouba Prophecy E-book I thought I'd put this here in case anyone is interested. Or if you know a friend who might be interested in the subject, please tell them about this. This is because I think this book is not getting the attention it deserves. Especially in the light of most recent findings. Now here is finally a book that "gives answers to most mysteries known to man", pyramids, antigravitation, meaning of number 9, ancient civilizations, Atlantis, the Christ, extraterrestials etc. Except that it doesn't... So one might think what is the catch, the book is even given away for free. Surely it can be of no importance? Now if you read the book and hope for definite answers you might be disappointed, there are no schematics for starship drive for one, but the book sure does provoke thought which seems the way things have to be for us. We have to learn ourselves. The story itself is presented in a very friendly way and a lot easier to relate than most of the UFO stories out there. This one doesn't have reptilians or repulsive insect-like ET's for that matter. Just civilizations like ours, and it actually claims we had once migrated from another planet. It doesn't provoke conspiracy but rather states we have lost our way, and that once our race was much more advanced. The reason for our lost knowledge are the various cataclysms that have happened on earth in long forgotten past. I suggest anyone interested take a look and kindly comment about it on this topic. The book has gave me great inspiration and while I can't / don't actually know whether it is true or not, I do know I wouldn't have searched and known what I do today without it. It also never stops amazing me every time I find out something new related to the information presented in the book. There seem to be a lot of very interesting connections to be found, which seem to indicate the writer has either done his homework very well or then it is just pure coincidence. Or maybe...
