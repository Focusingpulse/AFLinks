PREFACE The reader is informed about the reasons making the author publish his new theory more than 40 years after its foundation had been laid. Being a physician -- clinical neurologist and neurophysiologist -- he is but an amateur in physics and astronomy. A detailed discussion with experts in 1949 resulted in their refusal to accept the new conception.
