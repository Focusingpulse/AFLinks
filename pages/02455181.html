Decker: "Re: Non-Disclosures a Legal Entrapment" Hi Folks! Something interesting regarding fairness and rational requests. What would you do if someone contacted you claiming they had AN IDEA for a free energy, gravity control or other device, yet HAD NOT BUILT IT or proven it yet? They want you to sign a non-disclosure for their IDEAs, not a working device. What do you do? If you sign it, then you are forever restricted from discussing any of those IDEAS publicly without risk of legal action. Is this a logical or fair request? Of course not. Up front you have no idea what will be talked about, so once they 'share' that info through the non-disclosure release, you have sealed your fate and risk legal action in any future discussions if you happen to broach ANY OF THOSE IDEAS, either publicly or privately that can be overheard, copied, recorded or otherwise traced. It is simply an irrational and unfair teaser for information. Even if they claim to have a working device, saying if you want to know more, you'll have to sign a non-disclosure, up front, without ever seeing the machine, without having a chance to duplicate it or test it, is that rational? Of course not. It is a subset of greed and control, not blatantly exposed but certainly showing something that is most unpleasant and to be avoided like a rattlesnake with its tail in the air.
