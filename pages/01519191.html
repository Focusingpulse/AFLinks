Title A Quantum Mechanical Measurement Leading to Simultaneous Spin-Up and Spin-Down State of a Single Electron Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Vesselin C Noninski Keywords Measurement, electron , spin, Quantum Theory Published 2003 Journal PhilSci-Archive No. of pages 9 Abstract In is shown that when two observers carry out a simultaneous measurement on a pair of spin-? particles in a "singlet" state a possibility exists for an outcome that lacks physical meaning. More specifically, despite the fact that the two commuting operators formally possess simultaneous eigenvectors it is not possible for physical reasons these eigenvectors to exist simultaneously. It is pointed out that the possibility for such non-physical outcome is observed only in the case of "singlet" state which puts into question the physical meaning of such state, and following from it exotic notions such as "non-locality". This hints that probably QM in its present form should be reconsidered with regard to its limitations. Overview A Quantum Mechanical Measurement Leading to Simultaneous Spin-Up and Spin-Down State of a Single Electron is a nine-page paper by Vesselin C Noninski , deposited in the PhilSci-Archive in 2003. It argues that quantum mechanics, correctly applied to a pair of spin-½ particles in the singlet state, permits an outcome with no physical meaning, and that this impugns the singlet state itself and with it the notion of non-locality. The setup is the standard one. In the four-dimensional product space, the z -components of spin for the two particles are S 1 = σ 3 ⊗ 1 and S 2 = 1 ⊗ σ 3 , which Noninski writes out explicitly as the diagonal matrices diag(1, 1, −1, −1) and diag(1, −1, 1, −1) in the basis |++⟩, |+−⟩, |−+⟩, |−−⟩. The state considered is the singlet, ψ = (|+−⟩ − |−+⟩)/√2. He then makes his central observation.
