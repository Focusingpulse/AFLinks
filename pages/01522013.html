This Saturday: Aether: the Good, Bad, and Ugly - Discussion lead by David de Hilster Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Date 16 January 2021 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording This Saturday: Aether: the Good, Bad, and Ugly - Discussion lead by David de Hilster was a live session on 16 January 2021 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. The majority of CNPS modelers are aetherists. Yet there are other viable models. What gives??? David de Hilster leads a discussion on a topic much discussed by many critical thinkers. Summary David de Hilster on the ether — what he takes to be good about it, what is bad, and what he called the ugly. He stated his own position at the outset and repeated it: he is not an etherist, he thinks there are better models, he supports every etherist's work, he thinks about the question every day, and the ether could turn out to be right. Where the community stands His estimate, offered as observation rather than survey after thirty years: most of the people actually building models are etherists; a good many others assume there is an ether without having investigated enough to take a position; and there is a sizeable group who do not subscribe to it at all. He named Ray Gallucci as an example of the middle case — someone who has written on Duncan Shaw 's account, which de Hilster calls the Vancouver ether theory because the mist coming in and out of the harbour suggested it, and who, asked whether he is an etherist, would not answer. He showed a comparison table of the recent models he knows — Glenn Borchardt 's infinite universe theory, Jeff Yee 's energy wave theory, Lori Gardi 's om particle, Ionel Dinu 's, and the de Hilsters' particle model — all Newtonian, all accounting for light, none using charge, and all but one using an ether.
