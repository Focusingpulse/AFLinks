plane of motion

· archived # 1,482,382
"Their coil of motion, which caused the division, turns but one way, just as the sphere which you have held between your hands, turns but one way. The particles have been ejected from that coil in an angle of 90 degrees from their plane of motion yet it is now held that these particles turn right-handed from one end to turn in the direction of its motion , and particles ejected from the other end turn left-handed for the same reason."
