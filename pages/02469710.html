MANY NEW INVENTIONS Applications for Printing, Lighting, Heating and Cooking That Will Revolutionize Present Customs — Some of Them Enumerated. New York Correspondence Baltimore Sun. An electric machine, called the “Emick Electric Page Printer,” will be put in operation this week in various Wall Street offices which, it is claimed, will soon be indispensable to all newspapers or other institutions or persons desiring a quick transit of news. It is a modification of the old tape printing “ticker,” which the general public has so long seen in use in hotels, banks and brokers’ offices to record stock transactions and such other news as is generally bulletined in that way. The “page printer” uses pretty much the same little wheel to do the printing. In place of the tape of narrow paper a continuous roll a little wider than two columns of the Sun is automatically fed in front of this wheel and prints in a broad line of clear type whatever the matter is that is to be published. This roll of news can be 2 inches, 2 feet or two yards long, or any other length desired. An expert telegrapher is now needed to send out the messages, which are printed simultaneously on all the instruments on the circuit.
