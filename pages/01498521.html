Johnson Richfield, Minnesota 111 Perhaps no scientific marvel of the twentieth century is a better example than cold fusion of the visionary philosophy written by Eugene F. Mallove in Fire From Ice: "...the eternal challenge of science [is] not to follow where the worn path may lead, but to go instead where there is no path, and leave a trail." This effort in cold fusion theory is dedicated to the visionary scientists working in the field - not least of whom are Drs. Martin Fleischman and Stanley Pons - and without whom this theory would not have been possible. V Giuliano Preparata talked about the agony of having a blank piece of paper in front of him and facing the challenge of the unknown.
