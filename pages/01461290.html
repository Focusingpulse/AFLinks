Phrygian Mode

· archived # 1,461,290
The Phrygian Mode is created from notes of the Major Scale . Specificaly, Phrygian Mode uses the notes of the Major Scale , starting on the third note of the Major Scale . Example: If you have a C Major Scale (which contains the notes C, D, E, F, G, A, B, C), to create a Phrygian Mode you would start on the third note (E). So, your new E Phrygian Mode would consist of the notes E, F, G, A, B, C, D, E.
