New Series Vol. 1, No. 1 (December, 2015) Newsletter Announcing the 38th Conference of the USPA, 2016 The United States Psychotronics Association (USPA) will be hosting its annual conference, July 15th-17th, 2016 at the beautiful Wyndham Glenview Suites in Glenview, Illinois (near O’Hare airport Chicago). Our theme is: “Exploring Subtle Energy: The Past, Present, and Future” Further information will be sent out in the coming months as the conference invited speakers and accepted speakers join us for this special event involving leading edge sciences. Send your inquires to: uspsychotronics@yahoo.com The USPA website: www.psychotronics.org Join us on Facebook: United States Psychotronics Association CALL for PAPERS: For the opportunity to speak at 2016 USPA conference, a title, abstract and a short biography can be submitted to the email uspsychotronics@yahoo.com Please title your email with “2016 abstract - (your last name)” Abstracts are due by January 30, 2016. Please include a short bio of yourself. Accepted speakers will need to provide their own transportation and lodging. The Conference fees will waived. President’s Message -- Jon Klimo Dear fellow USPA members, This is my first message to you as the new president of the USPA (as of July 2015). I plan to periodically-- possibly even monthly or bimonthly -- share my thoughts with you in this way. We are now in the process of developing a new website for our organization together with a newsletter and blog, and we have already established a good Facebook presence. So, I hope by means of these and other ways of reaching out, the USPA can make itself increasingly visible and better known. I welcome any of you who would like to share with me and the USPA any ideas, questions, and suggestions you might have for our organization.
