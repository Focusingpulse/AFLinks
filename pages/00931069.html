Successful Substitution of Prednisone by Saccharomyces cerevisiae in Immune Thrombocytopenic Purpura: First Documented Case of Beta- Glucan-Mediated Immunomodulation via GALT Pathway Douglas Correa Cavasso Independent Researcher. Data collected under clinical monitoring at Hospital Erasto Gaertner (Liga Paranaense de Combate ao Câncer), Curitiba, Paraná, Brazil. Medical Record: 22004146 Corresponding Author: douglascorreacavasso@gmail.com ABSTRACT Background: Conventional treatment of Immune Thrombocytopenic Purpura (ITP) with corticosteroids presents significant adverse effects and variable efficacy. Beta-glucans from Saccharomyces cerevisiae demonstrate immunomodulatory properties in experimental autoimmune models, but clinical application in ITP remains unexplored. Objective: Document the first case of successful corticosteroid substitution by S. cerevisiae in ITP, with comparative efficacy analysis and mechanistic hypothesis centered on gut-associated lymphoid tissue (GALT)
