Moray Radiant Energy Device is a 1996 compilation by Bruce A Perrault , gathering patents related to the Radiant Energy device of Thomas Henry Moray . Overview The collection reproduces patents (Moray's own and related art) bearing on the design and components of the Moray "Radiant Energy" apparatus, as a reference for researchers attempting to understand or reconstruct the device. It is a companion resource to Moray's The Sea of Energy in Which the Earth Floats within the "free energy" research literature. About the compiler Bruce A. Perrault is
