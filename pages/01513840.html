It's day 11 of the Future Past of Electricity, an album that I have been working on over the period of 4 years dedicated to the rare, unique and uncelebrated engineer and scientist Eric Dollard. His controversial and ground-breaking theories in non electromagnetic transformer theory and Tesla's true wireless technology is from a life rich in experience and events. We have raised $1125 out of $15000 already. The album I have written tries to capture the technical nature of the traveling electric wave, in the formation and structure of rhythm and melody. Advanced techniques were developed by the author of the album based on the work of Nikola Tesla and Eric Dollard such as a parts per quarter impulse waves that were designed and used in several tracks including track 2 of the album Pulses per quarter. A new principle for a new world is one of the stories. Tesla poverty & tyranny another. Each track in itself tells a unique story that can be felt by the listener and recognised by the artist and scientist alike. Oth
