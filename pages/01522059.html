Transverse Paraphysics: The New Science of Space, Time, and Gravity Control Book Author Jerry G Gallimore Language English Subject Antigravity, paraphysics Published 1982 Publisher Tesla Book Company Pages 359 ISBN 096035364X Transverse Paraphysics: The New Science of Space, Time, and Gravity Control is a 1982 book by Jerry G Gallimore , published by the Tesla Book Company. Overview Gallimore surveys what he calls the "harder sciences" and their relation to unconventional energies, covering the dimensions of space, time, and gravity and claimed new discoveries in each — including anti-gravity research and anti-gravity patents. The book also treats "aura" energy fields around living things and proposes a biophysical basis for extrasensory perception (ESP), tying together gravity control, biophysics, and paraphysics. About the author J. G. (Jerry) Gallimore was an American researcher and writer on unconventional physics and "paraphysics," associated with the fringe-science and antigravity milieu of the late twentieth century.
