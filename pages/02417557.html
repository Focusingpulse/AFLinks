As one progresses in the study of the development of modern science, one can make one’s way—starting from this or that branch of knowledge—to another, to which one is led by certain external or internal necessities. But all of this essentially takes place without the participation of the deeper human being. One must admit: this path is traversed with a certain inner indifference.
