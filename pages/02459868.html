Hi all, Euejin, I agree with your second post on this thread. I did not mean to make it sound as if I thought all the claims out there are false. On the contrary, I believe a lot of them are real, and the reason that we do not see these inventions everyday in the world, is because they have been suppressed, and the inventor is no longer living, so that they may duplicate their own results, or tell us what we are doing wrong (including Sweet's VTA and some of the other's that you mentioned). My main concern is where people are asking for money, for claims that they cannot prove. As for the other's who are still living and giving away the info for free, I meant only to point out the fact that these people claim to be able to build the working device (and in fact claim that they already have, but have since lost it or it has been stolen), but will not rebuild it themselves. This seems rather suspicious to me. It is the work of the former inventors mentioned, and the work of some new inventors (not to mention some of our own ideas) that keep so many of us interested in this area. I even belive that some of the false claims out there may represent some good ideas that could be helpful but, unfortunately they lose credit by overstating tier claims, rather than just expressing tier ideas as just ideas. -Russ Previous message: Theo Paijmans: "Re: A good read./o?"
