Bankrupting Physics Book Author Alexander Unzicker , Sheilla Jones Published 2013 (German original 2010) Publisher Palgrave Macmillan Pages 288 ISBN 978-1137278234 Bankrupting Physics: How Today's Top Scientists are Gambling Away Their Credibility is a 2013 book by the German physicist Alexander Unzicker and the Canadian science writer Sheilla Jones , published by Palgrave Macmillan in its MacSci series. It is a polemic against what the authors regard as the drift of fundamental physics away from experiment and toward untestable mathematics. It is the first of Unzicker's book-length critiques, preceding The Higgs Fake and Einstein's Lost Key . Origins The book is the English edition of Unzicker's German work Vom Urknall zum Durchknall: Die absurde Jagd nach der Weltformel — roughly "From the Big Bang to the Big Crackup: the absurd hunt for the world formula" — published by Springer Spektrum in July 2010 and reworked with Jones for the English-language edition. The German original was named science book of the year 2010 by Bild der Wissenschaft in the category Zündstoff ("incendiary material") — awarded to the book judged to present a controversial subject most competently. It is worth noting that the award was for being provocative and well-argued rather than for being correct, which is close to the book's own self-description. The argument Unzicker and Jones take the reader on a tour of contemporary theoretical physics and argue that a series of heavily publicised programmes have reached dead ends.
