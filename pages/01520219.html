Contemporary experiments such as High-Frequency Gravitational Waves (HFGW) and Laser Interferometer Gravitational-Wave Observatory (LIGO) require mass to model gravitational acceleration and gravitational waves. A different approach to gravitational acceleration is presented here. This paper proposes that gravitational acceleration on any particle is the effect of the deformation of the shape and mass of the particle due to noninertia transformations present in that local region of the gravitational field.
