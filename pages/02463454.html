Introduction The nature of mass has been a longstanding inquiry in physics, with various theories aiming to elucidate its origin and characteristics. While the Standard Model of particle physics has successfully delineated many facets of the subatomic realm, it does not fully explain the fundamental nature of mass. The Higgs mechanism posits that particles acquire mass through interactions with the Higgs field. It offers a partial explanation but leaves numerous inquiries unanswered, as demonstrated by recent experimental results at the Large Hadron Collider [1]. The discovery of the Higgs boson has been pivotal in confirming the Brout-Englert-Higgs (BEH) mechanism, which underpins electroweak symmetry breaking and mass generation in particle physics.
