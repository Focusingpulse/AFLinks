Abstract Prof. Rati Ram Sharma got disillusioned with relativity & quantum theory even when he was a physics student (M.Sc.) during 1952-54. He has since been working on a realistic alternative to replace the modern theories of physics & cosmology. He has already published five books on Unified Theory in 1990, 1997, 1998, 2002, 2008 besides the 1984 book: Molecular Homoeopathy.
