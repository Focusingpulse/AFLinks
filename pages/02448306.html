Many people saw the crashed craft (which was actually not a disc but "delta" or "wedge" shaped)which was from 30-50 feet long and up to 30 feet wide. The next day they said that it was just a "weather balloon". Ever since then dozens of witnesses have came forward both civilian and military who have talked about the craft, the bodies, and the debris.
