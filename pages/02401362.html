kimo2026-main163 India’s Role in South and Southeast Asian Technological Cooperation Kamala Kumari1 1 Sharda University, India kamala.kumari@sharda.ac.in Abstract India plays a significant role in advancing technological cooperation across South and Southeast Asia. As one of the largest digital and industrial economies in the region, India leverages its strengths in information technology, digital public infrastructure, renewable energy, and others to foster collaborative development. This study examines India’s contribution to technological cooperation in South and Southeast Asia. The main objective of the study is to analyze how India leverages digital infrastructure, industrial corridors, energy grids, and innovation ecosystems to promote cross-border technological collaboration, capacity-building, and sustainable development among regional partners. A qualitative research methodology was employed, combining content analysis of policy documents, official reports, and regional cooperation frameworks (such as BIMSTEC and bilateral agreements). The results indicate that India has played a strategic role in enhancing digital connectivity, supporting industrial and energy infrastructure projects across the region. Initiatives such as digital public infrastructure platforms, and energy grid interconnections have strengthened regional integration, promoted knowledge sharing, and enhanced technological resilience among partner countries.
