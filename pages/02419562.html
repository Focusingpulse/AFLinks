Journal of Borderland Research >> Vol. 38 >> No. 04 [6] USING HYPNOSIS AS A PSYCHIC RESEARCH TOOL By Gene Hurtienne I am presenting to you BSRF members an theoretical idea concerning hypnosis as a research tool that would, if developed properly with the right statements, motive and attitudes, aid mankind in a number of life's areas. Those areas being Medicine, History, Religion, and Crime. I realize that hypnotism is used in some of the mention areas of life, but the results are not what they should be and achieving good results are hard to come by and is hardly worth the energy spent. In the areas where hypnosis is used, it is not used that much or that often. In medicine it is used mainly to ease pain, to quit smoking or aid in the birth of a child. Doctors dealing with mind and emotional problems use hypnosis only where the patient may request it or when the doctor might feel it would be helpful. In the area of crime, if the police force need greater detail or more clues from a witness, they are asked to go under hypnosis. Wherever hypnosis is used, there is one common denominator -- each person either as a patient or as a witness must be hypnotized. There was one exception to this - EDGAR CAYCE. He was able through self-hypnosis to project his mind and with a proper statement from his wife, read the Akashic Records or if necessary the higher mind of the person who requested a reading from him.
