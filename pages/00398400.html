We provide 17 insights into “Earthly areas” that might be utilized to increase the opportunity for reducing warming. We find that between 0.2% and 1% of the Earth would require an albedo modification 21 to resolve most of global warming. Results are highly dependent on modeling aspects like heat capacity, irradiance, 22 and albedo changes of the area selected.
