The trajectory indicatesthat this impact probably wasn’t related to the PT-Impact. With a long delay of maybe 10 to 20 million years the North-& South-American-Plate probably began to slowly break-off and move away from the African Plate. The Congo Impact Impulse (CII) in this way may be responsible for the final break-up of Gondwana.
