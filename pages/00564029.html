Test for mathematical abilities of LLMs Dainis Zeps, Riga, 2025. 21. December We see here two graphs, a sample of flower snark [1], published by Rufus Isaacs in 1975, and graph constructed by Emanuels Grinbergs in 1972 [2,3,4,5]. Prove that both graphs are isomorphic. The proof should be in one line, suggestedly no longer than five words. Take it as a test to compare different LLMs of their ability to solve simple mathematical problems. References 1.
