Using this method, we 12 are able to quantify a global warming range due to the UHI effect (including urban area). However, the model showed consistent estimates of about 0.096W/m2 14 per % Normalized Effective Amplified Area for the urbanized area feedback value. 15 These values increase when the UHI effective contribution to climate feedback estimates are included. The model is 16 additionally used to quantify a warming assessment due to sea ice feedback. Results provide insight into the UHI 17 area effects from a new perspective and illustrates that one needs to take effective UHI amplification factors into 18 account when assessing UHI’s warming effect on a global scale.
