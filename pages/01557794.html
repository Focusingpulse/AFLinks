My Experiments with hydrogen/oxygen gas Part two by Geoff Egel My Experiments with hydrogen/oxygen gas part 3. Geoff A simple to make gas collector. A word of warning If you fill a closed container with an explosive gas mixture, as an example one (1) part oxygen and two (2) parts hydrogen you will have set the stage for accidental explosion. Hydrogen and oxygen can and will explode with violent effect. To know of this, and to teach this, and not carefully warn all parties is to ignore your place as scientific ethical investigator.
