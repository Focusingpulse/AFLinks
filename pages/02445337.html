08/30/15 - Carbon Nanofibers Could Reduce Global Warming Researchers have found a way to pluck carbon dioxide from the atmosphere and turn it into a useful product � carbon nanofibers. They say, using their method across an area 10% the size of the Sahara Desert, enough CO2 could be removed to decrease atmospheric levels below pre-Industrial Revolution levels within 10 years. The research was presented at a meeting of the American Chemical Society (ACS) in Boston this week, and published online in Nano Letters.
