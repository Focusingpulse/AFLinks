Vol. 28 (1989) L2017-L2020 Part 2, No. 11, 20 November 1989 DOI : 10.1143/JJAP.28.L2017 Nuclear Fusion in Solid WADA, Nobuhiko / NISHIZAWA, Kunihide Department of Physics, Faculty of Science, Nagoya University / Radioisotope Research Center, Nagoya University Abstract: Spontaneous neutron emissions were intermittently detected from activated palladium rods well soaked with deuterium gas in a closed glass bulb. By the stimulation of the palladium rods with a high voltage discharge between the rods, a burst of neutron flux 2 × 104 times larger than background was detected. Atoms or molecules of mass number 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 were found in the residual gas. Nuclear fusion in solid is interpreted in terms of the supersaturation of the solid solution of deuterium.
