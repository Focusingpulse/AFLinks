Schauberger [1] Motion-of-creation and creation-of-motion : These terms relate to the German expressions ' Gestaltungsbewegung ' and ' Bewegungsgestaltung ', which encompass ' Bewegung ' meaning ' motion ' and the German concept of ' Gestalt ' or ' Gestaltung ', an expression that in English cannot be translated with a single word. The Collins English Dictionary defines ' Gestalt ' as "a pattern or structure possessing qualities as a whole that cannot be described merely as a sum of its parts." As such it can also apply to physical, energetic and spiritual forms, matrices and structures. ' Motion-of-creation ' and ' creation-of-motion ' could therefore also be respectively interpreted as 'form (or structure) - creating motion' and 'motion-creating form (or structure)'. — Ed. [ From Special Edition Mensch und Technik, Vol. 2, 1993, section 3.1 ]
