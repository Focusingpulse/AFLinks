INTERMOLECULAR SECOND SUBDIVISION

· archived # 1,459,880

The constituent atomic triplets rotate with a mass action similar to that governing the molecule and intermolecule , differing only in having a higher oscillating frequency. The intermolecular field is called in current physical and chemical terminology " atoms " that is, dissociated, uncombined, elementary substances which so far have defied their attempts at further dissociation and analysis.
