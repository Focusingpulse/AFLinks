We suggest a replacement to ad-hoc MOND type theories with the theory developed herein, in the light of recent DESI and JWIST discoveries, with a fully covariant version of our earlier gravitating dark-energy model: we show that dark-energy can gravitate if it is considered to be in compression by tidal effects; also it sets a natural cusp-like size limit to galaxies and clusters. A relation is found between the slope of the galactic rotation curve and the slope of the dark￾energy/matter zone is found too. An earlier proof by the author that MOND type theories are not Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle compatible is cited here. Finally we ask if an extra-repulsive form of dark-energy may prevent collapse to singularity in Black hole solutions. All-in-all, this paper is a semi-classical treatment of gravity with vacuum corrections that might manifest on the large-scale.
