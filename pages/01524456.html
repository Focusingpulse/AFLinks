Bostick was an American physicist who discovered plasmoids , plasma focus , and plasma vortex phenomena. He simulated cosmical astrophysics with laboratory plasma experiments, and showed that Hubble expansion can be produced with repulsive mutual induction between neighboring galaxies acting as homopolar generators . His work on plasmas provided evidence for finite-sized elementary particles and the composition of strings. "Winston H.
