Ruby Carat is an American mathematics educator, science communicator and advocate for research into Cold Fusion and low-energy nuclear reactions (LENR). She is the founder, publisher and host of Cold Fusion Now! , an all-volunteer media and outreach effort that documents and promotes the field of condensed matter nuclear science. Education B.S. in physics Temple University, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania M.S. in mathematics University of Miami, Florida Biography Before turning to science, she worked as a musician and performance artist.
