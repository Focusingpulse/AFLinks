Pygmalion Effect

· archived # 1,461,490
Did you know that if you tell and convince a teacher that the arriving new (average) student is a GENIUS - that the student will perform and test as a Genius - Every time! It is called the Pygmalion effect or Observer-expectancy effect or Rosenthal effect - a proven phenomenon whereby others expectations of a person affects that persons performance.
