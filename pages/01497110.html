universe of simulation

· archived # 1,497,110
"You must, however, learn that you cannot forever remain as body in the universe of simulation , or make believe, which motion is. There are intervals in which you must be wholly Mind to learn your part in the drama of Creation . You must then reappear on its stage in many thousands [p.158] of rehearsals until you manifest the divinity of the Man Idea instead of his flesh alone."
