When was the speed of light first measured?; http://zebu.uoregon.edu/~soper/Light/speedoflight.html Light moves with a speed c = 3 x 108 m/s. In 1868, he suggested using an interferometric method to measure stellar diameters by placing a mask with two holes in front of a telescope's aperture. He calculated that the fringes would vanish at a separation related to the size of the Star.
