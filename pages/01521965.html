This provides the link between Gravity and Magnetism. We learn how gravity is an irrotational aether flow that is permeated with tiny swirling sinks that siphon the aether away from the larger sink. These tiny swirling sinks are stacked on top of each other in their axial planes so as to form the double helixes that constitute Faraday's lines of force. The magnetic permeability represents the areal density in the equatorial plane of these vortices and hence it represents the flux density of a bundle of Faraday's lines of force measured perpendicularly to the lines. Magnetic permeability and electric permittivity both represent a leakage factor in the gravitational field. Overview David Tombe proposes that gravity and magnetism are the same phenomenon expressed at different scales, in a framework he subtitles "A Theory of Rotationally Siphoned Aether." The opening claim is mathematical: Kepler's law of areal velocity is equivalent to the Lorentz force, and this equivalence provides the link between the two fields. Gravity is then an irrotational inflow of aether toward a large sink, permeated by tiny swirling sinks that siphon aether away from the main flow. Those small vortices stack along their rotation axes to form the double helices Tombe identifies with Faraday's lines of force. Electricity and gravity are treated as the same thing at different scales — electricity as radial inflow or outflow of aether considered on the small scale, gravity as inflow considered on the large.
