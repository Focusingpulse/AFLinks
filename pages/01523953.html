PES, which stands for "Pure Energy Systems", is a news and directory service whose mission is to find and facilitate the best renewable energy technologies. It is aided by the New Energy Congress, which is an association of energy professionals from around the world who review the most promising claims to existing and up-and-coming energy technologies that are clean, renewable, affordable, reliable, easy to implement, safe, and legitimate.
