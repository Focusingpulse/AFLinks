Error code: 1 Watch this video on YouTube Talk Type Video Speaker Lori Anne Gardi Date 31 March 2020 Platform YouTube Recording Recording What is a Field? is a video by Lori Anne Gardi , published on 31 March 2020 on her FractalWoman YouTube channel. Summary An attempt to define a term Gardi holds has never been defined unambiguously, and the video in which she draws a sharp line between what is a mathematical description and what is physically real. It ends with an experiment designed to show that the isopotential lines she has been measuring are not an artefact. Wheeler's complaint She opens with a clip of Ken Wheeler on the subject. Asked to define the word, his answer is that physics never will: no branch of physics has ever defined a field, and when Maxwell's field equations are cited he replies that they do not define a field either, but only express it as an effect over a given vector over a period of time. The remark Gardi singles out as the most important is his distinction that descriptions are not explanations — explanations being what is actually wanted. She agrees with the substance while narrowing the claim: many people have offered definitions, but none unambiguous, and the task is therefore to disambiguate the term. Building a definition Her method is to start from ordinary usage. The first dictionary sense of "field" is an area or region of land, particularly one planted with a crop — a corn field. That is already a region of space, a region of the universe, and she treats it as a serviceable first iteration.
