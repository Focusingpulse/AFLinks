Special affine Fourier transform for space￾time algebra signals in detail Eckhard Hitzer Abstract. We generalize the space-time Fourier transform (SFT) [10] to a special affine Fourier transform (SASFT, also known as offset linear canonical transform) for 16-dimensional space-time multivector Cl(3, 1)- valued signals over the domain of space-time (Minkowski space) R 3,1 . All important results are proven in full detail. 1.
