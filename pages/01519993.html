Title Ether and Relativity Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Geoffrey Builder Keywords Ether , Relativity Published 1979 Journal Speculations in Science and Technology Volume 2 Number 3 Pages 230-242 Read the linked document here (this is the Cannata translation described below, not Builder's paper) About this record The metadata on this page and the document it links to do not belong to the same paper. The bibliographic details are Builder 's and are plausible. Geoffrey Builder (1906-1976) was an Australian physicist who published a series of papers on the ether and the clock paradox in the Australian Journal of Physics in the late 1950s, arguing that the paradox is resolved without recourse to general relativity and that a preferred-frame reading is empirically equivalent to the standard one. Speculations in Science and Technology 2(3), 230-242 (1979) is a posthumous reprint, and Builder's own page lists the paper under that year. The linked PDF is not that paper. It is a machine translation into English of an Italian article by Giuseppe Cannata, who has no page on this wiki and is not Builder. Its content is unrelated: it proposes an ether composed of nested vortices at every scale from galactic clusters to elementary particles, classifies all forces into seven kinds obtained by differentiating a momentum vector in cylindrical coordinates, derives E = mc ² from a force F = c d m /d t , and refers the reader to the author's own "Electromagnetism in the ether" in the proceedings of the 1999 conference Galileo Back in Italy II . The translation is rough enough that Italian words survive untranslated in several places, and etere appears throughout as "air". The abstract that previously stood here was the truncated import "Reprint of ?", which carried no information and has been removed.
