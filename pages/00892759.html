The stock market is essentially a non-linear, non- parametric, noisy, and deterministically chaotic system because of liquid money, stock adequacy, human behavior, news related to the stock market, gambling, international money rate, and so on. In a country like Bangladesh, it is very difficult to find any prediction of the stock market especially the Dhaka stock market. Because its trends and forecasting depend on various factors. Understanding the pattern of the stock market and predicting their development and changes are research hotspots in academic and financial circles. Because financial data contain complex, incomplete, and fuzzy information, predicting their development trends is an extremely difficult challenge. Fluctuations in financial data depend on a myriad of correlated constantly changing factors.
