Here is a timely and enjoyable odyssey across the United States by author Sharron Rose. Well known for her delightful book The Path of the Priestess , Sharron Rose takes us on a metaphysical tour of time, space, philosophy and spirituality. As she travels she interviews some of the most influential spiritual teachers of our time, including Jose Arguelles, Gregg Braden, John Major Jenkins, Rick Levine, Jay Weidner as well as many others. The big questions are concerned with the end of time as we know it, what is going to happen to us, and how these things are recorded in ancient prophecy. Sharron
