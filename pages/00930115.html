Analysis of COVID-19 Cases in India using SEIR, ARIMA and LSTM Models Souvik Sengupta Aliah University ssg@aliah.ac.in Abstract After one year from the start of the COVID-19 pandemic in India, the country is now having a steady decay in the number of daily new cases and active cases. Although the vaccination process is about to start from mid of January 2021, it would not affect the number of daily cases at least for the next three to four months for obvious reasons like phase-wise implementation and six to eight weeks time span required from the first dosage to develop the immunity. Therefore, the prime question is now, where would we reach at the end of the first quarter of 2021, and what could be the number of new cases and active cases before the start of vaccination immunity and any second wave (due to new strain).
