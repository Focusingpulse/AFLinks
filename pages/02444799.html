Chapter 15. The Therapeutic Process — Fundamentals of Therapy (1983)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 2,444,799
Our knowledge of the effects of therapeutic substances is based upon the understanding of the development of forces in the world outside man. For, in order to bring about a healing process, we must bring into the organism substances which will distribute themselves in it in such a way that the disease process gradually transforms itself to a normal one. It is the essential nature of a disease process that something is going on within the organism which does not integrate itself into its total activities.
