Glucose converts to fructose . Metabolism Metabolic reactions may be categorized as catabolic – the breaking down of compounds (for example, of glucose to pyruvate by cellular respiration ); or anabolic – the building up ( synthesis ) of compounds (such as proteins , carbohydrates , lipids , and nucleic acids ). Usually, catabolism releases energy , and anabolism consumes energy . [ Metabolism ] See Also fructose Fructokinase insulin gout sucrose sugar Uric Acid
