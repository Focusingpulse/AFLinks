So many greetings from all over the world have been received by Frank D. Higbee, director of the Twentieth Century Department of the Red Cross, that a magnificent souvenir book will be printed containing all these messages from noted men and women in every land. All the greetings are copyrighted. On March 2 the Red Cross will hold a monster benefit in this city, at which Miss Clara Barton will be present. It is understood that the twentieth century souvenir will be ready then. More greetings will be collected before that time. Many more were received yesterday. Here are some of them: Prayer by Pope Leo XIII—O God, grant to the American people at the beginning of this new century that they may love Thee above all things, that in pursuing their career they may always have Thy glory in sight and that under Thy protection they may always increase in strength and happiness. O God, grant wisdom and integrity to the officials of this nation and to all the bishops of the Church. O God, grant that this, Thy people, may be prosperous in all their enterprises; that they may overcome all enemies, both internal and external, and that they and the whole human family may repose in the blessings of perpetual peace. From Capt. Dreyfus. Capt. A. Dr
