What is Ozone? Simply, oxygen, three atoms of nature’s oxygen. It exists in a very active form for about 30 minutes before breaking down into two atoms of regular oxygen by giving up one atom of singlet oxygen. Where does Ozone come from? Nature. And nature does not make any mistakes. The new growth in the rainforests, and the plankton in the ocean are continually creating oxygen. As you read this, oxygen is rising up into the atmosphere to where the ozone layer is. At the altitude of the ozone layer, the rising oxygen is bombarded by the sun’s photochemical energy in the form of ultraviolet light rays. This UV energy bombardment changes the oxygen from O 2 -- two atoms of stable oxygen, into O 3 -- three atoms of unstable active oxygen. We call this Ozone. This is all part of the natural process of life on this living biosphere called earth. The chemical formula for this is 3O 2 >UV > 2O 3 Being heavier than the oxygen in the atmosphere ,this newly created Ozone falls back to earth and is immediately replaced by more rising oxygen which is also soon changed int ozone by the sun.
