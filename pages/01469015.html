Yakov Narkevich-Todka

· archived # 1,469,015
Yakov Narkevich-Todka (1847-1905), Jakob von Narkiewitsch-Jodko Belarusian scientists announced developed a "method for registration of energy emitted by a living organism when exposed to an electric field," which they have been called "electrography". Plate lights up blowing out direct discharges. They were given the task to register the process of absorption and emission of electricity by the body. Research in this direction, he devotes most of his scientific career.
