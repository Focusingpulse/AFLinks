EDITORIAL I see we have something for almost everyone in this issue–something, at least, for readers with any interest in ufology, psi phenomena, past-life memories, or ‘‘altered states of consciousness.’’ We are also adding something new. Carlos S. Alvarado has proposed that we feature a ‘‘Historical Perspective’’ in the journal, and I have enthusiastically accepted his proposal. As Carlos remarks in his introductory remarks, ‘‘one of the best ways to understand a discipline is through a study of its history.’’ Readers are encouraged to submit short essays or research articles on historical topics. For more information, see p. 395. As usual, no good deed goes unpunished, and there is a price for Carlos to pay: I have asked him (and happily he has agreed) to serve as Associate Editor for Historical Material. Now you have a venue for those sage reflections on the history of science and anomalies that you have so far been keeping to yourself. I take this opportunity to thank Dean Radin for taking over the duties of Editor-In-Chief for four weeks earlier this year, when I was (for not-very￾important-medical-reasons) hors de combat. Dean has shown that none of our editors (including the Editor-In-Chief) is irreplaceable. This was good news to this EIC. Welcome to Volume 22, Issue 3.
