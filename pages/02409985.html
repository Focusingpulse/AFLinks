Balasubramanian Centre for Advanced Studies in Earth Science University of Mysore Mysore-6 Objectives: You are all aware that Mining is a major economic sector of any country. Mining is a geotechnical industry. Geologist explore the economic mineral deposits for mining. A lot of methods are adopted to exploit the valuable economic mineral resources through open cast mining or underground mining methods. In all these methods, there is a need to dislodge the ore or mineral deposit from the main body of the rocks. Dislodging requires heavy energy application and machineries. To simplify the work, rock blasting is the easiest way. In mining operations, blasting has become a routine work. With proper understanding of the rocks and blasting methods, the mining work can be done effectively. The basic objectives of this report is to highlight the principles and practices adopted in rock blasting operations for mining sectors. 1 The aspects concentrated in this report are: a) Purpose and scope of blasting b) Steps involved in blasting c) Blast design and patterns d) Methods of blasting e) Controlling factors of Blasting. 1.0 Introduction: Most of the economic mineral deposits occur in association with massive hard rocks.
