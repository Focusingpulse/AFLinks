POSITION NAME NUMBER SYMBOL ATOMIC MASS MELTING POINT 0= Hydron 400= Hy. [ The Universal One , Page 94] "Of these elements two in the fourth octave, when discovered, will be of great use to man. Each of them will mark an epoch in human progress. Hydron (400=) the master tone of its octave , is a non-inflammable inert gas which is in every way superior for transportation to hydrogen because it is much lighter, absolutely non-injurious and easier to produce. It has all the safety qualities of helium and its carrying capacity exceeds that of helium by eight times and is double that of hydrogen . Both hydron and helium can be produced by transmutation in unlimited quantities at an expense which is negligible while helium , of a sufficient quantity to fill a dirigible , produced by the present methods now costs three quarters of a million dollars. The control of the production of this gas by any nation would insure the peace of the world." [ Russell , The Universal One , page 100] See Hydron
