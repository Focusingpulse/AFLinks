LEDs built into the tops of solar panels placed on highways could move lanes around, create crosswalks, display speed limits and even detect and warn drivers about road hazards like stopped traffic and crossing wildlife. Best of all, the panels could be laid down over existing asphalt. Though its beneficial to create solar roads but what about the cost. The infrastructure required to build and maintain them. The cost involved for maintaining asphalt road is much less compared to solar roads. Each panel cost approximately $7,000 to build. Also, the durability of the roads is questionable as the roads might have to bear the load of cars, trucks etc. and need some fixing working if accident takes place.
