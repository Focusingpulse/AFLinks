The person who reaches the bottom of the slope first, wins the cheese! There is a rugby team at the bottom to catch those who can't stop (most of them ). According to Wikipedia, "Cooper's Hill Cheese Rolling has been summarised as "twenty young men chase a cheese off a cliff and tumble 200 yards to the bottom, where they are scraped up by paramedics and packed off to hospital".
