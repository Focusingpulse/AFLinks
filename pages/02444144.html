Seaweed that tastes like bacon

· archived # 2,444,144
07/23/15 - Seaweed that tastes like bacon Scientists are researching a form of healthful seaweed which when fried, tastes remarkably like bacon, according to Grub Street. Dulse is "a strain of translucent-red seaweed that Oregon State researchers say tastes like bacon and packs twice the nutrients of kale," according to the report. Oregon State University's Hatfield Marine Science Center holds the patent for this particular strain of dulse, referred to as "baconweed," and scientists are researching the best way to use the product. After testing things like rice crackers and salad dressings that incorporated the dulse, they brought in a chef from the Nordic Food Lab. This is all well and good, but researcher Chris Langdon points out the true potential of this dulse strain: "When you fry it, which I have done, it tastes like bacon, not seaweed.
