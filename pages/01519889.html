Tabletop IEC devices, informally referred to as fusors, were first operated in the 1950s. (See "Fusors," by Richard Hull on pages 6-12.) More recently, conceptual design studies, undertaken at the Universities of Illinois and Wisconsin, explored the potential for IEC applied to large-scale power generation and space propulsion.
