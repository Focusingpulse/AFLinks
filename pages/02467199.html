So I did certain things that at the time seemed legitimate. I lit occasional campfires, for example. I had three supply drops parachuted from lowflying aircraft. I killed a rattlesnake. I slept inside an Anasazi cliff dwelling. I hope you will understand that because of today’s heavy travel in the Canyon by backpackers and river-runners such acts would now be neither legitimate nor legal.
