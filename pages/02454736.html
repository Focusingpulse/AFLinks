Strictly speaking, we should be talking about Relative Dielectric Constant (the ratio of the speed of signal in free space to the speed of the signal in a material), although almost everyone drops the "relative" aspect. The speed of light in outer space is virtually obstructed (zero loss) and clips right along at 186,282 miles per second just as it should. This known "constant" in physics is used to measure large distances by the time it takes for a pulse of light or radio waves to reach a target and return.
