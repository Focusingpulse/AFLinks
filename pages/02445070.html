08/24/13 - Australia drought caused drop in global sea levels A combination of weather patterns over the Indian and Pacific oceans funneled so much rain over Australia that the world's sea levels fell in 2011, scientists said. Unlike other continents, the soils and topography of Australia prevent almost all of its rain from running off into the ocean, causing dry areas of Australia to act like an enormous sponge.
