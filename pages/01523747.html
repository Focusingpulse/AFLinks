(18 March 1917 – 28 November 1996), was an English-born research physicist and former Royal Air Force officer who, after emigrating to the United States, worked as an experimental and theoretical physicist for the United States Navy . He is best remembered among independent-science circles for his lifelong study of classical electromagnetism in the tradition of James Clerk Maxwell , for a series of experiments probing fundamental questions such as the finite mass of the photon and the possible presence of neutrinos in the vacuum, and for his book Unified Physics (1988).
