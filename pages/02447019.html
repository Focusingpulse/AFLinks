Beginners find cross eyes produces a headache, so an easier method has been devised. Mirror assisted viewing (m stereo) requires one of the pictures to be flipped over. It returns to normal orientation when seen in a mirror. The normal image is looked at directly with one eye. The mirror is adjusted over the other eye until the two pictures superimpose.
