Randall Meyers Born 1955 (age 70–71) United States Residence Spoleto, Italy; Oslo, Norway Nationality USA Alma mater Guildhall School of Music and Drama; Hochschule für Musik und darstellende Kunst, Vienna; University of Western Sydney Known for Universe - The Cosmology Quest (2004); non-Big-Bang cosmology Scientific career Fields Composition and film music; astronomy and astrophysics Randall Bruce Meyers (born 1955) is an American composer, film-maker and astronomer who has lived and worked in Europe since 1970. He is known on this wiki as the director of Universe - The Cosmology Quest (2004), the first full-length documentary devoted to cosmologies that dispense with the Big Bang , and as a biographer of Halton Arp . He is unusual among the figures recorded here in having two full careers. In music he has written scores for more than a hundred short films and twenty-seven features, three opera librettos and works published by Chester Music and Wilhelm Hansen; in astronomy he took a master's degree in his forties and built one of the larger private observatories in Italy. Biography Education Guildhall School of Music and Drama , London — piano, composition and orchestration, 1970–1974 Hochschule für Musik und darstellende Kunst , Vienna — 1974–1978 M.A. in astronomy and astrophysics , University of Western Sydney — 2000–2004 ; his dissertation, A Brief History of Competing Ideologies in Cosmology and Evidence for Non-cosmological Red-shifts as a case for Alternative Theoretical Interpretations in Cosmology , was subsequently published as a book in Australia He also travelled through the Middle East and India in 1979–1980, studying the use of music in religious rites and Indian classical improvisation. The dates above are as given in Meyers's own biography. Some secondary sources give the Vienna years as 1975–1979.
