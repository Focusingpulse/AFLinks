In a recent discussion on the subject of lightning protection, Dr. Nikola Tesla of New York, brings out many interesting facts not generally known concerning the real efficacy of the ordinary lightning rod as installed on houses, barns and public buildings all over the world. Says, Dr. Tesla, "The efficacy of the ordinary lightning rod is to a certain degree unquestionably establisht thru statistical records, but there is generally a prevalent, nevertheless, a singular theoretical fallacy as to its operation, and its construction is radically defective in one feature, namely its typical pointed terminal." In his new form of lightning protecting rod and terminal here illustrated, Tesla avoids all such points on the metal parts facing skyward, and uses an entirely different form and arrangement of terminals. Nikola Tesla, Expert on High Frequency Currents, Such As Lightning Discharges, Has Recently Patented the New "Rounded" Form of Lightning Rod, Which He States is Superior to the Time-Honored "Pointed" Rods so Familiar to All of Us.
