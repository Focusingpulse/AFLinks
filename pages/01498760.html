Stakera,b a Department of Engineering, Loyola University Maryland, 4501 North Charles St, Baltimore, MD 21210, USA b American Patent Institute, 2817 Wesleyan Drive, Churchville, MD 21028, USA ARTICLE INFO Keywords: Superabundant vacancy structures High conductivity Nuclear active environment (NAE) Palladium – Isotopic hydrogen phases Delta δ and delta prime δ′ phases Low energy nuclear reactions (LENR) ABSTRACT The addition of three superabundant vacancy (SAV) phases, γ (Pd7VacD6–8), δ (Pd3VacD4 – octahedral), and δ′ (Pd3VacD4 – tetrahedral) to the palladium – isotopic hydrogen phase diagram was recently reported [1]. Also, in that study, production of excess heat from a nuclear source during electrolysis in heavy water indicated portions of the palladium (Pd) – deuterium (D) specimen were in the ordered δ phase, while a drop in resistance of the Pd during excess heat, with an increase in temperature, indicated portions of the specimen had shifted to the ordered δ′ phase. Both δ and δ′, create intersecting channels along the edges of the unit cells which are in effect long strings of Pd lattice vacancies for fast electron transport or a deuteron resonance condition. At high D/Pd ratio, the Pd-D alloy can be multiphase. An estimate of the volume fraction (fv) of δ phase is made from the amount of nuclear energy measured. An estimate of fv of δ′ is made from the measurement of the change in resistivity of the overall multiphase Pd-D alloy using the rule of mixtures.
