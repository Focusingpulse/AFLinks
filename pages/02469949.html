The opinion grants an injunction against the Weaving company, although it reserves decision as to whether the defendant may be permitted temporarily to use the motors which it now has installed. The opinion of the court in full is as follows: This is a petition for a preliminary injunction against infringement of patents No. 381,968, No. 382,279 and No. 382,280, granted May 1, 1888, to Nikola Tesla. These patents have been sustained, after full consideration, by the Circuit Court of the United States for the District of Connecticut in a suit against the New England Granite Company et al , 103 F. R., 951; and by the Circuit Court of Appeals for the Second Circuit, in the same case, 110 F. R. 753. The defendant cites the language of the Supreme Court of the United States in Mast, Foos & Co. vs. Stover Mfg. Co., 177 U.
