TITLE: Transition Evolution Theory: Interdimensional Energy Transfer via Compact Objects in Kerr Geometry as a Source of K-Essence Cosmology and Relic Tachyonic Fluid AUTHORS: Paweł Maleszewski (mara2327) CONTACT: pawelmal84@gmail.com KEYWORDS: Cyclic cosmology, tachyons, Kerr metric, k-essence, dark matter, cosmic microwave background, superluminal relativity, Transition Evolution Theory. ABSTRACT: This paper presents an extension of an independent cosmological hypothesis designated as the Transition Evolution Theory (TET). It proposes a multiverse model wherein universes with differing physical constants are interconnected via stable spacetime tunnels within Kerr geometry. We demonstrate that a photon emitted within a parent universe possessing a higher value of the speed of light (c_parent) manifests in our universe as a superluminal particle (a tachyon) upon traversing the ring singularity of a Kerr black hole. Utilizing the framework of superluminal extended relativity, we eliminate the paradox of infinite momentum. Furthermore, we model the energy loss process of tachyons using the k-essence formalism, demonstrating that the relic tachyonic field condenses into a stable, pressureless fluid with finite energy density, thereby providing a physical explanation for the nature of dark matter. --- 1.
