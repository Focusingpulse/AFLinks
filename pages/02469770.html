Mr. Tesla pointed out a simple method of producing alternating currents of great frequency based upon the properties of induction coils and condensers. Fig. 1. - Diagram of the arrangement employed for M. Radiguet's Experiments. A, beginning of the sector; B, circuit breaker; C, bipolar interrupter; D, amperemeter; E, potential reducer; F, vibrator; G, induction coil; H, the D'Arsonval arrangement; K, L, contacts upon the resonator; I, resonator; M, handwheel. The secondary terminals of a high tension coil are connected with the internal armatures of a Leyden jar which discharges itself disruptively. This disruptive discharge produces alternating currents of great frequency. This process presents a great interest, in that it permits of resuming and completing Tesla's experiments without having recourse to special apparatus that few scientists have at their disposal.
