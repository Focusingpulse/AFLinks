Lecture Seven - GA 170. The Riddle of Humanity

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 12 August 1916 · archived # 2,423,305
, Dornach Translated by John F. Logan Lecture Seven [ 1 ] When we speak of the great world and the small world, of the macrocosm and the microcosm, we are referring to the whole universe and to the human being. Goethe, for example, spoke in these terms in Faust . He called the whole cosmos ‘the great world’, and the human being ‘the small world.’ We have already had many occasions to observe how manifold and complicated are the relationships between man and the cosmos.
