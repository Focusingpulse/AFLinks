After an accidental meeting with an ear, nose and throat specialist at a concert, the singer learned of the biology behind his record breaking voice. "He said that my vocal chords were about twice as long as normal - than he's used to seeing anyway - and the arytenoid muscles around my vocal chords, they had a lot more movement to them," Storms said.
