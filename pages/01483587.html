spin direction

· archived # 1,483,587
" Electricity spins around all centers in this whole universe in one direction only. C. represents this universal direction of electric spinning around the same series of bar magnets . That one direction is clockwise , or anticlockwise , according to the position of the observer. If you look at the spiral turning from one end, in the direction of the other end, you will see a clockwise direction . If you look at it by reversing your position you see it anticlockwise . The direction of turning does not change because you change. You cannot pick out one half of a cycle, however, and reverse it as indicated in D."
