The curves shown in these figures are normalized to the secondary ion intensity of Pd obtained in each analysis run to carry out the discussion on the concentrations on the same elements. Evidently from the figures, the depth profiles of the elements with no nuclear effects are monotonous as expected from the electrochemical point of view.
