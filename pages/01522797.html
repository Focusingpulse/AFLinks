Carpenter Born ( 1923-07-24 ) July 24, 1923 Died October 3, 2002 (2002-10-03) (aged79) Residence Colorado Springs, CO, United States Nationality USA Knownfor Quantum Theory , Paranormal Donald Gilbert Carpenter (24 July 1923 3 October 2002) was an American physicist and engineer who worked on space physics for the United States Air Force and later taught electrical engineering at Colorado Tech in Colorado Springs. He is remembered outside physics as a co-author of the chapter on unidentified flying objects in the US Air Force Academy's 1968 textbook Introductory Space Science , and within the Natural Philosophy Alliance for a series of papers proposing an atomic mechanism for a "tired light" cosmology.
