The different kinds of conscious awareness come to expression in the seven planets Saturn, Sun, Moon, Earth, Jupiter, Venus and Vulcan. Seven worlds of life are gone through on each planet, and each of these in turn through seven states of form. [ 3 ] Our physical Earth is such a form state, the fourth form state or globe in the fourth life world of the fourth planet or state of consciousness.
