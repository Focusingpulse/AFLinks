Man, Woman, and Child in the Light of Spiritual Science Notes from a participant [ 1 ] Spiritual science is not merely a means of satisfying curiosity, or, to put it more nobly, a means of satisfying the drive for research. It is also intended to give people an impulse for living and acting. After all, a sense of security and fulfillment in life, as well as competence in our actions and in accomplishing our tasks, should flow from what the humanities offer us.
