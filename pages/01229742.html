Section 1.1: The Universe as a Computational Process: From Rechnender Raum to a Universal Hash The proposition that the universe is fundamentally computational is a speculative but recurring theme in modern physics and philosophy. The intellectual lineage of this idea can be traced back to the pioneering work of computer scientist Konrad Zuse.
