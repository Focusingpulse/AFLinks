INE Symposium for New Energy Event Type Conference Dates 26 October 2001 – 27 October 2001 Venue Quality Inn City Center Location Salt Lake City, UT Country United States Participants 17 Papers 25 Website Event website INE Symposium for New Energy was a conference held 26 October 2001 – 27 October 2001 in Quality Inn City Center, Salt Lake City, UT, United States. The INE 2001 Symposium For New Energy is jointly sponsored by the Institute for New Energy and EEMF (the Emerging Energy Marketing Firm). Details of the conference have been announced in the September issue of NEN. A Preliminary Program will be announced in the October NEN, due to emailed during the first week of October.
