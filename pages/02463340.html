It develops the Aether Physics Model (APM) 1 PURPOSE AND VERSION 2.0 SCOPE 1 Abstract This Version 2.0 reformulates the calculations of the Aether Physics Model (APM) Unified Force Theory in native Quantum Measurement Units (QMU). The paper separates physical ontology from coefficient bookkeeping and distinguishes the linear Ligamen Circulatus mass structure from distributed magnetic charge. Magnetic charge is treated as the full loxodromic scan of matter angular momentum through Aether conductance during a quantum moment, not as a detached point charge and not as a product of two independently localized magnetic charges. The gravitational, magnetic, and electrostatic coefficients are shown to factor from the same Aether force scale, Gforce, while retaining different geometric participation. The electron magnetic-force scale at one quantum length is derived as FM = Au eemax 2 /λC 2 = phtn/λC 2 = me λC Fq 2 . The Casimir relation is then used as a discriminating comparison: the APM predicts the coefficient 1/(16π 2 ) directly from Aether geometry, whereas the conventional ideal-plate Casimir coefficient is π/480 in the phtn convention. No additional residual factor is introduced to force the two predictions to agree. Version 2.0 also corrects the obsolete statement in Version 1.0 that the bound electron–proton magnetic orientation in the neutron is repulsive: in the present APM description the bound magnetic orientation is attractive, as is the electrostatic orientation.
