This approach allowed to correct and amend the field equation system, correct calculation of kinetic energy density and quantity of the motion of a charge, interaction force of the charges in motion, show the electric nature of gravitation and describe longitudinal electrostrictive waves, i.e. to set up a basic unified field theory as well as to define the mass of neutrino. Neutrino (graviton) mass and radius were calculated. Overview A 34-page booklet issued in St. Petersburg in 1999 by the National Security Academy , in an edition of 1,000 copies, by Anatoly A Denisov — Doctor of Technical Sciences and professor in the faculty of Technical Cybernetics at St. Petersburg State Polytechnic University. It is the electromagnetic half of a pair, its companion being Bases of Gravitation of the same year. Its governing claim is stated in one line: only the electric field is real . Magnetism, and everything else in the field description, are artefacts — distortions in the information a charge receives about another charge, produced by the medium between them and by their relative motion.
