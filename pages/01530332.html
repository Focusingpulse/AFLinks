In gen￾eral, two approaches have been employed. One approach was to indirectly detect charged particles by measuring Pd K-shell X-rays that should be created as charged particles traverse through the Pd lattice. The other approach utilized CR-39, a solid state nuclear track detector (SSNTD). With these detectors, a charged particle creates an ionization trail in the plastic that, upon etching, leaves a symmetric pit. The size, depth of penetration, and shape of the pits provides information about the mass, charge, energy, and direction of motion of the particles.
