Western Connecticut State University Operation Popeye ...and Weather Modification As a Weapon of War A Thesis Submitted to the Faculty of the History Department in Candidacy for the Degree of Master of Arts By Bernard George Kokinchak Danbury, Connecticut January, 2014 I Dedicated To: To all people that helped through this process. Your help I will never fully be able to be thank you for. Abstract This essay is about weather modification. The essay covers time period from end World War Two until sign of treaty Convention on the Prohibition of Military or Any Other Hostile Use of Environmental modification Techniques. The change would occur in the society over such time in the United States. The use of weather modification during the Second Indochina War would cause a series of events to unfold to cause change in world thinking about environmental change. Therefore this paper traces thechange that occurred over this time period.
