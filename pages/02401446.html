GC2026-SGG022 Instrumental Complex of the Baksan Temperature Antenna Valentin Gravirov1 , Dmitrii Likhodeev1 1 O.Yu. Schmidt Institute of Physics of the Earth, Russian Academy of Sciences vvg@ifz.ru Recently, a significant amount of tasks has been carried out at the Caucasian Underground Geophysical Laboratory, located in the far-reaching tunnel of the Baksan Neutrino Observatory of the Institute of Nuclear Research of the Russian academy of sciences, aimed at further improving the geophysical instrumentation installed there. The laboratory is located in the heart of Mount Andyrchi, approximately 25 kilometers away from Mount Elbrus, making it a truly unique scientific infrastructure facility of global significance for conducting geophysical research. The laboratory's instrumentation is not only characterized by its outstanding performance but also offers extensive capabilities for comprehensive analysis of various geodynamic processes [1]. One of the most interesting research areas that requires special attention is the study of the rock’s temperature regime in the vicinity of Mount Elbrus. This region, with its complex geological structure, is of great interest to scientists working in the fields of volcanology and geothermal energy. To address this important task, an electronic multi-channel precision temperature antenna has been developed [2], which is characterized by its high sensitivity and resolution.
