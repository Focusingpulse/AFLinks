Title On the Field Equations Governing the Origin and Evolution of Compactified Hyper-dimensional-energy Universes Read in full Read the full paper (archived) Author(s) Robert E Var Keywords cosmology, gravity, special relativity , electromagnetic field, general relativity , extra dimensions Published 2003 Journal Unpublished No. of pages 72 "On the Field Equations Governing the Origin and Evolution of Compactified Hyper-dimensional-energy Universes" is a 72-page unpublished paper of 2003 by Robert E Var , setting out what he calls hyper-energy theory . It is the most developed statement of a programme he began with his "space-energy theory" in Foundations of Physics in 1975. Summary Var proposes that space is not empty but a physical medium carrying an enormous intrinsic energy density, and that gravity is — following a gravitation theorem he attributes to Maxwell (1864) — a local reduction of that energy density caused by the presence of mass. From this single idea he builds a sequence of three increasingly general gauge-field theories: a velocity-dependent "w-gauge" theory, which he argues reproduces special relativity while reinterpreting gravity as the flow of the space-medium; an "Einstein–Maxwell" tensor theory, which he presents as a quantizable version of gravity harmonized with special relativity and offered as a solution to the quantum-gravity problem; and hyper-energy theory itself, a many-dimensional extension in which the universe is a compressible, inviscid medium of mass-energy with extra "compactified" dimensions sourced by the flow of time. He then sketches how the theory might account for big-bang -driven creation, cosmic inflation and Hubble expansion, the generation of the elementary-particle spectrum through symmetry breaking, and the physical nature of time and of superstrings.
