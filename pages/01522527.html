Coyne targets the cosmic microwave background, the observation most often cited in the theory's support. On the standard account the 2.7K background is relic radiation from photon decoupling some 380,000 years after the Big Bang. He argues that a microwave background can be accounted for without that scenario, and that the COBE spectrum and isotropy results are read as confirmation only because decoupling is assumed at the outset.
