What is Charge? and What is Mass? Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Speaker James Arathoon Date 26 October 2024 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording What is Charge? and What is Mass? was a live session presented by James Arathoon on 26 October 2024 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. The talk will highlight the key conceptual differences between Newton’s Law of Gravitation and Coulomb’s Law and demonstrate, using the Ritzian Aether toy model, that the fundamental underlying physical phenomenon resulting in Newton’s Law of Gravitation is a 1/R type effect, and not a 1/R^2 effect, as it is with Coulomb’s Law and Poisson’s Equation. Hosted by Ian Cowan. Summary James Arathoon presented this as a work in progress and said so repeatedly. The two concepts he has struggled with since he was a student are the two that physics disposes of in the opening minutes of the first lecture and never returns to: mass and charge. They appear together, and the force between two charges vastly exceeds the force between two masses; and one cannot explain mass with a formula that already contains mass, so making progress means finding something underneath. Ian J Cowan hosted. The approach He set out his commitments first. The unity of nature — not separate domains of gravitation, electricity and quantum field theory with inconsistent concepts between them. Realism, with unobservables admitted but kept to a minimum and justified by the structure they support; mainstream physics has unobservables everywhere. And a balance of belief and scepticism, since one must commit enough work to a bet to find out whether it pays, but not fifty years, as string theory has had. His methodological point about dimensional analysis is the one that drives the rest.
