Description of the theory and application to power-generation of Paramahamsa Tewari. Mass, Charge, and Gravity, from The Space-Vortex Structure of the Electron with Toby Grotz. Summary Toby Grotz 's account of Paramahamsa Tewari 's Space Vortex Theory and of the machines Tewari built from it. Ian J Cowan hosted, with Franklin Hu playing the videos; Grotz's PowerPoint could not be driven from his own machine, so Cowan advanced the slides on request throughout. Grotz framed the talk as a request for help rather than an exposition. He is an engineer, not a theoretician, has known Tewari's work since a correspondence begun about 1992 and seven visits to India from 1993 onward, and asked the audience — repeatedly — to go through the mathematics and tell him whether it holds.
