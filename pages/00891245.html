Generalized Quantum Evidence Theory on Interference Effect ⋆ Fuyuan Xiao School of Big Data and Software Engineering, Chongqing University, Chongqing 400044, China doctorfyxiao@hotmail.com Abstract. In this paper, CET is generalized to quantum framework of Hilbert space in an open world, called generalized quantum evidence the￾ory (GQET). Differ with classical GET, interference effects are involved in GQET. Especially, when a GQBBA turns into a classical GBBA, in￾terference effects disappear, so that GQB and GQP functions of GQET degenerate to classical GBel and GPl functions of classical GET, respec￾tively.
