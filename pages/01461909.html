Statement of Authorship and Lineage My work is grounded in the historical science of Sympathetic Vibratory Physics (SVP) as developed by John W. Keely and advanced through the documented contributions of Clara Bloomfield-Moore , Macvicar , Dougald Carmichael Ramsay , Walter Russell , Schauberger , and related investigators. I do not claim to have invented SVP. My role has been, since 1984, to research, reconstruct, test, extend, and modernize this body of work through original analysis, physical builds, mathematical reinterpretation, and public documentation. Any contemporary use of the term Sympathetic Vibratory Physics that implies sole authorship, origin, or “fatherhood” independent of this historical lineage is incorrect. This body of work has a clear provenance, and my contributions are offered in continuity with it — not in replacement of it. For clarity: I do not authorize, endorse, or recognize alternative claims of authorship over SVP or representations made on my behalf. — Dale Pond
