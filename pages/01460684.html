Maxwellian electron energy distribution

· archived # 1,460,684
Plasma theories such as magnetohydrodynamics may assume the particles to be in thermodynamic equilibrium . In this case, the distribution function is Maxwellian. This distribution function allows fluid flow and different temperatures in the directions parallel to, and perpendicular to, the local magnetic field . More complex distribution functions may also be used since plasmas are rarely in thermal equilibrium . Wikipedia, Distribution Function See Also Atomic Cluster Charge build-up Atomic Clusters
