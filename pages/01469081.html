action at a distance

· archived # 1,469,081
Action at a distance is seen in radionics , clairvoyance , telekinesis and all other mental phenomena - the basis of which is sympathetic vibration and sympathetic oscillation on the etheric and interetheric subdivisions . As mind is an interetheric substance whose velocity of propagation of a disturbance is near infinite such actions and near instant reactions are not strange. [See scalar , entanglement , Mind to Mind ] Nikola Tesla “It is necessary for us to assume a universal medium for the transmission of force. This results from the necessity of explaining the action of bodies at a distance . Energy transmitted from the sun to the earth through some very attenuated medium. To show how such medium may be used under certain conditions I may refer to a feat I once accomplished of converting a column of gas into a solid body by the application of electricity in a certain manner. The gas became rigid and vibrated like a steel wire. Scientific men have not noticed the importance of this as yet. Again, I have produced a solid body in the air on the end of a wire. I made a flame which consumed nothing and which showed signs of solidification, for it offered resistance to the passage of bodies through it.” [ Nikola Tesla . “Tesla’s Visit To Chicago.” Western Electrician, May 20, 1899.] See Also 9.11 - Love or Sympathy is Perfect Continuity Action Connecting Link continuity Controller of all action Quantum Entanglement Etheric Elements Gravity Knowing Love Mesmerism Mind Mind Forc
