Maki [6] introduced the notion of a -set and a V -set in a topological space and studied the associated closure operators. Ganster and Reilly [4] utilized locally closed subsets to prove a new decomposition of continuity. In [1], Arenas, Dontchev and Ganster considered -sets to obtain some other decompositions of continuity. Since then, numerous other authors have used these concepts in their work.
