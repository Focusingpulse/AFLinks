Handbook of advanced radioactive waste conditioning technologies © Woodhead Publishing Limited, 2011 Related titles: Geological repository systems for safe disposal of spent nuclear fuels and radioactive waste (ISBN 978-1-84569-542-2) The long-term fate of spent nuclear fuel and radioactive waste materials is of critical importance to the nuclear industry. Long-term safety must be assured without active human oversight, based on the requirement that we do not pass the burden of nuclear waste onto future generations. Geological disposal systems and technology, utilising both natural geological barriers and engineered barrier systems, have therefore been developed to isolate spent nuclear fuel and radioactive materials from the human environment. This book critically reviews state-of-the-art technologies, scientific methods and engineering practices directly related to the design, operation and safety of geological repositories. Advanced separation techniques for nuclear fuel reprocessing and radioactive waste treatment (ISBN 978-1-84569-501-9) Advanced separations technology is key to ensuring the environmental protection, criticality-safety and non-proliferation (security) of radioactive materials. Separation techniques are applied in reprocessing nuclear fuels for further power generation as well as in radioactive waste treatment for radiological-content reduction of materials due for storage and disposal. Initial sections review materials management issues and the characterisation and behaviour of the main radioactive materials produced by nuclear power generation.
