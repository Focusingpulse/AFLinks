Log in I forgot my password Sympathetic Vibratory Physics &raquo; Ottmar Mergenthaler Page actions PDF Print Share Send link Ottmar Mergenthaler ( click to enlarge ) Nineteenth century Baltimore immigrant and watchmaker Ottmar Mergenthaler may have been responsible for a boom in reading not seen since Johannes Gutenberg essentially invented the printing press in the 15th century. Mergenthaler’s invention of the Linotype, the first typewriter-like typesetting machine, replaced the previously used manual setting of type so efficiently that in the first decade of its use, American newspaper readership grew from 3.6 million to more than 33 million. Ever-practical and efficient, Mergenthaler didn’t waste a lot of time coming up with a brand name for his invention. According to Mergenthaler’s son Herman, “When Tribune publisher Whitelaw Reid saw Ottmar type on the keyboard and shortly after witnessed a thin metal slug bearing several words slide down into a tray, he exclaimed, ‘Ottmar, you’ve done it! A line o’ type!’ Thus the Linotype machine was born". Created by Dale Pond. Last Modification: Saturday August 23, 2025 18:35:29 MDT by Dale Pond.
