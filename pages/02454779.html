I don't know about that phasing business with utility power, its not impossible to do but I agree with the guy from Florida who sent me an email saying they require a 10 million dollar insurance policy for co-generators and some kind of special switch that will remove the power from the line if their lineman have to work on the power lines. I can't see any sense in trying to drive the load from the vagaries of the wind...have to have constant reliable power, that's why something a bit more than a trickle charge to keep a battery network charged would be the way to go.
