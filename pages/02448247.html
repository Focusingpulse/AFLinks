The scientist (can't recall his name but I want to eventually get and post the full details of his experiment) wants to use science to prove that the Bible claims are correct. He is of the opinion that the atmosphere long ago was at least 35% oxygen. Today, depending on where you measure it, it runs from about 18% up to about 25%. The experiment used a batch of copperhead snakes. One group were placed in a wire cage and allowed to live and grow breathing normal air. The other group were placed in a hyperbaric chamber that contained an enriched oxygen environment of 35% oxygen and regular air. After 6 months, the open air snakes were still as mean as before. When their venom was looked at under a microscope, the DNA was found to be twisted, knotted and gnarled, it was also highly poisonous. The snakes who lived and grew in the oxygen rich environment of the hyperbaric chamber were docile and could be handled with no problems. The DNA in their venom was completely without knots and could be laid out straight without twisting up. It also had the toxicity of spit. I always thought the story was fascinating...now that I think of it...wonder if the number of base pairs per twist in the DNA was greater?
