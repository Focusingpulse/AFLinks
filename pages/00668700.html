Introduction In our seminal work [1], we introduced the method of *circles of partition* (CoP), a novel approach grounded in a combinatorial structure that encodes specific addi￾tive properties of subsets of integers. This structure is equipped with a geometric interpretation, whereby the elements are viewed as points in the plane, with their weights corresponding to elements of the underlying subset. Formally, we define the set of points as: C(n, M) = {[x] | x, n − x ∈ M} .
