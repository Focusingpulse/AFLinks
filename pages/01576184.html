Emergent Spacetime and Mass Hierarchies in a Topological Quantum Graph Brian Thompson Independent Researcher Draft (2026) ABSTRACT We explore a model of emergent quantum gravity where macroscopic spacetime arises from an underlying fluid network of quantum graphity. To address non-invertibility and continuous parameter degeneracies in continuous gauge formulations, we introduce an integer-quantized U(1)3 Chern-Simons action with a non-singular K-matrix (det(K) = 2), providing topological vacuum stability on closed 3-manifolds. Spatial interface mismatches resulting from multi-scale polyhedral tessellations are incorporated natively through discrete Regge calculus, mapping volume scale discrepancies directly to localized deficit angle curvature tensors. To account for matter generation without imposing arbitrary Yukawa couplings, we propose an effective ansatz where fermion mass hierarchies are conditioned on the quadratic Casimir invariants of topological knot representations embedded in local graph cells. Finally, we discuss how coupling rest masses to a decaying dilaton field restored to an infrared fixed point provides a classical mechanism for trace-free stress-energy at late cosmological times. 1.
