Hi Guys! These synchronicities were to be addressed in a newsgroup I was invited to join when it was forming....at first it sounded totally cool because I hoped some of the readers would be amenable to experiments that would attempt to bias reality to PRODUCE synchronicities...however, it devolved to endless accounts of personal experiences...now don't get me wrong, these are important and the best prove is EXPERIENTIAL proof...but why be like cats chasing a bright light shined on a wall? There were only two people out of the group, never knew how many since it was a newsgroup...the group is still around and I drop in periodically, but like all newsgroups, it just faded...try; alt.psychology.synchronicity and you'll see what I mean. The person who started it asked for people who would be interested in posting or reading about it...so I with about 30 others made a case which led to its creation....but it went 'the way of newsgroups'...flames and tepid, non informative posts....just so. -- Jerry W.
