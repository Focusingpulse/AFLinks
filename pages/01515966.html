GHK-Cu Peptide Rejuvenation Patents -- Synthesis and applicationns rexresearch GHK-Cu Peptide Rejuvenation CN120504718 -- Synthesis method of GHK-CU [ Translation ] The invention belongs to the technical field of polypeptide synthesis, and particularly relates to a synthesis method of GHK-CU. According to the synthesis method of the GHK-CU (blue copper peptide), the raw materials are pretreated, the reaction activity of the raw materials is improved, the reaction system is improved, the specially-made functionalized ionic liquid is added into the reaction system, and the selectivity and efficiency of the reaction can be remarkably improved by adding the ionic liquid; according to the method, the reaction is promoted by means of microwaves, ultrasonic waves and the like during the reaction, and local overheating or side reaction is avoided, so that the yield and the purity of the product are improved, and the synthesized blue copper peptide has very good effects of tightening, wrinkle resistance, wound healing and the like, and has very good application value. CN115124593 -- Synthesis method of high-purity GHK-Cu [ Translation ] The invention relates to a synthesis method of high-purity GHK-Cu, which comprises the following steps: S1, adding a copper salt into a solution of a GHK product, and uniformly stirring to obtain a mixed solution, in which the GHK product contains by-product anions; s2, adding the mixed solution into anion exchange resin to react, and then filtering to obtain a reacted mixed solution; s3, concentrating the reacted mixed solution, adding an ethanol aqueous solution, heating to 40-70 DEG C, and carrying out ultrasonic dissolution to obtain a redissolved solution; and S4, the redissolved solution is cooled, and the GHK-Cu crystal is obtained. The GHK-Cu product obtained through the method is high in purity, high in yield and convenient to crystallize, and meanwhile the method is easy to operate, low in cost and high in efficiency.
