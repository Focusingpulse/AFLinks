Because health of the person depends on quality of drinking water, it is a lot of researches and inventions is devoted to this subject. In this work effect of the water structured by means of the special device — “The Tube Martsinyshyn”, on a power component of an organism of students was investigated. Research was conducted with application of a method of an aurografiya and the mathematical analysis of the received results of measurement of parameters of aura. The method allows to define the general level of energy of an organism, a condition of aura, to define dynamics of change of measured parameters under the influence of studied factors, and also to do the conclusion about influence of experiment on level of energy resources of an organism of students and on a state of health in whole.
