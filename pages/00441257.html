The fracture and the existential relation Vincenzo Peluso1 Present address: Via don Bosco, 11 00045 Genzano di Roma, Rome Italy August 6, 2024 ABSTRACT The fundamental element of everything, of nature as well as life, is an existential relationship here called Intention. It is the culmination and fulfilment of the natural sciences and philosophy, and their foundation. This research investigates intention and its three ingredients: the original I, being and the called I. Key words.
