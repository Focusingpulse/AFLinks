Roy Keys Inc.), Montreal Pages 425 ISBN 9781987980271 Wilhelm Weber's Main Works on Electrodynamics Translated into English, Volume III: Measurement of Weber's Constant c, Diamagnetism, the Telegraph Equation and the Propagation of Electric Waves at Light Velocity is the third volume of the five-volume edition of Wilhelm Weber 's electrodynamic works edited and translated by Andre K T Assis , published by Apeiron in 2021. Its centrepiece is the 1856 experiment of Weber and Kohlrausch , which measured the constant c appearing in Weber's force law and found it close to the speed of light — a result obtained before Maxwell's electromagnetic theory of light and independent of it. The volume contains, in translation and with editorial introductions: [Weber, 1852] Electrodynamic Measurements, Third Memoir , relating specially to diamagnetism [Weber, 1852] On the connexion of diamagnetism with magnetism and electricity [Weber, 1855] On the theory of diamagnetism — letter to Professor Tyndall [Weber and Kohlrausch, 1856] On the amount of electricity which flows through the cross-section of the circuit in galvanic currents — the measurement of the constant c [Kohlrausch and Weber, 1857] Electrodynamic Measurements, Fourth Memoir [Kirchhoff, 1857] On the motion of electricity in wires, and On the motion of electricity in conductors — the papers containing the telegraph equation [Poggendorff, 1857] Comment on the paper by Professor Kirchhoff [Weber, 1858] Report on experiments made at the Physics Institute in Göttingen [Weber, 1861] On the intended introduction of a galvanic resistance standard [Weber, 1864] Electrodynamic Measurements, Fifth Memoir , relating specially to electric oscillations Publication Published by Apeiron (C.
