Mr. Tesla supplements his previous communications to this journal with further remarks on his very interesting and valuable scientific work. The results achieved by Professor Enrico Salvioni, of Perugia, Italy, are chronicled by a well-known newspaper man who interviewed him for the Electrical Review . Messrs. Scribner and McBerty, of Chicago, send us an important communication on the source of the X rays, on which Prof. Elihu Thomson makes some very interesting comments, and at the same time refers to his own work in a similar direction. Altogether, the past week has been a very interesting one as far as the X rays are concerned, and as usual the Electrical Review presents the latest thought and achievement of the brightest minds that are at work on this very interesting topic. Our readers will appreciate the fact that while the real source and nature of the X ray are still a matter of speculation and doubt, important practical results have already been achieved in the development of Salvioni's cryptoscope and Edison's fluoroscope.
