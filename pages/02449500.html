You can access the ET website at www.teleport.com/~etimes A friend called the manufacturer of the motor to speak with the R&D director about Newman's claims. He had to ponder for a minute before recalling Neman's name, but when he did he said that they had looked at his motor several years ago but didn't find anything significant. He emphasized their motor design was quite different from what Newman developed. He was quite clear that there was absolutely no lawsuit or other legal action from Newman, which is contrary to what the Newman camp states. This manufacturer's own tests show a motor with an efficiency in the eight % range (if I remember correctly), but definitely not O-U. Newman demo'd this motor at the ANE conference last year, and from his math it wasn't O-U either.
