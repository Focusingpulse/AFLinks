Speculation on expanding Bedini technology

· archived # 1,514,028
I mentioned in the new users section my newtons cradle idea, and one of my findings was to think of magnetism like flashlight beams when dealing with 2 magnets and facing like poles at each other(North to North). The thing is, the beams are incompatible and repel each other. I further found that I could take a small magnet and move a much larger magnet with what appears to be much less mechanical force than if I tried to move the larger magnet with just my hand. This seems to be the same concept as the Tesla quote from the SG Intermediate Handbook: "Whatever electricity may be, it is a fact that it behaves like an incompressible fluid and the earth may be looked upon as an immense reservoir of electricity." The handbook goes on to illustrate how the Bedini circuit resembles the Hydraulic Ram Pump concept and how you might knock down a sheet rock wall if you only had a constant one pound of force distributed over one square foot of the wall... I have been pondering the thought of expanding the SG concept to exploit my observation of using a small magnet to move a larger magnet, PLUS some weight above it in order to do some extra work... The concept in my mind is to have the SG setup on a wheel as usual, but instead of discharging the capacitor into a battery, have it discharge into an external coil (in pulses of course)... The coil would be facing up, with a neo magnet above it so that like poles will face each other, and when the external coil fires it will cause a pumping action that could be used for some other kind of work. In a multi winding setup which you should be using, you could still still be charging your secondary battery bank, and re-route some of the voltage spike outputs to be the input to some other circuit.
