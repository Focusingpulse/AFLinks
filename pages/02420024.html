, Leipzig Translated by Anna Meuss 21. Who are the Rosicrucians? [ 1 ] The name ‘Rosicrucian’ has an indefinite, vague air for anyone who studies the theosophical literature, as if there were a secret behind it. Many consider it to be a term for people who involved themselves in possible and impossible magic in the 18th century.
