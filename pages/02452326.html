First of all I must state that the research on the MRA was openly discussed on the Keelynet BBS and covered a period of nearly a year with hundreds of messages and tech bulletins. I will not go back and re-write the book on the MRA. Please send me your address (mail) so I can send you a floppy that may help. All that you suggested has been covered in the research. Greg found that silver mica caps substituted nicely for the piezo element in a very low power version of the MRA. The ones we used were built for Branson Ultrasonic welders 32KHZ, 250V. Poling of piezo elements seem to be the problem as to suitability. I wish you had been on-board back in 1994 for this is too difficult to reconstruct to a level that you would understand.
