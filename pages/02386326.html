1995.07.24 / Dieter Britz / Blabblio update 24-Jul-95 Originally-From: britz@kemi.aau.dk (Dieter Britz) Newsgroups: sci.physics.fusion Subject: Blabblio update 24-Jul-95 Date: Mon, 24 Jul 1995 14:36:15 GMT Organization: Sci.physics.fusion/Mail Gateway Starry droogs, Two more, er, cold fusion papers plus two peripherals that I think are more interesting. The two CNF papers are not really, but both refer to CNF and are clearly motivated by it. Alekseev et al actually did a self targeting job, but talk about skewed tritium/neutron ratios in these metals. Hm, they don't say whether they actually measured neutrons. The Indian corrosion lot applied their craft to Pd's behaviour, and it's corrosion lore, with such phrases as open circuit potential (which normally gives you a hint about corrosion rate), galvanic current etc. Well, why not? The possible role of Li in CNF has been much discussed, and Yamazaki et al try to see whether Li can indeed be codeposited with hydrogen and enter the Pd bulk. There is now a fair amount of evidence that it can, and these people find rather high levels, despite the low overpotentials applied to the Pd. Looking at the Li % vs depth, and visually doing what the authors did, i.e. fitting a Cottrell diffusion profile to the curve (taking surface leakage into account), I'd say that their 7% figure is even a bit low, it looks more than about 10% right at the surface, before leakage.
