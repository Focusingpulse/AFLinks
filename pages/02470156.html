Dr. Arthur F. Payne , psychologist and psychoanalyst — I have a great admiration for Dr. Tesla and his wonderful work. But I don’t think he is right. The fact is that, as age comes on, even if the body continues to function, the mind gradually retrogrades. Fads in foods do not help, for eventually we all die of heart disease when the heart stops. It is true, however, that the normal person sleeps too much. If we are emotionally adjusted, we do not need more than five hours a day. For the emotionally unadjusted, sleep of eight, nine and ten hours a day is merely an escape. Brain Almost Perpetual Motion Dr. Richard H.
