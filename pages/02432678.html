(7) Law of Attraction "Juxtaposed coherent aggregates vibrating in unison, or harmonic ratio, are mutually attracted." That's J.W. Keely's 7th of 40 laws of harmonics, the Law of Attraction. Reading this forum sometimes though, you could be excused for thinking the LOA is a magical money making machine. WRONG. I'd like for people to just go ahead and post, in as few words as possible, what the law of attraction is, in their understanding. Perhaps by sharing as many viewpoints as possible on what each of us thinks the LOA actually is, we may move a little closer to actually understanding it. It is a physical law, as imutable as the law of gravity, and about as well understood from our extremely limited, blinkered viewpoint. And just like you'll find university educated physics majors who'll swear black and blue they fully understand the law of gravity, they'll swear just as blindly antigravity systems are physically impossible. Which basically just says to me they really don't fully understand the law of gravity. The same applies to the law of attraction. If it's simply a case of fixing your desire in your mind and being grateful that it's about to come true, where's your perfect health, mansion, dirty big pile of cash and garage full of sports cars? Well? So lets bang heads together and make ideas fall out. What's your take on the LOA? The floor is open.
