He is known for his interest in the Toroidal Ring and Atomic Structure . Biography Darrell Ayers was born in 1954 and resides in Minneapolis, Minnesota, in the United States. Work Ayers's research interests concern the Toroidal Ring and Atomic Structure , a physical approach to modeling subatomic particles as ring-shaped structures of distributed charge and circulating current rather than as point-like entities.
