Ideas for Raising Your Vibration When You're Going Through a Downer....

· archived # 2,432,596
Follow what feels good in the moment, and if nothing feels good, try this.... Because (due to the nature of the human experience) we all go through times when we feel down, dispondent, dissapointed, disillusioned, worried, frustrated etc. I thought it would be useful to share things here that we find are effective at raising our vibrations. I believe that laughter is one of the fastest and most effective solutions - especially when feeling disillusioned with life. When reading or listening to more spiritual and uplifting messages is met with resistance and an increase in frustration and mental arguments ... watching or reading something that completely distratcts you and causes an involuntary bubble of humour, can be very effective. For that reason, I'm sharing things that have that effect for me..... * Dogs in Elk - a blow-by-blow account from a dog forum, of a woman's struggle to solve an hillarious doggie situation. Reading this, never fails to make me laugh out loud! * Oprah on Ellen Degeneres - lots of laughs and "feel-good" - especially for those who particularly love these two The following one is not for everyone... it's only for those who are so fed up and disillusioned, that ideas for raising their vibration only makes them more annoyed... for whom only black humour from Dylan Moran will be acceptable... and who are not easily offended .... I challenge them to keep a straight face through this...
