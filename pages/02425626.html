The prevailing model argues the crust accumulated as plagioclase ﬂoated to the surface of a solidifying lunar magma ocean (LMO). Whether >98% pure anorthosites can form in a ﬂotation scenario is debated. An important determinant of the efﬁciency of plagioclase fractionation is the viscosity of the LMO liquid, which was unconstrained.
