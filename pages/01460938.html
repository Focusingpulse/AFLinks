New Age

· archived # 1,460,938
There are several definitions of this term and event. The New Age is a new era of peace and a closeness to God or Nature where individuals awaken to or remember their Whole Mind . It is an age of Oneness and harmony with the forces of Nature . It is more of a New Era of Consciousness than a New Age . We see this happening with the advent of the Global Internet.
