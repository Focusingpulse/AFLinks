Unification of Gravity with Electricity

Title Current Squared is Force - Unification of Gravity with Electricity Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Francis Viren Fernandes Keywords Unified theory, gravity, electricity, force, magnetism, magnetic levitation Published 2014 No. of pages 1 The paper itself is headed Force Equals Electric Current Squared and dated 3 December 2004 Abstract The unity of gravity with electric current is described by way of an experiment. Overview This is a single page, headed in the file itself Force Equals Electric Current Squared and dated 3 December 2004 though catalogued here under 2014. It consists of one worked arithmetic example built on the standard magnetic levitation of a current-carrying wire, from which Francis Viren Fernandes concludes that force in newtons is numerically equal to a current multiplied by a current, and hence that N = A 2 — a result he presents as unifying gravity with electricity. There is no apparatus description, no measurement and no data. Despite the phrase "experimental evidence by way of a levitation experiment", every number on the page is either assumed or computed from the assumed values. The argument The setup is the familiar one from an introductory course. A straight conductor of mass 8 grams (0.008 kg) and length 0.5 m is placed at right angles to a magnetic field of 0.40 T. Its weight is F = mg = 0.008 × 9.8 = 0.0784 N and the current needed to levitate it follows from the standard force law F = BIL : I = F / ( BL ) = 0.0784 / (0.40 × 0.5) = 0.392 A Fernandes states this correctly and labels it "conventional understanding". The paper's own step comes next. He forms the product of the field and the length, B × L = 0.40 × 0.5 = 0.2 and calls the result a current of 0.2 A — described as the "current generated by the magnetic field B of 0.4 T on 0.5 m length of the conductor".
