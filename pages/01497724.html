JOURNAL OF CONDENSED MATTER NUCLEAR SCIENCE Experiments and Methods in Cold Fusion VOLUME 37, September 2023 Preface This new volume 37 includes 8 experimental and theoretical articles. It is for me a great joy to see that scientists all around the world are working hard to discover the mysteries of Low Energy Nuclear Reactions. After more than 34 years since the announcement by Professors Martin Fleischman and Stanley Pons, we are still trying to find out what is happening in metals loaded with hydrogen. We need more experimental data so that theoreticians can develop good models. However, as the field develops, we realize that the two discoverers have opened the door to a large number of anomalies going from the production of anomalous excess heat to fusion and fission of elements, with also the presence of some neutrons and alpha particles. A lot more work is in front of us, and History will see these days of research as a time of a lot of excitement. J. Condensed Matter Nucl.
