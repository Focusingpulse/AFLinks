In the early 1840s, a man launched a vigorous attack on Hegel's worldview, which he had previously embraced thoroughly and intimately. That man was Ludwig Feuerbach (1804-1872). His declaration of war on the worldview from which he had grown out is expressed in radical form in his writings: “Provisional Theses on the Reform of Philosophy” (1842) and “Principles of the Philosophy of the Future” (1843).
