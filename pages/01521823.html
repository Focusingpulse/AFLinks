Overview The book applies field theory to photometry — the measurement of light — treating the distribution of radiant/luminous energy in space as a field. Moon and Spencer bring a formal, unified mathematical treatment to the portion of geometrical optics concerned with amounts of light, and develop it into a practical tool for applications such as solar heating, lighting (illumination) design, photographic exposure, and colour specification. It extends the field-theoretic approach the authors took in their earlier Field Theory for Engineers . About the authors Parry Hiram Moon
