What's The Delay?? Your Paths Results....

· archived # 2,431,586
What's the Delay?? Think of a house being built. You see absolutely nothing of the house for most of the building period, as the foundations are being laid below ground. All the work is being done below the surface - out of sight . No sign of a house. No bricks and mortar for aaaaaaaaages. It doesn't mean the builders aren't working It doesn't mean the house is not in the process of being built. Friends of ours took their little daughter to see their new house being built. It had been months since work had started on it. The little girl asked where it was, and when they pointed it out, she cried out "Oh no!! Mummy, it's fallen down!!" This was, of course, because all those months, the builders were working on the foundation, but there was nothing visable except for some building materials lying around. It looked nothing like a house! The VISABLE part of the house shot up in a few weeks. But they couldn't have done that without spending all that time on the foundation - it would have fallen down of course SO... I see PATHS as a building company. Imagine, you've hired PATHS to build a lovely new house for you. And it will depend on how much work needs to be done on the foundation - as to how quickly you'll see the visable results - the bricks and mortar. Some people, before hearing about PATHS, may have already started digging the foundations themselves - they may have (possbily even unknowingly) done a lot of the work on the foundations already, and therefore less time is needed on the foundation, and therefore, the visable results come sooner. Some people may have started working on the foundation themselves, but, since they're not professional builders, they may have unintentionally, done some things wrong - which may need extra work from the professional builders ( taking extra time ) to correct before then continuing with the foundation. Some people may have some challenges in the ground - there may be a river underneath the surface, or a cemetary, or the ground
