Wilhelm Reich (1897-1957)

· archived # 2,462,311
Editor's note: The following is a concise but informative biography of Dr. Wilhelm Reich's life and work, originally published by the Institute for Orgonomic Science (IOS) on their website, http://www.orgonomicscience.org . We would like to express our gratitude to the IOS and to Dr. Philip W. Bennett, author of the biography, for granting us permission to post it in this journal. At the end of the biography, Bennett suggests further readings, and mentions Fury on Earth by Myron Sharaf and Wilhelm Reich: A Personal Biography, by Ilse Ollendorff Reich, wife of Wilhelm Reich. However, Bennett cautions readers that the authors' personal antipathies toward Reich may have influenced the tone and content of both biographies; thus, he recommends David Boadella's Wilhelm Reich: The Evolution of His Work as one of the most accurate biographies of its time. It is the historian's responsibility to present historical information with as much objectivity as possible, ensuring that the writer's personal biases do not affect the reporting of historical events. However, some historians feel that an occasional critical or admiring remark is not only acceptable but necessary when the subject of the biography warrants such a remark, regardless of the author's biases. Readers of these biographies should thus form their own conclusions about the biographers' principles in documenting history. Likewise, readers of Ilse Ollendroff Reich's biography should be aware that because of her intimate knowledge of her husband, her biography contains deeply personal information that might otherwise have been kept from public scrutiny. And here is the biography: 1897-1918 Wilhelm Reich was born in 1897 in the north-east part of the Austro-Hungarian Empire, in what is now Ukraine. His native tongue was German; his cultural/ethnic background was secular Jewish. Reich was raised on a farm and educated at home by tutors.
