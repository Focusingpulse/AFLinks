APRIL – MAY 2021 www.nexusmagazine.com NEXUS • 59 Introduction At the end of the 18th century Antoine Lavoisier demonstrated that chemical elements cannot be created nor destroyed. He performed a number of chemical experiments showing that various elements can combine with each other, but without any change in their elemental compositions. This was the credo of science until the discovery of radioactivity at the end of the 19th century and later artificial radioactivity. However, for everyone now, it is out of the question that nuclear reactions can occur outside the nuclear world of radioactivity and high-energy physics.
