It was about franchising the conversion of normal automobiles to electric....the engine was removed, a high power, heavy duty, long life electric motor was hooked to the transmission, a battery network was installed in the trunk with hookup cables so you could recharge it quickly and easily from any 110VAC or 12VDC outlet. Our company got to keep the internal combustion engines from the cars and trucks we outfitted. We then installed the engines on a movable platform, adjusted them to be able to burn hydroxyl (the proper term for hydrogen and oxygen) along with outside air to run these motors to produce electricity for your house. This was all based on the company who used the engine flywheel to produce a continuous 7,000 watts from an idling car engine, see; http://keelynet.com/energy/dynamo.txt ....that's plenty of power....these engines provided much more than 7,000 watts because we not only had the magnetic coils to tap the magnetized flywheel, but we had the torque from the engine to drive a generator. In the dream, we were making money hand over fist and licensing hundreds of franchises per day to not only retrofit cars but to hookup the hydroxyl engines to power homes across the country...deja vu?
