A summary of its contents follows below. Overview This is Volume 8, Number 7 of New Energy News (ISSN 1075-0045), the monthly bulletin of the Institute for New Energy, edited by Patrick G Bailey and distributed from Salt Lake City. The issue opens with a note that from this point the newsletter will be circulated by e-mail in two forms, plain ASCII and PDF, with readers lacking Adobe Acrobat invited to mail $2 for a CD-ROM — a small detail that dates the publication precisely to the moment when the cold fusion and free-energy community was migrating from paper and floppy disk to the web. The issue is organised in five departments: Abstracts, Articles, Letters, Meetings and Advertisement. Its editorial character is that of a clearing house rather than a journal. Refereed abstracts from Fusion Technology sit alongside newspaper clippings, an unrefereed theoretical essay, a reader's letter describing a home-built antigravity experiment, and conference announcements from three continents. Nothing in the issue is peer reviewed by the newsletter itself, and Bailey's editorial interventions are limited to bracketed asides such as "[This is not energy related now, but it may be soon!]". The value of the document is therefore documentary: it is a snapshot of what the New Energy research community was reading, arguing about and organising in mid-2001. Abstracts Two papers from Fusion Technology are abstracted, both from 2001 and both concerning deuterium in palladium. Hiroshi Yamada and co-workers at Iwate University report on "Producing a Radioactive Source in a Deuterated Palladium Electrode under Direct-Current Glow Discharge" ( Fusion Technol. 39, no. 7, March 2001, 253-258).
