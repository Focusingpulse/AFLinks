👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 15 March 1911 · archived # 2,439,197
, Berlin Translated by Steiner Online Library Esoteric Lesson Record A As explained last time [January 17, 1911], our meditations should be guided by the motto: “Constant dripping wears away the stone.” The study of theosophical works is an effective preparation for the exercises. It is better to have read one work twenty-five times than five books five times each; and anyone who has read a book two or three times should not imagine that they have read it at all.
