This paper derives all of them from a single geometric requirement and shows that none is a free choice. The argument runs backward from the target. Pi is four times arctan(1), which is four times the angle of the 45-degree direction. A frame of n equally spaced directions contains that direction only when 8 divides n. The integration limit is cos(45 degrees) = 2^(-1/2), and it must be the coordinate of one of those marks. Combined with the requirement that the limit raised to the shell spacing equal the reciprocal of the base, this forces the pairing base = 2^m with spacing n = 2m, and then forces m = 4 and base = 16 as the minimum. The denominators 8k+r are then one per marked direction, and the shell index satisfies r = p+1 for numerator power p purely because integrating x^p produces x^(p+1)/(p+1).
