Log in I forgot my password Sympathetic Vibratory Physics &raquo; law of partial pressures Page actions PDF Print Share Send link law of partial pressures In chemistry and physics , Dalton 's law (also called Dalton's law of partial pressures ) states that in a mixture of non-reacting gases, the total pressure exerted is equal to the sum of the partial pressures of the individual gases. This empirical law was observed by John Dalton in 1801 and published in 1802 and is related to the ideal gas laws. Wikipedia, Dalton's law of partial pressures Created by Dale Pond.
