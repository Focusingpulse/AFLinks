👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 14 October 1911 · archived # 2,422,676

We will now touch briefly on the esoteric path—the path which leads to Christ in such a way that he can be found within the super-sensible worlds. [ ] First of all we must note that this esoteric path to Christ Jesus was also the way of the Evangelists, of those who wrote the Gospels.
