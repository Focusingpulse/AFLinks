Keene's claim, stated at the outset, is that all known gravitational effects may be accounted for by quantum-level mechanisms, from single nucleons to astronomical bodies, with no need for curved spacetime. Background His starting point is that the implicit postulate that space and time are continuous has failed, and — more strongly — that physicists will never explain how things work while they cling to it. Newtonian mechanics describes motion very well; physics is about the mechanism by which the described motion occurs, and that is what he says is missing. His alternative quantises energy, length and time. He noted that Planck reportedly wanted to quantise energy and never achieved it, arriving instead at a constant with the dimensions of energy times time. The values of his three primary constants were obtained by reverse-engineering: taking the measured but unexplained quantities — electron mass, light speed, Planck's constant, elementary charge, the magnetic moment — and asking what quantisation of length, time and energy would produce them.
