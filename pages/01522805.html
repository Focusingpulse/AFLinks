Less than a year later, in mid-1960, he became involved in the study of catastrophism and the reconstruction of the Solar System's cosmic history. He has since then acted as a Contributing Editor for KRONOS (1975-1978) and, later, as a Senior Editor for the same periodical (1979-1988) and, later still, as the Editor for AEON (1995-2006). He was one of the Founding Fathers behind the Canadian Society for Interdisciplinary Studies (now defunct), and has acted as a consultant on mythology and cosmogony for Chronology and Catastrophism Review , the official organ of the British-based Society for Interdisciplinary Studies.
