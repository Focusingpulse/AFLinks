The Magnetic Gate tests by Phil Bright The Bedini's Magnetic Gate test By Phil Bright created on September 27th, 1999 - JLN Labs - Last update September 27th, 1999 Enjoyed your recent Ohsaka Principle piece ( with a nice anthology of other permanent magnet phenomenon). TOMI looks interesting. As a retired Electrical Engineer my research has typically been in the areas of scalar waves, long line pulses, inductance, resonance, etc. I just cant resist reporting results of several magnet experiments after reading your article, however. My experiments and conclusions with the Hamel spinner are like those of Jean-Louis Naudin. No problem getting spin with a verity of disk and magnetic gate combinations. No combination of distance and angle adjustment on a ridged fixture will maintain spin. Until someone mechanically nutates the gate (presumably with active feedback) and measures the energy required to nutate and shows net Energy gain vs. the spun momentum energy; this will remain an interesting magnet top. I would like to share more specific results and thoughts on Magnetic gate compression experiments. This set of tests was inspired by JLN Labs work in 1997; part of which you link in the Ohsaka article . Basic experiments: Determine the Energy produced when a projectile magnet (PM) is passed through an all North magnetic gate. Determine the Energy required (if any) to position the PM at the trigger point where it is sucked through the gate on its own.
