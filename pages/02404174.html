Гончаров ЛАБОРАТОРИЯ ФИЗИКИ УЛУЧШЕННОГО УДЕРЖАНИЯ ПЛАЗМЫ ТОКАМАКОВ САНКТ-ПЕТЕРБУРГСКОГО ГОСУДАРСТВЕННОГО ПОЛИТЕХНИЧЕСКОГО УНИВЕРСИТЕТА Within the Research Laboratory of the Physics of Advanced Tokamaks, which has been founded in 2011 on the basis of a MEGA-Grant, the two groups from the University and the Ioffe Institute cooperate on the three tokamaks, Globus-M, TUMAN-3M and FT-2. The research of both partners benefitted from the means of the MEGA-Grant. The laboratory has given itself a transparent governing structure and its annual outcome is assessed by an international advisory committee. A graduate school has been founded to ensure proper education. The Laboratory could serve as a model for integrated research and education centre in Russian thermonuclear program. The urgent need to modernize the Russian fusion devices has become obvious.
