Dark Matter: Fantasy or Real? Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Speaker David de Hilster Date 7 August 2021 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording Dark Matter: Fantasy or Real? was a live session presented by David de Hilster on 7 August 2021 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. David de Hilster will be discussing whether or not Dark Matter is real or a fantasy. Much time and energy are spent by both dissidents and mainstream scientists trying to find this elusive "stuff". But David's question is: does it exist or are we looking for a ghost? Join him live this Saturday! Summary David de Hilster on why he thinks dark matter is unnecessary, built around a scripted video of his own and two papers from the society, with a panel discussion after. He was frank about his motive: he keeps seeing dissidents discussing dark matter when, on his reading, the work refuting it has already been done, and he wanted to get it off his chest. The argument The claim is that the anomaly is an artefact of treating galaxies as point masses. The Keplerian fall-off in stellar velocity is derived for a spherical, uniform gravitational field; a spiral galaxy is flat and full of arms, and its field is nothing of the kind. Overlaying the one on the other and finding a discrepancy does not establish an unseen mass. His strongest exhibit was the reports of galaxies without dark matter, which struck him when he first saw them: the two in question are spherical. A spherical galaxy behaves as a point mass, so the point-mass calculation works and no anomaly appears — which he took to be the answer sitting in plain sight in the mainstream's own articles. He took Bob de Hilster 's paper as the second exhibit.
