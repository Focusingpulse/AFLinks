Liber mutus in quo tamen "The Mute Book is regarded by enthusiasts of hermetic science as one of the most fascinating works in this field, due to the insights it contains regarding the operations of the Great Work and the matter that forms its foundation. It is true that the author has only used allegorical images, which are not comprehensible to everyone, but those who are predisposed to this science will grasp them easily. If the author had added the slightest commentary to these illustrations, this science—which must be kept secret from the masses—would have been entirely revealed. Such a thing would be contrary to the will of the Supreme Being, who has disclosed it to only a very small number of wise individuals so that it might be passed down to posterity solely to virtuous men. Even the adepts who have sought to unveil its mysteries have always preserved its secrets with the utmost care, to prevent the unworthy from misusing this sacred knowledge. To speak, they used hieroglyphs and allegories to communicate this marvelous secret only to worthy individuals. In this edition, we believed it necessary to fight against the prejudices of hermetic mysteries by providing the explanation of the lines of numbers found on the frontispiece of the work and supplementing it with verses of the same nature.
