When the fridge is running, if you place your hand on top and go around the perimeter, some of the fridge's we experimented with had hot spots that ran around the to perimeter about 1-2 inches inward from the very edge. If insulating the entire top, the insulation will lock that heat into the fridge casing when the fridge is running. The idea will work as long as you insulate the top of the fridge where the hot spots don't exist. The insulation on the back of the fridge is fine.
