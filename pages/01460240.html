Know Thyself

· archived # 1,460,240
Rene Guenon The Self The misunderstanding of the phrase 'Know thyself largely stems from the fallacious association of 'Self' with the individual self, that enclosed unity consisting of one's corporeal and psychological attributes. In traditional cultures the Self was viewed as something more reminiscent of a ladder consisting of many levels. It connects the corporeal with the Divine, the individual manifestation with the undifferentiated. The Self can be said to be synonymous with the individual person only on its lowest levels. Once higher knowledge (gnosis) is achieved through climbing up the ladder, all individuality dissolves. This knowledge is not to be sought in the corporal world outside, but within, by exploring one's inner essence, thereby discovering the possibilities of the Self. [ Rene Guenon ] Gurdjieff "The first reason for man’s inner slavery is his ignorance, and above all, his ignorance of himself. Without self-knowledge, without understanding the working and functions of his machine, man cannot be free, he cannot govern himself and he will always remain a slave, and the plaything of the forces acting upon him. This is why in all ancient teachings the first demand at the beginning of the way to liberation was: ‘Know thyself’." [ Gurdjieff ] Atwood M. A.
