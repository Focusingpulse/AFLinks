SPAF-2026-main023 CDU: UDC: 338.48-44 Developing intercultural communicative competence through educational routes: a school practice in Beijing Egor A. Denisov1 1 PEC "Educational Complex "Point of Future", Russia i@egordenisov.ru Contemporary educational development emphasises ICC as a key quality for effective interaction in cultural diversity [1; 5]. For students preparing for international project work, ICC is both a fundamental educational goal and a condition for successful academic and social adaptation abroad. Traditional ICC formation methods often remain purely theoretical, failing to provide the experience of “living through” language and culture or to develop empathy, communicative flexibility and the ability to overcome communication barriers with native speakers [4]. This limitation is particularly acute in school settings, where adolescent learners require concrete, experiential opportunities. Educational routes integrate education and cultural immersion, creating conditions for practice￾oriented acquisition of foreign culture. This article presents findings from implementing such routes with high school students in Beijing, demonstrating their effectiveness in ICC formation during project-based learning. Based on a comprehensive analysis of scientific literature (M. Byram, N.D. Galskova, A.P. Sadokhin), ICC is defined as a complex integrative formation comprising four interconnected components [2; 3]: Cognitive – structured knowledge about language, cultural features, norms and values of one’s own and other cultures.
