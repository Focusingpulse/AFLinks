GC2026-SGG030 The EMR method as a basic method of borehole monitoring of the preparation of strong Kamchatka earthquakes Valery A. Gavrilov1,2 , Evgenia V. Poltavtseva1,2 , Yulia V. Morozova1 , Yulia Y. Buss1 , Oleg V. Fedoristov 1,2 1 Institute of Volcanology and Seismology of the Far Eastern Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences 2 Vitus Bering Kamchatka State University kianara@mail.ru Complex borehole measurements have been carried out by the Institute of Volcanology and Seismology FEB RAS on the territory of the Petropavlovsk-Kamchatsky Geodynamic testing site continuously since August 2000. Currently, the network of complex borehole measurements (Network) is actually the information basis for the system of medium- and short-term prediction of earthquakes dangerous for the Petropavlovsk-Kamchatsky area. Quite detailed information about the Network is contained in [1]. The main features of the Network are conducting observations in sufficiently deep boreholes and their complexity. Currently, data obtained daily via telemetry channels for seven types of borehole measurements from four measuring points of the Network is used to monitor earthquake preparation processes. The main types of measurements are electromagnetic measurements with underground electric antennas, geoacoustic measurements at depths up to 1000 m, monitoring of changes in gas saturation and electrical conductivity of borehole water, measurements of temperature and water level of boreholes.
