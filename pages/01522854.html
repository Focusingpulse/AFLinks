It gathers Marinov's experimental and theoretical case against Albert Einstein's special theory of relativity , centred on his claim to have measured the absolute velocity of the Earth through the aether by detecting a direction-dependence in the one-way speed of light . The book The core of Eppur Si Muove is Marinov's account of the experiments he devised to detect the Earth's motion relative to absolute space.
