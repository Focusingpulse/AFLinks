Introduction 15 In this introduction, we consider three types of forcing due to 16  CO2 (ignoring other GHGs) 17  Hotspots (such as Urban Heat Islands and Roads) 18  Hydro-hotspots 19 Here a hydro-hotspot is a solar hot surface that creates atmospheric moisture in the presence of 20 precipitation. Urban Heat Islands (UHI) and other impermeable surfaces create hydro-hotspots [1] which 21 can contribute to global warming. Such surfaces then create rapid evaporation and excess moisture in the 22 atmosphere promoting a local greenhouse effect. This mechanism includes warmer air-surface 23 temperatures due to the initial hotspot, and then during precipitation, evaporation increases the local 24 atmosphere humidity GHG (as warm air holds more water vapor).
