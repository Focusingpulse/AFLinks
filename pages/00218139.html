The study ensures energy conservation through building absorption mechanisms and highlights the impact of higher yields on blast effects. 1 Introduction and Background Blast attenuation in cities refers to the reduction in blast wave intensity as it propagates through urban areas, due to interactions with buildings and structures. This is critical for assessing the impact of nuclear or conventional explosions in densely populated areas, informing urban planning, and enhancing safety measures.
