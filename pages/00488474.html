Algebraic Obstructions and Perturbation Identities for Collatz Cycle Uniqueness Nikola Chachev 01.04.2026 Abstract We present a new algebraic reformulation of the uniqueness problem for periodic orbits of the Collatz map c(n) = n/2 (n even) or c(n) = 3n+ 1 (n odd). The question of whether {1, 2, 4} is the only positive integer cycle is classically equivalent to an integer divisibility condition of the form (2S − 3 L) | N. We recast this condition as the vanishing of an explicit integer polynomial — the cycle polynomial PG(t) — evaluated at an arithmetic point t0 of multiplicative order L modulo D = 2S − 3 L. This perspective reduces the uniqueness problem to a question of polynomial non￾vanishing over Z/DZ, which we analyse through the 2-adic and 3-adic structure of the evaluation map G 7→ PG(t0) mod D. Using this framework we establish two partial results. First, for every mixed valuation sequence G — one in which the accumulated deviations εi take both positive and negative values — the cycle polynomial satisfies PG(t0) ̸≡ 0 (mod D) in the special case where exact integer vanishing A(G) = B(G) would be required; this follows from a parity obstruction on 2-adic valuations together with the step-size constraint Gi ≥ 1. Second, we identify a combined 2-adic and 3-adic obstruction that constrains any hypothetical solution PG(t0) ≡ 0 (mod D) to an increasingly rigid arithmetic structure. The case of non-zero multiples — whether A(G) − B(G) = kD for k ≥ 1 — remains open; we describe precisely the gap and the new ideas that would be needed to close it.
