Cold Fusion Now! attended the 21st International Conference on Condensed Matter Nuclear Science ICCF-21 held June 3-8 at Colorado State University in Fort Collins, Colorado, US and captured video and snapshots of the event. Pages summarizing the presentations are currently under construction , but take a peek at Monday and Tuesday’s summaries enhanced with audio files of presentation lectures: ICCF-21 Cold Fusion Now!
