👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 12 June 1911 · archived # 2,439,199
, Berlin Translated by Steiner Online Library Esoteric Lesson Record A My dear sisters and brothers! We must realize that there is a great difference between external, exoteric knowledge and the knowledge that theosophy imparts to us. When we allow an external view to influence us, mental images and concepts form within us; we learn about the thing we are looking at, we gain knowledge about it. Is it the same with theosophical knowledge?
