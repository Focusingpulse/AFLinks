Abstract GPS carrier phase integer ambiguity resolution is still the key issue and challenge for high accuracy RTK survey and navigation. Due to speed and memory limitations of OEM GPS receiver boards, the calculation efficiency is of prime importance since it affects the elapsed time before ambiguities can be resolved. Of course, the reliability of the ambiguity resolution process is also a significant concern. A new technique is presented based upon the Resid￾ual Sensitivity Matrix [8], which relates the search integer ambiguity set to each carrier phase residual directly.
