Section I – Masterclass #67 – A Geomantic Life with Alanna Moore, November 22, 2025- 1:00 pm Central Time Alanna was born in Sydney Australia. She was introduced to dowsing when living in London and joined the British Society of Dowsers in 1980. Returning to Sydney and befriending a well-known dowser Isobel Bellamy, she helped to found the New South Wales Dowsers Society in 1984. The only founder remaining, she helped the society celebrate its 40th anniversary in Sydney in November 2024. She now lives in Ireland. Alanna has been a professional geomancer for over 40 years, mostly in Australasia, Asia and Europe. She specializes in the more unusual applications of detecting spirits and applying dowsing to improved gardening.
