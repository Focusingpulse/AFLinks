Published 2002 Journal Journal of Theoretics Volume 4 Number 5 No. of pages 2 Read the full paper here Abstract It is emphasized that the previous arguments against "antigravity" are removed within the framework of relativistic gravidynamics (the theory of gravivector field). The search for this phenomenon is of paramount importance to elucidate the nature of gravitational interaction. According to Feynman, time flows backward for antiparticles (a consequence of T-symmetry) therefore the "antigravity" absence can mean (like in the case of weak interaction) the violation of T-invariance (the "time arrow" existence).
