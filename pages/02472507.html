In 1943, Tesla, the eccentric genie behind radio, radar, X-rays, the AC motor, and remote control, among other early-twentieth-century inventions, is nearing the end of his life in his lonesome aerie on the massive New Yorker's thirty-third floor. Louisa Dewell, a 24-year-old radio-drama- show junkie with a keen imagination, is assigned to clean his room. She and the hypersensitive scientist become fast friends, and, like Alice falling down the rabbit hole, Dewell soon finds that life just gets curiouser and curiouser. Laid out in chapters that alternate between the voices of Tesla and Dewell, the novel can be read as a sophisticated pastiche of science fiction, fantasy, melodrama, and historical anecdote.
