Lecture I — GA 138. Karmic Relationships IV (1957)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 5 September 1924 · archived # 2,436,093
, Dornach Translated by George Adams, D. S. Osmond and Charles Davy. Lecture I [ 1 ] Many friends have come here to-day for the first time since the Christmas Foundation Meeting and I must therefore speak of it, even if only briefly, by way of introduction. Through this Christmas Foundation the Anthroposophical Society was to be given a new impulse, the impulse that is essential if it is to be a worthy channel for the life which, through Anthroposophy, must find embodiment in human civilisation.
