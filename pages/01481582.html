meeting of the fifths

· archived # 1,481,582
Hughes Ascending , begin with A in the innermost circle, D being its root . The Key-note A becomes the root of E , E becomes the root of B , and so on. Descending , take the Key-note A in the outermost circle. D, the root of A , becomes the fifth lower Key-note , and G its root , and then G becomes the Key-note , and C its root . The same remarks concerning the writing of the meeting fifths , which are made below the corresponding diagram of the major gamut , apply to this one.
