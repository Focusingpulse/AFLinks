I envision the ball moving as a result of Fleming's rule. That is, if the donut is not toroidal wound, then this transformer would produce a magnetic field with lines of force entering one plate and exiting the other. With a magnetic field situated as such, a voltage high enough to cause corona discharge from the top of the ball and amperes sufficient to cause a small magnetic field in and around the ball, would cause the ball to move as described. This is also reminiscent of magnetohydrodynamics. As for why the ball does not fly off the plate from centrifugal force, I would say that it is a combination of the electrostatic field and the direction of the magnetic field lines near the outer edge, which could be parallel with the plate depending on the size of the "donut" transformer.
