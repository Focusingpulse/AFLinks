In a second one we showed a splitting of Genetic Code Table (GCT) into three equal and significant parts, using the harmonic mean (H(a, b) = 2ab/(a+b); a = 63, b = 31.5) [2]. In this paper, however, we will show that a specific unity of golden mean and harmonic mean appears to be the determinant of Rumer’s Table of 16 nucleotide doublets [3] (Tables 1 & 2 in relation to Tables 3 and 4).
