The Story as I got it...I offer no proof that this is true or false, just passing it on. During the late sixties out in the New Mexico Desert, Steve Baer (Domeworks) and another fellow named Day Chahroudi were working on all manner of weird solar stuff. Day had the wild idea that if concentrated sunlight could produce lots of heat, then concentrated shade or some such arrangement would produce the opposite effect...cold. The .GIF file (COLDGEN.GIF) shows a picture of this device. More info: I have also heard that this picture is of an early tracker design for solar panels. This may be true BUT if it is true, then where is the usual collector? The only surfaces visible in this picture are NOT black or do they in any way look like potential solar collectors. Quite to the contrary, they look more like REFLECTIVE surfaces! Except for the small panel in the center, which is SHADED..
