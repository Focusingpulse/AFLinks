, Berlin Translated by Steiner Online Library Compare 13. Lucifer [ 1 ] Persian mythology speaks of two opposing deities: Ormuzd, the good god, and Ahriman, the evil god. The two deities battle for humanity—and indeed for everything that unfolds here on Earth as life and striving. It is foretold that the good deity will one day triumph over the evil deity. [ 2 ] Whatever one may think of this legend, everyone can see a reflection of this legendary idea in nature, in the world around us. To give an example, consider fire on the one hand.
