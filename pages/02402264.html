Степанов nejapetr@yandex.ru Spectral-kinetic model of photoprocesses in dye-doped phospholipid liposomes with encapsulated plasmonic nanoparticles (Au, Ag) with the participation of singlet oxygen molecules 1О2 has been proposed (Fig. a) [1–3]. The effect of metal nanoparticles was taken into account both at the stage of light absorption (EXC) by erythrosine (ER) molecules and at the stage of plasmon-enhanced delayed fluorescence (DF) and phosphorescence (PH) accompanying the events of triplet-singlet annihilation (CA) 3ER + 1О2 –> 1ER + 3О2 . The vesicular effect manifested itself in the creation of a reaction nanovolume enclosing both the reagents themselves (ER, О2 ) and the plasmonic nanoparticles-activators (NP). It was taken into account that diffusing О2 molecules were partially reflected from the phospholipid shell of the liposome and partially escaped outside, leaving the reaction volume. The near-field characteristics of a group of nanoparticles were calculated using the Maxwell Garnett approach, while for a single nanoparticle inside a liposome, the exact solution of the Laplace equation in the spherical layer of the aqueous pool was used.
