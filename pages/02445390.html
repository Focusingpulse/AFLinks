08/30/16 - Scottish Firm Claims Commercial Tidal Power Breakthrough A power company in Shetland has claimed a breakthrough in the race to develop viable offshore tidal stations after successfully feeding electricity to local homes. Nova Innovation said it had deployed the world�s first fully operational array of tidal power turbines in the Bluemull Sound between the islands of Unst and Yell in the north of Shetland, where the North Sea meets the Atlantic. It switched on the second of five 100 kW turbines due to be installed in the sound this month, sending electricity on a commercial basis into Shetland�s local grid. The Nova-30 device will be built locally as well, thanks to a fabrication agreement between Leith-based Nova Innovation Ltd and Steel Engineering, which has a newly expanded Renfrew facility. The new premises, which will help the Steel Engineering meet its ambition to create 120 new jobs, were officially opened by Scotland�s First Minister Alex Salmond today. The tidal turbine looks very much like an above-ground wind turbine, using a three-bladed rotor to extract energy from the ocean tides.
