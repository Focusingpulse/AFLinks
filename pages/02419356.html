Journal of Borderland Research >> Vol. 21 >> No. 02 Image: Frank Eugene Austin, "Magnetic field of horseshoe magnet" (c. 1916) [PD] [18] EXPERIMENTS WITH CARBON ROD AND MAGNET By A. Goeke I was experimenting with magnets before I ever heard of Vitic, but since adding the carbon rod I get better results. When I attempt to analyze anyone I use a pendulum to check their polarity. I sometimes find both sides of the body negative, or both sides positive, or reversed, or the entire body neutral. I have proven many times that when polarity is incorrect that neither blood nor nerve force flow properly. Correct the polarity and the effected part warms up. It goes without saying that any time blood flow and nerve force are less than par, metabolism is also below par and we start to die in that part to the degree of interference. I have been correcting polarity by laying on of hands for years and could tell of some very interesting cases. The Multi-Wave Oscillator will also change polarity back to normal except where there is some local interference. Now I find that the carbon and magnet will also do it. This is how I set up an experiment. I have the subject lie down and hold the carbon in his hand, on the side we are working on. I use one magnet on a six-inch string, as a pendulum. If there is work to be done it will gyrate in a circle. When it does, I hold the magnet there, suspended on the string, until it stops gyrating. I start below the bottom of the foot. When the magnet stops gyrating, I move it up along the leg a foot or so. The magnet starts spinning again.
