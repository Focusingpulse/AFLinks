Points of View — Christianity as a Mystical Fact (1947)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 2,386,522
Natural science has deeply influenced modern thought. It is becoming more and more impossible to speak of spiritual needs and the life of the soul without taking into consideration the achievements and methods of this science. It is true that many people still satisfy these needs without letting themselves be troubled by its influence. But those who feel the pulse beat of the age must take this influence into account.
