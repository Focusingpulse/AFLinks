SPAF-2026-main003 CDU: 547.16 (Photo)dynamic system and self-tuning photoswitching of Pd-NHC- complexes Rustam B. Shnigirev1 1 Irkutsk Institute of Chemistry named after A.E. Favorsky, Siberian Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences bramarust@gmail.com N-Heterocyclic carbene (NHC) complexes are widely used in homogeneous catalysis. [1] Rational design of the ligands on the stage of synthesis allows for fine-tuning of electronic properties towards the better performance. [2] Another approach is control of relative disposition of ligands on the metal ion (trans/cis and syn/anti isomerism, Scheme A), which also could affect the catalytic properties. In recent years good progress was achieved in photoswitchable diarylethene-based NHC and some NHC complexes (Scheme B). [4, 5] Despite the impressive progress of photoswitchable catalysis during last decades, an application of photochromic NHC complexes and especially, palladium NHC species (Pd/NHC), remain poorly studied area. In our work, we describe the (photo)dynamic system based on the bis-NHC palladium complex 1oo (Scheme C), a model compound belonging to the family of efficient catalysts. 1oo features an efficient stepwise photoswitching, trans/cis and syn/anti isomerizations on the metal ion, proceeding thermally as well as photochemically. This unprecedented reactivity provides a controllable access to multiple isomeric states of palladium species within one molecular system. Fig. 1 The General concept References 1. 1. E. A. B.
