Deriving The Dot Product From Polar Coordinates Timothy W. Jones Abstract Using polar coordinates the dot product is easily derived. Polar coordinates How do you show one form of the dot product is A · B = |A||B| cos θ. If A = (xa, ya) = (a cos θa, a sin θa) and B = (xb, yb) = (b cos θb, b sinθb) and A · B = xaxb + yayb, we have A · B = ab cos θa cos θb + ab sin θa sin θb (1) = ab(cos θa cos θb + sin θa sin θb) (2) = |A||B| cos(θa − θb) (3) = |A||B| cos θ (4) where θ is the angle between the vectors. 1
