Plasmaspheric plumes are identiﬁed when the low-energy ions (<1 keV) with ∼90∘ pitch angle distributions are observed by the Cluster Ion Spectrometer/Hot Ion Analyzer instrument. The ionospheric outﬂows are characterized by unidirectional or bidirectional ﬁeld-aligned pitch angle distributions of low-energy ions observed in the dayside magnetosphere.
