INE CRITERIA FOR ANALYZING AND DOCUMENTING DEVICES Patrick G. Bailey President, INE We do have criteria for analyzing and discussing any new or old energy conversion device, machine, experiment, technique, or idea: its part of the INE device database. A 10 point scale for all considerations is listed on the INE website at http://www.padrak.com/ine/DBGUIDE.html. Why aren't you using this criteria scale? This site also includes links to the INE database of devices, where almost every "device" (etc.) that has come to our attention within the INE is ranked by this scale, and each device has its own database data webpage. Check it out - there are 137 listings there right now! We need to update and expand this database list! We also have two separate comparison rankings - one from the point-of-view of our interest, and one from the point-of-view of its possibility for commercialization - and the rankings of the devices on these two lists are different! We are sad to say that several new devices (and techniques) that recently have been actively promoted and given lots of press have not turned out as well as has been advertized.
