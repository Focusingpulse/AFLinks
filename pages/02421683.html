From Buddha to Christ — GA 109. The Principle of Spiritual Economy (1986)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 31 May 1909 · archived # 2,421,683
, Budapest Translated by Peter Mollenhauer From Buddha to Christ A Lecture Given at the International Congress of the Federation of European Sections of the Theosophical Society [ 1 ] I do not wish to offer you here an observation about the philosophy of religion or a treatise on literary history, nor do I wish to give you a scientific lecture about the subject matter. I simply want to tell you what Spiritual Science or occultism have to say about such great individualities as Buddha and Christ, more precisely what knowledge they can offer from the vantage point of Rosicrucian occultism.
