We find that only a very small percentage (~10 -39 %) of the vacuum fluctuations available within a proton volume need be cohered and converted to mass-energy in order for the proton to meet the Schwarzschild condition. This proportion is similar to that between gravitation and the strong force where gravitation is thought to be ~10 -40 weaker than the strong force.
