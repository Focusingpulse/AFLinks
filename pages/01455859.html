Atomic Clusters - intermediate state between molecules and solids. Atomic clusters have near solid density. Van der Waal bonded clusters can be formed in high pressure gas-jets (i.e. free-jet expansions) from the standard piezo or solenoid actuated pulsed valves used in pulsed laser experiments. Clustering occurs due to the cooling associated with the adiabatic expansion of the gas into vacuum . The cooling leads to a phase-transition and the coalescence of atoms into clusters.
