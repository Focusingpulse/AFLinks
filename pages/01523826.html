Sashibhusan Rath Residence West Singhbhum, India Nationality Indian Known for Cosmology , space and time Sashibhusan Rath is an Indian independent researcher, listed in The Worldwide List of Dissident Scientists , whose single catalogued work is a 1997 reflective essay on space, time and cosmology. His recorded address is West Singhbhum , which at the time of the wiki's roster entry was in the state of Bihar; since the reorganization of 2000 the district has formed part of Jharkhand , with its headquarters at Chaibasa. Not to be confused with Sashibhusan Rath (1885–1943) , the Odia social reformer, industrialist and politician who founded and edited Dainik Asha , the first Odia daily newspaper, published from Brahmapur, and who was known as Ganjam Byaghra ("the Tiger of Ganjam"). A web search for this name returns that considerably better-documented figure, who is a different person and unconnected with physics. Work Rath's one entry on this wiki is " Reflection on our Picture of STC Space, Time and Cosmology " (1997). Despite the title, the surviving abstract is not a technical paper but a meditation on the practice of physics — on the physicist's way of looking at the world, on the limits of formalism, and on what one generation of scientists owes the next.
