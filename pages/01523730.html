V. Gallucci (born December 15, 1953) is an American nuclear engineer and a dissident-science author who has written extensively for the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society (CNPS/JCNPS), critiquing mainstream relativity and cosmology. He holds a PhD in nuclear engineering and is a licensed Professional Engineer (PE). Much of his independent work applies elementary mathematical analysis to test whether the theories advanced by other dissident scientists are at least plausible – an unusual role within the dissident community, where most authors advance a theory of their own rather than auditing those of their colleagues. Biography Gallucci was born in Albany, New York, and resides in Frederick, Maryland. His formal training required extensive study of physics, particularly atomic and nuclear physics, and during his university years he developed an additional interest in relativity, quantum physics, and astronomy. He is a licensed Professional Engineer and completed a roughly 40-year career in nuclear power, in both the commercial and governmental sectors, including work at the U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission in the area of probabilistic safety assessment. Within his professional field he has authored more than 200 technical papers and journal articles, including work on the statistical characterization of heat release rates from electrical enclosure fires and on the risk-informed fire regulation standard NFPA 805. Gallucci is also known within CNPS for a lighter contribution to its annual meetings: he composes poems during the conference, one each day, which have become a fixture of the event.
