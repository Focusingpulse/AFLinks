Says an 80,000 Ton Battle Ship, Costing $50,000,000, Is Practicable WASHINGTON, D. C., Saturday. An 80,000 ton battle ship, mounting fifteen 18-inch guns, having a speed of 25 knots and costing $50,000,000, would be the largest war craft which the United States could employ, according to a report to Congress prepared by the Navy Department in response to a request for specifications of the biggest fighting craft that could use the Panama Canal and American harbors. The report says that such a vessel would have a length of 975 feet, a beam of 108 feet, a secondary battery of twenty-one 6-inch guns, four 21-inch torpedo tubes and a 12,000 mile radius of action. To develop the value of such a vessel it would be necessary to lay down not fewer than five, the report adds. The European war, the report says, has as yet brought no conclusive answer to the question of what types are most desirable, and the General Board is not prepared to recommend so radical a departure as the 80,000 ton ship would represent.
