ABSTRACT This paper analyzes one of the first reincarnation cases Ian Stevenson studied, in 1961, and reported in Twenty Cases Suggestive of Reincarnation. The case of Gnanathilaka Bad￾devithana had previously been investigated by H. S. S. Nissanka, who kept records of his interviews in writing or on tape, making this one of fewer than three dozen published cases with records produced before past-life memories were verified. Unfortunately, because the book Nissanka wrote about the case was published only in Sri Lanka, and not translated until 2001, his investigation is little known. These practices are shown to have led Stevenson to miss considerable detail but to have had no effect on his evaluation of Gnanathilaka’s case.
