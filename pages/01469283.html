arithmetic

· archived # 1,469,283
Arithmetic or arithmetics (from the Greek word = number) is the oldest and most elementary branch of mathematics , used by almost everyone, for tasks ranging from simple day-to-day counting to advanced science and business calculations. It involves the study of quantity , especially as the result of combining numbers . In common usage, it refers to the simpler properties when using the traditional operations of addition , subtraction , multiplication and division with smaller values of numbers .
