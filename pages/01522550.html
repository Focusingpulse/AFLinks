Borros Arneth Residence Bad Homburg, Germany Nationality Russian Known for The mass–charge quantity z = ( bq , m 0 ), unifying gravitation and Coulomb attraction Scientific career Fields Laboratory medicine and molecular diagnostics; immunology; foundations of physics Institutions Institute of Laboratory Medicine and Pathobiochemistry, Justus Liebig University Giessen and Philipps University Marburg Borros Arneth is a physician and laboratory scientist at the Institute of Laboratory Medicine and Pathobiochemistry, Molecular Diagnostics of the university hospital of Giessen and Marburg (UKGM), and the author of a long-running proposal in the foundations of physics that mass and electric charge are two components of a single physical quantity . He works in two fields at once, and the scale of both is worth stating, since this page previously carried neither. Some ninety-nine papers under his name are indexed in PubMed, chiefly in immunology, oncology and clinical laboratory medicine. Separately, and continuously from at least 2008 to 2024, he has pursued the mass–charge proposal through a series of papers in the peer-reviewed journal Physics Essays . Medical and laboratory career Arneth's institutional affiliation is the Institute of Laboratory Medicine and Pathobiochemistry, Molecular Diagnostics, at the Hospital of the Universities of Giessen and Marburg, associated with both Justus Liebig University Giessen and Philipps University Marburg . His indexed output is large and entirely conventional. It runs to immunology and immunometabolism, tumour microenvironment and breast-cancer biology, coagulation and mesenteric ischaemia after cardiac surgery, molecular diagnostics and neuroimmunology, in journals including Molecular Cancer , Cancer Letters , Cells , Scientific Reports and Journal of Neurology . Much of it is recent and collaborative, with co-authors in Germany, Egypt, Saudi Arabia and elsewhere. None of this bears on the physics, and it is recorded here only so
