He is listed in The Worldwide List of Dissident Scientists . Work Dameron published "Flux Leakage Tests for the Marinov Motor" in the journal Apeiron (Vol. 7, No. 3–4, 2000), reporting a series of tests intended to evaluate the effect of magnetic flux leakage on the motor's behaviour. He concludes that leakage may play a significant part in the observed effect but does not fully account for the motor's operation.
