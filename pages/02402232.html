GTM2026-main072 Opinion dynamics in a 2-level generalized Erdős–Rényi model Igor Zhukov , Aleksandra Mikhalchenko avmikhalchenko@etu.ru The study is devoted to the opinion dynamics in a social network modeled by the generalized Erdős–Rényi graph. The network is divided into two parts: "city" (high density of connections) and "suburb" (low density of connections), which reflects the real structure of social interactions. The purpose of this study is to investigate the impact of network topology on the structure of opinion change: the influence of the location of the stubborn vertex, the influence of the number of vertices in subnetworks. Model and parameters: We consider a generalized Erdős-Renyi graph containing 2 subnets: a "city" (G_1 (n_1,p_1 )) and a "suburb" (G_2 (n_2,p_2 )), where n_i — number of vertices, p_i — edge probability; the edge probability between a vertex in the city and a vertex in the suburb is also assumed to be p_2. Opinion x_i (t)∈{0,1}, where 0 — complete disagreement, 1 — full consent. The states of the vertices change according to the voter model with discrete time, which is defined by a certain transition function. There is one stubborn vertex S of degree 1 such that x_S (t)=1 at any moment t. The initial distribution of opinions: all vertices (except for the stubborn one) have a value of 0, with a value of 1. Stop criteria: reaching a stationary state (all opinions take the value 1) or T_max=1000 steps.
