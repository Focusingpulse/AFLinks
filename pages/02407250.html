10. What Does Theosophy Mean For People Today? — GA 34. Essays on Anthroposophy from the Journals “Lucifer” and “Lucifer-Gnosis” 1903-1908

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 2,407,250
What is proclaimed today as Theosophy is by no means a new spiritual doctrine. What is new is that people are speaking and working publicly in its spirit, and that societies are being founded for its cultivation, to which anyone can have access. Previously, its work was done in societies that did not go public.
