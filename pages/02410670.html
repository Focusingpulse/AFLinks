Sympathetic Vibratory Physics

The term "loosh" was coined by Robert Monroe, who studied out-of-body experiences. In his writings (particularly Far Journeys), he described loosh as a kind of energy produced by sentient beings, especially through strong emotions. While fear and suffering were common examples, he also mentioned love, joy, and passion as forms of loosh. So, is there positive loosh? Yes. Loosh is not inherently negative. It is emotional energy, and can come from positive emotions like: * Love * Compassion * Joy * Awe * Gratitude Positive loosh refers to emotional energy generated by uplifting, joyful, or loving experiences. It's less commonly discussed but is part of the broader idea that loosh includes all potent emotional output, not just the negative kind. Awareness of negative loosh, then transmute into positive loosh [anon]
