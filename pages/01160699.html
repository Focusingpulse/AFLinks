Abstract Purpose To evaluate the impact of a respiratory averaged computed tomography attenuation correction (RACTAC) instead of a standard single-phase computed tomography (CT) attenuation correction (CTAC) map on the quantitative measures of coronary 18F-NaF uptake in PET/CT. Methods This study comprised 23 patients who underwent 18F-NaF coronary PET in a hybrid PET/CT system, employing 18F-NaF (250MBq).
