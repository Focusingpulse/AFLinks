Marko Rodin Residence Indian Wells, CA, United States Nationality USA Known for Vortex-Based Mathematics; the Rodin coil Scientific career Fields Independent Researcher , Inventor Marko Rodin is an American independent researcher and inventor, the originator of Vortex-Based Mathematics (VBM) and of the toroidal winding pattern known as the Rodin coil . The coil is among the most widely replicated devices in the amateur new energy community, and a substantial body of experimental reports on it is catalogued on this wiki. Origin of Vortex-Based Mathematics By Rodin's own account, the number system did not begin as a physics investigation but as a religious inquiry . Raised in the Bahá'í Faith , he set out to find what he took to be the precise mystical significance of the Most Great Name of God, and converted its superlative form Abhá into numbers using the Abjad system, in which each letter of the Arabic alphabet carries a numerical value. Taking A = 1, b = 2, h = 5, á = 1, the letters sum to 9 — a number given repeated significance in Bahá'í scripture. From that starting point he developed the patterns he came to call Vortex-Based Mathematics. This origin is worth stating plainly because it explains the structure of the system, which is organised throughout around the number 9 and around digit sums. It should also be said that VBM is not Bahá'í doctrine and carries no standing within that religion, which cautions against numerology. Vortex-Based Mathematics The system works in modulo-9 arithmetic : every number is reduced by repeatedly summing its digits until a single value from 1 to 9 remains. Two features are then treated as fundamental. The first is the doubling sequence . Repeatedly doubling and reducing gives 1 → 2 → 4 → 8 → 7 (16) → 5 (32) → 1 (64), a closed six-step loop; halving traverses the same loop in reverse.
