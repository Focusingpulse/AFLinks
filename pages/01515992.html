For producing high-tension or high-frequency currents from a Ruhmkorff coil, a second primary coil 5 and a condenser 7 are connected across the interrupter 4 &c., so that the discharges of the condenser set up oscillations in the coil 5. The secondary 6 of the coil 5 may be connected to the secondary 3 of the usual primary coil 2, and the discharges from the secondaries may be utilized simultaneously or independently. A cautery 9 &c. may be connected in circuit with the coil 5 and the condenser. GB190629086 -- Improvements in, and relating to, Exciting Ruhmkorff and other Electric Coils. A reactance coil with an iron core consisting of a long bar or bundle of wires or thin strips is used for exciting a Ruhmkorff coil or other appliance where sudden generation of current is desired.
