Hydrostatic equilibrium

· archived # 1,459,856
In fluid mechanics , hydrostatic equilibrium or hydrostatic balance (also known as hydrostasy) is the condition of a fluid or plastic solid at rest . This occurs when external forces such as gravity are balanced by a pressure-gradient force . For instance, the pressure-gradient force prevents gravity from collapsing Earth's atmosphere into a thin, dense shell, whereas gravity prevents the pressure gradient force from diffusing the atmosphere into space . {https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrostatic_equilibrium] See Also Balance Equilibrium
