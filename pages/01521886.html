The possibility of the simultaneous coexistence of Newton instantaneous long-range interaction and Faraday-Maxwell short-range interaction is pointed out. Overview The Role of "Action-at-a-Distance" in the Electro-Magnetic Field Radiation Produced by an Accelerated Charge is a three-page note by Andrew E Chubykalo of the Escuela de Física, Universidad Autónoma de Zacatecas, published in Apeiron volume 4, numbers 2–3 (April–July 1997), pages 38–40. Its argument is compact and can be stated in full. Chubykalo sets up a thought experiment with a point charge moving along the X axis, and considers a point of observation P lying on that same axis, ahead of the charge. Along the direction of motion the magnetic field vanishes, H ≡ 0, which follows from Maxwell's equations by symmetry. The Poynting vector S = ( c /4π) E × H is therefore identically zero everywhere on the axis, and so is the momentum density P = S / c ². But the energy density W = ( E ² + H ²)/8π is not zero on the axis, and it changes with time.
