Introduction Stationary plasma thruster (SPT) is one of the plasma thruster (PT) types systematically applied in space since the past century [1]. Despite the fact that some SPT characteristics (efficiency, specific impulse, lifetime), are dominated by the ion plasma thruster (IPT) parameters, there is a great interest to in this type of PT. It is seen from a number of SPT significant advantages [2]. First of all, if to compare SPT with IPT, it has relatively simple design and technology of production. Second, SPT does not require a complex power supply unit and this is very important for the spacecraft design.
