The standard account James Clerk Maxwell built the theory in stages — "On Physical Lines of Force" (1861–62), which developed it from a mechanical model of molecular vortices in an aether ; "A Dynamical Theory of the Electromagnetic Field" (1865); and the Treatise on Electricity and Magnetism (1873). The decisive step was the displacement current , a term added to Ampère's circuital law which makes the system consistent with conservation of charge and, crucially, yields a wave equation.
