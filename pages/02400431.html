The central, executed result of this paper is a sharp separation between two read modes. A stream — the composed recurrence with its dependency ledger — is information-complete: an eight-step composed fold is exactly injective and reversible (200,000/200,000 distinct; 8/8 inputs recovered), and the SHA-256 schedule replays forward exactly from a parent ledger (48/48 rows).
