There not being a quorum present on January 25th (only nine Members being in attendance instead of ten as required by the Rules) the meeting stood adjourned to February 1st. The points of interest were, however, informally discussed on the 25th, the President in the chair. An audited statement of the receipts and expenditure for 1888 was presented to the meeting, and appears in another page. A statement of the current assets and liabilities at the close of 1888 showed that the position of the Society had very considerably improved during the year, there being now a balance of liabilities of £53 only.
