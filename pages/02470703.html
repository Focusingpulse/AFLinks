The plans of the Pan-American Exposition to be held at Niagara Falls during six months of 1901 are sufficiently advanced at this time for us to give an outline of the scope and purposes of this important exhibition and describe some of its features. First of all, it is to be a practical illustration of the vast and varied industrial resources of the Continent of America, its chief aim being to stimulate trade, commerce, and transportation throughout the length and breadth of the Western Hemisphere.
