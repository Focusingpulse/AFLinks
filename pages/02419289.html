Journal of Borderland Research >> Vol. 16 >> No. 08 Screencap: "Earth vs. The Flying Saucers" (1960) — wtf-film.com [16] WHEN THE SPACE PEOPLE COME By Associate Frank Gerard This article is not to be taken seriously. It is neither scientific nor educational. It consists merely of a musings about the assurances said to come from Space People that they are anxious to make mass landings and come among us and help us, once they know their reception will be friendly and no hostile action will be taken against them. Let's speculate as to what might really happen if they did land. We will assume that Major Donald Keyhoe has succeeded in persuading the Air Force to confess its duplicity, and that it now accepts the UFOs as real objects under intelligent control. Let's also assume an official admission that the visitors have no harmful intentions toward us and an official readiness to meet them when they do land. First, let us take up the idea that the Space People are capable of taking over TV and radio, and, regardless of what programs are going on at the time, can blanket the country with their message. Since it has not yet been tried, even in a small way, we must assume it is beyond their abilities or there is some other reason for ignoring this method of mass communication. Barging in on our TV and radio programs would be a bit of a jar to us but it would lessen the shock of a mass landing.
