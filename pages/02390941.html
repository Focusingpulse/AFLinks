Chapter 6: The Transformation of Dream Life — Knowledge of Higher Worlds and its Attainment (1947)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 2,390,941
An intimation that the student has reached or will soon reach the stage of development described in the preceding chapter will be found in the change which comes over his dream life. His dreams, hitherto confused and haphazard, now begin to assume a more regular character. Their pictures begin to succeed each other in sensible connection, like the thoughts and ideas of daily life. He can discern in them law, cause, and effect.
