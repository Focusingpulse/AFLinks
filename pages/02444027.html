Twenty-sixth Lecture — GA 343. Lectures and Courses on Christian Religious Work II

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 9 October 1921 · archived # 2,444,027
a.m., Dornach Translated by Steiner Online Library Twenty-sixth Lecture My dear friends! Today we have something to discuss that is very intimately connected with what can be felt from the whole of today's spirit of the age as the need for religious renewal. First of all, I have to present to you what can lead to the creation of a kind of breviary. This breviary should be what gives the pastor the strength to work, and perhaps I may take this opportunity to point out that in this respect, too, we should not confuse the intellectual with what, in the truest sense, can be the religious impulse
