He is listed in The Worldwide List of Dissident Scientists . Background Klingman earned a PhD in physics and worked as a research physicist in atomic and molecular physics. His doctoral work was later published as The Automatic Theory of Physics , which describes how a robot might derive a theory of physics from pattern recognition and entropy.
