The Aether Motor and the years of Gene's demos (2000-2004) Since our meeting in Denver, in 1996, Gene had been interested in the PAGD-driven energy converter. Our correspondence at that time was mostly through snail-mail - and it warms our hearts reading it. Down below we will dedicate a section to this part of our relationship and its early years. Gene's enthusiasm to know, to understand, to test our plasma technology, and to seek sponsorship for it, comes across those letters like limpid water. From 1997 to 1999, Gene made constant efforts, through Cold Fusion Associates and Cold Fusion Technologies Inc (CFTI), to find financial support for the commercialization of the PAGD Converter and Inverter technologies. A cycle of hope and disappointement was replayed with each of these prospective sponsors, whether the contact had gone through us, Gene or any of our other friends similarly engaged in this quest (like Uri, Rui, Malgosia). In December 1999, Mike Carrell, who at the time worked for Infinite Energy , came to visit us to witness an open-box PAGD demonstration. Out of consideration for Carrell, we also graced him with a demonstration of the early stages of the Aether Motor. It was during the following Summer that Gene first came to visit us - right after he had fired Kooistra and Hawkins Kirk and had asked for Carrell's resignation.
