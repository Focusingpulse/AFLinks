(Received for publication 23 June 1989) A~traet--Eight possible explanations for the heat produced in the Fleischmann-Pons effect are examined with the various conservative assumptions concerning the quantities used. No individual explanation is sufficient to explain the heat produced. All of them together can only explain heat as much as 3 W cm -3.
