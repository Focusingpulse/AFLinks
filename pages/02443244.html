Lecture Four — GA 326. The Origins of Natural Science (1966)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 27 December 1922 · archived # 2,443,244
, Dornach Translated by Maria St. Goar Lecture Four [ 1 ] In the last lecture, I spoke of a former view of life from which the modern scientific view has evolved. It still combined the qualitative with the form-related or geometrical elements of mathematics, the qualitative with the quantitative. One can therefore look back at a world conception in which the triangle or another geometrical form was an inner experience no matter whether the form referred to the surface of a given body or to its path of movement.
