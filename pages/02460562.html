All kinds of weird time shifts were taking place in just one very large area of space due to their various trials trying to achieve their goal. These time shifts are what drew the attention of the Voyager crew. It seems the captain of the time ship had lost his wife and daughter in a battle and he was trying to bring back a timeline where they would have lived...he didn't care what the other permutations were, just that his family was restored....he and his crew also DID NOT AGE signficantly while in their ship which was protected by creating its own time field and had apparently been doing this experiments over and over for hundreds of years.
