womb of earth

· archived # 1,497,413
"You have wondered much about the unfolding of a tree, or a human body, from its microscopic seed . Many years are consumed in that process after you have put the seed in your own yard to generate an electric current for again unfolding the record contained in that patterned electronic tube . The electronic tube seeds of Nature have their film patterns enfolded within them. Man has to carry the patterns for his cinema separately, also his projection machine - whereas Nature's projection machine is the womb of earth . It will always reproject the patterned microscopic image into a three dimensional form if the conditions for that projection are right ones."
