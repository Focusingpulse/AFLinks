My Ether Model Brain Dump Watch this video on YouTube Talk Type Video Speaker Lori Anne Gardi Date 14 October 2023 Platform YouTube Recording Recording My Ether Model Brain Dump is a video by Lori Anne Gardi , published on 14 October 2023 on her FractalWoman YouTube channel. Summary An unstructured survey of the aether model Gardi has been building toward across the series, presented as clues rather than a finished theory, and drawing together the Bose–Einstein condensate, ferrofluids, two of Maxwell's equations, the isopotential diagrams from her earlier experiments, and the electron orbitals. Why it is a brain dump She explains the title at the outset. Every time she sits down to make this video she gets bogged down: the model has too many aspects and they are difficult to explain in words. What she offers instead is the list of concepts the model has to accommodate — the Bose–Einstein condensate, magnetism, electrostatics, isopotentials, electron–positron pairs, the Ferrocell, Maxwell's equations, fluid dynamics — and, underneath all of it, the fractal paradigm , which she names as the reason she is doing any of this in the first place. The hypothesis is stated broadly. The aether exists and is the medium for the propagation of light, but it is much more than that: it is the foundation from which everything else emerges, the source of everything observed. Starting from the coldest possible universe Her point of departure is the Bose–Einstein condensate — a state in which supercooled particles behave as one large particle governed by a single wave function. Cool a gas toward absolute zero, she explains, and it stops being many particles with a complicated wave function and becomes something like a blank slate: no structure, one simple wave function. From this she takes her cosmological premise, and states it as the exact inverse of the standard one.
