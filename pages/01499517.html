We will also provide information on material and supply sources in order to build systems. All necessary data and support specs will be made available, however we must disclaim any liability for the infringement of patents, when such equipment may be offered for sale and openly marketed. In addition, we must disclaim any liability from personal injury which may occur during construction or operation of the equipment built from such Space Energy Journal plans. We will be depending on the test data on the equipment from the inventor/developer to alert and warn us of operational problems before all member/builders complete their projects, so that a normal degree of safety will be in place for such project work. Contrary to the policies of some other alternate energy journals, we do intend top endorse and support free energy units and systems which show a positive test and track record.
