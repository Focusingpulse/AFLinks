This goes back to how elemental charge is/was measured via the Millikan oil drop method. Note that the oil droplet and the gap between the capacitor plates (filled with air) are all of macroscopic dimensions. A charge is deposited on these oil droplets and the corresponding electrostatic force measured, and from this, the value of charge is computed. As is given in the well known equation: e^2 = 2ahEc Where e = elemental charge a = alpha E = permitivitty = kE_o u = permeability = iu_o (i = magnetic susceptability) h = Planck's constant c = li
