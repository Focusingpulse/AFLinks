The Round Robin >> Vol. 03 >> No. 02 [2] Will the Navy Sink the Remaining Bikini Fleet Ships ? (News flash reported to Round Robin by Walter Graham ) Boston, Jan. 19, 1947 (UP): The Boston Herald today said in a copyrighted story that the Navy is going to scuttle the remaining ships of the Bikini 'atomic fleet' because the ships are too "hot" to be allowed to remain afloat. The ships that appeared to survive, actually, did not do so . The Herald said "They are contaminated beyond all hope. They cannot even be used for scrap." The navy has decided to tow the ships into deep water and open their seadocks, the newspaper reported. "The Prince Eugen, which rolled over and sank, merely got a headstart on her fellow guinea pigs." The Herald said that the Navy had been reluctant to disclose its plan for sinking the remaining ships because it feared adverse public reaction to such wholesale destruction of ships by atomic power. In Washington, the Navy denies that it has decided definitely to scuttle the remaining Bikini ships, saying "No final decision has been made.
