Einstein’s Relativity Led to Failure of the Scientific Method - Roger Anderton Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Date 30 September 2023 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording Einstein’s Relativity Led to Failure of the Scientific Method - Roger Anderton was a live session on 30 September 2023 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. Einstein’s relativity needed a lot of modifications, because a lot of the things that Einstein said – he got wrong. As part of the Scientific method – a theory can be modified to sort out its problems. In the case of Einstein’s relativity – too many modifications were needed – and that leaves it confusing as to what the theory is supposed to now be. With some people believing one thing about relativity, and other people believing something else. For instance – some people believe that Einstein’s relativity is a relativity theory while other people believe calling it relativity is a misnomer. This confusing mess has resulted in the failure of the Scientific method. Summary Roger J Anderton 's argument that the trouble with relativity is not any particular error but damage to the scientific method itself. Ian J Cowan hosted. A one-way audio fault meant Anderton could not hear the room for much of the session, and heard everything with a delay when it was restored; he began typing replies before it was pointed out that the audience could hear him perfectly well. It is also the session at which Vladimir S Netchitailo and Stephan J G Gift both appeared in the green room for the first time, brought in by Nick Percival . The argument Anderton's thesis: a theory can properly be modified in the light of results, but relativity has needed so many modifications, made so quietly, that nobody can now say what the theory is — and a theory that cannot be stated cannot be tested.
