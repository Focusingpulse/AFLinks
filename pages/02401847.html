GTM2026-main032 On one equilibrium in a non-cooperative game with fuzzy interference Konstantin Kudryavtsev1 1 Financial University under the Government of the Russian Federation, Russia kudrkn@gmail.com A non-cooperative N-person game with fuzzy interference is considered. For this game, the concepts of guaranteed Nash equilibria in the strong and weak senses are introduced. The idea of constructing such solutions is to combine the concepts of the "analoque of saddle point" proposed by V.I. Zhukovsky [1] for non-cooperative games under uncertainty and the strong-sense solution for a single-criterion problem with fuzzy information [2]. A two-person linear-quadratic game with fuzzy interference is considered as a model example. REFERENCES 1. Zhukovskiy, V.I., Kudryavtsev, K. N. (2013). Equilibrating conflicts under uncertainty. I.
