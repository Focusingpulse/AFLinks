LATEST WORD ON COLD FUSION Edmund Storms, "The Latest Word about Cold Fusion," 21st Century Science & Technology", Summer 1998, pp 15-18. EDITOR'S SUMMARY Cold fusion, chemically-assisted nuclear reactions, low-energy nuclear reactions, or whatever phrase you use, is alive and well according to Edmund Storms (retired scientist from Los Alamos National Laboratory). Storms reports on the various presentations made at the 7th International Conference on Cold Fusion that was held in Vancouver, April 19-24, 1998. Storms cites several reproducible results as presented by various authors: Roger Stringham, Karabut & Savvitomova, Araka, Les Case, Celani, Ohmori, Tom Claytor, Melvin Miles, etc. Storms concludes his report with the following comment: "Until overcome, this skeptical attitude will continue to influence denial of funding and patents and, thereby, hamper a normal development which promises great benefits to the general public."
