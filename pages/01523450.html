Michael R. Treat , MD, is an American general surgeon, medical inventor and entrepreneur. He spent some twenty-five years on the surgical faculty at Columbia-Presbyterian in New York, where he published extensively on laser tissue welding and on the biology of laparoscopic surgery, and he went on to found companies in surgical instrumentation and surgical robotics. He appears on this wiki for something else entirely: a single 2011 paper, with David Rosignoli , reporting balloon-borne measurements of atmospheric electricity and asking whether it could be tapped for power. That paper is described below alongside the conventional career, because the two are easy to confuse and the record is not what the listing alone might suggest. Education and medical career MD — qualified as a physician; specialised in general surgery Treat was a member of the Department of Surgery at Columbia University , and describes a twenty-five-year career as a general surgeon at Columbia-Presbyterian. His research output there is substantial and entirely mainstream: some seventy-eight papers are indexed in PubMed. Three strands stand out. Laser tissue welding and soldering.
