Lecture Eight — GA 176. Aspects of Human Evolution (1987)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 24 July 1917 · archived # 2,423,464
, Berlin Translated by Rita Stebbing Lecture Eight [ 1 ] Alongside the content of these lectures I am concerned to show that truth, in the spiritual sense, is a living reality. It is especially essential in our time that a feeling should develop for the fact that truth is something living. What has life is different from one time to another; at one stage it may be formless, at another it may have a definite structure. A young child is very different from an old person.
