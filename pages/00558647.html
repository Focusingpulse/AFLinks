1 MAGIC SQUARES OF THE 4TH AND 8TH ORDERS: MATHEMATICAL PROJECTIONS AND PRACTICAL APPLICATIONS Andrey V. Voron (anvoron1@yandex.ru) August 14, 2026 Abstract The study demonstrates that projecting order-4 and order-8 magic squares onto a tesseract preserves their fundamental numerical invariants. Similarly, the 4×4 Dürer's magic square is projected onto the vertices of an octagonal prism with internal spatial connections. The results obtained in this study open up prospects for utilizing multidimensional geometry to develop new data distribution algorithms. The presented mathematical symmetries can find practical applications in high-tech and theoretical disciplines. 1 Introduction The earliest unique 4×4 magic square was discovered in an 11th-century inscription in the Indian city of Khajuraho.
