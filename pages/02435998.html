, Dornach Translated by Henry B. Monges III. Karma and Freedom [ 1 ] Karma is best understood by contrasting it with that other impulse in man—the impulse which we indicate by the word freedom . Let us first, in a very crude way, I should say, place the question of karma before us. What does it signify? In human life we have to record the fact of successive earth lives. By feeling ourselves within a given earth life, we can look back—in thought at least, to begin with—and see how this present earth life is a repetition of a number of previous earth lives.
