Everything I need to know, I learned from Noah's Ark. Funny but has a lot of truth to it! Noah's Ark Everything I need to know, I learned from Noah's Ark. ONE: Don't miss the boat. FIVE: Don't listen to critics; just get on with the job that needs to be done. SIX: Build your future on high ground. SEVEN: For safety's sake, travel in pairs. EIGHT: Speed isn't always an advantage. The snails were on board with the cheetahs. NINE: When you're stressed, float awhile. TEN: Remember, the Ark was built by amateurs; the Titanic by professionals.
