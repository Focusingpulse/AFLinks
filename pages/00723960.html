An information theoretic formulation of game theory, II Chris Goddard November 27, 2018 Abstract This short article follows an earlier document, wherein I indicated how the foundations of game theory could be reformulated within the lens of a more information theoretic and topological approach. Building on said work, herein I intend to generalise this to meta games, where one game (the meta-game) is built on top of a game, and then to meta-meta-games. Finally I indicate how one might take these ideas further, in terms of constructing frameworks to study policies, which relate to the solution of various algebraic invariants. 1 Foreword Herein I intend to build on [8] in order to indicate how one can make sense of ’meta-games’, such as simplified poker. Determination of an approach to computing strategies for optimal play is discussed.
