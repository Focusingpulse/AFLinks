Introduction We consider oriented circles and oriented lines. Two figures are said to touch if the orientations at the point of tangency are the same. Circles with counter￾clockwise orientation have radius with plus sign, otherwise minus. A line segment AB has also length with signs, and the line segment with initial point B and end point A is denoted by −AB. Hence we have AB + (−AB) = 0, which is denoted by AB − AB = 0.
