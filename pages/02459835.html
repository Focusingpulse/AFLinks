A permanent speaker magnet on the level with the bismuth about 3" from the reach of the arm. Well, it was set up in my office in the evening to give the bismuth time to settle. Comming in the morning, I just peeked around the doorway to see the bismuth in an irrelevant possition half way between the doorway and the magnet. ..
