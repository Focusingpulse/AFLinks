6. Goethe's Way of Knowledge — Goethean Science (1988)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 1,554,592
In June 1794, Johann Gottlieb Fichte sent the first sections of his Theory of Science 45 Wissenschaftslehre to Goethe. The latter wrote back to the philosopher on June 24: “As far as I am concerned, I will owe you the greatest thanks if you finally reconcile me with the philosophers, with whom I can never do without and with whom I have never been able to unite myself.” What the poet is here seeking from Fichte is what he sought earlier from Spinoza and later from Schelling and Hegel: a philosophical world view that would be in accordance with his way of thinking.
