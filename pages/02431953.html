Allergy Relief ~Breathe It In This Module is designed to assist you in eliminating the affects of allergies by correcting your immune systems response to allergen producing substances. These substances include, but are not limited to, pollen, animal dander and dust. By balancing your immune system so that it does not overreact to these substances, you will find relief from itchy and watering eyes, coughing, runny nose, rashes, itchy throat, etc. This Module is not intended to cure or treat serious allergic reactions. If you are acutely allergic to a substance, this Module will not take the place of your usual treatment. If you do experience a severe allergic reaction, please see a doctor as soon as possible or call 911.
