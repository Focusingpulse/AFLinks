Big Bang or Big Bust Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Speaker Glenn Borchardt Date 21 November 2020 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording Big Bang or Big Bust was a live session presented by Glenn Borchardt on 21 November 2020 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. Dr. Glenn Borchardt has spent decades revealing the contradictions and paradoxes of the big bang theory. Glenn will be going over a list of falsifying evidence to this problematic theory as well as what he sees as a better alternative. From the age of the universe, to new astronomical observations, to impossible paradoxes - it appears more and more like the big bang is a big bust. Join us as we discuss everything Big Bang with Dr. Glenn Borchardt. Summary Glenn Borchardt on the big bang, in conversation with David de Hilster , who worked through slides drawn from the course Borchardt was preparing. The infinite universe theory itself was deferred to a later session; this one was about the case against. A buzzword's career They opened with an ngram Borchardt had made. Expanding universe rises from the 1930s with Hubble's work; big bang hardly registers before 1960 and takes off after 1970 — Lemaître's book of 1950 needing a decade to work through. Steady state, the theory the big bang displaced, declines as it rises. From about 2000 both flatten while multiverse and parallel universe climb, which Borchardt read as reform attempts: modifications meant to rescue the big bang from its own problems. He noted that multiverse is an oxymoron, uni meaning one, and that all of these — steady state included — are creation theories, not infinite-universe theories. The objections The elderly galaxy problem. On NASA's own diagram of the expansion, the Hubble Deep Field sits at the far edge, and what is actually there is galaxies like ours — ten thousand of them in a patch a tenth the size of the moon, spirals among them, no thinning with distance.
