Overview This is Volume 8, Number 9 of New Energy News , the monthly bulletin of the Institute for New Energy (INE), issued as "Special Symposium Issue Part II" for September 2001. It is a compilation rather than a research paper: seven items of varying length, most of them submitted by Hal Fox or Patrick G Bailey , reprinted or summarized from outside sources with editorial commentary attached. The subjects are missile-defence testing, a heat-resistant coating invented by an amateur, energy-policy advocacy, classified energy patents, a Russian claim of a new current source, wind power economics, and — at the greatest length — Bailey's own position paper on whether UFO and antigravity research belong within the INE's remit. The unifying thread across the issue is institutional rather than technical. Every item is chosen to illustrate the same claim: that promising energy and materials work is being obstructed, either by credentialism inside professional science or by deliberate classification outside it. The issue contains no original experiment, no data and no derivation. Its interest to a reader of this wiki is documentary — it records what the new-energy community was arguing about in 2001, in that community's own words, and it includes an unusually frank statement by the INE's president about where he wanted the boundary of respectable inquiry drawn.
