Four-component tuples splitting Yuly Shipilevsky Toronto, Ontario, Canada Abstract. We consider a problem of splitting of the four- element tuples of integer numbers into two two-element tu- ples, providing a minimum of the difference of sums of the corresponding their elements. We conjecturing with the co- rresponding algorithm for its solution.
