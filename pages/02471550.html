O'Neill Brooklyn Eagle February 8th, 1932 Page number(s): 4 FINDS SUPER RAY Planets Send Out Impulses Which May Change an Individual's Course of Life — Interplanetary Communication Possible By JOHN J. O'NEILL. Science Editor of The Eagle Nikola Tesla. A super-cosmic ray far more penetrating than any of the cosmic rays now being studied emanates from the sun, according to Nikola Tesla, the famous scientist, electrical engineer and inventor. The cosmic rays, penetrating 14 feet of lead, now being studied are merely a secondary radiation produced by the super cosmic ray when they impinge on cosmic dust scattered throughout space, declares Dr. Tesla. A great deal of effort has been spent by many scientists in this country and Europe in trying to locate the source of the cosmic rays. They seem to come from all parts of the heavens with equal intensity. Generated Out of Space The reason they come with equal intensity from all parts of space is due to the fact that they are generated out in space, each quanta of the cosmic ray coming from a dust particle which has been struck by the super cosmic ray from the sun. Dr. Tesla states that his discovery is not at all recent but dates back to 1897. His announcement at that time was quite premature as far as public interest was concerned.
