Dorothy Gormley, one of the Waldorf-Astoria maids whose work it is to announce callers to the persons stopping at the hotel, feels that a knowledge of languages is almost necessary to accomplish her task as it should be done. She is a pretty girl and that's perhaps why every man who goes there rather likes the idea of having himself announced by Dorothy. She has a smile for all persons and a fetching brogue. Now that brogue does not go with some of the names she has to announce over the telephone. Yesterday two men called on Mr. Nikola Tesla, the electrical wizard. Dorothy attempted to make Mr. Tesla understand who they were, but it was a terrible job. She began: — "Sure Mr. Tesla Dr. Nikola (pronounced with long "i"). Vel-well-in-ro-yes 'tis Vel-rrrro — which — no vich, and then ro" — Dorothy had to give it up and try just spelling the name.
