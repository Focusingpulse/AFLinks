A subsequent publication demonstrated the uniqueness of this particular right-angled triangle due to the identified double mathematical identity [2]. Furthermore, a prior study [3] established a connection between the geometry of the King’s Chamber and the values of the smallest primitive Pythagorean triple — "3, 4, 5" (Figure). In that context, the "unit" value for this Pythagorean triple was specified as 2.618… meters (notably, the number 2.61803… is the square of the Golden Ratio, a dimensionless irrational constant).
