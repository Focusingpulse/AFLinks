GTM2026-main011 Strategic Design of Channel Structures and Digital Services for Reducing Returns in E￾commerce Platforms He Liu1 , Tianting Xu2,3 , Yan Zeng1 , Shouyang Wang3,4,5 1 School of Management Science and Engineering, Central University of Finance and Economics, Changping District, Beijing 102206, China 2 School of Advanced Interdisciplinary Sciences, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, No. 1 Yanqihu East Road, Huairou District, Beijing 101408, China., China 3 Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences, 55 Zhongguancun East Road, Haidian District, Beijing 100090, China 4 School of Economics and Management, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, 80 Zhongguancun East Road, Haidian District, Beijing 100090, China 5 School of Entrepreneurship and Management, ShanghaiTech University, 393 Middle Huaxia Road, Pudong, Shanghai, 201210, China liuhe@cufe.edu.cn E-commerce platforms face severe product returns and are adopting digital services to reduce returns across marketplace, reselling, and hybrid channel structures. These services not only affect pricing within each channel but also interact with the channel structure. We build a game-theoretic model to examine the platform’s joint decisions on channel structure and digital service deployment, yielding two key findings. First, by reducing mismatched purchases, digital services increase consumer utility and enhance demand, which supports higher equilibrium retail prices. Therefore, the platform adopts digital services under all channels, but the optimal service level varies by mode
