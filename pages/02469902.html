He began to look a little more interested, and urged his visitor to tell him what the scheme was and also to inform him what he could do for him. “Well, before I describe the invention,” said the prosperous man, “I’ll tell you what we’ve done. We’ve discovered a way to transmit power with absolutely no loss in transmission. Absolutely no loss!
