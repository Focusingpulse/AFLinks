Lecture VII - 346. Lectures to Priests: The Apocalypse

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 11 September 1924 · archived # 2,444,070
Lecture VII Before we go any further in our study of the Apocalypse we must add one more method to read it properly, which however is taken more from outside. Then the main thing is to apply what we read in the Apocalypse to our present time. In order to do this, we must first look at the spiritual foundations from which this Apocalypse arose. But I don't mean that one should do this by trying to explain a book through one's times in a trivial historical sense. This is not really applicable to writings like the Apocalypse which are conceived out of the spiritual world in the way I described.
