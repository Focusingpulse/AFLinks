Any water formed would have been immediately broken back up into hydrogen and oxygen plasma. More reasons are listed. Earth’s oceans took 700 million years to form. This is because: 1. Earth was far too hot in its beginning and earlier stages of evolution to maintain any large quantity of dihydrogen monoxide (water). The water molecules would be broken apart as soon as they formed. 2. It takes hundreds of millions of years for large quantities of water to form on a star, from its own oxygen and hydrogen gas combining together. 3. It takes hundreds of millions of years for a star to cool down enough to make a solid boundary between itself and an “ocean of water”. In other words, a crust needs to form, and that takes huge amounts of time, as is evidenced by the geological record. 4. The right pressures need to be on the star for the water to form. When the star is young, the internal pressures are too great. As the interior pressure loosens up, the hydrogen and oxygen can combine together in large quantities. 5. It takes extreme amounts of time to dissipate the heat of ocean water formation, esp. since the Earth when it was a gas giant was adiabatic.
