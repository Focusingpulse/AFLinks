Thanks to that, we will de ne a new multiplication between vectors which is analogue to what we have seen in our previous paper viXra:2510.0152 without complex numbers. This operation will allow us to construct a 3D fractal set which contains the Mandelbrot set in the planes OXY and OXZ.
