AN INTERNATIONAL SHOW. Charles F. Wingate, the Director, Asserts That the Exposition Promises to Be Entertaining and Instructive — The Display Will Cover a Floor Space of Over Three Acres — Sanitary Living Pictures One of the Chief Features. An International health exposition will be held at the Grand Central Palace in New York for five weeks, beginning April 25 next, under auspices and with a breadth of scope and an amount of invested capital of such a magnitude as are a guarantee of conscientious and intelligent effort, and which cannot fail to awaken widespread popular interest. A number of food shows have taken place in New York and other cities, beside a collection of building materials, notably in New York, Brooklyn and Chicago, while there is a permanent hygienic museum at Washington. But no attempt has hitherto been made to gather together under one roof the wonderful variety of objects which illustrate the immensely wide and important field of sanitary science and the marvelous progress which has been made in that direction of recent years. The time is ripe for such an undertaking.
