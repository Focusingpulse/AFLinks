GC2026-STP091 Estimates of precipitating fluxes of energetic electrons during the magnetic storm of 10- 16 October 2017 based on magnetospheric spacecraft data Andrei G. Demekhov1 , Tatiana A. Yahnina1 , Tatiana A. Popova1 , Alexandra R. Ivanova2 , Vladimir V. Kalegaev2 1 Polar Geophysical Institute of the Kola Scientific Center of the Russian Academy of Sciences 2 Skobeltsyn Institute of Nuclear Physics, Lomonosov Moscow State University demekhov@pgia.ru We analyze precipitation of energetic electrons into the ionosphere during the geomagnetic storm of 10-16 October 2017. This storm was moderate (Dst min = -52 nT) but was accompanied by a permanent substorm activity. We use the data of Van Allen Probes spacecraft that had their apogee in late morning to afternoon sectors. Wave measurements in the extremely low frequency and very low frequency (ELF-VLF) ranges (~100 Hz to ~10 kHz) are employed to calculate the pitch-angle diffusion coefficients, and based these results and the measured fluxes of energetic electrons we estimate the precipitating electron fluxes. Energy spectra of precipitating electrons are estimated. These results are compared with NOAA/POES and MetOp observations. We compare possible roles of ducted whistler mode chorus waves and the ELF waves around and below the lower hybrid frequency (plasmaspheric hiss) in providing the precipitation of subrelativistic (several hundred keV) and energetic (~10-100 keV) electrons.
