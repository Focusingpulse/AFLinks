PROPERTIES OF β-OPEN SETS IN IDEAL MINIMAL SPACES S. JAFARI, T. NOIRI, N. RAJESH AND R. SARANYA Abstract. In this paper, we introduce and study the class of β-open sets and other related classes of notions in ideal minimal spaces. 1. Introduction The notion of minimal structures which is a generalization of a topol￾ogy on a given nonempty set was introduced by Popa and Noiri [17]. They also introduced the notion of m-continuous function as a function defined between a minimal structure and a topological space. They showed that the m-continuous functions have properties similar to those of continuous functions between topological spaces. However, continuities between two minimal structures have already been in lit￾erature (see [8], [9], [10] and [14]). Let X be a topological space and A ⊂ X. The closure of A and the interior of A are denoted by Cl(A) and Int(A), respectively. A subfamily m of the power set P(X) of a nonempty set X is called a minimal structure [17] on X if ∅ and X belong to m. By (X, m), we denote a nonempty set X with a minimal structure m on X. Let X be a set with | X |≤ 2, where | X | denotes the cardinality of X. Then any minimal structure on X is always a topology on X. Thus the study of minimal structure will be interesting when | X |> 2. The members of the minimal structure m are called m-open sets [17], and the pair (X, m) is called an m-space. The com￾plement of an m-open set is said to be m-closed [17]. The concept of ideals in topological spaces has been introduced and studied by Ku￾ratowski [7] and Vaidyanathasamy [19].
