When atmost one runner prevents loneliness Deepak Ponvel Chermakani deepakc@pmail.ntu.edu.sg Abstract: - Consider the problem of finding the time T at which none of the n-1 moving runners are in the arc Z of length 2/n centered at the stationary runner 0 in an instance of the Shifted Lonely Runner Conjecture (SLRC). We show two important results for prime n and for large n. Firstly, there exist atleast 3 time intervals during which atmost 1 moving runner is in Z for the SLRC, and, there exist atleast 4(n-1) time intervals during which atmost 1 moving runner is in Z for the LRC. Secondly, for both the SLRC and the LRC considered separately, there are atleast 2 different moving runners which prevent the loneliness of runner 0 by being the “atmost one runner in Z” at some time. 1. Introduction The Lonely Runner Conjecture (LRC) and the Shifted LRC (SLRC) with n runners, both ask whether there exists a time at which there are no moving runners in an arc of length 2/n centered at the stationary runner 0. While the SLRC has already been proved to be false [1], the LRC is still open. The best known minimal gaps for the LRC tend to 1/(2n) as n tends to infinity [2][3]. There is also a well-known configuration of moving runners with equal speeds and positioned equidistant from each other, that always ensures the presence of atleast one moving runner in Z, with an upper bound of 1/(2n) on the minimal gap. However, the LRC and SLRC offer hope since each runner has a unique speed. For both the LRC and SLRC, it is still not known whether it is possible to improve the minimal gap to f/n where 0.5 < f < 1, and f is rational. One approach to improving this minimal gap is to study variants of the SLRC and LRC, in the hope that some clue might be obtained to improve the minimal gap. In 2020, we developed a general approach for all n, to maximize the fraction of runners not in Z [5].
