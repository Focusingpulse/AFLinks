It is the most widely used welding type. It is also known as manual metal arc welding (MMA). In his welding technique a flux coated electrode which is also known as stick is used with a combination of high voltage and current that melt and create a shielding environment for the weld pool and protect it from reacting with atmospheric gasses e.g. oxygen and nitrogen. so it is also known as flux shielding arc welding, due to this property this technique can be used in outdoor welding projects.
