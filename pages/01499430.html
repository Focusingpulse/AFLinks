TAPPING EARTH'S ENERGY "The Prentice Earth Energy Tap," Nexus New Times Magazine, vol 6, no. 1, Dec 98-Jan 99, page 43. EDITOR'S DISCUSSION A Frank Wyatt Prentice patent issued September 18th, 1923, claims that power can be obtained from 500,000 cps energy fluctuations located about six inches above the earth. To tap this energy, Prentice used a half-mile of wire. In addition, he located a closed oscillatory loop antenna 18 feet in length about 20 feet from the "transmission antenna." If this is true, then anyone with a good oscilloscope can put up an 6-inch high line and determine the energy fluctuations at 500 kilohertz. Prentice claims to have been able to power fifty, sixty-watt lamps. In the thirties, Dr. Henry Moray, a Utah scientist was demonstrating the ability to light a bank of lamps in a similar fashion.
