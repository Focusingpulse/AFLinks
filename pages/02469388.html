Large Force at Work. Nikola Tesla Makes a Remarkable Prophecy in Connection with the Enterprise. Great Depth of the Canal. The Waters of the St. Lawrence To Be Diverted to Grasse River. The village of Massena, St. Lawrence county, N. Y., once noted as a summer resort, and largely visited on account of its sulphur springs and pure air, is the seat of one of the largest proposed pieces of engineering work this country has seen for some years, being exceeded only by the Chicago Drainage Canal. The village is located on the Grasse River, about three miles south of the St. Lawrence, of which the Grasse River is a tributary. The country intervening between these rivers is comparatively low and flat and furnishes pasture for a large number of dairy farms, for which this section of New York State is noted. The enterprise consists of diverting a portion of the water of the St. Lawrence River just above the Long Sault Rapids and conveying the same a distance of three and one tenth miles through a canal to Massena, at which point it has a fall of forty-five feet into the Grasse River. It is proposed to construct this canal and utilize the energy of the falling water to drive turbines, which in turn will drive dynamos generating electricity equivalent to 150,000 horse power.
