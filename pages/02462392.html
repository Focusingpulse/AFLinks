Qu Harmonics – The incomplete Universe Distinction That Must Continue Why C0/C1 Is the Only Possible Start, and the Mathematics So Far Driven by Dean A. Kulik October, 2026 Abstract We begin from one statement held whole — nothing can change; everything must change — and ask what the smallest world is that can hold it. Read from a part, the statement says cannot hold still; read from the whole, it says that nothing leaves and nothing is beyond. Putting only that on a finite set of states and enumerating every candidate gives a unique answer. One state is inadmissible in every reading, because its only continuation is itself. Of the sixteen relations that two states carry, exactly one survives: the swap, a → b and b → a. It is a distinction (C0) and its continuation (C1) at once, and neither survives alone. It is also where every reading begins: every structure that obeys both prohibitions, read by any two-valued reader, shows both crossings of the swap, and the swap is the only structure that every such reader reads without an apparent rest. Its square is the identity, so the whole is at rest while each part moves; and because it has no fixed point, no part can read all of its own readings (Cantor, Lawvere). The start carries the reason that no reader is the whole. The mathematics as it stands is one map read in several ways, and interaction comes first in it. A reading is the recorded outcome of an encounter that may change both participants, so the reader sits inside the system it reads. The evaluation Φ sends a state to the profile of everything its admissible encounters can show, and the address of a state is its class under that map: the classes are exactly the profiles. With continuations as the tests this is Myhill–Nerode minimality; on the paper's 24-state walk three readers give 24, 8 and 3 addresses.
