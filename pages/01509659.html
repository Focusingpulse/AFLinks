My First Thread in years.

· archived # 1,509,659
Once upon a time I was very involved in the free energy community. I was a brash young inventor slash engineer slash perpetual student. I wanted to network and share all I had found with anyone who had a similar enthusiasm. I quickly found out that this could get me into trouble. And so I began posting working devices complete with theory, analysis, and conceptual understanding without showing the actual device. This kept it in the realm of concept (as opposed to "reality") and kept me safe from many would be predators. Eventually I simply grew tired of the game and disappeared into seclusion with respect to the "free energy" community. This however does not mean that I forgot or set down my interest in trying to understand how my conscious perception shaped my reality. I still am very hungry to understand how this all works. I am a bit wiser now. I am still a student. I still work. I have posted real working concepts and designs as have other people. Its funny how many people look for the answer only to have it right in front of their face the whole time. We are our own obstacles. Once you have freed yourself from you own self imposed limitations you notice how many people have been screaming the answers but you chose not to listen.
