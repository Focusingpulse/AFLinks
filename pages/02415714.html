It is mutual. In Russia, you are known and loved, your films are highly appreciated. I really like your feelings for Yuri Vlasov. This great man was also my idol. He was a candidate for President of Russia in 1996. I voted for him, not for Yeltsin. I cannot be indifferent to the events in Ukraine. More so, my father is Ukrainian, and my relatives live in central Ukraine and in the Donbas. Several months have passed, and you may have become more aware of what is really happening in Ukraine. Of course, this is difficult in the face of terrible anti-Russian propaganda. Your genuine sincerity gives hope that my appeal to you will be able to change your ideas about this terrible conflict in many ways. Russia is often depicted as a bear. A tamed bear is a friendly animal. But if you tease him, he endures for a long time, but there are limits to everything, and the bear shows all his strength. Let , s go back to 1991. The main reason for the destruction of the Soviet Union was that the people were disappointed in the existing social relations, considering Western democracy as a model. Gorbachev and Yeltsin tried very hard to make Russia a full member of Europe. Remember the slogan: Europe from Lisbon to Vladivostok? But it turned out that Russia is needed only as a raw material appendage, whose opinion can be ignored. This became clear in 1999 when Belgrade was subjected to a terrible months-long bombing by NATO aircrafts. Russia was categorically against it, but its opinion was ignored.
