Barnes (1911-2001), issued as Technical Monograph No. 4 of the Institute for Creation Research. In it Barnes argues that the Earth's magnetic field is produced by a freely decaying electric current in the Earth's core rather than by a self-sustaining dynamo, that the observational record of the past 130 years shows that field decaying exponentially, and that extrapolating the decay backwards sets an upper limit on the age of the Earth.
