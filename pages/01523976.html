Ricker treats the BBC Horizon programme as a case study in how science documentaries blend a little fact with a great deal of speculative fiction. He lays the blame on a scientific method that has drifted from its stricter traditional form into the sensationalising of discovery, and warns that a young astronomer drawn in by such programmes will before long discover how little of what was presented was established.
