Introduction Number of small bodies of water (less than 1 km2) (Padisák and Reynolds, 2003; Scheffer, 2004; Richardson et al., 2022) significantly exceeds the number of large objects of similar types (Meyer et al., 2020). For a long time, the focus was on economically significant water bodies, such as large lakes, rivers and reservoirs (Satybaldiyeva et al., 2023). However, recently, more and more researchers in the world community are recognizing the importance of small bodies of water. This is due to their abundance, total biodiversity, and role in providing ecosystem services (Bartout et al., 2015; Biggs et al., 2017; Downing et al., 2006; Lyubimova et al., 2021; Meyer et al. 2007; Verdonschot et al., 2011; Williams et al., 2004).
