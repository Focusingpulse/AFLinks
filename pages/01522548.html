Title Bohr's Atom Read in full Read in full Author(s) Ricardo L Carezani Keywords Autodynamics , Bohr atom, Sommerfeld fine structure, Dirac equation, quantum numbers, hydrogen spectrum Published 1996 Website https://www.autodynamics.org/bohrs-atom/ "Bohr's Atom" is a four-part paper by Ricardo L Carezani , dated July 1996, applying the Autodynamics equations to the hydrogen atom. It is published at autodynamics.org across four pages — The Hydrogen Atomic Spectrum , Wilson and Sommerfeld Elliptic Orbits , The Sommerfeld Fine Structure and AD's Approach — and its claimed result is the recovery of the Dirac equation for the hydrogen energy levels, with its four quantum numbers, without wave mechanics. Overview The paper is structured as a historical reconstruction followed by a substitution. Carezani retraces the standard development — Bohr 's quantisation of the orbital action, the Wilson–Sommerfeld extension to elliptical orbits, and Sommerfeld's relativistic treatment yielding the fine structure — and then repeats Sommerfeld's calculation with the Autodynamics energy relation in place of the relativistic one. His conclusion is that the substitution produces Dirac's formula directly. The site's own summary states the claim: "In another triumph, Dr. Ricardo Carezani derives Bohr's Atom without wave equations." The argument Part 1: the hydrogen spectrum Carezani sets out Bohr's 1912 application of classical mechanics to the hydrogen term scheme, and stresses a point of interpretation he regards as widely misunderstood. What Bohr quantised was the action of the orbital motion, in units of h / 2 π — not the energy of an independent particle. "AD students must scrutinize this idea carefully … many professionals of the Conventional Wisdom hold that Quantum Mechanics describes a quantum of energy for an independent particle."
