On this wiki it is the discipline that supplies most of the evidence in dispute: nearly every argument about cosmology , redshift and gravitation catalogued here rests on an astronomical measurement, and the disagreements are usually about what those measurements mean rather than about the numbers themselves. Category: Astronomy holds over 260 items. What is disputed Astronomical observation is indirect. Almost nothing beyond the Solar System can be sampled, weighed or repeated; distances, masses and ages are all inferred through a chain of assumptions, and the criticism collected here generally attacks a link in that chain rather than the observation at its end.
