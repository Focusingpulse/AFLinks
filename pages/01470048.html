Schauberger The motion of a free-falling water-droplet is characterised by the ' cycloid-space-curve ', which the droplet describes as it falls . To describe this form of motion , which gives rise to the metaphysical preconditions for life , in greater detail, which one or two doctors have called the ' life-creating curve ', would take too long here. It is therefore here only to be noted that the horizontal emission of dynagens is associated with a spiral configuration of curves , whose transverse radius of action broadens down the longitudinal fall-path .
