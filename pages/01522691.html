De Hilster argues that doubts once confined to dissident circles are beginning to surface in the mainstream press itself. He contrasts two kinds of coverage the headlines about multiverses, history-erasing quantum mechanics and time travel that he takes as a symptom of a field adrift, and recent pieces in the New York Times and Scientific American that he reads as genuine admissions that physics and cosmology need overhaul.
