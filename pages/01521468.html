Tesla Waves Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Speaker Roger J Anderton Date 8 July 2023 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording Tesla Waves was a live session presented by Roger J Anderton on 8 July 2023 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. When Einstein was interpreted as claiming that there was no ether, that was used as an excuse to ignore Tesla physics based on aether. Summary Roger J Anderton on Nikola Tesla 's physics and the longitudinal wave. Ian J Cowan hosted. A microphone test minutes before the session had produced no audio; it worked on the night. The argument Anderton's frame is the one he uses throughout his talks. Tesla's physics was ether physics, as was most physics before 1905; Einstein 's remark in the 1905 paper that the ether is superfluous was read as a licence to discard it; and discarding it meant discarding not only Tesla but the whole body of work resting on a medium. He noted that Einstein appeared to accept some kind of ether in the 1920 Leiden address and that the mainstream did not follow him there, sticking with the 1905 reading. His argument for why this matters is elementary and he pressed it hard: a wave has a medium. A water wave has water, a sound wave has air. A light wave without a medium is, he said, a string of words that sounds like a sentence — his comparison was Jabberwocky — and the 1905 paper does not explain how a wave without a medium could make sense; it substitutes a nonsensical statement for one that made sense. He quoted Tesla against relativity twice. From the well-known remark: the theory is "a magnificent mathematical garb which fascinates, dazzles and makes people blind to the underlying errors", like a beggar clothed in purple whom ignorant people take for a king, its exponents brilliant men but metaphysicists rather than scientists.
