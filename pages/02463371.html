Physical masses are counted as dimensionless multiples of this occupied Aether unit. In relativity E = mc2 appears as a consequence of Lorentz symmetry with c taken as a primitive postulate. We analyze what “mass” and “energy” mean within each ledger, highlight that relativity provides a principle-theory account (Noether charges and Poincaré invariants) whereas APM provides a constructive account (Singularity-induced occupancy of quantized Aether units), and outline experimental classes that test the shared propagation constant and those that can discriminate between a postulated speed of light and a derived, geometrically factored Aether propagation ledger.
