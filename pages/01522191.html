Author(s) Peter Rowlands Keywords Vacuum Published 2008 No. of pages 98 Abstract Vacuum has been used in QFT without a complete understanding of what it is, why it is necessary, and how it should be described mathematically. Answering these questions gives us major leads into many significant aspects of QM and particle physics. But the structure it reveals is also a generic one, not confined to fundamental physics, and has applications to all systems governed by holistic principles. Presented at Physical Interpretations of Relativity Theory XI (12 September 2008) What is disputed here This page is one item in a larger argument. These articles survey it — the standard account, and the case against it collected on this wiki.
