The Holy Grail of Health

· archived # 2,431,128
There was one particular sentence in the recent Time Magazine that really resonated with me. ( http://www.energeticforum.com/paths/...got-right.html ) "Health, by definition, is the sine qua non of everything else. If you're dead, serenity is academic." I have subscribed to this belief since I can remember. I have tried to share this belief 100 different ways with people for decades. Most look at me like I'm crazy, or stating the obvious, or, or, or.... But then I see them go about their lives, seemingly with no actions/priorities associated with what they say they believe. If you're dead, what good are all the "Things" you are working for? ("Things" ranging from homes, to toys, to all sorts of "self-improvement") #1 priority is to be alive and healthy! (disabled by Alzheimers', or other incapacitating dis-ease relegates many "things" we work for irrelevant). So, if the #1 priority is to be alive and healthy, then there would be actions to achieve and maintain that #1 priority. IF it truly is a top priority, and not just lip-service to what most will agree is the logical obvious. For decades I have spent a LARGE AMOUNT of Time, Energy and Money to achieve and maintain Life, for as Long as Possible. I have been ridiculed and looked at askance for living my life with this as a High Priority. However, I have discovered a GREAT many tools along the way to increase my health and extend my life as a result. I do not expect to convert any to my priority with this post, just wanted to celebrate that there is at least one other person (Jeffery Kluger) that saw, AND printed, the simple truth- "Health, by definition, is the sine qua non of everything else. If you're dead, serenity is academic."
