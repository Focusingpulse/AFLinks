On Consciousness Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Speaker George Staniforth Coyne Date 26 September 2020 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording On Consciousness was a live session presented by George Staniforth Coyne on 26 September 2020 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. Join Dissident Science Host David de Hilster as he talks about all things Consciousness with author George Coyne. Coyne's new book "Nofinity" which he has just completed talks about what it means to be conscious in the terms of philosophy of the 21st century. Don't miss a chance to interact with one of the most interesting takes on consciousness by critical thinkers today! Summary George Coyne on consciousness, and on the second edition of Notfinity Process , which the society was helping to publish. He had pulled the first edition entirely: the new book is 390 pages against a much smaller original, rewritten throughout for clarity and carrying research up to Duncan Shaw 's September 2020 paper on Maxwell's 1865 ether theory. The word Coyne explained his coinage. Infinity has come to be treated as something one can hold in the mind as a representation of reality, but every concept is finite, so any picture one forms is not it. Notfinity is meant as a negation rather than a picture — a reminder not to try to imagine the thing, and a standing warning about believing that any thought can represent more than the finite. Its content is the univironmental worldview he takes from Glenn Borchardt : microcosms in motion, the universe an abstraction of limitless matter. Consciousness is an abstraction His central claim is that consciousness does not exist or occur as a phenomenon at all. It is a category — and what a category contains are not things but processes: thinking, feeling, emotion, memory, the items that survive the filtering of sensory input.
