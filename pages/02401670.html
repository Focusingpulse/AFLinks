Ordjonikidze Russian State Geological Prospecting University, Moscow, Russia shchurov_no2@mail.ru This paper examines a neural network solution to the inverse problem (IP) of magnetotelluric sounding (MTS), which involves constructing the electrical conductivity distribution in the Earth's interior based on electromagnetic field components measured at its surface. This problem has a high input dimension (thousands of features), so it is necessary to reduce the input data dimension to obtain a more accurate and stable solution while simultaneously reducing computational complexity. The two-dimensional parameterization model discussed in this paper is an integral part of a general model designed to integrate three physical methods: gravimetry, magnetometry, and magnetotellurics. To ensure such integration, the problem statement must be consistent across all physical methods. This approach corresponds to the geometric formulation of the problem, which consists of determining the boundaries of geophysical objects.
