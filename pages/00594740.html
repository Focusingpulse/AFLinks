Group Theory: Problems and Solutions (Part 1) Harry Willow Abstract. There is nothing new about group theory in this paper. It presents group theory problems at undergraduate level and their solutions. Assume, by way of contradiction that G has no subgroup of index 5. (1) If Q is a Sylow 2-subgroup of G, show that Q is maximal. (2) Conclude that Q is a self-normalizing, so G has 15 Sylow 2-subgroups. (3) Show that there must exist two distinct Sylow 2-subgroups Q1 and Q2 which intersect nontrivially.
