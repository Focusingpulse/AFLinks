It is through this column, The Right Stuff!! , that information about this research and the men behind it is released to the public. The Tesla Turbine Researcher: Franklin Macon By this time, anyone who has been reading current issues of Extraordinary Science should be familiar with the virtues of the Tesla turbine as extolled by certain Tesla researchers. However, we did not have a turbine at the Museum, and, in fact, was unable to evaluate the claims made, let alone demonstrate the effectiveness of the turbine. We decided to make our own turbine with the assistance of Richard Pew. After further research into turbine building, it was determined that the toughest problem about this whole deal was the fact that neither High Energy or Rich Pew did not have a machine shop or access to one. Efforts came to screeching halt as we were exploring ways to procure a drill press in order to build the turbine.
