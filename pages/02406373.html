ISBN: 978-191024-239-1 (Hardback) 978-191024-240-7 (ebook) Typeset by Argil Services P reface During the past decade, nanotechnology has become an active field of research because of its tremendous potential for various applications. New and surprising properties often emerge if the size of many established, well-studied materials is reduced to the nanoscale or improved radically. Conducting polymers are relatively new materials, studied extensively since discovery of the first conducting polymer (polyacetylene) in 1977. Conducting polymers are unique photonic and electronic functional materials owing to their high π-conjugated length, unusual conducting mechanism, and reversible redox doping/dedoping process.
