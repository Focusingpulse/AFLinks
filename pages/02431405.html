focused healing

· archived # 2,431,405
Yesterday morning it felt like i had a muscle cramp under my chin, it hurt for awhile but the pain went away, later that night i felt this golf ball sized lump under my chin from the same area. It was pretty sore, I figured it must have just been swelling but i wasnt sure. I went to sleep and figured it would be gone by this morning, but it wasnt. It was still there all day today, when i came home for lunch i could hardly swallow from the pain, the chewing had started making it worse. I was afraid I would have to make a doctors appointment tomorrow. After lunch today i decided to meditate and focus on healing the area. I wasnt sure weather it would help or not but I gave it a shot anyway. I only spent about 2-3 minutes meditating, i cleared my mind and focused all my energy on my chin, i used my hands to direct all of the energy from my body to my chin. It was pretty powerful, i could almost feel the energy with my body getting goosebumps, I could hear the pulsing of silence. I wasnt expecting too much to happen though. 10 minutes later, all of the swelling was basically Gone. I was shocked. After 28 hours of this hugh lump being there, within 10 minutes of meditating today, It was practically gone. I can chew and swallow fine now. I've never seen anything like that in my life. Truly amazing.
