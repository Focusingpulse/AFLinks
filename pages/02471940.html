(Special to the Eagle.) Riverhead, L. I., October 26 — "Wizard" Nikola Tesla's big tower at Wardenclyffe is again advertised for sale. The "wizard" owes August Eisenmann $199.31 on a judgment, and if it is not settled before December 9 the tower of mystery will be put under the hammer by Sherlif Wells, who has advertised the sale. Tesla, however, has said he will pay up before the date set for the sale, and it is probable his tower will not be auctioned off after all.
