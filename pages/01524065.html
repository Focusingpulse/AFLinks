Title The Cross Radial Force Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Sankar Hajra Keywords force, Classical Physics, magnetic fields, electromagnetic, gravitation Published 2011 Journal Proceedings of the NPA Volume 8 No. of pages 6 Pages 235-240 Abstract Electric charges, electric and magnetic fields and electromagnetic energy are real physical entities (objects). Because, these entities possess momentum and energy and we could experience these entities with our sense organs. Now, all physical objects are subject to gravitation. Therefore, electromagnetic entities should similarly be subject to gravitation. This paper shows that classical physics with this simple consideration could explain a lot of hitherto unexplained puzzling physical phenomena. Overview Sankar Hajra, of the Calcutta Philosophical Forum, sets out to show that classical electrodynamics — Maxwell's equations in an absolute free space, with Heaviside's and Searle's results on moving charges — accounts for everything relativity is credited with, and that special and general relativity can therefore be dispensed with. The main propositions had appeared across four papers in Galilean Electrodynamics between 2005 and 2008; this is a condensed statement with an explicit request for comment from colleagues. The premise is stated simply. Charges, fields and electromagnetic energy carry momentum and energy, so they are real physical objects; all physical objects are subject to gravitation; therefore electromagnetic entities gravitate. Hajra's specific proposal is that the gravitating mass of a point charge is proportional to its longitudinal electromagnetic mass, m L = γ 3 m 0 , while the transverse mass γ m 0 plays no part. From this he writes a modified cross-radial equation of motion.
