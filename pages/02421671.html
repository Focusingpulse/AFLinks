Evolutionary Stages of our Earth before the Lemurian Epoch — GA 109. Rosicrucian Esotericism (1978)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 9 June 1909 · archived # 2,421,671
, Budapest Translated by Dorothy S. Osmond Evolutionary Stages of our Earth before the Lemurian Epoch [ 1 ] The lecture yesterday brought our study of the evolution of our planet to the stage known as Old Moon. We heard that the first embodiment of our planet was that of Old Saturn, the second that of Old Sun and the third that of Old Moon. We came to the point in yesterday's lecture where it was made clear that if everything had progressed exactly as hitherto, man would not have been able to keep pace with the tempo of the cosmic evolution of other beings. Hence a kind of severance took place
