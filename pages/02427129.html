Received 2025 October 5; in original form 2025 August 19 ABSTRACT Understanding the cold atomic hydrogen gas (H I) within cosmic filaments has the potential to pin down the relationship between the low density gas in the cosmic web and how the galaxies that lie within it grow using this material. We report the discovery of a cosmic filament using 14 H I-selected galaxies that form a very thin elongated structure of 1.7 Mpc. These galaxies are embedded within a much larger cosmic web filament, traced by optical galaxies, that spans at least ∼ 15 Mpc.
