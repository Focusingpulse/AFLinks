On Quaternions and John Macken's Electron Wave Model Watch this video on YouTube Talk Type Video Speaker Lori Anne Gardi Date 3 July 2024 Platform YouTube Recording Recording On Quaternions and John Macken's Electron Wave Model is a video by Lori Anne Gardi , published on 3 July 2024 on her FractalWoman YouTube channel. Summary The payoff of Gardi's run of videos on quaternions: she takes what is normally regarded as a mistake in quaternion rotation — using the whole angle in a single operation instead of the half angle in two — and shows that the shape it produces is exactly John Macken's soliton electron model. Why she has been labouring the quaternions She opens by explaining the sequence. She has been, in her phrase, flogging the dead horse of the quaternion for several videos, and this is why: she wanted the audience to understand them as she does, so that what she is about to do with Macken's electron model would be intelligible. Her assessment of Macken is unusually warm. She had begun reviewing one of his papers several videos back — his aether model, and in particular his electron model — and of everyone she has studied to this point she thinks his comes closest to the truth. She states her own position carefully around it, and the distinction she draws is deliberate: she believes there is an aether, and she believes there can be one, while allowing that it may not be provable in any absolute sense. The claim she is willing to defend is about consistency rather than proof. Macken's paper He recently sent her his latest, Oscillating Spacetime: The Foundation of the Universe , which she describes as a rewrite of the earlier paper. The occasion for the rewrite is one she reports approvingly: he tested the earlier version on students he was working with, found through those conversations that they were not taking it as he intended, and rewrote it to be more articulate.
