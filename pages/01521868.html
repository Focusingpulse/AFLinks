Title The Quantum Nature of Matter and Energy as a Function of the Ether Read in full Link to paper Author(s) John R Warfield Keywords ether , matter Published 2009 No. of pages 8 Abstract The purpose of the article is to relate an Ether Model with the formation of matter, the quantum nature of the matter and energy, the rest and relativistic inertial mass of the electron [matter], and finally matter's interaction with electromagnetic radiation. Overview This paper extends an ether framework the author developed in earlier work on relativity into the domain of quantum mechanics , with the aim of uniting the two. Its stated motivation is that relativity and quantum mechanics are "two disconnected individual worlds" whose "mathematical formulas are irreconcilable", while intuition says the macro and micro worlds belong to one universe. The complaint that drives it is not that quantum mechanics fails but that it does not explain. The author grants its accuracy without reservation: it "successfully predicts the outcome of particle physics/subatomic physics with extreme precision" and "is the basis for countless successful modern day inventions", so that "most likely it describes the true nature of the micro-world, although mathematically but not visually". What is missing is the picture — "visually rational input concepts enter a black box where they are mathematically manipulated to produce other rational output concepts", with no intuitive causal connection between them. A quotation from Nick Herbert is used to make the point: quantum theory "gives us no picture of what is going on to produce these results". The paper's proposal is that the quantum character of matter and energy is itself "a product of the Ether", and it asks the reader explicitly to accept the preferred-frame hypothesis of the earlier papers as a premise.
