The Round Robin >> Vol. 03 >> No. 02 [3] - ABOMINABLE SNOWMAN ? - In Science Parade (A.P. feature, Jan.12,'47) we note an account of "ape-men" of Labrador - reported by a Doctor and Mrs Forsyth of the Grenfell Medical Mission, stationed in Labrador since 1932. The Forsyths have not themselves seen the ape-men, but cite many reports about them, and consider the Labrador natives to be very literal-minded and truthful. Most of the stories concern giant-size barefoot tracks in the snow. These lead to 'nests' under trees, or run out on glare ice, but some have been traced for as much as 15 miles over rough country. "Whoever made them climbed easily over stumps and other obstructions where an ordinary man would have gone around." Whatever it was, walked on two feet. In Flying Roll Gamma I, this writer published data collected by Mr. Paul Doerr (203 E. State St., Sharon, Pa.) concerning the "Abominable Snow Men" of Tibet, human-like beings of great size, often seen at a distance by American airmen. Close encounters with them have also been reported, and the total accumulation of fact and legend is very considerable. Round Robin friend F.G.H., deeply learned in occult matters, tells us that these snow men are Nature Spirits, and not necessarily hostile toward humans. And in fact, there are alleged instances of their giving help to travellers. From present information it seems probable that the 'ape-men' of Labrador and the snow men of Tibet are identical in kind.
