Applications span the Double Helix Nebula, jet polarization and rotation-measure gradients, and stacked filament spins. Laboratory protocols using rmfd systems, conical Tesla resonators, and slit-tube relaxation are provided, offering falsifiable QMU predictions without translation to SI/MKS in the main text. A general SI→QMU transfer function and end-to-end data workflows are supplied in Appendix D.
