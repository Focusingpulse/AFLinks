Supercomputer computations and programming 121 Numerical modelling of open magnetic trap using parallel computers M. A. Boronina, I. G. Chernykh, G. I. Dudnikova, V. A. Vshivkov Institute of Computational Mathematics and Mathematical Geophysics SB RAS Email: boronina@ssd.sscc.ru DOI 10.24412/cl‐35065‐2021‐1‐01‐66 We consider two‐dimensional problem of beam‐plasma evolution in open magnetic trap. The result of the high current ion beam injection into the central region of the trap and its interaction with the magnetic field and the background plasma of the trap may lead to the plasma confinement [1]. The lack of the physical exper‐ iments compels using of numerical modelling, based on the problem‐oriented parallel algorithms. We present the complex work on the model, the parallel algorithms and the simulation results for the 2D3V case in cylin‐ drical coordinates. The work was performed within the framework of the state task of ICM&MG SB RAS supported by the Foundation 0251‐2021‐0005. References 1. Beklemishev A.D.
