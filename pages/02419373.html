And in spheres beyond the spheres Triumph over mortal years, And the hour to sorrow given Casts a shadow into heaven. Every word of hope and faith Is a triumph over death; Every syllable shall more Open wide the secret door; Only Love that knows not fear Learns at last to see and hear.
