Dark Energy: Is General Relativity Inside Out? Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Speaker Jeff Baugher Date 14 September 2024 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording Dark Energy: Is General Relativity Inside Out? was a live session presented by Jeff Baugher on 14 September 2024 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. Consideration of the postulation of Dark Energy in the context of General Relativity. Hosted by Ian Cowan. Summary Jeff Baugher came to this through elasticity. He was a doctoral candidate with only a dissertation left when, in 2011, reading about the tensors of general relativity, he met the cosmological constant — described sometimes as a constant of integration — and could not make sense of it. Having taken advanced calculus, he knew what a constant of integration is; the more he read, the more it seemed nobody else could say what this one was either. The attack he presented is aimed not at the right-hand side of Einstein's field equation but at the geometry on the left. Ian J Cowan hosted. He set the terms by quoting the Dark Energy Task Force report, deliberately not a dissident source: that cosmic acceleration, with dark matter, is the observed phenomenon that most directly shows our theories of fundamental particles and gravity to be incorrect or incomplete, and that most experts believe nothing short of a revolution in fundamental physics will explain it. Homogeneous and heterogeneous infinitesimals The historical core is a seventeenth-century dispute Baugher thinks should be part of natural philosophy's basic training. Are lines made of dimensionless points — the heterogeneous view — or of infinitesimal segments of line, the homogeneous view? Cavalieri wrote the books nobody read; Torricelli supplied the examples.
