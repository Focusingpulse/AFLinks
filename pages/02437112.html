Second Meeting with the Circle of Seven — GA 259. The Fateful Year of 1923 in the History of the Anthroposophical Society

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 17 January 1923 · archived # 2,437,112
p.m., Stuttgart Translated by Steiner Online Library Second Meeting with the Circle of Seven and the new participants: Carl Unger and the two Waldorf teachers Paul Baumann and Dr. Herbert Hahn. The following are proposed as the new board: Emil Leinhas, Dr. Hahn, Paul Baumann, Dr. Kolisko, who replaces Ernst Uehli, who has resigned from the central board. The meeting begins 1 In Marie Steiner's edition - based on the protocol of Karl Schubert - marked “Shortly after January 8, 1923”.
