The standard account The observation that bodies of different weight fall at the same rate goes back to Galileo, and Newton tested it with pendulums of different composition in the Principia . The empirical statement — that the ratio of gravitational to inertial mass is the same for all substances — is called the weak equivalence principle or the universality of free fall.
