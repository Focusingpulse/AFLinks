PERSONNEL OF THE COMMISSION Who the Men Are That Are Furthering Our Interests — National Building to Be Erected as a Testimonial of Our Friendship to France — The Various Exhibits. Not one of all the nations which have signified the purpose to take part in the great Paris exposition of 1900 is showing a deeper interest than the United States of America. Now that the people of America are assured of space sufficient for an adequate representation they are setting about the work of preparation for their exhibit with characteristic energy, and it is fair to predict that their share in the great enterprise will be in every way worthy of the great American people. Two great headquarters have been established, one in Chicago and the other in New York, and a large staff of busy employees is engaged in each formulating the work in hand. Stenographers and typewriters, heads of departments and chiefs of bureaus crowd each other in even the ample space provided for the service. In reality all of the work of classification and assignment for a world’s fair is going on in miniature, for the American exhibit at Paris is not to constitute a department but a fraction of every department.
