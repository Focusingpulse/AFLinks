Title Space and Time Read in full Link to paper Author(s) John Linus OSullivan Keywords Space and Time, Electromagnetic Field, Infinity. Published 2013 No. of pages 3 Abstract Abstract: How did the universe come into existence? This very question cannot be asked because everything came from energy without time. If the energy was not always there as time, then nothing would exist to ask. With this in mind, timeless energy and cause of action is explained outside the realm of time and matter where the speed of light is constant relative to matter but is standing relative to time. Magnetic field waves are shown as gravity force in the electric field and are curved from mass in space-time. Overview Space and Time is a three-page paper by John Linus OSullivan , presented at the 20th Natural Philosophy Alliance conference in July 2013. It proposes that everything — matter, light, gravity and time itself — is built from standing electromagnetic wavelets, and that time is a property of finite moving energy while "standing energy is the existence of infinity". The opening move is to dismiss the question of origins: "How did the universe come into existence? This very question cannot be asked because everything came from energy without time." From there the paper describes "open-ended standing wavelets" — "curved quadrant waves having electric and magnetic fields", with the magnetic field supplying "the gravitational forces on the electric field". The strength of gravity is explained by position within the standing wave: "Gravity forces are very weak compared to the other forces of nature because gravity forces are at the nodes in the magnetic field while the other forces are at the antinodes in the electric field." Photons occupy the antinodes and "pack a punch as particles with energy equal to frequency of the photons based on Planck's constant, E = hν".
