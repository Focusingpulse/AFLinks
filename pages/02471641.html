More than that, he has discovered that somewhere in the solar system there are beings trying desperately to get into communication with us. Tesla has all kinds of instruments, and he says that some of them have been behaving very queerly lately. He cannot explain their actions, and what Tesla cannot explain has an origin somewhere outside the bounds of mundane things. Therefore somebody in the universe is signaling the earth. It is all very simple, and at the same time most touching. It shows a kindly feeling of neighborliness on the part of those beings, we must say, and we hope that Tesla will master the dialect and work up a reply as soon as he can. And let him not forget to discover on what planet these well meaning neighbors are located. What he will tell them or what he will ask them when he is ready to begin the conversation no one can guess.
