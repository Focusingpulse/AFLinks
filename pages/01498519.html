The novel detector, recently proposed [3] and developed for soft X-ray micro￾radiography and microscopy applications, as well as for intense extreme ultraviolet (EUV) sources characterization [4], consists of a radiation-sensitive thin film of LiF, thermally evaporated on a glass substrate.
