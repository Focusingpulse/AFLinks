Hydrogen Fuel Cell

· archived # 1,459,853
A fuel cell is an electrochemical cell that converts the chemical energy of a fuel (often hydrogen ) and an oxidizing agent (often oxygen ) into electricity through a pair of redox reactions. Fuel cells are different from most batteries in requiring a continuous source of fuel and oxygen (usually from air) to sustain the chemical reaction, whereas in a battery the chemical energy usually comes from substances that are already present in the battery .
