At The Seasteading Institute, we work to enable seasteading communities - floating cities - which will allow the next generation of pioneers to peacefully test new ideas for government. The most successful can then inspire change in governments around the world. This is an audacious vision which will take decades to fully realize. We strongly believe in incrementalism -- breaking this huge vision down into manageable, practical steps. We believe the first key step is for seasteading to become not just possible, but sustainable -- technologically, legally, and financially. In other words, the cost of living on the ocean must be low enough, and the business opportunities promising enough, such that there is an economic incentive for people to live on seasteads.
