Ingo Swann , Remote Viewing Pioneer ( click to enlarge ) Ingo Swann - The Early Days By Sandy Frost Originally Published October 31, 2001 2-1-12 Prior to the 2001 Remote Viewing Conference held in Las Vegas, Nevada, I had no idea of who Ingo Swann was. During the conference presentations, I heard his name mentioned over and over with near reverence, like mentioning the name of a creator god. I prefer to think of Swann as a remote viewing creator demi-god; a creative genius like Walt Disney. Most of what I've learned about him is from his site. So who is Ingo Swann? He is the "Father" of remote viewing. He was born in Telluride, Colorado on 2:30 a.m. on 14 September 1933. He read his first dictionary when he was three. He read the Encyclopedia Britannica cover to cover by the time he was in kindergarten. When he was seven, he read "The Book of Tao" by the ancient Chinese philosopher, Lao Tzu. He'd also read the Bible a few times and would make "box and flow" diagrams or flow charts to illustrate what he was reading. Later, these diagrams would be studied by the Stanford Research Institute (SRI), the Pentagon and by the Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA) to discover the theoretical foundations of psychic mind functions. Swann began painting when he was five. In 1955, he went on to get Bachelor's degrees in both Biology and Art.
