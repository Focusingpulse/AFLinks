This article breaks the shackles that genes determine everything in life. Genes and neural networks are both important. In addition to innate genes, people’s ability to learn, update, shape themselves, and explore is particularly important. This paper points out the importance of neural networks in the evolution of species. Different from the view that neural network is only the intelligent organizational structure of human brain, this article extends the above view, neural network is also an important intelligent structure of cells, and neural network is also an important intelligent structure of organs. 2 Background Often people think that genetic mutations are completely random [1].
