Dynamic Latent Scale GAN for GAN inversion Jeongik Cho jeongik.jo.01@gmail.com Abstract Generator of generative adversarial networks (GAN) maps latent random variable into data random variable. GAN inversion is mapping data random variable to latent random variable by inverting the generator of GAN. When training the encoder for generator inversion, using the mean squared error causes the encoder to not converge because there is information loss on the latent random variable in the generator. In other words, it is impossible to train an encoder that inverts the generator as it, because the generator may ignore some information of the latent random variable. This paper introduces a dynamic latent scale GAN, a method for training a generator that does not lose information from the latent random variable, and an encoder that inverts the generator.
