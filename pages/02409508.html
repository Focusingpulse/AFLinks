INTRODUCTION Why Live Small? Most of us dream of having a place of our own. Some of us dream large, but more and more of us are dreaming of something small. Perhaps we long to own our first house — something on the smaller side because we’re just starting out. Perhaps we are empty nesters, downsizing into more practical space. Perhaps we want to build a little place of our own, with our own hands. Perhaps we want to build small in order to live mortgage-free. Perhaps we want to minimize the impact of our home on the environment.
