Wladimir Guglinski Born ( 1950-08-04 ) August 4, 1950 (age 76) Goiânia, Goiás, Brazil Residence Brazil Nationality Brazilian Known for Quantum Ring Theory , Toroidal Ring , Aether , Cold fusion Scientific career Fields Mechanical engineering , Theoretical physics Wladimir Guglinski (born 4 August 1950) is a Brazilian mechanical engineer and independent researcher known for developing Quantum Ring Theory (QRT), a self-published theoretical framework that seeks to reinterpret nuclear and quantum physics through a revived concept of the aether . His proposals lie outside the mainstream of contemporary physics and have circulated chiefly through self-published books, the Journal of Nuclear Physics , and the dissident-science community. Biography Education Degree in mechanical engineering, Engineering School of the Universidade Federal de Minas Gerais (UFMG) , Belo Horizonte, 1973 Career Guglinski was born on 4 August 1950 in Goiânia, in the Brazilian state of Goiás. He graduated in mechanical engineering in 1973 from the Engineering School of the Universidade Federal de Minas Gerais (UFMG) in Belo Horizonte. From 1974 to 1990 he worked as a contracted industrial engineer. In 1991 he left industrial employment and started his own pre-fabricated cement-block company in Miraí, Minas Gerais, pursuing his theoretical work in physics in his spare time. Scientific contributions Guglinski began developing his theoretical models around 1990, motivated by a conviction that current atomic and nuclear physics could not account for several ordinary phenomena. He states that he first became aware of cold fusion research in 1998, and subsequently drew connections between his own models and low-energy nuclear reaction (LENR) phenomena, though he says the theory was developed independently of cold-fusion research.
