Forensic analysis of Lucy I and Lucy II robert.bennett @rcn.com Abstract A quantitative test for the probability that two sets of photos are of the same woman. The result for 7 facial characteristics in each photo is that the odds are 30 million to 1 that Lucy I and Lucy II are the same person. ………………………………………………………………………………………………………….. Although facial recognition has advanced rapidly with technology, standard statistical methods are still often effective in achieving low tech solutions. A case history will be analyzed as to whether photo sets, Lucy I and Lucy II, represent the same woman at different ages. The fundamental assumption to be statistically tested is that Lucy I = Lucy II, the null hypothesis. The facial features of nine pix of Lucy prior to 1960 (Lucy I) will be analyzed versus five of Lucy post 1960 (Lucy II) by using the means and errors of seven facial dimensions. The t test is relevant here, since the means and standard deviations are measured and the distributions are normal. Statistical method of choice - the t test What is computed by the t test is the statistical probability P that the photos are of the same woman, but only differ in individual measurements by reasonable random errors. The more photos are tested, the greater likelihood of a correct interpretation.
