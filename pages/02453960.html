PROBLEMS WITH LOW-CURRENT GENERATORS Some types of static electric generators can only supply an extremely tiny current. The lower their current-generating capability, the more sensitive they are to stray leakage paths. In the realm of electrostatics, a millionth of an ampere (uA, or microamp) is a fairly high current.
