Beaty - Tiny paper envelope printed as business cards, contains polarizers, mica - hole with foil, pinhole for seeing eye-entopic phenom - hole with foil, dual pinhole for 2slit diffraction - hole w/congo blue - hole w/fresnel prism, walk funny or feel taller - hidden fluor stamp ink URL, for UV light activities - green film, use double-stick tape - FS mirror for loudspeaker w/laser pointer Print a starfield on the card (black random stars.) Photocopy the same pattern onto transparencies. Place them together, rotate slightly, and weird circular "swirl" appears. Move it very slightly and the "swirl" moves rapidly. A random-moire effect I stumbled across in the 1980s. Cut up a large thin flexible fresnel lens. Every piece still works, but most will act like lens+prism. 9" fresnel sheets can be found for $2
