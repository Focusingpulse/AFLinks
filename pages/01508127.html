Premontion Movie Remote Viewing

· archived # 1,508,127
Last night I watched the movie Premonition. The movie was so-so...BUT, one of the special features on the DVD was about real-life premonitions, and it was GREAT! On the persons interviewed was Joe McMoneagle. He was one of the first US military Remote Viewers, and part of the Government Stargate Project (which was the seed for the Stargate movie and TV series). His interview and short demonstration were quite interesting, along with the other natural precogs.
