👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 16 February 1921 · archived # 2,443,797
afternoon, Stuttgart Translated by Steiner Online Library Eighth Lecture In this lecture I would like to speak about certain colorations regarding the characteristics of the present spiritual life, which our lecture work will have to assume.
