It was then assumed that Light could travel in space as a wave-form, without the presence of any medium like Ether, and it would do so at the same constant velocity for all the observers traveling uniformly relative to each other in space. Both these presumptions appeared to be unreasonable by ordinary commonsense. It is however possible to explain the results of MMEx using the old classical Wave-Theory of light.
