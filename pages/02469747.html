A reception will precede and follow the dinner, at which the Hon. Robert B. Roosevelt, uncle of the President, will preside. Admiral and Mrs. Coghlan and Mr. and Mrs. Belmont will assist the president, Mrs. Roswell D. Hitchcock, in receiving. Invitations are confined entirely to club members, who are limited in the number of guests they may ask, owing to the large membership. When the Entertainment Club was organized one year ago many predictions were made that it could not live, as those to whom it catered were busier socially and philanthropically than all others in New York. Some feared that the entertainments could not be kept up to the very high standard established, but the club has grown to command respect. Applications for membership are pouring in.
