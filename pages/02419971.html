, Stuttgart Translated by E.H. Goddard and Charles Davy 7. Workings of the Law of Karma in Human Life [ 1 ] Today I want to speak about the workings of the law of karma through individual human lives. Any such explanation is bound to be incomplete, for I shall not be putting before you any speculations or theories. I shall limit myself, as occultism always should, to facts and experiences. I shall therefore tell you of a karmic influence of one kind or another only when I have observed a person in that particular situation.
