bmde2026-industry012 Research on Digital Transformation Pathways for Small and Micro Enterprises in the Context of the Digital Economy Haoyu Tian 17632769691@163.com With the advent of the digital economy era, digital transformation has become crucial for small and micro enterprises to adapt to market changes and enhance competitiveness. This paper delves into the significance of digital transformation for SMEs, addressing current challenges such as limited technological application capabilities, funding shortages, and talent scarcity. It proposes targeted transformation pathways, including strengthening policy support, improving technological application levels, and enhancing talent cultivation. The aim is to empower SMEs to achieve sustainable development amid the digital wave, providing insights for related research and practice. Looking ahead, as digital technologies continue to evolve and innovate, the pathways and models for SME digital transformation will also diversify and mature, necessitating further in-depth research and exploration.
