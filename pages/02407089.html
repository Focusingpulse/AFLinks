Deuteronomy , Chapter 23: 12, 13 Preface The third edition has been extensively revised and rewritten to include recent important additions in knowledge. The needs of medicine have dictated the selection of subject matter and the placing of emphasis upon the medical aspects of parasitology, such as pathology, symptomatology, diagnosis, and prevention. Therefore, discussion of the morphologic and biologic characteristics of the various parasites has been restricted to those that are essential for diagnosis. Treatment of the various diseases has been considered briefly, and only established methods of treatment and chemotherapeutic agents of proven value are presented.
