T. Brown Published 1916 (reprinted 1993) Journal Electric Spacecraft Journal Number 9 Pages 14-20 Abstract Note on dating: this is a reprint. Nipher's paper was published in the Transactions of the Academy of Science of St. Louis vol. XXIII, pp. 163–175, on 28 July 1916; Nipher died in 1926. The 1993 date carried in the paper archive is that of the Electric Spacecraft Journal issue in which it was reproduced. Oliver Nicholson has graciously provided copies of little-known publications on the research of Dr. Francis E. Nipher. Dr. Nipher did experimental studies in the 1911 to 1920 period in an effort to find a connection between electricity and gravity. These studies were financed by the Carnegie Institution of Washington, DC. This work predated that of T.
