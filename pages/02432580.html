Why Positive Thoughts are Not Enough

· archived # 2,432,580
We have all met or heard of people that have the "Midas Touch", people that are always Positive. We have all met people that want, try and work hard to be positive, but no matter how hard it seems they try, it just does not seem to work for them. Others that work diligently at applying the principles of LOA, The Secret, Tony Robbins, etc., yet their lives don't seem much different, or maybe they are even more discouraged. Here is why- In Dr. Bruce Lipton's book, The Biology of Belief, he makes many points...here are three of them that illuminate the conundrum: 1. "Positive thoughts have a profound effect on behavior and genes, BUT ONLY WHEN THEY ARE IN HARMONY WITH SUBCONSCIOUS PROGRAMMING. Huh Oh...pretty big BUT 2. "The actions of the subconscious mind are reflexive in nature and are NOT governed by reason or thinking." Oh My, we are starting to see the challenge here. 3. In the entire book he explains how important subconscious thoughts and subconscious energy are in the control of who we actually are, but he also states that it is EXTREMELY DIFFICULT, IF NOT IMPOSSIBLE, to change subconscious programming. He specifically states, many times, that changing conscious thought is NOT enough! So there are millions of people working diligently to think positively on a conscious level, and that energy is running smack dab into subconscious programming that is in conflict with their conscious thoughts. This causes turmoil, discouragement, cognitive dissonance, and millions of posts on the internet contesting the validity of LOA, etc. For Many, if not all of us, subconscious reprogramming is needed so that it is working in concert with our conscious thoughts. If you think it is a challenge to control your conscious thoughts.... The conscious mind processes about 40 bits of information a second, and with effort we can control that. The subconscious mind processes about 40 MILLION bits of information per second, making it near impossible (as Dr. Lipton states) to reprogram.
