Sympathetic Vibratory Physics

👤 Nikola Tesla · · archived # 2,410,955
"In Colorado I succeeded one day in precipitating a dense fog. There was a mist outside, but when I turned on the current the cloud in the laboratory became so dense that when the hand was held only a few inches from the face it could not be seen. I am positive in my conviction that we can erect a plant of proper design in an arid region, work it according to certain observations and rules, and by its means draw from the ocean unlimited amounts of water for irrigation and power purposes.“ [Nikola Tesla)), Presentation of the Edison Medal to Nikola Tesla, Minutes of the Annual Meeting of the American Institute of Electrical Engineers, Held at the Engineering Societies Building, New York City, Friday Evening, May 18, 1917.]
