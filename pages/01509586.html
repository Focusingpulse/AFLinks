Crystal Radios are free energy devices because they don't need batteries. They are powered by the very AM radio waves that they're tuned in to. This is a profound concept, but has its limitations because by the time the energy transmitted in those radio waves are received by the crystal radio, there isn't much left. There is barely enough to just power the headset so you can listen to the station. Some people have lit LED lights with Crystal Radios but its not really anything to write home about. These AM radio waves are electromagnetic in nature and are known as Transverse Waves. These waves conform to the Inverse Square Law, which means that energy is diminished by the square of the distance. The further the waves are from the radio station, the less energy there is at the receiving end.
