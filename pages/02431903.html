· archived # 2,431,903
To help shed some light on the different PATHS Modules, and what they do, we will be posting the different Module descriptions in here so that you can share your experiences and ask any questions you may have about the Module. We look forward to your responses! This Module was created to aid you in being the best baseball player you can be. It can help you with hand-eye coordination, hitting, running, sliding, throwing and catching. It can also help in increasing strength, focus and stamina while playing. In addition to this, it can also help you to foster an attitude of confidence that will be of great assistance while practicing and competing. This Module can assist your mind and body's ability to: Increase awareness on the field Increase flexibility Increase hand-eye coordination Increase strength Increase energy Increase self-confidence Increase capacity for teamwork Increase willingness to prepare Increase ability to maintain a game-winning attitude This Module has 2 different versions: One for those above 18 and one for those who are less than 18. No alterations will be needed unless the child is less than 6 years of age. This Module is ideal for kids and teens involved in the sport, as it will help foster the physical and mental qualities needed to excel.
