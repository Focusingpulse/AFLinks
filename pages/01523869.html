Science Chat Saturday sponsored by the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society Summary An open chat hosted by Franklin Hu with Bill Lucas , Ian Cohen and Roger, opening on the pandemic and its restrictions — Ian describing the confinement of the over-seventies in Ireland, which he criticised, and the two-kilometre limit — before turning to physics. Mass increase and photon mass Ian reported on an exchange with Dennis Allen about relativistic mass increase. Allen had pointed to an article which Ian concluded really is self-contradictory: it cites a cyclotron result telling against the effect and then treats Einstein's relation as confirmed. Ian noted that longitudinal and transverse mass vary differently — one as the cube of the gamma factor, the other as its square or as gamma itself depending on whose treatment one takes — and that positing them at all seems to require the force to be resolved into electric and magnetic components. He also corrected himself on photon mass from the previous week. One can divide hν by c ² to get an equivalent mass of order 10 −19 grams. If instead one postulates a zero rest mass and puts it into the gamma relation, both numerator and denominator go to zero at v = c , so the expression is undefined rather than infinite; whereas postulating a non-zero rest mass and accelerating to c makes the equation blow up and is illegitimate.
