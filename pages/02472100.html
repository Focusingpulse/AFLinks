A few days ago it was learned that Mr. Tesla had made a discovery the importance of which would arouse the envy and admiration of all other scientific explorers, but it was only yesterday the character of his triumph was made known. According to the interesting reports sent East from Colorado Mr. Tesla obtained an electrical connection with Mars through one of those fortunate strokes of chance which have been of frequent benefit to science. He was engaged in an effort to telegraph from the top of Pike's Peak without wires, and as this proceeding necessitates projecting messages into space with no great certainty as to where they will be received, it is not astonishing that the electric current Mr. Tesla happened to be using was on its way to another world instead of being headed for Omaha, as the investigator fondly believed.
