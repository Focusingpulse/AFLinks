As a result, I experienced persistent injection site reactions such as redness, swelling and hardening of the skin. I participated in a clinical trial commencing May 12, 2005 sponsored by the Canadian Immunodeficiency Research Collaborative to evaluate the efficacy of using the Biojector© CO 2 injector delivery system over standard hypodermic needles.
