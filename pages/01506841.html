Watching The Process Of Ascension

· archived # 1,506,841
I found this today & thought to share To outside observers watching the process of ascension, many will observe a glow around the physical body, similar to a visible aura or halo, and this is primarily where the idea of halos around saints comes from. If the observer is clairvoyant, he or she will see geometric patterns around the physical body and also emanations of light patterns protruding out from the body at various angles. As the light body becomes more and more predominant, the body might seem to shimmer and sparkle, and even appear waxy. Being in the presence of one who is close to ascension will be an exhilarating experience if you are open to receive it. Observers will likely feel electricity running through their bodies while in the presence of the ascending human. To the ascending human, the experience will be similar. It will feel as though there is an electrical generator within the body, and during the resistance phase the soul might have a burning sensation or hot flashes. Yet at the same time there will be a buoyancy and spring to the step, along with a sensation of floating. At times, the world will not seem real, and it might appear that all of life is merely a dream. At other times, as the resistance subsides, everything will seem "ultra-real" and full of life, even inanimate objects. At other times, objects might seem to dissolve, or fade in and out of reality. As the resistance decreases further, an overwhelming peace will come over the entire being and an envelope of protection will be felt around the body, which is of course the Merkabah. It will become quite apparent that nothing outside the self can disturb or harm the soul. One will glide effortlessly through all manner of outer danger while encased in the warm glow of God's Love. It will be as though the ascending soul is literally floating through the outer world rather than walking upon it.
