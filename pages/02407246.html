He makes it the interpreter of the secrets of the world. He has thus prophetically pointed to something that must be the ideal of those contemporary spirits who know how to interpret the signs of the times. The spirits envision an art that seeks to reconnect with the paths of the searching soul that lead to the sources of existence.
