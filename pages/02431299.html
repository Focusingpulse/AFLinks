A new respect for suffering

· archived # 2,431,299
To some of you this may seem trite. Over the last few days I have learned a new empathy for those that do not feel well. I have gone 9 years without a cold, flu, or any other illness. I had actually forgotten what it was like to be sick. It is HORRIBLE! I have had a fever, cough, runny nose, and aching body for a couple of days now. I am not sure why or how I am sick now...having avoided such misery for 9 years....but I am learning a good lesson. Never again will I hear someone say how they have the flu, cold, etc., and have the stand-offish attitude that I have had in the past. I will remember that they truly are not feeling well, and will empathize with them, understanding what it is like. Still under the weather, wondering how long it will last.
