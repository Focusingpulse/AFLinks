VTA Data Table V.T.A. Data Tables. [ By G.D.Mutch 1997.] The following Data tables will show the reader the enormous forces the aircraft designer/engineer has to contend with when designing an aircraft for a specific purpose. The following two tables are calculated around two disc shaped aircraft designs. The height an area change with respect to the radius. Calculations of the available atmospheric propulsion force are then calculated accordingly.
