i Geometry of the Ellipse and the Ellipsoid Par ABDELMAJID BEN HADJ SALEM INGÉNIEUR GÉNÉRAL GÉOGRAPHE Abstract: It is a chapter that concerns the geometry of the ellipse and the ellipsoid of revolution. We give the formulas of the 2D plane coordinates and the 3D Cartesian coordinates (X,Y,Z) in function of the geodetic coordinates (ϕ,λ,he). A section is devoted to the geodesic lines of the ellipsoid of revolution. We give the proofs of the differential equations of the geodesic lines and their integration.
