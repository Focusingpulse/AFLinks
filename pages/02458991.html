ABSTRACT Psychotic processes remain poorly understood mental states. This review aims to examine the spiritual challenges and discrepancies surrounding psychosis, and the role cultural backgrounds and beliefs play in determining whether a psychotic episode is considered a pathological condition. The article explores mediumship, shamanism, and mystical experiences, examining their similarities and differences with psychosis. Symptoms of psychotic episodes are often viewed as pathological rather than simply different or less understood. The spiritual world remains a controversial topic in the scientific community. However, an openness in exploring this realm may help provide meaning and guidance to patients with psychosis.
