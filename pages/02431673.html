Are You Missing Out?

· archived # 2,431,673
I thought I'd share with you what I've discovered, and my solution for it.... It could be just me ( ) but the weeks seem to go soooo fast! I realised a while ago that I would often miss watching my Paths theatres for 10 days to two weeks!!! It certainly didn't feel that long! AND ... the only reason I discovered this is because, being an affiliate, I can see in my stats, the last time I logged onto my theatres. I was shocked at how sneakily the time shot by under my nose without my realising it! It always felt like only three or four days ago I'd watched my Paths modules! SO.... I decided to watch my Paths theatres every second day. This means that even if I skip a viewing, I'm still guaranteed to watch it at least once or twice a week, which is of course the minimum for it to keep working. HOWEVER!!..... This morning I was about to watch them again (certain that I skipped one viewing, but it should still be only a few days ago) .... and before I did, I decided to check when I last logged on..... it was the 1st of July - one week ago!! SO.... I'm now going to aim to watch them every day, knowing that if I miss a viewing here and there it should still be at least twice a week. I'm also going to keep an eye on my stats to keep track of when I last logged in. Those who aren't affiliates, I highly reccomend keeping a note in your diary, of when you view your theatre - so you can make sure it's at least once a week, but prefferably twice a week. It's a funny thing ... even though each module is only around 3 minutes long, it's easy to put it off when you're very busy with other stuff and think "I'll do it later" or "I'll do it tomorrow".... Anyway, just thought I'd share that in case others are missing out too. Love and Light and Magic xxx
