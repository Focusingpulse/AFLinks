👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 17 October 1923 · archived # 2,441,950
, Stuttgart Translated by Steiner Online Library 10. A Meditation Quote for the Teachers of the Free Waldorf School Spiritual vision, Turn your gaze inward; Heartfelt Feeling Stir the tender essence of the soul; In the intuitive Spirit-vision, In the heartfelt groping of the soul, consciousness weaves itself. Consciousness, which from the Above and the Below of the human being binds the light of the worlds to the darkness of the earth. Spiritual vision Heartfelt groping Behold, feel Within the human being The light of the worlds weaving In the reigning darkness of the earth: My own human-image
