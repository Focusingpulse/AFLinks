107 CHAPTER 3 OBJECTIVE AND METHODOLOGY A single cylinder, water cooled, four stroke direct injection Kirloskar SV1 diesel engine with a displacement volume of 661 cc, compression ratio of 17.5:1, injection timing of 23º BTDC, injection pressure of 200 bar, developing 5.9 kW at 1800 rpm was used to achieve the objectives and methodology of this study. 3.1 OBJECTIVES OF THE STUDY The main objectives of this research can be listed as follows: i. To compare the performance, emission, and combustion characteristics of various flow rates of Oxygen Enriched Hydrogen (OEH) gas added diesel combustion with petroleum diesel combustion. ii. To find the best flow rate of OEH gas which gives higher brake thermal efficiency and reduced engine-out emissions. iii. To compare the performance, emission, and combustion characteristics of best flow rate of OEH gas added diesel combustion with petroleum diesel combustion under varied operating parameters such as injection time, injection pressure, temperature of diesel fuel, inlet air temperature, cooling water flow rate, and the combination of injection pressure and injection time of diesel fuel. 108 iv. To characterize and identify the operating parameters that controls the OEH gas combustion in a DI diesel engine. 3.2 METHODOLOGY OF THE STUDY The methodology of the present investigation is divided into phase I and phase II which are outlined below in Figure 3.1 and Figure 3.2.
