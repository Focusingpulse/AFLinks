We obtain fundamental properties of contra π gp-continuous functions and discuss the relationships between contra π gp-continuity and other related functions. 1 Introduction In 1996, Dontchev [5] introduced a new class of functions called contra-continuous functions. He defined a function f : X → Y to be contra-continuous if the pre image of every open set of Y is closed in X. In 2007, Caldas et.al. [4] introduced and investigate the notion of contra g− continuity. In 1968, V. Zaitsev [25] introduced the notion of π -open sets as a finite union of regular open sets. Zolotarev [26] proved that in a metric space every closed set is open [Theorem 1] (i.e. every closed set is the intersection of finitely many regular closed sets). This notion received a proper attention and some research articles came to existence. J. Dontchev and T. Noiri [6] introduced and investigated, among others, continuity and almost continuity. Ekici and Baker [8] and Ekici [9] used this notion to introduce and present some fundamental properties of a new type of generalized closed set and new forms of continuities.
