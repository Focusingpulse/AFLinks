Overview The volume gathers contributions on foundational problems in quantum physics and is associated with Krasnoholovets's program of submicroscopic quantum mechanics. In that framework, real physical space is treated as a tessellation of "primordial cells" (superparticles) — a "tessellattice" — and a particle is a local deformation of that lattice.
