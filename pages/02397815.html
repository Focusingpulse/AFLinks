Grimshaw, PhD, MPAff Research Affiliate, Energy Institute The University of Texas at Austin March 24, 2019 The Risk of Loss of LENR Research Results 1. LENR was rejected by mainstream science within a year or so after its 1989 announcement. 2. The field became a pariah science – and remains so. 3. Relatively few new researchers have been attracted to the field. The Risk of Loss of LENR Research Results 4. Many LENR researchers began investigations not long after the announcement. 5. Now 30 years later many are leaving the field. 6. Consequently the results of their work is at risk of being lost. 7. Such loss will be a tragedy not only for the LENR field, but also for the world.
