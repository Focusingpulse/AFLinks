This re-titled English Language edition is a newly combined re-translation and updated edition of Rael's three original French books Le Livre Qui Dit La Verite (The Book Which Tells The Truth) which first appeared in France in 1974, Les Extra-Terrestres M'ont Emmene Sur Leur Planete (Extra-Terrestrials Took Me To Their Planet) and Accueillir les Extra-Terrestres (Let's Welcome The Extra-terrestrials ). Similar English paperback editions were printed in Japan in 1986 under the titles The Message Given To me By Extra-Terrestrials (the first 2 books combined) and Lets Welcome Our Fathers from Space . In 1998 the combined first 2 books were released in England by Tagman Press entitled The Final Message and again in the USA with the title The True Face of God in 1999 and The Message Given by Extra-terrestrials in 2001.
