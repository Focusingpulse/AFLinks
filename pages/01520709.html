Overview New Energy News (ISSN 1075-0045) was the monthly bulletin of the Institute for New Energy (INE), edited by Patrick Bailey and drawing heavily on material submitted by Hal Fox . This issue — volume 8, number 6, June 2001 — is twenty pages covering an editorial, abstracts, a book review, articles, letters, meeting announcements and an advertisement. It is a primary document of the Free Energy research community at a particular moment: mid-2001, with the California electricity crisis underway, and the newsletter itself transitioning to email distribution in ASCII and PDF. What makes this issue worth reading is not a theoretical claim — it advances none — but its internal posture. Two of its longest items are attacks on free-energy fraud , printed without editorial defensiveness, alongside a technical letter challenging the measurement of the gravitational constant and an uncritical reprint of a UFO-disclosure document. That range within twenty pages is characteristic of the field's self-understanding at the time: the belief that the physics is genuine coexisting with an unusually blunt acknowledgement that most of what is sold under its name is not. Editorial: mid-year insights Patrick Bailey, INE president, opens with the California energy shortage, predicting rolling blackouts before October and hoping they will "attract more public attention to advanced energy conversion technologies."
