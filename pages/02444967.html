08/15/14 - Swaying the Mind This Tales from the Crypt - Smoke Wrings S07E09 episode recounts a palm sized transmitter which induces people to believe whatever they are told as if its really happening. Years ago, researcher Thelma Moss, who carried out research at the UCLA Gerontology center, among other things, was doing research with brainwave patterns. As I understood it, she and her team had tested a variety of volunteers for different stimuli so they could map the unique brainwave patterns associated with that particular stimulus. They found generic patterns that applied to all humans. These patterns reflected hunger, thirst, lust, sickness, pleasure, pain and a wide variety of other sensations and emotions. In electronics, there is a saying that "Oscillators amplify and Amplifiers oscillate" where it follows that if you can detect a signal you can also transmit that signal. And so it was, they found they could project a given brainwave pattern to induce the effect in humans.
