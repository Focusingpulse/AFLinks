Program Limitation of “The Coming Day” — GA 332b. Current Social and Economic Issues

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 23 March 1922 · archived # 2,443,347
, Dornach Translated by Steiner Online Library Program Limitation of “The Coming Day” The prevailing circumstances and the opposition of various circles with a vested interest in the economy are forcing the “Coming Day” to renounce a broader socio-economic program for the immediate present and to keep its activities within narrower limits. In the near future, it will consist of the association of several economic enterprises with spiritual undertakings that support each other. The spiritual undertakings: Waldorf School, Clinical-Therapeutic Institute, Biological and Physical Research Institute
