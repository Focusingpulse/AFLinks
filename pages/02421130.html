Jose Luis Lopez-Bonilla Residence Piso, Col. Lindavista,, Mexico Nationality Mexican Scientific career Fields Physicist José Luis López-Bonilla is a Mexican mathematical physicist at the Escuela Superior de Ingeniería Mecánica y Eléctrica (ESIME) of the Instituto Politécnico Nacional in Mexico City, in its Sección de Estudios de Posgrado e Investigación. He is a prolific and long-standing contributor to the technical literature of general relativity and mathematical methods, usually publishing with collaborators and students. Work His output is unusually broad for a specialist, and falls into several distinct strands. The Lanczos potential and general relativity His most sustained subject is the Lanczos potential — a third-rank spin tensor introduced by Cornelius Lanczos in 1962 that generates the Weyl conformal tensor, playing a role for gravitation loosely analogous to that of the vector potential in electromagnetism. López-Bonilla has published constructions of it for particular spacetimes, including Lanczos Spintensor for the Gödel Metric and A Potential for the Lanczos Spintensor in Kerr Geometry (both 1999), and the associated Lanczos scalars recur throughout his work on the geometry of spacetime. Embedding of spacetimes A related strand concerns the local isometric embedding of a four-dimensional spacetime in a flat higher-dimensional space. A Necessary Algebraic Condition for R4 Embedded into E5 ( Apeiron , 2005) derives a purely intrinsic algebraic condition that any spacetime of embedding class one must satisfy, intended as a screening test to be applied before attempting to construct the second fundamental form. Classical electrodynamics and antennas He has also published on the Liénard–Wiechert field of an accelerated charge, on quaternionic formulations of Maxwell's equations and the Lorentz transformation, and — reflecting the engineering setting of ESIME — on the numerical analysis of circular and cross antennas.
