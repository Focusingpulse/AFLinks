A FALCON Field GUIDE Mushrooms mushrooms Mushrooms highlights 80 of the most common and sought-after mushroom species in North America. Detailed illustrations and descriptions of each mushroom’s physical attributes make it easy to identify mushrooms, including the poisonous ones, in your backyard and beyond. This is the essential resource when you’re out in the field. TM Nature in Your Pocket Todd Telander is a natural science illustrator, a wildlife artist, and the author and illustrator of many FalconGuides, including Birds of California, Birds of Colorado, Birds of Florida, and the Scats and Tracks series. He lives in Walla Walla, Washington.
