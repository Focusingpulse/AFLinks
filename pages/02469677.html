Boston Transcript. The columns of the daily press have been filled during the past week with stories of wonderful electrical invention. First Guglielmo Marconi Is credited with inventing the wireless telegraph system. Experiments were made in wireless telegraphy before Marconi was ever heard of. Second comes an alleged statement from Nikola Tesla to the effect that he has produced "an electrical disturbance of intense magnitude, which is continuing throughout the entire earth. In other words, I am producing, a disturbance of the earth's charge of electricity which can be felt to the uttermost parts of the earth." Then the startled reporter goes on to describe the provers. He said: "Tesla had his rooms in the laboratory on Houston-st. darkened. From the room adjoining the one in which he and his visitors stood a current, was turned on invisibly. A huge black disk hung on a frame about eight feet in diameter, from the center of which protruded a brass electrode seven inches in diameter. As the group gazed at this the apartment was filled with a crackling sound, which increased till it sounded like the rattle of musketry.
