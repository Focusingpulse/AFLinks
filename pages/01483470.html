Division Ramsay "While vibrations are the sound-stuff, the protoplasm of notes , semitones are, as it were, the atoms of which music is composed. We may think and talk of quarter tones and commas , apotomes and skismas , and dots, but these have no place as intervals for the musical ear , nor any part in the compositions which so charm us of the great masters." [ Scientific Basis and Build of Music , page 20]
