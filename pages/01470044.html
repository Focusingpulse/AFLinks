Electrical conductivity , a measure of a material's ability to conduct an electric current Conductivity (electrolytic), also the specific conductance , is a measurement of the electrical conductance per unit distance in an electrolytic or aqueous solution Ionic conductivity , a measure of the conductivity through ionic charge carriers Hydraulic conductivity , a property of a porous material's ability to transmit water Thermal conductivity , the intensive property of a material that indicates its ability to conduct heat Rayleigh conductivity , describing the behavior of apertures concerning the flow of a liquid or gas Nikola Tesla " Capacity of electrical conductors is variable, not constant, and formulas will have to be rewritten - Capacity varies with absolute height above sea level, relative height from Earth and distance from the Sun .
