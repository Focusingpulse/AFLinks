Hyiq.org - Vacuum Triode Amplifier Floyd Sweet Archive from Mr John Bedini These pictures are from Mr John Bedini they were taken recently for the purpose of this project. John got one of Floyds first boxes out of storage that he has kept and took these two great pictures of the box. This gives an excellent insight of the coil structure in the box and on one of the pictures you can even see the dust that has accumulated over the past 15 years. This is the second picture sent. Mr Bedini sent me back a reply that blew me away, John took some photos of one of Floyds original boxes before the Alloy box models that he has kept from the early days. This is the contents of the mail he sent me also. John Bedini is one of my hero's and always will be. Chris, Here is two pictures of Floyd Sweets original box as you can see the tickler coil is vertical and the power coil is horizontal.
