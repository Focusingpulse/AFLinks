Every term is a phase {16n−k/(8k+j)} on one circle, present from step 0 and moved by the single map x ↦ {16x}; each hexadecimal digit of π is a difference that this motion emits. Organizing the machine by one Form (compile first, nest, run, encapsulate, invert, fold, retain, read) yields exact statements at every scope. A lane runs from step k + ⌈log16(8k+j)⌉ − 1, but its layer’s value arrives only near log16(64k²/15); in between, the lanes move valuefree carries. BBP is the only zero-sum member of the Adamchik–Wagon line of formulas, and that is exactly what makes π the maximum of its own partial integral. The whole line is the shadow of log(1+i) = Σ ((1+i)/2)n/n: π is its inversion-odd part, log 2 its fold-even part, base 16 is the base −4 formula read across two seams, and π and log 2 share one integrand kernel that changes sign at the wheel’s first step.
