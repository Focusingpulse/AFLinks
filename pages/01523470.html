The interferometer and red shift, theory and experiment in biology, as well as aspects of Ancient Philosophies, and a modern age of extended means of communication are discussed and compared in critique with respect to a visual model, created mathematically, of an egg, that is used to demonstrate proposed physical and conceptual form. Overview Marvin Kirsh writes from the Department of Anthropology at California State University Los Angeles, and this paper is an anthropologist's critique of physics rather than a physics paper.
