Esoteric Lesson — GA 266. Esoteric Lessons III

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 18 May 1913 · archived # 2,428,248
, Stuttgart Translated by Steiner Online Library Esoteric Lesson Record A The esotericist can only make progress if he becomes increasingly aware of certain things. He must thoroughly and seriously absorb what was discussed yesterday in the public lecture. It must become a real experience for him that, just as the air in our lungs is part of the atmosphere surrounding us, which we take in through the process of breathing, so too does the spiritual-soul element within us belong to the entire spiritual-soul environment. He must also become very clear that waking up and falling asleep are nothing
