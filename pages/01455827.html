Astor And Keely To Confer Again. FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 15, 1895, Page. 1/4 Astor and Keely to Confer Again PHILADELPHIA, Nov. 14. - A local paper says another meeting has been arranged between Inventor Keely and Mrs. Bloomfield Moore and the New York capitalists, headed by John Jacob Astor , who are desirous of becoming interested in the Keely Motor Company . It will probably take place Saturday or the first of next week. [The New York Times, Published November 15, 1895 - Copyright @ The New York Times] See Also Keely Chronology
