VI. Theory of Knowledge Free of Assumptions and Fichte's Science of Knowledge - GA 3. Truth and Knowledge (1963)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 1,576,330
We have now defined the idea of knowledge. In the act of cognition this idea is directly given in human consciousness. Both outer and inner perceptions, as well as its own presence are given directly to the “I,” which is the center of consciousness. (It is hardly necessary to say that here “center” is not meant to denote a particular theory of consciousness, but is used merely for the sake of brevity in order to designate consciousness as a whole.)
