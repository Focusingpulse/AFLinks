Nikola Tesla Born ( 1856-07-10 ) July 10, 1856 Died January 7, 1943 (1943-01-07) (aged 86) Residence New York, NY, United States Nationality Serbian / USA Known for Alternating-current power systems, the induction motor, the Tesla coil, wireless transmission of power, Radiant Energy , rejection of Relativity Scientific career Fields Electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, physics Nikola Tesla (Serbian Cyrillic: Никола Тесла) (10 July 1856 – 7 January 1943) was a Serbian-American inventor, mechanical engineer and electrical engineer whose work established the alternating-current electrical system on which modern civilisation runs. He is equally important to this wiki for a second reason: throughout his life Tesla held to a mechanical, aether -based conception of space, matter and energy, and in his final decades he publicly and repeatedly rejected Albert Einstein 's theory of relativity and the notion of curved space. For that reason Tesla stands as the central historical figure for the free-energy, wireless-power and aether research communities documented here. Biography Tesla was born in Smiljan, in what is now Croatia, into a Serbian Orthodox family, studied engineering at the Polytechnic in Graz, and emigrated to the United States in 1884. In 1888 he patented the polyphase alternating-current induction motor and a complete system of AC generation and transmission – the basis of the electrical power system still in use today. He patented the resonant air-core transformer known as the Tesla coil in 1891 and demonstrated a radio-controlled boat in 1898. From 1899 his work turned to the wireless transmission of power, described below. He never managed his finances and died in poverty in New York on 7 January 1943. Tesla and the aether Tesla was trained in the physics of the 1870s and 1880s, and he never abandoned it.
