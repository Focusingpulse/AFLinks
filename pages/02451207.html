Decker: "New List Starts Friday @ Midnite" Next message: Harvey Norris: "Phase conjugation circuit?/ correction" The idea of how two resonant circuits would appear when run backwards in time has recently intrigued me by the watching of a red dwarf episode where they landed on an earth that was running backwards in time. A single series resonant circuit appearing to an observer running backwards in time would be the same observance as its opposite 180 phasing. Both of these phasings can be made in a normal time mode and interacted. However when the picture of a parallel resonant circuit is made and run in reverse time there is no way that an equivalent mirror image of that circuit can be made as in the previous example. In the previous example all that was necessary to produce a "mirror image symmetrical with time" was to merely switch the positions of a coil and resonating capacitor with regards to its input. This produces its corresponding 180 degree phasing. When this is done to a parallel circuit the reflection is not different but identical. But the opposite phasing of a tank circuit surely must be possible! But yet it is somewhat dumbfounding to understand how this is to be done? The only way it can be qualified as a difference is if that the generators themselves must run in opposite polarities in the second case to demonstrate a 180 phasing on a tank circuit. This shows some important considerations that may have been glossed over in the past. What seems to be applicable in one case may be supposed to be all applicable when it is not!
