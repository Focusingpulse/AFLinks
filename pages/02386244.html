You will find that Taubes was wrong, the field did not die after all. Read the book a little more carefully next time, and compare it to the peer reviewed published papers describing the same experiments Taubes attempts to describe. You will see that in every case, he made gross errors. Since his book has no footnotes and since none of the CF scientists I know actually talked to him, I have to conclude that he wrote an entire book about a field of science without actually reading any scientific papers or observing experiments. This is, shall we say, an extraordinary and unusual method. It is like going to review a symphony orchestra with your ears plugged up and a towel around your head so that you cannot hear or see anything.
