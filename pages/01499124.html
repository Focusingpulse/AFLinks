www.padrak.com/ine/INECFP98.html June 3, 1998 Return to the INE Main Page Return to the INE Announcements Page INE Advanced Energy Conference, August 1998: Call For Papers INE NEW-ENERGY SYMPOSIUM August 14-15, 1998 Call For Papers Please submit abstracts by May 15, 1998 to the NEN office. --- This date may be extended. Contact Hal Fox! --- Deadline for papers is July 15, 1998. Meeting will be held at the University of Utah Union Building on Friday and Saturday, August 14-15, 1998. Cost, if paid by 1 August1998, is $100 for members and $125 for non-members ($150 at the door). All papers accepted for presentation will be published in the Journal of New Energy. The journal will be available immediately after the conference. Author's are encouraged to send their abstracts immediately. The editorial staff of the Journal of New Energy will select the papers to be presented. Author's will have from 30 minutes to one hour for their presentation depending on the number of papers that qualify for presentation. If excess papers are offered, new-energy experimental papers will be given preference. Also will include a meeting of the Board of Directors of the INE.
