Introduction B efore Dick Cheney was selected as George W. Bush’s running mate, EarthRights International (ERI) had decided to look into oil services giant Halliburton, where Cheney was CEO. The reason for our interest was Halliburton’s prominent role in a corporate coalition called USAEngage, an offshoot of the National Foreign Trade Council (NFTC’) that has become an obstacle to democracy movements in the United States and in Burma, two countries where ERI works actively. At the time, we did not know that while Cheney headed the company, Halliburton had done business with the notorious Yadana pipeline project in Burma – an environmentally damaging project
