He also invented the first graduated and calibrated compass, with which others went about discovering the rest of the world. Peregrinus also is believed to have invented the first magnet motor, consisting of 17 magnets, and three wheels connected to three gears. The simplicity of the design belies the inventor's understanding of both the mechanical and magnetic principles involved.
