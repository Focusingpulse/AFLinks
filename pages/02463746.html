Improved Thyroid Function Designed to help those with Hypothyroidism This Module is designed to improve thyroid function for those with Hypothyroidism (low thyroid function).* Hypothyroidism is a condition that affects over five million people in America alone. Symptoms of Hypothyroidism include fatigue, weakness, weight gain or difficulty losing weight, dry skin and hair, depression, memory loss, and the list goes on. The goal of this Module is to improve thyroid function. When thyroid hormones become balanced, optimum thyroid function will be restored naturally. This will result in the elimination of symptoms and problems that arise from Hypothyroidism. Some issues addressed in this module: - Increased ability of thyroid to produce T3 (thyroxine) and T4 (triiodothyronine) - Increased tyrosine absorption - Increased communication between the pituitary gland and the thyroid - Increased production of TSH (thyroid stimulating hormone) - Increase balance of TSH and thyroid hormones - Increased T3 and T4 balance - Increased communication between hypothalumus and pituitary gland Recommended Program Supplements: - Spirulina - Multi-Vitamin and Mineral Supplement Recommended Duration of Use: Unlike many of our modules, Increased Thyroid Function requires continued used to receive consistent benefits. The biological instructions can only work while you are using the module. *This Module is not intended to diagnose or cure any conditions. Continue with any doctor prescribed medication and scheduled visits.
