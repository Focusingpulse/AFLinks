Overview The book is a long-range analysis of world energy supply and demand to the years 2000 and 2020, prepared in connection with the work of the World Energy Conference (now the World Energy Council). It surveys energy resources, consumption trends, and the outlook for the various primary energy sources, offering scenarios for the global energy system over the following decades.
