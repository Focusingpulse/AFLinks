Author(s) Hector A Munera Keywords galaxy, Sun, Michelson-Morley experiment , absolute motion, New Energy Published 2013 No. of pages 6 Abstract Determination of the motion of our earth relative to external frames of reference is a non-trivial task. Some theoreticians of the past, like Poincar, declared it was impossible to measure the absolute motion of the Earth by experiments carried out here. Nonetheless, during the past century, observational astronomers, physicists and engineers managed to estimate the terrestrial motion relative to several external frames.
