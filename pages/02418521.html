9. Pitris— GA 90a. Theosophy, Christology and Mythology

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 2,418,521
The Pitris of the Moon—Manvantara—had the task of developing the faculty of perception in its most ideal form. They had attained the likeness of Yahweh and became seeds. The solar Pitris were those who had progressed beyond the lunar stage of development. The lunar Pitris had attained likeness. Those who had trained as masters on the Moon were intellectually superior to the lunar deity. The earthly deity extends only as far as earthly development.
