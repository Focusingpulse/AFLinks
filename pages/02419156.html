The Round Robin >> Vol. 03 >> No. 04 [5] " NOT FOR PUBLICATION " (Cavern, and Temple, and the Wrath to Come) Round Robin Editor is in receipt of the following remarkable letter. Since it was marked "Not for publication" all references to specific persons and places have been deleted. But what remains is in our opinion too important to be withheld from RR readers, and we were able to secure permission to offer it in this issue. Those who have taken note of the predictions of F.G.H., also printed in RR, will find the following paragraphs of extraordinary interest - whether or not one extends credence to the supernormal sources of information. Dear RR Editor: My March copy of RR arrived today, and I an very happy to find confirmation in black and white of information that was given "occultly" last year to my son, who was then 14, by his unseen Atlantean priest-teacher. In RR article "The Catastrophe of 1960 and the Zones of Comparative Safety" , he lists (a certain mid-western State) as lying within a safety zone. Now, here is our story: My son, whom I referred to, is a sensitive. He was recently attending school in a city on the east coast of this country. The plans for his Christmas vacation were upset and he was obliged to spend a week in a boarding house, where he was uncomfortable and homesick. His Atlantean teacher directed him to take impressions in his note book, and this was the substance of the information given. That in the early manhood of my son (which might be construed as about 1960) there would be a war of such destruction that few would survive.
