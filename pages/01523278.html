Education Graduated from Brno University , 1956 PhD, Institute of Nuclear Physics , Řež, 1968 Biography Kajfosz was born in 1933 in Czechoslovakia. After graduating from Brno University he joined the Institute of Nuclear Physics in Řež, near Prague, where he remained until 1972.
