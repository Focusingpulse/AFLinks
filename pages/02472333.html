This is an honest-to-goodness story, though a press agent is its inspiration and a vaudeville house its place of installation. If you are one of the many who have been reading extraordinary claims for an electrical room lately installed at the Palace Theatre, and duly advertised by Walter J. Kingsley, publicity man for that house, don’t lay it aside as a fake. It’s even more than good advertising. It belongs in the category of the truth that is stranger than fiction. The electricity in that electrical room is the genuine article, just as the lemonade out in front is made from real lemons; just as the flowers that bank the stairways to the balcony are real flowers and not the “everlastings” made of cleverly colored and subtly scented tissue paper. All of which may be straws in the wind to indicate that a new genius of press agent has arrived.
