A Discrete Topological Network Cosmology: Resolving Cosmological Tensions via Vacuolar Link Spawning and Path Bifurcation Author: Brian Thompson Date: 29 August 2026 Status: Completed Formal Manuscript Formulation Abstract. We propose a novel, non-local cosmological framework wherein spacetime is modeled not as a continuous Lorentzian manifold, but as a discrete, fully interconnected quantum graph of indivisible, fixed-length structural units designated as quantum links. In this architecture, elementary particles are defined as localized, topologically invariant link-packets (torsion configurations) characterized by invariant rest mass (me ) and an invariant local speed of wave propagation (c). We demonstrate that metric expansion induces macroscopic mechanical tension across the network. Because the internal geometry of localized matter knots is topologically locked against deformation, the vacuolar space between nodes absorbs this kinetic stress via the combinatorial spawning of new vacuum links. This mechanism provides a purely geometric, dynamical alternative to the Cosmological Constant (Λ), naturally generating an evolving dark energy profile that reconciles the Hubble Tension. Furthermore, we outline the macroscopic consequences of primordial cosmic seeds acting as galactic tension shields and predict a unique testable divergence: the discrete bifurcation (splitting) of optical signals encountering hyper-pressurized inter-core galactic cables. 1.
