Tucker Free Energy Device

· archived # 1,468,690
This is a device developed by John and Carol Tucker of Wichita, Kansas. They displayed this device at a Tesla Conference in Colorado Springs, circa 1990. It was producing a power at about 10V DC, running a small motor and lighting a small light bulb.
