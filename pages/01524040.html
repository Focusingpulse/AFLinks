Ricker argues that the difficulty most readers have with the Catt Question is not a difficulty with the question itself but with the word electricity , which he holds has never been given a clear and consistent definition in physical science. He traces the confusion to the term being used for two quite different concepts a property of fundamental particles on one hand, and something else again in the treatment that follows Oersted's discovery and contends that no meaningful discussion of the Catt Question is possible until that ambiguity is resolved.
