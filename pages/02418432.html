A Description of the Initiation Process [ 1 ] Our task will be, in the few hours we have left this winter, to explore as best we can [our reflections on] the mystical views of the Christian era, possibly extending as far as Scotus Eriugena. Starting today, we have six more sessions: March 22, 29; April 5, 12, 19, and 26. I hope to be able to proceed as far as the ninth century A.D. and touch upon the consolidation of Christianity, when St. Augustine deepened Christian doctrine.
