He set out his ideas in the 1998 book Das Universum funktioniert anders - Realität kontra Relativität . Scientific work Krausz rejects the standard account in which gravity is an unchanging quantity fixed by an object's mass. In his Alternative Gravitation Theory he treats gravity instead as the action of an aether a medium he pictures as a pressure wave issuing from space that pushes bodies toward a planet and toward one another.
