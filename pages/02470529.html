The visit of members of the Niagara International Section of the American Institute of Electrical Engineers (AIEE) was the first event to commemorate the 100th anniversary of the birth of Tesla, who died in 1943. The AIEE group toured the Adams Station — where Tesla's polyphase alternating current system was first put to practical use. After dinner in the La Salle Yacht Club tonight, the group will hear Gardner H. Dales, administrative assistant in the System Project Engineering Department of the Niagara Mohawk Power Corporation. His talk will be on the Niagara Mohawk Adams Station and Tesla's contribution to it. Today, nearly all electricity in the world is generated, transmitted, distributed and turned into mechanical power by means of the Tesla system. Tesla, although unheard of by most laymen, is credited with the greatest single group of inventions in the world of electrical engineering.
