Theurgia Goetia Book II of the Lemegeton Theurgia Goetia – The Second Book of the Lemegeton Edited, formatted, and converted to Acrobat by Benjamin Rowe, June 1999. This edition was assembled from various published versions, and later checked and modified against a microfilm of Sloane MS. 2731 from the British Museum. The text as shown does not precisely reflect any of the versions; it is closest to the British Museum version, but with some interpolated words to enable the reader to more easily make sense of it. The Seals of the Spirits are substantially the same as those in the BM version. The illustrations have been optimized for printing on a 300-dpi or higher laser or inkjet printer. Depending on your monitor and driver software, they may not appear on your screen with their best quality. Typeset in Adobe Caslon.
