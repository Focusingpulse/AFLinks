Structural Attractors in Hierarchical Cosmologies III: Derveni, Bundahishn, and Manichaeism Extend the Topology Corpus to 14 Traditions

👤 Erydir Ceisiwr · · archived # 2,389,858
**Purpose.** This study extends the computational graph-theoretic methodology established in Ceisiwr (2026a, 2026b) by adding three new cosmological hierarchies to the emanation topology corpus: the Zoroastrian *Greater Bundahishn* (9th c. CE redaction of older Avestan material), the Manichaean Realm of Light (3rd c. CE, from *Kephalaia* and Theodore bar Konai), and the Derveni Orphic theogony (4th c. BCE). The expansion tests four predictions: (i) that the two-family topology classification remains stable at 14 traditions; (ii) that the Iranian Bundahishn pre-figures Suhrawardi's Ishraqi architecture; (iii) that Manichaean dualism produces a branching tree; and (iv) that sub-clustering within the linear-chain family is detectable by node count.
