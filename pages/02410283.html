👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 3 December 1907 · archived # 2,410,283
, Munich Translated by Sarah Kurland Compare Illusory Illness [ 1 ] In the course of his life man finds himself set between two powers. There is the current of events, the steady flow of facts, around him that make the most varied impression on him. Opposed to this stands man's own power within his inner being. One need consider life but superficially to have it dawn upon one that man must find a necessary balance between the forces and facts that storm in from all sides, and what unfolds in his inner life.
