VAJRA DORJE

· archived # 1,468,777
( click to enlarge ) VAJRA DORJE-DIAMOND LIGHTNING. - Vajra is probably the most important symbol of Buddhism. The term means diamond, for the point, and refers to the indestructible nature of the mind itself, of awakening. This little sceptre seems to be, at the origin, the diamond lightning of Indra, a symbol of royalty and power. When combined with the bell, the Vajra symbolizes skilled means and compassion, while the bell represents knowledge and emptiness. Taken separately, it has a complete symbolism which is the following: the 5 upper branches (1) represent the 5 wisdoms, the 5 facets of the diamond which is the enlightened mind: - Mirror-like wisdom, which means that the awakened mind, like a perfectly polished mirror, which clearly reflects all things, has the ability to know everything. - The wisdom of equality, which recognizes that all the phenomena of Samsara and Nirvana are of the same nature, or of a single essence: emptiness. - The wisdom of distinction, which indicates that the awakened mind perceives not only the emptiness of all the phenomena but also, at the same time without confusion, all the phenomena as they occur. - Fulfilling wisdom, which enables Buddhas to create camps of pure emanations that work for the benefit of beings. - The wisdom of the universal space (Dharmadatu), which indicates that all phenomena, beyond every duality, remain in the pure knowledge of the mind.
