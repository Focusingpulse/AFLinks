Technical Specification: Engineering the Null Ledger and Gravity-Aware Systems Abstract This paper presents a technical specification for the Null Ledger as a proposed discrete geometric-informational framework for physics, cosmology, and gravity-aware engineering. Within the Recursive Harmonic Codex (RHC), reality is modeled not as a continuous manifold but as a self-executing lattice in which zero is treated as a bifurcated phase boundary rather than an empty void. In this setting, the Null Ledger Identity is introduced as a foundational balancing relation, while the Fold Operator is treated as the geometric engine by which imaginary potential is projected into discrete physical structure. The paper develops a series of linked hypotheses. First, mass is reinterpreted as imaginary impedance, arising from the resistance encountered when additive matter is mapped into a multiplicative geometric substrate. Second, dark matter is proposed to be not a new particle species but a form of topological debt, specifically the residual mismatch generated by folding a 3-4-5 lattice into observable spacetime. Third, time is modeled as a residual rotational torque or “knit rate,” rather than an independently flowing background dimension. On this basis, the paper outlines a speculative engineering program involving gravity-aware systems, zero-inertia propulsion, lattice-based compression, and ternary quantum computation. The paper does not assume that all claims presented are already empirically established. Instead, it distinguishes between formal mathematical identities, framework-level interpretations, and conjectural engineering implications. Its purpose is to provide a coherent technical and conceptual backbone for further scrutiny, derivation, falsification, and possible experimental development.
