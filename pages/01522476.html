On this wiki the disputes concern the models applied to the observations rather than the observations themselves: the photometry, the spectra and the redshift catalogues are largely common ground, and the argument is about what they are evidence for . This page is a guide to that material. Where a count is given, it is the number of pages on this wiki whose text touches the topic, which is a rough measure of coverage rather than of merit.
