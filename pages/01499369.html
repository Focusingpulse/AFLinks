ALL RIGHTS RESERVED. HOW DO WE GET NEW ENERGY DISCOVERIES ACCEPTED BY THE SKEPTICAL SCIENTIFIC COMMUNITY? By Patrick Bailey, President INE Here is how: We write short articles on these topics; we provide names and research addresses of where the work is taking place; we report the experiments, the results, data, and plots; and we summarize the results so that any high-school student can understand it. Next we publish the results in NEN. Then, we post those articles on the INE website, and also post the articles as email messages to various UseNet bulletin boards. This process will cause the search engines, like www.hotbot.com and www.altavista.digital.com to pick up these topics and include them in their search engine databases. About a month later, we post messages to high school teachers and college professors, providing them with a summary of these various topics, and cite the website links to look at per their interest. A real good reference is: http://www.padrak.com/ine/SUBJECTS.html which lists all of the material in the INE website according to subject. There are also pages organized by the author's names.
