Title Entropy Read in full Read in full Author(s) Ricardo L Carezani Keywords Entropy, second law of thermodynamics, Autodynamics , pico-graviton, Cosmology Published Society for the Advancement of Autodynamics Website https://www.autodynamics.org/entropy/ "Entropy" is a short cosmological note by Ricardo L Carezani setting out the Autodynamics position that the entropy of the universe is constant rather than increasing. It is one of three claims listed on the site's Cosmology page as consequences of the theory, alongside "gravity is a particle" and "mass increases from gravity over time". Overview The note opens by quoting the second law as stated in a textbook — "The entropy of the universe never decreases: during any process, the entropy either remains constant or else increases", from Franklin Miller's College Physics , 5th edition, p. 372 — and rejects it: "This is not true for AD. For AD, entropy in the universe is a constant." The argument Why decay lowers entropy The mechanism is the one that runs through the whole theory. "The fundamental action in the AD universe consists of mass decay and energy absorption which will create 'singularities' (stellar mass), 'mass-concentration' as systems of large internal energy, and consequently, of small entropy." Absorption concentrates dispersed energy into matter; concentrated matter is a low-entropy state; so the process runs against the direction the second law describes. The scope of the second law Carezani then narrows what the law governs. "It is important to realize that the entropy principle refers exclusively to heat, that is, caloric energy." He asks what relation holds between caloric energy — particles in motion — radiant energy, and gravitational energy, and treats the question as open. The closed cycle His answer is a cycle.
