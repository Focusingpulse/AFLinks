6. The Symbol of the Bee, Evolution on Earth, Kamaloka – Devachan, Immortality — GA 90c. Theosophy and Occultism

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 25 September 1903 · archived # 2,418,820
, Berlin Translated by Steiner Online Library 6. The Symbol of the Bee, Evolution on Earth, Kamaloka — Devachan, Immortality [ 1 ] Last time, I spoke about the moment when the human being’s supernatural essence unites with what we call the “earthly body.” This union was made possible by the convergence of two essences—an astral essence and a physical one. Together, they are then subject to the laws of physics. Let’s take the example of a house. The condition of the house does not depend on its inhabitants. The condition of the house may depend on the temperature, the weather, and so on. [ 2 ]
