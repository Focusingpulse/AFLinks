👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 20 January 1914 · archived # 2,423,044
, Berlin Translated by Steiner Online Library First Lecture [ 1 ] In these four lectures that I will be giving to you during our General Assembly, I would like to discuss the relationship between humanity and the universe from a particular perspective. And I would like to hint at this perspective with the following words. [ 2 ] Human beings experience within themselves what we might call “thought,” and in thought, human beings can sense themselves as something directly active, as something capable of surveying its own activity.
