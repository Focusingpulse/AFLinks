Sacred Texts Ancient Near East Sumerian Mythology By Samuel Noah Kramer [1944, 1961] The Sumerians were a non-Semitic, non-Indo-European people who lived in southern Babylonia from 40003000 B.C.E. They invented cunieform writing, and their spiritual beliefs influenced all successive Near Eastern religions, including Judaism, Christianity and Islam. They produced an extensive body of literature, among the oldest in the world. Samuel Noah Kramer spent most of his life studying this literature, by piecing together clay tablets in far-flung museums. This short work gives translations or summaries of the most important Sumerian myths. Frontispiece Title Page Preface Contents List of Illustrations Introduction Chapter I.
