I know this really interesting guy named Willis Winters who lives near Plainview, Texas. His email address is wwinters@texasonline.net Don't get ticked of with this email because it will cover ecology and something new with hydrogen that I've not heard of before. I've not heard from Willis now in many months though I've sent him several emails over that period. Now Willis is around 70 or so and is a very honorable and respectable guy who I had the pleasure of meeting through Jack Veach and Dr. MacDonald many years ago. Willis surprised me tonight with a phone call just to touch base and to let me know he'd moved from the city (Plainview) to a new place in the country. Seems he traded his custom built energy efficient house in the city for an ex-Emu farm with about 4 acres of land. Now Willis has been involved in all kinds of projects and activities over the years and I've never had the time to hang around with him for an extended period to find out much of what he takes for granted or has long since forgotten. Somehow tonight we got to talking about the rains which led to mosquitoes and Willis says a few years back, he developed a spray composed of garlic and other ingredients that will repel mosquitoes better than anything on the market. He says when he mixes habanajero pepper with it, it will kill mosquitoes, aphids and other pests. He sells this as a concentrate for those who might have problems. I asked him if he'd ever considered selling this stuff through the net and he said no, he's been selling it mostly to local buyers and friends of his across the country but he is open to distributors if anyone might want to do some testing and footwork.
