Surface Preparation for Epoxy Content on this page supplied from the web site of Jim Lux Surface Preparation for Epoxy Bonding Proper surface preparation before bonding with epoxies is essential. Here is a summary of prep techniques taken in a larger part from "the Bell Jar". Aluminum and Aluminum Alloys Degrease with trichloroethylene Dip in the following solution for 10 minutes: 3 parts sodium dichromate 20 parts water 10 parts 95% sulfuric acid Rinse in ambient tap water Rinse in hot (65-75 degrees C) distilled water Air dry Note: Some alloys, such as #360, only require one to three minute dip in the acid dichromate. Copper, Brass, and other Copper Alloys Degrease with trichloroethylene Dip in either of the two following solutions 6 parts ferric chloride 200 parts water 30 parts concentrated nitric acid (70%) OR 25% ammonium persulfate for 1-2 minutes Rinse with distilled water and dry Ferrous alloys other than Stainless Degrease with trichloroethylene Sandblast with 100 grit sand or etch in 15% hydrochloric acid (equal parts concentrated hydrochloric acid and water) for 10 minutes. Etched surfaces should be rinsed immediately and dried with hot air. Freshly sandblasted or etched steel begins to rust immediately; therefore adhesive should be applied as soon as the surface has been prepared. Stainless Steel, Chromium Degrease with trichloroethylene Etch with concentrated hydrochloric acid for 15 minutes at room temperature OR with a solution of 90 parts water 40 parts 96% sulfuric acid 0.2 parts Nacconol NR (National Aniline) for 10 minutes at 65 degrees C followed by 90 parts water 15 parts 70% nitric acid 2 parts 48% hydrofluoric acid Rinse in hot distilled water and dry with hot air.
