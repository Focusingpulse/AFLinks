One of the crucial reasons for its success was that the leaders of the pro- and anti-shogunate factions shared the view that, with the impending threat of the foreign powers, they should cooperate and avoid a civil war for the sake of Japan. Of course, history abounds with the opposite case where leaders of factions within a country, sticking to their positions, ended up with a civil war, which led to the destruction of the country they intended to save.
