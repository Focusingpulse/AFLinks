1 Somsikov A.I. Etymology of the word АЗ The reversal formed by a pair of words АЗ and ЗА is considered. АЗ is the name of the first letter of the old alphabet, nowadays known simply as A, meaning the word “I”. Let us consider its etymology presented in the dictionaries. Origins of the word АЗ АЗ – I from Old Church Slavonic sources, borrowed from Old Slavonic азъ (az). Cf. I. Origins of the word аз in Vasmer's Etymological Dictionary. COMMENTS. The way the meaning of "I" stems from the word АЗ, is not explained. For this reason, its etymology is not presented. Etymons Etymons or source words of the Russian language are formed by arbitrary combinations of consonant and vowel sounds and therefore their etymology, that is, their origin, is also arbitrary. Etymons usually appear in pairs, mutually defining each other in the form of local negation. It is formed by a phonetic reversal. These local negations have the opposite meaning – ДО and ОД, TO and OT, ДА and АД (agreement and disagreement), РАЙ and ЙАР (normalized record is ЯР, in the meaning of calm and swearing), ОН (HE) and НО (that is, not he), etc. One of such pairs is АЗ and ЗА (behind). So now let us consider the etymology of the word ЗА offered by the dictionaries. Origins of the word ЗА (behind) За. Common Slavonic word with the original meaning "behind". Origin of the word за in the etymological online dictionary by G. A. Krylov. За. Common Slavonic. The primary meaning is spatial ("behind"), others (connected to time, causal) are secondary. The origin of the word за in the etymological online dictionary by N.M. Shansky. COMMENTS. This case as well represents only the well-known meaning, instead of origin, i.e. etymology. Yet the etymology is not presented. The dictionary called "etymological" does not correspond to its content. Etymology suggested The meaning of the word ЗА is quite clear – following, behind, afterwards, later. In a spatial meaning this word can be defined as following someone or somethin
