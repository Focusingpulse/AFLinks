First, from the Arabs, who almost alone preserved the ancient sciences through the dark ages. Secondly, from Jewish scholars, Rabbis of Hebrew culture, who had received the traditional lore now identified by the name ‘Kabalah,’ that tradition of high magic to which ancient Chaldea and Babylon so largely contributed. Lastly, from Egypt, the ancient Egypt of the Pharaohs, ruled by those mighty priest kings of old, whose wisdom instructed Moses, and whose training enabled him to supersede all minor professors of magical arts, and to rule with some success a turbulent and rebellious people — the early Hebrews.
