It deals with what they still refuse to call 'aether'. http://xxx.lanl.gov/abs/gr-qc/9903094 Quintessence, the Gravitational Constant, and Gravity Authors: Takeshi Chiba Dynamical vacuum energy or quintessence, a slowly varying and spatially inhomogeneous component of the energy density with negative pressure, is currently consistent with the observational data. One potential difficulty with the idea of quintessence is that couplings to ordinary matter should be strongly suppressed so as not to lead to observable time variations of the constants of nature. We further explore the possibility of an explicit coupling between the quintessence field and the curvature.
