Title Pion Decay and Autodynamics Read in full Read in full Author(s) Ricardo L Carezani Keywords Autodynamics , pion decay, Muon, Neutrino, momentum conservation, PIBETA Published Society for the Advancement of Autodynamics Website https://www.autodynamics.org/pion-decay/ "Pion Decay" is a paper by Ricardo L Carezani applying the Autodynamics equations to charged pion decay. Carezani opens by conceding that the exercise is "redundant, because AD explains every type of decay", and states a different purpose: to illustrate "the state-of-the-art in momentum conservation for particle decay, as accepted by the scientific mainstream." Overview The paper has four parts. The first two work the decay chain π + → μ + → e + u n d e r t h e A u t o d y n a m i c s k i n e m a t i c s , with the products stopped by a water molecule so that the momentum balance can be followed to the end. The third repeats the calculation with the pion in flight. The fourth reproduces an email exchange with a physics professor and uses it to argue that the standard account of momentum conservation in pion decay is circular. A QBasic listing, PION1.BAS, is printed with its output. The argument A pion "at rest" is not at rest Carezani's first move is a criticism of the standard setup. A pion beam stopped in a target is conventionally treated as a pion at rest, but "for a 'target' to stop Pions in a beam simply means that other particles acquire kinetic energy equivalent to the momentum transferred from the Pion." Strictly, he argues, the pion's mass should be added to that of whatever stopped it, because the two travel together afterwards. This becomes the crux of his objection in part 4. The Autodynamics ledger In Autodynamics the muon's kinetic energy is simply the pion–muon mass difference, 33.94 MeV.
