(courtesy University of Science and Philosophy ) ( click to enlarge ) THE SPIRAL IN NATURE Within the wave field, centripetal spirals wind light waves into spheres and then cease to be spirals. Centrifugal spirals unwind spheres into cubes and also cease to be spirals. Centripetal and centrifugal spirals are electrically sexed mates. Their interchange borns all forms and likewise voids all forms. (Fig. 46) (courtesy University of Science and Philosophy ) ( click to enlarge ) Centripetal spirals are electro-positive. They wind light inward into incandescent spheres. They are of the father-light which refolds unfolding forms. They begin their half cycles at cube boundary planes of wave fields and end them at centers of spheres. Fig. 47 diagrams the six mirror planes which reflect gravitating light centripetally inward through the poles. (courtesy University of Science and Philosophy ) ( click to enlarge ) Centrifugal spirals are electro-negative. They unwind light of incandescent spheres into cold black light of space. They are of the mother-light which unfolds formlessness into form. They begin their half cycles at centers of rest in spheres and end them in the reprojection mirrors of wave field planes. Figure 48 diagrams the six mirror planes which project radiating light centrifugally outward from equators.
