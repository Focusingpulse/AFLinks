The rotating Ampere bridge represents an electromotor without stator, because it rotates under the action of internal forces thus violating Newton's third law. Nevertheless, back tension is induced at its rotation in contradiction with the relativistic concepts, according to which tension can be induced only if there is mutual motion between "magnet" and "wire."
