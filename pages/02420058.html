The Lord's Prayer [ 1 ] When speaking of prayer in the Christian sense, one must first and foremost realize that the form of prayer is little more than contemplation—surrender to the Divine. In those major religions that seek to achieve this surrender more through intellectual contemplation, it is called meditation; in those religions where devotion springs more from the heart than from the mind—more from the personality—this devotion is called prayer.
