Overview Black Holes — Fact or Fiction? is a paper by John O Campbell of North Hills, California, published in Apeiron volume 5, numbers 3–4 (July–October 1998), beginning at page 151. Its method is to treat black holes using "mundane static concepts" of mass energy and gravitational field energy. Campbell observes that the gravitational field, like the electric field, carries an energy density — following Stratton's treatment of the electrostatic case and Kibble's parallel between ∇· E = ρ q /ε and ∇· g = −4π G ρ m — and that for a spherical body the total energy in the external field is Gm ²/2 R . Every body at rest therefore has two energies: a mass self-energy mc ² and a gravitational field energy. Their ratio, which he writes ℜ, works out to 2 c ² R / Gm . Since the Schwarzschild condition is R ≤ 2 Gm / c ², it translates into ℜ ≤ 4 — "a critical ratio of mass energy to gravity field energy" rather than a critical radius.
