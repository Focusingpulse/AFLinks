Modern Soul Research The development of science in the last century could not wrongly be called a conquest of the scientific spirit over almost all areas of human cognition. The triumphant power of this procession can be seen nowhere better than in the character that research into the human soul has assumed in scientific circles over the last few decades. The modern psychologist, who tries to get to grips with the rising and falling phenomena of our inner being
