Preface to the 1923 Edition — Mysticism at the Dawn of the Modern Age (1960)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 1,586,282
In this work more than twenty years ago, I wanted to answer the question, Why do a particular form of mysticism and the beginnings of modern scientific thinking clash in a period from the thirteenth to the seventeenth century. [ 2 ] I did not wish to write a “history” of the mysticism of this period, but only to answer this question. The publications which have appeared on this subject in the past twenty years do not, in my opinion, furnish any grounds for making any changes in the answer.
