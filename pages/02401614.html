GC2026-SGG054 Inter-magnitude relations for the seicmicity of the Klyuchevskaya group of volcanoes Alisiya P. Molokova1,2 , A. A. Skorkina1 , V. B. Smirnov2 1 Institute of Earthquake Prediction Theory and Mathematical Geophysics, Russian Academy of Sciences 2 Lomonosov Moscow State University molokovaap@my.msu.ru In this study, the object of research is the volcanic seismicity of the Klyuchevskaya group of volcanoes (KGV), located on the Kamchatka Peninsula. The Klyuchvskaya group of volcanoes represents one of the largest and most active volcanic structures on Earth, formed in a subduction zone by magma generation at the plate boundary [1, 2]. Volcanic earthquakes are commonly divided into two main types: long-period (LP) and volcano-tectonic (VT) earthquakes. These earthquakes occur at or near volcanoes, are associated with volcanic processes, and differ from tectonic ones in their source mechanisms and characteristics of seismic signals [3]. Within the framework of this study, a preliminary identification of volcanic earthquake types was carried out based on characteristic frequencies by estimating the ratio of spectral amplitudes between low and high frequencies. For LP earthquakes, the frequency limits were selected as 1 Hz and 3 Hz; for VT earthquakes, as 4 Hz and 11 Hz. For this analysis, we used waveform data from permanent seismic stations of the Kamchtka Branch of the Geophysical Survey of the Russian Academy of Sciences (KB GS RAS), recorded during the period 01.02.2021–18.02.2021, prior to the Gorshkov vent eruption (18.02.2021) of Klyuchevskoy volcano [4].
