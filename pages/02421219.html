Reed Publication WISE Journal ; WISE Wiki and Digital Library Website instituteforscientificexploration.org The World Institute for Scientific Exploration ( WISE ) is an American non-profit research organisation based in Baltimore, Maryland , incorporated in that state as a 501(c)(3) charitable corporation. Its stated purpose is to conduct and promote research on subjects that fall outside conventional funding — complementary and traditional medicine, and energy medicine in particular; new and low-cost forms of energy; consciousness and parapsychology; unidentified aerial phenomena; and unexplained phenomena generally — and to apply the results to the development of devices and services in biomedicine, energy, agriculture and the environment. Its president and chief executive is the physician John H. Reed , who is also its treasurer. Organisation WISE is structured into a large number of small divisions grouped under four headings: Earth Sciences — agriculture, earth sciences and energy Health and Medical — eight divisions, among them behavioural medicine, nutrition and the traditional medical systems Humanities — thirteen divisions, including archaeology, history, philosophy and religion Science and Technology — ten divisions, including astronomy, chemistry, engineering and physics The breadth is deliberate and is the organisation's most distinctive feature: it is not a single-subject body but an umbrella intended to cover anomalous research of any kind. Reed heads the Health and Medicine division in addition to the presidency. Officers and directors As listed by the organisation: John H. Reed , M.D., MBA, MPH — President and Chief Executive Officer,
