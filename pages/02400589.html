It can say that the successor of an admissible state is itself admissible — that transformation never leaves the space in which transformation is possible — or it can say that no state is permitted to be its own only continuation. The two readings are usually treated as the same sentence. They are not. The first prunes nothing: measured across all total maps on a finite state space, it excludes exactly zero candidate laws, because it is a typing statement and a type declaration should be free. The second is the entire content of the principle, and its price is exactly log2(e) = 1.442695 bits, asymptotically independent of the size of the space. That price has a source. The maps invariant under a change of index origin are precisely the translations, and exactly one of them is excluded by the second reading: the translation by zero, which is the unique map under which the two origins coincide. The forbidden object is not a state that fails to move. It is the collapse of two simultaneous readings of one object into one reading.
