Our research utilized larvae and pupae of the tobacco hornworm, Manduca sexta, as host; various bacterial pathogens and the parasitoid, Cotesia congregata. It is my hope that I will be able to rebuild a research program focused more narrowly on defensive responses of the insect gut to microbes during larval growth and larval-pupal metamorphosis, and on hemolymph and gut peptides induced in response to fungal infection.
