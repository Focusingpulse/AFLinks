De Hilster argues that dark matter exists as a hypothesis because postulating something unseen is a better career move than finding an explanation within existing theory. The observation it was invented to cover stars at a galaxy's edge moving faster than Kepler or Newton predict is, he holds, a sign that the gravitational calculation is being applied wrongly rather than that invisible matter is present.
