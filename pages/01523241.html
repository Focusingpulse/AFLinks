Eichler Born ( 1941-07-14 ) July 14, 1941 (age85) Residence Little Rock, AR, United States Nationality USA Knownfor Expanding Earth , Cosmology , Computers Scientific career Fields Computer Scientist , Cosmology , Expansion Tectonics John B. Eichler is an American computer scientist and independent researcher known for his work on Expansion Tectonics — the view that the Earth has grown in radius and mass over geological time — and for proposing a physical mechanism, grounded in the electrical nature of the universe, by which the Earth could acquire that additional matter.
