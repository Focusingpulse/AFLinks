David Pantone Nationality USA Known for New Energy , GEET plasma fuel reformer technology Scientific career Fields Independent Researcher , Inventor Institutions GEET International Institute David Pantone is an American independent researcher and educator who develops, demonstrates and teaches the GEET (Global Environmental Energy Technology) plasma fuel reformer. He is the son of the GEET inventor Paul Pantone , and since his father's death in December 2015 he has led the GEET International Institute, continuing the family's work outside the mainstream energy-research establishment. Note on identity: David Pantone is a distinct person from his father Paul Pantone (1950–2015), who invented the GEET fuel processor and has a separate page on this wiki. Both have spoken at Tesla Tech conferences, and conference listings and video archives are sometimes confused between the two. Biography David Pantone is a son of Paul Pantone, the Utah inventor who developed the GEET fuel processor and was later confined to a Utah state mental hospital following legal proceedings that his supporters regard as a wrongful persecution. David was active in the campaign to free his father, publishing recorded interviews intended to demonstrate that Paul Pantone retained full mental faculties while institutionalised; Paul Pantone was released in May 2009. Paul Pantone died in December 2015 in Murfreesboro, Tennessee, after a long illness. David Pantone took over the promotion, licensing and teaching of the GEET technology and runs the GEET International Institute, which states that "the goal of Paul Pantone and his son David Pantone is to use GEET to wipe out pollution and reduce the cost of living up to 95% for all of humanity." Scientific contributions David Pantone's work is centred on the GEET fuel processor, which its proponents describe as a retrofit device that pre-treats fuel before it enters an internal combustion engine.
