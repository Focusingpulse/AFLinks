But the bottom line is that I can charge a battery with another battery and have much more in the charged battery than I took out of the source battery! I was working on building a full sized unit that would run our entire house without having an electric bill...when I got interrupted with PATHS. Within a year I will return some of my energy to finishing the full sized unit to power our house! Kevin
