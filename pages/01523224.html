Jeff Yee Born ( 1972-10-12 ) October 12, 1972 (age 53) Honolulu, Hawaii, United States Residence Dublin, CA, USA Alma mater University of Hawaii, Antioch University Known for Energy Wave Theory Scientific career Fields Theoretical physics, Wave structure of matter Jeff Yee (born 12 October 1972) is an American technology executive and independent theoretical physicist, known as the originator of Energy Wave Theory (EWT), a classical wave model in which particles are standing waves in an aether and the fundamental forces arise from wave interference. Yee came to physics from outside it. A mechanical engineer by training and a wireless-industry executive by career, he began researching particle physics in 2007 while writing a time-travel novel, and by 2014 was publishing mathematical models of the wave structure of matter. His stated aim is reductive: that the energies of all particles and the strengths of three of the four forces follow from four constants of the medium plus one property of the electron. He credits his intuition for the theory to an unlikely source — growing up surfing in Hawaii, where he says he came to understand ocean waves and their energy long before he applied the idea to matter. Career Jeff Yee is a technology executive, with various roles in the wireless industry at AT&T, Treemo Labs, Antenna Software, Moovweb and ZTE. Jeff brings product and entrepreneurial experience to theoretical physics, as a contributor to Energy Wave Theory . As an author, Jeff provides an overview of the subatomic world and its relationship to energy, in the The Particles of the Universe series on Amazon. As he states in the first book in the series, a better understanding of the subatomic realm should lead to new and innovative products. In the second book in the series, many of the issues within the quantum domain of physics is simplified and united into classical physics.
