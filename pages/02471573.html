USES CAMERA AT FALLS LAUGHS WHEN ASKED WHETHER WIRELESS TRANSMISSION OF POWER IS POSSIBLE. Special to The Buffalo Express. Niagara Falls, April 27. — Lord Kelvin, who is considered by many well-informed people as the greatest living man of science, visited Niagara Falls for the second time yesterday and to-day. Lord Kelvin has been greatly interested in the power development at the Falls since the inception of the great scheme, for he was one of the men consulted preliminary to attempting the work of harnessing the waterfall. In 1897 he first saw the result of the labors of the picked men who formulated the plan and at that time he wrote in the visitors’ book at the Niagara Falls Power House: “I am very much pleased to see the great success here achieved as a result of courageous undertaking and originality of invention and skillful design in construction.” To-day when an Express reporter asked him if the changes at Niagara Falls had exceeded his expectations, Lord Kelvin replied: “I’m sure I don’t know. They are very wonderful, but I expected wonderful things.” Lord Kelvin is a kindly faced, benevolent-looking old gentleman.
