Reciprocally, if a current I is passed through the wire, a motor force F = BIL tends to move the wire in a perpendicular direction. What is less well known is the case in which the wire is shielded from the magnetic field, for example, inserting it in a coaxial iron cylinder. Inside the shield B = 0.
