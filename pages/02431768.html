Neural pathways and Binaural beats?

· archived # 2,431,768

I am not a scientist, but maybe the constant exposure to the binaural beats has developed broader neural pathways and the instructions can flow more easily, utilizing less power (neurotransmitters) to deliver all the instructions? Maybe Aaron or someone else more educated in such matters could weigh in on this subject.
