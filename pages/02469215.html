THE LANGUAGE OF THE BIRDS THELANGUAGE OFTHE BIRDS OUR ANGELIC CONNECTION 1 THE LANGUAGE OF THE BIRDS The Language of the Birds is one of several names given to the secret hermetic language of esotericism. Since at least the Dark Ages (and I don’t mean the 1980’s) an idea has existed that there once was a language, or a particular word, which perfectly expressed the nature of things. This language was called the Language of the Birds. It is well known by scholars in the field of linguistics that Adam in the Garden of Eden spoke a mysterious primordial language, and that this language (symbolized by the apple) provided immense power and abundance to humanity. Through this Language Adam and Eve were given “dominion” over nature in Genesis 1:26. It is believed that all humanity shared this common language. It was the great unifier. Similar to the way the English word and becomes et in French and und in German, yet may be expressed world wide by the sign & -- all language was once universally understood. This Language was lost when Adam and Eve were expelled from Eden -- or so we have been taught to believe.
