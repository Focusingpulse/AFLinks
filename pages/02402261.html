MolPhot2026-main019 Influence of Plasmonic Silver Nanoparticles on the Transient Absorption of Rhodamine 6G Leila A. Bolatova1 , Niyazbek K. Ibrayev1 1 Institute of Molecular Nanophotonics, Karaganda National Research University named after academician Ye.A.Buketov, Karaganda, 100024, Kazakhstan bla.041997@gmail.com The spectral and kinetic properties of a hybrid system consisting of an ethanol solution of rhodamine 6G (10-5 mol/L) and spherical silver nanoparticles (NPs) (42.66 ±13 nm) were investigated using nanosecond laser flash photolysis (LP980, Edinburgh Instruments). It was established experimentally that the transient absorption spectrum of Ag NPs is concentrated in the UV region with a maximum at 380 nm and exhibits a regular vibrational structure with a 6-nm step [1]. For the rhodamine 6G solution, the laser pulse induces two coupled processes: ground-state bleaching at a wavelength of 530 nm and the appearance of an induced absorption band in the 400–480 nm range with a maximum at 440 nm (ΔOD= 0.037). This band is attributed to optical transitions of molecules between the excited states S1 →S4 of rhodamine 6G monomers, which is qualitatively consistent with data obtained for aqueous solutions of the dye [2]. The recorded decay time is τ=6.54 ns. A pronounced dependence of the spectral and kinetic parameters of the dye transi-ent absorption at a wavelength of 440 nm on the concentration of plasmonic Ag NPs (CAg) was established experimentally.
