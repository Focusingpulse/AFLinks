Z. Li or Xing Zhong Li) is a Chinese physicist and professor emeritus in the Department of Physics at Tsinghua University in Beijing, China. He is known internationally for his theoretical and experimental research on Cold Fusion and low-energy nuclear reactions (LENR), and in particular for the selective resonant tunneling model of sub-barrier fusion. Biography Li spent his academic career at Tsinghua University in Beijing, where he taught and carried out research in the Department of Physics and later held the title of professor emeritus.
