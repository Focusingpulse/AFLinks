Solid State Bedini SG on 3 Battery Power Supply

Hi All, here's a (relatively) quick video on my latest project. It's a Bedini solid state SG running on a 3 battery power supply - inspired by Peter Lindemann's rotored version he showed at the 2016 Energy Conference. I went solid state mainly because I suck at mechanical stuff and I have future experiments planned with the solid state version.
