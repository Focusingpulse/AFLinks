A DECOMPOSITION FORMULA FOR THIRD-ORDER REAL ANTISYMMETRIC MATRICES Luca Pettinari Alumnus of Polytechnic University of Marches Bratislava, April 2022 lalb.pettinari@gmail.com ABSTRACT A decomposition formula for an antisymmetric matrix Aω ∈ A3(R) is provided, where its axial vector is expressed as ω = Mν, with M symmetric and ν ∈ R 3 . The proof is based mainly on vector projection through Frobenius inner product. In the end, a vectorial identity involving cross product is proved as a corollary of the decomposition formula.
