He works on alternative paradigms in physics, with interests spanning the ether , angular momentum, fluid mechanics, and analytical philosophy. Beyond his own research, he is significant to the dissident-science community as a publisher : he owns ÉDITIONS D'ASSAILLY (Assailly Publishing), the house that issues Jean de Climont 's directory of dissident scientists.
