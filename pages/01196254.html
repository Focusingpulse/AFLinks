New cosmological words based in the idea of Geology. Juan Elías Millas Vera juanmillaszgz@gmail.com Zaragoza (Spain) February 2021 0 – Abstract: The etymology of the word Geology is the union of “ge” (from the term Gea the goddess of earth) and “logia” (the study of something). Following this rule, we can form a few words in reference to the study of different celestial bodies. 1– Formation of the words. In this creation of words, we should look at the analogy between the usually Latin -from Roman culture- names of the celestial bodies and their equivalence in Greek names. We are going to introduce a table with the results: Celestial bodies. (Planets) Greek god. Science of study. Mars Ares Aresology Mercury Hermes Hermesology Venus Aphrodite Aphroditology Jupiter Zeus Zeusology Saturn Cronus Cronusology Uranus Uranus Uranusology Neptune Poseidon Poseidonology We can also name some of the satellites: Celestial bodies. (Satellites) Greek god. Science of study. Moon Selene Selenology Phobos Phobos Phobosology Deimos Deimos Deimosology Io - Iology Europa - Europology Ganimede - Ganimedology Calisto - Calistology 2- Recommendations. For other science names of different bodies (for example, the satellites of Uranus or Saturn) we need to take the official name of the body and add the particle “-ology” (when it ends in consonant). When it ends in vocal, we need to replace the last vocal whit the “o” of the particle “-ology”. 3- Conclusions. In a world where space exploration is a fact, it is possible that in a few years we need to differentiate sciences of study of determining points, therefore, I wanted to contribute with my particular point of view.
