The Round Robin >> Vol. 02 >> No. 06 - SPECIAL ANNOUNCEMENT - We regret to announce that MAXIN 96, David D. Dagmar, Editor, has temporarily suspended publication, due to pressure of personal affairs on Mr. Dagmar and his lack of time for editorial work. Unexpired subscriptions may apply on Round Robin , the June issue of which is being sent to Maxin readers. If you are already a subscriber to Round Robin , or do not wish to make this change, please notify us promptly, and state when your subscription expires, if you have a memo. at hand. In any case, be sure that we have your correct address and postal zone number . Round Robin is published monthly (instead of bi-monthly) .25 a copy, 5 issues for $1.00, one year for $2.50. It is devoted chiefly to the fields of psychism, parapsychology, psychical research, and the borderland sciences, and is not published in the interests of any organization. It is a non-profit publication, and has not, so far, carried any paid advertising. In company with the numerous readers of Maxin 96, we greatly regret the suspension of that magazine; we take pleasure, however, in announcing that Mr. Dagmar has consented to act as Contributing Editor to Round Robin until such time as Maxin 96 can resume publication. ------ ------ A Few of the Articles Published by Round Robin in 1946 Mediumship of Jack Webber (Vincent Gaddis) . .
