Ramsay The CHROMATIC SYSTEM of chords is developed from these three primitive chromatic chords , and in the course of its development one or two notes are brought in semitonic progression to the middle , one or two to the root , and one or two to the top of all major and minor tonic chords . Likewise, at one time or another in the course of the system, there is one note in common with the middle , one note in common with the root , and one note in common with the top of all the major and minor tonic chords . [ Scientific Basis and Build of Music , page 57 ] PLATES XVII . & XVIII . CHROMATIC RESOLUTIONS , MAJOR AND MINOR . These two plates show the chromatic chord resolving into the twelve major and twelve minor tonic chords of the twenty-four scales . There seems to be twenty-five, but that arises from making G? and F# in the major two scales , whereas they are really only one; and the same in the minor series, E? and D# are really one scale . C in the major and A in the minor , which occur in the middle of the series, when both sharps and flats are employed in the signatures , are placed below and outside of the circular stave to give them prominence as the types of the scale ; and the first chromatic chord is seen with them in its major and minor form , and its typical manner of resolving - the major form rising to the root , and falling to the top and middle ; the minor form falling to the top , and rising to the root and middle .
