GC2026-PERM049 Two Stages of Chemical Remagnetization in the Paleoproterozoic Rocks of the Belomorian Mobile Belt Andrey V. Andreev1 , Natalia V. Lubnina1 1 Faculty of Geology, Lomonosov Moscow State University andreeff19653011@yandex.ru For the differentiation and typification of secondary alteration processes that affected remagnetization within the Belomorian Mobile Belt, a key study area was selected in the vicinity of the settlement of Ambarny. In this area, intensely metamorphosed gabbro-norites (2.4–2.45 Ga), metagabbros (2.2 Ga), and meta-ferrogabbros (~2.1 Ga) are spatially juxtaposed. Analysis of geological, petrographic, and microprobe data for mafic rocks of varying degrees of metamorphism and different compositions has allowed the identification of areas and rock types that are most promising for paleomagnetic studies within each of the investigated structures. In the Belomorian Province, from the perspective of the distribution, composition, and grain size of ore phases, intrusions of high-Mg mafic rocks are considered perspective for assessing remagnetization processes. In these rocks, the recrystallization of fine-grained ore phases occurs during the alteration of primary mafic magmatic minerals under metamorphic conditions.
