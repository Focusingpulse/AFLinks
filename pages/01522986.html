Felder is an American physicist and a Professor of Physics at Smith College in Northampton, Massachusetts. His research centers on inflationary cosmology , numerical simulations of the early universe, and computational and written tools for teaching physics. He is the co-author, with his brother Kenny Felder, of the textbooks Mathematical Methods in Engineering and Physics and Modern Physics . Biography Felder grew up in North Carolina and earned a B.A. from Oberlin College.
