👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 28 February 1911 · archived # 2,422,433
, Berlin Translator Unknown Excurse Five [ 1 ] For the goal we have set before us, which is connected with the study of the Gospel of Mark, is to be pursued further, it must be grasped in its widest meaning. It may perhaps be only after a considerable time that the reason will appear why one or another line of study has been pursued, and what connection these have with our subject. We will have to speak to-day, for instance, of certain things which apparently are far removed from our theme, but which will be of great assistance to us in our later studies.
