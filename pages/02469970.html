The inventor works for peace — his greatest war triumphs have been Industry’s greatest prizes. America will find through her call to invention a Nation Spirit, as France found it in Art, and Germany has found it in Kultur. Peace and progress are but little half-sisters of Invention. They come after Invention, never before it. “Hutch,” said the “Old Man,” — for so he is known through the antonomastic habit of those who work with him — “Hutch,” and the light of assurance and prophecy came into Edison’s eyes, “we’re the biggest nation on earth, and I tell you we ought to have the biggest, damnedest navy in the world. There’s no reason why we should be second to any nation.
