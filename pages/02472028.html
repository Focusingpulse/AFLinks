That is what Nikola Tesla’s latest invention in the field of electricity promises. This latest advance is described as an apparatus for the production of great electric power, and for the collection and utilization of that power at a point distant from the seat of its production after the power, or the electric current in which it is comprised, has traversed without wires the intervening atmosphere at an altitude such that the rarefaction has made the air of that stratum a conductor of the electric fluid. The invention, Nikola Tesla points out, was based upon the observations made by him of the conducting power of rarefied air in a closed vessel. In brief, Nikola Tesla’s invention may be described as turning the upper and rarefied strata of the atmosphere into an immense electric power storage reservoir, which may be tapped by any trolley wire long enough to reach up to its level.
