GC2024-STP022 Extreme growth of GIC in power lines on the Kola Peninsula and in Karelia during 11 years of observations Vladimir B. Belakhovsky1 , Vjacheslav A. Pilipenko2 , Yaroslav A. Sakharov1 , Vasiliy N. Selivanov3 1 Polar Geophysical Institute of the Kola Scientific Center of the Russian Academy of Sciences 2 Institute of the Physics of the Earth RAS, Moscow, Russia 3 Kola Scientific Center of the Russian Academy of Sciences belakhov@mail.ru It was analyzed cases with extreme values ​of geomagnetic-induced currents (GIC) in power transmission lines (PTLs) on the Kola Peninsula and Karelia for 2012-2022. The GIC registration system was created by the Polar Geophysical Institute and the Center for Physical and Technical Problems of Northern Energy of the Federal Research Center KSC RAS ​and includes 5 stations, oriented mainly in the north-south direction. Registration of GIC has been carried out continuously since end of 2011, and by 2022 a “quasi-solar cycle” of GIC registration has formed, including 24-25 cycles of solar activity. GIC data were compared with data from PGI magnetometers at the Lovozero and Loparskaya observatories, and with data from magnetometers in the IMAGE network. Extreme values ​of GIC and dB/dt were compared with the parameters of the solar wind and interplanetary magnetic field, and geomagnetic activity indices. The GIC data from the Vykhodnoy auroral station (VKH) and the Kondopoga subauroral station (KND) were considered.
