Minor historical tidbit about the original "egg of Columbus" story : according to modern historians, this sotry is bogus. There is no recorded (i.e. written when Columbus was alive) evidence that Columbus ever performed this spiel. Previously, the same anecdote was attributed to Florentine sculptor and architect Filippo Brunelleschi (1377 - 1446). These legends are interesting. Like the one about Galileo being tortured and saying "And yet it turns" (from an 18th Century dictionary).
