MolPhot2026-main029 Rational Design of Semiconductor Photocatalysts: Materials Engineering and Mechanistic Insights Yanjie Wang1 1 CAS Key Laboratory of Nanosystem and Hierarchical Fabrication, National Center for Nanoscience and Technology, 100190, Beijing, China, China Ponencia invitada wangyanjie@nanoctr.cn Semiconductor photocatalysis provides a sustainable route for converting solar energy into chemical energy, with CO2 reduction representing an important example toward carbon neutrality. The practical efficiency of CO2 photoreduction is still restricted by the inertness of CO2 molecules, fast recombination of photogenerated charges, sluggish multi-electron/proton transfer, and insufficient control over product selectivity. These challenges highlight general requirements for photocatalyst design, including efficient light harvesting, suitable band-edge positions, promoted charge separation and transfer, and well-defined surface reaction sites. In this talk, I will discuss our recent efforts to regulate the electronic structure, interface architecture, and active sites of semiconductor photocatalysts, and to understand the associated reaction processes by combining in situ spectroscopic techniques with theoretical modeling. As a representative materials-engineering strategy, solid￾solution design provides a flexible platform for tuning the structural and electronic properties of semiconductor photocatalysts.
