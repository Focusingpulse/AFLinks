This page intentionally left blank Functional and Evolutionary Ecology of Fleas Fleas are one of the most interesting and fascinating taxa of ectoparasites. All species in this relatively small order are obligatory haematophagous (blood-feeding) parasites of higher vertebrates. This book examines how functional, ecological and evolutionary patterns and processes of host—parasite relationships are realized in this particular system. As such it provides an in-depth case study of a host—parasite system, demonstrating how ﬂeas can be used as a model taxon for testing ecological and evolutionary hypotheses. The book moves from basic descriptive aspects, to functional issues and ﬁnally to evolutionary explanations.
