James McCabe ExtraOrdinary Technology October, 2006 Page number(s): 42 Many researchers have noticed that Tesla's Patent No. 1,119,732 1 has a huge donut type antenna at the top of the tower. When Nikola built his Wardenclyffe wireless tower on Long Island, New York, the donut was replaced by a dome or mushroom design. There has been speculation as to why the change… Is the dome just a better design? Perhaps there is a benefit to having a large interior cavity at the top of the tower? As usual the real reason isn't all that exciting. The original donut design was very expensive. The architects' presented Tesla with an estimate of $450,000 1 for the donut antenna. Tesla had to change the antenna to a more economical design. —RJM 1 Tesla, N. United States Patent: 1,119,732. APPARATUS FOR TRANSMITTING ELECTRICAL ENGERY. APPLICATION FILED JAN. 18, 1902. RENEWED MAY 4, 1907. Patented Dec. 1, 1914. 2 Anderson, Leland, I. NIKOLA TESLA ON HIS WORK WITH ALTERNATING CURRENTS AND THEIR APPLICATION TO WIRELESS TELEGRAPHY, TELEPHONY, AND TRANSMISSION OF POWER. Sun Publishing, Denver, Colorado. 1992, pp. 143-144.
