COMMENTARY “The Data Are Irrelevant”: Response to Reber and Alcock (2019) ETZEL CARDEÑA Lund University, Sweden etzel.cardena@psy.lu.se Submitted August 13, 2019; Accepted August 24, 2019; Published December 30, 2019 DOI: https://doi.org/10.31275/2019/1653 Copyright: Creative Commons CC-BY-NC Abstract—The rebuttal by Reber and Alcock (2019a,b) to an umbrella re￾view of multiple meta-analyses on the evidence for parapsychological (psi) phenomena (Cardeña, 2018) did not engage deliberately with its data or analyses. Instead, the authors proposed that because they and some physi￾cists consider psi phenomena to be impossible, “the data are irrelevant” (Reber & Alcock, 2019b). After presenting some background information, this Commentary discusses how: 1) Reber and Alcock’s disregard for the data goes against a core tenet of science, 2) eminent physicists have not considered psi phenomena to be incompatible with their discipline and some have even proposed theories to explain it, so no defi nitive conclu￾sion can be advanced with regard to the possibility or impossibility of psi phenomena based on physics, and 3) Reber and Alcock misrepresent the history and current status of psi research.
