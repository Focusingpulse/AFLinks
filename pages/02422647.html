, Lugano Translated by Pauline Wehrle The Christ Impulse in Historical Development I Not translated [ 1 ] We shall begin by discussing something of a general character. And since we are a small intimate circle, we can then elaborate on any aspect that especially interests you. I would like to make some general statements about the being of man in connection with World Being, with the Macrocosm. And I will not approach it so much in the style of my book Theosophy —you can all get sufficient knowledge from that—but I invite you to consider especially man's inner nature.
