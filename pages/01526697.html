Why and how this could happen is explained by our experimental, rare earth powder mixtures, that all consist of classically “stable” elements loaded with Deuterium. These powders, in some cases, start to produce well known, because predictable, gamma radiation even at room temperature and without any stimulation, while consuming Deuterium!
