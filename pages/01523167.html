Title Introduction to the Flyby Anomaly : the Gyrotational Acceleration of Orbiting Satellites Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Thierry De Mees Keywords anomaly, gravitation, gyrotation, prograde, retrograde, orbit Published 2010 Journal General Science Journal No. of pages 4 Abstract In a former paper, much attention has been given to spinning objects whereof the gyrotational acceleration has been calculated for particles in the spinning sphere and at its surface. The purpose of this paper is to calculate the gyrotational acceleration of orbiting satellites in an orbital plane under an angle with the Earth's equator. It is found that strong influence is possible, depending from the orbit's inclination. Overview Thierry De Mees published this four-page paper in the General Science Journal in July 2010. It applies his gyro-gravitation framework — the Maxwell analogy for gravitation, in which a second field Ω called gyrotation accompanies the Newtonian field — to satellites in orbits inclined to the Earth's equator, and offers the result as an approach to the flyby anomaly . The framework is the one used across his series: F ⇐ m ( g + v × Ω ), with mass replacing charge and the constants substituted accordingly. De Mees describes it as "the closest theory to the General Relativity of Einstein, while the universe remains Euclid and is not curved", suitable for steady and quasi-steady systems, with retardation neglected.
