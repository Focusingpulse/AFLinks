Chief Seattle " Every part of the Earth is sacred, You are a part of the Earth and the earth is a part of you. You did not weave the web of life , you are merely a strand in it. Whatever you do to the web, you do it to yourself. " ( Chief Seattle ) Zoroaster " Be good, be kind, be humane and charitable; love your fellows, console the afflicted, pardon those who have done you wrong. " ( Zoroaster ) Buddha " In dealing with others, one should do as he would have done him. " ( Buddha ) Mohammed " Let none of you treat his brother in a way he himself would dislike being treated. " ( Mohammed ) Confucious " Do not do to others that which you would not they do to you. " ( Confucious ) Jesus " All things whatsoever ye would that men do unto you, do you even unto them. " ( Jesus ) Cayce " As ye mete to others, it comes back to thee. As ye would that others should do to thee, do ye even so to them. " Cayce (1688-9) Rudolf Steiner "The golden rule of true spirit[ual] science : For every one step that you take in the pursuit of higher knowledge , take three steps in the perfection of your own character ."
