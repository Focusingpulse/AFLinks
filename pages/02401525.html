GC2026-STP040 The kinetics of electronically excited N2 and N2 + in sprites and a discharge chamber Andrey S. Kirillov1 , Victor F. Tarasenko2 1 Polar Geophysical Institute of the Kola Scientific Center of the Russian Academy of Sciences, Apatity, Russia 2 Institute of High Current Electronics of the Siberian Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences kirillov@pgia.ru Studying the properties of red sprites, including their emission spectra, is complicated by the high altitude of these discharges above sea level, the complexity of spectral measurements, and the spatial and temporal uncertainty of their occurrence. It is proposed to obtain preliminary information on the emission spectra of red sprites at high resolution in various wavelength ranges using their laboratory analogs – plasma diffuse jets (PDJs). To substantiate predictions about the emission spectra of red sprites, experimentally measured radiative characteristics of PDJs were compared with calculated data. A model describing kinetic processes involving electronically excited triplet states of molecular nitrogen N2 and the doublet state of the N2 + ion in a mixture of N 2 and O2 gases was used. The theoretically calculated and experimentally measured ratios of the spectral emission densities W of red and infrared bands of the molecular nitrogen (first positive system 1PG) are compared to the densities W of the ultraviolet bands of N2 (second positive system 2PG) and the N2 + band (first negative system 1NG) at medium pressures of 0.25−1.5 Torr.
