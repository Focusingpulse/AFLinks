It was on a final hearing on bill and answer raising questions of patentability and infringement of three patents granted to Nikola Tesla, May 1, 1888, for an electric-magnetic motor. The patents in suit relate to the art of electrical transmission of power by the use of mechanically generating alternating currents. They cover the rotating magnetic field.
