GC2026-SGG029 Estimation of the Reflection Coefficient at the Water-Seabed Interface Based on the Transfer Function Between Geophone and Hydrophone Sensor Data Andrey Ponomarenko1,2 , Alexander Stanyulis2 1 Saint-Petersburg State University, Russia 2 VNIIOkeangeologia, Russia andrew_spb2003@mail.ru In our previous works, we examined the so-called method for calculating the transfer function between geophone and hydrophone sensor data to align and correct registered multicomponent data. The transfer function under consideration is a complex number characterized by magnitude (amplitude-frequency response, AFR) and phase (phase-frequency response, PFR). Analysis of the PFR of the transfer function enabled us to correct for sensor non-identity, as demonstrated in our earlier studies. It turned out that by analyzing the AFR of the transfer function, it is possible to estimate the reflection coefficient at the water-seabed interface, which opens up the possibility of accounting for wave field interference at the registration point. As before, to obtain calculated data, we use a spatio-temporal window on the common depth point (CDP) seismogram, including only longitudinal refracted waves of the first arrivals from a single source signal for both the hydrophone and geophone sensors. Using the cross-correlation function in the time domain for the geophone and hydrophone trace signals, the delay time of the echo signal from the sea surface was calculated. The optimal reflection coefficient was determined by finding the minimum mean square deviation of the transfer function magnitude
