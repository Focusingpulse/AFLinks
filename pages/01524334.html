Turan Gerger Known for Cosmic Texture Theory (CTT); the Texton; "The Great Fracture" Scientific career Fields Systems engineering, software architecture; independent researcher in cosmology and theoretical physics Institutions Universal Hospitals Group (IT Coordinator) Website ORCID 0009-0007-0856-5419 Turan Gerger is a systems engineer, software architect and independent theoretical physics researcher, and the author of Cosmic Texture Theory (CTT), a mechanical, deterministic alternative to standard cosmology developed over several decades and published as a series of preprints from 2026. His working life spans more than half a century across two fields — large-scale enterprise computing and fundamental natural philosophy — which he treats as applications of a single method. He writes on physics without institutional affiliation and registers that work under ORCID 0009-0007-0856-5419 . Engineering career Gerger has been building software in UNIX and C environments since 1984. As IT Coordinator at the Universal Hospitals Group he was the architect of a Hospital Information System (HIS) covering fourteen hospitals, a body of work amounting to more than 1.5 million lines of Informix-4GL. His stated design principles are deliberately austere. He rejects object-relational mapping layers in favour of native SQL; he prefers direct control of memory and of the system over abstractions that conceal it; he designs databases to be immutable , versioning records by status rather than overwriting them, so that no history is ever destroyed; and he keeps business logic clearly separated within the application layer rather than dispersed through the storage tier. He continues to work as an architect, designing modern four-tier modular systems in Go, PostgreSQL, SQLC and Fyne.
