He obtained a diploma of electrical engineer and PhD in Physics. From 1973 to 1990 he participated in space research projects coordinated by the international program Intercosmos established by the former Soviet Union. From 1990 he was a visiting science for 1.5 years at Arecibo Observatory, Puerto Rico, operated by Cornell University and NSF. After immigrating to Canada in 1991 he used to work with the University of Western Ontario, the Center for Earth and Space Technology and currently he is with the York University, Toronto.
