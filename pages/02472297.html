It is nothing less than the hope that means may yet be found for establishing intelligent communication with the supposed inhabitants of some other planet. Our next neighbor away from the sun Mars is the world commonly chosen as the most feasible subject for these experiments. Among the eminent scientists who have given much attention to this matter is M. Flammarion, the European astronommer. He has discussed it in an interesting way and proposed that some system of gigantic signaling shall be adopted and practiced with the hope of evoking some response from our Martian neighbors. But the most sensational utterance from a recognized scientist that has yet been made on this subject is that of Nikola Tesla, the celebrated electrical investigator and inventor. His contribution to the watch night services of the Red Cross was a statement which would be received with amazement if Mr. Tesla in former written and oral declarations had not raised the suspicion that his judgment was a little off its balance.
