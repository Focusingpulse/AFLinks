In consideration of other methods nor requiring superconductors or outside power, as with Troy Reeds magnetic motor, he used an inkpen clicker mechanism that had a spring, a latch and a release. His motor had many of these clicker magnet mechanisms mounted on a wheel that passed fixed high power magnets. The idea was that this spinning wheel with magnetic clickers mounted on the rim, would pass a ramp that would cock the springs to compress and latch at a certain point. As the wheel rotated, it would pass a trigger that would release the latch mechanism, causing the magnet to be thrown out against the fixed magnet to be repelled with maxiumu effect which would add additional thrust to the wheel.
