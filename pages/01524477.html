Overview The book presents Bauer's aether-based physics — an attempt to account for physical phenomena in terms of a material ether medium, as an alternative to Einstein's relativity, in the tradition of the mid-twentieth-century German-language critics of relativistic physics. About the author Wilhelm Moritz Bauer was a German independent researcher who advocated an aether theory of physics.
