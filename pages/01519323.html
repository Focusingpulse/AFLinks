Title An Electromagnetic Force Containing Two New Terms: Derivation from a 4D-Aether Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Hector A Munera Keywords four dimensional ether, preons Published 2000 Journal Apeiron Volume 7 Number 1-2 No. of pages 9 Pages 67-75 Abstract The present author has postulated a 4-dimensional (4D) aether formed by a fluid of preons in continual motion in a 4D-space (w,x,y,z), where the time dimension w is treated exactly the same as the spatial dimensions. By specializing the equation of motion to an inviscid constant density region of 4D moving with speed c along the w-axis, we obtain Maxwell equations and the associated continuity equation. The process of derivation pinpoints physical constraints underlying MEs, and brings out the novel concept of force along the wdimension. Two new terms appear in the equation for electromagnetic force, that might help explain recent experimental observations of excess energy. Overview Published in Apeiron in early 2000, this paper by Héctor Múnera of the Centro Internacional de Física in Bogotá derives Maxwell's equations from the hydrodynamics of a four-dimensional fluid, and reports that the derivation yields an electromagnetic force law with two terms the standard theory does not have. The construction begins from a four-dimensional Euclidean space ( w , x , y , z ) in which the time dimension w = v w t is treated on exactly the same footing as the three spatial ones, and which is filled with a fluid of tiny particles Múnera calls preons, each of mass m , in continual motion. A 4D equation of motion for this fluid is postulated. Specialising it to an inviscid region of constant density moving at speed c along the w -axis — the hypersurface Múnera identifies with our world — recovers Maxwell's equations and the continuity equation, together with a new notion of force directed along w .
