Accelerometer

· archived # 1,455,662
An accelerometer is a seismic transducer which converts acceleration motion and/or gravitational forces capable of imparting acceleration into a proportional electric signal . (Bentley Nevada Corporation, Field of Rotating Machinery Measurement, Monitoring and Analysis) See Also Transducer Velocity Transducer Seismic Transducer
