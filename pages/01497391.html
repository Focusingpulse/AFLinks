Holism (from á½‚Î»Î¿Ï‚ holos, a Greek word meaning all, whole, entire, total) is the idea that all the properties of a given system (physical, biological, chemical, social, economic, mental, linguistic, etc.) cannot be determined or explained by its component parts alone. Instead, the system as a whole determines in an important way how the parts behave [or the whole collection of the parts, functioning as a whole system, are considered as one system]. The term holism was coined in 1926 by Jan Smuts. Reductionism is sometimes seen as the opposite of holism . Reductionism in science says that a complex system can be explained by reduction to its fundamental parts. For example, the processes of biology are reducible to chemistry and the laws of chemistry are explained by physics .
