No longer is it the capital of a vast empire, far vaster than the limits of present-day Peru. To-day it numbers only some thirty thousand souls, whereas the ancient city had more than TEN TIMES that number. Most of the ancient palaces and temples have been destroyed by the Spaniards, but the main outlines of the city as it was in the days of the Incas can still be traced.
