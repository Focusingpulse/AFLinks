I would love to discuss them and how they can help us achieve OU. Please point out any mistakes I have made as I explain the basics. First we will talk about homemade permanent magnets. Picture a weak magnet like a steel bolt that you have been able to magnetize by using another magnet. This bolt was magnetized because as you introduced a magnetic field to it the atoms in it lined up in such a way as to line up with the field you presented. When you removed that field, a good percentage of the atoms in the bolt stayed in their new orientation. This allowed the bolt to maintain some of the magnetic field. Pretty cool. Next take your bolt and introduce your magnet again. Only this time flip your magnet around. After you remove the field you will see that your bolt is still magnetic, but with the opposite polarity.
