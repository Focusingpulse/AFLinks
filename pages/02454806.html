Hi every subscriber to the net. I am writing this letter in the hope that together we may be able to solve a few puzzles. I have been extremely intrigued by the timing of many of the inventions and patents claiming free energy. Apart from some modern time inventors that claim sucesses, somehow those successes do not seem to penetrate the world but for the inventor who either by financial restraints or by the impracticability of the invention are prevented from getting any further. Jerry is a wizard in digging up information and Yes Jerry I read them all !! Cogitate on the information and store it away in my upstairs computor which although getting a bit dim still accepts more bits and bytes. For instance take the Hubbard coil information !! A newspaper article describing an event which reads like a dream coming true but like all news paper reporting suffers from inflating facts so people will read it. I for one am convinced it was a working device but when reading carefully, facts quoted do not stack up knowing what I know about the workings of electricity,the performance of displacement hulls,horsepower requirements of boats versus speed. So the object of this exercise is to ask all of you to carefully read the information given, think in 1920 terms and forget about modern concepts and go back to a mecano toy set which was essentially what they used to obtain results.
