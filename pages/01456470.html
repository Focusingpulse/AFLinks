Corner Cube Prisms

· archived # 1,456,470
Corner Cube Prisms are designed to reflect any ray or beam entering the prism face, regardless of the orientation of the prism , back onto itself. A mirror will only do that at the normal incidence. As a result, corner cubes are ideal where precision alignment is difficult or time-consuming. Retroreflectors will function even at very large angles of incidence .
