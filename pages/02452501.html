As I was driving to work today happened my mind was meandering more than usual and I happened to notice some apartment rooftops that had stacked heat vents. These looked like a cylinder with about 4 discs stacked on top of each other but separated by about 2-3 inches. The discs spilled over the cylinder. I happened to view them side on and was struck by the similarity to the Egyptian Djet figure which I had heretofore thought of as stacked capacitor plates separated by a dielectric. The djet is sometimes held by renderings of Pharoah or one of the gods though in the Dendera photos one of the vacuum tubes sits on a giant djet. What if instead of being capacitor plates that collect atmospheric or earth electricity for some unknown purpose, they are really thermocouples that rely on heat to produce current? I was thinking the disc plates could be radiators of excess heat or maybe smoke from burning fuel. There might even be a possibility that the plates could STILL be capacitive in nature and a thermocouple mechanism used to produce high current that was stepped up to high voltage that would be used to drive a vacuum tube...
