GTM2026-main036 A two-stage leader-follower game on a transportation network khaled alkhaled alkhaledkhaled@gmail.com A two-stage leader-follower game on a transportation network A two-stage game on a network is considered. On the first stage, leader selects a vertex in the network. On the second stage, other players choose their paths from their initial positions to the fixed point selected by the leader on the first stage. Since players' decisions follow leader's choice, the game has a hierarchical (leader-follower) form, which makes the problem more complex. We construct a family of Nash equilibria for this hierarchical game. In addition, we investigate a cooperative model of the game. For this reason the characteristic function is defined for all possible coalitions, and superadditivity of this function proved. Unlike [1], we assume that the trajectories of players (followers) can intersect, and here we did not have a fixed endpoint .This endpoint is chosen by the leader (player) . An example is presented. 1-Reference -Khaled Alkhaled, Leon Petrosyan Game theoretic approach to multi-agent transportation problems on network Contributions to Game Theory and Management, 2022, 15, 8–17
