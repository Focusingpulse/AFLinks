Varghese is a retired Indian electrical engineer who, in independent research carried out from Kerala, has built one of the most complete single-substance theories of nature catalogued on this wiki. Under the title Hypothesis on MATTER — later enlarged as MATTER (Re-examined) — he attempts to derive the whole of physics, from the origin of matter through gravity, light and atomic structure to cosmology, from a single postulated entity and without any second assumption. The programme is unusually ambitious and unusually consistent. Where most of the dissident literature here disputes one theory — relativity, the Big Bang, the nature of a force — Varghese sets out to replace the lot, and to do it mechanically: every phenomenon is to be a consequence of the contact interactions of one kind of basic particle, with no action at a distance, no fields in the abstract sense, no mass–energy equivalence, no duality of light, and no singularities anywhere. The programme Varghese begins from a single postulate: the quantum of matter , an indivisible, uncreatable bit of matter whose only inherent property is a tendency of its substance to coagulate, giving it self-adhesion.
