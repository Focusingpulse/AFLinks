Varghese Residence Adoor, Kerala, India Nationality Indian Knownfor Hypothesis on MATTER ; a single-substance mechanical philosophy of nature; push gravity Scientific career Fields Electrical Engineer , theoretical physics Nainan K. Varghese is a retired Indian electrical engineer who, in independent research carried out from Kerala, has built one of the most complete single-substance theories of nature catalogued on this wiki. Under the title Hypothesis on MATTER — later enlarged as MATTER (Re-examined) — he attempts to derive the whole of physics, from the origin of matter through gravity, light and atomic structure to cosmology, from a single postulated entity and without any second assumption.
