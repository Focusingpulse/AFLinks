Kelvin considered it possible to generate "longitudinal waves in the luminiferous ether" thru the phenomena of displacement current (capacity current ). He goes on to indicate his feeling that these waves must be faster than light, as the longitudinal waves in a steel rod move with much greater velocity than the transverse waves. Tesla claims that the waves from his transformer propagate at the velocity of light.
