KIPLING'S CONDITION ON THIS THE SIXTH DAY OF THE DISEASE IS VERY SERIOUS, GIVING RISE TO THE GRAVEST APPREHENSION FOR THE OUTCOME. It was said at 10.20 o’clock last night that Mr. Kipling was very low and unconscious. Mrs. Kipling sent for Dr. E. G. Janeway, and he returned shortly before midnight and made arrangements to stay all night near his patient. Dr. Janeway, his son and Dr. Dunham were in attendance at midnight and intended to remain at the bedside or within call while the crisis lasted. Several times yesterday the rumor that death was only a question of a few hours, if not minutes, was current in the lobby of the Hotel Grenoble.
