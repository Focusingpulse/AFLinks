Radiant heat

· archived # 1,461,552
" Radiant heat " is that property of the element heat , after its liberation from corpuscular imprisonment, by means of which it reaches out for reabsorption and return to its sympathetic environment. " [ HEAT - Snell ] "Dark radiant heat begins at absolute zero temperature, and extends through light , chemical rays, actinic rays, and infra-violet rays, up to the dissociation of all molecules to the 63rd octave ."
