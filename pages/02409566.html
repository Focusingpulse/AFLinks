Rather, it is a common domain for scientists of diverse disciplines. This prologue will in simple language introduce these scientists. Then the authors of the chapters that follow will present in the language of the specialists the problems, methods, and solutions that exemplify the study of agriculture and weather. Since much of agricultural meteorology deals with creation of and response to environment, this Monograph may have even wider application than agriculture alone. Biologists have, of course, always been aware of the effects of weather.
