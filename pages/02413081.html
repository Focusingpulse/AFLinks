The purpose of this test is to measure with more accuracy the INPUT electrical power of the Kapagen v3.3. There are 14 x 150 W halogen lamps connected in serie at the output of the Kapagen. I have used a Voltcraft LX-1108 Luxmeter to measure the light intensity of one of these lamps Vs the electrical power required, a calibration curve has been set. The total OUPUT power for the 14 halogen lamps is calculated with the calibration sheet, below. To measure the electrical INPUT POWER, I have used the high end energy meter Voltcraft Energy Logger 4000F connected at the output of the variac. The electrical ground conditions were very poor due to the dryness. I have added water to the ground points to improve the conductivity.
