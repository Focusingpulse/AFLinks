Two interesting Magnet patents

· archived # 2,444,266
07/29/16 - Two interesting Magnet patents Both of these offer lots of possibilities for new discoveries. 4,874,346 - Wachspress - October 17, 1989 - Free flying magnetic levitator - A free flying magnetic levitator that is self stabilized and fully maneuverable for magnetic structure establishing an odd number of poles for interaction with another magnetic field having an even number of poles to produce linear motion instead of rotation without a guideway. Longitudinally wound coils produce the odd pole magnetic field for maximizing coupling with an even pole field such as the magentic field of the earth. Much about this in Keelynet archives...Wachspress started charging $5,000 to pay for the costs when using supercooled wire for his sphere which always took off into space. - JWD 5,929,732 - Bushman - July 27, 1999 - Apparatus and method for amplifying a magnetic beam - An apparatus and method for creating a magnetic beam wherein a focusing magnet assembly (45) is comprised of a first opposing magnet pair (20) and a second opposing magnet pair (30) disposed in a focusing plane, each magnet of the respective opposing magnet pairs having a like pole directed towards the geometric center of the focusing magnet assembly (45) to form an alignment path, two like magnetic beams extending from the alignment path on each side of the focusing magnet assembly (45), each beam being generally perpendicular to the focusing plane. A like pole of an unopposed magnet (10) can be directed down the alignment path from one side of the focusing magnet assembly (45) to produce a single magnetic beam extending generally perpendicular from the focusing magnet assembly opposite unopposed magnet (10). This beam is a magnetic monopole which emits pulses, levitates, degausses, stops electronics and separates materials.
