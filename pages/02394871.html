Photo Highlights of 2008 India LENR Tour (Sponsored by the Indian Dept. of Atomic Energy's Board of Research in Nuclear Sciences, Pentagram Research Corp., New Energy Institute, SRI International) Jan. 5 -- M. Srinivasan looks on as A. Kakodkar, chairman of the Indian Atomic Energy Commission, signs "Rebirth of Cold Fusion." Jan. 8 -- M. Srinivasan and S. Krivit meet with R. Chidambaram, scientific adviser to India's prime minister and chairman of the Indian Atomic Energy Commission (ret.). Photo by M. McKubre Jan. 6 -- P.K. Iyengar, Bhabha Atomic Research Centre director (ret.) and chairman of the Indian Atomic Energy Commission (ret.), sits for an interview with S. Krivit. Photo by M. McKubre Jan. 9 -- S. Krivit presents "Low Energy Nuclear Reactions Research – Global Scenario" to nuclear physicists, chemists, metallurgists and other scientists at the National Institute for Advanced Studies on the campus of the Indian Institute of Science in Bangalore. Photo by M. McKubre Jan. 9 -- M. McKubre presents "Cold Fusion: Past, Present and Future – A Review of the Understanding Developed Over 19 Years of Research" at NIAS.
