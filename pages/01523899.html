Sjaak Uitterdijk Nationality Dutch Known for Critical mathematical analysis of Einstein's Special Theory of Relativity; the book Physics since Einstein Scientific career Fields Physics , Relativity , Cosmology Sjaak Uitterdijk is a Dutch electronics engineer and independent critic of modern theoretical physics. Trained in electronics rather than in academic physics, he has published a long series of papers on the viXra e-print archive arguing that Einstein's Special Theory of Relativity rests on manipulative mathematics, and that a great deal of twentieth-century physics built on it — space-time, black holes, gravitational waves, E = mc 2 , quark models of the nucleus and the particle–wave duality — must be rejected along with it. His critiques are collected in the freely distributed book Physics since Einstein . Biography Little biographical detail is publicly documented. In the prologue to Physics since Einstein Uitterdijk writes that he first heard, at about sixteen years of age, the claim that only a handful of extremely intelligent scientists could understand Einstein's 1905 theory, and that "after having successfully completed my academic study Electronics" he decided to take up that challenge and investigate the Special Theory of Relativity for himself. Having read the first ten pages of Einstein's paper "for the umpteenth time", he concluded that either the theory was comprehensible only to geniuses or it was simply wrong — and set out to test the second possibility. That investigation became his first chapter, "Einstein's and Galilei's Principle of Relativity", posted to viXra in 2016.
