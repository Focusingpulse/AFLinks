Das Universum funktioniert anders - Realität kontra Relativität Book Author Eduard Krausz Published 1998 Publisher Corona Verlag Pages 256 ISBN 3928084798 Das Universum funktioniert anders – Realität kontra Relativität ("The Universe Works Differently – Reality versus Relativity") is a 1998 German-language book by the engineer Eduard Krausz , published by Corona Verlag. In it Krausz sets out his Alternative Gravitation Theory and argues that the foundations of nuclear physics and astrophysics are far less secure than they are usually presented to be. Krausz's central claim is that gravity is not a fixed property tied to an object's mass but the effect of a physical medium — an aether he pictures as a kind of pressure wave streaming out of space and pushing bodies toward one another and toward a planet. On this view a fluid flows around and through all bodies and particles in proportion to their mass. He contends that such a model explains nuclear- and astro-physical phenomena more consistently and more plausibly than mainstream physics, which he regards as fixated on particles. The book connects this theory to his 1991 "Gelsenkirchen experiment," which he presented as evidence for the partial shielding of gravity. Written for a general readership, the book sits at the border between physics and the wider critical debate over the aether and relativity . Its argument is not accepted by mainstream physics. Original German description Realität kontra Relativität — Wesentliche Grundlagen der Kern- und Astrophysik sind weit davon entfernt, gesichertes Wissen zu sein. Deshalb lohnt es sich, den Entdeckungen des Autors und Forschers, Dipl.-Ing. Eduard Krausz, nachzugehen. Seine Alternative Gravitationstheorie kann kern- und astrophysikalische Phänomene durchgehend und wesentlich plausibler erklären als die herrschende Lehre, die ausschließlich auf Teilchen fixiert ist.
