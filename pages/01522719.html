In it Krausz sets out his Alternative Gravitation Theory and argues that the foundations of nuclear physics and astrophysics are far less secure than they are usually presented to be. Krausz's central claim is that gravity is not a fixed property tied to an object's mass but the effect of a physical medium an aether he pictures as a kind of pressure wave streaming out of space and pushing bodies toward one another and toward a planet.
