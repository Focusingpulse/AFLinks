Interact on KeelyNet Mail List: Re: Spinning wheel O/U claim Re: Spinning wheel O/U claim Bill McMurtry ( weber@powerup.com.au ) Mon, 22 Nov 1999 11:35:41 +1000 Messages sorted by: [ date ] [ thread ] [ subject ] [ author ] Previous message: Ian Bell: "Re: Spinning wheel O/U claim" Next message: Marcelo Puhl: "Re: Spinning wheel O/U claim" Hi Slavek & all, Back in '94 I had the opportunity to speak with Tom Beardon about John Bedini's motor generator devices. I had unsuccessfully attempted to reproduce Bedini's motor/generator a couple of years earlier and was asking Beardon about the type of battery Bedini used. Tom told me that, to the best of his knowledge, Bedini used a specific type of lead/acid battery and the system would not operate with anything else. This was interesting because Bedini assumed (based on his literature) that some sort of resonant condition was being created within the battery as a result of 'correct' switching waveform from battery to load and from generator to battery. If this is the case then it would seem that whatever is going on in Bedini's system has little to do with the motor/generator, in terms of the input/output power ratio, and everything to do with the the internal physics of the battery connected to the system. Its no surprise then that this sort of device can not operate without the battery. No battery - no chemical reaction - no stimulated energy release. The question is, how long will a battery last under these operatating conditions and is the effect ultimately useful (if consistantly achievable)? Regards, Bill.
