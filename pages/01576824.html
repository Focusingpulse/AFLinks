II. The Fundamental Desire for Knowledge - GA 4. The Philosophy of Spiritual Activity

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 1,576,824
With these words Goethe expresses a characteristic deeply founded in human nature. Man is not whole in the organization of his being. He demands always more than the world gives him of its own accord. Nature has given us needs; among these are such whose satisfaction it has left to our own activity. Abundant are the gifts apportioned us, but still more abundant is our desiring. We seem born to be discontented. One particular instance of this discontent is our urge to know.
