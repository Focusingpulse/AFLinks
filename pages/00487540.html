PROOF OF COLLATZ CONJECTURE PRANSHU TRIPATHI 1. abstract The collatz conjecture was introduced by Lothar collatz in 1937. It is also known as ”3n + 1 problem”. The conjecture states: Start from any positive integer, n. If n is even, divide by 2; if n is odd, multiply by 3 and add 1. Now, the conjecture says that if you keep repeating above steps, you will finally reach 1, no matter what value of n we choose. In this paper, we prove collatz conjecture using method of mathematical induction. We also use binary and trenary numbers to prove collatz conjecture. 2.
