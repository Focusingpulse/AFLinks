I have more detailed data of my own joule thief. I have it running on a variable power supply in order to get some measureable data from it. I have my scope hooked up across a 1 ohm carbon resistor(non inductive) to get a more complete view of the incoming current envelope. My toroid is a 7/8" dia. X 1/4 " thick ,I.D., 1/2 ". It is wound with 100 turns of #28 enameled wire, with two additional windings of 20 turns of #26 wire wound wire each. Using a 2N3904 transistor fed into one of the 20 turn windings and the other winding feeding a 1 K resistor into the base of the 2N3904. I have two white LEDS of 7000 mcd intensity connected across the emitter and collector of the transistor.With no load on the 100 turn winding, input waveform is a triangular wave of 28 ma @307 khz with 0.795 volts in. The leds are lighting with a peak voltage of 6.42 v peak and output of unloaded 100 turn coil is 41.6 v peak to peak, positive excursion is 30.8 v peak, negative is 10.8 v peak.
