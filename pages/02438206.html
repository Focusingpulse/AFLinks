98 — GA 263. Correspondence with Edith Maryon 1912–1924

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 12 May 1922 · archived # 2,438,206
My dear Edith Maryon! Thank you for your kind letter. Up to now I have been very busy. I have given two lectures, one in Leipzig to students, which went well except for a few rather inferior objections, and then in Berlin, which went very well. Please be assured that I am taking every possible care. I will send news of Munich as quickly as possible. But please don't be anxious. I would love to be in the studio and will be happy to be there again. For now, just these lines and warmest greetings from Rudolf Steiner. 98 Rudolf Steiner an Edith Maryon Berlin, 12. Mai 1922 Meine liebe Edith Maryon
