He is listed in The Worldwide List of Dissident Scientists . Work Mulaba-Bafubiandi is a professor at the University of Johannesburg, where he has been associated with the School of Mining, Metallurgy and Chemical Engineering and has headed a mineral processing and technology research centre. The bulk of his published output concerns hydrometallurgy, mineral beneficiation, metals recovery and related engineering topics.
