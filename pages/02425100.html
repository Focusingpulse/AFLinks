Report Fear of the human ‘‘super predator’’ pervades the South African savanna Highlights Authors d Lions have been viewed as the world’s most fearsome predator, the ‘‘king of beasts’’ d New global surveys show humans kill prey at much higher rates than other predators d Fear of humans vs. lions was tested in iconic savanna mammals from elephants on down d Fear of the human ‘‘super predator’’ far prevails over that of the ‘‘king of beasts’’ Liana Y. Zanette, Nikita R. Frizzelle, Michael Clinchy, Michael J.S. Peel, Carson B. Keller, Sarah E. Huebner, Craig Packer Correspondence lzanette@uwo.ca In brief Zanette et al. experimentally show that fear of humans far exceeds that of lions throughout the mammal community in a premier African protected area; strengthening the evidence that fear of the human ‘‘super predator’’ pervades the planet. Paramount fear of humans adds to our global environmental impacts as fear itself can reduce wildlife numbers.
