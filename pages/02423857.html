This text was originally published in Sweden in 2002 under the title: “Svart på vitt - Röster och vittnesmål om elöverkänslighet”. Its impact on public opinion has been considerable and is still growing. Now even politicians and mobile phone producers and operators are taking heed and try to find ways to stall further damage. Computers were perceived as the most usual triggering factor, followed by presence of amalgam/amalgam removing, fluorescent lights and low-energy lamps, cellular phones/base stations and ordinary telephones, chemicals and photocopiers.
