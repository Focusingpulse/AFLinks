It introduces the central theme of Bonilla's self-directed research program: that magnetism is not a fundamental property in its own right but instead originates in the Aether . The book marks the starting point of a chain of ideas that Bonilla went on to develop in a series of conference presentations and a later book. Where Aether-Magnetism argues that the aether is the underlying cause of magnetism, his subsequent work extended the same reasoning one step further — proposing that magnetism is in turn the cause of gravity.
