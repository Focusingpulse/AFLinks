Successful Living Phase 2 Harnessing the Power of Attitudes and Perceptions The Successful Living modules are based on the principles culled from many sources, such as "Think and Grow Rich" by Napolean Hill, Training of Anthony Robbins, "The Secret" DVD and many others. The success of many of the greatest minds to ever exist has been attributed to these principles. Unfortunately, harnessing these ideas and making them a reality can be a very challenging task... until now. We have taken these principles, these laws of the universe, studied and examined them, and created this series to aid you in making them a reality in your life.* This module, Harnessing the Power of Attitudes and Perceptions, is the second in the series and will help you in beginning to create the life you desire by focusing your passion on what you want. You will experience new attitudes emerging and new perceptions on how the universe works... and you will begin to learn how to make it work for you. By harnessing this understanding, and turning it into your new naturally occurring outlook, you will be ready for the third module in the series. Warning: This module may produce cognitive dissonance as it challenges non-productive paradigms. Cognitive Dissonance is when two cognitions (attitudes, emotions, beliefs, etc.), have a seeming incompatibility with one another. When one has Cognitive Dissonance, the contradicting cognitions can lead a person to invent new thoughts or beliefs, or to modify existing ones.
