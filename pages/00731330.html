Obenland Abstract In 1991, Gilbert Strang published a proof of Euler's formula using polar coordinates in Calculus, Wellesley-Cambridge, p. 389. In the following we show that this alleged proof is not valid. Comments The so-called proof uses the fact that all complex numbers can be expressed in polar coordinates.
