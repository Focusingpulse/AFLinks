Overview Radiation Pressure and Einstein's Mass-Energy Equivalence Formula is a five-page paper by David Tombe dated 19 June 2018 and published in the General Science Journal . It is one of several by the same author here; see also The Double Helix and the Compound Centrifugal Force , The Centrifugal Force Argument and Newton's Cradle and the Transmission Line . Its stated aim is "to show that the equation E = mc 2 was already implicit in Maxwell's 1861 paper... and that it doesn't mean that mass is equivalent to energy, but rather it relates to the propagation of electromagnetic radiation through an elastic solid." The argument has two parts. Part I reconstructs, accurately, Maxwell's route in "On Physical Lines of Force" from Hooke's law in an elastic solid, R = −4πk²h, through Newton's wave-speed relation V² = σ/ρ, to Maxwell's equation (135), V² = k²/μ. Tombe emphasises that the identification of this speed with the speed of light was not derived but came from the 1855 Weber–Kohlrausch measurement of the ratio of electrostatic to electromagnetic units of charge, compared with Fizeau's optical value. Part II supplies a mechanism. Maxwell's molecular vortices are taken to be dipolar, each an electron and a positron in mutual orbit.
