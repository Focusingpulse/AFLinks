DDD107498 vs Malaria http://www.sciencealert.com/new-drug-has-the-potential-to-ward-off-malaria-with-a-single-dose 18 JUN 2015 New drug has the potential to ward off malaria with a single dose And it’ll cost less than $1 per dose. Lab tests have highlighted the potential of a new drug to treat malaria in affected patients, prevent it from spreading, and ward off future infections with a single dose. Developed by chemists at Dundee University in Scotland and the not-for-profit group Medicines for Malaria Venture, the drug acts against each of the life stages of the malaria parasite, making it a promising option for those already infected and as a vaccination. "There are other compounds being developed for malaria, but relatively few of [these] have reached the stage we’re at," lead researcher Ian Gilbert said in a press release. "What’s most exciting is the number of potential attributes, such as the ability to give it in a single dose which will mean that medics can make sure a patient completes the treatment."
