1. Preliminary Remarks — GA 3. Truth and Science (1993)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 1,576,195
Epistemology is meant to be a scientific investigation of what all other sciences presuppose without examination: cognitive activity itself. Thus, from the very start, it is granted its character as the philosophical fundamental science. For only through it can we experience the value and significance of the insights the other sciences have attained. It forms in this regard the basis for all scientific striving.
