HOW PSEUDO-SCIENTISTS "SQUARE THE CIRCLE," EXTRACT GOLD FROM SEA WATER, MAKE COAL ASHES BURN AND REALLY "DISCOVER" PERPETUAL MOTION. AFTER extensive research I have discovered that the original lemons of the current vernacular grew on the shores of the Dead sea. They were golden in hue against the vivid green of foliage. Their shape was that of the perfect oval, and to the untrained and unsuspicious eye they were fair to the gaze and tempting to the palate. The sun scorched traveler of these arid wastes gazed with rapture on this smooth and luscious appearing fruit, plucked it eagerly, bit through the rind—and found his mouth filled with ashes. Nature had “handed him a lemon.” He was on a “dead one”—a Dead sea one. It is comforting to trace a popular expression back to so antique and respectable a source. Those of us who have stood where the Jordan pours its flood into the Dead sea are aware that the lemon which was “ashes on the lips” no longer grows along the shores of this saline lake, but we have biblical authority for the belief that this fruit once lured the wandering Syrians to their undoing.
