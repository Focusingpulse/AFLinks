Maxwell’s Final Wave Equation in A & his accompanying remark: a presentation for a specialist and non-specialist audience. Hosted by Ian Cowan. Summary Akinbo Ojo , presenting from Lagos, gave the session in two pre-recorded parts — a thirty-minute exposition and, after questions, a shorter piece he described as dessert to the main course, on the consequences of his reading, some of which he expected his audience to find unpalatable. He is a general physician by profession; his interest in physics began in 1995 with the word "omnipresence", which led him to space and time, to relativity, and eventually to the conclusion that theoretical physics is unwell — a diagnosis, he noted, shared by Lee Smolin and Sabine Hossenfelder as well as by the society. Host Ian J Cowan , who had persuaded him to give the talk, praised the paper for making vector calculus approachable, on the grounds that a good many people interested in the ether are frightened off by a curl. The remark The whole argument turns on a single sentence.
