Abstract I attempt a clear exegesis of the underlying principles of energy transfer resulting from my research into the interconnection of high speed logic in Motorola Phoenix in the 1960s, and my ideas as they developed during the next fifty years. The same title was given to my talk to the International Conference on EMC in Surrey University, UK, in 1984. It is now at http://www.ivorcatt.co.uk/x18j134.pdf .
