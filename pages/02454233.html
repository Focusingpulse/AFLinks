THE "GOLDENROD PAPER" SECRET (c)1996 William J. Beaty Office supply stores and Kinko's copy centers sell a type of paper called Astrobrights Galaxy Gold WAAB57A, from Wausau Papers. It's "goldenrod" in color, sort of a yellow/orange. Big deal. However, IF ALKALINE SUBSTANCES HIT IT, IT TURNS MAGENTA! Spray it with Windex, and it instantly turns bright red! Cool!!
