This Motor is a hybrid between a Bedini motor and a DC motor.It utiises both poles off the coil.The DC Motor/rotor is wired where a slave coil would be. The second Rotor is a free wheeling rotor and is powered by the power coil pulse and acts as an anti stall device if the powered rotor slows down to much.This motor produces a lot of torque (it will take the skin off your finger as i found out)for a not so big current draw. Another interesting thing about this motor is the amp draw decreases under load.The meter in the video is showing amp draw of 360mA.the power supply is 36v 0.5a.
