The main topic of the original text is based on linearised free surface water wave theory. For many years the first edition of the book is used by Aad Hermans as material for a course in ship hydrodynamics presented to Master students in applied mathematics and naval architecture at the Technical University of Delft. Influenced by the progress in the research in water waves and especially in ship hydrodynamics the contents of the course has changed gradually.
