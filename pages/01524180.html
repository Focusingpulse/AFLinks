The Past-Present-Future of Time in Physics Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Speaker Nick Percival Date 26 February 2022 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording The Past-Present-Future of Time in Physics was a live session presented by Nick Percival on 26 February 2022 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. Part of our series the "Evolving Perspectives on the History of Science". This is Part 1 of the 3 part series on "The Past-Present-Future of Time in Physics", draws from Prof. Lee Smolin's book "Time Reborn", to describe the history of physics which shows that the essence of "Time" has been removed from physics - culminating in relativity. Part 2 will look in detail on the transition from 19th century physics to Special Relativity. Part 3 shows how to put the essense of "Time" back into physics by, ironically, replacing the human concept of "Time" with an inherently physical, operationally defined physics construct that has all the essence of "Time" EXCEPT that it does NOT have its own dimension and which is currently used in GPS. Summary Part one of Nick Percival 's three-part series on the past, present and future of time in physics — the history, told through Lee Smolin 's Time Reborn . David de Hilster hosted and played the recorded talk, itself a redo of a presentation Percival gave in 2014 and the twenty-ninth in his own series. Percival gave his route in when asked. At Harvard he took a course on special relativity and saw what he thought was a flaw without having to work at it; then Life magazine reported that astronauts would return younger because of time dilation. He did not dispute that they would return younger — his point was that special relativity does not predict it. He took it to his professor, thought they had reached an understanding, and left academic physics for computing, keeping the subject as a hobby.
