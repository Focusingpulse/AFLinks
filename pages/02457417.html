============= 2400B HAYES MODEM COMMANDS LIST ============= Here is a list of the S-registers used by Hayes modem. Please be aware that not all modems support these registers and that some modems may have more registers than are listed. ALL S REGISTER COMMANDS ARE ENTERED IN THE AT COMMAND LINE.
