All the minor sevenths are a comma too low for their tonic notes. But as D, G, C, and F are a comma to high, if the tonic notes were right these four sevenths would be right with their present numbers . The other three scales, A, E, and B, which begin on their proper numbers , have their sevenths a comma too low."
