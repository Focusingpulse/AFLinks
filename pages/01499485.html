See the OBRL Web Site for more information on this and other educational workshops: http://id.mind.net/community/orgonelab/index.htm * Construction Materials Five-pound reels of fine "OO" grade steel wool are available. Two reels will make a 3-ply standing accumulator. Price of $39 per reel. Carded Sheep's Wool is available, the best natural material for stuffing orgone pillows, blankets and accumulator panels. We obtain this from a ranch in the Southwest, where the wool is sheared, cleaned, air-dried and then carded (combed) into a fluffy and resilient fiber.
