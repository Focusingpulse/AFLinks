Getting effects quicker

· archived # 2,431,832
I'm in a phase where I am just so pleased with the results I am getting from my PATHS modules...one thing I've noticed is that I am getting results so quickly now. When I first started in August 2008, I would say I could feel things happening, but it took about 2-3 weeks to experience what I would call 'results'. (Maybe I wasn't quite as good at noticing what was coming from the modules vs what was just occurring in my life?) Is that usual? I would think that PATHS would get more effective as you keep going with it because you are progressively clearing away limiting beliefs from different angles. For example, I was on 'deserving' for a month, and I would hypothesize that it helps me be more allowing of good things that can come from other, later modules. One module I am really enjoying is the 'take it off' module. I actually tried the ultimate body last month, but very quickly had the feeling it wasn't the right one. It didn't seem to have much effect (but I read somewhere that the more biological oriented modules can take longer to kick in) until the very last couple of days, where I was noticing a small change. But as soon as I got on this one, it was really noticeable from the first day. I think one of the reasons this module is really working for me, is that it is reinforcing things that are already things that I aspire to, and are a part of my values. So it's helping me just do those things more, and there's a real click of happiness that I am aligning my behaviour with my true values. The first day I was on it (Sat?), I found myself eating all sorts of things I don't really eat, that I usually avoid. White bread, sausages (normally they make me go ick), chocolate icing by itself! It was clear to me that I was rebelling! By the next day it was switching to the opposite - I kept drinking rooibos tea (I prefer caffeine) and had 2 big bowls of salad for lunch which is not something I usually do, especially in the winter. Neither extreme is how I normally am. Yest
