In it Chukanov sets out his theory of "quantum energy" and its basis in the physics of ball lightning. Overview Chukanov argues for a wholesale reform of physics grounded in what he calls General Quantum Mechanics, from which he derives a program for extracting usable "free" energy from quantum-scale processes.
