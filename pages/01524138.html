Overview The Inertial Helicopter is a four-page paper by David Tombe , dated 27 March 2017 and published on the General Science Journal . Like the rest of his series it builds on an aether model set out in earlier instalments rather than restating it. Its proposal is stated in the abstract: "A sea level object that is moving horizontally at a speed greater than 8km/sec is already in orbit and it will rise upwards due to centrifugal force. Two such objects tethered together while moving in opposite directions should therefore spiral upwards like a helicopter." The atom is then offered as the fundamental instance of the same device. The argument proceeds through four stages. An aerodynamic gyroscope — a spoked wheel carrying gimballed turbines — is described as reproducing gyroscopic behaviour through the aerodynamic P-factor when forcibly precessed, which Tombe takes as a clue that real gyroscopic forces arise from "a microscopic P-factor… in conjunction with a finer background medium which is not recognized to exist in mainstream physics."
