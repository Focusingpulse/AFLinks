United States Psychotronics Association Educational Non Profit 501(c) (3) A Compilation of Tables of Contents of the Six Journals Created by USPA from the years 1988 to 1993 The Evolution of the USPA Periodicals Since its first meeting in 1975, hundreds of top scientists, educators and researchers have made presentations at USPAs annual conferences. They are from the leading edges of their fields, especially regarding the phenomena of Mind-Body connection, energy and emerging technologies. The first audio recordings were made in 1978. In 1985, USPA produced a perfect-bound Proceedings book for its tenth conference. It is a comprehensive and fully edited collection of abstracts and/or final papers submitted by twenty-eight of the more than fifty presenters at that year’s conference. The difficultly of editing papers and formatting the collection for book publication, just weeks before a conference was rather daunting. In 1986 the USPA Board decided to have a committee professionally edit submitted papers and print them in smaller groups, hence creating a series of six journals published between November of 1988 and the spring of 1993. Each Journal contains six to ten talks given at US Psychotronics Conferences during its first eighteen years. These Journals represent some of the best written USPA material of that time! In 1993, with advances in computers and copiers, a change was made to print Annual Conference Proceedings again. Conference attendees could buy a copy right at the conference. At 260-400 pages, they were large spiral-bound hard-copy books! In 2010 another switch was made and the proceedings were produced on CDs. The 6 USPA Journals and 18 of our 19 Annual Proceedings can be purchased in pdf format from our webstore at https://www.psychotronics.org/product-category/uspapublications-journals-proceedings/ All USPA publications are copyright protected and are property of the USPA.
