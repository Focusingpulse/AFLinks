Tags: None p10e Junior Member Join Date: Jun 2008 Posts: 8 Share Tweet #2 06-07-2008, 08:01 AM Flame test You should never test for gas with a flame. Would you light a match to see if you had gasoline in a drum? If you are to test a flame for a torch you must have spark arrestors which are easy to make and can be found with a quick search on this forum or the net. They must be used on almost all applications so make it one of the first things you do. Look at bubblers. You can use them to see your gas. If you are tempted to light the bubbles DON"T.
