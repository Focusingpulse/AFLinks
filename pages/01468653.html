[ Harmonies of Tones and Colours , Supplementary Remarks and Diagrams , page 53] According to the Encyclopedia Britannica, the tree of knowledge , connecting heaven and the underworld, and the tree of life , connecting all forms of creation , are both forms of the world tree or cosmic tree. According to some scholars, the tree of life and the tree of the knowledge of good and evil , portrayed in various religions and philosophies, are the same tree.
