Ether Space-Time and Cosmology, Vol. 1: Modern Ether Concepts, Relativity and Geometry Book Author Michael C Duffy , Joseph Levy Published 2008 Publisher PD Publications, Liverpool, UK Pages 438 ISBN 1873694105 Ether Space-Time and Cosmology, Vol. 1: Modern Ether Concepts, Relativity and Geometry is a collection of eleven papers edited by Michael C Duffy and Joseph Levy , published in 2008 by PD Publications of Liverpool. It is the first of three volumes under the Ether Space-Time and Cosmology title; volumes 2 and 3 followed from Apeiron . Its contributors are drawn largely from the circle around the Physical Interpretations of Relativity Theory conferences, held at Imperial College London, whose September 1998 proceedings Duffy published and which several of the authors here attended. Every one of the volume's eleven papers is catalogued separately on this wiki. Publisher's description Researches undertaken during the last 20 years have confirmed that space possesses physical properties even where it is devoid of ordinary matter. In addition to the well known properties of permittivity, permeability and the ability to transmit electromagnetic waves, other features have been more recently associated with the nature of space. These include the Casimir Effect and a significant amount of energy. This medium, devoid of any trace of ordinary matter, is usually referred to as "Physical Vacuum" , "Plenum" or "Cosmic Substratum" along with other appellations. Despite the veil of equivalent terms, these names obviously refer to the Ether , a medium conceived in antiquity, which received much attention from Science between the 17th and early 20th centuries. Today it is commonly understood throughout the academic community that Einstein excluded once and for all the ether from modern physics with his Special Theory of 1905.
