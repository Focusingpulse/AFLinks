Any intelligent person can understand this entire book, including its derivation of the following equation, which gives the precise value of Planck's quantum of action, h: h = 2?rmc' in which m is the mass of an electron, c' is its orbital speed in an H atom, r is the radius of that orbit, and 2?r is the length of one orbital path.
