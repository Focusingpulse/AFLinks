He Asserts That His System Is Equal and Perhaps Superior to Marconi's Device. WASHINGTON, Dec. 29. — Nikola Tesla, the inventor, has been asked by Rear Admiral R. B. Bradford, chief of the Bureau of Equipment, to submit for trial a wireless telegraphy system perfected by him. Mr. Tesla claims that his system is equal and perhaps superior to that of Marconi, and has offered to install it on naval vessels. Admiral Bradford in his last annual report recommended that negotiations be entered with Marconi to secure his system for American vessels of war if a reasonable rate can be agreed on.
