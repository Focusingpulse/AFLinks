Ain Soph

· archived # 1,455,694
Ain Soph Ein Sof, meaning “the Boundless”, in the Kabbalah represents the infinite, unknowable essence of the divine, the ultimate source from which all existence emerges. Ein Sof gives rise to creation through a process of contraction (tzimtzum) and emanation. Within this, the divine unfolds through the sefirot, structured expressions of the infinite, through which reality gradually emerges, while remaining rooted in its source. Ain Soph is the absolute, infinite, and pre manifest essence of the divine, completely unknowable and beyond all names, while YHWH is the manifest, nameable aspect of that same divine, through which it becomes present and relates to creation. Thereby linking the divine presence to the fundamental process of life. The Tetragrammaton expresses the continuity of existence and the flow of time. In the image, this boundless state appears at the top, then condenses into a single point (the monad or primordial unity), from which energy descends and unfolds through successive levels. Descent of spirit into matter.
