12.06 - Mid-Tones and Neutral Centers

· archived # 1,455,078
The Neutral Center Tones are the same as Russell 's mid-tone s generated between the 4 and 3 tones. This is the zone of hyper-sex-activity or hyper- polar -activity wherein regeneration is initiated (mutual voiding). This intense action generates overtones ( mid-tones ) of increasing discord .
