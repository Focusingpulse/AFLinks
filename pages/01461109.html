Oneness of the universe

· archived # 1,461,109
Russell "The most important new thought in relation to electricity is that every action creates only one cycle of an electric current . That one cycle is the electric record of that action . The omnipresence - or Oneness of the universe - repeats that record throughout the universe. The motion of your little finger, or of a dynamite blast, will create one cycle for each action , for electricity is the recorder of Mind-thought , and Mind-thoughts are consecutive." [ Atomic Suicide , page 184] Heinrich Khunrath "Know thyself, from the book of the Holy Scriptures; The nature of the whole Universe, that is, and the whole Macrocosm, and the Microcosm, whether you yourself, according to both your body and your spirit; finally, and your mind, united to God."
