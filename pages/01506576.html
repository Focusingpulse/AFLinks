Whether working on yourself, friends and family, or Clients, we always want to get the most benefit from an EPFX session. There are a few important steps that can be done to optimize the benefits. These are things that I learned along the way, that in my experience are VERY Effective in clearing, energizing, and preparing a person to have the maximum benefits from their biofeedback experience. 1. At the very opening screen go to Shaping Function . Choose the option that will best benefit the person you are working with. By doing this, every time they rectify to 85 or better, they will receive reinforcement, thus entraining them to be more receptive to the biofeedback. Love is always a good one, so is serotonin. 2. Go to Programs/EEG ECG Freq, go to Autonomic Nerval System , rectify Sympathetic, Parasympathetic and Vagus Nerve all to 85 or better. 3. Go to Programs/Short Scarcode, go to Ear + etc. Rectify each sarcode down the left to 85 or better. (this process is clearing/cleansing them in preparation for the work you will be doing) Make notes on which ones take more than one or two passes to get to 85 or better. If one of them takes 3 or more passes it will be helpful for you later on in establishing patterns. 4. Go to Risk Profile In the upper right corner you will see Minerals and Amino Acids . Go through each of these lists and bring them to 85 or better. Again take notes on the stubborn ones. This will be of help to you. By doing these four steps you are cleaning and energizing them to take the most advantage of all subsequent work you will be doing. Try it out, you will be pleased with the results!
