Nickel hydroxide precursor, prepared by addition of sodium hydroxide (NaOH) to nickel nitrate Ni(NO3)2 solution and vigorously stirring until the pH becomes 7.2. Ultrasounds waves were applied during precipitation period and after drying in oven nanopowder NiO was achieved. Transient and scanning electron microscopy showed that the synthesized nanopowder NiO had dimensions about 20 nm. The X-ray diffraction pattern revealed that the produced well-crystallized and high purity nickel oxide nanostructure. Keywords: Nickel oxide; Sonochemical rout; Nanopowder; Characterization. Introduction Nickel oxide (NiO) is a p-type oxide semiconductor with a wide band gap (3.6−4.0 eV) and ~1.8 eV conduction band energy [1-2] which has been considered as a promising materials for optical, electronic and catalytic applications. NiO nanoparticles also may have many applications such as in the manufacture of superparamagnetic materials, p-type transparent conducting films, gas sensors, catalyst, alkaline batteries cathode, dye-sensitized solar cells, and solid oxide fuel cells anode [3–6]. Various methods such as sol–gel, hydrothermal synthesis, electrochemical methods, metal evaporation and spray pyrolysis, have been used and developed to produce nonopowder materials [3,7-8]. Recently, the sonochemical methods have been acknowledged as a promising route for preparation of a variety of materials with nanometer dimensions, including metallic and ceramic nanoparticles [9-11].
