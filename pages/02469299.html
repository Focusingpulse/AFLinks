EARLY TAKERS OF THE BOXES. Chauncey M. Depew Will Preside and Edison and Tesla Will Be Present. GREAT DOCTORS WILL TAKE PART. Prelates and Clergymen, Veterans and Schoolboys Will Add to Attraction of the Night. It is going to be a great night for the Red Cross Society and for everybody who goes to the Grand Central Palace on Saturday evening. All will take part in the entertainment given under the auspices of The World. The first box was taken by William T. Wardwell, Treasurer of the Standard Oil Company, and the second by Mrs. Platt, the wife of Senator Thomas C. Platt. John P. Faure, former Commissioner of Charities, took the third box. They paid $50 each for them, fixing the price themselves. Of course, anybody who wishes to pay more for a box is at liberty to do so, and he will have the satisfaction of knowing that every penny of it will go to the Red Cross Society. The Health Exposition shares the general-admission receipts, but not the money taken in for boxes. The World bears the other expenses as a part of its contribution. You do not have to buy a whole box if your resources are not equal to it. There are a number of box seats to be sold at prices ranging from $1 to $5, the higher-priced seats being near the presiding officer, Chauncey M. Depew. It would be wise to subscribe for seats
