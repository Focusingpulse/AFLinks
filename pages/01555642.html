VIII. Thinking as a Higher Experience within Experience - GA 2. A Theory of Knowledge (1940)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 1,555,642
Amid the unrelated chaos of experience—and, indeed, at first as a fact of experience—we find an element that leads us out beyond this unrelated-ness. This element is thought. Thought, as one of the facts of experience, assumes an exceptional position within experience. [ 2 ] As regards the rest of experience, so long as I limit myself to that which is immediately present to my senses, I do not advance beyond the separate units.
