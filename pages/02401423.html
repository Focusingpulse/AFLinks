GC2026-SGG015 Monitoring of the stress-strain state of rocks using data of audiomagnetotelluric soundings Alexander K. Saraev1 , Arseny A. Shlykov1 , Mikhail G. Dembelov2 , Dmitry V. Zaitsev1 , Sudha Agrahari3 1 St Petersburg University, St. Petersburg, Russia 2 Institute of Physical Materials Science of the Siberian Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences, Ulan-Ude, Russia 3 Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur, India a.saraev@spbu.ru Monitoring of variations in apparent resistivity (ρ a ) for earthquake prediction has been carried out for many years in various countries (China, Japan, India, Russia, etc.). Abnormal ρ a behavior is usually observed in periods from several months to 2-3 years before an earthquake, i.e. this is a medium-term precursor. The values of anomalies are usually the first percent, and can reach 10-20%. Considerable experience in recording variations of ρ a by the vertical electrical soundings (VES) method has been accumulated in China. The size of the current line AB in the monitoring system is usually 1 km and the receiving line MN - 200 m. So, the informative value of the method is limited by one size of the current line and, accordingly, one depth. Electromagnetic sounding methods with measurements of fields from controlled sources or natural electromagnetic fields, which allow obtaining information about changes in rocks properties at various depths, are more informative than the VES method.
