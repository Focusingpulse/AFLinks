The Round Robin >> Vol. 02 >> No. 03 [11] SPIRITONS AND SPIROCULONS in the LIGHT OF PSYCHIC MONISM by Philip S. Haley, Ph.C., D.D.S. (A continuation of Dr. Haley's article in the February issue. The author is the President of the California Society for Psychical Research, and the writer of numerous books and pamphlets.) (Editor's note: As we understand Dr. Haley, he believes that the surviving personality exists as a center of consciousness, without any particular size or shape, or anything like a human form. The forms seen in materializations are idioplastic productions . . . The argument from biological analogy seems to us worthy of close attention; nevertheless almost the whole mass of occult teachings and communications assert the existence of etheric and astral bodies of a human-like appearance. It seems to us probable that these assertions can be reconciled with Dr. Haley's opinions and facts . . .) The Editor of the Round Robin writes three paragraphs of comment, the first of which substantially outlines correctly the writer's idea of post-mortem existence . . . except for the following qualifications: The personalized superior dynamism is able to maintain itself without a form of material order, such as a biological cell, because it is like in quality with the world soul, from which it draws its basic existence . . . Hypothetically, the spiriton may be looked upon as a disembodied soul.
