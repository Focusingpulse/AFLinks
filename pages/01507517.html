They use an inflatable air form, they inflate it and then spray polyuretane foam insulation on the inside walls of the air form, after that they put some armature and cover that with spray concrete. Simple and fast. We also wanted to try something like this, but could not find a company here that could make those inflatable air forms cheap enough. So the idea stuck for some time till I discovered the simple and elegant geodesic dome idea. There are many web sites online with geodesic dome howto's, diagrams and calculators. You simply get some wood bars, cut them to appropriate length and connect them together according to a diagram. The structure is very rigid and strong. It costs much less than an air form
