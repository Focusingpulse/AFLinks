Solving Recurrence Relations Using Generating Functions Harry Willow Abstract. This paper explores the applications of generating functions in solving various recurrence relations. We present the explicit formulas for recurrence relations of different forms, demonstrating step-by-step transformations and manipulations using generating functions. Several cases are examined, including linear and nonlinear recurrences, factorial-based sequences, and Fibonacci-related expressions. The derivations leverage algebraic techniques and characteristic equations to obtain closed-form solutions. The results highlight the power of generating functions in simplifying complex recurrence relations and deriving explicit formulas efficiently.
