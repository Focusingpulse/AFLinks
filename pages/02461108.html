I will quote part of the story: "An elixir that will rejuvenate the hearts and other muscles of elderly people is being developed by scientists. the breakthrough could soon enable 80-year-olds to throw away their walking sticks and Zimmer Frames. Research on the drugs called breakers is being pioneered by one of Americas leading Medical Researchers, Professor Tony Cerami. Basicly, the article explains, over many years the glucose in our bodies is baked by the heat of our body causing proteins to clump together. This causes joints, arteries and and heart tissues to stiffen. The new drug ALT-711 breaks the bonds caused by the glucose reversing the effects of ageing. Thats why the drug is called a breaker. The important point to realise, in my opinion. is that this doesn't just slow this aspect of ageing it truly reverse it. According to the story, old dogs and monkeys were bounding around full of energy.
