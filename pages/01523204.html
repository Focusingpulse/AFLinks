James DeMeo Residence Ashland, OR, United States Nationality USA Alma mater Florida International University; University of Kansas Known for The modern defence of Dayton Miller 's ether-drift experiments; Saharasia ; experimental work on Wilhelm Reich's findings Scientific career Fields Geography, Earth and atmospheric science, orgone biophysics Institutions Orgone Biophysical Research Lab, Ashland, Oregon James DeMeo is an American researcher in geography and atmospheric science, and Director of the Orgone Biophysical Research Lab in Ashland, Oregon, which he founded in 1978. He matters most on this wiki for a single sustained piece of work: the recovery and defence of Dayton C Miller 's ether-drift experiments . DeMeo went back to Miller's original records and to the 1955 reanalysis that dismissed them, and produced the most detailed case in print that the dismissal was wrong. Much of what is now argued about Miller on this wiki rests on what DeMeo found there. His wider research programme — cross-cultural work on the origins of warfare, and laboratory work on Wilhelm Reich's findings — is more controversial and is described below. Education Studied Earth, atmospheric and environmental/social sciences at Florida International University PhD, University of Kansas , 1986 — where he undertook graduate-level research explicitly testing Wilhelm Reich 's claims Career DeMeo served on the Faculty of Geography at Illinois State University and the University of Miami , and is a former Research Associate of the American College of Orgonomy. He founded the Orgone Biophysical Research Lab in 1978, and in 1994 established the Greenspring Center , a high-altitude research facility in the Siskiyou Mountains near Ashland, Oregon. He has carried out field research in the arid American Southwest, Egypt, Israel, sub-Saharan Eritrea and Namibia.
