NEW YORK’S MAYOR IN A MIX-UP The Managers of the National Electric Show Over the River Much Affronted Because He Declined to Open the Fair Without Assigning a Reason — Mayor Wurster Fills the Breach Without Knowing All the Facts. The announcement that Mayor Wurster will preside at the big fair of the National Exposition of Electrical Appliances, at the Grand Central Industrial building, in New York, to-night, when the governor and many other notable persons are to be present, gave rise to much conjecture as to why Mayor Strong is not to fill the place of presiding officer. George F. Porter, secretary and treasurer of the exposition, said this morning: “Our committee invited Mayor Strong to preside, but he declined to do so. Why? He did not give any reason. He just said he wouldn’t. As far as I know the interview was pleasant enough. Frank W. Hawley, chairman of the committee, could tell you all about that. I know that a good many of the men in the fair were much piqued about the way Mayor Strong acted. You can say that Mayor Wurster of the greater New York — I think that would be a good way to put it — accepted very gracefully.
