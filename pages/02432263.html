How do I add Modules?

· archived # 2,432,263
Q: I currently have two modules in my Theater Presentation. How do I add another? And at that point, what would the costs be to do more modules? A: To add Modules, log in to your PATHS website and click on the "Add Modules" button on the left hand sidebar and follow the prompts. The cost is $50 per Module. The monthly recurring of $34.95 will not change. You can have up to 4 Modules per Theater. If you already have 4 Modules, you can set up another Theater and add up to 4 more Modules there. Set up fees and monthly recurring will apply. If you would like to sign up for the Project 1 World Team Module, the Diabetes Super Module, or the Platinum Package, you must delegate a whole Theater for these as they are Premium Modules. You will not be able to add these to a Theater with the add module process, and you will not be able to add Modules to a Theater containing one of these Premium Modules.
