(Dated: May 16, 2025) Abstract We study the Hausa head entries of the Dictionary of The Hausa Language by R.C.Abraham, the Second Edition, 1962. We draw the natural logarithm of the number of the Hausa language head entries, normalised, starting with a letter vs the natural logarithm of the rank of the letter, normalised/unnormalised.
