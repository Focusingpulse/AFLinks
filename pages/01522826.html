Edward Kapuscik Born 1938 (age 87–88) Residence 31 342 Krakow, Poland Nationality Polish Known for Quantum Theory Scientific career Fields Professor of Physics Edward Kapuścik (born 1938 in Racibórz, Poland) is a Polish theoretical physicist. He has spent his career at the Henryk Niewodniczański Institute of Nuclear Physics in Kraków, and has worked on the foundations of quantum physics, on electrodynamics, and on special and general relativity. He has authored more than ninety original scientific papers. Education Physics, Jagiellonian University , Kraków, 1956–1961 Career Kapuścik joined the Henryk Niewodniczański Institute of Nuclear Physics in Kraków in 1961 and was appointed to a professorship there in 1988. From 1986 to 1989 he served as Deputy Director of the Laboratory of Theoretical Physics at the Joint Institute for Nuclear Research in Dubna, and in 1991 he was Visiting Professor at Georgia State University. He was a professor at the Kraków Pedagogical University from 1992 to 1998, in its Department of Foundations of Physics, where he headed the Division of Modelling of Didactic Processes, and he later held an ordinary professorship in the Physics Department at the University of Łódź. He is Editor in Chief of the journal Concepts of Physics . Work recorded here Kapuścik's contributions gathered here concern the structure of electrodynamics and the status of its symmetries rather than any rejection of Maxwell's equations, which he describes as "the best theory for all electromagnetic phenomena." " Non-Lorentzian Gauge Fields in Maxwell Electrodynamics " (2000) observes that the usual potentials treat the electric and magnetic fields asymmetrically, so that the duality symmetry of source-free electrodynamics cannot be expressed in terms of them.
