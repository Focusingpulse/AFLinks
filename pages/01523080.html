Harry Jabs Known for Electric Universe cosmology, Tesla's "space energy", revival of the Aether concept in modern physics Scientific career Fields Physics , Cosmology , instrumentation, biophysics Institutions Institute for Frontier Science, California Harry Jabs is a physicist, engineer and inventor who works outside the institutional mainstream on the revival of the aether concept in physics, on Electric Universe cosmology, and on Nikola Tesla's claim that usable energy can be drawn from space itself. He is Co-Principal Investigator at the Institute for Frontier Science, a small non-profit research laboratory in California, where he collaborates closely with the biophysicist Beverly Rubik . Biography Jabs earned a Master of Science degree in physics at the University of Hamburg, Germany, and a second Master of Science degree in physics at Texas A&M University in College Station, Texas. He is described by the Institute for Frontier Science as a multi-disciplinary scientist and engineer with a track record of invention and research spanning physics, chemistry, life sciences and medicine, electronics, mechanics, micro-mechanics, material science and computer science. He holds seven patents and eight disclosures of invention, and has received two NASA certificates of recognition. Much of his laboratory work has involved designing detectors and instrumentation — in particular ultra-sensitive photon-counting equipment used at the Institute for Frontier Science to measure extremely weak light emission from living organisms. Scientific contributions Electric Universe cosmology and Tesla's space energy Jabs is a proponent of the Electric Universe view of the cosmos, the tradition associated on this wiki with Wallace Thornhill and David Talbott , in which electric currents and plasma — rather than gravity acting alone — are the dominant organising forces in space.
