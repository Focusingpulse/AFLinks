So various were the phenomena, that even the lecturer was from time to time in doubt as to the best way of presenting the delectable feast to his guests; and so incomprehensible were the effects that he confessed, "I cannot see the forest on account of the trees." Already the work of Mr. Tesla, with regard to currents of high frequency and high potential, had been received and appreciated in this country, as far as printed descriptions could make it plain to us. But something more was needed to impress us with the true sense of its importance. We wanted to see the phenomena themselves, and to have before us the man who had deciphered them from Nature's infinite book of mysteries. The lecture given before the Electrical Engineers at the Royal Institution last Wednesday, was only the second which Mr. Tesla had ever given in public. At the invitation of the managers of the Royal Institution, the lecture was repeated on Thursday to its own members.
