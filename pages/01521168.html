It is one of the core statements of his "186-ether" programme — an attempt to build all of physics on a single fundamental ether particle and, in this paper specifically, to show that light and gravity are one phenomenon expressed through that ether. Overview Fernandes's starting point is that radiation is not the locomotion of photons but the propagation of a wave through a sea of ether particles — the "186-ether." On this picture a black body is a " wave-maker ": pair production at the source ripples the surrounding ether particles, and it is that ripple, not any travelling photon, that constitutes light. From this premise he draws a series of identifications among the fundamental constants, all of which are meant to converge on a single claim — that the constants of electromagnetism, thermodynamics and gravitation are different faces of one etheric quantity. The central claims The paper is organised around several assertions, each expressed as a relation between measured constants: Gravity as an ether product. Fernandes writes the gravitation constant as G = ℘ · B · c ², where ℘ ≈ 1.859 × 10⁻⁹ kg is the mass he assigns to the 186-ether particle, B ≈ 1.38 × 10⁻³⁶ m is a "Boltzmann radius," and c the speed of light . This is offered as the deconstruction of G into etheric ingredients, and as the point at which "unity of light and gravity is elucidated." The fine-structure number as a radial expansion.
