For example, Robert Forward proposed a gravity machine working according to the Einstein’s General Relativity Theory, which requires ultra dense matter with the density of a dwarf star to produce useful thrust, but the proposed theoretical scheme cannot be realized by conventional technologies. The authors propose several concepts of a gravity machine utilizing an intense electromagnetic field that produces sufficient thrust to propel the spaceship, in accordance with Einstein’s General Relativity Theory. 1. Introduction A breakthrough propulsion method has been required for the purposes of interplanetary and interstellar travel. Instead of conventional chemical propulsion systems, field propulsion systems, which are based on the General Relativity Theory, have been proposed by many researchers. Some of them are based on solutions of the General Relativity Theory and are related to a connection between gravity and electromagnetic field.
