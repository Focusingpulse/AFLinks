Celani, F., et al. Sviluppo di catalizzatore ternario, skeleton type, per studi su anomalie termiche nei sistemi Metallo-Idrogeno ad alta temperatura (PowerPoint slides). in Coherence 2011. 2011. Ministero dell'Aereonautica, Italy. Introductory notes from Francesco Celani: Attached are both my short presentation (at Ministero dell'Aereonautica, 14 Dicembre 2011, Roma) and the old presentation about such material presented at the Annual Conference of Italian Physical Society (25-30 September 2011, L'Aquila), both in Italian. This is a SHORT report covering most (but not all!) experiments worldwide that gave excess heat. * The final, VERY SHORT, comment/information was that, after waiting a long time, we were able to detect some excess power, increasing slowly from 2 W up to about 10 W, using a NEW material based on an alloy of Ni-Cu with the surface NANO-Micro-sized. The material was studied systematically since last April and some previous explorative tests made on Sept. 2010. The geometry, as usual, was wire. The input power was about 60 W, so the gain was no so large compared to the results from Rossi and/or Defkalion. Compared to them, our results look like "noise". Anyway, the local temperature of the wire was large (500-600°C) and the temperature of the cell was enough large (>260°C). The excess power density was of the order of 50 W/g when you include the "inert" substrate. Considering only the portion at the surface that has nano-microparticles particles, excess power density was on the order of 500 W/g. * After 1 week of operations I stopped the test to run a control test: all was OK. The system was almost stable at 10 W excess in the final 2 days. The gas was Hydrogenated compound. The experiment with Deuterated compounds give lower values of excess power!
