equality of environment

· archived # 1,470,954
"Every potential state of pressure in this universe has its proper place in it, and it will seek the current of motion which takes it there. Raindrops will fall to seek equality of environment in the direction of gravity . Compression is the cause of that fall, not gravity . Water vapor will rise to seek equality of environment in the direction of space . When water vapor finds its equal pressure it will float weightlessly, for weight is but the measure of potential which is out of place. Matter which floats in equipotential environments is weightless in respect to any other matter in the universe, yet mathematicians spend much wasted time in calculating the weight of this weightless planet."
