Expansion Tectonics #2: Dr. Maxlow on Permian to Archaean Small Earth Modelling Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Date 17 October 2020 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording Expansion Tectonics #2: Dr. Maxlow on Permian to Archaean Small Earth Modelling was a live session on 17 October 2020 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. Dr. James Maxlow talks this coming Saturday will be the second of four talks James has planned and will be called “Permian to Archaean Small Earth Modeling”. This will extend on his previous talk and will use continental crusts to constrain plate assemblage further back in time. Using continental crusts to constrain plate assemblages back in time has never been done so this technique is considered unique. Once he models the continental crusts he will show an animation showing the entire small Earth modeling history of the Earth extending from the early Archaean to the present-day, plus an extra model at 5 million years into the future. He will also elaborate further on a proposed causal mechanism for increase in Earth mass and radius over time. Summary The second of James Maxlow 's four talks, extending the modelling from the Permian back to the early Archaean — the part of the work that goes beyond what most people know of the expanding earth, which is the reassembly of the present continents. Reverse engineering the continents Maxlow was again explicit that this is a data modelling exercise, not a theory modelling exercise, and made the contrast sharply. Conventional reconstructions of Rodinia are governed by apparent polar wander paths generated by each author, so the published assemblages differ at each author's whim; on a smaller radius earth there is only ever one fit available.
