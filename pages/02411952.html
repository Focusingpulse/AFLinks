Findings from Spiritual Research on Questions of Life and the Mystery of Death [ 1 ] The greatest mysteries of life, which have universal human significance, are not presented to us through specific scientific research, but rather we encounter them at every turn in life. And the greatest questioner is, after all, life itself, which continually approaches us—a questioner whose questions not only arouse our curiosity and thirst for knowledge, but whose questions can also bring joy and sorrow, satisfaction, or even despair to our souls.
