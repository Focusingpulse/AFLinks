"...the two electrodes undergo states of polarization.....the inner surface of the outer ring electrode (the electrode that is in contact with the fluid) becomes negatively charged" Both inventors claim the same effect in their Water Fuel Injectors, But what exactly is electron clustering? And how does a negative charge just form? The answer is known in physics as ELECTROSTATIC INDUCTION! Electrostatic induction is when one charged object charges another nearby object. If I have an object which has a positive charge (lack of electrons) and I put it toward another object the positive charge will attract electrons toward it, creating a negative charge (defined as an area with an excess of electrons) (Stan calls it electron clustering) on the surface closest to the positive charged object.
