And how does a negative charge just form? The answer is known in physics as ELECTROSTATIC INDUCTION! Electrostatic induction is when one charged object charges another nearby object. If I have an object which has a positive charge (lack of electrons) and I put it toward another object the positive charge will attract electrons toward it, creating a negative charge (defined as an area with an excess of electrons) (Stan calls it electron clustering) on the surface closest to the positive charged object.
