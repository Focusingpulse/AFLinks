1 A mathematical proof about spatial symmetry invariance of solution of the three-dimensional Navier-Stokes equations Shijun Liao∗ State Key Laboratory of Ocean Engineering, Shanghai, China School of Ocean and Civil Engineering, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, China Abstract We prove such a mathematical theorem of spatial symmetry invariance: solution of the three-dimensional incompressible Navier-Stokes equations under periodic boundary condition preserves the same spatial symmetry u 0 (x 0 , t) = u(x, t) for all t > 0 as its smooth initial condition, where x 0 i = xi+π and/or x 0 i = −xi (i = 1, 2, 3), if there exist the spatial symmetry u 0 (x 0 , 0) = u(x, 0) for initial condition and f 0 (x 0 ) = f(x) for steady-state external force, and besides the temporal Taylor series of u(x, t) exists and has a non-zero radius of convergence for t ≥ 0. This theorem of spatial symmetry invariance can be used to check the correctness and accuracy of numerical simulation of the Navier-Stokes equations for turbulent flows.
