Jeff Yee's Wave Energy Theory Watch this video on YouTube Talk Type Video Speaker Lori Anne Gardi Date 31 January 2020 Platform YouTube Recording Recording Jeff Yee's Wave Energy Theory is a video by Lori Anne Gardi , published on 31 January 2020 on her FractalWoman YouTube channel. Summary An account of the work of the independent researcher Jeff Yee and of Gardi's collaboration with him — the video in which she sets out, for the first time at length, a research partnership she says she had been holding back until her channel was large enough to make an impact with it. How the collaboration began Gardi came across Yee, who runs the Energy Wave Theory channel, through an interview David de Hilster gave him on the Dissident Science channel, and she recommends both. The interview marked the release of the second edition of Yee's book, Particles of the Universe — Disrupted , which she downloaded along with the first and read on a rained-out holiday in the Bahamas, where the weather gave her time to go through it thoroughly. She found it well written and an easy read, and notes that the Kindle edition costs practically nothing. The wave structure of matter Yee's model descends from Milo Wolff's work on the wave structure of matter, and Gardi reads out enough of the abstract to give the foundation. The structure of the electron, on this account, is closely entwined with the origin of natural laws: those laws have been measured for centuries without anyone knowing how nature produces them. She notes the lineage Yee claims — Clifford and Schrödinger, who held that only a wave structure of matter can explain the natural laws, and Einstein's recognition that matter is spherical and spatially extended, so that the electron is not a point particle but a structure of space. The geometry follows from that.
