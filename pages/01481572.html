medical intuitive

· archived # 1,481,572
A medical intuitive is an alternative medicine practitioner who uses their intuitive abilities to ascertain the cause of a physical, emotional, mental or spiritual condition. Other terms for this practice include medical clairvoyant , medical psychic or intuitive counselor. A medical intuitive may determine areas of concern from a spiritual or holistic health practitioner's point of view and make recommendations in regards to physical or psychological evaluation through alternative means or qualified health professional and give advice on general nutritional, thinking processes or life-style changes. In ever increasing cases medical intuitives are being hired by hospitals, clinics and medical offices, particularly in Calilfornia.
