The Big Breed Theory of the Creation of the Universe Book Author Ron Pearson Language English Subject Cosmology ; dark energy ; origin of the universe Published 2010 Publisher Lulu Enterprises Publication date 17 September 2010 Media type Paperback (black & white interior); PDF ebook in colour, 2011 Pages 65 ISBN 978-1446630242 The Big Breed Theory of the Creation of the Universe is a short illustrated book by the English engineer Ron Pearson , published through Lulu on 17 September 2010. At 65 pages it is the briefest and most accessible statement of his Big Breed cosmology, written for the general reader and carrying the argument in diagrams rather than in mathematics. Its stated aim is to show how what Pearson calls the Dark Energy Mystery was solved as a by-product of repairing what he regards as the Big Bang theory's fatal quantitative error. Publisher's description This book is the black & white version of the Big Breed theory of the creation of the Universe. For the lay reader this book shows by many illustrations and simple explanation how the Dark Energy Mystery was solved by the Big Breed Theory. Dark Energy is assumed by cosmologists as an explanation for the discovery that the expansion of our universe is speeding up instead of slowing down as they expected from the theory of creation described by the big bang. The latter, however, suffers from a hopelessly wrong prediction that gives a rate of expansion billions of times too high. We show how the Dark Energy Mystery was solved as spin-off due to solving the big bang's major problem. Size of universe is given for the first time! Overview The book is an exposition rather than a derivation. Pearson published the same material at several lengths in 2010 and 2011, and this is the entry point: no mathematics, many illustrations, and a running argument that a reader with no physics can follow.
