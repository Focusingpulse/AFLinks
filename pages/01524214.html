This is because the form of the Poynting vector follows from Ampère’s circuital law and Faraday’s law of time-varying electromagnetic induction. Although there is no known theoretical basis that would justify swapping the electromagnetic E field in S with an electrostatic E field, the dimensions of S would nevertheless remain unchanged if we did do so.
