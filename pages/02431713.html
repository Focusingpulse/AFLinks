SLP-1 and the double bind

· archived # 2,431,713
I'm on Successful Living, Gratitude, Synchronicity, and X Module. SLP-1 just made me aware of some double binds I have placed on myself that have prevented the manifestation of some things I want! I asked myself some questions, and got the following answers. "What would you do if you had everything you wanted?" --I would sit around on the couch all day like a new college graduate without a job. I wouldn't know what to do with myself. I would be useless. I would get depressed. I wouldn't be admirable or attractive in any way. I would not love myself anymore. "So you've put yourself in a real double bind. In order to love yourself, you have to remain in a state where you don't love yourself because you don't have what you want. Because you don't love yourself, you don't get what you want. You keep yourself unable to love yourself so that you don't wind up not loving yourself. You make sure you don't get what you want this way." --Yes. "So in a way, you have 'everything you want' by making sure you don't ever get what you want. Part of you is getting more out of not having what you want than the rest of you would get out of having it." --Yes. "Well then, maybe you should ask for more, so the part of you that wants it, wants it more than the part of you that is happy to be sour grapes." --Maybe.
