Dual Quaternion Geometric Algebra in PGA G(3,0,1) Robert Benjamin Easter and Daranee Pimchangthong Abstract. In Geometric Algebra, the algebra G(3,0,1) is known as PGA, the plane-based and point-based geometric algebras, or projective geo￾metric algebra, of points, lines, and planes in 3D space. The even-grades subalgebra of PGA, which we call Dual Quaternion Geometric Algebra (DQGA), represents Dual Quaternion Algebra (DQA). In the plane￾based algebra of PGA, there are entities for points, lines, and planes and many operations on them, including dualization to the point-based enti￾ties, reflections in planes, rotations, translations, projections, rejections, and intersections (meet products). In this paper, we derive a complete set of identities that relate all of the plane-based entities and opera￾tions in PGA to their corresponding entities and operations in DQGA. Therefore, this paper contributes into the literature on dual quaternions and PGA the complete details on how to use DQA or DQGA as a geo￾metric algebra of points, lines, and planes with many useful operations. All DQGA entities and operations are defined or derived such that the orientations of the entities are maintained correctly through all of the operations. We also define three new part operators for taking the point, line, or plane part of a dual quaternion, which may improve the compu￾tational efficiency of intersection (meet) operations. Dual quaternions already have some applications in computer graphics and kinematics.
