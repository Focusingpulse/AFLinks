We postulate the existence of two fundamental structures: the Internal Structure S(O), defined in Hilbert Space, and the External Structure S(O−1 ), defined in the complex field. The theoretical core of the work lies in identifying two mutually exclusive regimes of access to reality: the state of Observation (Potential Infinity) and the state of Understanding (Actual Infinity). We demonstrate that phenomenal reality and logical reality are not static, but the result of a continuous high-frequency exchange between cardinality increment and complex rotation.
