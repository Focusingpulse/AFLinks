PREFACE This is the proceedings of the 16th Meeting of Japan CF-Research Society (JCF16), which was held at Kyoto University, Higashi-Ichijyokan, Kyoto, Japan on December 11 -12, 2015. In this meeting, 19 presentations were given and 11 papers were submitted to the editorial board. Through the peer review processes, 3 papers concerning the experiment and 8 papers concerning the theory were accepted for publication as proceedings. They have been released on our web-site http://jcfrs.org/proc_jcf.html. We would like to welcome the responses from the world scientists. In closing, we wish to thank all the persons for their collaborations in organizing JCF16. We are looking forward to see you at ICCF20 and JFC17.
