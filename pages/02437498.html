Eulogy for Marie Hahn — GA 261. Our Dead

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 20 September 1918 · archived # 2,437,498
, Reinach Translated by Steiner Online Library Eulogy for Marie Hahn My dear friends! Our thoughts today, which are connected with the fact that five years ago today we laid the foundation stone of our building here in this place, were interrupted by the sad news that one of the oldest members of this area, our dear Mrs. Hahn, left the physical plane this morning. And I truly need not say much to stir the appropriate feelings in those of our dear friends who have known Mrs. Hahn. And those who knew her, who really knew her, truly loved her very, very much.
