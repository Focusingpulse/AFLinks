However, the model showed consistent estimates of about 0.096W/m2 15 per %Effective Normalized Area for the urbanized area 16 feedback value. The model is additionally used to quantify an assessment of sea ice feedback warming. Results 17 provide insight into the UHI area effects from a new perspective and illustrates that one needs to take effective UHI 18 amplification factors into account when assessing UHI’s warming effect on a global scale.
