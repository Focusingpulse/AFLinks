GA 238. The Individuality of Elias, John, Raphael, Novalis

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 28 September 1924 · archived # 2,436,109
, Dornach Translated by John Riedel The Individuality of Elias, John, Raphael, Novalis [ 1 ] It was impossible for me to speak to you yesterday or the day before. Yet the Michaelmas-Spirit of the present time, that in our hearts, in our souls, must certainly radiate out tomorrow, to this end I cannot allow the chance to pass by to have spoken to you, at least briefly, my dear friends. [ 2 ] That I am able to do so, is possible only due to the devoted care of our physician friend Ita Wegman. And so I hope that today I will be able to say to you, what I would certainly like to say to you concern
