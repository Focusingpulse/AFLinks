The factors include: atomic electric and magnetic rotational displacements, electric rotational vibration frequency, magnetic rotational vibration frequency, Planck's constant, Rydberg's constant, temperature, and molecular or crystal structure. Overview This is a 132-page Mathcad document by Ronald W Satz (2011), president of Transpower Corporation, presenting electric and magnetic material properties in terms of Dewey B. Larson's Reciprocal System of physical theory. Satz describes Larson, who was his mentor from 1965 until Larson's death in 1990, as "the first and only one to reduce all electric, magnetic, and gravitational concepts and equations to space-time terms alone", and the paper as a translation of Larson's books "into the language of mathematical physics". The Reciprocal System takes space and time as reciprocal aspects of a single entity, "scalar motion", and expresses every physical quantity in units of space and time alone. Atoms are characterised by integer "rotational displacements" written in the form 3-2-(4), and material properties are computed from these together with a geometric factor k G for the crystal cell, a refraction constant k r , and the density.
