There is a simplicity to the Electric Universe Theory that is at the heart of a unified picture of our world. This is possible because the forces employed are not only well known, but the picture they produce is already complete and employed daily in our lives. This post will be the first of 3 short posts introducing the proof that at the most fundamental level everything is electromagnetic in nature. The purpose of this first post is to introduce a simple basis for the claim that in the simplest portrait of our world we are looking at a 4 part circuit that arranges into patterns that form our reality. This theory satisfies one universal premise, the answer with the fewest assumptions tends to be the most correct. In an Electric Universe model there is no need for bosons, quarks, or dark matter, the entire theory is encompassed in the forces of Current, Voltage, Charge, and Flux. Current and Voltage formed Resistance, Voltage and Charge formed Capacitance, Current and Flux formed Inductance, and Charge and Flux must then form their own component he theorize called Memristance - Memory Resistance. If there is one single reason why this theory has not been previously proposed as a basis for all reality it is that the function of a Memristor was only a theory until the late 1980's, and not well articulated until the late 2000's.
