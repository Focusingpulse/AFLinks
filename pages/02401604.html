GC2026-PERM040 Paleomagnetic results of permian rocks from the greater Caucasus (Scythian plate) Ivan Fedyukin1 , Andrey Shatsillo1 , Tatyana Romanyuk1 , Irina Latysheva2 , Anastasia Novikova2 , Nikolay Kuznetsov2 1 O.Yu. Schmidt Institute of Physics of the Earth, Russian Academy of Sciences 2 Geological Institute of the Russian Academy of Sciences ivan_f88@mail.ru The Hercynian synorogenic molasse complex from Scythian plate is widespread in the Greater Caucasus and on its northern slope. Mainly red-colored terrigenous rocks are involved in the structure of the Permian part of this complex. Volcanogenic rocks intrude terrigenous rocks and often have conform contacts with matrix rocks. The results of paleomagnetic studies of sedimentary rocks composing molasses strata and intrusive rocks within the forward ridge of the Greater Caucasus are presented. The rocks lie monocline with fall orientations in a northerly direction with different degree of dip. The Aksaut formation is represented by red-colored sandstones and siltstones. They have been studied in outcrops from valleys of the Teberda, Aksaut, Belaya and Malaya Laba rivers. The age of the formation is determined by the presence of Early Permian flora. According to the results of temperature remagnetization a characteristic component of magnetization of one polarity with maximum accuracy is achieved in the ancient coordinate system, declination in western points with an inclination of -20°.
