Third World Energy Horizons, 2000-2020: A Regional Approach to Consumption and Sources of Supply

Third World Energy Horizons, 2000-2020: A Regional Approach to Consumption and Sources of Supply Book Author Jean-Romain Frisch Language English and French (parallel texts) Subject Energy demand and supply forecasting in developing countries Published 1981 Publisher Published for the World Energy Conference by Éditions Techniques et Économiques, Paris Pages 267 Third World Energy Horizons, 2000–2020: A Regional Approach to Consumption and Sources of Supply is a 1981 study by Jean-Romain Frisch , issued for the World Energy Conference by Éditions Techniques et Économiques of Paris. It is a forecast of energy demand and supply in the developing world over the four decades to 2020, prepared as a report to Round Table no. 6 of the XIth World Energy Conference , held at Munich on 11 September 1980. It is published in English and French in parallel , under the double title Third World Energy Horizons, 2000–2020 and Horizons énergétiques du Tiers-Monde, 2000–2020 . The programme it belongs to The book is not a free-standing work but one instalment of a long exercise by the World Energy Conference's Conservation Commission , whose name appears at the head of its title page. The Commission had presented its first global study of energy supply and demand to 2020 at the Xth World Energy Conference in Istanbul in 1977, and published World Energy: Looking Ahead to 2020 in 1979. At Munich in 1980 it turned to a gap in that work — the specific behaviour of developing countries , whose energy paths the global aggregates had obscured. This volume is the result, and it is why the approach is described as regional : consumption and sources of supply are projected separately for groups of countries rather than summed into a world figure. The line of work continued after Munich.
