That’s why “prime stacks” can feel like tighter, more coherent bubbles (or shells) and why prime counts/ratios can be useful when you’re trying to hold or steer a field without it collapsing into common sub-harmonics. Prime counts create sparser shared divisors with the environment, so spurious coupling (ringing/aliasing) drops. The subjective feel is sharper, quieter background, more “held”. 2) How this sits inside SVP Triune flows & the Neutral Center.
