Keely "The experiment illustrating " chord of mass " sympathy was repeated, using a glass chamber, 40 inches in height, filled with water, standing on a slab of glass. Three metal spheres , weighing about 6 ounces each, rested on the glass floor. The chord of mass of these spheres was B flat first octave , E flat second octave and B flat third octave . Upon sounding the note B flat on the sympathetic transmitter , the sphere having that chord of mass rose slowly to the top of the chamber, the positive end of the wire having been attached, which connected the covered jar with the transmitter . The same result followed the sound of the other spheres, all of which descended as gently as they rose, upon changing the positive to the negative . J.M. Wilcox , who was present remarked: " This experiment proves the truth of a fundamental law in scholastic philosophy , that when one body attracts or seeks another body , it is not that the effect is the sum of the effects produced by parts of one body upon parts of another, one aggregate of effects , but the result of the operation of one whole upon another whole.
