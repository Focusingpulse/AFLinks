Failure is only the opportunity to more intelligently begin again.” [ HENRY FORD ] Huginus a Barma "But if destiny calls you, and you are a diligent imitator of nature, all things will succeed according to your desire. Therefore, follow that teacher; imitate her; carefully investigate the causes, material, motion, and goals of the work. And whatever happily succeeds for you, direct it all to the glory of the triune essence and for the benefit of your neighbor."
