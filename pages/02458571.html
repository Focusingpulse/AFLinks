Expanding Parapsychology Research: Learnings from a Beneficent Funder HIGHLIGHTS Funding in frontier science is sparse, but some sponsors clearly invest in certain lines of research as the most promising for future discoveries or advancements. This knowledge can help to guide new and established investigators alike.
