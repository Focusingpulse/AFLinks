Overview Published in the Indian Journal of Science and Technology in August 2010, this paper is Mohammad Shafiq Khan's summary statement of the framework he calls the Energy Theory of Matter and Cosmology. It advances a single technical claim and builds a great deal on it: that the Michelson–Morley experiment was misread, that its null result arises from a Doppler compensation between the outward and return legs of the longitudinal arm, and that the experiment therefore confirms the existence of a light-carrying medium rather than refuting it. Khan identifies that medium with what modern cosmology calls dark energy. From that premise he argues that special relativity, general relativity and the Big Bang model all rest on a misinterpretation, and that a single absolute reference frame — the rest frame of the medium — must be restored. The second half of the paper extends the framework to the origin of matter, which is held to emerge from a primordial energy through a scalar field, and concludes that the initiation of that field requires a living creator. The paper is explicit that this theological conclusion is its purpose: the theory is presented as "the theistic theory of universe and a scientific proof of existence of the living universal creator."
