Here's some URLs to follow up on... http://evworld.com/reports2/lynxmotion.html http://www.katech.com/ http://www.mttc.org/techtfr/ttss.htm While it doesn't lend it credence, the USPTO granted patent number 5744896 on April the 28th of last year. If these things do, in fact, work as advertised and they don't consume stupid amounts of power, I could see someone coming up with an over-unity power source and drive each of the wheels of the vehicle with one of these motors...
