There is an energy flow directly proportional to ExB. This energy is not provided by the wires but comes from the surrounding space around the capacitor. (ref: "The Feynman Lectures on Physics : Electromagnetism vol2, Chap: 27-5, fig 27-3" by Addison-Wesley Publishing company.) During the charging process, in a flat capacitor with the wires connected in the middle of the armatures, the energy flow (S-Flow) is symmetrical.
