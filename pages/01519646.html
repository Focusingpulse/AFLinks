Developments in Quantum Physics Book Author Volodymyr V Krasnoholovets Language English Subject Quantum physics, foundations Published 2004 Publisher Nova Science Publishers Pages 280 ISBN 1594540039 Developments in Quantum Physics is a 2004 edited collection published by Nova Science Publishers, edited by Volodymyr V Krasnoholovets and Frank Columbus. Overview The volume gathers research contributions on foundational and applied problems in quantum physics. It is associated with Krasnoholovets's program of submicroscopic quantum mechanics, in which real physical space is modeled as a tessellation of primordial cells — elementary building blocks of nature — and quantum behaviour is derived in real space rather than in an abstract configuration space. Contributed chapters include work on the conceptual difficulties of quantum mechanics and related topics. About the author Volodymyr V. Krasnoholovets is a Ukrainian physicist known for developing a submicroscopic, deterministic approach to quantum mechanics and gravity based on a cellular structure of space. He has edited several Nova Science volumes on quantum physics, including Trends in Quantum Physics , New Research in Quantum Physics , and New Topics in Quantum Physics Research .
