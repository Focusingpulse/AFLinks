82a — GA 262. Correspondence with Marie Steiner 1901–1925

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 15 May 1911 · archived # 2,437,832
, the Scholl-Mitteilungen announced a lecture cycle by Rudolf Steiner in Helsinki: “The spiritual beings in the heavenly bodies and natural kingdoms”, which could not take place. It was rescheduled for April 1912. As a result of my constantly rushed life and the incessant traveling, I have now become seriously ill and have to take a long break from work. Since I have to stay in my room here, Dr. Steiner finds it necessary for me to spend the spring in the south. Now, at the beginning of April, he will be representing Theosophy at the Philosophical Congress in Bologna. A journey from there back
