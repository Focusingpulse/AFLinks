, Berlin Translated by E. H. Goddard and Dorothy S. Osmond 6. The Son of God and the Son of Man. The Sacrifice of Orpheus [ 1 ] The verses in St. Mark's Gospel which we were endeavouring to elucidate in the last lecture are followed by remarkable words in many ways similar to those found in the other Gospels, although their full significance can best be studied in that of St. Mark. The words are to the effect that after the Baptism and the experiences in the ‘wilderness’, Christ Jesus went into the synagogue and taught the people there.
