Chapter 6. Concerning the Astral Body and the Luciferic Beings; and the Nature of the Etheric Body - The Threshold of the Spiritual World (1922)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 2,396,214
There is another group of spiritual beings, who from the world of the spirit are seen to be active in the physical world (and also in the elemental world), as in an adopted field of action. These are the spirits who desire to liberate the feeling soul entirely from the physical world, and therefore in a certain way to spiritualise it. Life in the physical world is part of the cosmic order of things.
