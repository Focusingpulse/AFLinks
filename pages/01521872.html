Overview Beckmann argues that widespread public fear of low-level ionizing radiation — the "radiation bogey" — is greatly exaggerated relative to the actual risks, and that this fear has been used to obstruct nuclear power. Making "apples-to-apples" comparisons of risks, he contends that the hazards of nuclear energy are small compared with those of the alternatives, echoing the argument of his better-known The Health Hazards of Not Going Nuclear (1976). About the author Petr Beckmann (1924–1993) was a Czech-American professor of electrical engineering at the University of Colorado, a libertaria
