Biography Roth is an independent investigator active in the alternative-energy and aether-theory community during the 1990s. He presented his experimental work to the Institute for New Energy at its First Aetheric Conference, held on July 19–20, 1997, and his results were subsequently reported in New Energy News (vol. 5, no. 4, August 1997). Work Roth's best-known experiment used a suspended magnetic torsion beam, essentially a bar magnet hung from a string and balanced at its center.
