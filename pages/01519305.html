An Aether Model of Electricity : The Missing Secret of Magnetism Watch this video on YouTube Talk Type Video Speaker Lori Anne Gardi Date 1 September 2020 Platform YouTube Recording Recording An Aether Model of Electricity : The Missing Secret of Magnetism is a video by Lori Anne Gardi , published on 1 September 2020 on her FractalWoman YouTube channel. Summary Gardi's most-viewed video, and the one in which the fluid analogy of her earlier lectures becomes a worked model. The subject is Ionel Dinu 's paper Fundamentals of a Theory of Ether , uploaded to ResearchGate on 26 July 2020, on which she is named as a co-author for having implemented the model in software. The abstract states three things: that a new chemical element must be acknowledged; that the electric current is to be viewed as a diffusion wave in a wire; and that the production of a magnetic field by a current is explained in terms of the mechanics of surface waves . It is the third that occupies the video. What a surface wave is Because everything rests on it, Gardi spends the first third explaining surface waves from her own footage of Lake Huron, where she spends her summers. A surface wave occurs at the boundary between two different substances — classically air and water, though it could be water and sand, one substance being lighter and less inertial and the other denser. When wind blows across the surface it sets the water molecules circulating : each moves in a small circle, the circles shrinking with depth, and the wave propagates while the molecules themselves stay, on average, where they were. She illustrates with a stick floating in the water, which simply rises and falls as the waves pass, and with foam on the surface moving in circles rather than travelling shoreward. She then reviews the wave types, since the distinction matters later.
