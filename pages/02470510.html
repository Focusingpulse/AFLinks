New York, Feb. 15. — Nikola Tesla is reported as announcing that his experiments with wireless telegraphy have proved a great success. He is represented as being satisfied that he will be able to telegraph to any city of the world within eight months. He also says, it is stated, that he hopes to attain a speed of 1,500 to 2,000 words an hour, while the instrument at either terminal will be absolutely free from extraneous disturbances, and that two instruments can work independent of each other almost within touch.
