Totally Awesome Manifestation!

· archived # 1,507,334
OMG! I have been talking about doing this for almost two years! It is finally happening! I'm Moving to California! SATURDAY! So, it has been the most awesome and out of this world experience! When the Universe decides to move you, it MOVES YOU! At first, I thought my whole world was falling apart, and things could not get worse! Funny how the ego will tell you that when the most awesome manifestation in your life is taking place! Actually what was happening was every door imaginable up here in Walla Walla was being slammed shut and locked. Then out of the blue the doors to California (home) were being opened up wide with welcome signs! I am soooo excited! Now if someone could just pull me off the ceiling, so I can sleep! Actually you may need to pull me back down to earth, as I might be floating away! OMG! Okay, I am totally excited, but I also have to admit the ego mind is trying really hard to scare me... "What if this? What if that?" and so on and so forth! I am just trying to stay in Gratitude. Then in the middle of moving my teenage daughter to a new school she asked me if I would buy her a yearbook. I had to tell her NO! I felt so bad, but the Universe was not letting me do it for her, and yet I so wanted her to have her freshman year yearbook, and have all her friends sign it! Today, when I picked her up from school, she got in the car and said "Mom, you won't believe this... My friends all went together and bought me a yearbook, and they passed it around and got it signed by everyone too!" It brought tears to my eyes as I realized how special she felt and how loved by her friends she is. My buying the book would have taken that from her. The whole thing just brought tears to my eyes! Oh Oh Oh! The funny thing in this whole move is... Well if you think back two years, and how my X stopped me from moving and was the one who did not want me to move... This is cool! Now the Universe is having him facilitate my move, and even supply me with housing down there! OM
