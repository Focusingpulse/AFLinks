Here, we provide a large-scale analysis of the gut microbiome of 40 honey bee queens, during their developmental trajectory and through the multiple 41 colonies that host them as part of modern queen-rearing practices. We found that 42 queen microbiomes underwent a dramatic shift in size and composition as they aged 43 and encountered different worker populations and colony environments.
