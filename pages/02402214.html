SPAF-2026-main010 CDU: 547.759.6 2-Acylethynylpyrroles as a versatile building blocks for highly functionalized pyrrolo[1,2- c]oxazoles Sophia A. Stepanova1 , Denis N. Tomilin1 , Boris A. Trofimov1 1 Irkutsk Institute of Chemistry named after A.E. Favorsky, Siberian Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences, Irkutsk, Russia stepanova_sofia@irioch.irk.ru One of the most prominent topics of modern organic synthesis conserns the design of fused heterocyclic systems, especially those bearing biologically active groups or reactive structural subunits. Over the last decade, 2- acylethynylpyrroles have emerged as versatile building blocks for assembling complex molecular structures. This became possible due to the development of palladium-free cross-coupling of pyrroles and haloacetylenes in a solid medium[1]. Oxazoles possess a broad spectrum of properties, proven useful over the years. Fusion of oxazole and pyrrole scaffolds imparts to this core outstanding additional features, including anti-tromboembolic, anti-oxidant, anti￾bacterial and anti-fungal. Despite potential value, synthetic routes to their bi-congeners, namely bipyrrolo[1,2- c]oxazoles, are limited.
