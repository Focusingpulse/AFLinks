ISARD-2025-climate012 Current status of actinometric observations on the meteorological network of Roshydromet Anna E. Erokhina1 , Alexander N. Makhotkin1 1 "Main Geophysical Observatory named after A.I.Voeikov", Russia aktinom@mail.ru Actinometric observations carried out on the meteorological network are aimed at studying the radiation regime, which largely determines the climate of the territories of the Russian Federation. Network surface actinometric observations include determining a set of interrelated characteristics of solar radiation and thermal radiation of natural terrestrial objects reaching the earth's surface, as well as a number of atmospheric parameters. "Main Geophysical Observatory named after A.I.Voeikov" is the scientific and methodological center of Roshydromet for organizing actinometric observations. The main observation programs on the actinometric network are: - urgent actinometric observations (discrete observations at 6 actinometric times according to mean solar time); - registration of the radiation balance and its components - continuous observations to determine hourly, daily and monthly amounts of radiation balance components or individual types of shortwave radiation. Also, daily amounts of total radiation are determined by integration.
