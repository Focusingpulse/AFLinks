Keely "There is no missing link in the evolution theory, as laid down in Keely 's pure philosophy . Inasmuch as the Father of all is Himself a Spiritual Being , cosmical law leads us to expect that the type of created being, His offspring, shall be spirit also. Nor can Being in any object be so attenuated, or so far removed from Him who filleth all in all, but it must surely retain an aura of His spiritual nature. The corner-stone of this philosophy is one power, one law; order and method reigning throughout creation ; spirit controlling matter , as the Divine order and law of creation that the spiritual should govern the material - that the whole realm of matter should be under the dominion of the world of spirit . Nor is this a new truth. According to Diogenes Laertius , Thales taught that souls are the motive forces of the universe."
