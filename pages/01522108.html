Title Unity Between Gravitational And Electromagnetic Forces: A Concept Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Duncan W Shaw Keywords electromagnetic, gravitational, forces, Electricity, Magnetism Published 2012 Journal Proceedings of the NPA Volume 9 No. of pages 3 Pages 564-566 Abstract This paper ties together gravity, electricity and magnetism. It proposes that aether is the common denominator of those phenomena, with the bulk flow of aether being the cause of gravity and the interaction of cells of aether being the source of electricity and magnetism. The paper sets out implications arising from the unity proposal. Overview Duncan W. Shaw presented this three-page paper at the 19th Natural Philosophy Alliance conference in July 2012, published in the Proceedings of the NPA . It draws together two of his earlier papers — The Cause of Gravity: A Concept ( Physics Essays 25, 2012) and Electricity and Magnetism: A Return to Aether ( Proceedings of the NPA 8, 2011) — under a single proposition: that aether is the common substance behind gravity, electricity and magnetism, with bulk flow of aether producing gravity and interaction of aether cells producing electromagnetism. The governing commitment is mechanical. Shaw holds that these forces "are the result of a physical process of direct contact, as distinct from what is known as 'action-at-a-distance'", and explicitly rejects "any notion of attraction or repulsion that is not the result of actual physical contact". The gravity mechanism is a flow. Aether streams from space into cosmic bodies and is simultaneously expelled from them, both streams exerting ram pressure — which Shaw carefully defines as "that portion of dynamic pressure that actually pushes a material object" — on matter in their paths. The inward ram pressure exceeds the outward, and the difference is gravity.
