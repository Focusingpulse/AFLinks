Artemis0369@outlook.com 2. gzchengzhi@hotmail.com Abstract We present a unified quantum field theory (QFT) framework that reconceptualizes vitamins as Wess-Zumino compensation fields canceling gauge anomalies in metabolic networks, and thyroid hormones as Higgs-type scalar fields that set the coupling constants of the entire metabolic gauge theory. Within this framework, hyperthyroidism is rigorously defined as a vacuum instability in the thyroid Higgs sector, characterized by an abnormally elevated vacuum expectation value (VEV) that drives systemic gauge over-coupling. We distinguish two topologically distinct hyperthyroid pathologies: (1) transient topological charge release (Hashimoto's thyroiditis, subacute thyroiditis), where vortex-antivortex annihilation in thyroid follicles produces pulsatile T3/T4 bursts, mathematically described by a delta-function source term 𝐽vortex ∝ 𝛿(𝑡 − 𝑡rupture); and (2) sustained external driving (Graves' disease), where TSH receptor antibodies act as a constant classical source 𝐽TRAb that pins the Higgs VEV at a pathological value. From the Euler-Lagrange equations of the theory, we derive therapeutic strategies: blockade of anomalous source terms, stabilization of the over-coupled gauge sector, and quantitative replenishment of vitamin compensation fields depleted by the hypermetabolic state. The model predicts that vitamin depletion follows a power-law dependence on thyroid hormone excess, that tissue-specific T3 Yukawa couplings represent unexploited drug targets, and that ultimate cure requires topological immune repair—restoring gauge symmetry at the level of TSHR antigen presentation rather than destroying the target organ. This framework provides a rigorous mathematical foundation for understanding metabolic regulation and identifies several systematically neglected therapeutic directions in current hyperthyroidism management.
