The right-hand rule of Electricity says if current is traveling in the direction of your thumb then a magnetic flux is traveling in the direction of your curled fingers. So if the wheel goes clockwise, to generate thrust in a clockwise direction one must drop your current. (I think the force/torque formula for motors is the same also.) This current wants to go to ground: the only path to ground is through the center handle (core) which has been offset, (timing) which allows for the chain and collector bar to charge fully before discharging through the spark gap (hook and handle) when they get close. Filling the core with salt water, the most abundant resource on earth, you have a frictionless connection to the magnet's neutrals from the core allowing you to keep them charged as some of the energy on it's way to ground touches these points.
