GC2026-PERM034 The simplest model of the geodynamo Sergey Starchenko1 1 Pushkov Institute of Terrestrial Magnetism, Ionosphere and Radiowave Propagation of the Russian Academy of Sciences sstarchenko@mail.ru We integrate the scalar product of the induction equation and the magnetic field vector over the entire dynamo volume. We obtain an evolution equation for the magnetic energy supported by the integral power of the Lorentz magnetic force and counteracted by the integral power of magnetic diffusion. Approximating the corresponding integrals through the mean square magnetic field and a given Lorentz force power, we obtain a linear and inhomogeneous ordinary differential equation for the square of the field. For a given power constant over time and any initial condition (except zero), the solution stabilizes at a value dependent on the dynamo parameters. Moreover, the smaller the initial field, the larger the time derivative of the field, up to explosive growth. For the geodynamo, the aforementioned parameters are approximately determined from observations, resulting in a total magnetic energy of 30 ZJ and a field of 100 G, which are consistent with existing studies. If we specify the power as the sum of a constant and a harmonic function of time, then we can roughly model the observed dynamics of the geomagnetic field.
