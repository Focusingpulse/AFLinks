Red and Blue (courtesy University of Science and Philosophy ) ( click to enlarge ) The blue side or upper half of the light spectrum . These frequencies pertain to chemical activity or chemism . Blue light stimulates chemical activity which is why blue light is not used in photo processing dark rooms. The Ultraviolet frequencies, at the extreme upper end of the blue frequencies, accelerate chemical dissociation and are used (among many other uses) to dissolve plastics in photo plate making.
