Washington, July 15. — With Thomas A. Edison's acceptance of the chairmanship, assuring the organization of the Advisory Committee on Naval Inventions projected by Secretary Daniels, interest now centers on the identity of its other members. Mr. Daniels declined to talk of any but the general aspects of his plan, yet the group from among which the councillors will be chosen took pretty definite form yesterday. With the departure of Secretary Daniels to-day for West Orange, N. J., to confer with Thomas A. Edison in regard to the proposed navy bureau of invention and the civilian advisory board, naval officials looked forward to an announcement to follow immediately after the conference. The secretary will seek the views of Mr. Edison about some of the men to be invited to join the advisory board. Pending the conference, however, Mr. Daniels will not make known the names of other possible board members.
