Decker: "Question on time distortion" Next in thread: Wm Perry: "Re: Question on time distortion" Hey Jerry, Ron and all. I think what you mean is time dilation. It's a phenomenon where time in a fast moving object (e.g. a spacecraft) or a strong gravitational force (like a black hole) goes slower than somewhere else. It can only be measured at high speeds, close to the speed of light or near the event horizon of a black hole. Time is 'stretched out' and when the clock (inside the capsule) reaches the speed of light or the inner border of the event horizon it even stops. When the clock is compared to one on earth, time has run more slowly in space. I don't recall any spaceprograms where such research has been done. Cheers, Tim Jerry W.
