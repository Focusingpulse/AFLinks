TESLA, Edison, Westinghouse, Thompson, the giants of the electrical world, beside scores of others, drawn from the ranks of those who have contributed most freely to modern electrical science, are all working earnestly and harmoniously together to make the coming show of electrical appliances, to be held in New York next month, the most extraordinary scientific exhibition on record. Success will undoubtedly crown their efforts, and the fact that this will largely be due to the circumstance that there are now more wonderful things to show than ever before should not detract one whit from the praise to be awarded for the thoroughness of plan and detail and the keen appreciation of the effective that have been evinced in every step taken since the enterprise was first seriously considered. MASTERS OF THE MYSTIC CURRENT. In the matter of expense the coming show will greatly exceed all its predecessors. It will cost $150,000 to install the various machines and apparatus exhibited, and the total value of the exhibits will not be far from a half million — rather over than under. The total cost of the show, then, will be nearly $700,000, including the rent of the building. Officially, the show will be known as the National Electrical exposition.
