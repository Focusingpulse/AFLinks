The Early Sessions, Book 1 This book is the start of it all. The beginning describes the initial contact with Seth through the Ouija board. In this book Seth gives an outline of the inner senses and discusses our camouflage reality. Also includes psychological time and the 5th dimension. And the whole self which is composed of our outer self (conscious self and ego), inner self and the subconscious which is a buffer between them.
