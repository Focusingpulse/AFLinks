Watson with 450 illustrations by the author For those who would like to have the benefit of a woodworker’s personal experience with hand tools, this is the book to own. Crammed with practical information, it is the next best thing to looking over a craftsman’s shoulder as he works with his tools, asking questions and getting straight answers in plain language, seeing the tools in action, and hearing a running commentary about how each one is held and manipulated to get the best work out of it. Aldren Watson works exclusively with hand tools. Drawing on his long experience, he describes in detail the ways and workings of the tools basic to good woodworking from the bit brace, chisel, and mallet, to saws, specialized planes, drawknife, and spokeshave — pointing out along the way their capabilities and limitations. All the procedures are explicitly illus- trated with clear line drawings and diagrams — more than 450 in There is a practical chapter describing how to sharpen the tools with simple equipment, as well as an appendix that gives plans and dimensions all. making a workbench and other necessary pieces of shop equipment such as a bench hook, miter block, sawhorse, and shooting board. And for the woodworker whose available space is cramped, there are for plans for building a foldaway bench into a closet. book makes the skills of woodworking with hand tools less mysterious, less complicated, and far more attainable.
