"Subliminals" are images or sounds which occur very rapidly and are not perceptible by the conscious mind. However, the subconscious does see the subliminal and can react to it in various ways. In movies, they are intended to help establish a mood, usually of suspense or horror. Modern viewers have the distinct advantage of VCR's which allows them to slow done a movie and examine it frame by frame to see what is hidden there. Normal movie film shows 24 frames per second, anything less is perceived as having a flicker. Therefore, a single frame would take 1/24 of a second to flash on the screen. Videotape contains 30 frames per second. When movies are converted to video, every fourth frame of the movie is repeated which will generate the additional 6 frames to make 30 frames per second. -------------------------------------------------------------------- Spellbound, by Alfred Hitchcock is a black and white movie starring Ingrid Bergman and Leo G.
