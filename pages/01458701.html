Diminished Octave

· archived # 1,458,701
A musical ratio of 48:25. Same as Diminished Eighth . In classical music from Western culture, a diminished octave is an interval produced by narrowing a perfect octave by a chromatic semitone . As such, the two notes are denoted by the same letter but have different accidentals. For instance, the interval from C4 to C5 is a perfect octave , twelve semitones wide, and both the intervals from C4 to C5, and from C4 to C5 are diminished octaves , spanning eleven semitones . Being diminished , it is considered a dissonant interval . The diminished octave is enharmonically equivalent to the major seventh .
