His achievements in the field of invention, especially in the utilization of what is known as the alternating electric current, have brought him both fame and fortune. Mr. Tesla has devoted much of his time and energy to the solution of the problem of the economical long-distance transmission of power. It is by means of his theories, practically applied some ten years ago, that it has been made possible to transmit a matter of from 10,000 to 20,000 horse power from Niagara Falls as far as Buffalo, twenty-five miles distant. Granting all that has been achieved in connection with the commercial utilization of the energy of the Falls of Niagara, it is in no quarter claimed that the promises which seemed so roseate in the early nineties have been fulfilled. Not that enormous and profitable enterprises have not been built up in the immediate vicinity of the cataract; but the truth remains that long-distance economical transmission of energy in the form of electricity remains to be accomplished. Capital has been invested lavishly in power-producing plants, and more is being invested.
