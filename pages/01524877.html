WILSON, HERBERT W., Elm House, Todmorden. MEETING OF COUNCIL. At a Meeting of the Council held on the 23rd of April, the President in the chair, the following Members were present :-Professor W. F. Barrett, and Messrs. Walter H. Coffin, Edmund Gurney, F. W. H. Myers, Frank Podmore, H. Arthur Smith, and J. Herbert Stack. The Minutes of the previous Meeting were read and signed as correct. Four new Members and seven new Associates, whose names and addresses are given above, were elected. It was agreed that the names of thirteen Members and Associates whose subscriptions had remained for some time unpaid, and whose present addresses could not be ascertained, should be struck off the List.
