Geert Boersma Residence Zwolle, Netherlands Nationality Dutch Known for A compressible, irrotational, massless ether; gravity as a variation in the speed of light ; criticism of the twin paradox and of the relativistic reading of the Sagnac effect Scientific career Fields Engineering ; relativity and ether theory Geert Boersma is a Dutch engineer and independent researcher who has published three papers arguing that the theory of relativity is internally inconsistent and should be replaced by an ether theory of a particular kind: one in which the ether is compressible, irrotational and massless , and in which gravity is not a curvature of spacetime but a consequence of the speed of light varying from place to place. His work appeared in Galilean Electrodynamics in 2004 and 2006 and in Physics Essays in 2014. He is recorded in Jean de Climont's Worldwide List of Dissident Scientists under the heading of alternative theory, with his position summarised as an "energy ether" and his criticism directed at the relativist interpretation of the Sagnac effect. The argument Boersma's case is built in two stages across the first two papers, and the order matters: he clears the ground before proposing anything. Against relativity Relativity, Space and Time (2004) opens with the twin paradox stated as a flat contradiction rather than a puzzle: two twins who separate and re-meet will each, on the theory's own reckoning, be younger than the other. He then turns to the usual defence, that whatever its logical awkwardness relativity is carried by the experimental record, and argues that the evidence has been misattributed . What the experiments establish, on his reading, is E = mc ² — which he does not dispute — and not the relativity of space and time. The Sagnac effect he treats as the decisive case, and as evidence pointing the other way.
