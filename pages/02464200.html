An Explanation of Low-energy Nuclear Reactions (Cold Fusion) by Edmund Storms

· archived # 2,464,200
After delivering the John Chappell Lecture “ What is Cold Fusion and Why Should You Care ” at the 19th Natural Philosophy Alliance Conference in Albuquerque, New Mexico July 2012, Dr. Edmund Storms spoke with Cold Fusion Now’s Ruby Carat in a private interview about his new model for initiating the cold fusion reaction, also called low-energy nuclear reactions (LENR), lattice-assisted nuclear reactions (LANR), and quantum fusion . Dr. Storms is a former Los Alamos National Lab scientist and 23-year veteran of LENR research. Now with Kiva Labs in Santa Fe, New Mexico, he has developed the idea of a Nuclear Active Environment (NAE) to include a model of how the cold fusion reaction might begin. The interview focuses on his idea as outlined in the paper An Explanation of Low-energy Nuclear Reactions (Cold Fusion) [ .pdf ] published by the Journal of Condensed Matter Nuclear Science #9 2012 [ visit ].
