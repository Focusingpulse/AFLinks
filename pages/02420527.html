MAY 1994 A. THE LIBRARY OF SCIENCE "SELLS BOOKS" By Hal Fox In a capitalistic society, I have no quarrel with a company that has established itself as a bookseller. Somehow, over the years of being a member of the "Library of Science", I have been under the delusion that somehow my "sciencebooks" were representing a true picture of science. I was a bit miffed that a book that is hopelessly outdated on the topic of cold fusion would get a listing in the library of science selection.
