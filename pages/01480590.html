harmonic scale

· archived # 1,480,590
The Harmonic scale is a "super-just" musical scale allowing extended just intonation , beyond 5-limit to the 19th harmonic , and free modulation through the use of synthesizers. Transpositions and tuning tables are controlled by the left hand on the appropriate note on a one-octave keyboard.
