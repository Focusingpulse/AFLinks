GC2026-U002 Prediction of novel compounds in planetary interiors and atmospheres Artem R. Oganov1 1 Skolkovo Institute of Science and Technology Ponencia invitada a.kisil@skoltech.ru Artem R. Oganov Skolkovo Institute of Science and Technology, Bolshoy Blvd, 30, bldg. 1, 121205 Moscow, Russia, e-mail: a.oganov@skoltech.ru Prediction of novel compounds in planetary interiors and atmospheres. Crystal structure prediction, which became a popular tool after the development of the USPEX program (https://uspex-team.org) in 2005, has significantly influenced our understanding of Earth's chemistry and that of other planets. Recently, this method has been extended to predict compositions and structures of molecules. This lecture will cover several research areas: - Prediction and proof of the existence of counterintuitive compounds stabilized by pressure, such as Na₃Cl, NaCl₇, and currently known high-temperature superconductors (H₃S, YH₆, CaH₆, ThH₁₀, LaH₁₀). Some unexpected new compounds that may be relevant to planetary interiors will be mentioned. - A recently proposed hypothesis about the origin of water on Earth, with a key role played by the now-extinct mineral Mg₂SiO₅H₂. - Theoretical evidence for diamond formation in the interiors of Neptune and Uranus. This occurs due to the decomposition of methane (CH₄) under high pressures and temperatures and is crucial for heat generation in Neptune's interior. - Prediction of probable molecules in systems such as C-H and C-H-N, and discussion of their significance for astrochemistry and the origin of life.
