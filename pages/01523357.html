Ludwik Kostro Born ( 1934-11-15 ) November 15, 1934 Gdańsk, Poland Died July 29, 2023 (2023-07-29) (aged 88) Nationality Polish Known for Documenting Einstein's reintroduction of a relativistic ether after 1916 Awards Palmes Académiques (1985); Gold Cross of Merit (2001) Scientific career Fields History and philosophy of physics; relativity and the ether Institutions University of Gdańsk Ludwik Kostro (15 November 1934 – 29 July 2023) was a Polish physicist and philosopher of science at the University of Gdańsk , known for a single sustained piece of historical work: the demonstration, from Einstein's own writings, that Einstein reintroduced an ether after 1916 having rejected the nineteenth-century one in 1905. He pursued the subject for some thirty-five years, from papers in the late 1980s through his habilitation thesis to the book Einstein and the Ether (Apeiron, 2000). Education Physics and philosophy — the "Sapienza" University of Rome and the Pontifical Gregorian University, 1963–1970 Ph.D. — Pontifical Gregorian University, Rome, 1970 Habilitation — Adam Mickiewicz University, Poznań, 1995 , on Albert Einstein's concept of the relativistic ether Title of Professor (humanities) — conferred 2005 The habilitation is worth noting: the thesis that became Einstein and the Ether was examined and accepted as a formal qualification in the Polish academic system, not merely published. Career Kostro joined the University of Gdańsk in 1975, teaching first as a Lecturer and then as an Assistant Professor in the Physics Institute. From 1994 he was a Full Professor in the Institute of Philosophy and Sociology, which he directed, and later headed the Department of Logic, Methodology and Philosophy of Science. He also taught at the Ateneum School of Higher Education and at the Stanisław Moniuszko Academy of Music, both in Gdańsk.
