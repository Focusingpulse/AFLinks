Parambrahma

· archived # 1,461,202
Spirit or God " Parambrahma ( Spirit or God ) is everlasting, complete, without beginning or end. It is one, indivisible Being." ( Swami Sri Yukteswar , The Holy Science , Sutra 1) "The Eternal Father, God , Swami Parambrahma , is the only Real Substance , Sat , and is all iin all in the universe." ( Swami Sri Yukteswar , The Holy Science , Sutra 1) "In it (Parambrahma) is the origin of all knowledge and love , the root of all power and joy." ( Swami Sri Yukteswar , The Holy Science , Sutra 2) "The Almighty Force, Sakti , or in other words the Eternal Joy, Ananda , which produced the world; and the Omniscient feeling, Chit , which makes this world conscious , demonstrate the Nature, Prakriti , of God the Father."
