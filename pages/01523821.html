Biography Rowshandel resides in Fairfax, Virginia, in the United States. Work Rowshandel is associated with the Electric Universe model, an alternative cosmological framework which holds that electromagnetic forces play a central role in shaping the structure and behavior of the universe, alongside or in place of gravitation.
