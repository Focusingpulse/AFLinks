About Tao Lin (林韜) (born July 2, 1983) is a critically acclaimed Taiwanese-American novelist, poet, essayist, short-story writer, and artist who also has a keen interest in dissident science. He has published four novels, a novella, two books of poetry, a collection of short stories, and a memoir, as well as an extensive assortment of online content.
