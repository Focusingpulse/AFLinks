Kulik September 2026 What this is, and what changed This is not a theorem paper. It proves nothing and is separable from every result it draws on. Its purpose is the move the programme has always required: state the thing forward, run it backward, and look at where the two readings cancel. The first version of this piece concluded that a reader has two coordinates — resolution, which decides what can be told apart, and relation, which decides what counts as having happened twice. That was too weak, and the correction is the substance of this version. There are not two coordinates. There is one operation — declare some differences inessential, keep what survives — applied at successive orders of structure. Resolution is that operation acting on states and transitions. Relation is the same operation acting on routes. What looked like a second axis is the next application of the first. That correction is not adopted on its merits as prose. It makes a prediction, the prediction is testable, and it was tested: if the operation recurs, it must recur again at the order above, and each order must keep its own remainder. Section 5 reports that computation. It does recur, and the remainders disagree case by case — which is the strongest available evidence that this is a tower and not a metaphor for two things. Three computations anchor the piece and are marked where they occur. Everything else is interpretation and labelled as such.
