The idea first occurred to me when I was watching EFTV, part 7 I believe. John Bedini was explaining how he believes his batteries are charged. He said that the high voltage spike from the coil 'sucked' ions back so they were actually recharging the battery. This was due to charge. So what if I was to make a simple battery with a voltalic cell immersed in lead acid and two electrodes. On one of the electrodes would be an electret to attract the ions back to where they were. For those having troubles visualising how this would work think of what happens when you rub a balloon on your chest when your wearing say a woolen jumper. The balloon is charged and can attract objects like pieces of paper. Another way of thinking of this is when you charge an object (we'll just say a perspex rod this time) and hold it to a stream of running water. The stream of water bends around your charged object.
