TECHNICAL REPORT NO.24 Erection of Bamboo Scaffolds Erection of Bamboo Scaffolds Bamboo scaffolds have been widely used in construction applications in South East Asia, in particular, Hong Kong for many years. Because of their high adaptability and low construction cost, bamboo scaffolds can be constructed in different shapes to follow any irregular architectural features of a building within a comparatively short period of time. In general, bamboo scaffolds are mainly used to provide access of workers to different exposed locations to facilitate various construction and maintenance process.
