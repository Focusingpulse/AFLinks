Host range analysis of Rousettus Bat Coronavirus HKU-9 John F. Signus ABSTRACT The representative member of the genus Nobecovirus, Bat Coronavirus HKU-9, was one of the least known memebers of the genus Betacoronaviruses. Here we performed a detailed analysis of the known and potential host ranges of HKU-9.
