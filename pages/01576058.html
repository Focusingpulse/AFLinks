The Structure of the Higgs Field Paul Lillington 1 The Structure of the Higgs Field Foundations of Higgs Field Bubble Theory (HFBT) Paul Lillington Independent Researcher Email: tflux2@tpg.com.au Website: http://hfbt.com.au HFBT Foundation Paper No 1 July 2026 Abstract The Higgs Field Bubble Theory (HFBT) begins from a single physical hypothesis: the Higgs field is not merely a quantum field associated with particle masses, but the fundamental physical medium from which all matter, energy, space, and time emerge. This paper defines the proposed physical foundations of that medium independently of any specific particle model. HFBT describes the medium through two complementary effective variables: a density field, proposed to govern mass and gravitation, and a phase field, proposed to govern charge, magnetism, spin, and quantum behaviour. Stable matter is modelled as resonant bubble-like structures formed within this continuous medium. Rather than presenting detailed derivations of individual physical phenomena, this work defines the underlying physical framework upon which the remainder of the Higgs Field Bubble Theory is built. Later papers develop the gravitational, electromagnetic, quantum, particle, and cosmological consequences of the principles introduced here. 1.
