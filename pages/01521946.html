The paper covers the THETA DEVICE plans and related patents such as the Richardson patent. It is an excellent shortcut for those who do not have easy access to patent copies and is published as a service to save researchers time in digging out the relevant material from the libraries or ordering them from the patent office. Also included is the HYDE electrostatic patent which is being researched by many free energy researchers as the latest in this field and reputed to be getting 20 times more power output than input.
