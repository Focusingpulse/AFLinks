Evolution of the Universe: The Cosmic Game of Consciousness Book Author James C Sung Language English Subject Cosmology, Pixel Lattice Theory Published 1993 Publisher Scientific Publications (Woburn, MA) Pages 416 ISBN 1-884080006 Evolution of the Universe: The Cosmic Game of Consciousness is a 1993 book by Dr. James C Sung , a companion to his Pixels of Space-Time within his Pixel Lattice Theory programme. Overview Sung applies his Pixel Lattice Theory — in which space-time is modeled as a "super-crystal" of Planck-sized pixels, with matter and energy arising as pixel dislocations — to the evolution of the universe and to the place of consciousness within it. The book frames cosmic evolution as a "game of consciousness," integrating relativistic physics, quantum mechanics, and mind. About the author James C. Sung is a materials scientist and inventor (noted for work on synthetic diamond) who developed Pixel Lattice Theory as an independent unifying framework.
