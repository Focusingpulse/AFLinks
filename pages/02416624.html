Einstein in his special theory of relativity. A flagrant contradiction of this principle with the "mass defect" in the calculation of the energy balance of nuclear reactions has been revealed. The extremely low efficiency of hot fusion power plants in comparison with other methods of using the energy of aether-like media is substantiated.
