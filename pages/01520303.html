Gravity is Not Free: Pushing Gravity Beyond the Apple Book Cover of the 2015 edition, showing Woolsthorpe Manor and Newton's apple tree Author Robert "Bob" de Hilster Language English Subject Gravitation ; push gravity ; the Particle Model Published 2015 Publisher Xlibris Publication date 31 August 2015 Media type Paperback, 8.5 × 8.5 in Pages 192 ISBN 978-1503567924 ISBN-10 1503567923 Gravity is Not Free: Pushing Gravity Beyond the Apple is a 2015 book by the American electrical engineer Robert "Bob" de Hilster . It is the first of his three books, and the one in which he sets out the gravitational half of what he and his son David de Hilster later called the Particle Model : the proposal that gravity is not an attraction but the residual push of a flux of real particles, computed by summing individual impacts on bodies of real shape. The title carries the argument. Newton's apple gives the law of gravitation but not its cause, and to go "beyond the apple" is to ask what does the pushing. Free is the operative word: on de Hilster's reading, an attractive force reaching endlessly across empty space, drawing on no source and consuming nothing, is not a physical mechanism but a description dressed as one. A push, by contrast, has a supply and a cost. The argument De Hilster came to gravity through an experiment rather than a theory. In 2006 his son asked him to help build a variation of the Cavendish experiment, proposed by the Argentine physicist Ricardo Carezani , intended to detect gravity as a particle; the work was filmed for the documentary Einstein Wrong - The Miracle Year . What followed was an attempt to calculate what such a particle would have to do. The method is the book's substance and its departure from tradition. Rather than treating bodies as Newtonian point masses, de Hilster computes the gravitational force between them by summing the individual impulses delivered by particles entering, striking and leaving extended bodies of real geometry.
