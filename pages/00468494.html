We show that any such formal system is inconsistent. We establish a collection of nested sets of rational numbers in a descending hierarchy. The sets higher in the descending hierarchy contain element(s) that are not in the sets below them in the hierarchy. Given such a descending set hierarchy, it is easy to develop two arguments that contradict each other. The conclusion of Argument#2 is false. But, Argument#2 is a valid argument.
