GTM2026-main071 A Survey of Shapley-Value-Based Methods in Explainable Artificial Intelligence Jing Liu1 , Chi Zhao1 , Elena Parilina1 1 St Petersburg University, St. Petersburg, Russia st082130@student.spbu.ru The Shapley value, from cooperative game theory, has become a popular tool in explainable artificial intelligence (XAI) for measuring how important each feature is in complex machine learning models. However, its high computational cost, which grows exponentially with the number of features, makes it difficult to use in practice. In this paper, we give a survey of common Shapley-value-based methods and explain their main ideas, advantages, and limitations. We cover classical approaches such as Exact Shapley and SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations), including SamplingSHAP and KernelSHAP, as well as ShapG for graph-based explanations. Based on these methods, we also introduce three methods we have recently developed: an improved version of ShapG with graph construction optimization, a Center of Imputation Set (CIS) method that avoids enumerating all feature combinations, and a leverage-enhanced sampling method based on multilinear extension. Finally, we compare these methods in terms of explanation accuracy and running time, and provide practical suggestions on how to choose the suitable Shapley-based method for different machine learning tasks.
