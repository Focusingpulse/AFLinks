Please contact: International Association for New Science 1304 South College Avenue Fort Collins, Colorado 80524 PROCEEDINGS PREFACE When I heard the first discussions break about the "Cold Fusion" flap among the faculty and graduate students, I was in the lofty Gamow Physics tower of Boulder, Colorado. I held my breath. Anticipation filled the air - what news were we about to hear of a new breakthrough in observable, unexplained, phenomena? Surely there would be great excitement boiling within the scientific community! The answer was pursed on the lips of a revered faculty member. Our enthusiasm was to last at most five seconds before the cynicism and criticism bit the air. All we heard were words of fraud, ruined careers, and examples of bad science. My heart sank, and I immediately stuck my head back in the books in pursuit of my Ph.D. in physics. Any "awe" that could possibly be felt about physics would be limited to wondering how to last through finals, and the only "New Frontier" was the uncertainty of what contracts would be available for the summer.
