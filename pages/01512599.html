Coil 1, original bifilar 168 ohms, input current circa 380 ma Coil 2, trifilar, one trigger feeding two transistors, resistance 89 ohms, input current circa 910 ma coil 3, trifilar, one triger feeding two transistors, resistance 130 ohms, input current circa 750 ma Transisters are all MJL21154 Input current is the supply current when the other two coils are switched out.
