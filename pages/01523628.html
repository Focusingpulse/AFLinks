Paul Schroeder Born ( 1942-09-03 ) September 3, 1942 (age 83) United States Residence Wind Lake, Wisconsin, United States Nationality American Alma mater Beloit College (BA 1964) Known for "External gravitation" — Gravity as a push; paeps; rejection of the Big Bang ; a gravitational account of redshift Scientific career Fields Mathematician , computer programmer, MIS director Influences Georges-Louis Le Sage Paul Schroeder (born 3 September 1942) is an American independent researcher based in Wind Lake, Wisconsin, and the author of The Universe Is Otherwise: External Gravitation (2006). A mathematics graduate who spent his career in computer programming and management information systems, he has argued since the mid-2000s that gravity is a push rather than a pull — that what is described as attraction is really the net imbalance of a pressure arriving from every direction and partially absorbed by matter in its path. He is a member of the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society (formerly the Natural Philosophy Alliance ), presented six talks in its live session series between 2009 and 2014, and has continued to publish: his listing at the General Science Journal runs to 36 papers , the most recent dated July 2026, when he was 83. Education Beloit College , Beloit, Wisconsin — BA 1964, majoring in mathematics and economics Biography Schroeder graduated from Beloit College in the class of 1964. He went on to a career in computer programming and in directing MIS (management information systems) projects, and has said that this background is what shaped his approach to physics: programming, on his account, trains one to notice where a system's logic does not close, and he came to think that the accepted treatments of space-time and gravitation do not close. His interest in the subject began, by his own account and that of his college's alumni magazine, from reading astronomy articles in the physics library rather than from formal training in the field.
