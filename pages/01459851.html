Physical Review Letters (2010) Volume: 106, Issue: 11, Publisher: Hochschule St. Gallen, Pages: 115304 DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevLett.106.115304 PubMed: 21469874 arXiv: 1011.5192 Available from arxiv.org Abstract We report on the expansion of a Fermi-Fermi mixture of Li-6 and K-40 atoms under conditions of strong interactions realized near the center of an interspecies Feshbach resonance . We observe two different phenomena of hydrodynamic behavior. The first one is the well-known inversion of the aspect ratio. The second one is a collective expansion, where both species stick together and despite of their different masses expand jointly.
