Hello to everyone here on the Renewable Energy topic. I just wanted to take the opportunity to introduce Home - EnergeticTube.com*-*Where technology goes LIVE! . I built EnergeticTube.com to try and help provide a place that will reliably host all of the invaluable video content that is created by all of the members here at the energeticforum.com. I think that the work that is done here could be better replicated with a direct link from the video content description to the appropriate thread in the forum. My goal with this site is to promote and highlight the important discussions here on the energeticforum to existing members, while helping bring awareness to the world wide web at large. I hope that the development of this Youtube clone will serve our members well. Please note that a Video upload limit of 381mb per single video has been set as well as 3000 sec time outs, so compression should be good-very good quality in most cases, and most connectivity issues should be resumed automatically within that time limit. (Even Youtube can stall out on large uploads at times, so some patience is always required) Soon to come! I am currently installing flash scrolling video menus, watermark images, enhanced player features, mature content and bad words filtering to help enhance the site though out the next short while so please be patient with me and the site if there are occasional interruptions during this period. Thank you and i hope you enjoy the site. Please feel free to make suggestions hear, or by email to me below, at any time in this thread with regard to video categorizing that would apply to Renewable Energy. Darcy Klyne [email protected]
