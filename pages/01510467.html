I just wanted to take the opportunity to introduce Home - EnergeticTube.com*-*Where technology goes LIVE! . I built EnergeticTube.com to try and help provide a place that will reliably host all of the invaluable video content that is created by all of the members here at the energeticforum.com. I think that the work that is done here could be better replicated with a direct link from the video content description to the appropriate thread in the forum. My goal with this site is to promote and highlight the important discussions here on the energeticforum to existing members, while helping bring awareness to the world wide web at large. I hope that the development of this Youtube clone will serve our members well.
