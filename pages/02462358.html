WILHELM REICH AND ORGONE ENERGY IN ACTION Orgonomy is a body of knowledge which was discovered and founded by Dr. Wilhelm Reich who was a student of Sigmund Freud and psychoanalysis. Freud hypothesized the presence of a psycho-sexual energy that operates in the human organism and called it Libido Energy, but he failed to elaborate this concept as an essential factor in human life. Dr. Reich understood the central importance of the concept of libido as a real biological energy which all life is based on its presence and functioning. He named this energy " Orgone Energy" in relation to the body organs and the function of orgasm. In this conference, the speakers will describe the discovery of orgone energy, its significance in understanding human behavior and its central importance in the health and sickness of the human organism as well as its use in the treatment and prevention of physical and psychological illnesses, its sociological significance and its existence in the atmosphere.
