The first type of experiment used a surface-barrier particle detector positioned a few mm from the foil to provide a very sensitive monitor for possible fusion-generated protons at 3.02 MeV. is estimated to be 10 effect. -24 The detection limit for this arrangement fusions/deuterium/second, assuming a bulk fusion These experiments included cells with 5 and 25 pm thick Pd foils, 10 pm Ti foils, parallel experiments with 0.1 M LiOD (heavy water) in one cell and 2 LiOH (light water) in another, current densities up to 0.5 A/cm , and run times as long as 22 days. No evidence for fusion products was seen. The second type of experiment using these cells, both as an adjunct to the fusion tests and to provide new information, was the use of external beam nuclear reaction analysis to directly monitor the loading and unloading of 3 deuterium in the foil of an operating cell. Using a 1.5 MeV He ion beam in air, the deuterium in the outer 2 pm of the exposed Pd foil was measured for the 3 first time using the D( He,p) nuclear reaction. The maximum D:Pd ratios observed using this technique were 0.8-0.9.
