Fig 1 shows the system that allows force to be transferred for rotation. It's similar to a crankshaft piston setup, except it has gears that allows force to be transferred back from outside of the crankshaft via a secondary system. The shafts of the center gears pass through the central crankshaft axle, IE they are not fixed to the crankshaft and can turn separately from the crank. (double shafts). Fig 1, in this example the green gears of the red crank have 20 teeth, the pink gear has 40. when the crank turns 360 degrees CW the central green gear turns 360 degrees CCW The light blue gear of the yellow crank has 80 teeth, the dark blue 40 teeth. when the crank turns 360 degrees CW the central light blue gear turns 540 degrees CW (1.5X).
