Quality and Quantity The world consists of different quantities in the space. The quantities change over time within the scope of their quality. Quality and quantity are mathematically described by sets whose elements exclude each other. Examples include properties and their values, alphabets and their letters or number systems and their numbers /1/. Continuous Qualities have an infinite number of ordered quantities. Discrete ones usually a finite number, which can also be unordered (nominal scales). 1 Natural numbers are discrete, the real ones are continuous.
