Michael Goodspeed Residence Beaverton, OR, United States Nationality USA Scientific career Fields Freelance Journalist Michael Goodspeed is an American freelance journalist and writer associated with the Electric Universe movement. He served as a contributing editor and Thunderblog author for Thunderbolts.info, the website of The Thunderbolts Project, an organization that promotes the Electric Universe interpretation of cosmology. Biography Goodspeed is a freelance journalist based in the United States. He became a contributing editor to Thunderbolts.info, where his articles helped draw Internet attention to the Electric Universe model advocated by writers such as Wallace Thornhill and David Talbott . In addition to his written work, he has been involved in producing multimedia content for The Thunderbolts Project, including podcast and video material. Work Writing for the Thunderbolts.info "Thunderblog" between 2007 and 2011, Goodspeed authored commentary presenting Electric Universe perspectives on astronomy and cosmology and critiquing mainstream scientific views. His articles addressed subjects such as comets, planetary phenomena, and Big Bang cosmology, and included pieces critical of organized skepticism. Among his titles are "Is The Universe Electric?" (2008), "A Holistic Cosmology For the 21st Century" (2008), "The Skeptical Inquirer Embarrasses Itself" (2008), "Electric Comets Re-write Space Science" (2010), and "The Electric Comet: The Elephant in NASA's Living Room?" (2011). Contributing editor to Thunderbolts.info.
