He served as a contributing editor and Thunderblog author for Thunderbolts.info, the website of The Thunderbolts Project, an organization that promotes the Electric Universe interpretation of cosmology. Biography Goodspeed is a freelance journalist based in the United States. He became a contributing editor to Thunderbolts.info, where his articles helped draw Internet attention to the Electric Universe model advocated by writers such as Wallace Thornhill and David Talbott . In addition to his written work, he has been involved in producing multimedia content for The Thunderbolts Project, including podcast and video material.
