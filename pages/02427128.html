Frank Correspondence marcos.frank@wsu.edu In Brief Ingiosi et al. describe how astroglial Ca2+ signals change dynamically across sleep and wake and after sleep loss using in vivo imaging methods. Synchrony of astroglial Ca2+ signals decreases during sleep compared to wake and after sleep deprivation. STIM1 regulation of astroglial Ca2+ plays a role in the normal compensatory response to sleep loss.
