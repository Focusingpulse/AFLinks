Fedyai1 1 Togliatti State University vvvahnina@yandex.ru For the electric power industry, monitoring and recording hazardous levels of geomagnetically induced currents (GICs) is a critical task [1,2]. GICs cause saturation of the magnetic system of power transformers and create a risk of emergency conditions [3,4]. For engineering monitoring of hazardous GIC levels, it is necessary to: - identify critical nodes of the power network in which the highest GIC levels are expected, exceeding the amplitude of the no-load current of power transformers. The preliminary selection is performed according to a topological criterion – dead-end substations, nodal substations, and through substations with different geographical orientations of the connected transmission lines.
