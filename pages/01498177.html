Experimental Results Kohji Kamada National Institute for Fusion Science, Furo-cho, Chikusa-ku, Nagoya 464-01 (Received May 22, 1992; accepted for publication July 18, 1992) Abstract: Both H-H and D-D fusion reactions, detected via high energy particle emission on CR-39, are shown to occur when 200 and 400 keV electrons are bombarded onto H+ or D+ ion implanted Al thin crystals. Roughly 1-2×103 particle emissions, including both hydrogen and helium isotopes, in whole space were observed in each case. Collisions between recoilled D atoms due to the high energy electron impact give only 10-12 to 10-26 times smaller fusion rates than the experimental results.
