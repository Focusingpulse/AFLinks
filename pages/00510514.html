Some abc-properties of the generalized Pell’s equations x 2 − D · y 2 = ±N and x 2 − D · y 2 = N2 Janis Kuzmanis Riga, Latvia Email: yanisku@gmail.com Abstract Primary abc-triples, formed by the set of roots for the generalized Pell’s equations x 2 − D · y 2 = ±N (with N > 2), induce formation of secondary abc-triples in the set of roots for equations x 2 − D · y 2 = N2 . Mathematics Subject Classification: 11A55, 11C20, 11D09 Keywords: abc-conjecture; generalized Pell’s equations; abc-triples 1 Introduction The proposed article is a continuation of [1], where theoretical background concerning radicals of numbers, abc-conjecture, continuants and continued fractions was revealed. Mentioned [1] proves abc-triple induction in Pell’s equations x 2 − D · y 2 = N with N = ±1 and ±2, but [2] shows similar rules for N = ±4. The main result of the given article is the proof of formation of abc-triples in the set of roots for the generalized Pell’s equations x 2−D·y 2 = N2 with N > 2, which is induced by corresponding abc-triples from roots of the equations x 2 − D · y 2 = ±N. This induction is preserved partially also in the situations with even N values, only reduction to coprime terms become necessary for obtaining of correct abc-equations.
