A Tribute to Kaylinn

· archived # 1,507,121
Carlos, I want you to feel my being wrapped around your aching heart. I have known, and trained with you in Karate now for over 13 years. In those 13 years I have watched the children wrap their arms around you in Love and gratitude for dedicating yourself to teaching them Karate. They Love you so very much. Everyone of us does. This past Sunday morning you had to let go of your only child, your beautiful 19 year old daughter Kaylinn who chose to let go of her physical body. Your grief is immense and your challenge is great. You are in the Dark night of the Soul. But, the love that we all have for you, will not allow you to stay in this Darkness. I am holding your hand with my Heart. In all of our Hearts is the Light. This is the Truth and with Light there is no Darkness. We are with you and you are not alone. I love you, and I am here for you. Kaylinn, You are the light, and now in Peace. For You Carlos
