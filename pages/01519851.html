Overview The book presents Catt's unconventional approach to electromagnetic theory, built around the idea of the "energy current" — a transverse electromagnetic (TEM) step of electric and magnetic field that travels at the speed of light in the dielectric between conductors. From this starting point Catt reinterprets charge, current, and the behaviour of capacitors and transmission lines, and works toward what he called "Theory C" (the "C" for Catt), his proposed replacement for conventional electromagnetic theory.
