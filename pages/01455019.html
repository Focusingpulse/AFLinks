This electrical state is self or mutual assimilative / attractive . Keely and Russell held that neutral points or assimilative centers are infinite in number and extension ( Keely ) and atoms may form (and deform) around one or any of them anywhere at anytime. Atoms are an effect (recording or casting) of that electrical thinking but are not the thinking itself. Much like a wave is not the ocean but a manner of the ocean's expressionings of its being .
