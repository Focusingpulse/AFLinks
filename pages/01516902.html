The improved seal means, in specific embodiments, comprises a plurality of cylindrical rollers that serve as vane guides intermediate each pair of adjacent vanes. The cylindrical rollers adjacent each face of each respective vane engages its lateral face such that a satisfactory seal is made as the roller traverses radially inwardly and outwardly along the lateral face of the vane.
