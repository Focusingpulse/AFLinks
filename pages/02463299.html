Aether Displacement (“Gravitational”) Waves in APM: Dispersion, Polarizations, and QMU Benchmarks David W. Thomson Quantum AetherDynamics Institute October 19, 2025 DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17391604 Abstract We derive linear wave equations for Aether chronovibrations coupled to curvature in the Aether Physics Model (APM), propagate their coupled tensor–Aether modes, and obtain the gravitational (Aether displacement) radiation spectrum in Quantum Measurement Units (QMU). A dispersive relation Ω(k) emerges from Au and curl; to leading order it produces a small, achromatic (frequency–even) departure from luminal propagation and a single additional polarization beyond the transverse traceless pair. We present energy–flux and luminosity formulae in QMU and propose detector phase/timing ratio tests across multi–band networks that compare Ω/Fq directly, avoiding any translation to other unit systems.
