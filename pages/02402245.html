MolPhot2026-main003 Luminescence of composite systems made of plasmonic nanoparticles with a colored macrochain shell Michael G. Kucherenko1 , Tatiana M. Chmereva , Nikita Y. Kruchinin , Aleksandr P. Rusinov 1 Orenburg State University clibph@yandex.ru Hybrid nanosystems made of metallic nanoparticles (NP) with protein or synthetic macromolecules seem promising for a number of biomedical and technical applications. The luminescence features of composite nanosystems consisting of a charged spherical plasmonic nanoparticle and a polyampholytic chain adsorbed on it with triplet (T)-excited molecular centers connected to the links of a macromolecular chain are studied. Upon saturation of colloidal solutions with molecular oxygen, O2 -induced cross-annihilation delayed fluorescence (DF) of dye molecules was detected, the characteristics of which depended on changes in the conformational structure of the macromolecular fringe [1]. A model description of the kinetics of cross-annihilation of T centers with singlet excitations of O2 has been performed for the radially inhomogeneous distribution of reagents [2]. The effect of the Q charge of the nanoparticle on the pulse shape of cross-annihilation DF with plasmon support of the radiation reaction channel was found [2-3]. Calculations of the spectral density of the number of emitted photons for the dye molecule have shown that the distribution of this value near the nanoparticle is highly heterogeneous and anisotropic.
