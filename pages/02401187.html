iberorus2025-T.2.4.002 Latin America and the New Public Diplomacy: The Role of NGOs and Media Leaders Nikolay Yakovenko alabanco@gmail.com Modern international communication is undergoing a profound transformation: traditional state institutions are increasingly complemented — and often outpaced — by new non-state actors such as NGOs, media leaders, and activists, who are becoming influential players in the field of public diplomacy. In Latin America, where social mobilization and grassroots movements have deep historical roots, new public diplomacy is actively developing through the use of digital platforms and personal branding. Outline of the presentation: The talk will address three main blocks. 1. American Lessons How “soft power” technologies and project promotion models are being implemented in the region. The adaptation of American approaches to local cultural contexts. 2. New Actors of Public Diplomacy The role of think tanks and expert communities in shaping the international agenda. Political parties as subjects of international dialogue. The phenomenon of “media diplomacy”: from independent journalists to celebrities. 3. Game Changer: A New Reality Social media, streaming platforms, and media activism as battlegrounds for public opinion. The growing importance of cultural initiatives amid political turbulence. Prospects for the development of public diplomacy in the era of digital transformation.
