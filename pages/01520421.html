(United States) Publication history 1995–present Frequency Bimonthly Indexing ISSN 1081-6372 Links Journal homepage Infinite Energy is a magazine devoted to cold fusion (low-energy nuclear reactions) and "new energy" science and technology, founded in 1995 by Eugene Mallove . Based in Concord, New Hampshire, and published by the New Energy Foundation, it has been one of the principal ongoing publications covering cold-fusion research and related unconventional energy work.
