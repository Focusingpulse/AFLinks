Subsequently, many attempts have been made to extend many science notions to the fuzzy setting. Fuzzy minimal structure and fuzzy minimal space introduced in [3]. Further results about fuzzy minimal spaces can be found in [1, 3, 4, 5, 7]. In this paper, we introduce and investigate some new fuzzy minimal separation axioms. For easy understanding of the material incorporated in this paper, we recall some basic definitions and results. For details on the following notions we refer to [1, 3, 4, 5, 7]. A family M of fuzzy sets in X is said to be a fuzzy minimal structure on X if α1X ∈ M for all α ∈ I, where I = [0, 1]. In this case (X,M) is called a fuzzy minimal space [1]. A fuzzy set A ∈ IX is said to be fuzzy m-open if A ∈ M. B ∈ IX is called a fuzzy m-closed set if Bc ∈ M. Let m − Int(A)= _{U : U ≤ A, U ∈ M} and (1.1) m − Cl(A)= ^{F : A ≤ F, Fc ∈ M}. (c) m − Int(A) ≤ m − Int(B) and m − Cl(A) ≤ m − Cl(B) if A ≤ B.
