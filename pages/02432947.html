A spiritual solution to material problems.

· archived # 2,432,947
I once thought the answer to saving planet Earth was finding a new energy source, eliminating pollution, or bringing down the controllers of this world. I was wrong. I have come to realize that the problems we are facing stem from 3 social/ humanitarian issues. One is the inability for each one of us to recognize another human being as a living, immortal soul. The second factor is the unwillingness to ask for forgiveness. And the third factor is the unwillingness to give forgiveness. We have a tendency to feel only the pain others give to us, without the awareness of the pain we have caused others.
