Steam Powered Car

· archived # 2,444,951
08/14/16 - Steam Powered Car Solar Panels help charge a battery bank, which powers and electric water heater in a water fuel tank that creates steam to push the piston engine. The engine runs a belt driven generator 7,000 watts, which recharges the battery bank to power the electric water heater more. The steam travels from the fuel tank through the fuel line to the intake to push the piston to rotate the crankshaft, then leaves the outtake back into the fuel tank to recycle the steam and create more pressure faster. Once a certain PSI is reached a controller regulates the power to the electric water heater to reduce the energy and shut off if needed or in emergency. An emergency valve connects to the exhaust muffler, but you will lose the water and steam rather then recycle and condense it when the engine is not on. It would be recommended to drain the water every so often. Using an onboard or container power station, you can condense water from the air with a dehumidifier filtered, ran by the solar motor generator/steam/ hydroelectric or other engine designs or integration, as water fuel stations. Once the PSI reaches a certain level the controller lowers or shuts off the electric water heater, while the engine runs the belt driven generator to charge the battery bank while the solar panels do at the same time. Once the battery bank is full, the controller shuts off either the solar panels or the generator or both, and grounds it, or an inverter can be used to draw more energy.
