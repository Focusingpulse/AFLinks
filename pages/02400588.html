The Seam A Type System for Empirical Claims, and the Boundary Observation Cannot Cross

👤 Dean Kulik · · archived # 2,400,588
1 The Seam A Type System for Empirical Claims, and the Boundary Observation Cannot Cross Dean Kulik QuHarmonics Research Group · ORCID 0009-0003-3128-8828 August 2026 Abstract Results are ordinarily classified by subject matter and by confidence. This paper proposes classifying them instead by the logical operation that produced them, and shows that doing so partitions empirical claims into five types with sharply different burdens of proof. The first three types are reachable by observation: a value returned by a query, a witnessed distinction between two results, and a candidate equivalence supported by queries that failed to separate. The last two are not: a generating structure for a transformation family, and a proved invariance across that family. Between the third and fourth lies a boundary that accumulation cannot cross. Ten thousand observations that two things were not separated do not establish that no admissible query separates them, and no number of further observations changes the quantifier. What does cross the boundary is generation. If a transformation family is finitely generated, the universal obligation collapses to a finite list: an observable invariant under the generators is invariant under the whole family, by composition and induction on word length. For the symmetry group of the circle graph this reduces twelve obligations to two, verified exactly. For the rotation group of three-space it reduces an infinite obligation to three. The reduction is dramatic and it does not change the type of what remains — each generator obligation is still universal
