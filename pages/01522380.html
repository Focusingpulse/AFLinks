His work has appeared in Galilean Electrodynamics and in the Proceedings of the Natural Philosophy Alliance . Biography Newman was born in 1949 in Birmingham, England, the only son of Lilian and Cyril Newman. His mother, who had worked as a capstan operator in Birmingham during the Second World War and later as an office cleaner, died in 1997. Newman also had two older stepsisters, June and Rita.
