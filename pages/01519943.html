Scientific Theory Name Energy Wave Theory Type Wave structure of matter; unified particle and force model Author(s) Jeff Yee Keywords Aether , standing waves, wave centers, Wave Structure of Matter, particle mass, unified force Year 2014 (first papers); named 2017 Website https://energywavetheory.com Energy Wave Theory ( EWT ) is a classical wave model of particles and forces developed by Jeff Yee . It treats the universe as a physical medium — an aether — that oscillates to form waves, and holds that everything normally described as a particle, a mass, a charge or a force is a consequence of wave behaviour in that medium, computed with ordinary classical mechanics rather than quantum formalism. Its two central claims are structural. Matter is standing waves : a particle is a region where travelling longitudinal waves are reflected by wave centers, forming a standing wave of stored energy out to a radius where the amplitude decays back into travelling waves. That radius is the particle's edge, and the stored energy is its mass. Forces are wave interference : the same equation governs the strong, electromagnetic and gravitational forces, which differ only in wave form and amplitude, because particles move to the point of minimal wave amplitude. Yee's stated ambition is reductive. He argues that the energies of all particles and the strengths of three of the four forces follow from four universal constants and one property of the electron , with no free parameters beyond those five. Origins EWT descends from the Wave Structure of Matter tradition. Yee credits three independent sources as leading to his work: Milo M Wolff , whose wave structure of matter modelled the electron as spherical in-waves and out-waves about a wave center; Gabriel LaFreniere , whose wave simulations gave the picture a visual form; and Xavier Borg .
