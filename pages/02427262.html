VOJNOTEHNIČKI GLASNIK / MILITARY TECHNICAL COURIER, 2016., Vol 64, No 2 PRESENTATION OF THE RESULTS FOR DEUTERIUM RETENTION AND THERMAL RELEASE IN A NEW TYPE OF LOW ACTIVATION FERRITIC-MARTENSITIC STEEL EUROFER Sanja Lj. Korica University Union – Nikola Tesla, Faculty for Ecology and Environmental Protection, Belgrade, Republic of Serbia, e-mail: koricasanja@gmail.com, ORCID iD: http://orcid.org/0000-0002-7915-9430 DOI: 10.5937/vojtehg64-9327 FIELD: Materials ARTICLE TYPE: Original Scientific Paper ARTICLE LANGUAGE: English Abstract: This work presents the results of the investigation into deuterium retention and thermal release in low activation ferritic-martensitic steel EUROFER and its main components, Fe and Cr, using ion implantation, nuclear reaction depth profiling and the thermal desorption spectrometry technique. The samples were exposed to 24 -2 deuterium ECR plasma of 6.5×10 Dm fluence. Our NRA results show that, at 300 K, most of deuterium is trapped in the near-surface region. A part of the implanted deuterium diffuses into the bulk and is trapped by defects beyond the implantation range. The results of the TDS measurement of the Cr sample confirm the formation of an ordered Cr-hydride phase.
