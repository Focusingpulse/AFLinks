Introduction Levine [4] introduced the concept of generalized closed sets in 1970. The concept of gαe -closed sets was introduced by R. Devi et al. [2]. In 2013, the notion of Nano topological space with respect to the subset X of a universe U was introduced by Lellis Thivagar [3] which was defined in terms of approximations and boundary region of a universe using an equivalence relation on it. He has defined Nano closed sets, Nano-interior and Nano-closure of a set and also established certain weak forms of Nano open sets such as Nano α-open sets, Nano semi-open sets and Nano pre-open sets. In this paper, we introduce a new class of sets on Nano topological spaces called Nano gαe -closed sets and discuss the relation of this new sets with existing ones. 2.
