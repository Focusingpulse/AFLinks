BUT CHAIRMAN BOECKEL, WHO HAS BEEN AUTHORIZED TO ENGAGE AN ELECTRICAL EXPERT, IS AFRAID TO DO SO ON HIS OWN RESPONSIBILITY — OTHER EXPERTS IN MIND. In the minds of some of the City’s representatives in the Common Council, things have come to such a pass in dealing with the question of granting a franchise to the Niagara Falls company that they would like to engage the services of Edison or Tesla. It is interesting to know that there really has been some serious talk of employing either the great American wonder worker or his able Servian contemporary. At last Friday’s meeting of the joint power committee, a resolution was adopted authorizing Ald. Adam Boeckel, chairman, to hire an electrical expert to advise with the committee in handling the franchise matter. It was expected that Mr. Boeckel would have an expert in time to advise the committee at its next meeting, two weeks hence.
