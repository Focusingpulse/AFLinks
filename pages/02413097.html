The wooden balance used by Stavros Dimitriou for measuring the weight change with his RF pendulum device The pivot screws provide a minimum of turning resistance and at the same time serve as contacts for the supply rail to the experiment's end. The screws pass through a copper ring (shown brown) each, which provides contact to the wire leading to the experiment end of the beam. The still contacts, on the top of the support of the balance are made of two piecec of bronze, with a small inward bump in them, to accommodate the tip of the corresponding pivot screw. The pivot screws permits adjusting the center of weight of the beam. They also adjust the horizontal correctness of the beam. Any little discrepancy of balance is set by using a small trimming wait. I used a school rubber in the photograph. ( It can be seen where it should not have been in one of the photos of the beam! ). The counterweight is usually a 500 ml plastic bottle of some refreshment, filled with sand to the appropriate weight. The bottle hangs from its tap, which has been punched through in two places. A 5 cm loop of thread through these holes serves to hung the counterweight bottle.
