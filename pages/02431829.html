Synchronicity, stray dog, Nike shoes and Mary

· archived # 2,431,829
Mary told me a series of events that have occurred over the last week that were cracking me up! About a week ago she was looking at her tennis shoes and thought it was time to get another pair. "Something" told her no...wait...don't get any right now. She thought this a bit strange...but put off getting any. We have a stray dog that has made our house her home. She has been here for over a year (Mary started feeding her after she was here for about a week and would not leave) Yesterday the dog showed up in the yard playing with a shoe. Today the dog showed up with another shoe. Mary went and looked at them. It was an almost brand new pair of Nikes! She looked closer, and they are her size! Our neighbors are not missing any shoes...we don't know where they came from. Mary now has a practically new pair of $150 Nikes delivered to her.
