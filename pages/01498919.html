The extension of this mecha￾nism to (n, -y) reactions is outlined. In another paper,2 we considered, as an extension of this work, the (n, -y) reactions between prop￾agating neutrons and the nuclei on a crystalline lattice for production of intense gamma rays. The purpose of this tech￾nical note is to bring out the limitations on the enhancement of the deuteron-deuteron ( d-d) fusion rate under realistic con￾ditions and to discuss some additional aspects of the ( n, 'Y) reactions.
