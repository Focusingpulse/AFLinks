For example, if the original frequencies are 32hz and 48hz, the resultant frequencies will be 80hz and 16 hz. While this effect occurs at all frequencies, it is most effective to the human ear at low frequencies. (This same acoustical principle is used by celestes to produce a different effect.) These stops use this acoustical effect to produce tones in the 32' and 64' octaves, using smaller (and thus less costly) pipes than would normally be necessary. One of these stops, labeled as 32' or 64' pitch, is comprised of two ranks which sound an octave and a twelfth above the desired pitch (that is, at the 1st & 2nd harmonics ). For a 32' stop, the two ranks are 16' and 10 2/3'; for a 64' stop, the two ranks are 32' and 21 1/3'. In many cases both pitches are taken from the same rank of pipes, a fifth apart, as a further cost-saving measure.
