Influence of Attractive Interaction between Deuterons in Pd on Nuclear Fusion T. Tajima a), H. lyetomi *3>c), and S. Ichimaru c) (a) Department of Physics and Institute for Fusion Studies University of Texas Austin, TX 78712 DOE/ET/53088—369 DE89 013314 (b) Materials Science Division Argonne National Laboratory Argonne, IL 60439 (c) Department of Physics University of Tokyo Bunkyo-ku, Tokyo 113 Japan Submitted 14 April 1989; revised 20 April 1989 It is shown that in a heavily deuterated palladium metal a pair of deuterons exhibit attractive interaction at short distance (= 0.1 A - 0.7 A) due to strong Coulomb correlations in the ion-sphere model and due to the screening action of localized 4d electrons. This mechanism leads to enhanced thermonuclear reactions at room temperatures some 50 orders of magnitude faster than that in a D2 molecule.
