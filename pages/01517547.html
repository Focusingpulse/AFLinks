Hartikka (www.innoplaza.net) When professor Vigier from University of Paris prepared for his lecture one could sense from the behavior of the audience that something special was expected. That was what happened. This experienced professor had experimented with Prof. Z. Marit of University of Belgrade with a new energy source based on palladium and lithium-7. By Vigier, these type of reactions have previously been misinterpreted as cold fusion. "This is not fusion or conventional chemical energy", Vigier stated. "It is new type of chemical energy". This new energy is based on spin-spin and spin-orbit couplings. Vigier has suggested developing economic and clean Li-7 energy techniques instead of constructing more fossil or nuclear energy production in France. He also tells about the aether model that suggested particle-like solitons (vortexes) can carry superluminal (faster than light) interactions. "This explains why Alain Aspect has observed superluminal interactions of polrization between photons, why Van-Flandern has shown that the Newton interaction is superluminal between the sun and planets and why Tesla's force (the Coulomb force) is superluminal between charged sources."
