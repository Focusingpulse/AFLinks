18.03 - Hubbard Scale of Consciousness

· archived # 1,455,287
Hubbard Scale of Consciousness L. Ron Hubbard developed a comprehensive view of consciousness or the mind of man. It was his goal to understand consciousness sufficient to develop healthy humans. After years of field and clinical research he discovered enough about the mind and consciousness to help millions achieve degrees of health and happiness . One of his findings pertains to levels or degrees of consciousness that elevate consciousness towards health and happiness from those that suppress consciousness towards disease , sorrow and death . He was dealing with the Split Mind or Differentiated Mind as that portion that deals with life , self and others. After all, the Whole Mind is Whole, undifferentiated and One with the Universal Mind and needs no "fixing". His process went after the split parts he called engrams which were trauma thoughts held in the cellular structure of the body forming the reactive mind . By eliminating these or clearing them out of the thought skein one would eventually be clear of them and the mind would be clear and behavior would be sane. This state of coincidence he called being " clear " and those achieving this state of clarified mind were called clears. That Hubbard knew of the One Mind or Cosmic Consciousness , that required no clearing, we can see in his statement: "Perhaps someday a clear will attain the nebulosity the author used to hear about in India which marked the man who was all soul ."
