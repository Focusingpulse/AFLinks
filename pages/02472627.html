MANY FAMOUS MEN AND WOMEN VOLUNTEER TO AID. Chauncey M. Depew, Clara Barton, Bishop Potter, Thos. A. Edison, Nikola Tesla and Eloise Vitti Will All Take Part. SOCIETY HAS DISPLAYED A VERY PATRIOTIC INTEREST. The World’s Red Cross benefit at the Grand Central Palace to-night is assured of success. The cablegrams telling of our dead and wounded in the war have intensified interest in the work of Red Cross nurses — “the angels of the battle-field.” A most interesting programme has been arranged for this benefit. Clara Barton, although she is in Key West, will “open the exercises.” She will touch a button which will illuminate a mammoth electric red cross placed in the centre of the main hall. Then Bent Brothers’ Military Band will play for the first time a march composed for and dedicated to The World. Andrew H. Green, the “Father of Greater New York,” will call the assembly to order, and after telling briefly of the work of the Red Cross Society will act as presiding officer. Arthur Classen, the chorus leader, will direct a full chorus of 300 voices. Dr. Chauncey M. Depew will deliver the oration of the evening.
