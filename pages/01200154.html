Concerning the selection pressure and procedures of language evolution, it is scientifically decisive to focus on the symbolic duration of grammatical structures and implied meanings. This interplay of free creation and construction principles shows a time-resistant tendency, which governs the linguistic coding process and the human interpretation of reality. Further advancement of computational linguistics will help us to better understand the evolution of the human mind, in medical and psychological terms of improving education and cultural learning.
