Translated by Steiner Online Library The Events in Koberwitz and Breslau For some time, a number of anthroposophists working in agricultural professions had been hoping that I would hold a course covering the anthroposophical view of agriculture. From June 7 to 16, I was able to find the time to fulfill this request. Koberwitz near Breslau, where Count Carl Keyserlingk manages a large agricultural estate in an exemplary manner, was one of the locations chosen for such a course.
