In these lectures we can only give a kind of sketch, and some explanations that can help us understand this mighty work. Today we would still like to point out some particularly important things. We begin by returning to a specific question of human evolution. [ 2 ] Every age between the Atlantean catastrophe and the great war of all against all has its own unique task for our development.
