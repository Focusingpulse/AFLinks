In this classical paradigm, mathematical structures and hashing algorithms are treated as thermodynamic grinders—engines that consume input data, execute sequential procedural steps, and generate a terminal output derived directly from the destruction of the initial state. However, this measurement-first approach fundamentally misapprehends the origin of computational meaning. Frequency alone is merely surface counting; it is the presence of an enveloping context that transforms frequency into a readable language.1 Measurement is downstream of context, not the origin of meaning.
