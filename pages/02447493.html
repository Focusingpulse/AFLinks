I'm trying a new thing for 1998...rather than make a bunch of little URLs in the index98 list, I decided to post interesting from certain emails that I receive (those that can be posted without risking proprietary interference or where requested private)...and other intriguing URLs or comments made on the web. I compiled all the ones for January into a single file at; http://keelynet.com/unclass/emjan98.htm Also, I will make it a point of documenting what goes on at our local monthly Roundtables. That used to be the basis for the Vanguard Sciences newsletter, but it costs too much and takes too much time to write up in newsletter format....doing it as an html file is much cleaner and gets to considerably more people than the 200 on the newsletter list....though many of them weren't netheads...so, the January 1998 Roundtable notes posted earlier here are now locked into a file at; http://keelynet.com/unclass/vsrt0198.htm As you can see, I'm trying to plant as many seeds as possible by using new methods of interaction and sharing information....well, if you are here, you too are doing it...and that's good....we need to work together to try to understand enough that we can tinker up a working SOMETHING....preferably overunity, gravity control...something that will work and that we can USE... To that end, this year, KeelyNet will have the Researcher Network list at; http://keelynet.com/network.htm and this mailing list at; http://keelynet.com/maillist.htm now their will be the monthly email compilation (indexed for easy reference) and the Roundtable notes.
