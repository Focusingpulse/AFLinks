Bridey Murphy is a purported 19th-century Irishwoman whom U.S. housewife Virginia Tighe (April 27, 1923 – July 12, 1995) claimed to be in a past life. The case was investigated by researchers and concluded to be the result of cryptomnesia. The story of Bridey Murphy was first told in a series of articles by William J. Barker , published in the Denver Post in 1954. In early 1956, Doubleday released a book by Bernstein, The Search for Bridey Murphy. Movie rights had already been sold by the time of its publication. At her insistence, Tighe was given the pseudonym "Ruth Mills Simmons". Wikipedia, Bridey Murphy See Also Reincarnation
