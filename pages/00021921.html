Feinberg [57] ABSTRACT A fuel cell for generating a hydrogen and oxygen gas utilizing a water electrolyte and a catalyst selected from the group consisting of sodium hydroxide and potas- sium carbonate. The fuel cell includes a pair of elec- trodes connected in circuit with an electrical power supply and an outlet for directing the generated hydro- gen and oxygen to a drier where it is dried and mixed to form a combustible fuel mixture.
