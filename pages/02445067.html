Black holes are regions of spacetime in which gravity is strong enough to prevent anything escaping, even light. Black holes steer light in the same way by bending space-time. In fact, the mathematics that describe both systems are formally equivalent. Because of that, it should come as no surprise that engineers have used metamaterials to create analogues of black holes that prevent light escaping.
