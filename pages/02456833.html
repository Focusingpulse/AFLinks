S. Department of Commerce Contents: Current economic news from DoC Economic Affairs(EA) agencies including press releases, economic indicators, official DoC summaries of economic news, information on how to obtain data tapes, and summaries of reports and studies produced by EA agencies. Also included are press releases issued by the Bureau of Labor Statistics. Audience: Open to the public without charge. No preregistration required. Operations: Operated 24 hours a day, except when being serviced.
