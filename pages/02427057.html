This World Economic Forum and Boston Consulting Group report examines what corporations, governments and civil society have achieved since the accord was drafted in 2015 and assesses the current state of global climate action. This full report, The Net-Zero Challenge: Fast-Forward to Decisive Climate Action, follows “The Net-Zero Challenge: Global Climate Action at a Crossroads” Briefing Paper published in December 2019. Patrick Herhold Managing Director and Partner, Centre for Climate Action, Boston Consulting Group The findings for this report are based on quantitative and qualitative assessments of governments, corporations, investors and societies.
