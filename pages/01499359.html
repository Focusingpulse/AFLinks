LETTER FROM HENRY CURTIS Regarding the INE Conference I attended the INE meeting in Salt Lake this past week end. In general, it was excellent. Many of the conferences focusing on New Energy issues are quite lacking in serious content. I found this meeting to be quite the opposite. Several theoretical math papers on Maxwell's equations. Several on transmutation of lighter atoms liberating energy. In this group were Santilli, Fox et, a, continuing Shoulder's work with charge clusters. Pappas from Greece gave a strong presentation on biological transmutation of Sodium and Potassium. In short it was an excellent symposium which I enjoyed greatly. Henry Curtis Return to the INE Main Page www.padrak.com/ine/NEN_6_12_8.html Nov., 1999.
