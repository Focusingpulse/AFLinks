The Power of Positive

· archived # 2,432,508
Focusing on the positive has made a huge impact on my life. I found a list of "don't wants" the other day that I had written maybe a year or two ago. Every single one of them was no longer a part of my life. Thanks to Paths and my own effort! It made me very excited to begin a new list - I don't have many "don't wants" in my life anymore because of how I choose to perceive my life now but I can sure think of a lot of "oooh! I'd LOVE for this to happen!" So I'm going to compile a new list and watch the magic happen. Here's the other really cool part: I now observe how my positivity is impacting the people around me. Life is just getting better and better - for me and for everyone else around me. I am reminded daily that my own consciousness really does transcend just me and also influences many many many people. More people than I am sure I'm aware of. My friends, co-workers, family, even the grocery store clerk or the blockbuster guy. It makes me really excited when I think about all of you here at this forum that are also doing the same thing! We can't even begin to imagine the impact we are having on the world!
