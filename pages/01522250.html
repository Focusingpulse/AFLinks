Zero to Infinity: The Foundations of Physics (Series on Knots and Everything) Book Author Peter Rowlands Language English Series Series on Knots and Everything, volume 41 Subject Foundations of physics; Dirac equation ; particle physics Published 2007 Publisher World Scientific Publishing Company Pages 740 ISBN 9812709142 ; ISBN-13 9789812709141 Zero to Infinity: The Foundations of Physics is a 2007 book by Peter Rowlands , a research fellow in the Department of Physics at the University of Liverpool, published by World Scientific as volume 41 of its Series on Knots and Everything — the series edited by the topologist Louis H. Kauffman, whose remit is the connections between knot theory, topology, physics, logic and computation. At 740 pages it is the full statement of the programme Rowlands has pursued since the 1990s: that the whole structure of physics can be derived from zero — from the requirement that the totality of the universe sum to nothing — by way of a computational rewrite system , and that relativistic quantum mechanics then follows from a single operator whose square is zero. Unusually for a book recorded on this wiki, it is a commercial monograph from a mainstream scientific publisher, in a respected mathematics series, by a physicist holding a university post.
