08/20/16 - How to not get yourself killed by Police doing their job The big problem we are seeing is with how people react to police...so many times, they run, they try to fight or they reach in a pocket and wind up shot dead. Best way to keep out of trouble with the law is to keep your nose clean, have a basic knowledge of your rights & know how to act. Save lives and share with your friends and family by sharing with them how they should act when dealing with police. The police are just like you and me, trying to do their job so they can return safely to their families and friends. In my north Texas hometown of 6,000 people we all knew the police and their families and went to school with or hung out with their kids. They all looked after us like uncles and grandads and cut us some slack so we could learn. I will never, EVER forget the police who took care of all of us. You can bet if we got out of hand, they'd drive us home and let our parents know and thats when we got the real lesson. It was not so much what we did, but the embarrassment and shame we brought to our family and friends that taught us the best. And it followed us to school so we learned not to do that again.
