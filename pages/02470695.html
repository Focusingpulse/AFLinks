THE GREAT POSSIBILITIES. The Men Behind the Wonderful Project Represent Not Far From Half a Billion Dollars. CHAUNCEY DEPEW'S PREDICTION. Within 25 Years There Will Be a City of a Million People Between the Shores of the Niagara River and Lake Erie — Wonders of Electricity. Mt. Francis Lynde Stetson, standing over the vast shaft that leads to the great tunnel which has recently been constructed under the City of Niagara Falls, and through which the water which served its purpose by developing power, will be carried to the river below the falls, said: "I have all my life been a critic, an examiner of the plans of others, but I feel now almost as though I should be glad to abandon that vocation and become one of those who are organizing great works and constructing vast enterprises. This sight inspires me every time that I see it." Yet Mr. Stetson himself was one of those who organized that colossal syndicate which, without timidity has been spending enormous sums of money in securing a plant which will utilize some portion of the power which has hitherto gone to waste over Niagara Falls. There came to him a few years ago a young lawyer of Niagara Falls, Mr. Rankine, who was possessed of the idea, that the time had come for capital to avail itself of this power.
