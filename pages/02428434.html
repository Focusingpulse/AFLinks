Lecture V - GA 276. The Arts and Their Mission (1964)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 8 June 1923 · archived # 2,428,434
, Dornach Lecture V I should like to supplement last week's lectures on art. Often I had to emphasize that the spiritual evolution of mankind has proceeded from the unity of science, art and religion. In present-day spiritual life we have science, art and religion separated, yet can look back into the time when these three streams flowed from a common source. That source is seen most clearly if we go back four or five thousand years to poetry during the primeval ages; or, rather, to what would today be called poetry.
