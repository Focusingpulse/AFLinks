But Mr. Tesla is quite willing to have the public remember that he has been studying this subject for several years, and as long ago as 1893 outlined a plan from which he hopes to secure great results before the new century is a year old. There are two apparent points of difference between the Marconi and Tesla systems. The young Italian relies upon the ether to convey his messages, while Mr. Tesla employs the earth as a conductor. To be sure, Marconi discovered that his apparatus worked better after he connected both transmitter and receiver with the ground, and it is not altogether clear that his first theory concerning the medium which conveys his messages is correct. Then, secondly, the particular form of energy employed by Marconi and Slaby is believed by them to be the so-called Hertz waves, which are not electricity, though produced by electrical apparatus. Mr. Tesla, on the other hand, utilizes true electric waves, of great power and frequency, such as can be generated by his much talked about "oscillator."
