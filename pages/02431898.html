We look forward to your responses! This Module was created to support your ability to excel at tennis. When using this Module your hand-eye coordination, stamina and focus can all be increased as well as your ability to constantly improve your aptitude for important aspects of the game such as swing technique and serve. In addition to this, it can also help you to foster an attitude of confidence that will be of great assistance while practicing and competing. Increase focus Increase flexibility Increase coordination Increase swing skill Increase reaction speed Increase energy Increase confidence Increase ability to keep your head in the game after missing a shot Increase willingness to prepare Increase ability to maintain a game-winning attitude This Module has 2 different versions: One for those above 18 and one for those who are less than 18. No alterations will be needed unless the child is less than 6 years of age. This Module is ideal for kids and teens involved in the sport, as it will help foster the physical and mental qualities needed to excel.
