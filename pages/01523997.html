Since 2018 his work has shifted from fiction toward nonfiction concerned with natural health, environmental toxins, and the sociology of scientific orthodoxy, and in 2022 he published a widely read journalistic survey of non-Big-Bang cosmologies. He is not a professional physicist; his contribution to the dissident community is as a writer who brings suppressed and marginalized scientific models to a large mainstream literary audience.
