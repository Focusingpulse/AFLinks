Overview This entry records a 2008 paper by David Tombe under the title Bernoulli's Principle in the Antenna . The link now resolves to a document titled Bernoulli's Principle in the AC Transformer , carrying the same author, the same dateline — "25th January 2008, Philippine Islands" — and a "7th October 2016 Amendment" notice. The word "antenna" does not appear anywhere in it, and no separate antenna paper is findable under the original title. The paper appears to have been renamed and substantially rewritten in place, growing from the two pages recorded here to five; the abstract preserved on this page is the only surviving trace of the earlier version. The 2016 text keeps the physical picture and moves it to the power grid.
