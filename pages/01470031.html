Cayce "Think not more highly of thy opinion than ye ought. Neither be ye over righteous in self . For these are condemnations , and as ye condemn , so are ye condemned . Do that - and ye will find thy life opening to that of joy , harmony , beauty . For ye have much to do but of thyself alone ye can do little." [ Cayce 3800-1]
