A Strontium Titanate Crystal Sphere with Oriented Cobalt Ferrite Inclusions: A QMU–APM Experimental Test Instrument Based on Rotating Magnetic Field Encoding David W. Thomson III Quantum AetherDynamics Institute (QADI), Alma, Illinois, USA ROR: ORCID: 0000-0002-5830-5427 November 13, 2025 Abstract A spherical composite crystal device is presented for testing Aether Physics Model (APM) predictions involving rotating magnetic flux (mflx), distributed charge circulation, and hemispheric curl orientation. The instrument consists of a strontium titanate (SrTiO3 ) crystal sphere containing controlled cobalt ferrite (CoFe2 O4 ) inclusions. These inclusions are oriented using a three–axis coil system that generates a programmable rotating magnetic field with an imposed DC bias, encoding opposite magnetic curl handedness in the northern and southern hemispheres. The resulting crystal sphere supports preferential electronic circulation around its equator and enables photon–driven carrier injection through optically prepared polar regions. All physical quantities are expressed in Quantum Measurement Units (QMU), allowing direct comparison with APM ledger identities such as Au curl = Fq2 λ2C and demonstrating how the crystal sphere geometry enables global Aether coupling. This device provides a precise, tunable test instrument for evaluating solid–state manifestations of APM rotational and distributed–charge dynamics.
