Verifying Vedic science by aether detection during a solar eclipse. Presented by Toby Grotz, hosted by Ian Cowan. Summary The announced speaker did not appear. Toby Grotz had been due to present a proposal for detecting the aether during a total solar eclipse; when he had not joined fifteen minutes in, host Ian J Cowan was on the point of abandoning the broadcast. Attendance was further hampered by a green-room link in that week's mailing that did not work, which Cowan corrected in the chat. With James Keene , Roger J Anderton , Vladimir S Netchitailo and James Marsen eventually present, the session went ahead as an unscripted reading of the two documents Grotz had sent in advance. The documents The first listed ten objections to quantum mechanics and ten to relativity, and claimed both sets are resolved by the space vortex theory of the late Indian engineer Paramahamsa Tewari , in which particles are stable vortices in a fluid space. Grotz had compiled it with the help of a chatbot, which the participants noticed from the duplicated equations left by copy-and-paste.
