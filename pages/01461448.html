Progressive Evolution

· archived # 1,461,448
Melodic : the passage from tone to tone ; Harmonic : the passage from chord to chord . Volumetric or Geometric Progression : from volume to volume. Sometimes called Cascade. There are two kinds of progression , melodic or harmonic . Speaking in general terms, the former (Melodic Progression) is a "succession of sounds forming a tune or melody " but the term is also applied to an "imitative succession of melodic phrases" that is to a melodic sequence. Harmonic progression is "the movement of one chord to another" and is diatonic or chromatic . The term is also sometimes used as synonymous with sequence.
