EPEMC Gateway Organization Abbreviation EPEMC Founder Ramon Careaga Type Open-source research community and web portal Legal status active Purpose Collecting, publishing and teaching research in plasma and electromagnetic cosmology Region Online; based in Lexington, Kentucky, United States Key people Ramon Careaga Website https://sites.google.com/view/epemcgateway/home The EPEMC Gateway is an online portal and volunteer research community devoted to Extended Plasma-Electro-Magnetic Cosmology ( EPEMC ), a framework assembled by the American engineer and researcher Ramon Careaga . The site describes itself as "an open-source cosmology you can touch, interact with, critique, and test," and gathers papers, videos, primers, newsletters and links across plasma cosmology , the Electric Universe , electrical geology and meteorology, historic electrical science, and related subjects. The names PEM , PEMC , EPEMC and PEMS are described on the site as "open license trademarks of the community of the altstream." The Gateway has been online in its present form since at least late 2021, and hosts a newsletter archive reaching back to 2017. Framework The Gateway distinguishes several layers of its own vocabulary: PEM — plasma-electro-magnetic dynamics, the underlying physics of currents and fields in ionized matter. PEMC — plasma-electro-magnetic cosmology, the application of that physics to the cosmos in the tradition of Hannes Alfvén , Winston H Bostick and Anthony L Peratt . EPEMC — the "extended" programme, which carries the same physics into geology, meteorology, biology, medicine, archaeology, mythology and history. MIMS — the "Membranous Interface of Material & Spiritual," a companion philosophical framework treating questions the site regards as lying outside empirical reach.
