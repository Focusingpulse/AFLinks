1995.05.11 / jonesse@acoust / Re: Cold Fusion: The Musical Originally-From: jonesse@acoust.byu.edu Newsgroups: sci.physics.fusion Subject: Re: Cold Fusion: The Musical Date: 11 May 95 13:00:27 -0600 Organization: Brigham Young University In a post announcing "Cold Fusion: The Musical" [I have not received that original post yet] , Wes writes: "I do fear Dr. Steven Jones may not appreciate his namesake carrying a bullwhip and an old, beat-up hat; fighting snakes and beating up Ninjas..." Droege asks: "How about it Steve, is that your self image?" Quite. I have dressed myself that way in class a time or two -- the students seem to enjoy the allusion to Indiana Jones. My son Seth induced me to buy the correct style of fedora. The last time I did this, I demonstrated burning hydrogen in a container with an open bottom. After about 30 seconds, the flame enters the bottle which is now mixed with air, and an explosion occurs. It's quite safe (the heavy jar is surround by heavy wire mesh), and a lot of fun. Sounds like an amusing play, gentlemen. Have fun. --Steve Jones P.S. I do have a transcript of the original F&P press conference at the University of Utah (Note: our group did *NOT* have a press conference at BYU!) Let me know if you want me to dig this up, Wes. Note 2: This transcript shows Pons saying that the bump in the gamma plot is at 2.5 MeV -- it should be at 2.2 MeV for neutron capture on protons in water!
