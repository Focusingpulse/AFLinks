Thomson III (born 1960) is an American independent researcher, the discoverer and principal theoretician of the Aether Physics Model (APM), which he developed with James D. Bourassa . He is Science Director of the Quantum AetherDynamics Institute, the non-profit organization the two founded to develop the theory. Aether Physics Model Thomson says he arrived at the Aether Physics Model in February 2002, and in 2004 he and Bourassa incorporated the Quantum AetherDynamics Institute to develop it.
