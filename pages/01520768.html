Writing as an electrical engineer who spent a career designing circuit boards, de Hilster argues that there are no negative numbers in physics only directions. A sine wave describing an electromagnetic wave takes negative values in half of each cycle, but nothing in the physical picture is negative: there is an up amplitude and a down amplitude, each with a magnitude.
