Key Words and Phrases: Firm continuity, rm contra-continuity, S-closed spaces, strongly S-closed spaces, contra-continuity, subcontra-continuity, locally contra-closed graph. 1 Introduction Kupka [8] has used rm continuity to investigate compactness. Recently Caldas, et al. have used rm semi-continuity to study semi-compactness. In this note we continue this line of investigation by introducing a form of rm continuity, which we call rm contra-continuity, and using it to study strongly S-closed spaces. Dontchev [6] introduced strongly S-closed spaces and showed that contra-continuous images of strongly S-closed spaces are compact. Baker [2] extended this result by showing that subcontra-continuous images of strongly S￾closed spaces are compact. Quite recently, Ganster et al. [7] further investigated, among others, the notion of strongly S-closedness. Our purpose in this note is to characterize strongly S-closed spaces in terms of rm contra-continuity and subcontra-continuity. In particular, we show that a space X is strongly S-closed if and only if for every space Y every subcontra-continuous function f : X ! Y is rmly contra-continuous.
