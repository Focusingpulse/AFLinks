A solution to the Riemann Hypothesis Bittu Marley February 7, 2021 Abstract This paper discloses a proof for the Riemann Hypothesis. We define function f(s) such that sum of f(s) and f(1 − s) is zero at the non-trivial zeros of zeta function. Further, f(s) can be written as sum of − 1 s and a series function −λ(s) which is obtained on the expansion of integration term in the functional equation of zeta function. Either f(s) and f(1 − s) are individually zero or f(s) = −f(1−s) for an s, off the critical line if the Riemann hypothesis is false. From geometric analysis of λ(s) we find the f(s) 6= 0 any where in the critical strip.
