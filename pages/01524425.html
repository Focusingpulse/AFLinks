(Gravitation Explained) is a 2002 book by Frank M Meno , self-published through Aetherpress (Pittsburgh). Overview Meno presents an original, purely mechanical theory of gravitation intended to be easily visualized. It is the popular statement of his Gyron Aether Theory , in which space is filled with an aether composed of vast numbers of tiny spinning particles ("gyrons"); vortices of these gyrons make up fundamental particles, and an omnidirectional flux of superluminal gyron streams ejected from vortex "funnels" produces the force we experience as gravity.
