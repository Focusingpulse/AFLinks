How to get proper nutrition?

· archived # 2,430,970
Everyone should know the health benefits of eating a healthy balanced diet but not have exact idea how to get proper nutrition. Here we mentioned few important tips for getting optimal nutrition levels. 1) Green leafy vegetables and fresh fruits, raw or steamed 2) Lean meats such as chicken, turkey and fish 3) Whole grain carbohydrates such as breads and pasta 4) Beans, peas, and other lentils 5) Low fat dairy products 6) Avoid foods that rich in cholesterol or salt 7) Drinking plenty of fluids or plane water for proper hydration Even you can also consult with doctor for nutritional health supplements, which rich in vitamins and minerals. So try these healthy tips for getting required nutrition levels that body wants.
