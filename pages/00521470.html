An Algebraic Reformulation of the Collatz Map as a Modular Operation on Consecutive Integers Rayan Bhuttoo rayan.bhuttoo.32@gmail.com Abstract The Collatz conjecture remains a formidable open problem in number theory. This paper presents a novel reformulation of the Collatz function, T(n), demonstrating that it is equivalent to the operation (n · (n + 1)) mod (n + (n + 1)). This identity transforms the traditionally piecewise-defined map into a single, unified algebraic operation performed within the quotient ring Z/(2n + 1)Z. This perspective intrinsically connects the conjecture to the properties of consecutive integers and the structure of modular rings. Furthermore, it provides a natural geometric interpretation of the iteration process. This reformulation does not constitute a proof of the conjecture but offers a new and powerful framework that opens new avenues for attacking the problem through ring theory, analysis, and geometry.
