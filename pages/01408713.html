It has been commonly said that "a musical sound is composed of three sounds ," for every ear is capable of hearing these three , and with a little attention a few more than these; but many will be startled when told that there are twenty-five sounds in that sound . Eighteen of them are simply the octaves of the other seven , all of these seven except one having one or more octaves in the sound. Four of the seven also are very feeble, the one which has no octave being the feeblest of all.
