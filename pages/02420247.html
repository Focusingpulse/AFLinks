Lecture Two — GA 104. The Apocalypse of St. John

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 19 June 1908 · archived # 2,420,247
, Nuremberg Translated by Mabel Cotterell Lecture Two [ 1 ] Yesterday we described the spirit of the Apocalypse of John in a general way. We tried to give a few broad outlines showing that in this Apocalypse is described what may be called a Christian initiation. To-day it will be my task to present to you in general the nature of initiation, to describe what takes place in a man when through initiation he is enabled to see for himself those spiritual worlds which lie behind the sense worlds; and further it will be my task to give in broad outline a description of the experiences in initiation
