GTM2026-main018 Attack-Defense Game Model with Testing Jiarui Liu , Mazalov Vladimir st112117@student.spbu.ru The paper examines a model of Colonel Blotto's popular game in the form, proposed by Sonin, as a testing game. The model involves a number of objects of varying value, defended by one player (the "Defender"). The other player (the "Attacker") first tests all the objects, obtains imperfect information, and then attacks the objects. We study the problem as a matrix game between the Attacker and the Defender, allowing one to find equilibrium strategies for both the Defender and the Attacker. Target heterogeneity and test signal quality (sensitivity a and specificity b) jointly determine the optimal strategy selection of both Attacker and Defender. An analysis of the equilibrium strategies is provided, depending on the quality of the information received.
