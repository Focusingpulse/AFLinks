Smith WDSmith@fastmail.fm January 30, 2005 Abstract — We attempt to find all implications among 19 commonly used diassociativity, Moufang, Bol, alterna￾tivity and inverse-related properties in loops. There are 6 among these that appear to be valid in finite but not infi￾nite loops. Under that assumption, we completely settle the problem. We study in detail the apparently-simplest among the 6 nasty cases: the “LRalt=⇒2SI” question of whether a left- and right-alternative loop necessarily has 2-sided inverses. We construct an infinite loop in which this is false. However, X must have a 2-sided inverse in any LRalt loop with ≤ 185 elements or in which X n = 1 with n ≤ 13 (M.K.Kinyon has improved “13” to “31”), re￾sults suggesting this is the case in all finite loops. The problem of fully resolving this may be the hardest natu￾ral problem in mathematics that is this simply posed.
