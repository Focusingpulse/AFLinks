👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 25 October 1918 · archived # 2,424,720

Parker Lecture Four [ 1 ] Before turning to other matters I must speak of certain wider conceptions that follow from our consideration of the recent development of human history. We attempted to examine this development from the stand-point of a symptomatology; we tried to show that what are usually called historical facts are not the essential elements in history, but that they are symbols of the true reality that lies behind them.
