Possibly, it might be done, but it is not likely that any contractor would take the job at present. Nevertheless there are great possibilities in concrete, as every one who has kept in touch with the march of events must know. In all probability the concrete house will be popular in the near future, not merely because of its cheapness but on account of its durability and for many desirable features. When the concrete house comes it will come to stay, for it will be difficult to remove it by any process other than blasting. And then it is not So very new. either. Nikola Tesla, another light of the electric world, calls attention to the fact that some time ago Herr Krupp, the famous gun manufacturer of Essen, Germany, not only put up large numbers of houses of concrete for his employees, but actually proposed to cast an entire concrete fort in one solid piece.
