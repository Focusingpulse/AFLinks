AGI and HSA (Hypertopology/Semigroup/Acyclicity) August Lau ABSTRACT. AGI stands for artificial general intelligence and HSA stands for 3 areas (hypertopology/semigroup/acyclicity) in mathematics. The author will describe the connection between the two fields. In 1974, the author published a paper on "Acyclicity and dimension of hyperspaces of subcontinua" in Buletin de l'Académie Polonaise des Sciences" (Bulletin of the Polish Academy of sciences). The paper proved that certain hyperspaces are acyclic (no holes in the hyperspace i.e. all Betti numbers are zero) and are often infinite dimensional. It used topological semigroup theory (specifically semilattice) to prove the acyclicity of certain hyperspace. That was the paper that connected three areas of abstract mathematics: Hypertopology Semigroup Acyclicity. HYPERTOPOLOGY: Hypertopology is the contraction of two words hyperspace and topology. If X is a set (sometimes called a base space), then the hyperspace is the collection of certain subsets of X. So if X = {1,2}, then H(X) can be defined as the hyperspace of X composed of {1} and {2} and {1,2}. Hyperspace has been used to just indicate higher dimension like 10 compared to 4 dimension. In this note, hyperspace is a "construction" which collects certain subsets of a base space X. Why topology? A base space needs to be endowed with a topology if we want the concept of abstract distance or abstract geometry to be used on the base space. when measurements are made, the open sets of the topology of X is an abstract description of proximity. One connection to AGI: The more narrow definition of hyperspace has been used in AGI as hypergraphs connecting points. The points are connected by edges (connecting two points) and graphs (connecting more than two points).
