GTM2026-main045 Percolation in weighted directed low-degree graphs with high reciprocity rates Oksana Zimanova1 , Ivan Kozitsin1 , Alexander Gubanov2 1 V.A. Trapeznikov Institute of Control Problems of the Russian Academy of Sciences. 2 Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (State University), TSU zimanova.oksana@yandex.ru We investigate the percolation properties of a specific family of weighted directed graphs with high reciprocity rates (RF-graphs) and low average degrees. For any vertex, the sum of outgoing edges adds up to one. These graphs appear to be reasonably connected, with a percolation threshold of $ \sim 0.35 $. To approximate this percolation pattern, two random graph models are proposed: the WR-random graph model based on the Erdős–Rényi model and the WR-configuration model, which is based on the configuration model. The latter model reproduces the percolation behavior of the RF-graphs quite accurately, including the percolation threshold. For this model, we derive an analytical relationship that links the size of the giant cluster $(1-u)$ to the vertex degree distribution $p_m$ and the edge weight distribution function $\phi(k)$. Our theoretical estimations are in good agreement with computational experiments.
