Ikigai

· archived # 1,459,895
Ikigai is a Japanese concept meaning "a reason for being". Everyone, according to the Japanese, has an ikigai . Finding it requires a deep and often lengthy search of self . Such a search is regarded as being very important, since it is believed that discovery of one's ikigai brings satisfaction and meaning to life . What you love , what you are good at, what the world needs, and what you can be paid for. Inside of these convergences is the zero point that I did not have a name for until last week. "Everyone has a spirit that can be refined, a body that can be trained in some manner, a suitable path to follow. You are here to realize your inner divinity and manifest your innate enlightenment . One does not need money, buildings, power or status to practice the Art of Peace . Heaven is right where you are standing and that is the place to train." [ Morihei Ueshiba ]
