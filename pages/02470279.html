The new volume will include poems of considerable length of a patriotic character, entitled “The Voice of Webster” and “Hands Across the Sea,” beside a “Song of the Modern Greeks” and an “Apostrophe to Greece,” published last year and translated in part into modern Greek for the illustrated record of the Olympian games of 1896. The book will also contain a series of ballads and lyrics, entitled “Paraphrases From the Serbian,” versified by Mr. Johnson after literal translations by Nikola Tesla, the electrical inventor, from the originals by Zmai Iovan Iovanovich , who has been called the Serbian Longfellow and concerning whom Mr. Tesla writes a prefatory article. The ballads deal with the struggle of the Serbian against the oppression of the Turk, and thus fall appropriately under the title of the volume.
