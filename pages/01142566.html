Case Presentation: We report the case of “Z.,” a 17-year-old male adolescent with major depressive disorder, generalized anxiety, school refusal, and disordered gam￾ing, who had declined pharmacotherapy and electroconvulsive therapy. De￾pressive symptomatology reduced from severe to subclinical levels; suicidal ideation resolved by month four and did not recur. Conclusion: This case illustrates the potential efficacy of family-integrated subconscious￾state therapy for treatment-resistant adolescent depression. Controlled trials are warranted.
