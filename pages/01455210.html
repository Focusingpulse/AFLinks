Early workers had shown that water decomposed to hydrogen and oxygen , with some hydrogen peroxide , under bombardment from X-rays . A detailed study of the reaction was made in 1913 by Duane and Scheuer and their results were confirmed twenty-five years later by Lanning and Lind. Meanwhile Risse and then Fricke had shown that water under X-rays in a closed vessel appeared to decompose practially not at all. Fricke indeed found traces of gas resulting from irradiation , but on analysis this turned out to be composed of hydrogen and carbon dioxide , obviously arising from organic impurities in the water .
