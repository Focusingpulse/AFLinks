Next in thread: Jerry Wayne Decker: "Re: LapTop Power" Sarnoff lab in Princeton just developed a neat device. They made small hollow plastic beads, coated the inside with phosphrus and filled the cavity with radio active tritium. The wavelength of the plastic contains the radioactivity. The tritium excites the phosperus making the beads glow. They will be placed in lap top screens to produce light with an array of photo voltatic cells to use some of the light to run the computer, thus reducing the weight of the battery from two pounds to one ounce.
