COOKING WITH A CURRENT No Waste of Fuel or Unpleasant Heat in the Kitchen of the Future. DANGER TO LIFE IS PAST It Is Now Possible to Make a Powerful Current Harmless — Tesla’s Wonderful Experiments. A well known electrical expert said the other day: “Anything in our line nowadays to attract attention must be little short of marvelous.” But there are a great many things which an expert might not consider “marvelous” at all, but which the public would like to know about. For instance, the latest development in heating and cooking by electricity. One firm now has three manufactories running and is totally unable to supply the demand for electrical cooking utensils and heating apparatus. The model kitchen of the future will have no stove whatever. There won’t be any superfluous heat to make life a burden for the cook, and yet she will be able to prepare your dinner in half or at most two-thirds of the time it now takes, and wholly without dirt and ashes. This model kitchen will contain a heat service boiler. It looks just about like the ordinary boiler, except that it has two wires entering it.
