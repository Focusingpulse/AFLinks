US7171964 Instant chemical based flexible oxygen in a non-pressurized flexible or rigid containment system The invention is a portable, non-pressurized oxygen generation device. A first chamber holds an oxygen liberating chemical, and a catalyst from a second chamber begins the oxygen liberating reaction when the two chemicals mix. The chemicals are pre-measured, and oxygen generation can begin within seconds of activation. A number of materials can be used to generate oxygen, but the preferred mix is an aqueous solution of 7 to 10% hydrogen peroxide as first reaction material and reagent grade manganese as the second reaction material.
