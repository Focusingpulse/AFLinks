Title The Only Solution is the Revision of Classical Theory Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Viraj Fernando Keywords Sagnac effect , Einstein Published 2008 No. of pages 7 Abstract Einstein wrote: "The chief attraction of the theory lies in its logical completeness. ?If a single one of the conclusions is proven wrong, it must be given up; to modify it without destroying the whole structure appears to be impossible." Nevertheless, there have been Sagnac's experiment in particular and various other experiments that have proved the conclusions of SRT to be wrong. There have also been numerous logical flaws in the theory that have been uncovered over the years. Yet, contrary to Einstein's commandment ?thou shalt not cling to the theory if even one iota of it is contradicted', the adherents of the theory have not given it up. Just like Einstein himself completely ignored Sagnac's experiment, his followers too completely ignore whatever valid criticism levelled against the theory. Not only that, they at times even go to the extent of defending it in a hostile manner, as if their religious beliefs have been offended. Overview Viraj Fernando's seven-page essay is addressed less to physicists than to other critics of special relativity, and its argument is that they have been attacking the wrong target. Its thesis is methodological: special relativity is not the source of the crisis in twentieth-century physics but a device for concealing one, and the crisis itself lies in classical mechanics. Criticising relativity while defending classical principles, Fernando argues, is "trying to put the old ill-fitting clothes back on the emperor," and produces theories that are "distant relatives of SRT." The diagnosis runs as follows.
