· archived # 1,483,749
Ramsay The effects of different positions of chords . The first position is clear and solid; the second is light and pretty. The notes which have the most varied effect in a stream of harmony are the upper and under notes , the edges of the stream ; the two outlines determine the effect :- ( click to enlarge ) [ Scientific Basis and Build of Music , page 84 ] When the chords follow each other placed in such a way as to bring the upper note of the one as near the upper note of the next as it can be, the upper surface of this stream of harmony is the melodic scale and the harmony is the bottom and the body of the stream .
