Law of Electro-Chemical Equivalents

· archived # 1,460,336
"An atom vibrates sympathetically under the influence of electric energy, such undertones of which are absorbed as are a harmonic or harmony of the electric pitch ; the amount of energy absorbed being directly as the arithmetical ratio of the undertone of the fundamental electric pitch . Scholium : A table of electro-chemical equivalents on the normal basis will indicate the electrical conditions and amount of chemical change."
