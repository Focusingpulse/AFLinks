Roy Keys Inc.), Montreal Pages 270 ISBN 9781987980295 Wilhelm Weber's Main Works on Electrodynamics Translated into English, Volume IV: Conservation of Energy, Weber's Planetary Model of the Atom and the Unification of Electromagnetism and Gravitation is the fourth volume of the five-volume edition of Wilhelm Weber 's electrodynamic works edited and translated by Andre K T Assis , published by Apeiron in 2021. It covers the conservation of energy controversy that dogged Weber's law, Weber's planetary model of the atom, and the extension of his force to gravitation — including Tisserand's 1872 calculation of planetary motion under Weber's law, which yielded a perihelion precession for Mercury. The volume contains, in translation and with editorial introductions: [Gauss, 1867] Fundamental law for all interactions of galvanic currents [Carl Neumann, 1868] Principles of electrodynamics, and his 1869 notes on that essay [Weber, 1869] On a simple formulation of the general fundamental law of electric action [Weber, 1871] Electrodynamic Measurements, Sixth Memoir , relating specially to the principle of the conservation of energy [Tisserand, 1872] On the motion of planets around the Sun according to Weber's electrodynamic law — an early application of Weber's force to gravitation [Weber, 1876] Remarks on Edlund's reply to two objections against the unitary theory of electricity [Weber, 1878] Electrodynamic Measurements, Seventh Memoir , relating specially to the energy of interaction [Weber, 1878] On the energy of interaction [Weber, 1894] Electrodynamic Measurements, Eighth Memoir [Weber, 1894] Aphorisms The volume closes with an overview of Weber's law applied to electromagnetism and gravitation, and of possible future developmen
