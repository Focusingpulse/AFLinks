Chemical products: dielectrics and others / Produits chimiques: diélectriques et autres Magnets / Aimants How to buy in the U.S. from a foreign country The first thing to do is to know if the seller accepts to be pay by visa card: this can be done directly in a secure page, or you can use a reliable organism which will be an intermediate between you and the seller. Usually they use www.paypal.com or www.bidpay.com (Western Union - dedicated to auction payments), where you'll have to enter your card number (not your secret code!) and other datas. Then, ask the seller the exact weight of the item you want to buy. And go to http://www.usps.com/ , choose in the left frame: postage rates and fees/calculate international postage/package/ then select the country and the exact weight of the package / then in the list of the shipping service you will have three choices : - very speed and expensive : Global Express - quick and reasonable : air parcel post - slow and cheap : surface shipping We usually choose Air Parcel Post/insurance/enter the value for the insured article (I suppose it's what they will pay off the seller if they loose the item)/ then you will see the total amount on your screen. That's what you'll have to pay and what the seller will ask you. If he asks you something higher, then there's a problem: he made a mistake of he is trying to charge you more than needed... be careful. But in this way you will be prepare for (almost) everything !
