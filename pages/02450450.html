It would follow that IF the entire mass of a UFO were actually producing the thrust, then maybe in early models these hemispheres did the trick. They did glow during flight which indicates some kind of electron/photon and possibly boson interaction/release. So, based on all the above, I think this guy might have a theory worth pursuing.
