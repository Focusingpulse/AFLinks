Shiekh Nationality American and British Alma mater Imperial College, London University Known for Perturbative quantization of Einstein gravity; operator regularization; the quantum interference computer Scientific career Fields Theoretical physics, quantum gravity , quantum information Website https://sites.google.com/site/anwarshiekh/ Anwar Y. Shiekh — who goes by Andy — is a British-American theoretical physicist at Colorado Mesa University in Grand Junction, Colorado. He took his doctorate at Imperial College London in 1987 under Chris Isham , and has published across quantum gravity , quantum field theory, quantum information and black-hole physics, in journals including Physics Letters B , the Canadian Journal of Physics , the Journal of Mathematical Physics and the International Journal of Theoretical Physics . Shiekh describes the unifying theme of his work as "impossibility proofs and ways around them" — taking results that establish some programme cannot work, and looking for the assumption that can be relaxed to sidestep the obstruction. He is listed in The Worldwide List of Dissident Scientists . Education Ph.D., Theoretical Physics, Imperial College, London University, 1987 (advisor: Chris Isham) D.I.C.
