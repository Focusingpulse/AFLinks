Who told you that? I believe the record in a tokamak is around 35 keV. Even small tokamaks typically run with a couple keV, i.e., only one order of magnitude below reactor relevant temperatures. This is enough that the plasma is fully ionized and nearly collisionless. It is also hot enough that even mid-size tokamaks like ASDEX Upgrade have to worry about the radiation from the D-D fusion (1.5 m concrete walls, restricted access after high power shots). > I used a little sophmore physics to get a mean > energy around 3.5 Mev for deuteron fusion.
