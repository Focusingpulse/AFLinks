kimo2026-main162 India-Russia Ties in a Changing World Order: Re-defining Security Cooperation Ankur Yadav ankur.jnusis@gmail.com This paper situates India-Russia relations in the context of the Ukraine conflict and the changing world order. It examines India–Russia relations in the historical, regional and global context, and the ideological and political discourse in the two countries. It delineates the state of India-Russia bilateral relations, and seeks to answer the question: How has the Ukraine war and the geo-political shifts in the regional and global system impacted India￾Russia ties? It discusses India-Russia relations in the context of traditional and non-traditional security. It argues that though geopolitics and state interest might have been the immediate, even the compelling, reason behind the Indo-Soviet ties, their coming together was also in accordance with domestic political discourse in the two countries. The article also tries to answer questions such as: Why is Russia important in global politics and for India? What are the issues on which the world views and the national interest of the two countries converge? What are the major challenges and opportunities for India–Russia relations? It argues that the two countries need to find and agree upon certain ideational or ideological commonalities to give stability to their relationship. India and Russia are finding new ways to connect and remain time-tested partners.
