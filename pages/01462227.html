Substance vs Pretty Pictures

· archived # 1,462,227
I've noticed within the general public there appears to be a preference for pretty pictures instead of substance. This dynamic reminds me of the old Wendy's commercial that complains "where's the beef?" ^Invalid YouTube URL provided The beef of course is the substance. Sadly this dynamic is all over the Alternative or New Energy community. Not everyone but it is enough to make one wonder can't people see beyond the surface? Russell referred to this dynamic as seeing with the Mind instead of with the physical eyes ( senses ). The physical senses fool us but the Mind can perceive truth. The same dynamic permeates the political scene where silver tongued politicians speak marvels but deliver emptiness.
