, Berlin Translated by Steiner Online Library 1. Cosmic Wisdom and Human Wisdom [ 1 ] Today I would like to return to a few points concerning human principles. We must seek out what exists in the world just as it exists within ourselves. A clock is a device made possible only by an intelligence that designed and assembled it. It is foolish to say: The clock has given itself this ingenious design. It would be just as foolish to say: Human beings have created themselves in their own image.
