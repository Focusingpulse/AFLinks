Author(s) Jonathan Yalley Keywords electromagnetic radiation, quantum mechanics , particle Published 2000 Journal Galilean Electrodynamics Volume 11 Number 5 No. of pages 1 Pages 100 Abstract According to the complementary principle in quantum mechanics, an object can behave either as a particle or a wave, but not both at the same time. Contrary to the complementary principle, electromagnetic radiation behaves as both particle and wave simultaneously. ... The cornerstone for the new theory, called photo-particle pair dynamics, is the notion that rest mass in the form of particles can be created from pure energy in the form of a photon.
