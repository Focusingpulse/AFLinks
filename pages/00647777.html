Through a poetic-philosophical narrative, the (F1 and F2) foci are revealed as symbols of complementary opposites, approaching and separating in a continuous act of creation. The research demonstrates that the ellipse functions not only as a static locus of points but as a self￾regulating, dynamic structure governed by an "Internal Law of Balance" and a "Four￾Quarter Mathematical Repetition Program." This structure manifests as a continuous unit value exchange between the axes, analytically detailing how the ellipse is cyclically regenerated across four symmetrical quarters. This approach expands the current understanding of the ellipse, positioning it not merely as a defined curve, but as a structure that reveals an intrinsic and continuous mathematical process that necessitates radical revisions in the field of geometry.
