Title A Framework Hypothesis for the ZPE Field Read in full Link to paper (Internet Archive) Author(s) Paul A Stowe Keywords Zero Point Energy Field Published 1996 Read the full paper here (archived copy — the original link is no longer available) Abstract The discovery by Max Planck.s in 1900 that the emission of thermal radiation could only occur in discrete quantities related to a emission constant (called Planck's constant) and the subsequent evolution of quantum mechanics, has resulted in an inability to establish a standard foundation for all classes of physical phenomena. Recent efforts not withstanding (Superstring theories, SU(3)XSU(2)XU(1) ...etc), there still remains a distinct separation of quantum mechanics from other branches of physics, notably general relativity. This discussion suggests a common foundation for all physical phenomena and derives the known physical constants (h, e, o, ?o, , R). It also provides for the manifestation of the four known forces as well as a simple explanation of there existence. Overview Paul A. Stowe circulated this framework in July 1996 to the Usenet groups sci.physics , sci.physics.particle and alt.sci.physics.new-theories , describing it as the product of twenty-two years' work and offering it "in a spirit of cooperation and hope." The motivation is the treatment of fields. Stowe takes the divergences of quantum electrodynamics — an electron interacting with its own field — and the renormalisation procedure Bethe introduced to manage them as symptoms of a missing physical picture, and objects that virtual entities "can (and do) violate accepted physical laws," being licensed only by the uncertainty limit. His proposal is to eliminate both renormalisation and virtual fields by replacing the virtual field with a real physical medium. The medium is a superfluid.
