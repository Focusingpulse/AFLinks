We can still build that wall, but most of the illegal problem will be solved with the passing of a single law. Breaking the law has consequences. The Phoenix solution would be fair by giving them a chance to divest themselves of any property to have money for a new start when they return to their home country until they can apply legally to live in the USA. Note there is becoming a US legal resident or becoming a US legal citizen.
