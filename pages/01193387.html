(Dated: October 16, 2024) Abstract We study the head words of the Thai-English Student’s Dictionary compiled by Mary R. Haas, way back in 1964. We draw the natural logarithm of the number of head words, normalised, starting with a letter vs the natural logarithm of the rank of the letter, normalised.
