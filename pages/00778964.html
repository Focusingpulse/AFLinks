1 10/5/2025 A Practical Guide to Finding Optimal Strategy (An Intuitive Progression from 2x2 to NxN Games) Felipe A. Wescoup, PE 2320 Point Star Drive Arlington TX 76001 Abstract This paper provides a practical guide for determining the optimal mixed strategy in two-player, zero-sum games. It presents a method for calculating the Nash Equilibrium by starting with the well-understood 2x2 matrix and intuitively extending the logic to 3x3 and larger NxN scenarios. The purpose is not to derive new mathematical theory, but to make the powerful concepts of game theory accessible to a wider audience, such as coaches, athletes, and business strategists. This paper is supplemented by a GitHub repository containing a spreadsheet tool that performs the calculations, allowing for direct practical application of the concepts discussed. 1.0 Introduction: The Problem of Predictability 1.1 The Strategist's Dilemma Imagine a baseball pitcher with a fantastic 100-mph fastball. It's his best pitch. It’s powerful and hard to hit. Logic seems to dictate that he should throw it every single time. Why use a weaker pitch when he has a nearly perfect one? This is The Strategist's Dilemma. The dilemma is the conflict between using your single most effective option and the necessity of being unpredictable. 1. The Strength: Every competitor has a "go-to" move. It could be a tennis player's slice serve to the forehand, a soccer player's shot to the top-right corner of the goal, or a company's most popular product.
