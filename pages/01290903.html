Should we not wish for light , we turn a knob and we have sound , heat , motion , chemical action , magnetism . But let us consider how greatly superior is our cerebral mechanism . In order to light a theatre we require a wide space, a dynamo-electric machine of many horse-power, accumulators filling many receptacles, a considerable expense in fuel , and clever mechanicians .
