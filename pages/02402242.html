Tsentalovich2 1 Institute of Chemical Kinetics and Combustion. V.V. V.V. Voevodsky Institute of Chemical Kinetics and Combustion, Siberian Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences, Novosibirsk, Russia 2 Institute "International Tomographic Center" of the Siberian Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences v.tamozhnikova@alumni.nsu.ru Metal porphyrins are of great practical interest as catalysts for the oxidation of olefins and alcohols to carbonyl compounds [1], as well as for the oxidative cleavage of DNA [2]. Thin films of manganese (III) tetraphenylporphyrin chloride (Fig.1), which have thermal stability and good optical properties, can find application in solar energy [3]. The compound is also a model system for studying the photophysical and photochemical properties of photosensitizers used in photodynamic therapy (PDT). The paper studied the photochemistry of manganese (III) tetraphenylporphyrin chloride in an ethyl alcohol solution using stationary and laser flash photolysis methods. Photoreduction of the complex to manganese (II) tetraphenylporphyrin was observed. The quantum yields of the reaction varied significantly depending on the excitation wavelength. The ratio of the highest to the lowest quantum yield was 3800 (Fig. 2a).
