Lecture IV - GA 276. The Arts and Their Mission (1964)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 3 June 1923 · archived # 2,439,833
, Dornach Lecture IV The last two lectures concentrated on artistic feeling and creation. I wished to call attention to the fact that anthroposophical contemplation leads to a particular manner of beholding the world, which must lead, in turn, to an inner vitalization of the arts, present and future. At the end of yesterday's lecture I stressed the fact that, by gaining a direct relation to the spiritual, a person can acquire the forces necessary for the creation, out of his innermost core, of true art.
