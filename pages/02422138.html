PROGRESS ARTICLE PUBLISHED ONLINE: 31 JULY 2015 | DOI: 10.1038/NPHYS3404 Structured quantum waves Jérémie Harris1, Vincenzo Grillo2, Erfan Mafakheri3, Gian Carlo Gazzadi2, Stefano Frabboni2,3, Robert W. Boyd1,4 and Ebrahim Karimi1* The study of structured optical waves has enhanced our understanding of light and numerous experimental methods now enable the control of the angular momentum and radial distributions. Recently, these wavestructuring techniques have been successfully applied to the generation and shaping of electron beams, leading to promising practical and fundamental advances. Here, we discuss recent progress in the emerging field of electron beam shaping, and explore the unique attributes that distinguish electron beams from their photonic analogues.
