Author(s) William G Carnahan Keywords dynamic energy, stellar radiation, space, Cuanta, mass, momentum Published 1983 Pages 8-10 Abstract For several years we have felt challenged to throw more light on the good questions posed by splendid respondents relating to the above topic. They ask: "How many Cuanta are converging everywhere?" In other words, how much dynamic-energy-mass-momentum (DEMM) is converging today toward certain specific spots in space, - perhaps on the now vacant cc. of space where the pupil of your eye or the nail of your thumb was located six months ago?
