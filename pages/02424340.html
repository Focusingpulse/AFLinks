We obtained evidence that the light has a high degree of coherence because of (1) its photon count statistics, (2) its spectral distribution, (3) its decay behavior after exposure to light illumination, and (4) its transparency through optically thick materials. Moroever, DNA is apparently at least an important source, since conformational changes induced with ethidium bromide in vivo are clearly reflected by changes of the photon emission of cells. The physical properties of the radiation are described, taking DNA as an exciplex laser system, where a stable state can be reached far from thermal equilibrium at threshold. index Entries: Biological photon emission; coherent photon emission, from DNA; DNA-exciplex laser model; emission, of photons from DNA; photons, emission from DNA; laser, DNA photons as possible. *Author to whom all correspondence and reprint requests should be addressed.
