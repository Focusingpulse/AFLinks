Title Magnetic Force-Free Configurations for Thermonuclear Fusion Keywords force-free, spheromak, MHD theory Published 1988 Journal Physics Essays Volume 1 Number 2 Pages 92-101 Abstract The definition and properties of magnetic force-free states are analyzed rigorously. Actually, two inequivalent models are traditionally confused in describing force-free configurations. Topological and geometrical considerations determine the general type of magnetic field corresponding to a plasma in a sphere or compact torus and correct some long-standing errors. Magnetic flux conversion, observed in the spheromak and in the reversed-field pinch, follows from the principle of energy equipartition between orthogonal magnetic modes. These results suggest that the present-day theoretical conception of stable plasma equilibria may be based upon several fundamental misunderstandings.
