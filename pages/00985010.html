Prespacetime Journal | March 2022 | Volume 13 | Issue 1 | pp. 84-86 Physics of Virus: The Special Case of Novel Coronavirus Hosein Nasrolahpour* School of Computational Science and Engineering, University of Tehran, Tehran, Iran. Abstract In this short note we consider a possible application of fractal theory and fractional dynamics in the physics of virus. In particular, we investigate the special case of novel Coronavirus. We discuss about some physical characteristics of this virus, we emphasize on the decreasing fractal dimension of virus as a powerful strategy for a possible nanodrug design and finally we present a fractional modified model to the dynamical model of transmission of this virus. Keywords: Fractal Theory; Fractional Dynamics; Physics of Virus 1.
