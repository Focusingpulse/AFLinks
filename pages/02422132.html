SYSTEMATIZATION OF INFORMATION ON THE WEBSITE 2 3 A THEORY represents a contemplative and rational system of knowledge, in the frame of which some specific scientific issues, hypothesizes or laws lose their former autonomy, and on basis of which science builds itself. A theory has also a capability to predict events. 3 2 A LAW is an universal, verbally and/or mathematically expressed assumption of links between events, which can be demonstrated, and which describes the relationship between various scientific notions. Such laws are proposed to explain facts and are generally accepted as such by the scientific community. THEORETICAL KNOWLEDGE EMPIRICAL KNOWLEDGE 1 The scientific knowledge can be divided in two groups: the empirical knowledge and the theoretical knowledge, which are characterized by two interrelated, but also specific to each group, research activities. 1 EMPIRICAL KNOWLEDGE is the result of various representations of the reality, obtained through direct observations and studies of this reality. Empirical knowledge has a complex structure, with at least two sub-levels : observations and empirical facts (physical effects, rules, principles, heuristics, hypothesizes, models, patterns, axioms, lemmas, theorems, confirmed or unconfirmed facts, etc.) Valery Barygin
