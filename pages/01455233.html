The action that induces this link I call sympathetic planetary oscillation ." [ Keely , Keely and His Discoveries (underlining added)] Here we see clearly Keely 's use of the terms " positive " and " negative " which are exactly opposite to Russell 's use. To avoid this obvious and annoying confusion SVP uses the terms syntropy (attraction to center) and entropy (repulsion from center) wherever possible.
