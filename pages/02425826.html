Additional information on samples and methods Tektite samples analyzed in this study are ‘legacy samples’, meaning they have been described in scientific literature previously, by one of us (C. Koeberl). The tektite zircon grains were part of a secondary ion mass spectrometry study of U-Pb systematics by Deloule et al. (2001). Two of the grains analyzed, sample MN-d and MN-i, are listed in Table 1 of Deloule et al. (2001) as originating from tektite samples F16 and TC-1, respectively. The footnote to their Table 1 indicates that both samples are from Thailand. F16 was provided by D.S. Futrell, and TC-1 was provided by the Smithsonian Institution, Washington DC. No other sample location information (grid coordinates, etc.) was provided, other than a statement that both tektites originated from Thailand. Zircon sample MN-j was also part of the Deloule et al. (2001) study and is in one of 15 glass chips in the epoxy mount analyzed (mount MNbp). However, no data for grain MN-j was published in Deloule et al. (2001). The only information available for MN-j is that it is a tektite that originated from Thailand. Additional information on samples and methods. Additional SEM images. Additional EBSD images and data.
