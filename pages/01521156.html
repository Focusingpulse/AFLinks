Radar Signal Delay in the Dvali-Gabadadze-Porrati Gravity

· archived # 1,521,156
Title Radar Signal Delay in the Dvali-Gabadadze-Porrati Gravity Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Ioannis Iraklis Haranas , Omiros Ragos Keywords gravity, solar system Published 2012 Journal Proceedings of the NPA Volume 9 No. of pages 3 Pages 203-205 Abstract In this paper we examine the recently introduced Dvali-Gabadadze-Porrati (DGP) gravity model. We use a space-time metric in which the local gravitation source dominates the metric over the contributions from the cosmological flow. Anticipating ideal possible solar system effects, we derive expressions for the signal time delays in the vicinity of the Sun, and for various ranges of the angle theta of the signal approach. The time contribution due to DGP correction to the metric is found to be proportional to b 3/2 /c 2 r 0 . For r 0 equal to 5 Mpc and theta in the range [- Pi/3 , Pi/3 ], delta t is equal to 0.0001233 ps. This delay is extremely small to be measured by today's technology, but it could be probably measurable by future experiments. Overview This three-page paper, presented at the 19th Natural Philosophy Alliance conference in July 2012, computes a small general-relativistic correction within a specific modified-gravity model. It is conventional theoretical physics rather than a challenge to established theory, and the model it examines is a mainstream one. The Dvali–Gabadadze–Porrati model treats our four-dimensional spacetime as a brane embedded in a five-dimensional bulk, with a free crossover parameter r ₀ = κ²/2μ² marking the scale beyond which four-dimensional gravity gives way to five-dimensional behaviour. It was introduced as a way of producing accelerated cosmic expansion without dark energy, and it predicts small post-Einsteinian effects at local scales.
