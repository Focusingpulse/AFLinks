👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 10 June 1924 · archived # 2,436,348

Osmond Lecture IV [ 1 ] In the lecture yesterday we began to speak about the connection of man's life on Earth between birth and death with his other life in super-sensible worlds between death and a new birth, with special reference to karma.
