Kindness

· archived # 1,460,232
Cayce "Be kind and gentle. Remember, the soft word turns away wrath and it brings joy, the kind word as you have found in your own life has often made the day much brighter for you." [ Cayce (5098-1)] "Remember, the soft word turns away wrath and it brings joy , the kind word as ye have found in thine own experience oft has made the day much brighter for thee. Make many days brighter for others and in making them more and more in attune with love , patience , longsuffering , gentleness and kindness , ye will make for thyself a surety in those things that take hold on peace , harmony and joy . These should be a part of thine experience ever." Cayce (5098-1) " Try in thine own experience, each; that ye speak not for one whole day unkindly of any; that ye say not a harsh word to any, about any; and see what [such] a day would bring to you . . . " Cayce (262-106) " Not in some great deed of heroism ; not in some great speech or act that may be pointed to with pride - but rather in the little kindnesses from day to day. " Cayce (3795-1) "It isn't the great things. No great deeds of valor are accomplished without AGES of preparation in the SOUL of one that accomplishes same. It is just being kind , just being gentle, just being patient, FIRST with self and self's relationships to thy fellow man."
