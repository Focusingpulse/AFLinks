Determination of the Fatty Acids of Hibiscus Manihot Seed Oil by GC-MS Ri Hyon Il1 ,Yang Zheng Hyok2 , Son Kwen Il2 ,Ri Chel Jin3 (1,2,3 Department of chemical science, Kim Hyong Jik University of Education, Democratic People's Republic of Korea. Pyongyang.) Abstract The oil was extracted from Hibiscus manihot seed using Soxhlet extraction method, petroleum ethersoak method..The fatty acid compositions of seed oil were analyzed by GC/MS and Gas Chromatographic conditionwas optimized. The results showed that the content of linoleic acid, oleic acid and palmitoleic acid were 58.36 %, 1.66 % and21.95 % respectively and unsaturated fatty acids were 75.56 %. Key words GC-MS；fatty acids；Soxhlet extraction method. Hibiscus Manihot has the most medicinal and health functions in more than 200 okra plants and has a high medicinal value [1]. Hibiscus Manihot seeds are rich in unsaturated fatty acids such as oleic acid, linoleic acid, arachidonic acid and so on. Unsaturated fatty acids have a significant effect on lowering serum cholesterol in high-density lipoproteins, which in turn reduces the incidence of diseases such as hypertension, heart disease, and stroke, at the same time, unsaturated fatty acids play an important role in maintaining the structure and function of biofilms. Currently unsaturated fatty acid products have been widely used in medicine, nutritional supplements, health foods and other fields. Therefore, the determination of unsaturated fatty acids is of great significance for evaluating the medicinal value of Hibiscus Manihot seeds [2].
