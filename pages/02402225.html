GTM2026-main069 Prenucleoli-like solutions in the Cooperative Differential Games on Networks Yaroslavna Pankratova1 , Xinru Wang1 , Nadezhda Smirnova2 1 St Petersburg University 2 National Research University Higher School of Economics, Moscow, Russia yasyap@gmail.com Prenucleoli-like solutions in the Cooperative Differential Games on Networks Wang X., Pankratova Y. Smirnova N. We examine the differential cooperative game defined on a network, originally introduced by Petrosyan L. A. in 2010. Various cooperative solution concepts for this class of games have since been proposed and analyzed in the literature. The present work focuses on excess-based solutions, in particular the α‑prenucleolus, for differential cooperative games on networks. This solution concept generalizes the simplified modified prenucleolus introduced by S. I. Tarashnina for static cooperative games and can also be regarded as an extension of the classical prenucleolus, a well‑known single‑valued solution concept alongside the Shapley value. The underlying principle of the α‑prenucleolus is to balance the constructive (coalition formation) and blocking (opposition) capabilities of coalitions through a parameter α. Given a characteristic function defined along the optimal cooperative trajectory, the α‑prenucleolus is characterized as the lexicographic minimizer of the α‑excess vector. Several illustrative examples are provided to demonstrate the properties of this solution concept in the context of differential cooperative games on networks and to investigate its coincidence with Shapley value.
