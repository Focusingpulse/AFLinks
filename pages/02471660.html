A News Roundup of Tesla Technology (Jan. 1991) by Steven Elswick Extraordinary Science January, 1991 Page number(s): 35 About the Museum... At the Nikola Tesla Museum of Science & Industry, Ltd is a small - but growing - body of dedicated researchers who are re-examining many of the theories and patents of Nikola Tesla. At this time, the research is funded by the museum through donations, or alternately, out of the researchers' own pockets. Projects funded through donations are placed on permanent display at the museum, while projects funded by the researchers are often placed on loan to the museum. Results of the ongoing research will be on display at the museum and published in Extraordinary Science . A great time to visit the Museum and review the progress of these projects will be during the monthly Tesla meetings sponsored by the International Tesla Society, Inc. on the last Saturday of every month. These meetings are free and open to the public. General admission to the museum is FREE! The regular operating hours of the museum are 10AM till 6PM Monday through Saturday. However, private evening tours and demonstrations can be arranged. Organizations which would like to hold meetings or sponsor open houses at the museum need to contact museum to make arrangements.
