This is my first Window Motor Replication. I wanted to make it simple. So I hollowed a 2 X 4 and used that as my base. It is about 4.5"s in length. The rotor is made with six 1" X 2" X.5" neo magnets on it. They were taken off of another project. The shaft is .5" aluminum and the bearings are basic steel bearings I got for $5 each at VXB. I cleaned the heavy grease out of them. The wire is trifilar wound with 650 turns of 30ga x2 and 28ga x1. The battery is a Craftsman 19.2V NiCad that generally charges conventionally to 22.5 Volts. I am running it on a basic SSG circuit for know, but plan on putting together a half bipolar Bedini/Cole circuit to see what that can do. I have estimated the shaft to be turning between 450-500 rpm's. The amp draw is at 90ma.
