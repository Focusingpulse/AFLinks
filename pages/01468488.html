The wonders of Nature's laws in the developments of harmonies , consist in the beautiful adaption of keyed and all other musical instruments to a range commensurate with human powers. The chromatic scale of twelve notes (the thirteenth being the octave ) is not the scale of Nature . To construct a musical instrument upon real divisions of musical tones , each of them being in correct ratio with the others, it would be necessary to have a larger number of tones to the octave . In the development of harmonies on the natural system , we trace the perfect adaptation of means to ends, meeting the intricacies of every musical instrument , including that most perfect of all— the human voice .
