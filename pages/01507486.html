Ancient Engineering at Puma Punku and Tiwanaku

· archived # 1,507,486
Every once in a while you get the "how the heck did they do that!" moment, and here it is for you - http://www.world-mysteries.com/pumapunku1.jpg Lost civilizations of the Andes (2) Machined from diorite ( I say machined because saw marks have been found, and the accuracy of the fine holes is around 0.1mm), copper tools wouldn't touch the stuff, so we are left wondering what was used to make them? Also - why so complicated ? These "H" blocks interlock - and other stones have used metal tie-bars - Weiging up to 200 Tonnes - these stones were transported from over 100 miles away at an altitude where no trees grow (no sleds, no rollers) Plus - it was destroyed violently - then burried under 3 Metres of earth. Is this a "plunder ditch" - or a direct hit ? - Also worth looking at YouTube clips of it, as some of the carvings look like a settlement out of Star Wars!
