Natalie Nagel Born 1960 (age 65–66) Residence Surrey, British Columbia, Canada Nationality Israeli Known for Electromagnetism , Fractals , Unification , Electric Universe , Structure Scientific career Fields Biophysics Researcher , Productivity Consultant Natalie Nagel (born 1960) is an Israeli-born researcher and consultant based in Surrey, British Columbia, who works on parallels between plasma behaviour in astrophysics and the behaviour of water in living systems. She took part in the 20th Natural Philosophy Alliance Conference and the Electric Universe 2013 conference. Education Undergraduate degree — University of California, Irvine Graduate certificate — University of Cambridge Before turning to research she spent eleven years instructing engineers, dentists, doctors and other professionals at an international institute specialising in corporate globalisation, teaching some 3,000 people. Universal Fractal Flow Nagel's single published paper, Universal Fractal Flow: PLASMA to plasma (2013), sets out a framework she calls Universal Fractal Flow . Its premise is that a small set of forms — spirals, branching, hexagons, and the filamentary Birkeland current — recur from galactic to cellular scales because electron flow organises itself the same way at every size, and that the same principle operates within the body through what she terms "plasma water". The framework is explicitly synthetic, drawing together the Electric Universe cosmology of David Talbott and Wallace Thornhill, Mae-Wan Ho's proposal of quantum coherence in biological water, Gerald Pollack's work on ordered water near surfaces, Robert Becker's writing on electromagnetism and living tissue, and Benoit Mandelbrot's fractal geometry. Nagel also frames the argument through a reading of the opening chapter of Breshit , in which light spoken into existence is identified with electromagnetism organising water.
