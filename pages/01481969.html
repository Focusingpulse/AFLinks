nitrous oxide

· archived # 1,481,969
Nitrous oxide also may be used in a monopropellant rocket. In the presence of a heated catalyst, N 2 O will decompose exothermically into nitrogen and oxygen, at a temperature of approximately 1,070 °F (577 °C). Nitrous oxide is said to deflagrate at approximately 600 °C (1,112 °F) at a pressure of 309 psi (21 atmospheres). At 600 psi, for example, the required ignition energy is only 6 joules, whereas N 2 O at 130 psi a 2,500-joule ignition energy input is insufficient. Nitrous oxide may be used as an oxidiser in a rocket motor. Nitrous oxide is prepared on an industrial scale by careful heating of ammonium nitrate at about 250 C, which decomposes into nitrous oxide and water vapor .
