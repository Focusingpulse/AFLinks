Bibliographie 1919-1933 WWW.ORGONOMIE.NET Impressum Nachrichtenbrief Jerome Eden ORANUR Kinder der Zukunft Orgonometrie Buchbesprechungen Chronik Glossar Bibliographie Links BIBLIOGRAPHIE DER ORGONOMIE BIBLIOGRAPHY OF ORGONOMY compiled by Peter Nasselstein This bibliography presents the complete orgonomic literature since 1919. 100 years of continuous orgonomic research. The compilation is organized in five parts: 1. The psychoanalytic, sex-economic, and orgonomic writings of Reich and his co-workers between 1919 and 1957, organized chronologically. Only Reich's books and articles are indexed for cross-referencing. A list of contents of books and longer articles is included. Also the published diaries and correspondence is documented. Letters cited in Reich's published works are not listed as far as they were not posthumously published by others. 2. A list of the non-fiction books mentioned prominently in Reich's writings. 3. The orgonomic writings of Reich's and E.F. Baker's pupils after 1957. Not included is the kind of "Reichian" literature Reich disapproved of at the end of his life (cf. Record of a Friendship ). The material is organized systematically from Cosmic Orgone Engineering down to the humanities. Roughly following Reich's development in reverse. 4. A list of the non-fiction books mentioned prominently in the writings of the orgonomists. 5. The sources where the student of orgonomy can buy the necessary literature for his studies.
