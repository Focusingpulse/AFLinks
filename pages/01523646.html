Peter Moddel Born Ireland Residence Fribourg, Fribourg, Switzerland Nationality Irish Known for Philosophy of perception, matter and consciousness Scientific career Fields Philosophy , Astronomy Peter Moddel is an Irish-born philosopher, author and amateur astronomer based in the canton of Fribourg, Switzerland. His work centres on the philosophy of perception, the nature of matter, and the role of consciousness, developing an approach in which philosophical reflection is grounded in observation rather than in abstract ideas. Biography Peter Moddel was born in Ireland and settled in Switzerland, living in the Gruyère region of the canton of Fribourg. His formation drew on studies across several disciplines, including philosophy, literature, physics and pedagogy, and was shaped by periods of living in both the West and the East as well as intervals of personal retreat. Astronomy has been a central activity throughout his life. His interest in building telescopes led him to the study of vision and colour theory, and the formation of colour became a subject of his personal research. A follower of the work of the astronomer Halton Arp since the Palomar era, Moddel describes himself as a passionate amateur astronomer. He has also worked with students and teachers, helping them recognise the mental structures that guide individual ways of learning. Work Moddel's philosophical work explores the relationship between mind, self and the physical world, with the role of consciousness forming what he calls the common thread of his search for solutions to specific problems in philosophy. He examines how thought produces concepts, how the senses give rise to perceived qualities such as sound, colour and distance, and what the notion of "I" signifies.
