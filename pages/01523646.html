His work centres on the philosophy of perception, the nature of matter, and the role of consciousness, developing an approach in which philosophical reflection is grounded in observation rather than in abstract ideas. Biography Peter Moddel was born in Ireland and settled in Switzerland, living in the Gruyère region of the canton of Fribourg. His formation drew on studies across several disciplines, including philosophy, literature, physics and pedagogy, and was shaped by periods of living in both the West and the East as well as intervals of personal retreat.
