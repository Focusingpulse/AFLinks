Fig. 1. - This figure shows the major and minor measured in commas and placed directly as they stand related, major and relative minor , the minor being set a minor third lower than the major . The interval between C and E in the minor is an 8 -and- 9-comma interval ; between C and E in the major it is a 9 -and- 8-comma one. This difference arises from the minor D being a comma lower than the major D . In all the other intervals minor and major are the same. [ Scientific Basis and Build of Music , page 120 ] Fig. 2. - In this figure the two modes are placed in their inverse relation, in order to show the notes standing opposite each other in their duality . Here the two D's come also opposite each other, inasmuch as in the two modes the C-D-E interval is inverted, becoming C D E in the one and E D C in the other. And so the 9-comma second between C and D in the major comes opposite the 9-comma second between D and E in the minor , and the two 8-comma seconds , of course, come opposite each other also.[ Scientific Basis and Build of Music , page 120 ] Fig. 3. - This is a set of pendulum lengths for three octaves , given merely to assist any tyro who might wish to try them, but might find difficulty in calculating them.[ Scientific Basis and Build of Music , page 120 ] Ramsay PLATE VI .
