I don't post a lot of new threads. I tend to read more and think about what going on. I try to step back and see the big picture. It is a strange thing to me that there are so many claims from so many different areas of study of free energy. Granted, many claims can probably be dismissed due to errors or misunderstandings. But to me, there are enough claims to suspect that free energy is definitely showing up in many different ways. When I was younger and investigating free energy, I had this mindset that there must be particular way free energy can be harvested and I needed to find that one way. It was frustrating to invest time and money investigating, only to hear of claims of FE in other strange areas of study. At that point I started questioning myself wondering if I was studying in the correct area.
