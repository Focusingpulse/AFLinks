21. Gnosis and the Apocalypse — GA 87. Ancient Mysteries and Christianity

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 29 March 1902 · archived # 2,418,434
, Berlin Translated by Steiner Online Library 21. Gnosis and the Apocalypse [ 1 ] Last time, we began to characterize the Apocalypse. It represents a later product of the various views that took shape around the time of Christ’s birth—specifically, those that developed in the schools from which the deeper theological teachings of Christianity emerged, teachings that only later took on a popular form. We might imagine the process that took place there something like this: in the most diverse places of learning, in the most diverse mystery schools of the first and perhaps even the second century
