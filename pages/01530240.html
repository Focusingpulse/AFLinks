ERRATUM Further evidence of nuclear reactions in the Pd/D lattice: emission of charged particles Stanislaw Szpak & Pamela A. Mosier-Boss & Frank E. Gordon # Springer-Verlag 2007 Erratum to: Naturwissenschaften DOI 10.1007/s00114-007-0221-7 In our above-mentioned paper, we describe a model that results in the generation of low-energy neutrons. We find that Widom and Larsen have done a thorough mathematical treatment that describes one mechanism to create such low￾energy neutrons. This mathematical treatment has been described in the following reference: Widom A, Larsen L (2006) Ultra low momentum neutron catalyzed nuclear reactions on metallic hydride surfaces. Eur Phys J C46:107–111. We regret the omission of this reference.
