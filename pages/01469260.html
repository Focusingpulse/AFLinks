In other tuning systems, " semitone " refers to a family of intervals that may vary both in size and name. In Pythagorean tuning, seven semitones out of twelve are diatonic, with ratio 256:243 or 90.2 cents (Pythagorean limma), and the other five are chromatic, with ratio 2187:2048 or 113.7 cents (Pythagorean apotome ); they differ by the Pythagorean comma of ratio 531441:524288 or 23.5 cents."
