Chapter 8. The Phenomena of the World of Colour — Goethe's Conception of the World (1928)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 1,585,507
The feeling that “great works of Art are produced by men according to true and natural laws” was an ever-present stimulus to Goethe to search for these laws of artistic creation. He was convinced that the effectiveness of a work of Art must depend on a natural conformity to law that it reveals. He wishes to discover this conformity to law. He wanted to know why the highest works of Art are at the same time the loftiest productions of Nature.
