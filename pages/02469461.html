Astor is Buried, First Wife is Absent New York Sun May 5th, 1912 Page number(s): 11 Service at Rhinebeck and Interment in Trinity Cemetery. BODY PUT BESIDE FATHER The Third of This Name to Rest in the Family Vault. The funeral of Col. John Jacob Astor was held early yesterday afternoon in the Church of the Messiah at Rhinebeck, and within a few hours after the conclusion of the simple ceremonies his body was laid by that of his father in the family vault in Trinity Cemetery, at 155th street and Amsterdam avenue. Only Mrs. Madeleine Force Astor, his widow; his children, Vincent and Muriel; his sister, Mrs. Orme Wilson, and a few intimate friends were within the cemetery when the brief committal service of the Episcopal Church was read by the Rev. Dr. William T. Manning, rector of Trinity Church, but standing in the side streets, along Riverside Drive and clinging to the iron railing that tops the cemetery wall were thousands of curious persons who had stood for hours awaiting the cortege and who observed every movement made by members of the funeral party. This crowd was in direct contrast to the smaller gathering that greeted the arrival of the funeral party at Rhinebeck. Deputy Sheriff Witherwax of Dutchess county and six of his men, reenforced by men from the Astor estate, were there armed with heavy canes to prevent the curious from venturing beyond the ropes that were stretched about the church.
