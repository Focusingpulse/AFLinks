Proposal For Public-financed Independent Research Centers

· archived # 1,512,037
We know that large businesses and corrupt governments actively suppress technologies and research activities which could liberate humanity from modern financial (and economic), mental (propaganda) and military slavery. Before Internet era research carried out by lone enthusiasts was very inefficient because of lack of resources (information, equipment, materials, research force (usually only one person) and time). Everyone was isolated and nearly completely blocked with suppression from official science and own small resources. Even such geniuses like Tesla had their greatest inventions stolen and suppressed. With help of Internet and forums (like this one) lone researchers were able to unite, gain access to independent information, exchange with "unofficial" ideas (not published in official magazines) and start coordination of their research. This allowed significant increase in the efficiency and has lead to some research results. But there are still some drawbacks. Only few people are actively experimenting while majority is passive and only discussing ideas (ideas are useless without experiments). Those few enthusiasts are united on the level of information (exchange with knowledge and ideas) but separated in terms of resources - each of them has limited equipment, materials, usually work alone and can devote only small part of their time to the research and experiments.
