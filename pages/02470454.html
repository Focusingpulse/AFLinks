Your motor has reached that point of perfection which all builders of motors have attempted to reach, and must therefore be the pattern to be adopted in the future.” The Jencick Mfg. Co., Port Chester, N. Y., manufacturers of these motors, at the close of the show reported many sales of their popular six-cylinder type 206 h.p. racing model. A prominent feature of this engine is the hand-made crank shaft, turned from a solid slab of steel, which idea is quite without precedent in the construction of marine motors.
