Joseph Papp, Air Inert Gas Design, by Heinz Klostermann

· archived # 1,508,573
- Running a Papp engine on just atmospheric air. - No more mixing expensive noble gases in a vacuum. - Working test model already built. - Worked with Bob and Tom Rohner back in 2005. - Already has patents; US7076950; US20060101816. - Ready for mass production of a 2 or 4 cylinder engine within a year. - Electric generator design for 50 kWp (built by DOE) can run on his Papp air design. Heinz Klostermann has been working on this for the past couple of decades just released a video of his working air driven Papp engine model and talks about how it works.
