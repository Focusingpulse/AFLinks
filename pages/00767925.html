The kth power expectile estimation and testing ∗Fuming Lin School of Mathematics and Statistics, Sichuan University of Science & Engineering, Sichuan Zigong, China Yingying Jiang School of Mathematics and Statistics, Sichuan University of Science & Engineering, Sichuan Zigong, China Yong Zhou Institute of Statistics and Interdisciplinary Sciences and School of Statistics, Faculty of Economics and Management, East China Normal University, Shanghai, China Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing, China February 16, 2021 Abstract This paper develops the theory of the kth power expectile estimation and considers its relevant hypothesis tests for coefficients of linear regression models. We prove that the asymptotic covariance matrix of kth power expectile regression converges to that of quantile regression as k converges to one, and hence provide a moment estimator of asymptotic matrix of quantile regression.
