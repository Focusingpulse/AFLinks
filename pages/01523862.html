Saturday: Cosmology & Expansion Tectonics Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Speaker John B Eichler Date 12 December 2020 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording Saturday: Cosmology & Expansion Tectonics was a live session presented by John B Eichler on 12 December 2020 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. John will present his worldview of Cosmology and how it relates to the geophysics of Expansion Tectonics. He weaves together the concepts of many famous scientists into an understandable framework based more on laboratory experimentation than abstract theory. Summary John B Eichler — a computer scientist from the Illinois Institute of Technology who worked on Univac machines in the vacuum-tube era and designed blast shields for the Atomic Energy Commission — presenting the connection he sees between cosmology and expansion tectonics: that one must know how the solar system was assembled to understand what is happening to the earth. Method He began with what he thinks goes wrong. Where hands-on experiment is possible, results repeat; the earth and the universe do not allow it, and the strongest evidence is where laboratory work backs the theoretical construct. So one should question any proposed esoteric law that has no empirical verification, and remember that a concept being visualisable does not make it obey the laws of physics. He then defined worldview as what the scientist brings to the table — the paradigms absorbed from classes and books. These are two-edged: without a framework we could not make sense of anything, and with one we cannot see past it. To a hammer, everything looks like a nail. Alfvén's plasma universe Eichler found Hannes Alfvén 's Evolution of the Solar System by accident in a university library and it reorganised his thinking.
