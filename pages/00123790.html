Although these results are generally accepted individually, they should be considered in conjunction with each other. Here we examine the junction of the interior and ex￾terior vacuum intervals across a thin spherical shell. The results show Birkhoff’s theorem is not self consistent. The interior interval cannot be the Minkowski metric and, for a non-static shell, the overall solution can￾not be static. As such, Birkhoff’s theorem should not be relied upon as a basis for determining the properties of a given space-time.
