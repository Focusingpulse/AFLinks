GC2026-STP041 Modeling of O2 (b1Σ g +) Atmospheric bands in the polar ionosphere Andrey S. Kirillov1 , Yurii N. Kulikov1 1 Polar Geophysical Institute of the Kola Scientific Center of the Russian Academy of Sciences, Apatity, Russia kirillov@pgia.ru The quenching coefficients of singlet oxygen O2 (b1Σ g +) in inelastic collisions with O2 and N2 molecules are calculated using quantum-chemical approximations. The coefficients are used to determine the vibrational populations of electronically excited molecular oxygen at altitudes of Earth's high-latitude ionosphere during auroral electron precipitation. The results of calculations of the relative populations of singlet oxygen O2 (b1Σ g +,v) formed during the precipitation of high-energy particles into Earth's atmosphere are presented. The contributions of various processes to the formation of singlet oxygen O2 (b1Σ g +,v=0-2) and to the emission of Atmospheric bands at 762, 771, 780 nm of molecular oxygen associated with radiative transitions to the ground state of X3Σ g – are investigated. It is shown that the dominant contribution to the formation of singlet oxygen O2 (b1Σ g +,v=0-2) and the emission of Atmospheric bands at 762, 771, 780 nm is related with inelastic collisions of O2 molecules with auroral electrons and with metastable atomic oxygen O(1D).
