The ‘Physical Prophet’ and the Powers of the Imagination Part II A case-study on dowsing and the naturalisation of the moral (1685-1710) Abstract Relying on the results of the fist paper of this pair (Vermeir, 2004), which argued the importance of theories of the imagination in debates on divination, I unearth the role of the imagination in a discussion on dowsing. References to the imagination often stayed implicit because of its negative associations, but I show in detail how the imagination was used to negotiate between the material and the spiritual, and between the natural, the supernatural and the moral.
