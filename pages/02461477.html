Keywords: Benzene-1,2,4,5-tetracarboxylate; Metal-organic-framework, Crystal structure; Template synthesis; Diaminononane Abstract. Triclinic single crystals of Cu4(H3N-(CH2)9NH3)(OH)2[C6H2(COO)4]25H2O have been prepared in aqueous solution at 80 °C in the presence of 1,9-diaminononane. Space group P-1 (no. 2) with a = 1057.5(2), b = 1166.0(2), c = 1576.7(2) pm,  = 106.080(10)°,  = 90.73(2)° and  = 94.050(10)°. The four crystallographic independent Cu2+ ions are surrounded by five oxygen atoms each with Cu−O distances between 191.4(3) and 231.7(4) pm.
