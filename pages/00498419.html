A Geometric Argument For the Irrationality of Pi Timothy W. Jones Abstract Using tangent lines to the unit circle, we give an argument that shows π is irrational. Theorem 1. π is irrational. Proof. If π is rational, there exists an angle in (π/2, π) such that the slope of the tangent line to the unit circle at this angle equals π, but the slope of such a line at angle π is undefined, a contradiction. References [1] I. Niven, A simple proof that π is irrational, Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. 53 (1947) 509.
