Tesla Switch - updated December 12 2008 This is a self charging battery system originally developed by Ronald Brandt (Friend of Tesla). This circuit was also experimented with by John Bedini. The concept of this device is to run a load and keep the batteries charged. Applications include electric motor operation and home power usage.
