Heinlein's great story of alien invasion and possession, appeared in l95l first serially in Horace Gold's pioneering new magazine, Galaxy, and shortly thereafter in book form from Doubleday. Anticipating Jack Finney's Invasion Of The Body Snatchers by half a decade, this novel of the alien life forms who invade Earth to possess and conquer, was filmed in l994 and has never been out of print here or in most of the foreign countries in which it was reprinted. Heinlein (l907-l988) brought to The Puppet Masters his own compelling view of human individuality and its unconquerable will to struggle. A disabled naval Lieutenant Commander, and l928 graduate of the Naval Academy, Heinlein was forced for health reasons to retire from the Navy in l937 and turned to freelance writing and subsequently science fiction as consonant with the material independence he sought and as a vehicle for his powerfully individualized vision.
