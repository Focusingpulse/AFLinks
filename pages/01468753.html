Universal Law

· archived # 1,468,753
Universal Laws are laws of Nature that apply everywhere, universally. Also known as Law of Love . “ Universal law is as im­mutable as the seasons, as inevitable as the course of the stars, and no metaphysical maestro is going to alter these inevitables in ten lessons or in ten million lessons. The law of cause and effect is as in­evitable as day and night, as certain as the tides, and as constant as the ages. This law says that ‘as ye sow, so shall ye reap’. What you earn comes to you, what you have not earned can never be yours, and neither god nor man can alter the complexion of these facts.” [ Manly Palmer Hall ] Hughes CHAPTER V.
