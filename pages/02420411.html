(Slides 37‐40) 2. (2000‐2003) ‐ Invented an imaginary helium retention principle to explain what appeared to him as too little helium‐4 for sample #1 to support his hypothesis. (2007) ‐ Added a new, unmeasured, helium‐4 data point at 1500 hours (Slide 66) 9. (2007) ‐ Shifted helium‐4 data point #3 from 1172 hours to 530 hours (Slides 68‐ 69) 10.
