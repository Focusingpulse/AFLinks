caption Wikipedia Dispute : wikipedia:Spacetime This Natural Philosophy wiki page disputes content found on Wikipedia page wikipedia:Spacetime Spacetime curvature is the proposal, central to general relativity , that gravitation is not a force acting between bodies but a property of the geometry through which they move. Mass and energy deform a four-dimensional continuum of space and time; bodies then follow the straightest available paths through that deformed geometry, and what Newton described as an attractive force appears as an artefact of the shape of the arena. It is the single most disputed idea documented on this wiki. Roughly 184 pages engage with it directly — some 84 papers, 44 scientist biographies and 14 books — and around 450 discuss curvature in some form. That places it above push gravity , tired light and the Casimir effect in the volume of material collected here. What the standard account claims Albert Einstein 's field equations relate the curvature of spacetime at a point to the energy and momentum present there. The account is economical: one geometric statement replaces both the gravitational force and the flat background of special relativity, and it has passed every precision test put to it, from the perihelion advance of Mercury and the bending of starlight to the Shapiro delay, gravitational redshift, frame dragging, the orbital decay of binary pulsars and the direct detection of gravitational waves in 2015. Its critics do not generally dispute those measurements. What they dispute is the interpretation — whether geometry is the cause of gravitation or merely an efficient bookkeeping for it. The objection from mathematisation Before the individual alternatives, it is worth stating the objection that most of them share, because it explains why they take the form they do.
