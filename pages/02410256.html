Reincarnation and Karma [ 1 ] There are mysteries of the world that interest those who wish to delve deeper into the structure, into the fabric of our existence. Such mysteries of the world include, for example, the following: Where do matter and forces come from? Where does life in the world come from? Where does the purposefulness in nature come from, and where does that which we call consciousness come from?
