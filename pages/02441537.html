Lecture VIII - GA 293. The Study of Man

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 29 August 1919 · archived # 2,441,537
, Stuttgart Lecture VIII We saw yesterday that we can only understand memory, the power of remembering, if we connect it with sleeping and waking, which are more open to outer observation. You will see from this that it must be our constant endeavour in our pedagogy to connect the unknown with the known, even in the formation of spiritual ideas. You may say that sleeping and waking are actually even more obscure than remembering and forgetting, and therefore will not help much towards a comprehension of remembering and forgetting.
