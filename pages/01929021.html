The Department of Health commissioned a report two years ago to explore the legal and ethical implications of xenotransplantation, and to help formulate a strategy for proceeding with the contentious technology. The independent advisers from the University of Glasgow warn that xenotransplantation might have to be abandoned in favour of other alternatives. The report argues that not only has the Government ignored ethical and public concerns over the technology, but that using it on patients could prove so dangerous that the Government could face multi-million pound compensation claims. Professor Sheila McLean and Dr Laura Williamson from the University of Glasgow spent 16 months putting together the 700-page document.
