clang

· archived # 1,469,858
verb: if something made of metal clangs , or if you clang it, it makes a loud sound noun: a loud resonant repeating noise ("He could hear the clang of distant bells") verb: make a loud noise Music 1) Timbre . Quality of tone . 2) The peculiar "ringing" noise or din produced by the clash of metals, or the blast of loud wind instruments. CLANG : Tones sounded together not necessarily harmoniously. [See pages 145-147 of Tyndall, John; Sound; Longmans, Green, and Co., London, 1893] See Also Beat Chord Difference Tone Figure 8.6 - Difference Tones Great Pyramid Power of Beat Harmonics
