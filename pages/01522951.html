Franklin Hu Born ( 1963-11-01 ) November 1, 1963 (age 62) Residence Seattle, WA, United States Nationality US Alma mater Massachusetts Institute of Technology Known for Unification theories , Gravity , Atomic structure , electron–positron aether Scientific career Fields Software Engineer , Physics , Cosmology Franklin Hu (born November 1, 1963) is an American software engineer and independent researcher who develops alternative theories in physics. Based in the Seattle, Washington area, he has worked since 2003 on a self-styled "theory of everything" that seeks to explain a wide range of physical phenomena using only the electron, the positron, and the electrostatic force. His ideas lie outside the mainstream of accepted physics, and he has been an active member of the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society , both presenting his own work and hosting the society's online science discussion series. Education B.S. in computer science — Massachusetts Institute of Technology, 1986 His undergraduate thesis described a lexically based approach to natural language processing, work for which U.S. Patent 5,297,040 was subsequently awarded. Biography His professional career has been spent as a software engineer and software entrepreneur, with positions at Data General Corporation, Mosaic Software (where he worked on the TWIN spreadsheet), Software Publishing Corporation (Harvard Graphics), and Microsoft. In 1991 he founded Intellect Systems in Renton, Washington, which produced the WinProof grammar-checking program, and from 1997 he worked at Concur Technologies in Redmond, Washington, on early web-hosted expense-reporting software. He also developed the Seattle PictureBox screen saver for the retail market. Hu pursues his physics research independently in his spare time. He resides in the Seattle area and describes himself as the father of five children.
