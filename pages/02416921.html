From Buddha to Christ [ 1 ] Within our European intellectual culture today, one can utter the name of Buddha with feelings quite different from those of fifty years ago; indeed, one must bear in mind that the greatest mind of our culture, Goethe, knew nothing of Buddha. It is precisely here that we can see how feelings change. There is widespread interest in Buddha today. Where does this interest come from, which is present across broad segments of society?
