Introduction: The Shift from Functionalism to Computational Ontology In the discipline of computer science, algorithms and mathematical formulas are traditionally viewed through the lens of functionalism: they are defined by what they do. The Secure Hash Algorithm (SHA-256) is functionally categorized as a cryptographic primitive for ensuring data integrity through collision-resistant mapping. However, strictly functional definitions fail to address the deeper, almost metaphysical inquiry into the nature of these constructs. To understand "what is going on" when BBP(0) initiates an "infinite dump" of data, or when SHA collapses that data into a fixed state, we must pivot from functionalism to Computational Ontology.
