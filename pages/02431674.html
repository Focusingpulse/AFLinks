The anti-clutter module

· archived # 2,431,674
Anti-clutter mod. I have only been using this Mod for a short time. See details Here . It is one of those things that I was a test subject on but did not know it for the last month or so. Anyway. I finished building my barn about 10 months ago. But I never did finish building the work benches or doing any organization in the barn. It was a MESS. Stuff piled up everywhere, hardly any room to walk in a 1200 square foot barn!!! Well, last week I get bit by the bug of getting it done (not knowing that I was on this anti-clutter mod). I built the work benches, shelves, and hung the pegboard. I organized all the lumber, went thru all the piles of stuff, and moved some stuff from the shed into the barn. I now have more stuff in the barn than before, but have over 70% of the floor space has nothing on it! It looks so nice. My daughter walked into it yesterday and asked where did all the stuff go? She was surprised to learn there was even more stuff in there than before, it was just organized! I have to finish hanging tools up on the peg board, will get that done this week. This is a Great Module!
