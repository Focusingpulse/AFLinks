Hany Ahmed Residence Cranston, Rhode Island, United States Nationality USA Scientific career Fields Recorded interest: magnets and magnetism Hany Ahmed is an independent researcher in the United States, recorded as a member of the World Science Database (record 2785). His stated interest is given as magnets and magnetism . That record is, so far as this wiki can establish, the whole of what is known about him. This article is a stub, and the section below sets out what has been checked so that the search need not be repeated. What is recorded The World Science Database holds a member record giving his name and his country — nothing else. It carries no photograph, no title, no website and no dates, and the record's schema has no field for a city or for research interests. The two further details long carried on this page — that he lives in Cranston, Rhode Island , and that his subject is magnets — therefore cannot be traced to any source now held. They were present when the page was imported from a text file in December 2016, and presumably came from the member profile formerly published at naturalphilosophy.org. That profile cannot be recovered. The Internet Archive holds captures of 2,361 of those member profiles, numbered between 1 and 3102, but record 2785 is not among them . No publications are recorded. He has no paper, talk, book or conference presentation catalogued in the database or on this wiki, no page here links to his, and he does not appear in the Worldwide List of Dissident Scientists .
