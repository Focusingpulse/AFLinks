GUIDELINES FOR USE OF HYDROGEN FUEL IN COMMERCIAL VEHICLES Final Report November 2007 FOREWORD This document is intended to be a safety reference for commercial vehicle fleet owners and operators that use vehicles or auxiliary power units powered by hydrogen. It was designed to provide commercial vehicle owners and operators with a basic understanding of the properties and characteristics of hydrogen, descriptions of the types of systems that might use hydrogen fuel on commercial vehicles, and practical guidelines for the safe use of hydrogen, both on vehicles and in vehicle maintenance and storage facilities. Hydrogen properties and characteristics are significantly different from those of other commercial motor fuels, such as gasoline and diesel fuel, and commercial vehicle systems that use hydrogen fuel can also be significantly different from typical gasoline or diesel engines. An understanding of these differences is important to understanding what the operator of a vehicle powered by hydrogen should and should not do in order to maintain safety during transportation.
