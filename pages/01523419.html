Mayeul Arminjon Born ( 1954-12-06 ) December 6, 1954 (age 71) Residence Grenoble, France Nationality French Known for A scalar ether theory of gravitation; the Dirac equation in curved spacetime Scientific career Fields Mechanics of materials; gravitation; foundations of quantum mechanics Institutions CNRS, Laboratoire 3SR, Grenoble Mayeul Arminjon (born 6 December 1954) is a French researcher who spent his career as a Chargé de Recherche of the CNRS, in its section of theoretical physics, attached to the Laboratoire « Sols, Solides, Structures – Risques » (3SR) in Grenoble. He is unusual among the authors catalogued on this wiki in two respects. He came to gravitation in mid-career from an established position in a quite different field — the mechanics of materials, where he worked on polycrystal plasticity, homogenization and micro-macro transitions — and he has published his alternative theory in ordinary refereed journals and on arXiv rather than only in the dissident literature. His work since 1991 falls into two connected parts: a scalar ether theory of gravitation , a preferred-frame alternative to general relativity in which gravity is treated as a pressure force, and a series of papers on the Dirac equation in curved spacetime and what it takes for quantum mechanics to be well defined there.
