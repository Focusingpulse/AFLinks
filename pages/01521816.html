The Orgone Accumulator Handbook: Construction Plans Experimental Use and Protection Against Toxic Energy Book Author James DeMeo Language English Subject Wilhelm Reich 's orgone accumulator: theory, construction and use Published 1989; revised editions to 2010 Publisher Natural Energy Works, Ashland, Oregon Pages 155 (1989); 248 (2010) ISBN 9780962185502 The Orgone Accumulator Handbook is a manual by James DeMeo , first published in 1989 by his own imprint Natural Energy Works. It sets out Wilhelm Reich 's account of orgone energy , gives plans for building an orgone accumulator , and offers instructions for using one and for avoiding what Reich called deadly orgone. It carries a foreword by Eva Reich , Wilhelm Reich's daughter, and an appendix reporting a controlled experiment on the accumulator's physiological effects. What it contains The book is a practical manual rather than a theoretical work. Its three parts are the history of Reich's discovery, the construction of the device — an enclosure of alternating layers of organic and metallic material, which Reich held would concentrate an ambient life energy — and guidance on its use, including the circumstances in which DeMeo holds it should not be used. That last is a real feature of the book rather than a disclaimer. Reich distinguished orgone from what he called DOR (deadly orgone), and DeMeo devotes attention to atmospheric conditions, proximity to electrical equipment and radioactive sources, and symptoms he treats as signs of over-exposure. The controlled experiments The appendix reports work by two German physicians, Stefan Muschenich and Rainer Gebauer , from their University of Marburg thesis of the mid-1980s — a double-blind controlled trial of the physiological effects of sitting in an accumulator. A second and independent double-blind study was carried out by Günter Hebenstreit at the University of Vienna in 1991–92.
