The theory was published in details in the monograph of Ilya Lvovich Gerlovin, dated 1990, with the title: "Fundamentals of a unified theory of all interactions in matter" (translation of the original title in Russian). I consider it important to introduce the paradigm first of all, since Gerlovin believed that it contains the methodological and mathematical conditions necessary for any theory, and in particular, for the theory of the noosphere.
