INTRODUCTION Humanity has always sought to understand its place in the universe, grappling with the profound questions of existence: What is the nature of reality? How does consciousness arise, and what role does it play in shaping the world around us? These questions are more than intellectual curiosities; they touch on the essence of what it means to be human. In addressing these, civilizations have drawn upon diverse fields—philosophy religion, science, and art—seeking answers that resonate both rationally and intuitively.
