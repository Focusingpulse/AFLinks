👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 4 March 1905 · archived # 2,410,188

In my last lecture I will give a sort of summing-up of Schiller. If we want to describe Schiller's place in the Nineteenth Century, we can certainly not go into details; and so we shall not pause over single incidents if they are not of symptomatic importance.
