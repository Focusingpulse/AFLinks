Overview This is the January 2001 issue of New Energy News , the monthly newsletter of the Institute for New Energy, edited by Patrick G Bailey with material contributed by Hal Fox , Toby Grotz , Gary Vesperman , Win Lambertson , Eugene Mallove and others. It is not a research paper but a compilation: abstracts of recently published work, a book review, feature articles, reader correspondence, and conference announcements. Its organising sections are SHORT SUBJECTS, BOOK REVIEW, ARTICLES, LETTERS and MEETINGS. The issue is dominated by one event — the California electricity crisis of the winter of 2000–2001 — and by the community's reading of it. Where the mainstream account attributed the rolling blackouts to deregulation, market manipulation and a decade without new generating capacity, the newsletter treats them as vindication: proof that conventional energy policy has failed and that the "new energy" devices its contributors have long advocated should be funded and commercialised immediately. That framing runs through the technical items, the letters and the editorial asides alike. A second, subsidiary theme is the community's running conflict with Robert Park of the American Physical Society, whose January 2001 Playboy article attacking Cold Fusion draws two separate rebuttals here.
