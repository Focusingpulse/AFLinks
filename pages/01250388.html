Furthermore, heat transfer phenomena cause thermally induced mechanical stresses compromising the reliability of engine components. The ability to predict heat transfer in engines plays an important role in engine development. Today, predictions are increasingly being done with numerical simulations at an ever earlier stage of engine development. These methods must be based on the understanding of the principles of heat transfer. This work presents the principles of thermo-fluid dynamic behind heat transfer phenomena relevant to internal combustion engines. The emphasis is on the relations between heat transfer and fluid flows. The work provides an overview of the flow-fields characteristics of internal combustion engines. The different approaches to accurate three-dimensional transient modeling of heat transfer and fluid flow are introduced and compared.
