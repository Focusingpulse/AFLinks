Round Robin >> Vol. 09 >> No. 05 [17] CORRESPONDENCE OF PATRICIA SHERRICK (Reg. Director, BSRA) . . . . Speaking of Clarion, have you ever come across the reference to in Vol. iii, Secret Doctrine (the one published by Besant after H.P.B.'s death)? On page 562 & 563 is as follows: "There is now a planet, the 'mystery planet', behind the moon, and it is gradually dying. Finally the time will come for it to send its principles to a new Laya Center, and there a new planet will form, to belong to another solar system, the present mystery planet then functioning as a moon to that new globe. This moon will have nothing to do with our earth, though it will come Within our range of vision. "I have wondered, if this be the case, if these people from Clarion could be inspecting Earth, with thought of future residence. The ones not in interplanetary nirvana could possibly be desirous of leaving their dying planet soon. I would be interested in receiving any information you might have regarding this. . . . . . . . "The 'great separation' or day of judgment could be coming up soon, rather than in the Fifth Round. From the book 'The Solar System' I find: 'The Fourth Round being especially destined to the development of the desire-principle in man, it is only in the next or Fifth Round that man is intended to devote himself to the unfolding of intellect. Owing, however, to the stimulus given by the Lords of the Flame, the intellect has already been considerably developed, and we are therefore a whole Round in advance of where we should have been but for Their help .
