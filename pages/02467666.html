Oversoul Seven is: MA-AH-a beautiful young black woman who lived in 35,000 B.C. She and her mate were primarily concerned with warmth and survival, until an inexplicable knowledge led her to a door in a mountainside-and to the Land of the Speakers.... JOSEF-a seventeenth-century painter and lay about. He was locked in a windowless room and threatened with starvation by his irate patron ... and then he met an Old Man in his dreams. . . LYDIA-an aging twentieth-century poetess. She discovered that her poetry had really originated in an ancient civilization, and would reemerge in a future time. . . PROTEUS-who lived in the flying city Of A.D. 2300, and longed for the earth he had never seen. After months of secret planning, he finally descended through the clouds and arrived at a system of tunnels and passageways that led to a ruin not recorded in the history books-the Dig of the Tellers. . .
