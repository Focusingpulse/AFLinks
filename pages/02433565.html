Hellow, I'm from the UK and I'm very happy to be a member of this forum. Although from an engineering background I come with no assumptions about how things 'should work'. I am here to learn and hopefully in time contribute something uplifting, positive and maybe even useful. My belief is that energy is ubiquitous, free and limitless. The issue is not wether it exists, but how we can understand it and release it safely into our limited paradigm of useful work. For me understanding energy is as much a philosophical challenge as it is technical one. I sincerly respect those who have sacrificed time, reputation and many a good night's sleep in the quest to walk that lonely road for the benefit of others.
