This is not an overtone , as Helmholtz has called the harmonic partials of one sounding string, but an undertone , because it is a " grave harmonic ," away below the sounds of the two strings which awaken it. The subject of these undertones has been carefully studied since Tartini 's day, and more insight has been obtained since we are now able to count and register the vibration of any musical sound . Helmholtz has called these third sounds of Tartini 's " difference sounds ," because when awakened by two strings, for example, the vibration-number of the third tone is the difference of the vibrations-numbers of the two tones which awaken it.
