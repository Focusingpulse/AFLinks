Piskarev1,2 , Daria V. Elkina2 , Evgeny A. Gusev2 , Kirill V. Getman1,2 , Irina D. Bazhenova1,2 , Alexander S. Zholondz2 , Dmitrii V. GRAMBERG All-Russia Scientific Research Institute for Geology and Mineral Resources of the Ocean" (FSBI "VNIIOKEANGEOLOGIA"), Russia st076570@student.spbu.ru The age of the sedimentary cover on the northwestern shelf of the Laptev Sea is one of the key parameters for understanding the geological evolution of the region. The literature presents different interpretations of the age of these deposits, ranging from Cretaceous to older, which is largely due to the limited number of deep boreholes and the predominance of indirect geophysical data [1], [2]. To refine the age model, integrated studies were carried out, including paleomagnetic analysis in combination with isotopic and other geophysical methods. In 2024, three boreholes were drilled, yielding a total core length of 69 m. Paleomagnetic investigations were conducted on all sections, including thermal demagnetization of 70 samples. In one of the boreholes, which penetrated the oldest strata, alternating intervals of normal and reversed polarity were identified throughout the section.
