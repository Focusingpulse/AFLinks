All cosmic rays emanate from suns and some particles that compose these rays travel at speeds 50 times greater than light (182,000 miles per second), he announced with unconcern for the relativists. And, as if that were not enough for one day’s announcing, the scientist revealed “discovery of a method of producing direct electrical current by induction without using a commutator — something that electrical engineers since Faraday have agreed could not be done.” Then, to climax a five-hour interview with newspapermen, the septuagenarian explained new methods and apparatus for transmitting mechanical energy to any terrestrial distance.
