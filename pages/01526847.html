The highest temperature position of the maximum hydrogen isotopes intensity release corresponds to the samples heating in a metal vacuum cell by external coaxial furnace. The lowest temperature position of the maximum intensity hydrogen isotopes release corresponds to the heating by accelerated electrons beam. The difference in these positions of the maximum is DТ z 300 С.
