A lot of models try to explain this phenomenon : when a lightning strikes the ground or a tree, some small particles are vaporized. These particles are rich in carbon ( coming from the tree and/or the ground ). A lightning is a very short electromagnetic pulse generated by a very strong current ( million of amperes ) and its energy is enormous. Microwaves are produced and immediately absorbed by this cloud of thin conductive particles ( made with dusts of carbon ), a plasmoid is created and this produces a ball lightning. The cloud of carbon particles is produced with a pencil graphite lead. The electromagnetic energy of the microwaves generated by a magnetron is absorbed by the cloud of carbon particles and a "plasmoid" is produced...
