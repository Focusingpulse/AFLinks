adjective: having a pair of equal and opposite charges, opposition , sex , dipole , duality . adjective: characterized by opposite extremes; completely opposed. See Ion , Ionization Polar , or the state of being polarized , is sometimes referred to as having two seeming opposite poles or bi-polar , bias , dipole , charge , dual and sex . Examples are a magnet, an electric circuit, male and female, etc. A magnet for instance is polar while an unmagnetized piece of iron is said to be depolar , non-polar or not having poles. It's opposite is depolar ; not having a dual state, polarity or lacking charge and discharge . This state of a thing or condition having two seeming opposites mutually intermittently attracted then repulsed has been known and illustrated since antiquity.
