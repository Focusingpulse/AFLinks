Newton said that this subtle ether interpenetrates all matter and is concealed in their substance , through the strength and activity of which, bodies attract each other and adhere together when brought in contact, annihilating distance , as if objects might touch each other. Through this " life spirit " light also flows, is refracted and reflected and bodies are warmed. The Germans call it the " Welgeist ".
