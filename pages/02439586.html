I — GA 274. Introductions for Traditional Christmas Plays

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 26 December 1915 · archived # 2,439,586
, Dornach Translated by Steiner Online Library We have let two Christmas plays pass before our soul. We may perhaps raise the thought: Are the first and second Christmas plays dedicated in the same sense to the great human cause that is so vividly before our soul these days? The two plays are fundamentally different, quite different from each other. One can hardly imagine two plays that are more different and yet are dedicated to the same subject.
