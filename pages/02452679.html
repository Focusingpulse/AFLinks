Next message: Jim Shaffer, Jr.: "another flaw in relativity" I found this on a music page concerning instruments and resonance.... can anyone verify this and where can I find this also.....(conical vs. straight resonating tubes) In a "straight tube" standing wavelengths are 1/4, 3/4, 5/4, ... of a wave because the wave in the tube has a crest on one (antinode) and a through (node) on the other end. In a "conical tube" standing wavelengths are 1/2, 2/2, 3/2, ... of a wave because there's a crest (antinode) on both ends.
