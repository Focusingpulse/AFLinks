(in traditional Chinese) This paper introduces the essences of mass, time, length and energy, as well as their standard measurement units, analyzes mass-velocity relationships in different theories, and comments on these relationships. Also the paper negates the mass-velocity equation and the mass-energy equation in the special relativity; analyzes the source and generating mechanism of atomic energy. The author deems that mass is mass, energy is energy; mass cannot be transformed into energy and energy cannot be transformed into mass. These two concepts can??t be converted into each other. There are mass conservation and energy conservation, individually. Overview Zifeng Li, of the Petroleum Engineering Institute at Yanshan University, argues that mass and energy are distinct quantities that cannot be converted into one another, and that each is separately conserved. The paper was published in Scientific Inquiry in 2007 and in two Chinese-language outlets. He opens by defining the four quantities at issue. Mass is "the amount of matter that an object contains," and "there is nothing in the world whose mass is zero." Time is unidirectional and evenly passing. Length is the extensive property of three-dimensional isotropic space. Energy is an object's working ability, a state attribute. He traces the historical evolution of the kilogram, second and metre, noting the drift of the platinum–iridium prototype and observing that since 1983 the metre has been defined through a fixed value of c .
