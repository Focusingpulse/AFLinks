CHAPTER 1: NUMBER SYSTEM A number system in mathematics is a way of expressing numbers using digits or symbols, typically organized in a systematic pattern. It provides a consistent method for representing and manipulating numerical quantities. Natural Numbers Natural numbers are a set of positive integers that begin from 1 and continue indefinitely. They are the counting numbers used for basic counting and ordering. These numbers do not include zero or any negative values.
