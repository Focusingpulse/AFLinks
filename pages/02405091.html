The pretensions and methods of operating are almost precisely similar, and the alleged invention itself appears to be equally without substance. A Mr. Winter has, during the past few weeks, been? giving private exhibitions at No. 8 Columbia street, Brooklyn, of a so-called wonderful machine, which? he claims is capable of producing not only a power far exceeding that ascribed to Keely’s, but also by ?the same operation a rich illuminating gas, at a cost of 2 cents per 1,000 feet. Mr. Thomas B.
