Improvements in Reciprocating Engines and Means for Regulating the Period of the Same I, Nikola Tesla, of 35 South Fifth Avenue, New York, County and State of New York, United States of America, do hereby declare the nature of this invention, and in what manner the same is to be performed to be particularly described and ascertained in and by the following statement:- In the invention which forms the subject of my present application, my object has been, primarily to provide an engine which under the influence of an applied force such as the elastic tension of steam or gas under pressure will yield an oscillatory movement which, within very wide limits, will be of constant period, irrespective of variations of load, frictional losses and other factors which in all ordinary engines produce change in the rate of reciprocation.
