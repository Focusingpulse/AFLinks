For the Seventh Annual Conference on the Physics, Chemistry, and Biology of Water, I am announcing the availability of financial support for graduate students and postdoctoral fellows who wish to attend. Once again, we are encouraging young people to come, hear, and rub shoulders with the more senior people by offering to partially defray travel and lodging costs for selected students and postdocs who plan to present a poster at the conference. To apply for financial support: Supply a title and short abstract (one paragraph) of the poster you intend to present. In order to be considered for support, poster presentations are required. Have your research adviser write a brief note describing your capabilities and the need for financial support. Send both documents to Gloria Zucker at: gzucker@watercon.org. The deadline for submission is June 30, 2012 (Deadline has been extended to July 10th.) EARLY APPLICATIONS ARE ENCOURAGED, as the meeting may reach capacity. Applicants will be informed of decisions on or before July 13, 2012. In no case can full costs be covered. We have limited funds, so we must limit the selected students to 10. The conference will take place at the Grand Summit Resort at Mt. Snow, West Dover, Vermont, October 18 — 21, 2012 (Thursday through Sunday, with most attendees arriving the evening before, on October 17th).
