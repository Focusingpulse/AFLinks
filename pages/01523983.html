Suzanne Price Born ( 1947-08-29 ) August 29, 1947 (age 79) Residence Phoenix, AZ, United States Nationality American Alma mater University of Wyoming, Tulane University Known for Unified Theory , Electric Universe , Universal Spirituality , Ether , Vortex Theory , Geometry , Subtle Energy , Consciousness , Tesla Scientific career Fields Independent Researcher , Anthropologist , Communications , Public Relations Suzanne Price (born August 29, 1947) is an American cultural anthropologist , independent researcher, writer, and presenter who develops an interpretive "photon/resonance" model of energy intended to bridge quantum physics and universal spirituality . Drawing on the work of Nikola Tesla and on the energy practices of ancient civilizations, she explores what she describes as the common underlying characteristics and processes of energy that link science, spirituality, and human consciousness . Education B.A. in anthropology , magna cum laude — University of Wyoming M.A. — Tulane University , New Orleans Biography Before turning to independent research, she held a range of professional roles, including Communications Director for a finance agency of the State of Arizona, positions in public relations and marketing, philanthropy work, and health and education program and facility planning. A long-standing interest in the work of Nikola Tesla, sparked at the 2007 Extraordinary Technology Conference sponsored by TeslaTech, led her to research and develop a consciousness-based interpretation of the way energy works. She was based in Phoenix, Arizona, and relocated to Longmont, Colorado in 2018. Work Price proposes a "photon/resonance" model, which she presents as a holistic interpretive framework meant to highlight commonalities across many scientific and spiritual disciplines.
