VATICAN SECRET ARCHIVES Collection Index and description of related resources VATICAN CITY 2004-2005 Vatican City, July 15, 2004 Prot. N. 41.314 To all Vatican Secret Archives Researchers Listed below are a few important points that I would like to bring to the Your attention: 1. The obligation, as set by Art. 20 of the current “Researcher Regulations”, to provide the Secretary of the Vatican Secret Archives (ASV) with at least one copy of any publication in which any document of the ASV is quoted (even simply) or published (partially or in extenso), if a printed copy is not available, a photocopy will suffice. This is necessary in order to ensure the updates to the ASV Bibliography, which are compiled by the Archive and reprinted every three months. The failure to provide the ASV with a copy of any document making use of ASV material will result in the suspension of the Archives entrance ID renewal or its issue until the said publications are received. 2. Please remember that the use of mobile telephones is strictly prohibited in the Study Rooms. Mobile phones may be left in the lockers that are assigned daily to individual researchers or kept in your pockets (not on the table) but they must be switched off. 3. Furthermore, I would like to inform researchers that due to the vast revision programme of all the collections and series in the Pontifical Archives, in order to ensure a correct archival pertinence and sound designation, certain collections and series of writings might have new names and/or placements. Therefore, previously published Collection Index may contain faulty references. This operation (which is also part of the publication of a General Guide to the Vatican Secret Archives) is being implemented with the most scrupulous attention in order to maintain, as far as possible, the terms used in the previous markings.
