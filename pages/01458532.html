By the late 1990s, some around him began suggesting he might be the reincarnation of the famed psychic Edgar Cayce, a belief that Wilcock himself explored openly. Wilcock became a central figure in what is often called the disclosure movement, which calls for an end to alleged U.S. government secrecy surrounding UFOs, extraterrestrials, and advanced technologies. He authored several books that blended science, spirituality, and ancient civilizations. In 2004, he co-wrote The Reincarnation of Edgar Cayce? with Wynn Free, presenting the idea that humanity stood on the brink of a major energetic transformation. Later works, including The Source Field Investigations (2012), examined hidden science behind 2012 prophecies and predicted shifts toward higher consciousness. Two of his books, Awakening in the Dream and The Ascension Mysteries, reached New York Times bestseller lists.
