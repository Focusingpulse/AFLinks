Journal of Borderland Research >> Vol. 29 >> No. 06 [14] ETHER SHIPS New Insights on an Old Problem By Trevor James , BSRA When BSRA founder Meade Layne gave the name "ether ships" to UFO's in his epochal monograph THE ETHER-SHIP MYSTERY , he builded better than he knew. Modern thought and practical research both received a strong impulse towards that body of Cosmic law in which lie the keys to the UFO mystery in all its diversity. As the decades reel past, etherian physics emerges as a crucial tool in shaping the human future. When ML generously hailed my 1958 book "They Live in the Sky" as the "death knell of the old order", I was not only flattered, but flabbergasted. Such a tribute from a man whose knowledge and wisdom I respected was completely unanticipated. The plain truth is that in 1958 I did not understand why a man of ML's occult erudition could be so enthusiastic about my stumbling work. In those days, postulating, and then establishing empirically that UFO phenomena included both biological aeroforms unrelated to space ships, and ships containing entities with malefic intent toward mankind did not win me many friends. Support from ML, and corroboration from the Inner Circle, of my essential findings was in these circumstances most welcome and comforting. [15] Fifteen years later, one can appreciate that ML's gaze was directed to the future.
