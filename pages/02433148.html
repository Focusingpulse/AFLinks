I am passionate about different ways of creating energy, I have studied and read a great deal about many different concepts and inventions. I envision a research and development association, where each member grows and develops their own ideas or specialities and freely shares all the findings with the other members of the team, for the greater good of the group as a whole, say one team builds a free energy water heater and another builds a way to produce free electricity and another develops a way to purify sewage with nothing more than a large pond and bubbles with no chemicals. Now all of these members get together and share their ideas and eventually build a research and development community, Where other like minded people are allowed to prosper in their own research and development endeavours. Of course much work needs to be done, but imagine a fully self sustaining city of 10,000 people with no need for any outside oil or energy. I am actually surprised that places like this do not already exist, but the technology is already here we just need to organise and work together for the greater good, but we must start small and grow slow.
