Case (September 11, 1930 – July 15, 2010) was an American chemical engineer, inventor, and independent researcher who, in the later part of his career, became known within the Cold Fusion community for a gas-phase process he called "catalytic fusion." Biography Case was born in Tulsa, Oklahoma, on September 11, 1930. He spent his career as a chemical engineer and inventor, holding patents in the field of polymer chemistry; his work on polyether polyols was the subject of the reported patent-office case Case v.
