As of tonight, 10/10/99, the KeelyNet/Interact discussion list archive has been moved to Escribe. We have had an ongoing issue with spam creeping onto the archive yet being intercepted by the subscription list so that no one sees it until they view the arcive. The emails are evanescent but the archives remain long after the emails have been deleted or forgotten, therefore it is critical that they be as on target, useful and pertinent as possible. Spam drives me nuts so I had to go in and kill off the content of the spam messages but I could not kill off the header because it would mess up the index. Dan York did a lot of work getting the list together and then establishing the archive with the associated search engine and I certainly appreciate his efforts. I hope everyone feels the same as it has been exceedingly useful to be able to read and search posts to the list. The main thing the escribe move will provide will be a complete intercept of outside spams and it will also provide search capabilities. Also thanks to Scott at Escribe and again Dan York for working together to copy all the discussion list posts since we first began in January 1998 to the escribe list. It was a lot of work and again I am amazed they could do it so quickly and seamlessly. If you have a discussion list which needs archiving, consider that the escribe service is free and based on my experiences with Scott, the 'customer service' is impeccable.
