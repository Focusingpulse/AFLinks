You know, all this stuff about time travel is just so much BS at this point as is free energy and gravity control and whatever kinds of miraculous cures that are claimed to be out there. Why? Because it becomes real only when the claimant can PROVE it with an actual demonstration. In some cases such as 'cures' and possibly time travel it would be experiential, but with gravity control and free energy there would be no doubt because everyone could use it at will. In this PARTICULAR case, the guy said he could prove it, which means he is responding to the challenge with his own gauntlet......the logical thing to do is take him up on it and see what happens. All else on time travel is just speculation until someone proves it can be done, whether physically going there or just mentally and retrieving information that is verifiable. So, if he's up for it, the most it would cost me would be $10 for a video tape and some communications. Sounds like a simple and cheap enough investment. My main concern is how the information is transmitted from the future to the past. He indicates it will just 'appear' in the mind of his past target (while the original videotape is being made???) when he broadcasts from the future location.
