Log in I forgot my password Sympathetic Vibratory Physics &raquo; Charles Babbage Page actions PDF Print Share Send link Charles Babbage ( click to enlarge ) Charles Babbage (1791 - 1871) was an English mathematician and inventor. He is known as the father of the computer, due to his construction of the Difference Engine and his attempt to complete the Analytical Engine, the world's first programmable computer. His associate Ada Lovelace recognized its programmability and wrote the world's first computer program, to compute Bernoulli numbers. Babbage was born on this day (December 26) in 1791. Short Biography:- Both the date and place of Charles Babbage's birth were uncertain but have now been firmly established. In [1] and [12], for example, his date of birth is given as 26 December 1792 and both give the place of his birth as near Teignmouth. However, a nephew wrote to The Times a week after the obituary [18] appeared, saying that Babbage was born on 26 December 1791. There was little evidence to prove which was right until Hyman (see [8]) in 1975 found that Babbage's birth had been registered in St Mary's Newington, London on 6 January 1792. Babbage's father was Benjamin Babbage, a banker, and his mother was Betsy Plumleigh Babbage. Given the place that his birth was registered Hyman says in [8] that it is almost certain that Babbage was born in the family home of 44 Crosby Row, Walworth Road, London. Since his father was fairly wealthy, he could afford to have Babbage educated at private schools. After the school at Alphington he was sent to an academy at Forty Hill, Enfield, Middlesex where his education properly began.
