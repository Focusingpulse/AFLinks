I came across the following info on how to make your own Semiconductors/transistors. So I'm listing it here for reference. Seeing as a lot of experiments require the use of Semiconductors, some of which are used for specific effects or properties. One this that is covered in the videos is that you can make them operate at different, even at high voltages. Which could come in handy. Videos to watch: Lecture Given At Metalab About Build A Home Chip Lab on Vimeo Making Microchips at Home - Cooking with Jeri Part1 - YouTube Making Microchips at Home - Cooking with Jeri Part 2 - YouTube Homebrew NMOS Transistor Step by Step - So Easy Even Jeri Can Do It - YouTube Cleaving 100 Silicon Wafers on Vimeo Other links: SiO2 color chart for thermally grown silicon dioxide Amateur Scientist - DIY FET/home-made transistor Scientific American June 1970 FET Transistor Homemade From Cadmium Sulfide Photocell. Other interesting links: Electroluminescent Ink Project Introduction on Vimeo Things that are needed: P type, 100 crystal orientation silicon wafers from ebay Hydrofluoric Acid / or other glass etching fluid. phosphosilicate from Emulsitone CO (USA) (looks to be this one: PHOSPHOROSILICAFILM 5 x 10_20 ) Other stuff: Distilled water Multi meter Soldering iron Plastic container (fish tank) cups furnace kettle Type 575 Transistor-Curve Tracer Extra power supply Oscilloscope ...
