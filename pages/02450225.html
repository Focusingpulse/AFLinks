If we also assume that there are certain frequencies that the aether resonates at, then all these different "things" are going to be interfering with each other creating a "beat" pattern. If you are looking for electrical tapping then you can add a gas (or whatever you do) to create a plasma tube. The reason it does not matter to add this gas is that you can alter the frequency of the plasma by attaching a frequency generator to tune it to one of the frequencies that the aether resonates at. If you get it matched correctly then when both "waves" are resonating together, the outcome will be higher amplitude.
