We instead treat its numerator as a vector and characterize it algebraically. Fixing the denominator P(t) = 16 − t8 , the map sending an 8-coefficient numerator to the coordinate vector of 16 ∫0 1 N(t)/P(t) dt in a fixed generating family of transcendentals is a linear operator Φ over the number field ℚ(√2). We prove Φ has rank 7 (hence a one-dimensional kernel), so the preimage of the π-line is a two-dimensional space. A single linear functional — the coefficient sum Σ, which by a boundary-evaluation identity equals N(1) — cuts this two￾dimensional fibre to a unique line, and the classical BBP numerator spans it. Every load-bearing quantity is quoted from live symbolic and 60-digit numerical computation, with a reproducible verification script. We separate this proved core cleanly from its generalization: the phenomenon (a low-dimensional distinguished fibre selected by a natural boundary functional) is posed as an operator-classification problem over square￾free P, which the present result establishes as non-empty.
