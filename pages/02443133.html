Easy, Cheap way to Chase Birds Away

· archived # 2,443,133
06/28/14 - Easy, Cheap way to Chase Birds Away My house has a carport which seems to be a haven for birds to build their nests and crap all over everything. Plus they scream and chatter at various times all day, waking me up in the morning to that cacaphony. I saw on ebay where you could buy a roll of shiny mylar tape to put up around the area you want to protect, as hanging strips or running vertically. The claim was winds would cause the chrome mylar tape to shimmer and move which would frighten birds away. So I hung about 5 strips about 2-3 inches wide and about 2 feet long. The first day the birds wouldn't come near it, but the next day and thereafter they played games swooping between the moving strips. When I worked in a photolab, our warehouse would be invaded by birds so we bought two plastic owls and that scared them off. But they cost about $40 and twice that for shipping to Mexico so thats out. Another way was a motion detector that emitted ultrasonics to scare them off. Thought about a scarecrow too. Pondering this, I remembered reading in an old book that you could take a dead bird and nail it onto a board with its wings spread, then place that board at the top of a tree. When the birds come near, they look down or over, see the carcass and high tail it out of there. I tried to find a dead one but no luck. So three days ago I was making Caldo de Pollo (chicken stew) and had a plastic bag of liver, gizzard and two chicken legs. Aha, dead bird meat! So I hung the two feet up under the most favored nest location and two birds swooped in, saw the feet and did a 180 to get the heck out of there. The last three days have been bliss. So quiet, no chatter from the carport (next to my bedroom window) and no bird caca splatter for me to clean up. Guess I'll find out how long this lasts as the feet begin to dry. Now they congregate on a phone or power line outside the carport and try to piss me off. So I always make a huge batch of chicken stew and freeze it. Thaw
