Title Special Relativity as a Subset of Autodynamics Read in full Read in full Author(s) Ricardo L Carezani Keywords Autodynamics , special relativity , kinetic energy, decay, external energy Published 1991 Website https://www.autodynamics.org/ad-to-sr/ "Special Relativity as a Subset of Autodynamics" — published at autodynamics.org under the heading AD to SR and subtitled "Special Relativity a Physically Wrong Mathematical Subset of Autodynamics" — sets out the algebraic transformation, obtained by Ricardo L Carezani in 1991, by which the special relativity expression for kinetic energy is recovered from the Autodynamics one. Carezani regarded it as among his most important results, and the timeline on the site records it as the year he "discover[ed] the mathematical conversion indicating that special relativity is a subset of Autodynamics." Overview The paper turns on a distinction that governs the whole of Autodynamics. Autodynamics, Carezani writes, "was derived to describe decay cases: no external energy or kinetic energy is introduced into the system. All energy comes from the particle itself" — the rocket, whose thrust is bought with its own mass. Special relativity, by contrast, describes energy "applied externally to a particle", so that the total is the rest mass plus a kinetic contribution from outside — the cannon and the cannon ball. The transformation makes that difference into a substitution. The argument Write the Autodynamics kinetic energy as E c = M [ 1 − 1 − β 2 ] with M = m 0 c 2 for a decaying particle. For a particle receiving energy from outside, Carezani argues, the quantity in the bracket must act on the rest energy plus the kinetic energy already imparted, so M = m 0 c 2 + E c . Substituting, distributing, cancelling the common energy terms and isolating E c returns E c = m 0 c 2 [ 1 1 − β 2 − 1 ] which is the standard relativistic kinetic energy.
