You Are Not a Critical Thinker - How to be one by David de Hilster Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Date 17 June 2023 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording You Are Not a Critical Thinker - How to be one by David de Hilster was a live session on 17 June 2023 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. Everyone claims to be a critical thinker. Everyone claims that they weigh all the pros and cons. But in reality, no one really does. They convince themselves they are a critical thinkers and then they try to convince others. But you are not a critical thinker. David de Hilster is here to tell you why you are not and how to become one... Summary David de Hilster 's return to the Saturday sessions after a long absence, and the bluntest talk the society has hosted about itself. He was explicit at the outset that he was not there to make friends, said he expected people to drop out of the call as he went, and repeated several times that he holds the position of president and takes the criticism to heart on the organisation's behalf. The charge His thesis is that the society does not contain many true critical thinkers — more than the mainstream, certainly, but far fewer than it believes. He set out the mainstream's failings first, and agreed with the members about them: theoretical physics and cosmology claim critical thinking and do not practise it; papers are rejected unrefereed; a doctoral student is not permitted to argue that an accepted theory is wrong; and the requirement that a critic hold a degree and publish in an established journal is circular, since obtaining either requires agreeing with the theory. He offered his own instance: in 1994 he took Carezani's autodynamics work to physicists at TRW, who reported that they could find no fault with the argument but did not want the publicity of an "Einstein wrong" result.
