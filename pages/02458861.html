In an early outbreak, while Joasia was sleeping, plates and glasses hurtled through the air at tremendous speeds, smashing into walls and furniture. When her parents turned the lights on, shards of glass, as if pulled by some strange force, flew at her, some cutting her. Her blanket, charged with electricity, gave off sparks.
