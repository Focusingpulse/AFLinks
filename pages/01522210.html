Title Why there is no Gravitomagnetic Force Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Jerry Hynecek Keywords electromagnetic fields, Lorentz force, Einstein Published 2012 Journal General Science Journal No. of pages 5 Abstract Using a simple model this paper explains that there is no gravitomagnetic force contrary to a widely spread belief that such a force must exist. This is usually supported by the analogy with the Maxwell theory of Electromagnetic fields. The gravitomagnetic force, analogous to the force described by the Lorentz force equation, and the accompanied gravitoelectromagnetic field equations are derived from Einstein field equations by linearization for the weak gravitational fields. The nonexistence of the gravitomagnetic field thus questions the validity and correctness of Einstein field equations. Overview Jaroslav Hynecek, of Isetex Inc., argues that the gravitomagnetic force does not exist, and that since the gravitoelectromagnetic field equations follow uniquely from linearising Einstein's field equations, their failure calls those equations into question. His test is a pair of parallel plates, examined first with charge and then with mass.
