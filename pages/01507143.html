Maritime - or commercial contract law originates in the rules of trade upon the high seas between international merchants and is enforced by military organizations. Admiralty - is armed enforcement of the laws of commerce(the law merchant) All birth certificates, licenses, registrations, insurances, bank accounts, permits, titles, deeds, etc. are commercial contracts created under the UCC - (Uniform Commercial Code) and this is where the confusion begins.
