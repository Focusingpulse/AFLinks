In arithmetic and mathematics , cardinal numbers , or cardinals for short, are a generalization of the natural numbers used to measure the cardinality or size of sets. The cardinality of a finite set is a natural number - the number of elements in the set. 0, 1, 2, 3, n ... In linguistics, cardinal numbers are number words representing a quantity (such as one , two , three , etc. in English).
