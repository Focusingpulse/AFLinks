COMPLEX ANALYSIS AND THEORY OF REPRODUCING KERNELS SABUROU SAITOH Abstract. The theory of reproducing kernels is very fundamental, beautiful and will have many applications in analysis, numerical analysis and data sciences. In this paper, some essential results in complex analysis derived from the theory of reproducing kernels will be introduced, simply.
