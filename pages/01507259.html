Please Vote For Health Freedom

· archived # 1,507,259
Some of you have voted already in either of both of rounds 1 and 2 - THANKS! If you haven't voted in round 2, please do so now. We need more votes, please log in and cast your vote to protect your health freedom rights. Anyone, anywhere, can log in and vote! GO TO CHANGE.org TO VOTE NOW!!! Instructions for Change.org "Health Freedom" Vote We urge everyone to Vote for "Health Freedom" from January 5 through 15, 2009 on Change.org. This is an important message from Natural Solutions Foundation to health freedom advocates world-wide. Instructions: 1. Type "www.Change.org/ideas/view/health_freedom" into the URL address window on your browser. 2. Click on the small (probably blue) box in the upper left section of the page. It has a number and the word "vote" in the box. 3. You will now be asked to create an account on Change.org - any email address can be used to create an account. If you have several of them, each can be used to vote once. 4. Enter your email and your password and click "submit" 5. You will receive a message telling you that a confirmation link has been sent to the email address you have entered. 6. Open a new window or tab in your browser and go to the email account you just registered. If your email account is already open, simply look for the email from Change.org and click on the link provided in the email. That should take you back to Change.org. 7. Once you come back to Change.org, if you are not on the page where you can vote for Health Freedom ( Change.org - Ideas for Change in America , click on "Ideas" on the upper right hand corner of the page. 7. When you get to the "Ideas" page, scroll about 3/4 of the way down the page and find "Health Care". There will be 3 ideas there under the "Health Care" heading. Click on the one that says "Health Freedom IS Our First Freedom". 8. That will take you to the Health Freedom page. Click on the box that has a number and the word "Vote". 9. Your vote has been registered when the number in the box increases
