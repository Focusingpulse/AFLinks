Should Spacetime be Discarded? Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Speaker Roger J Anderton Date 4 May 2024 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording Should Spacetime be Discarded? was a live session presented by Roger J Anderton on 4 May 2024 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. There are problems unifying Einstein's relativity with quantum physics as is well-known. One proposal for unifying them is to discard spacetime. Introducing spacetime into modern physics has created many problems. Summary Roger J Anderton hosted this himself, continuing from his sessions on Minkowski spacetime. The question is whether adding spacetime to physics was a mistake, and his angle is that the answer is now being given inside the mainstream as well as outside it: one proposal for unifying relativity with quantum theory is to discard spacetime altogether. His framing is the historical one he uses repeatedly. The 1905 paper contains no spacetime; Minkowski added it in 1908; general relativity followed in 1915; and between those dates the theory turned into something else. He cited Alexander Unzicker as holding that the development went wrong in exactly that decade. Two videos The first was Sabine Hossenfelder on whether gravity is a force, played to set out the position he disputes. Her account: weight is a force and mass a scalar, a distinction that does not matter in daily life; in general relativity there is no force acting on masses at all, only masses navigating a curved spacetime whose curvature we see indirectly as gravity; the equivalence principle makes the effects of gravity locally the same as acceleration in flat space, so that Einstein and Newton differ over the man who jumps off a roof — Newton says gravity pulls him down, Einstein that the force which had been pushing him up has been removed.
