Lecture Three — GA 166. Necessity and Freedom (1988)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 30 January 1916 · archived # 2,423,263
, Berlin Translated by Pauline Wehrle Lecture Three [ 1 ] To continue last week's study I shall begin with a kind of hypothetical case. Where the deepest riddles of human existence are concerned the best way to avoid abstraction and to get close to reality is to give examples. My example will of course apply to every possible level of life. So let us begin with a hypothetical example. [ 2 ] Let us imagine we are in a school, a school of three classes, with three teachers and a headmaster. These three teachers differ tremendously in character and temperament.
