The House of St.Nicholas VOLKER W. THÜREY Bremen, Germany * November 9, 2024 Abstract We introduce two constants. They are suitable for finite graphs. The can be served as ‘characteristic numbers’ or ‘dimensions’. Further, we define for each finite graph nine sequences. We will not calculate them. MSC2020: 05C99 Keywords and Phrases: Dimension; Graph The defined numbers can be used as a ‘characteristic number’ or ‘dimension’ for a finite graph. The following well-known graph is called ‘ The House of St. Nicholas’, and it can be drawn without lifting the pencil from the paper. This idea can be extended.
