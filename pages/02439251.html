Esoteric Lesson — GA 266. Esoteric Lessons III

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 5 June 1913 · archived # 2,439,251
, Helsinki Translated by Steiner Online Library One of the complaints of esotericists is that they are bombarded by thoughts during meditation, but this is natural. These are the Luciferic and Ahrimanic beings that we did not notice before, but which become noticeable precisely through meditation. It does not matter that we cannot see Ahriman and Lucifer. To say that the thoughts that assail us are not there would be just as foolish as saying that someone we sense in a dark room is not there because we cannot see them. One must not fight against such thoughts. That would be like defending ours
