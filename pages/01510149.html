Ampex Corporation, Redwood City, Calif; This paper appears in: Magnetics, IEEE Transactions on Publication Date: Dec 1973 Volume: 9, Issue: 4 On page(s): 678- 682 ISSN: 0018-9464 Posted online: 2003-01-06 16:44:34.0 Abstract It is shown that a previously unknown class of magnetization patterns exists in planar structures which have the unique property that all the flux escapes from one surface with none leaving the other side. A simple case is a constant amplitude rotating vector magnetization where the sense of rotation dictates which surface has no flux. More complicated magnetization patterns are elucidated. The likelihood that the one-sided flux phenomenon occurs partially in the normal write, the contact-printing and the print-through processes of tape recording is discussed.
