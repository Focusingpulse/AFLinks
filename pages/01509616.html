I advise spinning inside a PVC coupling; Much thicker material then the pipe itself. I used the 2 1/2 inch diameter coupling. This thick walled material is ample protection against shattering, and should be the first item on your shopping list. I used one CD on the top, but gluing two or three together might make more sense. The CD's need a hole close enough to the diameter of the thread spool pulse coil to fit snugly, but loose enough to push and pull the coil up and down a few millimeters each way for positioning.
