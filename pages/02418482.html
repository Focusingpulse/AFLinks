The Christ as the macrocosmic human being in reverse—GA 89. Awareness - Life - Form (2001)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 13 January 1906 · archived # 2,418,482
Translated by Anna Meuss The Christ as the macrocosmic human being in reverse [ 1 ] Essential Christ nature must be thought of as the macrocosmic human being in reverse, who, however, is the same as the second aspect of the godhead, or of the Logos. - Imagine the moment before the ‘monad’ (the totality of monads) descended to incarnate in the animal-human body which had been prepared by then. The animal world up to that time, in so far as it had come into being, that is without the mammals, had everything physically spread apart which for the lower human being needed to be concentrated in one
