Dvoeglazov (born 1961) is a Russian theoretical physicist who has worked at the Universidad Autónoma de Zacatecas in Mexico since 1994. His subject is the representation theory of the Poincaré group and what follows from it for relativistic wave equations — in particular for neutral particles, Majorana-like fields, and the question of which features of Maxwell's electrodynamics are consequences of Lorentz symmetry alone and which are separate assumptions. He convened the 1999 Zacatecas workshop on the Lorentz group, CPT and neutrinos and co-edited its proceedings, and is a frequent commentator on the disputes in non-Maxwellian electrodynamics that this wiki catalogues — usually as an analyst of the arguments rather than a partisan in them. Biography Education MSc in Physics , Saratov State University, U.S.S.R., 1983 (studies from 1978) PhD (Candidate of Sciences) , Joint Institute for Nuclear Research, Dubna, 1991 — thesis "Description of the interactions of two relativistic particles with spins in the quantum field theory"; confirmed by the U.S.S.R. State Attestation Committee in April 1992 Russia awards two doctoral degrees, and the 1991 degree is the first of them. Career Dvoeglazov was on the staff of Saratov State University from 1986 to 1988, returning as a junior researcher from 1991 to 1993 after his postgraduate years at Dubna.
