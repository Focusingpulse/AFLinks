👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 22 February 1923 · archived # 2,437,090

My purpose in referring to it again is to make sure that correct thinking prevails on the score of a step about to be taken in Stuttgart in the next few days, a step in the direction of making a new life in the Anthroposophical Society.
