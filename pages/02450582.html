The reason for using spherical conductors is a very importantpoint to remember whenever designing anything that will use high voltage. That is, never use a conductor that is not smoothly shaped. You don'tnecessarily need to use spheres, you could use aluminum soda cans for example. The higher the voltage you're dealing with, the more likely the electronswant to crowd together and fly off any conductors which have irregularitiesin the surface areas.
