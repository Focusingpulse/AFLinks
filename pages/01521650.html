Krafft Pages 66 The Ether and its Vortices is a 1955 book by Carl Frederick Krafft , published by Carl F. Krafft . Overview Outside the stagnant channels of "mainstream" science, leading research is now showing the vortex to be a vital key in understanding the subatomic level of nature. This comprehensive book is Krafft's masterwork covering: The Hydromechanical Ether, Subatomic Vortices, The New Vortex Atoms, Radioactivity and Atomic Fission, the Nuclear Theory Disproved, and more. About the author Carl Frederick Krafft (b. 1892) was an American engineer who defended a 'hydromechanical' ether and a vortex-atom model of matter.
