Roy Keys Inc.), Montreal Pages 245 ISBN 9781987980233 Wilhelm Weber's Main Works on Electrodynamics Translated into English, Volume I: Gauss and Weber's Absolute System of Units is the first volume of the five-volume edition of Wilhelm Weber 's electrodynamic works edited and translated by Andre K T Assis , published by Apeiron in 2021. It gathers the papers in which Gauss and Weber created the absolute system of units — the practice, now universal in physics, of expressing magnetic and electric quantities in terms of length, mass and time rather than in arbitrary units of their own.
