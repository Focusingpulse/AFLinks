Ramsay "There are two distinct laws which rule in astronomy - viz., masses and distances ; and there are two distinct laws which rule in music - affinities and proximities . The notes produced by simple ratios as 1:2, 2:3, 3:4, etc. , are attracted to each other by the law of affinity ; notes which are beside each other in the octave scale and have moderately complex ratios as 9:10 and 15:16, are attracted to each other by their proximities . F and C , and C and G , and G and D are related to each other by affinity . C is related to the fifth below and the fifth above; G is related to the fifth above and the fifth below.
