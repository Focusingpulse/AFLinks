· archived # 2,468,589
A Ron’s Org Resource List Draft 1, 21 August 2015 This is an online, downloadable collection of materials that may be of interest to, or useful to, everyone on Ron’s Org / CBR Bridge lines. It constitutes a very significant treasure trove that does not exist anywhere else at the time of writing. The total size is a little over 60 Gb. While some items will be very familiar, some of them are NOT well-known or easy to find. A few items listed below are very rare indeed, and even some experienced Ron’s Org terminals may not be familiar with everything below. In some cases, the items have never been published on the internet before. Nothing here is above the level of OT3. This is Draft 1. It is likely to be updated, either because of corrected errors, or because more material may be added. Please freely communicate any apparent errors, bugs, typos, broken links or suggestions to BILL RYAN at bill@projectavalon.net.
