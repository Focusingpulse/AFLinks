"I need hardly say that I do not intend it to be supposed that the ether is actually made up of wheels and india rubber bands, nor even of paddle-wheels, with connecting canals. I think, however, that we may learn several things as to the conditions that the elements of ether should fulfill if they are to represent Maxwell's equations by motions in ways analogous to those of my model."
