Instead of one great cataclysm which caused one great ball of heat and flame all at one time in prehistoric times, substitute for it billions of microscopic heat producing balls for every cubic millimeter of this whole universe. These suns have one life-death frequency in fifty billion years. Time divides and extends as mass multiplies and compresses.
