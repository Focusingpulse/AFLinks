GTM2026-main064 Preemptive Service Systems with Time-Dependent Payoff Julia Chirkova1 1 Institute of Applied Mathematical Research of the Karelian Scientific Center of the Russian Academy of Sciences julia@krc.karelia.ru We apply a game-theoretic approach to a preemptive service system with a single server. Clients (players) access the preemptive system during the time interval $[0,T]$. As soon as a client's request enters the system, the server immediately begins processing it. However, when a new request arrives, the current request leaves the system, even if its service has not yet been completed. Each player's strategy is to choose a time to send his request to the system, maximizing his utility, which is the expected payoff. We assume that the payoff $k(t)$ that each player competes for changes over time, which, along with player behavior, also influences the utility value at each moment in time. Several scenarios of a two-player model are considered for various given payoff functions. We assume that the players are identical and act symmetrically. Each player chooses a request sending time $t$, knowing that the opponent sends his request by choosing a time according to a distribution on the interval $[0,T]$ with a probability distribution function $F(\cdot)$ and density $f(\cdot)$.
