De Hilster notes that Einstein's fame rests less on E = mc than on the prediction that gravity bends light, now routinely relied on in observational astronomy. He presents the contrary case made by a NASA laser-observation scientist Edward Dowdye that the Sun does bend starlight but does so through the plasma of its corona, and that the bending stops where the corona does, which is not what a gravitational account predicts.
