This document is essentially a promotional/interview transcript for Australian biologist Jeremy Griffith and his book "FREEDOM: The End Of The Human Condition". Its central thesis is that the human condition — aggression, selfishness, alienation, guilt, conflict — arose from a conflict between instinct and the emerging conscious intellect. Griffith argues: humans are not fundamentally “evil” or genetically savage, our distress comes from consciousness defying instinct, this produced psychological upset, and understanding this conflict supposedly “rehabilitates” humanity psychologically. There are actually several areas where it overlaps with SVP themes: Cause behind effects Griffith tries to move beneath surface behavior to an underlying causal mechanism. That aligns with your long emphasis on studying CAUSE rather than merely EFFECT. Human conflict as a condition His framing of humanity as psychologically “upset” rather than inherently evil parallels your recurring distinction between discordant condition versus intrinsic corruption. Original harmony disrupted His “Garden of Eden” / original cooperative state resembles SVP ideas of an original harmonic equilibrium or scalar stillness disturbed by differentiation. Need for reconciliation and reintegration Both systems ultimately seek reharmonization rather than punishment.
