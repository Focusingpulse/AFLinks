Lecture VIII - GA 321. Second Scientific Lecture-Course: Warmth Course

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 8 March 1920 · archived # 2,442,948
, Stuttgart Lecture VIII [ 1 ] Yesterday we carried out an experiment which brought to your attention the fact that mechanical work exerted by friction of a rotating paddle in a mass of water has changed into heat. You were shown that the water in which the paddle turned became warmer. [ 2 ] Today we will do just the opposite. We showed yesterday that we must in some ways seek an explanation for the coming of heat into existence upon the expenditure of work. Now let us follow the reverse process.
