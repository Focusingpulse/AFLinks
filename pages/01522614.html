Chandler is an American independent researcher and software developer based in the Baltimore, Maryland area. Largely self-taught in physics, he develops and publishes theories on astrophysics , geophysics and atomic structure that emphasize the role of electromagnetic forces, working outside the mainstream scientific community and within the Electric Universe and plasma-cosmology milieu. Biography Chandler describes having dedicated himself to philosophy at the age of 16. Before turning to science, he spent about five years in the merchant marines and roughly ten years as a carpenter, after which he moved into computing.
