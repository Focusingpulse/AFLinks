Biography Gamboa-Eastman is based in San Francisco, California, United States. Work Gamboa-Eastman's published work concerns the interpretation of quantum mechanics, in particular quantum nonlocality and the Born rule that governs probabilities in quantum theory. This line of inquiry connects to broader questions about the role of the observer and of consciousness in quantum measurement. Gamboa-Eastman also presented on quantum approaches to consciousness at a 1999 conference on the subject. Publications "Nonlocality and the Quantum Born Rules", Physics Essays , V7, N1, p. 3 (Mar 1994). External links Quantum Approaches
