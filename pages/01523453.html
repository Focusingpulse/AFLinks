Scientific career Fields IT engineer Michael Studencki (born December 3, 1982) is a Polish IT engineer and independent researcher based in Żywiec, Poland. He describes his interests as spanning atomic structure , quantum theory , quantum computing , IT , AI & cognitive science , consciousness , electrodynamics , gravity , the Ether , DNA , biochemistry , geometry , Vedic maths , ancient technology and cymatics . His work is pursued outside mainstream academic physics. Biography Studencki has described himself as driven from childhood by curiosity about how the natural world works. According to his own account he learned to read at about the age of five and, growing up in a house full of books, read widely on scientific subjects from an early age, including works on electricity, atoms, chemical reactions, molecules, combustion engines and living cells while still in primary school. He recalls dismantling toys to understand their mechanisms and experimenting with electricity, including an attempt to build a half-adder circuit from electromagnets. He has stated that he found formal schooling in physics and chemistry disappointing, feeling that the material was taught too simply or too late. He objected in particular to being taught the Bohr model of the atom, which he criticized as being two-dimensional and, in his view, unable to describe three-dimensional objects, and he questioned the wave–particle duality of matter.
