👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 22 October 1915 · archived # 2,437,055
, Dornach Lecture VII In a recent lecture I said something to which I want to return today, because in its logical conclusion it forms a kind of foundation for what I still have to say. I said that a spiritual-scientific Movement such as ours must be one which takes full account of the demands of the present cycle of the evolution of humanity and of the necessary consequences of this evolution.
