First Circle are 7 Minor Keynotes

· archived # 1,459,461
The first circle are 7 Minor Key-notes , their roots having been the last 7 Key-notes that have developed. The second circle is a continuation of the first, shewing the 7 previously developed Key-notes are the roots of the 7 higher Key-notes .
