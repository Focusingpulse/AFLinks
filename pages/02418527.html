On the Formation of the Cosmos [ 1 ] Since theosophy speaks solely from its own experience, it proceeds from a single point of departure: our Logos. — The fact that people today refer to what they see as a “solar sphere” as the “sun” is a peculiarity of our materialistic age. As recently as six centuries ago, people said: “This is a body; and it includes a solar soul and a solar spirit.” The Spirit of the Sun is now the Logos; the physical body of the Sun is its lowest manifestation.
