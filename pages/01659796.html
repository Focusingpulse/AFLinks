The Human Genome Project was followed by HapMap, a public-private research consortium dedicated to finding genetic variants that predispose people to common illnesses such as cancer, Alzheimer’s and cardiovascular disease. HapMap was launched in Washington in 2002 [4], involving scientists and funding agencies from Japan, the UK, Canada, China, Nigeria, and the US.
