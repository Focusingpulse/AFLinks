Tags: None Ingram Senior Member Join Date: May 2008 Posts: 284 Share Tweet #2 08-09-2011, 09:34 PM Odon, OEM is a wholesale version that is sold from the manufacturer to another manufacturer or retailer for integration into a product. Case in point, some years ago I had four pc's built at a computer shop & they provided an installed OEM version of Windows XP, which was perfectly appropriate. However, had he sold me OEM Windows XP by itself with no hardware, that would be a violation of his terms with his wholesaler (manufacturer). So by the book, yes, it is illegal to sell OEM as a stand alone.
