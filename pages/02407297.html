Enoch is capable of deep research, generating content, summarizing content, answering questions, basic reasoning and more. How Enoch Works Enoch refers to a very large knowledge base that is used to influence and alter the knowledge and output of open source text generation LLMs. A behind-the-scenes system prompt is also embedded in Enoch's prompt handling to further enforce compliance with the philosophies of truth, natural medicine, decentralization, liberty, economic freedom and other topics reflected in the data set. Because open source models are constantly improved and being released, Enoch will also be upgraded with those improved base models. Additionally, Enoch's knowledge base is constantly expanding, month by month. Note that a standalone, downloadable version of Enoch that runs locally will also be released soon.
