A SMALL LOSS EXPECTED. A Cable for Each 5,000 Horse-power Will be Laid — Many Points Are Yet to be Determined Upon as to the Apparatus to be Used. Prof. George Forbes, the celebrated English electrical expert, is a recent arrival in this country. He is the consulting electrical engineer for the great Niagara Falls tunnel, and to a reporter for the Electrical Review he has been telling some interesting facts about the development and transmission of the power. The interview in part is here reproduced: “What kind of generators will you use?” “Well, for Buffalo distribution we shall undoubtedly use the alternating current system, but as regards the local plants, we have not made up our minds whether the current shall be continuous or alternating. Of course, you understand that many arguments may be advanced in favor of both, and you may rest assured that the one which has the most commercial advantages will be adopted beyond any doubt.
