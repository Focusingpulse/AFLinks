Marek Pawlowski Residence Warsaw, Poland Nationality Polish Known for The Higgs-free conformal unification of the Standard Model and gravity, developed with Ryszard Rączka Scientific career Fields Theoretical particle physics; conformal symmetry; quantum gravity; detector instrumentation Marek Pawłowski is a Polish theoretical physicist, associated with the Soltan Institute for Nuclear Studies in Warsaw and latterly with the University of Warsaw. He is best known for a programme developed in the 1990s with Ryszard Rączka : a unified model of the strong, electroweak and gravitational interactions built on local conformal symmetry , which reproduces the mass spectrum of the Standard Model without a dynamical Higgs field . Unlike most figures catalogued on this wiki, Pawłowski published this work in the mainstream literature — Foundations of Physics , Physics Letters B , General Relativity and Gravitation , International Journal of Modern Physics A — and it was cited there. He appears here because of one paper given to a 1997 conference on special relativity, but that paper is a summary of a substantial published research programme rather than an isolated contribution. Work The conformal programme The idea Pawłowski takes as his starting point is a generalisation of the relativity principle itself, and he attributes it to Hermann Weyl . The principle of "democracy between reference systems" was formulated by Galileo, modified by special relativity and generalised by general relativity; Weyl's proposal was to extend the same democracy to reference scales — that is, to make the choice of unit of length a matter of local convention, exactly as the choice of frame already is. Taking that seriously means demanding local conformal symmetry , and the model Pawłowski and Rączka built on it is based on the symmetry group SU(3) × SU(2) L × U(1) × C , with C the local conformal group.
