Feeling Supported

· archived # 2,432,526
I've been pondering a bit about law of attraction and money lately. Some friends and I were talking about it bc one of them had a book on it and we were checking it out. I thought about what mental blocks I may have about being rich. For example, when I think about rich people I've met I tend to think that I wouldn't want to be that person. Either they don't seem to be having fun in life, they have too much responsibility, people treat them differently or whatever it is. So if I try to visualize being rich, it doesn't really feel that good to me. This is obviously my limitation as I'm sure there are people who are both rich and happy. Anyway it finally occurred to me that what does feel really good to me is to imagine having a ton of emotional and mental support from other people. Visualizing / imagining the feeling of this allows me to feel really really good and abundant, which is probably more helpful towards attracting $ anyway. Just thought I would offer the idea for folks to focus on feeling very very emotionally and mentally supported by people in general. I've enjoyed meditating on it recently XO Jessica
