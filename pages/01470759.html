electric potential

· archived # 1,470,759
STANDARD : In classical electromagnetism , the electric potential (denoted by V; also called the electrostatic potential ) at a point in space is electrical potential energy divided by charge that is associated with a static (time-invariant) electric field. It is a scalar quantity, typically measured in volts . There is also a generalized electric scalar potential that is used in electrodynamics when time-varying electromagnetic fields are present. This generalized electric potential cannot be simply interpreted as the ratio of potential energy to charge , however.
