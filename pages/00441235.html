My simple guide to free-fall motion in general relativity A.J.Owen v1 September 22nd 2024 Author's contact email address: ajowen@physics.org Abstract The free-fall behaviour of a test object near a static point mass has been calculated for two cases: the rst is the usual black-hole solution in general relativity resulting from using Schwarzschild coordinates, and the second is for a model described in previous papers, in which spacetime is completely regular with no event horizon.
