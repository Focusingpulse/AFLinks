He works in the museum that resides in a shadow, just as the most of the museums do. It was like that until members of Japanese Aum Shinrikyo cult, which bases its promotion on intellectual superiority and power, came. In order to obtain power, they recalled of Tesla and came to Belgrade. No matter how mystic and wrapped in secret their arrival was, regarding Tokyo subway poisoning (for which the cult was accused of), one thing is for sure - they awoke spiritual hunger for deeper knowledge of Tesla. That's what it was about in the interview with Marinčić. Controversy implies. We believe that this intellectual duel, which delights scientifically oriented people - despite their declaration - will propel a new, deeper study of Tesla.
