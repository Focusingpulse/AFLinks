Stephen John Crothers Born ( 1957-06-14 ) June 14, 1957 (age 69) Residence Sunshine Plaza, Queensland, Australia Nationality Australia Known for Black hole , Big Bang , General Relativity Scientific career Fields Theoretical astrophysics, General relativity , Special relativity , thermodynamics Stephen John Crothers (born 14 June 1957) is an Australian independent researcher known for his mathematical and philosophical criticisms of the theory of black holes , the big bang , and aspects of Einstein's General Relativity . He argues that black holes are neither predicted by nor consistent with general relativity, and that the expanding-universe cosmology is mistaken. He travels internationally to publish papers and deliver lectures on these views, which are rejected by mainstream physics. Biography Education Master of Science Part-time candidature for a Ph.D. in the School of Physics, University of New South Wales , from about March 2003 — not completed; Crothers states he was expelled from candidature Career Crothers holds a Master of Science degree and describes himself as an independent researcher in theoretical astrophysics. He resides in Queensland, Australia. In about March 2003 he commenced part-time candidature for a PhD in the School of Physics at the University of New South Wales (UNSW) in Sydney, under the supervision of Professor John K. Webb, with Professor Michael Ashley as support supervisor. He was initially engaged in developing a computer simulation program relating to extra-solar planets. About a year later he began working on general relativity as a sideline, and reported that he had obtained a result which he claimed showed black holes to be inconsistent with general relativity. Crothers states that he was subsequently expelled from candidature by UNSW, an account he documents on his personal website.
