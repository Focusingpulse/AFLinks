Modeling the Quantum World with Newtonian Physics - Jeff Yee Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Date 15 May 2021 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording Modeling the Quantum World with Newtonian Physics - Jeff Yee was a live session on 15 May 2021 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. We will be talking with Jeff Yee who is currently directing a project to model the universe from the quantum mechanical level on up. From neutrinos and aether, to quarks and neutrons, to protons and electrons. Yee's model correctly generates the SPDF orbitals of electrons around atoms and their isotopes. And anyone can download and play with the model. Don't miss this fascinating presentation and discussion with one of the great critical thinkers of our time! Summary Jeff Yee on modelling the quantum world with Newtonian physics — the simulation work that had grown out of his energy wave theory. David de Hilster hosted, and spent the first part of the session drawing out how Yee came to it. How he got here Yee's career has been in wireless — AT&T, Trimo Labs, antenna software — which is to say in electromagnetic waves; and he has a mechanical engineering degree from Hawaii and a master's in management. He wrote a novel about time travel, which he described as for fun and which nonetheless got him thinking about how things work. The research began about fifteen or twenty years ago with string theory, which he came to distrust — too many holes, and a bad instinct about it. Searching for people who had gone another way, he arrived, he thought at about the hundredth site, at Gabriel LaFreniere 's, and then at Milo Wolff 's, and matter made from waves became the path.
