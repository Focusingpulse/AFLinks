powerlite forms

· archived # 2,427,924
Metglas ® POWERLITE® FORMS Technical Bulletin www.metglas.com POWERLITE® forms are made by stacking iron-based Metglas® amorphous alloy 2605SA1 ribbons or laminations. The laminations are ld together by an adhesive rated for a continuous operating temperature of 155°C. The amorphous alloy itself has a higher continuous operating temperature. These forms offer a unique combination of high saturation induction (1.56 T), high permeability and low core loss. POWERLITE® forms can be used in medium frequency (<20 kHz) and high power (300 to 500 kW) inverters. These have core losses lower than those of 3% or 6% Si steels.
