Epilogue to the New Edition of 1918 — GA 6. Goethe's World View (1985)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 1,585,840

For I believe that in the course of what has been said I have shown that these ideas about nature rest upon a quite definite way of looking at the phenomena of the world.
