2013 GlobalBEM Conference

· archived # 2,464,679
Organized by Madeline Vilar and Jeroen van Straaten and co-hosted by Conscious Media Network and Gaiam TV ‘s Regina Meredith and Searle Magnetics Jason Verbelli , the 2013 Global Breakthrough Energy Movement ( GlobalBEM ) was held in Boulder, Colorado on October 10-12, 2013. Read about this event by Ruby Carat in Infinite Energy Magazine #112 November/December 2013. Find summaries of talks, reviews, and more, from the voice of GlobalBEM PULSE magazine . The event was livestreamed at http://new.livestream.com/accounts/5708490 and video has been uploaded to the GlobalBEM Youtube Channel and RWGresearchlive . Selected excerpts are linked below.
