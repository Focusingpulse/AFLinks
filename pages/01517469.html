One day, at the restaurant of the institute, I had sat with a table opposite two engineers and their guest, specialists in the transformers. I listened to the conversation, and the engineers said to their guest, that they could calculate the parameters of the transformers with an accuracy of 0.1%, but which they always find 2% excess electric output. This surplus of electric output remains unexplainable while taking account of ohmic losses etc I left the restaurant by thinking that these 2% of power in excess could come from an unknown source. A few years later, I took note of the theory of the Zero Point Energy (ZPE). I supposed that it should be possible to extract part of this phenomenal energy.
