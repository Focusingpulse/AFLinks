GC2026-PERM051 Preliminary magnetostratigraphic data on the boundary interval of the Lower-Middle Jurassic of the North Caucasus (Karachay-Cherkessia) Ilya M. Tonkoshkurov1 , Dmitry N. Kisilev2 , Oleg A. Lutikov3 1 N.G. Chernyshevsky Saratov National Research State University 2 K. D. Ushinsky Yaroslavl State Pedagogical University 3 Geological Institute of the Russian Academy of Sciences ilja.tonkoshkurov@rambler.ru Reconnaissance paleomagnetic sampling was conducted in a section of Lower–Middle Jurassic boundary deposits near the village of Nizhnyaya Yermolovka (Karachay-Cherkessia). The section is primarily composed of mudstones and sandstones and has a total thickness of 37 m. The deposits are characterized by moderate to high magnetic susceptibility (Km ) values (6.9 to 53.0 × 10⁻⁸ m³/kg) and natural remanent magnetization (J n ) values (0.1 to 5.0 × 10⁻³ A/m). Magnetic saturation data indicate a predominantly soft magnetic phase characteristic of finely dispersed magnetite. The presence of finely dispersed pyrite in the sediments is evidenced by an increase in Km (up to 647 × 10⁻⁸ m³/kg) after heating the rocks to 500°C for one hour. The anisotropy of magnetic susceptibility reflects deposition in a calm hydrodynamic environment and corresponds to the primary magnetic fabric of the rocks. Thermomagnetic curves obtained during the first heating reveal the presence of pyrite and siderite, indicated by increases in magnetization at ~500°C and ~350°C due to the FeS₂ → Fe₃O₄ and FeCO₃ → Fe₃O₄ phase transformations, respectively.
