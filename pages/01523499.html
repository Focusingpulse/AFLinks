This was accomplished by replacing Maxwell's partial time derivatives with total (complete) time derivatives, while leaving the spatial partial derivatives unaltered. To proceed to high-order approximations, frame time is replaced by field detector proper time. The resulting "neo-Hertzian" wave equation is solved in three dimensions by the method of d'Alembert.
