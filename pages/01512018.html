Having had quite a few non working models - no surprise - I have built up a new wheel (frame) as my test bed to explore some different mechanisms. I have come up with four different options that I think may work, and I will post them up as I go along. The wheel is quite large in size but should be easier to work with, and more forgiving of small imbalances. So to start with I have built a rim, 6 ft diameter, made from 6 glued laminations of thin plywood, with holes drilled at every 45 deg on the outside edge, to attach spokes etc to. The spokes are wood, attached to the rim by heavy duty L brackets, and to the hub by plywood plates. The hub runs on a solid 1/2inch steel shaft running in wood cutouts. It rotates very easily and so far so good. I will keep you posted. Regards John Last edited by john_g ; 03-15-2012, 05:30 AM .
