Lecture VII - GA 293. The Study of Man

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 28 August 1919 · archived # 2,441,536
, Stuttgart Lecture VII Your task is to gain an insight into what the human being really is. Up to now in our survey of general pedagogy we have endeavoured to comprehend this nature of man first of all from the point of view of the soul and then from that of the spirit. To-day we will continue from the latter point of view.
