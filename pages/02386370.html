Which argument? CF is based > upon experimental evidence, not argument or theory ***{Jed, I understand your central point here, and I agree with you. However, words are important, and so I am going to quibble with the way you are expressing your point. The problem I have with your wording is that the significance of a fact is always bound up in its implications. The statement that CF exists is an inference from experimental evidence. The chain of reasoning which supports this conclusion is called an argument. When Dick Blue, or Bill Rowe, or whoever, draws a different conclusion from the same evidence, his chain of inferences is also an argument. Thus when you say that "CF is based on experimental evidence, not argument or theory" your statement is a virtual contradiction in terms.
