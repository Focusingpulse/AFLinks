GTM2026-main001 Network formation and time consistency in dynamic oligopolistic cooperation Xinran Hao1 , Artem Sedakov1 1 St Petersburg University a.sedakov@spbu.ru This paper studies dynamic cooperation with endogenous link formation among oligopolistic firms. By employing a weighted objective, we formulate a trade-off model between total profit and social welfare, and then examine the optimal cooperation strategy under participation incentives. The optimal strategies are verified to be time￾consistent, and the analysis further reveals the mechanism governing the evolution of network density and core￾link stability. We further incorporate individual rationality constraints and use an artificial neural network method to approximate the optimal network structure and decisions in the high-dimensional setting. The results show that participation and individual rationality constraints improve distributive balance and network stability at only a small efficiency cost. Overall, a moderate balance between efficiency and fairness is more conducive to sustaining long-term cooperation. Keywords: oligopolistic cooperation; R&D investment; dynamic network formation; time consistency; social welfare This research has been supported by: 1.
