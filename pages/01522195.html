Coulomb's Law Author(s) Dimiter G Stoinov Keywords Coulomb's law, Charge, electromagnetic waves Published 2000 Journal Galilean Electrodynamics Volume 11 Number 5 Pages 97-99 Abstract This paper draws an analogy between interactions among statistical ensembles of oscillators and among charged particles. It develops the hypothesis that the electrons and protons are peculiar oscillators, having certain internal dynamics, and like atoms and molecules present sources of electromagnetic waves. Such waves are possibly the reason for interactions between the particles. Corroboration for the hypothesis is supplied by deriving the Coulomb,s law.
