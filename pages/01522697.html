Gagliardo (Cristina Nora Gagliardo) is an Argentine researcher associated with the Fundación Julio Palacios in Neuquén, Argentina, known for her collaboration with Jorge Guala-Valverde on experiments and interpretations of homopolar (unipolar) electromagnetic machines. She is listed in The Worldwide List of Dissident Scientists . Work Gagliardo has worked with Guala-Valverde on experimental and theoretical studies of the homopolar motor, including the correspondence "Updating Faraday," and is acknowledged for conceptual contributions in related papers published in the American Journal of Physics .
