, Cologne Translated by Steiner Online Library Esoteric Lesson John the Baptist heralded Christ Jesus in the middle of the fourth sub-race. Now, however, the individuality of the Master Jesus is leading humanity from the fifth to the sixth sub-race—back to John the Baptist, the Aquarius. Christ Jesus is the living Word. All beings in nature flow together in man and form the Word in him. This is the I in man—Jesus Christ.
