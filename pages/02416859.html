The Deeper Mysteries in Goethe’s “Faust” [ 1 ] Not long before completing the second part of his Faust , Goethe told his faithful friend Eckermann that he had made a special effort with this work to ensure it met theatrical and artistic standards, so that those who wish to enjoy it purely with their senses would be satisfied. And Goethe himself adds that those who are initiated into the mysteries will surely find something deeper behind these images. But everything is indeed sensuous, and, when presented on stage, will appeal to everyone
