THE PLACE Themes to remember as you read: • Each local Place is a whole system in itself - a whole ecological system, a whole cultural phenomenon, a whole spiritual reality; • Each Place is also part of a greater whole — A greater whole that is a group of local places, all in relationship with each other; • These Places (of all sizes) are “woven” together by exchanges of energy and materials, flowing in cycles. Physically, ecologically, and spiritually; • Because the local Places are interwoven with the other places in the bigger Place, they cannot be separated — conceptually or physically — without changing, or even threatening, the greater whole. One cannot understand a local Place without understanding its surrounding Places. Once a local Place is physically separated or altered in itself, the bigger Place is changed; • Local Places and the greater Place they are part of reflect each other. One learns something essential about the whole region by learning about the local Place, and the other way around; • A local Place experiences and contribute to cycles of renewal and change in the bigger Place. Seasons in the Zapata area return….. always the same in a fundamental way……. yet never the same in detail. What happens on Sierra Blanca influences what happens in the Valley. • Short, quick cycles happen in a place at the same time as long, slow-moving ones. Consider the cycle of a day and night in comparison to the cycle of a year. While three hundred and fifty five days and nights happen, one year passes.…….; • Different cultures think about Time in different ways. Some cultures teach that Time is moving in a line from the past to the future. Others teach that past, present and future are always all present, little cycles in one big whole. In this way of understanding, Time is the water in a lake; the ripples and waves are events in the past, present, and future. These ripples rise in the water and subside into the water.
