Title Theory of Gravity Machines Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Jovan Marjanovic Keywords gravity machine, gravity shield, over unity, pendulum Published 2010 No. of pages 10 Abstract This work is a presentation of simple theory of using conservative gravitational field as a fuel. In order to extract energy from conservative field, gravity shield effect is necessary. With proper usage of the shield, variable gravity field should be created in part of a system. Energy can be extracted only if difference in potential exists between two poles. The logic of this theory will also be used to explain two-stage mechanical oscillator of Veljko Milkovic and areas of its improvement. Overview Jovan Marjanović, writing from the Veljko Milković Research & Development Center in Novi Sad, sets out a theory intended to explain how a conservative gravitational field could be used as fuel, and applies it to Milković's two-stage mechanical oscillator. He states the obstacle clearly at the outset. A uniform gravitational field can do work once, by lowering a body, and restoring the body costs exactly the same work in the absence of friction. This, he writes, is why "official science is refusing any idea of using a conservative field as energy source." His proposed way round it is a gravity shield. Suppose a plate that can be switched on to nullify gravity on a mass M resting above it. With the shield on, a small mass m hanging over a pulley can raise M against inertia alone; with the shield off, M outweighs m and falls, and the cycle repeats. Provided the switching costs less than the energy extracted, the arrangement delivers a surplus. No such shield exists, so he proposes a dynamic equivalent. Consider a pendulum released from the horizontal. At the lowest point the string tension is three times the bob's weight, being the sum of the weight and the centripetal term.
