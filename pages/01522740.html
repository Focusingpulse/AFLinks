Walton Almamater University of Newcastle Knownfor Ivor Catt Scientific career Fields Atomic physics, electromagnetic theory, digital electronics David S Walton is a British physicist, electronics engineer and teacher, known chiefly as a long-time collaborator of the British electronics engineer Ivor Catt . With Catt and Malcolm Davidson he formed the "Catt, Walton and Davidson" partnership, whose late-1970s articles and books challenged the conventional textbook treatment of Maxwell's displacement current and put forward a model of the capacitor as a transmission line. Biography Walton took his first degree in Physics at the University of Newcastle and remained there to carry out research in experimental atomic physics, leading to a PhD.
