( click to enlarge ) See Also The Law of Cycles — A One-Page Explainer What the Law of Cycles Is The Law of Cycles describes why and how systems change state. It is not a law of force, power, or energy input, but a law of harmonic compatibility. A system changes state not because energy is added, but because its internal relationships can no longer remain compatible. The Core Principle Change of state occurs when harmony becomes unsustainable. “Harmony” here does not mean beauty or preference. It means stable phase relationships—ratios, timings, and motions that can coexist without conflict. When those relationships become overcrowded or constrained, discord arises, and the system must reorganize. Harmony, Discord, and Necessity Harmony (Concord) Stable, compatible
