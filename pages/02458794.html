These are enor￾mously important endeavors because the SAQ is a sobering case study of the very real and immediate threats to academic freedom (Dudley, 2020). But make no mistake, any￾one who assumes that there is consensus among well-informed scholars about the true identity of “Shakespeare” should think again. Think again is a famous idiom that sounds like something our acclaimed English writer could have coined. But it is aptly fitting in this context because it denotes what someone “believes or expects” is not actually true or will not happen (Merriam-Webster, n.d.). And it is standard practice for academics to “think again” about important events. For instance, consider another legendary topic in history and contemporary story-tell￾ing—the Knights Templar, aka, the Poor Knights of the Temple of King Solomon.
