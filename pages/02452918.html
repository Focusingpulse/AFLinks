Heartlink Set-up ---------------- The heartlink units consist of a hardware (box) interface and its unique software programme. It allows 2 person's EKG to be sampled or 1 person's EKG and EEG to be sampled and then displayed on the screen in real time. It has the potential of having additional probes added to the hardware to capture an additional source of input (more of this later). In very simple terms, traditional biofeedback systems would collect the raw EEG data, see the missing bandwidths (eg alpha or beta, etc bandwidths) and attempt to 'fill-in' the gaps with the use of interactive games to train the user to elicit those missing brain frequencies. The heartlink software goes a few steps further. It takes the raw data and performs a FFT (fast fourier transform) on it and gives a 1st FFT Plot called EKG harmonics (in Dan's web site). Then it performs another FFT on this 1st Plot to give the 2nd FFT called the EKG coherence (in Dan's web site). As I understand it, FFT takes into consideration all the various frequencies ie the ratios of one frequency to another and therefore takes into consideration the whole bandwidth of brain frequencies.
