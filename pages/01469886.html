coefficient of inertia

· archived # 1,469,886
"It is to this fundamental property of the invari- 17 ability of the mass that we had always to come back. Philosophers and scholars long ago gave up seeking an exact definition of matter. The invariability of the mass of a given quantity of substance - that is to say, its coefficient of inertia measured by its weight , remained the sole irreducible characteristic of matter."
