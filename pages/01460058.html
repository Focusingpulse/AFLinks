John Bedini of the Bedini Electronics company is an electrical engineer. He created the Bedini Audio Spectral Enhancer, an audio signal processor; and he has filed patents for a number of audio technologies. Additionally, on his personal Web site, Bedini has self-published a number of articles related to experimental methods for generating and transmitting electrical energy.
