About A collection of papers delivered at the 1988 Tesla Symposium in Colorado Springs. Speakers included Nikola Pribic, Dr. Rastko C. Maglic, Dr. Elizabeth A. Rauscher, Bruce E. DePalma, John E. Moray, George D. Hathaway, and Craig Spaniol. The general topics were: The Life and Times of Nikola Tesla; Tesla Coil Theory, Construction and Applications; Advances in Gravitics; and Advances in Electromagnetics. William H. Terbo, "Opening Address"
