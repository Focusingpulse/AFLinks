6. The Three Worlds — GA 68b. The Circular Flow of Man's Life

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 3 February 1906 · archived # 2,413,574
, Hamburg Translated by Steiner Online Library Compare 6. The Three Worlds [ 1 ] Ladies and gentlemen! When one looks at a cloud—or indeed at the cloudy sky—one has no idea that in the very next moment, lightning will flash from the cloud and thunder will roll. Lightning and thunder are phenomena hidden within the cloud. — This is a metaphor for the things around us; there, too, much is hidden that can be awakened from its slumber. [ 2 ] I will now attempt to characterize this hidden realm. It is what is referred to in theosophy as the three worlds. These three worlds are not separate from one
