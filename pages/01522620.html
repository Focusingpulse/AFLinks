ChatGPT Solves Dark Matter Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Speaker Franklin Hu Date 19 October 2024 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording ChatGPT Solves Dark Matter was a live session presented by Franklin Hu on 19 October 2024 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. The proof for Dark Matter in galaxy rotation curves depends on using the formula v = SQRT(GM(enclosed)/r). However, this formula can only be correctly applied to "spherical" mass distributions and cannot be directly applied to anything like a flat disk galaxy. The anomalous dark matter is appearing because we applying a formula that only works on spheres on a disk shaped galaxy. Papers show that you can recover the observed rotation curves if you calculate gravity correctly using a disk shape and no dark matter is needed. Ordinary Newtonian gravity can explain the galaxy rotation curves with no modification. Summary Franklin Hu 's claim is that dark matter is an artefact of applying the wrong formula, and that a language model stumbled into the right answer while making an arithmetic mistake. Roger J Anderton hosted and offered his own diagnosis at the outset: what is taught as Newtonian physics is a dumbed-down version, and a fuller one might account for what dark matter was invented to explain. The wrong formula The starting point is David de Hilster 's paper and video, which set Hu off. Newton's universal law is a sum over distances between masses; GM / r 2 with a single radius is a result Newton derived for the special case of a sphere, and holds only outside the mass. Hu's illustration was the Great Pyramid: stand on its apex and ask what radius to use. There is none, and the only honest method is to compute the attraction of every block and add them up — which is exactly what Newton did for the sphere, arriving after considerable labour at a formula that looks like the naive one. Two consequences follow that he thinks decide the matter.
