Chapter 5. Concerning Reincarnation and Karma; Man's Astral Body and the Spiritual World; and Ahrimanic Beings - The Threshold of the Spiritual World (1922)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 2,396,175
It is especially difficult for the soul to recognise that there is something prevailing within its life which is environment to the soul in the same way as the so-called outer world is environment to the ordinary senses. The soul unconsciously resists this, because it imagines its independent existence imperilled by such a fact; and therefore instinctively turns away from it. For though more modern science theoretically admits the existence of the fact, this does not mean that it is as yet fully realised, with all the consequences of inwardly grasping it and becoming permeated with it.
