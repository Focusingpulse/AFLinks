Its principle of operation is based upon the use of electric field potential; that is, variants of an electrostatic field imposed upon a base plate. The device has been tested for transmission of data over long distances without echo phenomena. The authors ask if the transmission is instantaneous and if the device undergoes weight loss due to microscale antigravity. What is disputed here This page is one item in a larger argument.
