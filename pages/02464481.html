GlobalBEM PULSE #3: “We ourselves are the biggest obstacle to breakthrough”

· archived # 2,464,481
The Global Breakthrough Energy Movement is a collective of artists, activists, and technologists creating events and forums for new energy researchers in order to power a revolution in human living arrangements, and they have published another edition of their flagship magazine . PULSE presents science and technology in the field of breakthrough energy. The gorgeous PULSE #3 is a sensory banquet of full-page, beautifully-printed art, and has articles on topics such as The Agonizingly Long Wait for Breakthrough of Breakthrough Energy by Fred Teunisson , who laments the lack of progress in commercial development of new energy technology, and ultimately takes a hard look in the mirror for why. This issue also features an article on The Explanation of Low Energy Nuclear Reaction by Dr. Edmund Storms , a book that compares the observational data from cold fusion experiments with the many theories of LENR.
