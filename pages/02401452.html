GC2026-PERM012 Reconstruction of the magnetic moment of magnetite and titanomagnetite nanoparticles from magnetic force microscopy data using an evolutionary algorithm Ulyana A. Puginskaya1 , Ruslan A. Rytov2 , Aleksey N. Tselebrovskiy1 , Roman A. Grachev1 , Valery I. Maksimochkin1 1 Lomonosov Moscow State University 2 Pushkov Institute of Terrestrial Magnetism, Ionosphere and Radiowave Propagation of the Russian Academy of Sciences, Moscow, Russia puginskayaua@my.msu.ru The Day-Dunlop diagram is widely used to determine the domain state of magnetic mineral assemblies in rocks. The method utilizes the parameters of the macroscopic hysteresis loop: the ratio of residual magnetization to saturation magnetization (Mrs/Ms) and the ratio of the coercivity of the residual magnetization to the coercivity (Hcr/Hc). They are plotted on a two-dimensional graph, where regions for single-domain (SD), pseudo-single￾domain (PSD) and multi-domain (MD) states of particles are empirically identified. However, the interpretation of such a diagram is ambiguous; for example, the PSD state of particles may correspond to several magnetization states. As a result, the assessment of the domain state of particles only using the Day diagram can be qualitative at best [1]. The Day-Dunlop diagram method can be supplemented by magnetic force microscopy (MFM), which allows for high-quality observation of domain states of individual particles in an external magnetic field [2].
