The Observer’s Sky Atlas Third Edition E. Karkoschka The Observer’s Sky Atlas With 50 Star Charts Covering the Entire Sky Third Edition 123 E. Karkoschka Lunar and Planetary Lab Space Sciences Building University of Arizona Tucson, AZ 85721 USA Cover photograph: Based on a photograph by the author, it shows the southwest part of the constellation Sagittarius together with the brightest Milky Way clouds. North is to the upper left. The red nebula near the left edge is the Lagoon Nebula M8. Many other objects can be identified with the help of chart E20. Frontispiece: The sword of Orion, containing the Orion Nebula. Looking at it with a large telescope on a dark night gives one of the grandest views in the universe. The faint reflection nebula NGC 1973 lies half way up to the top of the photograph where the stellar group NGC 1981 can be seen.
