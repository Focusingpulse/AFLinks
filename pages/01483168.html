salt

In chemistry , a salt is an ionic compound that results from the neutralization reaction of an acid and a base. Salts are composed of related numbers of cations (positively charged ions) and anions (negative ions) so that the product is electrically neutral (without a net charge ). Wikipedia, Salt Russell Just as sodium and chlorine find balance in their salt , and thus lose their metallic qualities, so do all pairs of metals lose their metallic unbalance in their salts . A salt in Nature is a balanced pair of elements.
