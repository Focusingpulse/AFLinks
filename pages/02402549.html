Unfortunately, this population has no identified members, which of course might due their short age. The surprising finding was that a population II star HE 1327-2326 contains unusually large amount of Zinc heavier that iron. This looks very strange since elements heavier than Fe should be produced much later. The proposal is that these stars collapse in neutron stars in asymmetric manner by generating jets so that the stars formed along jets would receive much more heavier elements.
