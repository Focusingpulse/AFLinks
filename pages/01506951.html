What's your oponion about Hell?

· archived # 1,506,951
Hi there, A question has been wiggling in my mind for a while and it was that does an entity named Hell exist? Some of the new age thinkers claim that there is no such thing as Hell. But can this claim be justified by the existence of a Just God? How can those who do harm others, suppress technologies, spread war, spread ignorance be no different from those who live the opposite in the afterlife? People who help the poor, give love to their neighbors, spread what they know surely aren't even comparable with evil people. I have concluded that there indeed must be a place where these criminals get what they deserve (like Hell). Indeed they will be suffering in there own deeds also. Anyone has any opinion?
