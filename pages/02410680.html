MRS. MOORE ON THE KEELY MOTOR MONDAY, DECEMBER 9, 1895, Page. 8/6 Privileges Sold by Mrs. B. Moore ; Letter to C. B. Collier MRS. MOORE ON THE KEELY MOTOR She Writes a Letter in Which She Discusses Certain Recent Transactions and Rumors. PHILADELPHIA, Dec. 8.—In answer to a letter from Charles B. Collier , for many years counsel of John E. W. Keely , the inventor, Mrs. Bloomfield Moore has addressed a letter to that gentleman in which she makes some interesting statements regarding recent events bearing on the Keely Motor Company . After referring to a late publication in which the transfer of her privileges to a wealthy syndicate was alluded to, she says: It was this word “privileges" which was construed by you all to mean my share in the inventions of the air-ship propeller and the railway traction engine, leading to the giving of information which was most incorrect, as no effort has ever been made to buy my interests.
