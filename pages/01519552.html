Consideration of the views of Jean de Climont on 'Aether' Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Date 24 June 2023 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording Consideration of the views of Jean de Climont on 'Aether' was a live session on 24 June 2023 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. The Failure of Maxwell and Einstein and related topics, through an examination of his videos. Summary A session without a speaker. Ian J Cowan had tried to persuade Jean de Climont to present in person and been declined — de Climont's reason being that English is not his first language — so Cowan played his recorded videos instead and led the discussion. The first showing carried sound but no picture, which Cowan did not discover until it had run its full twenty minutes, since he was watching the video rather than the chat; it was played again, and he apologised. He noted that "Jean de Climont" is a pen name, and that its holder had been an academic working in fluid dynamics. The video De Climont's presentation is a rapid statement of a complete alternative cosmology, delivered as a series of assertions. Against Maxwell. Maxwell's theory is a fanciful interpretation of Faraday's and Ampère's experiments. The Maxwell–Faraday equation's rotational electric field has no effect on electron beams, so it does not apply in vacuum and cannot represent the propagation of a wave there; the Maxwell–Hertz equation is a postulate with no experimental justification, and without the first it makes no sense. The theory did explain the transverse nature of light, at the price of a corpuscular nature incompatible with an ether — which is why the wave–particle duality had to be invented. Against relativity.
