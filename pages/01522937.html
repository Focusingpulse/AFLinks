Title Frames Derivation Read in full Read in full Author(s) Ricardo L Carezani Keywords Autodynamics , Lorentz transformation , frames of reference, simplified Lorentz equations, Mass variation Published Society for the Advancement of Autodynamics Website https://www.autodynamics.org/frames-derivation/ "Frames Derivation" is one of the two derivations by which Ricardo L Carezani obtains the equations of Autodynamics . It is the shorter and, in Carezani's words, "more classical" of the pair; the other is the Galilean Simplification . Both are published by the Society for the Advancement of Autodynamics at autodynamics.org. Overview The derivation has two halves. The first is a critique: Carezani walks through the standard relativistic derivation of velocity addition and of force, and argues that two steps in it are unmotivated. The second is constructive: he discards the second coordinate system altogether and derives, from a single frame, what he calls the Simplified Lorentz equations — the transformation that carries the rest of Autodynamics. Throughout, β = v / c , m 0 is the rest mass and c the speed of light . The argument The objection to the relativistic derivation Carezani starts from the Lorentz transformation applied to a point P at rest in frame F , with F moving at velocity v relative to the observer's frame F ′ . Differentiating and dividing d x ′ by d t ′ gives the velocity-addition expression v ′ = v x + v 1 + v x v c 2 His first objection is that this expression "has 3 velocities even though it must only have 2". The velocity v x , he writes, "appears spontaneously through the mathematical operation of the derivative" and "has no physical meaning. There is no energy to move point P (originally at rest in frame F ) with respect to the F coordinate system."
