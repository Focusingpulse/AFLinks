He is best known for arguing that the phenomena usually attributed to Einstein's special theory of relativity can instead be derived from a revision of classical mechanics, without recourse to a geometric space-time . Biography Fernando is of Sri Lankan origin and resides in Toronto, Ontario, Canada.
