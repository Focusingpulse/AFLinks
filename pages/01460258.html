L Drault

· archived # 1,460,258
( click to enlarge ) The Drault Influence machine ⚡️ This imposing machine was built by L Drault of Paris around 1916. It was built to energise X-ray tubes. X-ray technology (but not knowledge of the danger of the rays) was well established by this time. What type of machine it was is currently unknown. There appear to be six pairs of closely-spaced discs, and there is no sign of Whimshurst sectors. The machine was driven by the electric motor under the table, and there are a number of pulleys under the disc assembly which probably caused adjacent discs to rotate in opposite directions. On the side of the table is written 'L Drualt - constructeurs Paris'. Unfortunately L Drault is unknown to Google, and no further information has been found so far.
