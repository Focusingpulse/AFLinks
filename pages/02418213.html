U.S. PSYCHOTRONICS ASSOCIATION An All-Volunteer, Non-Profit 501(C) (3) Corporation USPA Compilation of the Proceedings Tables of Contents The following pages are the tables of contents of the proceedings created at each of our conferences. When USPA begins to plan a conference, a call for papers is sent out from those who wish to be considered for a speaker’s spot. From 1986 -1992, the speaker-submitted conference papers were thoroughly edited and formatted. These papers were put into six journals, and represent some of the best written USPA material of that time span. Not all who submit are speakers; and ultimately, not all who speak submit a paper in time to be in the printed proceedings. So, you may find papers which do not have corresponding lectures in our digital collection. You may find lectures in our website shop areas that do not correspond with the papers in the proceedings. All the papers in our proceedings are copyright protected and are the property of the United States Psychotronics Association. They may not be reprinted without written permission of the Board of USPA.
