(ISSN: 2473-7194) For previous issues of our newsletter: https://www.psychotronics.org/library/ New Year’s Greetings from USPA President, Jon Klimo Dear USPA members and friends, It has been very difficult the past almost 3 years getting used to what’s left of me after three successive strokes. Right now, I’m trying to orally dictate what I’m saying to you. I wrote a few books that way a few years ago, so I should be able to get back in the hang of this as I progress. Luckily, I have incurred no cognitive deficits from these strokes. I feel clear as ever. And with the dictation aspect, I should be able to get around my deficit with regard to speaking and writing. I am still wheelchair-bound, still being largely dysfunctional with the right side of my body. Though I know it’s not very funny, but I find myself telling people, “don’t ever have a stroke.” Of course one never wants to have one and its repercussions, but I say it nonetheless, because I really mean it. Don’t ever have your own stroke, based on the way I have had to experience mine. I’m trying to figure out how I can forward a book I wrote to Gail to publish parts of it in the newsletter. Between us so far, we are pretty klutzy at trying to figure it out. But who knows, maybe with the dictation system built into my iPad, I’ll be able to say a lot to you overtime without having to type out every letter of every word, as I’ve been reduced to doing for the past three years. Gee, because of the nature of dictation, I really feel like I’m talking to you all right now, so I really look forward to talking with you all a lot more in the near future as your USPA president. As long as I can continue to leave the month-to-month, nitty-gritty, USPA labors to Scott Beutlich and others, I can limit myself to these sporadic episodes of talking to you.
