We’re lucky to have a team of world-class people with decades of experience in tracking, treating, and preventing infectious diseases, and they were following COVID-19 closely. The virus had begun to emerge in Africa, and based on the foundation’s early assessment and requests from African governments, we had made some grants to help keep it from spreading further and to help countries prepare in case it took oﬀ. Our thinking was: We hope this virus won’t go global, but we have to assume it will until we know otherwise. At that point, there was still reason to hope that the virus could be contained and wouldn’t become a pandemic.
