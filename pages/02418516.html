Where Does Diversity Come From? — From Unity [ 1 ] Human beings consist of body, soul, and spirit, and of three epochs of the cosmos: past, present, and future. [ 2 ] Our present cycle: the present; the past that preceded it; what is to come—the subsequent cycles: the future. [...] [ 3 ] “The past” as an expression of the Primordial Spirit. Imagine the Primordial Spirit as water; everything physical is the solidified state of water. “The present” is the soul; “the future” lies within us as a seed.
