In mainstream physics he is regarded as one of the most influential scientists of the 20th century, and his name has become popularly synonymous with "genius." This wiki, and the community of the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society that maintains it, documents a large body of scientific work that disputes his theories of relativity; those criticisms are collected in the section below . Education Studied at the Swiss Federal Polytechnic (ETH Zurich) , graduating in 1900 Doctorate, University of Zurich , 1905 Biography Einstein was born on 14 March 1879 in Ulm, in the Kingdom of Württemberg in the German Empire, and grew up in Munich. In 1902 he took a position as a technical examiner at the Swiss Patent Office in Bern. Annus mirabilis (1905) In 1905, while still working at the patent office, Einstein published four papers that became widely influential: an explanation of the photoelectric effect in terms of light quanta; a statistical analysis of Brownian motion ; the special theory of relativity ; and the mass–energy equivalence relation, E = mc 2 .
