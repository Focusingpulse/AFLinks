Overview N-machines, Free Energy and Lanczos Potential is a four-page paper by J. H. Caltenco, J. López-Bonilla and R. Peña-Rivero of the Escuela Superior de Ingeniería Mecánica y Eléctrica, Instituto Politécnico Nacional, Mexico City, dated 2009. (The wiki's infobox credits only the second author; the paper carries all three.) Its own abstract is a single sentence: "We propose that the Lanczos spintensor may give theoretical support to N-machines via the Tewari's space vortex theory." The paper has three parts of very unequal weight. An introduction on zero-point energy, drawing on the popular free-energy literature. Space is "not a vacuum" but "a plenum: an absolute fullness of energetic potentiality"; matter is a local asymmetry in the cancellation of that energy; and "the amount of potential energy contained within the fabric of space has been independently calculated by several physicists to be somewhere around of 10 115 gr/cm³". Tapping it "literally disallows scarcity from being considered to be real." A survey of N-machines , the homopolar generators of Bruce DePalma, Shiuji Inomata and Paramahamsa Tewari, described as producing "a huge amount of current flooding into the fly wheel" above a threshold rotation rate. On the strength of a private letter of May 2000 the authors report that Inomata's machines are "finished", and predict that "almost certainly it will be only a matter of time before we see Japanese free energy devices dominate the marketplace."
