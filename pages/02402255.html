MolPhot2026-main012 Two-photon excitation of photoluminescence of perovskite nanocrystals CsPbX₃ in a glass matrix Maria S. Kuznetsova1 , Matvey N. Bataev1 , Ivan V. Ignatiev1 , Elena V. Kolobkova2,3 1 St Petersburg University, St. Petersburg, Russia 2 National Research University ITMO 3 St. Petersburg State Technological Institute (Technical University) m.s.kuznetsova@spbu.ru In recent years, materials based on halide perovskites in the form of single crystals, thin films, and nanocrystals (NCs) have attracted significant interest due to their potential for photovoltaics, optoelectronics, and spintronics. Perovskite nanocrystals encapsulated in a fluorophosphate glass matrix exhibit optical properties close to those of their colloidal counterparts: intense photoluminescence (PL), high quantum yield, and tunable emission wavelength from the UV to near-infrared range by varying the composition and size of the NCs [1]. An additional advantage is their stability against environmental factors, high-power laser radiation, and elevated temperatures. The study of nonlinear optical properties [2] is important for the development of laser sources and nonlinear radiation converters. This work presents the results of a study of photoluminescence excitation spectra under one￾photon (1P-PLE) and two-photon (2P-PLE) excitation of CsPbX₃ NCs (X = (Br,Cl), Br, I). For CsPbBr₃ NCs, the two￾photon absorption coefficient measured by the Z-scan method at a photon energy of 1.55 eV was 0.63 cm/GW, which is several times higher than the value for colloidal analogs [2] (Fig. 1).
