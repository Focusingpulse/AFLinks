Overview On Reviving Tired Light is a six-page paper by Toivo Jaakkola of the University of Helsinki Observatory, published in Apeiron no. 6 (Winter 1990), pp. 5–6. It is a reply, not a proposal. Halton Arp had criticised tired-light mechanisms as an account of anomalous redshifts, and Jaakkola answers his objections point by point, following Arp's own numbering. The register is collegial throughout: "This is extremely welcome and represents the beginning of a true discussion among those who are not content with standard big bang cosmology… And who has greater right to initiate the discussion than Chip Arp, the pioneer of the field?" He is candid about the state of the field he is defending: "The unorthodox theories are legion: there are almost as many theories as there are unorthodox thinkers in this domain. This situation is not conducive to advances in our understanding of a difficult problem." His replies draw entirely on his own observational work, published through the 1970s in Nature , Monthly Notices , Astronomy and Astrophysics , Astronomische Nachrichten and Astrophysics and Space Science .
