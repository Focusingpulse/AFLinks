👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 2,423,135
A significant piece of folk poetry is to be presented. It tells the story of the young Olaf Åsteson, who lives on in the legends of the Norwegian people. A dream of this Olaf Åsteson is told in a truly folk-poetic form. A dream which the people had as a mental image—that it filled a long sleep of thirteen days and nights—those thirteen nights and days that lie between Christmas Eve and Epiphany, on January 6.
