In the paradox proposed at the Solvay Congress of 1927, he showed that Born's probabilistic interpretation of the quantum mechanical wave function was unacceptable, since in the case of the reduction process, it implied the possibility of instantaneous actions at a distance, in contrast with one of the two postulates of special relativity.
