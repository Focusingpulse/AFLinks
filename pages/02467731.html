The Dennis Wheatley Library of the Occult Volume 24 In ancient Egypt a Pharaoh who was also qualified, by extrasensory powers and special training, to be a priest was called a ‘Winged Pharaoh’. This fascinating novel is the story of a princess who becomes such a ruler. The Winged Pharaoh recreates with force and vigour the life of ancient Egypt. At the same time, it affirms the immortality of the human soul as Sekeeta, the princess, expands the power of her mind to travel in time and space, mastering the secrets of reincarnation to help herself rule wisely and with justice.
