Perhaps it will interest some readers if I preface my remarks by saying that I first heard of the pendule from George Bernard Shaw - of all people! One day when on a visit to us he facetiously announced he had just been staying with an old friend in Ireland, a once-poor widow who had become rich thru poultry breeding. Her good fortune, he said, had come about thru the use of a gold ring on a string, by which she "sexed" the eggs and meted out summary treatment to the males. And of course farmers have done sexing of eggs, calves, colts and other animals for centuries the world over. Human embryos can also be sexed, so pendologists assert, before any sex organs develop, thus giving advance information often of great importance to the parents. Authorities on this subject assert that pendology goes far back into history, and was used by the ancient Chinese and Egyptians, and probably by the Aztecs and Toltecs. Altho as a science, in the modern sense of the word, it is still inchoate, it is employed for important purposes and has achieved official recognition.
