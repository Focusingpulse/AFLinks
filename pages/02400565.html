The chain closes without new assumptions. Change is conserved: no transformation may erase the distinction it acts on (Law 3), because a free change would be privileged and “all change is equal.” The conserved quantity is exhibited concretely as the carry: the overflow a modular sum “discards” is booked exactly, and two inputs that collide on the value axis are separated on the carry axis. Determinism is then not a separate fact but Law 3 holding — a deterministic map is precisely one that never spends a distinction, only relocates it. Direction (the carry propagates only upward) and inertia (mass as accumulated ledger, so that mass×acceleration = cost acquires an address) are downstream readouts. Growth and hashing are shown to be one bijection read from opposite ends: a chosen target cannot be reached by pushing a seed forward (blind search), but is recovered exactly by clamping the target and pulling — and the coordinate one pulls is the carry.
