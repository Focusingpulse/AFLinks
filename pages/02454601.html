I'll bit. "Compressed air is cold",when you compress air into a tank you release heat which is disapated through the metal skin of the tank. So whenever one compresses air one has heat loss and it has to go somewhere, same goes for the metal lines. You are further compressing air running through a small line not to mention the friction and the metal lines become the heat sink. So compress air,heat loss, let compressed air expand and apsorb heat. Its the principal that refrigeration / heat exchangers is made of. Larry Jerry W.
