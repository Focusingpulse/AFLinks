Hypersphere World-Universe Model, Part 3: Angular Momentum Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Speaker Vladimir S Netchitailo Date 27 April 2024 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording Hypersphere World-Universe Model, Part 3: Angular Momentum was a live session presented by Vladimir S Netchitailo on 27 April 2024 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. We discuss Angular Momentum problem in Cosmology; introduce Dark Epoch (spanning from the Beginning of the World 14.22 Byr ago for 0.45 Byr) when only DM Macroobjects existed in the Medium of the World and Luminous Epoch (ever since for 13.77 Byr); propose Explosive Volcanic Rotational Fission Model that is responsible for transition from Dark Epoch to Luminous Epoch; discuss Formation of Macrostructures; provide experimental results of JWST and their explanation; discuss Galactic Rotation Curves by mass currents without DM halos; stress Decisive Role of Gravitational Parameter G in Cosmology. Summary The third of Vladimir S Netchitailo 's eight-part series, and the one he regards as the most important. Ian J Cowan hosted, and said afterwards that if only one part of the series could be kept, this would be it. The angular momentum problem Netchitailo's case is that conservation of angular momentum is the test every cosmology must pass and that none of them does. He quoted Mestel's 1965 review on star formation, in which the angular momentum of a protoplanetary disc is misappropriated relative to the models and stars are predicted to rotate far faster than they do; and Sky and Peebles in Los Alamos Science , who called angular momentum the cosmic pollutant — there seems to be too much of it in the universe to allow stars to form or matter to accrete, and it prevents the game of concentration and collapse from being played to the limit.
