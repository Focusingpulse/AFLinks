GC2026-STP071 Ion Beam Formation and Its Statistical Cooling via the Hall Electric Field in Collisionless Magnetic Reconnection Dahua Zhong1 , Andrey V. Divin1 , Igor P. Paramonik1 , Vladimir S. Semenov1 1 St Petersburg University st112995@student.spbu.ru During collisionless magnetic reconnection, decoupling of ion and electron motions establishes a multi-scale diffusion region. The ion diffusion region (IDR) embeds the smaller electron diffusion region (EDR), giving rise to the Hall current system and the associated Hall electric field [1-6]. This field efficiently accelerates ions near the diffusion region and separatrices, particularly in regimes with cold ions [7-8], significantly deforming their velocity distribution function (VDF). Using 2.5D full kinetic "Particle-in-Cell" simulations, we investigate the evolution of the ion VDF as it passes through the effective acceleration regions: the diffusion region and the separatrix. Upon rapid crossing of these regions, we observe that the accelerated particle distributions become narrower. This narrowing can be explained by a simple energy model involving the instantaneous addition of directed kinetic energy to each particle. Thus, when crossing the acceleration regions (the diffusion region and the separatrices), we observe statistical cooling of ions compared to the temperature in the inflow region. Using test particle numerical simulations, we reproduce the configuration of the electric and magnetic fields and the characteristic particle behavior when crossing the acceleration regions.
