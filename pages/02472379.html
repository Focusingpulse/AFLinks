This method consists in producing a very great difference of potential between a high point in the atmosphere, reached by a wire connected to a balloon, and a distant point on a balloon, which in turn is connected through a step-down transformer with the earth. A step-up transformer produces the high potential at the sending station, or at the first balloon, and the difference of potential thus created produces a current of conduction through the rarefied air to the second balloon, and thence through the step-down transformer to earth. Mr. Tesla relies upon the good conductivity of rarefied air to high electro-motive force. Some recent experiments I have made with high electro-motive force are interesting in regard to the suggestion of Mr. Tesla, and are in continuation of those I described in the Scientific American for January 15, 1898. At that time my apparatus was capable of producing 1,200,000 volts. It can now produce 3,000,000. Up to the point of 1,500,000 volts the length of the electrical discharge in air appears to be closely proportional to the electro-motive force.
