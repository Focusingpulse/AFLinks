Alexandra N. Correa Born 1952 (age 73–74) Residence Concord, ON, Canada Nationality Canadian Known for Co-inventor of the Pulsed Abnormal Glow Discharge (PAGD) energy-conversion patents; co-author of Experimental Aetherometry Scientific career Fields Aetherometry ; plasma electrodynamics; energy conversion; scientific publishing Alexandra N. Correa (born Toronto, 1952) is a Canadian researcher, publisher and inventor, and with her partner Paulo N. Correa the co-author of the Experimental Aetherometry series and co-inventor of the Pulsed Abnormal Glow Discharge (PAGD) energy-conversion system. She is Senior Partner of Akronos Publishing , the imprint through which the Correas' work appears, and a member of the International Society of the Friends of Aetherometry (ISFA). Trained in psychology and sociology rather than physics, she has been her partner's co-author, co-inventor and collaborator for some three decades. Education H.B.A. in psychology and sociology — York University, Toronto She is also an associate glassblower, a painter and a graphic artist — the first of these directly relevant to the work, since the PAGD reactors are cold-cathode vacuum tubes. Patents The co-inventorship is the most readily checkable part of her record, and it holds. She is named with Paulo Correa as joint inventor on an international family of energy-conversion patents: US 5,416,391 — "Electromechanical transduction of plasma pulses" (1995) US 5,449,989 — "Energy conversion system" (1995) US 5,502,354 — "Direct current energized pulse generator utilizing autogenous cyclical pulsed abnormal glow discharges" (1996) US 7,235,945 — "Energy conversion systems" (2007) GB 2,284,093 — "Pulse generator" (1996), and CA 2,147,153 — "Energy conversion system" (1997) The division of labour is visible in the record.
