Correa (born Toronto, 1952) is a Canadian researcher, publisher and inventor, and with her partner Paulo N. Correa the co-author of the Experimental Aetherometry series and co-inventor of the Pulsed Abnormal Glow Discharge (PAGD) energy-conversion system. She is Senior Partner of Akronos Publishing , the imprint through which the Correas' work appears, and a member of the International Society of the Friends of Aetherometry (ISFA).
