GTM2026-main023 Development of Game-Theoretic Methods for Solving Problems of Differentiating Types of Intelligent Systems Pavel V. Konyukhovskiy1 1 Herzen State Pedagogical University of Russia, Russia aura4004@gmail.com In this study, one of the possible methods for solving the problem of differentiation (separation) of types of intelligent systems is proposed. It is proposed to organize the separation procedure as a multi-stage dynamic game, the participants of which can be of two types: those with natural or artificial intelligence. At each stage, players simultaneously perform a control operation, the success (or failure) of which changes their utility (payoff function). For a specific version of the game procedure, the control operation may be predicting the value of a certain indicator or solving an optimization problem with stochastic parameters. Based on the results of each stage of the game, the correct answer and the next winner are detected. The players' goal setting system includes both maximizing current payouts and concealing their player type. Unlike NI players, AI players are assumed to know the correct answer to the control task. Their goal is to avoid giving "suspiciously accurate answers." In addition to submitting solutions to the control task, the following actions are available to players at each stage, starting from the second. (a) Send an "accusation" against another player, claiming they are an artificial intelligence (an AI player). If the suspicion is true, that player is eliminated from the game.
