According to hotel officials, he had been in failing health for two years. The world-famous inventor, who lived alone, was found dead in bed by a maid. She called a hotel physician. The hotel management started a search for friends or relatives. It was believed a nephew is living in the city but his whereabouts are not known. Nikola Tesla was born in Smiljan, Yugoslavia, in July, 1856. His father, a Greek Orthodox Church minister, was a noted writer, orator, linguist and mathematician. His mother, Georgina Mandic, was an inventor. Came Here in 1884. Tesla studied at the Graz Polytechnic Institute and the University of Prague after preparatory work at the Realschules of Lika and Carlstadt. He came to the United States in 1884, and became a naturalized citizen. In 1888 he designed the alternating system. Two years later he invented the Tesla motor and designed a plan for the transmission of alternating current.
