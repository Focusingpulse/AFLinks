Lobaczewski is a 1989 book by Jorge Resines , published by Borderland Sciences . Overview The book presents the author's independent contribution to the foundations of physics, developed outside the scientific mainstream. About the author Jorge Resines was an independent researcher who wrote technical compilations on unconventional 'free energy' and propulsion devices for Borderland Sciences.
