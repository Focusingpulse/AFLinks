University of Technology, Gorakhpur-273010 (UP) India) Mathematical derivations of inscribed & circumscribed radii for three externally tangent circles Harish Chandra Rajpoot 21 Feb, 2015 M.M.M. University of Technology, Gorakhpur-273010 (UP), India 1. Introduction: In this paper, the analytic formulas for the radii of the inscribed and circumscribed circles by three externally touching circles have been derived using geometry and trigonometry. Further analysis has been done for three external touching circles with a common tangent and enclosed in a smallest rectangle. The analytic formulas for the area of intersection and length of common chord for two intersecting circles have also been derived.
