Einstein's Denial of the Ether in 1905 and its Reintroduction in 1916 Author(s) Ludwig Kostro Keywords Einstein , ether , general relativity , physical space and time, physical and philosophical reasons Published 1997 Pages 131-139 Abstract In the first part of this paper the author presents physical and philosophical reasons which, in 1905, finally impelled A. Einstein to deny the existence of the ether of 19th century physics, especially H. A. Lorentz's ether. Since Einstein, under the influence of P. Drude, identified Lorentz's ether with absolute space, the denial of Lorentz's ether meant, for Einstein, the denial of the existence of absolute space.
