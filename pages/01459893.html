These areas are the holes which center the rings of electric potential which continually multiply compression , or divide it by expanding, in accord with the desires of Nature , or man, to manifest that all-power which is within, and omnipresent in all things." It emerges from that point millions of times, and returns to it millions of times complexed by union with other Identities , which become a part of its purpose ."
