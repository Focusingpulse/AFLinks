Overview Introduction to the FOUR QUADRANT THEORY of Alternating Current which allows engineering of Tesla's inventions. Provides a more complete understanding of the use of versor operators (degrees of rotation), necessary to the understanding of the rotating magnetic field. The process of the production of electrical energy using the neglected QUADRANTS OF GROWTH is brought about via the use of these operators.
