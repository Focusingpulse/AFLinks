It is the sequel to The Twichell Manuscript - Volume I: Aether Gravity and, at 457 pages, is substantially the longer of the two. Where the first volume proposed a mechanism for gravity, this one extends the same full-space model a universe filled with a material aether rather than empty space to magnetism, force and inertia . Its organising device is a "Proton Magnet" model , which Twichell presents as simplifying chemistry and as accounting for quantum mechanics in ordinary language rather than in the formalism he regards as needlessly obscure.
