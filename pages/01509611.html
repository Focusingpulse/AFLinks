Energy is therefore realized by acceleration or deceleration from one frame of reference into another frame of reference. During a change all matter will experience a g force, whether that quantity of matter is a large clump or the smallest possible amount. In a star all of the atoms are supported by each other around a central point, yet the massive quantity of atoms creates a situation were each individual atom experiences a g force due to gravity.
