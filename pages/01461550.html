Radiance

· archived # 1,461,550
In radiometry , radiance is the radiant flux emitted, reflected, transmitted or received by a surface, per unit solid angle per unit projected area, and spectral radiance is the radiance of a surface per unit frequency or wavelength , depending on whether the spectrum is taken as a function of frequency or of wavelength. These are directional ( vector ) quantities.
