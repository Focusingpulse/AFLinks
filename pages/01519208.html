Time retardation is introduced by converting Poisson's equation to a wave equation with a time-dependent source. Neglecting time retardation, about 40 percent of the unaccounted portion of the precession of the perihelion of Mercury is predicted. The gravitational red shift, the slowing of the speed of light, and the bending of a light ray in a gravitational field follow from Newtonian gravitation and the behavior of photons.
