Enhancing Wellness: Reflections of Pete Radatti’s June 2024 Radionics Class in Peetz, Colorado By Vernon Nicolas In April this year, I lost feeling in most of my right arm and my dominant hand. A dull, constant pain ran from my neck and back through my right arm and fingers, causing many sleepless nights and loss of grip strength. Physician prescribed painkillers did nothing to eliminate the discomfort. Although chiropractic adjustments resulted in flexibility improvements, the pain I felt only decreased slightly. I was fortunate that Pete Radatti was scheduled to teach his one and only in-person workshop this year in early June. I was hoping Pete’s class could help me find answers I couldn’t find elsewhere to eliminate this pain. As a Radionics practitioner, I’ve personally experienced incredible outcomes applying Radionics for others. However, I was not having success using Radionics to restore the feeling in my own hand. I travelled to Peetz, Colorado with an open-mind and the hope I’d leave pain-free with newfound wisdom. Reflecting on Pete’s workshop, one of the most powerful practices I learned was the programming of Radionic rates, reagents, intentions, and operating instructions into simple plastic cards. While these cards innocuously look like credit cards or security badges, these cards allow any Radionic operator to achieve results like a master practitioner. The cards themselves are inexpensive, easy to store and organize, and portable. Because carbonbased materials can hold information, according to Pete, these cards can become very powerful when potentized via a Radionics instrument. The workshop gave me the confidence and inspiration to create new programs of my own, which I have been using to help others achieve meaningful shifts.
