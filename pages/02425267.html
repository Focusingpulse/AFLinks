Chemical and physical properties of the pure substance Description: Glyphosate acid is a colourless, odourless, crystalline solid. It is formulated as a salt consisting of the deprotonated acid of glyphosate and a cation (isopropylamine, ammonium, or sodium), with more than one salt in some formulations. Solubility: The acid is of medium solubility at 11.6 g/L in water (at 25 °C) and insoluble in common organic solvents such as acetone, ethanol, and xylene; the alkali-metal and 1 IARC MONOGRAPHS --- 112 amine salts are readily soluble in water (Tomlin, 2000). Volatility: Vapour pressure, 1.31 × 10−2 mPa at 25 °C (negligible) (Tomlin, 2000).
