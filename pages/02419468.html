Journal of Borderland Research >> Vol. 30 >> No. 01 [1] THE PLAYTHINGS OF DIONYSIUS Clips, Quotes & Comments by The Editor "A quote from an old Journal to help ask a question: 'Pythagoras advised the study of higher mathematics for those who were mentally or physically unfit, because contemplation of thee orderly sequences of numbers would subdue the intemperate impulses of the soul and body. It may be interesting to note that Emanuel Swedenborg is said to have become clairvoyant as the result of his addiction to arithmetic problems.' I am extremely interested in mathematics and geometry and have been searching borderland studies in this area. I am aware of only one study, Frank S. Lane's work and his discovery of the Cosmic Constant. Do you know of any other studies which have been or are being made?"
