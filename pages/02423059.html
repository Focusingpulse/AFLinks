Lecture One — GA 153. The Inner Nature of Man and Life Between Death and a New Rebirth (1928)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 9 April 1914 · archived # 2,423,059
, Vienna Translator Unknown Lecture One [ 1 ] Our aim in this course of lectures will be to describe the inner life of man in relation to the life between death and rebirth, in order to show how intimately these two realms of existence are connected. A secondary aim will be to point out, as the result of this knowledge, certain guiding lines which will enable human beings to take the right course in many difficult situations in life, and which are fitted in many respects to give a sure support to the life of the soul, affording as they do, a thorough understanding of this soul-life.
