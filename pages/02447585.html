Decker: "Re: Earth Resonance" Next message: Bob Paddock: "Re: Translator" I'm sure you'll get lots of answers but here's one that should give real good/cheep characteristics. Radio Shack IRFZ34 (ask them about a similar type) - it is a MOSFET with 26 amp capability, the IRLZ34 is the same but designed for 5 volt gate tuen on levels. The Drain to Source breakdown voltage is 55Vdc. The turn on ohms is .04ohms. All you have to do is connect the gate to the 555 output (with a resistor to V+ supply as required by the 555) and the Source to Ground. The Drain goes to your load and the V+ goes to the other side of the load.
