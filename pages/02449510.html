Alien and Artificial Intelligences By: Clark M. Thomas © October 2, 2026 Abstract Questions swirling around human artificial intelligence, and visiting space-alien intelligence, are featured all over the media. Speculation clutters major idea gaps where scientific evidence, or at least high quality logic, should be. Ideas range from optimistic curiosity, to existential dread. Scientists can fairly well document our human past. Can we wisely envision and prepare for hybrid futures? It is the thesis of this essay that space-alien intelligence, and human-created AI superhuman powers both exist. Soon they will interact in ways outside our quaint post-Stone-Age societies. This synergistic thesis is easier to support than disprove. In all varieties of emerging interaction there will be challenges to human hegemony. Arrogant humans have insufficient respect for the rapidly emerging existential dangers of self-programming software within data farms that we can neither understand nor control. We also have almost no knowledge about what is “out here” in proximal space, and may already be here with us in surprising ways. I will first discuss what we might learn about alien consciousness as it may synergistically interact with our weak control structures acting for greed more than social need. It is concerning that unforeseen bot behaviors may involve little if any elements of human wisdom, and ignore programmer controls in critical areas such as huge power grids. Is a future kill switch even possible within such a hyper-hybrid web? 1 of 3 Intelligence per se is not dependent on one brain, nor even on one planet. It likely is a primary negentropic feature of life itself, all over the multiverse within local biomes. Our beginner human science can, for example, already measure cooperating social behavior among apparently “primitive” forest floor fungi.
