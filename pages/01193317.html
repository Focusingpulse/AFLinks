(Dated: February 5, 2025) Abstract We study the Norwegian language head entries of two Norwegian Dictionaries composed by H. Scavenius. We draw the natural logarithm of the number of the Norwegian language head en￾tries, normalised, starting with a letter vs the natural logarithm of the rank of the letter, nor￾malised/unnormalised.
