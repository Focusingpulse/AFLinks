Talbott (born October 28, 1942) is an American self-taught comparative mythologist whose work offers a radical new vantage point on the origin of ancient cultural themes, myths and symbols. Working in the tradition of Immanuel Velikovsky , his research has been the primary catalyst behind the "Saturn Model" of recent planetary history, and is the subject of the feature documentary Remembering the End of the World . He is the author of The Saturn Myth and co-author (with Wallace Thornhill ) of Thund
