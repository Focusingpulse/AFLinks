David Wilcock Known for Aether/"Source Field" cosmology and consciousness writings Scientific career Fields Consciousness studies, "aether" cosmology David Wilcock (born 1973) is an American author, lecturer and media personality known for his writings on consciousness, ancient civilizations and an "aether"-based view of physics and cosmology. He is listed in The Worldwide List of Dissident Scientists . Ideas Wilcock proposes that physical matter is formed by what he calls an "aether" or "Source Field" — an invisible, conscious, fluid-like source of universal energy. Drawing on the work of the Soviet astrophysicist Nikolai Kozyrev, he argues that rotation and its direction can produce anti-gravity and related effects, and that mainstream science has overlooked evidence for a conscious, energetic substrate underlying matter. These claims lie outside the accepted framework of modern physics and are not supported by the mainstream scientific community. Wilcock develops these themes in books including The Source Field Investigations (2011) and The Synchronicity Key (2013), and through his website Divine Cosmos, where he publishes essays and lectures on "soul growth, ascension, and the evolution of consciousness." He has also appeared as a commentator in popular paranormal and "ancient aliens" media.
