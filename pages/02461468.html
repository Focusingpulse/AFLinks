This is the final report of the research performed at the Naval Air Warfare Center Weapons Division (NAWCWPNS) on anomalous effects involving deuterated palladium and palladium alloys. This report examines the possible production of excess power, helium-4, tritium, and radiation during the electrolysis of heavy water (D2O) using palladium and palladium alloys as cathodes.
