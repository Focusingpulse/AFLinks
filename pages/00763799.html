Introduction We study the ratios of exponential functions as a model of dependence of proportions on some other independent variables. The origin of such propor￾tions may range from categorical data as relative frequencies when absolute frequencies are known on the one hand to chemical composition on the other hand. Other examples on compositional data may be had in Aitchison (1986) or Pawlowsky-Glahn (2015). Ratios of exponential functions are also known as logistic functions, Agresti (1990).
