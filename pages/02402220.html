SPAF-2026-main014 CDU: 550.388 Study of dynamic processes in the high-latitude ionosphere: modern tools and future development Ilya Y. Dyukov1 , Oinats V. Alexey1 1 Institute of Solar-Terrestrial Physics, Siberian Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences, Irkutsk, Russia dyukov.ilya.yu@mail.ru Abstract The study of dynamic processes in the high-latitudinal ionosphere is important for creating a physics model of these processes. These studies are made possible by ionosondes, GNSS, SuperDARN HF radars, and cosmic ray monitors data. The data cover extensive areas of research such as ionospheric convection, auroral field-aligned currents, ionospheric irregularities, magnetohydrodynamic waves, geomagnetic disturbances. Geomagnetic disturbances rank among the most significant factors in ionospheric irregularities dynamics. In the process of a geomagnetic disturbances, various effects occur on the ionosphere: the auroral oval expands to the equator, electron concentration change [1,2], and irregularities of different scales occurs [3,4]. These effects affect the accuracy of radio equipment [5, 6], global navigation satellite systems [7,8], etc [9,10]. For example, in November 10 - 13, 2025, geomagnetic disturbances of class G4 (Kp = 8 – 8.667, Dst < −200 nT) and G5 (Kp = 9, Dst < −350 nT) were observed.
