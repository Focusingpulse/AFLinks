A cement and concrete industry publication National Structural Concrete Speciﬁcation for Building Construction Fourth edition complying with BS EN 13670: 2009 NSCS Standard Specification A technical committee of CONSTRUCT, the Concrete Structures Group, prepared this speciﬁcation as the 4th edition of National Structural Concrete Speciﬁcation for Building Construction, originally published in February 1998.
