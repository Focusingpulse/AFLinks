This thread is for those would like to explore how you can easily make alcohol and other bio-fuels to run internal combustion motors, cook with and heating systems etc, the possibilities are endless. Also the use of waste oils for the same purpose. I have been a bio-diesel producer and user for over 6 years and have made all my own processor and bio diesel making equipment from scrap junk, a few pipe fittings and hardware parts were bought new. But the over all costs were low being I have a well equipped shop with much welding equipment and tools. The techniques for making bio-diesel I use are not as of yet been revealed on the internet to my knowledge. The technique is much easier I believer as it eliminates the need for pretesting the oil for proper oil to methanol/hydroxy ratios. I have made lierally thousands of gallons to date and have put on over 100,000 additional miles on a 96 Dodge 1 ton duely with the Cummins turbo diesel engine and running 100% biodiesel. In the quest to make bio-diesel cheaper by recovering some of the alcohol from the glycerin biproducts I came up with a novel Idea for a simple alcohol still made from steel drums. These stills could also make ethanol or ethyl alcohol from funky wine you can brew from all sorts of stuff including some garbage. My newest still is currently in the process of being completed and is made from a used 300 gallon diesel fuel tank. I am at the final stages of the build making the babington style burner to fire it with waste oil or glycerin, glycerin being the more difficult to burn. May have to use a combo burn head with waste oil and glycerin both being used on separate babington burner heads side by side. My drum stills use propane fired burners used for cooking deep fried turkeys. These were purchased on sale for 20 bucks each new.
