Energy in the form of electromagnetic waves, or electrostatic charges in the ether, is absorbed by or radiated from a network of metallic conductors utilized in combination with an impinging stream of Roentgen rays. The aerial network consists of a rhomboid of fine wires a supported by a central conductor b, which is earthed at m. A motor or other receiving apparatus is connected between the points d, e. Roentgen rays from a discharge tube c are stated to form an ionized beam of air which increases the absorption of energy by the network a. A magnet m deflects any electron emission away from the path of the rays.
