Eulogy at the Cremation of Sophie Stinde — GA 261. Our Dead

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 22 November 1915 · archived # 2,437,486
, Ulm Translated by Steiner Online Library Eulogy at the Cremation of Sophie Stinde We look up to the spirit that has been snatched from our earthly existence. We look up out of the pain that has to arise in these days from the roots of that deep joy with which we worked for so long at our dear friend's side for what is dear and valuable to us in the world. From the beautiful soul traits of our dear friend and from what the affection of my soul for her has poured into my own soul in the last hours, I will try to send our feelings up to her. Guide you from earthly existence To the spiritual sun
