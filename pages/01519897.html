Overview The book is an early statement of Znidarsic's Quantum Transition Theory (later called "Z-Theory"), which uses a classical approach to address quantum puzzles (Planck's constant, quantum jumps, photon energies). Central to it is a characteristic velocity — about 1.09 million metres per second, which Znidarsic identifies with the "speed of sound" within the nucleus — that governs the transition of quantum states. He argues that this framework accounts for cold fusion / LENR and for antigravity as effects of an amplified magnetic component of the strong and gravitational force
