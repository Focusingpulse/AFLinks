Elementary Anti-Gravity Book Author Frank J Znidarsic Language English Subject Gravity , antigravity, cold fusion Published 1989 Publisher Vantage Pages 53 ISBN 0533083346 Elementary Antigravity is a 1989 book by the engineer Frank J Znidarsic , published by Vantage Press. Overview The book is an early statement of Znidarsic's Quantum Transition Theory (later called "Z-Theory"), which uses a classical approach to address quantum puzzles (Planck's constant, quantum jumps, photon energies). Central to it is a characteristic velocity — about 1.09 million metres per second, which Znidarsic identifies with the "speed of sound" within the nucleus — that governs the transition of quantum states. He argues that this framework accounts for cold fusion / LENR and for antigravity as effects of an amplified magnetic component of the strong and gravitational forces. A follow-up, Elementary Antigravity II , continues the theme. About the author Frank J. Znidarsic is a registered professional electrical engineer in Pennsylvania who has developed the "Z-Theory" of the quantum transition and written on energy, cold fusion, and antigravity.
