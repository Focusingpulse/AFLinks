The Gravitational Constant is defined. The reason why objects accelerate to Earth in accordance with a square law scale is explained. Gravity radiation then causes the nucleus of the neighboring atom to spin axially (the 'motor effect') and, at the same time, synchronizes the spin of the electrons in both atoms. The neighboring atom, in turn, generates helically polarized electromagnetic energy (the 'generator effect'). Both atoms spin axially in the same direction. Hence, the gravitational forces from both atoms pull in the same direction and the forces are additive.The gravitational Constant G is shown to be the instantaneous alternating magnetic force between any two electron-magnets in neighboring atoms and, hence, the gravitational force F is proportional to Gm 1 M 2 (where m 1 and M 2 represent the electron count of neighboring atoms).
