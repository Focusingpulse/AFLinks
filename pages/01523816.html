Russ Gries Known for Open-source experimental replication of new-energy, plasma and LENR devices Scientific career Fields Physics , experimental replication, alternative energy research Institutions RWGresearch Russ Gries is an independent experimental researcher who runs RWGresearch , an open-source "garage lab" project that attempts to build and test claimed new-energy, plasma, cold fusion/LENR and Tesla -related devices, and publishes the attempts as they happen through his YouTube channel and the open-source-energy.org forum. He is best known within the independent-inventor community not for a theory of his own but for a working method: rather than asserting that a claimed effect is real, he builds the device, instruments it, and shows the result — whatever the result is — to an audience that is invited to criticise it. Biography Published sources say little about Gries's personal background, and this article does not speculate about it. What is documented is his research practice. RWGresearch was created, in his own description, "to help Russ promote his work, share his results and work as a team with his subscribers", and it operates as a small home laboratory funded by viewer donations and built largely from salvaged parts. He has emphasised that serious experimental work does not require an institutional budget, noting for example that "old CRT TVs and computer monitors people are throwing out every day have a lot of good magnet wire in them", and that most of his early coils were wound from recycled material at essentially no cost. He maintains the RWGresearch website and forum presence, broadcasts live experimental sessions, and for several years ran an annual community "Pulse Motor Build-Off" (documented from 2012 to 2020) in which participants built and compared competing motor designs.
