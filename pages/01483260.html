Christ Returns - Speaks His Truth "I asked the Creator - ' Universal Consciousness ' - why mankind endured so much suffering and evil . It also manifested itself in the ' personality ' as a DRIVING NEED to take all that was best for the self , despite the opposition of others and a similar DRIVING NEED to hold on to personal possessions, be they relatives, friends, material goods or achievements, despite any opposition . I was also made to understand that without these TWO fundamental, eternal, undeviating ' impulses of creative being ' there would be no creation . This is the secret of creation - and the secret of existence and of ' individual being '."
