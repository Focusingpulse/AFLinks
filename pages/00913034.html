An ADMM Algorithm for a Generic ℓ0 Sparse Overlapping Group Lasso Problem Youming Zhao Email: youming0.zhao@gmail.com First draft: March 31, 2022 Last update: November 12, 2023 Abstract We present an alternating direction method of multipliers (ADMM) for a generic overlapping group lasso problem, where the groups can be overlapping in an arbitrary way. Meanwhile, we prove the lower bounds and upper bounds for both the ℓ1 sparse group lasso problem and the ℓ0 sparse group lasso problem.
