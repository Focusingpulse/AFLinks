Michael Twichell Born Michael Holdt Twichell Nationality American Known for "Aether Gravity", a theory deriving gravity from the absorption of the aether by matter Scientific career Fields Electrical and software engineering; aether theory; gravitation Michael Holdt Twichell is an American electrical and software engineer and a self-published theorist, known in the New Energy and Tesla-research community for Aether Gravity — a proposal that gravity is not a property of space curvature but the observable consequence of matter absorbing the aether around it . He has set the theory out in two self-published volumes, The Twichell Manuscript — Volume I: Aether Gravity (2011) and Physics of the Dark Matter: The Proton Magnet (2017), and has presented it four times at the TeslaTech ExtraOrdinary Technology Conference. His position is a full-space one: he holds that the replacement of the nineteenth-century aether by the empty-space model of relativity was a wrong turn, and that restoring a material medium removes the need for much of what he regards as the over-complication of modern physics. Aether Gravity The central claim of the first volume is a mechanism rather than a field equation. Matter, on Twichell's account, continuously absorbs the aether in which it sits; the resulting inflow towards a mass is what is observed as gravitational attraction. Weight is therefore a consequence of a flow, not of a geometric property of spacetime. From that single mechanism he argues for explanations of a range of otherwise unconnected phenomena, among them plate tectonics and the inner–outer core structure of the Earth, supernovae and black holes, the Pioneer anomaly , the Allais effect and the Coriolis effect . He also treats the theory as the route to gravity control, and has argued that the same principle was known to ancient civilizations — a theme he developed in a 2015 interview with Open Minds TV and in his later conference talks.
