Title Straight Line Motion Read in full Link to paper Author(s) David Tombe Published 2018 Journal General Science Journal No. of pages 4 Abstract The straight line inertial path will be examined from the perspective that it is caused by pressure equilibrium in a sea of tiny aethereal whirlpools that are pressing against each other with centrifugal force while striving to dilate. This is opposite to the traditional perspective whereby centrifugal force is considered to be a consequence of the tendency of a body to move in uniform straight line motion in the absence of any Newtonian forces. Overview Straight Line Motion is a four-page paper by David Tombe , dated 31 May 2018 and posted to the General Science Journal . It inverts the usual order of explanation: rather than treating centrifugal force as a consequence of the tendency to move in a straight line, it treats the straight line as a consequence of centrifugal pressure in a medium. The medium is Maxwell's, by way of Bernoulli, Tesla and Oliver Lodge, all quoted at length: "space is rendered into tiny whirlpools that are pressing against each other with centrifugal force while striving to dilate." Tombe proposes that in the absence of a radial field "the individual rotation axes of these tiny whirlpools form concentric rings around a moving object", so that the object's line of motion is at right angles to the pressure exerted on it. "When this centrifugal pressure field (inertial field) is symmetrical, the motion will constitute the straight line inertial path." A key assertion follows: "It's a geometrical fact that a moving object has a centrifugal acceleration to every point in space that does not lie along its path of motion. This fact largely goes unnoticed, disguised in the straight line inertial path, but it manifests itself as a physical reality when the moving object is opposed by a centripetal force."
