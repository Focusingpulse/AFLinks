Jim Day sent in this totally cool website on a collection of attempts through the ages to make a perpetual motion machine, along with this email; From: ad368@lafn.org (Jim Day) To: jdecker@keelynet.com Hello Jerry, If you haven't seen Randall Woods' website already, I think you might find it of interest. Dr. Woods is politely skeptical of any kind of free energy device but his website has a remarkable collection of classic perpetual motion machines, many of them illustrated by animated graphics. Each machine is carefully analyzed and the fatal flaw in each is fully explained. Anyone thinking of experimenting with perpetuum mobiles could avoid reinventing the wheel by studying Woods' examples of what has already been tried and doesn't work. Unfortunately, Woods dismisses zero-point energy devices by saying that no such device has ever been known to work, but I don't recall seeing any proof of that on his website. I found it fascinating to watch the self-moving wheels going round and round just as their inventors intended. The URL for Woods' home page is; http://prisoner.soe.bcit.bc.ca/rjw/default.htm That website is full of interesting nooks and crannies, and it took me an hour or so to see everything.
