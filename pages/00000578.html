Foundations of Physics, Vol. 12, No. 5, 1982 Are Phonons Particles? Stefan Marinov I, 2 Received February 27, 1981 If phonons are particles, they must be attached to absolute space through their proper masses, which depend on the absolute velocities. In such a case one should be able to register with phonons the Sagnac and Marinov effects which have been observed with massless and massive particles (photons and neutrons).
