Hexslinger wrote : > The cheezy idea of using 1 megaton nuclear explosive is not only > dangerously stupid - but relatively ineffective I recall an essay about that in a Freeman Dyson book (BTW 1 - Dyson, although a mainstream physicist, is IMHO one of the most intelligent man alive !) (BTW 2 - sorry I don't have right now the exact reference, all my books, since I moved premises, are still in carboard cases...) Anyway, Dyson told the story of a very serious research, in the 60's, about propulsion of a spacecraft by a series of nuclear explosions in space. According to him, it would have been pretty effective. But the project folded, due to general mis-apprehensions about nuclear explosions, whever they might be. Normally, in space, they wouldn't do much harm, but who knows ? So nobody's sad about the demise of that project, and neither am I...
