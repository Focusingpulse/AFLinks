GTM2026-main033 A search game with two intervals Lutsenko M. Mikhail1,2 1 St. Petersburg State University of Economics 2 St Petersburg University ml4116@mail.ru The paper considers a search game in which player 2 hides point y on the unit segment. Player 1 sets several intervals of known lengths a1 , a 2 ,..., am on the same segment. Player 1's gain is equal to the number of intervals into which the hidden object falls. The game under consideration is a partial sum of games on the unit square (Lutsenko M.M.) Using the method of laying and covering (Vorobiev N.N.), the solution of the game is reduced to solving a linear programming problem. The task is related to both statistical parameter estimation games and non-cooperative games of many individuals. The solutions of the examples for m =2, as well as for some other cases, are given in analytical form.
