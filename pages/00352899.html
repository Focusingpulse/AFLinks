Tribute to The Memory of My Friend and Colleague Abdelkader Sellal, Engineer Geodesist (1946-2017) - V2- August 2022- Abdelmajid Ben Hadj Salem Abstract : This document presents a tribute to the memory of my colleague and friend Abdelkader Sellal, a retired geodesist engineer from the Algerian Institute of Cartography and Remote Sensing. I published a report he had written in 2004 where he had proposed projects concerning the modernization of Algerian Geo￾desy in particular : - Establishment of a Network of Permanent GPS Stations and a Basic Geodetic Network. - GPS calculations. Re-Adjustment and redefinition of the Algerian national geodetic network using adjustment software. - Definition of a System and a Reference of Altitudes in Algeria. - The Geoid : Studies on the determination of a gravimetric geoid in Algeria.
