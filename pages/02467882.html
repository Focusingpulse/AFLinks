when prophecy fails Leon Festinger was a hugely inspirational social psychologist, and is best known for his theory of cognitive dissonance. He taught at numerous US university including Stanford University. He died in 1989. Henry W. Riecken was a professor of social psychology at the University of Minnesota when he coauthored When Prophecy Fails. He is the author of Social Experimentation and articles on the general topic of evaluating the effectiveness of educational and social programs. Stanley Schachter’s best know work was on emotions. A student of Yale and the University of Michigan in 1949, he taught at Columbia as professor of psychology. He died in 1997. Elliot Aronson (foreword) is one of the world’s most distinguished social psychologists and has received many awards for scientific research, teaching, and writing. His books include The Social Animal and Mistakes were made (but not by me). He lives in Santa Cruz, California. when prophecy fails Leon Festinger Henry W.
