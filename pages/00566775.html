We consider a problem of splitting of the four- component tuples of integer numbers into two two-comp- onent tuples, providing a minimum of the difference of su- ms of the corresponding their components. Let us find an algorithm that allows for any fixed vector: V0 = (i0, j0, m0, n0), to split it on two Two-component tuples: E1 0 = (k0, l0), E2 0 = (s0, t0), wherein: k0, l0, s0, t0 ⊂ V0 , so that it provides a minimum of the difference of the corresponding sums of the E1 0 and E2 0 components: | (k0 + l0) - (s0 + t0) |.
