He is recorded among the dissident scientists catalogued in Jean de Climont's Worldwide List of Dissident Scientists ; beyond that listing and his association with the Electric Universe, little biographical detail about him is publicly documented. Work Wishman is associated with the Electric Universe framework, an alternative cosmology which holds that electric currents and plasma rather than gravity alone are the dominant organizing force in the universe.
