HOPE OF ELECTRICIANS The Bell Telephone People Spending Vast Sums Yearly In Experiments — Tesla's Wonderful Work and Inventions. Philadelphia Times "The starting of the great electrical plant at Niagara may be said to open a new epoch in human progress," said Emile Berilner, the famous electrician, of Washington. "Power is to be furnished from the falls eventually at any desired point within a radius of 100 miles. It will light the city of Buffalo, and I should not be at all surprised if at a future day New York City were illuminated by its means. Water power will certainly be employed before long on an immense scale for the production of electricity. Take Switzerland, for example, which has unlimited water power. Twenty years hence all of the electricity utilized in that country will be obtained without burning any fuel. The greatest problem before electricians to-day is 'the transformation of heat Into electricity direct. As things are now, fuel must be burned to make steam, the steam works an engine, the engine operates a dynamo and the dynamo generates the current. This elaborate process involves an enormous loss of energy. When the problem I speak of has been solved life will be easier.
