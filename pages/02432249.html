Modules Listed at Checkout

· archived # 2,432,249
Here is a question from a customer: Q: When I proceed to checkout after selecting my Modules, it only says "Sleep + 3". What does this mean? A: "Sleep + 3" means that you have selected 3 Modules for purchase. If it says "Sleep + 1", this means you have selected 1 Module, "Sleep + 2" means 2 Modules, and "Sleep + 4" means 4 Modules. Sleep is included with all orders. Before you get to the checkout where it says Sleep + the number of Modules you have selected, your Modules will be listed by name, along with the total purchase price, and you will need to check the terms and conditions box and click on "Buy Now". This is where you should verify that you have selected the correct Modules.
