Title On a Possible Parity Non-Conservation in Gravitational Interactions Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Vyacheslav N Streltsov Keywords antigravity, antiparticles. Published 2004 Journal Journal of Theoretics Volume 6 Number 2 No. of pages 3 Abstract It is emphasized that antigravity (an effective repulsion of antiparticles) is a consequence of gravity invariance relative to the time reversal T. This allows one to distinguish true elementary particles from antiparticles among mesons. Attention is given to a possible violation of T-symmetry and parity non-conservation in gravitational interactions. This makes a search for antigravity a paramount task. Overview This is a short theoretical note — three pages — from a physicist at the Laboratory of High Energies at the Joint Institute for Nuclear Research in Dubna. It argues that antigravity , understood as an effective gravitational repulsion of antiparticles , is not an exotic add-on but a straightforward consequence of gravity being invariant under time reversal T . Three conclusions are drawn from that premise. Antiparticles should be repelled by a gravitational field without any need to assign them a negative mass; gravitational behaviour therefore offers an objective criterion for telling true particles from antiparticles, which the paper claims would revise the conventional assignments among the mesons; and since there is no experimental evidence that gravity respects either T or spatial reflection P , gravity may turn out to violate parity in the way the weak interaction does. The closing recommendation is that measuring how antimatter behaves in a gravitational field is "an actual task of our time". The paper is a continuation of the author's earlier work, and leans throughout on his own framework developed in Physics Essays , Apeiron and Galilean Electrodynamics , in which gravity is described by a vector force law modelled on the Lorentz force.
