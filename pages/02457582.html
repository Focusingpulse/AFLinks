In a free-market economy, the imposition of each type of tax will differently influence the choices of individuals with regard to income (i.e. work vs. leisure), savings/investment, and consumption. In the absence of taxes, people will presumably work, save and consume (spend) at levels commeasurate with their own ability and desires. This would theoretically produce an economically optimized society. However, given the necessity for taxes to support services that government can best provide, it seems desirable that taxes should be structured so as to at least not cause economic sub-optimizaton.
