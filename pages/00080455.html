INTRODUCTION In modern physics, a number of unsolved problems have accumulated related to the structure of matter and models of fundamental interactions: - mutual transformation of quarks and leptons in β-decay reactions; - formation of charge of elementary particles; - Dark matter; - the nature of Dark Energy; - experimental detection of gravitons; - the scheme of exchange interaction, through the “exchange” of different bosons between particles, raises many questions. Physicists have already developed a need and understanding of the necessity of forming a THEORY OF EVERYTHING that would unite all four types of fundamental interactions and explain the mechanism of formation of all fundamental particles. A certain consensus has already emerged among a large number of physicists that in order to construct such a theory, it is necessary to return to the concept of the physical environment (ether), from which all matter was formed, and through which all types of fundamental interactions are carried out. TWO MODELS OF THE PHYSICAL ENVIRONMENT In modern physics, the model (theory) of the physical vacuum (PVT) [2] is proposed as a model of the physical environment. The fundamental quantum numbers of this vacuum—momentum, angular momentum, electric charge, etc.—are equal to zero.
