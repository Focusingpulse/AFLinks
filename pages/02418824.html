10. World Justice, a Wisdom-Filled Plan for the World, the Symbol of the Whitsun — GA 90c. Theosophy and Occultism

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 16 October 1903 · archived # 2,418,824
, Berlin Translated by Steiner Online Library 10. Global Justice, A Wise Plan for the World, the Pringst Festival Symbol [ 1 ] The questions are as follows: — Can a systematic connection be discerned in the phenomena of the external world? — Can one assume that there is a systematic connection? — Are the events and phenomena part of a systematic connection? — Does wisdom or chance prevail in the world? [ 2 ] (These questions bring to mind war, earthquakes, a brick falling on a passerby’s head, and so on.) [ 3 ] In some respects, things in the world seem to be arranged quite wisely. Human being
