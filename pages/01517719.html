ENERGY SCIENCE REPORT No. 4 This Report was first published in 1994 at a time when the author was involved in experimental research aimed at seeing if one could tap energy from the vacuum medium. This might seem to be an absurd pursuit but there were certain anomalies in experimental findings and debatable issues of a theoretical nature that needed resolution. As this Report shows, the author was not alone in seeing the need to clarify some of the mystery which clouds our understanding of magnetism. This version of the Report now published on Internet some nine years on from its printed publication in 1994 is an unamended copy of that original text, made available here for historical record of the author's efforts...........
