Title Autodynamics' Universal Gravitation Read in full Read in full Author(s) Ricardo L Carezani Keywords Autodynamics , pico-graviton, Gravity, perihelion advance, Le Sage, mass increase Published Society for the Advancement of Autodynamics Website https://www.autodynamics.org/universal-gravitation/ "Autodynamics' Universal Gravitation" is the paper by Ricardo L Carezani setting out the theory of gravitation that Autodynamics adds to its relativistic mechanics. It proposes that space is filled with quanta of energy — pico-gravitons — travelling at or above light speed in all directions, that matter absorbs them, and that the absorption both produces the gravitational force and causes the mass of celestial bodies to increase with time. It is the parent paper of the four applications: the Allais anomaly , binary star precession , lunar distancing and the Pioneer slowdown . Overview Carezani opens with a long quotation from Feynman 's Lectures on Physics — the passage in which Feynman describes the shadowing mechanism for gravity, discovered "about 1750", and then dismisses it because a body moving through the flux would meet more particles in front than behind and would be slowed in its orbit. This is the standard drag objection to Le Sage gravity, and the paper is written against it. Carezani's reply is that the objection is "irrelevant", on two grounds. It is unscientific, he says, "because in science it is always possible to explain tomorrow what we cannot explain today". And it misses the point of his own version, in which the absorbed flux does not merely push: it adds mass , and the mass increase of the Sun and planets is what drives the motion. The increment required, he argues, would exceed what is needed to overcome the drag. The argument The mechanism All of space is taken to be filled with pico-gravitons moving in every direction.
