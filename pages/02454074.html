WASSERFADDEN EXPERIMENT 1996 William Beaty (WATER-THREAD) Two brimful glasses of water are placed adjacent to each other on an insulating panel, and moved so their rims are nearly touching. A short length of thread is wetted and placed so as to bridge across the rims of the glasses. A high voltage, low current power supply or electrostatic generator is connected to the water in the glasses, with one lead going to one glass, the other lead to the other. When the power supply is turned on, the water is reported to flow along the thread. If the thread is short and very thin, the flow will carry the thread along. The thread will be entirely pumped into the ?NEGATIVE?? glass, but the conductive bridge of water will not break when the thread has left it. A short thread of pure water will be left behind.
