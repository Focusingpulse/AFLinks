There is no evidence for the much larger amounts of 4He observed by Arata and Zhang in similar experiments. However, we have found a very large concentration of 2.3±0.5x 1012 atoms/cc STP 3He which we believe can be attributed to decay of tritium produced during electrolysis. We cannot specify any direct production of 3He, a result which is also different from the conclusions of Arata and Zhang.
