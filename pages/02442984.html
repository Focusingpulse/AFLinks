Lecture 1 — GA 324. Anthroposophy and Science (1991)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 16 March 1921 · archived # 2,442,984
, Stuttgart Lecture I The spiritual science that underlies this course in anthroposophy, must fight for its validity in the truest sense of the word. This can seem strange to one who has become familiar with the motivating forces of this anthroposophically-oriented spiritual science, for it stands solidly on a common ground with scientific and other cultural demands of our time. It deals with all that is necessary and basic for spiritual life in these times. One can see, however, that spiritual science must fight, if one takes into consideration the many prejudices that exist at present. Spiri
