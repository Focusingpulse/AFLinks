It traces the three-quarters of a century between Ampère 's definition of electrodynamics in the 1820s and Einstein 's reform of space and time, and is widely regarded as the standard scholarly account of that period. It is worth noting what kind of book this is, since much of the material catalogued on this wiki is not of the same kind.
