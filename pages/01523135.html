Guy Grantham extending Simhony's epola model to electromagnetic waves Scientific career Fields Foundations of physics; the electron-positron lattice model of the vacuum Ian Grant Montgomery (born 2 April 1952) is an Australian company owner and director, based in Melbourne, who works on the foundations of physics outside an academic post. His contribution catalogued here is a single paper, written with R. Guy Grantham of Lincoln in England and given to the PIRT XII conference in September 2010. It develops Menahem Simhony 's epola model the proposal that the vacuum is a crystalline lattice of bound electrons and positrons and draws from it a high-frequency limit on electromagnetic waves
