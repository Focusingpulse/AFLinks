Parametric Excitation of a Linear Oscillator Manual Eugene Butikov Annotation. The manual includes a description of the simulated physical system and a summary of the relevant theoretical material for students as a prerequisite for the virtual lab “Parametric Oscillations in a Linear System.” The manual includes also a set of theoretical and experimental problems to be solved by students on their own, as well as various assignments which the instructor can offer students for possible individual mini-research projects.
