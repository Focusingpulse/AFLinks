Ramsay THE GREEK MODES AND DUALITY BY THE EDITOR The Greeks most probably constructed their musical tetrachords in a symmetrical order in analogy with their sculpture, and showed the ear identical with the eye in its love of symmetry . With them, therefore, the Dorian mode would have a certain pre-eminence. Beginning this mode on D, without knowing the musical mystery that resides in D, they had two tetrachords with the semitones symmetrically in the middle in one mode ; it was next possible for them to arrange in pairs , symmetrically , the other tetrachords .
