TEXT OF READING 281-25 This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Edmonds' home on Pennsylvania Ave., Norfolk, Va., this 17th day of July, 1935, in accordance with request made by the Glad Helpers . P R E S E N T Edgar Cayce; Gertrude Cayce , Conductor, Gladys Davis , Steno. Florence & Edith Edmonds, Ruth LeNoir, Abbie Kemp, Myrtle Demaio, Alice Harris, Elizabeth Perry, Delle Connock, Hugh Lynn Cayce , Helen Ellington, Margaret Wilkins, Albert White, Frances Morrow & Esther Wynne. R E A D I N G Time of Reading 3:40 to 4:40 P. M. 1. GC: You will have before you the Glad Helpers , members of which are present here, and their study and work in relation to spiritual healing. First you will give six affirmations to be sent those on our prayer list. Then you will have before you the Temple Beautiful in Egypt at about the period 10,500 B.C. after the return of the High Priest RA-TA from banishment. You will describe in detail this temple and the services held in it, explaining how an understanding of the work carried on there may be of help to us now in carrying forward our healing study and work. You will answer the questions. 2. EC: Yes, we have the group as gathered here, as a group and as individuals, and the work of the Glad Helpers . 3. In giving the affirmations, let them be prepared in such a manner that they will be preserved by those to whom they may be sent. As for those in the present: 4. The FIRST: LORD , THOU ART MY DWELLING PLACE. ABIDE THOU, O GOD , IN THE TEMP
