"Improvements relating to the electrical transmission of power and to apparatus therefor." H. H. Lake. (Communicated from abroad by N. Tesla, of New York.) Dated May 1. 8d. A motor is employed in which there are two or more independent energising circuits through which are passed alternating currents, which effect a progressive shifting of the magnetism or of the "lines of force" which, in accordance with well known theories, produces the action of the motor.
