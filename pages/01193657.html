Potential of ChatGPT in Vietnamese Poem Creation Thu Nguyen1 Hanoi National University of Education Abstract: This academic paper explores the potential of ChatGPT, a state-of-the-art language model, in the context of Vietnamese poetry creation. While ChatGPT has demonstrated impressive capabilities in various natural language processing tasks, including text generation, its ability to create complex Vietnamese poetic forms such as "Thất Ngôn Bát Cú Đường Luật," "Thất Ngôn Tứ Tuyệt Đường Luật," and "Lục Bát" remains unexplored. This paper examines the challenges and opportunities of using ChatGPT for Vietnamese poem generation and discusses its limitations and potential enhancements in this specific artistic domain. Keywords: ChatGPT, poem, large model language. 1. Introduction Vietnamese poetry is rich and diverse, with a long history of intricate and structured poetic forms. Traditional Vietnamese poetry includes various forms such as "Thất Ngôn Bát Cú Đường Luật," "Thất Ngôn tứ tuyệt Đường Luật," and "Lục Bát," each characterized by specific rhyme schemes, line lengths, and tonal patterns. The creation of such poems requires a deep understanding of linguistic and cultural nuances. In recent years, language models like ChatGPT have gained prominence in natural language generation tasks, raising questions about their potential in the domain of poetry composition. This paper investigates whether ChatGPT can be effectively used to generate Vietnamese poems that adhere to the rules and aesthetics of these traditional forms. ChatGPT exhibits a versatile range of capabilities, including the proficiency to compose free-form poetry as demonstrated in prior studies [1], [2], [3]. Beyond its poetic prowess, ChatGPT showcases its aptitude in an array of intellectual domains for the Vietnamese language.
