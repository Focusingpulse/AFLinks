GTM2026-main013 Maximization of Efficiency of Advertising Expenditure Igor Bykadorov1 1 S.L. Sobolev Institute of Mathematics, Siberian Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences bykadorov.igor@mail.ru We consider an optimal control problem arising when modeling a firm's communications activities with the goal of maximizing their effectiveness. It is assumed that a firm's advertising efforts contribute to the growth of its goodwill, which, in turn, influences sales volume. The goal is to find the optimal advertising strategy that maximizes the firm's effectiveness index, calculated as the ratio of output to input parameters. Output parameters include the final level of goodwill and total sales for the period under consideration. Input parameters are represented by the firm's costs, including marginal costs and advertising expenses. The model is based on the ideas of [3] and is formulated as a fractional optimal control problem. Dinkelbach's method, widely used in fractional programming problems [4], is used to find the optimal advertising policy. In [2] we introduced the concept of an efficiency index, analyzed the case of a single advertising type, described the structure of optimal controls, and proposed algorithms for constructing optimal trajectories. However, from an economic perspective, a model that takes into account multiple advertising activity types is more realistic. In [1] we generalized the results of [2] to the case of two advertising types. Reference [1] Bykadorov I. Maximization of Efficiency of Advertising Expenditure // Matematicheskaya Teoriya Igr i Ee Pr
