PHYSICS NEWS UPDATE The American Institute of Physics Bulletin of Physics News Number 366 April 9, 1998 by Phillip F. Schewe and Ben Stein IN SANDIA'S "Z" MACHINE millions of amps of current are passed through a tiny spool of tungsten wires, producing a flood of x rays. Essentially the most powerful terrestrial producer of x rays, the Z device recently achieved the following milestones during a test shot: temperatures of 1.8 million K, a power output of 290 terawatts, and an energy release of 2.0 megajoules. The researchers believe nuclear fusion could be attained inside the device (by bombarding a fuel pellet with x rays) if the conditions were pushed further, to temperatures of 3.5 million K and power levels of 1000 terawatts. Sandia officials have encapsulated these ideas in a proposal for a larger machine, to be called X-1.
