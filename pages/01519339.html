Abstract The Standard Model (of Cosmology) is the collective name given to the generally accepted explanation of the origin and current behaviour of our Universe. It has at its heart, because it relies heavily upon it, the often quoted theory of "The Big Bang", along with Einstein's relativistic theories of gravity and light.
