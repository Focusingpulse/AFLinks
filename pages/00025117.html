My invention relates to antennae and more particularly to antennae of the tower or pole type. In application Serial No. 727,409, entitled An- tennae, filed simultaneously herewith by Clifford 5 A. Nickle and assigned to the same assignee as my present application is disclosed an antenna having a capacitance area at the top. An induc- tance coil is connected between said capacitance area and top of the antenna whereby the total reactance between the top of the antenna and ground may be adjusted. In accordance with the Nickle invention this reactance may be ad- justed from zero to high capacitive or inductive values. With such an antenna. it is necessary that the capacitance area and coil be insulated from the tower and further that losses in the coil be minimized.
