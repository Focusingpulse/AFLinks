This is Steve Krivit of New Energy Times. I met Martin Fleischman for the first time at the 10th International Conference on Cold Fusion in Cambridge, Massachusetts. I chased after him eagerly for two days at the conference trying to get an interview. Finally, on Monday night, I was able to talk him into joining me at the bar. As we began the interview, I was immediately shocked and surprised. Here I was all fired up and enthusiastic about attending my first cold fusion conference and interviewing a man who I thought was someday going to be widely recognized for his scientific brilliance. Yet from the first moment of this interview, it became quite apparent to me that he was a man who felt he had failed in life, who was depressed, and disappointed at what life had presented him.
