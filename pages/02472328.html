MARCONI’S The Hungarian Electrician Has Been Experimenting in Colorado and Says He Will Transmit Wireless Messages From New York to Paris. Special Dispatch to Democrat and Chronicle. Denver, Jan. 14. — Nikola Tesla proposes to telegraph from New York to his laboratory in Paris, without the use of wires. He arrived yesterday from Colorado Springs, where he has been working secretly for eight months, and announced that he had perfected a system of wireless telegraphy vastly superior to that of Marconi. He will spend the next six weeks in testing the device by sending messages from New York to Paris. He left for New York last night. Said Mr. Tesla to-day: “I did not come to Colorado for my health, although most persons, judging from my sallow complexion and delicate frame, think I am a consumptive. I came here to learn the effect of dry air and high altitude upon the machine I have perfected for the transmission of power without the use of wires. I have been successful beyond my fondest hopes. I am not at liberty to make public the details of the process.
