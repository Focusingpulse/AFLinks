The apparent survival of these ultra-diffuse galaxies (UDGs) in a rich cluster suggests that they have very high masses. Here, we present the stellar kinematics of Dragonﬂy44, one of the largest Coma UDGs, using a 33.5 hr +8 -1 integration with DEIMOS on the Keck II telescope.
