Memo. from J. Edward Morgan Donnelly was most widely known for two books: First, for his Lost Atlantis , a novel - but he seems to have thoroughly believed in the Atlanteans, and their high civilization. Then he achieved world fame by his controversial book, The Great Cryptogram (attributing the Shakespearean plays to Bacon). He also wrote a book on an alleged cipher in the four lines of verse, on the stone at Shakespeare's grave. Donnelly was a member of Congress for a season, was twice candidate for President on the people's ticket (Populist).
