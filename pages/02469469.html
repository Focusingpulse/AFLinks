It is so already that only about half a dozen of the principal nations can afford to prepare for a war footing, and the lesser nations must keep safely near to good terms with one of the principal nations. The nations which possess purses long enough to make it possible to indulge in war are the United States, Great Britain, France, Germany and Russia. Such other nations as Austria, Italy, Spain, Greece, Switzerland, Holland, Belgium, Denmark and Sweden, endeavor to maintain the appearance of keeping up with the procession, but they will have to come down at the drop of the hat of one of the great powers unless some other great power is ready to defend them. Spain has moved nearly the whole earth and tried to move heaven to procure a friend among the great powers to help her in her war with the United States, but so far her efforts have resulted in only failure. Tesla, though a Serbian by birth, has become an intense American and the Spanish war spurred him to develop a crude idea that had been scrambling about in his intelligence for considerable time.
