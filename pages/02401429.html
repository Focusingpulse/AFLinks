GC2026-STP019 Analysis of the unusual Forbush decrease in September 2025 Yury V. Balabin1 , Aleksey V. Germanenko1 , Boris B. Gvozdevsky1 1 Polar Geophysical Institute of the Kola Scientific Center of the Russian Academy of Sciences balabin@pgia.ru The global network of neutron monitors recorded an anomalous decrease in cosmic ray (CR) intensity on September 6–7, 2025. At some neutron monitors (NMs) the decrease exceeded 20%, while most NMs registered decreasing about 10%. Such depressing is typical of strong Forbush decrease (FD). However, within a few hours (at most 12 hours) the CR intensity restore to its previous level, which is not characteristic of FD. Another atypical feature was the presence of strong anisotropy. When the Earth is inside a plasma cloud, it is magnetically shielded from all sides, and the CR anisotropy is usually less than 10%. In this event several closely spaced polar NMs showed significant differences both in the magnitude of the decrease and in the timing of its onset. For example, in Apatity the decrease at the beginning of September 7, 2025 reached a minimum of 10%, whereas in Barentsburg it reached 17%. At the South Pole station Dome C, two local minima were recorded. The first occurred approximately simultaneously with the Apatity minimum and had the same magnitude, while the second occurred six hours later and reached 25%.
