Cotton Carbon Supercapacitor http://www.ideaconnection.com/new-inventions/cotton-super-capacitors-05914.html May-29-12 Cotton Super Capacitors A new conductive cotton fabric able to store energy could help power the new generation of flexible electronics and medical implants. Xiaodong Li, a mechanical engineering professor at the University of South Carolina, created the highly conductive cotton by soaking a $5 cotton T-shirt in a sodium fluoride solution for an hour and then drying it at two different levels of heat, which changed the cotton to activate carbon. The still-flexible material was then coated with a nano-layer of metal manganese oxide, transforming the fabric to a super capacitor with 97.3 percent retention.
