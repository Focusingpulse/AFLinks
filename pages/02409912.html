A TINY REVOLUTION I’ve spent the better part of the past fifteen years living in houses smaller than some closets, and I’ve loved it. Mortgage-free living has allowed me to spend my life more or less as I please. Most of my newfound time is put into making little dwellings for others and into exploring how super-sized housing affects ecosystems, economies, and our lives. It’s encouraging to see the small house message embraced by so many. When I finished writing the first edition of The Small House Book in 1999 it was one of just three books available on the subject. Now entire shelves are devoted to this new genre. Since forming my first company in 2000, I have watched the nationwide tiny house “industry” grow from my one company to more than a dozen.
