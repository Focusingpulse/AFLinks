The Pictogram One August day in 2009 I found on Youtube the file: “2012 - Gli Extraterrestri Torneranno 3/9”. Whilst watching the movie my attention was drawn to a picture [see minutes: (8:03 8:11)] on the façade of a pyramid. The curious fact was that whilst the camera rotated when changing the frame, the image persisted, so that there could be a particular instant interpretation by the brain! On the other hand, just before the movie showed some areas with different brightness or shadows, the probability is high that it is a form of painting that has remained in some way imprinted in the rocks making up the front of the pyramid. The image shows what appears to be an ancient warrior like Maja, holding what looks like a musical instrument whose shape is round. This certainly is a mirage before the mind but it is worth investigating why it should be so. This would be a discovery of great archaeological value.
