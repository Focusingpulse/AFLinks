Decker: "Re: Minato could be overestimated" Next message: Jerry W. Decker: "White Light LED & miscellany" Dear Ms. Chang et al, There appear to be some misunderstandings here that I need to correct. First, the file on the Water Fuel Cell is a report that was posted on the KeelyNet network which concerns the aledged results of Stanley Meyer. You should be made aware that Mr. Meyer died about 2 months ago. You should also be aware that the process of 'fracturing water', though it sounds amazing and really gets people excited, has, after years of claims, not been duplicated independently nor was it ever brought to the point of commercial viability, despite the claims of the late Mr. Meyer. That is to my knowledge, if it had, we would be driving cars today using this water fracturing technology. Though you asked specifically about water as a fuel, the claims are very similar to those of free energy, so I'd like to offer you some advice. Please be very wary of delving into any investments in any claims of free energy. There are a tremendous amount of people out there who are either outright crooks or have made errors in their analyses of the equipment they claim produces overunity (free energy). I don't like to see anyone, whether single people, groups or companies get swept up by all the possibilities and get taken to the cleaners financially by unscrupulous people. It hurts the entire alternative science movement so I urge you to be very careful. I recommend that you REQUIRE proof in the form of; 1) a demonstration where your engineers or techicians have full access to the operation, measurement and testing of the claimed device. (of course you will be required to sign a non-disclosure which should be expected) 2) if possible, before investing a single penny
