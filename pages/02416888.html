The Health Craze in the Light of Spiritual Science [ 1 ] The common belief is that there are so many different diseases, but only one kind of health. This, however, is a nonsensical view, for in truth there are as many kinds of health as there are people. Health is, in fact, something entirely individual and, ultimately, slightly different for each person.
