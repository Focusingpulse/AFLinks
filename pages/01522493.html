Barry Springer Residence Falcon, CO, United States Nationality USA Known for electric sun , electric weather , electric universe , electrodynamics Scientific career Fields Electrical Engineer , Aerospace Director Institutions United States Air Force, Lockheed Martin Barry R. Springer is an American electrical engineer, retired United States Air Force colonel and aerospace program director. He developed a lifelong interest in science and engineering after becoming a ham radio operator at age 13, and later pursued independent research in new energy technologies, space plasma physics and the electrical interaction between the Earth and the Sun. He has been active in the Natural Philosophy Alliance , serving as its General Secretary and later as Chairman of the Board. Biography Education Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering Master of Science in Electrical Engineering Career Springer earned Bachelor of Science and Master of Science degrees in Electrical Engineering. He is a registered Professional Engineer in California and holds four patents for electronic systems. He served a 28-year career in the United States Air Force in the Space Operations field, directing satellite control systems and on-orbit spacecraft operations. His assignments included Commander of the GPS Master Control Station and Vice Commander of the Space Wing at Schriever Air Force Base , as well as duty as a Space Operations director for the National Reconnaissance Office. He also held senior staff positions managing space research and development programs at Headquarters Air Force Space Command, Headquarters U.S. Air Force, and on the staff of the Secretary of Defense at the Pentagon. He retired at the rank of Colonel. Following his military service, Springer worked for Lockheed Martin Integrated Systems & Solutions as a senior program manager and strategic planner, and chaired the company's Program Manager Certification Board.
