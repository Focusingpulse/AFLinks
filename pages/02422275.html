Low-energy nuclear reactions A status overview summary of a controversial phenomenon Elforsk rapport 13:901 Margaretha Engström, Sten Bergman November 2013 Low-energy nuclear reactions A status overview summary of a controversial phenomenon Elforsk rapport 13:901 Margaretha Engström, Sten Bergman November 2013 ELFORSK Preface Elforsk (the Swedish Association of Electrical Utilities), which is jointly owned by the electrical power industry, provides a common forum and facility to discuss and investigate matters of strategic importance which may have a considerable effect on tomorrow’s energy systems. Elforsk’s Strategies and Systems programme area monitors current research and development in order to keep the industry up to date on important advances, and to provide the necessary knowledge to be able to question or support further development. Low-energy nuclear reactions, LENR, is a working area that has attracted increased attention over the last two or three years. When a dozen companies state that they are concentrating on commercialisation of LENR technology, it can possibly indicate that something unexpected has been discovered, and that this discovery could, in future, affect energy supply to society. For Elforsk, this is not a matter of deciding whether the LENR phenomenon exists or not, but of being prepared in case the unexpected should happen. Theories accounting for the phenomenon are still fluid, with practical verification of the function being an area of work in continuous progress. This report, which has been written by Margaretha Engström of Vattenfall Research and Development, and Sten Bergman, of StonePower AB, is an overview presentation of information from various sources of LENR technology. It does not make any value judgements concerning scientific rigour. Stefan Montin Strategies and Systems Programme Area ELFORSK 2013-11-15 ELFORSK Summary Are we on the threshold of a new era in respect of sustainable, low environmental impact energy supply?
