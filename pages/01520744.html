Title Newtonian Limit of Einstein's Field Equations Dark Matter and Dark Energy with Einstein's Lambda Read in full Link to paper Author(s) James G Gilson Keywords Einstein's Field Equations, Dark Matter , Dark Energy , Lambda Published 2009 No. of pages 13 Abstract The stress energy momentum tensor with its invariant contracted scalar from Einstein's field equations are used to show that the pressure term they involve is responsible for inducing additional mass density, above that which is historically thought to be present. This has significant consequences for both the dark matter and dark energy problems. The additional material in the case of normally gravitating material might reasonably be taken to be the missing dark matter required for the stability of galaxies. The negative pressure of the dark energy is shown to induce just the right amount of extra negatively gravitating material to account for its usually assumed equation of state and also confirming the physical dark energy density I have deduced as being present in astrospace in earlier papers. Overview Gilson, of the School of Mathematical Sciences at Queen Mary, University of London, wrote this paper in December 2009 as one instalment of a long sequence built on his "dust universe" cosmology — earlier papers in the series include A Dust Universe Solution to the Dark Energy Problem (2005), Existence of Negative Gravity Material: Identification of Dark Energy (2006) and Thermodynamics of a Dust Universe (2007). Unlike much dissident work on Dark Matter and Dark Energy , this paper does not reject general relativity. It works entirely inside Einstein's field equations and the Friedmann equations, and its claim is that the standard treatment of the Newtonian limit has overlooked a consequence of its own formalism.
