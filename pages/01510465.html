Tags: None Jetijs Gold Member Join Date: Aug 2007 Posts: 2137 Share Tweet #2 08-27-2008, 08:26 PM This might help you: Put the 555 chip on the desk so that it stands on its legs. Now align it so that the dot is pointing up. The upper left pin will be pin1, the second (from top) left pin will be pin2 and so on till the pin4.
