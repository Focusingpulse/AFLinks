His faith in the accuracy of his scientific deductions and the confidence he inspired in a few capitalists are responsible for the transformation of the Falls of Niagara into a gigantic engine for the use of mankind. When Gov. Morton pressed the golden key in the exposition building and released the current generated 462 miles away it typified to the world that the mind of man had brought into absolute subjection this phenomenal concentration of nature’s forces. All the other evidences of electrical progress that appear in the exhibition are insignificant compared to this overwhelming manifestation of the harnessed waterfall and the possibilities of transmitting its power to individual consumers at will.
