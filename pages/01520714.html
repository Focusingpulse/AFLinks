Overview New Energy News (NEN) was the monthly newsletter of the Institute for New Energy (INE), edited by Patrick G Bailey and distributed by email in plain ASCII and PDF from Salt Lake City. This October 2001 issue (Volume 8, Number 10; ISSN 1075-0045) runs to four departments — Articles, Letters, Meetings and Advertisements — and functions as the community's noticeboard rather than as a research paper. Its historical value on this wiki is as a snapshot of what the New Energy movement was working on, arguing about and reading in the autumn of 2001. Editorially the issue is notable for its breadth and for its refusal to police the boundary between conventional and unconventional energy research. Within the same twenty-odd pages it carries a defence of commercial fission by a retired General Electric vice-president, a wire-service report on a mainstream free-electron laser at Argonne, a proposal to abolish exclusive patents, a long interview with a zero-point-energy theorist about remote viewing, and a claim that a self-sustaining vacuum-energy generator is months from market. Bailey is himself scheduled to give a conference paper titled "Dangers of Using Volt and Amp Meters to Measure Device Efficiency: Examples of Fraudulent Over-Unity Claims" — an in-house warning that sits alongside the over-unity announcements without comment. Contents of the issue The 2001 INE/EEMF conference programme The lead item is the preliminary programme for the joint INE / Emerging Energy Marketing Firm conference held 26–27 October 2001 at the Quality Inn City Center, Salt Lake City, with proceedings to be published in the Journal of New Energy .
