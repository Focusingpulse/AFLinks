Peter Sujak Born ( 1957-07-10 ) July 10, 1957 (age 69) Šahy, Slovakia Residence Prague, Czech Republic Nationality Slovak Known for Fundamental physics , unification , relativity , aether , quantum mechanics Scientific career Fields physicist , nuclear physicist Peter Sujak (born 10 July 1957) is a Slovak nuclear physicist based in Prague, Czech Republic, who is known as a critic of the main physical theories of the 20th century, including relativity and quantum mechanics , and as a proponent of aether -based and unified approaches to fundamental physics . Education Master's degree in experimental nuclear physics — Comenius University , Bratislava, 1981, after study from 1976 Doctorate in nuclear physics — Comenius University , Bratislava, 1982 Additional study in the physical foundations of contemporary fundamental physics — Faculty of Mathematics and Physics, Charles University , Prague, 2008–2010 Biography Sujak was born on 10 July 1957 in Šahy, in the south of Slovakia. After graduating he worked for the Nuclear Power Plant Research Institute in Trnava. In 1985 he took part in a one-month research study at the Nuclear Research Facility in Dubna, Russia. From 1987 to 1990 he worked for the headquarters of the Radiation Monitoring Centre in Prague. Since 1991 he has worked for the computer and software company Elektra-Olymp in Prague, moving to part-time employment there in 2007. Since 2007 he has worked full-time as an independent physicist on fundamental problems in physics. He is chairman of the non-profit organization Gluon o.s., based in Prague and dedicated to promoting new scientific information. Scientific contributions Sujak's work advocates rejecting what he regards as the principal physical theories of the 20th century, particularly special and general relativity and quantum mechanics, in favour of an aether-based framework.
