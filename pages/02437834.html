84 — GA 262. Correspondence with Marie Steiner 1901–1925

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 5 February 1911 · archived # 2,437,834
Elberfeld M. l. M. So I will be able to greet you again soon. I will arrive in Berlin by train on Tuesday, which doesn't get in until around 9 o'clock in the morning. Then the first meeting will be least disrupted. In advance, I send you my warmest thoughts and kindest regards. Rdlf. 84 An Marie von Sivers in Berlin Sonntag, 5. Februar 1911 Elberfeld M. l. M.
