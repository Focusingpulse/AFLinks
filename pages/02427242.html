Lattice-Enhanced Screening Parameterization Equation Derivation and Test Procedure 1. Model Derivation This model is a phenomenological, unit-consistent formulation describing how lattice coherence and loading modify barrier penetration probabilities through an effective screening energy U_e. No first-principles nuclear assumptions are made; all enhancements are introduced as bounded, dimensionless modifiers applied to a baseline screening energy. Define two measurable, dimensionless observables: coherence C(t) and loading θ(t), both constrained to the interval [0,1]. These observables modify the baseline screening energy U0 multiplicatively: U_e(C, θ) = U0 · (1 + αC) · (1 + βθ), where U0 ≥ 0 and α, β ≥ 0. This preserves energy units while ensuring bounded behavior. The effective collision energy is shifted to E + U_e inside the tunneling exponential and prefactor of the standard astrophysical S-factor cross-section. The screened cross-section is written as σ_eff(E;C,θ) = [S(E+U_e)/(E+U_e)]·exp(−2π·η(E+U_e)), where η is the Sommerfeld parameter expressed in energy form and remains dimensionless. Ensemble averaging over an energy probability density p(E), normalized to unity, produces the effective reactivity ■σv■. Multiplication by particle densities yields the reaction rate density R, which integrates over volume to give nuclear power via multiplication by the reaction Q-value. 2. Energy Conservation Energy accounting is explicit. The screening term U_e modifies reaction probability only and does not contribute energy. Any energy required to create or maintain the screened state must appear in P_in or P_stored. Nuclear power is credited solely through the reaction Q-value. 3.
