THE MUSICAL PROGRAMME. One of the Novelties Will Be an Ambulance Corps in Field Operations. DEWEY'S SON TO BE IN A BOX. Interest in the Entertainment Is Growing So Rapidly that Its Success Is Assured. Interest in the Red Cross benefit tomorrow night at the Grand Central Palace, under the auspices of The World, grows deeper and wider each day. It is bound to be a very great occasion. The story of the dead and wounded brought into Key West yesterday, after the affair at Cardenas, brings home the work of the angels of mercy of the battlefield. When one reads of the faces whose death pallor is blackened by gunpowder and those unfortunates whose dreadful wounds speak of the horrors of war, it would seem that the heart of the nation would throb with sympathy and money would flow to the splendid society whose members devote themselves to the victims of battle.
