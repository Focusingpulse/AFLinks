Hall and David de Hilster Keywords Autodynamics , Pioneer anomaly, pico-graviton, solar mass, Gravity, JPL Published 1998 Website https://www.autodynamics.org/pioneer-slowdown/ "Extra Gravitational Tug" , published at autodynamics.org as "Pioneer Slowdown", is a paper by Ricardo L Carezani and Lucy Haye , edited by Brian F. Hall and David de Hilster , responding to the 1998 announcement by J. D. Anderson and colleagues of an anomalous acceleration in the tracking data of Pioneer 10 and 11, Galileo and Ulysses. It is prefaced by de Hilster, who reports Carezani saying that week: "Now 'I' even believe the AD work to be as big as Newton's body of work". Overview The anomaly was an apparent additional sunward acceleration of about 8 . 5 × 1 0 − 8 cm/s 2 on the outbound spacecraft. Carezani's proposal is that it is not a new force at all, and not a slowdown: it is the signature of the mass increase that Autodynamics gravitation attributes to pico-graviton absorption. The value of the solar mass used in the navigation software, he argues, is simply out of date, and correcting it removes the anomaly.
