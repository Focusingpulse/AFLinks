November, 2006 Microwave Review 55 Simple Transmission Line Representation of Tesla Coil and Tesla’s Wave Propagation Concept Zoran Blažević 1 , Dragan Poljak2 , Mario Cvetković 3 Abstract – In this paper, we shall propose a simple transmission line model for a Tesla coil that includes distributed voltage source along secondary of Tesla transformer, which is able to explain some of the effects that cannot be fully simulated by the model with the lumped source. Also, a transmission line representation of “propagation of current-through-Earth wave” will be presented upon. Keywords – Transmission line modelling, Tesla’s resonator, Magnifying effect, Tesla’s current wave. I. One of the most representative examples for this is the development of the radio. It took several decades for Tesla to prove an extremely obvious fact that this invention belongs to him, with respect to the recognized great contribution of Dr. Hertz.
