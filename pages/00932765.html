Integral forms for the quantum He Hamiltonian 1 approximation of chemical bonds in RNA and protein 2 scaling comparisons. 3 A numerical proof of concept 4 Han Geurdes1 5 GDS Applied Mathematics BV 6 CvdLinstraat 164 2593NN Den Haag Netherlands 7 han.geurdes@gmail.com 8 Abstract 9 In the paper a mathematical method, originated from studies of nonlinear par- tial diﬀerential equations, is applied to the He approximation of outer electron chemical bonds. The results can be used in the study of large molecules like RNA and proteins. We follow a pairwise atom by atom coordinates approxima- tion. Coordinates can be obtained from crystallography or electron microscopy. The present paper solely presents the proof of concept of the existence of an algorithm. It is expected that such algorithm can be employed in studies of larger molecules.
