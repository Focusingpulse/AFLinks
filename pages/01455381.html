You will answer the questions which will be presented relative to their work. 2. EC: Yes, we have the group as gathered here, as a group, as individuals; and the work of the Glad Helpers in spiritual healing. 3. In such a manner each may become, then, more and more attuned to that spiritual awakening which brings that peace as He promised.
