The Hamel spinning disc is build with a ring magnet (outer diam.70 mm, inner diam. 33 mm, 8 mm thick) sticked on a 67 mm steel ball (490 gr weight) with south pole UPWARD. This experience must be executed on a hard and clean surface ( glass is better ). Operation : Take your magnetic gate in hands under the Hamel spinning device.
