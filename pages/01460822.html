Modulation

· archived # 1,460,822
In electronics, modulation is the process of varying one or more properties of a high-frequency periodic waveform , called the carrier signal , with respect to a modulating signal. This is done in a similar fashion as a musician may modulate a tone (a periodic waveform ) from a musical instrument by varying its volume, timing and pitch . The three key parameters of a periodic waveform are its amplitude ("volume"), its phase ("timing") and its frequency (" pitch "), all of which can be modified in accordance with a low frequency signal to obtain the modulated signal . Typically a high-frequency sinusoid waveform is used as carrier signal , but a square wave pulse train may also occur.
