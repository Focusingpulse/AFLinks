But even the lowly resistor posses all four ideal circuit elements. More than a load to sink current into... but a current source! Not just a mere voltage drop... a voltage rise! Instead of a place where power is lost... a source of power! How is this possible? In a conductor electrons are loosely bound to atoms. Indeed they move from atom to atom in a conduction band. The tiniest bit of energy sends an electron flying throughout the matrix. Ambient heat creates many currents within a conductor. The higher the temperature or the smaller the resistance of the conductor, the greater the currents flowing thru a conductor. In a resistor the electrons tightly held by atom and can only move when hit by a large amount of energy. Instead, ambient heat causes atoms to vibrate randomly within the matrix. When atoms move together they move electrons in orbit around them closer together generating regions of higher voltage.
