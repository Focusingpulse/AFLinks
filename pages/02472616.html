The eighty-four-year-old inventor of the polyphase system, the man who, together with George Westinghouse , had established the revolutionary system for the utilization and transmission of alternating currents which in those days had made him wealthy and famous, offered his former employer a scientific and prestigious deal in exchange for a huge profit, immortal fame and prestige throughout the world. He offered him a new, previously unknown art of transmitting the Earth’s energy over a distance. He called it telegeodynamics. Of course, he was speaking of Nikola Tesla. Although already in a difficult material situation, at a time when help was being extended to him by the Yugoslav government, he was still convinced that he would live long enough and that he would not weaken his creative and research abilities even in his eighties.
