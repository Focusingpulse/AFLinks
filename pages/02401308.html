GC2026-SGG007 Earthquake focal mechanisms in Transbaikalia (Eastern Siberia) Alena I. Filippova1 , Valentina I. Melnikova2 , Yan B. Radziminovich3 , Nadezhda A. Gileva3 1 Pushkov Institute of Terrestrial Magnetism, Ionosphere and Radiowave Propagation of the Russian Academy of Sciences 2 Institute of Earth Crust of the Siberian Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences 3 Baikal branch FRC GS RAS aleirk@mail.ru Transbaikalia occupies inner parts of the Amurian plate and is characterized by weak and sparse seismicity in comparison with the adjacent areas of the Baikal rift and NW Mongolia. Due to a low energy level of instrumentally recorded seismic events and unfavorable configuration of the regional seismic network, the crustal stress-strain state is poorly constrained in the region. Our study partly fills this gap as it summarizes all the published earthquake focal mechanisms and presents revised and new solutions. The compiled catalog includes 49 earthquakes with M 3.3–6.4 occurred in 1957–2023 and it is the most complete up to date in comparison with its analogs. A variety of earthquake focal mechanisms is observed in Transbaikalia with approximate the same number of normal faults, strike-slips, and their combinations and a slightly reduced number of thrust faults. A lot of normal faults are weak seismic events occurred in the immediate vicinity of the Baikal rift border. In the inner parts of the region, normal faults can reflect local processes. At the same time, the strongest seismic events with determined focal mechanisms, occurred in different parts of Transbaikalia
