8. Findings from Spiritual Research on Questions of Life and the Mystery of Death — GA 69a. Truths and Errors in Spiritual Research

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 17 May 1913 · archived # 2,416,909
, Stuttgart Translated by Steiner Online Library Compare 8. Findings from Spiritual Research on Questions of Life and the Mystery of Death [ 1 ] Ladies and gentlemen! For a number of years now, I have had the privilege of speaking here to this audience on topics in spiritual science, and many of you who have frequently participated in these reflections will find in today’s concluding remarks various insights that are not new to you. Yet what I have been able to say this year about spiritual science provides ample reason to examine the topics discussed here from a wide variety of perspectives.
