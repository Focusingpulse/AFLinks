Overview Non-Lorentzian Gauge Fields in Maxwell Electrodynamics is a five-page paper by Edward Kapuscik of the Henryk Niewodniczański Institute of Nuclear Physics in Kraków and the University of Łódź, published in Apeiron volume 7, numbers 1–2 (January–April 2000), pages 33–37. Its claim is stated compactly in the abstract: "in the standard Maxwell electrodynamics the class of gauge fields is much larger than it is usually stated." Kapuścik is explicit that he is not challenging Maxwell's equations — "We take it as the best theory for all electromagnetic phenomena" — but arguing that the usual potentials are only one choice among many. The motivation is a real asymmetry. The standard representation E = −∇Φ − ∂ A /∂ t and B = rot A treats the two fields quite differently, and "with such non symmetric representations it is impossible to formulate in terms of the gauge fields the famous duality symmetry of electrodynamics." He therefore proposes the symmetric pair E = −∇Φ − ∂ A /∂ t + λ rot X B = −∇Ψ − ∂ X /∂ t + rot A introducing a new scalar Ψ and a new vector X , with λ a dimensional parameter. Unlike the standard forms, these do not automatically satisfy the two homogeneous Maxwell equations.
