Novak is an American independent scientist and writer with a background in microbiology. Working outside of academic and government institutions, he publishes critiques of mainstream physics and climate science on his personal websites, addressing topics such as relativity , the Big Bang theory, the physics of energy, and global warming . His conclusions fall outside the scientific consensus in these fields.
