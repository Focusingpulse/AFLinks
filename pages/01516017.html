Sarcopenia Therapies -- Traditional Chinese Medicine herbal formula patents rexresearch Sarcopenia Therapies Nutritional composition for preventing and treating sarcopenia as well as preparation method and application of nutritional composition -- CN122320238 The invention relates to a nutritional composition for preventing and treating sarcopenia as well as a preparation method and application thereof, the nutritional composition for preventing and treating sarcopenia comprises high-quality protein, leucine and beta-hydroxy-beta-methylbutyric acid , and the mass ratio of the high-quality protein to the leucine to the beta-hydroxy-beta-methylbutyric acid is (80-90): (8-14): (4-8). Application of traditional Chinese medicine composition in preparing medicine for treating sarcopenia -- CN122321055 The invention belongs to the technical field of biology, and particularly relates to application of albumin polypeptide gel beads in a product for treating or preventing sarcopenia. Egg albumin is used as a raw material to extract protein, the albumin dipeptidyl peptidase-IV inhibitory peptide is prepared through a biological enzymolysis method, the inhibitory peptide TPYMFP is screened out through bioinformatics analysis, and the IC50 value of the inhibitory peptide TPYMFP to dipeptidyl peptidase-IV reaches 3.12 +/-0.49 mmol/L. The optimal preparation conditions of sodium alginate SA and carboxymethyl chitosan CMCS-based gel beads CMCS/SA/TPYMFP are further researched, and when the concentration of SA is 1.5% (w/v), the concentration of CMCS is 0.5% (w/v) and the concentration of CaCl2 is 1% (w/v), the polypeptide encapsulation efficiency is improved to 98.26% +/-0.25%. The invention also verifies that the albumin polypeptide gel bead has a good pH response characteristic, and realizes efficient protection and intestinal targeted delivery of the sarcopenia inhibitory peptide.
