The Two Last Major Primaries

· archived # 1,468,547
This diagram represents the two last major primaries of a series of 12 ; 12 of a higher series follow, and the two first of a still higher series : the secondaries are written below the primaries , the sharps or flats belonging to the different harmonies are written to each note . Each primary sounds the same tones as the secondaries of each third harmony below, but in a different order ; and the double tones are altered sharp or flat as the harmonies require.
