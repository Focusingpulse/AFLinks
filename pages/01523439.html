Duffy is a British academic at the School of Computing and Technology of the University of Sunderland, best known not for a theory of his own but for the forum he built for other people's. In 1988 he began the Physical Interpretations of Relativity Theory conferences, and he has organised and edited them ever since. That makes him an unusual figure in this collection. Most of the people catalogued here are advancing a particular claim; Duffy's contribution has been to give claims about the foundations of relativity somewhere to be argued in public, at a recognised institution and in print. Biography Education Nothing about Duffy's education is recorded in any source available here, and none is asserted; see the note at the foot. Career Duffy's affiliation throughout the conference series is the School of Computing and Technology at the University of Sunderland. He lives in Burnley, Lancashire. Physical Interpretations of Relativity Theory PIRT began at Imperial College London in 1988 and has run there roughly every two years since, with the proceedings of the first appearing in 1989 under the auspices of the British Society for the Philosophy of Science. The series later spread beyond London: meetings have been held in Moscow, Calcutta and Budapest, and this wiki records Duffy at twelve of them between 1990 and 2010. Its stated purpose is the physical, geometrical and mathematical interpretation of the formal structure of relativity theory — that is, it takes the mathematics as given and asks what it means, which is a narrower and more respectable question than rejecting the theory outright.
