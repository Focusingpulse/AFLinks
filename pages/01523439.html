Duffy is a British academic at the School of Computing and Technology of the University of Sunderland, best known not for a theory of his own but for the forum he built for other people's. In 1988 he began the Physical Interpretations of Relativity Theory conferences, and he has organised and edited them ever since. That makes him an unusual figure in this collection.
