Title Several Schauberger articles Author(s) Viktor Schauberger , translated by Walter P Baumgartner Keywords Schauberger, implosion, vortex, water, translation Published 1988–1991 Journal Causes newsletter, Albuquerque, New Mexico Several Schauberger articles is not a single paper but a composite record . It stands for a series of articles by the Austrian naturalist and inventor Viktor Schauberger (1885–1958) that were translated from German and republished in the Causes newsletter between 1988 and 1991, the translation credited on this wiki to Walter P. Baumgartner . The entry carries no individual title, volume, issue or page numbers, because it does not correspond to one publication. It is best read as a pointer to a body of translated material rather than as a citation. Abstract Several of Viktor Schauberger's articles were translated and republished in Causes newsletter from 1988 to 1991. What the record stands for Schauberger wrote in German and published very little in English during his lifetime. The English-speaking alternative-energy community of the 1970s and 1980s therefore depended on a small number of translators, and this record marks one of those efforts. The World Science Database entry (abstract record 3867) gives the year as 1988 and the journal as Causes , with no volume, page range or article titles. The 1988–1991 span comes from the abstract text itself rather than from any bibliographic field, so the single date shown in most listings understates the series. The Causes newsletter Causes was a small-circulation newsletter published from Albuquerque, New Mexico , the city the World Science Database gives as Baumgartner's own. It is not held by the major library catalogues and no run of it was located for this article; what is known of its contents comes from citations in later Schauberger literature. Secondary accounts of the Schauberger revival describe the Schauberger translations in Causes as the work of Baumgartner together with Albert Zock
