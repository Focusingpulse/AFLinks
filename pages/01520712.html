Title New Energy News (November 2001) Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Patrick G Bailey Keywords New Energy Published 2001 Journal New Energy News Volume 8 Number 11 No. of pages 21 Abstract Overview This issue of the Institute for New Energy's monthly newsletter — Volume 8, Number 11, ISSN 1075-0045, edited by Patrick G Bailey — is the conference number. Its first half is given over to the 2001 INE/EEMF Symposium held at the Quality Inn City Center in Salt Lake City on 26–27 October 2001: a welcome by Bailey and Hal Fox , the final programme, and a session-by-session summary of what was actually presented. The second half carries a database announcement, five forwarded articles on conventional energy politics and technology, two letters, and meeting notices. The issue is a useful roll-call. It records who was speaking in the new-energy field at the end of 2001, what each was claiming, and how the INE's own leadership assessed the claims. It also documents the moment the INE closed its device database to the public: the files were re-uploaded with uppercase ".HTML" extensions so that members reaching for the capitalised filenames would get current data while "the general public will not". Contents of the issue Welcome to the 2001 INE/EEMF Symposium Bailey and Fox open by placing the symposium in a lineage: the four International Symposiums on New Energy of 1993–1997, then the INE's shift after 1997 to reporting through New Energy News and to conference proceedings published in the Journal of New Energy , which they note is abstracted by Chemical Abstracts . They list allied organisations — the Advanced Energy Network in South Africa, Antigravity News and Space Drive Technology in Nevada, Cycles in Australia, KeelyNet in Texas, the Planetary Association for Clean Energy in Canada, Infinite Energy magazine, and Cold Fusion Times .
