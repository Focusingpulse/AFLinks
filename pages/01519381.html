Title Atomic Clocks and Gravitational Field Strength Read in full Link to paper Author(s) David Tombe Published 2017 Abstract Owing to the fact that gravitational field strength has the same physical effect on the internal mechanism of an atomic clock as kinetic energy does, a redefinition of potential energy will be made which better emphasizes this reality. The existing definition of potential energy with its negative sign masks the cyclical oscillation of the internal energy inside a GPS clock in an elliptical orbit. See here, https://www.researchgate.net/publication/319366888_Atomic_Clocks_and_Gravitational_Field_Strength Overview Atomic Clocks and Gravitational Field Strength is a five-page paper by David Tombe of Belfast, dated 3 February 2017 and published in the General Science Journal (the posted version has been revised since, as one of its own references carries a 2019 date). The paper proposes a change of sign and a change of scale in the gravitational potential energy: Ψ = +2GMm/R = mu² (equation 1) with u the escape velocity. Tombe is explicit about why. The conventional negative sign "introduces instantaneous conservation of energy which has the disadvantage of masking the additive effects that gravitational field strength and kinetic energy have on the internal mechanism of atomic GPS clocks that are undergoing eccentric orbits". The factor of two is meant to supply "a latent component which is absorbed internally by the constituent molecules of the orbiting clocks" — half the gravitational force accelerating the body, half torquing its dipolar molecules and raising their internal energy. The mechanism is his standing aether model: space is packed with tiny vortices, each containing an electron sink and a positron source, forming an "electric sea" through which a fluid aether flows into gravitating bodies at the escape velocity.
