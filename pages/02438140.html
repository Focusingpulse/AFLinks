32 — GA 263. Correspondence with Edith Maryon 1912–1924

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 4 September 1919 · archived # 2,438,140
My dear Miss E. Maryon, I received your letter and was pleased to receive it. I am thinking a lot about the work in the sculpture workshop there and will be glad when I can take part in it again. There is a lot of work to be done here to set up the school. Every morning we start at 9 a.m. with a lecture on general pedagogy, followed by a quarter-hour break and then a second lecture on specific methodology and didactics.
