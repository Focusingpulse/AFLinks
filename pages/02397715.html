Naturforsch. 65a, 573 – 590 (2010); received October 1, 2009 Experimental studies of pulsed electric explosion of thin titanium foils in water with discharge power of ∼ 0.2 GW are described. The production of a considerable amount of molecular hydrogen is revealed whose origin can be explained neither by water decomposition nor by known chemical reactions. A nuclear mechanism of occurrence of the observed molecular hydrogen upon electric explosion is hypothesized. Emphasis is laid on some measurements confirming the hypothesis.
