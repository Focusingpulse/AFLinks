Thought Without Action is Naught!

· archived # 2,432,419
We all know that THOUGHT is the Creative Power and we all agree to that. But THOUGHT alone won't get you what you DESIRE. Personal ACTION is required to achieve what you DESIRE. There is no such thing as "something for nothing". You won't win the lottery if you don't buy a ticket, right? You won't have that position in your company that you dreamed of if you don't work hard. You won't get that rock hard abs if you don't work out. I don't think it's possible for you to manifest what you DESIRE without you even lifting a finger. Daydreaming won't get you anywhere. You must hold on to the vision of what you want then ACT NOW. Do not wait for the right time or opportunity for this. VISUALIZE yourself in the right position then ACT NOW. Picture it like this: DESIRE is your fuel, VISUALIZATION is you map and ACTION is your car. You can throw in the GRATITUDE and EMOTIONS in there to make your journey faster.
