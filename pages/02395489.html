Preface — The Spiritual Guidance of Mankind (1921)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 2,395,489
In the following pages are reproduced the contents of some lectures delivered by me at Copenhagen in June last, in connection with the General Meeting of the Scandinavian Theosophical Society. What is here set forth was therefore spoken to an audience acquainted with occult science, or theosophy. A similar acquaintance is assumed in this work. It is throughout based on the foundations given in my books Theosophy and An Outline of Occult Science .
