LaViolette The Starburst Foundation, Schenectady, NY 12309 plaviolette@starburstfound.org Dr. LaViolette has been president of the Starburst Foundation since 1984, where he has conducted interdisciplinary research in physics, astronomy, geology, climatology, prebiotic evolution, psychology, and cultural anthropology. He received his BA in physics from Johns Hopkins, his MBA from the University of Chicago, and his Ph.D. in systems science from Portland State University. He has conducted occupational safety research at Harvard University, and served as a solar energy consultant to the Club of Rome and Greek government. He has also consulted Hughes Aircraft on ways to improve company innovation. In 1974 he developed a thermodynamics of market behavior and demonstrated that stock price bubbles exhibit predator-prey oscillation characteristics. He is originator of subquantum kinetics, a new theoretical approach that places physics and astronomy on a general systems theoretic footing and whose predictions have had over 12 confirmations. He also is originator of the galactic superwave theory, the notion that cosmic ray outbursts from a galaxy's supermassive core travel rectilinearly outward through the galaxy at close to the speed of light. In testing whether past superwaves have affected the Earth's climate, he became the first to discover high concentrations of cosmic dust and gold in ice age polar ice. He is author of six books, which include Secrets of Antigravity Propulsion, Genesis of the Cosmos, Subquantum Kinetics, Earth Under Fire, Galactic Superwaves, and Decoding the Message of the Pulsars.
