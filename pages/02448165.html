Regarding the capturing of stellar radiations into mass, Hans Nintzel, one of our renowned local alchemists said that chicken eggs laid on or close to Easter would keep for a year or more without requiring refrigeration. He said he had eggs that when opened were not spoiled or otherwise corrupted, although the yolk and white were a bit smaller because the moisture escapes through the porous shell. I don't know of anyone who had tried this very simple thing to prove, so get after it and report back. Hans said it was the rejuvenation effect present during Easter that did the trick....the eggs 'capture' the rejuvenation/noncorruption.... -- Jerry W.
