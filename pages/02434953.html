Eleventh Lecture — GA 219. The Relationship of Man to the Starry World. The Spiritual Communion of Mankind

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 30 December 1921 · archived # 2,434,953
, Dornach Translated by Steiner Online Library Eleventh Lecture [ 1 ] Here, in this very place, I have often spoken of how, in earlier periods of human development, science, art, and religion formed a harmonious unity. Anyone who, in one way or another, can gain insight into the nature of the ancient mysteries knows that within these mysteries, knowledge and understanding were sought as a revelation of the spiritual in its symbolic form—in the manner in which such understanding could be sought in earlier times. This approach can no longer be ours, but in our own age we must once again advance
