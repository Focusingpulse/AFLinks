Overview The Quantum of Light , circulated under the running title Quantum of Energy — A Photon , is a four-page paper by Francis Viren Fernandes of Kodaikanal, Tamil Nadu, dated 2009. Its premise is that the photon is a material particle: "photons are particles with mass, charge, radial length, ether and pulsate about a mean position." Matter is "basically clusters of photons about seed 186-ether", and light is the ripple those pulsating photons raise in an ambient etheric sea. Fernandes distinguishes this from convection — cosmic rays, he says, are showers of particles arriving as particles — whereas in radiation the "186-field particles change shape about a mean position", contracting and expanding to make a wavefront.
