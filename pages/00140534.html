These discrepancies may indicate limitations of the standard ΛCDM framework when extrapolated across cosmic epochs. In this work, we develop a thermodynamically motivated cosmological model in which the dark energy component is not introduced as a fundamental constant, but instead emerges dynamically from the thermodynamics of the apparent horizon.
