Frolov Commercial Aspects of LENR Published in USA, Infinite Energy Issue 114, March/April 2014, page 4 Transmutation is transformation of one object into another. In physics, it is transformation of atoms of one chemical element into other atoms. This process usually is the result of radioactive decay or nuclear reactions. Reactions on low levels of energy are "low-energy nuclear reactions» (LENR). At present time there are several new technologies for producing heat energy, which in 2012 reached a serious level. Some were taking pre-orders for a 1 MW power plant. The most known effort by Andrea Rossi. The essence of this technology is the process of transmutation of nickel into copper with great heat output. The design of the reactor looks simple: Nickel nano-powder is placed in a reactor and saturated with hydrogen under high pressure, then an electric filament initiates a reaction, and then the process becomes self-sustaining. There is no chain reaction or explosion. Also there is no radioactivity. Selection of nickel is based on the fact that this metal has an affinity for hydrogen, i.e. it is capable to absorb hydrogen into its crystal lattice. This feature is important for creating the conditions of low-energy nuclear reactions, since this condition make possible easy dissociation of molecular hydrogen with a very low energy consumption. Read more about closed cycles of dissociation and recombination of hydrogen in articles about my experiments in 2003-2004, organized by "Faraday Laboratory Ltd" and in my Russian patent application # 2004104046 of November 2, 2004. Application of this technology is a heat source to provide hot water and steam for turbine power electrogenerators.
