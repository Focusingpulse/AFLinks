GTM2026-main046 Policy Iteration for Two-Player Linear Quadratic Games with Q-learning jing li1 , Mazalov Vladimir1 1 St Petersburg University, St. Petersburg, Russia lijingyqsl@163.com This paper develops a model-free policy iteration algorithm for two-player non-cooperative linear quadratic games with unknown dynamics. Each player learns a quadratic Q-function from trajectory data by convex optimization, and updates its policy analytically via first-order conditions. Under standard stabilizability and controllability assumptions with an initial stabilizing policy, we prove monotonic convergence to the unique Nash equilibrium. Simulations on a dynamic pollution game with asymmetric costs show rapid convergence, close agreement with the analytical solution, and robustness to noise. The method provides a theoretically guaranteed, data-driven solution for multi-agent LQ games without system identification.
