Steve Jobs - Steve Wozniak

· archived # 1,462,199
Steve Jobs failed repeatedly and is called a genius. Steve Wozniak built things that actually worked and most people barely remember his name. That tells you everything about how success really works. Let me explain. There are basically two personality types when it comes to building things. I'm generalizing but this will click for you fast. Type 1: The Action Taker. Makes a decision and starts moving. Fast. Incomplete information. No perfect conditions. Figures it out on the fly. Learns by crashing into walls and adjusting. I call this the Steve Jobs type. Type 2: The Planner. Maps out every detail before taking a single step. Every contingency. Every edge case. Every failure point accounted for before they even begin. I call this the Steve Wozniak type. Here is what I believe after building multiple 7 and 8 figure businesses and watching thousands of other people try to do the same. The Action Taker is more likely to succeed. Not because they are better. But because they are moving. When you are moving you stumble onto things that work. You get real feedback from the real world. You learn what no amount of planning could ever teach you. The Planner almost never stumbles onto anything. Because they are standing still. Markets reward motion. Luck only visits people already in the game. Reality gives you better data than any spreadsheet ever will. And here is the hard truth that Planners don't want to hear.
