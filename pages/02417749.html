👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 16 December 1912 · archived # 2,417,749
, Bern Translated by Steiner Online Library 14. Truths and Errors in Spiritual Research [ 1 ] The science of the mind, by its very nature, deals with the most significant mysteries of life—not merely those of the individual sciences, but [with] mysteries that we encounter at every turn in life, which not only satisfy our thirst for knowledge but also have to do with the strength, certainty, and hope of our minds. [ 2 ] [One] person [is]—at [his] birth—[this is a] fact—already condemned to misfortune from the cradle, [is] endowed with few abilities, [so] that he makes life difficult for himself
