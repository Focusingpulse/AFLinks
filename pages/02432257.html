Distractions While Watching Your Theater

· archived # 2,432,257
Here is a question from a customer: Q: When I watch my Theater I sometimes look away because my kids or husband walk into the room to ask a question. If I look away, do I need to start the Theater over again? A: If you have the sound turned up to where you can hear it while you are watching your Theater then there is no need to start the Theater over again if you look away. In fact, you do not have to watch it at all if you are listening to it. The instructions are embedded both in the sound and visual aspects of the Theater and either one will get the instructions into your subconscious. If, however, you are distracted for a significant period of time (more than several seconds) and during this time you cannot hear nor see the Theater, it is a good idea to watch it over again.
