Piestrup (M. A. Piestrup) is an American physicist and engineer based in the Palo Alto / Redwood City area of California, best known for his work on compact sources of x-rays, extreme ultraviolet light, and neutrons produced by relativistic electron beams. He is the founder of Adelphi Technology, Inc., a company he established to develop and commercialize novel radiation sources for medical, industrial, and scientific applications. Biography Piestrup earned a PhD in Electrical Engineering from Stanford University in 1972. His early research included work on the use of lasers to accelerate high-energy electrons.
