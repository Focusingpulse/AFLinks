The results obtained suggest that the clockwise rotational motion (viewed from above) of an object may give rise to the generation of a positive density of energy (positive gravitational poten-tial). This positive density of energy generates a gravitational repelling force (Anti-Gravity). Examples of re-sults obtained during inertial rotations from 18000 to 3000 rpm are shown.
