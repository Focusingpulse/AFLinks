In the last year (2019) we have made 3 forecastings of there terrestrial events for spring and summer 2019. Now we present the same prognosis for February, 2020. The area of the alarm will be from February 2 until February 14 (mag be, even more longer). So, we ask the citizens of the seismic dandgerous containes: BE CAREFUL!
