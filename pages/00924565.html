Knowledge representation is a classic problem in Knowledge graph. Distance-based models have made great progress. The most signif- icant recent developments in this direction have been those of Rotate[1] and PairRE[2], which focus on express relationships as projections of nodes. However TransX series Model(TransE[3], TransH[4], TransR[5]) express relationships as translations of nodes. To date, the problem of the Combination of Projection and translation has received scant atten- tion in the research literature.
