The Dennis Wheatley Library of the Occult Volume 3 Moonchild is a novel written by the British occultist Aleister Crowley in 1917. Its plot involves a magical war between a group of white magicians, led by Simon Iff, and a group of black magicians over an unborn child. Aleister Crowley, who was born in 1875, has become a legend as ‘The Evilest Man of his Generation’. And he certainly enjoyed being thought so, although in this tale of the creation of a type of homunculus his principle character appears to be on the side of ‘good’ rather than ‘evil’. The book needs no introduction from me because it already contains a long and excellent one by Kenneth Grant about Crowley, and many footnotes concerning his friends and the names of the real people from whom the characters in the book were drawn. It is these particulars that will interest readers who make a study of the occult. I will therefore confine myself to what I knew of Crowley personally.
