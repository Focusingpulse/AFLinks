The guide consists of these four units: eccnomic principles in agriculture (6 lessons), business management (17 lessons), planning the farm business (9 lessons), and operating the agricultural business (5 lessons). Each unit begins with a section entitled "Getting Ready for This Unit," which contains important teaching information. The "Contents" section contains objectives, competencies, motivational technique or interest approach, references, and a competency profile.
