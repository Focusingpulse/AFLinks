The book was prepared by a summarized combination of the most proper books in oil and gas processing with excessive illustration and explanation. The basics of the book is GPSA Engineering Data Book, Surface Production Facilities, Surface Petroleum Operations book, John-Cample technical books, API reference standards. All tables and graphs were collected from API, GPSA, Surface Production Facilities. The book are focused on the practical aspects of oil and gas processing that is valuable for engineers and technical staff, omitting parts and details that will never be used.
