Book Author George H Wiseman Language English Subject Home energy, "free energy" Published 2001 Publisher Eagle Research Pages 32 ISBN 1895882206 Reverse Your Electric Meter, Legally! is a short technical booklet by George H Wiseman , published by Eagle Research. Overview The booklet describes the author's "reverse electric meter" (REM) technique: over-driving an ordinary induction motor so that it acts as a generator, phase-locked to the utility grid, allowing a home power system built from wind, solar, hydro, combustion, or geothermal sources to feed surplus electricity back through the meter. Wiseman notes that U.S. federal law (the Public Utility Regulatory Policies Act) requires utilities to purchase such excess power. The work is aimed at do-it-yourself energy self-sufficiency. Ab
