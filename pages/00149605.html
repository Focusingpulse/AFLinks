Doppler Effect, Absolute Stellar Aberration, Stellar velocity, Stellar distance and Speed of the light Miloˇs Cojanovi´c ˇ June 10, 2026 Abstract Our assumption is that the Doppler Effect and Absolute Stellar Aberration are two complementary phenomena. This means that if we are able to use the result obtained by measuring DE to determine the radial velocity, then by using the result obtained by measuring ASA we should be able to determine the transverse velocity with which the star moves in relation to the observer. We will prove that if we know the velocity of the star, we can determine its distance in relation to the observer and the speed of light emitted from the star in the direction of the observer.
