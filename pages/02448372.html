In one of the books by the late Albert Roy Davis and the still living Walter Rawls (Jacksonville, Florida), they talked about experiments done with monopolar magnetic fields and chickens. The eggs were constantly exposed to magnetic fields of one polarity until they hatched. Then the chicks and eventually adult chickens were raised in special pens having magnets embedded in the floor so that only one polarity was exposed to the chickens at all times. Chickens grown under pure North pole energy were smaller, cleaner, smarter, rarely had sex, ate little and lived a long time. Chickens grown under pure South pole energy grew to 2-3 foot tall, were nasty, stupid, had sex as often as they could, were meaner'n'hell, gorged themselves, attacked each other and even cattle in a neighboring field (jumping on their backs and gouging them), and they had brief life spans. Walter confirmed this when I visited him one Veterans Day several years ago in Florida. He said they had to destroy the South pole chickens not only because of neighbor complaints but because they did not want the negative publicity. I always thought this would be great for food production...imagine chicken breasts the size of your plate or legs like turkey legs.
