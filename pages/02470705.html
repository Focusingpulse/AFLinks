CALLS HIM A LEADING INVENTOR Says the Main Trouble Is that the Scientist Is Ahead of His Age. Most of the electrical periodicals, and even some of the newspapers, are just now engaged in hurling uncomplimentary adjectives at Nikola Tesla. They are calling him a dreamer and insinuating that he has not invented anything at all. He has, they say, promised much and fulfilled little. Mr. Park Benjamin, whose prominence in the electrical world needs no exploiting, spoke warmly yesterday to a reporter for the Telegraph on this subject. “What has Tesla done,” said Mr. Benjamin, “that the trade papers and electrical journals bark at his heels like a pack of curs? Has he declared he can do anything that is impossible? He has done much that is practical and useful and that has advanced the world in the field of electricity. But he has not come out with any claptrap scheme like the Keely motor and gone down into Wall Street to get the dollars out of people’s pockets and give them nothing in return. “I was one of the first to fight the Keely motor, and I would be the first to fight Tesla if he came out with any such impossibility.
