Epilogue — Mysticism at the Dawn of the Modern Age (1960)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 1,586,057
Almost two and a half centuries have passed since Angelus Silesius gathered together the profound wisdom of his precursors in his Cherubinic Wanderer . These centuries have brought rich insights into nature. Goethe opened a great perspective into natural science. He sought to pursue the eternal, iron laws of nature's action up to that peak where they bring forth man with the same inevitability with which, on a lower level, they produce a stone (cf. my book, Goethes Weltanschauung , Goethe's Conception of the World).
