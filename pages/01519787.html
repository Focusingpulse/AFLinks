Title Electricity and Magnetism: A Return to Aether Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Duncan W Shaw Keywords Aether , Electricity, Magnetism, Electromagnetic Fields Published 2011 Journal Proceedings of the NPA Volume 8 No. of pages 6 Pages 558-563 Abstract This paper proposes a physical model of electromagnetic fields. The model draws upon the works of James Clerk Maxwell. It is based upon the proposition that the medium of aether that Maxwell believed existed, does in fact exist. This paper posits that aether consists of highly elastic cells that collide with each other. It ties the interaction of aether cell collisions to the transmission of electricity and magnetism. Overview Presented at the 18th Natural Philosophy Alliance conference in College Park in 2011 and published in the Proceedings of the NPA , volume 8, this paper asks a set of deliberately elementary questions — what an electromagnetic field is , what travels along a wire when a switch is thrown, why a magnetic field encircles a current-carrying conductor — and argues that they remain unanswered because the aether was abandoned. Duncan Shaw's case has three parts. He first assembles the evidence that the aether was never really disposed of, quoting Newton 's Opticks , Maxwell 's Dynamical Theory of the Electromagnetic Field , Einstein 's 1920 Leiden address, Dirac 's 1951 Nature letter "Is there an Aether?", and a series of modern physicists — Laughlin's "relativistic ether", Wilczek's "Grid", Wolfram's network of nodes, Cahill's quantum foam, Allais 's éther . He then takes up a task Maxwell explicitly left undone. Maxwell wrote that he had supposed the medium to be in a state of stress "but we have not in any way accounted for this stress, or explained how it is maintained", and added, "I have not been able to make the next step, namely, to account by mechanical considerations for these stresses in the dielectric."
