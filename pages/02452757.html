A body moving through the air pushes the air aside. Small disturbances move away at the speed of sound. Disturbances from a slowly moving body go out in circles, like ripples from a pebble in a pond. If the body moves faster, the circles are closer in the direction of travel. If the body is supersonic, then the circles overlap. The envelope of circles forms a cone. The angle of the cone is determined by its vertex moving in the body's travel direction at the body's speed, while the circles grow at the sound speed. [snip] The existence of the "Mach cone", "Mach waves" and the corresponding angle, was discovered by Ernst Mach in the nineteenth century.
