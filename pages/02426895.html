Fauci, released to the public by Senator Rand Paul BROWNSTONE INSTITUTE i About This Edition This volume reproduces, in edited and reformatted form, the contemporaneous diary of Dr. Anthony S. Fauci covering the period from late 2019 through December 2022. The diary was obtained and released to the public by Senator Rand Paul, in his capacity overseeing the U.S. Senate's investigation into the origins and handling of the COVID-19 pandemic. The original released document runs to more than eleven hundred pages and, in addition to Dr. Fauci's daily entries, contains numerous news articles, op-eds, and press clippings that he chose to save alongside his own notes. For readability, this edition preserves Dr. Fauci's diary entries and his own press summaries in full, and condenses the longest pasted news articles into brief, clearly marked editorial notes. Spelling and transcription artifacts from the original scanned record have largely been left as they appear in the source release. How to Read This Edition Three kinds of text appear in this book, set distinctly so they are never mistaken for one another: Dr. Fauci's diary entries — the main narrative, set in this serif type, exactly as he wrote it. PRESS: his own same-day list of media appearances — set in small indented italics, directly below the entry it belongs to. News clippings Dr. Fauci pasted into his diary — not his own words — appear in a shaded box in sans-serif type, labeled with the source and condensed for length.
