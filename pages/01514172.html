Bedini's "Free Energy Generation" machine aka "1984" aka "Watson"

· archived # 1,514,172
I didn't see a thread to discuss this particular machine, so I thought I'd create this one. This is the machine that makes up part one of the Bearden-Bedini book "Free Energy Generation". It's also been called the "1984" machine and also the "Watson" machine. Basically, it's a motor connected to a flywheel and wheel with magnets that turns in close proximity to a series of coils. Interesting machine. I'm intending to build one just to see if it's all it's cracked up to be. Will post more as things develop.
