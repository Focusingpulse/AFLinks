After a successful 40 years as an electrical engineer, Robert de Hilster has applied the Particle Model to electronic circuits and it has been eye-opening. Where mainstream physics has no real answers to the physicality of electronic circuits, Robert de Hilster has hacked electrical components such as the battery, resistors, capacitors, transistors, and even how electricity flows through a wire. Follow this fascinating journey into the possible physical makeup of electronic circuits. And most interesting of all, the Particle Model's predictions have been verified in two simple experiments with circuits. Hosted by Dissident Science's David de Hilster. Summary Bob de Hilster presented the first two experiments from the book he was then writing, The Physicality of Electronic Circuits , applying the particle model to ordinary DC circuits and testing a prediction it makes against standard circuit theory. The photon is a particle in the standard model and the thing ether models exist to explain. The graviton is postulated and unobserved, and many etherists derive gravity from the ether particle.
