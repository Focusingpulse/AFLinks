He was Professor at the Collège de France from 1964 to 1988 and Director of the Institut d'Astrophysique from 1972 to 1979, and served as General Secretary of the International Astronomical Union from 1967 to 1970 and as Secretary of the International Academy of Humanism. He wrote several books and hundreds of papers on astronomy, astrophysics, cosmology, human rights, pseudoscience, science
