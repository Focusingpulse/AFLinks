Ghost Particle

· archived # 1,459,630
A "Ghost Particle" with Impossible Energy Just Hit Earth Scientists are baffled by a recent event where a single cosmic ray particle smashed into Earth's atmosphere with an energy level that shouldn't be possible. Dubbed the "Amaterasu" particle, it carried so much energy that if it were a baseball, it would feel like a professional pitcher had hurled it at you. What’s terrifying is its origin: when astronomers traced its path back, it pointed to a completely empty void in space. No star, no supernova, no black hole—nothing that could have launched it. It’s like a bullet fired from an empty room, suggesting there are powerful, invisible cosmic accelerators out there we can't even imagine.
