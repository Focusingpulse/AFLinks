We fully realize that fact, but over and above it lies the greater fact that loving service to one's fellow man comes first, before all things else. This universe is founded upon love . Man's actions must, therefore, be founded upon the principle of love. We have no motive for writing this book other than the motive of service to our fellow man. We have new knowledge which is purposeful in the building of an enduring civilization upon the present one that has been rapidly declining since the year 1900 toward another long Dark Age period. During that fifty year period it has fallen farther than it had formerly risen in eight centuries. The wars of the last forty years have been the main cause for developing nuclear fission . Our fuel need was not primary. It was secondary, yet the development of it continues with seeming full assurance that its admitted dangers can be voided. We know that they cannot be voided, and can tell our fellow man the cosmic reason why their use, as now intended, would make of [p. xiv] this earth a barren, oceanless rock for many thousands of years.
