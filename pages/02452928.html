Reiter and Jo Robinson ( MELATONIN - Bantam Books 1996 ) http://www.teleport.com/~jor/ WHAT ARE POSSIBLE NEGATIVE SIDE EFFECTS? There is anecdotal evidence that a small percentage of the people taking over-the-counter melatonin have experienced atypical reactions, including headaches, agitation, wakefulness, and nausea. People who experience negative side effects should stop taking melatonin. Also, there is some indication that melatonin worsens depression in some people. (It should be noted that others have found profound relief from depression while taking melatonin.) We advise that people who are depressed consult their physicians before taking melatonin. ARE THERE ANY PEOPLE WHO SHOULD NOT TAKE MELATONIN? It is always best to err on the side of caution.
