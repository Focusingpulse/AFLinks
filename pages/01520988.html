About Legends from around the world point to global cataclysms in Neolithic times. There would have been consequences: "sloshing" of oceans, sedimentation of "slurry", melting of basalt layers in place, mountain-size "dunes" that become lithified electrically, coal layers. Michael Steinbacher will guide a tour to view likely features of such events. A caravan will visit sites in Colorado, Utah, Arizona, and New Mexico. And we're flexible.
