The above relationship holds generally. It essentially yields T~r 2 , for the classical vibrational period, versus the square of the internuclear distance at different electronic states of a given molecule, which happens to be an approximate relationship known since 1925, but not disclosed so far. Note that, not much is reported about the quantum numbers of complex systems, in the literature.
