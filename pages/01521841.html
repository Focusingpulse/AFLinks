Overview The Poisson Equation, the Cosmological Constant and Dark Energy is a four-page paper by Jeff Baugher of the Department of Electrical Engineering at Wright State University, published in Progress in Physics volume 1 (January 2013), pages 15–18, and presented as a talk in December 2012. Its thesis is that the interpretation of the cosmological constant rests on an unexamined reading of the Poisson equation, and that the choice between calling a field attractive or repulsive is a convention rather than a fact. "A metaphysical interpretation of the Poisson equation during introduction of Λ could account for the confusion."
