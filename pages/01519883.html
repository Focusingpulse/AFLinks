Establishment physics admires Einstein's GRT gravity theory because of its perceived great mathematical Riemannian 4-dimensional geometry and its curved space time beauties, but does this religion of mathematical beauty coincide with the physical truth of experimental gravity? No, because it's based upon the assumption that gravitational and inertial masses are the same ... or at least always equal, but Russian Professor Alexander L. Dmitriev has done a number of experiments showing that horizontally (fast) spinning rotors do not usually fall with the usual local gravitational acceleration. Furthermore, Morton F. Spears has given a model of gravity that involves spherical capacitors modeling both electrons & protons which tends to explain why Newton's gravitational constant G cannot be calculated accurately to more than 5 significant figures (i.e. due to the local permittivity {or dielectric constant} then being a hidden variable in Newton's theory). Summary Dennis P Allen on Morton F Spears 's electrostatic account of gravity, with a long preamble on why he thinks general relativity is not the theory to build on. Ian J Cowan hosted and displayed the papers on request; several minutes went to file-format trouble, and one key paper had to be emailed mid-session.
