Lewis Niagara Falls Gazette April 15th, 1954 Page number(s): 9 By CLARENCE O. LEWIS Niagara County Historian Niagara County has been the scene of many spectacular projects that, due to some unforeseen circumstances have failed, despite the fact that they were planned and directed by brilliant men. Such a project was that of the Niagara Power and Development Co. They proposed to dig a power canal 25 feet deep and 40 feet wide from the Niagara River at LaSalle to the lower Niagara at Lewiston. This canal was to pass over the Mountain Ridge near the west boundary line of the Tuscarora Indian Reservation. At the base was to be a power house which would develop 140,000 horsepower. A lesser amount was to be developed at the Lewiston end. This canal was to furnish water power as well as electric power for an industrial city of an estimated 600,000 persons. Briefly that was the plan and it appeared so practical that the promoter, William T. Love, a New Englander I believe, soon attracted capital. Associated with him on the board of directors were men from Illinois, Tennessee, and Pennsylvania.
