Author(s) Joseph Levy Keywords Galileo, electromagnetism , Maxwell , simultaneity, relativity , synchronization of clocks Published 1997 Pages 39-45 Abstract Due to their conviction that the laws of nature must be identical in any inertial frame, the physicists of the beginning of the twentieth century were led to extend the relativity of Galileo to the electromagnetism of Maxwell, but this seemed to imply the abondonment of universal time and absolute simultaneity. In a previous paper* we have criticised, from a logical viewpoint , the criteria intended to demonstrate the relativity of simultaneity and we have proposed replacing them by other criteria.
