Bob de Hilster Born ( 1937-08-21 ) August 21, 1937 (age 89) Wyandotte, Michigan, United States Residence Delray Beach, FL, United States Nationality USA Known for Particle Model , Gravity , Light , Electricity Scientific career Fields Electrical Engineer , Physics Robert "Bob" de Hilster is an American scientist and retired electrical engineer best known for his work on The Particle Model with his son David de Hilster and for his proposed solution to the wave–particle duality of light. Over a career spanning more than 40 years in telecommunications he was awarded several patents, and in retirement he has become a prominent voice in dissident physics, developing a particle-based model of gravity in which gravity is a pushing force rather than an attractive one. He was one of the central figures in his son's feature-length documentary Einstein Wrong - The Miracle Year , which premiered in 2013. Bob de Hilster is the author of three books: a work on gravity, Gravity is Not Free (2015); Principia Mathematica 2 (formerly titled Universe Hack 3.0 ), which sets out the Particle Model developed with his son David de Hilster ; and The Four Universal Motions in Physics . Early Life Robert "Bob" de Hilster was born on August 21, 1937 in Wyandotte, Michigan, to a Dutch father and an American mother. Bob attended a Lutheran school through the 10th grade before transferring to Wyandotte High School, where he met his future wife Pat de Hilster . In 1957 he married Pat and moved to Ann Arbor, Michigan, to study engineering physics at the University of Michigan. While he was in college, he and his wife had their first son, David de Hilster . Education 1960 – B.S. in Engineering Physics, University of Michigan Professional Career Robert de Hilster worked as an electrical engineer in research for over 40 years, specializing in telecommunications, a field in which he holds several patents.
