A couple of years ago I was contacted by Tibor, who lived near to the Belgrade museum and had good contacts there. He was able to get scans of well over 10,000 documents written by (or to) Tesla. Within these I found the blueprints of the tower and a draft of the blueprints of the accompanying laboratory and many notes on experiments that Tesla did there and much, much more. From these thousands of notes, letters, drafts, articles, patents, blueprints I was able to reconstruct, in reasonable detail, what Tesla was building at Wardenclyffe. We can have hours long discussions about how it would have worked and until we rebuild it we will never know for sure. But we now have a reasonable good idea of what Tesla was building.
