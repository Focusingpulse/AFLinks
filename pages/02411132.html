Negative Space Rudolf Steiner , when discussing the sun, described its interior as "negative space": "... this antithesis of space is the condition in the Sun, The Sun is negative space. And just because of this, the Sun is the abode, the habitual abode, of the Beings who rank immediately above man: Angeloi, Archangeloi, Archai." [30 March, 1924]
