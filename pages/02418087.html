Educational Non Profit 501(c) (3) United States Psychotronics Association Conference Audio and Video Recordings 1978 to 2019 by the Year Since the founding of USPA in 1975, hundreds of top scientists have made presentations at our annual conferences. These speakers are from the leading edges of science, especially the phenomena of Mind-Body connection, energy and emerging technologies. We’ve recorded over 1400 talks during 38 of our 41 annual conferences. AUDIO RECORDINGS: In 1978, we only had audio cassette tapes. The first eighteen years (plus 2015) of these cassette recordings have now been converted to .mp3 files. These audio files can be ordered and transferred via email. (Please see the Audio order sheet on the last page of this document.) Individual .mp3 Audio lectures are $6.00 each; $5.00 each for purchase of ten or more in one order. Many of these .mp3 talks are now readily available in our store on our website at https://www.psychotronics.org/shop/ VIDEO RECORDINGS: From 1982 to 2002 we recorded to VHS Video tapes. In 2003 we switched to DVDs. In 2015 we upgraded again to digital Audio / Visual formats. In December of 2020, in the middle of the Covid Pandemic, Scott Beutlich, secretary / treasurer of USPA, had a great idea. With the blessing of the board of directors, he vetted several companies for conversion of all of our videos to digital media! He selected two companies and took all the tapes and DVDs to them and when the transfer was complete, he volunteered to go through each of them, adding leads, titles and legal disclaimers. It is an ongoing labor intensive project. Our downloadable Video lectures on .mp4 are $10.00 each; five or more in one order are $9.00 each; ten or more in one order are $8.00 each, twenty or more in one order are $7.00 each.
