Evans Residence St. Augustine, FL, United States Nationality USA Knownfor Unification , Time , Light , Gravity John J. Evans is a researcher based in St. Augustine, Florida, United States, known for his work on the unification of physical phenomena through the concept of Time , which he formulated as the Theory of Time Mechanics. Work Evans proposed the Theory of Time Mechanics, which he described as a plain-English unified theory. In this framework, the speed of light , the expansion of the universe, the accelerating expansion of the universe, gravitation , and the gravitational constant are treated as functions of a single variable, time .
