Title Human Consciousness, Human form and the Void: Observed Alternating States of Being in 1990 Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Bill Ramsay Published 1990 No. of pages 8 Abstract In 1990 the author had completed some electrical tests on an odd combination of materials when the anomaly detailed in this paper began; one which lasted several days. He became locked into two distinct alternating states of consciousness and being. In one, the longest duration, everything except his perception of time seemed normal. In the other, of much shorter duration, he became invisible to his wife and experienced what he describes as, "My awareness then was of a void, a nothingness, being perceived by a sensory detached remnant of consciousness, an Observer, which seemed inseparable from this void." Overview Bill Ramsay reports an experience of early April 1990, written up eight years later, in which he says he alternated between two states of being over several days — one apparently normal except for disordered clock times, the other a state in which he became invisible to his wife and experienced what he calls a void. The setting was a bedroom laboratory in a concrete block house in upstate South Carolina. At about 3:12 in the morning, after a long session, he noticed his head "stopping-up then unstopping, over and over, like cold or flu symptoms only much stronger." Glancing at the bench clock he read 3:12, then 3:20, then 2:52; watching it, the hands "jerked to one time, stayed put a moment then jerked to another time" within about a half-hour window. All five other clocks in the house did the same. He shut down the equipment and disconnected the device under test — "a simple if odd combination of materials" — and the cycles continued. He then had his wife examine him.
