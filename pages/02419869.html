(Sweetwater,TX) we 22 have designed, constructed, and evaluated a 3 in. x 3 in. deuterated￾23 xylene organic liquid scintillator (C8D10; EJ301D) as a fast neutron detector. 24 Similar to deuterated benzene (C6D6; NE230, BC537, and EJ315) this 25 scintillator can provide good pulse-shape discrimination between neutrons 26 and gamma rays, has good timing characteristics, and can provide a light 27 spectrum with peaks corresponding to discrete neutron energy groups up 28 to ca. 20 MeV. Unlike benzene-based detectors, deuterated xylene is less 29 volatile, less toxic, is not known to be carcinogenic, has a higher flashpoint, 30 and hence is much safer for many applications. In addition EJ301D can 31 provide slightly more light output and better PSD than deuterated-benzene 32 scintillators. We show that, as with deuterated-benzene scintillators, the 2 33 light-response spectra can be unfolded to provide useable neutron energy 34 spectra without need for time-of-flight (ToF). An array of these detectors 35 arranged at many angles close to a reaction target can be much more 36 effective (x10 to x100 or more) than an array of long-path ToF detectors 37 which must utilize a narrowly-bunched and pulse-selected beam.
