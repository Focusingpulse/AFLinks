It develops the Aether Physics Model (APM) CONTENTS 1 Abstract This paper records a standardized operator package intended to serve as a reusable mathematical foundation for future Aether Physics Model (APM) work that touches analytic number theory. The package has two components on the Hilbert space L2 (R, dq): (i) the self-adjoint d generator of log-scale translations P = −i dq , and (ii) a bounded self-adjoint convolution operator W whose Fourier multiplier is the Riemann xi function Ξ(t) = ξ( 12 + it). The kernel for W is built from Jacobi’s R ∞ theta function via an explicit real, even, rapidly decaying function Φ(q) satisfying Ξ(t) = −∞ Φ(q)eitq dq. We provide a lemma–theorem–proof chain establishing domains, boundedness, self-adjointness, and Fourier diagonalization, and then interpret the construction in QMU-normalized log-scale terms to support subsequent APM operator development.
