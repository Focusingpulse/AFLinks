John P. Rahart Known for ORMEs/ORMUS "m-state" materials; aether-based accounts of gravity and magnetism John P. Rahart , DDS, is an American dentist and independent researcher who speaks and teaches on two connected themes: the class of materials that their proponents call ORMEs or ORMUS , and an aether-based physics in which gravity, magnetism and electricity are all effects of a universal medium. He presented at the ExtraOrdinary Technology Conference organised by Tesla Tech in 2015, 2016 and 2017. Biography He is listed in the TeslaTech conference programmes as John P. Rahart, DDS, and is addressed there as "Dr. John Rahart". Beyond the dental degree indicated by that title, no published account of his education, practice or affiliations has been located, and none is asserted here. In his 2016 workshop material he states that he had been teaching on these subjects for around two decades. Aether physics Rahart's starting point is the aether. He notes that a material medium for the transmission of light, gravity and magnetism was the common consensus of nineteenth-century physics, and argues that its replacement by an "empty space" model was a wrong turn — one which, in his words, "has done nothing but to confuse and perplex even the best scientific minds for over 100 years." In an earlier presentation he had set out an account in which gravity arises from the flow and stretching of the aether as matter absorbs it.
