Curtis E. "Curt" Renshaw (born 20 December 1957) is an American electrical engineer, defense contractor and independent relativity theorist. Over more than two decades he has developed a Galilean-Newtonian alternative to Einstein's special and general relativity, built on a "Radiation Continuum Model" of light that replaces Einstein's second postulate. His central claim is that once light is modelled correctly from first principles, the whole apparatus of time dilation, length contraction, relative simultaneity and velocity-dependent mass increase becomes unnecessary — and with the basis for special relativity removed, that of general relativity falls with it. He has argued this case in some thirty conference papers, in Galilean Electrodynamics , Apeiron , Physics Essays and the IEEE Electronic Systems Magazine , and at length in his book-length treatment Newton's Revenge: The Restoration of Space and Time . Biography Education B.S. in Electrical Engineering — Georgia Institute of Technology, 1982 Second degree in physics — Georgia Institute of Technology, 2013 Master's-level work in electromagnetic theory — George Washington University and Georgia Institute of Technology The electromagnetic theory is the background from which his later objections to relativity are drawn. Engineering career In 1982 Renshaw joined the Navy EHF Satcom Program (NESP) Office of the Space and Naval Warfare Systems Command, then NAVELEX, in Washington, D.C. NESP was the Navy component of the MILSTAR communications program and placed the first operational EHF satellite into service. In 1985 he founded Tele-Consultants, Inc.
