LAND LINES AND CABLES. Went Out One Side of the Grand Central Palace and Entered on the Other. MR. EDISON SOON RECEIVED IT. Probably the Quickest Piece of Telegraphing and Cabling Ever Accomplished. The Grand Central Palace was filled last evening, the special reason for the unusual gathering being the fact that Mr. Chauncey M. Depew was to send a message around the world, a distance of 28,600 miles. The hall was en fete in honor of the occasion, as electric lights blazed from every side, while flags were tastefully festooned along the galleries or hung in streamers from the ceiling. Eclat was given to the occasion by the music of the Seventy-first regiment band. As the visitor entered his eye was attracted to an outline map of the world hanging against the rear wall and even with the gallery. The places at which Mr. Depew’s message was to be relayed were indicated by small incandescent lamps that glowed like stars against the pale background.
