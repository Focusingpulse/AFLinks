Hohenberger (born 13 November 1946) is an American engineer, poet and independent theorist who has developed a geometric and fractal theory of the aether , from which he claims to calculate the masses of the electron, proton and neutron together with the fine structure constant , Planck's length and the golden ratio Φ . Born in northwest Ohio and holding an engineering degree, he describes himself as a philosopher of the heart and the mind . That phrase is not decoration: his work divides between a structural programme, in which matter is built from the geometry of a fractal medium, and a perceptual one, in which the world we see is an illusion of the mind and the real world must be reached by imagination. He regards the two as a single argument. The Aether Fractal Plenum Hohenberger's central construct is the Aether Fractal Plenum . His statement of it is deliberately ecumenical — an attempt to gather the competing dissident accounts of the medium under one description: The various philosophies of the physics of aether can and must be integrated for there is only one real truth. Aether, whether it is called cosmic lattice, quantum foam or the neutrino sea, is a multi-dimensional, multi-generational, hyper-dynamic fractal, which can be thought of as both a fixed lattice and a dynamic fluid, and which is always in constant motion. — W. R. Hohenberger, The Structure of Aether and the Mechanics of the Electromagnetic Wave Spectrum (2009) Two features distinguish it from the classical aether.
