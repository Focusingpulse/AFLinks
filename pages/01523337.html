The paper was given at the ICPST 2010 conference in Hong Kong. He opens with the history: particles, then waves, then the wave–packet of the 1920s, after which "theorists had by then lost hope of discovering what light 'is' and turned their attention to describing how it behaves." He argues that a physical model deserves reconsideration, noting that we can interrupt light with a hand and cast a shadow, and that Einstein's photoelectric explanation tacitly invoked physical bundles. The rope differs from the classical electromagnetic wave in two ways. The Fresnel–Maxwell wave is "an abstract mathematical artifact" and a two-dimensional tool; the rope is a real medium binding two atoms.
