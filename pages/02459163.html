Faber & Faber, 2010. 304 pp. $15 (paperback). ISBN 978- 0865479180. [First published in 2008 in the UK by 4th Estate] Ben Goldacre is a British celebrity, columnist in The Guardian, non￾practicing MD, whose Bad Science sold 400,000 copies (Goldacre 2013). I had heard of him some years ago because he had committed a culpably misinformed column about the HIV/AIDS dissident Christine Maggiore (Bauer 2009). I expected only the same from his book, but was very pleasantly surprised: I urge others to read it for its sound discussions of how the media’s coverage of science is generally misleading; how misleading, too, is the way in which statistics are disseminated by drug companies and the media; and how the drug companies are not to be trusted.
