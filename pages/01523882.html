He went to the University of British Columbia where he pursued a biochemistry and physics degree. He failed his second year because he questioned everything that he was being taught, became obsessed with the idea that the nuclei are structured, and spent more time developing a mechanical model of the subatomic realm-than studying. He is bitter because most of his time was taken up writing lab reports, on subjects not yet taught in his classes, and it was only later that he learned that all of his classmates were simply copying ones they had bought... His only criticism is that his professors were adept at research, than actually teaching.
