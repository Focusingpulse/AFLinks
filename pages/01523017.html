Overview One of Borderland's trailblazing writers from the 1950?s, physicist and Ether researcher Krafft swept the cobwebs from many minds in the early days with his progressive articles. Here is a selection of his best including: The Antiquity of the Ether Concept; The Existence of an Ether; Further Evidence of an Ether; and Anti-Gravity and Saucer Propulsion. About the author Carl Frederick Krafft (b. 1892) was an American engineer who defended a 'hydromechanical' ether and a vortex-atom model of matter.
