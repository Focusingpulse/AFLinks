5. Reincarnation — GA 92. The Occult Truths of Old Myths and Legends

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 22 July 1904 · archived # 2,419,731
, Berlin Translated by Steiner Online Library 5. Reincarnation [ 1 ] Today I would like to speak to you about something that is loosely related to what I have told you before. Although the Theosophical Movement has existed for 29 years, it is still the case that its fundamental teachings are often misunderstood. For example, the doctrine of reincarnation is often misunderstood by those who may never have heard anything other than the name or a few terms, as if we taught that souls migrate through a wide variety of bodies, including those of animals. We are, in a sense, reproached for this. [ 2
