November 23, 1881 Pg 2/4 KEELY ASKED TO EXPLAIN. ?PHILADELPHIA, Nov. 22.—A thinly attended meeting of stockholders of the Keely Motor Company was held in the Board of Trade rooms tonight. No one seemed to know for what purpose the meeting had been convened, but it was finally decided to organize. Mr. C. W. Schuellerman, Secretary of the company, stated that the meeting was not called by order of the Keely Motor Company, but by a clique. He read a statement made by Mr. Havemeyer at the last meeting, to the effect that a demand be made upon Mr. Keely to impart the plans and specifications of the generator and its workings. He suggested that the meeting approve the same, which, after a lively discussion, was agreed to.?? [Published: November 23, 1881?Copyright © The New York Times] Created by Dale Pond.
