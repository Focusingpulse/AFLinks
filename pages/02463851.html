(Vienna, VA) of measurements on their “Morningstar Energy Box” which have replicated a Russian magnetic energy converter (MEC) patented (6,822,361) in 2004. The Morningstar team “found similar phenomenon regarding temperature loss and the discrete magnetic walls during tests of the Energy Box”, which consists of a series of rotating, cylindrical magnets around a magnetic ring. These anomalous “magnetic walls” and regions of cool temperature, both occurring in the space surrounding the device, have never been duplicated before by any independent team as the Morningstar Physics team.
