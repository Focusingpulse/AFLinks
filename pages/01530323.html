(Received August 10, 2012; accepted October 23, 2012; published online November 29, 2012) TiH2 and TiD2 thick targets were bombarded with 170 keV protons and with 170 keV deuterons. Charged particles from nuclear reactions were observed by means of a surface barrier particle detector shielded by an energy absorbing foil.
