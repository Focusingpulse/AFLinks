Hi Everyone, For quite a while now I have felt that I would like to assist people in greater numbers rather than my just continuuing to offer my one on one client based sessions. These sessions are the culmination of my practise over the past few years of various "healing" modalities, research of many others & thousands of hours studying the effects of consciousness. I do not pretend to have all of the answers, but I feel that I have assimilated enough ideas & intuitions to become comfortable with my own knowingness in this area & to now offer this to others in a wider context on a group basis. I work from the principle that nobody needs to be fixed, healed, cleared or corrected, that you already have within you everything that you need to bring about your own desired change & transformation, you just may not have realised that yet or believe it yet. So until such time as you get your own knowingness around this I act as the gateway or the pathway for change & transformation for you & to hold the space for you. This does not mean for what is possible for you but to allow an energetic space for the miraculous to happen. I also do not believe in healing crises or things having to get worse before they get better. So my intent is always to have change & transformation occur with ease & grace. I will not be "doing" anything to you, deciding anything for you but I will be "BE-ing" with you, holding you in my heart in the presence & vibration of the unconditional love of Source. I will be setting a designated time aside for these sessions on a weekly basis, however that does not mean that you have to be free to attend.
