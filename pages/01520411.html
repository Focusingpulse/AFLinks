Title Inertial Modulation: A New Tool for Probing the Law of Electromagnetic Force – Part I Author(s) Thomas E Phipps Keywords Ampere force, longitudinal force, Lorentz force Published 2005 Journal Electric Spacecraft Journal Number 39 Pages 6-11 Abstract The existence of longitudinal forces between elements of electrical current was claimed by the earliest investigator, Ampere, but has been denied by modern relativistic theory. Until recently, experimental difficulties have prevented an empirical resolution of the issue. In the last decade, however, applications of a method, termed "inertial modulation" of force, have made it possible to circumvent the difficulties, and clear resolution has emerged in favor of Ampere. It thus appears that electrodynamic forces violate the universal covariance of relativity. What is disputed here This page is one item in a larger argument.
