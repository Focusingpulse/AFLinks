Chapter XXX — The Story of My Life (1928)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 2,404,955
The decision to give public expression to the esoteric from my own inner experience impelled me to write for the Magazine for August 28, 1899, on the occasion of the one hundred and fiftieth anniversary of Goethe's birth, an article on Goethe's fairy-tale of The Green Snake and the Beautiful Lily , under the title Goethes Geheime Offenbarung . 1 Goethe's Secret Revelation. This article was, of course, only slightly esoteric. But I could not expect more of my public than I there gave.
