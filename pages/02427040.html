PRAISE FOR DAVID ROCKEFELLER’S MEMOIRS “Rockefeller’s 500-page autobiography … gives us an inside look at the man and his complex career. . . . His discussion of his upbringing and of the obligations imposed by great wealth is fascinating, as are his personal reflections on four generations of Rockefellers. What the book also reveals, unconsciously but with great clarity, is the decency, integrity, and humanity of David Rockefeller himself.” —DR.
