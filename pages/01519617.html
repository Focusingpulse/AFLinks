Title Decay and Energy Absorption Read in full Read in full Author(s) David de Hilster Keywords Autodynamics , mass-energy equivalence, decay, energy absorption, Compton Effect Published Society for the Advancement of Autodynamics Website https://www.autodynamics.org/decay-and-energy-absorption/ "Decay and Energy Absorption" is a white paper published by the Society for the Advancement of Autodynamics setting out the physical picture underneath the Autodynamics equations. It is the plainest statement of what the theory takes the fundamental process of nature to be: mass decaying into energy, and energy being absorbed into mass. Overview The argument starts from a gap the author identifies in E = m 0 c 2 . "Einstein gave us energy mass equivalence. What he did not give us was examples of how one changes into the other." Decay supplies the first direction and is easy to picture; the reverse is harder. Autodynamics's contribution, on this account, is not the equivalence but the mechanism: "AD does not explain WHY decay and energy absorption occur: it liberates them. Einstein gave us energy mass equivalence but not the power to use it." The argument Billiard balls against plastic particles The central contrast is between two pictures of what happens when particles interact. In special relativity , the author writes, "it treats all energy transfers as external phenomena. Particles are billiard balls that bash into each other and break into pieces. This has been the perception of modern physics since the advent of powerful particle accelerators. Scientists smash matter into smaller and smaller pieces trying to find the most fundamental 'unifying' particle of the universe." The alternative offered is that particles are "plastic": they "would join and break apart not always as separate entities but actually 'meld' together in some intermediate form."
