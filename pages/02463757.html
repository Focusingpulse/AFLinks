Break Through to Enlightenment Take Control of Your Life ~ Shattering Reactive Paradigms This Module will aid you in breaking free of the cycle of non-productive thought patterns and habits that can be accumulated by everyday living. Negative mind-sets and non-beneficial societal and cultural learnings will be replaced with a new understanding and belief in yourself and what you are truly capable of. You will also experience a heightened connection to the spiritual realm. Warning: This module will produce cognitive dissonance as it challenges non-productive paradigms. Cognitive Dissonance is when two cognitions (attitudes, emotions, beliefs, etc.), have a seeming incompatibility with one another. When one has Cognitive Dissonance, the contradicting cognitions can lead a person to invent new thoughts or beliefs, or to modify existing ones. This module will most likely cause a period of inner turmoil as new paradigms are established. Addresses: - Breaking free of negative thought patterns - Eliminating negative self-talk - Heightened sense of the spiritual realm - Heightened understanding of yourself and your place in the world - Heightened confidence Recommended Supplement: - Spirulina for necessary Amino Acids Recommended Duration of Use: Break Through to Enlightenment helps you develop new thoughts, habits and behaviors that can often be internalized after only 60-90 days, providing long-lasting benefits and lifestyle changes.
