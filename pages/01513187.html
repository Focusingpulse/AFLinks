The primary, center coil is fed by a 20ohm wirewound resistor, while the transformer core material is ferrite. The primary core is twice as thick as the secondaries, as may be somewhat apparent from viewing it, while the upper and lower E-Core center legs have been ground down to decouple the secondaries. The signal generator is a standard dual-555 pulsewidth modulator with the transistor hookup identical to how it is shown in the Desulfator schematic attached below. The secondary coils are connected to the bridge in parallel, start to start, end to end.
