The device is claimed to produce 11,000 btu's from 600 watts input. I've built a small model using a regular 100 watt light bulb, though i'm using an aluminum cylinder which completely surrounds the bulb. I drilled many 1/4" diameter holes in the cylinder and have a small fan blowing into the other end of the cylinder, with an exhaust hole in top of heater box.
