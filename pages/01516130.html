The other diagram shows a layer of vibrating graphene sandwiched between two layers of conductive electrodes. The two electrodes capture the energy of the graphene’s alternating concave-convex vertical motions to cause an electrical current to flow. A vibrating sheet of graphene 10 microns by 10 microns in area can produce 10 microwatts of power. 20,000 of these sheets can fit on a pinhead. Photovoltaics can only operate as a single layer exposed to direct sunlight. When stacked bottom layers of neutrinovoltaics equally operate as top layers, even when underground.
