While editing the Snell Manuscript there appeared on FaceBook a quote, posted countless times, attributed to Nikola Tesla : "If you only knew the magnificence of 3, 6 and 9". Why would Tesla say this? What is the "magnificence of 3, 6 and 9"? What makes those numbers so special? Why are they special? To find answers to these and other questions I began a page for this quote from Tesla and because these numbers are constantly showing up in Keely quotes another similar page was started to track these Keely quotes in an effort to make some sense of this mystery. Some interesting things immediate became evident; (1) though I requested a reference or source for this Tesla quote no one has yet provided one, (2) there is nothing else Tesla said (that we could find) about these numbers and (3) there are many quotes from Keely on and about these numbers. One suspects Tesla knew of Keely's work with these numbers yet had nothing more to say about the subject. The only meaningful materials we've been able to find are indeed the Keely quotes which originate from the 1880s. These numbers (actually relative proportionate frequency values) are in reference to Celestial Radiation which manifests as triune Sympathetic Streams that support and control all materiality and motions in the Universe by sympathetic entanglement of/between Neutral Centers .
