Nota Técnica Especial: Acoplamiento Topológico Helicidad-Carga, Simetría $\Omega$ y el Mecanismo de Aparición del Positrón (FCD-RB pre-XXXI) Ricardo J. Miralles — independent researcher, Valencia, Spain FCD-RBv4 Special Addendum — August 2026 Abstract: In this special addendum to the FCD-RB model, we present a fundamental refinement of the charge assignment mechanism, decoupling the sign of the observable electrical charge from the strict spatial distribution of mass within the mirror brane. We postulate that the charge sign is dictated instead by the topological helicity (braid chirality) of the EntangledRung (ER) endpoints, formalized via the discrete global orientation operator $\Omega = \pm 1$. This theoretical adjustment resolves the thermodynamic paradox of $\beta^+$ decay by enabling in situ charge inversion without requiring the macroscopic displacement of mass across the bulk. We detail the complete four-phase thermodynamic cycle of positron emergence: initial $\Omega$ inversion (transient proton-to-antiproton conversion), positron emission, spontaneous $\Omega$ reversion due to local energetic instability, and the subsequent trans-brane A/B quark commutation to yield a stable neutron. Furthermore, we demonstrate that this helicity-charge coupling naturally gives rise to phase-synchronized, non￾radiative electron rails, laying the necessary topological and thermodynamic groundwork for the introduction of Double Helicoidal Fields in the upcoming Part XXXI Tema: Refinamiento del mecanismo de asignación de carga, formalización del operador $\Omega$ vía helicidad y termodinámica de la desintegración $\beta^+$ en el esquema de branas conjugadas. Resumen Ejecutivo Esta nota técnica introduce un ajuste fundamental en el corpus del modelo FCD-RB. Históricamente, el modelo aproximaba el signo y magnitud de la carga eléctrica observable en nuestra brana como una función directa de la distribución espacial de la masa en la brana espejo (la regla métrica $Q_{TBMAR}$).
