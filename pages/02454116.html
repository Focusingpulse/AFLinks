This invitation is being posted to these newsgroups in hopes that the new alt.sci.amateur newsgroup might become a resource for science students and hobbyists to further their science education. Ask your ISP to subscribe to alt.sci.amateur of catch it on Deja News. Welcome and enjoy! Please abide by the charter. Charter: alt.sci.amateur is an unmoderated newsgroup for discussion of all matters related to the amateur science hobby. It is not intended to be a general science discussion group except as relating to an experiment or project discussion appropriate to the group. Flames and inappropriate crosspostings are off charter.
