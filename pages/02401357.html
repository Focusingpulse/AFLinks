kimo2026-main158 The Experience of the USSR in the Spanish Civil War as Historical Legacy for the New World Order Josep Puigsech Farràs1 1 Associate Professor and Director of the Department of Early Modern, Modern and Contemporary History. Autonomous University of Barcelona (Barcelona, Spain) josep.puigsech@uab.cat This paper analyses the participation of the USSR in the Spanish Civil War (1936–1939) as a disruptive element within the international order and explores the dialogue that may be established between that historical experience and the current role of the Russian Federation in the context of globalisation and the configuration of a new world order. To this end, the study draws upon documentary holdings from the Russian State Archive of Socio-Political History (RGASPI), the Historical Archive of the Spanish Communist Party (AHPCE), and the Archive of the Juan Negrín Foundation (AHFJN). The Spanish Civil War coincided with the Soviet foreign policy doctrine known as Collective Security and, more specifically within the international communist movement, with the Popular Front strategy. These two elements enabled the USSR to break the international isolation to which it had been subjected by the liberal powers up to that point. Furthermore, they positioned the USSR on the international stage as a staunch defender of anti-fascism, as well as a state favourable to international cooperation, particularly with liberal political organisations and states, as well as with liberal political parties in Spain and other anti-fascist organisations.
