P. D. Prussov Native name Пруссов Пётр Денисович Born 19 November 1925 Yavkino, Nikolaev oblast, Ukrainian SSR Residence Nikolaev (Mykolaiv), Ukraine Nationality Ukrainian Known for Ether physics, ether theory of the red shift , ether structure of the periodic system Scientific career Fields Physics , Mathematics Institutions Nikolaev Shipbuilding Institute Pyotr Denisovich Prussov (born 19 November 1925) is a Ukrainian physics teacher and mathematician from Nikolaev — now Mykolaiv — who spent half a century working out an account of nature in which everything physical is built out of a single gas-like medium, the ether . He published it late: four instalments of the monograph Явление эфира ( The Phenomenon of the Ether ) between 1992 and 1998, and then, in 2003, a 286-page volume he called the first encyclopedia of the ether. The Worldwide List of Dissident Scientists records his country as Russia; he lived and worked in Ukraine. Biography Prussov was born in the village of Yavkino in the Nikolaev region. His family survived the famine of 1932–33 there only because his father, Denis Kharlampievich, went to work in the city of Nikolaev, where the rest of the family followed. He had finished eight classes of school when the war reached them. On 4 April 1942 he was deported to Hamburg as forced labour. The prisoners were housed in a warehouse behind barbed wire and worked in the warehouse next to it, where the Stülcken shipyard had set up workshops; he later described the hunger as the thing that dominated everything. He escaped in the summer of 1944, was betrayed on the road by Russians he met, and in prison refused to wish the officer in charge "good night" — for which he was sent to a Gestapo camp on the outskirts of Hamburg. Sixty years later, when he came to publish Физика эфира , he printed his thanks to the staff of the Neuengamme concentration camp memorial in Hamburg among the acknowledgements.
