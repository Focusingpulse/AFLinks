Patents Externally applied phoenix mesona herb, cinobufagin skin and fructus cnidii oil ointment based on skin disease conditioning -- CN111870629 The invention relates to the technical field of external ointment for treating skin diseases, in particular to externally applied phoenix mesona herb, cinobufagin skin and fructus cnidii oil ointment based on skin disease conditioning. The externally applied phoenix mesona herb, cinobufagin skin and fructus cnidii oil ointment based on skin disease conditioning is prepared from the following raw materials in parts by weight: 70-90 parts of fructus cnidii, 40-60 parts of fructus kochiae, 70-90 parts of radix sophorae flavescentis, 20-40 parts of divaricate saposhnikovia roots, 40-60 parts of cortex dictamni, 20-40 parts of phoenix mesona herbs, 40-60 parts of chaulmoogra tree seeds, 40-60 parts of Chinese angelica, 10-30 parts of safflower, 40-60 parts of cortex pseudolaricis, 20-40 parts of tribulus terrestris, 10-30 parts of cinobufagin skin, 10-30 parts of honeycombs, 40-60 parts of rheum officinale, 70-90 parts of borneol, 40-60 parts of cactuses, and 20-40 parts of amur corktree bark . The externally-applied phoenix mesona herb, cinobufagin skin and fructus cnidii oil ointment based on skin disease conditioning is mainly used for treating skin diseases such as skin tinea diseases, dermatitis, fungal infection, dry skin, rhagadia, viral herpes, skin ulcer, mosquito bites, redness, swelling, heat pain and pruritus vulvae, is quick to absorb after use, has good and stable curative effects, does not have any toxic or side effects, and has the advantages of being wide in application range and the like.
