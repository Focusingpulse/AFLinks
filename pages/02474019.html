KOZLOVA 2 RELEVANCE OF THE TOPIC Recently, much attention has been paid to the study of the properties of magnetically ordered substances in various electromagnetic and magnetic fields. This is directly related to the wide possibilities of their practical use, as well as to the variety of interactions arising in connection with this, which are of interest to the theory. Of particular interest in this regard is the case of nonlinear ferromagnetic resonance (NFMR) on unfixed specimens with translational and rotational degrees of freedom.
