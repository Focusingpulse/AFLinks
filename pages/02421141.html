Mark M. Hendershot Born 1951 (age 74–75) Residence Okanogan, WA, United States Nationality American Known for Documenting the Hendershot "fuelless" generator; magnetic motors, New Energy Mark M. Hendershot (born 1951) is an American writer and researcher, the son of the inventor Lester J. Hendershot (1898–1961). He is known for having written the definitive account of his father's Hendershot fuelless generator , drawing on the family's papers and his own recollections to preserve the history of the device. Biography Mark Hendershot grew up with the story of his father's "magnetic motor" and, as an adult, set out to document it before the primary material was lost. He assembled Lester Hendershot's surviving notes, correspondence, diagrams and photographs into two books — The Hendershot Motor Mystery and From the Archives of Lester J. Hendershot — which remain among the principal sources on the generator within the free-energy community. The device's claimed fuelless operation has never been independently verified and is not accepted by mainstream science. 1995 - " From the Archives of Lester J.
