Jose Francisco Garcia Julia Residence Valencia, Spain Nationality Spanish Known for Tired-light accounts of the cosmological and intrinsic redshift Scientific career Fields Cosmology José Francisco García Juliá is a Spanish independent researcher based in Valencia who works on alternative interpretations of the cosmological redshift . He is known for proposing non-expansion, " tired light " mechanisms in which light loses energy through interaction with other electromagnetic radiation, offering an account of the redshift of distant galaxies that does not require an expanding universe. Biography García Juliá works independently in Valencia. Since 2007 he has published a long series of short papers — more than fifty — chiefly on the electronic preprint archive viXra , together with work in the alternative-cosmology journal Apeiron: Studies in Infinite Nature . He presented at the 17th Natural Philosophy Alliance Conference in 2010. No institutional affiliation appears on his papers, which give only a Valencia address. Work Redshift García Juliá's central claim concerns the origin of the redshift observed in the light of distant galaxies. In place of the standard reading, on which the redshift measures the expansion of the universe, he argues for a tired light mechanism: photons lose energy gradually in transit through an exponential decay E ( t ) = E (0)e − t /τ , so that the Hubble constant is reinterpreted as the inverse of an energy-loss time constant rather than as a rate of expansion. The distinctive feature of his treatment is that one mechanism is made to cover two phenomena. Light crossing intergalactic space interacts with the microwave background and acquires the cosmological redshift; light generated inside a quasar interacts with that object's much denser radio-wave field and acquires an intrinsic redshift on the way out.
