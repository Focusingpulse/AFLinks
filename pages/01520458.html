Author(s) Jacques Moret-Bailey Keywords matter, wave-particle duality, electromagnetic waves, Maxwell's Equations, quantum mechanics Published 2003 Journal Galilean Electrodynamics Volume 14 Number 1 Pages 11-16 Abstract In a nonlinear medium, waves may be self-trapped in structures called solitons. The solitons studied up to now propagate at a speed the order of the speed of the free wave. New solitons studied here are called 'static solitons? because they remain in a defined region of space. Maxwell's equations are particularly convenient to build these solitons. The creation of electron-positron pairs from electromagnetic waves in the vacuum shows a nonlinearity of the electromagnetic medium.
