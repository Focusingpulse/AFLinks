Kasagi, J., et al., Energetic Protons and alpha Particles Emitted in 150-keV Deuteron Bombardment on Deuterated Ti. J. Phys. Soc. Japan, 1995. 64(3): p. 777-783. Energetic Protons and α Particles Emitted in 150-keV Deuteron Bombardment on Deuterated Ti Jirohta KASAGI, Tsutomu OHTSUKI, Keizo ISHII1 and Masayuki HIRAGA2 Laboratory of Nuclear Science, Tohoku University, Mikamine, Taihaku-ku, Sendai 982 1 Cyclotron and RI Center, Tohoku University, Aramaki, Aoba-ku, Sendai 980 2 Department of Chemistry, Tohoku University, Aramaki, Aoba-ku, Sendai 980 (Received September 7, 1994) Energetic charged particles have been measured in the bombardment of 150-keV deuterons on deuterated Ti. Protons and α particles were observed with energies up to ~17 and ~6.5 MeV, respectively, which can never be attained in the D+D reaction. A bump structure at around 14 MeV seen in the proton spectrum can be well explained as emitted in the sequential reaction involving three deuterons. However, protons and α particles distributed continuously up to the maximum energies can never be understood as products of the conceivable nuclear reactions.
