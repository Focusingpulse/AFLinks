Rosicrucian came into public notice early in the 17th century. In 1610 the Fama Fraternitatis; or a Discovery of the Fraternity of the most Laudable Order of the Rosy Cross, circulated in manuscript among groups of German occultists. It was published in 1614, at Cassel, and early the following year its sequel, the Confessio Fraternitatis, was printed. From those two original Rosicrucian pamphlets the story of the Order immediately following has been condensed. The Story of C. R. C. Born in 1378, the most highly illuminated Father, our Brother C. R. C., chief and original of our Fraternity, in the fifth year of his age was placed in a cloister, where he learned indifferently Greek and Latin, and (upon his earnest desire and request), being yet in his growing years, was associated to a Brother P. A. L., who had determined to go to the Holy Land. This Brother died in Cyprus, yet our Brother C. R. C. Did not return, but went to Damascus, minding from thence to go to Jerusalem. But by reason of the feebleness of his body, remained still there, and obtained much favor with the Turks by reason of his skill is physic.
