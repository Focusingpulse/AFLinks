Larry spent his early school years in Healdsburg, Corte Madera and Cloverdale CA. In 1919 the family bought a remote ranch where Larry learned resourcefulness and a developed a keen sense of observation. As a boy he raised bees, carrier pigeons as well as berries and vegetables. He grew up fishing and hunting and collecting Indian arrow heads.
