Preface to the sixth edition Apart from a general revision and updating of the text, some specific alterations and additions have been made to this book. Additional subject-matter includes the brittle and ductile behaviour of solids, ductile fracture, and the velocity of crack propagation. A description of the new and promising welding process, friction stir welding, is given in Chapter 2. A new chapter on adhesive bonding has been written.
