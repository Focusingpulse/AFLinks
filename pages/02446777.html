Wrapping a cold flannel around a wrist is a quick way to cool the body down, because there are pulse points in the wrist. At these points, blood vessels are closer to the surface of the skin than in other body parts. The body's natural cooling system circulates blood to move heat from inside of the body to the surface of the skin. Sweat then helps cool the skin by releasing some of this heat as it evaporates. The blood near the surface of the skin recirculates through the body and absorbs more heat to be released.
