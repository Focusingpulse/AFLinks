Celestial Forces - Opposite to Terrestrial Forces ( matter ) and originating from or in Outer Space and/or from or in celestial bodies such as the Sun and detectable on Earth . Also sometimes called etheric , God , Infinite , Spirit or tenuous Mind or that influence that originates away from Earth but has a profound impact upon things of the Earth. Associated to Walter Russell 's Father Forces . An example would be solar rays, acteinic rays , Cosmic Rays, thermal solar rays, solar x-rays, magnetic portals or FTEs.
