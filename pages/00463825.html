Proving the Axiom of Choice of Subsets Ralf Donau 22.05.21 A proof of The Axiom of Choice of Subsets proposed by Antoine Balan. Introduction Let ω be some fixed cardinal number. Let (Xi ) be a family of sets such that Card(Xi )≥ω for all i . The Axiom of Choice of Subsets proposed by Antoine Balan yields a family (Yi ) of sets such that Yi⊂Xi and Card(Yi )=ω for all i , see viXra:2104.0143. The Proof For a set X we obtain the set P(X) of all subset of X . For a given cardinal number ω we define Pω(X):={Y ∈P( X);Card(Y )=ω} . Now let (Xi ) be a family of sets such that Card(Xi )≥ω , where ω is some cardinal number. We apply the Axiom of Choice to the family (Pω(Xi )) to complete the proof.
