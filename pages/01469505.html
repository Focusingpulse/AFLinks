"Every single person who realises that from their eyes is radiated potent life-force to those at whom the vision is directed, can know that their glance, that penetrating look, that smiling gaze has benefited the one who received it. For everything we - yes, you and I, the CHRIST , think and do is an act of consciousness - and consciousness is life-force . With the activity of our minds , yours and mine, we shape our consciousness , our life-force into different forms which will bless or curse the environment."
