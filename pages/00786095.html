Multicast Distance Vector Forwarding in NDN Networks Hin Ching Hou, Bradford Lowe UCLA Abstract Multicast forwarding in Named Data Networking (NDN) with distance-vector routing presents a challenge, as current implementations often rely on inefficient broadcast approaches. This research investigates alternative multicast forwarding strategies to improve network efficiency in NDN-dv. A custom-built Python simulator was developed to evaluate different strategies, comparing a baseline flooding approach with a lowest-cost ”greedy” approach. The simulation results compare the correctness and performance of both methods. While the simulator cur￾rently has some limitations, this work lays the foundation for future testing of the two strategies outlined or future strategies to be developed.
