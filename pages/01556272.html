We have arrived at the view that it holds a very privileged position within this world, that it plays a central role . Let us disregard that now. Let us limit ourselves here to the inner nature of thinking. Let us investigate the thought-world's very own character, in order to experience how one thought depends upon the other and how the thoughts relate to each other .
