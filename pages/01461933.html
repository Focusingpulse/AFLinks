SYMPATHETIC INDUCTION - Snell

· archived # 1,461,933
A cord in vibration tends to induce in another cord placed near it, a vibration of corresponding frequency , whether or not the receptor corresponds in frequency to the generator . This property, called " sympathetic induction " belongs to all vibrating mediums and the " order " or " degree " of sympathetic induction " is directly as the resonance of the receptor . " Sympathetic induction " is simply "forced vibrations." [See 8.26 - Law of Force ] By use of the dominant mode , which is allied to the " order " of etheric vibrations, we can induct, " sympathetic negative attraction " or " sympathetic positive propulsion " in any mass , according to its mass chord .
