Robert A. Kerr Born 1926 Residence Oro Valley, AZ, United States Nationality USA Known for Aether Scientific career Fields Senior Engineering Specialist and Consultant Robert Archer Kerr (born 1926) is an American engineer and independent natural philosopher. Over a career spanning several decades he worked as a draftsman and as a geological, civil and aerospace engineer, culminating as a Senior Engineering Specialist with the Garrett Turbine Engine Company. After retiring, he devoted himself to developing a model of physical reality based on a universal aethereal fluid , setting out his ideas in a series of conference papers and in the book The Fluid Universe: A Concept of Local Action Reality (1999). Biography Kerr served in the United States Navy as an Electronic Technician's Mate. Education Bachelor of Arts, University of Idaho (minor in philosophy) His engineering career included work as a Chief Draftsman and Geological Engineer, an Architectural Designer, a Civil Engineer, and a Technical Illustrator, before he joined the Garrett Turbine Engine Company as a Senior Engineering Specialist. There his work centered on the design, development and certification of aircraft gas turbine engines and flight power management systems. Among his assignments were the design and fabrication of a space-based Rankine cycle power generation system using mercury vapor produced by a nuclear reactor, the derivation of equations for the orbital light and dark periods of solar-powered satellites, and the coordination of power management systems for Learjet and Falcon aircraft. His final assignment was the TPE331-14 gas turbine engine for the 40-passenger Jetstream 41 aircraft. Kerr settled in Oro Valley, Arizona. Scientific contributions After his retirement, Kerr set out to describe the physical world in what he termed logically conceivable terms.
