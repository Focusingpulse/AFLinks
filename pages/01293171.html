I consider the Dialogue on Awakening as one of the important documents of all time. If people would read it with an open mind their lives would be transformed for the better. I’ve read this book nearly everyday since a copy was given to me in 1999. It is an inspiration from word one to the last word. My life has been transformed by it and continues to be transformed as I grow in understanding what it conveys. If you are interested in awakening , who you are and why you are here this is the one to read, study and apply. This book has its origins in A Course in Miracles . It is created and written by Tom and Linda Carpenter . This index below was created from the second edition (1993) because I wanted an index to better use the book. The hyperlinked texts do not necessarily link to quotes from Dialogue on Awakening but mostly link to similar and common definitions and uses from other sources.
