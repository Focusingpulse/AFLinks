Schmidt Institute of Physics of the Earth, Russian Academy of Sciences gribov@borok.yar.ru Laboratory experiments [1, 2] were continued to create a “primary” full TRM on basalts from the submerged Reykjanes Ridge (upon cooling in air from 600 to 20°C in a constant magnetic field of 50 μT after exposure for 0 minutes (scenario 1), 4 minutes (scenario 2), 20 minutes (scenario 3) and 80 minutes (scenario 4) at this temperature) following by inducing “secondary” CRMs perpendicular to TRM with subsequent 200-hour exposure at 450°C in the same field and pTRMs imposed during final cooling of samples to 20°C. Additionally, at different stages of a similar two-stage heat treatment of duplicates of the original samples, a complex of mineralogical and magnetic studies was conducted on individual pieces, including observation of polished sections under a scanning electron microscope, X-ray phase and thermomagnetic analyses, and measurement of magnetic hysteresis loop parameters. Monitoring of TRM changes (TRMrem ) and CRM acqusition with annealing time were monitored directly at 450°C.
