Pancreatic cancer: a split type therapy idea Edgars Alksnis e1alksnis@gmail.com +371 26696822 Despite decades of research, non- resectable pancreatic cancer remain uncurable with 5 year survival rate not more than 8% of patients. Factors behind that unsatisfactory statistics could be split to: 1) genetical, 2) physiological, 3) metastatic, 4) unreachable place and 5) dangerous environment within pancreas. Tumors in pancreas are poorly accessible to chemotherapy, their hypoxia and low blood supply adds to chemical resistance. Metastasis are growing fast, giving unbelievably low blood pH and high levels of tumor growth driving lactic acid. Pancreas is hard to access from skin for methods of complementary medicine. Injections in pancreas could trigger dangerous pancreatitis. Mainstream is clearly lacking adequate ideas here, help could probably come from holistic approach (cf.
