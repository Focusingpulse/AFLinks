Three Laws of Spiritual Mechanics Thomas.A.Nicewicz@protonmail.com 17 May 2026 The ancients once said, “As above, so below.” Let us recall Newton’s three laws. 1. Inertia. An object in motion stays in motion, and an object at rest stays at rest, unless acted upon by an external or internal force. 2. F = ma. Physical force equals object mass multiplied by object acceleration, equally acceleration amplified by mass. 3. Cause-effect. Equal-opposite reactions. Every action has an equal and opposite reaction. We observe three laws parallel in the higher spiritual dimensions. 1. Inertia. A spirit in motion stays in motion, and a spirit at rest stays at rest, unless acted upon by an external or internal force. 2. F = ma. Spiritual force equals spirit mass (willpower, causal plane matter) multiplied by spirit acceleration, equally acceleration amplified by pure will. 3. Karma. Equal-opposite reactions. Every spiritual action has an equal and opposite spiritual reaction. With great power comes great responsibility. Wisdom must be used wisely. Mass is energy. Physical mass is confined to three spatial dimensions and one time dimension. As it refines, the associated energy accesses higher dimensions: the emotional dimension, the mental dimension, the dimension of intent, the dimension of compassion, the dimension of silent awareness, the dimension of pure love, combinations thereof, emergent dimensions, all connected at one point.
