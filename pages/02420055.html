, Leipzig Translated by Steiner Online Library 21. Who are the Rosicrucians? [ 1 ] For those who study theosophical literature, the name “Rosicrucians” carries a sense of something vague and unclear, as if there were a mystery behind it. Many see it as a term for people who, in the 18th century, dabbled in magic—both possible and impossible. In works by scholars seeking to study the Rosicrucians from a scientific and historical perspective, one senses a benevolent shrug when they write, for example: “There was once a kind of brotherhood that held high ideals and ideas of moral progress.” — Per
