We may here trace the twelve , each fifth note becoming the higher key-note . But the sixth and seventh notes of the scale are discords . For example, in the key of A , the sixth note , F? , is a discord with the second note , B? ; and the seventh note cannot be sounded as G# falling into the eighth , without being a discord with the third note , C? . No octave can be sounded in the Minor Scale , as it has risen into the fifth higher key of E .
