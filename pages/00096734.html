Starting with a brief review of the theory, and how Non-inertial Relativity redefines the notion of mass, the phase space particle trajecto￾ries in D = 2 + 2 are revisited by emphasizing the key difference between a truly U(1, 1)-invariant mass M and the Lorentz-invariant mass m. The derivation of the generalization of the special relativistic expression of E = m(1 − v 2 ) −1/2 (c = 1) to the non-inertial relativistic case follows. This, in turn, leads to the non-inertial relativistic version of an analog of Milgrom’s modified Newtonian dynamics (MOND) law. Subsequent modifications to Newton’s law of motion in the Galilean limit v << 1 are derived. By recurring to the tools of Finsler geometry, we finalize by writing the generalized gravitational field equations in curved phase space in the presence of matter sources, like particles and cosmic strings. As a result, both spacetime and momentum space are curved. We con￾clude with some remarks as to why curved momentum space should play an important role in quantum gravity. In particular, why the fusion of non-inertial relativity with quantum mechanics should lead to a novel for￾mulation of thermal quantum field theory (TQFT), and in turn, should cast some light into the quantization of gravity due to the equivalence principle.
