Decker: "Re: Clem experiment" Hi Folks! I know this is PURE coincidence but I thought some of you might like to know about it and possibly experience it. Here's the story: a friend sent me one of those chain emails which said it wasn't a solicitation for anything. It said to make a wish and think about it as long as it took to scroll line by line down through the entire email and you would get the wish in as many minutes as you were old (something to that effect). It then said to send it to 5 friends which I did not. Well, I blew it off as nothing but happened to think of something that I wanted as opposed to something I needed, in this case a reliable vehicle as mine is acting up and the mechanics can't seem to resolve it. Ok, I had need to look for some papers 10 hours later and in a blank white envelope were two checks from my bank from a credit card consolidation loan back in October. Those were a complete surprise to me and included enough to buy a used vehicle. Now, in NO WAY do I think this was FACTUALLY related to the appearance and 10 hour result of this wishmail....for that to remotely be rational, it would have to be a lottery win or some other source of income completely out of my control. This source was within my control and turned out to be something I had neglected to complete at the time (October 1998). My best excuse for such a glaring error is that I was under a lot of job related stress....and that was very true at that time period. So, I don't want to spam the list with such a file as the wishmail but I do plan to send it out to some friends and ask they report back if any weirdness results within a short time of its receipt. If anyone here wants a copy, I'll be glad to forward it
