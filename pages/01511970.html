What is Electric Current? Really, when we use an ammeter to give us a value in Amperes, or we measure a voltage drop across a current sensing resistor and divide that by the resistance, what is the actual thing we are measuring? At their base function, our measuring equipment is really giving us an after effect of the reality that occurs before hand. The d'Arsonval Movement common in analog ammeters and Galvanometers is the real physical thing that we are measuring as Amperes? Oliver Heaviside really wanted to understand these equations.
