Tanzella is a chemist and experimental researcher known for his long-running work on cold fusion and low energy nuclear reactions (LENR). He is listed in The Worldwide List of Dissident Scientists . Career Tanzella holds a Ph.D. in chemistry from the University of California, Berkeley, and carried out post-doctoral work in electrochemistry. He joined SRI International in Menlo Park, California, in 1986, where he became a program manager and principal investigator in the institute's energy research program.
