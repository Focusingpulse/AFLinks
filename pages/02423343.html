Hydrogen production methods PRODUCTION OF PURE HYDROGEN © Scientific Technical Centre «TATA» D. I. Slovetsky, E. M. Chistov, N. R. Roshan A. V. Topchiev Institute of Petrochemical Synthesis RAS, Joint Stock Company «SYNPLAZ» Leninsky prospect , 29, 119991 Moscow, Russia Fax: 7 (095) 230-22-24, e-mail: slovetsk@ips.ac.ru The main energy vector in the future energetics will be pure hydrogen (>99.9999%), and it will be used primarily in fuel cells. Consumption of cheap pure hydrogen for diverse applications varies in wide range  from some m3/hr for portable clean energy sources, some decades and hundreds m3/hr for clean landed transport and autonomic energy sources, decades and hundreds thousands m3/hr for chemical and large energetic plants. All existing and perspective industrial methods produce technical hydrogen (9599.98%) or gas mixtures, containing from 30 to 95% hydrogen. In any case additional purification or extraction is needed for production of pure hydrogen. The object of this work was the development of a membrane method and optimal modules based on palladium alloy membranes for extraction of pure hydrogen from industrial gaseous mixtures and evaluation the perspective of its industrial using. Specialists of A. V. Topchiev Institute of Petrochemical Synthesis (TIPS) RAS, Joint Stock Company «SYNPLAZ» have developed a design of membrane elements and modules, using flat disc membranes with diameters of 50150 mm from palladium alloy foils, having thickness of 30150 µm. Membranes can be produced from any palladium alloy. The known alloys without silver are most perspective such as Pd93.5%In6%Ru0.5%, Pd60%Cu40%, Pd93%Y7% and series new alloys, developing now jointly with A. A. Baikov Institute of Metallurgy and Materials Sciences (IMET) RAS. These alloys have increased physical and chemical properties and high hydrogen permeability.
