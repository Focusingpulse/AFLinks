Simultaneously, the global atmospheric temperature has varied by 6°C, as can be inferred from the data of the European Project for Ice Coring in Antarctica (EPICA) [2]. The Anthropocene has been defined by Paul Crutzen as the epoch after James Watt's design of the steam engine in 1784 which is characterized by growing man-made global concentrations of the greenhouse gases carbon dioxide and methane [3]. The man-made global warming during the Anthropocene is mainly driven by the greenhouse gas carbon dioxide which is liberated by burning coal and mineral oil. Between AD 1880 and AD 2020 the global atmospheric carbon dioxide concentration has increased from 280 ppm to 410 ppm, that is by a factor of 410/280 = 1.4.
