Nevertheless, it contains two essential theoretical defects. First, it only provides a macroscopic empirical formula without revealing the microscopic physical origin of gravitation, making it impossible to unify gravitation with electromagnetic interaction. Second, it suffers from an inherent circular reasoning loophole: celestial body masses are inversely derived from gravitational orbit data, and these deduced masses are subsequently used to verify the gravitational formula, lacking independent physical verification basis. Furthermore, classical gravitation cannot naturally explain typical observational anomalies, including the perihelion precession of Mercury and the spontaneous orbital decay of low-Earth orbit satellites. This study innovatively defines the celestial body polarization order coefficient λ, which is determined by the internal elemental composition, material density, compactness, thermal motion intensity and material state of celestial bodies, rather than a universal cosmic constant.
