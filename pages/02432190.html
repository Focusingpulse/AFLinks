Modules I would like to see next

· archived # 2,432,190
There is a thread on the forum entitled "What modules would you like to see next?" Energetic Forum http://www.energeticforum.com/paths/72-what-modules-would-you-like-see-next.html It is full of good information, and we encourage you to keep adding to it. We do read it and take all the suggestions into consideration when working on new Modules. Many of the suggestions are now a reality! We have many more in different stages of development. Some are fairly easy to develop and we see good benefits in the test subjects relatively quickly, others are quite complicated and we have work and re-work them over and over until we get at least 75% of the test subjects experiencing desired benefits. Sometimes you will see a new Module come out that was not even on the list. This is likely because it grew out of pre-existing research or as a by-product from related research. One of the most requested Modules has been eyesight. This is a particulary involved request, there are many different types of eyesight challenges. We have been working on it and experiencing only mediocre results to date. However, we will continue, and it will only be a matter of time until we have at least two Modules: One for nearsightedness and farsightedness. We look forward to hearing more of your requests.
