All you can possibly say is that the two pieces of equipment behaved differently. If they are both disipating the same power, and one gets hotter than the other, then the logical conclusion is that one is more efficient than the other at dissipating heat. No other conclusion seems possible, given the facts. " The two experiments are probably not dissipating the same power. The CETI experiment is acting exactly as is expected if there were salt crystals mixed in with the cell outflow. The flow calorimetry numbers would give a bogus high power value from the error of neglecting the heat of fusion of the salt crystals and then much less heat would be rejected in the rest of the system as most of the heat goes into melting the salt crystals instead being rejected into the air.
