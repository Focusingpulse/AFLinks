It has so mystified science, so revolutionized industry and so upset all preconceived ideas of the possible that one is prepared to accept even the wildest prognostications regarding what yet is to be accomplished through the aid of the mysterious fluid. One scarcely ceases to wonder over some new marvel in things electric before something newer and still more marvelous is presented, and so the human mind ever is looking for fresh discoveries, and it takes a big one to secure more than a moment’s attention. But Nikola Tesla, Edison’s twin wizard in matters electrical, at last has struck the direct road to imperishable glory and honor. If the half that is claimed for his latest invention proves true, when rewards are passed around his will not be a tin halo. The only thing to be regretted in connection with his latest achievement is that it will throw many deserving men out of business. But in the crowded state of the ladder of fame it is impossible to reach the top without forcing some one from the rungs, and the majority of people will believe that Tesla has been wise in his selection of victims. It is the doctors who must make way for the electrician’s ascent.
