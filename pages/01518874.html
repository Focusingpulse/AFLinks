University of Utah Research Park 505 Wakara Way Salt Lake City, UT 84108 Dear Mr. Fox: Your November 2, 1990 letter, with an enclosure of a recent issue of Fusion Facts, to Dr. James F. Decker, Acting Director, Office of Energy Research, has been referred to me for reply. The Department of Energy continues to monitor reports of observations attributed to cold fusion. For example, we have been aware of the experimental results reported by the research team in Hawaii for some time. The DOE is receptive, at a modest scale, and through the regular funding process, to high-quality research proposals on cold fusion aimed at an elucidation of the pertinent physical phenomena. This policy was formulated in 1989 from the findings and recommendations from the Cold Fusion Panel of the Energy Research Advisory Board. Results to date on cold fusion are not sufficiently compelling to justify any modification to this policy. Thank you for taking the time to write to the Department of Energy. Please accept my best wishes for your future endeavors. Sincerely, Original Signed tiy Walter M. PolanskY Walter M.
