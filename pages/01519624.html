The zero-point energy can be modeled as an electric flux from the fourth dimension intersecting our three-dimensional space. It manifests as a turbulent, virtual plasma. The observation of self-organizational modes in plasmaa suggests experiments that may cohere the zero-point energy and produce corresponding gravitational anomalies. The suggested experiment uses sharply pulsed, bucking magnetic fields produced within a caduceus coil whose core is a plasma tube resonating in ht eion-acoustic mode.
