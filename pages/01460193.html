It is believed this article is the clearest attempt to explain the basic concepts Keely discovered, developed and worked with and how he used them. After all, Keely was present to directly answer Brinton’s questions. Therefore it behooves us to closely examine this article to discern understanding and hopefully practical use. The terms and phrases in this article are linked to supporting materials within the svpwiki.com where the bulk of the known Keely materials are located. Comments are given immediately below a Brinton quote. These comments are quite important because they represent a synthesis of years of learning Keely’s and Russell’s jargons as well as comparative contemporary concepts and research.
