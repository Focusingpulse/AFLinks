GC2026-STP073 Space weather phenomena observed in experiments on Cubesats of the MSU “Sozvezdie 270” during the heliogeophysical disturbances in 2025-2026 Andrey V. Bogomolov1 , Vitaliy V. Bogomolov1 , Valery E. Eremeev1 , Anatoly F. Iyudin1 , Irina N. Myagkova1 , Vladislav I. Osedlo1 , Sergey I. Svertilov1 1 Lomonosov Moscow State University aabboogg@srd.sinp.msu.ru At present Lomonosov Moscow State University (MSU) has been implementing its own space program, which observes particle fluxes in near-Earth space and electromagnetic transients of various natures, including hard X￾ray and gamma radiation from solar flares. Part of this program is the "Sozvezdie-270" project for the deployment of a constellation of CubeSat nanosatellites. This paper uses data from five CubeSats operating in a near-Earth polar orbit at an altitude of ~500 km since June 27, 2023 (Avion, Monitor-2, Monitor-3, Monitor-4, UTMN2) and data from two CubeSats operating in a similar orbit since November 5, 2024 (Altair, ArcticSat). Each of them is equipped with scintillation detectors of hard X-ray and gamma radiation as well as charged particles, specially developed at the MSU Skobeltsyn Institute of Nuclear Physics (SINP MSU) for such experiments. Data from the MSU CubeSats is available on the SINP MSU space weather website at https://swx.sinp.msu.ru/tools/davisat.php in the form of graphs and tables of monitoring data with a time resolution of up to 1 second.
