Normal Subgroup : In abstract algebra, a normal subgroup (also known as an invariant subgroup or self-conjugate subgroup) is a subgroup that is invariant under conjugation by members of the group of which it is a part. Normal subgroups are important because they (and only they) can be used to construct quotient groups of the given group. They can be realized via simple operations from within the group itself, hence the adjective "inner".
