Purpose of forum, censorship, beliefs, respect

· archived # 1,507,482
A few issues AND questions have come to the attention of the Admins of The Energetic Forum in recent weeks. This post will clarify some of these issues, and perhaps give a more complete understanding of the purpose of The Energetic Forum and how it operates. 1. There are Four (4) individuals that Admin the forum. We all log in under the Username Admin when doing Admin work. 2. The Admins do NOT read all the posts...so sometimes things get posted that should not and we do not see them until someone brings them to our attention. If you see a post that is in violation of the simple forum guidelines please PM Admin or send an email to [email protected] letting us know about it. 3 . The Terms of Use for The Energetic Forum can be viewed here . 4. The Energetic Forum is an arm of Energetic Science Ministries . You can read about Our Mission and our Statement of Faith at the above link. 5. The Four Founding Members of Energetic Science Ministries have some very different views/beliefs in a variety of areas, including spirituality and politics. However, we all wholeheartedly agree on the basics outlined in the Mission and Faith Statements. 6. The Purpose of The Energetic Forum is simple: Energetic Forum is a place for the sharing of positive uplifting ideas, technologies, modalities and discussion. 7. The rules about posting are simple also- Show Respect to all fellow Members on the Forum. (full details can be seen here ) As stated in the Terms of Use , we do not endorse, denounce, criticize, modify or censor* any posts. *The one exception is that we may delete a post if it is in violation of the Forum Guidelines. If we do delete a post it is NOT because we disagree with the subject matter, it is because it is in violation of the Forum Guidelines. 8. There are thousands of posts currently on the Forum that some or all of the Admins completely disagree with. In fact, we might "know" them to be "untrue". Just like you may read a post and just know that it is absolutely false.
