This file courtesy of Dan Davidson. -------------------------------------------------------------------- The Story of the Keely Myth Mr. Julius Moritzen makes an interesting tale in the April COSMOPOLITAN out of "The Extraordinary Story of John Worrell Keely." Mr. Moritzen says that Keely was in truth a genius, as none else than a genius could have kept the world's most eminent scientists guessing for twenty years. His "vibratory generator" was first exhibited to a dozen well-known Philadelphians on November 10, 1874. Even in the minds of the most skeptical there was always at least a doubt in the mystery about Keely's projects until the day of his death, when his house was investigated and the commonplace trickery of his methods exposed.
