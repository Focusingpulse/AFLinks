After hearing Nassim Haramein talk about it and finding John Hutchison's take on it ( as seen here ) this seems like something worthy of further study. Apparently this thing is a giant capacitor and capable of picking up charge from just about anywhere. If anyone is going to replicate this thing I would strongly suggest that keeping the thing shorted when not experimenting with it, as it seems the voltages that can build in it can be lethal. Replications, ideas, theories are all welcome.
