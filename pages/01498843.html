The experiments indicate that a high fraction of the dissolved H is located in grain boundaries (this agrees with previous studies). They show further that the H atoms in the grain boundaries perform a diffusion process which (i) is partially locally restricted and (ii) exhibits jump rates that are up to a factor |100 higher than those of diffusion within the grains.
