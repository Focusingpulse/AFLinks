An alternative to the expanding universe theory Jorma Jormakka Vantaa, Finland jorma.o.jormakka@gmail.com Abstract: The article points out several errors in the expanding space theory that is currently believed to explain the cosmological redshift. The article proposes a tired light theory that gives the correct reduction factor. This tired light theory includes a mechanism that slows down transitions of electrons in the atomic level. The mechanism is not explained: it is only proposed that the fine structure constant changes over long time periods. The article suggests that Cosmic Microwave Background radiation did not come from the Big Bang and that it comes from lost energy of photons traveling through the space as in tired light theories.
