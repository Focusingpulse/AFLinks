Kidney Herbs -- 15 Chinese patent abstracts rexresearch Kidney Herbs CN110694032 -- Traditional Chinese medicine composition for treating gallbladder, kidney and bladder stones and preparation method thereof The invention belongs to the technical field of traditional Chinese medicines, in particular to a traditional Chinese medicine composition for treating gallbladder, kidney and bladder stones and a preparation method thereof. The composition consists of the following active ingredients in parts by weight: 48-55 parts of purple gentian roots, 196-202 parts of endothelium of chicken's gizzard, 18-22parts of common aucklandia roots, 30-32 parts of green tangerine peels, 9-12 parts of toosendan fruit, 38-42 parts of aromatic tumeric roots, 18-22 parts of raw Chinese hawthorn fruit, 18-22 parts oflanceolate starwort roots, 38-42 parts of christina loosestrife herbs, 78-84 parts of christina loosestrife herbs, 38-43 parts of barbary wolfberry fruit, 19-20 parts of immature bitter orange and 20-22 parts of common burreed rhizome. According to the traditional medicine composition, the composition is reasonable and precise in the composition, conforms to the principle of 'monarch, minister, assistant and envoy', conforms to the pathogenesis of gallbladder, kidney and bladder stones, on the one hand, the composition promotes gallbladder to remove stones, detoxifies and relieves inflammation, and on the other hand, the composition promotes qi circulation and relieves depression, so that the composition is suitable for patients with multiple gallbladder, kidney and bladder stones.
