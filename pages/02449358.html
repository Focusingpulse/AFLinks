Here are some sources and contacts; -------------------------- Carnegie Foundation (possibilities with this one) How to contact; http://www.carnegie.org/contact.htm Under Carnegie's will, grants must benefit the people of the United States, although up to 7.4 percent of the funds may be used to benefit the people of some countries that are or have been members of the British overseas Commonwealth. Carnegie Corporation makes grants primarily to academic institutions and to national and regional nonprofit organizations for projects that have potential for national or international impact. Those that are too local in scope are likely to be turned down. Grants of more than $25,000 must be approved by the board of trustees upon the recommendation of program staff members. Those of $25,000 or less are made on the approval of the president and reported to the board.
