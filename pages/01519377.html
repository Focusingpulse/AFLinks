Overview "ATMOSPHERIC ELECTROSTATICS" by Lars Wåhlin contains chapters on Ben Franklin, lightning, ball lightning, lightning protection, fairweather electricity, and thundercloud charging. The electrochemical model of thundercloud charging is proposed and is shown to be supported by simple experiments.
