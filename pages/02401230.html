kimo2026-main061 Economic Relations between Russia and Myanmar: Strategic Interests and Contemporary Collaboration Kyaw Y. Aung Ponencia invitada kyawye63459@gmail.com Myanmar and Russia have enjoyed strong and good relations for over 77 years, sharing common views on global issues and regional peace and stability. This study focuses on the transition of trade relations and investment opportunities between the two countries from a normal relationship to a strategic partnership. Since the signing of the bilateral trade agreement in 1995, Myanmar and Russia have considered trade and investment, as well as cooperation in regional energy development. The two countries recently signed a MOU on investment cooperation in a special economic zone to develop energy in the region. The two countries have established an intergovernmental commission on trade and economic cooperation, direct flights, the opening of consulates, and the historic sending of Myanmar workers to Russian companies, demonstrating the deepening of economic integration. The introduction of visa-free travel and the adoption of the Russian Mir payment system further facilitate people-to-people contacts and economic exchanges, reflecting the strategic partnership between the two countries. In the 19th century, bilateral relations were noticeable by routine interactions; by the 21st century, they had advanced into strategic partnerships.
