School and University I I On November 21, 1898, Professor Wilhelm Förster gave a lecture: " School and University in the Light of the New Conditions of Life ." This lecture was the first public manifestation of the efforts of a number of men from all parts of Germany and Austria to give higher education the position it deserved among the sciences. The center of these efforts must naturally be Berlin
