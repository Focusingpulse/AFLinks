He showed us video tapes of these effects, such as levitation, matter 'warping', and more. At the conference John had some metal samples on display which he allowed everybody to examine. These were the most extraodinary lumps of metal I'd ever seen. Some looked as though they had been exploded from within, while maintaining the most beautifully delicate crystaline structures. There was a block of aluminium that had wood inside it, as though a Star Trek transporter had combined the two sustances. Perhaps one of the most impressive pieces was a bar, I think is was tungsten, that was tied into a knot. All these samples, John claimed, were the result of interaction with the feilds emenating from his setup. Anyway, to the point. While examining John's various metal samples it occured to me that some of the samples shapes did not look like the product of random interaction with strange fields - they looked as though there was design to the randomness, as though a childs mind was given the power to transform and combine the impossible. John also told an interesting story of a demonstration he performed for some area of the military, I believe. The demonstration went well and all were impressed, so the story went, but it was later found that equipement had'nt been plugged into the mains! Spooky stuff.
