According to Nikola Tesla patent: Tesla Patent 454,622 - System of Electric Lighting A minimum of 20,000 volts and 15-50khz pulse will light bulbs using one wire. The potential and frequency of the voltage is great enough to excite the gas in the vacuum of a bulb. Much higher voltages and frequencies will also work. It does explain many of the effects we have witnessed recently from other peoples experiments. This means all these bulbs can be lit: 1. Incandescant 2. Fluorescent 3. Modified CFL 4. Neon 5. Metal Halide 6. High Pressure Sodium The gas is in a vacuum and cannot escape. There is no current running through the light, so in theory the bulb lasts forever unless it is smashed. The device to power it will only use milliamps and can be self powered using a solar panel and one of the many circuits that is currently being used, eg joule thief, ringer etc.... So far I have tried 2. Fluorescent 3. Modified CFL 5. Metal Halide 6. High Pressure Sodium All bulbs produced light using one wire!!!!!!!
