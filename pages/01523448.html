Michael Jefferson Lawrence Residence Ipswich, Suffolk, United Kingdom Known for Theories of everything , Maldwyn Centre for Theoretical Physics , zero point energy , quantum mechanics , quarks , G , time , big bang , electron , electromagnetism Michael Jefferson Lawrence is a British theoretical physicist based in Ipswich, Suffolk, who writes under the banner of the Maldwyn Centre for Theoretical Physics. His work is centred on dimensional analysis and the construction of unit systems, from which he argues that the fundamental constants and the observable properties of matter stand in simple algebraic relationships that conventional SI units obscure. Work Adjusted Planck units Lawrence's method is to rebuild the Planck system with the factors of 2π removed and the gravitational constant absorbed into the base units, producing what he calls Adjusted and then Double Adjusted Planck Units. In " A Toolkit for Improving the Understanding of Relationships between Physical Parameters based on Adjusted SI units and a New Planck Unit Framework " (2009) he sets out the framework; in " A Reinterpretation of the von Klitzing and Josephson constants " (2009) he applies it to the two quantum electrical standards, arguing that they emerge as natural maxima of resistance and inverse magnetic flux, and that many parameters can then be expressed as simple powers of h and c alone. He supports the construction with a dimensional grading in which every fundamental constant except c carries zero net dimension. " Interpreting Why Triple-Adjusted Planck Unit Observable Fundamental Properties Are Simple Powers " (2013) extends the same programme a further step. Velocity addition A separate 2013 paper, " Accepted Relativistic Addition of Velocities and Energies are Only Two-Variable Versions of a New Infinite Variables Formula ", argues that the standard relativistic velocity- and energy-addition formulae are special cases of a more general expression admitting arbitrarily many terms.
