Together with Catt and David S. Walton he formed the "Catt, Davidson and Walton" partnership whose late-1970s papers and books challenged the conventional textbook treatment of Maxwell's displacement current. Biography Little biographical information about Davidson has been published. He became associated with Ivor Catt and Dr. David S. Walton during the 1970s, when the three worked jointly on the theory and practice of high-speed digital logic and on the electromagnetic behaviour of capacitors and transmission lines.
