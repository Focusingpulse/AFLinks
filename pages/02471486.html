Mrs. Vanderbilt’s guests were Mr. and Mrs. F. Egerton Webb and Mr. and Mrs. Karrick Riggs. Mrs. P. Cooper Hewitt and Mrs. John Magee were with Mr. and Mrs. Henry Clews and Miss Anna Sands was with Mr. and Mrs. Ogden Mills. Mrs. Clarence G. Dinsmore had as her guests in the Astor box Mrs. Jordan L. Mott, Nikola Tesla and Robert Perkins. Mr. and Mrs. Henry S. Lehr and Miss Katherine Steward were guests of Mr. and Mrs. J. Stewart Barney. With Mr. and Mrs. Lloyd S. Bryce were Mr. and Mrs. Augustus Jay and Mr. and Mrs. J. Sergeant Cram. Mr. and Mrs. Cornelius Vanderbilt had as their guests Mr. and Mrs. F. Gray Griswold and Mrs. Whitney Warren. With Mrs. John Jacob Astor were Mr. and Mrs. W. B. Osgood Field and Mr. and Mrs. Allan A. Robbins. Clarence H. Mackay’s guests were Mr. and Mrs. Edmund L. Baylies and Mr. and Mrs. Charles De Loosey Oelrichs. Mr. and Mrs. George L. Rives were with Mr. and Mrs. Luther Kountze. Mr. and Mrs. Orme Wilson were with Mr. and Mrs. Francis C. Bishop and Mr. and Mrs. Orme Wilson, Jr. Among others in the audience were Mr. and Mrs. August Belmont, Mr. and Mrs. C. O’Donnell Iselin, Mr. and Mrs. Lewis Iselin, Mrs. Frederick Pearson, Miss Eleanor H. Green, Miss Lesley Pearson,
