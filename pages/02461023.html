By utilizing an inverse cosmological luminosity equation grounded in pure three-dimensional spatial expansion (1 + z)³, this model enables the empirical testing and falsification of cosmic distances without the introduction of arbitrary free parameters or unobservable entities. Through an elementary kinematic analysis of the cosmological redshift (z)—empirically defined as the index of the average expansion velocity during the total photon travel time (Vm = c · z) —we demonstrate that global Type Ia Supernovae (SN Ia) observational datasets (including recent NOIRLab/DESI Hubble diagrams) mathematically signify a continuous and progressive deceleration of spatial expansion from primordial epochs to the present day. The ESQ model successfully resolves the deep epistemological and thermodynamic paradoxes of orthodox cosmology by strictly adhering to the First Law of Thermodynamics (Energy Conservation) and rigorously satisfying the methodological criteria of Occam’s Razor. The intrinsic geometric foundations of this theoretical framework and the conceptual transition from the standard mathematical metric to the physics of space quanta were formally delineated by the Author in the foundational paper titled "From Apparent to Real Universe”.
