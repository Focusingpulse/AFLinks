[1] These indices can act as predictors of significant climate change. A total of 27 indices of climate extremes were calculated. The calculation was based on daily values ​of surface air temperature and precipitation according to ERA5 reanalysis data with a grid step of 0.25°×0.25° from 1979 to 2014. The interannual variation was obtained with a test at the 5% significance level. The difference between the index values ​averaged over two fifteen-year periods was calculated.
