Ultrasonic Welding

· archived # 1,468,719
Ultrasonic welding is an industrial technique whereby high-frequency ultrasonic acoustic vibrations are locally applied to workpieces being held together under pressure to create a solid-state weld. It is commonly used for plastics, and especially for joining dissimilar materials. In ultrasonic welding , there are no connective bolts, nails, soldering materials, or adhesives necessary to bind the materials together.
