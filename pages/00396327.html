Introduction 16 There have been a number of proposed albedo solutions [1-5] to reduce climate change. The main problem 17 with the reflectivity (albedo) solution is that it remains relatively unknown and historically it has been 18 overshadowed by CO2 concerns. Furthermore, since Global Warming (GW) has come to the forefront, there 19 has been widespread disregard for albedo controls compared with CO2 legislation and other efforts. This lack 20 of controls has increased the strengths overtime of these historically known additional forcing problems that 21 also have needed attention. By assessing GHG-albedo interactions for all forcing issues and using historical 22 information, we illustrate why albedo solutions are optimum compared to CO2 methods in climate control. 23 We also assess the GHG-albedo interactive strength. Therefore, it is concluded that albedo methods and 24 solutions to reduce climate change pose much less risk in their ability to prevent the tipping point when 25 compared to CO2 reduction methods.
