Fermi 38, 00044 Frascati (Roma), Italia (ricevuto il 29 Aprile 1989) Summary. -- We report on neutron emission in palladium and titanium electrolitically charged with deuterium. The detection of neutrons is observed after thermal treatment of the electrode. In the hypothesis that neutrons came from cold fusion processes, we estimate a fusion rate as high as 1.3-10 -21 fusions/deuteron pair/second. PACS 64.75 - Solubility, segregation and mixing. PACS 28.20 - Neutron physics. Since the first announcements (1,2) that cold nuclear fusion is possible when deuterium atoms are electrochemically inserted into palladium (Pd) or titanium (Ti) lattices, there have been many attempts all around the world in trying to reproduce those results. Until now confirmations are very few and quite confused except the very recent experiment reported by Scaramuzzi et al. (3). The reasons are that all the scientists facing the problem obtain, as first result of deuterium charging process on Pd or Ti, a very low neutron emission rate not very far from the background level.
