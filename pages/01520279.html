Title Gravity Particles and the Strong Force Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Miguel Kovac Keywords gravity, light speed , strong force, wavelength Published 2012 Journal Proceedings of the NPA Volume 9 No. of pages 4 Pages 277-280 Abstract Is shown a graphical model for gravitational particles to determine the gravitational waves wavelength. Wavelength obtained graphically let to calculate the gravity acceleration due to a body on any point of the space measured from its center. The objective is to determine the gravity acceleration, the wavelength of the gravitational wave, speed of the wave and its specific internal energy. To do the study are used formulas for acceleration and velocity obtained from hypothesis and well sustained experiment. Are shown results tables to compare the results for gravity acceleration obtained in this model, the results of Newton's equation and the results for latitude 45? taken from a physics book. Is concluded that gravitational force is a strong force and gravitational waves speed is equal to the light speed in the center of the earth and diminish while move away from its center. Overview Gravity Particles and the Strong Force is a four-page paper by Miguel Kovac of Acarigua, Venezuela, published in the Proceedings of the NPA volume 9 (pages 277–280) after presentation at the 19th Natural Philosophy Alliance Conference in July 2012, and given earlier as a talk in August 2011. The paper proposes a geometric model in which gravity is carried by particles flowing inward toward the Earth's centre, and derives from it a formula for gravitational acceleration at any distance. Kovac describes the construction as "inspired from the Reynolds apparatus", and the vocabulary throughout is drawn from fluid mechanics: a Reynolds number, a kinematic viscosity, a threshold speed at equilibrium. Three relations carry the argument. The wavelength of a gravity particle at distance r = R e + h from the centre is taken to be the particle's diameter
