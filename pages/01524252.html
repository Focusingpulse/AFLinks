Title The True Meaning of Einstein's Relativity Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Robert F Beck Keywords relativity , mass, particle physics, quantum mechanics , string theory, Higgs boson Published 2012 Journal Proceedings of the NPA Volume 9 No. of pages 6 Pages 27-32 Abstract This paper examines the extent to which an interpretation of Einstein's relativity suggested in The Special Theory of Reality might provide clearer answers in areas such as gravity, quantum mechanics, string theory and particle physics generally. The extent to which the simple concepts of rings of tiny particles and rings of such particles becoming spirals suggest explanations and solutions in the area of particle physics specifically is explored and explanations suggested for all four fundamental forces. Most significantly, a description and unrealized properties of the Higgs boson are presented, and explanations are suggested for the very nature of quantum mechanics and string theory in the context of what is suggested as the true meaning of relativity. An explanation for the significance of specific frequencies in new energy and health technologies is also suggested. Overview This paper develops the author's "Special Theory of Reality", a picture in which every particle is built from rings and spirals of much smaller particles, and in which mass is a consequence of spin. Its central proposal is that general relativity should be read as saying that mass produces curvature of motion rather than curvature of space — and, crucially, that this curvature is directional : a spinning body curves its motion in the plane of spin, while motion along the spin axis is facilitated by a corresponding reduction of mass in that direction, tending towards straight-line Newtonian motion.
