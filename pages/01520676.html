Reiter reports beam-split tests with gamma rays that he holds contradict the assumption that energy is always both emitted and absorbed in quanta. His experiments, performed with both matter and light, indicate to him that conservation is preserved only if absorption is continuous and emission alone is quantised which in turn implies a pre-loaded state in matter. He treats wave-particle duality not as an irreducible feature of nature but as a riddle left unsolved since 1905 by problematic assumptions and biased interpretation. Read the full articl
