EDITOR'S SUMMARY Scientists in America claim to have a solution to the handling of high-level radioactive wastes. "Independent confirmation has come from one of Europe's most respected nuclear laboratories, the Italian government CISE, near Milan, who say they have proved that it is possible to transmute radioactive waste into non-radioactive titanium and copper." The article states that "Big Science" says that the process is impossible. However, both national laboratories and reputable universities have confirmed the American results. Some of the hot-fusion scientists are dismissing the American report as nothing but medieval alchemy. For those who are serious they can get a test cell for $5,000 with a money-back guarantee! The article cites Trenergy, Inc. as the contact for further information and lists the Trenergy and INE email and WebSite for further information.
