Puthoff 10JUL95 Introduction For the 3rd series of tests, we modified our Potapov device (a Yusmar-1) by adding a "bypass" line that connects the vortex chamber to the outlet end of the cavitation tube and a diameter reduction at the outlet end of the cavitation tube. Both of these features can be seen in various photographs of the Potapov device (i.e. in Infinite Energy #2 and on John Logajan's web page). We used the photographs and information obtained during Chris Tinsley's visit to Moldavia to design these modifications. As of this report, we have tried one diameter for the bypass line and two different diameters for the reduced diameter outlet. None of the new tests show any detectable over- unity performance from the Potapov device. Apparatus Since this is our third report, a brief review of the apparatus is in order. The Potapov device is mounted in a vertical orientation (with the vortex chamber on top) above an open 55 gallon barrel. The outlet, which points straight down, is positioned so it is submerged a few inches under the water level in the barrel to prevent air entrainment.
