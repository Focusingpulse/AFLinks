Borchardt takes the large-scale structure map produced by the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument at Kitt Peak a five-year survey of more than 47 million galaxies and quasars rendered as a 3D map centred on Earth and reads it against the theory it was gathered to support. What he finds notable is what the image does not show: any sign of the expansion it is meant to record. He presents the feathery web of clustered galaxies as a picture of an infinite universe instead.
