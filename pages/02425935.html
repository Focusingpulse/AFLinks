Article A Paleogenomic Reconstruction of the Deep Population History of the Andes Graphical Abstract Authors Nathan Nakatsuka, Iosif Lazaridis, Chiara Barbieri, ..., Bastien Llamas, David Reich, Lars Fehren-Schmitz Correspondence nathan_nakatsuka@hms.harvard.edu (N.N.), reich@genetics.med.harvard.edu (D.R.), lfehrens@ucsc.edu (L.F.-S.) In Brief Genome-wide data from 89 ancient humans illuminates the changes to the genetic landscape in the Central Andes over 9,000 years, revealing large-scale gene flow and cosmopolitan societies in the Tiwanaku and Inca polities.
