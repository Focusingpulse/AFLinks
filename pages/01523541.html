It is Volume I of the revised and expanded edition of Larson's The Structure of the Physical Universe , and the primary statement of his Reciprocal System of theory. Overview The book sets out the basic postulates of the Reciprocal System — in which the physical universe is composed of nothing but motion, and space and time are the two reciprocal aspects of that motion.
