Jacques Bars De Tavannes …… Herr Reiss De Retz …… Signor Viviani De Meru, Maurevert …… Signor Dufriche In the performance of “Les Huguenots,” the first of the season, at the Metropolitan Opera House, last night, M. Edouard de Reszke was the towering figure. His Marcel is familiar from many previous hearings, but in his representation it gained in breadth and dignity from the fact that the surroundings were not as “large” as in former years. A perfect picture of the bluff Huguenot campaigner, he sang and acted in a manner that brought out the full significance of this noble rôle. Signor De Marchi’s Raoul was followed with interest. Physically it lacked in stature and consequently in heroic impressiveness. Vocally it had much to commend it, although the singer relies for his effects
