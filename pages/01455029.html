When the string was struck with the hammer of a pianoforte , whose contact with the string endured for 3/7ths of the period of vibration of the fundamental tone , the intensity of the same tone was 9. In this case the second harmonic was nearly quenched. When, however, the duration of contact was diminished to 3/20ths of the period of the fundamental , the intensity of the harmonic rose to 357; While, when the string was sharply struck with a very hard hammer, the intensity mounted to 505, or to more than quintuple that of the fundamental .
