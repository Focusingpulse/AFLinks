It develops the Aether Physics Model (APM) 1 INTRODUCTION 1 Abstract This paper develops a saturation-layer interpretation of galactic black holes within the Aether Physics Model (APM) and Quantum Measurement Units (QMU). Rather than treating singularities as points of infinite density, the model proposes that gravitational collapse terminates when local Aether occupancy reaches a maximum allowable state. The limiting density is determined by the Aether maximum mass and quantum volume. The resulting structure is a finite saturation layer whose idealized boundary may be represented as a saturation surface. The Bipolar Trumpet Coil Model is reinterpreted as the rotational geometry assumed by Aether units as occupancy approaches unity. Larger galactic centers do not require densities beyond the Aether limit; additional mass is accommodated by recruiting additional saturated Aether units and increasing the extent of the trumpet-coil layer. The paper introduces an occupancy coordinate, derives the maximum-density boundary condition, relates saturation geometry to QMU curl structure, and provides an appendix defining the concept of a universal closure coordinate without asserting that the present model completes such a program.
