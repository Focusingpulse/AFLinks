Baumgartner (United States) Publication history 19781987 Frequency Quarterly, latterly irregular Energy Unlimited was an American magazine on unconventional energy published by Walter P. Baumgartner of Albuquerque, New Mexico, between 1978 and 1987. Its subjects were magnetism, water and vortex phenomena, and the cosmologies of Viktor Schauberger and Walter Russell , on both of whom Baumgartner wrote and lectured.
