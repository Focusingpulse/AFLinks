Baumgartner (United States) Publication history 1978–1987 Frequency Quarterly, latterly irregular Energy Unlimited was an American magazine on unconventional energy published by Walter P. Baumgartner of Albuquerque, New Mexico, between 1978 and 1987. Its subjects were magnetism, water and vortex phenomena, and the cosmologies of Viktor Schauberger and Walter Russell , on both of whom Baumgartner wrote and lectured. History The magazine began in 1978 as a quarterly, with four issues that year, and continued at that rate into the early 1980s before becoming irregular: issues 17 and 18 were published together as a single number for spring and summer 1984, and the final three issues appeared one a year in 1985, 1986 and 1987. Little independent documentation of the magazine has been located beyond the issues themselves and Baumgartner's own later writing. Its circulation, print run and the circumstances of its closure are not recorded in any source consulted here, and nothing is asserted about them. Holdings in the Aether Physics archive An offline research archive assembled by John Reed and surveyed in September 2026 holds all 21 numbered issues , 1 to 21, covering the magazine's whole run from January–March 1978 to 1987. Issues 17 and 18 are a single combined number, as published. The documents are not hosted on this wiki.
