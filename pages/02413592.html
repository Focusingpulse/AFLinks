Richard Wagner and the Spiritual World [ 1 ] The Theosophical worldview seeks to deepen our spiritual life, not arbitrarily, but because it aims to satisfy the deeply felt longing of our time. That the Theosophical worldview is not arbitrary can be seen when compared with other spiritual movements. [ 2 ] Today we want to examine a cultural movement in art in relation to theosophy. We want to talk about Richard Wagner. Richard Wagner always emphasized that he wanted to serve an ideal that could permeate people just as any religious idea
