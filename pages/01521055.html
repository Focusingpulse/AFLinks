Proceedings of the 1986 International Tesla Symposium Book Author Steven R Elswick (editor) Published September 1986 Publisher High Energy Enterprises ISBN 9992617195 Proceedings of the 1986 International Tesla Symposium is the published record of the 1986 International Tesla Symposium , the second of the Colorado Springs symposia run by the International Tesla Society . It was edited by Steven R Elswick and issued by High Energy Enterprises in September 1986. The volume is a non-peer-reviewed collection, and its range is characteristic of the series: biographical and historical papers on Nikola Tesla sit alongside detailed engineering analysis of coils and resonators, atmospheric and ball-lightning physics, and speculative work on energy extraction from the vacuum.
