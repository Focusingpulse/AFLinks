As far as I know, the Scott Stones reports could be a hoax, but there WAS a short burst of reports about mysterious stones uncovered in Florida while excavating for new construction. I don't know if this is related. Reports of an ancient culture which was at its peak while most of mankind was still wielding stone axes and living in caves means this culture must have had an understanding of mathematics and possibly technology well before what orthodox records would have us believe. The reports have to do with the recent discovery in Florida of stones which appear to be very ancient and possibly of Egyptian construction. Unfortunately, with the pollution of some new age fantasies, there is a lot of confusion about Atlantis and other lost continents. Crystals, pyramids, more recently time travel and dimensional shifts, etc. have been woven into the Atlantean stories. The book, even if fantasy, describes vailxi (flying machines) that used a natural force the author calls Navaz (the dark side of nature). These flying ships used a redirection of navaz/aether to produce lift and thrust. They could travel in air, underwater and apparently in space without problems.
