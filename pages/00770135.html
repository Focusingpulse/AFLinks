Emails: roberto.sanmillan@urjc.es, eduardo.morgado@urjc.es 4 5 Abstract 6 In this study, we propose and examine two procedures for constructing intervals that 7 capture the uncertainty associated with determining the effective number of components 8 in model selection problems or the shrinkage parameters in a regularization problem. 9 The output of these methods is an interval (defined by two integer bounds) representing 10 plausible values for the number of components and/or shrinkage parameters. Notably, 11 these methods do not rely on the availability of a likelihood function, making them broadly 12 applicable across various domains, such as regression, classification, feature and/or 13 order selection, clustering, and dimensionality reduction. These techniques leverage the 14 geometric properties of the error curve to construct intervals. Extensive experiments 15 on both synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrated the effectiveness and practical 16 utility of the proposed procedures.
