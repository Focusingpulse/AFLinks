Bob Lazar

· archived # 1,456,068
Bob Lazar discussing what he originally thought about the captured UFO at Groom Lake, otherwise known as Area 51 and S4. From SAUCER on TT - “Bob Lazar is seen by many as the man who blew the lid off the government’s UFO secrets. In 1989, he revealed that he had worked at a covert facility called S4, just south of Area 51, where he witnessed and studied advanced spacecraft of non-human origin. According to Lazar, at least nine flying saucers were hidden in hangars built into the desert mountains, each powered by a mysterious element later known as Element 115—that allowed them to bend gravity and move in ways no human technology could replicate.”
