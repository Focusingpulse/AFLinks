The nonlinear nature of the field causes it to exhibit properties now atrtributable to "matter." It follows that elementary particles may be considered to be electromagnetic field patterns. If particles are electromagnitic field patterns, then the forces affecting them are all electromagnetic.The forces discussed are the Lorentz force, gravity and the nuclear or stong force.
