Motion Tracking with Raspberry Pi ─ Saarang Srinivasan National Public School Koramangala, Bengaluru Intern at Mistral Solutions Pvt Ltd, Bengaluru saarangsrini35@gmail.com Motion Tracking with Raspberry Pi 1 Abstract The aim of this project is to detect the motion in a video and accordingly follow the motion. This program uses python and contour detection to find the moving objects in the video and determine which direction we must move in order to follow the motion. We move the camera in the direction of the motion in order to follow it. 2 Table of Contents 1.1 Introduction 3 1.2 Block Diagram 4 1.3 Features and Applications 4 2.
