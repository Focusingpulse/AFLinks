Ching-Chuan Su Residence Hsinchu, Taiwan Nationality Taiwan Known for Local-Ether Model , Quantum Electromagnetics , Aether , Relativity , Electrodynamics Scientific career Fields Electrical Engineering , Physics , Electrodynamics Institutions Department of Electrical Engineering, National Tsing Hua University, Hsinchu, Taiwan Ching-Chuan Su (also published as C.-C. Su ) was a Taiwanese professor of electrical engineering at National Tsing Hua University in Hsinchu, Taiwan, best known for the local-ether model of electromagnetic wave propagation and its corollary theory, Quantum Electromagnetics . In a series of papers published between 2000 and 2006, and in his 2008 book Quantum Electromagnetics , he set out a fully worked alternative to Einstein's Special Relativity and General Relativity founded on the classical principles of absolute time and three-dimensional Euclidean space. He died in about 2007, and the website he maintained for the theory at National Tsing Hua University has since been taken down. Biography Su was a member of the Department of Electrical Engineering at National Tsing Hua University in Hsinchu, Taiwan; that affiliation appears on his papers, including his 2001 European Physical Journal C article. Much of his published output was in mainstream electrical-engineering and physics venues — the Journal of Electromagnetic Waves and Applications , IEEE Transactions on Magnetics , Electronics Letters , the European Journal of Physics , Europhysics Letters , Foundations of Physics Letters , and the IEEE Antennas and Propagation Society International Symposium — together with presentations at American Physical Society March and April meetings in 2000, 2001 and 2002. He maintained a website devoted to the theory at qem.ee.nthu.edu.tw, which collected his papers under the heading "Quantum Electromagnetics".
