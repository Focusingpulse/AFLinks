The Demi currency is being used specifically in the Gaspesie region of northern Quebec and is actually accepted by a number of local businesses. The founders of the currency have specified that the half dollar notes are worth half of whatever value the original note was intended to be. For example, half of a $10 bill is worth $5, while half of a $20 bill is worth $10. The idea for the Demi formed from a conversation that Gaspesie resident Martin Zibeau had with his friends who came from areas of Europe where alternative currencies were being used in place of the Euro. The idea behind using pieces of already existing currency was used because state currency is difficult to counterfeit, making it a secure currency. �We joked about [how] the Canadian dollar is pretty secure," Zibeau said.
