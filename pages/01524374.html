Valery P. Dmitriyev Residence Moscow, Russia Nationality Russian Valery P. Dmitriyev is a Russian theoretical physicist based in Moscow, affiliated in his published work with Lomonosov Moscow State University. Since the early 1990s he has pursued a single programme: the construction of explicit mechanical analogies, drawn from the mechanics of continuous media, for the equations of quantum physics and electromagnetism. Work Dmitriyev's substratum is what the nineteenth century called the vortex sponge — an ideal fluid threaded in all directions by thin, straight vortex filaments — and his method is to identify the equations governing disturbances of that medium with the equations of physics. Three strands recur across his papers. The elastic model of the physical vacuum. Treating the vacuum as a solid-like medium whose displacement field corresponds to the magnetic vector potential, he recovers the structure of electromagnetism from elasticity. This line was published in Mechanics of Solids in 1992 and developed in Nuovo Cimento A in 1998. Vortex filaments and the quantum equations. A helical disturbance on a vortex filament, evolving under the local induction approximation of classical vortex dynamics, obeys an equation of Schrödinger form; adding the filament's elastic response produces a Klein–Gordon-like equation. On this reading the wave function is the position vector of a curve rather than an abstract amplitude. Mechanical Analogy for the Wave-Particle: Helix on a Vortex Filament and Mechanical Interpretation of the Klein-Gordon Equation , both published in Apeiron in 2001, set this out. Turbulent advection and relativistic effects. In a series of papers from 1993 onwards he argues that the effects usually attributed to relativity — and the mass–energy relation in particular — can be recovered from the behaviour of discontinuities advected in a turbulent fluid, work summarised in The Substratum Origin of Relativistic Effects and E=mc2 in the Turbulent Aether
