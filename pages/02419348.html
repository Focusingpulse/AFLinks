[17] THE KAHUNA AND THE SCIENTIST David Bray, Adept of the Hawaiian Mystery Tradition, Takes Dr. Andrija Puharich, Author of “The Sacred Mushroom” and “Beyond Telepathy” , Through the Veil. In late May and early June your Director and his wife made their first return trip to Hawaii in seven years. The most fruitful contact of the trip was a two-hour interview with David “Daddy” Bray in his Honolulu headquarters with his good friend, devoted student, and business manager, Gini Cameron, also present, along with another Hawaiian student of the Mysteries. The interview was recorded. Following is that portion used in our illustrated talk on the Hawaiian trip, given Friday night, July 3, 1964, at our Borderland Convention at Harmony Grove. “Our first class was really wonderful,” said Daddy Bray. “After about half an hour of lesson, we light one candle; then we meditate. All the people in the class are sitting down, looking; of course I look too, -- see what they get -- what -- to know if they are improving. Some can sit there all through the class and can’t see nothing. Well, you know there is something wrong.” “Do they see clairvoyantly, after a few lessons?” asked Mrs. Crabb. “Oh, yes,” replied Daddy, “we have thirteen lessons.
