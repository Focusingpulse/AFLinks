Decker: "Re: Question about the aether" Next message: Robert: "Re: Bedini motor" --------------------------------------------------------- BOB'S DENTAL AWARENESS PAGE http://members.aol.com/_ht_a/Rjj5019/index.html OCCURRENCE OF CAVITATIONS - CAVITAT Bob Jones, the inventor of the CAVITAT (an ultrasound insturment designed to detect and image cavitations) found cavitations of various sizes and severity in approximately 94% of several thousand wisdom teeth sites scanned. He also found cavitations under or located near 100% of root canal teeth scanned in both males and females of various ages from several different geographic areas of the United States. Note: Cavitations (jaw infections) can be entirely painless, or they can cause referred pain elsewhere in the body, such as in the lower back.
