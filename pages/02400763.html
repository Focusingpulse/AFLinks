GC2024-STP062 Space weather effects on low altitude satellite orbits Sergey Apatenkov1 1 St Petersburg University apatenkov@mail.ru We discuss space weather parameters that can cause upper atmosphere heating and satellite drag. We compare linear regression and NARX neural network models for possible forecast of satellite drag. Using numerous Starlink orbit database and their drag we can further construct the upper atmosphere density models.
