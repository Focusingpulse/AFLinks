Overview The pamphlet sets out Rendle's proposed physical cause of gravitation — an attempt to explain gravity in mechanical terms rather than treating it as an unexplained fundamental force or as spacetime curvature. About the author Alfred Bernard Rendle was an independent author who proposed a mechanical account of gravitation.
