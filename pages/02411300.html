USPA - Stanley Hotel - 1994 Conference

· archived # 2,411,300
( click to enlarge ) United States Psychotronics Association From Toby Grotz … Tom Bearden of Smart and Smarter meets Jim Carey of Dumb and Dumber. In the summer of 1994, a group of scientists, engineers, shade tree mechanics and dissident physicists gathered at a retreat at the Stanley Hotel in the Colorado Rockies. We had settled in for a few days preceding the 1994 International Symposium on New Energy. All of a sudden there was a ruckus and commotion as the actors, directors and film crew showed up on set for the movie Dumb Dumber. Later that night as the film crew mixed with the conference speakers at the bar, both groups learned of the reasons for the presence of the other. A good time was had by all and by the end of the night it was agreed that there had been a convergence of the Dumb Dumber and the Smart Smarter. Such was the camaraderie that by the next morning a group photo was taken on the steps of the Stanley featuring the stars from both groups of the Dumb Dumber and Smart Smarter crowd, Jim Carey front and center in bathrobe and Tom Bearden , left in sunglasses. I’ll leave it to the reader to identify the rest of the crowd. Those were the days my friend... I was there. I think I'm at dead center but cannot see my face, blue shirt. Victor Hansen just above me. Ben Iverson and his wife, Ida, are behind Bearden . Some of those are no longer with us. [ Dale Pond ] ( click to enlarge )
