The asymmetrical capacitor is composed of two aluminum electrodes : - the biggest electrode, placed at the top, is an arcuate electrode connected to the positive output of the HV power supply, - the smallest electrode, placed at the bottom, is a cylindrical armature connected to the negative output of the HV power supply. Three balsa wood legs maintain a gap between the two electrodes. The Brown-Bahnson Saucer ( BBS v1.0 ) is hanged through the thin copper wire which carries the positive High Voltage. A thin balsa wood stick maintains the negative wire connection in the main axis of the device so as not to disturb the shape of the electric field around the device ( see the photo below ). The weight of the BBS v1.0 is 4 g When the HV power supply is swiched on, the BBS jumps upward quickly and translates at a high speed.
