KEELY MOTOR STOCK IN THE ASSETS. APRIL 24, 1886 Page 8/3 KEELY MOTOR STOCK IN THE ASSETS. The schedules of the stock brokerage firm of Brown & Anderton were filed in the Court of Common Pleas yesterday. They show liabilities, $111,543 45; nominal assets, $60,485 62, and actual assets, $24,578 15. Among their nominal assets are many uncollectible claims and some stock of little or no actual value, including 100 shares of Keely motor stock. The largest creditor. Is N. Nicoll, whose claim is for $85,299 87, partially secured by the value of Ralph L. Anderton's seat in the Stock Exchange.
