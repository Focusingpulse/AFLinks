"One of the purposes of this magazine is to advance the new concept of matter originated by Professor Twining, a mathematical genius who has devoted six decades of thought and experiment to his subject. Another purpose is to announce results of research into the potentialities of of the mind." March-April issue includes Evolution of the Solar System - Telepathy - Formula for Happiness - Changing the Future - Evolution is Progress .
