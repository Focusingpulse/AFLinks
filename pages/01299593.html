Upon sounding the note B flat on the sympathetic transmitter , the sphere having that chord of mass rose slowly to the top of the chamber, the positive end of the wire having been attached, which connected the covered jar with the transmitter . The same result followed the sound of the other spheres, all of which descended as gently as they rose, upon changing the positive to the negative . J.M.
