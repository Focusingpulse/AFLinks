I was thinking on a rotary variable capacitor instead of leyden jars. When the variable capacitor is totally open, charge it with a spark from the Wimshurst generator. When the capacitor closes, connect it to a load. This way, the high voltage - low current charging the open capacitor will be transformed into a low voltage - high current when the capacitor is closed. As the capacitor is rotating, the cycle is repeated at a given frequency. Questions : 1. What would be the voltage ratio between the open/closed capacitor ? 2.
