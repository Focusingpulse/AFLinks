Sounds arise in association . There are no bare simple sounds in music ; it is a thing full of the play of sympathy . Such a thing as a simple solitary sound would be felt as a strange thing in our ears, accustomed as we are to hear affiliated sounds only. These affiliated sounds , called " harmonics ," or " partials " as they have also been called, because they are the parts of which the sound is made up, are like perspective in vision . In perspective the objects lying in the line of sight , seem smaller and smaller, and more dim and indefinite as they stretch away into the distance; while nearer objects and those in the foreground are apparently larger, and are more clearly seen.
