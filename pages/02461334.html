Thanks, Richard Trivangeant Regarding "Question about Bedini" posted by Mark van Tinteren: Mark raises a good question, which John Bedini can perhaps address with a suggestion. But also check out his original north pole motor design, as well as the Adams type motor, at this link: http://www.nidlink.com/~john1/idea.html You will see he uses a Hall-effect sensor which provides some isolation from the coil firing circuit. Now, I agree that, as much as possible, we should follow Bedini's suggestion to follow his construction details closely. But in order for these small demonstrator motors to give an effective demonstration of the O/U effect, I am now beginning to wonder if some sort of auto-recharge circuit should be added--instead of depending upon manual exchange of the two batteries. (see circuit at the bottom of this web page:) http://www.keelynet.com/bedmot/bedmot.htm Or, could the recharge capacitor be discharged automatically to the SAME battery in between motor firing pulses? To do either of these jobs automatically and with a minimal amount of loss, another trigger magnet/Hall effect sensor combination could be added. The sensor would then drive a transistor.
