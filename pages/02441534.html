Lecture V - GA 293. The Study of Man

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 26 August 1919 · archived # 2,441,534
, Stuttgart Lecture V Yesterday we discussed the nature of will in so far as will is embodied in the human organ. Today we will use this knowledge of man's relationship to will to fructify our consideration of the rest of the human being. You will have noticed that in treating of the human being up to now I have chiefly drawn attention to the intellectual activity, the activity of cognition, on the one hand, and the activity of will on the other hand.
