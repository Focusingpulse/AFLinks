reflux

· archived # 1,482,925
noun: an abnormal backward flow of body fluids noun: the outward flow of the tide Schauberger 1. It is a known fact that in high pressure boilers a reflux from high to medium pressure takes place under certain circumstances without the hermetic seal being broken[10]. Up to now this occurrence could not be explained and because of it the manufacture of miniature pressurised boilers was abandoned, which meant, of course, that veritable mechanical monsters had to be put up with, which consumed a great deal of space and material .
