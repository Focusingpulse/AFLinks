Karlsson, Napier & Guthrie, Halton C Arp and others Keywords redshift periodicity, quantization, Karlsson peaks, galactocentric, intrinsic redshift, Big Bang Year 1976 onward Redshift quantization , also called redshift periodicity , is the claim that the redshifts of galaxies and quasars are not distributed smoothly but cluster around preferred, regularly spaced values . If the claim holds, it is difficult to reconcile with redshift as a simple measure of recession velocity, since a genuine distribution of velocities and distances should be continuous.
