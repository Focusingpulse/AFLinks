Beaty Everyone is familiar with bubbles! Here are the three basic types: fig. 1 Pockets of air under water fig. 3 Bubbles which float in the air fig 2. Air trapped under a water film Besides the above bubbles, there are three other kinds which are not as familiar. These are the ANTIBUBBLES. An Antibubble is similar to a bubble, but the roles of the water and the air are reversed. The first type of antibubble is familiar. it is simply a drop of water falling through the air. It is the opposite of an underwater bubble: rather than being a blob of air in the water, it is a blob of water in the air.
