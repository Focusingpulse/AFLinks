Cars with MDI engine can store enough air for 125 miles' driving, or, in city, about 10 hours. It carries three tanks which hold a total of about 300 litres of air. The versatility of the MDI engine, is that it can be adapted for transport which needs higher speeds over longer distances, because it can also run on petrol. At speeds over 50 mph, the engine switches automatically to petrol supply and emits far lower of CO2 and NOx than a normal car.
