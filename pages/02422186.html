However, existing XFELs supply only linearly polarized light, precluding studies of chiral dynamics. A device called the Delta undulator has been installed at the Linac Coherent Light Source (LCLS) to provide tunable polarization. With a reverse tapered planar undulator line to pre-microbunch the beam and the novel technique of beam diverting, hundreds of microjoules of circularly polarized X-ray pulses are produced at 500-1200 eV. These X-ray pulses are tens of femtoseconds long, have a degree of circular polarization of 0.98+0.02 −0.04 at 707 eV, and may be scanned in energy.
