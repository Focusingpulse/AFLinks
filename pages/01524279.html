More recently, Sandage and J-M. Perelmuter have concluded (with reservations) that the universe is expanding based on the analysis of the surface brightness of large elliptical galaxies. These are conflicting studies which do not prove conclusively that the universe is expanding or non-expanding. To show that the universe is non-expanding, both a viable theory and observational data supporting the theory must exist.
