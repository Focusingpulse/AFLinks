In cycle 1 it puts the charged battery in series with the energy source thus the capacitor charges to the battery voltage + the voltage in the energy source. In cycle 2 the cap, now containing the batteryvolts + the volts from the energy source, dumps back into the battery charging it. As long as the energy source is more than 0volts the battery recives a charge regardless of the battery voltage. It needs a 2phase drive signal not shown.
