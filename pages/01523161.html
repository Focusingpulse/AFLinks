Forces and interactions are consequences of such particle properties. In the model presented the traditional view is reversed and particle interactions, expressed as coupling constants, are taken as the fundamental objective entities while charge and mass are re-defined as nonobjective parameters. Electromagnetic and gravitational forces are interpreted as components of a unified long range force.
