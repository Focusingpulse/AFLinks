Introduction There is hardly a formal introduction to the concept of an inner product and associated space in the literature. The inner product space is usually a good place to go for a wide range of mathematical results, from identities to inequalities. In this situation, the best potential result is frequently obtained. The Cauchy-Schwartz inequality obtained in the case of the Hilbert space [1] is a good example. The notion of the local product and the induced local product space are introduced in this study.
