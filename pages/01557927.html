Mag experimental A magnetic experiment When I was playing around with some magnets I noticed this interesting effect The apparatus is something like above. It consist of two 1'' round magnetic I purchased from a Radio shack or Tandy store other electronics hobby shop may be able to provide something simular. They are mounted on two metal plates the magnets will hold to , the ones I used were purchased from a hardware store for a couple of dollars and are used for patio construction you may use, something simular as parts are not critical. Mount so that the two magnets oppose one another that is North to North or South to South. and about 1/4" inch apart or closer. The magnets can be mounted to the plates with insulating tape for ease of construction.. Now if you get a separate piece of metal and place it near the two magnets you will notice a stronger pull than if the magnets are in a north to south alignment. I don't claim this motor would have any overunity possbilities but could be used in driving such.
