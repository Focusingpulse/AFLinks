Planck's constant is known to be one of such foundations, which serves as a basis of quantum mechanics. Let us consider how a refinement of interpretation of the physical essence of this constant allows to make a theoretical penetration into the depth of electromagnetic structure of the electron and to connect this structure with the results of the experiment.
