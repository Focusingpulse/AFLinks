Proof that angle is not dimensionless quantity Seungtae Kim Department of Life Sciences, The university of Tokyo, Tokyo, Japan E-mail 1: steven-kim@g.ecc.u-tokyo.ac.jp E-mail 2: kstt0924@gmail.com Abstract In the current International System of Units (SI), it is conventionally assumed that rad = 1, thereby treating angle as a dimensionless quantity. However, this convention presents a conceptual problem because of the relation sr = rad2 , so that the fundamental physical distinction between solid angles and plane angles is blurred. In this paper, I revisited the nature of physical equations based on the principle of dimensional homogeneity and the mathematical properties of the dimensional analysis function, demonstrated that angle is a physical quantity possessing its own fundamental dimension which means angle must be treated as one of the base quantities. Finally, some well-known equations are reconsidered comprehensively for dimensional homogeneity. 1.
