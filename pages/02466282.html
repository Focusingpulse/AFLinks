PARAMETERS OF TRIANGLES FORMED BY THE PEAKS OF THE GIZA PLATEAU PYRAMIDS AND THE HIGHEST POINT OF THE "GREAT SPHINX" MONUMENT Andrey V. Voron (anvoron1@yandex.ru) October 13, 2026 Abstract Based on calculated and recorded data, this paper demonstrates the use of two length systems – the metric system and the ancient Egyptian system (royal cubit) – by the builders of the Giza plateau. The study analyzes the numerical linear dimensions of each pyramid in the Giza pyramid complex, as well as the base area and visible surface area of the complex pyramids. Spatial 3D segments were obtained, which formed the basis for calculating the areas of two triangles formed by the peaks of the Menkaure, Khafre, and Khufu pyramids and the highest point of the "Great Sphinx" monument. 1 Introduction Prior studies conducted by the author [1, 2, 3] indicated that mathematical regularities, specifically the Fibonacci numbers, are encoded within the parameters of the Giza plateau pyramids. The present study aims to harmonize individual data into a unified, logically coherent system, allowing for the formulation of a scientific hypothesis. The objective of this work is to calculate the spatial (3D) parameters of the triangles formed by the physical peaks of the three Giza plateau pyramids and the highest point of the "Great Sphinx" monument to search for numerical patterns. 2 The main part Research Methodology. This paper utilizes a method of comparative analysis matching recorded geometric parameters of the objects against their theoretical calculated models, adjusted for the thickness of the lost casing stones and structural degradation. Using artificial intelligence tools, true spatial 3D segments between key objects were reconstructed based on our previous research. The calculation of the areas of the formed triangles was performed using the ancient Egyptian unit of length, the "Royal Cubit" (0.5236 m).
