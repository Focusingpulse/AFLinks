Part II. Preliminary Remarks — GA 260a. The Constitution of the General Anthroposophical Society

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 2,437,463
Wilhelm Hübbe-Schleiden dated August 16, 1902, with the following words: “I want to build on the power that enables me to bring ‘spiritual disciples’ onto the path of development. That alone will have to be my inaugural act. That is why I want to be positive in everything.” For this task, a separate institution was established in connection with the German Section, the so-called Esoteric School. Rudolf Steiner began to build it up after his stay in London in mid-May 1904, where Annie Besant authorized him as head of this Esoteric School founded by H.P. Blavatsky to work independently in Germa
