Title Energy Impulse Secrets Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Philipp M Kanarev Published 2010 No. of pages 9 Abstract Energy impulses keep preserving their secrets. The errors in Newton's dynamics and electrodynamics are the main causes of incomprehension of these secrets. A recovery of these errors will open new energy possibilities before mankind; these possibilities will rescue mankind from an environmental disaster, which is generated by hydrocarbon energy sources. It is not a naked forecast. Its authenticity is proved both theoretically and experimentally. Overview Philipp Kanarev, Professor Emeritus of Physics at Krasnodar, argues that Newtonian dynamics contains errors that have concealed the true nature of impulsive forces, and that correcting them opens "new energy possibilities before mankind" capable of rescuing us from the environmental consequences of hydrocarbon fuels. The paper has three parts. First, a critique of the textbook treatment of impulse and impact force, addressed throughout to "dear mathematicians" whose symphonic derivations, he says, leave engineers without any way to compute the quantities that matter. Second, an application to a real disaster: the destruction of turbine unit 2 at the Sayano-Shushenskaya hydroelectric station in August 2009, in which a 2,580-tonne assembly was thrown fourteen metres into the turbine hall and 75 people died. Third, a proposed explanation of that event in terms of pressure exerted by infrared photons released when water clusters re-form, and a pulsed electrical generator said to exploit the same principle. Kanarev's alternative framework, which he calls mechanodynamics, adds an "inertial force" F i = mb i to the list of forces resisting motion, and defines impact force as the change of momentum divided by the time over which it changes.
