Effective Communication at Home Understanding Family & Being Understood This module differs greatly from the Effective Communication in the Workplace module as it is specifically designed to aid in communications with family members. Increasing your ability to communicate lovingly and respectfully in a manner that is productive and understood by family members is accomplished by altering past methods and patterns of dysfunctional communication. You will experience increased sense of humor, feelings of relaxation, safeness, and tranquility which will aid in confidence and comfort while expressing yourself and respecting your families thoughts and feelings. Addressed: - Increased ability to understand other points of view - Increased feelings of joy and satisfaction when you react with love and caring - Desire to interact peacefully with family - Desire to be courteous - Increased patience - Increased sense of humor - Increased feelings of peace - Increased desire to live in harmony - Increased ability to be expressive in communication - Stimulate perception - Increased ability to communicate lovingly Recommended Supplement: - Spirulina for necessary Amino Acids Recommended Duration of Use: Effective Communication at Home helps you develop new habits and behaviors that can often be internalized after only 60-90 days, providing long-lasting benefits and lifestyle changes.
