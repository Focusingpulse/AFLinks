Monoclinic single crystals of Ba2(H2O)[µ10C6H2(COO)4] (1) and Pb2(H2O)[µ10-C6H2(COO)4]H2O (2) have been obtained using the silica gel method. Space group C2/c (no. 15), Z = 4; 1: a = 780.89(4), b = 1756.19(8), c = 914.80(5) pm, = 114.512(5)°; 2: a = 756.70(10), b = 1772.8(2), c = 890.2(2) pm, = 113.590(10)°. There are two crystallographically independent M2+ ions (M = Ba, Pb). M(1) is surrounded by eight carboxylate oxygen atoms and one water molecule forming a tricapped trigonal prism. M(2) is coordinated by ten carboxylate oxygen atoms in the shape of a tetracapped octahedron. The connection between the M(1) and M(2) polyhedra leads to infinite layers parallel to (010) which are linked by [C6H2(COO)4]4 anions to form a three-dimensional framework. The [C6H2(COO)4]4 anion adopts a µ10 coordination mode.
