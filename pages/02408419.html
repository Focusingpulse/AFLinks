GA 35. Mathematics and Occultism

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 21 June 1904 · archived # 2,408,419
, Amsterdam Translated by M. H. Eyre It is well known that the inscription over the door of Plato's school was intended to exclude anybody who was unacquainted with the science of Mathematics, from participating in the teachings of the Master. Whatever we may think of the historical truth of this tradition, it is based upon the correct understanding of the place that Plato assigned to mathematics within the domain of human knowledge.
