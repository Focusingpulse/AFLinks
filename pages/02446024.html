09/04/14 - Lost Walt Disney UFO Documentary This uncut documentary was broadcast in just a few states back in 1995. It was originally produced to promote the new alien encounters ride at Disney World in Florida but became a serious look at UFOs. Alien Encounter Documentary - This version is the complete uncut feature, recorded to VHS video when it was first broadcast. UFO - Lost Walt Disney UFO Documentary, Alien Encounters: In March of 1995, Walt Disney Television aired a most intriguing one-hour television special on UFOs titled Alien Encounters from New Tomorrowland. Disney's then-CEO (later President) Michael Eisner opens the program and the host, the late Robert Urich (star of the TV series SWAT and Vega$) asks: "So why have aliens chosen to visit our small blue planet hidden on the distance fringes of an insignificant star cluster? Well, we invited them here." There have been reports of alien encounters throughout recorded history. But since the end of World War II, alien encounters have adopted a darker, more menacing demeanor. No longer just spirited lights dancing in the sky, UFOs turned more brazen, announcing themselves with surprising ferocity. But while the Pentagon refused to publicly admit aliens had arrived on earth, their top secret internal memos told a different story, even detailing the various ships and the creatures they had autopsied. Government Cover Up - By the early 1960s, UFOs were having a chilling effect on our defense operations. Their tremendous speed often caused them to be misidentified as incoming intercontinental ballistic missiles, putting American air bases on red alert.
