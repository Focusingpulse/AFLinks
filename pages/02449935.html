Seifer. ISBN 0-8065-1960-6, published by Carol Publishing Group. It has been released in paperback form. Quite a nice job. Seems to do justice to serious scholarship, without trashing the advanced Tesla technology that mainstream science does not yet appreciate. The last two chapters, detailing the struggle over Tesla's papers after he died is quite interesting. The overall tone of the book is quite positive, and It is a great gold mine of references.
