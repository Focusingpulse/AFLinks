The ARDA flying wing Mk V will be used for validating the flight model in real conditions and the wing will be used as an R/C glider during all the summer time ( July-August 2000 )... I shall be glad to post some nice videos and photos about the flights in my web site... After this validation phase of the flight model, a new larger model will use the EHD propulsion system ( based on the Biefeld-Brown effect), this is planned for August-September 2000...
