December 29, 1990 AGE6.ASC -------------------------------------------------------------------- Hormones and Aging Dr. W. Donner Denckla, working at the National Institutes of Health, Harvard and at the National Institute of Alcoholism and Alcohol Abuse discovered a hormone called DECO (decreasing consumption of oxygen). DECO is commonly referred to as the "death hormone" due to an apparent link with its presence and accelerated aging processes. Denckla removed the pituitary gland from rat brains and injected them with thyroid, growth and other hormones, he noted that the organ systems of rats with an absence of DECO appeared much younger than normal. Aged rat hearts and lungs seemed to become rejuvenated. However, when these same rats were injected with DECO, their organs began to age at a much accelerated rate. Another contributor to the aging process is the thyroid hormone, Thymosin. Dr. Allen Goldstein of George Washington University Medical School, found that thymosin helps the immune system to remain strong.
