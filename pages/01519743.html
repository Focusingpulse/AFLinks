Roy Keys Inc.), Montreal Pages 242 ISBN 9780968368947 Einstein and the Ether is a 2000 book by the Polish physicist and philosopher of science Ludwik Kostro , published by Apeiron of Montreal. It argues that the familiar account of Einstein's relation to the ether that he abolished it in 1905 and never looked back is wrong in its second half. Kostro's case is that Einstein rejected the nineteenth-century mechanical ether and then, from 1916, reintroduced an ether of a different kind, developed it through three successive conceptions, and held to it for the rest of his life.
