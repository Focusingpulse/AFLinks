vector field

· archived # 1,497,206
In vector calculus, a vector field is an assignment of a vector to each point in a subset of Euclidean space . A vector field in the plane for instance can be visualized as an arrow, with a given magnitude and direction, attached to each point in the plane. Vector fields are often used to model, for example, the speed and direction of a moving fluid throughout space , or the strength and direction of some force, such as the magnetic or gravitational force, as it changes from point to point.
