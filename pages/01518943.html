Grimshaw, Ph.D. LENRGY, LLC Austin, Texas April 15, 2026 2 Introduction A report1 has been prepared to document the LENR contributions of Dr. Ludwig Kowalski as a project of the LENR Research Documentation Initiative (LRDI). A major part of that report references Dr. Kowalski’s “Learn Cold Fusion” project website2 , which has nearly all of his observations and records concerning his LENR work. This addendum to the report provides a complete copy of Dr. Kowalski’s website entries. The website consists of a total of 1809 pages. It has three major sections: 1. A description of his “Learn Cold Fusion Project” 2. A list of Dr. Kowalski’s 416 entries with links to each one 3. The full collection of the website entries.
