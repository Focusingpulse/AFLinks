Can I boost all 4 Modules?

· archived # 2,432,245
Here is an email we received from a customer: Q: It possible to boost the Modules in my Theater? Can you boost all four or is there a maximum amount of boosting per theater? A: There is a limited amount of room for boosting that can be done per Theater. You will see the most results if only 1 or 2 Modules are boosted. Something to remember when requesting a boost is that when you increase the power of a Module, it is taking power away from another Module as there is only an allotted amount of room per Theater. The change is usually negligible with 1, or sometimes even 2, boosted, but if you were to boost 3 Modules, the one that is not boosted will fall behind in power. Many times we have "supporting" unreleased mini-Modules that will be of great assistance to the general Modules you have in your Theater. If you feel you could use some extra power behind a Module, you can email [email protected] and ask them if they have anything that would be good for your situation.
