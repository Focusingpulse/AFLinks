The HydroStar Preface Congratulations for investing in your personal copy of The HydroStar System book! I feel fortunate in being given the opportunity to bring this valuable information your way. Now, you too can be involved in efforts to help publicize an incredible and priceless invention; the HydroStar water powered engine! All I ask is that you don't keep this information to yourself; please share it with your friends, relatives, and neighbors. That way we can all be part of the small, but growing, community of thinkers, inventors, and tinkerers determined to bring inventions such as the HydroStar System to the forefront. This book presents complete plans and detailed descriptions sufficient to build a workable HydroStar System. The information contained here has been placed in the public domain, and as such has not been presented as a patented invention. This has been done in hopes of discouraging suppression of such a valuable invention but, even more importantly, to encourage the free flow and dissemination of the HydroStar System information contained within this document. Being a tinkerer at heart, I'm actively involved in building my own HydroStar System. I hope you'll join with me by becoming involved in your own venture; that's what is needed most. Let's all benefit by sharing information and offering encouragement to one another. If you do decide to become involved in your own HydroStar project, or just want to offer advice, an opinion, or constructive criticism on how to improve this book, please feel free to contact me. I’ll do my best to get back with you, but I can't guarantee a timely response. Sometimes I’m away from the office for weeks at a time, testing and perfecting new concepts, or just chasing down parts. Here’s where you can write me: Power Products, 7017 N Litchfield Rd #388, Glendale, AZ 85307. Or e-mail me: SuperEngines@aol.com. Have fun with your HydroStar project, and happy driving! William S.
