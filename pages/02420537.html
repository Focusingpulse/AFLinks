DOES LOW TEMPERATURE NUCLEAR CHANGE OCCUR IN SOLIDS? Low-Energy Transmutation Conference held at Texas A&M University, June 19, 1995. INTRODUCTION BY DR. BOCKRIS Dr. John O'M. Bockris sponsored this historic conference and welcomed the invited attendees. The purpose of the conference was to share experimental and theoretical findings to answer the question, "Does Low Temperature Nuclear Change Occur in Solids?" Bockris mentioned that some evidence has been gathered. For example, 120 papers have reported the finding of tritium produced from experiments using palladium.
