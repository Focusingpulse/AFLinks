Education University of Wroclaw, Faculty of Natural Sciences in Geology (1966) History Jan Koziar was born in 1943, Wola Mielecka, Poland (then occupied by the Germans). Graduated from the University of Wroclaw - Faculty of Natural Sciences in geology (1966). Then hired as a researcher-lecturer at the Institute of Geological Sciences, University of Wrocław.
