According to this equation, a body at the Earth's surface, moving at the velocity of light relative to the Earth's gravitational field in the direction of, or opposite to, the Earth's orbital velocity about the Sun, will have a mass 120,000 times its rest mass. This is the minimum mass a body at the Earth's surface can have when moving at the velocity of light relative to the Earth's gravitational field.
