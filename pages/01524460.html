Xavier Borg Residence Malta Nationality Maltese Known for Gravity , Electromagnetism , Radiation interaction with matter , Nuclear Structure Scientific career Fields Electrical Engineer , Mechanical Engineer Xavier Borg (B. Eng. Hons.) is a Maltese electrical and mechanical engineer and independent researcher, and the founder of Blaze Labs Research in Malta . He is known for his work in alternative physics, including the Electro-Magnetic Radiation Pressure (EMRP) push-gravity theory, the "ST system of units" proposed as a route to the unification of physics, and experimental studies of high-voltage electrohydrodynamic ("lifter") devices. His theoretical proposals fall outside mainstream physics and have been received critically by academic commentators. Biography Education B.Eng. (Hons.) in Mechanical and Electrical Engineering, 1992 Career Borg graduated from a Mechanical and Electrical Engineering course in 1992. Over the course of his engineering career he worked in a semiconductor manufacturing plant, as a cellular network manager, as a designer of test equipment for television production lines, as a designer of high-voltage power supplies, as a designer of radio broadcast transmission stations, and as the manager of a satellite business firm, before dedicating himself to independent scientific research. He carries out this research through Blaze Labs Research, based in Malta. Scientific contributions Borg's independent research spans several topics in fundamental physics, all of which sit outside the framework of mainstream theory. His best-known theoretical proposal is the Electro-Magnetic Radiation Pressure (EMRP) gravity theory , a "push gravity" model in which gravitation is not treated as an attractive force but as the net result of an external pressure exerted on matter by high-energy electromagnetic radiation.
