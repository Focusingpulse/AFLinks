Introduction Recently, the journal American Psychologist published a paper by Etzel Cardeña that summarized the meta-analyses on various modes of psi and provided as well historical and theoretical background (Cardeña, 2018). Cardeña’s paper is most notable with its comprehensive approach. The presented meta-analyses give us perhaps the best bird’s-eye view of psi research to date. The combined studies include various modes or categories of psi, as well as different experimental designs for each one.
