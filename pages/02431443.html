Collective Synchronistic Dream Experiment

· archived # 2,431,443
Hey, all you synchro module dreamers and other interested parties! What would you say to a Collective Synchronistic Dream Experiment, in which we all meet up in dream consciousness and intend a breakthrough? We seem to be experiencing remarkably lucid and meaningful dreams; maybe this is a great time to have a coalition of the willing dreamers and see what we can create together! We wouldn't even have to worry about all the different time zones -- we would simply set a date and ask our Dream Minds to "synchronize our watches"; we could relax and allow our powerful unconscious intention to sort out the details of traveling backwards or forwards or whatever in time in order that we're all Dreaming Together, regardless of what time we go to sleep. Remember in the '60s they held Be-In's? We could throw a Dream-In! September 11th is a good date! What if, for instance, we were to generate a collective intention of having a breakthrough in, say, Peaceful Self/Peaceful Planet. We could give ourselves a day or two to gestate and cogitate, then begin posting, comparing, and contrasting in our waking lives. Do you think that would be a fun and inspiring experiment?
