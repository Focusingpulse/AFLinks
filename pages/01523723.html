Beil (born 1938) is an American mathematical physicist. Over some two decades he developed a programme that uses Finsler geometry to construct a unified field theory, deriving electromagnetism and other interactions from the geometry of the tangent bundle. The Beil metric , a Finsler metric he introduced for this purpose, is named after him and has been taken up by later workers in the field. Scientific work Beil's work belongs to the tradition, going back to Weyl, Kaluza and Klein, of seeking to unify the forces of physics within an enlarged geometry. His distinctive move was to base that geometry not on ordinary Riemannian spacetime but on Finsler geometry a generalization
