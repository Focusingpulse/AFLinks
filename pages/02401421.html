GC2026-SGG014 Confirmation of the predicted values of the Earth's seismicity in the 21st century according to monitoring seismological observations in the Russian Federation Ivan Y. Lobodenko1 , Luiza M. Fikhieva1 1 SEC NRC, Russia fihieva@secnrs.ru According to the requirements of Federal Norms and Regulations in the field of the use of atomic energy [1], seismic activity in the area and site of atomic energy facilities, including nuclear power plants, must be established and justified with an estimated probability of occurrence in the range of one year 10-4 and above, while seismological monitoring must be performed with taking into account the data of global and unified state monitoring systems operating on the territory of the Russian Federation. It also provides for ensuring the safety of nuclear power plants by, among other things, taking into account operational experience and the current level of development of science, technology and production [2]. For more than a quarter of a century, starting in 1994, SEC NRC, being the organization of scientific and technical support for Rostechnadzor, has been analyzing safety justifications regarding the seismic characteristics of NPP sites, programs and results of monitoring seismological observations of the area and the site. Special attention is paid to evaluating the results of monitoring the failure to exceed the design values of external impacts in the area and at the nuclear power plant site, taking into account the modern development of science and technology.
