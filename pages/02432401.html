The more you give, the more you receive.

· archived # 2,432,401
Since young, books has always been one of my greatest enemy. I have never finish reading a book, even story book before. It was until when I was 24 years old when one of my mentor introduce this famous book "Rich Dad Poor Dad" by Robert Kiyosaki to me, which took me 1 full month to finish reading it. Since then my life start to change. Through lots of struggles, challenges, and of course opportunities, I decided to become an entrepreneur and motivational speaker, going around to inspire and help people to realize their dream. However, since I wasn't given a physical platform to do so, I decided to start out with social media first to reach to people. And that was how Signature Success was founded. Today Signature Success is very active in Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, Vine, Dubbler, LinkedIn and Pinterest, reaching out to more than 3000 over people. My aim in life is very simple. . to leave a blueprint in other people's life. Soon enough you'll found out that I'm just an ordinary guy with a really big dream. Today, I'm really proud & humble to publish my 1st ever eBook that I've written. Titled " Signature Success - The Principles of Self Empowerment " It talks about how every single one is able to empowerment themselves to reach their fullest potential to achieve something great. I also share more on how to improve your personal confidence and image, and positive affirmations to help you make better and wiser choices in life. Well, instead of selling the book on eBay and Amazon, I've decided to give it away for free. . .Simply because I always believe in the more you, the more you received. And I sincerely wish to inspire and help people to achieve their goals. Please also feel free to share it around with your friends who you think will benefit from this book =) Thank you=) To Your Success, Alexander Goh
