Figure 14.04 - The Alchemical (Astral/Etheric) Light ( click to enlarge ) Eliphas Levi "Depth is equal to height, darkness is contrasted with light, matter is but the garment of the spirit . That which is below is like that which is above. The breath of God is borne along over the waters, and the waters remain aloft carried on by the breath of God. That which we call the Breath of God is the Life Essence spread over the worlds; it is that warm luminous fluid which gives a soul to the planets; the common reservoir of progressive animal life; the universal basis of the sympathies between bodies, and the medium of love between souls; it is the vehicle of the will, and common basis for all the varied modifications of the Creative Word. The Breath of the mouth of God leads to the corresponding human breath. God said, "Let us make man," and man answers, "Let us make to ourselves a God." When God made creation manifest as a temple, He illumined the planets as light-bearers, He decorated the earth with flowers as an altar, He gave mysterious properties to metals and plants, and He drew with His finger circles, triangles, and the cross, as eternal pantacles, traced in living fire upon the immense vault of the skies. The Magus should imitate his God upon this earth, his dwelling. Know, Will, and Act; these are the three essentials for sacerdotal and royal high magic.
