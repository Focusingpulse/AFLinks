The change in temperature was forced on the electrolysis cell while it was producing nuclear energy at excess power levels of 240 W/ cm3 and after producing a continuous excess heat amount of 150 MJ/cm3 (14 000 eV/atom of Pd). The temperature change (from 82.5 C to ~0 C) resulted in a total loss of excess power (nuclear power) and indicates the regions of the microstructure that were ordered superabundant vacancy (SAV) phase transformed from d to the ordered superabundant vacancy phase d0 . The d phase is responsible for producing excess heat and power while the d0 phase produces no excess heat or power.
