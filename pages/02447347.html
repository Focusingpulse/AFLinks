Intriguing, much like Beardens description of the 'negative resistor'. The Richard Clem engine is based on an effect he witnessed while working for the city of Dallas. He noticed when a hot asphalt sprayer was shut down, that it would continue to operate for up to 30 minutes. Since asphalt at ambient temperatures is a solid, but when heated, becomes liquid, the hotter, the more liquid, thus the lessening of the viscosity...based on his observations, Clem basically copied the design and substitued cooking oil for the fluid, because it was cheap and would not break down at high temperaturs. I think this lessening of viscosity and the correlation with tornadic activity is brilliant! Good stuff Larry and Knuke!
