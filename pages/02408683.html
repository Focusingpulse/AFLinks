"Africa is waking up" Zambia reaffirmed her rejection of GM maize-aid as famine looms and despite strong pressures from the US and certain UN agencies. But the rest of the world strongly supports her stance. African consumer leaders produced a Lusaka Declaration after a stormy 3-day conference, rejecting both GM technology and patents on genetic resources for food and agriculture, affirming that African countries can address food security themselves. An exclusive interview with Zambias agriculture minister, Mundia Sikatana, gave grounds for believing "Zambia will feed herself from now on".
