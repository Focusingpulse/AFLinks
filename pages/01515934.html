[Microscopic Radiological Treatment Using EVOs] 5/5/07 (3 pages) 1 Microscopic Radiological Treatment Using EVOs by Ken Shoulders 2007 Abstract A non-invasive radiological treatment and analysis method having characteristic dimensions in the 10-micrometer range is proposed. The method is based on the use of EVOs swept in a 3 dimensional array by both angular deflection and timed excitation to cause local disintegration without disturbing material previously transited. This disintegration gives rise to highly energetic electron emission for treatment and diagnostics as well as a wideband electromagnetic emission used as a location marker. Data is made available on known guidance and trigger variables and limitations as well as errors found in measurements made in several nuclear energy assessment methods.
