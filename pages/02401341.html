bmde2026-tourism007 Privacy-Preserving AI Decision Support for Airline Service Recovery in Digitised Tourism Services Khaled Yousuf1 , Khaled Yousuf 1 Hamad Bin Khalifa University (HBKU), College of Humanities and Social Sciences (CHSS), Doha, Qatar, Qatar khyo89803@hbku.edu.qa Digitalisation across tourism and hospitality has intensified the volume and sensitivity of traveller data, while simultaneously increasing expectations for fast, fair and transparent service recovery during disruptions. Airlines are a core tourism infrastructure where service failures (delays, misconnections, baggage problems, denied boarding, documentation confusion, special assistance breakdowns) become immediately visible and emotionally charged. Although airlines collect rich post-trip feedback, these signals are rarely translated into compliant, real￾time frontline guidance. This paper proposes an evidence-based requirements framework for an AI decision￾support tool that assists passenger-facing staff in high-friction scenarios without relying on personal identifiers. Grounded in SERVQUAL and Service-Dominant Logic and informed by privacy-by-design strategies, the framework specifies minimal operational inputs, auditable outputs, and security and governance controls (data minimisation, role-based access, audit trails, policy grounding, and human accountability). A sequential mixed￾method validation design is outlined using anonymised feedback analytics, staff interviews, and scenario-based evaluation.
