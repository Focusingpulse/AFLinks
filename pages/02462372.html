Resisting Authoritarianism with a Life-affirming Agenda: A Psychoanalytic Perspective

· archived # 2,462,372
Editor’s Note: I met Andreas Peglau quite by accident at the Reich exhibit at the Jewish Museum in Vienna in 2007. With excitement he showed me a page from Bertholt Brecht’s personal copy of Reich’s “Dialectical Materialism and Psychoanalysis” with Brecht’s marginal notes. We chatted, swapped email addresses, and thus began an on-going relationship. We mainly stay in touch through frequent email exchanges, but Peglau stayed in our home when we came to the States in 2012 to do research in the Reich archives, and I have stayed in his apartment in Berlin more than once. We have co-authored two articles, one of which, on the Nazi removal of Reich’s German citizenship, was published in German Studies Review In addition to “Suggestions for a Psychoanalysis of Europe’s Shift to the Right,” Peglau has published many articles in his native German. His personal webpage is: Andreas Peglau Psychoanalyse Most important for those interested in Reich is his doctoral dissertation, now published in book form, Unpolitische Wissenschaft? Wilhelm Reich und die Psychoanalyse im Nationalsozialismus (An Unpolitical Science? Wilhelm Reich and Psychoanalysis during National Socialism) (Gießen: Psychosozial-Verlag, 2013). An English translation of this text would greatly benefit those who do not read German easily. But there is an available review in English In addition to his continued research on Reich, Dr. Peglau is a therapist in private practice in Berlin. It is a pleasure to introduce him and his work to followers of Dr. Simonian’s web-journal. Philip W. Bennett, PhD This article was written in summer 2014.
