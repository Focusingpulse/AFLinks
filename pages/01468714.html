[see 7.10 - First ] Second : In the aggregation of matter , force or energy is stored up or conserved. [see 7.11 - Second ] Third : In the dissociation of matter , force is liberated. [see 7.12 - Third ] Fourth : All matter is in a state of perpetual activity, whether the substance under consideration be inanimate or animated, visible or invisible . [see 7.13 - Fourth ] Fifth : There is no dividing of matter and force into two distinct terms, as they both are ONE. FORCE is liberated matter . MATTER is force in bondage. [see 7.15 - Sixth ] Seventh : All structures, whether crystalline or homogeneous, have for their unit structures minute bodies called molecules .
