This sounds fantastic, but science and invention have accomplished such miracles in recent years that it is foolhardy to doubt predictions further achievements. Tesla gives no inkling of what his new invention is to be, but it's pathered that it involves the generation and transmission of power possible the transmission of power by wireless, or radio waves. It would be wonderful to drive an automobile through the country drawing the motive power from the air and with little or no expense! Invention has done much towards increasing the capacity of man. It has enabled him to see further, to hear a longer distance and to travel at about 100 times faster. The abundant production capacity of today is entirely due to science. The advancement of science has been so rapid in the field of industry that it has upset the former scheme of things and brought about a topsy-turvy state of affairs. It may be worse yet when Tesla puts his wonderful invention on the market. And yet, it is entirely a problem of adjustment.
