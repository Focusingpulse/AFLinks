Albert Poisson "It has often been repeated that alchemists worked blindly, this is a serious error, they had very rational theories which, put forward by the Greek philosophers of the second century of the Christian era, were maintained almost without alteration until the 18th century. At the basis of Hermetic theory, we find a great law: the Unity of Matter . Matter is one, but it can take various forms and in these new forms combine with itself and produce new bodies in indefinite number. This raw material was still called seed, chaos, universal substance. Without going into further details, Basile Valentin posits the unity of matter in principle. “All things come from the same seed, they were all originally born by the same mother” (Char de triumph of antimony).
