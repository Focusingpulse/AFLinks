EVEN-ODD SUBSERIES OF THE MULTIPLE ZETA VALUE ζ(2, 1) RICHARD J. MATHAR Abstract. The Multiple Zeta Value ζ(2, 1) is a double sum over two positive integer indices. If these indices are restricted by parity to cover either only even or only odd positive integers, the Multiple Zeta Value splits into four nonoverlapping subseries. The manuscript evaluates these four series in terms of the Riemann Zeta function, π and log 2. 1.
