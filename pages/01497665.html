PACS 25.70.Ji – Fusion and fusion-fission reactions. 1. – Introduction In a previous paper [1], the absence of penetrating radiation, during processes of anomalous heat production, was reported. Such a result was based on measurements performed, for safety reasons, by using dosimetric instrumentation and by comparing the radiation level near and far from a working cell. Successively, such a cell (cell A) was modified and a new cell built (cell B). Both cells allow the measurement of their external wall temperature in order to detect any anomalous heat emission from the system.
