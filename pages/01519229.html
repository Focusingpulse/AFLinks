It was originally published in Russian (ST-Center, Moscow, 1993), with a second edition, Theory, Experiments and Technologies (Nauka, Moscow, 1996). Overview Shipov presents his Theory of Physical Vacuum , a geometrized "Universal Relativity" that seeks to describe gravitation, electromagnetism, and quantum phenomena within a single vacuum structure.
