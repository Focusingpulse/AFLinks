Overview The section covers the most ambitious phase of Tesla's career: his experiments at Colorado Springs (1899) with the Magnifying Transmitter — a giant resonant transformer for wireless power and communication — and his subsequent plan for a "World Broadcasting System" at Wardenclyffe.
