The Energy Solution Revolution Book Published 2008 Publisher Bridger House Publishers Inc. Pages 276 ISBN 0979917646 This book explores the reality, promise and ongoing suppression of breakthrough clean and free energy research, from the perspective a veteran futurist and energy scientist, who has witnessed and reported on the experiments first-hand. "This is truly a groundbreaking book by one of the world's leading-edge scientists and philosophers in New Energy. Dr. Brian O'Leary pulls no punches in speaking out against what he aptly terms " a paradigm paralysis" embedded in world governments, multinational corporations, and scientific institutions. This book is outstanding. It pushes the envelope for scientific thinking, and presents a fresh new paradigm for weaning us from our fossil fuel addiction. Dr. O'Leary urges us to shift our thinking from the destruction, pollution, and controlling interests involved with fossil fuels, and to move ahead together into a new future scenario. By developing zero-point technologies and other advanced systems, we can eliminate centralized energy, outmoded power grids,and make clean, affordable electricity available for everyone. With his unique background in conventional sciences and consciousness research, Dr. O'Leary makes a strong case that when the overall environmental costs are factored in, Solar, Wind, Tidal, and Wave Power will only serve as temporary...and ultimately ineffective...measures for solving the current global energy/pollution crisis. This book is high on my list in the category of 'Breakthrough Thinking.'" -- Elliott Maynard, Ph.D., Arcos Cielos Research Center, Sedona, Arizona.
