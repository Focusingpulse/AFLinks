Special New York Letter. Nikola Tesla. The combination between the Westinghouse Electric Company and the Baldwin Locomotive Works, which has been already announced, seems to be an assured fact. The purpose is to develop the electric motor, with special reference to railroad travel, and the fact that the combination will control the Tesla patents incites the highest interest in the electrical world. Nikola Tesla is in many respects the most interesting man to-day in the department of electrical investigation. In is due largely to his genius that electricians belleve that a motor will eventually be developed that will draw railroad trains at a speed of from 150 to 300 miles per hour. It is the Tesla motor that the Westinghouse-Baldwin Interests will seek to develop. Tesla, who was so recently burned out, is now located at No. 46 East Houston street, where, surrounded by all necessary appliances, and aided by skilled mechanics, he is devoting his principal attention to the development of the electric motor.
