GC2026-STP018 Features of electromagnetic radiation from industrial power lines in the upper ionosphere Natalia V. Savelyeva1,2 , Vyacheslav A. Pilipenko1,2 1 O.Yu. Schmidt Institute of Physics of the Earth, Russian Academy of Sciences 2 Geophysical Center of the Russian Academy of Sciences nasa2000@ya.ru Subject matters of the present research are parameters of electromagnetic field (EMF) generated by large-scale industrial power lines in industrialized regions. Theoretical basis of the present research is the model of EMF created by power transmission line represented as horizontal conductor of finite length, in the upper ionosphere [1-4], developed by Fedorov, Mazur and Pilipenko (FMP model). This model uses an approximation of a horizontally stratified ionosphere with parameters set by IRI-2016 and MSIS models. Amplitudes of power line emissions (PLE) are expected to be higher above a high-resistive crust, according to FMP model. Data on changes in electric and magnetic fields in the upper ionosphere were obtained using electric field detector (EFD) and search-coil magnetometer (SCM) on the Chinese seismo-electric satellite (CSES-01), orbiting at about 500 kilometers above the earth. Every day, the satellite completed approximately 15 orbits, each orbit being divided into two segments, covering the illuminated and dark sides of the planet. CSES revisit period is about five days.
