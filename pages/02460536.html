At least one author stated that light itself is the ether. It is hard to accept the notions of space, time, and mass change in relativity. Still consider, for example, that electron beams are spiraling, and as more energy is added, the beam spirals more, the energy goes into spiraling motion, not more mass, and the distance traveled is longer because of the tightening spiral path. What is interesting about general relativity, according to a friend with PhD in astrophysics, is that it allows for extraction of energy from gravity. Unlike Newtonian gravity that is conservative - cannot get more energy out than put in. Note that Nasa used the so-called slingshot effect stating less fuel was used with a longer path. http://quest.arc.nasa.gov/galileo/edbrief/galileopage5.html Now, can the slingshot effect be used with electrons on magnets?
