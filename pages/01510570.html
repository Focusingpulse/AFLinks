I mean this one: http://f1.grp.yahoofs.com/v1/8EbpSK6kCtLyXkNsy9GnTKX5NgC38uW7MPZg01YLi01GcdbNMH2j45SA7IxdVtoAKAEN_7MTHeaSbZW-Ss0kdrQ_ZgmTlc09WAus/Fuelless%20Engine%20PDF/878%20-%20Fuelless%20Heater%20a.pdf Thank you Rick for the link It is really easy and I threw it together in few hours. The outer cylinder is about 25 cm tall and 98mm in diameter with 2mm thick walls. The inner cylinder is about 20cm tall and 86mm in diameter also with 2mm walls. The gap between the cylinders is about 6mm. The inner cylinder has glued on caps so that its ends are shut and it can not fill with oil. The main shaft is just a piece of lead screw 10mm in diameter.
