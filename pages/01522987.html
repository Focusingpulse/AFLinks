Elsesser (born 1959) is an American mathematician and computer scientist specializing in compiler optimization for parallel and vector supercomputers. He is interested in developing computer simulations of the atom based on Common Sense Science models. Education BS in mathematics and computer science, University of Wisconsin–Madison MS in computer science, University of Wisconsin–Madison Ph.D. in computer science, University of Minnesota Biography His research career has focused on compiler technology for high-performance computing.
