Spiral Swimmer

· archived # 1,462,162
Here are photos of making the Spiral Swimmer beginning with a 1.5" x 1.5" piece of wood. The piece is first turned on the lathe. The piece must be bored all the way through its center to accommodate a centering support dowel (5/8").? The spiral pattern was drawn on the uncut wood blank. A bandsaw was used to kerf the material to be removed. Beginning to remove the unwanted material.
