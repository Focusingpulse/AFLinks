The tip of the rod is coloured to improve automated tracking and recording of the angle. The rod is suspended by a thin nylon thread. The construction is fragile but sensitive. If left alone and protected from air currents by the casing the rod spins with speed of around 30 degrees per hour. The speed can vary. The direction of rotation changes over time, creating a random like signal. If the angle is recorded over time, an unusual long continues rotation in one direction followed by a similar long counter rotation appears to look like a peak. Kozyrev used this as a sensitive detector for his time impact that is related to torsion [73].
