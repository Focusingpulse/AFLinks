This model considers the same potential V(r) used in the Model of Layers, but with an additional consideration: the nucleus can suffer distortions. The distortion allows to the model to behave in order to reproduce the electric quadrupole moment and the magnetic moments, according to Nuclear Theory. Nevertheless, if a nuclear model has the ambition of reproducing theoretically the quadrupole moment, it must have some nuclear features, otherwise it will be unable to reproduce the phenomenon.
