Kresenn Ltd, 5a Frascati Way, Maidenhead SL6 1PA, United Kingdom * E-mail of the corresponding author: Francesco.Celani@lnf.infn.it Abstract This study shows in detail how even a low cost material, like commercial Cu-Ni-Mn alloy (named Konstantan or ISOTAN 44), as far its surface is properly modified from the point of view of dimensionality, can be used as material able to produce anomalous heat effects due to close interaction with Hydrogen (or Deuterium, but at lower intensity) at high temperature. Keywords: ISOTAN, Konstantan, hydrogen, deuterium, anomalous heat, nano structure, copper-nickel alloy 1.
