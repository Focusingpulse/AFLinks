When I was at the science fair last week, with my project "Basic Aether Engineering", I saw another project which did vapor carbs. I talked to the two guys, who were in Gr.9, and they showed me how it worked. They took a lawn mower engine off a small push lawn mower, and removed the blade, etc. The engine had only a few minor changes made. The gas tank was heated by a in-line heater, to 37 degrees Celsius. They had a hose going into the gas from the outside to keep the pressure equalized in the tank. They ran a vacuum line from the top of the tank over the motor, to the other side of the motor. The carb was removed, and gas fumes were fed directly into the motor. They had some thing that let air in, without letting the fumes out, at this point as well. I don't know the all the correct terms for all the pieces, but they were simple things you should be able to pick up at hardware stores. They also had a electronic timing system to help the engine run better. The normal idle time for the engine was 4 hours, but with this vapor carb it was a bit over 16 hours! And they were still using flea temps and pressure! My cousin, who is very good with motors, was there too and we went over it, and figured out how it worked. We had designed a very similar system just with information from the net. We never built it, but now we will. This summer we are planning to build this lawnmower experiment.
