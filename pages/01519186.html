Title A Proposed Unified Theory of Human Nature Based Upon Thermodynamics, Electrodynamics and Emergence Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Robert Marion LaFollette , Lou Ellen LaFollette Keywords Unified Theory, electrodynamics, Thermodynamics Published 2011 Journal Proceedings of the NPA Volume 8 No. of pages 12 Pages 327-339 Abstract This theory envisions a universe which is finite and competitive rather than infinite and harmonious. It is a universe which is lawful, yet unpredictable in many of its emergent expressions. It is a causal universe, but not a determined universe. It is a universe which can be understood within the potential of the "tools" presently available. This is not to suggest that we (the generic "we") are there yet. It is a "work in progress." Overview Robert M. and Lou Ellen LaFollette present a synopsis of a book they had been writing since 1970, provisionally titled The Politically Incorrect Guide to Human Nature . Its ambition is to run a single causal chain from the laws of thermodynamics up through chemistry, biology, psychology and political organisation. The organising question, posed in an earlier NPA abstract, is "What drives the formation of complex structure?" Their answer is the second law of thermodynamics: increasing entropy means, on their reading, that "all matter is programmed to give up energy", and the energy so released is what forms the bonds out of which new structure is made. The rules governing how those bonds actually form are electromagnetic. Two further pillars carry the argument to human affairs. The first is a proposed corollary to the conservation law: in a universe with a finite amount of matter and energy, "some things necessarily exist to the exclusion of other things", so competition for existence is a core principle of the universe rather than a merely biological fact.
