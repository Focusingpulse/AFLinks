(1-4) Various arguments have been invoked, some suggesting that symmetry breaking effects similar to those observed in the Standard Model play a dominant role. (1-3) Other authors suggest that the feebleness of gravitational coupling reflects a natural cut-off in the frequency of electromagnetic waves composing the vacuum. (4) These arguments, while useful, fail to generate a straightforward derivation of Newton's constant. Be that as it may, there are mechanisms which produce close approximations.
