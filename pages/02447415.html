Introduction The aim of this work is to find correlation between local deuterium concentration and bulk and surface characteristics of the cathodes used in pulsed electrolysis. A high peak current (about 15 A) and very short pulses (800 ns) generator has been used to perform electrolysis in 0.3 N LiOD-D20 solution. We tested pure palladium, palladium-silver, palladium-cerium and palladium-yttrium alloys, to see if a higher hardness (A g) or the addition of a rare earth (Ce, Y), that has a high capability of absorbing deuterium, could be useful for increasing D/Pd ratio. We also tried to verify if a surface oxidization treatment [2] influences the local concentration of deuterium.
