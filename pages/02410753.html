Pearson wrote three books in a series, but only his first book survives - Psycho-Harmonial Philosophy - which includes these famous colored plates; which I've pulled directly from this original source. You'll see these elsewhere on Facebook, but copy after copy with no context. Pearson lists but a few references, including Andrew Jackson Davis (The Great Harmonia), C.W. Leadbeater (Theosophy), and freemasonry. Pearson writes about cosmogeny, spirit and matter, auras, natural human vibrations, clairvoyance and telepathy, polarity, harmony, the cosmic scale of vibration, and human spiritual evolution. He starts putting it all together with the Chromatic Scale. "Does God geometrize? is the question asked by the author of "Arabula" when he was sitting on the bank of a stream in Texas, examining the petals of a certain flower." "In this chapter we shall endeavor to show that nature's laws are grounded on geometry, that the universal plan of world building is in perfect conformity to geometry and mathematics-that the masonic emblems, the square, triangle, compasses and plumb line, go hand in hand With geometry, mathematics and music. The square, triangle and compasses are geometrical figures founded upon musical laws, and inasmuch as nature is established upon a musical basis, these masonic emblems become symbolical expressions of nature's laws."
