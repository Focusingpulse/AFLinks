Demystifying Eric Dollard Part 1: Planck's Energy Equation and the Electric Wave Watch this video on YouTube Talk Type Video Speaker Lori Anne Gardi Date 16 February 2021 Platform YouTube Recording Recording Demystifying Eric Dollard Part 1: Planck's Energy Equation and the Electric Wave is a video by Lori Anne Gardi , published on 16 February 2021 on her FractalWoman YouTube channel. Summary The first instalment of a review of Eric Dollard's 1985 paper Symbolic Representation of the Generalized Electric Wave , in which Gardi sets out to show that the equation Dollard opens with is Planck's energy equation in disguise, and that the electrical and optical domains are the same domain written in different notations. Provenance and a long detour through her own history The paper was sent to her by a viewer named Martin, whom she thanks. She calls it action-packed and warns that covering it properly may take two or three videos. Its date matters to the account she gives: 1985. That was the year she finished her undergraduate degree in computer science, and the summer she began working full time for the astronomy department, having started there as a summer student in 1983. She stayed until 1993, when she moved into medical imaging. The digression that follows is unusually long for her, and she offers it as background on why she is not approaching an electrical engineering paper as an outsider. Everything she knew at the time about electronics and computers she learned in that department, apprenticed to her boss Gray Simons and the electrical technician Mike DeBroon. She is blunt about the comparison with her formal education: the ten-year apprenticeship was immensely more useful to her life than any course she took, though she values the degree for having let her stay in a university community she loved.
