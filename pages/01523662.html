On this wiki it is also the name of a dispute: most of the researchers catalogued here hold that twentieth-century physics took a wrong turn, and that the turn was philosophical before it was empirical. This page is an entry point. Over 3,000 researchers and some 20,000 papers are catalogued on this wiki, and the substantive arguments live on the topic pages linked below. For Roland H.
