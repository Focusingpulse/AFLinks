Magic carpet could raise fall alarm for elderly

· archived # 2,446,064
09/05/12 - Magic carpet could raise fall alarm for elderly There's help for the elderly with the invention of a carpet that can help raise an alarm when they're heading for a fall. The Daily Mail reports British researchers say their invention could sound a warning before the fall, simply by monitoring a person's every step. Plastic optical fibres woven into the carpet are the key to the new system from the University of Manchester. Sensors relay signals to a computer and can show a steady deterioration or change in walking habits. The inventors believe the technology could be used in carpets at care homes and hospital wards, as well as people's homes if necessary.
