This paper closes the one step they left open and then follows the carry out of the adder. The open step closes as a two-block statement. A single unread gap of depth g with bg ≥ m splits the reader's state count into the product of the two sides (455 of 455 instances), and the conditional product law follows by induction. More strongly, the count always factorizes over the blocks that such gaps separate, whatever the narrower gaps do (1,349 of 1,349 cuts). The threshold is shown to mean that the gap can impose every carry value, not merely one, and the interface is shown to obey an exact budget, transmit + impose = m + 1. Closed forms are given for the reach and for the entrance ramp of the reading profile. The carry chain itself turns out to be classical. It is Holte's matrix, and its base-independent eigenvectors and b −j eigenvalues are what the series had been rederiving. What is new is the observer. A change of base is then read as a whole divided down: the digits are the residues of repeated division and the carry is the quotient in transit. Residues of this kind carry behaviour wherever a repeating unit exists, and the paper asks where the repeating unit comes from. Five mechanisms are given with their mathematics: closure, balance, instability, coupling and history.
