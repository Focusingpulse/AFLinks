GC2026-PERM013 Paleomagnetic studies of bottom sediments of the Bering Sea Egor A. Kuznetsov1 , Tansylu I. Khosnullina1,2 , Alexey V. Bochkarev2 , Yaroslav D. Yarzhembovsky2 , Victor A. Bogin2 1 St Petersburg University, St. Petersburg, Russia 2 All-Russian Scientific Research Institute of Geology and Mineral Resources of the World Ocean named after Academician I. S. Gramberg, Russia egor.kuz05@bk.ru The waters of the Russian Arctic and Far Eastern shelf possess significant resource potential. To explore this potential, as part of the creation of the 1:1000000-scale State Geological Map, the Federal State Budgetary Institution «VNIIOkeangeologiya» conducted expeditions in two key areas in 2024–2025: in the area of the Commander Islands (Sheet N-58) and on the Bering Sea shelf (Sheet R-1). The primary methods used were seismic acoustic profiling and bottom sampling. Preliminary results of paleomagnetic studies of cores collected in the Russian sector of the Bering Sea (PMK-2420T, PMK-2401T, and PMK-2410T collected in 2024, and PMB￾2527T collected in 2025) are presented. Previously, unlike the deep-sea surveys of IODP Expedition 323 [1], such data were virtually nonexistent. Materials and Methodology. During the 2024–2025 expeditions, bottom sampling was performed at 56 stations using a box corer for undisturbed surface samples and a 4.5-meter straight-through gravity corer. Magnetic susceptibility measurements were taken for the PMK-2401T, PMK-2410T, PMK-2420T, and PMB-2527T cores using a portable meter.
