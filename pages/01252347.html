INTRODUCTION Boundary layer is a transition layer of velocity adjacent to a solid surface, and it is the place where vorticity is created. Boundary layer can be separated from the wall. Separated layer with vorticity is a common source of the vorticity in flow fields. All these essential properties of the boundary layer were established in the celebrated paper [1] of Prandtl in 1904. Given a velocity field v(x), the vorticity ω is defined by ω = rot v, which is a measure of local rotation of a fluid element. From the point of view of Str¨omungslehre, one of the important characteristics of the boundary layer is that it pumps vorticity into the flow field. Prandtl explained in the same paper [1] that the boundary layer is prone to be separated from the solid wall when pressure increases along the direction of flow, and showed some experimental evidence in the case of flows around a circular cylinder. Along a cylinder in a free stream, the pressure increases over the rear part of the 1 cylinder surface.
