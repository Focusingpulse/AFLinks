BS EN 60745-2-3:2011+A13:2015 National foreword This British Standard is the UK implementation of EN 60745-2-3:2011+A13:2015. It is derived from IEC 60745-2-3:2006, incorporating amendments 1:2010 and 2:2012, and corrigendum February 2011. It supersedes BS EN 60745-2-3:2011+A12:2014, which is withdrawn. A list of organizations represented on this committee can be obtained on request to its secretary. BSI, as a member of CENELEC, is obliged to publish EN 60745-2-3 as a British Standard.
