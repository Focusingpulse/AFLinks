Thyroid Herbs -- Chinese Traditional Medicine patents rexresearch Thyroid Herbs "Traditional Chinese Medicine" (TCM) Patents TRADITIONAL CHINESE MEDICINE COMPOSITION AND PREPARATION METHOD AND APPLICATION THEREOF CN118434431 A traditional Chinese medicine composition for treating thyroid nodules, lipomas, mammary gland hyperplasia and mouth ulcers, the composition comprising as a primary formula of main active ingredients three medicinal/edible source materials consisting of: Lonicera japonica or Lonicera hypoglauca or a mixture of Lonicera japonica and Lonicera hypoglauca, and Taxacum and Prunella vulgaris . The foundation of the primary formula is supplemented by a secondary formula consisting of Scleromitrion diffusa, Hippophae rhamnoides, Poncirus trifoliata and Pholidota , and different preparations are prepared, suitable for different types of thyroid nodules. The traditional Chinese medicine combined formula can be a combined formula of source herbs, and can likewise be a combined formula of extracts thereof, and can likewise be a combined formula of source herbs and extracts. The traditional Chinese medicine composition has a significant therapeutic effect on symptoms of an enlarged thyroid gland caused by inflammation, autoimmunity and new organisms.
