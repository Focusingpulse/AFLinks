We have estimated the recycling factors for the charged particles in fusion reactions with consumable nuclei d, 6 Li and 7 Li. It is concluded that, based on the established nuclear fusion cross sections and electronic stopping power, the recycling factor is four to five orders of magnitude less than required for close to critical conditions.
