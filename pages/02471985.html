The system is based upon a novel principle, which is very clearly set forth in the claim of one of the patents, namely, producing a continuously progressive rotation of the polarities of either or both elements (the armature or field magnet or magnets) of a motor, by developing alternating currents in independent circuits, including the magnetizing coils of either or both elements; and as a modification of this, shifting the poles of one element by alternating currents while magnetizing the other element by direct or continuous current.
