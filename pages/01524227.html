Overview This theory, based on observation and astute insight, is that the atom is a vortexian structure, rather than the "swarm of flies" type of atom conceived through high energy electrical experiments. Also included is an Alternative Explanation for the Red Shift, and the Constitution of the Sun and Stars. About the author Carl Frederick Krafft (b. 1892) was an American engineer who defended a 'hydromechanical' ether and a vortex-atom model of matter.
