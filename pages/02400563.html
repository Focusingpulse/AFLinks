Positional digit extraction requires an integration endpoint u that is simultaneously a mark coordinate of the frame (u = cos θ for a frame angle θ) and a half-integer power of an integer radix (u n = 1/b, b ∈ ℤ). This paper proves that these two conditions, taken together, admit exactly two angles: θ = π/3 and θ = π/4. The proof is an intersection statement between a real-cyclotomic field and the rational powers of 2. The key object is x = 2 cos θ = ζₙ + ζₙ⁻¹, an algebraic integer; the radix condition forces x n = 2n−k, hence x is a dyadic rational power of 2; and the abelian nature of the maximal real cyclotomic field forces that power to have denominator at most 2. The two special right-triangle cosines survive. The minimal frames realizing the two angles are (b, n) = (64, 6) and (16, 8); every admissible frame lies on one of two towers rooted at these minima.
