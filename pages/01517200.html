Accordingly, the force which we call gravitation, he called 'sheer concentrated protonic energy'. He supported his theory with a design of an antigravity machine which liberated itself from the force of gravity by an electric field rotating at the speed of light around the vehicle. This rotating field also suppressed mass inertia! His design principle was tested successfully on small pieces of mica. http://www.meridian-int-res.com/Aeronautics/APS.htm Marcel Pages was one of the leading French post-war researchers in this field.
