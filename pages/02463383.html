Abstract In the Aether Physics Model (APM), space and matter arise from a unified volumetric– chronovibrational structure defined by the Aether unit. While volumetric coherence establishes the substrate-level origin of orbital behavior, the emergence of subatomic particles requires a mechanism by which matter becomes localized within this primary structure. This paper develops a model in which vibrating dark-matter strings, produced during Singularity bifurcation, interact with Aether units. A subatomic particle forms when an Aether unit captures a finite segment of mass string and closes it into a ligamen circulatus subject to loxodromic confinement. In this framework, particle identity, mass, spin, and magnetic charge arise from the geometry of confinement, specifically from the allowed combinations of loxodromic winding and signed torsion within an invariant Aether-unit surface measure. Photon propagation is interpreted as the transfer state of the same ligamen circulatus after release from confinement, so that matter and radiation become complementary boundary conditions of a single underlying process. Forces arise only when confinement activates magnetic charge, and the resulting magnetic interaction law governs the dynamics between confined states without serving as the origin of the Aether unit itself.
