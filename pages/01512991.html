This means that the more you change the future, the more you change the past. Loops of causality form from this. Loops fall upon stable paths or diverge over their billions of iterations. If you invent a device that allows mankind to get off of the planet and move throughout all the stars to make massive engineering projects that affect immense amounts of matter and antimatter, then many of these loops run through you. These loops run through a stable valley that you, as that person, must march through. Let's say that you notice that whenever you try to march up the slope, you are pushed back down into the valley. Then let's say that you decide to base your actions on pure randomness to defeat this valley of fate. You begin to notice that random is no longer random. Now, I have seen this non-randomness.
