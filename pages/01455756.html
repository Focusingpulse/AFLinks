Cayce (Q) Anger causes poisons to be secreted from the glands. Joy has the opposite effect. The adrenal glands are principally involved, reacting through the solar plexus to all parts of the body. (A) The adrenals principally, but ALL of the glands are involved; as: A nursing mother would find that anger would affect the mammary glands. One pregnant would find the digestive glands affected. The liver , the kidneys and ALL glands are affected; though it is correct that the reaction is PRINCIPALLY through the adrenals . [ Cayce , 281-54 ] " Anger is correct, provided it is governed."
