In German the term for shear force is ' Schubkraft ', meaning 'to push, to shove' as well as 'to shear', whereas Viktor Schauberger uses the word ' Schleppkraft '. The verb ' schleppen ' means to drag , draw or pull . Viktor Schauberger 's choice of ' Schleppkraft ' here is quite specific, since in his view the movement of sediment is due to the sucking action of fast flowing, dense cold water downstream , rather than to the mechanical impact of the water coming from upstream .
