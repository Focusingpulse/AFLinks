Decker: "Lightning & Tornaoes" Right, my thoughts on the yogi - anti-time stuff mentioned elsewhere on the list: All things, from all times, past, present, and possible futures all exist in the same place, separated by frequencies which we interpret as time, distance, etc... This suggests that all information from all times exists in the same place...always. My theory on the yogi with the ability to "turn back time" to rejuvenate and repair their body, is that they don't actually turn back time, but "replay" the information that exists at different frequencies around us, in reverse-time order. It is kind of like making a recording on a tape. The event has already happened, but still remains on the tape. If you want to experience what happened you play the tape, if you want to experience it in reverse, you play the tape backwards. It would be far easier to do this than to try and reverse time itself.
