Shebalin1 1 Institute of Earthquake Prediction Theory and Mathematical Geophysics, Russian Academy of Sciences 2 Lomonosov Moscow State University, Moscow, Russia 3 Unified Geophysical Service of the Russian Academy of Sciences molokovaap@my.msu.ru One of the key parameters of regional seismicity is the characteristic depth of recorded earthquakes. Like the parameters of the Gutenberg-Richter law or the maximum possible magnitude, depths significantly influence the resulting seismic hazard and seismic risk assessments [1]. Сurrently, despite the increased number of seismic stations, earthquake catalogs for the Russian Federation still frequently contain fixed depth values [2]. Clearly, this approach ultimately leads to misconceptions of depth distributions in the region. Most importantly, such values are completely unsuitable for seismic hazard assessment, synthetic catalog modeling, and other tasks aimed at obtaining the most reliable estimates of macroseismic manifestations and probable impacts on infrastructure.
