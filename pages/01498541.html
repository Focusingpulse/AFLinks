As sponsors of the conference, we at NCFI and the University of Utah wish to thank you for your participation in the conference. We found that the exchange of ideas and information at the conference stimulated new experimental and theoretical research at our institute, and hope that many others were similarly prompted in their study of the interesting phenomena which were discussed during our meetings. The papers which are presented in the enclosed proceedings will give interested readers an in-depth perspective on the material which was presented at the conference. Like the conference itself, we feel the proceedings will lead researchers to a greater depth of understanding in this emerging field of inquiry. We wish to extend our thanks to those who prepared the material presented in this volume, to those who attended the conference, and to those who will pursue research in these important phenomena.
