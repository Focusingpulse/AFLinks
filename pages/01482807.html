qutrit

· archived # 1,482,807
Physicists just confirmed a genuinely three-valued quantum logic state — a qutrit — that cannot be reduced to any combination of classical binary states, opening computational possibilities that qubit-based quantum computers cannot access even in principle. A team at the Austrian Academy of Sciences manipulated individual photons into superpositions of three orthogonal polarization states simultaneously — zero, one, and two — using a specially designed optical fiber interferometer with three-arm geometry. Quantum state tomography confirmed all three values coexisted with equal probability amplitude and genuine quantum coherence across all three simultaneously — not a statistical mixture. The qutrit coherence survived 2.1 milliseconds — longer than most qubit coherence times — at room temperature using the photon's orbital angular momentum degree of freedom. A single qutrit encodes log2(3) equals 1.585 bits of information — 58.5% more than a qubit. Ten qutrits compute across 3 to the power of 10 equals 59,049 simultaneous states versus 1,024 for ten qubits — a 57-fold computational density advantage for the same number of physical particles. Source: Austrian Academy of Sciences Institute for Quantum Optics, Science, 2024
