Remember me? Been out of the loop for a while and designed this motor about a year ago but only just got around to having the parts made. It runs off a basic Bedini SSG circuit with 150 turn primary and 75 turn trigger winding. There are 6 neo magnets in a NSNSNS configuration. There is only one coil on this design that wraps around the rotor in a way to minimize parasitic resistance. The distance between the windings is double the diameter of the windings for maximum Q. (ala Brooks) In the video the motor is running off the small 1.5w solar panel. In the middle of November in the UK the sun isn't all that bright but it still runs quite nicely.
