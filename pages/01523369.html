It is among the oldest observed effects in physics and among the least explained. Mainstream physics describes magnetism with great precision and declines to say what it is ; a large part of the literature catalogued on this wiki holds that this is not a limitation to be tolerated but the central problem, and that magnetism has a mechanical cause which can be shown.
