Fermion ( click to enlarge ) Fermion General name for a particle that is a matter constituent, characterized by spin in odd half integer quantum units ( 1 / 2 , 3 / 2 , 5 / 2 ...) (independent of frequency and energy level). Named for Italian physicist Enrico Fermi . Quarks , leptons and baryons are all fermions . As a consequence of this peculiar angular momentum , fermions obey a rule called the Pauli Exclusion Principle , which states that no two fermions can exist in the same state at the same time. Many of the properties of ordinary matter arise because of this rule.
