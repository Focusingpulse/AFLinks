They run on hydrogen that can be produced from water, check out the site http://www.plugpower.com Plug Power was originally formed as a joint venture between DTE Energy Co., (the parent of Detroit Edison and Michigan's largest electric utility), and Mechanical Technology Inc.or MTI (an early developer of fuel cell technologies). In 1999, General Electric and Southern California Gas Company, a subsidiary of Sempra Energy joined as equity holders in the company. Our goal is to develop and manufacture affordable fuel cell systems for residential, small commercial and automotive applications. Plug Power is the largest PEM fuel cell development company in the US, and we have the resources, desire and determination to be the best. The company was formed in June 1997 with 22 people and $9 million in funding. Since that date, our employee base has grown to more than 260 employees, and we have also received a $15 million award from the US Department of Energy (DOE). This is the single largest award ever granted for automotive fuel cell research. Three months after receiving this award, Plug Power, along with the DOE, its Los Alamos National Laboratory, and Arthur D. Little, was first (and to our knowledge is still the only) team to demonstrate a gasoline to electricity fuel cell (see Press Releases). The fuel cell used in this test continues to perform extremely well in our laboratory today, and in May 1999 we demonstrated a high efficiency, low emission integrated automotive fuel cell system.
