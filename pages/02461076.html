Decker: "Re: Science Fair motor runs 5 days on 9VDC battery" Hi Folks! John Bedini is up to mischief again, but mischief of the kind we all seem to hunger for...<g>...he sent an interesting video which I have now posted along with some stills showing a motor built by a 10 year old girl and entered in several science fairs to win prizes. Though I don't know if this motor ever ran down, John says it ran for 5 days and powered an LED, all from a 9VDC transistor as the single power source. He just sent more details which I added to the original file which is all now posted at; http://www.keelynet.com/bedmot/bedmot.htm along with the page at his site which gives the details on how to build such a motor. These Gravity field motors got John and his brother in a lot of trouble back in the 1980's I believe when they were both warned NOT to sell them. You'll have to get the story from him but he chose rather than risk problems to just shut up. It is only in the last few years with Internet that he decided to create his own website and post his plans for free so that anyone could experiment with them and they could be copied by many. John says often people try to build the motors, but they always decid on making changes in the design since they don't truly understand how it can work as designed...those changes defeat the original results and people go away frustrated, saying the motor plans are bogus. As John points out, his best success comes from kids who follow the plans exactly, without using their 'learned knowledge'. -- KeelyNet - From an Art to a Science Jerry W.
