The thick wiring seen around the horseshoe, and at other places in the circuit is normal for high-voltage high-frequency circuits, whereby the electric current moves on the outer surface of the wire rather inside it. This 'skin effect' is counteracted by the use of multi-strand "Litz" wire or by using thick tubing (of 1/8" or 3mm dia).
