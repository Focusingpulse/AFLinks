fundamental note

· archived # 1,471,358
And as the notes of this family are born of one fundamental note , so the chords into which they are grouped are like so many younger families. The fundamental note is the common root of three chords , each of which is a little family of notes having its own generating root ; although the top of the first is the root of the second , and the top of the second, the root of the third . This also is illustrated in the Genesis of the Scale . [ Scientific Basis and Build of Music , page 20] See Also Fundamental keynote
