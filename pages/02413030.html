An upward force in the Lifter confirmed by Greg Vizza and Francis Daran Pittsburgh Lifters Group Pittsburgh Pennsylvania USA Gregg Vizza and Daran Francis Tubular Lifter Experiments Original source : http://www.gravityforum.com/lifters/usa/pittsburgh/tubular-lifter/ Two identical tubular lifters were constructed. The only difference between the two being that one had a foil skirt, and one was tubes only. Both lifters had exactly the same grams-lift/watt ratio of 16gm at 73Kv-1.5ma. The foil tubes were not fastened to the balsa frame. With the tubes hung loosely on the frame it easily confirmed that the lift was being produced by the aluminum being pulled upward.
