The Dionysian Course III — GA 277a. The Origin and Development of Eurythmy 1912–1918

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 18 September 1912 · archived # 2,439,844
, Binningen-Bottmingen Translated by Steiner Online Library The Dionysian Course III How often in later years did I regret that Miss von Sivers, later Mrs. Marie Steiner, only began attending the afternoon sessions, which started at 4:30 p.m. and usually lasted until 6 p.m., Dr. Steiner's favorite meal time, the “Jause,” on this third day, which I am about to describe. On that day, Dr. Steiner brought a book with him, Brevier der Tanzkunst (Breviary of Dance Art). I was to read it up to a point marked with energetic pencil strokes and, if possible, copy it out. But really only up to that point
