Collins New Naturalist Library 7 Mushrooms & Toadstools John Ramsbottom Editors: JAMES FISHER M.A. JOHN GILMOUR M.A. JULIAN S. HUXLEY M.A. D.Sc. F.R.S. L. DUDLEY STAMP B.A. D.Sc. PHOTOGRAPHIC EDITOR: ERIC HOSKING F.R.P.S. The aim of this series is to interest the general reader in the wild life of Britain by recapturing the inquiring spirit of the old naturalist. The Editors believe that the natural pride of the British public in the native fauna and flora, to which must be added concern for their conservation, are best fostered by maintaining a high standard of accuracy combined with clarity of exposition in presenting the results of modern scientific research. The plants and animals are described in relation to their homes and habitats and are portrayed in the full beauty of their natural colours, by the latest methods of colour photography and reproduction.
