My best guess of the number of magnets around the circumference of his main rotor was between 28-33 as there was no way to know for sure by observation and still shots of the videos. So I then started drawing each of the five guesses between 28 magnets to 33 magnets and discovered only the 30 magnet position formed 30 perfect triangles, it was the only choice I believed worked and also gave the angle of the holes to be drilled into the large disk so that the torque pushed not towards the center but between the center and the outside of the rotor I thought I would share this and a pic of the template that I came up with.
