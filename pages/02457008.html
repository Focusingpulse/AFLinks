One of the things that bugged me was my inability to measure voltages that exceeded 700 volts AC. This made working with multi-kilovolt inverters kinda sticky business. I never knew exactly what my output levels were and I trashed many expensive components. It now seems so basic and obvious to me now what the solution is but it wasn't then. I hope to save those who are as HV naive as I was from committing the same dollar intensive mistakes. The answer came from an old T.V. technician I know. He was from the old days of (gasp) vacuum tube circuitry. "You need a voltage shunt!" Ok, what the hell was this guy talking about?
