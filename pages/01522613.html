He is listed in The Worldwide List of Dissident Scientists . Work Laster published a series of papers through the General Science Journal in 2011, including "A More Universal Atomic Model", "Spin and Charge in Particles", "The Electron a Double Solution", and "Pair Production: The Origin of Charge, Mass, and CP Symmetry". Building on Wolff's Wave Structure of Matter, he proposes treating the electron as a standing-wave center formed by intersecting spherical waves, aiming at an atomic model he argues is compatible with quantum mechanics while remaining amenable to classical description.
