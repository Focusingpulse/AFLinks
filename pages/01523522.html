Nick Summers Residence United Kingdom Nationality English Known for Gravity Nick Summers (also published as Nicholas Summers ) is an English independent researcher and author known for his work on the nature of Gravity , matter and dark energy. He is best known as the author of the 2012 book Matter, Gravity and Dark Energy , in which he explores the causes of gravity and the relationship between matter and dark energy from an aether -based perspective. His name appears on the Worldwide List of Dissident Scientists. Biography Summers is based in the United Kingdom. He works outside the mainstream academic community as an independent author, presenting his ideas about gravity and cosmology for a general readership rather than through formal academic channels. Work Summers's work centres on the questions of what causes Gravity and how matter relates to dark energy. He approaches these problems from a philosophical rather than a mathematical standpoint, and his writing is aimed at the general reader. In 2012 he published Matter, Gravity and Dark Energy , an ebook that presents his views on gravity and matter's relationship with dark energy. The book contains no mathematics and is described as a philosophical treatment of some of science's unanswered questions, intended to be accessible to readers without a specialist background, though some prior knowledge of physical principles is helpful. He has also circulated draft material developing a dynamic theory of the aether , in which gravity, light, electricity and magnetism, and matter are treated as manifestations of a single underlying medium.
