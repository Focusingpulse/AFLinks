Mike Dibell 9-23-2025 It would be difficult for me to try and introduce a man like Chester Lee Hopson (Nickname Bones). He was a very abstract thinking person and I had some of the same thoughts he had only he was years ahead of me in the experiments he did and some of the things he built. We would get together and brain storm for hours over some of these ideas. I was shown his ideas for a free energy device and a proposal for a propulsion system and it blew my mind. This device was also shown with skepticism to some of the scientist that worked at JPL. But... Not all the scientist turned their noses up at what they were shown, a few of them were intrigued at what they were shown. Over the years Bones got the reputation for being a very deep abstract thinker. He received an invitation to participate in the retrieval of a down UFO. Bones turn down this opportunity and knowing him the way I did it wasn’t difficult to understand why. He told me once that a man only has so many miles to walk the earth and he didn’t want to give up any of that to someone else. He would have had to signed an NDA, and wouldn’t have had the freedom to share information and he just didn’t want to give up that freedom. Even though he didn’t participate in the retrieval of the craft he was given a follow up on this event. It seemed that the craft when loaded on a flatbed was heavy.
