Intervals, scales, keys, and chords having intervals a semitone less than major . John Stainer Less, smaller. (1) intervals are said to be minor when they contain one semitone less than major . (2) A scale is said to be in the minor mode when its third and sixth are minor . Formerly minor music was described as "with the lesser third ." (3) Flute minor , Klein flute, s small flute-stop on the organ, or 4 ft. or 2 ft pitch.
