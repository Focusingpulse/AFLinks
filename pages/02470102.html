GREAT IS TESLA. Electricity’s Master Welcomed Amid Wild Enthusiasm. HIS ORATION A GEM. STETSON’S APPEAL FOR AN EXTENSION OF TIME ON THE FRANCHISE. THAT WAS THE KEY-NOTE. New-Yorker Gave a Detailed Account of the Company’s Progress. NEARLY 400 GUESTS WERE PRESENT IN THE GREAT DINING-HALL OF THE CLUB—MAYOR JEWETT RESPONDED TO THE TOAST “THE CITY OF BUFFALO”—COMPTROLLER ROBERTS UPHELD THE STATE OF NEW-YORK IN A GOOD BIT OF ORATORY — ALMOST EVERY MAN OF MONEY OR BRAINS WHO HAS BEEN CONNECTED WITH THE HARNESSING OF THE CATARACT WAS AT THE TABLE — TEXT OF NIKOLA TESLA’S SCHOLARLY SPEECH, AND THE ADDRESS READ BY MR. STETSON — THE BANQUETERS GAVE TESLA A “CHAUTAUQUA SALUTE” Editorial note: This transcription of the Buffalo Express, January 13, 1897, report on the Ellicott Club power banquet was prepared from a damaged two-page scan. Large stretches of type are washed out, splotched, or broken at column edges. Where the print could be read letter by letter, the wording, punctuation, and period spellings were kept as they appear. Where the scan failed, the missing lines were restored by: reading adjacent columns and matching wrap-around sentences; comparing the Tesla address with the text printed as “On Electricity” in Electrical Review, January 27, 1897 (the same speech given that night); checking contemporary Niagara / Cataract company
