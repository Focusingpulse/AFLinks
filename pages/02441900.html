Thirty-Second Meeting—GA 300. Faculty Meetings with Rudolf Steiner (1998)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 10 May 1922 · archived # 2,441,900
, Stuttgart Thirty-Second Meeting Dr. Steiner: I want to discuss a number of important points. A teacher: What should we do in the eleventh-grade art class? Dr. Steiner: It is certainly possible to cover the relationship of art to the development of culture, so that the students have a good understanding of that. You could point out why music as we understand it today arose relatively late. What the Greeks called music, and so forth. Do such things.
