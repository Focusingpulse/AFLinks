Chauvet, jerome.chauvet.msc@gmail.com, Nîmes (France) September, 25th 2025 Abstract We adress here the TPC (Twin Primes Conjecture). Considering the unique growing list of twin primes as elements of a dynamical system with invariant transformation law on ℕ , we infer a lower boundary for their density along the real axis incompatible with their finiteness.
