Inlet Valve Area Even though the Great Pyramid has been badly damaged through the centuries one can still get an idea of how the moving parts looked and functioned before they were destroyed. This check valve is one of these areas. The original "valve seat" is still there as are the 8 inch sockets that held the moving part. This "pivoting door" makes for a very good check valve but a very poor barrier to tomb robbers.
