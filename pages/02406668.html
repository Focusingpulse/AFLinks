Communication Publisher: Dokvej 3 6700 Esbjerg Denmark www.offshoreenergy.dk An overview of the North Sea offshore industry OffshoreBook Oil & Gas serves as an introduction to the offshore oil and gas industry. Through a number of easy to read and clear chapters, the industry is presented at a basic level, suitable for everybody. The target group is new employees, students and employees in need for an overview or insight into specialities different from their own. OffshoreBook Oil & Gas is mainly focused on Danish and North Sea conditions but is also suitable for offshore industries based elsewhere. OffshoreBook Oil & Gas is 3rd edition of the OffshoreBook edited by the Danish knowledge center and innovation network Offshoreenergy. dk in collaboration with external editors.
