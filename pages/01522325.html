Title A Reinterpretation of the von Klitzing and Josephson constants Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Michael Jefferson Lawrence Keywords Planck , units, von Klitzing, Josephson, G, gravitational constant, dimensional analysis, metrology Published 2009 No. of pages 6 Abstract A consideration of the von Klitzing and Josephson constants is made, showing what their values would be in a system with Planck sized charges and reinterpreting the relationships uncovered. The reinterpretation is based on a dimensional analysis that concludes with the representation of many Planck and fundamental parameters in terms of ratios of only R k and K j or only h and c and may enable advances in metrology using the more precisely known values of R k and K j . Overview Michael Lawrence, of Maldwyn Theoretical Physics, constructs a unit system in which the von Klitzing and Josephson constants appear as natural maxima, and argues that the resulting relationships between fundamental parameters may allow metrological advances using values of R K and K J that are known more precisely than h . He begins from what he calls Adjusted Planck Units, built on h rather than ħ so that M 0 = √( hc / G ) and L 0 = h / M 0 c , which removes the factors of 2π that would otherwise clutter the analysis. He then defines Double Adjusted Planck Units by absorbing the gravitational constant into the mass and length: M * = M 0 √ G and L * = L 0 /√ G . The values of c and h are unchanged. To make the charge and mass sides align, the base charge units are reduced by √(10 −7 ) and the permeability is defined as μ 0 = √ G rather than 4π × 10 −7 . A factor d = √(α/2π) is introduced as the ratio of the electronic to the unit charge. He anticipates the objection that absorbing G into a mass ignores its units, and answers with a dimensional argument.
