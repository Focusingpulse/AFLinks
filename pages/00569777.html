Solution of an Open Problem Concerning the Augmented Zagreb Index and Chromatic Number of Graphs Tariq Alraqad, Akbar Ali∗ , Hicham Saber Department of Mathematics, Faculty of Science, University of Ha’il, Ha’il, Saudi Arabia t.alraqad@uoh.edu.sa; akbarali.maths@gmail.com; Hicham.saber7@gmail.com Abstract Let G be a graph containing no component isomorphic to the path graph of order 2. Denote by dw the degree of a vertex w in G. The augmented Zagreb index (AZI) of G is the sum of the quantities (dudv/(du + dv − 2))3 over all edges uv of G. Denote by G(n, χ) the class of all connected graphs of a fixed order n and with a fixed chromatic number χ, where n ≥ 5 and 3 ≤ χ ≤ n−1. The problem of finding graph(s) attaining the maximum AZI in the class G(n, χ) has been solved recently in [F. Li, Q. Ye, H. Broersma, R. Ye, MATCH Commun. Math. Comput. Chem. 85 (2021) 257–274] for the case when n is a multiple of χ.
