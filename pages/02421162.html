Peter Grandics Residence Carlsbad, CA, United States Peter Grandics is a Hungarian-born chemical and biochemical engineer based in Carlsbad, California, who has worked in biomedical research and, more recently, on atmospheric energy harvesting. Education MS in chemical engineering PhD in biochemical engineering Work Grandics spent most of his career in biomedical research and bioseparation technology before turning to energy. His 2007 paper " The Pyramidal Electric Transducer ", published in Infinite Energy , proposes that the global atmospheric electric circuit could be tapped for commercial power by a conducting pyramid acting as a wideband antenna for electrostatic discharge impulses, coupled to a lumped-element resonant circuit that converts random impulses into damped sinusoidal wave trains. The paper combines bench measurements — swept-signal analyses of the coil system, differential measurements of energy transfer between primary and secondary windings, and discharge trains driven by a laboratory Van de Graaff generator — with an argument that the dimensional ratios of the Great Pyramid of Giza encode both sine-wave parameters and the golden ratio, and that the structure may therefore have been designed as an antenna. He holds patents on the transducer design and presented the work at the ExtraOrdinary Technology Conference in 2009.
