The present work proposes that the charges flowing in a wire and constituting the electric current drag the aether that exists outside and permeates the inside of the wire. This leads to the conclusion that the force discovered by Ampere is simply a Bernoulli effect occurring within the fluid aether. What is disputed here This page is one item in a larger argument.
