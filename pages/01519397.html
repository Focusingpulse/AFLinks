Title Bernoulli Equation for the Aether and Ampere's Effect Author(s) Ionel Dinu Keywords aether , Ampere effect, Bernoulli equation Published 2007 Journal Electric Spacecraft Journal Number 43 Pages 7-10 Abstract Ampere's effect is the force that develops between two current-carrying conductors, and no theory exists that explains how this effect actually occurs in nature. The present work proposes that the charges flowing in a wire and constituting the electric current drag the aether that exists outside and permeates the inside of the wire.
