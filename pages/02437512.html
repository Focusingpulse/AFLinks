Eulogy for Georga Wiese — GA 261. Our Dead

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 6 January 1924 · archived # 2,437,512
, Dornach Translated by Steiner Online Library Eulogy for Georga Wiese My dear friends! This morning the anthroposophical movement has suffered a great loss and experienced a great pain. Our dear friend Georga Wiese from Norway has passed away from the physical plane. It can fill us with a deep sense of tragedy when we consider that Georga Wiese worked intensively at the Goetheanum for many years, even after it had been destroyed.
