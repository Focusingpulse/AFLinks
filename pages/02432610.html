Simply Believe / Mask's Legacy

· archived # 2,432,610
It's amazing to me that this guy touched my life because he is know for his mixed martial arts brand, Tap Out. Because of some trauma in my past, I normally avoid anything to do with violence, fighting, crime, etc. But I found out about Mask's story by working as an extra on a movie, Warrior, that is being filmed here. Mask co-founded the now successful Tap Out brand in poverty and on nothing but a dream and his strong belief in his vision. The rest of the Tap Out team is on the movie set and they and a lot of other guys on set are wearing shirts memorializing Mask. So I asked one of them what it was about and learned about how inspiring Mask was to so many.
