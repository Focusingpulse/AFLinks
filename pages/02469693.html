EXHIBITION IS STARTED WITH FORMAL LUNCHEON Gift to "Wizard" Records Great Increase in Copper Output Due to Uses of Electricity. Presenting a cubic foot of copper to Mr. Thomas A. Edison to-day at the luncheon which opened the Electrical Exposition at the New Grand Central Palace, the copper producing interests recognized the inventor’s service to them in expanding the copper industry by the extensive use of copper since the beginning of the electrical age. Mr. Edison sat at the principal place at the speakers’ table to-day, while an American flag was removed from a pedestal before him, exposing the perfect cube of copper, weighing 486 pounds. It is the first block of that size ever cast and required six castings to produce the perfect specimen, beautifully finished and engraved, and mounted on an ebony pedestal. Some time ago, when conversing with some representatives of the copper industry, Mr. Edison jokingly remarked that they ought to present him with some token to show their appreciation of his inventions having boomed the sale of copper.
