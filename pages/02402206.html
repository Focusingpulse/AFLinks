GTM2026-main063 Time‑Consistent Cooperative Solution in a Pollution Control Game with Randomly Switching Payoffs Feiran Xu1 , Anna Tur1 1 St. Petersburg State University, Russia a.tur@spbu.ru We study a continuous-time cooperative differential game of pollution control with a single state variable, the pollution stock. The key feature is a one-time random change in the public damage weight, which creates a regime shift and induces a transition between two regime steady states. Using dynamic programming, we characterize the grand-coalition feedback solution from the Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman equations and derive closed-form expressions for cooperative optimal emissions, pollution dynamics, and the associated steady states and transition paths. Under constraints on emissions, we construct characteristic function against worst-case outsider behavior. Finally, we extend the framework to a threshold-triggered shifted-exponential specification. This extension provides a computable objective for the optimal threshold-hitting time and a diagnostic condition for selecting smooth solution branches.
