Alcyone

· archived # 1,455,709
The Great Central Sun about which rotates the Milky Way . Alcyone (Tau, Tauri, Eta Tauri) is a star system in the constellation Taurus. It is the brightest star in the Pleiades open cluster, which is a young cluster, aged at less than 50 million years. Alcyone is approximately 370 light years from Earth. It is named after the mythological figure Alcyone , one of the mythological Pleiades . It is known as (the Sixth Star of the Hairy Head) in Chinese.
