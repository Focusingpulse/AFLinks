Yada As the global protein demand increases, cell-cultivated meat and seafood may address some key food system challenges linked to conventional agriculture and help feed a growing global population. The policy environment for these products can aid or hinder their entry and success in the market. This article reviews the federal- and state-level regulatory and legislative landscapes for cell-cultivated meat and seafood in the United States (U.S.), creating a catalogue of proposed bills and enacted laws
