harmonicity

· archived # 1,480,601
n. A scalar measure of how sympathetically and immediately a system responds to vibratory input. Systems with high harmonicity require no energy transfer to achieve resonance; they are already phase-aligned. Opposed by discordance. Analogous to “conductivity” in scalar field dynamics. (See Inductance ) The degree to which a sound's timbre conforms to a harmonic series . Harmonicity Harmonicity can be determined arithmetically by factoring which finds the aliquot parts of a frequency . FOR EXAMPLE: A = 432 or 440? 432 vs 440 - Harmony is determined by having low numbers (Pythagoras). Higher numbers therefore can have many discords . A frequency is a composite or resultant of its aliquot parts. 432 = 2^4 * 3^3 are all low numbers. 440 = 2^3 * 55 * 11 are dangling higher numbers. Therefore 432 has greater harmonicity than 440. Of course, there is a lot more to this but the above ought to get you thinking.
