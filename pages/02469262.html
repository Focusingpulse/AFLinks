Today they have spread to every State in this country, even to Alaska; and South America cherishes many Yugoslav millionaires in Argentina and Chile. They came bringing gifts, these sturdy, seafaring folk from the east shore of the Adriatic Sea, and they have added much to that composite of ideas, ideals and aspirations known as the soul of America. Divided in Three Groups. In 1918 there sprang into the vocabulary of the world a new word — Yugoslavia — denoting that kingdom on the east shore of the Adriatic comprising the Croats, the Slovenes and the Serbs, who are sometimes called the Southern Slavs. The Slovenes live in the northwest of the kingdom, the Croats in the center and the Serbs in the south and east, and each with their own capital city. And in America each has a particular city which is the metropolis of his own group.
