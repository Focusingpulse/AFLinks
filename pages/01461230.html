Return to Table of Contents Solar Rings and Planetary Formation By: Jerry Williams 02-18-02 Introduction The universe is a most wonderful and curious creation to inquisitive humans, both on the micro and macro scale. Walter Russell gave us a wonderful set of keys to understand this creation. As a devoted student of his work, I know that once we gain an understanding of Walter Russell 's prime tool, Rhythmic Balanced Interchange , we too can see the corollaries of the workings of Universe. It is my hope that this article will give the reader an appreciation of the possibilities. We need to start answering fundamental questions from a causal perspective, rather than the process of observation by effects . I too, look at the effects to answer my questions of unknown natural functions. Most times I do not see the relationships that are present within an observation to answer how something operates, matures, and evolves. Armed with the knowledge of cause , it does not take long to become inspired and motivated to probe deeper into a question. Take the viewpoint of looking at a cause instead of its effect . Always armed with the tools of the cause , a person will find him or herself grabbing a hold of a hinge point that unlocks knowledge much more rapidly than before. This article is devoted to the application of Russelian principles focusing on the question of; how do spheres form rings around primary bodies in space .
