The tenuity of this element accounts for it. It is only when these sympathetic streams come in conflict with the cruder elementary conditions, either the molecular or atomic , that heat is evolved from its latent state, and a different order of light from the etheric luminous is originated, which has all the high conditions of thermal force associated with it; the sun being the intermediate transmitter. Thus is shown the wonderful velocity of these sympathetic streams emanating from the celestial ."
