GTM2026-main050 Best-Response in a Bounded Area near Corner in a Downsian Game Denis N. Fedyanin1,2,3,4 , Daria Volkova1 1 Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (State University) 2 V.A. Trapeznikov Institute of Control Problems of the Russian Academy of Sciences. 3 National Research University Higher School of Economics 4 St Petersburg University dfedyanin@inbox.ru Recent advances connect Voronoi-based payoff structures with differentiable opti- mization and learning in games. In particular, differentiable formulations of Voronoi diagrams enable gradient-based optimization over partition boundaries [1], while recent learning models incompetitive facility locations how that agents can approximate best responses via data-driven methods [2]. In contrast to these approaches, we pro- vide an explicit analytical characterization of the gradient and Hessian near domain boundaries, highlighting non-smooth effects induced by corner constraints. We analyze a best-response problem in a non-1D Downsian spatial game restricted to a rectangular domain Ω = [0,a]n ⊂Rn. Voters are uniformly distributed over Ω, and candidates compete for votes based on Euclidean distance [3,4]. The payoff of a candidate is the area (Lebesgue measure) of their Voronoi cell intersected with Ω [5]. We study the gradient ∇u(x) and the Hessian matrix H(x) in the vicinity of the corner. Using the fact that the Voronoi cell boundaries are linear (perpendicular bisec- tors), we derive an explicit expression for the derivative of the area with respect to the candidate’s position.
