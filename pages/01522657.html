He is listed in The Worldwide List of Dissident Scientists . Work Salas Cano was a co-author, with Hideo Kozima, John Warner and John Dash and others, of papers applying the trapped-neutron catalyzed fusion (TNCF) model to cold fusion phenomena. This work included a "consistent explanation of topography change and nuclear transmutation in surface layers of cathodes" observed in electrolytic cold fusion experiments, a topic associated with the Portland State University cold fusion group.
