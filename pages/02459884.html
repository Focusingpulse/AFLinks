I finally received an answer from Don Gerrard as to the status of the Paper Doctor and if we could have permission to post it on KeelyNet, here is his letter and my reply; Subject: Re: Status of Paper Doctor Date: Fri, 17 Sep 1999 21:15:26 EDT From: DonGerrard@aol.com To: jdecker@keelynet.com Dear Jerry Wayne Decker Thank you for your inquiry regarding the Paper Doctor. That book was written by me after 15 years of research. I published it in 1991 and it went out of print a couple of years later due to harrassment by authorities in Texas, and other states. I am at work on a new edition, but it will not be publicly available. I am convinced that - after sufficient testing - that downloaded patterns will reproduce inaccurately and give indifferent results. Therefore, Paper Doctor patterns will not appear on the Net. I had "johnny feel good" remove the Paper Doctor from his web site because he had posted it there without my permission. I own the registered copyright to this book and will act to protect it. In fact, the law is very clear. If you post another person's work on a website without their permission, you can be prosecuted. Thank you for your interest but at this time I authorize no one to post any portion of The Paper Doctor on any website. Be well! DonGerrard 9.17.99 --------------------- Hi Don!
