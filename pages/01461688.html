So Euler's chromatic scale of 12 semitones is all wrong except F# , which, by accident, is right. 1 ----- PLATE VII . KEYS AND THEIR CHORDS . The Plate shows the Twelve Major and Minor Scales , with the three chords of their harmony - subdominant , tonic , and dominant ; the tonic chord being always the center one. The straight lines of the three squares inside the stave embrace the chords of the major scales , which are read toward the right; e.g. , F, C, G - these are the roots of the three chords F A C, C E G, G B D. The tonic chord of the scale of C becomes the subdominant chord of the scale of G , etc. , all round. The curved lines of the ellipse embrace the three chords of the successive scales ; e.g. , D, A, E - these are the roots of the three chords D F A, A C E, E G B. The tonic chord of the scale of A becomes the subdominant of the scale of E , etc. , all round.
