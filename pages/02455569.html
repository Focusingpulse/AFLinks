If you want the credit and as well want to retain control (financial rewards) of the technology, you are going to have to apply for a patent in every country, no way around it. Make Patent applications and start doing deals with companies. Your risk is you are not protected until the patents are issued, but at least the info is out in the public domain. To late for the gov. to step in, even if they scoop your patents. If its a realatively simple technology, chances are people will by do ityourself kits.
