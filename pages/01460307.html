Law of Atomic Pitch "Atoms have each a different and definite pitch , at which they naturally vibrate. Scholium : atomic pitch is determined directly from its simple spectrum . Scholium : atomic pitch is determined by computations from its associate spectrum with all other atoms, as in known spectra . Scholium : atomic pitches are more important working data than Atomic weights ; tables of atomic pitches must be precise."
