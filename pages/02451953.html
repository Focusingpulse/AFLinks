The Dendera images are in a crypt under the temple, you have to ask this one old Egyptian about it...Dan and I looked all over that place and nothing remote...then I drew a crude diagram and asked about 8 guides, finally one got all excited and took me and Dan to the old Egyptian guarding this locked door in the floor...I showed him the picture and he held out his hand..yep, tip time all around, again...<g>..but it was worth seeing these beautiful COLORED carvings.....Dan and I were amazed to see these are real images...part of why I went in the first place...we couldn't go to Abydos because of terrorist activity, that was about 4 months before the shootings at Hatshepsut and the bombing at Cairo Museum. Anyway, yes, you do have to ask about the crypt...the biggest problem is the walls are only about 3 feet wide so you have to have a special lense on your camera to be able to pick up these short focus images.
