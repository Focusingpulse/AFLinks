About Science Saturday Chat sponsored by the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society Summary An open chat hosted by Franklin Hu with James Keene and Dennis Allen , given over to a paper Keene had posted the night before on the fine structure constant. Alpha as a variable Keene's proposal is to define alpha as the probability of an electromagnetic interaction with a charged particle in his simulator, the proportion of charge-carrying quanta accelerated by scalar (electrostatic) or vector (magnetic) events during a tick.
