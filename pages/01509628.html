It would be an axial coil rotor, with permanent magnet on one side of rotating coils and a ferromagnetic core with low eddy current losses on other side of rotating coils. The idea is, that when the rotating coil approaches the permanent magnet on one side, the other side of the coil facing the ferromagnetic core, will be attracted to the core. In hopes that this will cancel out at minimum, the lenz opposing forces to the rotating coils when generating current. It is possible that a biasing magnet could be placed at the back of the ferromagnetic core of low flux strength, to help boost this attraction of the coil to the ferromagnetic core. Of course, commutator or slip rings would be needed to extract power from rotating coils.
