Attraction Generator Using Rotating Coils

· archived # 1,509,628
Hi folks, i had an idea come to me last night, it is a generator using rotating coils. It would be an axial coil rotor, with permanent magnet on one side of rotating coils and a ferromagnetic core with low eddy current losses on other side of rotating coils. The idea is, that when the rotating coil approaches the permanent magnet on one side, the other side of the coil facing the ferromagnetic core, will be attracted to the core. In hopes that this will cancel out at minimum, the lenz opposing forces to the rotating coils when generating current. It is possible that a biasing magnet could be placed at the back of the ferromagnetic core of low flux strength, to help boost this attraction of the coil to the ferromagnetic core. Of course, commutator or slip rings would be needed to extract power from rotating coils. Let me know what you folks think about this idea. peace love light Edit: oh one more thing, a diode could be added to prevent current flowing on rotating coil departure, to prevent lenz force pulling it back and negating the device goals. And air coils might be best to start with.
