👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 27 January 1916 · archived # 2,423,262
, Berlin Translated by Pauline Wehrle Lecture Two [ 1 ] The day before yesterday I endeavored to show you the universal mystery of necessity and freedom in its two equally significant aspects: world processes and human action. I began by drawing your attention to the full significance and difficulty of this mystery that is both cosmic and human, and today we will continue along the same lines. I used a hypothetical example demonstrating this difficulty in regard to world events.
