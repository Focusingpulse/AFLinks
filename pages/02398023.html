That abstraction is useful for security modeling, but it can obscure the fact that SHA-256 is also a fully deterministic 64-round operator field with a rigid internal topology. This paper develops a bounded structural model of SHA-256 as a geometric trace projector: the digest is treated as a boundary projection of a larger execution trace rather than as the full trace itself.
