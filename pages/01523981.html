Surrounding Versus Dark Matter Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Speaker Frederic Lassiaille Date 30 August 2025 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording Surrounding Versus Dark Matter was a live session presented by Frederic Lassiaille on 30 August 2025 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. Surrounding, an alternative theory to dark matter, is presented. It is a modification of Newton's law. With this model the gravitational force is roughly inversely proportional to mass density at the location where this force is applied. After presentation of the model, its dynamic is quickly applied to cosmology and astrophysics. The result is that a solution is given to 10 of the most important gravitational mysteries of today in a straightforward manner. Hosted by Ian Cowan. Summary Frédéric Lassiaille 's third appearance of the year, and the one that presents the gravitational model itself rather than the derivation behind it. The session began badly — some minutes were lost to a screen-share problem in which the audience saw only the opening slide while the speaker worked through several more. The surrounding effect The model is a single modification of Newton's law, stated in one sentence: the gravitational force is inversely proportional to the surrounding energy at the location where the force is exerted. Practically, one computes the energy within a sphere around the attracted location, excluding the test mass, and inserts a multiplying factor whose denominator is that quantity and whose numerator is a constant — the surrounding energy in the solar system, so that Newton's law is recovered there exactly. Lassiaille was careful about its status. Surrounding is a standalone model needing nothing else to exist; but its motivation is his earlier demonstration that relativity, correctly worked out, predicts a variable G , from which the surrounding effect follows in two lines.
