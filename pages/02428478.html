The Apollonian Course X — GA 277a. The Origin and Development of Eurythmy 1912–1918

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 26 August 1915 · archived # 2,428,478
, Dornach Translated by Steiner Online Library The Apollonian Course X The color gestures given in this lesson were supplemented on the last day of the course, September 11, 1915, with some information that is included in this lesson for reasons of thematic coherence. Rudolf Steiner's drawings of the color gestures (NZ 5913/14) in Marie Steiner's notebook 138 were probably not created in the context of the 1915 lessons, but somewhat later, presumably in 1916/17. The minor scale in C sharp minor given on this day (with the same third tone E as in the major scale in C major) was recorded by Tati
