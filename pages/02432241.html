Speed of Theater Presentation

· archived # 2,432,241
Question: I noticed the theater runs slower on my home computer than it does on my work computer. Does that make any difference in its effectiveness? Answer: It does not affect the effectiveness of the PATHS modules at all. The main reason that it runs slower on your home computer is that it has a slower processor than the computer you have at work. Other possible reasons: Amount of cache, amount of RAM, other programs running in the background, and in some instances, internet connection speed. However, as long as you see and/or hear the presentation, no matter how long it takes to play, you are getting full effectiveness.
