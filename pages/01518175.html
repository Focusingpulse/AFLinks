What this is, it is the purpose of the author to inquire into with his readers by presenting to them a summary of what has been accomplished in the comparatively new science of electro-horticulture, and by discussing with them the rationale of the action of electricity upon vegetation. That he may afford assistance to some who are already at work, and possibly influence others to investigate into this fascinating subject, is his sole motive in offering to the public this monograph. He has had in view the popular rather than the scientific aspect of the subject, and hence has limited himself to what he felt would most appeal to the average reader. G. S. II.
