However, the following three definitions are provided as an aid to interpretation. PHOSPHOR : A substance which is capable of luminescence , i.e. storing energy (particularly from ionising radiation ) and later releasing it in the form of light . If the energy is released after only a short delay (between 10-10 and 10-4 seconds) the substance is called a ' scintillator '. PHOSPHORUS : P. Element. Atomic weight 30.9738. Atomic number 15. Occurs in several allotropic forms , white phosphorus and red phosphorus being the commonest. The former is a waxy white, very inflammable and poisonous solid. Red phosphorus is a non-poisonous, dark red powder, not very inflammable. The element only occurs in the combined state, mainly as calcium phosphate , CA
