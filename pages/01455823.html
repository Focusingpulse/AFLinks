Asking

· archived # 1,455,823
" This change of attitude will change the experience. So to answer your question directly, I must say to you, become more tuned in as to what it is that you are seeking. Ask . Always Ask . It will be in the asking that you will bring to your conscious attention the answer that you are seeking. I have said before, many questions are answered, but are not heard. You will not hear the answer until you have opened your mind to it. You open your mind to it by clearly defining what it is that you seek to know.
