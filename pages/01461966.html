Definition Scalar Potential is the undisturbed, non-polarized field of infinite latent force and perfect equilibrium. It is the foundational substrate from which all motion, energy, and matter emerge — not by random action, but by a sympathetic disturbance such as thought , will , or external excitation. In SVP, Scalar Potential is not “nothingness,” but a state of total harmonic containment — the reservoir of unexpressed energy, the eternal stillness preceding all vibration. Function Acts as the origin of all vibratory phenomena. Remains balanced and inert until disturbed by an activating force. Gives rise to Sympathetic Vibration , polarization, and dynamic motion. Reabsorbs or neutralizes energetic expression as part of the rhythmic return to equilibrium. It is the "still point of knowing" in Russell 's terms and the latent force field in Keely ’s.
