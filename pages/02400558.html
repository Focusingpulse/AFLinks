To our knowledge, no analog implementation of BBP has previously been built or described; every prior implementation, including the one known hardware version, is digital. The central idea is that BBP is not a formula that computes π but a mechanism that reads it. The circuit makes this literal: a bank of relaxation oscillators performs modular arithmetic directly in the analog domain, a weighted summing amplifier combines four phase channels, and a comparator bank reads out the resulting hexadecimal digit. The design has been verified in SPICE (ngspice) at the component level and in a numerical model of the complete signal path, which correctly reads the first four hex digits of π (2, 4, 3, F).
