De Hilster notes that both galaxies reported as lacking dark matter share one feature: they are spherical, and the stars at their edges move as Newton and Kepler predict. He argues the explanation lies in the geometry of gravitational fields rather than in any missing substance a spherical galaxy acts as a point mass, while a disc does not, so the anomaly that dark matter was invented to explain is a consequence of shape.
