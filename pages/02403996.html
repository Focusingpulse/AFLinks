The correspondence of experimental results to statements of the torsional field of brain consciousness enables us to draw conclusion that water has consciousness implying that it possesses the ability to thinking. The correlation of findings about the alcohol blocking the spin restructuring of water with negative influence of it on human consciousness testifies to the veracity of the inference.
