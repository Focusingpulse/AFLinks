In Germany, young Bismarck, who was to be Germany's first Chancellor, and responsible for the militarization of the Fatherland, received his first decoration. In England, Queen Victoria was forging into a unified whole the far-flung British Empire and casting knowing eyes upon the Suez Canal, which had been started the previous year.
