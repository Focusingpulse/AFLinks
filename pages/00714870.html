Introduction The concept of ideals in topological spaces has been introduced and studied by Kuratowski [7] and Vaidyanathaswamy, [10]. An ideal I on a topological space (X, τ) is a nonempty collection of subsets of X which satisfies (i) A ∈ I and B ⊂ A implies B ∈ I and (ii) A ∈ I and B ∈ I implies A ∪ B ∈ I.
