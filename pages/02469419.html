Nikola Tesla, who was among the first to experiment with the Roentgen rays and who achieved some remarkable results, has an interesting article in the current number of the Electrical Review , in which he describes in detail his recent experiments. As a result of them he says he can see clear through the human body. Tesla's ambition has been to develop electrical power enough to enable the rays to fully illumine the human body, and thus enhance their value to surgery. He shadowgraphed a man's ribs and the bones of his shoulder and arms some time ago, but he was not satisfied with the picture, because it failed to show the bones distinctly. This result, he says in his article, he has just obtained, and he has a shadowgraph in which the bones appear clearly outlined. This picture was taken four feet from the vacuum tube and after a comparatively short exposure. An equally satisfactory shadowgraph, Mr. Tesla says, was obtained at a distance of twelve feet from the vacuum tube and after an exposure of only five minutes. But the interesting portion of Mr. Tesla's article is that in which he tells how he was enabled to see through a man's body.
