The Name You Enter When Setting Up

· archived # 2,432,266
I should have noticed this from the start. Two people I knew very well signed up on my site and I neglected to notice that they used names that are the names they were properly given but NOT names they normally go by. It is very important to use the name you go by, sometimes even if it is a nickname. The modules are not transferrable and may/will not work if the name you enter is not what people usually call you. If you have any questions or concerns about this, log in on the PATHS site you signed up on and go to the control panel (look left after you are signed in). If you have any questions about nick names or what name you should use or want to change the name you entered, send an e-mail to the address that appears on that control panel page.
