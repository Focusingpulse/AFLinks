In this paper we study the cosmological quasar redshift and their internal redshifts and buleshifts via a new geometry, called isominkowskian geometrY, which is constructed as a covering on the Minkowskian geometry for the representation of electromagnetic waves and extended particles propagating within inhomogeneous and anisotropic physical medica. The complementary isoeuclidean and isoriemannian geometries are also indicated. Presented at Frontiers of Fun
