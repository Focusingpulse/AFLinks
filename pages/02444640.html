What would You Do?

· archived # 2,444,640
08/10/16 - What would You Do? I have heard this story several times by inventors who claimed to have world changing technology that works. Some I think might not be telling the truth but at least one of them I trust as being honest and real. The story goes that the inventor demonstrates his device, others verify it by testing his prototype or building and testing their own. Investors are ready to fund it to bring to market. In the dark of the night, the inventor is visited by strangers (often called Men in Black) who warn that this technology cannot be allowed in the marketplace, most often for the reason that it would destroy our economy by obviating many existing technologies, companies and vested interests. Therefore the inventor is offered the warning that his life isn't so much at risk as the lives of all his family and friends. Stop your research, stop promoting your research, do not seek a patent (it will be classified secret), do not seek investors because no product based on your discovery will ever see the light of day....OR ELSE, we will kill your family and friends. The inventor could also be jailed as a political prisoner for life along with the termination of his family and friends. What would you do? Which is exactly what my friend did and you no longer hear anything about his research along the lines of free energy though he is active in other less risky areas. I don't know who these 'men in black' represent, government agents or some protection group for vested interests. I fully suspect the criminal element know about these 'men in black' and use them in appropriate cases. It appears to be the case with the Bush and Clinton crime families where a word from Bill Clinton at a 'chance' meeting with DOJ Lynch results in her complete buckling to protect Hillary at all costs.
