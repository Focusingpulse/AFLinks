It is accessible to junior mathematics majors who have studied multivariable calculus. The text presents the fundamental principles of topology rigorously but not abstractly. It emphasizes the geometric nature of the subject and the applications of topological ideas to geometry and mathematical analysis. The following basic premise motivated the writing of this book: Topology is a natural, geometric, and intuitively appealing branch of mathematics which can be understood and appreciated by undergraduate students as they begin their study of advanced mathematical topics.
