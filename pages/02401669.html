GC2026-SGG064 Some results of comparative tests of a group of molecular electronic seismometers and a small-aperture seismic array "Mikhnevo" Andrey N. Kotov1 , Alexey L. Sobisevitch1 , Dmitry A. Presnov1 , Ruslan A. Zhostkov1 1 O.Yu. Schmidt Institute of Physics of the Earth, Russian Academy of Sciences and250195@yandex.ru Currently, when conducting seismological research, the approach of combining seismic equipment into single synchronized groups is becoming quite popular and relevant to increase the sensitivity threshold for recording remote weak seismic events, as well as determining the spatial characteristics of their sources. In July 2023, employees of the Schmidt Institute of Physics of the Earth of the Russian Academy of Sciences at the Mikhnevo Geophysical Observatory of the Sadovsky Institute of Geospheric Dynamics of the Russian Academy of Sciences (Mikhnevo GFO) A field experiment was implemented aimed at evaluating the effectiveness of three-component autonomous molecular electronic seismometers integrated into a single synchronized seismic network within the framework of seismological monitoring of low-amplitude events at local and regional epicentral distances. Synchronous data recorded by the Mikhnevo small-aperture seismic array (MSA Mikhnevo) installed on the territory of the observatory of the same name in 2004 were used as a reference in the comparative analysis of the measurement results.
