Any wire or metal is good for coils; - for winding - any metal; it only needs to conduct electricity. - for core - any metal; but those attracted to magnet is better Best coil wire (for common people like us) Copper with Aluminum in second place. Best core is Carbon. CO2 welding wire are of many kinds - and they all conduct electricity. We are using mild steel, 304 & 316 stainless steel, aluminum and copper; thus the answer remains: Yes they are all good for coil winding. BUT: They are not insulated. So you will need to work around that with various options. Therefore we need to find NEW ways, NEW experiments and NEW lines of thoughts.
