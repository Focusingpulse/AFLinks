Synchronicity, stray dog, bunny rabbit and Mary

· archived # 2,431,845
Mary and I have both been on the Synchronicity Module since it came out. Here is another experience we had that worked out perfectly, at least in part, due to the synch mod. First the background- "Little Girl", the stray dog has pretty much become part of the family. (see http://www.energeticforum.com/paths/...hoes-mary.html for another experience with her) Back in June of 2007 Little Girl brought a 2 day old baby bunny to the door. He was so small and injured that we did not think he would live. But we fed him with an eyedropper every 2-3 hours for several weeks and he has turned out great! He has a floppy ear, and can't see very well out of one eye, but he gets along just fine. His name is Bun Bun. He follows us around the house, smells everything, likes to have the top of his head and his belly scratched. He eats from our hands. He has a little ball he plays with...chases it, picks it up and tosses it. He is a blast! After about a year, Mary started talking about getting him a friend. Sometimes we are gone for 3-4 days at a time and she knew he got lonely all by himself in the house. Even though we hold him, pet him, etc. he is still a wild rabbit....very different than the domesticated rabbits you buy at a pet store. I did not think it would work out so good. But Mary knew he needed a friend. So she got one from the pet store. ROFL, it was NOT a match! They did not get along at all....Mary took the store bought one back, but was still very concerned about him having a friend. ONE WEEK later, Little Girl showed up at the door with another baby bunny. This one was about 1 week old and not injured. WOW! Perfect timing. We fed her with an eyedropper for a week or so. Her name is Clover and her and Bun Bun run around the house and Mary does not have any concern leaving him alone anymore. Here is a pic of Bun Bun at about 3 weeks old.
