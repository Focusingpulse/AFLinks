Unified Theory: A Complete Paradigm Shift in Physics & Cosmology Book Author Rati Ram Sharma Language English Published 2010 Publisher Lulu Press, Raleigh, North Carolina Pages 352 ISBN 9780557320608 Unified Theory: A Complete Paradigm Shift in Physics & Cosmology is a 352-page book of 2010 by Rati Ram Sharma , published through Lulu. It is the fullest single statement of the system Sharma calls Unified Theory , which he had been developing since Unified Physical Theory (1990) and which he offers not as a modification of accepted physics but as a replacement for it. The claim The book's governing claim is stated without qualification: that Unified Theory supersedes relativity, quantum theory, the quark–lepton model, the Higgs mechanism, supersymmetry, electroweak theory, string theory and the Big Bang together. Sharma's objection to the mainstream picture is one of economy and of ontology. Contemporary physics, he argues, recognises more than two hundred particles yet none of them is an element in the strict sense — something indivisible out of which everything else is built. In its place he sets a scheme with two of everything. There are two elements , the positive positrino and the negative negatrino , jointly termed cosminos; two charges , mass and electric; and two forces , gravitational and electromagnetic. A bound pair of cosminos forms the sharmon , and a kinetic gas of sharmons constitutes the all-pervading sharmon medium in which light propagates and out of which, on this account, every form of matter and energy is composed. The mass of the cosmino is innate rather than conferred, from which Sharma concludes that the Higgs boson does not exist. Consequences Sharma draws The book presents the theory as resolving, rather than sidestepping, the phenomena usually cited for relativity and quantum mechanics: The bending of light by gravity is derived from the medium rather than from curved spacetime.
