Gardi dates her first eureka moment to 1985, when Scientific American published the algorithm for the Mandelbrot Set in A. K. Dewdney's Computer Recreations column. Getting the program running on a Commodore 64 left her more amazed still: that so simple a formula and so few lines of code could generate such intricate complexity suggested to her that comparably simple rules might underlie the structure of the universe.
