1 Periphery of the financial system (outer circuit) Dmitrii V. Guryanov dmitriigur76@gmail.com Abstract: Nowadays among various participants of the global financial interactions there is a certain need for having a reserve and settlement currency that is not fiat currency. This paper describes a way of how the international financial set of rules could look like when not based on fiat but rather based on commodity type of currency, crowned by a unified global currency of the same type. This paper is a scientific approach to the problem which ultimately describes the methodology of the provided solution, having a full set of required equations and their annotations. Key word: commodity currency, multi-commoduty currency, currency exchange, reserves, settlement, reserve currency, reserve aggregator, global reserve aggregator, global currency exchange. Table of Content Intro 1 Currency exchange 2 Reserve currency 10 Reserve aggregator 19 Global reserve aggregator (Re-globalization) 38 Conceptual global financial scheme 43 Intro The presentation of the model, and to some extent the model itself, is community-centric, considering the global financial architecture from the point of view from within the Community. For this reason, in this case, the periphery of the financial system is understood to be precisely that part of the model that describes the interaction of the Community with the outside world, including with other communities similar to it, where its own instances of the system may be launched, or simply with other economic clusters of the planet, individual states, and therefore with their residents, legal entities and individuals. For the same reason, the description of the peripheral part of the model is not given relatively much in comparison with the description of the internal part, but nevertheless, key fundamental considerations are given, and the global architecture is described in a form sufficient to understand the principles of its functioning.
