👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 10 April 1906 · archived # 2,417,722
, Hanover Translated by Steiner Online Library 81. The Ideals of Humanity and Its Initiates [ 1 ] Dr. Rudolf Steiner of Berlin gave a lengthy theosophical lecture on Tuesday evening in the auditorium titled “The Ideals of Humanity and Its Initiates.” The speaker explained that there are no more fruitful feelings than those of devotion; they are the first step toward the ideal. The first ideals were prophetic (the idea of the Messiah); these were followed in the Middle Ages by the ideals of Christ and Mary, and then by the ideals of freedom, equality, and brotherhood of the French Revolution.
