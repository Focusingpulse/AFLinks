Einstein-Cartan electrodynamics: physical four-potential, geometric photon mass, and axial spin coupling Renato Vieira dos Santos ∗ Instituto de Ciência, Tecnologia e Inovação – ICTIN, Universidade Federal de Lavras – UFLA, Campus Paraíso, MG 37950-000, Brazil (Dated: October 11, 2026) If the electromagnetic four-potential is a physical field rather than a gauge class, its irrotational component is physical as well, and the geometry of spacetime can respond to it directly. We develop the consequences of this reading in the context of Einstein-Cartan gravity at the classical level. Imposing metric compatibility and linear dependence of the affine connection on the potential, we derive a unique one-parameter extension of the Levi-Civita connection. The resulting torsion is purely axial, and its Ricci p scalar contributes a term +6λ2 Aµ Aµ that generates Proca electrodynamics with photon mass mγ = 3/(4π) λMPl , where λ is a dimensionless coupling. The same coupling also produces a universal spin-dependent force on matter and an axial-vector coupling to fermions. Because a single parameter controls all four observable sectors, the four sectors are correlated by construction, and the correlation is a signature of the geometric mechanism. We verify that the construction does not generate parity violation in the vacuum propagation of photons, and we confront the framework with laboratory, astrophysical, and cosmological bounds. The solar-wind bound on the photon mass forces λ ≲ 2 × 10−46 , which replaces the unexplained smallness of the photon mass by an unexplained smallness of the geometric coupling. We emphasize that the paper is a classical study; the quantization of the theory and its consequences for unitarity and for the cosmological abundance of longitudinal modes are left open.
