I've made up my mind to begin, and I thought up the ending.” Of course, the novel is a work of art, that's why it is homocentric, by humans for humans, just like the art of the Lepena humans. But science has advanced so much in the meantime that it threatens a paradox, E=mc2 : the A-bomb and H-bomb destruction of civilization itself, far, far before our solar system completes its stellar evolution. It is necessary to overcome homocentrism philosophically. So, the beginning is a chance, and the projection is inertia, according to the law of causality in symmetry with chance, as the Lepena humans also felt. That's something that all people feel; they just need to become aware of it. Let us overcome inertia and the desire for eternal power at all costs, even if it may mean that I will be killed. Let us also embrace symmetry and relativity—let us enjoy diversity and freedom.
