GC2026-SGG011 Theory of the microseismic sounding method in the weak scattering approximation Alexey A. Tsukanov1 , Andrei V. Gorbatikov1 1 O.Yu. Schmidt Institute of Physics of the Earth, Russian Academy of Sciences a.a.tsukanov@yandex.ru Using the weak scattering approximation (Born approximation), a direct problem of the microseismic sounding (MSM) method is posed and solved in a three-dimensional formulation. Green's functions of a linearly elastic isotropic half-space [1, 2] were used to obtain the solution. We propose a theoretical model that establishes a relationship between the parameters of a subsurface heterogeneity and its reconstructed image. This image is obtained by constructing the spatial distribution of the relative power spectrum of the Rayleigh wave vertical component on the surface, assuming an isotropic surface￾wave field, as implemented in the microseismic sounding method [3, 4]. The nature of the heterogeneity can be assessed by the increase or decrease in the signal level in a certain region of the spectrum relative to the average level in the same frequency region, and its depth is related to frequency [3-5]. Within the proposed theoretical model, the formation of the primary informative signal in the MSM is considered as the interference of a probing Rayleigh surface wave with coherent waves from secondary (virtual) sources at each frequency ω (at each observation point x, y), arising from the interaction of the incident wave with the inhomogeneity and its boundary.
