Seems to me that since Minto's wheel operates off a temperature differential, you couldn't consider it overunity unless you created TWO temperature regions and kept one hotter (by at least 3 degrees F i assume) than the other. The entire device (the wheel and equipment to create the temperature regions from its motion) would need to be insulated to an extent where the system would be reasonably close to a thermodynamically 'closed' system. If the wheel appeared overunity under those conditions, i think it would be reasonable to call it overunity. Hoping that made sense..
