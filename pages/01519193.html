The Abstract section above reproduces the Apeiron journal's statement of editorial policy rather than the paper's own abstract, an artefact of the original import. The paper's abstract reads: "The quantum concept of light as photon, elaborated with possibly non-zero rest mass, is used to re-examine the classical problem of retarded electromagnetic potentials. A conflict with the classical Liénard–Wiechert formulation is revealed. An alternative formulation is recommended." Cynthia Kolb Whitney, then at the Tufts University Electro-Optics Technology Center and long-time editor of Galilean Electrodynamics , argues that the Liénard–Wiechert potentials — the standard expression for the fields of an arbitrarily moving charge, dating from 1898 and 1900 — carry a hidden error. Her route to it is to ask what the calculation would look like if force were mediated by photons rather than by continuous waves, and in particular if photons carried a small rest mass so that a population of them would possess a centre of mass. The proposed replacement is a new denominator for the potentials, R 0 = √( R ⊥ ² + γ² R ∥ ²), evaluated at the retarded position, in place of the classical γκ R ret .
