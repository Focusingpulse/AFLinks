Common Sense Science Organization Abbreviation CSS Formation 1997 Founder David L. Bergman Type Non-profit research and education corporation (501(c)(3)) Headquarters Roswell, Georgia, United States Fields Elementary particle structure, electrodynamics, atomic and molecular structure Publication Foundations of Science (quarterly) Website commonsensescience.net (defunct) Common Sense Science (CSS) is an American non-profit research and education corporation, founded in 1997 by the electrical engineer David L. Bergman , dedicated to developing a classical, geometric alternative to quantum mechanics in which elementary particles are real physical objects with real shape and size rather than dimensionless points. The name states the programme's premise: that physical theory should be answerable to ordinary standards of intelligibility — that every effect has a cause, that objects have definite properties whether or not anyone is observing them, and that a model which cannot be pictured is not yet an explanation. Its founding claims are that the universe and all natural phenomena are fundamentally electrical in character , and that matter is best described using geometric models set in absolute time and Galilean space . CSS is unusual among alternative-physics efforts in three respects: it produced a sustained, multi-decade research programme rather than a single thesis; it published a regular quarterly journal, Foundations of Science , for more than twenty years; and it is openly and explicitly grounded in a Christian creationist worldview , which its members regard as the source of the realist and causal commitments that classical physics once shared and modern physics abandoned.
