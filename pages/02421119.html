Jamie Buturff Born ( 1968-05-03 ) May 3, 1968 (age 58) DeKalb, Illinois, United States Residence Sedona, AZ, United States Nationality USA Known for Rodin coil replication; A = 432 Hz tuning and the "True Solfeggio" system Scientific career Fields Independent researcher; sound and tuning Jamie Buturff is an American independent researcher based in Sedona, Arizona, known on this wiki for his replications of the Rodin coil and, more widely, for his advocacy of A = 432 Hz musical tuning and of a scheme he calls the True Solfeggio Pythagorean Tuning System , which he connects to Marko Rodin 's number groups. Biography The following is drawn from Buturff's own account. He was born in DeKalb, Illinois, moved to Atlanta, and graduated from Riverside Military Academy in 1986. He worked in the construction industry, managing large projects for MM Systems and developing several UL-approved life-safety devices installed at Toronto International Airport, later becoming operations manager for Mercedes International. He also consulted at the Pentagon on waterproofing computer rooms. After moving to Florida he ran his own construction logistics company, and following what he describes as a spiritual awakening he relocated to Arizona in 2006 and founded Spiritual Results , an early client of which was the Chicago White Sox, where he worked with two players recovering from Tommy John surgery. He is the author of The Pre-Secret . In January 2009 he began research into alternative energy, which led him to Rodin's Vortex-Based Mathematics. He presented several devices of his own design at the ExtraOrdinary Technology Conference in Albuquerque in July of that year, and his work was reported on the front page of the Sedona Red Rock News . Rodin coil work Buturff is among the more frequently cited replicators of the Rodin coil.
