WAHL PREFACE The material comprising this book was written as a contribution to Methods ofExperimental Physics, Volume 24: Geophysics, where it is being published by Academic Press. Although many books are available on the subject of log interpretation and formation evaluation, there is a dearth of English-language texts addressing the physics of logging measurements. Because the originally intended readership was physicists, chemists, and engineers having little or no knowledge of geophysical field-measurement methods, there was no attempt to make the treatment all inclusive.
