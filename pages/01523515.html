New Websites, 4 Universal Motions, Finding the Right Current Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Speaker the de Hilsters Date 4 April 2026 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording New Websites, 4 Universal Motions, Finding the Right Current was a live session presented by the de Hilsters on 4 April 2026 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. CNPS president David de Hilster talks about the new websites and forum for the CNPS. He will also talk about what he and his father call the 4 universal motions. Finally, Robert de Hilster will present his work on "Finding the Right Current". Hosted by David de Hilster. Summary A three-part session: David de Hilster returning after a long absence with society business and an account of the Four Universal Motions, followed by a recorded talk from Bob de Hilster on how an electrical circuit settles on its current. Society business De Hilster opened by thanking Ian J Cowan and Roger J Anderton for carrying the Saturday sessions during his absence, and set out what has been rebuilt. The main society website has been redone; the Beyond Mainstream site, which had been hacked, was recovered; the wiki software was updated; and the World Science Database — over a hundred thousand lines of PHP he wrote in the 2000s, which had stopped running as the language moved on — is partly working again. Expenses have been cut, a sponsor now provides the server, and the membership software has been migrated. His main appeal was for the new Discourse forum to replace the long private email chains that have become the group's default. His objection to those chains was partly practical — asking to be removed does not work, because anyone replying to an older message puts you back in — and partly that including people who never asked to be included is not the right way to do it.
