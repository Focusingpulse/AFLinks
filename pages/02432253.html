Can I Have More Than 4 Modules?

· archived # 2,432,253
Here is a question from a customer: Q: There are just too many great modules to only keep 4 going at once. When can our subconscious take on more? A: Your subconscious can handle many more Modules, the limitation is in the Theater. At this point we can only accommodate 4 Modules plus sleep per Theater without compromising the integrity of the Theater. We have many people that have multiple Theaters, in fact, about a third of PATHS customers do. We even have people on 32 Modules plus Platinums! To use more Modules, you can sign up for a new Theater. Set-up fees and monthly recurrings will apply.
