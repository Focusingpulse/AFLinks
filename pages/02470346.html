O. Lewis Niagara Falls Gazette August 24th, 1960 FOR MORE THAN 200 years tourists in ever increasing numbers have come to see the world renowned Niagara Falls. Probably few if any cities in the world have been visited by so many tourists. No one scene in the world has been painted and photographed and described so frequently as Niagara Falls. After World War I the German Kaiser or one of his generals is said to have remarked “If Niagara Falls with its unlimited power had been in Germany we would have won the War.” Niagara’s industry had a large part in furnishing our military forces with many essentials of war in both World Wars I and II, and the Korean conflict. Comparatively few people realize the historical heritage of Niagara Falls, one phase of which is the romantic history of the evolution of electric power. A MOVEMENT HAS been started by a small group of historians to awaken the latent interest of Niagara County people to the importance of this rich heritage. While the written history of Niagara goes back to 1678 when Father Hennepin and LaSalle first saw the Falls, it was decided by the aforementioned group to first concentrate on the romantic history of the origin of electric power at Niagara Falls.
