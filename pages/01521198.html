It covers increasing numbers of experimental results in the pre-statistic realm. The ensuing 2-tear quantum aspect opens up a topological venue for highly ordered quantum structures while calling for abandoning the non-classical statistics of Copenhagen Doctrine. ("2-tear" is a typographical error for 2-tier , the two-tier scheme — one pre-statistical, one statistical — that Post sets out at length in Quantum Reprogramming .) Overview Presented at the 17th NPA conference in Long Beach in June 2010 and published in the Proceedings of the NPA , this two-page paper is a compressed statement of the position Evert Jan Post argued for some three decades and set out fully in his book Quantum Reprogramming (Kluwer 1995; Springer 2005). Post's claim is that quantum physics has two branches and recognises only one. Alongside the familiar statistical branch descending from Schrödinger, he says there is a pre-statistical branch in which quanta are simply counted, and whose laws are period integrals of differential forms: countable, exact, and independent of any metric. Flux comes in whole units, charge comes in whole units, and where both can be counted at once the result is a highly ordered structure — the quantum Hall effect being his standing example — which he says no statistics could have produced. The paper's title is the programme: single-system quantization is not something to be recovered as an average over an ensemble, but something that exists in its own right and prior to any statistics.
