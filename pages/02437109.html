👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 6 January 1923 · archived # 2,437,109
9:30 p.m., Dornach Translated by Steiner Online Library Central Council Meeting Regarding the Re-buildiing of the Goetheanum No minutes were taken of the proceedings, only of the remarks made by Rudolf Steiner at the end of the meeting. The shorthand notes record that the following had spoken beforehand in succession: Uehli, Vreede, Vacano, Unger, Uehli, Leinhas, Steffen, Vreede, Kaufmann-Adams, Erikson, Moser, Frau Grosheintz.
