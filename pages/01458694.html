Dimension

· archived # 1,458,694
Dimension , in SVP , refers to the strata of subdivisions of matter and force ; i.e., varying proportional mixtures of syntropy and entropy . In the chart below we can see nine subdivisions of matter from Molecular (solid matter ) to Compound Interetheric ( Mind ). Each subdivision (horizontal row) is contemplated or contexted as a " dimension " in SVP . [See Russell 's Eighteen Attributes or Dimensions , subdivision ] Illustration of the interpenetration of these dimensions or subdivisions . Each of these represents a Dimension , Subdivision , Plane , Realm or Field . Fill a volume of space with golf balls. The volume of space is full of golf balls but still has empty (non-occupied) space between the gold balls. Fill the spaces between the gold balls with small pebbles. The volume of space is full of golf balls and pebbles but still has empty (non-occupied) space. Pour in sand which fills the holes between the pebbles. The volume of space is full of golf balls, pebbles and sand but still has empty (non-occupied) space. Pour water into the volume of space filling the remaining spaces. NOTE: The volume of space never was empty.
