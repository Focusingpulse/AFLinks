GC2024-SG037 Disturbances of the lower ionosphere during strong seismic activity in 2023-2024 as observed on the Kamchatka peninsula Maria S. Solovieva1 , Galina N. Kopylova2 , Galia M. Korkina2 , Nadezhda R. Bulatova2 , Victoria A. Kasimova2 1 Schmidt Institute of Physics of the Earth, Russian Academy of Sciences, Russia 2 Geophysical Survey of the Russian Academy of Sciences, Kamchatka Branch rozhnoi@ifz.ru Data from the very low/ low frequency (VLF/LF) receiver in Petropavlovsk-Kamchatsky have been used to study the response of the lower ionosphere to two strong (М≥6.5) earthquakes. The M =6.5 earthquake has occurred w on April, 3rd, 2023 in offshore area of the Avachinsky gulf (Pacific coast of the Kamchatka peninsula); the М =7.5 w earthquake has occurred on January, 1st, 2024 on the Noto peninsula, Japan. Disturbances of the electromagnetic signals were observed for some days before the earthquakes and in days of earthquakes in the radio wave paths from two transmitters which crossed epicenters’ areas. During the intervals under consideration the influence of other factors (magnetic storms, cyclones) which could cause similar disturbances was absent. We suppose the anomalies in the radio signals behavior in the two wave paths have been caused by preparation and realization of these strong earthquakes. An additional argument in favor of such statement is absence of the anomalies in signal variations in other radio wave paths passing far away from the epicenters of the earthquakes.
