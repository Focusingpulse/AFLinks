Robert "Bob" Greenyer is a British engineer, entrepreneur and independent researcher who is a co-founder and the best-known public spokesman of the Martin Fleischmann Memorial Project (MFMP), a volunteer group that carries out open-source replication of cold fusion and low energy nuclear reaction (LENR) experiments. Greenyer is an advocate of what the project calls "live open science": apparatus drawings, procedures, raw data and even failures are published on the web in near-real time so that anyone may follow, criticise or repeat the work. His more recent research concerns what he and others call "exotic vacuum objects" (EVOs) — also described in the literature as charge clusters or condensed plasmoids — which he argues underlie LENR, transmutation and a range of other anomalous phenomena that mainstream physics does not presently accept. Education Bachelor of Engineering in manufacturing engineering — Brunel University, London He has said that he comes from a farming background.
