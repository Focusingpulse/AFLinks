Barley Disease Handbook Stephen Neate and Marcia McMullen Department of Plant Pathology, North Dakota State University Diseases are just one factor that may produce spots and other symptoms on barley. They may be caused by the genetics of the cultivar, the environment, agricultural chemicals or even nutrient deﬁciencies. Determining the cause of any severe symptoms is important because a disease may lead to economic losses, but could be prevented or treated.
