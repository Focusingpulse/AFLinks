New Years Resolution Solution

· archived # 2,431,984
New Module Alert! New Years Resolution Solution Make it. Keep it. Live it. This Module is specifically intended to help you make and keep your New Years resolutions. Many of us make them hoping to improve our lives and start the new year off on the right foot, but unfortunately, they rarely stick. Recent research shows that while 52% of participants in a resolution study were confident of reaching their goals, only 12% actually achieved their goals. However, what was also found was that men achieved their goal 22% more often when they engaged in a system where small measurable goals are used (lose a pound a week, instead of saying "lose weight"). Women succeeded 10% more often when they made their goals public and got support from their friends. Because of this difference we have created 2 versions, one for men and one for women. But whether you are a man or women, this Module will help you to realize your most advantageous goal and easily keep it. Keys to doing this are: Letting go of fear that will hold you back Effectively planning Pushing through those "blah" times Successfully rewarding yourself Visualizing reaching your goal Doing this will not only improve your life through the reaching of your resolution, but it will boost your self-esteem and develop your ability to set and maintain future goals. This Module will only be available for purchase until January 1st, 2010. It is not available for swapping. Happy New Year!
