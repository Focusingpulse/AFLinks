BUSH California State Polytechnic University, Physics Department 3801 West Temple Avenue, Pomona, California 91768 COL D FUSIO N TECHNICAL NOTE KEYWORDS: coldfusion, calo￾rimetry, transmission resonance modeI Received January 11, 1991 Accepted for Publication April 30, 1991 The experimental details of calorimetric experiments that provide support for the transmission resonance model (TRM) to explain cold fusion are presented. For the first time, a the￾oretical model provides a good fit to calorimetric data and permits an understanding of that data. After the first exper￾iment in which excess power was achieved, the model was em￾ployed to guide further experiments. Not only does the TRM suggest which experimental parameters to hold fixed and which to vary, it also predicts significant nonlinear structure and guides the search for that structure. The following are de￾scribed: calorimeter and cell designs, electrode preparation, electrode charging, and excess power measurements.
