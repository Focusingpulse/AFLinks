E-mail: aduev_scorpion@mail.ru BLOOD FEUD AND RECONCILIATION IN THE CUSTOMARY LAW OF THE CHECHENS (HISTORICAL AND LEGAL ANALYSIS) Abstract This article explores the nature of the blood feud, which was for more than one century a constraining factor and a regulator of social relations in the customary law of the Chechens. Besides, the author examines issues of reconciliation of the warring parties (maslaat). Key words: blood feud, customary law, adat, genus, family, conflict, blood enemy, union, reconciliation, reconciliation commission, criminal law.
