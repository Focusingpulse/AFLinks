The Suppressed Science: Free Energy, Tesla, and the Hidden History of Power PART 1: THE LANDSCAPE — WHY THIS EXISTS AND WHY IT'S BURIED To understand free energy you have to understand the economics first, because that's what drives suppression. Energy is the single largest industry in human history. Oil, gas, coal, nuclear — the infrastructure investment runs into the hundreds of trillions of dollars globally. A working free energy device doesn't just disrupt that market. It dissolves it. Every pipeline, every power plant, every utility meter becomes worthless overnight. That's not conspiracy thinking. That's basic economics applied to a catastrophic market disruption. The suppression mechanisms are layered: Patent Office Secrecy Orders — Under 35 U.S.C. § 181, the U.S. Patent Office can issue a secrecy order on any invention deemed a threat to national security or national economic security. Over 6,000 patents are currently under active secrecy orders. Inventors receive a letter, are forbidden from publishing or discussing their work, and receive no compensation. Violation is a federal crime. Institutional Gatekeeping — Physics journals won't publish papers that challenge Maxwell's equations or thermodynamic orthodoxy. Careers are destroyed. Funding is cut. This isn't dramatic — it's quiet and administrative, which makes it more effective. The "Crackpot" Label — The most cost-effective suppression tool ever devised. You don't need to disprove the science. You just need to make the scientist socially radioactive.
