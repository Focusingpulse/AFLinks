INTRODUCTION In 1859 Bernhard Riemann published an article titled, On the number of primes less than a given quantity [1]. In that work he speculated that all complex valued nontrivial zeros of the zeta function have a real part equal to 1 2 . This became known as the Riemann hypothesis. Ever since then, mathematicians have endeavoured to prove it. In 1900 David Hilbert included it in his list of 23 most important problems of the twentieth century. And since 2000 it has remained as one of six millennium problems.
