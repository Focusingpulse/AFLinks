mysticism

· archived # 1,481,866
noun: the belief that you can understand God directly by praying and meditating, or the practice of doing this noun: a religion based on mystical communion with an ultimate reality The practice of a mystic , who is as a walking question mark who persistently and consistently questions everything especially his own beliefs and all socially accepted beliefs , is in a never ending quest or search for Greater Truth . Living in harmony with the Cosmic reveals talents and abilities we little realized we possessed.
