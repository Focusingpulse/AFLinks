The transmission device consists of a pendulum (14) that is pivotally fixed on a support frame (12) and is arranged on the oscillating body (18) in such a way that it can swing. The pendulum (14) can me moved against a stop (28) located in the support frame. Another way to produce a directed force from a rotational movement is to ensure that the additional weight causing imbalance is stationary in relation to the device as opposed to the oscillating body.
