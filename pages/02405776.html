HARMONIC 695 BRUCE CATHIE is a Fokker Friendship captain flying with the National Airways Corporation of New Zealand, but he is known in UFO circles throughout the world as the author of Harmonic 33 (1968), the book that broke entirely new ground with its theory of the power grid that governs a whole array of extraordinary and hitherto unrelated world phenomena. At that time Captain Cathie had every reason to believe that his discovery of the grid was a “first”; but since then his further researches have convinced him that at least a portion of its secrets were already known to—and being exploited by —the scientists of certain governments. In Harmonic 695 he and his co-author Peter Temm prove their case. Some of their findings are a challenge. Why are certain of the secret projects maintained in New Zealand by a certain great nation always located on the grid? Why are so many ham radio stations always located on the grid? Why have New Zealand UFO sightings always been located on the grid? Why are certain key points of ancient history always located on the grid? And how can sceptics explain away the photographic proof (reproduced in this book) of Captain Cathie’s uncannily accurate forecast of the exact time of the 1968 French nuclear tests on Mururoa Island? How can the several attempts to scare or bribe him away from his researches be explained? The authors believe, with deep conviction, that certain scientists already hold the key to secrets of a terrifying importance —and that the ordinary citizens of the world have a right to be told what these natural forces are, and how dangerously they may be used. The authors’ own research-results are opened, in the book, for inspection by all interested. The professional sceptics will smile at Captain Cathie’s claims.
