To summarise the relevant ideas of the earlier paper, I proposed that there is an aether and that the aether is an elastic tetrahedral lattice structure. The inverse of the permittivity of free space, 1\ε0 is its shear modulus and that the permeability of free space, μ0 is its density. The speed of a wave (light) in this medium is therefore the familiar 1/(μ0ε0) 1/2 . In order for the units of 1\ε0 and μ0 to have the same units as shear modulus and density respectively it is only necessary for the units of electric charge to be m2 instead of Coulomb. The reason why electric charge should be measured in m2 is because in the model electric charge is caused by waves emitted by particles and the energy in a wave is proportional to its amplitude squared.
