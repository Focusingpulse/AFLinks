Under standard frameworks of Kolmogorov complexity, the sequence is measured by its resting mass: it is either a compressed or uncompressed representation of a specific state within a finite phase space. However, an archaeological mapping of discrete topology and algorithmic continuation reveals a foundational inversion of this model. A discrete string does not passively store data waiting to be retrieved. It acts as a topological substrate—a dense, unyielding field of potential that generates mathematically exhaustive, addressable state spaces only when subjected to continuous, lawful transformation. The analysis presented herein constructs the mechanics of this phenomenon, charting the structural physics of positional continuation, geometric admissibility, and topological severance.
