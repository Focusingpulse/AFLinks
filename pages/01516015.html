Screwworm Patents rexresearch Screwworm Patents Reagent for inhibiting eclosion of screwworm and application of reagent CN108077333 The invention relates to the technical field of plant control and discloses a reagent for inhibiting eclosion of screwworm and an application of the reagent. The reagent is prepared from musa basjoo seed powder and gibberellin and the balance of water. The musa basjoo seed powder and gibberellin are selected as effective components of the control reagent, and the eclosion number of the screwworm can be obviously reduced by spraying control on the screwworm. In response to the current lack of technology to inhibit fly larvae emergence, especially the lack of plant-based control technology, this invention achieves the inhibition of fly larvae emergence by combining banana seed powder and gibberellin. Preferably, the banana seed powder has a weight percentage concentration of 0.4-5%; and the gibberellin has a weight percentage concentration of 0.9-1%. Step 1: Dilute the reagent described in this invention with water and spray it on the place where prevention and control are needed, or spray it directly into the water for use. Then, after an interval of 48 hours, spray again... After the reagent described in Step 1 is sprayed again in Step 2, repeat Step 1 once after an interval of 72 hours. The reagent is diluted with water at a volume ratio of water:reagent = 100:15. Similarly, when spraying directly into water, the amount of reagent used should be determined based on the volume ratio of the water used and the dilution ratio described above.
