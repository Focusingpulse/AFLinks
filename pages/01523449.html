He contributed two papers to the Natural Philosophy Alliance and led its geology field tours , taking participants through the mountains and deserts of the American west to look at landforms in the light of his own reading of them. His thesis, set out in " A New Approach to Mountain Formation " (2011), is that mountain ranges are lithified dunes.
