Signal Resolution

· archived # 1,462,059
A measure of the smallest amplitude signal that a system (a filter) can discern and condition to within some specified voltage or as a logarithmic ratio relative to a reference input voltage and impedance . See Also Sensitivity Signal Signal Attenuation Signal Conditioner Signal Gain
