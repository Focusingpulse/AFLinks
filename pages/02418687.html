But both, the dependency of the eigenfrequency of a nonlinear oscillator on the amplitude and the nonharmoniticity of the oscillation lead to a behavior that is impossible in harmonic oscillators, namely, the foldover effect and superharmonic resonance , respectively. Both effects are especially important in the case of weak damping. 1.
