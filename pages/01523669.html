Physics on the Fringe: Smoke Rings, Circlons, and Alternative Theories of Everything Book Author Margaret Wertheim Published 2011 Publisher Walker & Company Pages 336 ISBN 0802715133 Physics on the Fringe: Smoke Rings, Circlons, and Alternative Theories of Everything is a 2011 book by the Australian-born science writer Margaret Wertheim , published by Walker & Company . It is the best-known mainstream trade book about outsider and dissident physics, and is built around the work of James Carter — the trailer-park owner, former abalone diver and self-taught theorist known in the book as Jim Carter. Wertheim uses Carter's life and his "circlon" theory of matter as the entry point to a wider survey of the thousands of unaffiliated theorists who build complete alternative cosmologies outside the universities, and to a set of questions about who is entitled to decide what counts as physics. Synopsis For roughly fifteen years before the book appeared, Wertheim collected the self-published manuscripts, pamphlets and letters sent to her by "outsider physicists" — people who, with little or no formal training, had constructed their own theories of matter, energy and gravity and were convinced they had found the truth the profession had missed. She followed one of them, James Carter , for about a decade, and his story supplies the book's spine. Carter rejects the whole line of orthodox development from Newton through Einstein and replaces it with a thoroughly mechanical picture of nature. In his account, all matter and energy are built from circlons — sub-atomic particles shaped like coiled rings of spring, nested in what Wertheim describes as endless hierarchies. Carter demonstrates the behaviour of these ring-shaped particles empirically, in his own backyard — with garbage cans, water and a disco fog machine he generates large, stable smoke rings and photographs their collisions, bounces and recombinations as physical models of atomic interaction.
