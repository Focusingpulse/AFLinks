· archived # 1,455,591
In his Astronomia Nova 1609 , Kepler pronounced: 1st Law - The orbit of a planet is elliptical and the Sun the source of motion is in one of the foci of this ellipse. 2nd Law - The apparent diurnal arcs of one eccentric are almost exactly proportional to the square of their distances from the Sun. 3rd Law - The periodic times of any two planets are to each other exactly as the cubes of the square roots of their mean distance.
