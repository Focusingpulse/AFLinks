The Aether Re-emergence of the Aether The aether concept has been around for some time, it was first put forward in the 18th century in order to explain the wave nature of light. This was later abandoned because certain experiments seemed to contradict this idea. The chief of these being the ‘null’ result of the Michelson-Morley experiment. To this day scientists unquestioningly accept the idea that light exists independent of any other medium. The notion that one can have a wave without anything doing the ‘waving’ seems to this author to be an absurdity, and is in itself a good reason to re-examine the whole issue of the aether. In fact, there are several other advantages to the aether model, many of which we will consider in these pages.
