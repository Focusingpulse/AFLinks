By positioning three large artificial satellites at 38,000 km from Earth, which would be relatively stationary in relation to the Earth, platforms in space would be obtained. On these, in two phases for the near and distant future, stations of enormous power would be built for supplying the entire world with electrical energy. In the first phase, magnetohydrodynamic generators could be used for direct generation of electrical energy from heat, and in the second phase, these would be replaced by devices for controlled thermonuclear fusion. From these stations, energy would be directed to specific points on Earth using masers and lasers, where it would be converted and distributed to all points on Earth.
