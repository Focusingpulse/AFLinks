SURPRISING EXPERIMENT. Perhaps We Are on the Verge of Finding a Way to Produce Artificial Sunlight — What Prof. Pupin Can Do with the Current. Holland in the Philadelphia Press. Prof. Pupin of Columbia College on Saturday afternoon made some experiments which seemed to experts present quite as interesting and important, although not so brilliant to the eyes, as were those of Nikola Tesla. It was before an assemblage of Columbia College students and experts that Tesla made those experiments a year ago which have given him fame. Prof. Pupin was present and was very greatly impressed by the demonstration which Mr. Tesla made. Purely in the interests of science and in pursuance of his duties as instructor of the students, Prof. Pupin continued certain investigations and experiments upon which he had been occupied for some time before Tesla revealed that it was possible to procure an electric glow or faint light without any such medium as incandescent lamps. Tesla was able to procure an alternating current of vibration sufficient to produce a glow, and, in fact, to create a light in an electric lamp when he held it in his hand and yet without any communication by wire or otherwise with the electric dynamo.
