Eleventh Lecture — GA 205. The Christ Impulse and the Development of Self-Awareness

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 15 July 1921 · archived # 2,433,820
, Dornach Translated by Steiner Online Library Eleventh Lecture [ 1 ] Today I will summarize a few truths that will then serve as a basis for further elaboration in a certain direction over the next few days. When we consider our inner life, we can say that at one pole of this inner life lies the element of thought, while at the other pole lies the element of will; between the two lies the element of feeling—that which we in everyday life call “feeling,” the content of the heart, and so on. In real soul life, as it unfolds within us in our waking state, of course neither thinking nor the will
