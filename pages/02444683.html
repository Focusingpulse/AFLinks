Chapter 1-1. The Character — Friedrich Nietzsche, Fighter for Freedom (1960)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 2,444,683
Friedrich Nietzsche characterizes himself as a lonely ponderer and friend of riddles, as a personality not made for the age in which he lived . The one who follows such paths as his, “meets no one; this is a part of going one's own way. No one approaches to help him; all that happens to him of danger, accidents, evil and bad weather, he must get along with alone,” he says in the preface of the second edition of his Morgenröte , Dawn.
