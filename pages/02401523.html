GC2026-SGG034 Small aperture seismic array synthesis in the Yamal-Nenets autonomous district of the Russian Federation Andrey N. Kotov1 , Mikhail S. Pyatunin2 , Irina A. Sanina3 1 O.Yu. Schmidt Institute of Physics of the Earth, Russian Academy of Sciences 2 Unified Geophysical Service of the Russian Academy of Sciences 3 Institute of Dynamics of Geospheres of the Russian Academy of Sciences and250195@yandex.ru At present, in conducting fundamental and applied seismological instrumentation studies, the approach of combining seismic equipment into unified synchronized groups of small apertures (1-3 km) is becoming increasingly relevant. The purpose of this test is to increase the sensitivity threshold for recording remote weak seismic events, and to determine their coordinates and the nature of source. The effectiveness of this approach is confirmed by the small-aperture seismic array "Mikhnevo" (MSA "Mikhnevo"), founded in 2004, located on the same named geophysical polygon of the Institute of Geospheres Dynamics of the Russian Academy of Sciences. More than 20 years of observational experience demonstrates higher sensitivity and efficiency compared to networks from single seismic stations, recording events at local and regional epicentral distances with a magnitudes threshold less than 2.
