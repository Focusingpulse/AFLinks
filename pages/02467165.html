The shenu itself, a circle tangent to a horizontal line below it, depicts the field of the undying stars: those that never set because they are near the celestial pole. They simply circle down, some just touching the horizon but never descending below it — never setting and hence never rising: never dying andhence never having to be recycled. These are the imperishable ones that stand within the magic circle of immortality: .Q.. Anything within that circle shares in that immortal essence that was the substance of our discussion some years ago, of the close relation between the shenu glyph and the royal cartouche.
