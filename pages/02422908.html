St.Petersburg, RUSSIA e-mail: nila_lip@mail.ru Radio sources, stars, optical identifications,radio refraction in ISM. Abstract. Identification of radio sources with associated optical analogues (stars) in Puppis constellation on area of one square degree has been completed. Nine radio sources stronger than 0.005 Jy were identified with nine stars brighter than 9m. Actual presence of radio refraction in previously detected interstellar medium is confirmed. Investigated area is located close to Galaxy plane in space with abundance of gas and dust, which facilitates considerable radio refraction in interstellar medium, which on investigated sky area is rrRA(J) =23,8 s and rrDEC(J) = 3,5.
