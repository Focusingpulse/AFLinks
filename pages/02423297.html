Lecture Two - GA 170. Memory and Habit (1948)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 27 August 1916 · archived # 2,423,297
, Dornach Translator Unknown Lecture Two [ 1 ] I should like to add something to what I have said on the subject of memory as it exists in our age. As it manifests on Earth, memory is a metamorphosis of other forms of the life of soul which belonged to the Old Moon period of evolution. I said that during that period of dreamy, imaginative vision, man had no need of memory such as he now possesses; he had no need of it because he drew after him, like a comet's tail, impressions (which were inscribed in an objective world) of what he experienced in his dreamlike imaginations.
