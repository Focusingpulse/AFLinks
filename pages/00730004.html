In 1873, Charles Hermite proved the transcendence of e, which was a fundamental achievement in the theory of transcendental numbers. Introduction The mathematical constant e, which is about 2.71828, appears naturally in most branches of mathematics and physical science. Introduced initially in connection with compound interest and calculus of exponential growth, e quickly established itself as a fundamental part of mathematical analysis.
