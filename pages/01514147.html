Veljko Milkovic's mechanical oscillator does not have an opposing force on the swing back, it gives help in the SAME direction. If that prop/wheel job really works like shown on the treadmill, then my idea proposed above should work. Take off the propeller, RPM should slow. Add it back on and it should speed a bit. That simple test will tell the truth.
