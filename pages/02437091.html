Lecture VI - GA 257. Awakening to Community (1974)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 27 February 1923 · archived # 2,437,091
, Stuttgart Lecture VI The background mood out of which I shall be addressing you today is not the same as that that prevailed on earlier occasions when I was privileged to speak here. Since New Year's Eve 1922, that mood is conditioned by the dreadful picture of the burning Goetheanum. The pain and suffering that picture inevitably causes anyone who loved the Goetheanum because of its connection with anthroposophy are such that no words can possibly describe them.
