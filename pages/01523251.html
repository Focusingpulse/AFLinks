Hunter is a British researcher in cosmology and gravitation who proposes that the observed expansion of the universe is better understood as a global rescaling of all lengths and physical constants, and that dark energy is an artefact of misreading the relationship between redshift and scale factor. Work The rescaling symmetry principle Hunter's central proposal, set out in " The Rescaling Symmetry Principle " (2008), is that every length in the universe and every physical constant changes continuously and simultaneously, at a rate governed by the number of length dimensions in the quantity concerned: d Q / Q = aH d t , with H half the Hubble constant. Lengths and the speed of light carry a = 1, Planck's constant 2, the gravitational constant 3, the permittivity of free space −3,
