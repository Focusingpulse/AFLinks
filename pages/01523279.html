Assis ); Ampère scholarship Scientific career Fields History of physics; classical electromagnetism; physics education João Paulo Martins de Castro Chaib is a Brazilian physicist and historian of physics whose work is devoted to the electrodynamics of André-Marie Ampère . He is the co-author, with André Koch Torres Assis , of Eletrodinâmica de Ampère (Editora UNICAMP, 2011), which won the Jabuti Prize in 2012.
