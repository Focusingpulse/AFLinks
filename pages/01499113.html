"Other Voices: A Summary of Research Not Present" Return to INE Main Page "OTHER VOICES: A SUMMARY OF RESEARCH NOT PRESENT" by Patrick G. Bailey, Ph. D. Vice President, Institute for New Energy P.O. Box 201 Los Altos, CA 94023-0201. Presented at the Second International Symposium on New Energy (2nd ISNE) May 12-15, 1994 Denver, CO Abstract A review is presented from researchers that are not present at this conference of the theory and experimental evidence relating to Zero-Point Energy and Free-Energy Devices. It is found that there is increasing momentum and a growing network of researchers taking place in these fields, despite widely evident efforts to suppress this information. Nevertheless, sound theoretical foundations for understanding so-called "Zero-Point Energy" and "Free-Energy" devices have been documented and are available. There are several reports by various authors of experiments that indicate a much higher than normal operation efficiency, and even over-unity operation. Unfortunately, these results have to date not been replicated by other researchers. Since energy is conserved, pure perpetual motion cannot exist. However, several examples are known to exist where energy efficiency can appear to be "Over-Unity," and the motion can appear to us to be "Perpetual." For these types of systems, the idea of "Free-Energy" can be applied, and lessons learned. Valuable references with real mailing addresses and telephone numbers are included.
