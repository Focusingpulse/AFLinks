Think about it this way, the shark, just living in its home was hooked in the mouth by a foreign vessel up above, reeled onto a platform and painfully ripped out of its habitat. Immediately paralyzed by an equilibrium imbalance from its body being sideways. The shark being paralyzed while temporarily suffocating until a tube of water is shoved in its mouth. As the shark lay there breathing out of a tube, he is surrounded by creatures he never sees in his home habitat. He would have no idea what they are going to do to him and the next thing he knows, they are drilling holes into his fin and sticking needles into his body. He wouldnt realize a tracking device was just screwed into him or that they were just checking to see how healthy he was. Worst of it yet the shark would have complete and full memory of everything the foreign creatures just did to him. For the rest of his life he would be haunted by the fact that one day while getting a bite to eat he was abducted by aliens. Personally, I believe aliens have abducted people, but i dont think they are any worse than human beings for doing so. Do i think its right for humans to be abducted, No. But nobody can preach from a high horse about abductions being evil when human beings do the Exact Same Thing to every other creature we encounter.
