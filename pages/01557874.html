clem motor/generator � Water Powered Motors It seems of late and with some recognition of the work of Viktor Schauberger Interest in converting rotary motion by means of water power devices is starting to grab the interest of energy researchers. An interesting device is the diagram shown below that uses air and water to produce energy force by means of forcing the water under great pressure by means of sixteen small jets the edge of a rotating cylinder. Apparently the main problem with this type of unit is trying to get the rotor to balance properly. Viktor Schauberger also proposed using a spinning disc wheel that pulled water upwards and then outwards to edge of disk by means of several pipes special shaped to intensify the force of the water leaving the jets at edge of disk. The water was then returned to a water collection area by means of gravity and then used again. Experiments in the United States have been carried out and while results have been good, I am unaware if over unity has been accomplished. For the home experimenter the above experiments are probably out of the reach of your normal handyman. For the home experimenter the following design of mine may be interest. The main components consists of three flat cylinders driven by a power pulley or motor. If exit points are cut out properly no additional jets should needed to be connected to the outer surface of the central disk. Some experimentation with the exit angle of the spiral pathway may be needed ,but I suggest exiting point of the disk should be at an angle of about 45 degrees to outer contact surface.
