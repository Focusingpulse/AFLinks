8. Minutes of the General Assembly of the German Section of the Theosophical Society — GA 250. The History of the German Section of the Theosophical Society

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 19 October 1903 · archived # 2,436,692
, Berlin Translated by Steiner Online Library 8. Minutes of the General Assembly of the German Section of the Theosophical Society [ 1 ] Secretary General Dr. Steiner presides. He welcomes the representatives of the various branches in Germany. [ 2 ] The following are present: Mr. Hubo from Hamburg Mr. Günther Wagner from Lugano Mr. Richard Bresch from Leipzig Ms. Geheimrat Lübke from Weimar Mr. Julius Engel from Charlottenburg Mr. Fischer from Hanover Mr. Arenson from Stuttgart [ 3 ] From the Berlin branch: Secretary General Dr. Steiner Treasurer Ms. von Holten Secretary Ms. von Sivers [ 4 ]
