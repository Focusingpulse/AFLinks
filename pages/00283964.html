In this paper, based on the comprehensive consideration of the wireless power transmission technology currently being studied in the world, the scientific and technical problems to be solved in this field are described. 1. Introduction Wireless power transmission technology refers to a technology that can transmit power without using an electric line. It's incredible to send power without electricity, but we already have wireless technology. If such a wireless power transmission technology is realized, the attention is focused because the ripple effect is very large. If we can deliver more than a few tens of W of power wirelessly, the model of IT technology is expected to change drastically. Especially in the case of the portable IT machine, which is the core of the wireless communication technology, communication is freely performed wirelessly through the network such as mobile communication and wireless LAN. However, since a charged battery is used for the power supply of the machine, the interest of the wireless power source device will increase.
