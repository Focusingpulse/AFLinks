Thomas Jefferson " Yet Jefferson also believed in compromise . He advised his daughter Patsy to approach all people and all things with forbearance . "Every human being, my dear, must thus be viewed according to what it is good for, for none of us, no not one, is perfect; and were we to love none who had imperfections this world would be a desert for our love ," Jefferson wrote in July 1790. "All we can do is to make the best of our friends: love and cherish what is good in them and keep out of the way of what is bad: but no more think of rejecting them for it than of throwing away a piece of music for a flat passage or two. " [ Thomas Jefferson : The Art of Power, Meacham, Jon; Random House 2012, page 244] Fyodor Dostoyevsky " Tolerance will reach such a level that intelligent people will be banned from thinking so as not to offend the imbeciles."
