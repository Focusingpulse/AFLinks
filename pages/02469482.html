The Herald today in its cable letter from London says the most remarkable results have been achieved and an elaborate establishment has been fitted up. The consumptive patient has been absolutely cured. By a certain process the patient has been able to receive a current of eighty thousand volts. The awe inspiring force of this voltage may be best appreciated when it is remembered that only five hundred volts are employed for driving trains on a railway.
