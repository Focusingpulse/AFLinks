It develops the Aether Physics Model (APM) ii Abstract The Aether Physics Model (APM) proposes that physical reality is organized by a discrete Aether geometry, complementary temporal structure, distributed charge, and a unified metrology called Quantum Measurement Units (QMU). This booklet presents that scientific promise in language intended for a general reader. It also investigates a broader interpretation developed through contemplative experience: the Universe is eternal; the Singularity and duality coexist continuously; the present moment is the dualistic manifestation of the Singularity; and embodied physics describes the rules by which matter changes within that present. From this viewpoint, enlightenment is not the permanent abolition of duality but freedom from imprisonment by it. The ego is treated not as an impurity but as a mechanical, path-dependent organ of embodiment, built through physiology, memory, chronology, and choice. Morality is then approached not as a fixed list of commandments but as a living inquiry into the actions and conditions that produce sustainable health, agency, happiness, and communal flourishing. Science investigates consequences; philosophy clarifies what flourishing means and how competing goods should be weighed; temperance responds to context and proportion. The Heart Sutra’s movement “beyond” and the Holy Instant described in A Course in Miracles are considered as contemplative approaches to the same metaphysical boundary, without being presented as experimental proof of APM physics.
