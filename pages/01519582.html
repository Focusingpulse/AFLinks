Roy Keys Inc.), Montreal Pages 546 ISBN 9781987980332 Coulomb's Memoirs on Torsion, Electricity, and Magnetism Translated into English is a 2023 volume by Andre K T Assis and Louis L. Bucciarelli , published by Apeiron of Montreal. It presents in English the papers in which Charles-Augustin de Coulomb (1736–1806) established the inverse-square law of electric force and the science of torsion measurement. Bucciarelli, Professor Emeritus of Engineering and Technology Studies at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, brings to the volume a background in the mechanics of elastic structures; Assis brings the history of electricity. The commentaries are signed separately, so a reader can see where the two editors read the evidence differently. The volume translates: Coulomb's 1777 prize essay on the best method of making and suspending magnetized needles his 1784 paper on the force of torsion and the elasticity of metal wires — the work that produced the torsion balance a description of a compass needle suspended by a silk thread the seven memoirs on electricity and magnetism (1785–1791), including the First Memoir, in which the electric balance is described and the inverse-square law of repulsion stated These are accompanied by the notice of the French Society of Physics on the 1884 reprinting of Coulomb's works, Alfred Potier 's introduction to that reprint, a chapter on the background to Coulomb's researches, and separate sets of remarks by Bucciarelli and by Assis on each memoir. The question of Coulomb's data One chapter is titled "Did Coulomb Experimentally Obtain the Results He Described in His Articles?" — and the volume does not treat the question as settled.
