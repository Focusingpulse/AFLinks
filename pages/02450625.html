His name is Don=E1t B=E1nki, he was also the co-inventor of the carburett= or in 1893. He started to work on improving the efficiency of the internal combustion engine and invented the water injection as an internal cooling (at that time compression was not an easy task to improve because of the low octane-rating of the gasoline caused knocking). He patented the water injection in 1894. = When Diesel invented his engine, B=E1nki said that the gasoline engines will never be competitive to that efficiency so he finished his research on water injection. I give you a link to the Hungarian Patent Office where is a short page about B=E1nki. http://www.hpo.hu/English/inventor/ebanki.html Note that he was also an university teacher and one of his students was Joseph Galamb who we know later as the chief engineer of the legendary T-Ford.
