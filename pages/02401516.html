GC2026-STP039 Solar noise storm in May 2024 by observations on the LPA LPI radio telescope Vladislav R. Lukmanov1 , Chashei V. Igor1 , Tyulbashev A. Sergei1 , Oreshko V. Vasilii1 1 Lebedev Physical Institute of the Russian Academy of Sciences. P.N. Lebedev Physical Institute of the Russian Academy of Sciences lukmanov@prao.ru The results of the Sun observations during a period of high activity in May 2024 at the LPA LPI radio telescope are presented. The flare activity of the solar corona in our data is manifested in a significant increase of the radiation flux at the frequency 111 MHz, which is recorded not only in the main lobe of the radiation pattern, but also in the distant side lobes. The increased radio emission turns out to be highly fluctuating in time. It is shown that changes in the radio emission flux are delayed by about two days in relation to changes in the X-ray radiation flux of the corona.
