Maybe in reply to: donadams: "Solution?- Gary Magnetic Motor" Hi Ed, Just though I might outline a simple little tool I've constructed and used for determining magnetic polarity. It is essentially a tiny compass and has proved very useful. Take a lenght of thin wooden dowl, I used a meat skewer which I think are made from bamboo, and fix a tiny magnet to the end in such a manner that it can pivot freely on 2 copper needle points. The magnet should be made as small as possible - a chip broken off a ceramic magnet will do. My pivoting probe magnet is only about .5mm diameter and 1mm long, the north end painted red for visual ease. This simple tool allows one to probe around in any magnetic field to provide a quick indication of polarity. Very cheap and very useful. Regards, Bill.
