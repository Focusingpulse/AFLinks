Santosh Keywords: Sedimentary pyrite Palaeo-ocean chemistry Mass extinction Ocean nutrients Metalliferous shales Evolution a b s t r a c t Availability of nutrients in the ocean can be a major factor affecting bioproductivity, burial of carbon and release of oxygen. However, the nutrient trace element (TE) composition of the palaeo-ocean cannot be measured directly. Here we present a comprehensive global dataset on the TE content of marine sedimentary pyrite in black shales, dating back 700 million years, and demonstrate a systematic cyclic evolution of pyrite TE composition with time.
