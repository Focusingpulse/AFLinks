It later had a chapter in the United Kingdom, and it remains the publisher of essentially the whole Autodynamics literature. Activities Publishing The Society produced a book early on but sold only a few copies. Its two substantial titles are Autodynamics: Fundamental Basis for a New Relativistic Mechanics (1998), the first comprehensive statement of the theory, and Autodynamics: Storm in Physics (2005).
