Society for the Advancement of Autodynamics

· archived # 1,523,901
The Society for the Advancement of Autodynamics (SAA) was founded in 1994 in Southern California by David de Hilster , Dr. Ricardo Carezani , Amnon Meyers and others, with the purpose of advancing the theory of Autodynamics as developed by Carezani. It later had a chapter in the United Kingdom, and it remains the publisher of essentially the whole Autodynamics literature. Activities Publishing The Society produced a book early on but sold only a few copies. Its two substantial titles are Autodynamics: Fundamental Basis for a New Relativistic Mechanics (1998), the first comprehensive statement of the theory, and Autodynamics: Storm in Physics (2005). The 1998 book was the first to be mass-printed, putting copies into the thousands. Beyond the books, the Society publishes Carezani's papers, derivations and expository notes at autodynamics.org . That collection is indexed at Ricardo L Carezani and grouped by subject at Autodynamics ; it includes the derivations ( Frames Derivation , Galilean Simplification , The Superfluous System ), the decay papers ( Muon Decay and Autodynamics , Pion Decay and Autodynamics , Nucleus-Nucleus Collision , The Compton Effect and Autodynamics ), the gravitational programme ( Universal Gravitation in Autodynamics and its four applications), and the neutrino papers ( Origin of the Neutrino , Super-Kamiokande: Super-Proof for Neutrino Non-existence , Neutrinos at Fermi Lab , SN 1987A and the Neutrino ). Several members appear as authors or editors across that body of work: Lucy Haye as co-author of the astrophysical papers, de Hilster and Meyers as editors, and Brian F. Hall as editor of the Pioneer paper. Public advocacy The Society has twice intervened publicly against the neutrino hypothesis.
