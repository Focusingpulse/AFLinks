Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Speaker David de Hilster Date 3 April 2021 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording Convince Me Aether Exists! was a live session presented by David de Hilster on 3 April 2021 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. About Host David de Hilster wants you to convince him aether exists. Light needs physicality. Is aether the only solution? Let's talk about it. Summary David de Hilster invited the society's etherists to convince him, and spent the session setting out precisely what he would need to be convinced of on the ground that an argument is useless if the two sides do not agree what is being argued about.
