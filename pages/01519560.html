Convince Me Aether Exists! Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Speaker David de Hilster Date 3 April 2021 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording Convince Me Aether Exists! was a live session presented by David de Hilster on 3 April 2021 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. Host David de Hilster wants you to convince him aether exists. Light needs physicality. Is aether the only solution? Let's talk about it. Summary David de Hilster invited the society's etherists to convince him, and spent the session setting out precisely what he would need to be convinced of — on the ground that an argument is useless if the two sides do not agree what is being argued about. He was repetitive on one point: the ether could be right and his own model could be wrong. Where he stands His complaint, stated bluntly, was about certainty. He said he knows one etherist — not anyone who appears on the programme — who will say the ether might be wrong, and quoted Glenn Borchardt telling him on camera that he would come round. What he wants from an etherist is the form his father used when first describing their own model: this is what we think it is, and it could be entirely wrong. His second complaint was about the word. He objects to everything that fills space being called ether, and wants the term kept for what he thinks it means and what he believes Borchardt and Jeff Yee mean by it: waves transmitted through a medium by collisions. On that definition a gravitonic model is not an ether model, whatever Newton, who did not have the distinction available, may have written; and reading Einstein's remarks about spacetime, or quantum fluctuations, as endorsements of the ether he treated as the wrong sort of argument — science is not politics and does not proceed by collecting endorsements.
