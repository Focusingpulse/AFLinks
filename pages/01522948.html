Znidarsic (born 16 June 1953) is an American electrical engineer and independent researcher, known in the new energy community for proposing a single "velocity of the transitional quantum state" that he argues underlies cold fusion , the reported superconductor–gravity anomalies, and the possibility of controlling the natural forces. Biography He has worked as an engineer in the steel, mining and utility industries, most recently commissioning power plants for Alstom Power in North Carolina. His physics is a spare-time pursuit carried on alongside that career, and he has been candid from the start about its uncertainties — his first public statement of the work, the 1998 " Letter from Frank Znidarsic: Znidarsic Tells All ", opens by admitting "the results to date have been less than expected." Education B.S. in electrical engineering — University of Pittsburgh, 1975 A.S. in business administration — St. Francis College Study in physics — Indiana University, in the 1990s He is a Registered Professional Engineer in Pennsylvania. The velocity of the transitional quantum state Znidarsic's central idea is that energy flows within matter as a mechanical wave with a single characteristic speed , which he calls the transitional velocity and writes V t .
