Andrew Knight We have been exploring the potential of the simple Werner complex, cobalt hexammine "Cohex" for use as a broad spectrum antiviral and antibacterial therapeutic. Cohex is a coordinatively saturated complex of Co(III) surrounded by six ammonia ligands, is air and water stable and has low toxicity. We have have reported on its antiviral activity against both Sindbis virus (SV) and adenovirus. Due to its kinetic inertness, Cohex interacts principally with its environment via outer-sphere coordination and through simple electrostatic interaction. One consequence is that, unlike previously studied Co(III) systems, Cohex does not hydrolyze nucleotides, but does show potent inhibition of protein synthesis and dose-dependent antiviral properties.
