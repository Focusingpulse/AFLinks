When a body disintegrates then those energies 8 that created it will again be freed. They are never lost. If they lose their place of abode with the decaying of the body , then they are willingly taken up by the water that eternally circulates in, on and above the Earth and which conducts them once more to a new life . Therefore wherever we look there is life , constant formation and transformation .
