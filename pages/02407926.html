The BOTANY, PRODUCTION AND USES Edited by Roy Navarre and Mark Pavek The Potato: Botany, Production and Uses presents a comprehensive discussion of the most important topics for potato researchers and producers. Covering recent advances in marker-assisted selection, gene mapping and genomics, this book discusses how breeders have been able to quickly identify desirable and undesirable traits in new cultivars, improving the chances of finding beneficial new varieties. It assesses the latest research on plant anatomy, physiology and development, such as tuber development, water use and seed production; covers all aspects of pest identification and management; and reviews postharvest issues such as storage, global markets, nutritional value and flavour.
