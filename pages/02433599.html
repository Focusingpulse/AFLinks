Generalized Harmonic Numbers Jose Risomar Sousa October 18, 2018 Abstract This paper presents new formulae for the harmonic numbers of order k, Hk (n), and for the partial sums of two Fourier series associated with them, denoted here by Ckz (n) and Skz (n). I believe this new formula for Hk (n) is an improvement over the digamma function, ψ, because it is simpler and it stems from Faulhaber’s formula, which provides a closed-form for the sum of powers of the first n positive integers. We demonstrate how to create an exact power series for the harmonic numbers, a new integral representation for ζ(2k+1) and a new generating function for ζ(2k+1), among many other original results. The approaches and formulae discussed here are entirely different from solutions available in the literature.
