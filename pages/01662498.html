Full House for Food Futures Now Toggle navigation Home Article Index Search Contact Online Store Full House for Food Futures Now Sam Burcher It was the warmest day of the year so far and inside the sumptuously decorated Jubilee Room in the House of Commons there was barely standing room. The capacity audience were gathered for the launch of the much acclaimed I-SIS report Food Futures Now *Organic *Sustainable *Fossil Fuel Free [1], a compelling manual of the many proven and practical methods of organic and locally based food production that ensures both food security and food sovereignty in the face of the fuel and food crises and the challenges of climate change. GM is not the answer whatever the question was First to speak was the Rt Hon Michael Meacher MP, who paid tribute to Food Futures Now as the most authoritative and comprehensive statement of an alternative and better world agricultural and ecology that he has yet seen. He also supported the findings of the recent International Assessment of Agricultural Science and Technology for Development (IAASTD) [2] (see “GM-Free Organic Agriculture to Feed the World” , SiS 38 ), which are much in line with those of Food Futures Now , and maintained his stance on GM crops by saying, “GM is not the answer, whatever the question was.” He made an explicit link between the recent food riots in over thirty countries around the world and the reckless investment and subsidies by the US Government and international agencies that have misdirected a further thirty million tonnes of maize grains to biofuels.
