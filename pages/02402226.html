SPAF-2026-main018 CDU: 519.6 FROM NUMERICAL SOLUTION OF CHEMICAL KINETICS PROBLEMS TO APPROXIMATION OF THERMAL PEAKS Ksenia P. Saktoeva k.saktoeva@alumni.nsu.ru Ksenia P. Saktoeva Postgraduate student, engineer, e-mail: k.saktoeva@alumni.nsu.ru Melentiev Energy Systems Institute SB RAS, 130 Lermontov St., Irkutsk, 664033, Russia The report presents the CCODE software library, which implements L‑stable (m,k)-methods for solving stiff systems of ordinary differential equations (ODEs). Such systems are known to arise in the mathematical modeling of chemical kinetics. The results of testing the library on a number of characteristic problems are presented, including the Robertson reaction, laser pyrolysis of ethane, combustion of hydrogen, and combustion of small hydrocarbons. Further, a solution based on the analysis of heat flow curves obtained by differential scanning calorimetry (DSC) for solid fuel samples at heating rates from 5 to 50 K/min is proposed. An effective approach for processing DSC curves and separating overlapping peaks is their approximation by Gaussian functions. This is necessary for subsequent thermochemical analysis. Both developed tools will be demonstrated on real experimental data. Keywords: stiff systems, chemical kinetics, DSC, Gaussian approximation, numerical experiment. References 1. Emelina A.L. Differential Scanning Calorimetry. Moscow: Moscow State University, 2009. 42 p. 2. Saktoeva K.P., Laevsky Yu.M. CCODE Software Library for Solving Stiff Systems of Ordinary Differential Equations. Siberian Journal of Industrial Mathematics. 2025. Vol. 28,
