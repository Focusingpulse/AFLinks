She has since been the victim of a scandalous attempt to discredit her work by the mainstream journal, Nature Biotechnology ( Science and Scientist Abused , SiS 36) , and no satisfactory redress has yet been forthcoming (see Letter to Nature Biotechnology: Systematic bias in favour of no adverse impacts from GM feed , and Letter to Nature Biotechnology: Prepublication Review Yes, But Review Panel Must be Balanced , SiS 37). A second member of the panel, Dr. Christian V&eacute;lot, has lost his funding. There is a committee of support for V&eacute;lot and they have organised a petition that has attracted 9288 signatures as of 26 January 2008. You can sign it on line by going to http://sciencescitoyennes.org/spip.php?article1638 . Please also send the link to your friends. The biotech industry is working very hard to suppress independent science and scientists; working through the very academic institutions that are supposed to defend them.
