Kundalini

· archived # 1,460,252
( click to enlarge ) Kundalini is described as a sleeping, dormant potential force in the human organism. It is one of the components of an esoteric description of the 'subtle body', which consists of nadis (energy channels), chakras ( psychic centres), prana (subtle energy), and bindu (drops of essence). Kundalini is described as being coiled up at the base of the spine, usually within muladhara chakra . The image given is that of a serpent coiled three and a half times around a smokey grey lingam . Each coil is said to represent one of the three gunas , with the half coil signifying transcendence. Wikipedia, Kundalini Kundalini is the divine force that is here (on the earth as your Prana / breath ) and is there (in the cosmos as your Mother Goddess (the Dasa Mahavidya ) continuously in all actions and interactions behind every subject and object. Esoterically, she is known as Mahamaya or as Upanishads" call her Uma Haimavati or Paramshakti ", the teacher of the Gods. In Geeta she is referred as Para Shakti " or divine goddess and in Tantra as Tripura (three in one).
