Just to make sure we're clear about that table. Tsoukalas's group used two neutron sources for their experiments. The 1Ci based data are indeed positive on the aggregate whereas, the 10Ci based data gave them null results on occasion due to onset of different expt. conditions (nonspherical shapes of bubbles) - an effect even Xu et al. discuss in their NED paper. Even then, upon aggregating the good and bad data Tsoukalas's group still had experienced positive signs for tritium per the 5/14/04 emal of Tsoukalas something they have withheld from the public.
