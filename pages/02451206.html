Dan York has been very busy getting the archives back into shape and arranging for the move to the new list. This will take place around midnight of Friday the 30th of April, the KeelyNet@DallasTexas.net list will then be NO MORE. If you are receiving this, your subscription will automatically be signed up for the new list which is called interact@keelynet.com Please do not post dual emails to both the old KeelyNet@DallasTexas.net AND interact@keelynet.com They can be edited out of the archives but it is unnecessary hassle for us to have to do it, so please just switch over completely to the interact@keelynet.com address....thanks! Dan has arranged for keelynet.com to have 3 search engines, though only two are working at the moment. One search engine at; http://www.keelynet.com/search.htm looks up information on the website and ignores the archives The second search engine at; http;//www.keelynet.com/interact.archives.html searches ONLY the discussion list archives and includes all three versions, KeelyNet@lists.kz, KeelyNet@DallasTexas.net and this new incarnation interact@keelynet.com The third search engine will be specifically for all the emails from the old KeelyNet BBS which comprises something on the order of 10MB of discussions. Those are currently being edited since they include the private discussions not meant for the general public. There are some great comments and useful information which will be accessible with the BBS search engine. Thanks to Norman Wootan for saving all that material a
