Nikola Tesla, the electrician, announces that he has at last accomplished the great achievement of his life. His discovery, if put into practical use, will revolutionize many things in the world and cause all its intelligent inhabitants to wonder that they have been so slow as not to avail themselves of the forces which nature has put into the earth to help them in the daily problems of life. Tesla’s latest discovery is a bigger thing than the Klondike gold fields, and will have far more effect in the commercial world, once it is put into practical operation. To put it in a way which every one will understand at once. The earth is full of electrical currents. Tesla has perfected an apparatus which disturbs those currents in exactly the way he wishes to disturb them. The transmission of the electric waves is instantaneous. They can be recorded on instruments for the purpose. This creates another form of wireless telegraphy, which is superior to that developed by Marconi, the young Italian inventor, who seems to be creating such a furor among scientific men and commercial companies in Europe.
