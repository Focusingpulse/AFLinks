Corrections des Sujets d’Examens de Géodésie et de Cartographie Mathématique - Partie I - Par Abdelmajid BEN HADJ SALEM, Ingénieur Géographe Général Abstract : This is the part I of the correction of the collection of exams of geodesy and mathematical cartography. These exams are from the German school, namely from the Institute of Geodesy of the University of Stuttgart where the eminent professor Erik W. Grafarend (1939-2020) taught geodesy courses and in particular mathematical cartography. This is an opportunity for French-speaking students to share the German methodology.
