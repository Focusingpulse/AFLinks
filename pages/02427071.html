Article Viroid-like colonists of human microbiomes Graphical abstract Authors Ivan N. Zheludev, Robert C. Edgar, Maria Jose Lopez-Galiano, Marcos de la Peña, Artem Babaian, Ami S. Bhatt, Andrew Z. Fire Correspondence zheludev@stanford.edu (I.N.Z.), afire@stanford.edu (A.Z.F.) In brief Obelisks are widespread RNA-based agents found in diverse environmental and human-associated microbiomes. These apparently unbranched and circular RNAs represent a previously uncharacterized class of genetic elements.
