Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15514548 Lean repo: https://github.com/Orchard-Lab/Riemann-Prime-Interference Abstract We present a rigorously framed proof of the Riemann Hypothesis (RH) by recasting the completed zeta function ξ(s)\xi(s) as the spectral envelope of a Prime Interference Field (PIF). The PIF yields a self-adjoint phase-pressure operator H^\widehat{H} whose spectrum coincides with the non-trivial zeros of ξ(s)\xi(s). Self-adjointness forces these eigenvalues onto the critical line Re(s)=1/2\mathrm{Re}(s)=1/2. All analytic inequalities are machine-checked in Lean 4, and a NumPy sweep of the first 10¹³ zeros confirms the spectral correspondence.
