With regard to the web and pc accelerator programs, I had it installed on 3 machines since last Friday. It seemed to work ok and was in fact faster than before, in one case, blindingly faster. My friend sent me an email asking me to come over and 'get that thing off my computer'...his computer seemed to be working ok, but he says he didn't like logging on to their webage everytime as it took so long to load. He had things to do and considered this a nuisance...so I explained how to use add/remove to uninstall....he's back to being slower but happier. Now, my experience with the two computers has been identical, both negative. As of last night, I removed both programs from both computers. The reason? When on the web, my mouse pointer will just freeze for no apparent reason. Ctrl/alt/del and no keys respond, even after a 3 minute wait. I uninstalled it on one computer and reinstalled thinking I had a corrupted file or two. It ran ok for a day or so, then bam, back again with the lockups. No time or patience with Microsoft clone software with mystery undocumented failures, so I removed both from the computers and informed the others here to watch out for similar problems. I also wrote the webmaster about this problem with their 'free' software and to date haven't received a response. So, if it starts locking up on anyone, you might try the reinstall but I just cleaned it out...not worth the hassle. Interesting how it worked fine for about 5 days before the problems, at least in my case. Thanks for bringing it up as I was intending on sending my experience to the list in case anyone else had specific problems with the programs. Th
