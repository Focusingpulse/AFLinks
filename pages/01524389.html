"Vince" Lopes is an American environmental scientist and educator who is a professor of Environmental Studies at Texas State University in San Marcos, Texas. Trained as a watershed hydrologist, he later broadened his work toward cultural ecology and ecophilosophy, developing an integrative outlook he describes as "engaged ecology" that connects nature, people, and place through the study of watersheds.
