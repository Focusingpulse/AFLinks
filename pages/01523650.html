The phase shift was discovered by Sagnac in 1913. It implies accelerations so that it can be explained only by the General Relativity theory. Attempts to explain this result by a Euclidian approximation failed as it leads to the Selleri's paradox. Sagnac favours an explanation by an ether motionless with regard to the laboratory. Pr Stedman noticed that a frequency shift is also occurring enabling to detect the Sagnac device rotation. This report is intended for explaining the frequency shift.
