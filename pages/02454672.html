A site you might want to check out which offers a gravity fed engine; http://gravityengine.com/ Once I had established that it was possible to move the mass into the positive gravitational pull &shy; I then set about to design a machine that would force mass to move in this way. The result of this design effort is what I call the Gravity Engine. The Gravity Engine is only an idea &shy; it has not been built and a prototype doesn't exist anywhere. I have done some initial tests using a fixture of my own design &shy; and based on my tests, believe that the concept is worth further testing. My mathematical models are relatively crude and they don't absolutely prove the design, however, they do demonstrate potential. I am making this design available on the web because I think there is potential, but I know that I don't have the resources to bring it beyond the current design phase. There is additional analysis, prototypes, and further research on the feasibility of this machine.
