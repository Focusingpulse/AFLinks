I found an Abstract which states a fire piston reaches 750 kelvin = 890 degree Fahrenheit. A fire piston with a narrow diameter and long stroke can get very hot with just a small amount of effort, but might hold less tender than a larger piston that would require a little more effort but can hold more tender?
