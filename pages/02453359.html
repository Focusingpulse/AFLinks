Hi; I have my Dad's January 1965 Glossary of Electronic Properties by Emil Schafer EPIC Report No. S-7. Under Hall Coefficient: The Corbino effect is a special case of the Hall effect which occurs when a disc carrying a radial current is placed in a magnetic field which is perpendicular to the plane of the disc. I did a web search but I could only find that, while the Hall effect is resistive in nature, the Corbino effect is a conductance phenomenon. Any comments are welcome.
