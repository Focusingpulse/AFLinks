A novel experiment in the classes of defective children in the public schools of New York is to be tried this fall, provided the sanction of the board of education is obtained. This experiment consists of electrifying a school room, which process, it is maintained by experts, arouses the children to greater mental and physical ability and tends to make them brighter in every respect. It is proposed to experiment with the subnormal pupils first, since they have more need of a brain stimulator. Provided the school commissioners are willing to allow the tests the work is to be in charge of Nikola Tesla, the inventor. The scientist, City Superintendent of Schools William H. Maxwell and Dr. L. B. Blan have had several conferences on the matter, and when the subject was thoroughly explained to Doctor Maxwell he expressed his willingness to have the scheme tried out. It is likely that the proposition will be put up to the board of education at an early fall meeting. City Superintendent Maxwell said that since the electrifying process is still in the experimental form his first demand was an assurance from Mr. Tesla that, let alone helping the pupils, the test would in no wise be detrimental.
