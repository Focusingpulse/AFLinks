Fourth Meeting — GA 300. Faculty Meetings with Rudolf Steiner (1998)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 22 December 1919 · archived # 2,441,872
, Stuttgart Fourth Meeting The teachers took turns providing afternoon child care. A teacher asks a question about what they should do with the children. Dr. Steiner: The children should enjoy themselves. You can allow them to play, or they could also put on a play or do their homework. In afterschool care, you should be a child yourself and make the children laugh. The children should do something other than their normal school activities.
