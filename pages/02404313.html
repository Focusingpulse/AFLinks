Reshetnev Information Satellite Systems 52, Lenin Str., Zheleznogorsk, Krasnoyarsk region, 662972, Russian Federation 2 JSC “Scientific & Industrial Сentre “Polyus” 56v, Kirov Av., Tomsk, 634050, Russian Federation * E-mail: erm@iss-reshetnev.ru A common way to form an electric propulsion subsystem of the spacecraft is to create specialized equipment or to select the most suitable one from the ready-made ones. However, there are cases when the use of existing equipment is not optimal enough and leads to an unjustified increase of the subsystem mass. Therefore, the question of creating a minimum equipment set possibility from which it would be possible to form propulsion subsystems in optimal way is of interest. The set of tasks, variants of use and possible schemes of placing orbital correcting propulsion on the spacecraft are presented.
