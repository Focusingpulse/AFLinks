Fleischmann-Pons boil off accompanies Introduction to Cold Fusion

👤 Martin Fleischmann · · archived # 2,464,520
Published on May 8, 2015 by Jed Rothwell Youtube Channel : This video was provided to LENR-CANR.org by Prof. Martin Fleischmann , IMRA Europe. It shows a time lapse video of four cells driven to boiling. This experiment was described in the following paper: Fleischmann, M. and S. Pons. Calorimetry of the Pd-D2O System: from Simplicity via Complications to Simplicity . in Third International Conference on Cold Fusion. http://lenr-canr.org/acrobat/Fleischmancalorimetra.pdf IMRA time lapse video of boil off experiment https://youtu.be/Tn9K1Hvw434 This original Fleischmann and Pons video shows the dramatic heat effect of the cold fusion reaction on the water fuel. On Jed Rothwell Youtube Channel , it accompanies the Brief Introduction to Cold Fusion : A six-minute introduction to cold fusion (the Fleischmann-Pons effect). The script and Explanatory Notes and Additional Resources are here: http://lenr-canr.org/wordpress/?page_id=1618 This video explains why we know that cold fusion is a real effect, why it is not yet a practical source of energy, and why it will have many advantages if it can be made practical. For more information, please see http://lenr-canr.org Brief Introduction to Cold Fusion https://youtu.be/HjvL4zNLOGw
