In attempts to explain this anomaly it was supposed that the neutron is described by a wave packet that contains plane wave components with high enough energy to propagate over the potential barrier. Then the losses can be ascribed to the probability of penetration through barrier in non tunneling fashion that is proportional to part of the energy spectrum which is shared by high energy components.
