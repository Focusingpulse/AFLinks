I know this sounds complicated but it works in space and vacuum engines don't do much work unless you have some hugh piston areas to deal with. An ordinary gas engine (internal combustion) have combustion chamber pressures in the thousands of pounds of pressure at ignition of the fuel air mix before the adiabatic expansion and exhaust from the cylinder.
