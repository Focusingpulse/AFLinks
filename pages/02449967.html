Basically, no one has demonstrated a working tabletop demo that others can build independently to verify the effect. Whether that be in a kit or plans that by god work, when others can directly experience the wonder of a working device, it will take off like wildfire and the inventor will be bombarded with all kinds of opportunities, offers and later awards for his/her sharing the basic technology. That is why we had the shareware offer at; http://www.keelynet.com/share.htm Instead the inventor goes nuts and seeks big money to build an overcomplex unit that fails to meet the inventors promises and the investors expectations. That in addition to ego, power and greed, all of which come into the mix, either singly or combined. One other MAJOR flaw is that of wanting to PERFECT THE DEVICE. He kept it close to his chest all those years and died with many of his secrets LOST.
