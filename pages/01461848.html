Roller Bearing

· archived # 1,461,848
Did you know that one of the most revolutionary inventions in mechanical engineering was born thanks to an accident in a textile factory? Sven Gustaf Wingqvist, a Swedish engineer, witnessed how friction in the machines caused constant failures and even fires. Instead of giving up, he decided to find a solution. This is how the self-aligning bearing was born—an invention that not only solved his factory’s problem but also changed the world of mechanics forever. In 1906, he patented his design, and a year later he founded SKF (Svenska Kullagerfabriken), a company that would become a global engineering giant. His idea was simple but brilliant: a metal bearing with balls that could align themselves, minimizing friction and extending the lifespan of machines. What started as a local need turned into a key invention for modern industry, and today it remains essential in countless machines and devices.
