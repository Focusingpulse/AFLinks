Dissolution of the German Theosophical Society (DTG) Minutes of the General Assembly of the Berlin Branch of the Theosophical Society (DTG) At 9 o'clock, the chairman Paul Krojanker opened the General Assembly convened by circular letter dated January 3, 1906. Adele Schwiebs and Messrs Richard Arendt and Referendar (junior lawyer) Richard Fränkel were present. Count Cay and Countess Sophie von Brockdorff as well as Helene von Borcke had sent their votes by letter, and City Councilor Luise Eberty and Comtesse Eva von Krockow had authorized Adele
