This was heavily discussed in 1994 on Keelynet, many people here are already familiar. It involves AC, coils, and capacitors. The short version: if volts and current in an AC wave are at zero phase angle or 180deg phase, there is a continuous energy flow in the pair of wires. But if the phase angle is 90deg or 270deg, there is no energy flow. So, if you ONLY measure voltage and current, and ignore the phase between them, you know nothing about energy flow.
