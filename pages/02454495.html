Pete, The short answer: UV light produces hydrogen peroxide (H2O2) in water, or ozone (O3) in air, and either of these products is germicidal. If that 'UV gizmo' you saw is a quality unit, then it should work well. I got mine from Jim Nigra, 818-889-6877 -- a Coast Filtration (factory phone 714-990-4602) model UVMB-6, 6 watts, 125 vac, 12-18 months life, 1 gal/min (2.5" dia x 13" long, with 1/4" tube fittings), was $70. Intended for use between your prefilter and main filter (it should keep germs from breeding in your main filter). --Bill ~~~~~~~~~~ Pete, The 'life' rating is for the fluorescent UV light tube. The tube may 'light up' longer, but the UV-emitting phosphors eventually degrade, and usually don't emit enough germicidal UV-B and UV-C for more than 12 - 18 months of full-time use. I only turn my unit on while I'm filling several 5-gallon bottles, so it should last for 25 years, assuming that all the on-off cycles don't limit it's life.
