Sohn is a member of the Natural Philosophy Society (Council for Natural Philosophy Studies). Biography Sohn resides in Ellicott City, Maryland. Working independently of mainstream academia, Sohn studied the original Vedic and Sanskrit texts of the Rigveda and compared their imagery with concepts drawn from plasma physics and the Electric Universe and Plasma Universe schools of thought. Work Sohn's principal work is the book The Magneto-Plasma Cosmology of the Ṛgveda (Rig Veda): An Alternative to the Big Bang
