The exposition, as a whole, will be modeled after that held in Philadelphia in 1884, and the general manager will be C. E. Stump, who was connected with the direction of the Philadelphia display. Although 12 years have elapsed, during which two world's fairs have been held, one at Paris and the other at Chicago, and although electricity has made immense strides since then, yet the Philadelphia exhibition still remains as the most satisfactory display in that department of science that has ever been made. The coming exhibition will be what its Philadelphia predecessor was a complete exhibition, historically, scientifically, mechanically and commercially, of the development of electricity in all its applications down to the present hour. It will be opened by Governor Morton striking a golden key at Albany, and the electric current will be transmitted over Postal wires to San Francisco and back again to the hall of the exposition. The golden key used will be the same touched by President Cleveland in opening the Chicago Fair. Both Edison and Tesla are taking a personal interest in the exhibition, and models of nearly all their inventions will be on view. Mr. Edison's exhibits
