Lindner (born 1958) is an American physician and philosopher known for his criticism of the foundations of modern theoretical physics and for his advocacy of an aether -based, materialist model of the Cosmos. A critic of Relativity and quantum theory , Lindner argues that these are mathematical models of observers' measurements and experiences rather than physical descriptions of the Cosmos, and he proposes instead a "physical space" or "flowing space" conception in which space is a substantial medium that self-organizes into matter and, ultima
