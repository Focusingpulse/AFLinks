On Shocks Tariq Khan Omaha, NE USA A speculative and philosophical essay on the possibility of gene pool regulation of large-scale human population, akin to ant colonies in Nature. Human gene pools are considered as oscillating entities wavering between expansionist, peaceful, and open phases to phases of consolidated, militaristic, and closed societies.
