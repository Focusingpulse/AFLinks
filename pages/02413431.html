Hal Treacy , one of the founders of the original International Tesla Society in Colorado Springs, passed away around December 8, 2021 near Dayton, NV. A vital member of our community... Hal was instrumental in assisting Dr Elizabeth Rauscher in producing the Proceedings of the 1984 Tesla Centennial Symposium. For months he made a monthly pilgrimage to Colorado Springs from the Bay area to assist us in developing our bylaws and making the transition from a conference committee to a non-profit organization. Hal introduced me to the world of Wilhem Reich , orgone energy , and other esoteric fields of research. Due to his influence, I was able to broaden my horizons and, like Tesla , break free of the constraints conventional thinking. He will be missed!
