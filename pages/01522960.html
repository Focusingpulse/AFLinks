Overview Assuming the existence of a fundamental aether frame and the anisotropy of the one-way speed of light in the Earth frame — assumptions Levy argues are supported by theory and by experiment — the book derives a set of space-time transformations more general than the standard Lorentz–Poincaré transformations.
