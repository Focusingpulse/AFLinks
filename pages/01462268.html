Sympathetic - Terms and Phrases

· archived # 1,462,268
Sympathy or Sympathetic means or occurs when two or more individual objects or conditions are in harmony or synchrony . They behave "As One". For instance, the objects may be said to be in a state of Love . Keely used these and related terms throughout his research. The concept was and is still used in music as in Sympathetic String . In today's language we use the terms in tune , entangled or entanglement .
