MolPhot2026-main036 Possibility of modifying CsPbBr3 single crystals using electron beam irradiation and the dependence of the domain structure of CsPbBr3 single crystals on the choice of antisolvent during synthesis Nikolay M. Kireev1 1 St Petersburg University n.kireev@spbu.ru Inorganic halide perovskites, which use cesium as the A+ cation, have greater thermal and structural stability than organic ones, which may use methylammonium or formamidinium. In this work, the domain structure is studied, as well as the irradiation of CsPbBr3 single crystals with an electron beam. The work examines the effect of electron beam irradiation on the photoluminescence of a CsPbBr3 single crystal. This effect has already been studied using the example of MAPbCl3 [1], however, in the case of CsPbBr3 , only superficial changes in photoluminescence are observed at higher doses. When cooled to helium temperature, these changes become insignificant, which allows us to conclude that this halide perovskite is resistant to electron beam irradiation up to an energy of 10 keV and a dose of 3∙1016 cm-2. We also studied the reorientation of domains of CsPbBr3 single crystals when heated to a temperature of 410 K and cooled to a temperature of 293 K, as well as the dependence of the domain structure on the choice of antisolvent (acetone, methanol and ethanol) during synthesis.
