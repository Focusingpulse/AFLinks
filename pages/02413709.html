In this age, nothing. In the early 19th century, the meanings of motor and engine had already begun to converge, both referring to a mechanism providing propulsive force. “The first recorded use of ‘engine’ to mean an electrical machine driven by a petroleum motor occurs in 1853,” Fuller. There is an ENGINE in your IC powered automobile. There is MOTOR on your lathe. The usual convention is that an engine is mechanical, using some sort of fuel and energy from expansion of gases. Thus, there are internal combustion engines, steam engines, Stirling engines, and jet engines. Motors are usually powered using electricity and magnetism. But it may also apply to engines. So you have motorists, motorboats, outboard motors, and motor vehicle laws. There are also motor neurons. As I have always understood it, an engine is a prime mover. An engine uses some kind of fuel and converts the energy, usually chemical energy, to mechanical energy. Thus we have steam engines that can burn a variety of fuels, gasoline engines, diesel engines, jet engines, rocket engines, etc. All of these convert the chemical energy that is stored in the fuel into mechanical energy. A motor is a secondary device that takes energy that has already been extracted from some source and converted to electrical or hydraulic or pneumatic or some other form of energy and converts it into mechanical energy. So we have electric motors, hydraulic motors, pneumatic motors, windup/spring motors, etc. Even a simple elastic band as used in model aircraft can be considered a motor.
