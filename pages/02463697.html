It develops the Aether Physics Model (APM) 2 LEDGER ONE ANCHOR (ROTATION–TORSION BRIDGE) 1 Abstract We present a discrete, torsion-first constitutive law for nuclear binding energies using the Pauling fill sequence as encoded in the QMU torsion ledger. The state space is strictly discrete (integer occupancies of the 29-step Pauling ordering through the 184 closure), and we define a discrete feature basis consisting of bulk torsion slots, closure events, proton–neutron coclosure locks, a/b conjugation closures, and integer defect distances-to-closure. Two kernels are evaluated on a strict holdout split of 2550 nuclei derived from AME2020: (Kernel A) a monotone nonnegative law (NNLS) that provides a cohesion baseline, and (Kernel B) a signed torsion-redistribution law (ridge) that resolves fine structure. The signed kernel achieves test-set RMSE ≈ 37.24 (in enrg-coefficient units) with R2 ≈ 0.99849, while the monotone kernel achieves R2 ≈ 0.99088. The results demonstrate that a discrete torsion mechanism can reproduce the empirical binding surface without probabilistic inputs; the remaining interpretive task is to refine the ontology of “defects” into distinct discrete objects when a strict penalty-only axiom is desired. 1 Scope, data provenance, and QMU stance 1.1 What is being modeled This paper targets a binding-energy coefficient bemp (Z, N ) expressed in QMU energy units enrg (no SI presentation).
