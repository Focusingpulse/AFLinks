Torsion Field Spectroscopy Diana Wojtkowiak (1), Kazimierz Raduszkiewicz (2), Marian Wojtkowiak, Andrzej Frydrychowski (1) POLAND (Gdańsk 2017) (1) Establishment of Human Physiology, Faculty of Health Sciences with the Department of Nursing and the Institute of Maritime and Tropical Medicine, Medical University of Gdańsk, ul. Tuwima 15, 80-210 Gdańsk. (2) POLIMOR sp. z o.o., Gdańsk Authorised by the Author, translation by Piotr Bein June 30, 2025, from undated Polish version captured on Dec 30, 2020, by Wayback Machine at https://web.archive.org/web/20201230075915/http://www.torsionfield.eu/wordpress/wpcontent/uploads/2018/07/Spektroskopia-pól-torsyjnych-9F.doc Abstract The publication describes the construction of a torsion field spectroscope built on the basis of the previously unknown properties of torsion fields discovered by the authors, which in the first approximation, satisfy all the laws of geometric optics, taking into account the typical wavelength of the torsion field of approx. 2 mm and the fractional refractive index of glass for torsion fields. The possibility of analytical and preparative separation of torsion fields originating from elements differing in the number of protons and/or neutrons and their ionized forms was demonstrated, as well as the study of the composition and properties of chemical compounds. It was established that torsional radiation originating from radioactive decays represents mainly decay products, including elementary particles, as well as a signal from the illumination of other atoms by this torsion field.
