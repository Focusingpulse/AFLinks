, Paris Translated by René Queirdo XI. The Devachanic World (Heaven) I [ 1 ] Devachan is the Sanscrit term for the long period of time lying between the death and rebirth of man. After death, in the astral world, the soul first learns to cast off the instincts that are connected with the body. After this, the soul passes into Devachan for the long period that lies between two incarnations. The devachanic world is a state or condition of existence.
