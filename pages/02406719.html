II TI-59 About the Book Programmable calculators have been on the market for the past few years and have added a new dimension to engineering calculations. The programmable calculator eliminates the tedious and repetitious calculations which have been a source of numerous errors in the past. Once a set of calculations is determined, it is programmed, recorded, and stored for future use. Since its inception, engineers have recognized the value of the calculator for its convenience, efficiency, and unlimited potential. Programs relating to most aspects of reservoir engineering have been published in several magazines and trade journals, but the TI-59 Reservoir Engineering Manual is the first book published specifically for hand-held calculators. Its 27 reservoir engineering programs can be used in conjunction with the TI-59 programmable calculator or, using the RPN Simulator Reverse Polish Notation module, with the Hillard-Packard model. The easy-to-read manual is divided into five sections. The first section is on basic reservoir engineering, which offers the calculations most frequently used by the engineer. The other four sections are designed for economic analysis, geology, log interpretation, and advanced reservoir engineering. Within each section, the programs are presented with methods, equations, and flow charts which describe the required input and resulting output. Also included with each program are an example and a program listing for the user’s convenience. The TI-59 Reservoir Engineering Manual is concise and complete; it is easy to use and comprehend. This is a book every reservoir engineer will want.
