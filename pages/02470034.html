How could I have forgotten anything so salient I do not know, but I did, and it has ever since much vexed me. I had been describing my discovery of the identity of the Lennard-ray and the Roentgen-ray, and when I showed the photographs I failed to say that most of them had been taken by means of the Lennard bulb. As you can see, the photographs are wonderfully clear, showing even the muscles of the subject, and of course are vital confirmation of my discovery of the identity of the Lennard with the Roentgen-ray. What I did was first to take Lennard's bulb and use it in connection with the ordinary transformer, the result being the same as in the experience of Lennard, namely, a failure to produce a light. But when I used it with my new transformer it proved perfectly effective, and enabled me to take these fine photographs."
