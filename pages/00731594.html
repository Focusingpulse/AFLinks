A Study of Continuous Frames and Operators in Quaternionic Hilbert Spaces Najib Khachiaa Laboratory Partial Differential Equations, Spectral Algebra and Geometry, Department of Mathematics, Faculty of Sciences, University Ibn Tofail, Kenitra, Morocco. Contributing authors: khachiaa.najib@uit.ac.ma; Abstract The aim of this work is to study continuous frame theory in quaternionic Hilbert spaces. We provide a characterization of continuous frames in these spaces through the associated operators. Additionally, we examine continuous frames of the form LF : Ω → H, where (Ω, µ) is a measure space, L : H → H is a right H-linear bounded operator and F : Ω → H is a continuous frame for H. Keywords: Continuous frames, Operator theory, Quaternionic Hilbert spaces MSC Classification: 42C15; 42C40; 42A38; 46C20; 47A13. 1 Introduction and preliminaries Frames in quaternionic Hilbert spaces offer a powerful way to analyze and rebuild sig￾nals in higher-dimensional spaces. The special features of quaternionic spaces open up new possibilities, especially in areas like signal process￾ing and communication. This work aims to explore the theory of continuous frames in quaternionic Hilbert spaces.
