How the Nazis Split the Foundations of 20th Century Physics Alan M. Kadin (Retired) Princeton Junction, NJ amkadin@alumni.princeton.edu Poster for Submission to APS April Meeting 2023 1 Summary • De Broglie first derived quantum waves in 1924, directly from special relativity, and Einstein approved. • But in Germany in 1930s, the Nazis viewed relativity or anything related to Einstein as subversive “Jewish Physics”. • I argue that German physicists obscured the relativistic basis for quantum mechanics, in order to avoid dismissal or worse. • Early German QM textbooks led to a split in the foundations of physics that has continued to the present. • An alternative “quantum relativity” picture could reunify physics, but has never been considered. 2 De Broglie Waves • De Broglie in 1924 assumed a relativistic particle given by E = gmc2 and p = gmv, [or E2 = (pc)2 + (mc2 ) 2 ] associated with a distributed oscillation w = w0 = mc2 /h in its rest frame. • Lorentz-transform to moving reference frame, time delays lead to wave with l = h/p, de Broglie wave with w2 = (kc)2 + w0 2 . • He then showed that group velocity of wave packet vg = w/k is same as particle velocity v = E/p, so they move together. • De Broglie always believed in the reality of both a point particle and a distributed wave packet that move together. – (I am partial to a purely wave picture with no point particles.) 3 Relativity and Simultaneity • In classical physics, events simultaneous in one reference frame are also simultaneous in other reference frames. – For example, simultaneous wave crests define the wavelength l of a sound wave, so l remains the same in all reference frames. • But this is NOT true for relativistic waves because of space￾time mixing. – Simultaneous events such as wave crests are NOT simultaneous in other reference frames, leading to time-shifts and changes in wavelength. – For example, the wavelength of an EM wave can be red-shifted or blue-shifted by changing reference frames.
