In this paper, we propose a method to improve the performance of dynamic latent scale GAN by integrat￾ing perceptual VAE loss into dynamic latent scale GAN efficiently. This random variable can be used for both VAE and GAN training. Considering the intermediate layer output of the discriminator as a feature encoder output, the generator can be trained to minimize perceptual VAE loss. Also, inference & back￾propagation for perceptual VAE loss can be integrated into those for GAN training. Therefore, perceptual VAE training does not require additional computation.
