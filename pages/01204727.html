(Dated: August 14, 2025) Abstract We study the head words of the Pali-English Dictionary by T. W. Rhys Davids and William Stede, the 5th reprint, Delhi, 2018. We draw the natural logarithm of the number of head words, normalised, starting with a letter vs the natural logarithm of the rank of the letter, normalised. We conclude that the dictionary can be characterised by BW(c=0.005), the magnetisation curve of the Ising Model in the Bragg-Williams approximation in the presence of external magnetic field, H. c = H γϵ = 0.005 with ϵ being the strength of coupling between two neighbouring spins in the Ising Model, γ representing the number of nearest neighbours of a spin, which very large. ∗ anindya@nehu.ac.in 1 FIG. 1.
