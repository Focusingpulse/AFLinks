Abstract In this paper we introduced new types of open and closed sets in bi-topological spaces, where we have introduced the definition of pre-Nα-open sets in bi-topological spaces, as the we have created the relationship between them and open and closed sets in these bi-topological spaces, we studied the basic properties of pre-Nα-open sets in this spaces.
