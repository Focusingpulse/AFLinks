Roy Keys Inc.), Montreal Pages 460 ISBN 9781987980257 Wilhelm Weber's Main Works on Electrodynamics Translated into English, Volume II: Weber's Fundamental Force and the Unification of the Laws of Coulomb, Ampère and Faraday is the second volume of the five-volume edition of Wilhelm Weber 's electrodynamic works edited and translated by Andre K T Assis , published by Apeiron in 2021. It centres on Weber's First Memoir of 1846, the paper in which he stated the fundamental force law between two electric charges that depends not only on their separation but on the rate at which that separation changes. The volume contains, in translation and with editorial introductions: The Gauss–Weber correspondence of 1845 [Fechner, 1845] On the connection between Faraday's induction phenomena and Ampère's electrodynamic phenomena [Weber, 1846] Electrodynamic Measurements, First Memoir — the memoir in which Weber's fundamental force law first appears [Weber, 1848] On the measurement of electrodynamic forces [Weber, 1848] On the excitation and action of diamagnetism [Kirchhoff, 1849] On a deduction of Ohm's laws, in connexion with the theory of electrostatics [Weber, 1851] On the measurement of electric resistance according to an absolute standard [Weber, 1852] Electrodynamic Measurements, Second Memoir , relating specially to measures of resistance Publication Published by Apeiron (C. Roy Keys Inc.) of Montreal in 2021, 460 pages, ISBN 9781987980257. The complete text is available without charge from the editor's page at UNICAMP.
