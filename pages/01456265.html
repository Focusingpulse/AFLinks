Chapter XVII Four Kinds of Suggestion Table of Contents Mental suggestion produces its effect upon the minds of people along one or more of four general lines or paths of action. All the phenomena coming under this head may be placed in one or more of the four classes. These four paths, or lines of action, along which Mental Suggestion operates, are as follows: 1. Obedience; 2. Imitation; 3. Association; 4. Repetition. I shall now proceed to consider these four paths, or lines of action, separately, in order, and in detail.
