Lecture Ten - Vol. 123. The Gospel of Matthew (1946)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 10 September 1910 · archived # 2,422,408
, Bern Translator Unknown Lecture Ten [ 1 ] We showed in the last lecture that what Christ Jesus means for human evolution is the gradual equipment of the human ego with those forces and capacities formerly only possible of attainment in the Mysteries of the past, when the ego was to a certain extent suppressed. In all ancient initiations it was possible to rise up into the spiritual worlds, into what we called the Kingdoms of Heaven, but with human nature constituted as it was in pre-Christian times, this could not be done while within the ego or while the nature of the ego remained as it was
