IS NOW AS CHEAP AS GAS Wholesale Users Can Get Electricity at Even a Lower Price Than the Older Illuminant. FURTHER CUTS POSSIBLE. To meet the reduction in electric light rates made by the Edison Company on October 1, the United Electric Light and Power Company yesterday issued a new schedule, with a rate considerably lower than that of the rival company. This reduction, which will operate on bills for this month, places incandescent lights on the same footing with gas, practically, and large users will get the light even cheaper than gas. An attempt was made last winter in the Legislature at Albany to make electric light companies lower the price for incandescent lighting from the one cent an hour basis per sixteen candle power lamp to a three-quarters of a cent basis, but the bill did not become a law. Now the companies have made a cut voluntarily. Likely to Affect Gas Companies. This reduction, made by the two electric lighting companies, is the result, in a way, of a war between them, but the effect, it is said, will be felt the most by the gas companies.
