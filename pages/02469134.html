OPERATION MIND CONTROL Walter Bowart was born in Omaha, Nebraska, in 1939. He was awarded a McMahon journalism scholarship to the University of Oklahoma and has since worked as an editor, publisher and writer. His articles have appeared in many journals including The East Village Other, the underground newspaper which he founded in 1965. Walter Bowart lives in Tucson, Arizona, with his wife and three children.
