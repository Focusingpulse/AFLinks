Today we can still read its text, but a lecture, and especially one by Nikola Tesla, is so much more than just text. Kyle dell'Aquila has added hundreds of illustrations to this text that will take you back in time and give you a glimpse of what this lecture may have looked like. This is all combined into a beautiful soft cover photobook that is now available on blurb.com .
