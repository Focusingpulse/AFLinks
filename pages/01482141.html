Schauberger The open secret of water consists in the fact that processes of organic synthesis take place in the naturalesque conduction of water , whereas processes of organic analysis occur when it is conducted unnaturally . In the former case, the growth of water and an increase in latent energy occurs, hence a qualitative improvement . In the latter case, it results in the disappearance of water , to losses in latent energy and logically to a decline in quality .
