In order to demonstrate how his 3D printed MicroCubes could revolutionize the way we generate renewable energy, Yost, the founder of American Wind, will drive across the United States in an electric vehicle powered by four MicroCubes without stopping even once to plug in the battery for a recharge. Each individual MicroCube stands at only 9x9x9 inches and weighs 3 lbs, but is capable of generating 3-phase AC power, which can either be converted to DC power for backup storage, or directly connected to power grids. For increased power, MicroCubes can be stacked behind one another in what Yost calls a wind wall to capture each others output.
