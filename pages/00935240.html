One of the most promising candidate to trigger the abnormal immunological host response is the SARS-CoV open reading frame 3a (ORF3a). The ORF3a gene encodes an accessory protein that, expressed in infected cells and localized at the endoplasmic reticulum/Golgi intermediate compartment, forms multimeric complexes acting as ion channels and regulating virus release (Ding et al., 2017).
