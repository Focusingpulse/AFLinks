Roy Keys Inc.), Montreal Pages 464 ISBN 9780986492600 Ether Space-Time and Cosmology, Vol. 3: Physical Vacuum, Relativity and Quantum Physics is a collection of thirteen papers edited by Michael C Duffy and Joseph Levy , published by Apeiron of Montreal. It is the last of the three volumes in the Ether Space-Time and Cosmology series, following volume 1 (2008) and volume 2 (2009). Where volume 1 surveyed the routes by which a modern aether concept had been arrived at, this volume turns to the physical vacuum — its energy, the Casimir effect , and the question of whether the properties routinely attributed to empty space amount to a substratum under another name — and to what follows for quantum theory, dark matter and dark energy . Its contributors are drawn from the circle around the Physical Interpretations of Relativity Theory conferences, and several of the papers were given at PIRT XI in September 2008 before being collected here. Publisher's description The ether space-time & cosmology program comprises several volumes designed to inform the physics community of the resurgence of the ether in modern science. The reality of the concept and its importance, which were denied at the beginning of the 20th century, aroused renewed interest by the end of the 20th century and the beginning of the 21st century. Research undertaken during the last 20 years has confirmed the existence of physical properties within space, even where it is devoid of ordinary matter. In addition to the well known properties of permittivity, permeability and the ability to transmit electromagnetic waves, more recently other features have been associated with the concept of space.
