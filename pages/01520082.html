Loops of action combining in fractal iterations: three loops (a) and nine (b) Scientific Theory Name Field Structure Theory Type Structural physics; classical alternative to the Standard Model and quantum mechanics Author(s) Charles Donald "Don" Briddell Keywords fieldstructures, Structural Skew Topology, action loops, plenum, Sierpinski hierarchy, Particle Hierarchy Chart, chirality, torsion, knots Year 1966 (thesis); 1999 (particle hierarchy); 2005 (first NPA paper); 2021 (book) Website https://web.archive.org/web/20251106234228/http://www.fieldstructure.org/ Field Structure Theory ( FST ) is a structural interpretation of physics developed by the American sculptor, industrial designer and independent theorist Charles Donald "Don" Briddell (b. 1944). It proposes that the natural world is a product of fieldstructure geometry : that particles, waves, mass, energy and charge are not primitive entities but configurations of loops, twists and knots in an underlying medium, and that their properties — including their mass values — follow from how those loops are counted, twisted and oriented. FST is a deterministic and background-dependent theory, offered as an alternative to the Standard Model and quantum mechanics . Its geometry is formalized in a companion system Briddell calls Structural Skew Topology (SST). The theory has been developed privately since the 1960s and presented publicly since 2004, chiefly at conferences of the Natural Philosophy Alliance and the Alternative Natural Philosophy Alliance, and in the book The Particle Hierarchy Paradigm: According to Field Structure Theory (2021). It lies outside mainstream physics. Origins FST grew not out of physics but out of design — a fact that shapes its whole character.
