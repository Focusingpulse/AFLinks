SPAF-2026-main021 CDU: 547.593.3 Chemo- and stereoselective assembly of (2,4-dihydroxycyclohex-1-en-1-yl)methanones based on the reactions of CF3 -enones with 1,3-dicarbonyl compounds Nikolai I. Borodin1 1 A.E. Favorsky Irkutsk Institute of Chemistry SB RAS niborodin@mail.ru Fluorinated compounds are ubiquitous in pharmaceuticals and agrochemicals. In particular, trifluoromethyl￾containing compounds are important because the CF3-group can improve the metabolic stability, lipophilicity and selectivity of bioactive molecules. Therefore, the development of efficient and flexible methods to construct trifluoromethyl-substituted compounds has received much attention from academia. Among these compounds, α,β￾unsaturated trifluoromethyl ketones have proved to be ready available building blocks for synthesis different hetero- and carbocycles [1]. Earlier, we described synthetically simple and effective method for the synthesis of α,β-unsaturated trifluoromethyl ketones, based on the condensation of aromatics aldehyde with 2,2,2-trifluoroacetone in the presence of organocatalyst [2]. With our continuing interest in the synthesis of small hetero- and carbocycles, herein, we report an asymmetric Michael 1,4-addition/cyclisation cascade reaction of α,β-unsaturated trifluoromethyl ketones with 1,3-diones to construct chiral ((4R,6R)-2,4-dihydroxycyclohex-1-en-1-yl)methanones bearing CF3-group with yield up to 92% (Scheme 1).
