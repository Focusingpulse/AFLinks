fficial Organ of the Union of Ancient Freemasons of the Scottish, Memphis and Misraim Rites. Grand Orient in Germany”, 5th year, issue 1, January-June 1906. Dr. Rudolf Steiner, 33rd, 95th, in Berlin and the brothers and sisters associated with him have been granted permission to found a chapter and a grand council of the adoption masonic lodge in Berlin under the name “Mystica aeterna”. Dr. Steiner was appointed deputy Grand Master with jurisdiction over the members he had accepted or was to accept. Sister Marie von Sivers was appointed General Grand Secretary for the Adoption Lodges. Berlin E
