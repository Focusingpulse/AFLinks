Overview Baseless and Irrationality of Modern Cosmology (近代宇宙学的无据性和无理性) is a three-page paper by Zifeng Li with Wu Huan and Li Jiwei of the Petroleum Engineering Institute at Yanshan University, Qinhuangdao, published in 2011. It is written in Chinese with an English abstract, keywords and title. Li's own note gives his background: born in Hebei in 1962, he graduated in drilling engineering from the Daqing Petroleum Institute in 1983, took a doctorate at the China University of Petroleum (Beijing) in 1992, held a mechanics postdoctoral position at the Harbin Institute of Technology from 1992 to 1994, and is now a doctoral supervisor at Yanshan University. The paper is a position statement rather than a derivation. It takes six pillars of modern cosmology in turn — the Big Bang, stellar redshift, Hubble's law, multidimensional space, curved space and black holes — and argues in each case that the theory is 无据 (without evidence) and 无理 (without reason). The objections are brief and mostly epistemic. On the Big Bang: if the universe came from a singularity, where did the singularity come from?
