GC2026-SGG048 Heterogeneities of S wave attenuation field and ring-shaped seismicity structures forming before large earthquakes Yuri F. Kopnichev1 , Inna N. Sokolova2 1 O.Yu. Schmidt Institute of Physics of the Earth, Russian Academy of Sciences 2 Unified Geophysical Service of the Russian Academy of Sciences sokolovain@gsras.ru Short-period shear waves high attenuation anomalies in the crust and upper mantle associated with fluid migration were observed prior to large and largest earthquakes in various seismically active regions. In these regions, ring-shaped seismic structures are formed at various depth ranges. Analysis of the characteristics of these structures allows us to identify potential sites for the preparation of large earthquakes and estimate its magnitudes and preparation time. We are considered of successful forecasts of large and largest earthquakes in various regions of the globe (the Kamchatka mega-earthquake of 2025, the Simeon and Chignik earthquakes in the Alaska region, and others). We have been studying S wave attenuation field structure in the Caucasus lithosphere. The high attenuation area has been identified in the Northwest Caucasus region, where no earthquakes with Mw≥6.0 have been registered based on instrumental or historical data. This area is highly likely associated with the preparation of a large seismic event.
