In my definition of atom I do not confine myself to the sixth subdivision , where this luminiferous ether is developed in its crude form, as far as my researches prove. I think this idea will be pronounced, by the physicists of the present day, a wild freak of the imagination . Possibly, in time , a light may fall upon this theory that will bring its simplicity forward for scientific research.
