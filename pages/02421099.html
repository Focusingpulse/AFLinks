ExtraOrdinary Technology Former name Extraordinary Science ISO 4 abbreviation Find out here Discipline Tesla technology, new energy , alternative medicine Peer-reviewed NoNo Language English Edited by Steven R Elswick Publication details Publisher TeslaTech, LLC (United States) Publication history 2003–present Frequency Quarterly Links [ Back issues Journal homepage] ExtraOrdinary Technology is the quarterly magazine of TeslaTech , published and edited by Steven R Elswick . The first issue, Volume 1 Number 1, is dated January/February/March 2003. It carries the strapline "Bridging the Gap... Between Conventional ... and ExtraOrdinary!", and in later years the title has also appeared as ExtraOrdinary Science & Technology . History and character The magazine descends from Extraordinary Science , the periodical of the International Tesla Society which Elswick edited and produced, and it succeeded his Exotic Research Report (1996–1999). It was offered in the mid-2000s as a benefit of a US$30 annual TeslaTech membership, and it functions as the journal of record for the community around the ExtraOrdinary Technology Conference : several issues carry the conference speaker list and write-ups of the talks. The magazine is not peer reviewed , and it does not present itself as such. The publisher's column in the very first issue took, in its own summary, "a firm stance on the issue of PEER REVIEW" — the position, consistent across TeslaTech's output, being that conventional review acts to exclude the independent inventors the magazine exists to publish. Readers should treat the contents accordingly; claims of over-unity output, antigravity and energetic healing appear throughout and are not accepted by mainstream physics or medicine. Content divides between construction and do-it-yourself articles (the aluminium-air battery and aluminium-can fuel cell projects of 2005 are typical), experimental and device reports from contributors, theory pieces, and conference coverage.
