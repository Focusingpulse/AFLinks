potential density

· archived # 1,482,513
"The potential gradient actually is a change to the ambient potential, and so it contains an excess energy density (the magnitude may be either positive or negative ). We then collect this potential (actually this potential density ) on a certain number of coulombs , which places tiny little gradients of potential across (coupled to) each free electron. The local excess energy density of the potential gradient multiplied by the amount of collecting mass gives the amount of excess energy collected (on the interacting charges/coulombs)."
