We settle an open problem about such layers and then identify what actually governs them. First, the conjecture that two vertex orbits force εharm(K4) = 0 is refuted by an exact 12-vertex counterexample with |Aut| = 24, reproduced by two implementations whose constructions of the invariant cochain space share no logic. Second, we localize the failure: it is not a shortage of cells. On the witness's two-dimensional residue, all 1083 arena 4-cycles supply rank 1, while 20 arena 3-cycles of an excluded shape supply rank 2, and the two nonzero square families have the same image, a single line. Third, we refute the natural mechanism for this — that the residue is an odd-cycle blind spot — by exhibiting bipartite arenas, containing no odd cycle of any length, that still carry residue. The operative property is omission from the declared family, not parity. Fourth, we prove that εharm(K3,4) > 0 on an infinite family — every cycle graph with n ≥ 5 and 2 ≤ K ≤ n−1, under the trivial group and the rotation subgroup, and zero under the full dihedral group — answering a question the odd-cycle reading had posed. Fifth, we define closure depth Lclosure, prove it exceeds 4 for every member of that family, and measure it as exactly n for Cn and 4 for the witness, so no short vocabulary closes the family.
