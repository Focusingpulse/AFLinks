Experiments using four different liquid types were conducted in which bubbles were self-nucleated without the use of external neutrons. Four independent detection systems were used (i.e., a neutron track plastic detector to provide unambiguous visible records for fast neutrons, a BF3 detector, a NE-113-type liquid scintillation detector, and a NaI  ray detector). Statistically significant nuclear emissions were observed for deuterated benzene and acetone mixtures but not for heavy water. Neutron emission rates were in the range 5 103 n=s to 104 n=s and followed the inverse law dependence with distance. Control experiments did not result in statistically significant neutron or  ray emissions.
