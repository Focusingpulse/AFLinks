Unfortunately, Priore, who died in 1983, never revealed some crucial settings and parameters of use...). At Science Frontieres, we met Bernard Murzeau, a medical doctor, who made an attempt to replicate the device a few years ago, with former Priore assistants and electronics engineers, but could not keep his team on the job long enough. They assembled 3 successive small versions, just to get the knack. Testing for health effects gave irregular results. There were other Priore replication attempts, but they all disposed of the tricky plasma tube and concentrated on radio waves and magnetics. All were blatant failures. Murzeau was the only one to stick to the plasma tube approach, and he thinks he was making progress. Now, at Science Frontieres, Murzeau was invited for a lecture, and was amazed by the interest.
