Kelly This pre-World War I patent shows the details of a device which can produce electricity without mechanical or chemical inputs. The second prototype developed 8 volts DC. The flow of electricity is said to be constant. It operates day and night and works in all locations capable of receiving radio waves. While it will operate indoors, the output is greater if it is located outdoors. The inventor recommends the use of a lightning arrester as used on telegraph lines.
