He invented mathematical Quantum Theory to circumvent visualization and inadequacy of language. But mathematics too is a language to supplement description. He used it to superimpose hidden concepts leading to the Uncertainty Principle. The wave-or-quantum DUALITIES of Quantum Theory appear due to experimental limitations to observe only one and not both of the two aspects simultaneously. Conservation of mass-energy & momentum are inviolable. Matter's creation from, or dissolution into, ?nothing' is unrealistic. So the Heisenberg's Uncertainty Principle is rejected as unrealistic and new Principle of Null Action with universal applications is introduced, which is based on the conservation of mass-energy, momentum and ?Action'. The Copenhagen interpretation of Quantum Theory is reappraised critically. Overview This paper — headed in the PDF "Unified Theory Replaces Quantum Theory & Uncertainty Principle" — is Rati Ram Sharma 's application of his Unified Theory to the quantum domain.
