In places of extreme heat, the search for fresh water and the dangers of sunstroke were never far. In frigid climes, the difficulty of remaining warm and staving off frostbite while securing enough food could not be overemphasized. Even today, in this Era of Progress, Mankind is unable to fully compensate for the hostility of his surroundings. Whenever Man faces a seemingly insurmountable task, it falls to Science to provide a solution. And so it is with this. What could Mankind do with a Device that allows him to heat or cool anything he wishes at any time and to any degree?
