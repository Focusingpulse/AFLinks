The Voltage is set at 2kV/div, the current is set at 50mA/div ( the current has been measured on the R1 non inductive 10 ohms resistor , with a x10 probe, the scope input channel ratio has been set to 1000mV/A ), the time base used is 50us/div. The right side of the scope picture shows datas measured by the scope itself : on Ch1(Voltage) : freq=6.024 kHz at 5kV and on Ch2(Current) : 154mA. Notes : The setup of Ch1 and Ch2 channels used for measuring the voltage and the current has been checked on an external power supply ( see the Probes setup checking ), this avoid some eventual errors in the channels calibration. - The upper right scope picture , shows the third channel of the scope, this is the Math channel, ( Math=Ch1xCh2 ) this gives in real time the curve of the power flow.
