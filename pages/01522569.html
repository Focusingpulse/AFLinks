About With David de Hilster of Dissident Science Summary Franklin Hu hosted with Ian Cohen, Bob Gray , Alistair Riddoch and Cornelis Verhey in the chat, and David de Hilster joining for most of the hour. Two arguments ran side by side: whether the medium filling space is a solid, and how the society should run its own wiki. Is space a solid? Hu began by reading out the week's email exchange.
