9. Goethe's Epistemology - Goethean Science (1988)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 1,554,616
We have already indicated in the previous chapter that Goethe's scientific world view does not exist for us as a complete whole, developed out of one principle. We have to do only with individual manifestations from which we see how one thought or another looks in the light of his way of thinking. This is the case with his scientific works, with the brief indications he gives about one concept or another in his Aphorisms in Prose , and with his letters to his friends.
