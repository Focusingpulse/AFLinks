(C. 47-13) This invention relates to a method of and ap paratus for activation or suppression of biologi cal processes, especially those processes substan tially affected by high frequency electric fields. This method provides for practical treatment of substantial quantities of stock by industrial concerns so efficiently that the benefits of such treatment are available to effect substantial in dustrial economy. For example, it is advanta geous to treat seed bean stock having an untreat ed germination factor of from two to four percent and render that stock forty percent germinative. In like manner the method is effective in the activation or deactivation of fruits to accelerate or retard their life processes, and to delay Or advance their maturation, as Well as to other wise control plant life through promotion of plant health as by devitalization of parasitic, fungus, and insect pests. Industrial adaptation of high frequency treat ment requires the expenditure of large amounts of energy and equipment capacity not hereto fore available.
