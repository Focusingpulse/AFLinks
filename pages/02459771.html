Some researchers have interpreted these skeletons as being a race that built the mounds. Others have conjectured about these skeletons but were unable to determine where the giants came from and how they came to be buried at these sites. If one did a through search of the archives of information, you would find a plethora of references to giant skeletons being located all over the eastern U.S. If you went further in your research covering Europe and other locations, more references would be found of giant skeletons. And in the African Continent, you would find many references to giants in African tribes even today. And, if you read your Bible, you would find references to giants existing in Biblical times. Because many of the giant skeletons have been lost or misplaced in museum archives, there are numerous authors who have offered debunking theories about the existence of the giants.
