During his professional career he specialized in high-voltage switching technology, and after retiring in 1992 he turned to theoretical physics and cosmology, developing an alternative framework he calls the Naturalistic (or Natural) Model of Nature. Biography Abadžić spent his engineering career at the Institute for Electric Power Engineering of ENERGOINVEST in Sarajevo, where he developed and studied high-voltage circuit breakers, switchgears and electrical arcs.
