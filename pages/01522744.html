Connell Born ( 1927-11-01 ) November 1, 1927 Residence Portimao, Algarve, Portugal Nationality English Knownfor Relativity , Gravity Scientific career Fields Physics; instrument development; systems analysis David V. Connell (born 1 November 1927) is an English physicist and former development engineer and systems analyst who, after retiring to Portugal, returned to physics and became a critic of both relativistic and gravitational theory. In a series of papers presented to the Natural Philosophy Alliance between 2009 and 2013 he argued that the changes to mass, length and frequency usually attributed to motion and to gravitation follow instead from the conservation of energy, and that gravity itself is electrostatic in origin rather than a property of mass.
