Resistance shifts of a PdjH electrode in measurement and electrolysis with direct currents Wu-Shou Zhang * Institute of Chemistry and Center for Molecular Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences, P.O. Box 2709, Beijing 100080, China Received 19 January 2004; accepted 3 May 2004 Available online 2 July 2004 Abstract Some mistakes in the paper published in the Journal of Electroanalytical Chemistry 528 (2002) 1 are corrected. The resistance changes of a PdjH electrode caused by the co-conduction of the electrolyte, the concentration-cell effect and collection of electrolysis current in the in situ resistance measurement using direct currents are calculated analytically.
