Let's say someone from this forum figures out a real source of free, clean abundant energy that is universally accessible. What next? Does the person who has the break through in his/her garage while burning the midnight oil share their discovery? Because if they have a miraculous discovery, surely it will be in part because of the community discussion happening here. Or is it more the situation of the first one to the patent office wins? In that case the discussion that are happening here are more information leaching and not really sharing. Is it the case where one shares just enough information to get some help, but not enough information so that another user could replicate the apparatus being talked about? I think it is an important question to ask oneself because are some serious advantages and disadvantages to these ways of perceiving how this forum should operate and what one expects to get out of being a contributing member of the forum.
