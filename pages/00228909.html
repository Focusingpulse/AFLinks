1 cubic ellipsoid nucleus - part 1: the model and its mass formula Ronen Yavor Abstract This paper examines the hypothesis that the structure of the nucleus determines that of the atom and its properties and attempts to construct a geometric model of the nucleus that contributes to this hypothesis. The model proposed here suggests that the structure of the nucleus is, in general, an ellipsoid with the nucleons connected by cubic bonds and the nucleus shells correlate with those of the atom. In accordance with the model, a simplified theoretical mass formula was created to compare it with the experimental data; the test included 82 stable nuclei from Ar18 40 to Pb82 210 .
