We are talking about the invention of G. A. Dotto and W. L. Linn; they have eccentrically installed into an elliptical chamber, a rotor-device which forms 16 partitions, using also 16 dividing mobile walls. Because of the eccentricity of the rotor during its rotational movement, the volume of the active chambers is variable. The position of the dividing walls � which must remain permanently in contact with the inner surface of the body � is very precisely stabilized by a cam which is mounted inside the rotor, in the middle of the rotor shaft. The engine supply with air and oil is made by pping inside the stator. The mixture air / oil at low pressure is sent by an axial blower which is mounted on the shaft.
