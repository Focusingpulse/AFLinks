On account of their cosmopolitan character they need, and they have, no compounding with anything else. They are themselves at home everywhere; like a universal joint , they can turn any way, and affiliate in all directions. Being 4-note chords , and all of minor thirds , their effect is always minor , and they fall with loving softness to the diatonic chords to which they resolve . How this chord in its germ is found in the diatonic chord-scale ; how it becomes a 4-note chord of minor thirds ; how it variously resolves, each one of the three, in three manners with 24 tonic chords - all this is so fully set forth in the pre-note and treatise on the chromatic chord that it need not be more discussed in this place.
