What do they have in common, and what is the difference between them? Satisfactory answers for these questions have not been found so far. That is why an effort is made here to develop more understandable answers to these questions, and mainly to the question of why there exists an impression that there is a magnetic force perpendicular to the direction of motion. It is noted that there is no real reason for the electricity and magnetism to be treated separately.
