INTRODUCTION In quantum mechanics, wave function collapse occurs when a wave function—initially in a superposition of several eigen￾states—reduces to a single eigenstate due to interaction with the external world. This interaction is called an observation, and is the essence of a measurement in quantum mechanics, which connects the wave function with classical observable such as position and momentum. Collapse is one of the two processes by which quantum systems evolve in time; the other is the continuous evolution governed by the Schrodinger ¨ equation. II.
