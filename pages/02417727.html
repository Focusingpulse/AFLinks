The Bible and Wisdom [ 1 ] Yesterday we attempted to bring to mind the meaning through which what we call theosophy seeks to draw closer to the religious texts. Today we will attempt, at least through a few examples, to delve more deeply into the religious text that is surely still closest to contemporary civilized humanity: the Bible. The easiest way to approach the Bible from the standpoint of what is called spiritual science is to begin with the New Testament, specifically the Gospels.
