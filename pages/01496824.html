top of the seventh octave

· archived # 1,496,824
Ramsay The simple natural scale is the fifth ; the compound natural scale is the octave ; the harmony scale , or chord-scale , is the three fifths ; the great genetic scale is six octaves ; for, like the six creation days, it takes the six octaves to give birth to the elements of which the wondrous structure of our music is built up; the birthplace of B, the seventh of the octave scale , is the sixth octave of the great genetic scale . The area of the twelve major and twelve minor scales is twelve fifths or seven octaves, the twelfth fifth being a comma and the apotome minor in advance of the seventh octave . This is a quantity so small that it can be ignored in real music ; and the two notes , say E# and F, joined to close the circle of this horizon of our music world. E# is the top of the twelfth fifth , and F is the top of the seventh octave ; and they are practically, though not exactly mathematically, the same note . Illustrations of this will be found among the plates of this work. [ Scientific Basis and Build of Music , page 79 ] See Also E Sharp 3rd Octave top
