1st International Symposium on New Energy Event Type Conference Dates 16 April 1993 – 18 April 1993 Venue Denver Hilton South Location Denver, CO Country United States Participants 13 Papers 10 Proceedings Proceedings of the International Symposium on New Energy Website Event website 1st International Symposium on New Energy was a conference held 16 April 1993 – 18 April 1993 in Denver Hilton South, Denver, CO, United States. Purpose: To bring together outstanding New Energy Researchers and their hardware, to share and discuss their latest research findings, to stimulate new research ideas, to coalesce researchers with industry, and to begin to attract major funding sources.
