· archived # 1,468,982
William Delbert Gann ( click to enlarge ) William Delbert Gann (6 June, 1878 - 14 June, 1955 - also known as W. D. Gann), was a finance trader who developed the technical analysis tools known as Gann angles, Square of 9, Hexagon, Circle of 360 (these are Master charts). Gann market forecasting methods are based on geometry , astronomy and astrology , and ancient mathematics . Opinions are sharply divided on the value and relevance of his work. Gann wrote a number of books on trading.
