UNITED STATES PATENT OFFICE. NIKOLA TESLA, OF NEW YORK. N. Y. APPARATUS FOR TRANSMISSION OF ELECTRICAL ENERGY I must admit when I first saw this patent application by Nicola Tesla I didn't think much of it. I tried to relate it to our present form of energy transmission by wires and the way normal radio transmission occurs and couldn't really see any great difference. But now on looking at it once more I can see that he was really on to something and could indeed transmit useable energy to locations away from the generator without wires and is worthy of further investigation. Whilst many of us have tried to get useable power from radio waves that used the Hertian method of transmission and received only feeble amounts of power. I have heard and seen on video decent amounts power that could be used for lighting needs, admittedly only at short distances of few yards but that proves the concept is indeed valid. Their set up has been also be used with short wave transmitters that sent a vary clear signal at distance with little distorting with the need for a large antenna array. I believe Tesla called his type of transmission the Longitudual wave. A simple demonstration to show the concept idea is shown is thus. (1) If you have a rope between two people ,ask one of them to flip the rope and produce oscillations that can be seen running down the rope to the other person holding the rope.(this is how Hertian waves are produced and can be easily seen by observer. (2) Now ask the same person to just pull the rope tight, you can only see with great difficulty the rope has move with great difficulty, this is the way ,to show the difference between Hertian and Longitudual wave forms.
