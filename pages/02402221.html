SPAF-2026-main015 CDU: UDC: 528.8:550.3:551.24 Software-based analysis of UAV thermal imaging data for fault mapping in the Baikal Rift Zone Dmitry A. Rukosuev1 1 Institute of Earth Crust of the Siberian Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences dim.ruk@mail.ru Institute of the Earth's Crust, Siberian Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences, Irkutsk, Russia e-mail: rukosuev@crust.irk.ru, caa@crust.irk.ru, biv@crust.irk.ru Keywords: UAV, thermal imaging, software, data analysis, fault zones, Baikal Rift Zone Local thermal anomalies on the Earth surface may indicate zones of increased fracturing and fluid discharge related to active faults. UAV thermal imaging provides high-resolution data for studying such near-surface processes. However, raw thermal images are strongly affected by atmospheric conditions, surface emissivity, relief, time of day, and survey geometry. Therefore, reliable interpretation requires software-based processing and correction of the data. This study presents a software-based approach to the analysis of thermal data obtained from unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs). The approach includes thermal image correction, generation of thermal orthophotomaps, normalization of temperature fields, and spatial analysis of temperature anomalies. The main aim is to support the detection of geological patterns associated with fault zones and areas of increased permeability.
