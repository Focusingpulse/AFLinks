Chubb Born ( 1953-01-30 ) January 30, 1953 Died March 25, 2011 (2011-03-25) (aged58) Residence Burke, VA, United States Nationality USA Knownfor New Energy , Cold Fusion Scientific career Fields Research Scientist , Editor of Infinite Energy Scott R. Chubb (30 January 1953 – 25 March 2011) was an American theoretical and computational physicist at the Naval Research Laboratory, and for the last two decades of his life one of the principal theoreticians working on low energy nuclear reactions. Education and early career A.B. in physics Princeton University, 1975 Ph.D. in physics State University of New York at Stony Brook, 1982, with research carried out at Brookhaven National Laboratory After his doctorate Chubb received several offers and went first to Northwestern University
