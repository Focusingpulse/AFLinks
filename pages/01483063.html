revolving globe

· archived # 1,483,063
Keely Keely announced in 1888 that he had proved the uselessness of building engines to employ the ether as a motive power , which could only be used as a medium for the power which he had discovered, namely a condition of sympathetic vibration associated both positively and negatively with the polar stream . The revolving globe was never created to be the " source of power " and Keely never affirmed that he could produce with it " an indefinite amount of horse power without current expense.
