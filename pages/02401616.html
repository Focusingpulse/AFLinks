GC2026-SGG055 Some results of an experimental study of the seismic situation on the Baikal-Amur Mainline section Andrey N. Kotov1 , Sergey A. Toshchov1 1 O.Yu. Schmidt Institute of Physics of the Earth, Russian Academy of Sciences and250195@yandex.ru In September 2024, a comprehensive expedition of the Russian Geographical Society took place with the involvement of many scientific research organizations in the field of Earth sciences, dedicated to the 50th anniversary of the Baikal-Amur Mainline. The research was carried out along the BAM section covered by the seismically active Baikal rift zone, along the route from the village of Severobaikalsk to the village of Taksimo. Researchers from the Institute of Physics of the Earth, Russian Academy of Sciences, measured background seismic noise in the vicinity of open areas of the BAM, in the settlements of Severobaikalsk and Severomuysk, as well as in the technological drainage adit of the Severomuysk tunnel of the BAM. Based on the results of the research, the spectral composition was assessed, as well as an attempt to quantify the energy of seismic events recorded during the measurement period. Also, in the spectral composition of the wave field recorded in the tunnel of the Severomuysky tunnel, high-quotient harmonics were identified, corresponding to resonant effects occurring in the tunnel structure, associated, among other things, with the high seismic activity of the region in which it is located.
