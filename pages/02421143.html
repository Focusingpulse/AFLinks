Matti Pitkänen Born ( 1950-10-30 ) October 30, 1950 (age 75) Kiuruvesi, Finland Residence Hanko, Finland Nationality Finnish Alma mater University of Helsinki Known for Topological Geometrodynamics (TGD), Topology , Magnetic Fields , New Energy Scientific career Fields Theoretical physics Institutions University of Helsinki Matti Juhani Pitkänen (born 30 October 1950, in Kiuruvesi, Finland) is a Finnish theoretical physicist. He is best known as the originator and lifelong developer of Topological Geometrodynamics (TGD), a personal research program that he describes as an attempt to construct a unified theory of the fundamental interactions. He has worked for most of his career as an independent researcher and lives in Hanko, a small seaside town in southern Finland. Biography Pitkänen began studying physics, theoretical physics, and mathematics at the University of Helsinki in 1970. He completed the degree of Candidate of Philosophy in theoretical physics in 1977 and the degree of Licentiate of Philosophy in theoretical physics in 1982. He received his Ph.D. in theoretical physics from the University of Helsinki in 1982 with a thesis titled Topological Geometrodynamics . During and after his studies he held a series of academic and research positions, including assistant posts in the Department of Theoretical Physics (1976–1979), a research assistantship at the Helsinki University of Technology (1980–1981), a research post at the Neste Oy research center (1984–1985), a teaching position in the Department of Theoretical Physics (1987–1992), and a research position in the Division of High Energy Physics at the University of Helsinki (1996–1997). Since completing his thesis he has continued to develop TGD largely as a self-described "free researcher" outside a permanent university appointment. Pitkänen has four children.
