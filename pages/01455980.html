[ Bloomfield-Moore ] [ 3rd octave ] "The experiment illustrating " chord of mass " sympathy was repeated, using a glass chamber, 40 inches in height, filled with water, standing on a slab of glass. Three metal spheres , weighing about 6 ounces each, rested on the glass floor. The chord of mass of these spheres was B flat first octave , E flat second octave and B flat third octave . Upon sounding the note B flat on the sympathetic transmitter , the sphere having that chord of mass rose slowly to the top of the chamber, the positive end of the wire having been attached, which connected the covered jar with the transmitter .
