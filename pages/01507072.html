I'm Moving!!!!!

· archived # 1,507,072
Tomorrow is Moving day for the kids and I! We got our house, and as of 3:30 pm this afternoon it passed inspection allowing us to move into it tomorrow!!!! I am sooo psyched, excited and just bouncing off the walls! It is a perfect little 3 bedroom 1.5 bathroom home in good condition (well it has to be to pass inspection for HUD). I was sitting on pins and needles all week waiting for the second inspection to happen. That one had to pass by 5:30pm tonight for me to be able to move in. It was soooo awesome as I could just feel the energy in that place as the inspector was going through it... At one point he went out to his car and got a 9volt battery to put into one of the smoke detectors to help it pass. Then a clogged drain started to drain! It was a slow drain and the plumber was still coming to work on it, but it was draining just fast enough for the inspector to let it pass. It was amazing!!!! Beyond amazing!!!! Blessings and Hugs Sallyjane
