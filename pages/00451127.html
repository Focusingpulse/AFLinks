On Shocks Tariq Khan Omaha, NE USA A speculative and philosophical essay on the possibility of gene pool regulation of large-scale human population, akin to ant colonies in Nature. Human gene pools are considered as oscillating entities wavering between expansionist, peaceful, and open phases, to phases of consolidated, militaristic, and closed societies. Religion is considered as a beneficial evolutionary fitness creation to help safeguard excess extremism during consolidated phases and as a critical archive of techniques to assist populations when presented with rare and dangerous existential shocks.
