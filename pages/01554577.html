3. How Goethe's Thoughts on the Development of the Animals Arose - Goethean Science (1988)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 1,554,577
Lavater's great work Physiognomical Fragments for Furthering Human Knowledge and Human Love 17 Physiognomische Fragmente zur Beförderung der Menschenkenntnis und Menschenliebe appeared during the years 1775–1778. Goethe had taken a lively interest in it, not only through the fact that he oversaw its publication, but also by making contributions to it himself. But what is of particular interest now is that, within these contributions, we can already find the germ of his later zoological works. [ 2 ] Physiognomy sought, in the outer form of the human being, to know his inner nature, his spirit.
