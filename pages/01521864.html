Overview The Quantum Law of Gravity is a three-page paper by Faheem Murtaza of Abbottabad, Pakistan, published in the Proceedings of the NPA volume 8 for the 18th conference at College Park in 2011. It proposes a single relation from which Newtonian gravity, general relativistic effects, a gravitational analogue of the magnetic field, orbital inclinations and the Planck time are all said to follow. The paper is unusual in a way a reader should be told about before anything else: the published version contains the reviewer's and editor's comments interleaved with the author's text, along with unfilled parts of the Proceedings template . Section 1 reads in full "Introduction — Pose the problem and your approach to a solution", and section 2 "Main Section(s) — Introduce your equations with appropriate text", which are the template's own instructions. Immediately after equation (1) the text reads: "Problem: the right hand side is differential, the left is not. How was the equation derived? A lucky guess?" The phrases "Please show your steps" appear twice more, together with "Define Y in terms of other constants" and, parenthetically inside a sentence, "precisely the steps you must show".
