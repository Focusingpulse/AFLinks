HOBBYIST SCIENCE FAIR PROJS GOOD STUFF NEW STUFF SEARCH MESSING ABOUT WITH INFRARED LIGHT William Beaty 2/24/95 I've always been fascinated with "heat radiation;" otherwise known as longwave infrared light. You don't need a military night-vision camera, since there is an inexpensive imaging detector for this: liquid crystal postcards. These postcards are available from several science supply catalogs, as well as local museum stores, also some mall gift shops, etc. If you heat the postcard until it just begins to change color, it becomes sensitive to small amounts of IR light, and acts like instant camera film. Some things to try: Shine a hot (100 watt) light bulb on the IR postcard, and make a shadow with your hand. A thermal shadow appears on the postcard.
