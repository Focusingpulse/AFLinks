GC2026-STP006 Two-mode approximation with memory for Solar dynamo Evgeny A. Kazakov1 , Gleb M. Vodinchar1 1 Institute of Cosmophysical Research and Radio Wave Propagation FEB RAS, Russia Kazakov@ikir.ru Describing the generation of cosmic magnetic fields is a crucial fundamental problem in hydromagnetic dynamo theory. The significant interest in this problem stems from the special role of magnetic fields in the formation of the Earth's ionosphere, changes in solar activity, star formation in galactic disks, and many other processes and phenomena [1-3]. The memory effect (hereditary behavior), whereby the change in the current state of a physical system depends on its past states, plays an important role in dynamos. Taking these effects into account can provide a more accurate description of magnetic field generation. This report is devoted to the discussion of the results of modeling a two-mode hereditary model of a cosmic dynamo, applied to a real cosmic object, namely, the solar dynamo. Memory in the system under consideration is realized as negative feedback distributed across all past states of the system. The physical source of this feedback in a real dynamo system is the Lorentz force. The feedback is represented by an integral term, a quadratic convolution of the phase variables with a fairly general kernel. This term models the quenching of the turbulent field generator (α effect).
