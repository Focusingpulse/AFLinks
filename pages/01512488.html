From driving to and from our jobs that support our families, to heating and cooling our homes to a liveable level, about 80 to 90 percent of the 474 exajoules of energy consumed in 2008, came from the combustion of fossil fuels. Coal continues to be the fastest growing fossil fuel to be used for energy; coincidentally, coal is also the dirtiest form of energy. In 2004, 87% the world's total combined energy consumption was obtained using the combustion of fossil fuels.
