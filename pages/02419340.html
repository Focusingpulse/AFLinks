Journal of Borderland Research >> Vol. 19 >> No. 08 [7] AN ANXIOUS ASKER ANSWERED The Editor Replies to the Probing Questions of a 15-Year Old Saucer Researcher. About the BSRA (Q1-6) • About the U.F.O.s (Q7-11) About the Men in Black (Q12-14) Gentlemen: My name is Joseph Marinelli. I live in Philadelphia, PA. I am 15 years old and I am a researcher on U.F.O.s. What I would like to know is some of B.S.R.A.'s orthodox theories about "UFOs", "Life on other planets", "Death", "Heredity" and so on. 1. Does the B.S.R.A. delve from the inner mind to the outer limits of theoretical Science and fact? Ans: Yup, we sure do. We take up where science and fact leave off, Joseph; we push on into areas of consciousness where the inner mind is the only research tool for making quantitative and qualitative measurements of reality -- whatever that is. 2. How many members are there in the United States? Ans: BSRA has over 500 members in the United States at present, and not quite 100 in foreign countries. 3. What kind of people join? Ans: Mostly older people, Joe; but like you they are interested in what the inner mind might be, and in those areas of experience disdained by theoretical science -- Flying Saucers, for instance. 4. Does the B.S.R.A. think or know that there is life on Mars, Venus, Neptune, Alpha Centauri, Betelgeuse? Ans: The only way we know anything is by direct experience, Joe.
