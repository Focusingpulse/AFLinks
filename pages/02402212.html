SPAF-2026-main008 CDU: 662.612, 519.63 Numerical assessment of the influence of noise parameters on the thermal runaway process in battery modeling Roman N. Moiseev romanorius@gmail.com In recent years, there has been rapid development of energy systems based on renewable sources such as solar and wind energy. A characteristic feature of these systems is the variability of power generation, caused by fluctuations in environmental conditions. In this context, energy storage systems become particularly important, as they enable load balancing and improve the stability of power grids. The flow of electric current through a battery is accompanied by heat generation due to the Joule effect, with the amount of heating determined by the instantaneous current values. Under stochastic energy generation, this process becomes random in nature, which may lead to local overheating. In some cases, this results in the onset of thermal runaway - a phenomenon of uncontrolled temperature increase that threatens the reliability and safety of battery operation. From a mathematical perspective, these processes can be described using the non-stationary heat conduction equation with a nonlinear heat source and stochastic perturbation.
