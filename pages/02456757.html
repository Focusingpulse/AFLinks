Freeman 12 September 1991 It has been noted that if one were to strike two tuning forks of slightly different tone pitch at the same time, one would find that there were three distinct tones rather than two. The third tone is the difference in frequency of the two tones. For example, if one fork resonated at a frequency of 202Hz,(cycles per second) and the other resonated at a frequency of 200Hz the resulting third tone would be the difference of the original tone, and would modulate at 2Hz. These modulations are know as beats. A phenomena was discovered by Dr. Gerald Oster in 1973, at the Mount Sinai school of Medicine in New York.
