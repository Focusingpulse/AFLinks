Edward Henry Dowdye Born ( 1943-11-13 ) November 13, 1943 Washington, D.C., United States Died December 31, 2020 (2020-12-31) (aged 77) Residence Greenbelt, MD, United States Nationality USA Alma mater Hampton University, University of Heidelberg, Howard University Known for Extinction Shift Principle , emission theory, gravitational lensing critique Scientific career Fields Laser optics, Laser spectroscopy, Electromagnetism, Gravitation Institutions NASA Goddard Space Flight Center Edward Henry Dowdye, Jr. (13 November 1943 – 31 December 2020) was an American laser optics physicist, retired from NASA Goddard Space Flight Center, and the author of the Extinction Shift Principle — a purely classical emission theory offered as an alternative to both special and general relativity. Dowdye was unusual in this literature on two counts. First, his objection to general relativity was observational rather than philosophical : he argued that light bending near the Sun occurs only at the thin plasma rim and not in the vacuum beyond it, which is what refraction predicts and not what gravitation predicts, and he pursued this claim through the published deflection and timing data. Second, he was by his own description the only working emission theorist of his generation — the tradition having lapsed since Walter Ritz (1878–1909) — and his version was distinctive in applying to gravitation as well as electromagnetism, where earlier emission theories addressed light alone. He was one of the featured scientists in the feature-length documentary Einstein Wrong - The Miracle Year (2013), in which he appears alongside Patricia Anne de Hilster , Robert de Hilster and David de Hilster .
