Log in I forgot my password Sympathetic Vibratory Physics &raquo; Kachina Page actions PDF Print Share Send link Kachina White Bear (1905–1996) was an honored elder of the Hopi nation and the following is his account of the Legend of the Kachinas and the origins of the Hopi The original homeland of the Hopi was called Kásskara it was located in the Pacific Ocean, mostly below the equator and the Hawaiian Islands are a remnant of Kassakara. The Hawaiian term "kahuna" has the same meaning as the Hopi word "kachina", referring to a highly esteemed being with supernatural attributes. The Hopi believe there are four "worlds" or epochs of human civilisation, and these epochs experience various kinds of cataclysms. They say the First World was called Tokpela and it was destroyed by fire the Second World was known as Topka and it was destroyed by ice, the Third World was Kásskara,which sank and now humanity currently find themselves in the Fourth World called Toowakachi, which has yet to experience it's cataclysmic destruction in this endless and reoccurring cycle. White Bear said the cataclysms of the Second and Fourth Worlds both occurred to the Hopi when they were on the South American continent, which had submerged and reappeared over a long period. Though the exact timing of this reoccurring cycle have been lost over time, the Hopi believe geological changes such as the submersion of continents can occur abruptly. White Bear's account, which generally accords with Plato's Atlantis story, describes a war between two great civilisations: Kásskara and Talawaitichqua, which had lain far to the east of Kásskara before sinking into the sea.
