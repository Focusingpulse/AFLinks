Exploring the Association Between SNP rs7903146 and Type 2 Diabetes in a Bangladeshi Population Adam Mohib, Kiran Abraham, Karan Ramesh Urs, Tamilchezhiyan Kalidass, Rishi Chandrasekaran, Rayna Jindal, Vivitsa Labh, and Mostafa Khan California High School Type 2 diabetes, often referred to as T2D, is a widespread health condition. This study focuses on the specific ge- netic variation in a single nucleotide poly- morphism (SNP) rs7903146 in the gene TCF7L2 and its potential association with T2D. We examined DNA samples from Bangladeshi individuals to see whether the genetic variant rs7903146 is associated with T2D in this community. We discov- ered that the CC variant of rs7903146 was extremely prevalent, appearing in all of the samples from people without T2D and in the majority of the samples from people with T2D out of the 38 sequences they looked at (16 from people without T2D and 22 from people with T2D).
