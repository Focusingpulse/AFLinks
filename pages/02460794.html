Decker: "altering consciousness with acoustics" Hello all, I had some personal experience with sound, apart of my own business of piano and organ service. One of my customers, a well known holistic doctor, asked how I felt. I had to answer, that my heart was giving me some trouble. She looked at my aura, told me it was between green and yellow, and blew for two minutes on a reed sounder (the type that choirs use to start at a certain pitch) and told me that the next day I would feel much better. The next day I felt super. I use now a music program to generate that pitch for me whenever I feel not 100%. The tone for me was E, the second octave above middle C. Maybe some people, that do not feel 100%, might try to experiment with a computer generated tone (I used a reed type tone, as all the harmonics are in it). You may have to try the C above middle C and go chromatically up the scale by day to see if it has any effect.
