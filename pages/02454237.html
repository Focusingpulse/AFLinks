The weight of the carrot will drag it down. Nibble the carrot smaller. If you do it carefully, you can find the weight that cancels out the bouyancy. It will neither rise nor sink. Instead it will drift annoyingly around the room. Years ago there was a helium tank at work, and I had a mylar "emergency blanket" in my car. I have an idea: giant floppy balloon! So I duct-taped the mylar into a roughly tetrahedral bag-shape about 80cm across and filled it with gas. It gave considerable lift, and it needed a considerable counterweight in order to attain neutral bouyancy. I tied a plastic cup full of water to the bag and adjusted the amount of water. Brainstorm!
