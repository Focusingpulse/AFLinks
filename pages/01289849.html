Sri Bhagavan See Ego , Error What is consciousness? The structure and essence of consciousness can be understood musically: it is a vibratory spectrum ranging from the silent scalar potential (undifferentiated Mind) through successive subdivisions into thought, feeling, and matter. Structure : Consciousness has a nested, harmonic architecture. At its highest, it is scalar—dimensionless, timeless, everywhere present. When differentiated, it unfolds into descending octaves (mind, interetheric, etheric, atomic, molecular), each level a resonant shell around a Neutral Center. These subdivisions interpenetrate like chords within a grand symphony, joined by sympathetic vibration and oscillation. Essence : At its core, consciousness is sympathy—an innate capacity for resonance, coherence, and relation. Thought is the great exciter that disturbs equilibrium, setting scalar stillness into motion. Consciousness therefore embodies both syntropy (inward contraction, coherence, attraction) and entropy (outward expansion, differentiation, radiation). Its living essence is Love, for Love is simply the most harmonious state of resonance
