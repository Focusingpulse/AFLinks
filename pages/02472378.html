Improvements in his transmitter give Tesla his confidence. The electrical transmission of power long distances for industrial purposes is now impracticable owing to the loss of energy in transit. The country has enormous water power for the cheap generation of electricity, and with the means of conserving this power during long distance transmission it could be made highly useful over a large extent of territory. The new motive force has proved thoroughly practicable on short circuits, and when such power as Niagara possesses can be successfully transmitted, it may turn every wheel in New York city. Forty or fifty miles is now the limit of its carriage to advantage. It may be utilized at a greater distance for street lighting purposes than for driving machinery. The falls of the Neckar are 108 miles from Frankfort, Germany, yet the city is now lighted by electricity generated there. The mountain streams eighty miles from Los Angeles furnish water power that generates electricity and lights the town, but from 40 to 50 per cent. of the original power is lost in transmission.
