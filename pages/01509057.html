Application Notes The SSR3A Solid-State Relay board is designed for switching applications requiring high voltage, low current loads. The installed 1200V IGBTs enable the device to act as a bidirectional, isolated relay with a standoff voltage of 1200V. The on-board driver, isolator, and DC-DC Converter chips are all protected against high voltage transients if one or both of the IGBTs should breakdown.
