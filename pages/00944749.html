Textiles have non/andbiodegradable substances, which decompose with time. The chemicals on them may not decompose andmight be toxic to microorganisms present in ground. At present, a lot of research is performed on thedecomposition of textile materials in natural soil, converting into biomass, water and carbon dioxide, posing no harm to the environment. In this respect, this research work aimed to see the decompositionof chemicals present on textiles, in order to understand the biodegradation phenomenon of textilematerials and chemicals when buried in soil.
