(ISSN: 2473-7194) For previous issues of our newsletter: CLICK THIS LINK Section I – Masterclass #67 – A Geomantic Life with Alanna Moore, November 22, 2025- 1:00 pm Central Time Alanna was born in Sydney Australia. She was introduced to dowsing when living in London and joined the British Society of Dowsers in 1980. Returning to Sydney and befriending a wellknown dowser Isobel Bellamy, she helped to found the New South Wales Dowsers Society in 1984. The only founder remaining, she helped the society celebrate its 40th anniversary in Sydney in November 2024. She now lives in Ireland. Alanna has been a professional geomancer for over 40 years, mostly in Australasia, Asia and Europe. She specializes in the more unusual applications of detecting spirits and applying dowsing to improved gardening. She studied Permaculture Design and gained 3 diplomas from Bill Mollison, the originator in the 1990s; while also assisting the movement by helping produce the Permaculture International Journal. Alanna’s talk will describe some of these experiences, plus dowsing adventures and how her understanding of the subtle earth energies and Other dimensions of life has developed over time.
