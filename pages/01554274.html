Volume II: Preface — GA 18a. World and Life Views of the 19th Century (2024)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 1,554,274
Ernst Haeckel, to whom this book is dedicated, provided an answer to the question “What level of knowledge have we truly attained at the end of the nineteenth century?” from his point of view in his work “Die Welträtsel” (The Riddle of the Universe, Bonn 1899). This paper aims to show the path that worldview development has taken over the past century to reach the current level of knowledge. The reader will see that the author is not a fanatical adherent of the modern scientific creed, even though he reveres Ernst Haeckel as a monumental representative of modern thinking.
