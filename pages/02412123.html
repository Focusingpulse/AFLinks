This tests platform allows me to conduct a parametrical study of the braking effect (common Lenz effect) and the acceleration effect (delayed Lenz effect) of the turn speed of a magnetic rotor when a secundary stator coil is loaded or shorted. This tests platform DLE-TB v1.0 is composed of different parts : a magnetic rotor taken from a water pump commonly used for sea aquariums ( Maxi-Jet 480 L/h MJ 500 ). an excitation coil without core (air core) used as the motor coil, an electronic circuit controller with a Hall sensor, a secundary stator coil which is able to slide longitudinally along its main axis on a length of 80 mm. a soft iron rod 120 mm length and 8 mm diameter, a switch to short the outputs of the secundary coil.
