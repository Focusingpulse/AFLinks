Anna Marie Carter Anna Marie Carter, widely known as “The Seed Lady” of Watts, is a community activist, organic agriculture advocate, and educator based in Watts, a neighborhood of South Central Los Angeles, California. A certified Master Gardener through the University of California, Carter has devoted decades to promoting self-sufficiency, health, and community empowerment through organic gardening and food education. Carter is the founder of the Watts Garden Club and has led initiatives to build free organic gardens for underserved populations, including individuals affected by HIV/AIDS, chronic illness, and poverty. Her work extends into schools, housing projects, rehabilitation centers, and community organizations, where she teaches sustainable food cultivation, nutrition, and agricultural entrepreneurship. Beyond grassroots work, she is also the founder of “Anna Carter’s The Seed Lady,” an enterprise focused on organic, non-GMO food production and global agricultural trade, reflecting her broader vision of linking local empowerment with international food systems.
