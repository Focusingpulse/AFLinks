Thailand tagging tourists in wake of brutal beach murders

· archived # 2,446,775
10/01/14 - Thailand tagging tourists in wake of brutal beach murders Tourists travelling in Thailand could be given wristbands to keep track of those who get into trouble, following the murders of two British backpackers. Tourism and Sports Minister Kobkarn Wattanavrangkul said she had approached hotels over the idea of handing out the bands, and even raised the idea of electronically tagging travellers. She said: "When tourists check-in to a hotel they will be given a wristband with a serial number that matches their I.D. and shows the contact details of the resort they are staying in so that if they�re out partying late and, for example, get drunk or lost, they can be easily assisted. "The next step would be some sort of electronic tracking device but this has not yet been discussed in detail." She added that a "buddy system", pairing tourists with a local minder at tourist destinations, was also being looked in to. The new measures are being discussed following the brutal murders of Hannah Witheridge, 23, and David Miller, 24, whose bodies were found on a beach on island of Koh Tao earlier this month.
