free energy or free power

· archived # 1,511,707
Free energy is what most people are after. But we need power. free power for all. Power is the fusion (product) of two energy forms: Voltage (EMF) and Current. Current is the flow of energy We have the choice of two current forms: Conventional current, related to AC magnetic fields Or displacement current. Which is the energy flow between the plates of a capacitor when the voltage difference between the plates is changing. Since every conductor has inductance AND capacitance. We can use the capacity of a coil (use it as a capacitor plate), and use a capacitive coupled high voltage coil to induce a displacement current. When that induced displacement current is combined with an induced EMF (voltage from a changing magnetic field), you get Real power (in phase). this sounds easy to me, but engineering it is a bit more tricky. Since the capacity is low, the voltage needs to be high, so instead I now choose to have a much larger capacity which can allow lower voltages to get the same high currents needed to power a load. Now the inductance of the coil (cap plate) is very low. 3 uH. It thus allows large current. but holds only small amounts of energy per cycle. But as I said before we are not looking for free energy. we are looking for free power. thus the concept is use displacement current, and turn it into conventional current. (this can be done as it is the working principle of a resonant LC)
