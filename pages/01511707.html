But we need power. free power for all. Power is the fusion (product) of two energy forms: Voltage (EMF) and Current. Current is the flow of energy We have the choice of two current forms: Conventional current, related to AC magnetic fields Or displacement current. Which is the energy flow between the plates of a capacitor when the voltage difference between the plates is changing. Since every conductor has inductance AND capacitance. We can use the capacity of a coil (use it as a capacitor plate), and use a capacitive coupled high voltage coil to induce a displacement current. When that induced displacement current is combined with an induced EMF (voltage from a changing magnetic field), you get Real power (in phase). this sounds easy to me, but engineering it is a bit more tricky. Since the capacity is low, the voltage needs to be high, so instead I now choose to have a much larger capacity which can allow lower voltages to get the same high currents needed to power a load.
