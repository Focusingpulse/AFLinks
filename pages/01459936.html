Infinite Intelligence

· archived # 1,459,936
Another term for God , Universal Consciousness Plotinus “You ask: ‘How can we know the infinite?’ I answer — not by reason. The task of reason is to distinguish and define; therefore, the Infinite cannot be placed among its objects. The Infinite can be apprehended only by a higher faculty of the mind, by the ability to enter into a state in which you cease to be your finite self, in which the Divine Essence is communicated to you. This is ecstasy, the liberation of your mind from its finite consciousness. Only the like can know the like: when you cease to be finite, you become one with the Infinite. The higher part of your soul, your simplest self, when brought to the Divine Essence, realizes this union — this identity.” [ Plotinus ] Christ Returns - Speaks His Truth "This is the true ideal , the true aspiration , the highest goal - to understand and experience the Reality behind and within all things giving them their individual being. YOU CAN HAVE NO HIGHER ASPIRATION THAN THIS - to understand and experience the Reality behind and within all things - giving, maintaining and sustaining all individual being.
