I believe this is my first post here. I'd like to throw around a few ideas and see what you folks think. This is based out of my desire to find a simple way to make a common experimental setup for magnetically biased spark gaps or filled with materials such as electrets or piezoelectrics. -Length of pyrex or quartz lab tubing, cut to size -Ring magnet polarized through the axis mounted on a grommet that fits over the tube -Electrodes in either side of tube -Stoppers for electrode pass-through and outfitted with vacuum and gas inlet connections All kinds of things can be changed and experimented with here. If I had the resources I'd cast relatively thin tubes with layered electret waxes and fine piezoelectric materials, heated and pressed. I've been thinking it would be an interesting test to apply a large zamboni pile, or a van de graff or other suitable static generator, connected to the leads of such a tube while melting then cooling the waxes.
