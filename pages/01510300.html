The scotch yoke bottom end (crankcase) was specifcally designed to allow for perfect sinusoidal operation. Russell Bourke spent many years figuring out just what the perfect ratio and geometry was for the reverse cam effect scotch yoke design which had been around for decades before his research began in the late 1940's. The primary difference and what makes the Bourke scotch yoke design unique is that Russell Bourke was the first in history to figure out the correct and best way to use the scotch yoke in reverse. Prior to that time, the scotch yoke mechanism was used to multiply the torque input at the shaft (crankshaft) into pressure many times greater than otherwise needed to produce work.
