GC2026-STP051 Seasonal variations in F2-layer critical frequency trends according to data from the Yakutsk ionospheric station Sargylana E. Kobyakova1 , Artem Y. Gololobov1 , Karimov R. Rustam1 , Alexandr E. Stepanov1 1 Y.G. Shafer Institute of Cosmophysical Research and Aeronomy of the Siberian Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences karimovrim@gmail.com This study investigates seasonal variations in long-term trends of the F2-layer critical frequency (foF2) based on vertical sounding data from the Yakutsk station (62.01° N, 129.43° E; 57.12° MLAT) covering the period 1956 to 2017. Analysis of six solar activity cycles (19–24) shows that the foF2 trend varies with both season and time of day. The negative trend is most significant at midnight during summer, whereas its peak values occur at midday during the equinoxes. The weakest negative trends are observed in winter months. This work was supported by the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of the Russian Federation (project FWRS-2026-0058).
