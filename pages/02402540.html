One might want to know the number of points of sub-variety belonging to the number field defining the coefficients of the polynomials. This problem is very relevant in M8 formulation of TGD, where these points are carriers of sparticles. In TGD based vision about cognition they define cognitive representations as points of space-time surface, whose M8 coordinates can be thought of as belonging to both real number field and to extensions of various p-adic number fields induced by the extension of rationals. If these cognitive representations define the vertices of analogs of twistor Grassmann diagrams in which sparticle lines meet, one would have number theoretically universal adelic for￾mulation of scattering amplitudes and a deep connection between fundamental physics and cognition. 2.
