meridian arc

· archived # 1,481,607
In geodesy, a meridian arc measurement is the distance between two points with the same longitude , i.e., a segment of a meridian curve or its length. Two or more such determinations at different locations then specify the shape of the reference ellipsoid which best approximates the shape of the geoid.
