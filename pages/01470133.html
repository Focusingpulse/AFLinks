cooperation

· archived # 1,470,133
Cayce " . . . they that would have cooperation must cooperate by the giving of self to that as is to be accomplished - whether in the bringing of light to others, bringing of strength, health, understanding , these are one in Him . . . This is a natural consequence of self service, self sacrifice , self bewilderment, in Him . Being the channel is cooperation . Being a blessing is it in action . In whatever state of being , meet that upon the basis of their position - and lift up, look up - and this is cooperation . " [ Cayce (262-3)] "Learn the first lesson of cooperation . Become less and less selfish, and more and more selfless." [ Cayce (262-29)] Manly Palmer Hall " Competition is natural to the ignorant; and cooperation is natural to the wise." [ Manly Palmer Hall 1901 - 1990] See Also Blessing Harmony Unison
