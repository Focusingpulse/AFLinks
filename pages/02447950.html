Gary's claims have always been intriguing, particularly the final one where he placed coils of wire near the moving magnets to extract electricity. I think the Floyd Sweet device used a similar principle, yet in his case, he was actually moving the bloch wall so that you got an instant polarity change just by vibrating the bubble at 60hz (or whatever frequency you used).. Sweet said you had to 'program' a magnet which put a magnetic bubble in the magnet...it is the damndest thing to see it under flux viewing plastic...especially when you bring a small rod magnet near the bubble and it MOVES across the surface, like a bubble of air under a plastic film laid over water... After seeing this, it made me think the bubble could be moved in and out in response to an outside frequency, so that an instant flux/polarity change would occur....if that is truly the case, a coil of wire placed near this, would have current induced in it that could be absorbed by a capacitor or other deep dielectric. The many millenial type claims about the earth pole 'flipping' indicate this will be a rapid effect, not gradual in the least... Kind of like the Barkhausen clicks you get when domains instantly switch....you can bend the field only so far, then it flips. Is the energy necessary to reach and surpass this point greater than the amount of energy you could extract from the coils? -- Jerry W.
