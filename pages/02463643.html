The stationary asymmetry ϵ∗ defines the physically admissible first closure, and the fine-structure constant is identified with the normalized asymmetry residue of that state. The result is not yet a final numerical derivation of α, but a variational program in which the charge duality relation e2 = 8πα eemax 2 is reinterpreted as the quotient of closure invariants. This places α within the Aether ledger as a necessary consequence of first stable bifurcation rather than as an externally inserted parameter.
