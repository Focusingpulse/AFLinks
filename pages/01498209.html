Theory of Bose-Einstein condensation nuclear fusion (BECNF) [1] has been developed to explain many diverse experimental results of deuteron induced nuclear reactions in metals, observed in electrolysis and gas loading experiments. The theory is based on a single conventional physical concept of “nuclear” Bose-Einstein condensation and provides a consistent theoretical description of the experimental results (both electrolysis and gas loading).
