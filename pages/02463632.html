It develops the Aether Physics Model (APM) 2 HOW TO USE THIS MANUAL 1 Abstract This paper develops a practical engineering and metrology manual for light in the Aether Physics Model (APM) using Quantum Measurement Units (QMU). The central quantity is ligt, a conserved transport content that admits many ledger-closed factorizations. Each factorization is treated as a distinct laboratory interface (a “knob”), specifying what an apparatus can directly control and measure. We systematically elucidate 25 card factorizations spanning resonance/rate control, radiometric transport, opto-mechanical coupling, and magnetic-charge electromagnetic coupling. We also provide a one-page knob decision tree, metrology hygiene rules, and a worked photoelectric-style example showing how to hold transport fixed while diagnosing receiver gating. 1 Motivation: why a “knob manual” is needed Standard optics and radiometry provide effective computational tools but often obscure geometrical constants and charge-basis choices behind compressed symbols and mixed conventions. In APM/QMU, light is treated as a conserved transport content whose “manifestations” are different ledger-closed projections into what a particular apparatus can observe.
