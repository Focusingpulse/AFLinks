Objective and Methodology: In lenr devices, are neutrons generated one at a time in a random fashion following Poisson statistics or in bursts of 2, 5, 10 or even more neutrons as in a spontaneous fi ssion neutron source? The multi￾plicity distribution of neutron emission can throw much light on the mechanism responsible for neutron generation – could some neutrons be emitted during a type of chain nuclear event for example? The statistical time spread (typically 25 µs) that occurs during the slowing down process when a bunch of simultaneously produced fast neutrons impinges on a large hydrogenous moderator assembly is exploited for detecting these neutrons separately in a time resolved manner. Thermal neutron detectors such as BF3 or He3 gas proportional counters embedded in the moderator block can register these neutrons individually and sequentially. 2.
