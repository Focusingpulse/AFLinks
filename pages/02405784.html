ANDBOOK OF UNUSUAL NATURAL PHENOMENA WILLIAM R. CORLISS This Handbook is the res t of a long, often tedious trek through thousands of volumes of scientific journals In this venture, the entire files of A/aio-u-; Science, Meteorological Magazine, Monitor Weather Review, and other serials have been scanned for accounts of unusual natural phenomena. The result is an incomparable collection of rare and curious observations — mostly by experienced scientists. The phenomena range from ball lightning to rain from a cloudless sky to the strange whispering sounds heard in the vicinity of Yellowstone Lake. Although designed primarily as a reference book, this handbook reads as intriguing nonfiction. Open it anywhere and you will find fascinating eye witness accounts of perplexing natural events and processes. It is an ideal stimulus for high school and college science classes and a unique source of ideas and data for term papers and theses.
