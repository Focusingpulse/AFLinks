Background The International Conference on Cold Fusion (ICCF) is the principal meeting of researchers working on cold fusion and related low-energy nuclear reactions. The series began in 1990, the year after Fleischmann and Pons announced their results, and has met roughly every twelve to eighteen months since, rotating between host countries. The World Science Database records 13 meetings in this series, from 1990 to 2007. Recorded locations include Salt Lake City, Lake Como, Nagoya, Lahaina, Maui, Monte-Carlo, Lake Toya, Vancouver and Lerici, among others.
