(NASA Inc.), Vancouver Peter Van Bakel is a Canadian company director based in Vancouver, British Columbia. He was chief executive of North American Sales & Advertising Inc. — trading as NASA Inc. , and unconnected with the United States space agency — and appears on this wiki as an attender of the Natural Philosophy Alliance and ExtraOrdinary Technology conferences between 2010 and 2012. His association with the Rodin coil is commercial rather than experimental: his company marketed a set of "advanced technologies" whose descriptions draw directly on Marko Rodin 's Vortex-Based Mathematics. The World Science Database records no paper, abstract or presentation by him. North American Sales & Advertising NASA Inc. presented itself as a Vancouver sales, advertising and production company — "bridging the gap between ideas and reality" — whose main pages concern advertising services, product sourcing and distribution. Alongside those it carried a section headed Advanced Tech . The company's website at nasa-inc.com is no longer his ; the domain now resolves to a parked advertising page. The account below is taken from copies archived between 2010 and 2013, the period covering his conference appearances.
