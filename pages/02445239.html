Before you Buy a Hydrogen Generator (HHO)

· archived # 2,445,239
08/25/15 - Before you Buy a Hydrogen Generator (HHO) If your intentions are to purchase or build a hydrogen producing generator that will totally power your motor/car/vehicle/truck, think again. Electrolysis uses amperage. Your vehicle alternator is limited to the amount of amperage it can constantly produce and any amperage puts a strain on your engine power. Causing your engine to do extra work is not going to help. After all, you are looking for better efficiency - right? You should also consider the fact that your internal combustion engine was designed to be an air pump. HHO does not need Air. That air dilutes any amounts of HHO. So if you accomplish powering your engine with 100% HHO, it will be diluted by the air --- by design. The engine pistons' movement sucks air in, through the air intake valves, compresses it, and lets the air out through the air exhaust valves, Air is an essential part of combustion for fossil fuels; that is where the Oxidizer comes from. Oxidizers are needed in order to have combustion. As for HHO, air is not needed or wanted. HHO contains its own Oxidizer, a perfect air fuel mixture of 66 and 2/3 % hydrogen and 33 and 1/3 % Oxygen. The presence of air dilutes the new hydrogen fuel; which means it will not be as powerful or as effective; Something to think about. Your engine emissions computer is programmed to maintain 14.5 to 14.7 parts air for each part of fuel vapor. As mentioned, air is the oxidizer. It contains 10% to 20% oxygen. It is the oxygen that is needed to burn the fuel. It is the oxygen that the engine monitors in the exhaust. If oxygen levels are low, the computer calls for less fuel. If the oxygen levels are high, the engine calls for more fuel. Changing the Air ratio, even slightly, changes your fuel economy. This is what the so called fuel experts tend to ignore.
