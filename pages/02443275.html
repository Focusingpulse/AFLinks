👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 22 April 1919 · archived # 2,443,275
, Stuttgart Translated by Steiner Online Library I. The Meeting of the Signatories of the Appeal “To the German People and the Cultural World» In accordance with the program of today's meeting, my task this evening will be to say a few words about the appeal “An das deutsche Volk und an die Kulturwelt” (To the German People and to the Civilized World), which is in your hands. You will allow me to speak more aphoristically today, when I am addressing an assembly that is essentially familiar with the content of the appeal. I will speak about the social views that underlie the appeal and my book
