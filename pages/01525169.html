Kelly This patent application shows the details of devices which can produce ordinary electricity from Tesla longitudinal waves. If these claims are correct (and there does not appear to be the slightest reason for believing that they are not), then implementations of this patent application are capable of producing free electrical power and the importance of this information is enormous. Preferably the reactor operates in pulsed abnormal gas discharge mode, and the motor is an inertially dampened drag motor.
