95 — GA 263. Correspondence with Edith Maryon 1912–1924

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 4 March 1922 · archived # 2,438,203
My dear Edith Maryon! So far, all has gone well with my lectures; in Halle and yesterday in Leipzig, where people were afraid again that there might be a disturbance. But everything went quite well. I only had to make the concession of answering questions, but that went quite well too; it just lasted until 11 o'clock. I am also doing quite well in terms of health. The eurythmy performance in Leipzig was, however, less well received; it shows how much opposition we have.
