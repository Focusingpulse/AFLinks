Title Aethereal Fractal Structures for the Electron & Proton Read in full Link to paper Author(s) William R Hohenberger Keywords electron , fractal, proton, electromagnetic waves Published 2012 Journal Proceedings of the NPA Volume 9 No. of pages 6 Pages 215-220 Abstract Three-dimensional fractal structures that are created by electromagnetic waves oscillating within the aether fractal plenum are developed for the internal structures of electrons and protons. These structures are derived from previously defined hexagonal fractals, twist-loop fractals and fractal periodic tables; and are then structurally integrated with Sierpinski's Triangle, Pascal's Fractal, and the dodecahedron quark ball, and mathematically integrated with electron mass, proton mass, the Phi Pyramid and Planck's Length. Overview Given at the 19th Natural Philosophy Alliance conference at Albuquerque in July 2012, this paper is a further instalment of William Hohenberger's Aether Fractal Plenum, applying the geometric machinery of his earlier papers to the internal structure of the electron and the proton. The programme rests on four stated assumptions: that an aether pervades all space; that its fine structure sets a shortest wavelength related to the Planck length; that every particle is a fractal built from scalar multiples of that smallest wave; and that the aether is a hyperdynamic, non-homogeneous elastic substance whose motions constitute all fields and mass distributions. The paper's method is to assemble a set of geometric objects — hexagonal and twist-loop fractals, the Phi Pyramid, Sierpiński's triangle and pyramid, Pascal's triangle, and what Hohenberger calls the Dodecahedron Quark Ball — into a single nested structure, and then to test that structure numerically against three measured quantities: the ratio of the electron's Compton wavelength to the Planck length, the proton-to-electron mass ratio, and the proton charge radius. The electron emerges as an octahedral hexagonal fractal
