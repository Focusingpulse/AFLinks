Boundary-Controlled Propagation A Formation-Based Interpretation of Electromagnetic Wave Steering within the Electromagnetic Energy Onlyverse (EMEO) Carl Max Reed, M.D. Independent Researcher Six Mile, South Carolina, USA cmxrmd@icloud.com August 1, 2026 Abstract The Electromagnetic Energy Onlyverse (EMEO) holds that physical reality consists of one continuous electromagnetic substance: electromagnetic energy is its own and only medium. This paper defines Boundary-Controlled Propagation, the principle that a physical boundary or neighboring electromagnetic formation changes which propagating formations persist or changes their measured propagation rate, direction, or persistence. Established observations in refraction, guided propagation, reflection, diffraction, and conducting-boundary arrangements are separated from their conventional interpretations. Differential propagation is proposed as the steering mechanism in transverse and longitudinal cases. Worked evidentiary comparisons distinguish detector measurements from imported ontology and specify the quantitative derivations and experiments required for evaluation by Nature. Keywords: electromagnetic ontology; boundary-controlled propagation; differential propagation; wave steering; refraction; continuous electromagnetic substance 1. Introduction Physical theories commonly begin with mathematical relationships among measurable quantities. EMEO adopts a different order of development. It first asks what is proposed to exist, then what forms that physical reality can assume, how those forms persist and interact, and only afterward how those relationships should be described mathematically. Physical reality consists of one continuous electromagnetic substance. Electromagnetic energy is its own medium and the only physical medium that exists.
