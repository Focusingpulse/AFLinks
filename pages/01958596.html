Fritz Popp initiated and inspired me to learn quantum physics from him so I could discover the delights of the quantum coherent organism for myself, and Jim Oschman convinced me that energy/quantum medicine is for real. My role is to prepare you for all the exciting things we’ll be hearing in the rest of this historic conference. I’ll try and paint you a picture of what it means to be a quantum coherent organism, so you can feel it in your very flesh and bones, and then give some examples of the implications for health. The quantum coherent organism is the basis for quantum medicine.
