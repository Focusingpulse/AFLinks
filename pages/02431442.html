At the time, it was the best thing that could have ever happened to me. I started to have a positive view of the world and even stopped taking my anxiety medication on which I had felt dependent in order to deal with life. This new found view of reality had my life full of joy and wonder. However, as time progressed, I stopped feeling the way that I had when I first started using it. I emailed PATHS and they told me that eventually people will hit a 'plateau' and the newness will be gone. The initial feelings of using PATHS were so great that I couldn't help but think that the theater instructions were just not being listened to by my subconscious mind. Every now and again, I would feel the instructions working pretty well but the feeling always was overcome with my old mindset, a frustrating experience. I got the antenna hooked up similar to a Tesla Coil (TC) where I am discharging a 500pF capacitor @ about 5000 volts into the outer most ring on the antenna at about 60 times a second. On the second day of experimentation, I noticed that I was feeling my PATHS instructions a little more than expected but just blew it off as the occasionally occurring feeling that usually goes away within a few days.
