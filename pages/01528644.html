Further information on the Society, and introductory materials to the field, are available at our website. As part of the Society’s expansion of our communications, we are pleased to share our first quarterly newsletter. The scope of the newsletter is broad. It includes news about experimental progress, published papers, upcoming events, and other items of interest to the CMNS community. We encourage readers to forward the newsletter to their friends and colleagues. If you have been forwarded this newsletter and would like to subscribe to future issues, please click here. To submit items to the newsletter, please use our contact form.
