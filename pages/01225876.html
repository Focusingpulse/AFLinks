Globoeconomics N. S. Alsaud∗ April 8, 2026 Abstract The theoretical framework of globoeconomics is introduced in this paper as a global generalization of the economical analysis. This coherent treatment scientifically explains many perplexing economical, political and societal phenomena which necessitates rereading the historical development of humankind from novel perspective. Since the approaches used in this treatment provide more plausible and simpler justifications, they are suitable to be adopted based on the philosophical reasoning of Occam’s razor. In fact, the endeavors to optimize the efficiency of any source of energy lies at the heart of this concept (Santarius, T. 2015), and the attempts to organize the animal resources and environments for their benefits are examples of the economical behavior in the largest sense. Based on the previous thoughts, any rational hu￾man behavior lies under the economical umbrella even if it, historically, constitutes or is related to other disci￾pline.
