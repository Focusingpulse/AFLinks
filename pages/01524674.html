This Treaty invites Nation States to become Signatories to this Treaty and invites Parties, including Nation States, Indigenous Nations, and Cosmic Cultures to commit to plan and assist in the orderly development and implementation of a framework and procedures that will assure and verify that space is and will remain a neutral realm from which all classes of space-based weapons are banned in perpetuity. This Treaty will put a lid on the war industry and mindset while allowing it to be transformed. It recognizes that information and data gained and shared from cooperative space exploration and development will provide unlimited benefits and opportunities to all humankind in areas of health, education, the economy, mutual security, energy, and the environment…as wars end. Together, we can and will get this Treaty signed into world law!
