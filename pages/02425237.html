Chapter 1 August 9, 1942 It’s a blistering hot day in the Mojave Desert as a caravan of three automobiles travels at approximately 70 miles an hour over an unpaved road. The combination of speed and dirt creates a 30-foot high plume of dust that is visible for miles. The first two vehicles are marked 'San Bernardino County Sheriff Department', and in the trailing vehicle, a black 1939 Oldsmobile coupe, sit two FBI agents. A young 26-year-old, Lee Abramowitz, is buried in an FBI file on top of a history book, while his senior partner, agent Garth Ladel, a straightforward, self-absorbed bureaucrat is trying to maneuver the car through the blinding dust.
