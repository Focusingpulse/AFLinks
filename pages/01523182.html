Author(s) O N Oliveira Keywords Faraday's Law, abstract entities, electromagnetism , closed circuits Published 1993 Journal Galilean Electrodynamics Volume 4 Number 3 Pages 54-62 Abstract A critique is directed at the use of abstract entities--such as fields--in electromagnetism, in particular the need to resort to imaginary closed circuits when applying Faraday's Law.It is suggested that a corrected direct action-at-a-distance scheme that considers retardation effects within a certain approximation, as deduced by Ritz and reviewed by O'Rahilly, is a plausible alternative which is firmly anchored in physical grounds.
