NIKOLA TESLA, OF NEW YORK, N.Y. ELECTRICAL TRANSMISSION OF POWER. SPECIFICATION forming part of Letters Patent No. 382,280, dated May 1, 1888. Original application filed October 12, 1887. Serial No. 252,132. Divided and this application filed March 9, 1888. Serial No. 266,755. (No model.) To all whom it may concern: Be it known that I, N IKOLA T ESLA , from Smiljan, Lika, border country of Austria-Hungary, and residing in the city, county, and State of New York, have invented certain new and useful Improvements in the Transmission of Power, of which the following is a specification, reference being had to the drawings accompanying and forming part of the same. This application is a division of an application filed by me October 12, 1887, No. 252,132. The practical solution of the problem of the electrical conversion and transmission of mechanical energy involves certain requirements which the apparatus and systems heretofore employed have not been capable of fulfilling. Such a solution primarily demands a uniformity of speed in the motor irrespective of its load within its normal working limits.
