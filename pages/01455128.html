"The great difficulty in constructing a sphere that will revolve when vibrations are applied, is in equating its interior volume, which is altered on insertion of the shaft and accessories, with the chord of mass of the shell itself. The differentiation induced in the interior volume must be equated so as to harmonize with the sphere mass chord . Concordance is established between both through certain conditions of differentiation and in accordance with the laws of harmony . "If the mass chord of the sphere is B flat, and the internal parts when introduced displace the internal volume 1/20th, this 1/20th represents an antagonistic 1/20th against the concordance of the sphere mass and the internal parts must be so graduated as to get at the same chord an octave or more nearest approaching the concordance of the internal volume. No intermediates (music intervals) between octaves would ever reach sympathetic union . "For example, we have a spun (or hydro-formed) shell of 12 inches internal diameter and 1/32 inches thick (wall), having an internal volume of 904.77 cubic inches.
