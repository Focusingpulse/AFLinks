To Mathilde Scholl in Cologne — GA 264. The History and Content of the Esoteric School I

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 1 May 1903 · archived # 2,438,452
, Berlin Translated by Steiner Online Library To Mathilde Scholl in Cologne On February 11, 1903, Mathilde Scholl had written to Rudolf Steiner: ...In connection with our conversation, I would now like to ask the following. On plate XXVI of Leadbeater's book [“Man visible & invisible”] is the aura of one of Buddha's disciples, a disciple who has reached the fourth stage on the path, the stage of an arhat. The aura is concentrically arranged, the color sequence from the center: 1) yellow = intelligence 2) pink = love 3) light blue = devotion 4) green = compassion (sympathy) 5) violet = spiritua
