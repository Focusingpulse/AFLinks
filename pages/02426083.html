Not Quite Bashing Bashar Book Review Bashar: Blueprint for Change Cross-examined by Monty Tyson Part I A large part of the otherwise fragmented New Age is still identified, if through nothing else, with the slogan "You Create Your Own Reality". This has been a constantly-reiterated theme, particularly of channeled sources, since its philosophical outline was suggested in the Seth material. It has become the conventional wisdom of the "new age" every bit as much as "a penny saved is a penny earned" anchors the conventional wisdom of the culture at large—and, we would argue, makes ultimately about as much "cents" (sorry—ol Monty must have picked up some of MT's more egregious habits).
