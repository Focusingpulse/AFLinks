Cheiro, whose real name was Count Louis Hamon, was born in 1866. He wrote his first book on palmistry at the age of twelve, expanding his career as an author to include ghost stories, occultism and Hollywood scenarios. Widely travelled, he worked as a press correspondent during the Japan-Chinese War and the Russo-Japanese War. He was also a founder member of the Pacific-Geographic Society and a Fellow of the Royal Geographic Society. He died in 1936.
