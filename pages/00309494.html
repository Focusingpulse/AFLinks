Georgiev E-mail: propulsion@ldg2.com (July 27, 2026) Abstract. This article examines a closed mechanical system consisting of two hulls. Using this system, we can obtain an uncompensated momentum for one of the hulls in the closed system by employing the design described below and performing the required processes in sequence. It should be noted that, according to the laws of classical mechanics, Newton in “The Principia: Mathematical Principles of Natural Philosophy” discusses the law of conservation of the center of mass’s motion, but only for classical bodies. The device under consideration, however, consists of two hulls, each with different properties. In this case, to simplify this brief theoretical discussion, we have omitted some of the processes. This is because obtaining a single uncompensated momentum for one of the hulls in the closed system is sufficient to demonstrate the device’s functionality.
