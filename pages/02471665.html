He was 86 years old. Considered one of the most important scientists of the age, among other things, Tesla was the discoverer of alternating current power transmission and distribution of electrical energy. On his 78th birthday he startled everyone by announcing he had invented a “death beam” powerful enough to destroy 10,000 airplanes at a distance of 250 miles and annihilate an army of 1,000,000 soldiers instantly.
