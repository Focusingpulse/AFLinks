He is president of S.R.P. Inc. (Service de recherche pédagogique) in Québec and serves as an editor and administrator of the General Science Journal . Biography Michaud was born in 1944. His initial professional career was in computer sciences, systems analysis, data processing, computer languages, electronics and equipment–computer interfacing. Since 1982 he has been president of S.R.P. Inc., a Québec company
