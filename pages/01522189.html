Come Join the Live Discussion hosted by David de Hilster was a live session on 30 January 2021 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. Critical Thinkers have many answers to the question of what is gravity. What do you think it is? Is it a particle? Is it space-time? Is it caused by aether? Is it electric? Join in the discussion this Saturday hosted by Dissident Science's David de Hilster. Summary A session on what gravity is, with Ray Gallucci as guest and three of his recorded conference presentations played in turn — on Le Sage push gravity, on Duncan Shaw 's ether account, and on the electric universe account. David de Hilster hosted, and set the four families out at the start: spacetime, which he would call a mathematical theory rather than a model; gravitons and particles; the ether; and the electric universe. Gallucci's method and history Gallucci described what he does and why. He came to it around 2010, going back over relativity and the twin paradox with the physics he had from college, and finding that he could follow the explanations without their making much sense. Searching, he found Paul Marmet 's site, where classical physics is used to account for a couple of dozen of what he called the sacred cows; and Stephen Crothers 's, where Schwarzschild's original paper is shown to contain no black holes, the mathematical existence of them resting instead on Hilbert's misreading of a parameter — Schwarzschild having been killed in the war before he could clarify anything. His own contribution is deliberately narrower than either.
