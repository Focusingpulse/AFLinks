Advanced continued fraction approximations and bounds for the Gamma Function and the generalized Wallis ratio YunJong Kang, HyonChol Kim* 1 , HyonChol Kang, Kwang Ri ABSTRACT In this paper, we provide a main method for construction of continued fraction based on a given power series using Euler connection. Then we establish very innovative results in continued fraction approximation for the Gamma function as applications of our method. Also new continued fraction bounds for the Gamma function are obtained. Finally new continued fraction approximations and bounds for Wallis ratio are established. Keywords: Euler connection, Continued fraction, Gamma function, Bernoulli number, Wallis ratio 1.
