Maradudin Ab initio Hartree-Fock self-consistent field calculations for hydrogen atoms in a titanium atom matrix have been performed. Computed potential energy surfaces for the deuterium motion are used to calculate fusion rates. These calculations suggest that no stable state of titanium deuteride exists with the small inter-deuteron distances required for measurable fusion rates. 1.
