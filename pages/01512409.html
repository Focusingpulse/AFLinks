One thing though. It could just be the way you've drawn it but geometry of the wedge will actually work against the motion of the spinning wheel. As the magnet passes further and further down the wedge more and more diamagnetism is acting upon it which will force it to travel back the way it came since that's the easiest path for the magnet to travel (remember energy likes to take the shortest route possible, no exceptions. The route ultimately depending on it's environment and the type of energy in question). A rectangular piece of bismuth parallel with the face of the magnet on a spring would be a better shape I believe. Also you've got to remember the strength of diamagnetism is very weak, that said I don't want to immediately discount the idea so bite the bullet and try it.
