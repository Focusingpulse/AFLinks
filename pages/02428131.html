From the fact that I am writing from Wiesneck, you will be able to conclude that many things have turned out quite differently than we expected. We came here on July 31, stayed in the so-called country house, which is furnished for convalescents, and hoped to regain our strength by Michaelmas so that we could then resume our normal life in Dornach.
