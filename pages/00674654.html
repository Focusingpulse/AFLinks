Therefore, quadrature (or squaring) of the circle with compass and straightedge (or ruler) has been deemed to be impossible. We show that this was a prejudice and proof that quadrature is possible and clearly delivers π = 3 in the formula π r 2 for calculating the area of the circle. We also show a physical experiment that unambiguously proofs this result.
