Mechanics of Stellar Composition in Quantum Measurement Units (QMU) A Composition–Structure Inference Scheme from First Principles David W. Thomson III Quantum AetherDynamics Institute October 7, 2025 Abstract We develop a first-principles, fully QMU formulation for stellar structure and composition. The ideal (thermal) sector is encoded by the composition-blind identity pres volm = mass temp θ, i.e., pres = ρ temp θ with temp = λC 2 Fq 2 . Composition enters through the electron fraction Ye = Z/A = 1−fn when electron support is relevant, giving ρ ∝ 1/Ye at fixed electron degeneracy pressure. We parameterize Ye (r) by a monotone spline and constrain it using (i) helioseismic sound-speed shapes (fixing θ(r) in QMU) [4, 5], (ii) neutrino flux ratios (pp vs. CNO; constraining deep catalysts) [2, 3], and (iii) photospheric/solar-wind abundance ratios at the surface boundary Au M λC [1]. Exterior light deflection α(b) = 4π depends only on mass and geometry, separating it 2 m a b cleanly from interior composition. As a quantitative preview using ratio-only cards, we obtain a high Yesurf and a mildly decreased Yecore , e.g. Yecore ≈ 0.867±0.010. Figures show “posterior-lite” bands suitable to be replaced by full posteriors from the joint likelihood; all code and cards are provided for reproducibility and collaboration.
