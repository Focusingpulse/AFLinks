It offers advice on how these practices can be avoided, for example through encouraging optimal fetal positioning, optimizing the birth environment, and using drug-free pain management methods, and includes tried-and-tested guidance on sleep, exercise, diet, and therapies. With invaluable contributions from midwives, doulas, mothers, and doctors, this book will ensure that professionals can present a balanced account of the wide array of options available and enable parents to be confident in their decision-making.
