Escape from Einstein Book Author Ronald R Hatch Published 1992 Publisher Kneat Kompany, Wilmington, California Pages 242 ISBN 0963211307 Escape from Einstein is a 1992 book by Ronald R. Hatch setting out an ether gauge theory as a replacement for special and general relativity . It is the first and only book-length statement of the programme Hatch pursued for the rest of his life, later refined under the name Modified Lorentz Ether Theory (MLET). What gives the book its standing in this literature is who wrote it. Hatch was not an outsider to the subject he criticised: he was one of the principal algorithm designers of the Global Positioning System, inventor of the carrier-smoothing technique universally called the Hatch filter , and later President of the Institute of Navigation. His argument is that the operational reality of GPS — a system he helped build — tells against Einstein rather than for him. The book's own summary of what distinguishes the theory: a return to absolute simultaneity while allowing clocks to run at different rates; the replacement of the Lorentz transformations by gauge transformations (scaled Galilean transformations) ; a unification of the electromagnetic and gravitational forces; an account of the source of inertia ; and a physical mechanism underlying the equivalence principle. "Rather than curved space, the ether gauge theory postulates an elastic ether ." Publication Kneat Kompany appears to have been Hatch's own imprint: the ISBN prefix carries only this one title, and the company's address in Wilmington, California is Hatch's own. He cited the book himself simply as "Kneat, Wilmington, CA". It ran to a single 1992 hardcover edition with no reprint. Library and dealer records give the extent as 242 pages, though some listings — including this page previously — give 232. The theory An elastic ether, and matter made of waves Hatch's ether is a McCullagh-type elastic solid , possessing shear elasticity (needed to carry transverse light)
