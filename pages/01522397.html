Kornilova is a Russian physicist known for experimental research into the nuclear transmutation of isotopes in growing biological cultures, a topic associated with low-energy nuclear reactions (LENR), sometimes called cold fusion. She is listed in The Worldwide List of Dissident Scientists . Work Working extensively in collaboration with Vladimir I. Vysotskii, Kornilova reports the experimental observation of accelerated transmutation of stable isotopes and the deactivation of radioactive isotopes within growing microbiological cultures.
