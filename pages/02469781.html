Why Tesla Did Not Exhibit — Complaints of Prices at the Fair Restaurants — Ups and Downs of the Midway Shows — A Threatened Invasion of Big Signboards. BUFFALO, July 13. — Those who go to the Electricity Building at the Pan-American to see the latest work of the inventors find much to interest them, but generally go away complaining that they expected 600 exhibits by Edison and Tesla and found none. So far as Edison is concerned that complaint soon will be met. From the beginning of the Exposition his phonographs and like inventions have been on exhibition, but now the wizard means to put on view his two most recent and valuable inventions. These are the new thermopile and the new storage battery. Edison's confidential representative was here this week and arranged for space for the proper exhibition of these inventiors. Among persons interested in the development of electric energy the thermopile exceeds in importance the new battery for the reason that it is expected to revolutlonize the method of producing electrical power. It generates electric power direct from heat.
