Glyphosate is the active ingredient in Monsanto’s Roundup herbicide and over a hundred other commercial formulations. It is the cause of great concern as evidence of its harmful effects keep piling up from independent scientific studies in recent years, including endocrine disruption, DNA damage, reproductive and developmental toxicities, neurotoxicity, cancer, and birth defects (see [2] Glyphosate Toxic and Roundup Worse , SiS 26; [3] Death by Multiple Poisoning, Glyphosate and Roundup , SiS 42; [4] Ban Glyphosate Herbicide Now . SiS 43; [5] Lab Study Establishes Glyphosate Link to Birth Defects , SiS 48).
