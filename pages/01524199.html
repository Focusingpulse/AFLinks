The Relativity Principle is Wrong Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Speaker Roger J Anderton Date 6 September 2025 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording The Relativity Principle is Wrong was a live session presented by Roger J Anderton on 6 September 2025 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. As dealt with in previous talks it was pointed out that the metaphysics of Einstein's relativity is a mess. A proposal is thus made that the relativity principle should be revised and the existing principle discarded as wrong. Hosted by Roger Anderton. Summary Roger J Anderton , who hosts most of the society's Saturday sessions, here gave the talk himself, continuing a series on the metaphysics underlying Einstein's relativity. The claim is structural rather than technical: the relativity principle is the foundation everything else is built on, and if the foundation is misstated then the building is unsound. He prepared the material in dialogue with ChatGPT and said so openly, explaining that the published literature says very little about Lorentz's ether theory in the detail he wants, and that of the assistants he has tried this one is the most willing to set out a case against the mainstream position rather than simply restating it. What Einstein actually said The starting text is the statement from the 1905 paper: the laws by which the states of physical systems undergo change are not affected whether these changes of state be referred to one or the other of two systems of coordinates in uniform translatory motion. In plain words, all physical laws are the same in any inertial frame and no experiment performed in uniform motion can reveal whether you are moving or at rest. To this Einstein added the second postulate, that the speed of light in vacuum is the same in all inertial frames independently of the motion of source or observer — and it is the second postulate, Anderton argued, that does the damage.
