Amir M. Abbassi Residence Tehran, Iran Nationality Iranian Known for Machian ("relational") relativity; exact non-static solutions of the Einstein field equations with a cosmological constant; density-metric unimodular gravity Scientific career Fields General relativity ; cosmology; gravitation Institutions University of Tehran Amir M. Abbassi is an Iranian physicist in the Department of Physics at the University of Tehran , working in general relativity, gravitation and cosmology. His published work catalogued on this wiki was carried out with Amir H. Abbassi of Tarbiat Modares University, and appeared in Apeiron and on the arXiv between 2002 and 2008. A note on names. The two collaborators are different people at different institutions — A. M. Abbassi at the University of Tehran (North Kargar) and A. H. Abbassi at Tarbiat Modares University — and the surname is transliterated variously as Abbassi, Abbasi and Abasi across the papers. A. M. Abbassi is the second author on all three works below; A. H. Abbassi does not have a separate page on this wiki. His work is notable within the dissident literature for being technically orthodox : it does not reject general relativity but works inside it, deriving exact solutions and modest extensions of the standard formalism, and its heterodoxy lies in taking seriously two things mainstream relativity tends to set aside — Mach's principle, and the non-uniqueness of the spherically symmetric vacuum solution once a cosmological constant is present. Work Relational relativity In " Relational Relativity " ( Apeiron 9(2), 2002) the Abbassis present a "Machianized" version of both special and general relativity built on a simple model of inertia.
