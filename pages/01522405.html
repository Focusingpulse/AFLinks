Abbassi Residence Tehran, Iran Nationality Iranian Knownfor Machian ("relational") relativity; exact non-static solutions of the Einstein field equations with a cosmological constant; density-metric unimodular gravity Scientific career Fields General relativity ; cosmology; gravitation Institutions University of Tehran Amir M. Abbassi is an Iranian physicist in the Department of Physics at the University of Tehran , working in general relativity, gravitation and cosmology. His published work catalogued on this wiki was carried out with Amir H. Abbassi of Tarbiat Modares University, and appeared in Apeiron and on the arXiv between 2002 and 2008. A note on names.
