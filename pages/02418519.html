, Berlin Translated by Steiner Online Library 7. On the Formation of Planetary Systems [ 1 ] First, the purely physical theory that is usually taught in our schools. According to the Kant-Laplace theory, it is assumed that where our solar system is today, there was once a thin, gaseous nebula, similar to the mass of the Orion Nebula, so that all the planets—Jupiter, Saturn, Mars, Venus, and Earth—were, by analogy, like simple fat globules, like fat globules floating on water.
