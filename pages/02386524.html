The Mysteries and Mystery Wisdom — Christianity as a Mystical Fact (1947)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 2,386,524
A kind of mysterious veil hangs over the manner in which spiritual needs were satisfied during the older civilizations by those who sought a deeper religious life and fuller knowledge than the popular religions offered. If we inquire how these needs were satisfied, we find ourselves led into the dim twilight of the Mysteries, and the individual seeking them disappears for a time from our view. We see that the popular religions cannot give him what his heart desires.
