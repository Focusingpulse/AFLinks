Journal of Borderland Research >> Vol. 34 >> No. 04 [12] John Keel , A Wilderness Crying for a Voice Probably the most indefatigable Flying Saucer researcher of the 1960s was John A. Keel. Desperately he searched for Light in the murky gloom of conflicting data and opinions; but his mountain of evidence produced only a molehill of conclusion, largely because of a built-in conceit which made him reject all opinions but his own. Thus he blinded himself to the Truth he was seeking and became a sucker for Looeamong's propaganda. In a May 1969 letter to your editor, three pages long, Keel writes: "Unlike you, I have tediously collected evidence and documentation through extensive nationwide investigations which indicate that there is absolutely no validity to the extraterrestrial thesis. Rather, I am obliged to conclude that we are dealing with another space-time frame composed of matter constituted of energies on a different frequency. I am not talking about 4-D. The forces involved are able to transmogrify into any kind of object or entity and they are able to convince individual witnesses of damned near anything. In short, I now believe, after long and careful study, that reincarnation is a hoax. Spiritualism is a hoax. Religion is a hoax. FLYING SAUCERS ARE A HOAX. Human beliefs are exploited on every level.
