Bell's Theorem, Quantum Theory and Conceptions of the Universe (Fundamental Theories of Physics) Book Author Menas Kafatos Language English Series Fundamental Theories of Physics Subject Bell's theorem, foundations of quantum mechanics Published 1989 Publisher Springer Pages 348 ISBN 0792304969 Bell's Theorem, Quantum Theory and Conceptions of the Universe is a 1989 volume edited by Menas Kafatos , published by Kluwer Academic (Springer) in the Fundamental Theories of Physics series. It grew out of a Fall Workshop of the same title held at George Mason University in October 1988. Overview The book collects contributions on Bell's theorem and the foundations of quantum mechanics, addressing quantum nonlocality and nonseparability, hidden-variable theories, the measurement problem and wave-function collapse, and the wider implications of quantum theory for cosmology, the anthropic principle, and the nature of consciousness. Contributors include several leading figures in the foundations of physics. About the editor Menas Kafatos is a Greek-American physicist and cosmologist, professor at George Mason University and later Chapman University, who has written extensively on quantum theory, cosmology, and the relationship between physics and consciousness.
