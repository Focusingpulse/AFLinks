Wildlife Services has been having their way for almost a century, killing millions of wild animals each year, as well as maiming, poisoning, and brutalizing countless pets. They have also seriously harmed more than a few humans. They apparently thought they were going to continue getting away with it. But with your help, we're not going to let them. Learn more and support our efforts to end America's war on wildlife at http://www.predatordefense.org/exposed. EXPOSED won the award for Best Wildlife Activism at the 2014 Wildlife Conservation Film Festival in New York, the premier wildlife film festival in North America. It also won the award for Best Short Film at the Animal Film Festival in Grass Valley, CA, in February 2015.
