Update 2001: using "spectrograph carbons" does not eliminate the magnetic grains. Dan Y. found some pure carbons that analyze to a couple of ppm of silicon and manganese, but no iron. Grinding up this carbon does not get you any magnetic grains, the carbon has to be arced at about 10 amps to produce magnetic detrius. Could the magnetic grains be a paramagnetic compound of carbon, nitrogen, and/or oxygen? Must repeat the experiment in a He or Ar atmosphere. (To produce an arc, I jammed the carbons into slightly small holes drilled in brass blocks, then wired them in series with a 1200W 120V electric heater.) Update 1999: at a "Weird Science" meeting in Seattle, Frank S. attempted the "carbon rods" experiment in air. We found that within the black debris created by the arc there were tiny dark grains which were slightly magnetic, and would leap to the face of a 1cm NIB disk magnet.
