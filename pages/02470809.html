Boldt, Jr., executors of the estate of George C. Boldt, late owner of the Waldorf Astoria Hotel, Manhattan, to foreclose two mortgages on the property owned by Nikola Tesla, scientist. The property involved was 200 acres of land at Shoreham, twelve miles from here. Besides Tesla, there were twelve other defendants. According to the complaint, Tesla, for the purpose of payment, gave a mortgage of $7,270.35 to Boldt several years ago and on April 27, 1904, also gave a mortgage to Thomas G. Sherman. It was also brought out that in May, 1908, Tesla gave a third mortgage on the property to the Waldorf-Astoria Hotel for $8,379.82. In order to settle the estate the executors brought the proceedings and Referee Miles held many hearings. His decision, which will be handed to the court to-morrow, will favor the executors and suggest that they go to court and secure an order to have the property sold.
