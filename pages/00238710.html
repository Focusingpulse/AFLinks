1 Universality of Superconducting Transition Temperatures Hans Peter Good Sargans, Switzerland e-mail: hp.good at catv.rol.ch March 20, 2023 Abstract It is hypothesized that superconducting transition temperatures are universal properties, which are amenable to a simple theoretical ansatz. The conjecture is supported by measured superconducting transition temperatures of many high quality samples. The experimental data contradict the mainstream view that an interaction between electrons and lattice vibrations is responsible for superconductivity. Keywords: Superconductivity; transition temperature; universality; fundamental constants; number constants.
