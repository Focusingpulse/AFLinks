HELLO, i am new here and have been reading about cracking WMO. I made a really simple setup with a potbelly stove, half size gas bottle some metal tube and a good oil cooler. This may seem a bit dangerous! I got a 20 ltr plastic drum and inserted a rubber pipe 4 inches from the bottom, connected the rubber pipe to some metal tube wide enough for wmo to flow when cold. Got a 2.5ltr paint tin cut a hole insert WMO fuel pipe, cut another hole insert a metal pipe and attach leaf blower to it. Remove gas bottle valve, pour in 10 ltrs WMO find screw fit pipe to go were valve was, weld some more pipe to that, wel on a cooler of some sort. Bit more pipe, through cold bath job sorted! Only power needed is electric for leaf blower! I get abou a gallon of usable fuel an hour once up to temp.
