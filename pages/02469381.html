The Republic Committee for the Celebration of the 150 th anniversary of the birth of the genius from Smiljan, which was presided by the Serbian premier Vojislav Kostunica, reached a decision to give a special meaning to this jubilee by erecting the memorial, for which the author had been awarded the second place prize at the international competition in Canada during the preceding year. The monument is the gift by the Electric Power Industry of Serbia, by which they repay the debt to their countryman, the innovator, since on the basis of his patents the first hydro power plant was built in the history of the mankind. The airport is, in the opinion of the sculptor, an ideal place for the memorial to a Serb who made the whole world his debtor.
