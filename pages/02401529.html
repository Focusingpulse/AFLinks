GC2026-STP042 Comparison of the results of theoretical calculations of night glow emission with measurement data obtained by both ground-based methods and from space shuttles Olga V. Antonenko1 , Andrey S. Kirillov1 1 Polar Geophysical Institute of the Kola Scientific Center of the Russian Academy of Sciences, Apatity, Russia antonenko@pgia.ru In this paper, we compare the calculated values of emission intensities of the Herzberg I, Chamberlain, and Atmospheric bands with experimental data obtained from the space shuttles «Discovery» and «Endeavour» [1]. We also compare the calculated values of the intensities of the Herzberg I and Chamberlain bands with ground￾based experimental data obtained from the Ebert-Fastie spectrograph (Kitt Peak National Observatory, Arizona, 32° N, USA) [2]. The calculated values for intensities of Atmospheric bands are compared with the data obtained by the High Resolution Imaging Spectrograph (HIRES) on the Keck I telescope (Keck Observatory, Mauna Kea Peak, 4145 m, Hawaii, USA, 19° N) [3]. The measurements were carried out during a period of low solar activity. References 1. Broadfoot A.L., Bellaire P.J. Jr. // J. Geophys. Res. 1999. V. 104. Iss. A8. P. 17127–17138. 2. Broadfoot A.L., Kendall D.K. // J. Geophys. Res. 1968. V. 73. №.1. P.426-428. 3. Slanger T.G., Cosby P.C., Huestis D.L., Osterbrock D.E. // J. Geophys. Res. 2000. V. 105. №.D16, P. 20557- 20564.
