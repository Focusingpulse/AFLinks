Ahluwalia, M. Evans and H. Munera et al. Overview This three-page essay by Valeri V. Dvoeglazov and Myron W. Evans appeared in Apeiron 4(2–3) in 1997, in a special issue that C. Roy Keys had commissioned on longitudinal solutions of the relativistic wave equations. Its purpose is diagnostic rather than constructive: several groups had recently reported unexpected additional solutions to the free-field equations, and the authors ask why so many appeared at once. They list five: Ahluwalia and Ernst's E = 0 solution of the j = 1 Maxwell equations, obtained from the characteristic equation in momentum representation; Evans's own B (3) Evans–Vigier field, formed as the cross-product of the transverse modes, B (1) × B (2) = iB (0) B (3)* and cyclic permutations; Múnera's non-plane-wave solutions of the Klein–Gordon equation using unconventional basis functions; the Múnera–Guzmán generalised potentials; and Chubykalo and Smirnov-Rueda's "method of separated potentials". The analysis proceeds by writing the dynamical Maxwell equations in the two-component Weinberg-like form ∇ × ( E ± i B ) ∓ i∂ t ( E ± i B ) = 0, noting that the second is the parity conjugate of the first, and looking for solutions of the generalised form A ~ a exp(λt + k · r ) rather than restricting to plane waves at the outset. With the spin-1 matrices (J i ) jk = −iε ijk , the characteristic polynomial det(λδ ij + ( J · k ) ij ) = 0 has roots λ = 0 and λ = ±k, so the general solution is a three-term superposition.
