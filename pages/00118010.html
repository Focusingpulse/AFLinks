Utilizing the Newman-Penrose (NP) formalist, I project the metric onto a null tetrad {l, n, m, m¯ } to evaluate the Weyl and Ricci scalars. We demonstrate that the resulting stress-energy tensor Tµν = T (null) µν + T (em) µν satisfies the Weak Energy Condition (WEC) and the local conservation law ∇µTµν = 0.
