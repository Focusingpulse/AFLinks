Mechanics and general physics Covers the main concepts of physics, e.g. forces, energy and the properties of matter. Waves Looks at the properties and effects of wave energy and examines sound, electromagnetic and light waves in detail. Electricity and magnetism Explains the forms, uses and behaviour of these two linked phenomena. Atomic and nuclear physics Examines atomic and nuclear structure and energy, radioactivity, fission and fusion.
