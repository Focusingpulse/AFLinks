Approximately 21,000 persons of a metropolitan population of 136,500 are employed in manufacturing. There has been a decline in industrial employment since the Schoellkopf rock slide in 1956. There were 26,579 jobholders in industry shortly after the Schoellkopf plant was destroyed as compared with 21,289 in December of last year. But business and industrial leaders here expect this trend to be reversed now that more favorable power rates have been made possible by the startup of the 2,190,000 kilowatt Niagara project. When the national recession is overcome — where steel rallies and the national economy regains its bounce — Niagara Falls hopes to be in the forefront of the boom. It is blessed with natural and man-made assets that should give it a lift. These include abundant low-priced hydroelectric power, considerable available land for industrial development, abundant fresh water and a skilled, highly trained labor force.
