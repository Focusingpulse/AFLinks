The most complete extractable dataset of experimentally measured two-state protein folding rates is the Ivankov et al. (2003) table of 57 proteins, reproduced in full below with PDB IDs, chain lengths, ln(kf), and contact order values. The larger PFDB database (141 proteins, temperature-corrected to 25°C) exists at balalab-skku.org/PFDB but its tabular data lives behind a web interface and supplementary .docx file. For intrinsically disordered proteins, DisProt (3,201+ entries with sequences) is freely downloadable via REST API. Critically, no prior work has applied FFT spectral entropy of amino acid property sequences to predict folding rates — this appears to be a genuine gap in the literature.
