He was (now deceased) one of the early pioneers of 'binaural beat' technology. Brainwave entrainment is a major result of this method. For example, a tone of , let's say 120 hz is routed (via headphones) to the left ear; then another tone, say 128 hz, is routed to the right. Well, your brain 'hears' the difference between the two--which is 8 hz. As your brain hears this 8 hz tone, it becomes entrained to that frequency; this is call a frequency-following-response (or, FFR). There are many high-tech meditation devices, called light/sound machines, that utilize this concept. (CAUTION, Though, the lights aspect is not reccomended for those with epilepsy or any type of seizure disorder).
