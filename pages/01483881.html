Keely "The normal brain is like a harp of many strings strung to perfect harmony . The transmitting conditions being perfect, are ready, at any impulse , to induce pure sympathetic assimilation . The different strings represent the different ventricles and convolutions . The differentiations of any one from its true setting is fatal, to a certain degree , to the harmony of the whole combination . If the sympathetic condition of any physical organism carries a positive flow of 80 per cent on its whole combination , and a negative one of 20 per cent, it is the medium of perfect assimilation to one of the same ratio , if it is distributed under the same conditions to the mass of the other. If two masses of metal , of any shape whatever, are brought under perfect assimilation , to one another, their unition , when brought into contact, will be instant.''
