interesting experience

· archived # 1,507,211
I just got back from voting. I am looking at my voter registration card. It tells me to go to 6506 Hwy. 309 South to vote. When Mary and I drove to this address, it was a soy bean field. Actually, there is no such address. The addresses of the farmhouses went from 6472 and then the next one was 6608. After going back & forth on the road a few times to determine that there really was no such address, we headed back toward where we thought the voting might be. On the way we saw two little signs about 8 inches off the ground...about half the size of a real estate sign you would see in a yard. They said "Vote Here". We pulled in and looking at the address saw that it was 3776 Hwy. 309 South. Not very close to the 6506 printed on our Voter Registration card. In we walk, very nice people. They ask our names, decline to look at our voter registration cards or our drivers licenses. Have us sign the book, and then we went and voted at some diebold computerized dealymabobbers. That was it. Back home now.
