Binary Mechanics Cosmology Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Speaker James Keene Date 15 February 2025 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording Binary Mechanics Cosmology was a live session presented by James Keene on 15 February 2025 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. First, the proposition that cosmology modeling must be based on a successful TOE (theory of everything), such as binary mechanics, is discussed. Second, many phenomena predicted and demonstrated by binary mechanics have implications for building a successful cosmological model. Summary James Keene 's proposition, put to the session in one sentence, is that a cosmological model should be built on the best theory of everything available — and that binary mechanics is it. The talk came in two parts accordingly: the case that binary mechanics is a successful theory of everything, and then, in his own phrase, let us make a universe. The session was hosted by Ian J Cowan . What binary mechanics is Keene traced the origin to the complex amplitude of quantum mechanics. Splitting a complex number into parts and quantising the magnitudes of x and y gives the first two equations: M = 0 or 1, L = 0 or 1. Nothing of the amplitude is discarded; what emerges is a pair of quantum types making up a spot unit, three spot units making a spot, and the sign of x , the sign of y and a position swap defining eight elementary particles uniquely by three binary values. He made a point of the mathematics required: one-bit binary numbers, the AND function and the NOT function. Any three-year-old who has mastered a language already has all three. His argument that it is the leading theory of everything had four supports.
