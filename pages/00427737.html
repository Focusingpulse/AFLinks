Towards a Floer theory for Mars II - Floer Hessian field almost extends Urs Frauenfelder Joa Weber∗ Universit¨at Augsburg UNICAMP June 13, 2026 Abstract In part I, [FW26a], we showed that collisional periodic orbits of twisted Zeeman systems can be detected variationally by a non-local Hamiltonian action functional. In this part II we show that the linearized gradient flow of this non￾local functional is a Fredholm operator and prove a non-local elliptic reg￾ularity result. These results are obtained with the theory of almost extendability of weak Hessian fields introduced in [FW26c].
