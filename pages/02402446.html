Space-time surfaces form an algebra with respect to multiplication and this algebra de￾composes to a union of number fields. This suggest a dramatic revision of what computation means physically. The standard view of computation as a construction of arithmetic functions is replaced with a physical picture in which space-times as 4-surfaces have interpretation as almost deterministic computations. Space-time surfaces allow arithmetic operations and also the counterparts of functional composition and iteration are well-defined.
