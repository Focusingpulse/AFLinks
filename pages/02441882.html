Fourteenth Meeting — GA 300. Faculty Meetings with Rudolf Steiner (1998)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 24 July 1920 · archived # 2,441,882
, Stuttgart Fourteenth Meeting Dr. Steiner: Perhaps Mr. Molt would say a few words. Mr. Molt thanks the teachers for their work in the past school year and gives particular thanks to Dr. Steiner. He recalls Dr. Steiner’s words about strength, courage, and light at the beginning of the course in 1919. Dr. Steiner: I too must think of the time when we began our course last fall. It is certain that what we attempted to bring from spiritual life into our own spirits has had an effect upon our souls. I would like to recall that moment and again ask those good spirits who are watching over our deeds
