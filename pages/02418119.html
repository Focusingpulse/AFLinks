Nick Franks was educated at Manchester University where he received an Honours degree in Economics. In 1973 he co-founded AMEK SYSTEMS & CONTROLS LTD (later AMEK TECHNOLOGY GROUP Plc) with Graham Langley. AMEK manufactured professional audio mixing consoles and its large customer base included many of the world's leading broadcast. In 1997 Nick departed the professional audio industry and developed an interest in Radionics and Homoeopathy. Almost entirely self-taught in both Radionics and Homoeopathy, his current main line of work is the development of Radionic instruments and codes.
