MolPhot2026-main033 Generation of optical vortices with different angular moments based on thin polycrystalline MAPbI3 film Elena A. Bashegurova1 , Kapitonov V. Yury1 1 St Petersburg University e.bashegurova@spbu.ru This work investigates diffraction gratings belonging to the class of diffractive optical elements (DOEs) whose operation is based on the phenomenon of material resonance in semiconductors. Such DOEs combine the properties of traditional DOEs with the capability of selecting a narrow emission spectrum resonant with the resonance in the studied material. The subjects of the study are fork gratings fabricated by irradiating the surface of a thin polycrystalline halide perovskite MAPbI3 (MA+ = CH3 NH3 +) film with a focused electron beam at an energy of 10 keV. The period of the gratings is 4.4 μm, and their size is 176x176 μm. Spectroscopic study of this thin film reveals the presence of an exciton resonance in the range of 1.60–1.64 eV [1]. Diffraction studies of continuous-wave laser radiation on the grating were performed at temperatures of 3.5–294 K in a closed-cycle helium cryostation. Fork grating patterns were irradiated on the surface of the thin perovskite film. The beam reflected from such a DOE corresponds to a Gaussian beam intensity distribution. However, in the first order diffraction for a fork grating with a difference of 1 in the number of periods between the upper and lower parts of the grating (Fig. 1a), the reflex corresponds to the first Laguerre-Gaussian mode (Fig. 1b), with an orbital angular momentum l = 1.
