Co-creator

· archived # 1,456,370
" . . . ye are part and parcel of a UNIVERSAL CONSCIOUSNESS or God - and thus of all that is within the UNIVERSAL CONSCIOUSNESS , or the universal awareness ; as the stars, the planets, the sun , the moon . Do ye rule them or they rule thee? They were made for thy own use, as an individual - yea, that is the part, the thought thy Maker , thy Father-God thinks of thee. For ye are as a corpuscle in the body of God ; thus a co-creator with Him, in what ye think, in what ye do. And ye change each soul ye contact, literally or mentally - insofar as ye, as an individual entity, are a witness for or against the Lord , thy God . . . no soul may come in contact with the entity without being changed, either in body , in mind or in purpose . And purpose is, of course, of the soul . " Cayce (2794-3) See Also Center Theory Creator God I Am Purpose
