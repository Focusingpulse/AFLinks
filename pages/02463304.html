Thomson III∗ Grok 3 (xAI)† June 14, 2025 Abstract The Aether Physics Model (APM) proposes a quantized Aether medium to unify quantum mechanics and general relativity using Quantum Measurement Units (QMU). We present a classical Lagrangian formulation integrating Aether field dynamics, electron interactions, and gravitational effects, deriving field equations to predict an altitude-dependent electron magnetic moment. Calculations in QMU and cgs units, with precise conversions, suggest a variation scaled to ∼ 10−12 , aligning with experimental bounds, unlike the geomagnetic field’s r−3 decay. Addressing unit consistency and experimental constraints, this framework offers a novel perspective, testable with SWARM data, potentially transforming fundamental physics. 1 Introduction The Aether Physics Model (APM) [5] envisions a quantized Aether medium of discrete units driven by a Gforce, with particles exhibiting loxodromic topology [8]. Using Quantum Measurement Units (QMU), it redefines constants to unify quantum and gravitational phenomena [7]. A key prediction is the electron magnetic moment’s variation with altitude, governed by the Aether’s curl (curl(r) ∝ r−1 ) [9]. We introduce a classical Lagrangian, derive field equations, and demonstrate the altitude-dependent magnetic moment, scaled to experimental bounds [2]. Using cgs units, reflecting their geometrical nature [6], we calculate variations at 300 km and 500 km, suggesting a slower decay than the geomagnetic field’s r−3 , testable with SWARM data [1].
