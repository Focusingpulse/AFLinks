Even after both Hoagland and Allan were corrected on this by C. Frazier, formerly IE's Managing Editor, they persisted in not changing the text! The record indeed is a very different one. Gene spent the afternoon and evening of Thursday, May 13th, writing two letters that capped nearly four months of intense negotiations with two interested parties.
