· archived # 2,432,155

You will not know what the test module is about (we do not want placebo results). The only thing we will say is that it is not biological in nature. If you are one of the 10 test subjects, anything that you notice, that you have no other explanation for, you can post in that thread. After 1 month or so, we will see if any pattern emerges that is consistent with the Test Module, and proceed from there. If you would like to be a test subject for this module, please send an email to [email protected] with "Test Module" in the subject line and a note in the body giving us permission to piggy back this module onto your Theater Presentation.
