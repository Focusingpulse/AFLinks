GTM2026-main062 Construction of Characteristic Functions of Differential Network Games Using Neural Networks Xiang Li1 , Leon A. Petrosyan1 1 St Petersburg University, St. Petersburg, Russia st135125@student.spbu.ru In cooperative differential network games, the characteristic function must account for network topology, capacity constraints, and cross-coalition externalities. The rigidity of the grand-coalition's optimal network gives rise to two opposing paradigms: the embedded function evaluates a subcoalition's worth within the fixed grand-coalition network, while the autonomous function assumes complete severance of external ties and internal re-optimisation. We formalise both within a unified framework of capacity constraints and bilateral consent, prove their superadditivity, and propose a novel hybrid function: a subcoalition first optimises its internal network under scaled capacities, then greedily selects external links using residual capacity, thereby suppressing free-riding while preserving superadditivity. Evaluating all 2n subcoalitions requires repeatedly solving maximum weight subgraph problems with capacity constraints. To overcome this combinatorial difficulty, we design a Graph Neural Network (GNN) surrogate trained via behavioural cloning on optimal solutions obtained from integer linear programming; the GNN maps payoff matrices and capacity vectors to edge selection logits decoded by beam search in a single forward pass.
