Just what this conception was, Mr. Keely , with business-like caution, keeps to himself. The public, for the present at least, must be content with its visible results; but these are sufficiently noteworthy to amply repay consideration. They indicate two things: First, that Mr. Keely is no longer dealing with a power arising from gaseous pressure ; and, second, that he has made this power capable of producing rotation , levitation , and other effects of mechanical energy .
