Forces Leading to Health and Illness in Education - GA 302a. Deeper Insights into Education (1983)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 16 October 1923 · archived # 2,441,941
afternoon, Stuttgart Translated by René Querido Forces Leading to Health and Illness in Education [ 1 ] Not translated [ 2 ] Today I wish to speak about the following question: with what forces are we really working when we work educationally? Actually, this question cannot be answered in any definite sense by the culture of today. We can say, of course, that the outer life within which human beings stand, making it possible for them to earn a living, requires them to have capacities that they cannot have yet as children. We must impart such capacities to them. The behavior proper for adults
