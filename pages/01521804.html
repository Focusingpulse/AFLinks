When the concept of the aether became untenable, two obvious procedures suggested themselves: Keep Maxwell's equations but replace the Galilean transformation by the Lorentz transformation, Keep Galilean relativity but replace Maxwell's equations by an extension of the Ampère-Gauss formulation. The first of these procedures was chosen by Einstein in his celebrated paper of 1905. But there is no necessity for such a deinfication of Maxwell's equations.
