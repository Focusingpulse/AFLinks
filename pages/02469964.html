AND SO DID NIKOLA TESLA. The Great Electrical Expert Couldn’t Talked Though Well Attacked by Newspaper Men — The Visitors Are to Attend the Great Power Banquet in Buffalo This Evening. A very interesting party of visitors arrived in the city this morning on a special car from New York over the New York Central Railroad which reached the Falls Street station at 9 o’clock. They are the men who furnished the money and enterprise that has made the great power development here a success. In the party were Edward D. Adams, president of the Cataract Construction Company, Francis Lynde Stetson and Edward A. Wickes, vice-presidents, and F. W. Whitridge, Charles F. Clark, Arthur H. Masten, directors of that company. Then there were as guests Nickola Tesla, the famous electrical expert and inventor; Clemens Herschel, hydraulic engineer and expert; Lemuel Bannister, first vice-president of the Westinghouse Company; L. B. Stillwell, electrical engineer of the same company, and Mr. Terry, the company’s attorney. These visitors were met at the Prospect House by Secretary William B. Rankine of the Cataract Construction Company and after taking breakfast all went out to the power house, where most of the day is being spent in viewing the work of enlarging the plant of the Power Co
