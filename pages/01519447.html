It is published in the General Science Journal and is one of a group of his papers from that autumn, alongside Displacement Current and The Telegrapher's Equations . Tombe is candid that the medium itself is not explicable — "the substance of the aether itself can never be explained, because the entire language of physics is built upon the effects of the aether" — and argues instead from the success of hydrodynamic analogies.
