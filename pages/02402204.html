GTM2026-main061 Cooperative network in the game of pollution control Mengfa Tian , Leon A. Petrosyan , Junli Hao 18606875043@163.com In the paper, cooperative differential network game with pairwise interactions is considered. We suppose that at the initial time instant (and at prescribed intermediate instants) players choose simultaneously and independently neighbors from given neighbor sets under degree bounds, forming an admissible network to maximize the joint payoff. However, the network that is optimal at the initial time instant may cease to be such in subgames along the cooperative trajectory. A six-player pollution-control game with pairwise interactions confirms the instability of the optimal network.
