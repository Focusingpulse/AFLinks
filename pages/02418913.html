Title (en) Method and apparatus for generating energy through inertial confinement fusion Title (de) Verfahren und Vorrichtung zur Erzeugung von Energie mittels Trägheitsfusion Title (fr) Procédé et appareil de génération d'énergie par fusion par confinement inertiel Publication EP 2680271 A1 20140101 (EN) Application EP 12173401 A 20120625 Priority EP 12173401 A 20120625 Abstract (en) The present invention relates to a method of generating energy by nuclear fusion. The method comprises the steps of: bringing (100) hydrogen in a gaseous state into contact with a hydrogen transfer catalyst (14) configured to cause a transition of the hydrogen from the gaseous state to an ultra-dense state; collecting (101) the hydrogen in the ultra-dense state on a carrier (3) configured to substantially confine the hydrogen in the ultradense state within a fuel collection portion (16) of the carrier; transporting (102) the carrier to an irradiation location (9); and subjecting (103), at the irradiation location, the hydrogen in the ultra-dense state to irradiation having sufficient energy to achieve break-even in energy generation by nuclear fusion.
