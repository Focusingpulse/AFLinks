Einstein's original difficulty was that his field equations, applied to a uniform distribution of matter, gave no static solution: gravity would cause the whole to collapse. Λ supplied a uniform repulsion, tuned to balance the attraction exactly. The balance is unstable, as Arthur Eddington pointed out, and the motivation disappeared when the redshift –distance relation was published in 1929 (see Hubble Constant ).
