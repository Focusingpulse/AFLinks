Illustrator’s Guide – From Stillness to Motion: The Secret Dance of the Universe 1. The Big Stillness Drawing idea: A child lying on a grassy hill at sunset, hands behind their head, eyes closed. The world around is completely still — trees, clouds, and even a butterfly hovering in midair. Key mood: Calm, peaceful, quiet. 2. The Invisible Ocean Drawing idea: Same child now standing in the middle of a swirl of glowing, faint, wavy lines (like invisible water currents). They’re smiling in awe. The lines fill the air around them but don’t touch anything. Key mood: Mysterious, magical energy all around. 3. The First Tickle Drawing idea: A kitten gently tapping the surface of a still pond with its paw, making ripples spread. The child watches and points. Key mood: Small action starting something bigger. 4. The First Wiggle Drawing idea: A leaf in the pond starting to bob up and down because of the ripples. The child’s hand reaches toward it. Key mood: Motion beginning from stillness. 5. Two Sides of the Dance Drawing idea: Two kids holding hands and slowly spinning in a circle — one standing still, the other moving — then switching places. Swirling motion lines around them. Key mood: Partnership and rhythm. 6.
