Just a note on scaling of the 45 gallon drum experiment. I do recall reading somewhere, but I must admit I can't remember where, that to get the ionization (or whatever) effect, some certain minimum size is required. Even granted the different construction of the two, there does seem to be a relation to the "volume" of magnetic repulsion involved. Although another concern I recall hearing his that the magnets must be "non-"conductive, otherwise the effect could be shorted out? I not sure of the certainty of that.
