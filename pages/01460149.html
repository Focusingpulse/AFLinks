Keely Motor Company

· archived # 1,460,149
Keely Motor Company Board of Directors ( click to enlarge ) The Keely Motor Company was founded by John Ernst Worrell Keely and a group of businessmen in Philadelphia, 1874. The purpose was to fund Keely 's research into and eventual perfection of the famous Keely Motor . Keely built, in the course of his work, over 129 models and variations of his motor. Sometime around 1889 he succeeded in making his motor run properly demonstrating a 250 hp unit designed to power a locomotive from Philadelphia to New York and back.
