Lundquist Filaments in Quantum Measurement Units (QMU): Coaxial Shells, Polarization Cancellation, and a QMU Data Pipeline for Orion A David W. Thomson Quantum AetherDynamics Institute October 20, 2025 Abstract We recast the classical Lundquist force-free filament entirely in Quantum Measurement Units (QMU) using the Aether Physics Model (APM) ledger with base units {me , λC , Fq , e2 , eemax 2 } and the Aether unit Au . The force-free parameter becomes a Compton-curvature α = κα /λC with dimensionless twist κα . This recast yields a calibration-free framework: radii are measured as r̂ = r/λC , frequencies in Fq , currents normalized by curl, and field/currents constrained by the ledger Au · curl = Fq 2 λC 2 . We prove that the linear polarization from ν coaxial return-current shells satisfies a polarization-cancellation invariant Π(ν) with an O(1/ν) bound under a mild unimodal slow-variation hypothesis. We then present a QMU-only data pipeline for Orion A: shell counting, κα estimation from Bessel zeros, and a compact ledger test for the force-free state. We emphasize Clarage-inspired diagnostics (polarization cancellation, Doppler antisymmetry, species segregation) and show how they embed naturally in QMU ledgers.
