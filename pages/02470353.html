EXPERTS ACCUSED OF DECEPTION. They Promptly Force the Editor of the Electric Railway Gazette to Retract. TELEGRAPH WIRES BRING THE POWER It Is Made Small in Quantity to Avert Injury to Western Union Property. The greatest object of scientific interest in the electric show has been the long-distance transmission of power from Niagara Falls to operate the model of the power plant and several small motors. In the last issue of the Electric Railway Gazette the charge was made that this transmission was not fairly accomplished and that the public had been deceived. Figures were given alleging to prove the statement. The appearance of this article created great excitement among the electricians concerned. T. C. Martin, editor of the Electrical Engineer, says: “The Niagara power transmission to the exposition building in this city is not only an actual fact, but I take pride in having suggested it and in having assisted in carrying it out. The idea had been in my mind for nearly a year, and was generally ridiculed except by Mr. Tesla, who encouraged me to go ahead and offered to contribute $500 towards the expense. “I asked Gen.
