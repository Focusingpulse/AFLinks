Syracuse Telegram January 10th, 1901 Page number(s): 1 This Message Nicola Tesla Is Preparing to Send to the Men and Women Up There Soon. New York, Jan. 10. — An attempt to open communication between the world and Mars is to be made from this city. The transmitter and receiver for this great interplanetary wireless telegraph is today being completed in the laboratory of Nikola Tesla, the great electrical expert, at No. 47 Houston street. “I am preparing,” said Mr. Tesla yesterday, “for the greatest experiment that has ever been made on this planet. Sooner or later I shall establish communication with Mars. “I am certain that the inhabitants of that planet have many times endeavored to signal this earth, but we have never had instruments of sufficient delicacy to receive their messages, nor instruments powerful enough to answer them. “At last we have the former, and I will soon have the latter. It will remain then only to decipher their messages and answer them. “It is more than possible that the Martians will read our messages to them before we can read theirs. I believe they are a more advanced people than we are.” Expects Results Soon. “Already I have a transmitter that is, I am certain, of sufficient power to send a message to Mars with strength enough to operate a delicate telegraph instrument.
