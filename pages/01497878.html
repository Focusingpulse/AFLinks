And there has been a sort of groundswell, all over the whole world, of new information. And during the course of the last five years or so, I've slowly become convinced, from my original skepticism, to 99% certainty that it is for real. When the Wright brothers first flew in 1903, no papers covered it at all, because everybody was convinced—certainly the American press—that heavier-than-air flight was totally impossible. All the top scientists said, "This is nonsense."
