WOODWARD — BRANDOW. — On August 16, BESSIE VAN WIE BRANDOW, New York city, to FRANCKE LEONARD WOODWARD, of Albany, N. Y., by the Rev. George S. Avery. This notice, which appears in to-day’s HERALD, tells of the happy romance of two young persons who tired of waiting to overcome such slight objections to their union as family objections and business considerations. All persons having forgiveness to extend, are requested to call at the Hotel Manhattan, where Mr. and Mrs. Woodward are staying. Mrs. Woodward lived at the home of her father, L. A. Brandow, of No. 102 West 134th street, up to three weeks ago.
