A thermionic converter functions by heating a metal to a temperature where it emits charged particles, which are collected by an collector plate. The technology is not new and has been known since Edison was working on incandescent light bulbs. Research into the devices became popular between the 1950's and 1970's as a means to provide electric power for spaceships and satellites.
