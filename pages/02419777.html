Lecture III — GA 93a. The Foundations of Esotericism (1982)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 28 September 1905 · archived # 2,419,777
, Berlin Translated by Vera and Judith Compton-Burnett Lecture III [ 1 ] There are three elements in evolution which must be differentiated: form, life and consciousness. Today we will speak about the different kinds of consciousness. [ 2 ] We can regard plants and lower animals as the means whereby higher beings extend their senses into the world in order to behold this world through them. Let us take our start from the sense organs of the plants. When we speak of these we must be clear that we are not only dealing with the sense organs of the single plants, but with beings in higher worlds.
