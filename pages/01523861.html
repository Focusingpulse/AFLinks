About LIVE: Saturday Science Chats Summary David de Hilster hosted in Franklin Hu 's absence, with Ian Cohen, Harry Ricker , Bob Gray and Carl Rife. The greater part of it is Ian's account of how he came to the subject, which is the most complete the series has recorded, followed by Ricker on unipolar induction and de Hilster on where the society might go.
