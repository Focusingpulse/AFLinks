Joseph R. Burwell Residence Austin, TX, United States Nationality USA Scientific career Fields Patent Attorney; Electrical Engineer Joseph R. Burwell is an American patent attorney and electrical engineer based in Austin, Texas. In addition to his professional work in intellectual property law, he has taken an interest in foundational questions of theoretical physics, contributing ideas on gravity, the aether, relativity, cosmology and unified field theory. Biography Burwell trained as an electrical engineer before entering the legal profession. He earned a Juris Doctor from the University of Houston Law Center in 1993 and was admitted to the State Bar of Texas the same year. He is registered to practice before the United States Patent and Trademark Office. Through the Law Office of Joseph R. Burwell in Austin, Texas, his practice focuses on intellectual property, including electrical and electronic patent prosecution, computer and software patents, and trademark and copyright matters. Work Burwell's interests in natural philosophy span a range of foundational topics in physics, including gravity, the aether, relativity, electrodynamics, cosmology, the electric universe, particle physics and unified theory. His engineering background informs an approach that emphasizes physical and mechanistic descriptions of these phenomena.
