M th O Wi D-R C EE FR Lloyd’s Maritime Atlas 23rd Edition Featuring full colour world, ocean and regional maps the Lloyd’s Maritime Atlas 23rd Edition is the definitive guide to the location of the world’s ports and shipping places. Lloyd’s Maritime Atlas 23rd Edition includes: • FREE Electronic CD-ROM version • Global Maritime Statistics Section • World Distance Tables • Weather Hazards at Sea • International Load Line Zones • Geographic Port Index • Alphabetical Index Commercial ports are shown in a clear and concise manner with particular attention being paid to the busiest shipping areas, where larger scale, more detailed maps are used. For more detailed information on individual ports, Lloyd’s Maritime Atlas 23rd Edition is fully crossreferenced with its sister publication, Lloyd’s Ports of the World. Lloyd’s Maritime Atlas 23rd Edition Each full colour regional map features distance tables and ports in bold type which are located at the front of the Atlas.These accurate figures have been extracted from the BP Marine Distance Tables and are quoted in nautical miles. Each map also details major road, rail links and airports serving a port or harbour.
