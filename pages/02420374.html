We have him with us today, from his head office in Foster City, California. Welcome to Radio Ecoshock. [George] Hi there. [Alex] Hi. Well Russ, can you give us a little bit of your background - how you got interested in environmental solutions? Subsequent to that, I've been working on environmental managment projects, for both government and industry, both in Canada and the U.S., and around the world.
