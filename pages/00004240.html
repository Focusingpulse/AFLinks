Chem 302L / Su07 The Synthesis of Lidocaine Introduction Lidocaine (trade names Lidothesin, Xylocain, Anestacon) is an important member of Class 1b drugs used as local anesthetics. Procaine, Isocaine, Procainamide, Tocainide, and Mexiletine are other members of this family of compounds. All of them have an amine functional group in common, which appears to be essential for enzyme binding.
