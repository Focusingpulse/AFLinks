MARCH 1994 The University of Utah officials called a press conference on March 23, 1989 where Drs. Stanley Pons and Martin Fleischmann presented their experimental results which became labeled as cold fusion. One year later at the time ofthe First Annual Conference of Cold Fusion (University Park Hotel, Salt Lake City, Utah, March, 1990) officers of the American Physical Society labelled the conference as a wake held by faithful believers around a twitching corpse. It is March, 1994, and the initial discovery by Pons and Fleischmann has progressed to where the 2 kilowatts per cubic centimeter heat output from a palladium cathode rivals the heat output from a similar volume of any other power source. Although the heat-producing processes are not fully understood, the devices that produce excess heat have proliferated.
