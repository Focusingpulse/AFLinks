Lindemann advancing a single thesis: that the "cold electricity" demonstrated by the California inventor Edwin V. Gray in the early 1970s and the radiant energy discovered by Nikola Tesla in the late 1880s are one and the same phenomenon, and that Gray's patents can be read as a working implementation of it.
