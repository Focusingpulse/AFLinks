It answers a question from David de Hilster as president of the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society : how Borchardt first came to think the universe infinite. He describes fourteen years from 1962 on laboratory and field work in soil science, geology and chemistry, an insistence on logic and a dislike of contradiction, and general-science reading that included an article daring to suggest space was not perfectly empty.
