A few days later, a motion to promote ecoagriculture appeared on the agenda of the upcoming 3 rd IUCN (World Conservation Union) World Conservation Congress in Bangkok, Thailand, (17-25 November 2004). Angry critics had described ecoagriculture as “an organic agriculture that is very friendly to agribusiness”. A protest letter from civil society participants at a recent ecoagriculture conference organised by IUCN in Nairobi maintained that, “ecoagriculture is fundamentally incompatible with food sovereignty” and hence unacceptable. Suddenly, it seems, agribusiness is taking over ‘sustainable agriculture' in a big way.
