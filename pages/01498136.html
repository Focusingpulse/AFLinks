The palladium cathodes were charged in an elec￾trolytic cell[0.1 MLiOD (99.8% deuterium), platinum anode] at a current density of 25 mA/cm2 from 20 to 140 h. One unique aspect of the experiment was the radiation detection system, consisting of a CR-39 track-etch detector, bare for proton detection (sensitivity limit 4.8 x 10~2 fusion/s), com￾bined with a polyethylene fast neutron radiator (0.95 fusion /s), a boron thermal neutron radiator (26 fusion/s), a BD-100 bubble damage polymer detector (5.2 fusion/s), an array of six 3He proportional counters (126 fusion /s), a CaF2 ther￾moluminescent dosimeter (11.4 fusion/s), and a germanium semiconductor spectrometer (17 fusion/s). The D-D fusion rate in cast, annealed, and cold-rolled palladium is <3 x 10'22 , <7.8 x 10~21 , and <1.2 x 10~21 (D-Dn) fusion/D-D pair s'1 , respectively.
