GC2026-PERM018 Magnetic state of iron oxides in synthetic analogs of Zhamanshin impact glasses: Influence of precursor composition and quenching regime Elena Sergienko1,2 , Danil Dubeshko1 , Petr Kharitonskii3 , Kamil Gareev3,2 , Svetlana Yanson1 , Ivan Levitskiy4 , Zhavokhir Shermatov5 , Valeriy Kozlov6 1 St Petersburg University 2 Ioffe Institute of the Russian Academy of Sciences 3 St. Petersburg Electrotechnical University "LETI" 4 Belarusian State Technological University 5 Institute of Materials Science, "Physics-Sun", Uzbekistan 6 NRC «Kurchatov Institute» – PNPI e.sergienko@spbu.ru A series of synthetic silicate glasses produced from target rocks of the Zhamanshin impact crater was investigated as physical analogs for natural impact glasses. The study aims to establish the dependence of the valence￾coordination and magnetic states of iron on the starting charge composition, melting temperature, and cooling regime, as well as to evaluate the fidelity of these analogs in replicating the properties of natural impactites. The synthesis was performed across a wide range of chemical compositions using two types of facilities: a gas-flame furnace (~ 1500°C) and a solar furnace (~ 2500°C). By varying heating rates, isothermal holding times, and quenching regimes, we simulated distinct stages of the impact event—ranging from melting within the central zone to the formation of aerodynamic bodies in the ejecta plume.
