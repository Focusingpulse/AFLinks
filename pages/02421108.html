Giuseppe Pezzella Residence Napoli, Italy Nationality Italian Alma mater University of Naples "Federico II" Known for Aerothermodynamics of re-entry and hypersonic vehicles Scientific career Fields Aerospace engineering , aerodynamics, aerothermodynamics Institutions University of Campania "Luigi Vanvitelli"; Italian Aerospace Research Centre (CIRA) Giuseppe Pezzella is an Italian aerospace engineer specializing in the aerodynamics and aerothermodynamics of atmospheric re-entry and hypersonic flight vehicles. He is a lecturer in aircraft aerodynamics and aerothermodynamics at the Department of Engineering of the University of Campania "Luigi Vanvitelli", and was previously a senior research engineer at the Italian Aerospace Research Centre (CIRA). Education Master's degree in aerospace engineering — University of Naples "Federico II", 1999 Ph.D. in aerospace engineering — University of Naples "Federico II", 2004 Biography Before his academic appointment, he worked as a senior research engineer at the Italian Aerospace Research Centre (CIRA), where he specialized in hypersonic aerodynamics and aerothermodynamics. He later joined the Department of Engineering of the University of Campania "Luigi Vanvitelli" in Caserta as a lecturer in aircraft aerodynamics and aerothermodynamics. Work Pezzella's research addresses the configuration design, aerodynamics, and aerothermodynamics of vehicles that fly at hypersonic speeds or return from space through a planetary atmosphere. His work applies computational fluid dynamics to the aerodynamic and aerothermodynamic performance analysis of reusable and unmanned re-entry vehicles, spacecraft returning from low Earth orbit, launcher configurations, and planetary entry vehicles for Mars exploration.
