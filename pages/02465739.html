Mark of the axes for a three-axle ellipsoid (the blue cartesian co-ordinate system with λ 0 = -13,5 degree of west) and registration of all magnetic extreme values found up to now produces both pictures 10.1 and 10.2. The colours in it signify: Green stands for the basic field ZS, Magenta for the tesseral field and orange for the appearing extreme values of the whole field from chapter 2.6 .
