Next message: Dr Jones: "Re: [OFF TOPIC] Economics of FE/Plan of attack!" Hi all A few months ago here 20/20 showed a piece about heart transplants, based on several studies. Apparently, (according to at least the central character, herself a heart transplant patient), donees begin to take on some of the tastes and behaviour of the donors, although this effect wears off over 10 years or more. In the woman on the interviewed, she received a young bikies heart, and afterwards she began to behave a little more aggressively, pick up a liking for beer and nuts etc, all things that were totally out of character. Anyway, she got in touch witrh the donors family and they confirmed that these new tastes she had were very much in line with the tastes of their deceased son. I haven't found anything more about this on the Net as yet; however now you know. Perhaps this info will be useful to someone.
