We will name these forces male and female , or positive and negative , or genero-active and radio-active , or Electricity and magnetism , or gravitation and radiation , or attraction and repulsion , or life and death , or composing and decomposing, or generation and degeneration , or integrating and disintegrating, or cold and heat , or any other names of opposites one can think of. One cannot think of any two opposite names which could not appropriately be added in these two columns. For the second step, one must consider that the One Force had a purpose in so dividing itself other than just to create a mechanistic universe. To avoid long philosophical and scientific discussions, let us describe this purpose as the idea of a Creative Force desiring to express the images of ideas thought out by it into the forms of those ideas.
