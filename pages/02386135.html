These parts have the respective names * CNF-BKS * CNF-PAP1, CNF-PAP2, ... (this needed to be sliced up for size reasons) * CNF-PAT * CNF-CMNT * CNF-PERI * CNF-UNPB and a further file, CNF-BRIF, which is CNF-PAP but without the annotations, and is archived in a single file. In most cases, the customary publication citation system is followed, i.e. author(s), journal name, volume, year, page number and title, if any. Unless otherwise stated, the papers are in English. Some comments are appended to each citation; these are the compiler's and may reflect a given personal interest.
