2. Spiritual Science as Preparation for the New Etheric Vision — GA 118. The Advent of Christ in the Ethereal World

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 27 January 1910 · archived # 2,422,070
, Heidelberg Translated by Steiner Online Library 2. Spiritual Science as Preparation for the New Etheric Vision [ 1 ] We would make little progress in our branch lectures if we did not occasionally speak about the more intimate processes of humanity’s spiritual life. What we are to acquire in our branches is, after all, a preparation for grasping higher spiritual truths, and in this preparation we must not think merely of learning theories or ideas; rather, what we call preparation for the higher truths should actually consist more in a certain emotional and spiritual disposition of our soul.
