Terrestrial and Celestial Poles ( click to enlarge ) " The One begat the Two which begat the Three and from the Three all things are made. " [ Lao Tse , 'The Tao te Ching'] Tao Te Ching - Lao Tzu - chapter 42 The two initial states being 1) to separate or be individual and 2) be Unified or One are nothing more than tendencies or desires . The first, entropy , tends in a direction directly away from the center. The second, syntropy , tends to a direction towards the center. Ideally the two opposing become each other intermittently. Since there are only two states they are initially in perfect equilibrium one against the other. There is no motion or substance with the desire and tendency to direction and motion held latent . In this state of perfect latency there is pure potential waiting to be transformed into motion and matter by introducing idea ( desire ). Desire and all other mental forces are of the interetheric and compound interetheric subdivisions . Therefore what we call desire is actually one of the currents of the sympathetic streams .
