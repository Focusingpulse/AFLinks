Hypersphere World-Universe Model, Part 1: Medium of the World Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Speaker Vladimir S Netchitailo Date 16 March 2024 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording Hypersphere World-Universe Model, Part 1: Medium of the World was a live session presented by Vladimir S Netchitailo on 16 March 2024 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. World-Universe Model (WUM) addresses some known problems with the currently accepted cosmological model and is supported by empirical data from a variety of sources, and both those topics will be presented as the series of Presentations progresses. In Part 1, we discuss the most important issues in Classical Physics; give principal points of the developed WUM; introduce the Medium of the World, consisting of protons, electrons, photons, neutrinos, and dark matter particles; calculate parameters of Intergalactic Plasma, Cosmic Microwave Background Radiation, Far-Infrared Background Radiation, which are in good agreement with the latest experimental results; explain Hubble Tension problem. Summary The first of Vladimir S Netchitailo 's eight-part series on the World-Universe Model, negotiated with the society as a sustained exposition rather than a single talk. Ian J Cowan hosted and introduced the plan: parts one and two in succession, a break for other topics, then the remaining six, ending on 6 July. Netchitailo read a prepared text and displayed it on screen throughout, at Nick Percival 's suggestion, so that the audience could follow the wording as well as the argument. He introduced himself: a master's degree from the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology, a PhD in quantum electronics from the Lebedev Physical Institute, a doctorate in laser physics from the Institute of General Physics, and over 200 papers, forty of them with Alexander Prokhorov, the 1964 Nobel laureate.
