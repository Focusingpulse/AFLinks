The X-cohomology A.Balan October 5, 2018 Abstract With help of a closed form over the manifold, a X-cohomology of the manifold is defined 1 Definition We consider a closed 1-form X over the manifold M; dX = 0. Then we can define: dX(α) = d(α) + X ∧ α It is easy to verify that: Theorem 1 dX ◦ dX = 0 Demonstration 1 Indeed: d(dα + X ∧ α) + X ∧ (dα + X ∧ α) = 0 So, we can have a X-cohomology of the manifold M: Definition 1 XH∗ (M, R) = Ker(dX)/Im(dX) 2 The X-cohomology as a module Theorem 2 The X-cohomology XH∗ (M, R is a module over the coho￾mology of de Rahm viewed as an algebra, H∗ (M, R).
