KEYWORDS: Knots, Mapping, Metric, Proteins, Folding, Genetic, Aging, AI, Pharmaceutical. Method Mathematical description of knot in this approach presents a list of symbols (i.e. metric), each of which corresponds to a certain opening in a hollow scaffolding used to construct the knot. As a proof of concept, rigid 5 x 5 x 5 bamboo-straw￾made cubic scaffolding was used to develop the knot building approach (Fig. 1). Sequence of symbols in metric exactly follows the sequence of openings a body of knot (i.e. strand, rope, …) should go through in the scaffolding in order to construct the knot. Openings of the scaffolding should have been conventionally labeled. As an example, a following labeling system may be used for cubic scaffolding. Each opening of the scaffolding cube is labeled as i j x where x is one of the letters: a, b, c, d, f , or g, depending on which facet of the cube an opening belongs to. Upper index i denotes the row and the lower index j the column. 2 For example, label 1 3 g stands for the opening in the first row and third column of G - facet. Facets are labeled A, B, C, D, F, G in such a way as openings 1 1a then 1 1b then 1 1 c then 1 1 d then 1 1f then 1 1g follow each other clockwise, being viewed from one of the vertices along its space diagonal (Fig. 2). The method is best explained with mapping a simple overhand knot [1].
