Large Outdoor Fireplace Outdoor fireplaces can be decorative and functional additions to your home. Building an outdoor fireplace is simpler and safer than building an indoor fireplace. Choice of materials for the construction of the fireplace can be varied , in this case it is the brick but you can use other materials such as concrete , stone and other fire-resistant materials for masonry. Fireplace interior is made of chamotte bricks but can be made from larger chamotte plates ,it is essential that the material contained within the combustion chamber is resistant to high temperatures. In this book you will find some detailed plans for large outdoor fireplace.
