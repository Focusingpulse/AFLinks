However, like or unlike magnetic poles become attractive when their frequency ratios become 33 1/3 of one against 100 of the other, simply by sympathetic resonant vibration ." If a differentiation of 33 1/3 against 100 is established between them, whether like or unlike, they become attractive to each other. They become repellent after differentiating them, 66 2/3 of the one against 100 of the other, by sympathetic vibration .
