Cosmology Corrected, A Commentary With Thermodynamics Book Author Bernard R Bligh Published 2000 Publisher Bernard Ramsay Bligh Pages 112 ISBN 0953877604 The Big Bang Exploded is essential reading for all cosmologists and their students because existing textbooks on Cosmology and Astrophysics contain numerous errors in Thermodynamics and this book aims to correct these mistakes. It will also be of considerable value to students and lecturers in all science and technology, conveying "a feel for Thermodynamics". The Hot Big Bang Theory postulates that the early Universe was at a very high temperature, and that the expansion of the Universe automatically brought about cooling from high to low temperatures.
