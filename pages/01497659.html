How does one quantify this scientifically, when random process disturbances and random mea￾surement noises preclude perfect linearity? This question is answered under the assumption of Gaussian (or ”normal”) departures from ideal linearity regarding the two mentioned statistical aspects. This is a generalization of the protocol proposed by Bass [1], which is more realistically flexible in several respects. An arbitrary number N ≥ 3 of similarly prepared samples is allowed, and neither the voltage nor the current is required to be constant. However, the previous protocol may be recovered as a special case when N = 5. 1.
