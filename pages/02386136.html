About the Fusion Archives (This page is under contruction.) Newsgroup Archives The "sci.physics.fusion" newsgroup continued the "alt.fusion" newsgroup, which was formed in the spring of 1989 after Pons & Fleischmann's astounding announcement of electrolytically induced "cold fusion". Thanks primarily to the efforts of Scott Hazen Mueller, virtually all the traffic on this group has been captured and archived for ftp access at vm1.nodak.edu in the directory FUSION. The process is handled by a LISTSERV at that site. Filenames in this directory are of the form FUSION-YY.NNNNN where YY is the year (e.g. 92 ) and NNNNN is a sequentially-assigned number. Each file consists of a "digest" consisting of (typically) a dozen or so postings. Be warned that a listing of the FUSION directory is quite long, and it may not be simple to locate a particular posting. The same information (archives of the newsgroup traffic) is maintained for wais access at sunsite.unc.edu. Keywords may be used to search the entire archive. At present the search engine does not support boolean operators, so precision searching may be difficult. Hints: Under swais, you may wish to use the "O" (options) command and select widetitles on and increase the number of allowed results ( maxitem ) beyond the default of 40.
