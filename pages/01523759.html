It is shown that relativity predicts a variable G; this provides a solution to the gravitational mysteries of today in astrophysics and in cosmology, as well as to the Yang-Mills Millennium problem. Hosted by Ian Cowan. Summary The foundational session of Frédéric Lassiaille 's three appearances during 2025, and the one that presents the derivation rather than its consequences. Where the later talks show what the surrounding model does, this one argues that it follows from relativity itself. The session was hosted by David de Hilster , whose questioning was more direct than the society's usual. Lassiaille described the status of the work in one sentence: a chapter is missing from relativity; add it, and no gravitational mystery remains. Add a further unifying assumption based on gravity and the Yang–Mills millennium problem — the confinement of the strong force — is solved, along with several old puzzles of nuclear physics. He noted that when he presented this in Bulgaria two years earlier the argument depended on the relativistic speed of quarks; it no longer does, resting now on theoretical grounds alone. Why is there no corresponding relation using the first derivative? The demonstration comes in six parts, preceded by a prerequisite.
