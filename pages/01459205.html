Figure 15.06 - Mind Centered Thought Rings of Motion courtesy University of Science and Philosophy ( click to enlarge ) A MIND CENTERED THOUGHT-RING OF MOTION Fig. 48 is a section of a body cell, magnified millions of diameters, showing relation of Mind to body. All matter is composed of thought-rings of motion compressed together in spherical cells, atoms, planets and suns. Thought-rings are what we call our senses. They are Mind-centered and Mind controlled through electrical messages sent to them from within, and are received as instinct, intuition, inspiration or other states of intelligence in accord with the ability of a body to recognize the nature of the message thus received. Man alone has conscious awareness of the divine nature of the messages, but man must be far on his way to spiritual unfolding before he is fully aware that they are directly from the Creator to his Self. These centers in the omnipresent universe of Mind, constitute the "kingdom of heaven" which Jesus bade all men to seek.
