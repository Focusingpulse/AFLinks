THE VIBRATION SERIES A Reader’s Guide to Volumes 1–75 An Index and Road Map to the Continuing Investigation of Sympathetic Vibratory Physics The Vibration Series is a continuing investigation into vibration, resonance, harmony, sympathetic association, scalar potential, Neutral Centers, consciousness, matter, energy, and the largely forgotten work of John Worrell Keely and other investigators of Nature’s underlying causes. Although each volume may be read independently, the Series has developed progressively. Early volumes establish the foundational concepts and explore their possible applications; subsequent volumes move increasingly toward the distinction between cause and effect, scalar and kinetic, condition and manifestation; and the most recent volumes return directly to Keely’s original writings and theoretical charts in an attempt to reconstruct what he was actually describing. The Vibration Series Page 1 of 17 ©Dale Pond, 2026 This guide is intended both for new readers deciding where to begin and for established students who need a quick reference to the subjects contained throughout the growing collection. Volume 2 — Applied Vibratory Power and Zero Point Activation Moves from theory toward application by exploring vibratory systems, latent or zero-point potential, energetic feedback, biofields, coherence, healing concepts, and possible methods of detecting and utilizing sympathetic effects. Volume 3 — Societal Integration & Civilization Design Asks what a civilization consciously organized around resonance, sympathetic relationship, scalar principles, education, and cooperation with Nature might look like.
