Balancing Emptiness

· archived # 2,432,681
I was thinking about this yesterday. I thought about how a feeling of emptiness and unfulfillment can be more about others' lack of ability to receive than anything else. If you view it this way, you can balance emptiness with compassion. Compassion for those who do not consider themselves worthy of receiving your light.
