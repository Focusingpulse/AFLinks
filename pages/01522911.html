Faheem Murtaza Born 1970 (age 55–56) Residence Abbottabad, Pakistan Nationality Pakistani Known for gravity Scientific career Fields Lawyer , Mathematician Faheem Murtaza (born 1970) is a Pakistani lawyer and independent researcher on the unification of the fundamental interactions, based in Abbottabad, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. He holds a Master of Science in mathematics and an LL.B., and practises law; as of 2011 he had been working on unification for some eighteen years. Work Murtaza presented two papers to Natural Philosophy Alliance conferences, Quantum Gravity at the sixteenth meeting in 2009 and The Quantum Law of Gravity at the eighteenth in 2011, the latter published in the Proceedings of the NPA . Both pursue a single programme: a proposed relation between a particle's momentum, its gravitational acceleration and a mass differential, from which he argues that Newtonian gravity, general-relativistic effects and quantum behaviour all follow as special cases. The central move is to identify an "induced charge" carried by any orbiting body in proportion to its angular momentum, q = K mvr/M with K = c/√(kG), so that gravitational attraction becomes a magnetic force between induced charges and gravity and electromagnetism are unified. Subsidiary results include a proportionality between gravitational acceleration and magnetic flux density, a formula giving orbital inclination from the mass ratio of a planet to its primary, a relation gt = c between gravity and time, and an identification of the Planck time with the ratio of a particle's radius times a mass differential to its momentum. The 2011 paper is notable for a publication accident rather than its content: the Proceedings version appeared with the reviewer's queries and the unfilled section headings of the submission template still embedded in the running text.
