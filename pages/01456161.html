CRISPR TECHNOLOGIES - The Upgraded GMO's. The FDA has approved a vast number of synthesized food ingredients and additives, rather than entire "synthesized foods" as a single category. These ingredients are used in countless processed food products available in grocery stores today. Specific examples of FDA-approved synthesized or novel ingredients include: Sweeteners and Flavor Enhancers Acesulfame potassium (Acesulfame-K): A non-nutritive, synthetic sweetener used in beverages, baked goods, and other processed foods. Aspartame: Another common synthetic sweetener used in many diet and sugar-free products. Monosodium Glutamate (MSG): A flavor enhancer that is "Generally Recognized as Safe" (GRAS) by the FDA. Artificial and Natural Flavorings: A wide array of synthetic and natural chemicals are approved for use as flavor agents, such as acetaldehyde and ethyl acetate. Color Additives FD&C Blue Nos. 1 and 2, Red Nos. 3 and 40, Yellow Nos. 5 and 6: These are synthetic color additives that require FDA certification for use in foods like candies, snack foods, and beverages. Galdieria extract blue and Butterfly pea flower extract: Newer color additives derived from unicellular algae and plants, approved for use in specific products like beverages and cereal coatings.
