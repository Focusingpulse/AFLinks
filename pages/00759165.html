Sampling from the Maxwell-Juttner distribution Luc Devroye School of Computer Science McGill University May 25, 2025 Abstract. The Maxwell-J¨uttner distribution pertains to the speeds of particles in a hypothetical gas of relativistic particles. We consider the generalized form of this law in R d , and show how to generate a ran￾dom variate from this distribution. In particular, one can sample the radius of the d-dimensional random vector in expected time uniformly bounded over all dimensions d and shape parameters (temperatures). Keywords and phrases.
