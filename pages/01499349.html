NEW ENERGY NEWS TO GO E-MAIL By Hal Fox, Editor-in-Chief, New Energy News Starting in December of 1999, the monthly issues of the New Energy News will be immediately published in an electronic format and available over the INTERNET by sending E-mail letters directly to subscribers. Subscribers without any E-mail potential will be sent laser printed copies until their subscription expires. We will send each subscriber an E-mail letter at least once a month and more often if the New Energy News warrants a special emailing. We urge all members to provide us with their E-mail address. However, laser printed copies will be available.
