Title Can Physics Help to Verify Other Evidence of Reincarnation? Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Robert F Beck Keywords Reincarnation, DNA, Information (encoding & transmission), Plasma, Consciousness Published 2012 No. of pages 7 Abstract Evidence has recently come to my attention in support of the possibility of reincarnation, including serious, long term, extensive research, by someone clearly respected in his field. This paper sets out this evidence and looks to physics contained in my recent papers and other studies for any further justification of the possibility. Death is a natural phenomenon; birth is a natural phenomenon. Scientific evidence strong enough to suggest a link needs to be investigated via further science, rather than by categorizing the subject via the unhelpful term, ?paranormal', the relevance of which is discussed, or worse still, by allowing religious belief to influence such categorization. Evidence is presented to suggest that in some cases, what has been termed ?paranormal' might be explained by simple physical processes at the smallest level. Overview This paper asks whether the physical model the author set out in The True Meaning of Einstein's Relativity — matter built from rings and spirals of tiny particles — can supply a mechanism for reincarnation, and more broadly for phenomena usually filed as paranormal. Its opening move is definitional. The author quotes a dictionary: "paranormal" means beyond the range of normal experience or scientific explanation, not in accordance with scientific laws, while "normal" means in accordance with them. It follows, he argues, that if a phenomenon can be explained by scientific evidence which also shows the laws of science to be incomplete, it should no longer be classed as paranormal at all. The paper's purpose is to argue that both conditions are now met for reincarnation.
