TO ASK FOR PERMISSION Marconi’s Motto Is, Time Is Everything “I cannot remain idle. Ever since I was a child I have had this feeling. Time means everything. If you cannot do a thing here do it elsewhere. An hour saved is an hour gained, and in that hour gained may be accomplished the one thing you have been striving for.”—Mr. Marconi to HERALD correspondent at Newfoundland. ST. JOHNS, N. F., Tuesday. — In the face of express protest by the Anglo-American Telegraph Company, Governor Sir Cavendish Boyle, Premier Bond and members of the Executive Council went to Signal Hill to-day to witness proposed experiments by Mr. Marconi in receiving communications from England. The superintendent of the telegraph company visited the Governor this morning to protest against the intended official visit, inasmuch as his company had taken legal steps against the inventor. The company’s representative asserted that the government should not identify itself with either party. The officials decided, however, that the scientific value of the Marconi tests warranted their recognising him, and accordingly went.
