· archived # 1,483,181
Ramsay Here, then, we have an order of modes entirely symmetical in pairs placed thus; the only mode that can stand alone being the Dorian , built on D, whose duality has been discovered to reside in itself. All this build of symmetry , which was the watchword of Greek art, as it is also one of the watchwords of Nature , presupposes that the tones of the scale , with lesser and larger intervals lying between them, were resting in their ears exactly as they are in ours, 1 and as they are in all humanity, save where it has sunk down into the savage condition , benighted in the evil that is in the world.
