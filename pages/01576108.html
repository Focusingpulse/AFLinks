Adopting the single-wavelength, full-twist double-loop topology of Williamson and van der Mark, this paper proposes a direct physical account of electron–positron annihilation. When the two mirror configurations overlap, their opposite winding signatures cancel and the confinement topology unbinds. The 511 keV result is a consistency result using empirical electron energy; the distinct EMEO claim is the finite-volume topological mechanism. Polarization, angular correlations, scattering structure and magnetic moment are identified as routes toward discriminating tests.
