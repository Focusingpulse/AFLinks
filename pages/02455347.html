My "understanding" of CCD light sensors is that there is a matrix of little capacitors that are sequentially charged and read repeatedly, much the same as dynamic "ram" is. I am having a problem with the idea of "various duration exposures". These little capacitors can only hold their charge for small fraction of a second and consequently the "read-and-recharge" rate can only be slowed down to some minimum rate. I think the difference between "normal" factory preset exposure and the maximum exposure is only a matter of microseconds? milliseconds? Then, of course, which brand and model of CCD allows external timing control?? I want one.
