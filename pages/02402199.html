GTM2026-main059 A Multi-stage Venture Investment Model with Incomplete Information Suriya Kumacheva1 1 St Petersburg University skumach@gmail.com In the current literature on mathematical modeling of economic issues, risk management has become an important topic. In the context of resource-like risks, it is generally accepted that additional benefits can be gained by taking on more risk, but only up to a certain point. This is because management is focused on balancing the costs of managing risk and the potential losses that could be avoided. Venture capital investments, for example, are a type of resource-like risk that differ from traditional investments in terms of their lower probability of return but potentially higher potential returns. In [9], the venture investment problem was modeled as a Bayesian game with incomplete information. A Bayesian￾Nash equilibrium was found for a given prior distribution of market conditions, and constraints on the model parameters were obtained, including the investor's share in the project and interest rate. The current study proposes the development of a new model. A multi-stage approach to the game has been adopted, where investments in the project are made at different stages. Key parameters are clarified from one stage to the next. This includes the development of a Bayesian procedure, which involves recalculating posterior probabilities of market conditions and estimating the payback period based on data from the first stage, as well as adjusting the discount rate based on the actual profits of the project.
