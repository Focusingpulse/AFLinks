Global Breakthrough Energy Movement Conference report

· archived # 2,464,247
The new issue of Infinite Energy Magazine has an article by Jeanne Manning on the Global Breakthrough Energy Movement conference held this past November 9-11 in Hilversum, Holland. Issue #107 for January/February 2013 can be purchased from Infinite Energy at http://www.infinite-energy.com/ . Manning’s article Global Breakthrough Energy Movement Conference can be accessed compliments of Infinite Energy Magazine here . The conference brought together new energy advocates, entrepreneurs and researchers to discuss the current state of the field, which included cold fusion. Gobal Breakthrough Energy Movement Conference 2012 Group Photo on Thursday evening of the Conference An update on GlobalBEM developments was sent out by Jeroen van Straaten , one of the organizers of the conference. A new website called Globalbemvoices will be launched January 2013 and will focus on all Globalbem related media things like a new Globalbem podcast show, content from the conference, Globalbem magazine, Globalbem soundcload, Globalbem books and much more Our conference beneficiaries offered 5 people from the Globalbem team to be full paid for the time period of three months to get things going on a more professional bases. The team members selected for that for now are Robert Dupper, Michael Chichi, Barry Visser, Madeline Villar and myself. In this period we hope with the rest of the Globalbem team to find more funding to expand the team and be a self sustaining foundation. This last week we as Globalbem had an opportunity to be part of the annual board meeting of the New Energy Movement to see if we can join forces . This was a very positive meeting and progress was made…to be continued. The presentations from the conference will be online for all of you to be viewed for free this January 2013, sorry for the delay.
