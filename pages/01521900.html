Published 2011 No. of pages 12 Read the full paper here Abstract The goal of this work is to demonstrate the importance of length of a pendulum with movable pivot point on its energetic efficiency and power. Maximal possible over unity quotient will be determined under some conditions. Influence of critical angle (angle when pivot point start to move) on energy of the pendulum will also be shown.
