Published 2011 No. of pages 12 Abstract The goal of this work is to demonstrate the importance of length of a pendulum with movable pivot point on its energetic efficiency and power. Maximal possible over unity quotient will be determined under some conditions. Influence of critical angle (angle when pivot point start to move) on energy of the pendulum will also be shown. Overview The Secret of Free Energy from the Pendulum is a twelve-page paper by Jovan Marjanovic , written at the Veljko Milković Research and Development Centre in Novi Sad, Serbia, and dated 5 May 2011. It analyses the two-stage mechanical oscillator associated with Veljko Milković — a pendulum whose pivot point is mounted on one arm of a lever, so that the pendulum's swing drives the lever and the lever drives a load such as a water pump — and argues that the arrangement can deliver more work than it consumes. Marjanovic is explicit that this reverses his own earlier conclusion. His previous paper had found that the law of conservation of energy holds regardless of whether the pendulum's rod is shortened or lengthened, and that a pendulum treated as a parametric oscillator cannot give an energy surplus. The present work he describes as "the author's most positive work, concerning the existence of over unity energy of a pendulum with a movable pivot point, but only under some conditions", and he records that the insight came to him on a tractor while thinking about mechanical analogues of electromagnetic over-unity schemes. The paper's structure is an energy ledger. The pendulum bob of mass M hangs on a rod of length r 0 from a pivot that is free to descend a distance Δ r .
