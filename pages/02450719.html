Internic who controls primary DNS in the U.S. is moving very slowly in getting the DNS name servers changed. When the change is finally done by Internic it will begin propagating out to the DNS servers worldwide. In the mean time everything is in place to effect the move but it is pointless to actually do it until Internic does it's part. At this point I don't know when Internic will update it's database with the changes. As soon as I see the changes start to take place at Internic I will move the server. It could take place any time from now till Monday. I have no way of knowing when, but when it does the server will be off line for only a few hours. However you might see a longer period of down time while the changes propagate to the DNS server used by your ISP. At worst case it could take up to 3 or 4 days once Internic completes it's changes before all the DNS servers on the Internet are updated.
