A world-wide network, it connects governmental and non-governmental organizations and the private sector. INBAR provides leadership, coordination and support for research and development. INBAR’s R&D programs cover natural and cultivated raw materials; genetic resources, processing and utilization; economic and other social aspects; and supporting services. INBAR aims to enhance the quality of life of poor and disadvantaged people in developing countries and to make favourable impacts on forests and degraded environments.
