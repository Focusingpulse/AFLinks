Tartini was a prolific composer, composing over a hundred of pieces for the violin with the majority of them being violin concertos. However, today, he is most famously remembered for his Violin Sonata in G Minor (the Devil's Trill Sonata). Tartini stared; he was cold, his mouth open; time seemed to halt as the devil played. The moment Tartini woke up, he reached desperately for the violin lying by his bed, eager to recreate the tune - in that state in which the worlds of wakefulness and dream mingle, and sleep’s fantastical logic floats above the solid and prosaic.
