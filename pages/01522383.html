They are listed in The Worldwide List of Dissident Scientists . Work Through his laboratory website, Cau presents a self-funded research program that seeks to interpret traditional alchemy using modern scientific tools and data. His stated topics include the synthesis of super-heavy elements, transmutation, and low-energy ("soft") nuclear fusion.
