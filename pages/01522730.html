He is listed in The Worldwide List of Dissident Scientists . Ideas Jonsson is the author of the book Holographic Universe: Law of Attraction & Money (2013), in which he argues that the visible world is a hologram generated by the observer. In his framing, the "real object" is a person's inner thoughts, while the outer, visible world is a reconstructed interference pattern created by the mind. These ideas fall outside the scientific mainstream and are presented by the author in a self-help and metaphysical register rather than as peer-reviewed physics.
