11. An excerpt from the Jewish Haggadah — GA 167. The Present and the Past in the Human Spirit

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 23 May 1909 · archived # 2,423,277
, Berlin Translated by Steiner Online Library 11. An excerpt from the Jewish Haggadah [ 1 ] Last time, we became acquainted with the surprising fact that a large number of those in the West who speak of Christ Jesus as a matter of duty do not believe as much about this Christ Jesus, do not hold as much to be true, as every true believer in Islam, every true adherent of the Qur’an, holds to be true about Jesus. And we have seen that our understanding—which we have just gained regarding the figure of Christ Jesus, whom we acknowledge—can indeed help us in our conception of the Jesus of the Qur’a
