Hi Folks! I know I'm going to regret posting this so soon, but I got the photos back of the Dendera crypt with the Egyptian vacuum tubes...laid them out on a table and they are a good 2 feet across!!! I wish now I'd taken a couple more of the connection to the djet. But they are quite good. Now the question, how best to present these photos?? I had them done in 5" X 6" format so they would be as clear as possible. Does anyone have any ideas on how to do this? I can probably scan them all in and reduce the combined image to one that will fit across an html screen, then include a higher resolution, larger version that is zipped for download if people want the full thing. I have to get some new software as my old computer had picture publisher and another program that I used for many images, now the drive is squirrely and those programs no longer run...so have to do some hunting for software. What I need is to know how best to do these pictures. What software could best do it? I was thinking of scanning each image, then overlaying them into one giant horizontal composite. Perhaps cutting out each of the interesting features (there is a stelle containing writing behind one of them that just might have useful information or help to describe what the heck is going on, so I will extract that and some other interesting carvings into separate images). It is amazing to see how LONG these tubes are, if they are in fact representing something real, based on an average humans size, these things must be 8-10 feet long!! I'll put in a scale on the drawings also so an idea can be given of how big they must have been, thickness of cable, size of djet, etc.. Other photos of interest that I'd forgotten about
