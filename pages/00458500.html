On V-Categories Ryan J. Buchanan 1 rjbuchanan2000@gmail.com Abstract Lawvere introduced a deceptively simple category, V, which is complete, symmetric, and monoidal closed. Here, we extend this construction to describe a rather general notion of localization called 𝛤-truncation. We show that this procedure produces tame, realizable n-cells in a standard Grothendieck universe, 𝖀. Finally, we clarify our notion of smallness for objects of stable rings in 𝖀. I.
