Keely and His Discoveries

· archived # 1,460,168
( click to enlarge ) book by Clara Jessup Bloom-field Moore , published in 1893. This book is a compilation of articles she had written to various newspapers and magazines prior to 1893 . Usually these original articles were edited going into the book. Many of the attached articles/chapters are the original unedited articles which I had found in diverse libraries in the early 1980s.
