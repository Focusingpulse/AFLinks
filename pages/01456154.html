COMPOUND INTERETHERIC NEUTRAL CENTER SEVENTH SUBDIVISION

· archived # 1,456,154
This is the ultimate plane of matter which Keely 's subdivisions reached through his system of vibratory disintegration . It is the neutral center itself. This infinitely small particle, so minute that no mechanical means at man's command will ever be able to measure its dimension, is the foundation of the physical Universe we see all about us. The one compound interetheric point or neutral center of the earth controls all the earth's motions of rotation and revolution , and attracts the sympathetic flows that give us light and heat . The neutral center probably has the essentials of mass in the highest degree, since affinity for this neutral center on the part of the other subdivisions gives rise to the various phenomena of mass which we can sense. The degree of mass is dependent on the affinity of the component particles for the neutral center , and the greater this affinity , the greater the mass . The vibratory condition of the neutral center , however, is not known. It is very likely that it transcends anything we will ever be able to sense in any form of matter recognized by means of our senses, since it displays affinity for all vibrations, dividing them into harmonic ratios . It self unmoved, it moves all things. Rotation about itself as an axis is caused by the exercise of its inherent controlling tendency on matter .
