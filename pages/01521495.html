Abstract Since this is an NPA presentation, I will assume you all have some basic belief in the aether. I'll mention the field forces ( E-field, Magnetic field, Gravitation field) and how they are related to Aether. Everything in our observable universe is some form of aether from the subatomic realm to the galactic levels. Many years ago (1950s) I studied with a world class expert, Larry Wilson, on John E. Keely. Larry had actual working models of a couple of Keely's machines and I had the privilege of getting at least a cursory knowledge of how they worked
