Studies on a thermal energy system © Etiam Oy 2011-2016 Part 1 An overview of the LENR test system DANGER Hydrogen gas is highly flammable. High concentration of argon gas is suffocating. Any contact with metal and metal oxide nanoparticles must be avoided. Over 50 Vrms AC voltages are dangerous. Replication of presented experiments shall not be designed, arranged or attempted by any person without appropriate knowledge and experience. Safe and appropriate practices must be followed at all times when dealing with chemicals, pressurized systems and/or high voltage systems. Fig. 1. A view of the assembled test system in operating condition. The reactor is covered with thermal insulators and heat reflector foil. A heated gas line is connected with a manual diaphragm-sealed valve (V4) and a particle filter (F1, Fig. 2) to the reaction chamber. Cooling water inlet and outlet tubes are connected to the heat exchange unit. 1. Components of the test system Fig. 2. Process instrumentation of the test system. Test material was placed in a reaction chamber between a heating block and a cooling block. The power level of thermal energy transferring from the reaction chamber to the cooling block was restricted to a suitable range with a small aluminum metal plate (between RC and CB) that had a specified thickness and diameter. P1,in + P2,in – P4,out – P5,out = P3,in/out Thermal losses P5,out are caused by thermal conduction, convection and thermal radiation. P3,in/out is either negative (endothermic reaction), zero (inert, no reaction) or positive (exothermic reaction). HB, heating block, material aluminum metal. Heater 1 was a band heater around the heating block. Heating was based on resistive heating. RC, reaction chamber, material 316L, volume 95 cm3, design specification max. 40 bar at 450 °C.
