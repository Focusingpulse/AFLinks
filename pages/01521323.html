Space, Propulsion & Energy Sciences International Forum 2009 Space, Propulsion & Energy Sciences International Forum 2009 Event Type Conference Series Space, Propulsion & Energy Sciences International Forum Dates 24 February 2009 – 27 February 2009 Venue Huntsville, AL, Von Braun Center Location Huntsville, AL Country United States Participants 41 Papers 15 Proceedings Space, Propulsion & Energy Sciences International Forum (SPESIF-2009) Space, Propulsion & Energy Sciences International Forum 2009 was a conference held 24 February 2009 – 27 February 2009 at Huntsville, AL, Von Braun Center, Huntsville, AL, United States. The World Science Database records 15 papers and 41 recorded participants for the meeting. Background The Space, Propulsion & Energy Sciences International Forum (SPESIF) was an annual technical forum organised by the Institute for Advanced Studies in the Space, Propulsion and Energy Sciences. It ran from 2009 to 2012, meeting first at the Von Braun Center in Huntsville, Alabama, then at the Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory, and finally twice at the University of Maryland in College Park. The World Science Database preserves the following text for this event. Space, Propulsion and Energy Sciences International Forum is a international technical forum organized by the Institute for Advanced Studies in the Space, Propulsion & Energy Sciences (IASSPES)* as part of their mission to: Integrate the sciences toward rapid advancements in basic research that will lead to new developments in the SPACE, PROPULSION, and ENERGY sciences toward new earth and space uses with a eye on their applications for the exploration of space beyond present day visions, Educate the public on the scientific and technical reasons why basic research in certain space, propulsion and energy related areas should be funded, and Support the STEM program toward educating teacher and student toward this understanding.
