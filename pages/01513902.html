Brock Junior Member Join Date: Mar 2020 Posts: 23 Share Tweet #1 Physical Model Representation of Electrical Distribution System 05-22-2020, 08:01 PM I'm working collaboratively with Eric Dollard, on a project concerning a physical model of a three phase distribution system. This table top model, solely consists of a generating end, distribution line (analaouge computer network), and a receiving end. The purpose of this setup is to present information on the various transformer connections involved in practice, and the response with particular loads, specifically ones of non-linear form. Another intention is to bring light into the malpractice of wye - wye connceted transformers, and various other wrongdoings. The entire setup will employ medium-scale distribution poles and insulators, as well as surplus parts. As goes for the generator, a General Electric Selsyn Control Transformer was first employed to produce 3 phase power.
