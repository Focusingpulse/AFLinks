ESSAY The Unbearable Fear of Psi: On Scientific Suppression in the 21st Century ETZEL CARDEÑA Lund University, Lund, Sweden Submitted August 19, 2015; Accepted September 5, 2015; Published December 15, 2015 Gracias a Stephen Braude and Sophie Reijman for their help in improving this paper. Abstract—This paper describes various examples of blatant attempts to suppress and censor parapsychology research and those who are doing it. The examples include raising false accusations, barring access to journals, suppressing papers and data, and ostracizing and persecuting scientists in￾terested in the topic. The intensity of fear and vituperation caused by para￾psychology research is disproportionate even to the possibility that the psi hypothesis could be completely wrong, so I speculate on the psychological reasons that may give rise to it. There are very few circumstances in which censorship might be appropriate, and the actions by parapsychology cen￾sors put them at odds not only with the history of science but with the his￾tory of modern liberal societies.
