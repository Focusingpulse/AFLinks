bmde2026-analytics025 From "Filter Bubbles" to Informed Choice: Research on Safeguarding User Rights in the Era of Algorithmic Recommendation Manqi Xing1 , Yuxia Wu1 1 Cangzhou Normal University 19253062806@163.com Abstract: In the era of digital information dissemination, personalized recommendation algorithms have deeply penetrated fields such as e-commerce, social media, and news. The "filter bubble" phenomenon they engender not only limits information diversity and solidifies individual cognition but also infringes upon users' right to informed choice, sparking dual controversies over algorithmic ethics and rights protection. This paper focuses on the formation mechanism of "filter bubbles" and the pathways of rights infringement, exploring strategies to safeguard the right to informed choice from the perspectives of platforms, government, and users, providing references for algorithmic governance and the construction of a healthy information ecosystem.
