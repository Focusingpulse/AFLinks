In case you've not seen this, it relates to the many claims of how rotating masses somehow sling 'something' outwards to alter weight. DePalma as well as the many inertial drive claims refer to it as using 'matter currents'. It appears to be a redirection of inertia by using angular momentum. Like a sponge full of water (aether producing 'weight' and inertia), being spun to cause the water to be thrown out, so the sponge becomes lighter. But it will refill once the spinning stops. The best analogous model I've seen of this in practical use is the 'Levitron' which isn't antigravity but appears to use centrifugal force to prevent the magnet from flipping. Check out the page as the author has put a lot of thought into it; http://agram.phy.hr/~bertovic/antigravity.html We are therefore left with the option of controlling it in the same way as we have always done, as, for instance, with the flights of artificial satellites, where gravitation is used as centripetal force which is controlled by the centrifugal force created by the rotation of the satellite around the Earth. At the speed at which centrifugal force becomes equal to centripetal force (gravitation) the weight of the satellite disappears and astronauts find themselves in a weightless condition, etc. At this point in time this is the only method we have to control gravitation. This work, however, indicates how this method can be considerably improved, as well as demonstrating how centrifugal force which controls gravitation can be created when a mass we want to become weightless is not rotating around the Earth.
