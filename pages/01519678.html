Title Distinct E and B Fields Identified Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Francis Viren Fernandes Keywords Magnetism, electricity, field, drift current, charge Published 2014 No. of pages 4 A four-page paper by Francis Viren Fernandes arguing that electricity and magnetism are not two aspects of one thing but two substances with separate material sources: electricity arising from the motion of atoms and photons, magnetism from the motion of the "186-ether" of his own theory. The document linked here is headed Distinct Electric and Magnetic Sources Identified and dated 30 July 2011, which is neither the title nor the year recorded above. It is the same file as the one linked from Distinct Electric and Magnetic Sources Identified , a separate page on this wiki; see the note at the foot. Abstract The exact meaning and source of electric and magnetic fields is experimentally depicted in this paper. The source of drift current is empirically shown by way of experiment. The paper The document's own summary is fuller than the abstract recorded here, and states the thesis directly: "Distinct electric and magnetic substances exist in nature. A drifting atom or photon produces electricity and 186-ether drift produces magnetism." Its subtitle puts it more compactly still — Ether causes magnetism, Atoms cause electricity, Current is momentum of ether and Atoms . Two moves underpin the argument. The first is a redefinition: drift is taken to mean velocity squared rather than velocity, so that for ordinary matter it is the speed of light times the drift velocity, and for the 186-ether it is a second quantity, written v # , times the drift velocity. The second is a picture of matter in which atomic mass is the frequency of a single invariant oscillator, the different frequencies of which yield the masses of the periodic table, and in which "the electric atom rolls in the moving magnetic 186-ether torus".
