FOREWORD In the past ten years, there has been an explosion in the material published on low back pain. Franco Postacchini and his group have been active in this area for some twenty years. This book represents the experience of Professor Postacchini and his co-authors and their vast exposure to the lumbar disc. This unique textbook offers a broad yet concentrated look at the single topic of lumbar disc herniation. While it is rare indeed to devote a full textbook to a single topic, lumbar disc herniation deserves such attention. It is the most common diagnosis for which operative intervention occu rs, yet on the other hand we know, given time, that lumbar disc herniation can be treated non-operatively in many patients with equal success.
