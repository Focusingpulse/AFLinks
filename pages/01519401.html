Beyond Pyramid Power Book Author Patrick Flanagan Published 1975 Publisher Innergy, Inc. / DeVorss Pages 67 ISBN 0875162088 Beyond Pyramid Power is a 1975 book by the American inventor and author Patrick Flanagan , published by Innergy, Inc. and DeVorss & Company. A sequel to his earlier Pyramid Power , it presents what Flanagan describes as four further years of research into "pyramid energy," and introduces a family of devices he calls Tensor Field Devices said to put that energy to practical use. Overview In the early 1970s Flanagan — a self-taught inventor best known for the Neurophone, an earlier device of his for transmitting sound through the skin — was a leading popularizer of the "pyramid power" movement: the claim that the shape of a pyramid concentrates an unusual energy capable of sharpening razor blades, preserving food and affecting living things. Beyond Pyramid Power extends those claims. Flanagan reports having moved pyramid energy, in his phrase, "from the realm of the mystical toy" toward the beginnings of a new technology, and describes constructing "Pyramid Energy Plates" and a range of Tensor Field Devices — among them a Tensor Field Voice Communicator, Tensor Field Propulsion, an electro-catalytic seed treatment said to raise the vigour of seeds, and water-treatment systems. These claims belong to the pyramid-power and free-energy currents of 1970s alternative science; they are not accepted by mainstream physics or biology, and no such energy field or device has been demonstrated under controlled conditions. The book is of interest chiefly as a primary document of that movement and of Flanagan's work.
