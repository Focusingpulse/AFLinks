A sequel to his earlier Pyramid Power , it presents what Flanagan describes as four further years of research into "pyramid energy," and introduces a family of devices he calls Tensor Field Devices said to put that energy to practical use. Overview In the early 1970s Flanagan a self-taught inventor best known for the Neurophone, an earlier device of his for transmitting sound through the skin was a leading popularizer of the "pyramid power" movement: the claim that the shape of a pyramid concentrates an unusual energy capable of sharpening razor blades, preserving food and affecting living things.
