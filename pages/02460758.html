I have a friend that built a version of the dean drive in the 1960's. after much playing around, they got it to rise vertically in the air a couple feet. a bystander watching, reached over and turned up some knobs controling the oscillations, the drive sped extremely fast straight up till it reached the end of the cord supplying power. at that point, the cord broke loose, and the drive continued up till all momentium was gone, then came back down and crashed. he got the plans from an artical in popular science(i think) from the 60's.
