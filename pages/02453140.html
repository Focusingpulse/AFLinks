Decker: "Re: Lets go fly a kite! / finance our projects" To everyone who is rattling on about the Y2K problem. Look, I'm trying to be nice about this, but you guys just don't get it. You see, a LOT OF PEOPLE AND GROUPS read the KeelyNet discussion list archives. I know because I get lots of emails from people complimenting or commenting on something useful or intriguing that was posted to the list, then publicly archived. And I know people get private emails from others not on the list asking them or informing them about things they posted to the list and which these people read about in the archives...I think that is just the neatest thing, that we all have such a wide audience who WANT to read what is being talked about here. So you see, I want to keep the integrity of the list as pure as possible and with useful information....this Y2K crap can be found anywhere on the Net and really doesn't belong here...it simply pollutes the archive and that is my main concern. If you want to talk about it and such material, please don't do it here. Do it between yourselves or go to newsgroups that live on such material, but not here. I like to think that the archive will live on after us and I know for a fact many people read what is posted NOW, so let's give them information or ideas that are interesting and novel. I've been debating how to handle this aperiodic pollution with the least possible rancor from those who will be affected. I don't see that the concept is too difficult for some to comprehend, it seems to be more like a game. As in, How far can I go before something happens. Censorship, dictator, myopic, whatever, I really don't care what people think, the point is, make your posts usef
