My Full Scale Invasion on my Subconscious!

· archived # 2,431,923
I got my first paths theater in february and since then I've bought three more. I've fiddled around with modules and switched around to see what suits me. After having mini shifts and testing and observing how I responded to modules, I've finally chosen the right modules and have been consistent with them for just over two weeks. I remember a long time ago I was happy. Everything was going for me and I thought I had found happiness. I had that "feeling" of loving myself and being happy. Unfortunately, the outside circumstances changed and soon I rockedbottomed to a deep depression that lasted years. I've gotten better in the recent years with the help of hypnotherapy cd's. I guess I'm not depressed anymore, but I'm not happy either. I find myself sighing a lot and depending on the future for salvation. A lot of what has happened to me has left me in low self esteem and self worth. I guess right now, I want to know what it feels like to really love myself and love life, and to be living in the present moment. I miss having that "feeling" that I had a long time ago. It's been so long to the point that the "feeling" is probably unfamiliar. I chose modules that concentrated more on being happy unconditionally and having a strong inner self-love. I chose modules that would help me into being happy now, as opposed to waiting for me to obtain some external object to be happy. I chose these inner modules as opposed to "goal" modules, such as having a nice body (which I would want at a later point of time, just not now). Now, I have Four theaters, and 12 of the 16 modules are dedicated to helping me acheive that inner feeling. I'm going to wait and see how I develop develop and hopefully PATHS full scale invasion will help me rediscover that "feeling". Wish me luck!
