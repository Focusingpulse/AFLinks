First layer of speaker wire is the primary oscillator bifilar coil (double series winding), wired per Nikola Teslas patent. Second layer is the secondary bifilar coil, also wired per tesla's patent, using the same type of speaker wire (smaller gauge if needing higher volts output). Adjustable stingo oscillator is used to pulse the primary coil, with provides very low duty cycle and sharp pulses to primary bifilar coil.
