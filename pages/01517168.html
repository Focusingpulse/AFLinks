It usually doesn't take that much to stop someone, as we learned in a demonstration in the company parking lot. The acoustic "weapon," in the demonstration model, looks like a huge stereo speaker, except this one sports urban camouflage. Police departments and the Pentagon are flocking to Norris' headquarters in San Diego to see this revolutionary technology for themselves.
