SUCCESS- build a wall brick by brick

· archived # 2,432,373
Hey guys, I just wanted to post on the power of consistent action and how over time small things can snowball into massive things. Here is a little thought experiment. Sorry to use money but it makes an easy example. Obviously money alone doesn't equal success. If you take an action that gets you 5 dollars per day. It doesn't matter what that action is Then you do it again the next day and the next day. So call action for day 1 A1, action for day 2 A2 etc Day 1 - 5 dollars from A1 (action on day 1) Day 2 - 5 dollars A1 and 5 dollars from A2 = 10 dollars that day Day 3 - 5 dollars A1, 5 from A2, from A3 = 15 dollars that day if you keep this going to day 90 Day 90 - you get 450 dollars daily! and have made a total of 20475 dollars in the 90 days. Even if you follow this idea getting only 1 dollar per day you will have by day 90- 90 dollars per day and a total of 4095 dollars over the 90 days. This isn't a particularly realistic example but it just shows the power of how a small piece of action taken consistently can snowball into something great. Consistent action is the key to success in every pursuit, it's how you build up skills in anything learning guitar, sport and business! Do you guys have any thoughts on this one? This is just the kind of random stuff I think about. Peace
