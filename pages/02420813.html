NCURA - Peter Dunn http://www.ncura.edu/content/educational_programs/workshops/spaii/faculty/peter_dunn.php NCURA National Council of University Research Administrators Peter Dunn Peter E. Dunn received the B.S. (Chemistry) from Fordham University in 1968 and the Ph.D. (Biochemistry) from Purdue University in 1973, and served as a postdoctoral Research Associate in the Department of Biochemistry, University of Chicago, from 1973-77. He joined the faculty of Purdue’s Department of Entomology in 1977 and was appointed Professor in 1988. In October 1995, he was appointed Assistant Vice President for Research for both Purdue University and the Purdue Research Foundation. In late 1997, he assumed additional responsibility as Director of the Purdue Research Foundation Office of Technology Transfer, a responsibility that he held until March, 2000. In June, 2001, Prof. Dunn returned to his faculty responsibilities. In April 2003, Prof. Dunn rejoined the central research office as Interim Associate Vice Provost for Research and served in this role until July, 2004, at which time he was appointed Associate Vice Provost for Research and Director, University Research Administration. In this capacity, Dr. Dunn provides leadership and oversight for research administration at Purdue University. His areas of responsibility include research integrity, protection of human and vertebrate animal subjects of research, biosafety and use of select agents, conflict of interest management, and export control.
