Abstract COVID-19 is caused by the coronavirus SARS-CoV-2 and is now a pandemic affecting humans at a global scale. Researchers are still trying to find a cure and the vaccine to fight this disease. Drug-based cure and vaccine are overwhelmingly virus-specific and newer drugs and vaccines are needed to resist new novel viral infections. The use of herbal remedies and plant-based extract to fight viral infections is an ongoing work which has accelerated to a fast pace due to the severity of the current pandemic.
