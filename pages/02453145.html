He said they were doing research with the human nervous system, realizing that we too are 'clocked' with a complex of various frequencies. They figured the brain sends signals to the medulla which acts like both an amplifier and a transformer to send nerve impulses to the various muscles and organs to cause them to react to mental commands. He said they discovered that a frequency of around 13 cps had to be precisely phase matched, then cancelled when applied to the intended subject, this could cause the nerve force to be minimized to such a degree that the subject was rendered harmless. This did not affect autonomous muscle activities such as breathing, only those effort subject to will. He said the device was tested with a small group and it would only affect a couple of people in a crowd and others only to varying degrees. The problem was that everyone has a slightly different frequency and phase, so the device could only be used to affect those it was tuned to.
