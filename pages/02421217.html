Walt Jenkins Residence Florida, United States Nationality American Known for The H2G "black box" on-board water-to-fuel system; ultrasonic water-fuel vaporization; plasma spark plugs Scientific career Fields Water-fuel systems, hydrogen on demand, high-voltage plasma ignition Institutions H2 Global, LLC Walt Jenkins (also given as Walter P. Jenkins ) is an American independent inventor, based in Florida, who develops on-board systems intended to let internal combustion engines run largely on water. His work is marketed under the name H2G through his company H2 Global, LLC. Jenkins is a familiar figure in the independent-energy community and has been described within it as a modern successor to Stanley Meyer; his central claim is that his process is not electrolysis and does not derive from prior water-fuel art. Biography Jenkins came to energy research from an unrelated technical career. He has said that he worked in the motion picture industry in Hollywood during the 1970s, where he developed an early virtual set system and other computer-based production tools, and that he managed a broadcast television station in the Orlando, Florida market during the late 1990s. He began experimenting with alternative fuel systems for cars as a hobby in the mid-1980s. By the early 2000s he was working on hydrogen extraction from water by electrolysis, and he is credited within the HHO community as the original developer of the "dry cell" electrolyser design that many later experimenters adopted. He subsequently abandoned electrolysis, saying it could never return enough fuel energy for the electrical power it consumed, and moved to a different approach. His company H2 Global, LLC is registered in Florida and also trades online as H2GE and H4Gas. He has conducted demonstrations for accredited investors near Sarasota, Florida, and has shown a water-fuelled dune buggy on Anna Maria Island.
