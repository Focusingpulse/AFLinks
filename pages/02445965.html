09/04/14 - Could Remote Viewing Coordinates lead to Teleportation? Back in the 1990s, when we were running the monthly Vanguard Roundtable meetings for almost 10 years, we had an invitation only crowd who could bring guests. We didn't 'do' UFOs, conspiracy, politics or religion and when people would try it, they were told to change the topic or leave. The idea was to share useful information, ideally science, health, paranormal or alt science, where people would bring a photocopy of the interesting things they came across over the past month. They would then present their information and place it in our public pool. At the end of the meeting, Chuck would calculate how much it would cost to copy the entire set at 5 cents a page. Usually $10-$15 or so of focused information. People would sign up and pay for their copy to be delivered to the next meeting which we held on the third Thursday in a Furrs' Cafeteria banquet room that held 150 people. We got the room for free as long as we ate $200 in food and we always far exceeded that. We would discuss the topics brought up at the meeting and often have surprise speakers who happened to be in town for a paid presentation elsewhere so they would kindly give a brief, 30 minute or so preview for our group. Sometimes people would bring a short video clip and even little machines and devices to demonstrate, always very interesting and free, since we did not charge because we had no actual costs, not sending out a newsletter, just remember 3rd Thursday, every month, like clockwork. Among the list of surprise speakers, we had about 3 fellows talk about Remote Viewing at different meetings. I don't recall all their names but one was Jim Marrs.
