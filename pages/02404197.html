Zeidan 2 1 Faculty of Pharmacy, Al-Wataniya Private University, Hama, Syria; 2 Department of Physics, Homs University, Homs, Syria ✉ nassifalaa85@gmail.com; alaa.nassif@wpu.edu.sy Abstract. Using the Lee code version (RADPFV5.15de), we carried out a series of numerical experiments to find the parameters for the plasma focus and the soft X-ray yield for each of nitrogen gas (molecular) and neon gas (atomic) within an appropriate temperature range for PF400 dense plasma focus device. The results showed that the highest value of the soft X-ray yield in neon was 0.148 J at 3.2 Torr pressure, while for nitrogen it was 0.0634 J at 4.4 Torr. This is because of the higher atomic number and effective charge of neon as compared to nitrogen.
