Different Types of Modules

· archived # 2,431,997
As our list of Modules expands, so has the different types of Modules that are offered. We have Free Modules, Special Packages of all sorts, Quantum Meditation, Quick Checkout (all Premium) and General Modules. Each of these different types of Modules require their own Theaters because of the different formats they are in. Each non-general Module is shown by reading "Premium Product" in the title. This is how you will know it requires its own individual Theater. If it is Premium, it must be alone in a Theater. This means that if you would like 2 Premium Products, you must have 2 separate Theaters. One for each Premium Product. If it is not marked Premium, then you can have up to 4 Modules (plus sleep) per Theater.
