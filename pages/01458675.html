Diatonic

· archived # 1,458,675
Notes proper to a key . diatonic chord is one having no note chromatically altered. diatonic harmony is formed by the tones of a single minor or major scale ; diatonic interval is formed by two tones of the major or minor scale . A diatonic interval is one formed of two notes of a diatonic scale unaltered by accidentals. diatonic melody is one not including notes belonging to more than one scale . diatonic modulation is one by which a key is changed to another closely related to it.
