My Capacitor Discharge Circuit

· archived # 1,511,922
Hi all, I came up with a simple cap discharge circuit that gives great results. It's basically a diac triggering a triac when the cap reaches 32v. I saw someone using a neon to trigger the triac but the voltage is much higher like that. The output of this circuit is very similar to the 555 timer setup Bedini uses on the trifilar monopole motors but is much simpler. The downside is i cannot control the voltage at which the cap is discharged. It is fixed at 32v due to the diac's breakdown voltage. If anyone can improve this, please go ahead. The charging results are great.
