Decker: "Heartmath Institute & a question" Hi Folks! While looking for a .gif of a pump, I found this incredible page on nothing but all the types of pumps. The pump serves as a means of feeding an energy storage system, whether as fluid in a gravity fed reservoir or compressed gases, or as electrons in a capacitor or battery. This remarkable page is called the Internet Glossary of Pumps, it is really interesting if you are curious about such matters; The home page is; http://www.animatedsoftware.com/pumpglos/pumpglos.htm Teslas disc pump; http://www.animatedsoftware.com/pumpglos/teslapum.htm The ram pump which looks suspiciously like the 6,000 pound iron sphere that Keely was supposed to have used to store his 'etheric plasma'; http://www.animatedsoftware.com/pumpglos/ram_pump.htm and a 'rag pump' which is said to have been in use for over 400 years...it is interesting because of some experimenters in the 1940s who found that a rope could be made to carry very high volumes of water when looped in such a fashion as described, amazing; http://www.animatedsoftware.com/pumpglos/rag_pump.htm and a lot of other intriguing designs. -- KeelyNet - From an Art to a Science Jerry W.
