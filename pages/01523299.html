Kipley Farr Residence North Ogden, UT, United States Nationality American Other names Kip Farr Known for Cosmology and Religion (1994); Electric Universe Kipley A. Farr , known as Kip Farr, is an American author and independent researcher in Weber County, Utah, who writes on cosmology and its relationship to religion. His book Cosmology and Religion (1994) sets the scientific and the Christian theological account of the origin and nature of the universe alongside one another. In the World Science Database, from which this wiki's membership records are drawn, Farr gives his research interest as the Electric Universe — the alternative cosmology holding that electric currents and plasma, rather than gravity acting alone, are the dominant organizing forces in the cosmos. Biography Farr lives in the Ogden area of northern Utah. His World Science Database record places him at North Ogden; other listings give the neighboring town of Farr West. He holds no university or institutional post, and his work belongs to the tradition of independent inquiry this wiki documents: writing outside the professional academy on questions that sit between physical cosmology, theology and the origin of the universe. Farr is recorded in the database as a supporter member rather than as a presenting researcher, and as attending the 19th Natural Philosophy Alliance Conference , held 25–28 July 2012 at the Albuquerque Marriott Pyramid North in Albuquerque, New Mexico — one of the largest of the NPA 's annual meetings, with 125 papers and some 220 recorded participants. No conference paper, journal article or recorded talk is attributed to him in the database, so Cosmology and Religion is the whole of his recorded published output. Education Cosmology and Religion Cosmology and Religion was published in December 1994 by Northwest Publishing of Salt Lake City, Utah, as a 320-page paperback.
