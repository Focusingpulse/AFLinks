Richard Wagner and His Relationship to Mysticism [ 1 ] Theosophy, or spiritual science, is not meant to be something one-sided that merely satisfies human curiosity or the thirst for knowledge; rather, it is meant to represent a spiritual movement that is called upon to have a deeper impact on everything we call the culture of the present and the near future. We will gain a sense of how theosophy can be called upon to do this when we see how what pulsates through it is, in essence, not confined to it alone, but is already manifesting itself
