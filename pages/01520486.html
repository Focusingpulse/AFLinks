Focus The "Big Bang Never Happened" cosmology series, alongside dense-plasma-focus fusion research YouTube @LPPFusion Website www .lppfusion .com LPPFusion is the YouTube channel of the New Jersey fusion research company of the same name, led by Eric J Lerner . It carries two quite different bodies of work: the company's laboratory programme to build a generator powered by aneutronic proton–boron fusion, and Lerner's long-running cosmological argument that the Big Bang did not happen. Opened in February 2014, it is among the larger channels in the index on this wiki, and unusual there in being a working company's channel rather than a presenter's. The fusion programme The company pursues the dense plasma focus approach to fusion — a compact device in which a plasma sheath collapses into a tiny, extremely dense plasmoid — with hydrogen–boron fuel, which yields charged particles rather than neutrons and so allows direct conversion to electricity without a steam cycle. The channel documents this work in a manner closer to a laboratory notebook than to a corporate communications channel: playlists cover individual experiment preparations, specific firing days, switch tests, and the repair of light leaks, alongside shareholder meetings and crowdfunding appeals. Recent uploads have taken a combative line on the wider fusion industry, arguing that the largest and best-funded companies are overstated and that ITER represents a costly mistake. The cosmology argument Lerner is the author of The Big Bang Never Happened (1991), and the channel is the principal current vehicle for that argument. The The Real Crisis in Cosmology series is its most-watched work, covering the galactic origin of the light elements, the case against dark matter, and the claim that the universe is not expanding.
