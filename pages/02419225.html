Round Robin >> Vol. 09 >> No. 05 [21] CORRESPONDENCE OF ROLF TELANO - THE FLYING DISCS . (Note: "Rolf Telano" is an electronics engineer resident in the Middle West. He has long had personal contacts of a psychic nature but has never publicised them except through BSR publications and under a pseudonym. A brochure by him, under our title "Release 1-B-52," was published in mimeo. in January 1952, and awakened much interest by its detailed description of the types of aeroforms used by the Nors, or planetary Guardians. We believe that the following excerpts, from a recent letter by R.T., are of equal importance. -ml). Rolf Telano to BSRA Director M.L. This is partially in reply to your letter concerning the proposed booklet about the aeroforms, and the same cautions apply here as in the case of the material printed by you in 1-B-52. That is: any statement must necessarily be incomplete and over-simplified, since the possible exceptions are without number. What has been attempted is to present a reasonably correct typical picture. The descriptions of the craft have been limited (with the exception of the circular lunar fliers) to the craft used by the Nors. Since they are the assigned Watchers or Guardians at the present time, their craft are the most numerous and the nearest typical. However, there are countless other varieties of other craft which come here occasionally, for various reasons.
