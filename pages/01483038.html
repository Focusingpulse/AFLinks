retrogression of harmonies

· archived # 1,483,038
noun: passing from a more complex to a simpler biological form noun: returning to a former state Hughes In the retrogression of harmonies , a key-note and its trinities , by sounding the same tones as when ascending , leads the ear to the same notes , and the root of each key-note becomes the fifth lower key-note . F, the root of C , becomes key-note ; B? , the root of F , the next key-note , and so on.
