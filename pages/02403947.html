Introduction Quantum parametric processes are very important mechanisms for constructing and manipulating of quantum states in a variety of aspects of quantum science and technologies. The most impressive properties of such processes are stimulation of quantum vacuum and real photon production under conditions near resonant in various quantum systems [1].
