Keywords – Brazil, coronavirus, Covid-19, France, Germany, Italy, pandemic, Russia, Spain, United Kingdom, US. § 1 – Introduction In a previous article [1], I did an estimate on when Brazil would overtake other countries in numbers of confirmed cases and deaths in this present pandemic, the countries in the 8 first positions of ranking of the Covid-19 pandemic in May 11, 2020. Respect to confirmed cases, the forecast has been very good even today, taking into account a small number of days. Brazil overtook Germany, France, Italy and Spain in the expected time (2, 2, 6 and 6 days, respectively), and probably tomorrow (05/19/2020) it will overtake the United Kingdom (8 days).
