Bailey (Thomas Frank Bailey), who appears in conference programmes as Thomas Bailey PhD and Tom Bailey , is an American chemist and independent researcher working on pure-carbon materials. He is a co-inventor of a nanocarbon allotrope named crossene , and is known within the independent-research community for arguing that the standard "geodesic dome" picture of the C60 buckyball is geometrically and energetically wrong. He has been a recurring speaker at the Tesla Tech ExtraOrdinary Technology Conference since 2022. Bailey should not be confused with other researchers of the same surname documented on this wiki, including Patrick G Bailey and Jacques Moret-Bailey , with whom he has no connection. Biography Public biographical detail on Bailey is sparse. He is named as an inventor, together with Danny Cross and Larry Herbert Kirby, on a family of United States patents covering the crossene allotrope, assigned to Structured Nano Carbon LLC . The earliest of these has a priority date of 17 March 2017; US 11,718,530 B2, "Allotrope of carbon having increased electron delocalization", was granted on 8 August 2023, with a continuation issued as US 12,084,350 B2 and further continuations pending. He is listed in the 2026 ExtraOrdinary Technology Conference programme with a doctorate. Scientific contributions Crossene: a new allotrope of carbon Bailey's starting point is that carbon runs the modern world — petroleum, coal, natural gas, plastics, photosynthesis — but that pure carbon holds value well beyond its use as a fuel, in the form of distinct allotropes whose very different properties follow from different bonding regimes.
