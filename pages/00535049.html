AN OPTIMIZATION PROBLEM FOR ADDITION CHAINS THEOPHILUS AGAMA Abstract. In this paper, we formulate an optimization problem for addition chains. 1. Introduction An addition chain of length h leading to n is a sequence of positive integers so = 1, s1 = 2, . . . , sh = n where si = sk +ss for i > k ≥ s ≥ 0. The number of terms (excluding the first term) in an addition chain leading to n is the length of the chain. We call an addition chain leading to n with a minimal length an optimal addition chain leading to n. In standard practice, we denote by `(n) the length of an optimal addition chain that leads to n.
