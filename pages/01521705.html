Abstract An analysis of the basic principles, laws, formulas and pivotal experiments of physics leads to a new understanding which allows a surprisingly simple generalization of mechanics and electrodynamics. The speculative theories of the last century may have value in their ability to stimulate original thought and experiment, but must necessarily be modified or abandoned when they are proven to have no basis in reality.
