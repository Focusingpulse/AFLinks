Title Chapter: The Inert Gas Technology Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Maurice B Cooke Keywords Aether , inert gases, channeling, aetheric healing No. of pages 15 Abstract Perhaps no other scientific-sounding information said to have been paranormally received has had more people experimenting with it in the past 20 years than what has come to be known as the inert gas technology. From the beginning, this information has been distinguished by its balance between a consistent theoretical level, which describes in a totally new way the nature of certain fundamental aspects of physical reality, and a much more nuts-and-bolts engineering level, which explains exactly how to get realworld results. Given the dozens of people over the years who have pursued this inert gas technology and who have claimed success in their experimentation, two of the chief tenets of science seem to have been satisfied: being able to successfully predict and replicate. Overview The archived document is not a research paper but a working draft, dated 10/12/06 and signed only "J.K.", of a chapter from a book then in progress under the title Scientific Information from Paranormal Sources . It still carries its editorial scaffolding — footnote markers written as "[f]", a bracketed note to the editor about breaking up long quotations, a placeholder for a Puthoff citation the author has not yet located, and a closing "Still to do" list stating that the conclusion is unwritten. The wiki record attributes the piece to Maurice B Cooke ; Cooke is in fact its principal subject and the source of most of its quoted material, alongside the American engineer Jon C. Fox. Read as what it is — a documentary chapter about a body of channeled material and the experimenters who pursued it — it is coherent and carefully sourced to its own primary texts.
