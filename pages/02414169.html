It is proposed to consider the original magnetic loop in a geometry that has an inverse closure topology. This variant of the geometric representation of a magnetic loop corresponds to the universal principle of natural dualism, which is based on the ontology of the principle of complementarity, the physical embodiment of which is the phenomenon of magnetism. The paper analyzes two main previous models of a hydromagnetic dynamo proposed by Nobel laureate H. Alfv&eacute;n and scientist Ya. Zeldovich, and voices those questions that require more specific answers. The paper shows how the proposed new model of a hydromagnetic dynamo answers these questions and reduces both previous models to a common denominator based on natural universalism. Together with the proposed model of transformation of the magnetic loop through the method of its bifurcation, the conclusion is made that this method of doubling the magnetic flux is a method of forming baryonic matter from ether monopoles, that is, a method of forming atoms.
