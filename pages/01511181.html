There is a nice? path near a mason lodge with high tension wires overhead. Its basically a greened out, paved, high tension track. I was riding in a fashion to be insulated from the metal bits of my bike, just rubber soles on pedals and hands on rubber grips. My finger was barely touching the handle brake which was metal, and I felt a very familiar unexpected feeling! Voltage. If I grabbed any metal, the feeling would dissapear, and I would only feel it when I barely touched metal. So the people who ride with their hands fully on the brake would never feel this. The feeling intensified as I stood up, halved when I was sitting, and stopped when I left the bike path. The sparks must have been 1/2 to 1/4 of a MM, all I saw was a white dot between my bike and my finger. Soo, maybe 1500 or less volts.
