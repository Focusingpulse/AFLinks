Letter: Spatial space as a time-averaged quantum clock caused by an orthogonal state-system – an alternative explanation to LQG and CDT Abstract: A model is proposed in which space itself is not a fixed background object, but arises from ultrafast rotating of orthonormal quantum states. The model provides natural explanations for: Lorentz invariance as effective symmetry, gravitation as a statistical mean of rotational dynamics and cosmic fluctuations as frozen rotational phases.
