Complex Systems of Charged Particles and their Interactions with Electromagnetic Radiation 2018 FIELD CIRCLES AS PLASMA CHEMICAL SOIL MODIFICATION IN CONDITIONS OF ATMOSPHERIC ELECTRICITY V.L. Bychkov, A.R.Bikmukhametova, N.V.Ardelian, R.V. Kosmachevskii M.V. Lomonosov MSU, Moscow, Russia, e-mail: bychvl@gmail.com One of the poorly studied areas of atmospheric electricity during the thunderstorm is its interaction with the Earth's surface and soil and the role of long-lived luminous formations (LLO) and ball lightning (BL) with soil. Such interaction with the Earth's surface, can lead to appearance of, so-called, "circles" in the fields that arise during the vegetation period and thunderstorm activity. To understand role of plasma chemistry of this we made modeling of the environment heating, leading to the destruction of herbide cover. In studies we applied our plasma-chemical code the chemical kinetics of humid air. It takes into account electronmolecular reactions in an external electric field in air and water gas mixture: we used reactions involving 27 plasma components and 23 reagents containing hydrogen, including: positive ions О+, O2+, O4+, O6+, O8+, Н+, Н2+, OH+, НО2+, H2O+, О2+(Н2О), Н3О+, Н3О+(Н2О), Н3О+(ОН), Н3О+(Н2О)2, Н+(Н2О)2, Н+(Н2О)3, Н+(Н2О)4 negative ions О-, О2-, О3-, О2(Н2О), О2-(Н2О)2, Н-, ОH-, atoms О and Н, molecules Н2, О2, Н2О, О3, free radicals ОН, НО2, Н2О2, O(1D1), O(1S0), electrons and molecular components of air, including hydrogen. The number of reactions in dry air is about 200 and more than 250 for humid air components containing hydrogen.
