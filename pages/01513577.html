In the 1970's during the oil crisis a man called Vincent Carman came up with a system to increase car mileage dramatically, with a hydraulic transmission hybrid. The Carter administration sat on him. Carman thought he would get a hero's welcome but was stonewalled at every turn. I think his ideas were practical and do able and hopeful internet buzz will finally get his ideas out. Vincent Carman's prototype got 38.5mpg City with a 4200lb behemoth that normally gets 15mpg (a 250% improvement!). A Prius gets ~50mpg city in a chassis that would get about 25-30mpg city (a mere 100%improvement). This would suggest a hydraulic Prius could get 65-70mpg city! Of course a Prius only has a 100lb battery, so it is hardly a hybrid with so little storage. Thus the poor mileage. The gas engine is really doing most of the work.
