When a closed coil was placed in the generated changing electric field, an induced current and an induced electromotive force were generated in the coil, and the scissors in the coil can be magnetized. The 1- watt bulb can be lighted by the generated changing electric field, when one end of the plastic rod was rubbed by fur.
