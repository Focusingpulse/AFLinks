Franklin Hu Talk Type Live session Speaker Franklin Hu Date 27 April 2013 Time 07:00 (US/Pacific) Platform Fuzemeeting Video Conference Participants 28 Is Gravity Just a Bad Case of Static Cling? was a live session presented by Franklin Hu on 27 April 2013 in the Natural Philosophy Alliance live session series. Abstract Since the dawn of time the question has remained - what the heck is gravity? Newton described it and showed how it keeps the universe together, but he didn't have a clue as to how it worked. Others have said that it is a push and not a pull, others say it due to a universal expansion. The mainstream offers that gravity is leaking out of some 'brane' or it is due to gravitons or some such exotic mechanism. This presentation will explore some of these ideas.
