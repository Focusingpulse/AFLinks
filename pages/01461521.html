Quasi-neutrality

· archived # 1,461,521
Quasi-neutrality (quasi, from the Latin, "as if", "resembling") describes the apparent charge neutrality of a plasma overall, while at smaller scales, the positive and negative charges making up the plasma , may give rise to charged regions and electric fields. Since electrons are very mobile, plasmas are excellent conductors of electricity , and any charges that develop are readily neutralized, and in many cases, plasmas can be treated as being electrically neutral .
