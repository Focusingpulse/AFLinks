THE WORLD HAS ARRANGED FOR A GREAT SHOW. Dr. Depew, Bishop Potter, Bishop Farley, Tesla, Edison, Dr. Shrady and Other Distinguished Persons Will Take Part. MANY PROMINENT SOCIETY WOMEN WILL ACT AS PATRONESSES. It is going to be a very fine show, one that should not be missed. To begin with, there may be people in this broad land who know nothing about the Red Cross Society, but they do not read newspapers and books. Anything that helps the work of the society, particularly at this time, when the country is at war, appeals to the whole nation. The men who fall on battlefields may need the gentle, fearless devoted nurses with the red crosses on their arms, who care for friend and foe alike, carrying to all who suffer mercy and care. The Red Cross nurses are willing to risk their lives, give them if need be, but something more than devotion on their part is necessary to make their work successful. They need money and a deal of it for hospital appliances are costly and the maintenance of the corps means a great sum. It is to meet these necessary expenses that the entertainment will be given in the Grand Central Palace next Saturday night. It will realize an enormous sum.
