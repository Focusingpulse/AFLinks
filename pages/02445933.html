The inclusion of fewer moving parts also makes construction of bladeless wind turbines more reliable than the conventional ones. Bladeless wind turbines are also less expensive than traditional wind turbines and are easy to install. These factors together are likely to drive the growth of the global bladeless wind turbines market.
