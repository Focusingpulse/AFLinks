5. Anthroposophy and the Youth Movement — GA 217a. Knowledge—The Task of Youth

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 8 September 1921 · archived # 2,434,655
, Stuttgart Translated by Steiner Online Library 5. Anthroposophy and the Youth Movement [ 1 ] Opening Remarks: First, on behalf of everyone, I would like to thank Dr. Steiner for organizing this gathering, which he made possible for us despite his busy schedule. The insights Dr. Steiner shared with us at Easter have continued to resonate with us in the meantime. We haven’t heard much from one another in the meantime, but when we gathered here again and discussed things, we realized that we had all made some progress. Some issues that were still problems at Easter are no longer problems today.
