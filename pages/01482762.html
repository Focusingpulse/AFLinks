This occurs immediately if excessive influences of heat and pressure are brought to bear by the opposite form of motion . [ Nature as Teacher , Volume II, page 108] All over-illumination and overheating immediately provokes the release of decomposive energies; whether heating is the result of sunlight or processes of combustion is immaterial. The products of this emulsion give rise to all kinds of inflammation in all organisms, as a result of which, due to the lack of qualigen , the regressive course of cancer then becomes inevitable. In the tissues minute explosions occur, which result in their destruction. Goethe called Nature's principal, formative essence the 'Eternally Female' and 'All-uplifting'.
