It develops the Aether Physics Model (APM) CONTENTS 1 Abstract This paper introduces an Aether Physics Model (APM) quantification layer for longitudinal (scalar) wave transport that is explicitly compatible with Quantum Measurement Units (QMU). The central move is to treat pulse-driven longitudinal phenomena through a mixed-derivative operator whose natural unit is the QMU scalar-wave primitive sclw := Fq /λC . This choice ties longitudinal excitation to a ledger-scaled conversion between temporal oscillation and spatial compression, and makes short, high-potential impulses a first-class driving primitive. A concrete engineering benchmark is provided by wireless acoustic-electric feed-through (WAEF) devices, which transfer power through a solid wall using stress waves. We recast the WAEF transfer problem into QMU using energy and power primitives enrg and powr (with powr interpreted as power and enrg as energy), and we propose pulse-domain experimental gates that distinguish resonance capture from longitudinal transport. The narrative also links the scalarwave bookkeeping to the Maxwell (1864) twenty-equation ledger via dual-potential definitions and the longitudinal Ampère–Maxwell time-derivative structure.
