Ionizing radiation is produced by nuclear energy (radioactivity) as unleashed by nuclear explosions and power plant accidents. It is also present in nature as attested to by the continuing concerns about radon gas in our homes. Non- ionizing electromagnetic radiation (NIEMR) is more insidious, everywhere we look in our electric world we see its evidence, in our homes, offices, vehicles and even on the open road. The suspicion that radio waves and other forms of electromagnetic (EM) pollution may be harmful has been with us for decades.
