The Theory of Guided Electromagnetic Waves (Marconi Series Covering Advances in Radio and Radar) Book Author Richard A Waldron Language English Series Marconi Series Subject Electromagnetism, waveguides Published 1970 Publisher Van Nostrand Reinhold Pages 520 ISBN 0442091672 Theory of Guided Electromagnetic Waves is a 1970 technical treatise by Richard A Waldron , published by Van Nostrand Reinhold in the Marconi series covering advances in radio and radar. Overview The book is a systematic, graduate-level account of the propagation of electromagnetic waves in guiding structures — waveguides, transmission lines, and related systems. Starting from Maxwell's equations, it develops waveguide modes, boundary-value methods, and the analysis of a wide range of guided-wave problems relevant to microwave and radar engineering. About the author Richard Arthur Waldron was a British physicist and electromagnetics researcher who wrote several texts on wave theory, waveguides, and the mathematics of physics, and who also published on foundational and unconventional questions in physics.
