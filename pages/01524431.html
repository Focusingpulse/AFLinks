Overview This four-page essay of 2008 by Karl Teppo sets out what he calls the Radiation Coupled Theory of Gravitation, or RC Gravity, and with it a cosmology of two alternating "eon cycles" divided by absolute zero. It is written in an informal, essayistic register, without equations, and documents its claims by footnotes to press releases and popular reports rather than to data. The cold base Teppo's central posit is a gravitational medium — an ether in the old sense — existing "at zero Kelvin" and called the cold base . Ordinary thermal atoms radiate into it, and it is "fired at core of the atomic nuclei" in what he describes, drawing on his own trade, as a Diesel action: the cold base is the fuel, the atom the engine, and gravitation the coupling between radiation and this sub-zero medium. Its "light barrier" is c , "just like a sound barrier is in the air", with faster-than-light emission — written c + — escaping from the poles of rotating bodies.
