09/25/16 - Amsterdam researchers create robots that can mate and reproduce One of the trademarks that distinguishes robots from humans is the ability to reproduce. This dividing between man and machine just got blurrier. Researchers in Amsterdam have created robots that can mate and spawn offspring through a process similar to human reproduction. In addition to replacing jobs, robots could be replacing themselves.
