GTM2026-main057 A Hybrid Evolutionary and LLM-Based Approach for Modeling and Optimal Control of Social Media Topics Vladislav A. Taynitskiy1 , Elena A. Gubar1 , Ivan S. Blekanov1 1 St Petersburg University v.taynitsky@spbu.ru In light of the increasing influence of social media platforms, this study introduces a novel methodological framework that integrates BERTopic, evolutionary game theory, and multi-agent simulation within the NetLogo environment. This approach addresses the inherent limitations of conventional topic modelling approaches. The primary objective of this study is to simulate the propagation and dynamic evolution of thematic content on social networks. From a methodological perspective, semantically coherent topics are initially derived from large-scale Weibo datasets. Consequently, a multidimensional metric for evaluating user influence was developed to derive payoff matrices, which in turn served as the foundation for analysing the evolution of topics. The simulation employs an evolutionary mechanism implemented in NetLogo that accounts for the bounded rationality and stochastic decision-making behaviour that is characteristic of actual social media users. The proposed framework is further extended through the incorporation of a controlled experimental setting, where in artificially inflated engagement indicators—such as likes and reposts—are employed to promote selected target topics.
