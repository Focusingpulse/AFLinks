Harper Keywords solar interior, solar neutrino problem, stellar condensation, iron core, nucleosynthesis Published July 1990 Journal The Toth-Maatian Review Volume 9 Number 2 No. of pages 7 Pages 4473-4479 An Alternative Theory Concerning the Solar Interior is a seven-page paper by Walter E. Currah and George W. Harper , both then of Tacoma, Washington, published in The Toth-Maatian Review in July 1990. It proposes that the Sun and other main-sequence stars have a non-reactive iron core with the nuclear furnace resting on top of it rather than at the centre — and that this follows from stars having condensed around a solid "seed" mass rather than out of gas alone. Abstract The paper survives only as a scan whose optical character recognition is badly damaged; what follows is a transcription. Recent neutrino observations have cast doubt on all existing models of the solar furnace and suggest we may have made a fundamental error in our initial postulations regarding the internal conditions of this and other stars on the main sequence. It is also noted that the general problem of discovering a mechanism for the condensation of stars out of the interstellar medium has not been resolved satisfactorily. In the present paper we explore the possibility that the two problems are related; that main sequence stars of the present generation may have a nonreactive core region with the nuclear furnace resting atop it. This would be a consequence of a "seed" mass of iron which serves as a condensation nucleus about which the necessary hydrogen gases condense to create a star.
