Clint Eastwood

· archived # 1,456,365
He has a very simple but powerful philosophy that keeps him moving forward while others his age might be slowing down. He refuses to give in to the idea that being old means being weak or useless. At 96 years old, Clint Eastwood is not just surviving; he is absolutely thriving. While most people his age have been retired for decades, this Hollywood icon is still waking up at dawn to head to a movie set. He is currently finishing his latest film, Juror No. 2, showing everyone that the fire inside him hasn't dimmed a bit. Clint Eastwood is a living legend who proves that aging is a state of mind. He doesn't spend his days looking at old photos or talking about the "good old days." Instead, he is focused on the next scene and the next big project. His secret isn't found in a gym or a special diet. It is found in his refusal to let a certain kind of attitude take over his brain. "I don't let the old man I've become enter my life," Eastwood says. This is his daily battle cry. He believes that the moment you start acting like an "old person," you invite in all the physical and mental problems that come with it. By keeping the "old man" outside, he keeps his spirit young, sharp, and ready to work. He is very clear about what he avoids. "I don't leave space for the old, critical, hostile, envious, complaining man, full of anger and recriminations," he explains.
