Built this 100 hp engine fueled with noble gases. Operating Principles—Papp Engine • 1. The Papp Engine operates like a standard internal combustion engine, except that "NO" fuel is consumed. • 2. The cylinders are sealed, require no fuel or air, and produce no exhaust. • 3. The cylinders are charged with a mixture of inert gases. These gases, when activated by a proprietary process, and then “ignited” with an electrical spark, explode, driving the piston downward, as in a conventional engine. • 4. Once the spark is stopped, the gases contract again, and the piston is drawn back up in the cylinder. • 5.
