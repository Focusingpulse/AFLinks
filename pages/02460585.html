Decker: "Antigravity gas" Hi Folks! Read this particular version of the claim that the universe is not only expanding but accelerating. This was I believe first proclaimed by orthodox science in the form of reports of spacecraft leaving our system which rather than slowing down, appeared to accelerate. The way this article was written made me think of the three phases of matter which are; manifestation (where a pattern is provided in which a material seed appears and continues to aggregate or precipitate as more matter and grow), maturation (where the mass reaches its maximum size and density) and dissolution (where the mass begins to degrade and break down into its constituent components). When thinking on two scales, the micro and the macro, the universe or portions thereof could be analogous to atoms, molecules and mass aggregations making up ever larger constructs, as has been covered in many sci-fi articles. It struck me that the expansion of what is being seen as a portion of the universe would indicate dissolution by expansion, that this portion of a vaster universe is dying, though it would not mean a lot to us because of the time scales involved. I guess the point here is the old 'as above, so below' adage showing that matter can absorb, stabilize and radiate away. It is the push, balance and pull that Keely experimented with by using resonance and phase conjugation. To my view, it is aether roiling onto itself to slow down by interference and crystallize as mass yet still absorb additional aether to feed it and keep it together, at some point, the mass 'fills up', like a sponge and no longer wants to absorb but now begins to radiate its precipitated/crystallized form of matter back
