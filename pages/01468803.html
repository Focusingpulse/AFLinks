Vector model of the atom

· archived # 1,468,803
In physics , in particular quantum mechanics , the vector model of the atom is a model of the atom in terms of angular momentum . It can be considered as the extension of the Rutherford-Bohr-Sommerfeld atom model to multi-electron atoms.
