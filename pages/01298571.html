A vector is a mathematical quantity that encompasses both magnitude , which represents the size or extent of the value, and direction, indicating where the value is pointing or moving towards. In contrast, a scalar is a type of value that only holds magnitude and does not have a specific direction associated with it. Vectors are commonly utilized in various fields such as physics, engineering, and mathematics to represent physical quantities like velocity, force, and displacement, while scalars are used to denote quantities such as speed, temperature, and mass. The distinction between vectors and scalars is fundamental in understanding how quantities are represented and manipulated in the realm of mathematics and applied sciences.
