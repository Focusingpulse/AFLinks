Academician Vladimir Fortov considers this invention to be a serious achievement. The whole lot of work performed under supervision of academician Gennadiy Mesyats allows creating generators emitting very short and high-power pulses. Peak capacity of such generator reaches billions of watts, comparable to nuclear power plant capacity. Above-mentioned developments allow producing new radiolocators with greater range and resolution than existing ones. Data, obtained form the research, can also be the basis for electromagnetic weapon development. Analysis of difference between responses is used for judging functional destruction of object.
