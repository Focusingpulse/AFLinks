GC2026-STP022 Comparative analysis of the influence of geomagnetic pulsations of various frequency ranges on the growth of geomagnetic-induced currents in power lines of the Murmansk region Vladimir B. Belakhovsky1 , Vjacheslav A. Pilipenko2 , Vasiliy N. Selivanov3 , Yaroslav A. Sakharov1 1 Polar Geophysical Institute, Apatity, Russia 2 O.Yu. Schmidt Institute of Physics of the Earth, Russian Academy of Sciences 3 Center for Physical and Technical Problems of Northern Energy, Federal Research Center, Kola Science Center, Russian Academy of Sciences, Apatity belakhov@mail.ru The paper presents a comparative analysis of the influence of geomagnetic pulsations of the Pg type and low￾frequency pulsations of the Pc5-6, Pi3 type on the growth of geomagnetically induced currents (GICs) in the power transmission lines (PTL) of the Murmansk region using magnetometer data at the PGI Loparskaya (LOP) observatory and data from the Vykhodnoy (VKH) substation located near Murmansk. The influence of Pg pulsations (giant pulsations) on the growth of the GICs has not previously been considered in the scientific literature, as these pulsations have a relatively small amplitude (within a few tens of nT) and are quite rare in ground-based magnetometer data. Pg pulsations belong to the Pc4 frequency range and have a period of ~1.5-2.5 minutes. It represent a resonant wave structure in the magnetosphere, excited by ring current protons as a result of the development of kinetic instabilities.
