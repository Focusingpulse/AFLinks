Think about it. If a device is truly O/U, then by definition it produces enough power to run itself, AND a load. Even if it needed a battery to get started, once it was up to speed a voltage regulator would then kick in and remove the battery from the circuit, provide enough power for itself and a load, and then dump the excess power to ground. The battery could then be physically removed from the device, and it would still "keep-on-ticking"!
