Please visit the website http://www.intechopen.com/books/advanced-electromagnetic-waves/the-electromagnetic-force-between-two-parallel-current-conductors-explained-using-coulomb-s-law Overview The Electromagnetic Force between Two Parallel Current Conductors Explained Using Coulomb's Law is a thirty-one-page book chapter by Jan Olof Jonson , published by IntechOpen in Advanced Electromagnetic Waves in November 2015. It argues that the attraction between parallel currents follows from Coulomb's law alone, once propagation delay and the Lorentz contraction are applied, and that the Lorentz force law is therefore unnecessary. Jonson places the chapter in a longer programme. He cites his 1997 paper in the Chinese Journal of Physics as having shown that the Lorentz force cannot explain the repulsive force between collinear currents in Ampère's bridge, and later papers as refuting Feynman's derivation of the Liénard–Wiechert potentials, disputing Grassmann's reconciliation with Ampère, and treating electromagnetic induction, radiation and the Sagnac effect on the same basis. The geometry is chosen deliberately. Rather than two idealised infinite wires, Jonson uses two rectangular circuits of side L laid alongside one another at a separation a ≪ L , so that a complete closed circuit is treated.
