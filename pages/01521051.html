Proceedings of the First International Symposium on Nonconventional Energy Book Author George D Hathaway Published 1982 Publisher Toronto; no publisher named in the record Pages 344 The proceedings of the First International Symposium on Non-Conventional Energy Technology , held on 23–24 October 1981 at the University of Toronto. George Hathaway , then a young Toronto engineer, was its general editor. The symposium was the first of a series, and one of the earliest occasions on which the loose community of free-energy, zero-point and homopolar-machine researchers met formally in one place. The papers collected here recur as citations across this wiki. The symposium The meeting was held over two days at the University of Toronto and drew together researchers in what its organisers called non-conventional energy: work outside the mainstream of energy physics on machines and effects said to draw power from unorthodox sources. Andrew Michrowski and the Planetary Association for Clean Energy, of Ottawa, were acknowledged as having contributed to its success, and PACE would later put out its own printing of the proceedings. The proceedings The volume appeared in Toronto in 1982, under Hathaway's general editorship. It was announced through the United States Department of Energy's technical reporting system in February 1983 under the report number CONF-8110214, and made available through NTIS in print and on microfiche — with the cataloguer's note that "portions are illegible in microfiche products", which gives some sense of how it was produced. A second printing was issued in 1987 by the Planetary Association for Clean Energy , carrying the ISBN 0919969186 and running to 335 pages. That printing is recorded separately on this wiki, with its cover and its full table of contents, as Proceedings of the First International Symposium on Nonconventional Energy Technology .
