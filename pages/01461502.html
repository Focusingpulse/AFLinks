Nobody does.” [ Richard Feynman ; QED: The Strange Theory of Light and Matter] The quantum electrodynamic vacuum or QED vacuum is the field-theoretic vacuum of quantum electrodynamics . It is the lowest energy state (the ground state) of the electromagnetic field when the fields are quantized. When Planck's constant is hypothetically allowed to approach zero, QED vacuum is converted to classical vacuum , which is to say, the vacuum of classical electromagnetism. Another field-theoretic vacuum is the QCD vacuum of the Standard Model. Wikipedia, QED Richard Feynman helped explain how light and matter affect one another. He built a theory called quantum electrodynamics also known as QED. This theory shows how charged particles such as electrons interact by sending tiny packets of light energy called photons back and forth. QED describes these interactions with great detail. When an electron moves or changes direction it can exchange a photon with another particle. This exchange controls how particles attract or repel each other.
