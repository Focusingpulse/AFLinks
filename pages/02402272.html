MolPhot2026-main030 Quantum-chemical modeling of the effect of substituent and silica substrate in charge￾transfer luminescent systems based on the DBMBF2 dye molecule Alexander A. Samolyga1,2 , Elena A. Rykova3,2 , Andrey A. Safonov3 1 National Research Center "Kurchatov Institute" 2 Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (State University) 3 Photochemistry Center of FNIC "Crystallography and Photonics" RAS kenegire@gmail.com Interaction of the dibenzoylmethanate boron difluoride (DBMBF2 ) and its pentafluorinated and methoxylated derivatives with the silica surface and the formation of exciplexes with benzene and toluene by the adsorbed dyes was studied using stationary and time-dependent density functional theory calculations. The energy and spectral characteristics of the complexes were calculated, and the geometries of the systems were analyzed. Based on the calculated Mulliken charges on the atoms, charge transfer in the complexes and corresponding changes upon the introduction of substituents into the DBMBF2 molecule and the adsorption of exciplexes on silica were estimated. The shifts in the fluorescence spectra of the studied systems upon the introduction of substituents and the adsorption of the dyes on silica are shown and explained. The calculations were performed in the ORCA quantum-chemical software package (v5.0.3) using the BHHLYP exchange￾correlation functional and the SVP (geometry optimization) and TZVP (energy characteristics) basis sets.
