Keely The peculiar conditions as associated with the gaseous elements of which water is composed , as regards the differential volume and gravity of its gases , make it a ready and fit subject of vibratory research . In submitting water to the influence of vibratory transmission , even on simple thirds , the high action induced on the hydrogen as contrasted with the one on the oxygen (under the same vibratory stream ) causes the antagonism between these elements that induces dissociation . The differential antagonistic range of motion , so favoring the antagonistic thirds as to become thoroughly repellant . The gaseous element thus induced and registered , shows thousands of times much greater force as regards tenuity and volume than that induced by the chemical disintegration of heat , on the same medium .
