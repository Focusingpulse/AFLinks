To: Faculty and Staff in the School of Nuclear Engineering From: Leah H. Jamieson Subject: Head’s Review Process Date: May 30, 2006 According to University policy and the CoE practice of periodic reviews of heads, we are beginning a formal review of Lefteri Tsoukalas as Head of the School of Nuclear Engineering (http://www.purdue.edu/policies/pages/human_resources/iv_14_3_print.htm ). We have employed an independent party, the Renewal and Transformation Group (RTG), to help us with the development of the surveys and the analysis of the responses. I will initiate the process by sending faculty, staff and some other Heads and/or members of my Cabinet a survey form to be completed and returned in sealed, confidential envelopes to my office within a two-week period. I also will complete a survey form that will include my assessment of the Head’s performance in areas of management and leadership. All survey forms will be collected from my office by the Renewal & Transformation Group, opened and used to create a summary report. You are asked to put your name, telephone number and email address on the survey form in order for the Renewal & Transformation Group to contact some of you to set up one-on-one interview sessions to obtain additional information on the strengths and weaknesses of the Head. Names, telephone numbers and email addresses will not be included in the summary report. The Renewal & Transformation Group will review the summary report with me and then I will meet with the Head to discuss the results of the evaluation.
