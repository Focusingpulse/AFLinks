Tesla proposed that greater energy levels could be created within resonant circuits by, using these terms, conservatively saving lots of nickels over time. What Tesla was pointing out had nothing to do with any overall work gain in the resonant circuit, but rather that the 'bank' could be emptied in very short time intervals to provide results and effects that could not otherwise be achieved. It is possible to have enormous energy levels belting around a tank circuit that are driven by relatively small excitation levels. This obviously does'nt mean you have a free energy device just because the input power is, say, 1 Watt and the tank circuit indicates hundreds of Watts trapped in the resononant system. Charles Wilde's experiment illustrates this. But you can extract high tank circuit energy levels to provide an effective short duration pusle of very high power levels. Consider the capacitor - you can trickle charge a cap with very small energy levels over long periods of time - but the 'instantaneous' discharge could kill you. That's the very effect that Tesla points out. Regards, Bill. At 14:01 15/08/98 -0500, Jerry W.
