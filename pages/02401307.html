GC2026-SGG006 The lithospheric thermal state under the Barents Sea from geomagnetic and surface wave data Alena I. Filippova1 , Sergey V. Filippov1 1 Pushkov Institute of Terrestrial Magnetism, Ionosphere and Radiowave Propagation of the Russian Academy of Sciences, Moscow, Russia Ponencia invitada aleirk@mail.ru The thermal state of the lithosphere influences the lithospheric strength and rheology and can provide insights into geodynamics. Recently, geomagnetic data, namely, Curie point depth (CPD) models, are widely applied to infer the lithospheric thermal state. In this study, we construct a new CPD distribution under the Barents Sea applying a centroid method and the EMAG2v3 lithospheric magnetic field model. This distribution is used to estimate surface heat flow and to reveal variations in lithospheric temperatures under some structures of the considered region. To confirm our results, S-wave velocities in the upper mantle are inverted from a distribution of Rayleigh wave group velocities. Lithospheric temperatures are modeled on their basis that allows us to trace variations in the thermal lithosphere thickness. The obtained results are self-consistent and demonstrate the following. Significant lithospheric thinning and heating under Svalbard are inferred. The colder and thicker lithosphere is observed under the eastern shelf (east of 35° E) in comparison with the lithosphere underlying its western part.
