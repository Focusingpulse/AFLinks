Scientists in Japan are now reporting development of an improved �biomass charcoal combustion heater� that they say could open a new era in sustainable and ultra-high efficiency home heating. Their study was published in ACS� Industrial & Engineering Chemistry Research, a bi-weekly journal. Wider use of biomass could tap that sustainable source of fuel and by their calculations cut annual carbon dioxide emissions by 4.46 million tons.
