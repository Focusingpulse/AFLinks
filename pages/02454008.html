Beaty With Podkletnov's announcement of reduced gravity over rotating superconductors comes the question: what shape is the distortion pattern? Is there a quick and easy way to detect and map forces produced by these devices? Here is an untried suggestion. Schlieren optics is traditionally used to map the 2-dimensional distribution of variation of refractive index of fluids, for example pressure distribution in shockwaves in air, temperature differences in fluid, etc. This same method can be harnessed into displaying a distortion of gravity force if the force is a significant percent of earth's gravity. Simply incorporate a shallow pool of reflective fluid into the optical path, allow the gravity field to distort the fluid surface, then view the result on the Schlierien system's screen.
