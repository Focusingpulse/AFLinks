GTM2026-main005 Dynamic Models of Coopetition with Different Discount Factors in Cournot Oligopoly on the Global Oil Market Guennady Ougolnitsky , Alexey Korolev gaugolnickiy@sfedu.ru The basic model is a dynamic version of the Cournot oligopoly [1] with the following features. First, oil-producing countries are considered as players instead of firms. Each oligopolist country divides its resources between oil production (competition as in the standard Cournot oligopoly) and the production of some public good (cooperation): for example, environmental protection, scientific research, and the fight against poverty and crime. Thus, the model introduces the effect of coopetition. Secondly, environmental pollution is taken into account: the volume of pollutants is a state variable for which a linear dynamic equation is written out. It is assumed that pollution increases directly in proportion to oil production and decreases inversely in proportion to the amount of pollution due to self-cleaning. Accordingly, each country's gain functionality includes three components: competitive income from oil sales, cooperative income from participation in the use of the public good, and pollution damage. Thus, for independent behavior (competition) of countries, the model is a differential game in normal form. For a detailed analysis of the distribution of the received cooperative winnings between individual players, it is natural to use games in the form of a characteristic function.
