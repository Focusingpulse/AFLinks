Please do not post images or attachments to the list as you agreed NOT to do in the guidelines....it takes up a tremendous amount of space and these images are not reflected in the archives. If you have volumes of information, DO NOT POST THEM to this list.... Post your information on a website, and post the URL to that website where all your drawings and ideas or results or experiments are TOGETHER. It is extremely DISCOURTEOUS to clog up peoples mailboxes with ream after ream of emails on a particular subject where a URL with a short description of what it contains would OFFER PEOPLE THE CHOICE of looking at your material, rather than having volumes of it rammed into their mailbox. Remember this is a discussion list...you post the information leading to the site or a short abstract that might evoke interest...if people want more, they can either contact you directly or visit your URL. The idea for a discussion list is stated in its very name...'DISCUSSION' to EVOKE discussion ABOUT a website, idea or comment posted or to participate in an ongoing There are many free websites out there who will give you up to 5MB of space to post your information and pictures...not only will you establish a web presence but it is so much easier for you to promote your ideas in that form by simply directing people to your website URL and let them read for themselves. K.I.S.S.
