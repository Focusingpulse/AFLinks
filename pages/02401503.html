GC2026-PERM022 Experience of application of the wavelet transform on marine magnetic anomalies to identify temporal cyclicity in the change of magnetic field Irina Demina1 , Sergei Merkuriev1 1 St. Petersburg branch of Pushkov Institute of Terrestrial Magnetism, Ionosphere and Radiowave Propagation of the Russian Academy of Sciences dim@izmiran.spb.ru We present the results of a wavelet transform study of the fine structure of linear marine magnetic anomalies formed at the high-spreading centers. The study used the results of systematic magnetic surveys conducted in the deep-sea Arabian and Somali basins, where intense ancient magnetic anomalies (A24–A26) formed during the high spreading stage (~5 cm/yr) and dating back to the Paleocene (52–58 Ma), as well as a number of short-period “tiny wiggle” marine magnetic anomalies. These anomalies on two conjugate profiles were reduced to the pole to remove the effect of induced magnetization on the shape of magnetic anomalies and interpreted using the continuous Morlet wavelet transform. It was found that the spectrum of the identified magnetic anomalies contains a long-wavelength trend and cyclicity with a period varying in the range of 0.6-0.8 Ma. This determines all the reversal anomalies and, depending on the phase ratio of this and the long-wavelength components, the observed short-duration, low-amplitude anomalies, which we propose to consider as excursions.
