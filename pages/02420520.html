COLD FUSION MAGAZINE DEBUT by Dineh Torres A new star has risen on the horizon of popularscientific publications. The name is "Cold Fusion." The magazine is bright, to say the least, both intellectually and visually, and promises more to come. The publisher is Wayne Green, who is known for starting Byte magazine (and a host of other computermagazines, books and software centers) and also the 2 FUSION FACTS well-known Digital Audio magazine. The editor is Eugene Mallove, who has been a science writer and proponent of cold fusion since its beginning. This first issue gives an overview of the history of the cold fusion research effort, a look at the people involved and the opposition against. Looking at the Contents page, you will find: "2001: The Coming Age of Hydrogen Power," by Arthur C. Clarke.
