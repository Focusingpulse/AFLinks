purify thy mind

· archived # 1,482,743
Rosarium Philosophorum "Alphidius: Know, My Son, that thou canst not obtain this science until thou purify thy mind onto God, and till God knoweth thee to have a staid and upright mind, and then he will make thee to reign and rule over the world." [ Rosarium Philosophorum ] Robert Adams "The world that you are in, is a creation of your own mind. When the mind becomes still, the world begins to disappear. And you’re in divine harmony and joy. Therefore, happiness comes to you when you stop thinking , when you stop judging, when you stop being afraid. When you begin to contemplate what is happiness. All the answers are within you. Everything you’re looking for is within you, everything. Nobody can help but your Self . Know who you are. You are the power. All the power of the universe is within you." [Silence of the Heart: Dialogues with Robert Adams ] Buddha “We are what we think. All that we are arises with our thoughts. With our thoughts, we make the world.” [ Buddha ] See Also Awakening Consciousness Mind Mind of God Philosophers Stone
