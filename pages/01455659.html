Abeggs Rule

· archived # 1,455,659
For use in regard to a helical periodic system. If the maximum positive valence exhibited by an element be numerically added to its maximum negative valence , there is evidently a tendency for the sum to equal 8. This tendency is exhibited especially by the elements of the 4th, 5th, 6th, and 7th groups and is known as Abegg's Rule.
