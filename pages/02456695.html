Far from the media crowd surrounding the cellular controversy , however, several researchers have expressed the opinion that studies of ELF's potential hazards are more likely to affect American industry and society in the long run. ELF, defined as electromagnetic radiation at frequencies below 300 Hz, includes the 60-cycle radiation emitted by everything from high-tension lines to toasters to video display terminals.
