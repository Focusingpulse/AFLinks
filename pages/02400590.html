Kulik September 2026 Abstract An observer interrogating an unknown transformation does not recover the transformation. It recovers whichever equivalence class survives its access constraints, and the class depends on what the observer is permitted to do rather than on how much of it it does. This paper fixes a finite model in which that dependence is computed exactly and, in the central cases, proved: a permutation σ of a finite set, an observer whose resolution is a partition P, and four access regimes running from passive trajectory observation through single-step intervention to trajectory-tracking intervention. The organising identification is elementary once stated. The record of a single-step intervening reader is exactly the contingency table N(P,σ) with entries N_ij = |Bᵢ ∩ σ⁻¹B_j|, so the recovered object is the corresponding double coset KσK, where K is the Young subgroup of within-block relabellings, and its size is the classical contingency count (∏|Bᵢ|!)² / ∏N_ij!.
