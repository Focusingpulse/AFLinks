Bayesian network and information theory Ait-taleb Nabil * Abstract In this paper, we will expose the BIC score expressed as a function of the Bayesian network’s entropy. We will expose the Gaussian multivariate probability and compute its joint diﬀerential entropy and conditional dif- ferential entropy. We will then give the deﬁnition of the Bayesian network and how show to compute its entropy. We will also expose the entropy of two particular Bayesian networks: one with a chain rule and one without edges.
