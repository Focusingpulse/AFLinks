GA 98. The Mysteries

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 25 December 1907 · archived # 2,420,084
, Cologne Translated by Unknown The Mysteries [ 1 ] If you were in the Cathedral last night you could have seen written there in illuminated lettering: C. M. B. As you will all know, these letters represent the names of the so-called Three Holy Kings, according to the tradition of the Christian Church: Caspar, Melchior, Balthasar. These names awaken quite special memories for Cologne. An old legend tells us that some time after they had become bishops and died their bones had been brought here.
