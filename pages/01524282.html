Theoretical Work By Ernest Sternglass Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Speaker Mark Creek-water Dorazio Date 28 October 2023 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording Theoretical Work By Ernest Sternglass was a live session presented by Mark Creek-water Dorazio on 28 October 2023 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. Mark Creekwater leads a discussion of Sternglass who describes the structure of all the particles discovered in the last 100 years as combinations of relativistic electrons and positrons, with no reference to quarks. Summary Mark Creekwater Dorazio 's account of Ernest Sternglass 's theory of matter, the first of the two talks he gave the society. Ian J Cowan hosted. Dorazio was late — a time zone confusion — and arrived using his sister's computer, his own being too old; the first twenty minutes were filled by Lori Anne Gardi , who had come in through the social media feed and asked to speak, with Dennis P Allen standing ready to give a whole talk on particle models if needed. Gardi's story Gardi keeps Sternglass's Before the Big Bang on her desk as a good-luck charm, and told two stories about him. The first is from the book. Sternglass, newly graduated and working in a laboratory, wrote to Einstein asking to discuss his theory, and was invited to his home; they talked in the garden. As he was leaving, Einstein asked whether he intended to go to graduate school, and, on being told yes, urged him not to — his originality would be crushed out of him. Stay in applied physics, study on your own what interests you, work on the fundamental problems in your spare time so that you need not worry when nothing comes of it for long periods, and make your mistakes in private. Always keep a cobbler's job. "Don't do what I have done": at the Kaiser Wilhelm Institute he had no duties but to wake and solve the problems of the universe every morning, and nobody can do that.
