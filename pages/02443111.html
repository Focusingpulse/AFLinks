06/27/13 - Electric Water Cannon (Most people don't know it, but in an old Scientific American article there was a story about Edison working on a high pressure water cannon that uses salted water to carry an electric current. I don't recall if they actually did it or he just designed it or tested it. The idea was to protect forts in the wild west from "marauding indians"...hit them with a blast of electrified water and thats all she wrote. Didn't kill them, just knocked them to the ground where they could be picked up at will. I mention this because of the robbery of my house which is NOW protected with razor wire, videocam motion detectors front and back which upload video to my FTP site, window alarms and now I want to electrocute anyone who enters my property when my gates are locked. I could string wire or chicken wire over my windows and electrify it with pure AC, a fence charger or a 15,000 volt neon transformer. Then I remembered I had 3 lab pumps that have plastic impellers so that reminded me of Edison and the electrified water cannon. I'm thinking a dense mix of salt feeding two pumps from above that would pump a continuous stream of very salty water carrying sufficient voltage and current to knock them on their butts. So I did a search to see if anyone had been working with this and yes, there are several systems with an average range of 20 feet. All I need is about 5-8 feet or so. Years ago at a Tesla conference, Dr. Corum and son described how they were able to massively extend the length of a bolt of high voltage electricity by firing a stream of precisely timed BB pellets. The high voltage would jump from one BB to the next until it reached the target. They showed photos but I can't find anything like it. I wonder if projecting timed very salty water droplets would transfer high voltage like the Corum's achieved using brass BBs?
