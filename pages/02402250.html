MolPhot2026-main007 Conformational-spectral analysis of aromatic amino acids zwitteriones Irina V. Krauklis1 , Anna A. Nazarova1 , Yuri В. Chizhov1 , Anna V. Shurukhina1 1 St Petersburg University st068699@student.spbu.ru Raman spectroscopy, especially surface-enhanced Raman spectroscopy (SERS), has significant potential for non￾invasive cancer diagnostics. It is known that disruption of aromatic amino acid (AAAs) metabolism, manifested by their increased content in cells and biological fluids, is a common biochemical feature of many oncological diseases [1]. Therefore, conformational spectroscopic study of phenylalanine (Phe), tyrosine (Tyr), and tryptophan (Trp) as potential biomarkers using Raman spectroscopy and B3LYP(+GD3)/def2TZVPP density functional theory is of interest. Under physiological conditions, AAAs exist in the zwitterionic form NH3 +–CHR–COO- (ZW), which determines their physicochemical and spectral properties. Due to the presence of two rotational angles χ1 and χ2 in the aromatic side chains, the zwitterions Phe(ZW), Tyr(ZW) and Trp(ZW) have several stable structural conformers that need to be considered in spectroscopic studies. The influence of the water solvent was taken into account by us within the framework of the discrete-continuum models Phe(ZW)+9H2 O, Tyr(ZW)+11H2 O and Trp(ZW)+9H2 O (Fig. 1), which ensured the saturation of the functional groups –NH3 +, –COO- and –OH with hydrogen bonds.
