Open Debate on Unified Theory & Composition of Electron, Proton, Neutron Rati Ram Sharma Talk Type Live session Speaker Rati Ram Sharma Date 7 November 2009 Time 07:00 (US/Pacific) Platform DimDim Video Conferencing Participants 5 Open Debate on Unified Theory & Composition of Electron, Proton, Neutron was a live session presented by Rati Ram Sharma on 7 November 2009 in the Natural Philosophy Alliance live session series. Abstract Prof. Rati Ram Sharma got disillusioned with relativity & quantum theory even when he was a physics student (M.Sc.) during 1952-54. He has since been working on a realistic alternative to replace the modern theories of physics & cosmology. He has already published five books on Unified Theory in 1990, 1997, 1998, 2002, 2008 besides the 1984 book: Molecular Homoeopathy. He made a combined presentation of his four papers at the 16th NPA conference 26 May at Storrs besides the Videoconferences 3/15 & 4/25. He now proposes to defend his theory in an open debate spread over a series of Videoconferences to which all are most welcome to participate with well argued and reasoned comments. Prof. Sharma will thankfully acknowledge your valuable contributions in the preface of his forthcoming book entitled: UNIFIED THEORY: The 21st Century Physics, Cosmology & More.
