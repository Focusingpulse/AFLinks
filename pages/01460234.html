Kingdom of God

· archived # 1,460,234
Christ Returns - Speaks His Truth "In the gospels are frequent references to my parables describing the reality of the Kingdom of Heaven or Kingdom of God , whichever term the gospellers used but nowhere has any attempt been made to reach into the words themselves, explore the figures of speech, or lift out the spiritual meaning of the Kingdom of God or the Kingdom of Heaven ." [ Christ Returns - Speaks His Truth , Letter 2 , page 1] Paracelsus "There is a light in the spirit of man illuminating everything, and by which he may even perceive supernatural things. Those who seek in the light of external Nature know the things of Nature; those who seek knowledge in the light of man know the things above Nature, which belong to the kingdom of God."
