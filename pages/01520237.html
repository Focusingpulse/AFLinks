Gravity, Electricity, Matter Book Author Wilbur L Mayo Language English Published 2003 Publisher Reality Science Pages 124 ISBN 097258580X Gravity, Electricity, Matter is a 2003 book by Wilbur L Mayo , published by Reality Science. It is a short work — 124 pages — setting out conclusions Mayo reached about gravitation, electricity and the structure of matter, both during and after a long career as an aeronautical and space-flight analyst with NACA and NASA. The book's claims Mayo presents the book as the resolution of problems he encountered before and after his retirement from NASA. Its publisher's description advertises sixty-five discoveries , summarised in the first twenty-three pages without equations, tables or graphs, with the supporting argument given afterwards in what are described as simple equations avoiding algebra and calculus. Three claims are singled out as the major ones: a relation between gravity and electricity ; an account of the strength of matter following from that relation; a distinction between time reckoned by days and time reckoned by the seasons , which Mayo holds to be crucially different. The deliberate avoidance of higher mathematics is presented as a feature rather than a limitation: the book is pitched at a reader who is expected to follow the reasoning without specialist training. The author Mayo's technical background is a matter of record rather than of claim. He worked in the Theoretical Mechanics Division of NACA at the point when it became NASA; his NACA papers on the hydrodynamic impact of seaplanes on water, and on impact loads in systems with elastic modes, are held in the NASA technical reports collection and date from the middle 1940s to the early 1950s.
