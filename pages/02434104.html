Lunar Ark - 09/22/97 The Lunar Ark - 09/22/97 There have been many millenial claims of looming catastrophes in the last few years, with the ever present admonition that time is running out and that we need to get on the ball and take steps to save as much of mankind as we can. The proposed catastrophes include tidal waves, flooding, massive crop failure, drought, desertification, tornados and windstorms, volcanic upheavals, radical temperature changes, genetic mutation and disease due to increased UV exposure, extended periods of darkness to cause freezing including crop and animal loss. This includes a near total failure of public utilities and services which would close down industry and businesses, as well as associated conflagrations as people take whatever steps they must to ensure the survival of themselves and their families. Being a firm believer that gravity can be controlled and that energy can be freely extracted from the fabric of space, I guess these warnings have preyed on my subconscious more than I realized. The options for survival include going underground, moving to mountain tops, building giant boats like the Ark, moving into space stations or colonizing another planet. If massive flooding occurs, the idea of living underground and being subject to possible internal flooding and earthquake is not acceptable. Earthquakes also dispose of the idea of mountain top security. Our best option is to move out into space and that is yet another reason why we need to get on with research necessary to make free energy and gravity control a reality. On Monday the 22nd of September 1997, I had a most peculiar dream that presented something that I'd never heard of before. There was a group of friends and acquaintances who had hired a riverboat for the weekend to travel around a very large volcanic lake.
