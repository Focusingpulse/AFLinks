GC2026-STP074 Study of growth and decay rates of sunspot groups in solar cycle 24 based on Greenwich data Ksenia Kozlova1 , Nadezhda Zolotova1 1 St Petersburg University, St. Petersburg, Russia st107433@student.spbu.ru This work investigates the rates of growth and decay of sunspot groups. According to the classical concept (the Gnevyshev-Waldmeier rule), the evolution of sunspot-group area during the decay phase is well described by a linear law. Our aim is to define quantitative patterns in the evolution of sunspot groups at different phases of solar cycle 24. The Royal Greenwich Data Observatory data of the period of 2009 – 2019 are analyzed. The rates of growth and decay are considered separately for the cycle rising phase, the maximum epoch, and the declining phase. Distribution histograms of growth and decay rates are also obtained, which allowed for a comparative analysis of the evolutionary characteristics of the sunspot groups depending on the phase of the solar cycle.
