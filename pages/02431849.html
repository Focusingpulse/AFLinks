A few upgrades to the PATHS websites

· archived # 2,431,849
There have been a few upgrades made to the PATHS Websites: 1. The "Scroll Bar" on the home page now shows featured PATHS Modules instead of all of them. This allows the home page to load faster, and makes the scroll bar easier to use. 2. On the "Products" page there is a new button, "Featured Modules". Clicking this button will display the same Modules that are on the "Scroll Bar" on the home page. These will change periodically. 3. On the Home Page, the words Theater Modules are now clickable and lead to the Products page. 4. On the Home Page, the image of the Theater is now clickable and leads to the "Theater Sampler" video.
