Core hypothesis (quoted): An insulated conducting plate elevated in open air, connected through a capacitor to ground, accumulates charge from the environment (radiant energy) that a load can draw — per Tesla's US patents 685,957 and 685,958 (1901).

Variable checklist — with modern affordable equivalents

Safety & calibration

Home scale is low-voltage and safe; keep the plate below any live line, use a high-impedance meter, and never attach to mains.

Modern interest in atmospheric electricity and energy harvesting has revived exactly the question Tesla posed — how much usable energy can an elevated insulated plate draw from the environment? Cheap multimeters and data logging make the measurement rigorous.
