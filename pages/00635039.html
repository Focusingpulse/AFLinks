Hodge classes Bin Wang (汪 镔) April 10, 2026 Abstract We show that on a complex projective manifold X, for G = R or Q, a class in Hp,p(X; Z) ⊗ G is represented by a particular type of an infinite series of subvarieties. Contents 1 Introduction 1 2 Proof 2 A Extraction of algebraic cycles 5 1 Introduction Let X be a complex projective manifold. For G = R or Q and a non-negative p ≤ dim(X), a class is called a Hodge class if u is in Hp,p(X; Z) ⊗ G. More specifically, a class in Hp,p(X; Z) ⊗ Q is called a rational Hodge class, and a class in Hp,p(X; Z)⊗R is called a real Hodge class. In this paper, we prove that the Hodge class is approximated by algebraic cycles in a particular way called an infinite cycle class. We recall the definition (see [4]). Definition 1.1.
