Just a houmorus moment

· archived # 1,507,159
Hi! Ever been stung by a yellow jacket? I have upon a few occasions and have noticed the tendency toward a primal "I will dive off the roof without a thought" reaction to them. Know what I mean? Where I am living currently in northern Nevada I go out and wander around in the mountains for exercise and to find interesting mine shafts to peer into and I can honestly tell you that Rattle snakes have the exact same effect. I was climbing a hill this evening near a rock outcropping and heard that buzz & without a thought I pole vaulted off the hill. I might break my neck but hey....no bites on me! Gets the heart rate up too!
