The Cathode used is a pure tungsten rod ( Th-loaded W ) 6 mm diameter and 45 mm length from tungsten welding rods. The Anode used is composed of stainless steel mesh maintained with a stainless steel shaft. All the wires connections are made with a 1.5 mm 2 copper flexible wire gained with silicon.
