Mallove to Branover, January 30, 2004 To AKRONOS Main Page Table of Contents Gene Mallove's letter to Herman Branover, January 30, 2004 January 30, 2004 Dr. Herman Branover Energetics Technologies Ltd. 189 W. 89th Street New York, NY 10024 Dear Herman, What a great pleasure and a joy it was to meet you for the first time and to have such an extended conversation with you! I thank you so very much for your kind hospitality at The Box Tree, which was such a warm place with very excellent food. I do hope that you managed to be able to bring all those Infinite Energy magazines and books back with you. (I believe there are two issues - #4 and #40 - that I did not give you, so I will send them to you by mail.) We really needed much more time to discuss the many other things about the Correa work on PAGD reactors, and their plans for the closely related (cold) fusion reactors that they have in mind - which would generate electricity, helium, and heat simultaneously. May I suggest that if you have some time in the next two weeks that I come to New York again, but this time bringing with me Uri Soudak? The three of us could discuss the possibilities for getting funding support in a parallel cooperative effort with the one now going on at Energetics Technologies. Please let me know your thoughts about this. My great fear is that the existing program in Israel, as excellent as it is in what it has already achieved, may not succeed to the fullest extent that it needs to. In fact, it may well fail to reach the stage of a prototype that could be commercialized. This has been the history, so far, of all attempts to do the work empirically - without understanding the basic mechanisms. I think there is a very good chance that the Correas fully understand the best way to move forward. I want to give you and the investor - whoever this "Mr. X" is - the best possible chance to succeed.
