The theory is that the electrostatic charge of the earth responds by greatly quickened vibrations to the manifestation of energy on the surface of the sun. But this is simply telegraphy without wires across an interval of 92,000,000 of miles. It is practically instantaneous, too. This fact was recognized years before a ready explanation of it was found in the close correspondence between the velocity of the electric vibration and that of light. Nearly 40 years ago the eruption lasting only 5 minutes of a bright spot on the face of the sun was found to have an almost simultaneous accompaniment of a general disturbance of magnetic needles and of the traveling of electric wires all over the world by so-called “earth currents.” The electrical storm in the sun had an immediate response in a profound disturbance of what is known as the earth’s magnetism.
