Ether Occurrence, Part 2 Book Author P D Prussov Original title Явление эфира. Часть 2 Country Ukraine Language Russian Series Явление эфира ( The Phenomenon of the Ether ), four parts, 1992–1998 Published Nikolaev (Mykolaiv), Ukraine, 1994 Ether Occurrence, Part 2 is the 1994 second instalment of Явление эфира — The Phenomenon of the Ether — a four-part monograph issued in Russian at Nikolaev, now Mykolaiv in southern Ukraine, by the physics teacher P D Prussov between 1992 and 1998. It is not a free-standing book but one brochure in a serialised argument: that light, charge, the fields, the elementary particles and the planetary systems are all built out of a single gas-like medium, the ether , and that physics went wrong at the moment it agreed to do without one. The title used here is the literal rendering carried by the Worldwide List of Dissident Scientists catalogue. Явление in Prussov's title means the manifestation or showing-forth of the ether rather than an occurrence of it; The Phenomenon of the Ether is the closer English title, and is how the work is cited in the Russian-language literature. The four parts Prussov, born in 1925 and a lecturer in higher mathematics at the Nikolaev Shipbuilding Institute until his retirement in 1989, began publishing his ether physics only in his late sixties. The four parts appeared, in his own words, на благотворительной основе — on a charitable basis, meaning they were paid for by well-wishers and given away rather than sold through a trade publisher: Part 1 — Явление эфира . Nikolaev: RIP "Rionika", 1992. 134 pp. Part 2 — Явление эфира. Часть 2 . Nikolaev, 1994. Part 3 — Явление эфира. Часть 3 . Nikolaev, 1996. Part 4 — Явление эфира. Часть 4 . Nikolaev, 1998. The sequence was then folded into a single volume, Physics of the Ether ( Физика эфира , Nikolaev: Privat-Poligrafiya, 2003, 286 pp.), which Prussov described as the first encyclopedia of the ether and which is the only one of his books to have been digitised.
