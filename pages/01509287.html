Methane Gas is cheap and easy to produce. All you need is a large container, water, and plant and/or animal waste. Some time for the oxygen to be sucked up by the aerobic bacteria so the anaerobic bacteria can form and begin to break down the waste to produce methane. Once the methane begins to pour out of, lets say, a 5 gallon (~20L jug) a constant flame is easily maintained. A constant heat source or methane source can be used for dozens of different electric generators. Mind you one 5 gallon (~20L jug) is not enough to power the average home (at ~12kw), but it can certainly prove a concept and is cheap to do.
