The Adsitt Motor generator

· archived # 1,511,882
Does anyone know Don Adsitt or his motor unit he built a few years ago? I can't seem to find his web site or anything current on him. I forget the details of this motor but I bought one from him pre-built when he had the web site theverylastpageoftheinternet.com - which is no longer there other than one of those domain parking ad pages with ads related to the former site interest. Don's motor used a coil, 2 9v batteries and a small circuit with a transistor or Mosfet, an LED and neon bulb, couple diodes, resistor and cap. It has 4 magnets in the rotating part that pass by a coil and when it runs it charges one battery while using the other. It runs a very long while on one 9 volt battery IIRC. I'm interested if there is anything else I can do with this or if anyone is familiar with it. It seems somewhat similar to some of Bedini's work. I can't remember if it was a bifilar wound coil but I think it may be.
