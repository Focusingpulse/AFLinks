Title An Epiphany On Gravity Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Ben Solomon Keywords gravity Published 2001 Journal Journal of Theoretics Volume 3 Number 6 No. of pages 8 Abstract The laws of physics are invariant with respect to frame of reference because the cumulative probability density of a particle cloud is an unity for any frame of reference. It is theorized with supporting data herein that time dilation causes gravity and not the other way around. With a stationary particle, it is demonstrated how a particle's probability cloud can become distorted under time dilation. This distortion of the probability cloud causes the center of mass to shift in the direction of time dilation. This effect, in the presence of a continuous non-linear time dilation well, is called "gravity". Solomon proposes that gravity is caused by time dilation rather than the reverse: that a gradient in time dilation distorts a particle's quantum probability cloud, shifting its centre of mass down the gradient, and that this shift is what we call gravitational attraction. Four axioms set up the argument. A particle is a probability cloud whose cumulative detection probability is one. Every particle at rest has a probability density invariant in a "velocity-space" with coordinates ( s x / t x , s y / t y , s z / t z ), allowing different time dilation along each axis. The volume a particle occupies in velocity-space is the volume of its probability function. And the mass of a particle is equivalent to that volume.
