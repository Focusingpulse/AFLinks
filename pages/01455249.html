16.18 - Magnetism - Feynman

· archived # 1,455,249
It is not possible to understand the magnetic effects of materials in any honest way from the point of view of classical physics. [Richard Feynman , 1965 Nobel Prize winning physicist.] We gotta love Feynman's honesty. He demolishes arrogance in pursuit of truth . I agree with him that orthodox science does not have so much as a clue concerning the innermost functionings of either electricity or magnetism . Therefore they do not have an explanation we can work with.
