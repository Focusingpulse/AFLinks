The transmission involved the generation of longitudinal ether waves. Whether Tesla accomplished this is not known, but the idea was considered by other notables such as Kelvin and Maxwell. Kelvin* considered it possible to generate “longitudinal waves in the luminiferous ether" thru the phenomena of displacement current (capacity current dD/at). He goes on to indicate his feeling that these waves must be faster than light, as the longitudinal waves in a steel rod move with much greater velocity than the transverse waves.
