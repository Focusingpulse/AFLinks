Tesla’s wonderful discovery by which all this is to be accomplished, is as simple as it is great. He utilizes the attractive power of the common horseshoe magnet, only Tesla’s new magnet is in the form of a ring, which surrounds the axle or driving shaft of the machine to which it is applied. A current of electricity is then turned on, which sets into motion the circular electro-magnetic force, and instantly starts the shaft to spinning around at an inconceivable rate of speed. “It reminds me of nothing so much as of the human heart,” said Dr. Franklin Miles, the great Chicago specialist in diseases of the heart and nerves, “that marvelous and much neglected motor of the body by which the life current is forced through the arteries, veins and capillaries. It is said that the least flaw in the Tesla circular magnet changes the rotary current to one so erratic that the machinery to which it is applied is instantly rent to atoms.
