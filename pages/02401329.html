GC2026-STP008 Electric field at the dayside magnetopause in case of southward IMF Nikolai V. Erkaev1 1 Institute of Computational Modeling, Siberian Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences erkaev@icm.krasn.ru The objective of this study is to find an analytical relationship between the tangential electric field at the dayside magnetopause and solar wind parameters for a negative B z component of the interplanetary magnetic field (IMF). The magnetic field, plasma density, and pressure in the magnetosheath were obtained from the MHD solution for the solar wind flow around the magnetosphere for various Alfvén-Mach numbers. To calculate the tangential electric field at the magnetopause, the obtained values of the magnetic field strength and plasma parameters were used, as well as the dimensionless reconnection rate (ratio of the normal plasma velocity to the Alfven speed ), which is a decreasing function of the local plasma beta parameter and the clock angle. The maximum reconnection rate corresponds to the case of low plasma beta and antiparallel magnetic fields (clock angle =180 degrees). At the subsolar magnetopause, the ratio of the electric field intensity at the subsolar magnetopause to the solar wind electric field is a slightly increasing function of the solar wind Alfvén-Mach number and a decreasing function of the clock angle. By integrating the tangential electric field along the reconnection line, a formula for the total electric potential difference was derived.
