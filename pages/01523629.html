Sheridan is an American engineer, automotive-safety analyst, and independent researcher based in Dearborn, Michigan. Trained in the physical sciences, he spent much of his professional career in the automobile industry before becoming a prominent automotive-safety whistleblower and expert witness. He is also associated with the Electric Universe and Plasma Cosmology community of alternative cosmology.
