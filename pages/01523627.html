His stated areas of interest include Thorium power , fusion energy , quantum gravity , and the Electric Universe model. Biography Gayet resides in McLean, Virginia. He is listed among the researchers of the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society (CNPS), a community of independent investigators who examine and propose alternatives to aspects of mainstream physics and cosmology. Work Gayet's interests span several topics that are debated within the dissident-science community. In the area of energy, he is associated with work on Thorium power and fusion energy as potential approaches to power generation. In physics and cosmology, his interests extend to quantum gravity
