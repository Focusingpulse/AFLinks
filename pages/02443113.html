06/27/13 - The Dual Interlocked Tetrahedral Structure and Beaming (Many years ago I met Bob Dratch at a conference where he demonstrated his microwave horn invention that could actually measure physical pain. He called volunteers from the audience to scan them. The machine normally made a steady squeal at a low volume. When it detected pain, depending on intensity, it would increase in volume and I think pitch. One woman stood on the stage as he scanned her. When he came to her shoulders the machine volume increased. He asked if she was in pain "there" and "there" where the machine was squealing loudly and she said yes, very much so. He asked if she knew why and she said she had gone to one of the hotel rooms the night before and was testing kinesiology (muscle testing) on various items which strained her muscles to make them very sore this day. Bob did this on several volunteers and we were all amazed. He said he hoped his machine would be used by hospitals, clinics, insurance companies where many people fake pain because it can't be measured normally but his invention could quantify the difference between true physical pain versus imagined pain for whatever reasons, usually insurance scams. In my opinion Dratch is clearly a genius and has many other curious and useful inventions. So I did a search and found this paper.
