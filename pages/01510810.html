Lindemann Motor + Tesla Switch

👤 Nikola Tesla · · archived # 1,510,810
Suppose you had a three pole [Peter] Lindemann Motor switched like a Tesla Switch and a three battery system using the third battery as the recovery? The motor itself could propel an energizer of some sort to charge and power a load. The switching best requires a commutator and alters position of the three batteries in the circuit.
