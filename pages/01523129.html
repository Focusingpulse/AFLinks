His argument is that the word was coined when astronomers had only telescopes and their eyes. Michell's dark stars were dark to him; Cygnus X-1 is the brightest persistent source of hard X-rays up to 60 keV, and the binary galaxy system found in 2000 shows the 21-centimetre line. Contemporary astronomy observes from radio waves to gamma rays, and these particles are not dark at all — they self-annihilate and radiate over eighteen orders of magnitude of rest energy, and ordinary particles are the by-product.
