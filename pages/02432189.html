Clarification on Modules

· archived # 2,432,189
There have been a few questions regarding some of our new Modules. Eat Right vs. Take it Off Skin Repair vs. Defying Gravity, Clear Skin, Young Again Grow Your Gratitude vs. Successful Living Series Deserving vs. Self Esteem At first glance, the titles on some of these Modules may lead you to believe that they are the same or similar in nature, that there is overlap between them. If you will read the Full Description on each one, you will see that they are very different and there is little or no overlap at all. Here is a brief overview (read the Full Descriptions to get the full picture). Eat Right is designed for those that are 30 lbs or more overweight and designed to reduce appetite. Take it Off is designed to encourage better metabolism, desire for healthy foods, desire to excercise. Skin Repair is designed to help your body repair damaged skin anywhere on your body...scars, burns etc. Defying Gravity is designed to hydrate skin, increase collagen, reduce pore size on the face and neck only. Clear Skin addresses the root causes of acne. Young Again addresses reversing the aging process via a multitude of factors. Grow Your Gratitude is specifically designed to increase an Attitude of Gratitude. The Successful Living Series has many of the same instructions in them, but they are only a small part of the entire Modules. The Deserving Module addresses the specific subconscious mindset regarding your self-worth, importance and how deserving you are to participate in ALL the Universe has at your disposal. Self Esteem and Confidence Module addresses self-talk, chemical imbalances, feelings of safety, sense of humor, etc. Again, the Full Descriptions will help you decide which is best for your situation. If, after reading them, you have any questions or comments regarding these Modules, post them here.
