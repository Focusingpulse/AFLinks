Collapse of GRT by Sankar Hajra Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Date 2 August 2025 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording Collapse of GRT by Sankar Hajra was a live session on 2 August 2025 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. Electromagnetic fields, electric charges, and light possess momentum and energy that we can experience with our sense organs. Therefore, these are real physical entities (objects). All physical objects are subject to the action of gravity, and so are those entities, too. Implications for General Relativity Theory. Hosted by Ian Cowan. Summary The second of Sankar Hajra 's two talks, following the same method — only Newton and Maxwell, no theory of his own — and turning from special relativity to general relativity. The session was again dogged by technical trouble: audio feedback throughout, difficulty getting the slides shared, and a request from the host that the speaker slow down. The premise carried over Where the first talk established that electromagnetic fields possess momentum and energy and are therefore real physical objects subject to gravity, this one extends the same reasoning to electric charges . They too are carried with the Earth and experience the Coriolis force when moving relative to its surface — and this, Hajra argued, is enough to account for the results general relativity claims. He restated the boundary he had drawn before, and which he regards as the crux. The classical and relativistic calculations agree for point charges, line currents and point-charge radiation. They diverge for extended charged bodies, surface and volume currents, and extended-body radiation — and they diverge most of all for the case of an electromagnetic system at rest on the Earth observed from a moving frame.
