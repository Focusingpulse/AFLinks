Scientific Theory Name Prime Matter Particles Type Particle / matter-creation theory Author(s) Neal Adams Keywords prime matter, pair production, positron, electron, matter creation, Growing Earth , Growing Universe Year 2000s Website http://nealadams.com Prime Matter Particles is the particle theory proposed by the American comic-book artist and independent theorist Neal Adams (1941–2022) to supply the missing mechanism for his Growing Earth — the proposition that the Earth and the other bodies of the solar system are steadily increasing in size and mass because new matter is continuously created within them. Adams held that there is only one kind of particle in the universe, which he called the prime matter particle . The particle is a bound electron–positron unit; the electron and the positron are not two species but two halves of the one thing, separated only when a prime matter particle is broken. An unbroken ocean of these particles, he argued, fills space from edge to edge, and the process conventionally called pair production is not the creation of a pair from energy but the splitting of a prime matter particle that was already there. Where a natural magnetic field keeps the halves apart instead of letting them recombine, the result is new matter — and in the core of a planet that new matter accumulates, so the planet grows. The theory is Adams' answer to the standing objection against expansion tectonics: that the reconstructions may look convincing but no known process can add the required mass. It is the least accepted part of his work, and the part from which even most expansion researchers on this wiki keep their distance while accepting the geological case. The problem it was meant to solve Adams' geological argument, set out in his animations and in his paper Subduction: The Extent and Duration (2011), was that the continents close onto a globe of roughly half the present diameter with no oceans at all, and that observed subduction is far too limited
