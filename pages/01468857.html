Volt

· archived # 1,468,857
The volt (symbol: V) is the SI derived unit of electromotive force , commonly called "voltage". It is also the unit for the related but slightly different quantity electric potential difference (also called " electrostatic potential difference "). It is named in honor of the Italian physicist Alessandro Volta (1745-1827), who invented the voltaic pile , possibly the first chemical battery .
