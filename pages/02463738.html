New module experiment for me: Your Golf Game~Get the Edge

· archived # 2,463,738
For a very long time I have wanted to start playing golf and finally got going a few months back. I have had tremendous success on the PATHS sports module in the past so I have decided that next week I will go on PATHS Get The Edge Golf module. It always seemed like a tough game for me to get started in because you have to play with others and I never wanted to go out there and ruin anyone's day by playing bad and holding them up. Surprisingly, after starting I wasn't too terrible, but lately I have had extremely mixed results, sometimes doing OK, sometimes playing terrible, but never playing with any consistency. I have read up on the "Golf Game: Get the Edge" module and I am very interested in many of the things it addresses, especially swing accuracy and consistency. I will be trying it out over the next few months and will be reporting my results. There are a million golf aids on the market but after playing golf even for a short period of time you realize that the game is almost completely mental. The majority of whats available are all physical which I find funny. As of right now, my score is far from anything to brag about so I will see any changes being made right away. I'll keep everyone updated!
