Title A Proposed Experiment to Verify Gravitational Electromagnetic Interaction Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Athanassios A Nassikas Keywords electromagnetic, gravitational Published 2011 Journal Proceedings of the NPA Volume 8 No. of pages 3 Pages 421-424 Abstract This paper describes an experimental device to create a propulsive force verifying gravitational electromagnetic interaction. The device consists of a converging nozzle made of a superconductor, conjointly with a permanent magnet, to form a self-propulsion mechanism directed toward the converging area. Overview A Proposed Experiment to Verify Gravitational Electromagnetic Interaction is a three-page paper by Athanassios A Nassikas of the Technological Institute of Larissa, Greece, presented at the 18th Natural Philosophy Alliance Conference in July 2011 and published in the Proceedings of the NPA . It proposes a bench-top device that would produce thrust with no exhaust, and argues that if it works, the momentum must come from an interaction between the gravitational and electromagnetic fields. The device is a converging nozzle made from a type-II superconductor with a permanent magnet inside it.
