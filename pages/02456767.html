Grey Walter of the Burden Neurological Institute published his book 'The Living Brain' (Pelican Book) which should be consulted by students. In 1967, the Elsevier Publishing Co. published the book 'The Evoked Potentials' (ed. Wm. Cobb & C. Morocutti) consisting of papers read at the International conference in Siena, June 30th, 1966. One of these was a paper by Dr. Grey Walter entitled 'Slow potential changes in the human brain associated with expectancy, decision, and intention'; this phenomenon become known as 'E-Waves' or 'Expectancy Waves' and a summary of this appeared in Vol. 2, No. 5 of the Journal of Paraphysics'. In effect, it was found that when a subject made a decision to act, an E-Wave built up in his EEG just prior to the action, and this, by suitable filters and amplifiers, could be made to trigger off relays. Thus, one could switch on a TV merely by willing it, as it were, without having to push a button. Follo
