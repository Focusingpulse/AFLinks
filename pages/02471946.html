New York, Dec. 15. — Nikola Tesla, the noted inventor, and winner of the Nobel Physics Prize for 1915, has filed applications for patents on the essential parts of a machine to make war electrically. His device is an engine that will paralyze or kill an enemy at any point on the earth’s surface. Ten miles or a thousand, it will be all the same to the machine, the inventor claims. Straight to the point, on land or on sea, it will be able to go with precision, delivering a blow that will paralyze or kill, as desired. That a man on a tower on Long Island could shield New York against ships or army by working a lever is the prediction of the inventor, if the full end of his hopes is realized. Dr. Tesla says it would be possible with his wireless mechanism to direct an ordinary aeroplane manless to any point, over a ship or an army, and to discharge explosives with electrical devices from the base of operations. “This idea is founded upon a principle that means great things in peace and great things in war,” says Dr. Tesla. “With transmitters we are able to project electrical energy in any amount to any distance and apply it for innumerable purposes, both in peace and war.
