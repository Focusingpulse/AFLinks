Earth Ground On Battery

· archived # 1,511,248
I noticed when using an earth ground on the negative side of the battery I can light a LED off the positive side of the battery using an AV plug. Notice how it likes the human touch to light up. To simulate that I used a capacitor going from the negative diode to the positive side of the cap then the negative side of the cap going back to ground on battery. After running a couple min the cap seems to have stoped charging at 10.38v. Next I'll have to try a JT with this and see if I can run it back to the source. Here is a video of it.
