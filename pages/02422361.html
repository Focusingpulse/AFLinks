Preface — GA 121. The Mission of Folk-Souls

👤 Rudolf Steiner · · archived # 2,422,361
In these lectures, which were given at Christiania in June 1910, I ventured to give a sketch of the psychology of the development of peoples. The lectures are based upon the principles of anthroposophical spiritual science laid down in my books Theosophy , An Outline Of Occult Science , The Riddle of Man , Riddles of the Soul , etc. I was able to build upon this foundation because my hearers were acquainted with the scientific views set forth in my works.
