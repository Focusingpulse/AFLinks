Eric Dollard is the official YouTube channel of the American electrical engineer Eric P Dollard , known for his work in the analog electrical tradition of Nikola Tesla and Charles Proteus Steinmetz — longitudinal waves, telluric transmission, and the transformer theory behind Tesla's Colorado Springs experiments. The channel is an anomaly in the index on this wiki. It holds only twenty-one videos, all published between October 2013 and March 2014, yet it has drawn a substantial audience and has more subscribers than most of the far larger and still-active channels indexed here. Dollard's influence in this field is real and current; it simply does not travel through this channel. Content The uploads are interviews, lectures and question-and-answer sessions recorded in late 2013 and early 2014, rather than produced material. Among them are Origins of Energy Synthesis , History and Theory of Electricity , The Tesla Marconi Wireless System , a history of the Marconi–RCA station at Bolinas, California — a facility central to Dollard's own account of what was lost in twentieth-century electrical engineering — and interviews conducted by Aaron Murakami and by the Tesla Science Foundation. The most-watched by a wide margin is The Supernatural Power of Music (March 2014). Status and other sources Nothing has been published on the channel since March 2014. The description carries a mailing address in Lone Pine, California, for support of Dollard and his laboratory.
