Eric Dollard is the official YouTube channel of the American electrical engineer Eric P Dollard , known for his work in the analog electrical tradition of Nikola Tesla and Charles Proteus Steinmetz — longitudinal waves, telluric transmission, and the transformer theory behind Tesla's Colorado Springs experiments.
