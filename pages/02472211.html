Ten horse power from a tiny engine that a man could dangle from his little finger by a string! Five hundred horse power in a package that a man could lift easily in one hand! A thousand horse power motor occupying hardly more space than the cardboard box in which your hatter sent your new derby home! Marvellous? Wait until you hear the rest of it!
