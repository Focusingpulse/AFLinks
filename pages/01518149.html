METHOD FOR IMPROVEMENT OF SOIL BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION This invention relates to a method for the improve ment of soil to be carried out for the protection of farm products against diseases, and more particularly to a method for extirpating molds by electrical treatment of soil. Heretofore, as a means of electrically extirpating nematodes infesting roots of farm products, the method which effects the extirpation by applying high-voltage electric pulses to the soil near the roots of farm products has been known to the art (Japanese Patent Application Laid-open No. 216533/1984).
