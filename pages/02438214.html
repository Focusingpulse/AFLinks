106 — GA 263. Correspondence with Edith Maryon 1912–1924

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 3 June 1922 · archived # 2,438,214
My dear Edith Maryon! Today I just want to start by saying, with my warmest greetings, that so far everything here has gone well. The Kolisko lecture has probably only led to the majority of “experts” staying away. I have not yet been to the afternoon discussions, where their participation would have been considered, but I am told that in fact no one has discussed it yet. I would like to go back to work in our studio and send my warmest greetings there today from Rudolf Steiner Vienna I, Hotel Imperial 106 Rudolf Steiner an Edith Maryon Wien, 3. Juni 1922 Meine liebe Edith Maryon! Heute will
