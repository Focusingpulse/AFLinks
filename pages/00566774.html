Number of Non–Zero Coefficients of (1 + x a + x b ) n over Fp Andy Zhuang Abstract The paper studies explicit formulas for Np(n; a, b), the count of coefficients that remain non–zero modulo a prime p in the trinomial power (1+x a+x b ) n with 0 < a < b < p. Lever￾aging Lucas’ digit–wise criterion and the matrix–automaton framework of Amdeberhan– Stanley, we first prove a carry‑free theorem: if every base‑p digit of n does not exceed ⌊ (p − 1)/b⌋ and the generated x-exponents do not overlap at every digit position, then no cross‑digit carries occur and the exponents are unique for each digit position. This leads to Np(n; a, b) being factorized as ∏ l (nl+2 2 ) , where nl are digits of n under base-p. The paper next derives a upper bound Np(n; a, b) ≤ 3 wp(n) , where wp(n) is the sum of the base‑p digits of n, and shows that equality holds precisely when every digit of n is 0 or 1. Worked examples—including the case (1 + x + x 3 ) n over F7—demonstrate the formulas in practice, and the discussion shows our contributions within earlier studies on automatic sequences and multinomial Lucas theorems. 1 Preliminaries The problem of deciding when a coefficient of a polynomial power f(x) n ∈ Fp[x] disappears modulo a fixed prime (or prime power) sits at the intersection of additive number theory, automatic sequences, and algebraic combinatorics.
