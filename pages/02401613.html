GC2026-PERM043 Paleomagnetic studies of the Permian-Triassic Bolshoe Bogdo section (south-east of the Eastern European platform) Anna Fetisova1,2 , Roman Veselovskiy1,2 , Liubov Ladygina1,2 1 O.Yu. Schmidt Institute of Physics of the Earth, Russian Academy of Sciences 2 Lomonosov Moscow State University anna-fetis@yandex.ru Bolshoe Bogdo section is a unique geological site where the Triassic system was first identified in the European part of Russia. The upper sections combine horizons characterized by both marine and continental organic remains, which allows for confident correlation of the continental Lower Triassic, which is widely developed in the Eastern European Platform, with the Global Stratigraphic Scale based on ammonites. We present the combining results of 2-year comprehensive studies, which have been done on Bolshoe Bogdo section. In our presentation we discuss some aspects of paleomagnetism and rock magnetism of sediments, such as inclination shallowing, anisotropy of magnetic susceptibility and so on. The main achievement of our work is getting the new mean Permian-Triassic paleomagnetic pole for the East European platform as well as calculation of its Late Permian and Early Triassic poles. We also present new version of the magnetostratigraphic correlation of studied section within the Russian Basin and with Geomagnetic Polarity Time Scale. The research was supported by the state research programs of the IPE RAS FMWU-2025-0033.
