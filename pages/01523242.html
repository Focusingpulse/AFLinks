Kizer Born 1947(age78–79) Residence Portsmouth, OH, United States Nationality USA Knownfor Le Sage gravitation; Association for Pushing Gravity Research essay prizes Scientific career Fields Gravitation, biology, economics, psychology John B. Kizer (born 1947) is an American independent researcher who has written on gravitation, relativity, biological regulation and economics. He is best known on this wiki for a series of prize-winning essays defending Le Sagian — that is, pushing — theories of gravitation, and he assisted John Chappell in the years before the founding of the Natural Philosophy Alliance . Biography Kizer appeared on the General Electric College Bowl in 1967 while an undergraduate at Ohio State University, which he described as his most singular honour there.
