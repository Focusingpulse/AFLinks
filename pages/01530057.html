Block2,4 This supplement provides the following data and information: 1) Figure 1 showing the electronics layout and key components used for conducting pulse-shape discrimination. 2) Figures 2 showing energy spectra with and without pulse shape discrimination for a variety of situations. 3) Figures 3 showing the time spectra of sonoluminescence (SL), pulse neutron generator (PNG) neutron emission, and time spectra of counts (via. blocking of counts from the PNG for the first 24 µs) for chilled C3H6O and C3D6O with and without cavitation. Information is provided on the change in counts with cavitation, clearly showing the statistically significant increase in counts with cavitation during the time span corresponding with bubble implosion.
