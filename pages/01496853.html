transducer

· archived # 1,496,853
A device for translating the magnitude of one quantity into another quantity . The second quantity often has units of measure different from the first and serves as the source of a useful signal . Vibration transducers convert mechanical motion into a proportional electronic signal (typically a voltage-proportional signal ). [Bentley Nevada Corporation; Field of Rotating Machinery Measurement, Monitoring and Analysis] The component in the converter housing that converts electrical energy to mechanical energy.
