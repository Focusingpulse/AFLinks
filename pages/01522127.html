We address a critical deficiency of the standard model - the nature and origin of space-time. Although gravitation has no place in the standard model, it can be shown that within the constructs of quantum electrodynamics gravitational energy can be calculated in a straightforward manner, yielding the experimental value of G . The assumption alone is made that the vacumm is a cold plasma in the form of a conserved, boson-lattice of electron-positron pairs, and these pairs are the dark matter of the universe.
