Had I read it then, I would have become an enraged activist against nuclear power. But now I am too old. Sternglass shows in correlation after correlation the connection between low level fallout and infant mortality. There is no question that excees infant mortality occurred downwind of nuclear testing and that it continues half a century later downwind of nuclear power plants.
