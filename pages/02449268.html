New age bookstores in major cities sometimes have an entire shelf devoted to this subject. Titles I would recommend (in descending order) are: "In Search of the Miraculous" by P.D. Bennett "The Fourth Way" by Ouspensky Many other titles... Also, the Rosicrucian Order has some very relevant information. When truly understood, this knowledge is identical to the esoteric truths behind every major religion.
