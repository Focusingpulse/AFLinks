Lecture V - 306. The Child's Changing Consciousness and Waldorf Education

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 19 April 1923 · archived # 2,442,232
, Dornach Lecture V Between the ages of seven and approximately fourteen, the teacher's main concern must be directed toward the students' evolving life of feeling. It is really very important that educators acquire the ability to create the kind of mental imagery that can guide pupils through the tender transitional stages characteristic of this period. When children enter school, remnants of the previous “bodily-religious mood,” as I call it, still exist.
