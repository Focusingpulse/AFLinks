Barrett and Dale M. Grimes, World Scientific Publishing, 1995. Of particular interest is the article "Six experiments with Magnetic Charge", V.F. Mikhailov, p. 593 ff., which discusses a modern look at the work of Felix Eherenhaft (1879-1952). Eherenhaft performed the magnetic equivalence of a Millikan's oil drop experiment in the hopes of isolating magnetic monopoles and measuring magnetic charge. Surprisingly, Eherenhaft had positive results, obtaining a value for magnetic charge in the range of 10^-9 to 10^-14 Gauss*cm^2.
