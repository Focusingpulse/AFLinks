[ Atomic Suicide , page 147] CONFUSION CONCERNING LIGHT CORPUSCLES There is much confusion concerning the many kinds of particles of matter such as electrons , protons , photons , neutrons and others. These many particles are supposedly different because of the belief that some are negatively charged , some are positively charged and some are so equally charged that one supposedly neutralizes the other. There is no such condition in nature as negative charge , nor are there negatively charged particles. Charge and discharge are opposite conditions, as filling and emptying, or compressing and expanding are opposite conditions. Compressing bodies are charging into higher potential conditions. Conversely, expanding bodies are discharging into lower potential conditions. To describe an electron as a negatively charged body is equivalent to saying that it is an expanding-contracting body.
