Energy from Cold and Vacuum

· archived # 1,458,909
HI Andrew. Did I tell you about the division of AVRO Aircraft that had a company down on Merton street in Toronto in the 50’s and early 60’s called Designex and they had supplied a lot of the Engineering talent to AVRO. They also got some Ex-NAZI scientists and at Designex they developed a system for “Energy from cold & Vaccuum”. They successfully got a system going in the office building at 37 or 39 Merton Street and it supplied power for cooling and running lights etc but Toronto Hydro got angry that their Hydro bills were getting lower so they checked it out and to teach them a lesson, hydro cut off the electricity supply to the building. And to their great dismay the lights were still on and air conditioning running and all other systems ran fine. So the provincial government got angry and actually passed a law that the only ones legally allowed to generate electricity in the province of Ontario was Ontario Hydro. As I recall the story this was about 1960. Designex went about trying to promote the invention , first with Dieffenbaker, then with Eisenhauer in the States, but were told that it had been decided that the entire economy would be based on oil. In 1963 when President Kennedy made the famous statement that they were planning g to put a man on the moon before the “End of the decade” and they would need new technologies. So the people from Designex thought this was a good opportunity to promote their invention. Energy from “Cold & Vaccuum” just like they have in Spa
