Jim Johnson Born ( 1942-12-22 ) December 22, 1942 (age 83) Residence Olympia, WA, United States Nationality USA Known for Electric Universe , Plasma Scientific career Fields Managing editor , The Essential Guide to the Electric Universe Jim Johnson (born 22 December 1942) is an American writer and editor based in Olympia, Washington, and the managing editor of The Essential Guide to the Electric Universe , the introductory resource hosted on the Thunderbolts Project website. Work The Essential Guide grew out of a request made in 2009 by David Talbott to Bob Johnson, for a summary that would give newcomers enough grounding in plasma behaviour to follow Electric Universe arguments without finding them far-fetched. Jim Johnson took on the task of building that summary into a structured online resource, organised to move from familiar material — stellar distances, the aurorae, planetary magnetospheres — through the history of electromagnetic science to a more technical treatment of plasma, with appendices, a glossary and links to video material. He presented the project at the 18th Natural Philosophy Alliance conference in 2011, in a paper whose interest lies less in its physics than in its frankness about the state of the field it serves. Johnson describes the Electric Universe as a "paradigm" rather than a theory, on the grounds that its ideas "are less than full-blown theory and are still in a very qualitative state of patterns and examples", and that "underlying mathematics is not yet the EU's long suit". He defends that status as a legitimate stage of enquiry by appeal to the qualitative-physics literature in artificial intelligence, particularly Johan de Kleer's work on causal reasoning about circuits, and argues that the task ahead is to deepen the model with quantitative work rather than to defend it as complete.
