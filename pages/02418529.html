17. On Devachan — GA 90a. Theosophy, Christology and Mythology

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 18 February 1904 · archived # 2,418,529
, Berlin Translated by Steiner Online Library 17. On Devachan [ 1 ] Eight days ago, we followed the human being through the various stages of the so-called spiritual realm, the Devachan. We saw how the human being’s destiny unfolds between two incarnations in this spiritual realm, the Devachan. We have seen that, depending on the degree of his development, a person is able to ascend higher and higher or remain longer and longer in the realms of this land of causes, in order to bring from this land of causes into this earthly world the capacities for ever higher and higher development.
