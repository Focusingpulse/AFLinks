Anderson Electric Spacecraft Journal August, 2006 Page number(s): 13-19 The following article is a collection of final thoughts from Leland Anderson regarding Nikola Tesla's work. These are the result of a lifetime of study based on the author's interviews with living contacts who knew Tesla. The author is known to have owned and maintained over the last half century the largest holding of original Tesla documents and images to be found in private hands anywhere in the world. All of the foregoing; coupled with his engineering background and personal contacts with many modern Tesla experimenters, researchers and writers; make his comments particularly valuable in both an engineering and scientific sense. The reader is also treated to a number of rare historical tidbits related to Tesla's work. We are honored and pleased to present to our readers this cumulative effort of this premier Tesla historian, author and researcher. After more than forty years studying the technical work of Nikola Tesla, I thought it appropriate at this time to review certain aspects of it. During the latter of those years, pieces of information have come together making more of his work clear.
