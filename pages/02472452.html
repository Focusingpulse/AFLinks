This too low. The number of those disabled through wounds and disease was recently placed at 500,000. This, also. is too moderate. It would be less than two and one-half per cent., and it must be remembered that in the most recent great war proceeding this, the Balkan struggle. the casualties were ten times greater, or twenty-five per cent. “Observe that it is reported Belgium lost $300,000,000 in two weeks fighting while there were no large cities in the path of the Germans. Cost of Military Operations Not All “The difficulty with most statisticians of war losses is that they simply consider the cost of military operations. This is a small fraction of the waste. “To measure the losses due to war merely by statistical standards is absurd. An example will make this clear.
