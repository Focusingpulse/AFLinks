MolPhot2026-main038 Enhanced Fluorescence of the Green Fluorescent Protein EGFP in the Near Fields of Silver Nanoparticles with a Crescent Shape under One-Photon Excitation Мария А. Шокова1 1 Lomonosov Moscow State University shokova-masha@mail.ru It is known that irradiating noble metal nanoparticles with visible light can excite localized surface plasmon resonance (SPR)—collective charge oscillations that lead to the appearance of regions with increased electromagnetic field intensity [1]. Recently, anisotropic metal nanostructures with multiple plasmon modes have been actively studied. From a practical standpoint, particles with resonances in the near-IR range, which corresponds to the transparency window of biological tissue, are of particular interest. The production of such plasmonic structures using colloidal lithography with polymer spheres (nanosphere lithography) opens up significant possibilities for the creation of multifunctional sensors [2]. This work presents an experimental and theoretical study of the enhancement of EGFP fluorescence near the surface of crescent-shaped silver nanoparticles under single-photon excitation. Silver nanoparticle arrays shaped like symmetrical crescents were produced using colloidal lithography. The size and shape of the resulting structures were characterized using scanning electron microscopy, and their spectral properties were determined using spectrophotometry.
