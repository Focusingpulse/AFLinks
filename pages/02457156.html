This device consists of two cylindrical magnets fixed on opposite sides of an inclined track and an identical cylindrical magnet that serves as a roller on the track. In operation, the roller is placed at the lower end of the track even with the ends of the track magnets and given the slightest nudge upwards. The roller then races up the track between the track magnets and travels beyond their upper ends with significant kinetic energy.
