A classical approach to electrodynamics adapted to relate to lepton interactions yields the unifying link with the gravitational interaction. The tau-lepton is found to be a primary "graviton" mediator. The theory has a common foundation with methods of deriving the fine-structure constant and the proton-electron mass ratio, already published in summary form.
