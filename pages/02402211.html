SPAF-2026-main007 CDU: 523.985.3 Study of plasma parameters of weak solar flares based on microwave observations Daria V. Rozhkova1 , Larisa K. Kashapova1 1 Order of the Red Banner of Labor Institute of Solar-Terrestrial Physics of the Siberian Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences super.dariarozhkova@yandex.ru Weak solar flares usually show a simple temporal and spatial structure. They are considered as components of more powerful and complex events. Also, these phenomena are assumed to play a key role in heating the solar atmosphere. Therefore, estimation of plasma parameters of these events is very important for understanding processes in the solar atmosphere. The most evident, but not so simple, technique to derive this numerical characteristic is the fitting of microwave spectra with numerical models. This type of diagnostic requires spectral observations with high sensitivity and high spectral resolution. Since the start of regular observations by the Siberian Radioheliograph, it has been possible to develop such research. We present the results of a study of the microwave spectrum of a few weak solar flares, using gyrosynchrotron emission modeling based on fast gyrosynchroton code [1]. We estimated plasma parameters using two assumptions of emission generation: by only accelerated electrons and by a combination of electrons with thermal and non-thermal velocity or energy distributions.
