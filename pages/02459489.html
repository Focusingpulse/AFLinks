7 7. Angular momentum: The momentum or "leverage" of the momentum of a moving body, with respect to an axis or reference point. The quantity "action", or energy x time, or momentum x length, etc. Most physical processes can be analyzed deeply in terms of action (angular momentum) and some of the very deep principles of physics are expressed in that type of analysis.
