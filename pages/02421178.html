Roberto Blas Residence Córdoba, Argentina Nationality Argentinian Known for Unipolar Induction , Inertia Roberto Blas is an Argentine physicist at the Universidad Nacional de Río Cuarto in Córdoba, working on the electrodynamics of homopolar machines. His published work is a collaboration with Jorge A Guala-Valverde of the Fundación Julio Palacios in Neuquén, and in most cases with Max Blas as a third author. The programme is experimental: it uses Faraday-disc and homopolar motor-generator arrangements to ask what determines the electromotive force in a rotating magnetic system — motion relative to the magnet, relative to the field-shaping iron, or relative to the laboratory. The group's distinctive apparatus is the "confined B-field" machine described in Non-Local Motional EM Induction (2005), in which an iron yoke channels a rotatable magnet's flux into a narrow air gap, so that magnet, yoke and coil can each be held or spun independently. The interpretation the group favours is a relational one drawing on Weber electrodynamics and on Amperian surface currents, and the same theme runs through their other papers: "Inertial Mass: a Changing Entity? Weber vs. Einstein, Weber Plus Einstein or None?" (2005), "Maxwell Helps Explain Microphysics" (2002), and "Non-Local Motional Electrodynamics" (2009). Guala-Valverde's related work on homopolar induction has also appeared in the American Journal of Physics and Physica Scripta .
