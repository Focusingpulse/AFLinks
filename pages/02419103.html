The Round Robin >> Vol. 02 >> No. 10 [25] - OMISSIONS AND COMMISSIONS - Both Round Robin and Flying Roll are very late this month. We are in constant difficulty about paper supply, and also have no clerical help. The Kareeta affair came up at the last moment, and it seemed to us that it should appear promptly. Excellent articles from Round Robin friends have had to go over to next issue. Our thanks to all of these contributors. We omit our Variorum section: we had a little comment on The Bell Witch (a great mystery) - on Troll business in Colorado - on the Black Mass in Milan - the Ghost of Antigonish - Cries of Anguish from Readers. And under Ca et La there was something about Necrophilia in Turkey - Dr. Nandor Fodor's "Explanations" in Digest and Review - Notes on booklets and articles by Professor Ryan, "F.G.H.", Dr. Fodor, Vincent Gaddis, Dr. Haley, William G. Randall (attorney, investigator), and others. Acknowledgments of many publications received, and of excellent articles in them... We omit our page of book titles (for sale), and of special notices. Nearly all of this will appear at the earliest possible date. If you want Flying Roll for inspection or purchase (.50 copy) send us a post card - and please state your occult affiliations, if any. Don't forget that we have had to increase the price of Round Robin - the cost of paper is almost prohibitive - when one can get it. We hope to have articles by Gina Cerminara, and by E.C.
