MolPhot2026-main028 Synthesis and Chiroptical Modulation of Novel Chiral Manganese (II)-Based Metal Halides Jing Li1 , Qi Pang1 1 School of Chemistry and Chemical Engineering, Guangxi University, Nanning 530004, Guangxi, China qipang@gxu.edu.cn Chiral metal halides are promising for 3D displays, quantum communication, and biosensing. However, current lead/tin-based systems face toxicity, instability, and limited circularly polarized luminescence (CPL) performance performance (Figure of Merit, FM = PLQY × |glum|). Chiral manganese(II)-based halides offer environmental friendliness and unique magnetism due to their 3d5configuration, yet they often suffer from low FM factors. This study addresses these challenges through crystal phase control and functional group engineering. Here, we report novel chiral Mn(II)-based metal halides with tailored chiroptical properties. First, by controlling crystallization temperature, we synthesized two pairs of isomers: R/S-α-(L)2MnBr4 and R/S-β-(L)₂MnBr4, crystallizing in C222₁ and P2₁ space groups, respectively. Both exhibit strong green CPL and near-unity PLQY. The α-phase achieved a superior figure of merit (FM = 1×10-3) and a remarkable second-harmonic circular dichroism (gSHG-CD= −0.56). This enhancement originates from a dense hydrogen-bond network that distorts [MnBr₄]²⁻ tetrahedra, boosting the dipole moment. Second, we designed 1D manganese chlorides via functional group modification. Replacing methyl with hydroxyl groups in R/S-(3-HPip)MnCl3 created a rigid structure, suppressing non-radiative decay.
