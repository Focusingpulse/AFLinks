Now in this follow-up we will talk more about the Unified Field specifically and how it relates to transcendental time travel, or travelling with the mind. Mankind exists on this planet to seek his Destiny and so we will look at how the "Wheels of Destiny" actually send messages backward in time to aid man in his quest to attain harmony. Overview The Unified Field is a two-page paper by Lee Field Valstad of Ruidoso Downs, New Mexico, presented at the 18th Natural Philosophy Alliance Conference in July 2011 and published in the Proceedings of the NPA (vol. 8, pp. 660–662). It is a sequel to his "The Unified Field Symbol" of the previous year. Despite the title, it contains no field equations and no physics in the conventional sense. It is a first-person narrative, and it is best read as one. Valstad's premise is that the number 1/0 — represented by a "marriage symbol" of two joined circles, and denoting "all-possibility" — is the definition of the Unified Field, and that from this follows a structure he calls the "Wheels of Destiny". The Wheels send messages backward in time. Déjà vu, sudden love, panic and moments of understanding are those messages, arriving to keep a person on their proper path; he calls the principle "biological convergence", and identifies free-energy inventors, leaders and freedom-fighters as people especially attuned to it.
