atmospheric light

· archived # 1,469,329
Schauberger The vertically propagating energy-form , which in a vacuum tube produces an intense and dazzling light - or a Sun in statu nascendi as it were - likewise describes a cycloid-space-curve path , but one in which the transverse radius reduces . The faster the water-droplet falls and the higher the vacuum-bulb is positioned on the longitudinal axis , the more dazzling and intense the light becomes. The further the vacuum-bulb is removed from the source of the horizontally emitted energy-form , the darker the strongly pulsating blood-red glow becomes. If heavily insulated shields (such as paraffin wax ), which have a braking effect , are interposed around this radiant energy-bundle , then under the influence of atmospheric light and heat , positively charged water is produced . However, if the same process is repeated with the exclusion of light and heat , then negatively charged water results.
