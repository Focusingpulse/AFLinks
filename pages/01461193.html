Pancha Tattwa

· archived # 1,461,193
The five aura electricities, each having three attributes or Gunas . "The five electricities, Pancha Tattwa , from their three attributes, Gunas - Sattwa ( positive ), Rajas ( neutralizing ), and Tamas ( negative ) - produce Jnanandriyas (organs of sense ), Karmendriyas (organs of action ), and Tanmatras (objects of sense ).." ( Swami Sri Yukteswar , The Holy Science , Sutra 7-10) See Also 7.8 - Matter and Spirit are Opposite Poles Chitta Entropy Intelligence Mind Motion Neutral Parusha Sense Syntropy
