GC2026-PERM017 Rock-magnetic characteristics of Quaternary formations of the Tukaevo section (Kiginsky district, Bashkortostan) Svetlana E. Urbanovich1,2 , Aleksandr V. Gusev2 1 Geological Institute of the Russian Academy of Sciences 2 Lomonosov Moscow State University sveta.urbanovich.04@mail.ru During field work in the summer of 2025 on the outskirts of the village Tukaevo (Kiginsky district, Bashkortostan) we studied a section of Quaternary sediments of the first upper-floodplain terrace on the left side of the Kigi River valley. The valley has a U-shaped section, the sides are steep. The upper part of the section with a vertical thickness of 0.5 – 0.7 m is composed of lacustrine, alluvial, and to a lesser extent deluvial loams and sandy loams with sparse grus and rubble. There is lenticular bedding, sometimes cross-bedding. According to its position in the section and lithological characteristics, it corresponds to the Kudashevsky (Polar Ural) horizon of the Upper Pleistocene [1]. The lower part of the section mainly belongs to the Tabuldinsky (Nevyansky) horizon of the Upper Pleistocene of the local stratigraphic scheme. At the base of the slope there are beige lightweight loams overlain by chanell fill and oxbow deposits containing numerous thin shells of gastropods and bivalves near the top. A similar streak has been described in the reference section of the Tabuldinsky horizon on the outskirts of Novobelokatai village [2].
