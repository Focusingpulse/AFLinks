Klyushin Born 1940(age85–86) Leningrad, Soviet Union Residence St. Petersburg, Russia Nationality Russian Knownfor Electrodynamics , generalized Maxwell equations, electro- and gravidynamics Scientific career Fields Physics , Mathematics Institutions St. Petersburg State University of Civil Aviation Jaroslav Grigorievich Klyushin (also transliterated Yaroslav Klyushin ; born April 1940 in Leningrad , Soviet Union) is a Russian physicist and mathematician based in St. Petersburg. He heads the Chair of Applied Mathematics at the St. Petersburg State University of Civil Aviation and serves as editor of Galilean Electrodynamics East (GED-East). He is known for his work on a generalization of Maxwell's equations and for a unified treatment of electrodynamics and gravitation that he terms "gravidynamics." Biography
