Fluid Dynamics Research 40 (2008) 399 – 426 Variational formulation of ideal fluid flows according to gauge principle Tsutomu Kambe IDS, Higashi-yama 2-11-3, Meguro-ku, Tokyo 153-0043, Japan Received 6 February 2007; received in revised form 23 November 2007; accepted 1 December 2007 Available online 29 January 2008 Communicated by Y. Fukumoto Abstract On the basis of the gauge principle of field theory, a new variational formulation is presented for flows of an ideal fluid. The fluid is defined thermodynamically by mass density and entropy density, and its flow fields are characterized by symmetries of translation and rotation. The rotational transformations are regarded as gauge transformations as well as the translational ones. In addition to the Lagrangians representing the translation symmetry, a structure of rotation symmetry is equipped with a Lagrangian A including the vorticity and a vector potential bilinearly. Euler’s equation of motion is derived from variations according to the action principle. In addition, the equations of continuity and entropy are derived from the variations. Equations of conserved currents are deduced as the Noether theorem in the space of Lagrangian coordinate a. Without A, the action principle results in the Clebsch solution with vanishing helicity. The Lagrangian A yields non-vanishing vorticity and provides a source term of non-vanishing helicity. The vorticity equation is derived as an equation of the gauge field, and the A characterizes topology of the field. The present formulation is comprehensive and provides a consistent basis for a unique transformation between the Lagrangian a space and the Eulerian x space. In contrast, with translation symmetry alone, there is an arbitrariness in the transformation between these spaces. © 2008 The Japan Society of Fluid Mechanics and Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.
