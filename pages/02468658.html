SCIENTOLOGY 8-80 The discovery and increase of life energy in the genus Homo Sapiens by L.Ron Hubbard 1952 To my good friend, the late Commander “Snake” Thompson (MC) USN and his friend and teacher, Sigmund Freud EDITORIAL NOTE 1952 L. Ron Hubbard was one of the first students of nuclear physics in the United States. In 1932 he believed that Life and its behavior obeyed natural laws of the same order as electrons and other particles of motion. He began a study and search which led him through many fields of science and across many continents. In 1948 he published the “Original Thesis” on his findings for the medical and psychiatric professions. In 1950 he permitted a popular work to be published and was considerably astonished that it became a best seller quickly. Since 1950 he has worked constantly upon the original project of discovering and conduiting the source of Life Energy and with this work announces the attainment of that fact. This volume is a detail of the discovery, its formula, and the factors necessary to bring the fact into application. For three years, scientific controversy has raged around L. Ron Hubbard’s work. The most degrading attacks and flowery praise have greeted his activities from every quarter, lay and scientific. He has given the acceptance of his work and the attacks and praise alike little thought but has continued to follow his route of discovery. His issuance of these findings caps a scientifically stormy career. It is doubtful if the storm will grow calmer in the face of the calm announcement that one has discovered and isolated life and provided the techniques for its use and conducting. Careful testing by many persons already has assured the truth and usefulness of this work. It is not experimental but has been applied scientifically and confirmed in many quarters. 2 FOREWORD WHAT IS SCIENTOLOGY? “Scientology” is a new word which names a new science. It is formed from the Latin word, “scio”, which means KNOW, or DISTINGUISH, being
