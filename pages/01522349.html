Overview A Variable Model of the Fine-Structure Constant is an eight-page paper by Ioannis Iraklis Haranas of the Department of Physics and Astronomy at York University, Toronto, published in 2003 in the Romanian Astronomical Journal volume 13, number 1, pages 25–30. Its subject was, at the time, one of the liveliest questions in observational cosmology. Webb, Murphy and collaborators had reported from high-resolution quasar absorption spectra taken at Keck that the fine-structure constant appeared to have been smaller in the past, with Δα/α = (−0.72 ± 0.18) × 10⁻⁵ over roughly the last six to ten billion years. Haranas takes that result as given and asks what could produce it. His answer is a varying speed of light.
