He has an unorthodox cell design and an idea how to make electrodes produce better gas by using copper cores. The concept of using these cores is to lower the resistance of the plates, reduce heat and increase the efficiency of series cells for better gas production. Despite Bryan having a patent application, he feels this technology should be shared publicly and is willing to let people use it at their leisure for non commercial use.This is a gesture which allot of proprietary engineers can learn from, Bryan's gesture ensures consumer awareness and demand and that this idea will be used in real time and not suppressed.
