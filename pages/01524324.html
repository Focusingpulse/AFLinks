About This presentation will discuss issues in modern cosmology. The ideas of Dark matter, Dark Energy, The Cosmic Microwave background and other topics basic to modern cosmological models will be discussed and critiqued. Summary Harry Ricker continued his survey of standard cosmology with the two hypotheses that carry most of its weight, and closed on a conclusion about how the field works rather than about the physics.
