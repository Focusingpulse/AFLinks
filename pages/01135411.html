INTRODUCTION Viruses can be extremely simple in design, consisting of nucleic acid surrounded by a protein coat known as a capsid. The Viral Capsid is made of many smaller, identical protein molecules called capsomers [1]. Here, we show that, several types of Viral Capsid can be easily destructed by means of a specific violet radiation.
