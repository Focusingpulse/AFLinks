The purpose of this document is to provide the basic information to design and build your own radionics instrument and to share some guidelines for the advancement of radionics using the latest available technology. The document will be updated as new insight and knowledge is gained. Many seasoned radionics practitioners report that they no longer require the instruments for balancing and it can thus also be seen as a personal consciousness and intent enhancement training tool.
