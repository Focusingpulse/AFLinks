The theory has a preferred reference frame and hence violates Lorentz invariance . History Einstein-æther theories were popularized by Maurizio Gasperini in a series of papers, such as Singularity Prevention and Broken Lorentz Symmetry in the 1980s. 1 In addition to the metric of general relativity these theories also included a scalar field which intuitively corresponded to a universal notion of time .
