Bob Duggins Residence Smithsburg, MD, United States Nationality US Known for Electric Universe , Superfood Nutrition Scientific career Fields IT Professional Robert "Bob" Duggins is an American information-technology professional based in Smithsburg, Maryland, who is associated with the dissident-science community as a proponent of Electric Universe cosmology. He is also known for an interest in Superfood Nutrition . Biography Duggins resides in Smithsburg, Maryland, in the United States, and works as an IT professional. He is a member of the alternative-science community, where he is listed under member identifier 2184. Work Duggins is identified with the Electric Universe framework, a set of alternative cosmological ideas that emphasize the role of electricity and plasma over gravitation in shaping astronomical phenomena. He is catalogued among proponents of this view in Jean de Climont's survey of dissident scientists, which records his area of interest as the electric universe. Alongside his cosmological interests, Duggins has taken an interest in Superfood Nutrition .
