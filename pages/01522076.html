The two newest theories, (TIFFE) (The Theory of Internal Formation of Fuels and Elements) and (CSPT) (The Comet-Star-to-Planet Theory), were originally explored in the first three editions. They are key concepts that permit a more accurate evaluation of current theories, and tie together all the more valid ones into a nearly complete jigsaw picture of creation. They can explain the newly discovered puzzle of why it snows up instead of down on planet Jupiter, for example; and why oil and gas are found at ever greater depths on planet Earth.
