"We want to do something transformative, to move beyond current silicon-based solar technology," says Prashant Kamat, John A. The team's search for the new material, described in the journal ACS Nano, centered on nano-sized particles of titanium dioxide, which were coated with either cadmium sulfide or cadmium selenide.
