Proceedings of the 1990 International Tesla Symposium Book Author Gary L Peterson Published 1990 Publisher Tesla Book Company This is an excellent collection of papers delivered at the 1990 Tesla Symposium in Colorado Springs. The speakers included William H. Terbo , Dr. James F. Corum , Kenneth L. Corum , Prof. James M. Hardesty , Dr. Charles A. Yost , Dr. Paul A. LaViolette , and Panos T. Pappas . The topics covered range from a tour of the Nikola Tesla Museum in Belgrade to an exhaustive history of early gas discharge vacuum tubes and disclosure of rediscovered techniques for the production of ball lightning.
