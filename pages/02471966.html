The principal defense was a publication by Ferraris in 1888 on the rotary field and of the means for obtaining polyphase from single-phase currents. These disclosures were made in a paper read by Prof. Ferraris before the Royal Academy of Sciences of Turin, Italy, in March, 1888, a portion of which was published on April 22 following, in L’Elettricita . The Tesla patents in suit were not applied for until December 8 following, but the court held that the testimony in the case showed conclusively that Tesla had, early in May, 1888, a complete grasp of the split-phase idea; and not only is the invention beyond question carried back by it to that period, but the patent not being able to be worked out in a day, ground is thus laid for an earlier date if there is fair evidence to warrant it. The court holds there is such evidence, testimony showing that Tesla disclosed the principles of his invention at a date prior to April 22 to his patent solicitor, the date being somewhere between the 8th and the 18th of April.
