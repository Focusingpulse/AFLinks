ABSTRACT This paper explores the possibility that matter may be capable of traveling through time, an idea derived from the convergence of anomalous findings in parapsychological research and advanced theoretical interpretations in contemporary physics. Studies in precognition and remote viewing—despite their controversial status—have repeatedly produced results suggesting that information can be accessed outside the conventional forward flow of time.
