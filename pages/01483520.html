" Power over music does not depend solely on the mere agreement of 'how to do it.' The student in song will never learn the perfection of beauty except from the preparation of the heart . To make a real musician, there must be a sense of the ever-presence of the Creator of all beauty . The boy-musician must begin his day with prayer , and end it with praise .
