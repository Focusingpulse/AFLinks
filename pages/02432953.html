Healed From Alcohol

· archived # 2,432,953
I was in the US Navy for twenty three years. I had not really drunk much of any type of Alcohol before I entered active duty. After I got on my first ship and deployed overseas there was not much of anything to do when we got into port except to drink at the clubs. It did not take too many years before I knew I had a drinking problem, but thought I could handle it. I was always broke and had a hard time paying my bills and feeding my family. I kind of was a mean drunk, and would argue and fight anyone anytime. I began to come to work a little late on a frequent basis, but always called my Chief to offer some excuse of why I was going to be late. I would go to the bars after work in the US and at lunch I would have a few shots and a picture of beer with my lunch. I made it back on base and to the ship or the shop when I was on Shore duty and spent most of the afternoon doing paperwork in my office. Actually it was spent half dozing off and answering the phone if it rang. After numerous meetings with my Chief or Operations officer I knew something had to change, but I did not know how. I was warned that if I did not seek some help They may have to take me to Captains Mast and that could have been disastrous to my career. It was about that time I was invited to a Bible Study at the home of one of the Shipyard workers. I attended on and off for two years. At or near the end of the two years I was working on my ship in a work space where a group of Navigators met at lunch to study the Bible. They asked me a few times, and I said No I got work to do and they would ask me the next time. One day, I was working on a ladder in that space and more of my attention was focused on their Bible study lesson than on my work. I realized later that they were talking just loud enough tor me to hear what they were discussing. They again asked me to join in, and I said, Sure why not.
