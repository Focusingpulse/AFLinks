R. Careaga and is known in his martial-arts practice as Shifu Careaga — is an American electrical engineer, traditional-medicine practitioner and independent researcher from Lexington, Kentucky. He is the founder of the EPEMC Gateway , an open-source research community and web portal for Extended Plasma-Electro-Magnetic Cosmology (EPEMC), a framework that carries the physics of plasma cosmology and the Electric Universe beyond astronomy into geology, meteorology, biology, medicine, archaeology and mythology. Education B.S. in Electrical Engineering (BSEE) — University of Kentucky M.S. in Traditional Oriental Medicine (MSTOM) Work in cosmology Careaga's cosmological writing follows the plasma tradition of Hannes Alfvén , Winston H Bostick and Anthony L Peratt : that the universe is overwhelmingly plasma, that electric currents and magnetic fields shape its structure, and that cosmology should be extrapolated from laboratory plasma behaviour rather than from gravity alone. On that basis he rejects the Big Bang , dark matter and dark energy, and treats the objects called black holes as plasmoid phenomena of a kind produced in the laboratory since the 1950s. What distinguishes EPEMC from plasma cosmology proper is its deliberate breadth. Careaga's stated position is that a cosmology "should describe not only space... but also how your world works, and why things are the way they are," and the EPEMC corpus accordingly extends to electrogeology, electrometeorology, electrobiology, the megafauna extinction, and the interpretation of rock art.
