R. Careaga and is known in his martial-arts practice as Shifu Careaga is an American electrical engineer, traditional-medicine practitioner and independent researcher from Lexington, Kentucky. He is the founder of the EPEMC Gateway , an open-source research community and web portal for Extended Plasma-Electro-Magnetic Cosmology (EPEMC), a framework that carries the physics of plasma cosmology and the Electric Universe beyond astronomy into geology, meteorology, biology, medicine, archaeology
