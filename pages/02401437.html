GC2026-SGG018 Crustal structure of the Lena-Anabar trough from gravimetric and geomagnetic data Albina S. Kulyandina1,2 , Elena Y. Sokolova3,4 , Alena I. Filippova5 1 Yakutsk Branch of FRC GS RAS 2 M.K. Ammosov North-Eastern Federal University 3 All-Russian Research Geological Oil Institute 4 O.Yu. Schmidt Institute of Physics of the Earth, Russian Academy of Sciences 5 Pushkov Institute of Terrestrial Magnetism, Ionosphere and Radiowave Propagation of the Russian Academy of Sciences, Moscow, Russia aleirk@mail.ru We present the results of analysis and interpretation of the gravimetric and geomagnetic fields within the Lena￾Anabar trough (northwestern Yakutia). The potential fields were analyzed based on maps compiling results of 1 : 200 000 surveys. Technologies of GIS INTEGRO allowed us to trace the main features of the spatial morphology, calculate statistical parameters of the potential fields, and construct density and magnetization crustal models. The models were constrained by regional seismic and magnetotelluric data. Three 2D profiles, crossing different parts of the Lena-Anabar trough and covering the adjacent territories of the Lena River delta, were considered in detail. In particular, their analysis allowed us to trace the seismotectonic positions of the May 20, 2011 Khastakh (Mw = 4.8) and August 3, 2024 (mb = 4.8) earthquakes, which are of particular interest due to their occurrence in the areas characterizing by a low level of seismic activity.
