Detailed reports are not available at this time although the experiment is known to have been conducted out of doors. The output from a 2KW microwave oven was attached to a TUBE OF COPPER WIRE MESH which then served as an OPEN WAVEGUIDE. When the oven was turned on, the tube produced a glowing ball 2 to 3 centimeters in diameter that floated along the mesh for nearly 2 minutes. During this period, the ball was seen to change from white to a dazzling orange. -------------------------------------------------------------------- Vangard notes...
