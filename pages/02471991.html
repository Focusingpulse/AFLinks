Time for an Exploration of the Universe, He Says. New York. Jan. 3 — The Sun today prints a further interview with Nikola Tesla, who, as stated in these dispatches yesterday.claims to have communicated with the planet Mars. Mr. Tesla found it necessary, in order to carry on his investigations and his experiments to the extent he desired, to work at an attitude of several thousand feet. He found conditions suitable for his purposes in Colorado and built a laboratory about 10 miles from Pike's Peak. The result of his investigations were first made known when he spoke before the National Red Cross society's new century meeting. While he was conducting his investigations in nis Colorado laboratory one day, the instrument he was using to observe the electrical conidtion of the earth was affected in an unaccountable manner It recorded three distinct though very faint movements, one after the other. These movements were observed not once, but many times, the number of impulses varying, and Mr. Tesla now firmly believes that, with improved apparatus, it will be quite possible for the people of the earth to communicate with the inhabitants of other planets. "I have incessantly thought of this for months," said Mr. Tesla, "until finally I arrived at the conviction, amounting almost to knowledge, that they must be of plaretary origin.
