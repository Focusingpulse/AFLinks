I used about a liter of old rancid cooking oil (unused), a few hundred ml's of methanol and a few grams of lye (sodium hydroxide - red devil drain cleaner). Specifically for unusued oil, it is 5 parts oil and 1 part methanol so 1 liter oil with 200ml of methanol and 3.5 grams of lye. It 'may' be 4:1 ratio on oil/methanol...can't recall off hand. Anyway, with used oil, you have to do a titration (micro batch) to see how much lye you need..maybe 4-6 grams for the above amounts. Mixing methanol and lye until pretty homogenous and lye is dissolved...then blend that to the oil until homogenous...blender helps and so does some heat. Never reuse blender for food. Then pour that mix into some container...I used glass jars....after about 8 hours, the lye has acted as a catalyst to break the fat and oil apart...at the bottom you will have the glycerine settled (soap) and on the top is the more clear and liquidy biodiesel.
