Photobiology

· archived # 1,461,286
( click to enlarge ) Photobiology is the scientific study of the interactions of light (technically, non-ionizing radiation) and living organisms. The field, a branch of radiometry , includes the study of photosynthesis , photomorphogenesis, visual processing, circadian rhythms, bioluminescence , and ultraviolet radiation effects. The division between ionizing radiation and nonionizing radiation is typically considered to be 10 eV, the energy required to ionize an oxygen atom.
