Hi All, Seems I've seen free energy capture device drawings all over the web. Your supposed to put a metal plate in the air, a stake in the ground. Then you collect the small potential difference and store it in a capacitor prior to feeding it to a battery. Has anyone made one of these? I noticed that George Wiseman has a book for sale that shows how to build one. Don't know how much it can collect. I don't recall if the metal plate could be painted or if it had to be unfinished or clearcoated. I believe that most homes are well grounded (to give the third prong on plugs something to do). I've also noticed a lot of buildings being made with metal roofs. Seems like you would have half the circuit already. I don't know if the paint on the metal roofs would hinder collection or not. You would definitely have the height and surface area. I wonder how it compares (powerwise) to Photovoltaics. You wouldn't have to worry about metal roofs being as fragile (get a lot of hail in our neck of the woods don't we Jerry) as PV roofs would. Has anyone built even a simple one to see what it can do?
