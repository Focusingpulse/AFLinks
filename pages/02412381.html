The "Ossie motor" uses four AIR CORE coils and four NdFeB magnets mounted on a old mechanic from a computer fan. The blades of the computer fan have been removed and the Neodymium magnets have been fixed with cyanoacrylate glue on the rotor. Magnets specifications: 4 NdFeB magnets (Bremag 27) of 27 MGoe (208 kJ/m3) polarisation N outward.
