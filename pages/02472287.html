These patents, numbered consecutively from 685,953 to 685,958 , were issued by the United States Patent Office on November 5th. From their evident basic nature the patents may have an important bearing upon the project for the transmission of messages long distances through space, which Mr. Tesla is known to have been working upon for some time. In his introductory remarks Mr. Tesla recognizes that in wireless telegraphy, as commonly known, the electrical impulses reach a very distant receiving station in an extremely feeble and attenuated condition. So extremely feeble are these impulses that it is easy to discern the limitations of wireless telegraphy as today practiced.
