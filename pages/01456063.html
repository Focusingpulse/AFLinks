Bloomfield-Moore withdraws support of Keely Motor Company

· archived # 1,456,063
( click to enlarge ) Mrs. Bloomfield Moore has withdrawn her support. (Press clipping pasted into Mrs. Bloomfield Moore's personal copy of her book " Keely and his Discoveries ".) Her handwritten comment shows that this firm resolution of hers did not last very long: "The day after the publication of the above in 1891, the president of the Keely Motor Company called to tell me there was not a penny in the treasury, soliciting my aid. I left it with Mr. Keely to decide and there was no interruption to the work". Mrs. Bloomfield Moore continued to support Keely until 1898, the year of his death. He died on November 18. She died shortly thereafter, on January 5th, 1899. See Also Keely Keely Motor Company Keely Chronology INVENTOR KEELY’S WILL FILED Inventor Keelys Will Filed Obituary Post Obituary
