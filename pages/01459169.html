The diversion of the dominant current would mean destruction to any mechanical medium used for that purpose, and death to the operator. Sudden unition induces the same effect; but demonstration shows that the concentration of this triple force is as free of percussion as is the breath of an infant against the atmosphere; for the three currents flow together as in one stream, in the mildest sympathetic way, while their discharge after concentration is, in comparison to their accumulation , as the tornado's force to the waft of the butterfly's wing.
