The Apache WWW server (www.apache.com) is, at the time of writing, the most popular WWW server software, and it is free for anyone to use. It often runs on the free Linux operating system, and the two together provide the backbone of the WWW. The immense quantity of useful information and opinion available on the WWW is, in most cases, provided freely, for the general good of all. Sharing gives us power over our own lives. No person can control a thing when it is freely available to all. Sharing is a basic human impulse that binds people together.
