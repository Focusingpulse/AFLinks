Title Particle Propellant Read in full Read in full Author(s) Ricardo L Carezani Keywords Autodynamics , particle propellant, pico-graviton, virtual particles, uncertainty principle, Gravity Published Society for the Advancement of Autodynamics Website https://www.autodynamics.org/particle-propellant/ "Particle Propellant" is a white paper by Ricardo L Carezani answering the most obvious objection to the Autodynamics picture of decay: if a particle propels itself by ejecting part of its mass, like a rocket, where is the exhaust? It is written as a reply to two questions from a physicist in New Hampshire, the first of which Carezani congratulates his correspondent for asking: "You are the first to realize that this question derives from the rocket fuel example." Overview The paper's setting is the absence of a mechanism in gravitation. Newton offered none; general relativity offers space curvature, which Carezani does not accept as machinery; and the graviton, he argues, "represents the first machinery for explaining gravitational fields." Autodynamics's graviton is meant to differ from the virtual particles of field theory in being real. The particle propellant is the same idea applied to decay. The argument The question "Shouldn't the decay particles analogous to 'rocket fuel' be detectable? If not, why not?" And, more sharply, in the follow-up: Autodynamics eliminates the neutrino but adds an undetected particle carrying away part of the mass — "Doesn't the addition of the particle(s) in (b) counter-balance any advantage by eliminating the neutrino?" The answer: orders of magnitude Carezani's reply is that the two are not comparable, because they differ by many orders of magnitude in energy.
