plane of balance

· archived # 1,482,377
"Note the orbit of the earth . Its potential was maximum when its orbit was a true circle around its source . An elliptical orbit , and a straying from its plane of balance with the sun , not only widened its circle of control , but unbalanced its relations with its source by dividing its power by perihelion and aphelion positions in its orbit ."
