Highly reminescent of the TOMI and the SMOT with some new twists. ==================== Anyone researching the pre-history of modern devices based on magnetic ramps with accelerated steel balls may find David M. MacMillan's Rolling Ball Web page of considerable interest. The URL is http://www.database.com/~lemur/rb-index.html It presents a wealth of information about rolling-ball clocks, rolling-ball sculptures, and rolling-ball perpetual motion machines. For example, it discusses the work of Johannes Taisnier (a.k.a. Taisnierus), a Jesuit Priest of the sixteenth century. He designed a device in which a steel ball ascended a magnetic ramp, fell through a hole, and returned to the bottom of the ramp to begin the cycle anew.
