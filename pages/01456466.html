Copper Globe

· archived # 1,456,466
Copper Globe, 48 inches in diameter, 250 horse power See Keely Spinning Motor , 19.05.06 - New York Times - Keely Spinning Motor Also called, dynasphere , musical dynasphere , globe motor , copper globe , musical sphere , etc. Keely made a wide variety of spheres (rotating and not rotating) of different sizes, materials and for different purposes. Of course we don't have pictures of every type and size of globe or sphere design Keely created. Here are a few of what we do have. Sometimes called ' musical dynasphere ', musical sphere , musical globe , sphere motor and similar names. John Worrell Keely developed these devices as a power source and to demonstrate (prove) the principles of science he was discovering. (See What is a Dynasphere .) The globe motor was one of the many different motors developed by Keely .
