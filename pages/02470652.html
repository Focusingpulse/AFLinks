A note of triumphant sense of power sounded in his voice as he proceeded. “Always there would be in its place a soft radiance and pale glow, as when the moon shines through clouds.” The audacity of those men who lead where electricity is to conquer and be conquered is superb to minds untutored in the ways of electricity. Nikola Tesla has said that darkness of night may be converted into day by electricity. He will juggle with night and lift more than a corner of the mantle of the creator of day and night. When Tesla first referred to the idea he himself characterized it as a “dream, nothing more.” But within the week Tesla has declared in private conversation that it is not a “dream,” as he had been content to believe. He has said it is not only practicable but practical to abolish much of night. Tesla believes that immense areas of upper air may be illuminated so that they will shed a faint moonlight sufficient for ordinary street and town illumination. A distance of a hundred miles might be made luminous. From Philadelphia to New York might be lighted. This change in his views from a “dream” stage to a practical possibility is the result of the developments of Tesla’s vacuum tube system of lighting known as “cold light.
