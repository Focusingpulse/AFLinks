Transformation of distribution networks. Modern distribution networks are no longer passive elements; they have become complex dynamic systems with bidirectional power flows and stochastic generation. 2. Key complicating factors: Growth in distributed generation and renewable energy sources — around 23–24 gW of total installed capacity, which is approximately 10% of generating power of Russia's Unified Energy System. Digitalization and creation of "digital power districts" with smart devices and real-time data collection and analysis. 3. Problem with traditional methods. Classical models and tools designed for radial networks with deterministic loads are inadequate for analyzing steady-state and transient processes, stability, and development forecasting under new conditions. 4. Software classification and analysis.
