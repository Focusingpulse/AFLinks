G. F. Leal Ferreira Residence São Carlos, São Paulo, Brazil Nationality Brazilian Known for Co-founding the "Prof. Bernhard Gross" Polymers Group at São Carlos; late papers questioning the standard interpretation of the electromagnetic field Scientific career Fields Electrets and dielectrics ; charge transport in polymers; the foundations of electrodynamics G F Leal Ferreira (Guilherme Fontes Leal Ferreira) was a Brazilian physicist at the Instituto de Física de São Carlos of the Universidade de São Paulo (USP). He spent his career on electrets, dielectric materials and charge transport in polymers, and co-founded the research group in which that tradition took root in Brazil. He appears on this wiki through a different body of work: a series of short papers written after his retirement, questioning how the concepts of electromagnetism — fields, potentials, the Poynting vector — should be understood. Career Leal Ferreira worked at São Carlos, where the Instituto de Física de São Carlos (formerly the Instituto de Física e Química de São Carlos) developed one of Brazil's principal research traditions in dielectrics and electrets. He is counted among the Brazilian scientists formed under the influence of Bernhard Gross (1905–2002), the pioneer of electret research in Brazil, alongside such figures as Joaquim Costa Ribeiro, Sérgio Mascarenhas, Yvonne Mascarenhas and Roberto Faria. His role, however, was more than that of an inheritor. The institute's own annual reports record that the Grupo de Polímeros "Prof. Bernhard Gross" was created in the mid-1970s on the initiative of Leal Ferreira and Milton S. Campos , arising out of Gross's visits to São Carlos: O Grupo de Polímeros "Prof. Bernhard Gross" foi criado em meados dos anos 70, por iniciativa dos Profs. Guilherme F. Leal Ferreira e Milton S.
