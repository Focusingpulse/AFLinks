Overview Displacement Current 2 is a four-page paper by David Tombe — Frederick David Tombe of Belfast — dated 11 October 2008 and published in the General Science Journal . The file itself is titled Displacement Current and the Electrotonic State . It is the second of a group on the subject, alongside Displacement Current and Displacement Current in the Coulomb Gauge . The framework is a two-tier aether. Electric current is "a flow of the primary substance that fills all of space", to be distinguished from the luminiferous medium, which is "a sea of tiny aether whirlpools". Charge is pressure and current is momentum in this fluid, positive particles are aether sources and negative ones sinks, and "gravity is an all pervasive radial electric current which constitutes a rarefied flow of aether that gives rise to a tension, and hence to a pull force." The paper's thesis is an identification: Maxwell's displacement current is the magnetic vector potential A. Tombe's route is short. Faraday's law gives E = −∂A/∂t, so ∂E/∂t = −∂²A/∂t² (1) A = ε∂E/∂t (2, "Displacement Current") A = −ε∂²A/∂t² (3) which he reads as simple harmonic motion "in which ε is the inverse of the elastic constant".
