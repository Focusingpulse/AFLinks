Improve a little everyday!

· archived # 2,432,389
Hi guys I am new! It's great to be here and it seems to be a really good forum. I would like to share something with you that has helped me everyday for years now. That is to try and improve a little everyday working at things like your relationships, money, or just general goals in life. Working on one of these everyday, learning something new, spending a bit more time with the ones you love most or spending a little time earning some extra cash or improving your chances of promotion at work. I found it amazing just by working on these areas everyday for just an hour or two has gradually bit by bit has helped my life enormously. Hope this helps a little. Chris.
