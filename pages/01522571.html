Summary An open chat hosted by Franklin Hu with Bill Lucas , Dennis Allen , Harry Ricker , Ian Cohen and Bob Gray , on a new streaming platform that gave everyone trouble a Google account needed to comment, no visible flag to catch the host's attention, an echo until Hu put headphones on, and Ricker's connection so poor that he had to turn his video off to be heard at all. The subject was light, and specifically polarization.
