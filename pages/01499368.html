THE ENVIRONMENTAL NEWS NETWORK - YOUR FREE ENERGY CHANNEL ENN home page at enn.com, have an entire channel devoted to energy. By submitting press releases to ENN Direct, your content will appear in the appropriate channel as well as in the ENN Direct section, which works as a promotional tool for your Institute. This service is free of charge. If you wish to sign up for ENN Direct you may go to http://www.enn.com/affiliates. There you will see the registration form. When asked for a user name and password simply make up your own. You will then see our template into which you can cut and paste your content. We welcome your registration and submittals pertaining to environmentally related topics and encourage you to contact us with any questions you may have.
