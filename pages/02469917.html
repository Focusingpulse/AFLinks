Cook of Chicago, were placed on exhibition at the American Museum of Natural History yesterday. Edwin Markham was present at the presentation of the pictures, which were accepted by President H. Fairfield Osborn. Although not a scientist in a strict sense of the word, Mr. Markham was a friend of Burroughs, John Muir and other naturalists and the author of poems of nature and of a volume largely devoted to a description of the natural beauties of California. Portraits of President Osborn of the Museum and of Joseph H. Choate by Princess Lwoff are already a part of the Museum’s collection.
