[Special Despatch to The Evening Post] PITTSBURGH, June 14. — F. K. Hain, Frank Jay Gould, and William Northrop of the Manhattan Elevated Railway Company went out to Brinton and Wilmerding to-day to inspect the plant of the Westinghouse Electric Company, in connection with bids for an $8,000,000 contract for appliances and supplies. They deny the report that the contract has been let to the Westinghouse Company. F. K. Hain, speaking for the party this morning, said: "There is not a particle of truth in the report that the Manhattan Elevated Railway Company has let this big contract to the Westinghouse people. The contract has been let to no one, and we are not even prejudiced in favor of the Westinghouse Company and against the General Electric. We have asked for bids, but the matter has gone no further. Our purроse in coming here is to inspect the Westinghouse plant, that we may be fully informed in connection with the bids." At the offices of the General Electric Company it was said that the reported signing of the contract for the equipping of the Manhattan system with electricity was incorrect.
