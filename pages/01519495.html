Cold Fusion Now! Podcast Host Ruby Carat Format Podcast Language English Launched 2018 Ended 2019 Status Ended — complete archive of 26 episodes Focus Interviews with leading cold fusion / LENR researchers Podcast coldfusionnow.org/cfnpodcast Website coldfusionnow .org Cold Fusion Now! is an audio podcast presented by Ruby Carat , recorded across 2018 and 2019, in which she surveyed scientists and technologists in the cold fusion / LENR community on the state of research and development toward a new atomic power from water. The series ran to twenty-six episodes and is complete; the archive remains available. It takes its name from the wider outreach effort Carat founded in 2010, after the Deepwater Horizon oil spill, to build a public presence for scientists working in the field — an all-volunteer project through which she has also written several hundred articles and made documentary films. The interviews The value of the series is as a primary record: a set of long conversations with the field's principal figures, recorded at a single point in time and by an interviewer who knew the technical ground. Guests included the nuclear chemist Edmund Storms , formerly of Los Alamos; Michael McKubre, formerly director of the energy research centre at SRI International; Yasuhiro Iwamura of Tohoku University; Pamela Mosier-Boss, the analytical chemist behind the US Navy SPAWAR work; and David J Nagel of George Washington University. The production was a cooperative effort — music and editing by Esa Ruoho, website support from Eureka Web Design — reflecting the volunteer basis of the project as a whole. Status The podcast concluded in 2019. Carat continues the wider Cold Fusion Now! effort, and coldfusionnow.org remains active with the episodes archived for download.
