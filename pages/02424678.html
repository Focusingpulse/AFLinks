Detection of intact lava tubes at Marius Hills on the Moon by SELENE (Kaguya) Lunar Radar Sounder T. Kaku1,2, J. Haruyama2, W. Miyake1, A. Kumamoto3, K. Ishiyama2, T. Nishibori2, K. Key Points:  We investigated the SELENE Lunar Radar Sounder (LRS) data to detect underlying lava tubes.  A distinctive echo pattern was found in the LRS data obtained around the Marius Hills Hole (MHH), a possible skylight of a lava tube.  The LRS echo pattern indicates the existence of an intact lava tube.  Around an area (13.00-15.00°N, 301.85-304.01°E) around MHH, similar LRS echo patterns were found at several locations.  The locations exhibiting the echo pattern are consistent with mass deficits suggested by the GRAIL gravity data analysis. © 2017 American Geophysical Union.
