SPAF-2026-Session 1025 CDU: 551.2:551.71/.72 Capabilities of an integrated set of geophysical methods for predicting ground collapses in the nukutsky district of the irkutsk region Pavel A. Chasovitin1 , Buddo V. Igor1 1 Institute of Earth Crust of the Siberian Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences chasovitin@crust.irk.ru The relevance of this study is determined by the increasing occurrence of karst sinkhole in residential and agricultural areas within the Nukutsky and Osinsky districts of the Irkutsk Region. This paper proposes an integrated set of geophysical methods for solving the problem of predicting ground collapses in the Nukutsky District of the Irkutsk Region. The study area is located in the Nukutsky District, 45 km north of Khadokhan village, on the shore of the Bratsk Reservoir. The sulfate-carbonate karst in the southern part of the Bratsk Reservoir has remained active throughout the reservoir’s operational history and is classified as a hazardous regional geological process. Cases of large collapses in arable fields and activation of deformation processes in karst areas within the Khadakhan-Melkhituy karst massif were recorded, in 1976, 1977, the spring of 1983, and in 1990. Fluctuations in the reservoir water level act as a trigger for karst processes. The close relationship between the groundwater level and the reservoir level determines karst activation throughout the backwater zone and high rates of leaching of sulfate-carbonate rocks.
