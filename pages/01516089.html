The spin-off RIFT (Renewable Iron Fuel Technology) from the TU Eindhoven wants to use iron fuel on a global scale as a sustainable alternative to fossil fuels as a way to lower CO2 emissions. The pilot project that it is running together with Ennatuurlijk is an important step towards achieving this goal. In the Dutch city of Helmond, the start-up is now working on a proof of concept boiler with a capacity of one megawatt. This can be used – via the Ennatuurlijk heat network – to provide heating to five hundred households. An infinite system The system works as follows: iron powder is burned in the boiler. A lot of heat is released in this process, for example, for heat networks. What is left over is rust powder. This can be converted into iron powder again by using hydrogen, which in turn ‘charges’ the powder and creates a circular fuel. The start-up is now building a test plant for both of these components.
