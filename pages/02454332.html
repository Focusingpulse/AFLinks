Science fair project: make a huge prism SCI. HOBBYIST | PROJECTS | GOOD STUFF | NEW STUFF | SEARCH Google: CREATE A GIANT PRISM 1997 William Beaty Years ago I *made* a big prism from scratch, using polyester casting resin. This material has very good dispersion (makes good rainbows.) For an earlier science museum exhibit, our machinist had milled and polished a Plexiglas prism, but the dispersion wasn't great and the rainbow was narrow. We had to find something better. We ended up using holographic diffraction film such as PS-08A from Project Star . But while trying various things, I ended up making a nice polyester prism which casts good rainbows. For the prism mold, I took a couple of 4" x 16" plates of glass 1/4" thick, cleaned with alcohol, put a tiny bit of spray-on silicone mold-release on them, taped them together on one edge to form a V-shaped trough, and plugged the ends of the trough with big chunks of plasticine clay.
