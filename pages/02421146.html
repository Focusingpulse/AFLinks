Michael Marino Born 1944 (age 81–82) Residence San Rafael, CA, United States Nationality USA Known for The Space Energy Newsletter and the Space Energy Association Scientific career Fields New energy and antigravity research; scientific publishing Michael "Mike" Marino (born 1944) is an American researcher associated with the new-energy and antigravity research community, and a resident of San Rafael, California. His recorded connection is editorial rather than experimental: he is associated with the Space Energy Association and its publications, which for roughly a decade from the early 1990s were among the principal outlets for the free-energy device community in the United States. The Space Energy Association The Space Energy Association (SEA) operated from Post Office Box 1136, Clearwater, Florida, and its leading figure was Donald A. Kelly . It described itself as existing "to offer top quality free energy information to our membership," and it issued two publications: the Space Energy Newsletter , in circulation by December 1993 the Space Energy Journal , a quarterly, running through at least 1996 and still taking subscriptions in 1998 The Journal was dedicated, in its own words, to the work of Nikola Tesla, Victor Schauberger, T. Henry Moray, T. J. J. See, Alfred Hubbard, Erwin Saxl and Hans Coler. Its editorial policy was unusually explicit about where it stood: unlike "some other alternate energy journals," it intended "to endorse and support free energy units and systems which show a positive test and track record," publishing construction plans and putting members in touch with the inventors, while disclaiming liability for patent infringement and for injury during construction. The association was not merely a publisher. It undertook at least one construction project of its own, a prototype of the Swiss M-L converter — the electrostatic machine developed by Paul Baumann at the Methernitha community, a development of the Wimshurst generator.
