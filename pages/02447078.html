10/15/16 - Keely - Astor finances Keely Interest in the mysterious discoveries of John W. Keely, whose experiments with a new source of power have puzzled the world for a score of years, has been suddenly revived and intensified by the announcement that John Jacob Astor is to take the place of Mrs. Bloomfield Moore as financial backer of the inventor. Mr. Astor has a marked inclination for scientific matters, and has visited Mr. Keely's workshop at #1,432 North Twentieth street on several occasions. Concerning his recent work Mr. Keely said yesterday that he had succeeded in obtaining a new power, the principle of which consists of enormously rapid vibrations. Mr. Keely said that after procuring 40,000 vibrations per second he had overcome gravity. He had succeeded in producing as high as 3,000,800,000,000 vibrations per second.
