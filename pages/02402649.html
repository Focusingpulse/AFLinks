The condition for the existence of metaplectic structure is same as of the spinor structure and not met in the case of CP2. One however expects that also the modified metaplectic structure exists if one couples spinors to an odd integer multiple of K¨ahler gauge potential. For triality 1 representation assignable to quarks one has n = 1.
