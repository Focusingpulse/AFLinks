Section II - Sponsors’ Spot Section I - What’s Happening SOON: Saturday, August 19, 2023, 1:00 pm CT Beverly Rubik will present the 45th free USPA Masterclass, “How to Discern the Truth:- Lessons from a Scientist” Beverly’s Presentation: Dr. Beverly Rubik has witnessed the stifling or suppression of novel scientific discoveries that challenged mainstream dogma, from alternative therapies to studies on parapsychology and subtle energies. Today humanity is suffering from an even larger problem, a global “pandemic” of disinformation and misinformation. Censorship, downright lies, and scientific fraud have become rampant for any information or ideas that are perceived to challenge the “official narrative.” Our freedom of speech has been seriously curtailed. So, how can we know what is true? Dr. Rubik will delve more deeply into this enormous problem and will share her strategies toward discerning reliable information, as well as identifying the "red flags" where more critical questioning is appropriate. About Beverly Rubik: She earned her Ph.D. in biophysics at the University of California, Berkeley. In 1997 she founded Institute for Frontier Science, a nonprofit laboratory near San Francisco. Her scientific contributions include studies on the biofield, energy medicine, water, consciousness, and adverse health effects of wireless radiation. She has published almost 100 scientific papers and two books. She is a board member of the USPA.
