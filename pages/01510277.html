Would +N-N+N-N+ work? That would be like 4 cells with only 2 electrolyte gaps each, in parallel with each other. It would be OK for a 4 V power supply, which would give you 2 V per electrolyte gap. If they are all in the same common electrolyte bath, it will not be a true series cell and the neutrals may be somewhat innefective due to current leakage around them, though.
