As stated in IAEA Safety Standards Series No. GS-R-2 “Preparedness and Response for a Nuclear or Radiological Emergency”, which establishes the requirements for an adequate level of preparedness for and response to a nuclear or radiological emergency in any State, “…first responders shall take all practicable and appropriate actions to minimize the consequences of a nuclear or radiological emergency…”.
