L. Chan Residence Hong Kong Knownfor Cosmology , Big Bang , Regenerating Universe Adam W. L. Chan is a Hong Kong-based independent researcher whose work centers on cosmology . He is known for his ideas concerning the origin and structure of the universe, including the Big Bang and a model he describes as a Regenerating Universe . Work Chan's research addresses questions in cosmology, in particular the origin of the universe. He has advanced the concept of a "Regenerating Universe" as an alternative framework for understanding cosmic origins and the Big Bang . External links Natural Philosophy Wiki: Adam W. L.
