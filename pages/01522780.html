Visualize water as the omnipresent medium and tiny bubbles swirling around to eventually give form to everything that can be seen. You'd have an analogy of the way Paramahamsa Tewari, a nuclear engineer with a passion for physics sees the universe. Tewari's Space Vortex Theory (SVT) explains universal reality in a radically different way from that proposed by physicists today. A new book titled Discovering Universal Reality is available from his site ( PDF download - 1.3MB ) or in print from Stonehill Foundation in Kerala, India. Tewari summarizes and explains the principles of SVT in a way that is understandable to everyone. You do not have to follow his equation
