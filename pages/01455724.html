Allah

· archived # 1,455,724
noun: Muslim name for the one and only God Allah (English pronunciation: is the Arabic word for God (literally 'the God ', as the initial "Al-" is the definite article). It is used mainly by Muslims to refer to God in Islam , Arab Christians, and often, albeit not exclusively, by Bahas, Arabic-speakers, Indonesian, Malaysian and Maltese Christians, and Mizrahi Jews.
