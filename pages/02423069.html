Understanding the Spiritual World I — GA 154. The Presence of the Dead (1990)

👤 Rudolf Steiner · 📅 18 April 1914 · archived # 2,423,069
, Berlin Translated by Christian von Arnim Understanding the Spiritual World I [ 1 ] When you remember a dream, it will probably be quite obvious to you that during the dream you merely observed the images weaving before your soul without having a clear awareness of yourself. Self-awareness, then, is not as clear in dreaming as it is in waking consciousness. The images weaving before the soul present two types of scenes. There are either series of images familiar to the dreamer because they relate to recent or not-so-recent events, or scenes where such events are changed in all sorts of ways,
