ON THE LOW ENERGY ADDITION CHAIN EQUATION THEOPHILUS AGAMA Abstract. We develop a criterion for an addition chain to have low energy and pose a related classification problem. 1. Introduction Let Cn : s0 = 1 < s1 < · · · < sh = n be an addition chain leading to n with si = sσ(i) + sτ(i) (σ(i) ≥ τ (i), 1 ≤ i ≤ h). In [1], we define the energy of the addition chain Cn as E(Cn) := X h i=1 sτ(i) . We say that the energy of the addition chain is low if En = n − 1. On the other hand, we say that the addition chain has excess energy if En ≥ n. In this paper, we introduce a criterion for the energy of an addition to be low. We pose the following classification problem related to low-energy addition chains: Problem 1.1. Let n ≥ 3.
