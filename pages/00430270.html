1 On the Planck mass and length Branko Zivlak, bzivlak@gmail.com Novi Sad, December 2025 Keywords: Planck, proton, gravitational constant, CODATA This paper presents a somewhat shortened version of a work previously published on the General Science Journal platform, with a simultaneous correction of the sign in equation (4). The paper presents two complementary formulas for the Planck mass and the Planck length, which yield higher accuracy than the values reported in the CODATA reports. The formulas are proton-oriented and are analyzed on the basis of data from the CODATA reports covering the period 1986–2022. For this reason, we first present the input data used [1].
