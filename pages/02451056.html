You wrote; > I'm looking for a device that can detect a portable recorder (in = > function) at the distance of about 1 or 2 meters. That is a concern a lot of people I know have, of being taped either voice or video without their knowledge or consent. Not that we are criminials with something to hide, but its no ones business what people do privately. So fight fire with fire. I think scrambling or destroying their equipment would be easier to do than detecting. A audio tape recorder usese a biasing frequency to 'loosen' the ferrous oxide on the tape and make for better recordings due to the increased tape sensitivity. I believe it runs around 20khz but you'd have to look that up. I'd scramble it before trying to detect it as there are so many other devices around, watches, radios, even fluorescent lamps that could give you false readings... I've not ordered any to of these devices or plans but would one day like to. Seems like they would be perfect if they work as described..it is after all hardware using regular electronics for novel purposes.
