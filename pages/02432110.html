Project Collective

· archived # 2,432,110
Greetings fellow PATHS users! We are very pleased to announce the start of a humanitarian project that is unlike anything ever done before. Kevin and Aaron brought this idea with them to the ESM-PATHS convention that took place recently in Las Vegas, NV. The working title of this project is "Project Collective." We all now know and appreciate that through the Law of Attraction, we draw all manner of experiences into our being. Place your attention on anything long enough and with enough emotion, and it will become reality. If the power of a single individual's thoughts is enough to attract a new reality, what would the power of thousands be capable of attracting? Can we cause rain to pour in drought-sticken countries? Can we create clean drinking water for the inhabitants of third-world nations? Can we feed the millions who go to sleep each night hungry? Aside from this, Aaron and Kevin introduced us, at the conference, to Princeton University's Research Project entitled PEAR: (Princeton Engineering Anomalies Research). Here is an introduction, taken from the site: Scientific Study of Consciousness-Related Physical Phenomena. From: Princeton Engineering Anomalies Research The Princeton Engineering Anomalies Research (PEAR) program was established at Princeton University in 1979 by Robert G. Jahn, then Dean of the School of Engineering and Applied Science, to pursue rigorous scientific study of the interaction of human consciousness with sensitive physical devices, systems, and processes common to contemporary engineering practice. Over the next 28 years, an interdisciplinary staff of engineers, physicists, psychologists, and humanists has conducted a comprehensive agenda of experiments and developing complementary theoretical models to enable better understanding of the role of consciousness in the establishment of physical reality.
