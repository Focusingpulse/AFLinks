This study addresses these challenges by using highaltitude frozen lakes as pressure-sensing surfaces to produce accurate observations of the water content of snowfall at a range of sites in the European Alps, west–central Himalayas, and central Rockies, which are subsequently used to test and constrain snowfall output from a 1.5-km resolution version of the atmosphere-only Met Ofﬁce Uniﬁed Model (MetUM). The model resolution is on a similar scale to the size of the lakes, as well as sufﬁciently ﬁne to represent the critical interactions between atmospheric ﬂows and the complex orography that inﬂuences snowfall and especially extremes.
