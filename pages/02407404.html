INDOOR MUSHROOM GROWING TECHNIQUE FOR THE BOIL - A - BAG. by D.H Jarrold. Firstly: Getting some “Boil a Bags” is all important in using this technique to grow your mushrooms. Boil a bags are plastic bags that can go in the pressure cooker or microwave without melting. the brand i use are made by a company called Lakeland Plastics, in packs of 50 at a size of 8 x 12 or that’s 20cm by 30cm: cost is about £2.50p per pack of 50.
