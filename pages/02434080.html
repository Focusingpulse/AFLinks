KeelyNet Donation Methods - 2001 Making a Donation to Support KeelyNet Thanks for your interest in helping to keep KeelyNet continue its presence on the web, gathering and posting new and useful information. We have always sought to post as many files as we can so they will be freely available to all interested parties. It does cost bucks to keep the website up and running, buy equipment, add new information from many sources and spend the time it takes to do it. We plan to expand into our own experiments and report findings back to KeelyNet, that too takes bucks, so we do appreciate any donations or book/video purchases you can make . As of 2001, we have now spanned a continuous 13 year online presence since we began as a BBS in 1988, moving to the Internet in 1995 and we intend to continue far into the future with your help. Our files have been posted on many sites across the Internet and spawned the creation of other websites with similar goals, i.e. to help bring about working free energy, gravity control and electronic health technologies by free sharing of online files.
