A friend says he was stationed on an island, very small out near Guam...working at a military base...the wives and families lived on a slightly larger neighboring island... As I recall the story, his wife and a lady friend used to ride their bicycles around the island on a paved road, part of which ran past the airfield...remember the island was small.... One day, while riding, they stopped to rest near the landing strip and next to the beach....his wife noticed something glint in the air, looked up and saw a man hovering there, wearing a metallic suit. She told her friend who also saw this floating man...they remained as quiet as possible since the man seemed to not pay them any attention, just hanging there about 100 feet up and slightly over the ocean...he seemed to be looking at something near the hangar of the airstrip....after awhile, he slowly spun around to face the ocean, then began to move away with increasing speed until he was gone from sight....they could not believe what they had seen....they swore to tell no one but their husbands.....thinking it was either a top secret experiment from the nearby base or someone with advanced technology was spying....
