TUESDAY, MAY 19, 1896, P. 4/4 IS THIS THE NEW MAHATMA? The President of the Theosophical Society announced publicly, on Sunday last, the name of the Society’s new Mahatma or Occult Director, which it had been the intention of the Society to conceal for one year from all persons except the fourteen members of the Inner Circle. The premature disclosure was due, it appears, to the “Great Unknown’s” indiscreet admission to her “hired girl” that she was indeed the extraordinary personage in question. President HARGROVE says that “the real leader of the Theosophical movement throughout the world and the heir to the occult succession of those preceding her” is Mrs. KATHERINE A. TINGLEY of this city. We learn from the published biographies of this associate of Koot Hoomi and Morya that she was born in Newburyport, Mass., forty years ago; that she has studied Spiritualism , Theosophy , and the like for ‘‘thirty-five years’’; that her first husband was one GEORGE W. PARENT , a barkeeper and afterward a detective, who died nine years ago; and that her second husband is a clerk employed by the Red Star Steamship Company .
