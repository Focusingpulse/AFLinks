Seances / Channelling

· archived # 1,508,033
Anybody ever had any interesting experiences with seances? When I was in my early teens (many years ago!) my mother, sister & I had one at our house. My then ballet teacher was very "into" this sort of thing so she showed us how to set it up & called forth the spirits. So there we were at night, lights out & candles lit,hushed & eerie atmosphere, sitting around our timber dining table. We'd cut out paper with letters of the alphabet written on them & placed them in a circle on the table with a "yes" & "no" at either end & a kitchen glass upturned in the middle of the table. Christine asked for the spirits to come & we all placed one finger on the upended glass. At first we got an entity that wasn't very cooperative but after trying for a bit longer we got one that Christine recognised, that had come through to her on previous occasions. This was a young boy who had died in a fire in England in the 1800's. So initially we were learning a bit about who he was whilst thinking that it was really Christine moving the glass herself to spell out the responses. However things quickly progressed & became a whole lot more scary . My mother asked it a question about something in the future & the glass moved with all of our fingers on it & spelt out NO FUTURE. Well my mother kept trying to ask something about the future. Each time the glass moved with our fingers on it more & more jerkily to spell out NO FUTURE. By this time it was starting to dawn on us that this was really happening & that it wasn't a trick Christine was playing on us. Even now I can still remember the feel of the jerk & pull of the glass as it moved across the table, you could sense the energy there. Anyway my mother asked one more question about the future & the glass went ballistic. It just shot all over the table in every direction, moving so quickly that none of us were able to keep a finger on it. We all just sat there in stunned silence watching this glass flying across the table all by itself. Needles
