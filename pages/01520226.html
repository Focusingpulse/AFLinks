Title Gravitational Fields and Magnetic Fields Read in full Link to paper Author(s) Ralph Sansbury Keywords magnetic fields, Gravitational attraction, planets, stars Published 2012 Journal Proceedings of the NPA Volume 9 No. of pages 5 Pages 480-484 Abstract The Higgs boson and graviton supposedly exchanged between pieces of neutral matter give matter the property of mass and the gravitational attraction between masses that cause matter to clump together in stars, planets, moons etc. We are proposing another rationale based on the spinning orbiting nature of stars planets and their moons. Electric dipoles are produced by spinning orbiting astronomical bodies which give rise to their magnetic and gravitational fields. Overview Ralph Sansbury proposes that gravitation and planetary magnetism are the same thing, and that both arise from electric dipoles induced inside atomic nuclei by the spin and orbital motion of the body. The paper belongs to a wider programme — Sansbury also argues that light propagation is instantaneous and that observed delays occur in the receiver — set out in his book Faster Than Light and in companion papers to the same conference. The mechanism runs as follows. Mechanical forces are ultimately electrical, since matter is made of charges. A rotating or orbiting body's atoms are therefore under a constantly changing electrical force, and because the force changes, the surrounding electrons cannot fully screen it before the nuclei respond. Each change leaves a small charge polarisation inside the nucleus. Because the Earth spins about its axis and orbits the Sun, these induced dipoles align radially and along lines of longitude. Collinear radial dipoles attract, which Sansbury identifies with gravity. Parallel longitudinal dipoles repel, which orients a compass needle.
