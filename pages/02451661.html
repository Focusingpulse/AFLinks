Hyiq.org - Vacuum Triode Amplifier D-LINK DSL-604+ 4 Port Wireless Router Tips Tricks and Configuration. I must say, the first D-LINK DSL 604+ Wireless Four Port Router, I bought didnot run very well at all. I had to have it replaced after a couple of weeks, after it was a complete nightmare to make visible on my Network, and then it just stopped responding all together. This was quite obviously a firmware instability issue. It did have the first release of firmware on it which appears to be rather unstable. The latest version of firmware can be downloaded here http://www.dlink.com.au/tech/drivers/files/routers/dsl604+.htm it is much more stable and seems to keep the D-LINK DSL-604+ Four Port Wireless Router running fairly smooth. There is an issue of intermittent freezing of the Router however due to Proxy DNS issues.
