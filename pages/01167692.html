To prioritize genes and regulatory 8 features associated with early host responses, we reanalyzed public leaf RNA-seq data from 9 Guiwei and Yurong cultivars (GSE201243; mock and inoculated samples, three biological 10 replicates per group). Differential-expression analysis identified 17 significant DEGs in Guiwei 11 and 117 in Yurong at adjusted P < 0.05, suggesting a broader transcriptional response in 12 Yurong. Eighteen highly responsive genes were selected for structural, motif, promoter, and 13 candidate interaction analyses. These candidates included annotations associated with defense 14 or stress responses, including jacalin-like lectin, pathogenesis-related protein, heat-shock 15 protein, thaumatin/osmotin-like proteins, cytochrome P450, lipid-transfer proteins, and calcium￾16 binding proteins. A selected-gene interaction contrast prioritized LITCHI019519, LITCHI001510, 17 LITCHI028401, LITCHI017676, LITCHI028104, and LITCHI019183 as potential cultivar￾18 dependent response genes. Promoter analysis of 2-kb upstream regions detected stress- and 19 hormone-associated cis-elements, and a random-promoter background test supported 20 enrichment of ARE, MeJA-responsive motifs, TCA-elements, and TC-rich repeats.
