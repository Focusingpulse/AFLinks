Plasma may be dispersed into greater tenuity by dissociation or condensed into 'solid' matter . See Etheric Elements for the different kinds or types of ether and plasma . Same as Keely 's interatomic and etheric subdivisions. One form of plasma Keely called " low atomic ether ".
