UNRAVELING THE BIOLOGY OF CELLULAR AGING We grow old because our cells grow old -- a process we think of as inevitable and immutable. Cancer cells, however, seem to escape the process of aging and become "immortal." This review of recent research sheds some light on the cellular aging process and suggests a connection between the biology of cellular aging and the biology of cancer.
