Source of your Being

· archived # 1,462,142
" And when I say: 'making true contact with It', I mean that the prayer of supplication for this benefit or that is not making true contact with your SOURCE OF BEING . Your prayer is certainly received into the Source of Being , and an answer is frequently received swiftly and the need is fulfilled, even as you have asked. But true contact with the Source of your Being , is only experienced when you have sufficiently cleansed your consciousness of the gross human ego-drive and have spent some time in meditation and a regular emotionally powerful ' reaching out ' in consciousness to your Source seeking contact and renewal and refreshment of spirit . This is the true purpose behind existence. A constant and mutual reciprocation of communication between the Source of all Being and creation ." [ Christ Returns - Speaks His Truth , Letter 5 , page 5]
