Patera1 Abstract The Spherical Pythagorean Theorem is derived by performing an infinitesimal rotation of a spherical right triangle about a vertex not containing the right angle. The infinitesimal areas swept out by the sides of the spherical triangle are easily computed and used to derive the Spherical Pythagorean Theorem.
