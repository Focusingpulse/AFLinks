1 Viktor Strohm vfstrohm@yahoo.de Kepler's laws as properties of the kinematic equations of motion of a point along curves of the second order Abstract. Differential equations of motion on curves of the second order are inferred. Solutions to equations are made by computer programs. The results of the calculation are compared with Kepler's laws. keywords: kinematic equations, Kepler's laws, ellipse.
