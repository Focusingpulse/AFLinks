Iraci (born July 22, 1981) is an American author and philosopher based in Buffalo, New York. He is known for his writings on the Aether and the Expanding Earth . Biography Iraci was born on July 22, 1981, and resides in Buffalo, New York, United States. Work Iraci's interests lie in natural philosophy, with a focus on the concept of the Aether and the theory of the Expanding Earth .
