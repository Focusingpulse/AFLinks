We need only accept that the universe is a ‘logical’ construct that includes the observer as an integral part. By “referent” is meant that which logically validates or confirms the nature of an object or state, by qualitative or quantitative comparison. 0-logic is absolute chance, i.e. that which cannot, in principle, be predicted. We observe both order and chaos in the universe. Chaos is that which takes logic out of the perfect symmetry of thesis-antithesis, thus allowing observable interactions to occur, instead of displaying a frozen, sterile logical structure without relative motion. 1-logic is self-referential. An example is Russell’s Paradox. Let R be the set of all sets that are not members of themselves. If R is not a member of itself, then by definition it is a member of itself; if it is a member of itself, then it must not be a member of itself, because it is the set of all sets that are not members of themselves.
