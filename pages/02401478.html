GC2026-PERM016 Numerical Modeling of the Influence of Sedimentation Hiatuses on the Preservation of the Magnetostratigraphic Record Dmitrii A. Ushakov1,2 , Vladimir E. Pavlov2 1 Lomonosov Moscow State University 2 O.Yu. Schmidt Institute of Physics of the Earth, Russian Academy of Sciences ushakovda@my.msu.ru Sedimentary sequences provide a major source of information on the evolution of the geomagnetic field. However, the magnetostratigraphic record may be significantly distorted by erosion and sedimentary hiatuses, and up to 90% of geological time may be absent from individual sections. Additional distortion results from discrete paleomagnetic sampling, which defines the final magnetostratigraphic section. The aim of this study is to quantitatively assess the influence of hiatuses of varying duration and sampling resolution on the preservation of the magnetostratigraphic record and the accuracy of reconstruction of the original geomagnetic signal. To address this problem, numerical modeling was performed. Model sections with a duration of 5 Myr [1] were generated under two geomagnetic reversal frequency regimes: 4 and 20 reversals per million years[2]. The duration of transitional zones was set to 10 kyr. Sedimentation hiatuses were simulated using both uniform and Poisson distributions of durations [3], with total section loss ranging from 10% to 90%. The proportions of lost magnetozones, completely removed transitional zones, and the reduction in transitional zone thickness were evaluated.
