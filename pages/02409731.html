HOSPITALS医院建筑 Hospital is an indispensable part of the human life. It is one of the most complex but also the most interesting architecture types, which is a unity of technology and construction. The book is a collection of selected hospital projects completed during the last five years, which not only illustrate the functional importance of such purpose-built edifices but furthermore give consideration to the aesthetic criteria of the architecture. It will give the readers comprehensive information and suggestion about the key points of hospital design. Through these projects we hope the book could explain the essence of hospital architecture to the readers.
