Desyatov5 5Federal State Unitary Enterprise "Keldysh Research Center", Moscow, Russia New Energy Times 2 INTRODUCTION The aim of the report is to present some preliminary results of both experimental and theoretical investigation of the processes and phenomena that are connected with the optimal fusion reactions in liquid targets. It is well known that the most perspective and ecologically safe type of fusion reactions is connected with the p + B11 → 3 He4 reaction with ∆E = 8.7 MeV energy release and without the creation of neutrons and formation of any radioactive waste. For this reaction the optimal energy of interacting moving protons is about EpB,opt = 675 KeV. In usual uniform systems like cold or warm stationary plasma the probability of such reaction is very low. It is the direct result of high Coulomb potential barrier presence. In our opinion one of the most perspective methods for optimization of such reaction is connected with using of turbulence and cavitation phenomena in the volume of a liquid (in this case - in volume of light water).
