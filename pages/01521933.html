Magnetic field lines carry a pressure in their equatorial plane and a tension along their axis. The paper's stated purpose is to separate two things the model treats as the same substance in different states. Gravity is "a rarefied aether inflow" toward the Sun; electromagnetic radiation is "a pressurized outward movement of this same aether", and the question is why the two do not interfere.
