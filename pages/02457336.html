These resonances or "orders of vibration" could be so projected as to inducea variety of effects in the experimental test mass. The most important of these two properties is the realization that all flows of force are composed of triple currents. These triple currents have specific phase relationships which can be so manipulated as to generate unusual effects in mind and mass. Each current can result in a "mode" of action which will govern the overall manifestation of a mass.
