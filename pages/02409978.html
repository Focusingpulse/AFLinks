i Principles and Practices of Rock Blasting This book enriches the readers with the advances in rock blasting techniques for mining and civil construction rock excavation projects, including the need for and importance of rock blasting, related theories, challenges in rock blasting operations and rock blasting methodology for excavation of coal and overburden benches of opencast coal mines. It also discusses methods to estimate charge factors and dimensional parameters for different rock types of iron ore mines, blasting methodology for rock strata and the blasting technologies for civil construction projects. The book: • Provides a comprehensive review of the prediction methodologies of blasting results. • Reviews insights into advanced blasting methodologies for rock excavation near sensitive structures. • Includes case studies of coal mining, iron ore mining, limestone mining and civil construction projects. • Explores the detailed theoretical background of rock blasting. • Discusses methodologies of secondary blasting. This book is aimed at graduate students and researchers in mining, geotechnical and rock engineering. ii iii Principles and Practices of Rock Blasting Vivek Kumar Himanshu, Narayan Kumar Bhagat, Ashish Kumar Vishwakarma and Arvind Kumar Mishra iv Designed cover image: Vivek Kumar Himanshu First edition published 2025 by CRC Press 2385 NW Executive Center Drive, Suite 320, Boca Raton FL 33431 and by CRC Press 4 Park Square, Milton Park, Abingdon, Oxon, OX14 4RN CRC Press is an imprint of Taylor & Francis Group, LLC © 2025 Vivek Kumar Himanshu, Narayan Kumar Bhagat, Ashish Kumar Vishwakarma and Arvind Kumar Mishra Reasonable efforts have been made to publish reliable data and information, but the author and publisher cannot assume responsibility for the validity of all materials or the consequences of their use.
