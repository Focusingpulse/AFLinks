The first is arithmetic. The capacity ladder p₀, p₁, p₂, … records how many independent transformation channels a symmetric observer gains each time a distinguished site is allowed to hold one more token. That paper proved each rung is the first Betti number of a small quotient graph, proved a formula for the top rung, and observed that the whole ladder is a single function P_X(k) of the remaining occupancy k = K − r − 1. It asked for a recurrence or closed form and left it open. There is a closed form: one polynomial assembled from the cycle index of Stab(m) together with three integer statistics per group element. It never builds an arena, and at trivial site stabiliser it collapses to a quadratic times a binomial power. The second is not arithmetic.
