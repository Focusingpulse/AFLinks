Shortcomings of DC Power The limitations of our present power generation and transmission systems are becoming more and more evident as time goes on. This is ironic considering the fact that the original inventor of the polyphase electric system warned of its eventual shortcomings nearly one hundred years ago. During the early 1900's, Nikola Tesla made announcements concerning the limitations of his alternating current system, and the development of a novel means of electrical power transmission that could service the energy needs of the entire globe without wires. So fantastic were these new claims, and so far beyond the abilities of his engineering peers, that they were harshly ridiculed and rapidly dismissed by the scientific community. But, was there any validity to Tesla's claims? The historical development of electrical physics has proceeded along a very definite evolutionary course, and is quite easy to follow.
