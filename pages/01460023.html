James Roland Bishop

· archived # 1,460,023
( click to enlarge ) James Roland Bishop , castle builder, was born in Honolulu, Hawaii on Feb. 24, 1944, to Willard and Pauline (Perse) Bishop. They and his sister, Johanna Trimble, preceded him in passing. His surviving siblings are Susan (Charles) Steigerwalt, Will (Jim Kearney) Bishop and Otis Trimble. He was freed from the bonds of this earth on Nov. 21, 2024. The family was devastated by Roy’s death in 1988, and Phoebe’s passing in 2018. Through grief, Jim reshaped the castle to honor their unfinished lives and catch his unending tears. Jim is survived by his children, Daniel and Connie “Sage” Bishop, Valerie and Scot Moore, Donita Bishop and Nico Ruiz. His grandchildren are Ethan Bishop, Scarlet Bishop, John Trujillo, Schon Moore, Skyler Moore and Vincent Bishop. He will be missed by a host of in-laws, nieces, nephews, cousins and friends. Jim counted the thousands of guests who visited Bishop Castle among those friends. Giving even after death, he donated his body to Science Care. Memorial Service will be at 10 a.m. Friday, Dec. 6, at St. Mary Catholic Church, 217 E. Mesa Ave, Pueblo, CO 81006. Everyone who loved Jim and his work is welcome to attend. In the serene expanse of the Colorado mountains, a monumental vision began to take shape, defying convention and igniting dreams. Jim Bishop, only 15 years old, purchased a modest plot of land for $450, envisioning a simple stone cabin. But what emerged from his hands, over decades of relentless labor, was Bishop Castle
