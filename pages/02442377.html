The Thanax Effect

· archived # 2,442,377
06/17/14 - The Thanax Effect I had a most vivid dream today. In it, I was walking through a city I'd never been in before and someone on the street handed me a flyer saying lifes' mysteries explained, "The Thanax Effect.'" It gave directions which I followed until I came to a large, multi-storied old building with an iron gate. It had a big step so I sat there a moment and happened to lean against the gate. Someone from inside, wearing a robe I think, came to the gate, opened it and asked me to enter, they had been waiting for me. We walked through a large hall with many antiques and machines like I'd never seen before. I was led into another great hall and asked to have a seat at a large table. On one side was an elderly white haired man with a large beard and flowing white hair and several other men seated as if on a panel. I never saw any women. He said he was Dr. Thanax and my time had come to be shown something that might help explain several mysteries. Dr. Thanax had me look outwards into the empty hall and asked me what I saw. I said nothing...he waved his hand over a box in front of him and suddenly the room was filled with machines and devices that weren't there before. He explained it as the Thanax Effect, a means of making anything invisible and a method to wipe memory as little or as much as was necessary in the event someone accidentally saw what they should not. The doctor said what if I told you there were huge islands, buldings and small cities all over the planet, bustling with scientific research but completely invisible and unknown to "mortals". That these had been there, watching and sometimes guiding humanity for millenia. Occasionally people were brought into the secret temporarily but later their memories wiped and sometimes it wasn't complete. This led to many stories about strange things which could not be proven because they were not detectible to our senses, machines or recording media.
