Mrs. Samuel Untermyer was the hostess at a reception to Count Albert Apponyl at her residence, 675 Fifth avenue, Manhattan, yesterday afternoon. The women who received with Mrs. Untermyer were Mrs. William J. Gaynor, Mrs. Imre Josika-Herczeg, Mrs. Henry Villard, Gertrude Atherton. Mrs. George C. Rigga (Kate Doulas Wigin), Mrs. Myron T. Herick, Mrs. Robert Erskino Ely, Mrs. Ernest Thompson Seton and Mrs. Louis Putnam Myers. Among the guests were: Baron and Baroness Ladislaus von Hengelmuller, Right Hon, Mr. and Mrs. Henry Ritter von Loeventhal. Count Mark Pejacsevich, Hon. Steven von Hedry, Dr. And Mrs. Imre Josika- Herczeg, Baron Paul Forster, Arpad Kraomer. Mr. and Mrs. Adolph Ochs, Miss Ochs, Mr. and Mrs. Ernst Thalmann, Mr. and and Mrs. Forbes Robertson, Count and Countessa Steven Bethlen, Hon, Joseph von Pinkovich, Mr. Dijlor von Smialovsky, Mr. and Mrs. Walston Hill Brown, Mr. and Mrs. William Loeb, Jr.. Herrick, Louis Wiley, Judge and Mrs, Holt, Frank Lawrence, Davis S. Warfield, Judge and Mrs. Alton B. Parker, Nikola Tesla, Mr. and Mrs. Aloysius Clark, John Bigelow, Miss Bigelow, Poulteney Bigelow
