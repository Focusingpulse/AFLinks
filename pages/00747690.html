Key Words: Sobolev Hilbert spaces of one dimensional Hilbert spaces, reproducing kernel, norm inequality, product of reproducing kernels, Green function, isoperimetric inequality, division by zero calculus, multiplicative operator. 2010 Mathematics Subject Classification: Primary 46E22. 1 Introduction In this paper, we shall consider norm inequalities for one dimensional Sobolev Hilbert spaces by using the theory of reproducing kernels as fundamental inequalities and as an extension of [15]. It seems that any elementary proof is impossible, however, the result will, in particular, mean a fairly simple and fundamental inequality: For two functions φ and ψ of H2(D), analytic Hardy space, we obtain the generalized isoperimetric inequality 1 π Z Z D |φ(z)ψ(z)| 2 dxdy ≤ 1 2π Z ∂D |φ(z)| 2 |dz| 1 2π Z ∂D |ψ(z)| 2 |dz|, (1.1) and we can determine completely the case holding the equality here.
