Law of Specific Gravity

· archived # 1,460,377
Ramsay "The three notes of the dominant chord resolve by each note going to the next note upward - G soars to A, B to C, D to E . The three notes of the subdominant resolve by each note going to the next note downward - C sinks to B, A to G, F to E . The two upper notes of the dominant resolve into the tonic chord according to the Laws of Proximity and Specific Levity ; and the two lower notes of the subdominant resolve into the tonic chord according to the Laws of Proximity and Specific Gravity . And in this way Nature , in chord-resolution , has two strings to her bow ." [ Scientific Basis and Build of Music , page 96 ] See Also Specific Levity Law of Specific Levity
