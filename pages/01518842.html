March 15, 1989 Dr. R. Gajewski Director Advanced Energy Projects USDOE Washington DC Dear Richard: THE UNIVERSITY OF UTAH I am enclosing a copy of my statement made to you earlier today regarding my position in the Steven Jones I review process matter. I again want to emphasize that since we are obligated to submit a paper with Steven Jones next Friday (which we do with continued protest), we must spend every available moment toward the goals of the research. Further, I intend to withhold further comment on this matter until I have inspected the manuscript submitted by Steven Jones. At that time, I have agreed to further discuss the situation with you, in the event you or I want to proceed with the investigation. Best regards.
