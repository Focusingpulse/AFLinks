News, The Geo-Monitor, Electrostatics Newsletter, Fusion Facts, New Energy News, Infinite Energy, The Space Energy Journal, Planetary Assoc. for Clean Energy Newsletter, IPS Newsletter, Untapped Technology in Review, "Cold Fusion," Fulcrum, New Science News, and Borderlands.
