The Local-Ether Model – A viable alternative to Einstein’s Relativity Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Date 12 June 2021 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording The Local-Ether Model – A viable alternative to Einstein’s Relativity was a live session on 12 June 2021 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. A viable alternative to Einstein’s Relativity presented by James Marsen. An overview of a novel ether concept called the Local-Ether Model that was proposed by the late Prof Ching-Chuan Su in 2000. It is founded on the classical principles of absolute time and Euclidean Space. It is the medium for the classical propagation of electromagnetic waves. It is a real substance with a minute variable mass density proportional to the magnitude of the * gravitational potential field. Its distribution in space is analogous to the gravitational potential field. It provides alternative explanations of a comprehensive scope of fundamental phenomena including those that are commonly cited as evidence supporting Einstein’s Relativity. Summary James Marsen presented the local-ether model of the late Ching-Chuan Su , proposed in 2000, as an alternative to relativity. David de Hilster hosted. Marsen was explicit throughout that the work is Su's and that his own role is to set it out and promote it. Asked how he came to it, he gave the sequence. His father had a theory of his own — the tron theory, on which Marsen had spoken to the society in 2016, and which predates the Disney film. He came to Petr Beckmann 's work through an advertisement in a science magazine, bought the book, and learnt about Galilean Electrodynamics , the journal Beckmann founded. Su's model is, he said, almost identical to Beckmann's. He never met Su: he was composing an email to him when he learnt from a secretary's reply that Su had died in 2006 at fifty-five. He was doubtful whether Beckmann and John Chappell had known one another, thought Cynthia Kolb
