His was the reverse of the Edison approach, where the systematics were a large-scale multifactorial tinkering approach made possible by a large infrastructure with unhindered access to research funding. In Tesla's case, the systematics were singular - an individual life committed to science and interested in all facets of the basic functions of nature. Tesla was led by both a powerful and benevolent intuition.
