Saturday Morning Video Chats with Franklin Hu Summary An open chat hosted by Franklin Hu with Bill Lucas , James Keene , Ian Cohen, Alistair Riddoch , Bob Gray and Michael Teske. It opened on an email storm and settled into the best-focused argument the series has had about gravity: is it a property of the mass itself or something acting from outside? The email storm The previous week's brief mention of evolution had taken over the list, and Hu was plainly irritated by where it went — theological argument about Adam and Eve rather than science. He put the substantive points that had emerged: that what Darwin observed is variation within a species, beaks and colours and sizes, and that selection experiments run into a limit past which the organism cannot reproduce, so that a bird remains a bird; that the automobile analogy offered on the list fails because car design is intelligent selection, not a random process; and that chimpanzees have twenty-four chromosome pairs to our twenty-three, which someone had taken to mean they are the more evolved. Ian's reply was measured on both counts. On the theology, he declined to take part: one either believes or one does not, and arguing about the Abrahamic god is no more physics than arguing about what Thor or Odin did. On the biology he made two corrections — chromosome count does not track development, and the orthodox view is not that humans descend from chimpanzees but that both descend from a common primate ancestor. Riddoch added that many small organisms have far more chromosomes than we do.
