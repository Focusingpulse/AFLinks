In March 1962 AVCO-Everett was selected by NAA to design and install an ablative material heatshield on the Apollo spacecraft outer surface. Parallel to the Apollo development of ablative materials the AVCO-Everett Research Laboratory studied active plasma shields for heat protection by hydromagnetic braking in re￾entry and radiation screening [1]-[4], [7]. In 1970 Ali Bülent Cambel published his article “MHD for Spacecraft” and discussed the idea of hydromagnetic braking in reentry [6].
