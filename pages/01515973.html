He is Professor Emeritus of Nuclear Physics at Osaka University, where he has been director of the Research Center for Nuclear Physics (RCNP) and organized and chaired international symposia. He founded the RCNP ring cyclotron facilities. Hidetsugu Ikegami is guest professor at Uppsala University.
