HARMONIC 33 One summer evening in 1952, a young New Zealand pilot and six companions stood outside the local aero club at Mangere airfield, Auckland, and for twenty minutes watched a phenomenon that, they agreed, could only be an Unidentified Flying Object. From then on the pilot—Bruce Cathie, who is a Foker Friendship captain with the National Airways Corporation of New Zealand—began a serious study of UFOs. And he has, since that day in 1952, made five more UFO sightings. After a close analysis of these and other confirmed UFO sightings in New Zealand, he discovered that they occurred in a regular pattern that conformed to a systematic grid pattern. Further research proved that the New Zealand grid was a small part of a worldwide grid and that UFO sightings are inter-related with other natural but hitherto unexplained phenomenon. From these discoveries Captain Cathie has evolved a theory that is startling in its audacity and originality: “Even while you read this, interplanetary space-ships are rebuilding a world grid system from which, it appears, they can draw motive power, and they are possibly also using the grid for navigational purposes . . . .” With characteristic modesty, Captain Cathie refrains from claiming that he has found all the answers. He says that it will take a team of scientists, aided by computers, to do that. But he has provided a key that may unlock the secret of the UFOs. Since this book was first published in 1968 further researchers have convinced Captain Cathie that at least a portion of the grid’s secrets are already known to—and being exploited by—the scientists of certain countries. In a second book, Harmonic 695, he and his co-author, Peter Temm, proved their case. The sceptics may smile, and talk of “hallucinations”, but UFOs are being taken very seriously indeed by the Governments of more than one great world power.
