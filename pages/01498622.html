Martin was an outstanding scientist with a great vision that allowed him to initiate a number of fields of activity. Martin was essentially “an ideas man.” Indeed, often his ideas were ahead of the ability of equipment to carry out the experiments, and it was only a few years later that the ideas came to fruition and it became possible to obtain high-quality experimental data. As can be seen by the authorship of the following chapters, this ability to be ahead of “the state-of-the-art,” combined with inspirational leadership, made him a reliable stepping stone to successful careers for many of his coworkers.
