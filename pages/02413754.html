[p. 149] V An electrically charged body is still a charged body until the last vestige of motion leaves it. Your car battery may be discharged to the last mile, but as long as it can produce motion it is positively charged. You might properly say that it is approaching a negative , or voided condition, but one cannot properly say that a discharging body is negatively "charged" under any condition. To charge means to be added to. It is not logical or proper to use a term such as negative charge , which literally means added to by subtracting. Electricity does not expand, nor is there a kind of electricity which does expand. It merely weakens by becoming lower in potential and speed .
