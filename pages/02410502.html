Preface to first edition The First Part of Workshop Technology has made so many friends that I have every confidence in offering this, its second volume. Here will be found all the aspects of machine-tool work that the reader should require for his basic knowledge of workshop engineering, together with chapters dealing with the elements of accuracy, measurement and gauging. The two parts together provide a complete introduction to the technique of the workshop itself and contain elements of all the knowledge a student should require whilst serving an apprenticeship, or learning period in the shops. Although primarily written in detail for the inhabitants of the workshop, the general terms of the text include nothing but important fundamentals in the knowledge of any mechanical engineer, so that its reading should be of help and interest to any student of engineering. The text covers most of the work necessary for the City and Guilds Intermediate and Final Examinations in Machine Shop Engineering, and the Ordinary National Certificate in Workshop Technology.
