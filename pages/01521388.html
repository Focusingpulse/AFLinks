This conclusion emerges from a new method for analyzing quantum states, called "Maxwellian decomposition". This decomposition shows the motion of the electron in hydrogen 2P, 3D, and 4F, etc., excited states, to consist only of radial motions along essentially degenerate ellipse orbits, exhibiting essentially no angular momentum. Hence, what customarily has been called "orbital angular momentum" should rather be called "orbital directionality".
