If you plot the flows, that's essentially what Joe's beer keg charger does. An aether/water collider. The geometry of the charger, and pattern of water circulation, serves to prevent its ordinary dissipation. Once charged, the water itself appears to retain this engineered balance of aetheric charge, allowing some to be drawn off, while more is admitted freely from the energetic vacuum.
