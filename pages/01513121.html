This is astounding! In my opinion, nuclear energy is the cleanest form of energy production available at this point in time. The output capabilities are astonishing. Nuclear plants are the lowest-cost producer of baseload electricity. The average production cost of 2.14 cents per kilowatt-hour includes the costs of operating and maintaining the plant, purchasing fuel and paying for the management of used fuel. I'm wondering what everyone else's thoughts are concerning the viability of nuclear energy as an endless source of clean power. Admittedly, the production of more nuclear power plants will necessarily be taxing in a post-global financial crisis atmosphere. However, as compared with fossil fuel based forms of energy production, nuclear energy is superior because it is cleaner and safer. The recent events in Japan are a testament to this point.
