The equations for the fly-by of satellites near the Earth, or near planets in general are deduced. Overview Expected Gyrotational Orbit Accelerations of an Elliptical Fly-By is an eight-page paper by Thierry De Mees , dated October 2011 and posted to the General Science Journal . It is the third in a sequence applying what De Mees calls the Maxwell Analogy for Gravitation to solar-system dynamics, following two earlier papers on the spin-vector behaviour of main-belt asteroids. The framework is gravitomagnetism, which De Mees calls gyrotation . A rotating mass generates, in addition to its ordinary gravitational field g , a second field Ω analogous to a magnetic field, and a moving body experiences a Lorentz-like force F = m ( g + v × Ω ). For a spinning sphere the gyrotation field falls as the inverse cube of distance and depends on the angle to the rotation axis, vanishing in the equatorial plane. De Mees cites Heaviside 's 1893 paper as the origin of the analogy and Jefimenko's later work on it. The paper's purpose is computational rather than conceptual.
