Uh, well, is there anybody else than me in that position ? Been wondering for a while... Anyway, if you exist, be sure to grab the latest issue of "Fusion" magazine. Fusion is a bi-monthly French magazine dealing occasionnally with alternative science. I don't read it regularly, but I jumped when I saw the issue today at a railway station newsstand. It's dated may-june 98 so act quickly ! It has a 23 pages cover article on Alexander Gurwitch (sometimes spelled Gurvitsch), written by one of his last remaining living disciples, Michael Lipkind, an old Russian biologist now emigrated to Israel. Gurwitch did wonderful research on "biological fields" and "mitogenetic rays" in the 30's. He's often branded by skeptics as a classical example of "pathological science", but in truth that man was a real genius ! I've been aware of his work thanks to Christopher Bird's books, and more recently Fritz-Albert Popp, the German biophysicist who re-discovered Gurwtich's findings with his startling studies of "bio-photons". Details about Gurwitch are very hard to come by nowadays, so this article is a gem ! --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Jean-Pierre Lentin --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Previous message: Matthew Redmond: "RE: Dr Reich Orgone Energy" Next message: Jean-Pierre Lentin: "Lyndon LaRouche"
