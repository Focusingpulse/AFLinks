Grist takes the May 2019 redefinition of the kilogram from the metal artefact in Paris to a value fixed by fundamental constants and reads it against growing Earth theory, which holds that the planet gains roughly two million tons a second and that surface gravity is therefore rising by about three billionths of g per year.
