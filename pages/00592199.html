Proof That e Is Rational Assume e = r n , where r and n are very large positive integers. r n = e Multiply both sides by n! to obtain r(n−1)! = n! e The left side is an integer, while the right side could be an integer if and only if n is a very large positive integer n! e = n!∑ k=0 ∞ 1 k ! = ∑ k=0 n n! k ! + ∑ k=n+1 ∞ n! k !
