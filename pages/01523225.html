Jeffery D. Kooistra Born 1959 (age 66–67) Residence Grand Rapids area, Michigan, United States Nationality American Known for Experimental work on the Marinov Motor; the "Alternate View" column in Analog Scientific career Fields Freelance physics ; experimental electrodynamics; science fiction Institutions New Energy Research Laboratory, New Hampshire; Infinite Energy magazine Jeffery Dean Kooistra (born 1959) is an American freelance physicist and science fiction writer. He is one of the two contributors to "The Alternate View" , the science column of Analog Science Fiction and Fact , which he has written since 1998 , inheriting the chair from G. Harry Stine; his short fiction appears in the same magazine, and his first novel Dykstra's War was published by Baen in 2000. Within the research community documented here he is known for something more specific: he was a research scientist at the New Energy Research Laboratory in New Hampshire, the laboratory associated with Eugene Mallove 's Infinite Energy magazine, where he built and measured the devices that others were arguing about. He was later an associate editor of the magazine, and wrote its column "Beyond the Cutting Edge". The Marinov Motor Kooistra's one paper on this wiki, given at the 7th Natural Philosophy Alliance Conference in 2000, is " The Marinov Motor and Warlock's Wheel: Conventional Physics with a Twist ", and its conclusions are unusually double-edged . The Marinov Motor — a device of Stefan Marinov 's, analysed by him with Paul Wesley — was widely taken in these circles as evidence for something beyond conventional electrodynamics. Kooistra experimented with suspended magnets and freely rotating rings, and reports that the surprising results have a conventional explanation , at least to first order, and that Marinov critically misunderstood his own device .
