Equivalence of Energy and Time Friedhelm M. Jöge1 Abstract A formula is developed that shows the equivalence of energy and time. Keywords: Energy, time, PLANCK time, PLANCK quantum of action Derivation of a formula that describes the equivalence of energy and time In the International Journal of Physics and Astronomy, June 2019, Vol.7, No.1, pp 1-7 [1] a formula for calculation dark energy was developed under the title „Calculation of Dark Energy and Dark Matter“. It is: Ed = h tu / tp 2 This formula is now expanded below to E = (h/tp2) ∙ t Starting from E = h/t is obtained by substituting tp for t Ep = h/tp for the energy in the PLANCK time. For the energy per one second we get: E1 = h/tp 2 and for energy in time t E = (h/tp 2 ) ∙ t This is the general formula for the equivalence of energy and time.
