After all, the cause remains a mystery. All we can say for certain is that on the morning of June 30, 1908, an explosion ripped a hole in the sky above Siberia with a force 2,000 times greater than the atomic bomb that destroyed Hiroshima. If it was some kind of experiment gone awry, could we pin it on Tesla? It sounded like an entertaining idea. Science writer Oliver Nichelson had published an article that offered circumstantial evidence linking Tesla to Tunguska. We had some vague notion of Tesla as a colorful if obscure inventor, a minor mad-scientist type celebrated for his coil. In short, all the ingredients for a fun, speculative segment.
