The completion of the preparation of The Origin and Development of Babylonian Writing and the fulfilment of an obligation to prepare a Commentary on Job made it impossible until now to make the copies of the remainder of the Haverford Library Collection. Thus the appearance of this concluding portion of the work has been unavoidably delayed. As the forms of the signs in these tablets have been included in Part I of the work on Babylonian Writing, it has seemed unnecessary to include a sign-list in the has also seemed best to refrain from issuing a vocabulary until the numerous business documents from the dynasty of Ur and earlier present volume, periods, as originally planned. 1 It which are now known and are in process of publication, can be studied, and a general vocabulary of Sumerian business documents can be prepared. Instead of these, a list of the personal names and the names of places, which occur in the three parts of this work, is included in the present volume. In preparing this list the Semitic arrangement of the alphabet has been followed.
