Six corrections are proposed. Lighter bodies fall marginally faster than heavier ones, contrary to both Aristotle and Galileo. Faster objects become lighter , not heavier, so Einstein's mass factor is inverted. Newton's first law should read "uniform acceleration in a straight line" rather than uniform motion, and his law of gravitation should use the square of the sum of the masses.
