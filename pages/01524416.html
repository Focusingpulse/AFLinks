Webster Kehr (born October 7, 1946) is an American writer, mathematician and accountant known for his self-published critiques of mainstream physics, particularly of Einstein's Special Theory of Relativity and the photon theory of light, which he rejects in favour of a classical luminiferous ether . He is also widely known as the founder of the alternative-medicine website Cancer Tutor and of the Independent Cancer Research Foundation. His scientific views fall well outside the mainstream scientific consensus and have not been published in peer-reviewed journals. Biography Education Degree in mathematics, Brigham Young University Degree in accounting, Brigham Young University Career Kehr was born on October 7, 1946, in Jefferson City, Missouri. He served in the United States Marine Corps and is a veteran of the Vietnam War . Following his military service he spent two years as a missionary for the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints . He subsequently attended Brigham Young University , from which he graduated twice, earning degrees first in mathematics and later in accounting. Kehr worked for about eight years at a telecommunications company in Overland Park, Kansas, where, because of his mathematics background, he became involved in a fiber-optics research project that he credits with prompting his interest in the physics of light. He later worked as a contractor for Northrop Grumman supporting the U.S. Army at Fort Leavenworth , Kansas. He married Marit Olaug Liset, of Norway, with whom he has seven children. Beginning around 2002, Kehr turned his attention to alternative cancer treatments, launching the website cancertutor.com and later founding the Independent Cancer Research Foundation in 2006.
