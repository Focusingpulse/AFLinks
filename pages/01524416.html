Webster Kehr (born October 7, 1946) is an American writer, mathematician and accountant known for his self-published critiques of mainstream physics, particularly of Einstein's Special Theory of Relativity and the photon theory of light, which he rejects in favour of a classical luminiferous ether . He is also widely known as the founder of the alternative-medicine website Cancer Tutor and of the Independent Cancer Research Foundation.
