Decoding Eric Dollard Versor Algebra Watch this video on YouTube Talk Type Video Speaker Lori Anne Gardi Date 31 August 2022 Platform YouTube Recording Recording Decoding Eric Dollard Versor Algebra is a video by Lori Anne Gardi , published on 31 August 2022 on her FractalWoman YouTube channel. Summary A third pass at Eric Dollard's Symbolic Representation of the Generalized Electric Wave in Time , this one addressed directly to Dollard himself, in which Gardi maps his versor algebra onto her 2×2 matrix notation and then does something neither notation does on its own: shows what the successive multiplications by i actually look like as waves. A video with an intended recipient She says plainly why she is making it. Dollard sometimes watches her videos, particularly when his name is in the title, and she has questions she would like him to answer. What she wants to know is whether the 2×2 matrix form of complex numbers is of any use to him. She characterises the difference in temperament between them exactly: he is keen on symbolic representation, she is keen on a more transparent one. The hope is that he will comment on the formalism. Dollard's wave factor His symbolic language begins at section 4.2, on time variance and time invariance. To distinguish the time-invariant power factor a from the time-variant induction factor b of a complete alternating wave, Dollard proposes marking the time-dependent component with a distinguishing index — the addition of what he calls an otherwise meaningless symbol, a Greek character Gardi is not sure how to pronounce. She notes a probable typo in his text, where "time invariant" appears where "time dependent" is meant, and raises a genuine question. In his list of symbols the character is defined as the function-of-time wave factor, but he writes a superscript 2 on it, and she cannot tell from the paper whether it is a square or an index of some sort; nothing in the text she could find explains it.
