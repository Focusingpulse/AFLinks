Scientific men have countered Professor Tesla’s announcement by stating that such a revolutionary discovery, of tremendous importance to humanity, calls for presentation before one of the recognized courts of judgment. The experts said that four organizations would be glad to hear an elucidation of the Tesla system, presented in the customary form of a paper to be read at the next meeting. These organizations are the American Institute of Electrical Engineers, Institute of Radio Engineers, American Physical Society, or the National Academy of Sciences.
