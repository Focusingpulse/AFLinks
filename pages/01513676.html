The food tower is a simple yet ingenious way to take advantage of vertical growth space. With this technology you can grow 14 to 24 plants within 2 to 4 square feet of horizontal space. It is basically a 6 foot tall PVC pipe with 2 inch holes drilled in the sides and filled with soil. The plants grow out from the holes. A drip water irrigation system eliminates water evaporation and maximizes water conservation. Most importantly, the plants grow extremely well. These food towers fit perfectly in the raised-bed gardens or on your patio. These food towers can even be put in large planting pots and be placed in-doors near a large window or under grow-lights, for dense urban settings. The use of soil and water is minimal and the food production per square foot of growth space is maximized. These make gardening easy for even the least experienced gardener.
