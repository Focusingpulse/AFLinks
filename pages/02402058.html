GTM2026-main035 Hypergraph-Based Market Formation under Cournot Competition with Economies of Scope Wei Yang1 , Elena Parilina1 1 Saint Petersburg State University, Russia st112116@student.spbu.ru This paper builds a hypergraph model to study how economies of scope affect two-market competi- tion under Cournot competition. Firms compete in overlapping markets and benefit from cost savings when operating across multiple markets. We find for equilibrium quantities and profits, and define when a market structure is stable. We get following main results: (i) Economies of scope give multi-market firms a large and growing profit advantage over single-market firms; (ii) More overlapping firms increase total surplus, and consumers benefit from higher output and lower prices; (iii) However, producer sur- plus shifts sharply from single-market to multi-market firms. We further characterize the class of stable hypergraphs, showing that a two-market structure is Cournot stable if and only if economies of scope exists but bounded below the effective marginal cost threshold. This stability result provides a the- oretical foundation for predicting which overlapping market configurations can persist as equilibrium outcomes. Furthermore, we provide numerical simulations to verify the stability of these equilibrium market structures. This research has been supported by: 1. "Russian Science Foundation", grant N25-21-00817
