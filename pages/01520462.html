Author(s) Euan J Squires Keywords orthodox quantum theory, history, reactor, neutron, true Published 1994 Pages 465-473 Abstract The Problem of History In a recent conference at Oviedo in Spain, Roland Omnes introduced us to an imaginary experimentalist who is describing an experiment he has performed (fig. 1): "A neutron, produced in a reactor, passed through the slit S, and proceeded to an interaction region..." What, asked Omnes, does the statement in italics mean? Rather, perhaps, we should ask what meaning can we give to it in order that it will be a correct statement? This is a question of history, which is very much a fashion in quantum theory these days, and which is appropriate in the wonderful surroundings
