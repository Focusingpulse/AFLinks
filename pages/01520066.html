Summary The announced talk did not happen. Harry Ricker was to have given a third session on Faraday's original experiments in electromagnetic induction, following his two earlier talks; the first half hour went on trying to get his screen share and then his audio to work, and neither ever did. Ian J Cowan hosted and Franklin co-hosted; a telephone call was tried and abandoned. The attempt failed again a month later. What survives is the unplanned session that followed — a short clarification from Bob Gray and an open discussion. Cowan opened with the annual fundraiser, then closing at the end of the month, and noted that David de Hilster was away because his father was unwell. Gray's clarification Gray came up to correct an impression left by his own talk the previous week on Weber electrodynamics. He had said Weber's force equation predicts charge separation, and so an EMF, in every case but the wholly stationary one, with the residue too small to measure. What he had skipped was that this depends on the model. Assis and Thober's treatment puts the conducting plate inside two concentric charged spheres, the negative one rotating slightly faster than the positive to simulate a current, giving a uniform field within.
