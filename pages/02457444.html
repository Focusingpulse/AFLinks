U.S. TO HEIGHTEN BASE SECRECY BY SEIZING MOUNTAIN NO PEEKING FROM PEAK: AIR FORCE WANTS TO SEIZE MOUNTAIN TO PROTECT SECRET BASE 10/17/93 THE SALT LAKE TRIBUNE RACHEL, Nev. -- Hikers who make it to the top of 6,089-foot White Sides Mountain get a clear view of dry Groom Lake. Trekkers can spot long runways, barnlike hangars and crops of communications equipment in the distance. Some people even pull out binoculars and telescopes trying to get a peek at the latest U.S. Air Force jets that routinely take off and land at the facility. The Pentagon wants it stopped. It plans to seize 4,000 acres around White Sides Mountain and end public observation of an air base so secret officials refuse to say it exists.
