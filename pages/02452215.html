Resonant energy buildup meaning a capacitive like charging, then a quick dump...all the nickels dumped at one time, but they do have to be refilled. Energy sourcing as being a constant stream of nickels at either a certain velocity/potential/voltage (assuming one a time) or many nickels thrown out at the same time and repeated over and over for a certain 'current'...<g>....
