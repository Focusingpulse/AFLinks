These properties range from mechanical strength to high wear resistance, light weight, low cost, and so on, making them a suitable alternative to other materials such as glass and metals. Several petroleum-based synthetic polymers such as polyethylene and polypropylene dominate the plastics market because of their low cost, light weight, high strength, easy processability, and good water barrier properties. However, these synthetic polymer-based materials, especially plastics used for a short time, generate a huge amount of waste, are not biodegradable for quite a long time, and may accumulate in the environment and cause environmental pollution after being used.
