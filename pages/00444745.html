Equipossibility, over-inflated basket balls, photons and polarizers Georgina Woodward Oct 2022 Classical probability is the likelihood of an outcome where there is equipossibility. Such as the outcome of a die roll or choosing one card from a pack. We can assume, near enough, equiposibility in some circumstances, where not strictly true, by ignoring small variables. Such as a light breeze over the basketball court, or stickiness of the dice rollers hand. Lets think about when there isn’t equipossibility. The chance of catching a fish of a particular species from a pond in which there are an equal number and size of different fishes and same rod, bait and angler is used. Sounds fair only different species have different bait preference. How about basket ball hoops, only the balls will have different inflation.
