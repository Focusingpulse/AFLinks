Speakers included Marc Seifer, J. & K. Corum, Oliver Nichelson, Moray B. King, Tom Bearden, and Andrija Puharich. A number of excellent papers on various aspects of Dr. Tesla's work in electrical engineering were presented. The book includes a bonus paper written by Tesla himself in 1937 on the particle beam device. Contents William H. Terbo , "Opening Address" 9 H. W.
