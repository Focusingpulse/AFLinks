N. 4th year student Department of Physics and Astronomy LATEST FUNDAMENTAL RESEARCH IN THE FIELD OF NUCLEAR PHYSICS, ACHIEVEMENTS AND FUTURE PLANS Abstract. The article reviews the most important fundamental research, achievements and future prospects of nuclear physics for 2024–2026. The main areas are: thorium-229 nuclear clock technology, new measurements of quantum tunneling and resonances, quark-gluon plasma (QGP) research, antiproton spin control, records achieved in fusion, as well as the latest results of accelerators such as RHIC and LHC. The article is based on the results of international laboratories (CERN, RHIC, NIF, EAST, PhET, etc.). Future plans for AI integration, the creation of new generation accelerators and fusion power plants are discussed. Key words: nuclear physics, thorium-229 nuclear clock, quark-gluon plasma, quantum tunneling, antiproton spin control, fusion, RHIC, LHC, QGP, “Digital Uzbekistan – 2030”. Qosimjonov R. V.
