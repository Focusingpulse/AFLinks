Within the body, qi is said to circulate through channels known as meridians, which interconnect the viscera and limbs and the deeper and superficial layers of the body in a branching network of increasingly fine mesh. The meridians and their acupuncture points have no known relationship with anatomical systems in western medicine, despite many attempts to search for correlations. I show how traditional Chinese medical concepts, from the most general to the more specific, can be translated into contemporary Western science in a new physics of organisms. The vital energy, qi , of traditional Chinese medicine corresponds to coherent energy in living organisms, which is stored everywhere over the entire range of space-times.
