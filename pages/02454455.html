Now, 86 degrees (before) TDC (top dead center) is determined by the angular position of the crankshaft -- which determines the position of the piston -- at the point the spark plug fires. This 86 degrees is exactly 4 degrees past the half-way point to TDC (TDC being the point of maximum compression). (Ordinarily, the plug would fire only a few degrees before TDC.
