December 6, 2019 Abstract Various anomalies supporting TGD vision are accumulating also in condensed matter physics. In this article I discuss 3 anomalies. Second anomaly violates Anderson theorem for BCS superconductors provides support for TGD based view about high Tc super-conductivity. Third anomaly breaks so called Wiedemann-Franz law stating that good conductors are also good heat conductors with electric and heat conductivity proportional to each other.
