Brown, electrogravitics, electrokinetics, levitation Published 1991 Journal Electric Spacecraft Journal Number 1 Pages 6-12 Abstract Thomas Townsend Brown was a physicist, inventor, and researcher whose work is largely unknown and hard to find because it does not fit easily into mainstream science. Brown invented the "gravitor," a device that demonstrated what was called the Biefeld-Brown effect. This effect is defined as "the observed tendency of a highly-charged electrical condenser to exhibit motion toward its positive pole." T. T. Brown was intensely interested in demonstrating electrogravitational phenomena. His lab notes testify to the constant and fundamental nature of his quest.
