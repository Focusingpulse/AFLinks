An attractive run of 200 miles through the most charming sections of Long Island through Babylon along the Great South Bay and the ocean, to Good Ground; and returning along Great Peconic Bay and Long Island Sound, through Port Jefferson, Oyster Bay and Roslyn; a trip that passes magnificent estates, parks, country clubs, woods, fields and seashore; over hard smooth roads; and which will prove generally invigorating and refreshing, has been prepared by the Bureau of Tours of the Automobile Club of America. The route picked out down and back covers roads that give the best running conditions at the present time.
