Introduction Since 1989, there has been accumulated evidence of anomalous excess heat effects in correlation of 24MeV/4 Hegeneration (Miles [1], McKubre [2], and others) without correspondingly intense emission of particles (n, p, t, 3 He, γ) from experiments with Pd–D systems. Recently, another anomalous and long lasting excess heat effect at higher temperatures in Ni–H systems without easily detected nuclear products (the so-called ash) have been reported by Piantelli [3], Rossi [4], Celani [5], Kitamura–Takahashi [6] and some other groups. The level of observed anomalous excess heat looks too large to be explained by known chemical reactions. If the anomalous heat effect is of nuclearreaction origin, why ‘lethal’ radiation (i.e., neutrons and gamma-rays) is not associated with the reaction is the big challenge to nuclear theory. The author has made serious efforts to establish theories to give consistent answers to the big question why so radiation-less heat results may happen in Pd–D and Ni–H systems.
