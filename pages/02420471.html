VOLUME 1 NUMBER 6 SALT LAKE CITY, UTAH DECEMBER, 1989 ASME HOSTS SPECIAL COLD FUSION UPDATE SESSION PRESENTATIONS FROM ORNL, BUSH, AND HAGELSTEIN CONTENTS DECEMBER ISSUE the ASME Winter Annual Meeting held in San Francisco, CA December 12, 1989. This session on cold fusion is probably the most important public presentation of papers that has been given. The session was organized and chaired by two scientists from Oak Ridge National Laboratories, A. R. Sadlowe and Gordon E. Michaels. A. 'TWAS THE NIGHT BEFORE CRISIS Vern Rogers set the stage for the session by reviewing the experimental work that has been reported in replicating the F-P Effect. Extensive lists were presented of those laboratories that have successfully replicated one or more of the results of the Fleischmann-Pons cold-fusion experiment. More laboratories are being added to the success list every week from many parts of the world. The article lists 18 laboratories that measured excess heat; 14 laboratories that found neutrons above background; and 11 laboratories that measured tritium. E.
