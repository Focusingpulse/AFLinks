Title The Neutrino Spectrum Read in full Read in full Author(s) Ricardo L Carezani Keywords Neutrino, neutrino mass, Autodynamics , Muon decay, four-momentum, Speed of Light Published Society for the Advancement of Autodynamics Website https://www.autodynamics.org/neutrino-spectrum/ "The Neutrino Spectrum" is a white paper by Ricardo L Carezani arguing that what is called the neutrino spectrum is nothing more than the difference between two calculations. Its conclusion is stated as a pair of equations: SR values − EXPERIMENTAL values = Neutrino spectrum AD values − EXPERIMENTAL values = 0 Overview The paper is built around two quotations from correspondents at American physics departments, which Carezani takes apart in turn. The first concerns neutrino mass and light speed; the second is a textbook account of three-body decay and four-momentum conservation. The argument The mass and speed contradiction Carezani quotes: "photons travel at the speed of light because their mass is identically zero. That is to say, if the 'particle' doesn't have zero mass, it is impossible for it to travel at light speed." He sets this against the range of neutrino masses then in circulation — "0, 1 0 − 7 , 1 0 − 4 , 20, 30, 1000, 2000, 17000 eV" — of which 20 eV was the most commonly quoted, alongside the routine assertion that neutrinos travel at c . "The contradiction is evident throughout SR." He then closes the other exit. If a physicist retreats to zero rest mass to preserve light speed, "Now they confront another evident contradiction: A Neutrino with zero rest mass cannot 'oscillate', because there is nothing to change." He also objects to the logic of setting an upper limit: if one is looking for an upper limit on a mass, the value must be greater than zero, so "we can tell the neutrinos have zero rest mass" and "there is no reason they should [have zero mass]" cannot both be maintained.
