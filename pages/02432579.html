A Gratitude Thread!

· archived # 2,432,579
I just felt like I needed to start this thread tonight. I have been feeling very down lately, and well just had the blahs. Today one of my Gen 2 modules asked me a question... Do I have a Passion for Life? and at that moment I had to stop and think, cause I have not felt a passion for anything lately! That started the search for my passion! What was missing? Hmmm.... WHOAAAAAA! It hit me like a Semi Truck going down a mountainside with NO BRAKES! Gratitude was missing! I had lost focuss somewhere along the way on what was important and started focussing on what I do not have, where I am not at, and what I am not doing... Suddenly, I could not pick up my paint brush cause it weighed too much! I was tired all the time (okay some of that had to do with buying the wrong brand of spirulina, but even then I still was lacking something). I had lost my passion for life! I could no longer see how anything was ever going to come my way... With that focus nothing was coming my way! Well tonight I am singing a new song and it goes like this... "Thank you Thank you thank you thank you thank you thank you... Thank me thank me thank me thank me thank me thank me... Thank you thank you thank you thank you thank you... Well you get the point! I have to tell you that I am already starting to feel something again, finally!!!! Thank you/me ahhhh for that simple enlightenment today! Suddenly I don't feel so alone... I can feel my love again! I can feel again! So this is just a reminder to all of you... I know the holidays can be a bit of a drag at times as they add so many pressure to our lives, so I thought I would challenge each of you to list 8 things you have to be grateful for! Who I am My children (Izzi and Nat) My home (Hey I remember a time when I did not have one not all that long ago!) Huge blessing! My wonderful little tiny car that is not phased by the snow The rain that melted all the snow today! The beautiful snow that perfected our holidays and made driving so much fun! A
