Beginning in the mid 60?s, theoretical and experimental investigations were conducted. These investigations showed that numerous phenomena previously difficult or impossible to explain had a rigorous theoretical interpretation within the framework of torsion field theory. This paper gives a comprehensive review of the literature pertaining to torsion fields and their relatinship to problems in theoretical physics and other potentially related areas.
