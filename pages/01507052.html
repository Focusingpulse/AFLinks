Applied Kinesiology

· archived # 1,507,052
Hey all, This is targetted more towards the founders and makers of PATHS -- have you guys looked into applied kinesiology, also known as muscle testing? It's a technique to quickly access the Matrix and get a [Y/N] answer to any question, as long as the right question is formulated / held in mind. It is primarily a two-person technique (seems to be most accurate that way), though there are also self-testing techniques available. Dr. David Hawkins has researched heavily into this, mostly as an aid to confirm spiritual truths, but it can be used for many different purposes, including business, research, science, personal issues, guidance, etc. It is similar to dowsing and very easy/quick to perform, though it takes awhile to get used to it and get repeatably accurate results. I was reading the TRV articles Kevin posted, and I was reminded that TRV and AK testing is very similar -- both have their pros and cons, and it would be very helpful to use both in tandem. I was thinking it could also be used in PATHS research and business. It seems to be able to answer questions whether a particular action is for the highest good for someone / the company / all, what direction the future is most likely to take (based on present conditions and allowing for free will), whether a scientific theory is true or not and which parts of it, etc. Anyway, happy to share what I know about it. I personally don't do it, but I am planning to learn and practice it soon. Highly recommend the book "Power vs. Force" that talks about it in detail, especially in its applications to society. Namaste
