In modern mathematics, elementary functions of a single variable form the foundation of our analytical tools. When graphed on a two dimensional coordinate plane, each function produces a characteristic curve. Extending these functions into three dimensions does not fundamentally change their nature; the resulting surfaces are simply extrusions of the original curves.
