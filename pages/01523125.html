Hypersphere World-Universe Model – Natural Extension of Classical Physics Watch this session on YouTube Talk Type Live session Speaker Vladimir S Netchitailo Date 16 November 2024 Platform YouTube live stream Recording Recording Hypersphere World-Universe Model – Natural Extension of Classical Physics was a live session presented by Vladimir S Netchitailo on 16 November 2024 in the John Chappell Natural Philosophy Society live session series. Hypersphere World—Universe Model (WUM) is radically different from the prevailing Big Bang Model. The main advantage of WUM is its elimination of the "Initial Singularity" and "Inflation," providing explanations for many unresolved problems in Cosmology. This Digest offers an overview of WUM covering the period from 2013 to 2024 and explores various themes of the World. Summary Vladimir S Netchitailo returned to give an overview of the World-Universe Model, drawing together thirty-eight papers published in the Journal of High Energy Physics, Gravitation and Cosmology between 2013 and 2024. Harry Ricker hosted and set the terms in advance: the standard model requires a miracle at t = 0, needs inflation and hypothetical dark matter, cannot synthesise the elements beyond hydrogen and helium without ad-hoc additions, has an angular-momentum problem in forming galaxies and stars, needs dark energy to match observation, and rests the quasars and active nuclei on supermassive black holes that are not observationally supported. The invitation to the audience was to watch how the model addresses each.
