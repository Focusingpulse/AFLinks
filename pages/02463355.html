It develops the Aether Physics Model (APM) 2 LEDGER CLOSURE CHECK AND PROPAGATION ANCHOR 1 Abstract Crookes’ radiometer is an unusually clean cross-domain experiment: an incident light field produces a steady mechanical torque via a thin rarefied gas and a temperature-gradient boundary layer. Mainstream treatments typically describe the mechanism in mixed unit systems (radiometry, thermodynamics, rarefied-gas kinetics, and rigid-body dynamics), which obscures the dimensional structure. This paper reframes the radiometer as a “ledger-closure” test for Quantum Measurement Units (QMU) within the Aether Physics Model (APM). We present a QMU-first model and a lab-ready reporting template that separates (i) delivery of light transport (ligt), (ii) surface conversion into a thermal state, and (iii) rarefied-gas rectification into torque. The resulting invariants and nondimensional groups provide a compact metrology pathway from measured spin-up curves to a closed QMU ledger without introducing SI quantities in the main derivation.
