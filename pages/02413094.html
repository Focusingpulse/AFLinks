" On the pendulum oscillations of a suspended RF resonant circuit " by Stavros G. Dimitriou << Abstract : The period of the pendulum oscillations of a suspended electromagnetic resonant circuit formed by quarter-wavelength transmission line sections is found to be affected by electrical parameters of the oscillator driving it. Of particular influence appears to the magntitude of current at resonance, which depends on the effective quality factor (Q) of the RF tank circuit and the input driving power. >> Stavros has said in his paper that " The maximum equivalent reduction of g locally is calculated to -1.3% " : << Conclusion : A gravity - interacting field can be generated electromagnetically and used to reduce the gravity vector locally.
