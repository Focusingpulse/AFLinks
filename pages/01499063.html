I would be very pleased if you would add it to your New Energy News letter as you suggested. PERPETUAL MOTION - AN ANCIENT MYSTERY SOLVED? A new book has just been published which appears to be promoting a thesis that rejects certain hallowed beliefs held since Newton's time. Namely that perpetual motion is possible. The impression is false. What in fact the author is suggesting is that a rather infamous man whose real name was Johann Bessler but who used the pseudonym Orffyreus was not the criminal that everyone believes him to be. Having spent most of his spare time in the last ten to fifteen years researching the life of Bessler, John Collins, the author of the above book, has uncovered numerous documents which appear to show conclusively, that the inventor was genuine in his claim that he had discovered a way of making a perpetual motion machine. Obviously this information contradicted accepted fact, i.e. even if you could make a machine which ran for ever, it could not do work. John Collins decided to accept for argument's sake, that Bessler was genuine, and taking this as a starting point he worked backwards taking into account all the information he had gathered about the Bessler machine. Ultimately he was able to show that if we accept that Bessler was wrong about assigning the term 'perpetual motion' to his machine, then we are half-way to understanding how Bessler was able to achieve the astonishing results credited to him.
