Castor oil is a thick, pale yellow vegetable oil obtained from the seeds of the castor plant (Ricinus communis). It has been used for centuries as a laxative, anti-inflammatory, and skin moisturizer. Edgar Cayce on Castor Oil Edgar Cayce often recommended castor oil packs in his readings. Here are the main points: 1. Healing Tool for Many Ailments Cayce recommended castor oil packs for: Liver and gallbladder detoxification Constipation Poor circulation Lymphatic congestion Joint pain and inflammation Menstrual disorders 2. He sometimes recommended a small dose as a laxative, but only under specific circumstances. 4.
