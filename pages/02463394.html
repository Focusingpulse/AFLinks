The theory makes several testable predictions, including specific angular distribution patterns in beta decay, correlations between neutrino behavior and environmental magnetic fields, and geometric signatures in oscillation patterns. These predictions suggest new approaches to neutrino detection and potential methods for influencing nuclear decay rates through geometric manipulation.
