GC2026-STP050 Variations in the F2-layer critical frequency: dependence on the choice of solar activity index Sargylana E. Kobyakova1 , Artem Y. Gololobov1 , Rustam R. Karimov1 , Alexandr E. Stepanov1 1 Y.G. Shafer Institute of Cosmophysical Research and Aeronomy of the Siberian Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences karimovrim@gmail.com Variations in the F2-layer critical frequency (foF2) as a function of the solar activity index choice were studied using annual mean foF2 values from the Yakutsk ionospheric station (62.01° N, 129.43° E) over the period 1957–2017. It is shown that both the magnitude and sign of the foF2 trend at subauroral latitudes are significantly influenced by the choice of solar activity index. Trends derived from six different solar activity indices—F10.7, F30, Mg II, He II, SSN, and Lyman‑α—were compared. The F30 and Mg II indices demonstrated the best performance, followed by F10.7 and Lyman‑α, while the SSN and He II indices proved to be less suitable for trend analysis. The results indicate that the negative trends obtained with F30 and Mg II are approximately two to three times larger in magnitude than those derived from F10.7 and Lyman‑α. Therefore, for studies of long-term ionospheric variability in the subauroral region based on data from the Yakutsk station and employing annual mean values, the F30 index is recommended as the most appropriate solar proxy.
