GC2026-PERM033 Potential barriers and transformations of the domain structure in submicron magnetite particles exposed to an external magnetic field determined by micromagnetism methods Valery Shcherbakov1 , Natalia Sycheva1 1 Geophysical Observatory "Borok", O.Yu. Schmidt Institute of Physics of the Earth, Russian Academy of Sciences sycheva@borok.yar.ru Without a comprehensive understanding of DS transformations under influence of different physical factors, such as temperature T, external magnetic fields B, or stress, it is not possible to quantitatively understand magnetic remanence acquisition in rocks. Using the finite-element code MERRILL by means of a nudged-elastic-band technique to find least-energy saddle points of the micromagnetic energy functional [1], the potential barriers between local energy minima (LEM), which represent various configurations of magnetic domains in pseudodomain (PSD) particles, were calculated. LEM as the starting state for calculating energy barriers was determind using MERRILL code for a given particle size and the magnitude of the external magnetic field B by minimizing the particle energy at the initial state of the magnetic moments of unit cells along the easy axis [001], the final state in this case is the inversion of the starting state. At B < > 0, the starting and ending states for calculating energy barriers were calculated by minimizing LEM, taking into account the magnitude and direction of B.
