COMING ASHORE The Stargate-traveling Transhuman The galaxy may be in for a rude awakening. A device that can drill holes in space that take us from point A to point B is no longer a sci- fi fantasy. Each day now we move closer to designing the pipeline (or lifeline) that will take humanity into the galaxy. We are 50 years (or less given a twist of fate) away from opening the throat of a stargate or wormhole large enough for the first human to enter. Next time you find yourself wondering why we’re so excited about re-engineering the human body think about stargates. The current, interim, human body will not survive entering the wormhole as portrayed in movies. Only the transhuman will have at the greatest discovery in human history. ZOOM-ZOOM From the beginning, humanity has sought higher ground. Eve thought she could hop from A to B by eating the fruit of the serpent. Instead, she found she was naked. In the original story of king of the hill, Go(d) clothed Adam and Eve and slammed shut the gate to Eden in retaliation. Earth life is a role-playing game set in 3-D. Humanity’s goal is to recapture the fruit, reclaim our original skin and reopen the gate, which allows us to return to the garden (the hundreds of billions of galaxies in the known universe). Today’s serpent is the wormhole, called a serpent rope by the ancient Mayans. The fruit of the serpent is the exotic matter of which the wormhole is composed. It transforms the ordinary human on contact into quicksilver. We’re talking a fully functional human being. Scientists hope to ‘lift the serpent’ and eat the original controlled substance, the emission (the exotic matter) of the serpent (the wormhole) and go far into the universe. Like Eve, it will help if they soon find that they are naked. THE HIGH PRIEST OF STARGATES It is Carl Sagan who is considered the original high priest of stargates.
