Robert Toru Kiyosaki (born April 8, 1947) is an American businessman and author of Rich Dad Poor Dad. Kiyosaki is the founder of Rich Global LLC and the Rich Dad Company, a private financial education company that provides personal finance and business education to people through books and videos. The company's main revenues come from franchisees of the Rich Dad seminars that are conducted by independent individuals using Kiyosaki 's brand name for a fee. He is also the creator of the Cashflow board and software games to educate adults and children about business and financial concepts.
