Roy Keys Inc.), Montreal Pages 414 ISBN 9781987980356 Wilhelm Weber's Main Works on Electrodynamics Translated into English, Volume V: Unipolar Induction, Galvanometry, Biographical Studies, and Weber's Electrodynamics Versus Different Field Theories is the fifth volume of the five-volume edition of Wilhelm Weber 's electrodynamic works edited and translated by Andre K T Assis , published by Apeiron in 2024. It collects Weber's two papers on unipolar induction , his work on galvanometry and on the motion of electricity in molecular bodies, together with the biographical and memorial literature: Riecke's memorial speech, Heinrich Weber's biographical sketch, and the prefaces to the collected works. The volume contains, in translation and with editorial introductions: [Gauss, 1840] Introduction to General Propositions Relating to Attractive and Repulsive Forces [Weber, 1840] and [Weber, 1841] Unipolar induction — both papers, with editorial introductions [Weber, 1862] On galvanometry [Weber, 1874] On the equivalent of vis viva [Weber, 1875] On the motion of electricity in bodies of molecular constitution [Riecke, 1891] Memorial speech for Weber [Heinrich Weber, 1893] Wilhelm Weber: a biographical sketch Prefaces to the collected works of Wilhelm Weber (1892–1894) [Weber, 1894] Electroscopic and electrodynamic actions of free electricity in closed circuits — a posthumous paper [Weber, 1894] On electrothermism [Voigt, 1899] The Gauss–Weber monument It ends with three editorial essays: on the weber as an electrical unit of measure, on velocity in Weber's electrodynamics compared with the velocities appearing in field theories, and on Weber's electrodynamics versus different field theories.
