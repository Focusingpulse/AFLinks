Below there will be shown that the degenerations of the interfer￾ometer’s sensitivity to effects of first order can be lifted changing the traditional configurations of the device. My experiment demonstrated that a two-media device operating at effects of first order can reliably measure the shift of the interference fringe (and thus the speed of “aether wind”), and much more successfully than by Michelson inter￾ferometer operating at effects of second order. The shift of interference fringe is reliably regis￾tered (in rotation of the interferometer by 90o ) even at gas light carrying pairs with arm’s length up to 1 m. With this the fringe shift appears to be proportional to υ / c and difference 12 ε −ε .
