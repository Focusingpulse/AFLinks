His creation of the system by which electrical power is transmitted by alternate currents effected a revolution in electrical apparatus, and the list of his important inventions is a long one. His process for producing light superior to the incandescent without the use of carbons is nearly ready for commercial application, and there is little or no doubt that the use of carbons will soon be disposed with altogether. One of the most interesting and far reaching of his inventions is the oscillator, which is referred to intelligibly In a recent article on Tesla's work in the New York Sun . The oscillator is for use In connection with the creation of electrical energy. It is estimated that nine-tenths of all the dynamos in the world are operated by steam power. Every step in the production of electricity by steam power is attended with waste and los8 of force and energy. The problem Tesla set out to solve was the saving of the 95 per cent of energy that was wasted.
