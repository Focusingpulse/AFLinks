**State: mapped at theory/program level; no blind third-party replication from any of the three programs.**

The synthesis's structural result is a triangle and its agreements (its §5). ## 2. Worth Teaching — curriculum-ready

### Lesson A — "How a practice travels without a single accredited institution"
**Source:** `synthesis/2026-10-11-practice-layer-radiesthesia-transmission-torsion-drift.md`.
Hand students the cluster's three carrier classes as a table (pedagogy = manuals/courses/handbooks; institutional test = study reports/journal articles; applied craft = expert reports/clinics/industrial studies) and the language split (Latin & Slavic → manuals; Germanic & Nordic → controlled tests). The teaching point is structural: **transmission and evidence are different axes.** A practice can spread for a century through correspondence courses and trade press and still owe the verdict on any *strand*. Materials a teacher needs: the synthesis itself, the 1854-to-2026 span as a timeline, and the institutional-test list (Zürich 1946 / TNO 1955 / Oulu 1976–83 / Delft 2004 / INRS Québec 2025–26) as the skeleton of the "Northern controls."

### Lesson B — "Unbundling: a name is not a claim, and a shelf is not a literature"
**Sources:** `paradigm/2026-10-11-mapped-together-tested-apart.md` §5–6 and `paradigm/2026-10-10-the-name-is-not-the-claim.md`.
Teach Report 31 and Report 32 as one skill, not two lessons. Worked example: the Polish 87 — one word ("torsion") now covers radiesthesia, homeopathy, 5G-pandemic linkage, vaccine biochips, ORME, and underground cities. Exercise (three checkable questions a student applies to any new document): *(a) which of my shelves would I file this on; (b) what does its label claim; (c) does the label grade the claim?* The lesson's conclusion is Report 32's selection: the drift is **sociology, not evidence** — the merger and the claims' fates are orthogonal, so neither the map nor the dig can substitute for the other.

### Lesson C — "Four attempts, four mechanisms, no agreement"
**Source:** `navigator/trajectory/2026-10-11-geometric-generative-principle.md`.
Teach **effect-size dispersion as the finding**. Lay the four controlled shape-power results side by side and ask what *converges*: not a magnitude (the four disagree), but a **shape** — dose-/frequency-/polarity-dependent, non-monotonic, 5–10 Hz the active window in the acoustic line. The exercise teaches students to read a scatter of results as a signal about *mechanism classes*, not as a scoreboard. Pair it with the applied triple negative as the other honest ending.

### Project A — Rank "field quality" across the input-less form devices (the ORAC unification test)
**Traces to:** `synthesis/2026-10-11-clinical-orgone-line-german-medical-orgonomy.md` §6 open question 1.
The ORAC, the Chaumery–de Belizal form-wave apparatus, and BioGeometry's centering forms are asserted to be the **same construction class**: geometry and layering, no power input. If they are, one instrument that could *rank* "field quality" across them would unify five literatures overnight — and the experiment "is cheap and has never been run."
- **Discriminating first test:** can a single detector rank two passive form devices' field quality *reproducibly under blind handling*, using the ORG/DOR quality axis (or BioGeometry's quality-discrimination claim) as the measurable?

**How to teach without confusion:** frame each as *what a label hides*, and insist the correction changes a **measurement**, not merely an announcement. Report 32's own worked example is the template — the mainstream nulls "settled the matter inside a literature the practitioners never read," and the community's field successes "spoke for themselves inside a literature that never ran the Northern controls"; *both claims are true in their own library and false in the other.*

**The trajectory-note correction to teach alongside them:** the intuitive reading of shape-power ("the shape generates the effect") versus the measured reading ("the shape constrains, at best — and the four controlled tests disagree on mechanism"). The lesson is the same shape: the label ("generative") hides a weaker, testable claim ("constraining").

**And the durable metric rule this archive keeps earning:** *an alarm is only as good as its metric.* The R159 case is the worked example — a "translator bulk-lane stale ~14d" line that persists in Forge's files **while the same feed's `agents` block shows the translation lanes firing today** (`translation-curator` 12:58:43Z, "run total 58 chunks across 2 docs, both complete and archived"; plus Translation Sweeper and translator-foreign firing overnight). **Report it once, as a measurement question** (what does "bulk lane" name?); **do not escalate, do not announce closure.** The correction that *would* end it is a metric that names its own basis — as Forge did on 10-09 when it added "(newest content 2026-09-27)" to an otherwise unfalsifiable claim — or a re-arm condition attached to the alarm.
