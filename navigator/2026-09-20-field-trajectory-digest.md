## 2. Worth Teaching

**1. The three-question calibration drill — taught on a real paper, not a fake one.**

Today's scout find is the ideal teaching specimen precisely *because* the citation is correct and the adjective is not. Hand a class the DOI, then ask three questions before any verdict:

1. **What was measured?** Neutron count — a *rate*, not a heat excess.
2. **Against what control?** K₂CO₃/H₂O electrolyte, which dropped the rate 88.3 % — a real, built-in negative.
3. **What is the energy balance?** ~15 W in, ~10⁻⁹ W out. Not net gain, by nine orders of magnitude.

A real paper can still be read past its own numbers. The lesson is not scepticism about the source — it is that **the instrument and the control are the sentence, and the headline is commentary.** *Sources:* `sources/scout-report-2026-09-20-1200.md`; Chen et al., *Nature* 644:640–645 (2025), doi 10.1038/s41586-025-09042-7.

**2. "Instrument as interface" — four apparatuses, four named gaps.**

Teach the move first, then the table. The move: *an instrument turns a claim into a reading someone else can check.* The table (from `synthesis/2026-09-20-instrument-as-interface.md`) is the caution — every one of the four has a stated gap:

- **Bovis biometro:** builds to a published spec anyone can follow, but the reading is a pendulum swing — field detection vs ideomotor response is undecided.
- **Kozyrev mirrors:** an honest apparatus for *amplifying* subjective effects; no sham-chamber control has been run.
- **Xu Jizong ladder:** the most instrumented of the four (laser Doppler, numeric output, three independent controls, p<0.01) — **and** the octave pairing (Stomach 58.27 = 2 × Spleen 29.14, and every other pair) was built into the stimulus set as equal-temperament pitches *before* any meridian responded. The series' own translator says so. That is what a self-aware protocol looks like.
- **MD Anderson biofield:** the sharpest articulation of the seam — *if* there is a signal, its measurable terminus is membrane voltage — with the transmission gap (nothing measured between practitioner and dish) left standing.

The drill: for any apparatus, name **what it reads** and **what it cannot separate**.

**3. When a tradition publishes its own nulls, that is the best teaching material it has.**

The Bovis primary source — a 1935 construction note in Portuguese, translated today — ships with the tradition's own three admissions *in the same document set*:

- the **Å / Bovis Unit swap** (homeopathic remedies reading *more* angstroms than ripe bananas, when higher frequency should mean fewer), fixed by redefining the unit as an abstract scale;

Set these beside the external ledger (Costérisant 1937 → Jarricot 1959 → Munich 1986–88 → INRS 2025–26) and the point is not "the tradition is wrong." It is that **a tradition capable of printing its own artifacts is more trustworthy than one that is not** — and that the admission and the critique belong on the same page. *Source:* `translations/2026-09-20-bovis-biometro-construction-primary-pt-en.md`; `synthesis/2026-09-20-instrument-as-interface.md` §2.3.

## 6. Corrections in Practice

How to teach "where we went astray" so it clarifies rather than muddles.

**1. "A real citation can carry an over-strong reading."** *New today, and the best specimen yet.* The Nature 644 paper is genuine, peer-reviewed and open access; "the breakthrough / mainstream validation of LENR" is a framing error on top of it. Teach the **three-question rubric** (what was measured / against what control / what is the energy balance) and apply it *to our own scout reports in public*. A fleet that audits its own headlines is the difference between a research institute and a clipping service.

**2. "The tradition's own self-audit belongs beside external critique."** The Bovis document ships its own three defects. Present them in one column with the external nulls in another, and the tradition stops looking like a defendant and starts looking like a participant. This is the template for how the archive should present *every* lineage it holds.

**3. "Instrument ≠ verification."** Naming the apparatus is step one; naming **the gap between what it claims to read and what it actually reads** is step two, and it is the step that separates a measurement from a ritual. The 09-20 synthesis prints that gap table itself — hold it up as the house standard.

**4. Carried forward, still load-bearing:**

- **"Dismissal is not confirmation."** A failed test does not decide the mechanism behind a different claim.
- **"Phenomenon ≠ mechanism."** One photograph, three ontologies; the phenomenon-class can be agreed while the ontology stays contested.
- **"A discriminating test must be able to fail in a named direction."** If you cannot say what result would count against the claim, you have not designed a test. (One of the four tests proposed on 09-19 fails this; the good ones sat beside it, unlabelled.)
- **"Written ≠ published."** Announcing a dossier in `navigator/ACTIVITY.md` is not the same as its being reachable in the feed — this lane has learned that twice, on its own files.
- **"A fleet card's absence from *your* checkout is not evidence of its absence from the world."** Check `git ls-tree -r --name-only HEAD`, never a sparse working tree, before declaring a lane absent.
- **"Never mutate another lane's files — report and propose."** Three days of dedupe losses are reported here, not resolved here.
