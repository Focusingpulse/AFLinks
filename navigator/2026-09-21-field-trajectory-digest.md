## 2. Worth Teaching

**1. The four-tradition verification rail — taught as one arc, with the primaries in hand.**

Hand a class the rail and ask the structural question before any verdict: *what did each tradition have to build before its claim could be graded?*

- **Chevreul 1854** built the blindfold + fixed-arm rig and named the mechanism ("involuntary muscular movement") — the origin of every ideomotor test since.
- **Drbal 1949** named testable parameters (exact pyramid proportions, a named object) and let anyone build it.
- **Sergeev 1993–94** used real instrumentation and **kept the adverse rows** (BP null, 2 deteriorations of 24).
- **MD Anderson 2026** named the terminus: *if* there is a signal, its measurable stop is membrane voltage.

The lesson: **the rail is made of apparatuses, not conclusions.** Every rung is built, published, and gradeable by a stranger. *Sources:* `synthesis/2026-09-21-verification-rail-methodology.md` §2–§5; the translated primaries in `translations/` (Chevreul 1854, Argenton 2007, Bricage 2025); Forge's verification-rail note.

**2. "Instrument ≠ verification": name what it reads, then name what it cannot separate.**

Every apparatus on the rail has a stated gap, and the corpus now prints those gaps itself:

- Bovis biometro — reads a pendulum swing; **cannot separate** field detection from ideomotor response.
- Kozyrev mirror chambers — amplify subjective reports; **no sham-chamber control has been run.**
- Xu Jizong laser-Doppler ladder — numeric, controlled, p<0.01; **and** the octave pairing was built into the stimulus set before any meridian responded (the translator says so).
- MD Anderson DiBAC₄(3) — reads *relative*, not absolute, potential; **nothing measures the space between practitioner and dish.**

The drill is two columns: *what it reads* / *what it cannot separate*. A measurement is a reading plus its stated blind spot. *Source:* `synthesis/2026-09-20-instrument-as-interface.md` §2; `synthesis/2026-09-21-verification-rail-methodology.md` §6.3.

**3. "An award is not a replication" — taught on the psi-track dossier.**

Dossier 029 is a good teaching specimen precisely because the source's own honor is ambiguous. The Swedish psi-track work won the **Imich Prize (Society for Psychical Research, 1994)** — and the dossier states, up front, that the committee *split* the prize because "none of the entries merited the 1,000-dollar prize" and explicitly **excluded professional parapsychologists.** The reported result (32 of 40 double-blind trials, six occasions, three years) has never been reproduced outside the original group.

Teach the distinction: **recognition is a signal of interest, not of independent confirmation.** Ask the class which of the two the Imich citation is — and let them find the sentence in the source. *Source:* `synthesis/replication/2026-09-21-dossier-029-psi-track-dowsing.md`; `translations/2026-09-18-psi-track-dowsing-sweden-sv.md` (live origin now 404; archived).

**4. "An award is not a replication" — NEW.** Taught on the psi-track dossier (§2.3): the Imich Prize was split because none of the entries merited the full prize and professional parapsychologists were excluded. Recognition ≠ independent confirmation.
