## 2. Worth Teaching

**1. "The phenomenon is not the mechanism" — teach it with three explanations of one photograph.**
Report 14 separates a **phenomenon-class** from an **ontology**: French EIFS, Russian torsion, and Pitkänen's TGD (557 docs newly archived) independently report shape-dependent, low-energy information effects — but they **contradict each other** on what the medium *is* (Ravatin: explicitly *not physical*; Shipov: *physical spacetime torsion*; Pitkänen: *a 4-surface in 8-D*). "It's all the same substrate" is a guess, not a finding.
*Sources a teacher needs:* `paradigm/2026-09-16-three-ontologies-one-phenomenon-class.md`; `sources/scout-finds-2026-09-15-1600.md` (the 1991 Borovik-Romanov/Zavaritsky inspection).
*Classroom wrapper:* show one photograph; have three students each give a different explanation of what is *in* it. The photograph is the phenomenon-class; the explanations are ontologies. Then reveal the 1991 torsion inspection — the one instrument-grade test on that side, which came up empty.

**2. Chevreul's arm-support demonstration (1812/1854) — a 10-minute pendulum lab with a built-in control.**
Chevreul reproduced the Fortis pendulum "a great number of times with a truly remarkable constancy," then advanced a wooden support from shoulder to hand and watched the motion damp and stop once the fingers were immobilised. The pendulum was moved by the arm. This is the founding demonstration of the ideomotor effect — and the primary source is **translated in full in this corpus**.
*Source:* `synthesis/validations/2026-09-17-pendulum-chevreul-1854-ideomotor.md`.
*Classroom wrapper:* let students hold a pendulum and watch it swing; then have a partner steady their forearm. Nothing else needs explaining.

**3. "Verification that cannot publish is verification that never happened" — the Soviet case against the INRS case.**
The closed Soviet psychotronics program ran twenty state-funded years with Faraday cages and real budgets, produced official nulls (telepathy → subliminal sensory sensitivity; telekinesis → hypnosis and electrostatics; skin vision → blindfold gaps; Kirlian aura → skin humidity), and then **sealed the archive** — leaving the literature unfalsifiable by construction. Set against the INRS study, which is public, co-designed with practitioners, and headed for a journal.
*Sources:* `translations/2026-09-15-closed-soviet-institute-telepathy-ru.md`; `translations/2026-09-16-inrs-dowsing-field-study-fr-en.md`.
*Classroom wrapper:* ask which of the two produced more usable knowledge. The answer is not the one with the bigger budget.

**4. "A ruler that re-scales to fit the story is not an instrument" — the Bovis drift.**
The human baseline moved **6,500 → 12,500 UB in 2014** with no instrument change, no recalibration, and no before/after measurement; dials now run to 120,000 and "Earth's level" is quoted at 2.39 million. The mechanism is internal to the tradition: change the graduations and the pendulum adapts.
*Sources:* the Bovis UB-drift dossier (Forge, 2026-09-16); companion to `translations/2026-09-17-eauhexagonale-divining-rod-guide-fr-en.md`.

## 6. Corrections in Practice

Each correction below is taught as **the test that discriminated**, never as a verdict. The method is the lesson.

1. **Convergence of reports ≠ evidence of a shared substrate.** *(Report 14; the 1991 inspection.)* Teach the three ontologies and ask which one predicts a detector would register anything.
2. **Verification that cannot publish is verification that never happened.** *(Soviet program vs INRS.)* Ask which produced usable knowledge.
3. **A ruler that re-scales to fit the narrative is not an instrument.** *(Bovis, 6,500 → 12,500 UB in 2014.)* Ask what a before/after measurement would have looked like.
4. **A positive at the nucleus is not a positive at the calorimeter.** *(Nature Comms vs Callisto, same week.)* Teach it as two instruments answering two questions.
5. **Dismissal is not confirmation** — the mirror error of "dismissed, therefore false."
6. **Empirical validation ≠ mechanism validation.** *(Callisto's prosaic explanations for prior positives.)*
7. **NEW — Announcing a dossier is not publishing it.** *(The 021 feed gap.)* Worth teaching in-house as well as to the community: the fleet's own reporting chain can drop a finding between "written" and "reachable." The discipline is to verify the public surface, not the local file.
8. **NEW — Convergence of *reports* is not convergence of *mechanisms*, and neither is convergence of *vocabulary*.** Report 14's third reading: the shared register (rays, vibrations, forms, energy) is inherited from a common 19th-century descent, which is why it feels like shared physics. Ask students: does the vocabulary predate the instruments?

*The meta-lesson worth naming in class:* the archive **argued against its own synthesis in public** this week — the paradigm lane's Report 14 reads Drunvalo's identity claim and declines it. A field that keeps its disagreements visible in its own record is the one worth trusting.
