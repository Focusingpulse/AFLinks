## 2. Worth Teaching

Three curriculum-ready items. Each carries the sources a teacher needs.

**Item 1 — Read the dossier backward: from the unrun control to the claim, never from the claim to the excuse.**
Report 26 supplies a *portable reading method*, and it is teachable in one sitting because the corpus now demonstrates it five times across four languages. The lesson: within any row, **the famous result is the least informative element** (it is the element that was never controlled); the **control that was never run carries the entire evidential weight, and its cost is known.** Ask of any claim: *what is its cheapest unrun control, and who could run it this afternoon?* Then file the row by that cost. The paired lesson is the register rule from Issue 19, still standing: a court row and a certification finding are not measurements (*"institutions certify, censor, sue, and fund; only instruments measure"*).
*Sources: `paradigm/2026-10-04-the-unrun-control.md` (Report 26) §1, §5, §6; `paradigm/2026-10-03-institutions-are-not-evidence.md` (Report 25) §5.*

**Item 2 — "The effect is real" and "the claim is real" are different sentences.**
The single cleanest teaching case in the archive's practical layer, because the instrument did both jobs at once: IGF Waldaschaff 2001 **reproduced** Meyl's striking transmission (so the effect was not nothing) and **refuted** the over-unity claim (45% efficiency; an ordinary Lecher-line standing wave). Teach students to ask, of every vivid demonstration, *which sentence is being asserted* — the phenomenon, or the mechanism, or the magnitude — because the archive's own validation file names this conflation as *"precisely the failure the archive exists to prevent."* Run it alongside the crop-formation grades (§Trajectory 3), where the same fork appears as *practice vs mechanism*.
*Sources: `navigator/trajectory/2026-10-04-longitudinal-non-hertzian-waves.md` §3–§5; `synthesis/validations/2026-10-01-scalar-wave-overunity-igf-weidner-2001.md`; `synthesis/2026-10-03-crop-formations-evidence-and-method.md` §8.*

**Item 3 — A blinded operator, a pre-stated criterion, and an assay proven able to see a difference: the three-line checklist.**
Assembled from three of this week's cards and portable to every applied claim in the archive.
1. **Blind the person who can see the key, not just the analyst.** Applied kinesiology is the clean case: **Schmitt 1998** reported ~90.5% concordance **open-label**; **Staehle 2005**, **double-blind**, returned **35% — below chance — after excluding 28% of the sample**. The two numbers are a **design effect, not an average**; do not average 90.5% and 35%, ask *who could see the key.*
2. **State the inclusion criterion before collecting, and permit no exclusions** (the 2005 study's failure mode is named inside Dossier 062's protocol itself).
3. **Prove the assay can detect a known difference, or a no-difference result is VOID, not null** (Dossier 062's mandatory Phase 0 — prove the cotton-strip decomposition assay can separate a compost-added bed from an untreated one; if it can't, *"no difference" and "the assay could not have seen a difference" get filed identically*).
*Sources: `synthesis/validations/2026-10-03-applied-kinesiology-mmt-schmitt-staehle.md`; `synthesis/replication/2026-10-03-dossier-062-biodynamic-preparation-500.md`; the underlying review: Uwe Geier et al., *La Agricultura Biodinámica: una síntesis científica* (2016/2021).*

## 3. Worth Building / Testing

Three candidate projects, deliberately **on three different channels** — an electrostatic bench, a plant-biology field, and a microbiology plate count. That spread is the point: Yang et al. 2026 (arXiv:2602.03794) found that **two diverse channels beat sixteen identical ones**, so a community that wants to learn fast should run **different kinds** of test rather than a bigger version of one. Each below is chosen after competing options were set side by side — Maryanskyy 2026 (arXiv:2603.20324): **select the standout rather than blend**, and note its **weak-model paradox**: the modest cheap experiment teaches more per dollar than the grand one.

**Project 1 — Build Dossier 060 and run the four-arm scalar / monopole field detection test.**
*The discriminating first test:* a suspended **brass** ring's angular displacement per 60 s, driven by a ~5 kV / ~10 MHz Tesla transformer, across **four pre-registered arms** — (a) driven + **brass** ring; (b) driven + **matched insulating ring of the same size and mass**; (c) **static** sphere + brass ring; (d) driven + brass ring **behind grounded mesh**. **What would prove/disprove:** if the brass ring rotates while the matched insulating ring does not, and the grounded mesh does not stop it, then the rotation is conductor-specific, non-shieldable and oscillation-dependent — the first controlled datapoint in the line's history. **If both rings rotate, the effect is ion wind and the scalar-field reading is refuted — the expected result, and a complete one.** The material swap is the whole experiment; a 5 kV sphere guarantees an ion-wind alternative and nothing else in the line has ever separated the two.
*Effort envelope:* **~$150–400** (6-turn 5 mm Al primary, 1500-turn 0.35 mm Cu secondary on a 50 mm polyethylene tube, Brovin generator, sphere, two rings, mesh). **One afternoon.** **Dependencies:** the 2008 Shipov/Lobova primary is **not in the vault**, so the protocol comes from the card, not the source; a written, dated pre-registration sheet photographed before the first run. **Safety: adult-only — ~5 kV at ~10 MHz.**
*Sources: `synthesis/quest-queue/2026-10-02-scalar-field-detection.md`; `synthesis/replication/2026-10-02-dossier-060-scalar-field-detection.md`; ranked as the line's decisive question in `navigator/trajectory/2026-10-04-longitudinal-non-hertzian-waves.md` §5.*

**Project 2 — Run the blind crop-formation replication: the experiment the argument has needed since 1999.**
*The discriminating first test:* make a **man-made control circle** with board and rope in the **same crop at the same maturity** as a target formation (the Hoeven 1999 row is the one with printed numbers), using the **same diameter, sampling scheme, six-day delay and measuring procedure** — then measure node lengths and cell-wall structures with **the measurers not knowing which samples came from which formation**, having **decided the inclusion criteria and analysis before collecting**. **What would prove/disprove:** the 2014 attempt (below) found the man-made circle produced a **144% peak** node increase with **no radial symmetry**, against the 1999 formation's **214% peak / 171% all-stem average** and clean radial symmetry — and its authors still concluded the 1999 structure *"could not be explained and remains anomalous."* That was **one experiment, not blind, and not independently repeated.** A pre-registered blind replication either reproduces the radial structure in a formation whose making is controlled, or it does not; either way it is the first clean datapoint, and *"unexplained"* stays the honest word until it exists.
*Effort envelope:* **~$0–100** (a field, a board, a rope, a micrometer, a crop-sharing farmer); **one growing season.** **Dependencies:** raw data + weather/soil records from the **Hoeven 1999** row (see §4); a cooperative grower. **Safety:** none. **Note the discipline required:** a man-made circle *did* produce a large node increase, so large elongation is **not by itself evidence of anything** — the discriminating quantity is the **radial structure** and the blind comparison, not the magnitude.
*Sources: `synthesis/2026-10-03-crop-formations-evidence-and-method.md` §5–§6; **Haselhoff, Boerman & Bobbink (2014)**, *JSE* 28(1):17–33 (the control experiment); **Grassi, Cocheo & Russo (2005)**, *JSE* 19(2):159–170 (the critique from raw data); **Levengood (1994)**, *Physiologia Plantarum* 92:356–363; **Levengood & Talbott (1999)**, *Physiologia Plantarum* 105:615–624.*

**Project 3 — The Dodonov dead-water plate count: a blindfold in an afternoon.**
*A different channel again — microbiology, not instrumentation.* *The discriminating first test:* the Dodonov patent corpus's cheapest unrun falsifiable is a **blinded plate count** — grow microbial cultures in "live" vs "dead" (boiled / treated) water, with **the counter not knowing which plate is which**, and a **pre-stated** inclusion criterion. **What would prove/disprove:** a blinded, repeated, above-chance difference between the two waters reopens the corpus's water-structure claims on the cheapest substrate they have; a null at proper blinding closes a row that has stood **thirty years stale**. **The blinding is the experiment** — the archive's own Chevreul rows (six documented on the radiesthesia rail) show exactly how a non-blind count manufactures a difference.
*Effort envelope:* **~$0–50** (agar, petri dishes, a pot to boil water, an incubator box or a warm cupboard); **one afternoon plus incubation.** **Dependencies:** a clean bench and aseptic technique — or a cooperating school biology lab, which removes the only real barrier. **Safety:** standard microbiology hygiene; no pathogens, non-pathogenic culture only.
*Source: Forge dossier **FAL-ru-259-1..4** (the Dodonov wood-cylinder patent corpus) as reported in `paradigm/2026-10-04-the-unrun-control.md` §1 and §6 — **a colleague's report; the dossier itself is behind the publish boundary and was not read by this lane.***

## 6. Corrections in Practice

How to teach "where we went astray" without confusing learners — and without repeating the error in the opposite direction.

- **The unrun control is a location, not an accusation — teach the three readings together.** Report 26's A ("fraud's signature"), B ("frontier poverty"), and C ("the recurrence *is* the finding") must be taught as **rivals kept alive**, not blended: A as a **per-case fraud test** (the cheapest unrun control is also the cheapest fraud test, because a fraudster has a reason never to run it and a believer has every reason to run it first), B as causal context (institutional closure explains why the deficit is *uniform*), C as the selection. **The teaching trap is turning C into a licence for suspicion**: a tradition that runs its control and fails is not thereby a fraud, and one whose inventor scatters across 44 orders of magnitude (the TET row: 0.75 Nm vs 2e-45 Nm, one day apart, neither retracted) is not thereby representative of its neighbours. **Keep the rivals alive and judge on the next datapoint.**
