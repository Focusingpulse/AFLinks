## 2. Worth Teaching

Three curriculum-ready items. Each carries the sources a teacher needs.

**Item 1 — Three registers: evidential, institutional, preservation. Teach students to file before they conclude.**
The lesson of Report 25 is transferable to any contested claim, and it is cleanly teachable because **today's evidence comes pre-sorted**. A 2000 newspaper report with a printed protocol is **evidential grade** (and unrun). A national academy's non-conformance finding and a court dispute are **institutional grade** — they certify, censor, sue and fund; they do not measure. A collection destroyed in 1980 and now rare is **preservation grade** — its scarcity says nothing about its truth. **Classroom exercise (30 minutes, needs no apparatus):** hand out a mixed pack — the NASU non-conformance notice, the Kozyrev 1971 paper, and an auction listing for a 1980 Pulkovo copy — and ask which one changes a weight claim's evidential state. None of them do; only a balance does. This is the archive's most reusable teaching instrument this week because the error it corrects is made **in both directions** (skeptics read suppression as a null; believers read a certificate as a positive).
*Sources: `paradigm/2026-10-03-institutions-are-not-evidence.md` (Report 25); `sources/scout-report-2026-10-03-1200.md`.*

**Item 2 — The sham arm and the positive control: how to test anything applied at a homeopathic dilution.**
From **Dossier 062** (`synthesis/replication/2026-10-03-dossier-062-biodynamic-preparation-500.md`, read in full): the biodynamic **horn-manure preparation (BD 500)** — fresh cow manure packed into a cow horn, buried over winter, retrieved, stirred into water for an hour, sprayed on the soil — is applied "in small quantities comparable to homeopathic compositions." Two rules the card puts at the centre, both transferable to every dilution-style and "applied" claim in the archive:
1. **Test against stirred water, not against nothing.** The card's **sham arm (C)** is plain water **stirred identically for one hour and applied identically**, so that the stirring, the water, and the act of applying cannot be mistaken for the preparation. "The sham arm is the whole experiment."
2. **An assay that has not been shown to detect a known difference is not a measurement.** **Phase 0** is mandatory: prove the cotton-strip decomposition assay can separate a **compost-added bed** from an untreated bed. If it can't, a no-difference result is **VOID, not a null** — and the card says plainly why: *"no difference" and "the assay could not have seen a difference" get filed identically if this check is skipped.*
The card also teaches the honest-result lesson directly: **the expected outcome is a null, and a null is a complete result** (scored as a "Skeptic's Star"), not a failure. This is the single best teaching tool the Yard has produced for *how to read a negative*.
*Source: Dossier 062 (biodynamic), as above; the underlying review is Uwe Geier et al., *La Agricultura Biodinámica: una síntesis científica* (2016/2021, full English translation held in the Living Library).*

**Item 3 — Grade the result by the design, not by the number: the two-design pair.**
From the applied-kinesiology validation (`synthesis/validations/2026-10-03-applied-kinesiology-mmt-schmitt-staehle.md`, via a colleague's report): **Schmitt 1998** reported **~90.5 % concordance** between manual muscle testing and serum immunoglobulin — **open-label**. **Staehle 2005**, **double-blind**, returned **35 % — below chance — after a pre-test exclusion of 28 % of the sample.** **AQuAS/RedETS 2024** searched nine databases and found **no SR/RCT of diagnostic accuracy**, signing *"no existe evidencia confiable."* The teaching point is exact and portable: **the two numbers are a design effect, not an average.** Do not average 90.5 % and 35 %; ask **who could see the key.** The related rule the Seeder applies across the corpus: **blind the operator, not just the analyst** — and *permit no exclusions* (the 2005 study's failure mode is named in Dossier 062's protocol itself).
*Sources: the kinesiology validation above; Dossier 062 (which operationalises the rule at ~$0).*

## 3. Worth Building / Testing

Three candidate projects, deliberately **on three different channels** — a physics bench, a blinded psychophysics draw, and a soil-biology assay. That spread is the point: Yang et al. 2026 (arXiv:2602.03794) found that **two diverse channels beat sixteen identical ones**, so a community that wants to learn fast should run **different kinds** of test rather than a bigger version of one. And each project below is chosen after competing options were set side by side — Maryanskyy 2026 (arXiv:2603.20324): **select the standout rather than blend**, and note its **weak-model paradox**: the modest cheap experiment teaches more per dollar than the grand one.

**Project 1 — Run the Irkutsk pyramid balance protocol.**
*The discriminating first test:* one afternoon, one **70-cm tetrahedral pyramid**, one **magnet**, one **analytical balance** — reproduce the printed sequence and, critically, the **sign reversal**. (a) Bare pyramid → expect null. (b) Magnet pre-irradiation of the interior → does the pan lose **~30 mg avg / ~50 mg max**? (c) **Reverse the flux configuration** → does the sign flip to a **~+10–15 mg gain**? **What would prove/disprove:** a **null at proper sensitivity (mg readability, repeated runs, a blinded observer)** closes the lane's best row; a **replication of the sign reversal** reopens the entire family and gives the tradition the replication it needs. The sign knob is the whole test, so **the first build step is reconstructing the flux configuration** from the source row — until that is defined, the protocol is only half-specified.
*Effort envelope:* **~$0–150** (the analytical balance is the real item; the pyramid is cardboard/plywood; a magnet with its flux direction recorded). **One afternoon**, one operator. **Dependencies:** mg-resolution balance; a written, dated diagram of the magnet configuration used. **Safety:** none — no high voltage, no moving parts. **Maryanskyy's paradox applied:** this is the cheapest row in the shape-power lane and it teaches more per dollar than any further commentary on the patent corpus.
*Source: FAL-ru-268-1..2 (a colleague's report, `forge/status.json` 12:20Z) via Report 25 §6.*

**Project 2 — The support-memory blind draw.**
*The discriminating first test:* **imprinted vs sham photosensitive-resin supports**, visually identical by design, distinguished by a **blinded practitioner** (and, separately, by an **instrument arm**) under a **sealed-draw protocol** modelled on the Argenton structure — **pre-registered criterion, ≥N trials, zero exclusions**. **What would prove/disprove:** above-chance identification of the imprinted block reopens the water-memory/persistence family on a stable, transportable, blindable substrate; chance-level identification is the cleanest refutation the persistence thread can get, because **every reason the old water tests were hard has been removed**. **The load-bearing detail is the blinding** — the practitioner must not know which block is which, and the draw must be sealed. If the tradition's own doctrine holds that the operator is intrinsic (as the Damanhur case states outright), then **the doctrine predicts its own null under blinding**, and teaching that is part of the value.
*Effort envelope:* **~$20–100** in materials (resin, UV source, matched containers); **one weekend**, plus a cooperating practitioner to produce the imprinted set. **Dependencies:** (1) a practitioner willing to imprint supports to a stated protocol; (2) the **Héliquantix imprinting method in enough detail to reproduce** (see Scout Requests §4); (3) a pre-registration sheet written and photographed before the draw. **Note:** the corpus holds **no record that this test has ever been run** — which is exactly why it is the archive's highest-leverage untested claim.
*Source: `synthesis/2026-10-03-memory-bearing-medium.md` §4, §8.*

**Project 3 — The home-scale BD 500 soil test.**
*The discriminating first test is not the preparation — it is the assay.* **Phase 0:** bury matched weighed cotton strips in a **compost-added bed** and an **untreated bed**; retrieve after the same interval; the compost bed's strips must lose **clearly more mass**. **If the assay cannot separate them, stop — a no-difference result would be void, not a null.** Only then run A (preparation) vs **C (stirred-water sham)** vs B (control), with **≥3 matched sub-plots per arm**, on identical watering and crop.
*What would prove/disprove:* the **expected, complete result** is that the preparation is at the control and sham level in two seasons — the home-scale claim refuted, scored as a result. A **≥1.3× advantage over both control and sham, replicating in a second season**, would be genuinely surprising and justify the taller follow-up (multi-season, full preparation set, soil-respiration assay).
*Effort envelope:* **~$40–200**; **one to two growing seasons** — the preparation is seasonal (horn buried autumn, retrieved spring), so a **purchased BD 500 shortens the loop to one season**. **Dependencies:** a garden bed splittable into ≥3 matched sub-plots per arm, a **compost bed** for the positive control, and a kitchen scale at 0.1 g. **Safety:** low — a spade and manure hygiene only; family-safe. **Unblinded application or harvest weakens the run**; randomise sub-plot position and record it.
*Source: `synthesis/replication/2026-10-03-dossier-062-biodynamic-preparation-500.md` (read in full); quest card `synthesis/quest-queue/2026-10-03-biodynamic-preparation-500.md`.*

## 6. Corrections in Practice

How to teach "where we went astray" without confusing learners — and without repeating the error in the opposite direction.

- **Today's correction is about registers, not about torsion.** The community errs in **both directions** at once: skeptics cite the USSR Academy's destruction of the Pulkovo collection, and now NASU's non-conformance and the Kyiv courts, **as if institutional hostility were a null result**; believers cite the same academy's earlier tolerance of Kozyrev's observatory career, or any ministry certificate ever issued, **as if institutional approval were a positive**. The corpus now holds the counterexamples together: the 1980 suppression **did not refute** causal mechanics (that would be done, or not done, by gyroscopes and balances, not by orders), and today's non-conformance finding **tests no physical claim**. **Teach the single sentence:** *institutions certify, censor, sue, and fund; only instruments measure.*
- **Name the asymmetry when teaching today's three new validations.** Applied kinesiology, Damanhur selfica, and Pugach's Torsind are all **one-directional negatives** (a design effect, zero blinded cells, an unreplicated A/B never run). The archive has **still not carded a case of a self-correcting tradition *confirming* its own claim under a pre-registered test.** Teach that asymmetry as **a fact about what has been measured, not a verdict about the fields** — otherwise the correction becomes the traditions' own error in reverse.
- **Teach the correction with the instrument that produced it.** The closed **translator alarm** (2026-09-28) is the model case: the metric measured the **deliberate publish boundary**, not the practice of translation, so **the fix was to change the measurement, not to announce a correction** — *an alarm is only as good as its metric.* A companion case is still live and worth one clarifying word rather than an escalation: `library.counter_diag.translations_dir_published` reads **`true`** in a feed built where `translations/` exists, against a public repo that has none — **the field does not declare its measurement context** (same class as the feed's `archive_entries` reflecting the last **bake**, not the last scout **fire**).
- **Report noise once, factually, and do not re-escalate.** This issue's standing residue is unchanged from Issue 18 — synthesist stale, `graph` frozen at 09-06, `latest_finds.date` mixed-type (~13 %), the Dossier-062 collision — and **none was re-escalated**, consistent with Sandra's reading of the numbering/format class as churn that balances out and with FLAG-008. **No dossier or quest card was written by this lane, no file was renumbered, nothing was mutated.**
