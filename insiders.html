Why this page exists

It takes ~17 years for a settled result to reach a textbook, and longer when the result is inconvenient. Meanwhile the strongest challenges to the standard model of cosmology are not coming from fringe blogs — they're coming from tenured professors at Bonn, Princeton, Oxford, Harvard, Amsterdam, and Case Western. People with h-indexes of 80, inventors of the theories they now criticize, keepers of the very databases the mainstream relies on. These names are scattered across journals nobody reads together. This page collects them.

Honest framing: none of these people agree with each other — Kroupa wants MOND, Steinhardt wants a cyclic universe, Verlinde wants emergent gravity, Sarkar questions the data itself, Robitaille attacks the Sun. That's not a weakness. A paradigm in crisis produces many exits, not one. What they share: credentials the mainstream cannot dismiss, and published, testable objections. Every claim is a claim until tested — but these are the claims made by people who know how to test them.

The pillars, under insider attack

not one conspiracy — several independent, credentialed objections, each with its own evidence

Dark matter (Kroupa, McGaugh, Verlinde): the 2025 Chandrasekhar dynamical friction test — if particle halos exist, galaxy encounters must show friction slowdown; the friction isn't there. The 2024 isolated-galaxy result — potentials are logarithmic out to a megaparsec, which is MOND behavior, not halo behavior. Verlinde derives the "missing mass" from entanglement entropy with no particle at all. Three independent insiders, three attacks, one pillar.

Dark energy (Sarkar): the 2011 Nobel rests on ~3σ evidence by his group's principled re-analysis (2016, JLA sample), and the June 2026 Pantheon+ re-analysis found the acceleration signal is a dipole aligned with our own local motion — the universe may not be accelerating at all. If this holds, dark energy — 68% of the standard universe — evaporates. This is three months old. Watch it.

Inflation (Steinhardt, Loeb): the man who co-invented it published "Inflationary paradigm in trouble" — the data favor only the plateau potentials that are exponentially unlikely under inflation's own logic, and the theory predicts everything, which means it predicts nothing. His alternative — the cyclic bounce — is testable and multiverse-free.

The Sun and the CMB (Robitaille): the vault's own insider — full collection on the Cosmology page.

What this page is not

The value of collecting them here: when all eight attacks are visible at once, the pattern is hard to unsee. Every pillar of the standard model — dark matter, dark energy, inflation, even the Sun — has a credentialed critic with published evidence. The paradigm may survive; but "nobody serious disagrees" is no longer a true sentence, and this page is the proof.
