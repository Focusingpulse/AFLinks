# Jean de La Foye — *Ondes de vie, ondes de mort* (Waves of Life, Waves of Death)

*Translated dossier (FR→EN) by Forge, 2026-09-12. Sources: studylibfr.com excerpt of the 1975 Robert Laffont edition (translated passages quoted below), dartigne.blogspot.com (Bragi Bellovaque, 2017), wiredalchemy.com (2022), fr.wikipedia.org "Radiesthésie", centre-coherence.fr. The book is out of print (ISBN 2-221-00207-5, 235–244 pp., collection "Les énigmes de l'univers"); the studylibfr excerpt truncates mid–Chapter 1 — translated passages are labeled by section, and the excerpt's end point is noted.*

## Who he was

Jean de La Foye was a French **agronomic engineer and amateur radiesthésiste**. By profession he solved concrete problems in agricultural settings, and it was this pragmatic work — not esoteric curiosity — that led him into form-wave research. In his own words (Introduction, translated):

> "Put yourself in our place. By profession, we had to solve concrete problems that arose in an agricultural setting, and it was necessary to go back to origins in a very pragmatic way, each time we ran up against, as the saying goes, a bone."

He names his lineage explicitly in the dedication: the book is dedicated **"To my Master, Monsieur André de BELIZAL, who introduced me to the fascinating universe of form waves and, with admirable patience, guided my first steps"** — with thanks also to **Monsieur BARDET** (Jean-Gaston Bardet, architect and specialist of the square Hebrew script) "for the precious time he made me save by associating me with some of his research, by making me discover the possibilities of Hebrew."

French Wikipedia situates him in the chain: "Jean de la Föye continued this research and established connections with ancient Hebrew in collaboration with Gaston Bardet."

## The book's thesis (publisher's summary, translated)

> "Our era, familiarized with waves of all kinds, including those that animate transistors and TV, is mostly unaware of how deeply we are invested by other waves called 'low-energy vibrations' or 'form waves' — and above all, what an enormous influence these have on the behavior of the living and on their health: they can kill or heal, and no one escapes them. The Ancients knew these waves, and certain enigmas of vanished civilizations cannot be explained without them. Today, multiplied by unconscious techniques, they can shed light on numerous so-called 'civilization diseases.' The present book, extending the research of Léon Chaumery and André de Bélizal, has no other goal than to initiate the reader into these mysterious waves in which the roots of the living plunge, and to sketch some of their applications. Invisible forces of all times, no one should ignore them."

## Introduction (translated from the studylibfr excerpt)

> "For many of our contemporaries, the radiesthésiste is a somewhat eccentric individual who, with a ball on the end of a string, finds — or doesn't find — all sorts of things hidden from common mortals. Hence the pejorative tinge that often colors radiesthesia among people who consider themselves sensible.
>
> Yet we will have to take the risk of appearing to be that individual... The means of radiesthesia are in fact currently the only ones that allow us to penetrate this somewhat mysterious vibratory world in which the roots of the living plunge: the world of form waves.
>
> Our approach, a very simple one, will be that of the researcher for whom only facts have authority. A pox on prejudices! It is stupid to close oneself a priori in theories or systems that stifle the imagination, in artificial limits that are perhaps only the truth of a day.
>
> The pendulum will in any case only be a tool of perception, like the eye or the ear. It will make it possible to detect phenomena that still escape classical instruments, but that are quite real through their influence on health, for example, or through their possibilities in analysis. Moreover the pendulum in no way dispenses with solid common sense or sustained reasoning.
>
> This immediately eliminates the divining pendulum of pure mentalists (without pejorative intent) and so many radiesthetic or para-radiesthetic activities that have depreciated this art. The pendulum cannot do everything or resolve everything, and apart from rather rare exceptional faculties, it is unsafe to use it outside one's own professional activity, which allows controls.
>
> Which does not prevent us from deepening certain laws, notably those of the low-energy vibrations that interest us here and that are only one of the ignored branches of physics.
>
> The basis of what follows, even if unexpressed, will be the **'Vital Field'** — the invisible warp of this living tapestry that is nature, of which we are on earth an integral part. Once one has understood this field, one easily follows the idea of Pasteur, taken up in our day by Popper, namely that it is not matter that engendered life in the course of its evolution (one wonders, moreover, by what 'motor'), but, inversely, Life that shaped over time, by act of the Creator, the conditions of its successive manifestations, from mineral to vegetable, from vegetable to animal... We will go no further: we exclude in principle the domain of Spirit, which escapes all experimental control, all mastery, even though it is what conditions the whole. We will not cross the impassable threshold of mystery."

## Chapter 1: Low-energy vibrations called "form waves" (translated)

> "To enter directly into the heart of the subject, here is a little story.
>
> Some years ago, following research on the vibrations of plants, M. de Bélizal had attached, in the full month of July, a special wooden form 30 centimeters long to the trunk of a young, vigorous apple tree, binding the whole with string. He had taken care to invert the natural polarities of the form relative to the normal.
>
> Three weeks later, the apple tree had lost its leaves.
>
> This very simple and reproducible experiment shows you the power and the effectiveness of forms wielded with knowledge. 'Bits of wood!' you will say. Yes, bits of wood — but which, depending on the case, can kill or heal.
>
> The whole exposition that follows aims only to deepen the why and the how of such phenomena, which belong to the domain of form waves.
>
> The expression **'FORM WAVE'** was created in radiesthesia by MM. Chaumery and de Bélizal following research we will approach later. An outline of the notion it represents will become clearer as we advance in our exploration, but already let us become accustomed to the importance of form waves in everyday life.
>
> Invisible, they are there surrounding us, investing us, penetrating the least fibers of our body. Everything that has form, everything that gives off energy — the subsoil, buildings, familiar furniture, objects carried on one's person, electrical appliances, food, and I could go on — all of it emits form waves on which our health, our behavior, our well-being partially depend. Some are beneficial, others harmful. How to keep the former, eliminate the latter? If you will, let us learn together to know them, to handle them, to protect ourselves from them when needed. The subject is vast, sometimes difficult, but it deserves effort, especially at the start, to enter into the intended perspective.
>
> Are we more foolish than others? For this effort, others have made it... thousands of years before us... The people who built dolmens and raised menhirs had notions of form waves. The builders of the pyramids of Egypt had mastery of them. When one analyzes the plastic arts of dead civilizations, sometimes very distant from one another in time and space, one cannot help but evoke in nearly all of them a certain science of what we call form waves — a science whose degree of mastery lets one evaluate the civilization's level. For the study of form waves is within man's reach without demanding the complex and sophisticated apparatus of the modern sciences. It takes only a certain sensitivity, aided or not by radiesthetic procedures, a curious and organized brain in the service of gifts of observation, and a relatively simple geometry.
>
> But this science — for it is a true science, experimental, having as its object reproducible phenomena — was certainly formerly the appanage of a small number. We cannot imagine placed in all hands these means of action on the living that are selected and directed form waves, capable of acting at practically unlimited distance, and therefore factors of power.
>
> These knowledges, probably as old as humanity, have come down to us deformed, in scraps, drained with profits and losses at the mercy of civilizations, forming a part of what is now conventionally called Tradition (with a capital T), whose esotericism poorly conceals enormous stretches of ignorance.
>
> It took the genius intuition of MM. Chaumery and de Bélizal to find again the lost entrance of the Enchanted Palace, which, under the harsh lighting of severe experimentation, gains in solidity what it loses in intellectual phantasmagoria."

### The pendulum (translated)

> "An object of suspicion on the part of many scientists, the pendulum is here the obligatory tool as long as form waves remain at their current low powers and no replacement control independent of the human body has been found. We must therefore make do with the pendulum and get the best from its technique.
>
> What is a pendulum? Roughly, it is a mass varying from a few grams to a few tens of grams suspended from a flexible string. This string is held between thumb and forefinger, palm of the hand facing down, the wrist keeping its suppleness.
>
> ... In certain places, if we are sensitive, the pendulum will leave its plane of swinging and — if we do not oppose it — will begin to turn, the string describing a cone. This happens despite us if we are sensitive, for certain people can walk about indefinitely without the pendulum ceasing to swing. The pendulum is like the violin and its degrees of virtuosity...
>
> The search for water is in any case an excellent school for all beginning radiesthésistes; it puts them in contact with a controllable physical reality.
>
> Let us perfect the operations. Take in hand a pendulum whose mass is a sphere of a well-defined color. Swing this pendulum above cloths or papers of different colors, one of which is exactly that of the pendulum. It will swing over all the colors except one — the one identical to it, which triggers the gyration. Why? Apparently, there is resonance. We will not dwell on the hypotheses advanced to explain the physiological reasons that trigger the pendulum's movements. We will content ourselves with saying: everything happens as if the pendulum acted as an amplifier of the hand's reflexes. By its form, its matter, its color, etc., it enters into resonance with a phenomenon exterior to it, and this resonance acts on the nervous system, which triggers the reflex of setting it in motion.
>
> Instead of a colored pendulum, we could have used a so-called 'neutral' pendulum, capable of reacting to anything, and held tightly in the free hand a piece of colored cloth. The swung pendulum can only turn over the cloth from which the piece was taken. This piece then takes the name of **'witness'**, which is, in short, a relay of resonance between the 'subject-cloth' and the pendulum.
>
> A good number of radiesthésistes operate with a neutral pendulum and obtain resonance with the most diverse phenomena by mental accord alone. Without denying their real performances, we will not follow them on this terrain — at the risk of making them cry out in horror. On the contrary, we will seek to eliminate as much as possible the mental influence while remaining present to what we are doing. We will seek a physical resonance by the passage from swinging to gyration, without worrying about the direction of gyration. For us, the pendulum turns or it does not turn — that is all."

### The colors on the sphere (translated — Chaumery & de Bélizal's discovery)

> "How did these gentlemen discover what they called 'form waves'?
>
> They had the idea of placing a sphere of natural wood on an independent support, taking spherical colored pendulums, and using what is called a 'searching point' — that is, quite simply, a small copper rod filed to a point at one end. Swinging a blue-sphere pendulum in one hand, for example, they moved the searching point as an 'antenna' over the wooden sphere with the other hand, looking for the places on the sphere that triggered the gyration of the blue pendulum. They proceeded in the same way with pendulums of other colors, and found the following.
>
> Each color localizes on a spiral running from a (+) pole to a (−) pole of the sphere. The different spirals intersect on the equator at two diametrically opposite points, one red, the other violet.
>
> Curious thing: the spirals follow the sun and move on the sphere around the fixed axis of the poles, describing the complete circle in the course of a day of 24 hours. This property was not without causing some worries to MM. Chaumery and de Bélizal when they wanted to create apparatus based on the colors detected on the sphere. They had to find tricks of artificial fixation described in their work: *Essai de Radiesthésie Vibratoire*.
>
> The (+) pole, which makes a neutral pendulum turn clockwise, corresponds to normal green and was called **Positive Green**. The (−) pole, which gives an inverse gyration to the neutral pendulum, was named **Negative Green** by simple opposition to the other — for it corresponds to no known color.
>
> A deeper study differentiated two perpendicular meridians of the sphere and led to two identical 'spectra' from the color point of view (including the invisible ones that 'fill the holes') but in two 'phases': one called **'Magnetic'**, giving the neutral pendulum a positive gyration; the other called **'Electric'**, giving the neutral pendulum a negative gyration.
>
> On these two meridians and on the equator, the fundamental colors are distributed at equal intervals of 30 sexagesimal degrees from one another — 'Magnetic' or 'Electric' on their respective meridians, 'Electromagnetic' on the equator.
>
> The spectrum and the abbreviations of the color names in the two Magnetic and Electric phases: Negative Green (VN), Black (N), Infrared (IR), Red (R), Orange (O), Yellow (J), Positive Green (V+), Blue (B), Indigo (I), Violet (Vi), Ultraviolet (UV), White (Be). One calls 'Symmetric' colors those symmetric with respect to the V+, V− axis (e.g. Be and N, I and O). One calls opposed colors those diametrically opposed (e.g. Be and J, UV and O)."

### The Universal Pendulum (translated)

> "The study of the preceding sphere gave birth to a spherical wooden pendulum that is only its fixed reproduction. It was called the **Universal Pendulum**, being capable of detecting all possible form vibrations present at all points of the sphere. To this end it is furnished with a half-circle of rigid copper wire axed on the line of the poles, so that the point of attachment of the suspension string, sliding on this half-circle, is vertical to the chosen color marked on the sphere. A belt of copper wire with a sliding index fits in an equatorial groove, so that at the chosen setting the index is under the copper half-circle. The authors' works give a detailed description and a precise user's manual for this pendulum.
>
> Now this pendulum, once set, can replace the colored spherical pendulums, reacts to the colors. But it also enters into resonance with other phenomena, and in particular with geometric forms — hence the name **Form Waves** given to these emissions detected on forms."

## La Foye's own contributions (from the secondary sources)

The tablet des cercles réciproques (Reciprocal Circles Board) was developed in the 1960s–70s. Bellovaque (dartigne.blogspot.com, translated):

> "In the 1970s, it was Jean de La Foye, French agronomic engineer and radiesthésiste, who — in contact with Count André de Bélizal — developed this emitting board. It works thanks to four concepts developed by M. de La Foye:
> 1. The notion of **color emissions of a polarized body** (12 non-manifested colors on the circle's radius and 24 manifested colors on the double of the radius), via the study of the sphere, the equatorial disk, and the study of the vital field;
> 2. The presence of **guiding axes of the field of form emissions**, via the square Hebrew Tetragrammaton 'I AM' representing the name of God;
> 3. The **law of compensation**;
> 4. The **vital field**."

**The 24 manifested colors.** La Foye took the pioneers' sphere work onto the circumference of a tree trunk: he found the 12 colors again on the trunk's diameter, and discovered that the colors **double up to twice the trunk's radius** — 12 so-called "Electric" colors and 12 "Magnetic" colors, of opposed polarities. He also found that a nail driven into the trunk selects the color present at that point and projects it onto the south (terrestrial magnetic) axis, at 180° of a circle whose 0° is magnetic north −5°. To decouple the colors' course from the sun, he grooved a wooden sphere equatorially and fixed a selection ring (like the trunk's nail), creating a selective color emitter: the **equatorial pendulum** — then, by the same method, the equatorial disk, and finally the Reciprocal Circles Board.

**The guiding axes.** Stuck in a "color" language he found too restrictive, La Foye was unblocked by Jean-Gaston Bardet, architect and specialist of the square Hebrew script — a script which, per Bardet, has "the capacity to reproduce the energy developed by the breath of the pronunciation," creating a witness-effect between the written word and the object of research. When La Foye measured each letter of the Hebrew Tetragrammaton radiesthetically against the color emissions of the sphere or disk, he could draw **axes directeurs du champ de formes** — "guiding axes of the field of forms" — on the circle, with the capacity to permanently balance the emitter's emissions. Engraved on a board, these axes keep it from ever saturating and isolate it from exterior influence, "creating a true little universe sufficient to itself: a microcosm."

**The law of compensation.** A derivative of the law of similars: draw a geometric form with an axis of symmetry (a circle), place a witness on one half — its "ghost" is detectable on the other half.

**The vital field.** The agricultural engineer's contribution: the pendulum-detectable signature of the living is manifested by **series of circles repeating fractally and duplicating**, like cell division; where the living crystallizes in circles, the physical field of matter overlaps in **hexagonal fractals**. (Wired Alchemy's English account adds: "The life force crystallizes in circles, and matter overlaps in the form of hexagonal fractals. Matter contains life, just as the circles are contained within the hexagram.")

## The Reciprocal Circles Board itself

A rectangular wooden board, minimum 2 cm thick, of good-density material (mass favors the emission radius — as with megaliths). Three interlocking circles: the **north circle** (origin, oriented to magnetic north −5° = 355°; a needle through its center selects the color from the 24-color dial), the **center circle** (the "microprocessor" or "heart," from which the guiding axes of the form field depart and balance the board; amplifiers may be placed here), and the **south circle** (which, per the law of compensation, emits perpendicularly at its center the emission selected in the north — place a photo witness for remote treatment, an object to charge, or a carrier substance to copy a vibration onto; a copper wire can guide the emission to a target or to another board's north circle, making a series connection).

Two hypotheses for the mechanism are recorded by Bellovaque (translated): the **scientific** hypothesis of Dr. Laurent Souriau — locally solar-generated neutrinos acquiring an electromagnetic charge on contact with the Earth's field, becoming monopoles attracted by geometric forms acting as accumulators (the pyramid's mummifying effect as the known example); and the **"esoteric"** hypothesis of Jacques Ravatin's Groupe Totaris — the local coherence field (our everyday reality) and a pre-existing global coherence field at the origin of "unexplained" phenomena, certain objects being partially immersed in both. *(This is the same Ravatin documented in the 2026-09-12 Ravatin dossier — La Foye and Ravatin are two links of the same post-Chaumery chain.)*

Bellovaque notes the modern POM (polyoxymethylene) version: 2.5 kg, 3.3 cm thick, 30 × 18.6 cm ("close to the golden number"), measured at 24,000 Bovis units and 75% vibratory density vs. the oak board's 55% — "a gain of 20% in emission capacity."

## Place in the French school

La Foye is the fourth link of the physical-radiesthesia chain: **Chaumery & de Bélizal** (sphere, spectrum, Universal Pendulum, negative green, the 1936 patent) → **Enel** (Omega ray, form emissions in Egypt) → **de Belizal & Morel** (post-Chaumery consolidation, *Physique Micro-Vibratoire*) → **La Foye** (24 manifested colors, vital field, Hebrew guiding axes, the Board) → **Ravatin** (coherence-field reframing: form waves are not waves but emergence phenomena). His book is the primary source for the "waves of life / waves of death" distinction — the Magnetic colors (western hemisphere) beneficial, the Electric colors discharging — and for the warning that these instruments, misused, harm the operator (radiodermatitis).
