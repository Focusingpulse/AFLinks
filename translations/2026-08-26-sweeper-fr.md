## Abstract (English Translation)

This repository presents two fundamental works establishing the theoretical framework of the Wave Universe Theory (TUO), a novel unified approach to vacuum physics and gravitation.

### Part I. Classical Formalism and the Vacuum Tension Field (Tv)

The first article — *"Classical Formalisms and Correspondence with the TUO: From Maxwell and Einstein to the Vacuum Tension Field Tv"* — establishes the mathematical structure of the TUO by introducing the scalar Vacuum Tension Field (Tv). The dynamics of Tv are derived from a covariant Lagrangian (L_TUO), allowing the deduction of field equations via the principle of least action. The formal correspondence with classical equations (Maxwell and Einstein) is detailed, showing that the Vacuum Energy-Momentum Tensor (T_μν(v)) replaces the cosmological constant (Λg_μν) with a dynamic and local term. The enriched mathematical demonstrations — Friedmann-TUO equations, linear perturbations, Noether current — prove the complete integration of the TUO into the framework of General Relativity and provide the necessary tools for cosmological modeling of dark energy.

**Key mathematical objects:**
- **Tv** — scalar vacuum tension field (the fundamental field of the theory)
- **L_TUO** — covariant Lagrangian from which field equations are derived via least action
- **T_μν(v)** — vacuum energy-momentum tensor, replacing the cosmological constant Λg_μν with a dynamic, local term
- **Friedmann-TUO equations** — modified Friedmann equations incorporating Tv dynamics
- **Noether current** — conserved quantity derived from the symmetry of L_TUO
- **Linear perturbations** — stability analysis of the TUO field equations

### Part II. Conceptual Bridge and the Origin of Tv

The second article — *"From Confined Electromagnetic Field to Cosmic Vacuum Tension: A Bridge between Michaud's Theory and the TUO?"* — provides the physical foundation for the Tv field. It proposes that vacuum tension is not an arbitrary fundamental field, but the macroscopic, homogenized result of the internal electromagnetic (EM) energy of elementary particles (Michaud's model). This conceptual bridge locally derives the gravitational field (g) as the negative gradient of vacuum tension:

> **g = −∇T_v**

The article thus demonstrates the transition from confined electromagnetism to gravitation, justifying the physical interpretation of Tv as a geometric state property of the medium — fundamental for unification.

**Key physical claims:**
- Vacuum tension Tv emerges from confined electromagnetic energy inside elementary particles (Michaud model)
- Gravity is not fundamental but derived: g = −∇T_v (gravitational field as gradient of vacuum tension)
- Tv is a geometric state property of the medium, not an arbitrary scalar field
- The cosmological constant Λ is replaced by a dynamic, spatially-varying term — making dark energy a local phenomenon rather than a universal constant

## Broader TUO Framework (from related papers in the series)

The TUO is an evolving multi-paper framework. Based on the related deposits:

1. **Electron as Q-ball soliton** (zenodo.17315452): The electron is modeled as a stable 3D charged soliton (Q-ball) of the Tv field. A sextic nonlinear Lagrangian is constructed; the 3D radial equation, virial identity, Coleman existence criterion, and orbital stability condition (dQ/dω < 0) are derived. Spatial scale = reduced Compton wavelength λ̄_e = ℏ/(m_e c); confined energy E_sol = m_e c². Numerical validation via shooting method confirms asymptotic decay ρ(r) ~ e^(−r/R)/r with R ≈ λ̄_e.

2. **Maillot Invariant I** (zenodo.17270974): A universal invariant I = V_f³/v³ is proposed, claimed constant across particle spectra (photon, electron, muon, neutrino, quarks, proton, neutron). Mass is reframed as the stability of a wave node in Tv, not a local entity. EM and strong/weak interactions are resonance phenomena governed by harmonics of the same Tv field.

3. **Multi-scale unification** (zenodo.17587234): The TUO is extended to four regimes — microphysics (mass scales, nuclear binding), early cosmology (TUO-BBN with ΔN_eff ≤ 0.3), strong gravity (horizon thermodynamics, singularities replaced by stable solitons), and a falsifiable multi-domain synthesis (χ²_TUO/χ²_ΛCDM < 1).

4. **Unification with quantum mechanics** (zenodo.14965717): Earlier deposit framing TUO as a novel approach to unifying gravitation and quantum mechanics.

## Assessment

**Strengths:**
- Mathematically structured: covariant Lagrangian, derived field equations, Friedmann-TUO equations, Noether current — not hand-waving
- Attempts to replace the cosmological constant with a dynamic local term (addresses a real problem in ΛCDM)
- Multi-scale ambition: from particle physics to cosmology
- Falsifiability framework proposed (χ² comparison with ΛCDM, BBN constraints)
- Published on Zenodo (CERN-hosted), open access

**Limitations:**
- 0 citations across all papers in the series (as of Aug 2026)
- No peer review indicated (Zenodo deposits, not journal publications)
- The Michaud model (confined EM energy → vacuum tension) is itself not mainstream-validated
- The Maillot Invariant I = V_f³/v³ is postulated, not derived from first principles
- No experimental predictions tested against data yet (BBN constraints stated but not demonstrated in the abstracts)
- The claim g = −∇T_v, while elegant, would need to reproduce all tested GR predictions (gravitational lensing, perihelion precession, gravitational waves, etc.)

**Classification:**
- Work type: Theoretical (unification framework)
- Practical applicability: Conceptual — no buildable device; value is as a mathematical framework
- Verification status: Unverified — no experimental validation, no peer review, zero citations
- Significance: Ambitious alternative to ΛCDM with a real mathematical structure; too early to assess viability
