## 1. Biography (ORAEDES encyclopedia, translated)

**General data.** Michel Vladimirovitch Skariatine — "Enel" — born 6 May 1883 in Saint Petersburg, Russia; died 1963 (aged about 70) in Switzerland; buried at the cemetery of Glion. Period: 19th–20th century. Places: Russia, France, Egypt, Switzerland. Disciplines: occultism, astrology, Qabalah, radiesthesia, Egyptology. Current: neo-occultism.

**Relations and circle.** Collaborators: Colonel de Rochas, Chaumery, de Bélizal, Howard Carter, Morel, Gaston Maspero, Maître Philippe, Papus, Piobb. Influence on: Guy Thieux.

**History.** Related to the Romanov family, Enel first embraced a military career, commanding the Regiment of Chevaliers-Gardes of the Imperial Guard then in the service of Nicholas II. He met Philippe and Papus during their voyages in Russia. He subsequently came to live in Toulon, in 1917, following the revolutionary events in Russia which overturned his social and economic position.

An Egyptologist, he pursued research on-site between 1931 and 1953, took part in archaeological excavations in Egypt with Howard Carter, and frequented Maspero.

**Thought.** A polyglot, he mastered numerous dead languages — Latin, ancient Greek, biblical Hebrew, Sanskrit. He had many celebrated collaborators in the domain of radiesthesia and radionics, among them Rochas and Lakhovsky. He took a close interest in Lakhovsky's oscillator, and from 1953 to 1963 he used it in the course of cures.

**Selected works.** *La Langue sacrée* (1932); *Trilogie de la Rota* (1934); *Les Origines de la "Genèse" et l'enseignement des temples de l'ancienne Égypte* (1935); *Gnomologie* (1959); *Le Mystère de la vie et de la mort d'après l'enseignement des temples de l'ancienne Égypte* (1966).

**Citations (translated).**

In both cases, a moral discipline, a complete empire over oneself, and an absolute purity are indispensable to attain the goal one has set for oneself." — *Gnomologie*

## 2. The Wikisage record (translated from Dutch)

Enel, pseudonym of the French-Russian author Michel Vladimirovich Skariatine (Saint Petersburg, 6 May 1883 [Wikisage gives 1893 — note the discrepancy; ORAEDES and the Russian Wikipedia give 6 May 1883] – Glion, 1963), was a colonel of the Russian imperial army, of the Skaryatin family. He was an erudite, occultist, and researcher. Skariatine was known above all as a researcher of Kabbalah and as an Egyptologist who lived twenty years in Egypt.

From the Russian Revolution onward, Enel settled in France, where he developed into a capable amateur Egyptologist. His master in Egyptology was Gaston Maspero. He further devoted himself to radionics. He was likewise a specialist in astrology, Kabbalah, and the work of Antoine Fabre d'Olivet. In 1931 he moved to Egypt, and in 1953 he settled definitively in Switzerland, where for a time he worked as a researcher for Nestlé. Although a discreet person, we know through his publisher that he knew the great French occultists and parapsychologists of his time, among others Papus, Alexandre Saint-Yves d'Alveydre, Albert de Rochas, Charles Lancelin, and Master Philippe.

**Works (radiesthesia line):**
- *Cures magiques au XXe siècle* (Toulon, Cabasson, 1929; Paris, Omnium Littéraire, 1959)
- *Radiations des formes et Cancer* (Le Caire, Al-Maaref, 1951; Paris, Dangles, 1959)
- *Premiers pas en Radiesthésie thérapeutique* (Le Caire, Al-Maaref, 1949; Paris, Omnium Littéraire, 1958)
- *Traitement à distance par radiations* (Paris, Dangles, 1959)
- *Mystères de la vie et de la mort*

## 3. The Energeia 2025 reissue (publisher's catalog, translated)

*Radiations des formes et cancer*, Enel, Energeia, 158 pages, ISBN 9791093492728, 10 June 2025 — the current in-print edition.

Enel was one of the most eminent practitioners and authors of the twentieth century in matters of therapeutic radiesthesia and esotericism. In *Radiations des formes et cancer* — originally published in Egypt by Éditions Al-Maaref, following his on-site work from the interwar years to the 1950s — Enel unveiled his own methods and procedures for using radiesthetic rays and "ondes de formes" [form waves], notably through the exposition of different types of experiments he had carried out with therapeutic aims, and precisely in the face of grave health disorders encountered by certain of his consultants.

The ALIP catalog adds: Enel is the pseudonym of Michel Vladimirovich Skariatine. A polyglot, he specialized in Latin, Greek, Sanskrit, and Hebrew. He lived in France from 1917 onward. He became an eminent Egyptologist, met Maspero, and settled in Cairo in 1931. In 1953 he settled definitively in Switzerland, at Glion-sur-Montreux. He was influenced by the works of Fabre d'Olivet and sought the [initiatic] tradition in Egypt. He was an eminent specialist in Kabbalah, astrology, radiesthesia, and the detection of form waves. A collaborator of Colonel de Rochas, he was an assiduous seeker of incontestable proofs of what is called "unreal." A half-century of study of ancient civilizations allowed Enel to make certain discoveries which would lay a solid base for his experiments, orienting him along the path of the ancient initiates.

## 4. The Omega ray: Enel and the negative green (elishean777.com dossier, translated)

It was Enel, in 1940–1950, who used the principle of Chaumery's pendulum to study the energies of the tombs of Christian saints in Egypt.

Enel discovered that [all the tombs] emanated a certain quality of vibration in the zone of what we call "Negative Green."

Negative Green is a quality of carrier wave which has very powerful communication properties at every level of nature's energies, and is a door onto other dimensions.

> **Enel named this quality of energy "Omega."**

In the qualitative color scale of radiesthesia, Omega belongs to the zone of Horizontal Negative Green, in the grey zone between white and black, opposite to green. Enel concluded that the presence of this Omega energy indicates the presence of a spiritual energy. [Ibrahim Karim, on whose BioGeometry this dossier builds, confirms: "Their work and that of Enel are a reference in the world of so-called scientific radiesthesia."]

**Translation notes (Forge):**
- The Chaumery–de Bélizal "negative green" (V−) was patented in April 1936 (FR 816,132 — see my translation 2026-09-11-chaumery-belizal-patent-fr816132-fr-en.md). Enel's contribution was to apply the Chaumery pendulum method to the tombs of Christian saints in Egypt and to identify the *horizontal* component of V− as a marker of spiritual energy, renaming it Omega. On the Universal Pendulum's electromagnetic meridian, Omega sits at grade 395, between White (366.5) and V− (0/400) — the sector de Bélizal associated with radioactivity, and which the school's own usage table prescribed (with Rho) for tuberculosis and similar conditions.
- The Omega/negative-green lineage runs: Chaumery & de Bélizal (1930s, the spectrum and the patent) → Enel (1940s–50s, Omega as spiritual-energy marker on saints' tombs) → Jean de la Foye (*Ondes de vie, ondes de mort*) → Ibrahim Karim (BioGeometry, BG3 = horizontal negative green + higher harmonic of ultraviolet + higher harmonic of gold). The elishean777 dossier documents the Karim end of this chain in detail (sacred monuments using horizontal V−; the pyramid as natural mummifier; the "cancer points" of Gustav Freiherr von Pohl; the BG3 quality in power places from Chartres to the Well of Zamzam).
- Stéphane Cardinaux's critique (quoted in the dossier, from *Géométries sacrées*, p. 28): the Chaumery–de Bélizal spectrum inserts black, white, and "negative green" between scarlet-red and purple-violet. "They drew and named these colors for convenience, but never said that 'negative green' was really green in color... As a vibration, 'negative green' exists indeed and well — but it is... white!" — the color of the 7th chakra and of the human soul in anthroposophy. The pseudo-colors only differentiate what happens between magenta and purple.
- The dossier's own skeptical frame: the vertical component of V− is the harmful one ("When the vertical aspect of Negative Green is present, it disturbs the energy of our cells" — Karim); the horizontal is the beneficial carrier. No instrument has ever measured either (de Bélizal & Morel concede this explicitly — see 2026-09-10-de-belizal-morel-physique-micro-vibratoire-fr-en.md).

## 5. The Carnets inédits (Guy Thieux edition, ibacom.fr catalog, translated)

The posthumous trilogy, edited by Guy Thieux — geophysicist, Enel's literary depository, and his interview partner 1960–1963 — from manuscripts written between 1930 and 1950, when Enel lived in Cairo under the protection of King Farouk:

- **Tome I: Le Monde Astral et l'Occultisme** — the esoteric constitution of man, illustrated by the experiments of the pioneer occultists (Dr. Osty, the Durville brothers, Colonel de Rochas, Leadbeater, Commandant Darget, Professor Richet, Warcollier, Crookes): astral body, chakras, magical chains, egregores, clairvoyance, radiesthesia, spiritism, auras, dédoublement. Second part: the doors of Initiation.
- **Tome II: Science égyptienne et Médecine de l'Astral** — thirty-five years of study of the Hebrew Cabala applied to decoding the symbolic sense of the hieroglyphs beyond their phonetic and figurative meaning; the funeral rites of ancient Egypt (mummification, the secret name, the doubling, the Opening of the Mouth); the Ka and the Ba. Enel worked over thirty years with the greatest Egyptologists and archaeologists of the era, notably Howard Carter's team, and held that Champollion had suspected the hidden sense of the hieroglyphs without having the time to discover it.
- **Tome III: Radiesthésie thérapeutique** — geobiology, form waves and their therapeutic implications: gravitational colors, radiant-form apparatus, essences of woods and their use with Enel's unknown radiation, ritual instruments (the Six Ritual Instruments of the Emperor of China), force fields, Paracelsus's Lilium, potable gold, resonator oscillators, telluric factors, natural magnetic and electrotelluric fields, the maquettes built in continuation of the work of Georges Lakhovsky (the "OLOM," Oscillateur à Longueur d'Ondes Multiples) and of Enel, and a chapter on Henri Mager: the dowsers and their procedures, pendulums and automatic apparatus. With a photo notebook of apparatus built by Enel's own hand.

The Thieux trilogy is the primary-source record of Enel's Egyptian-radiesthesia synthesis: the Cabala-decoded hieroglyphs of Tome II are the theory of forms that Tome III's apparatus and protocols operationalize.

## 6. Where Enel sits in the French school (Forge's synthesis)

The founder series now stands: Bouly (the name, the AAR), Mermet (the method), Viré (the institutional science), Henry de France (the press and the instruments), Chaumery (the vocabulary and the patent), de Bélizal (the theory), Morel (the consolidation), the Servranx brothers (the diffusion), Turenne (the instrument bench), Bovis (the scale). **Enel is the bridge figure** — the one who carried the Chaumery–de Bélizal spectrum out of the Paris laboratory into the field (Egyptian tombs), gave its most contested band a name that stuck (Omega), and connected it to the Lakhovsky MWO line (the only instrument in the school's orbit with a genuine, if contested, electrobiology pedigree). His *Radiations des formes et cancer* (1951) is the school's therapeutic manifesto — and its most dangerous claim: cancer treated at a distance by form radiations, on testimonial evidence only. The honest-bench record (Rendu 1935, Munich 1986–88, OZ 2010) stands against it uniformly.
