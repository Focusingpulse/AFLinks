## Presentation of the Reciprocal Circles Board

*by Bragi Bellovaque*

The "Reciprocal Circles" board is an emitter of emissions due to forms, developed in the 1960s–1970s by Mr. Jean de La Foye, a French agricultural engineer and amateur radiesthesist. The apparatus is described in his book *Ondes de vie, ondes de mort* ("Waves of Life, Waves of Death") as:

- a radionics tool (sending emissions over a distance),
- an ambiance rebalancer (geobiology),
- but above all a formidable instrument for experimenting with "emissions due to forms."

This apparatus works on the energetic principle of emissions due to forms — strange emissions that are also found on megaliths, Egyptian pyramids, and other Moai...

### How do emissions due to forms work?

It would be a lie to say that what follows is a truth recognized by all. I will therefore speak of working hypotheses, since to this day no autonomous measuring instrument has managed to detect these emissions through quantified results recognized by science — this despite the uncontested effects of these strange "low-energy" emissions on biological processes. Only the water-diviner, and more generally the radiesthesist with his pendulums, is currently capable of sensing these things.

There are two ways of explaining these emissions.

**1/ The scientific hypothesis.**

This is the hypothesis of Doctor Laurent Souriau, whom I met personally. According to him, these emissions would originate in particles called neutrinos, generated locally by our sun (and more generally by other distant stars). On contact with the Earth's electromagnetic field, a very small fraction of these neutrinos would acquire an electromagnetic charge and become monopole neutrinos, carrying either a positive or a negative charge. These particles would then have the capacity to be attracted by their entire environment, and more particularly by certain geometric forms acting as accumulators, making it possible to generate unexplained phenomena. The pyramid is the best-known example. Its mummifying effect cannot be denied!

Our board might have the capacity to capture these magnetic monopoles, to focus them, and to direct them toward a precise point.

**2/ The "esoteric" hypothesis.**

This is the viewpoint of the Totaris Group, founded by the scientist and mathematician Jacques Ravatin. According to him, there exists: a local coherence field, with its own laws — likewise local (this is simply our everyday reality with its familiar reference points: the flow of time, for example, or the speed of light) — and a global coherence field, pre-existing ours, which would be the origin of so-called unexplained phenomena. These are our ghosts, UFOs, and other phenomena in which the observer loses his local bearings and has the impression of literally entering into a waking dream. Jacques Ravatin speaks of the flight of reference points by "cumulo-decal" arithmetic. To illustrate this, he took the example of an observer standing on a railway track who is asked to count the sleepers. At some point, the sleepers merge at the horizon into a single point. The reference points flee, and one enters the global (it is like placing two mirrors facing each other...). Certain objects would be capable of being partially immersed in these two realities, or coherence fields. This may be the case with our board.

### How does the board work?

In the 1970s, it was Jean de La Foye — French agricultural engineer and radiesthesist, in contact with André de Belizal — who developed this emitting board. It works through four concepts developed by Mr. de La Foye:

1. the notion of colour emissions of a polarized body (12 non-manifested colours on the circle's radius, and 24 manifested colours on the double radius), via the study of the sphere, the equatorial disk, and the study of the vital field;
2. the presence of directing axes of the field of emissions due to forms, via the square Hebrew Tetragrammaton "I AM," representing the name of God;
3. the law of compensation;
4. the vital field.

**1/ The 12 non-manifested colours of the Belizal field, and the 24 manifested colours**

It all began when the Count de Belizal and Mr. Chaumery studied the strange characteristics of an antique Egyptian ring with astonishing vibratory properties (the "Atlantean ring") and the study of a strange object of the same provenance (the Egyptian pendulum). The two amateur researchers realized that certain objects with specific (geometric) forms had the capacity to charge themselves with unknown emissions, capable of acting on their near and far environment (they themselves took up the work of Enel). In short: ancient civilizations knew of a language between Matter and Spirit.

To start on solid foundations, they went back to the study of the simplest geometric form there is: the sphere. Armed with a pendulum and a stylus, they measured the emissions present on a wooden ball and discovered that it was the seat of emissions which, by mental convention, they named "colours." As an anecdote: it was with little scraps of coloured cloth, used as witnesses, that they found these emissions...

An important fact: the 12 emissions, called colours, seemed to move over the sphere, one against the next, following the course of the sun.

Mr. de la Foye took up these pioneers' discoveries and studied these emissions using a tree trunk as his support. There again, he found the 12 colours on the trunk's diameter, but noticed two things: the colours seemed to double beyond the trunk's diameter, out to twice the trunk's radius. He found 12 colours called, by mental convention, "electric," and 12 colours called "magnetic" — in short, of opposite polarities. He also found that if he drove a nail into the trunk, the colour present at that nail was selected and projected onto the south axis (the Earth's magnetic south), at 180° of a circle whose 0° would be magnetic north minus 5°. Later, Mr. de la Foye had the idea of uncoupling the course of these colours from the sun, by grooving the wooden sphere along an equator (directing axes) and fixing a selection ring onto it (like the nail in the tree). This created a selective colour emitter: the equatorial pendulum.

Using the same method, he then created a disk (the equatorial disk), and then the Reciprocal Circles board.

**2/ The directing axes of the field of emissions due to forms**

At the start of his research, Mr. de La Foye followed the work of his mentors, but at one point he found himself stuck in this "colour" language, which was too restrictive (we were in almost "optical" experimentation, if I may put it that way, though with nothing visual in the end). What he lacked was a measurement procedure that could give a qualitative meaning to these measurements. This is where he was helped by Mr. Jean-Gaston Bardet, an architect and also a specialist in square Hebrew script. This script, like other ancient scripts, has the capacity to reproduce the energy developed by the breath of pronunciation. In short: if you write in square Hebrew "here shall spring water," and you glue that word onto a pendulum, you automatically parameterize your search, because according to Mr. Bardet, a witness effect is created between the word and the object of the research. This is how Mr. Bardet unblocked Mr. de La Foye's research.

When Mr. de La Foye studied the Hebrew Tetragrammaton giving the nature "of God," he realized that if one measured radiesthetically each of the letters, relating them to the colour emissions of the sphere or the emitting disk, one could trace directing axes on the circle that had the capacity to permanently balance the emissions of the sphere or disk: what he called the "directing axes of the field of forms"!

These axes, traced on an emitter, have the capacity to never let that emitter saturate, and to isolate it from all outside influence — creating a true little universe sufficient unto itself: a microcosm.

**3/ The law of compensation**

The law of compensation is a derivative of the law of similars used in operative magic. In short: Mr. de la Foye realized that when drawing a geometric form with an axis of symmetry, such as a circle, if one placed a witness object on one half-circle, its "ghost" could be detected on the other half. I leave you to meditate on the practical applications of this phenomenon...

**4/ The vital field**

Mr. de La Foye, being a good agricultural engineer, had also studied the emissions generated by the Living. He had realized that the signature of the living, detectable with a pendulum, was manifested by series of circles repeating fractally and duplicating themselves, a little in the manner of cellular multiplication. Where the living seemed to crystallize through circles, the physical field of matter, for its part, seemed to overlap it — but in the form of hexagonal fractals.

The board is thus the synthesis of these principles!

### Construction

It consists of a rectangular wooden board, minimum thickness 2 cm, cut from a material of good density to favour the weight of the emitter, and therefore its emission diameter.

The holes are the most important part, because — like a card read on a barrel organ — they carry the information, transforming the board from an inert object into an object with new characteristics (absorption of magnetic monopoles, or creation of a link with the global). The holes will not only mark the existence of the three circles, but trigger the object's capacity for accumulation and emission.

The grooved vertical and horizontal axes also add a plus: by creating a link between the main holes, we amplify the quality of emission. Finally, the directing axes of the field of forms will bring the energetic frequency, measured in Bovis units (UB), back down to an equilibrium threshold, preventing saturation of the emitter.

### The three circles

Let us move on to the user's manual for the board.

It consists of three circles:

- the north circle (oriented on magnetic north minus 5°, i.e. at 355°),
- the centre circle,
- the south circle.

The **north circle** is the circle of origin (the starting point): it is from this circle that the emission to be generated departs. It can be a colour, selected by means of the needle passing right through the centre of this circle, onto which I have glued the dial of the 24 manifested colours.

The **centre circle** is "the microprocessor" or the "heart" of the whole board: it is from this circle that the axes of the field of emissions due to forms depart, which balance the board. On the centre of this circle one can place amplifiers (amplification by adding weight, or by other emitters of form emissions: a pétanque ball, grooved boards...).

The **south circle**, answering to the characteristics of the notion of the "law of compensation," will emit, perpendicular to the centre of this circle, the emission of the north circle. On it one can place the witness of a person to be treated (a photograph, or any other organic witness of the subject), an object to be charged (a stone, an electret, etc.), a container and its contents (alchemical products...), or else use a copper wire to guide the emission to a target or to the north circle of another board: one then has a series connection.
