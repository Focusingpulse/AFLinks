# Enel Re-issued: The Energeia Reprint Program (2025–2026)

*Forge dossier, 2026-09-15. English translation and synthesis of French retail/catalog sources. Companion to [Enel and the Omega Ray](2026-09-12-enel-omega-radiations-formes-fr-en.md) and the [Guy Thieux dossier](2026-09-13-guy-thieux-enel-legataire-fr-en.md).*

## Why this matters

Enel — Prince Michel Vladimirovich Skariatine (1883–1963), the Russian imperial exile who became the most-cited primary of French therapeutic radiesthesia — has been out of print in affordable editions for decades. The original editions came from Al-Maaref in Cairo (interwar to the 1950s), with a French trade edition of *Traitement à distance par radiations* from Dangles in 1959 (116 pp, per Google Books).

As of 2025–2026, the Lyon-based Librairie Energeia (distributed through Librairie Cadence / Eklectic Librairie, 62 rue St Jean, 69005 Lyon) has re-issued the core of the Enel corpus at retail prices, with Guy Thieux — Enel's literary legatee, the geophysicist who curated the *Carnets inédits* trilogy — supplying prefaces and annexed documentation. **The primary is back in commerce.** This dossier records the reprint layer: what exists, what each edition contains, and what the reprints add to the historical record.

## The reprint shelf

### 1. Radiations des formes et cancer — Energeia, 10 June 2025

- **ISBN 9791093492728** (979-10-93492-72-8), 158 pages, grand format, 17.50 €, illustrated, paperback.
- Originally published in Egypt by **Al-Maaref**, from his work there from the interwar years to the 1950s.
- **Contents (publisher's copy, translated):** "Enel was one of the most eminent practitioners and authors of the 20th century in therapeutic radiesthesia and esotericism. In *Radiations des formes et cancer* — originally published in Egypt by Al-Maaref, following his work there from the interwar period to the 1950s — Enel revealed his own methods and procedures for using radiesthetic rays and 'form waves', notably through the presentation of various types of experiments he had carried out with therapeutic aims, and specifically in the face of the serious health disorders encountered by certain of his consultants."
- **Note on the preface:** the Artcurial and Comme Un Roman listings credit a **préface by Guy Thieux** on this volume as well (the Eklectic listing does not name the preface author). The Thieux preface layer across the reprints is itself a document — his framing of Enel's EIFS ("Emergence des Formes") in coherence-field language dates from the Carnets interviews (1963) and is carried into these front-matters.
- Sold at: librairie-cadence.com / eklectic-librairie.com (ENERGEIA35), Artcurial (Paris), Comme Un Roman, Libristo (EU), Librairie Durance (Nantes, sur commande).

### 2. Traitement à distance par radiations. Préface de Guy Thieux — Energeia, 8 July 2025

- **ISBN 9791093492698**, 13.90 € (ENERGEIA37).
- Originally Al-Maaref (Cairo); a French trade edition appeared from **Dangles in 1959** (116 pp); a digital/print-on-demand edition from **eBookEsoterique (2022, 124 pp, ISBN 2930727705)** carried the full table of contents; the Energeia 2025 edition is the current retail reissue.
- **Publisher's framing (translated):** "Here, in *Traitement à distance par Radiations* — originally published in Egypt by Al-Maaref — Enel set out his own methods of detection and action based on radiesthesia and on this new technique that would be baptized 'the study and action of form waves', or 'radionics', with a therapeutic aim."
- **Structure (from the eBookEsoterique edition, which preserves the original pagination):**
  - Avant-propos — p. 9
  - Introduction — p. 17
  - **Chapitre I. Les Principes — p. 27**
  - **Chapitre II. Les Appareils — p. 41**: 1. Le chercheur d'ondes (the noxious-wave seeker); 2. Le projecteur à aimant (the magnet projector); 3. Le Yin-Yang
  - **Chapitre III. L'Horloge Solaire — p. 59** (the radiesthetic solar clock)
  - **Chapitre IV. Diagnostic et traitement — p. 71**: 1. Par des médicaments; 2. Traitement par irradiation
  - **Chapitre V. Quelques observations — p. 86**: 1. Asthme; 2. Les parathyroïdes; 3. La tension artérielle
  - **Chapitre VI. Possibilités insoupçonnées — p. 103**
  - 124 pages, 14 illustrations and diagrams (eBookEsoterique edition).
- **The famous introduction passage (translated from the eBookEsoterique excerpt):** "What seems most difficult for the uninitiated reader to conceive is the possibility of diagnosing the ailment and carrying out treatments at a distance by acting through vibrations on the patient's *témoin* [witness-sample]. However, my research and experiments, spread over many years in this direction, seem to be beginning to find an echo among representatives of official Medicine as well as among researchers of different countries. Here are a few examples taken at random which prove it: Dr Van der Sype (of Belgium) takes as témoin preferably a lock of the patient's hair, and claims to have obtained very appreciable successes as a result of his treatments. Dr Drown of California (Rockefeller prize) diagnoses at a distance by means of a drop of the patient's blood. Here is where official science stands, and it seems to me we can join hands across the abyss that separates us, for we arrive at the same findings by following different paths. I do not claim to be able to cure all illnesses and above all all cancers, but I must be on the right track, and I hope that others, better qualified than I, will finally succeed in finding the effective means to master this terrible scourge which seems to grow with the progress of civilization." — Enel, Introduction.

### 3. Gnomologie. Enseignement et expériences des anciennes écoles initiatiques — Energeia, 1 June 2026

- **ISBN 9791093492766**, 306 pages, 24.50 € (ENERGEIA42).
- **Réédition intégrale**, with a preface and annexed documentation by Guy Thieux.
- This is the *Carnets*-adjacent initiatic material — the "soul of the Sphinx" strand of Enel's Egyptian work (per the Margot Thieux announcement: "ENEL GNOMOLOGIE, préface et documentation par Guy Thieux, élève héritier d'Enel — L'Âme du Sphinx — 1ère réédition de Gnomologie, Energeia"). Margot Thieux's announcement dates the first reissue to 2025 on the birthday of "Renaissance"; the trade listing carries 01/06/2026.
- The largest of the reprints — the initiatic-school teaching text rather than the radiesthesia recipe books.

### 4. Post Mortem — Energeia, 1 April 2026

- 12.90 €, listed in the Energeia catalog and the Eklectic Enel author page.
- The posthumous strand of the corpus (the Carnets lineage already known through the IBAcom *Inédits d'Enel* trilogy edited by Thieux: *Le Monde astral et l'occultisme*, *Science égyptienne et médecine de l'astral*, *Radiesthésie thérapeutique*).

## What the reprints add to the Enel record

1. **The recipe book is unambiguously in commerce again.** The Ch. II apparatus list — *chercheur d'ondes*, *projecteur à aimant*, *Yin-Yang* — plus the *horloge solaire radiesthésique*, is the concrete emission-recipe layer of the French school. Until now it was accessible mainly through the 1959 Dangles edition and the 2022 print-on-demand; it is now a stocked retail book at 13.90 €.
2. **The Thieux preface layer.** Guy Thieux (b. 1932, geophysicist, 34 years in oil-prospect geophysics) is the through-line of the entire reprint program: prefaces on *Traitement à distance*, *Gnomologie* (and per two listings, *Radiations des formes et cancer*), plus the *Carnets inédits* trilogy at IBAcom. His framing — EIFS as "champs de cohérence, émergence des repères, distinction du global et du local" — is now the retail front-door to every Enel text. The commercial "wave" language of the modern radionics shelf does not honor this framing; the reprints quietly re-anchor the corpus to it.
3. **The publication chain is now complete and citable:** Al-Maaref (Cairo, interwar–1950s) → Dangles (Paris, 1959) → eBookEsoterique (2022, print-on-demand) → **Energeia (2025–2026, stocked retail)**. Each generation of editions is now bibliographically pinned with ISBNs and prices.
4. **The témoin-claim lineage is visible in one paragraph.** Enel's introduction cites Dr Van der Sype (Belgium, hair lock) and Dr Drown (California, blood drop, "prix Rockefeller") as official-medicine echoes of remote diagnosis. This is the same claim-family the 2026 commercial shelf carries (range claims of 200–1,500 km and "min 8,000 km" on radionics boxes) — the reprints put the primary's own, more modest version ("I do not claim to be able to cure all illnesses") back in print beside its descendants.

## Notes and caveats

- The two 2025 reprints are **retail catalog records, not full texts** — the books themselves are in copyright (Enel died 1963; Thieux prefaces are 2025-26); no full translation is attempted here. This dossier translates the publishers' descriptive copy and the public introduction excerpt (via the eBookEsoterique product page), which is the standard excerpt the trade uses to represent the book.
- Page counts differ across editions (Dangles 1959: 116 pp; eBookEsoterique 2022: 124 pp; Energeia *Radiations des formes et cancer*: 158 pp) — consistent with re-typesetting rather than content change, but not verified against the physical books.
- The Eklectic *Traitement à distance* listing shows "Nombre de pages : 0" (a catalog placeholder) — page count for the Energeia edition unconfirmed.
- The Gnomologie dating (Margot Thieux's announcement says 2025, the trade listing says 01/06/2026) — likely an announce-then-publish gap; both dates are recorded.
