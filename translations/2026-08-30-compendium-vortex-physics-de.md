## 1 Introduction

### 1.1 Starting Point

The human mind strives, in science and philosophy, to recognize a common law within the diverse. Already in ancient wisdom teachings, the vortex — or spiral — is pointed to as an archetypal phenomenon and as a bridge between the visible and the invisible. In nature, nothing moves in straight lines — all movement is spiral and can be regarded as a vortex. Vortex flows are thus omnipresent in nature and technology.

In the mid-19th century, the vortex even stood at the forefront of scientific research into matter. Like a torch, it illuminated the path for an entire generation of physicists toward a unified understanding of light and matter. At the beginning of the 20th century, however, through a series of remarkable events in the world of physics, this illumination was lost — and quantum mechanics and relativity theory became the path and paradigm of official physics. Yet despite the undeniable successes of this path, more and more researchers recognize that fundamental questions remain open and that physics still relates only to the material side of our reality.

In parallel to official science, creative outsiders have always developed alternative ideas and concepts. And so, in the field of vortices over the last 150 years, many new ideas have emerged and some have even been put into practice. Unfortunately, these researchers mostly had to operate outside mainstream science and therefore had difficulty publishing their findings. As a result, their ideas are often inadequately documented, poorly accessible, and scarcely processed.

If we expect specialists of orthodox physics to judge these ideas without prejudice for their scientific merit, these outsider ideas must first be brought into a readable, logical, and consistent form.

And this task is now being undertaken with this Compendium.

The motivation to actively promote vortex physics is based on the hope that the reanimation, research, and further development of the old vortex and ether concepts can make an important contribution to the expansion of natural science from the purely material to higher planes of existence. In this way, vortex physics could develop into a key for implementing our holistic understanding of Free Energy and gain research-strategic significance.

May this work serve as a starting point for those called to examine and further develop the vortex concept.

### 1.2 Scope and Objectives

The domain of science is limited by its methodology to a small section of total reality. The purpose of scientific methodology is to continually expand this domain and to wrest ever more territory from the outer region that is not yet scientifically graspable (but in daily life often long familiar to humans).

> **Compendium Objective:** The processing of the vortex-relevant outer domain, with provision of the minimally necessary inner foundations.

In the outer domain, we concentrate on the ether vortex hypothesis and the motion hypothesis:

**The Ether Vortex Hypothesis:** "Elementary particles are vortices in a sub-material medium, the ether."

**The Motion Hypothesis:** "We move incorrectly; natural motion is spiral-shaped."

With the ether vortex hypothesis, we therefore clearly assume a non-material foundation of our physical reality (see [Laszlo]).

In the inner domain, on one hand, a repetition of mathematical-physical basic knowledge is necessary, and on the other, an overview of the most important vortex laws known to orthodox science. Such an overview was fortunately provided in 1996 by the dedicated vortex researcher Hans Lugt in his extensive and excellent work *Introduction to Vortex Theory* [Lugt, 1996].

We must also count today's axioms among the physical foundations! For if a description of matter based on the vortex concept is to be more general, it must be able to derivably explain certain of these axioms (possibly from new, even more elementary axioms).

We further delimit ourselves from societal and science-policy questions. As interesting and important as it is to show and discuss the implications of vortex-physical conjectures and results for our daily life, we restrict ourselves for now to the scientific side of the topic of vortices. This delimitation is made easier by the fact that within SAFE, the neighboring TransForum working group deals precisely with the questions we have excluded.

### 1.3 Approach and Focus Areas

The Compendium will understandably have the character of an eclectic summary — i.e., the best material on the topic of vortices from everywhere is brought into a logical context, and an attempt is made to work out the fundamental concepts in a didactically understandable way.

We pursue a combined approach:

- On one hand, we collect material per vortex researcher, and on the other, we attempt to build a hierarchically ordered topic overview of vortex physics from this material.
- With this simultaneous author- and topic-based presentation, we strive for a comparative overall view of vortex physics. The redundancies that inevitably arise in this process are not considered disadvantageous, but rather didactically meaningful.

For the hierarchical topic overview, the basic hypotheses organically lead to the following chapter structure, oriented toward the utility of the vortex concept:

The **Motion Hypothesis** ("we move incorrectly — all movement is spiral") relates to vortices in material media (gases, liquids, solids) and is to be treated in the chapter *Natural Technology* with the aim of improving technical-biological processes.

For the **Ether Vortex Hypothesis** ("elementary particles are ether vortices"), it should first be examined in the chapter *Deepening of Physics* whether it can provide new or deeper explanations for relationships that are still insufficiently understood. Then, in the chapter *Expansion of Physics*, the potential of the vortex concept should also be demonstrated for phenomena from the domain of the mental-living and made accessible to physical research.

### 1.4 Status of Version 1

The first year of the Vortex Physics Working Group should be regarded as a settling-in process. In a spontaneous, rather unsystematic manner, a rapid gathering and rough sorting of available vortex material took place. A genuine processing could only be accomplished in the area of Jakob Huber's etherics. In the other areas, beyond immersion in the fundamentals, there was not much more than a rough structuring.

Thus, the Compendium in its present Version 1 presents itself mainly as a research program — i.e., as a large structured vessel that now wants to be successively filled with content in subsequent versions, section by section.

What has been achieved is a good beginning on the way to the formulated Compendium objective. The framework is now staked out, and the work ahead for the next Compendium versions can be realistically assessed.

Our sincere thanks for diverse support go to the remaining members of the Vortex Physics Working Group: Ben Jansen, Dr. Hanspeter Seiler, and Dr. Hans Weber.

### 1.5 Reader's Attitude

Constructive engagement with outsider topics requires the moral quality of intellectual honesty:

- We should be willing to revise any of our views — i.e., "be open to the (currently still) impossible."
- We should change a view when a compelling reason exists to do so.
- We should not capriciously change a view without good reason.

This inductive attitude aims to adapt our views to reality as appropriately as possible.

Since in the field of Free Energy much is operated with half-knowledge and hope, it is first necessary to cleanly think through, formulate, and understand the theoretical foundations. But the best theory is worth less than the paper it is printed on if it cannot be put into practice. And so, summary documents like this can only ever be a basis for stimulating interested readers to prove the described theories through practical implementation. For only the practical confirmation through concrete experiments and presentable devices will provide those compelling reasons needed to help the visionary ideas — so necessary for our world — achieve a broad breakthrough.
