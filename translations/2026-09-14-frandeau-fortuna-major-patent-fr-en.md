## 1. Who he was

**Dr Francis Frandeau de Marly** — French researcher in radiesthesia and radionics. The publisher's presentation of his treatise gives his credentials (Éditions Labussière, French; translated from montolieu.org / lebouquinfrancais.fr):

> "The book is the culmination of several years of research in the fascinating field of subtle energies. It is the fruit of rigorously scientific work carried out by a great French researcher, Dr. Francis Frandeau de Marly. This talented academic — an engineer in electro-mechanics, a pharmacist of the University of Bordeaux, a doctor *honoris causa* of several foreign universities — developed a pendant that is used as a radiesthetic pendulum, but also and above all as a radionic instrument emitting a beneficial energy. Dr. Francis Frandeau de Marly named his invention the 'Pendentif Cosmo-Magnétique'®."

And the lineage claim, from the same presentation:

> "Francis Frandeau de Marly thus takes his place in the line of those great researchers named Louis Turenne, Bovis, Chaumery, and de Bélizal."

No birth/death dates are verifiable in the fetchable record; his publication window (Labussière editions, 1984 patent through ~2006 reprints) places his active career in the late 20th century. He is the **second generation after Chaumery and de Belizal**: where the 1936 patent (FR 816,132) covered the Universal Pendulum and the negative-green discovery, Frandeau's 1984 filing covers a *harmonization generator* built on the same radiesthetic-battery principle — the patent record itself draws the connection, as the technical sheet shows.

## 2. The two patents, as the technical sheet cites them

The Porte des Étoiles technical sheet ("3.1.U", everest.imust.info; French, translated below) documents the device by its patent numbers. The sheet's opening frame (French first, then translation):

> FR: "Pendentif Cosmo Magnétique du Docteur Francis FRANDEAU de MARLY. Cosmo de l'axe Est Ouest de l'équilibre cosmique et tellurique. Magnétique de l'axe Nord Sud de l'équilibre magnétique et électrique. Emetteur complet et parfaitement stable. BREVET 84.03970."

> EN: "Cosmo-Magnetic Pendant of Dr. Francis Frandeau de Marly. 'Cosmo' from the East–West axis of cosmic-telluric equilibrium. 'Magnetic' from the North–South axis of magnetic-electrical equilibrium. A complete and perfectly stable emitter. PATENT 84.03970."

The name is thus a double-axis claim: **Cosmo** = the East–West (cosmic-telluric) balance axis; **Magnétique** = the North–South (magnetic-electrical) axis. The device is presented not as a detector but as an **emitter** — "complet et parfaitement stable."

The construction section of the sheet (French, translated; bracketed clarifications mine):

> FR: "Pile radiesthésique à quatre éléments: un diamètre de 12 millimètres (brevet n° 816.132/10.04.1936 – auteur : Léon CHAUMERY) + 1957 Pendentif Cosmo magnétique (capteur) : hauteur : 39mm – diamètre : 11 mm – Poids : 22 Gr – matière : laiton doré à l'or fin – 3 éléments d'un diamètre de 12 mm avec hauteur de 6 mm – 1 élément d'un diamètre de 11 mm avec hauteur de 20 mm doté d'un générateur d'harmonisation (sélecteur) de type Fortuna Major (brevet n° 84.03970/13.03.1984 – auteur : Docteur Francis FRANDEAU de MARLY)"

> EN: "Radiesthetic battery of four elements: one of 12 millimetres diameter (patent no. 816.132 of 10.04.1936 — author: Léon Chaumery) + 1957 Cosmo-magnetic pendant (detector): height 39 mm — diameter 11 mm — weight 22 g — material: brass gilded with fine gold — 3 elements of 12 mm diameter and 6 mm height — 1 element of 11 mm diameter and 20 mm height, fitted with a **harmonization generator (selector) of the Fortuna Major type** (patent no. 84.03970 of 13.03.1984 — author: Dr. Francis Frandeau de Marly)."

Two things to note for the patent record:

- The sheet explicitly cites **Chaumery's 1936 patent as a component** — the 12 mm element is "brevet n° 816.132/10.04.1936 – auteur : Léon CHAUMERY." Frandeau's 1984 patent is thus a layered filing: a radiesthetic battery whose base element is the 1936 patented form, topped by his own Fortuna Major selector. This is the French school's patent lineage in one object: Chaumery (1936) → de Belizal (consolidation, 1950s–70s) → Frandeau (1984).
- The date "13.03.1984" for the 84.03970 filing matches the year-prefix convention of French patent numbers (84.xxxxx = filed 1984).

## 3. The Fortuna Major selector — the patent's core idea

The heart of the 1984 filing is the **Fortuna Major selector** — a harmonization generator based on the geomantic figure *Fortuna Major*. The technical sheet specifies its design parameters (French, translated):

> FR: "Système d'intégration du motif radiant : quaternaire (tétravalence de notre atome de base - carbone) - Groupe de sélecteur : Binaire (air.terre = violet.rouge = équilibre cosmo tellurique) - Modèle du sélecteur : segmentaire (2X1 segment brisé Yin & 2X1 segment entier Yang) - Type de sélecteur : Fortuna Major"

> EN: "Integration system of the radiant motif: **quaternary** (the tetravalence of our base atom — carbon). Selector group: **binary** (air·earth = violet·red = cosmic-telluric equilibrium). Selector model: **segmentary** (2×1 broken segment Yin & 2×1 whole segment Yang). Selector type: **Fortuna Major**."

And the sheet's explanation of the figure (French, translated):

> FR: "Fortuna Major = la meilleure figure géomantique complètement équilibrante (2X1 pair & 2X1 impair) qui possède autant de Yin que de Yang avec le Yin au dessus du Yang signifiant ainsi Forte Résistance Physique - Grande Chance en amour – Triomphe absolu aux jeux de hasard."

> EN: "Fortuna Major = the best completely balancing geomantic figure (2×1 even & 2×1 odd), which possesses as much Yin as Yang with the Yin above the Yang, signifying thereby: Strong Physical Resistance — Great Luck in Love — Absolute Triumph in Games of Chance."

*Fortuna Major* is one of the sixteen figures of medieval geomancy: two dots above, one dot below (in the standard dot-notation: ●● / ●), read as "greater fortune." Frandeau's reading of it is structural: the figure has an equal count of Yin (broken/even) and Yang (whole/odd) elements — hence "completely balancing" — with Yin positioned above Yang. The selector is thus a *form-wave* implementation of a geomantic figure: the same logic by which Chaumery and de Belizal mapped the negative green and the color spectrum onto pendulum hemispheres, Frandeau maps a geomantic balance-figure onto a physical selector element. The claimed correspondences (physical resistance, luck in love, triumph in games) are the traditional geomantic divinatory meanings of the figure, carried over into the radionic claim.

The quaternary/binary/segmentary triple is the patent's design grammar: the radiant motif integrates at the **quaternary** level (four — matching carbon's tetravalence, "our base atom"), the selector groups at the **binary** level (air·earth = violet·red, the cosmic-telluric pair), and the selector is physically **segmentary** (two broken Yin segments, two whole Yang segments — the Fortuna Major dot pattern rendered as physical segments).

## 4. The radiesthetic battery — physics of the pile

The sheet specifies the operating parameters of the four-element battery (French, translated):

> FR: "Position verticale : Pôle supérieur (sommet) = Emission Vert + / Pôle inférieur (base) = Emission Vert –. Position Horizontale: Pôle supérieur orienté vers le Nord = Emission Vert + / Pôle inférieur orienté vers le Sud = Emission Vert -. Pile à diamètres constants entre 13 et 16 cm = progression linéaire de puissance. Pile à diamètres croissants = Succession d'octaves. Tension = fonction du nombre d'hémisphères superposées. Intensité = fonction du diamètre identique de chaque hémisphère."

> EN: "Vertical position: upper pole (apex) = **Green+ emission** / lower pole (base) = **Green− emission**. Horizontal position: upper pole oriented toward the North = Green+ emission / lower pole oriented toward the South = Green− emission. Battery with constant diameters between 13 and 16 cm = linear progression of power. Battery with increasing diameters = **succession of octaves**. Voltage = a function of the number of superposed hemispheres. Intensity = a function of the identical diameter of each hemisphere."

The Green+/Green− pair is de Belizal's post-Chaumery correction of the spectrum — the positive green (beneficial) and negative green (the "dehydrating" ray Chaumery died investigating, later split by de Belizal and Morel into the beneficial V+ and the dangerous V−). Frandeau's pendant emits Green+ from one pole and Green− from the other, with polarity set by orientation (vertical: top/bottom; horizontal: north/south).

The battery "physics" is stated in explicitly electrical metaphors: **tension** (voltage) scales with the number of stacked hemispheres; **intensité** (current) with their diameter; constant diameters give linear power progression, increasing diameters give octave steps. This is the French school's signature move — reading the radiesthetic battery through the formalism of electric piles — going back to the Chaumery–de Belizal patent's own language.

The sheet adds two referenced calibrations (French, translated):

> FR: "AMC (Anti-mitotique cosmique) de Hans Heinrich HECKMANN = tension optimale pour la cellule vivante = 4 hémisphères de diamètre 12 mm (3X4 piles de diamètre 12 mm = principe ternaire). Métronome radiesthésique de STEÏMANN = cadence vibratoire ni trop rapide, ni trop lente, ni trop forte, ni trop faible = effet de forme maintenant toute cellule vivante en parfaite harmonie vibratoire."

> EN: "The AMC (cosmic anti-mitotic) of Hans Heinrich **Heckmann** = the optimal voltage for the living cell = 4 hemispheres of 12 mm diameter (3×4 batteries of 12 mm diameter = ternary principle). The radiesthetic **metronome of Steïmann** = a vibratory cadence neither too fast nor too slow, neither too strong nor too weak = a form effect holding every living cell in perfect vibratory harmony."

Two further lineage references: **H. H. Heckmann** (the German researcher of the anti-mitotic calibration — his "AMC" designation is here tied to a four-hemisphere, 12 mm configuration) and **Steïmann** (a radiesthetic metronome calibration). These are the international cross-references the French school routinely cites alongside its own apparatus.

## 5. Operating modes and configurations

The sheet's usage section (French, translated):

> FR: "Le pendentif cosmo magnétique peut être utilisé comme pendule détecteur et émetteur ondulatoire (bandes étroites) et vibratoire (larges spectres). Le pendentif cosmo magnétique peut être utilisé comme pendentif seul ou combiné (2-3-4), en apparence ou en inclusions (volume pyramidal, octogonal, dodécagonal, à 16 côtés), vertical ou horizontal, sur soi ou sur témoins."

> EN: "The cosmo-magnetic pendant can be used as a detector pendulum and as a **wave emitter (narrow bands) and vibratory emitter (broad spectrums)**. The cosmo-magnetic pendant can be used alone or combined (2–3–4), as an external form or as inclusions (pyramidal, octagonal, dodecagonal, 16-sided volumes), vertical or horizontal, on oneself or on witnesses."

The dual-detector/emitter claim is the same doctrine the editions-exclusif.com product page develops (French, translated):

> "Contrary to what is usually believed, the handling of a pendulum is not limited to a 'capture' of subtle energy in relation to the bodies, witnesses, or objects of the research. The other face of this art-science, just as captivating and fertile, is the possibility of **emitting** with a pendulum. Radiesthesia is not simply a way of perceiving, receiving, and detecting. It allows one to emit energetic influences, just as radionics strives to do by various apparatuses, active drawings, etc."

> "The cosmo-magnetic pendant, by the rich range of frequencies with which it can enter into correspondence, by the powerful 'carrier wave' with which it is endowed, thus enters perfectly into the world of the 'pendulums of power.' It offers its user two categories of energies at once: that produced by **voluntary gyrations** for a massive charge, above a point of the body, a photo, or an object; and that produced by **involuntary gyrations up to the blocking point** for more subtle charges, with or without a radiesthetic witness."

The publisher's presentation of the treatise makes the same point with the dynamo metaphor (French, translated):

> "By turning the 'Pendentif Cosmo-Magnétique'® voluntarily and rapidly clockwise, it transforms into a veritable **dynamo of fluid energy**. It then substitutes for human magnetism and — wonder of wonders — you then need not have the GIFT for it to work."

The claimed applications, from the same presentation: "Capture micro-vibratory energies with biological benefits; transmit these energies onto a photograph; neutralize and eliminate harmful energies of every origin."

The "no GIFT required" claim is doctrinally significant: it moves the device from the mental-radiesthesia tradition (operator-dependent) toward the physical-radiesthesia tradition (apparatus-dependent) — the same distinction the Geobios Lecher-antenna page (2026-09-07 translation) draws as the French school's defining line.

## 6. The book lineage

Two Labussière books carry the system:

|---|---|---|

The Servranx catalogue carries the hardware downstream — the **Émetteur octogonal** (octagonal radionic emitter, ±6.8 × 2 cm, €83), described as (French, translated):

> "Radionic emitter of Dr. Francis Frandeau de Marly. It powerfully activates all the energies that are usually the province of feng-shui. … Francis Frandeau de Marly had the ingenious idea of associating the cosmo-magnetic pendulum with four particularly active forms: the pyramidal emitter, the dodecahedral emitter… The results obtained are astonishing, and these four inclusions should quickly become your 'tools of success.'"

## 7. Where this sits in the corpus

- **Patent lane:** second entry after FR 816,132 (Chaumery & de Belizal 1936). The 1984 filing extends the lineage: 1936 Universal Pendulum → 1957 cosmo-magnetic detector (unpatented per the sheet) → 1984 Fortuna Major selector. Further patent-lane candidates: Frandeau's later filings if any exist (the INPI register would show them — FocusOptimized lane), and the Puyuelo gravito-diode patent FR 2661295 (1990, rexresearch.com PDF is fetchable — a different lane but adjacent: the "gravitational wave emitter-receiver" tradition).
- **French-school chain:** Chaumery/de Belizal → Enel → Bardet → La Foye → Ravatin now has a post-1980 continuation: **Frandeau de Marly → Marquette**. The founder series (Bouly through Ravatin, 14 dossiers) covers the 1865–2011 arc; Frandeau fills the 1980s–2000s generation.
- **Cross-references opened:** Heckmann (AMC calibration), Steïmann (metronome) — both are thin in the fetchable record but may have German/French sources worth scouting; geomantic-figure emitters (the Fortuna Major selector is the first geomancy-based apparatus in the corpus).
