### Low Energy Nuclear Reactions

Our technology uses LENR reactions (Low Energy Nuclear Reactions) that use water and electricity, without the need for fuels or radioactive elements, and have the potential to change the paradigm of energy production.

The acronym LENR defines a series of phenomena — including metamorphosis, transmutation, cavitation, cold fusion, and low-energy fusion — that stimulate matter in order to extract energy. Unlike fission and fusion, LENR generates energy without using elements that produce radioactive waste and without the need for extremely high temperatures.

### Institutional LENR Project

To date, LENR technologies are the subject of international studies and research aimed at testing and certifying experimental phenomena characterized by excess heat or isotopic changes. Among the main governmental players are the USA, Japan, and the EU, where researchers and companies carry out initiatives related to research and experimentation in this field.
