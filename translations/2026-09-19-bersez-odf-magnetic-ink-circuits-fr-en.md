## Part I — The Doctrine (translation)

### Radionics and Form Waves

**Did you know that…**

Many specialized books very seriously indicate the character and personality linked to your own first name. And this often proves surprisingly accurate. Likewise, your signature (even if it presents itself as a scribble) represents at once your name and your consent. Thus your initials or signature constitute a **graphic witness** of your entire personality — the reason a false signature is a criminal offense.

In the same way, your **name** — the assembly of different letters — forms a graphic witness that reflects your personality exactly, whose value is superior to that of your social-security number or your bank account. Thus your first name and family name are **witnesses of your personality** in the same rank as a photo, a lock of hair, and other physical witnesses. And each letter composing your name is a pictogram corresponding to your inner and outer nature.

One often says that **a drawing is worth more than a long speech**. This is well proven, for drawing with its expression was significant long before cuneiform and hieroglyphic writing. Thus all writings, whatever the language, become an assembly of ideograms — as Jacques Bersez explains in his revealing book *Les Codes du Succès* (The Codes of Success).

A kind of **magical signature**, the name — or the assembly of letters composing it — forms a witness of the moral and physical personality of an individual. It is moreover the reason the name of God is never revealed in the monotheistic faiths.

If the name presents itself as a personal witness just like a magical influence, what is one to think of drawings, paintings, and other significant symbols? We know that cave paintings are magical acts. But what, then, of religious symbols, sacred verses, and alchemical graphics?

In fact it is quite possible that political movements and wars of religion squabbled so much that the known symbols fell into the jumble of legends. This will no doubt be judged the greatest error of the age, for the **sign** (directed by the Spirit) will always be an **active element** for the present as for the future.

Thus the primitive, chemical, electronic, alchemical, astrological, medical, masonic and other symbols are so many references that will serve as **active witnesses** in a modern science that the sorcerers of old lacked: **radionics**.

### The Language of the Gods

We have known since antiquity that symbols represent the language of the gods. When the symbol is in two dimensions, it is an **active drawing**, a "diogram." In three dimensions, it becomes an object of cult. In both representations, it is of formal evidence that the symbol contains within it a **particular energy** which has, over the course of time, impassioned the initiates, the mages, the radiesthesists, and the high-timber scientists in quest of infinitesimal energies.

### The Extraordinary Adventure of an American Radionist

At the beginning of the twentieth century, a prodigy of radionics had built an emitter using the tubes [radio amplifier valves] of the day. Having gone away, the current feeding the apparatus was cut. Some days later the man returned home and noted with surprise that his apparatus had **functioned without any energy**. This discovery spread like wildfire, invading the United States, England, but also Belgium and France.

**And yet it works!**

Following these curious revelations, the whole intelligentsia of radiesthesia and radionics worked on the problem — a problem that culminated in the appearance of **non-wired apparatuses**. That is to say, it was not necessary to wire different electronic elements to one another, but simply to align them in a logical sense of functioning.

At the same time, the magazine *Astounding Science Fiction*, published under the pen of Mr. Campbell [John W. Campbell, ed.], makes surprising revelations. Indeed, he discovers that the drawing of an organ suffices to represent its function — and he also opens the great doors of radiesthesia and radionics at a distance.

Under the impulsion of the **Servranx Brothers** in Belgium, the symbols and active drawings propagate in all directions. But it is only toward the 1980s that **Jacques Bersez, printer by trade**, decides to print these active drawings with **magnetic ink**: the result is a **complete success**.

### 25 Years Later, Science Takes Over

Nobody having innovated in this domain, and to give a sequel to ever-more-insistent demands, Jacques Bersez resumes the fabrication of the **active circuits** with **magnetic ink**.

You will discover the **new activated radionic circuits**, allowing you to possess a powerful arsenal for a minimal investment. Activated radionics represents **a small step toward the miracle**. It is not the panacea or the elixir of life, but it will be for you a formidable means of **managing to your profit all situations, whatever their scale**.

How? Simply thanks to the new **ODF circuits**. These are realized with the **true magnetic ink** — the kind used to print bank checks and other paper currency. Of course these circuits are produced in our own print shop by Jacques Bersez himself, typographer and master printer.

### General Remarks on the Traced Circuits

All tracings, whatever they are in general, are based on **emissions due to forms**. Of course, certain circuits are more efficacious than others — whether because of their form, or because of the **intention** that permitted constructing the circuit in question. As a general rule, **the circuits orient themselves to the North when advised**. This aspect of the question is very important, and it is precisely for this reason that Jacques Bersez prints his circuits with special magnetic ink.

There are two sorts of circuits:

1. The first sort can be considered **protective**, for it introduces no exterior element beyond the goals aimed at. Such is the case of the classic geometric figures. These apparatuses (for they are apparatuses) are specially designed to **protect the witness** in question, which one generally places at the center of the drawn figure.

The use of these circuits is just as simple, but it nevertheless demands a more subtle attention and reflection. Thus, for example, the witness of a sick person is an easy thing to find, whereas the "remedy-witness" demands serious research.

### Terms Employed (the Témoin Taxonomy)

- **TEMOIN (WITNESS)** — represents the thing or element capable of replacing the REAL in cases where the REAL cannot be used. If the REAL can be used, it becomes the WITNESS.
- **TEMOIN SUJET (SUBJECT-WITNESS)** — represents the subject. It can be their photo, their drawing, a lock of hair, an item of clothing, a handkerchief, etc.
- **TEMOIN REMEDE (REMEDY-WITNESS)** — it can be the remedy itself, allopathic or homeopathic. It can also be a written formula, a radionic reference, etc. One understands by "remedy-witness" the element capable of bringing a correction to a given state. That is to say, the remedy-witness applies not solely to an illness, but to feelings, to affairs, etc. In that case it is also termed a **TEMOIN CORRECTEUR (CORRECTOR-WITNESS)**.
- **TEMOIN BUT (GOAL-WITNESS)** — it generally consists of an expressive formula of a result to obtain. Not to be confused with the other witnesses. Thus, for example, for a headache, the "goal-witness" will be a written formula in the style of "healing of the headache." It goes without saying that innumerable "goal-witness" formulas can be used.
- **TEMOIN ARTIFICIEL (ARTIFICIAL WITNESS)** — a fabricated witness, generally by an expressive formula. Example: VIN [wine] is the expressive witness of wine.
- **TEMOIN VALORISE (VALORIZED WITNESS)** — represents any witness, generally an expressive formula, having been exposed or valorized at the décagone.
