# The AI-Pendulum Gap Breaks on French

*The fr AI-pendulum census, verified at source — Tarotki FR, Tarotoui, Tarotsi, and the AETHER holdout.*

## What broke

For six consecutive scout passes (fires 25/34/43/52/61/70), the French lane held a clean record: **every online pendulum in the fr esoteric mint was an oracle — a randomizer with canned prose — and none of them used an LLM to interpret the reading.** The family called it the "AI-pendulum gap": the one corner of the online-divination market where no AI interpretation layer had landed. The same gap had already broken on German (fire 71), Italian (72), Spanish (73) and Portuguese (77) — every time through the same platform, Tarotki. As of fire 79 (2026-09-15), **the gap breaks on French too**, and it breaks twice over: platform-driven (Tarotki's FR locale is now reachable) *and* with two fr-native AI pendulums that no platform import explains.

**Family scoreboard after fire 79: AI-pendulum gap breaks on de / it / es / pt / fr; holds only ru / uk / ja / zh.**

## The three surfaces, verified at source (2026-09-16)

### 1. Tarotki — the platform break reaches French

Tarotki (tarotki.com) is a 6-locale free esoteric-tool platform ("Deutsch, Englisch, Spanisch, Französisch, Portugiesisch und Italienisch") with 50+ tools. Its **Mantik** (mancy) section includes the **Pendel** (pendulum) among eight traditional methods (coffee grounds, pendulum, salt, candles, dice, smoke, dominoes, snail oracle). The platform's core promise, stated identically in every locale: *"Au lieu de textes préfabriqués : chaque lecture est interprétée par IA pour votre question spécifique."* — instead of prefabricated texts, every reading is AI-interpreted for your specific question.

The FR locale (tarotki.com/fr/) is now fully reachable and lists the pendulum under "Manties" ("Marc de café, pendule, dés, bougies, sel, fumée et plus"). The German mantik page carries the sharpest framing of the pendulum's mechanism — worth translating in full, because it is the platform's *own* answer to the skeptics:

> "Ist Pendel-Mantik nicht einfach der ideomotorische Effekt? — **Ja — und genau das ist der Punkt.** Der ideomotorische Effekt beschreibt unbewusste Mikrobewegungen der Hand, die das Pendel zum Schwingen bringen. Skeptiker sehen darin eine Entzauberung der Methode. Eine andere Lesart: das Pendel macht sichtbar, was dein Körper schon weiß, bevor dein Bewusstsein zugreift. Es ist ein Verstärker für leise Körperintuitionen."

> ("Isn't pendulum-mancy just the ideomotor effect? — Yes — and that's exactly the point. The ideomotor effect describes unconscious micro-movements of the hand that set the pendulum swinging. Skeptics see that as a deflation of the method. Another reading: the pendulum makes visible what your body already knows before your consciousness reaches for it. It is an amplifier for quiet body-intuitions.")

This is the platform that broke de/it/es/pt; fr is its fifth locale. No fr-native explanation needed — the platform simply arrived.

### 2. Tarotoui — fr-native, AI-interpreted, honest about the mechanism

Tarotoui.net is a fr-native site ("plus de cinquante outils ésotériques gratuits... chacun accompagné d'une interprétation par intelligence artificielle"), with a dedicated **Pendule Oui ou Non** tool under its mancie section: "Lecture en ligne avec interprétation par IA en quelques secondes, sans inscription."

Its mancie page is unusually honest about what the digital pendulum actually is:

> "Le pendule physique repose sur des micro-mouvements involontaires de la main. **La version numérique remplace ce mécanisme par une réponse générée et interprétée.** Ce n'est pas la même pratique, mais l'outil reste utile pour structurer une question binaire et obtenir une réponse claire."

> ("The physical pendulum relies on micro-movements of the hand. The digital version replaces this mechanism with a generated and interpreted response. It is not the same practice, but the tool remains useful for structuring a binary question and getting a clear answer.")

That is the cleanest one-sentence description of the whole AI-pendulum category anywhere in the corpus: the physical instrument's mechanism (ideomotor micro-movements) is *replaced*, not simulated — the digital pendulum is a randomizer plus an LLM reading, and the vendor says so. Tarotoui's FAQ is equally direct: "L'IA ne lit pas l'avenir, elle structure les correspondances symboliques" ("The AI doesn't read the future; it structures symbolic correspondences").

### 3. Tarotsi — the fr-native AI pendulum that disconfirms itself

Tarotsi.org/fr offers a "Pendule gratuit en ligne avec IA" — and its own glossary entry for the pendulum carries the lane's sharpest vendor-side honesty:

> "Le principe physiologique est l'effet idéomoteur : de petits mouvements involontaires de la main amplifiés par le pendule."

> ("The physiological principle is the ideomotor effect: small involuntary movements of the hand amplified by the pendulum.")

And in its FAQ:

> "Est-ce scientifique ? **Non. Les études en double aveugle de radiesthésie n'ont pas confirmé de capacités supérieures au hasard. Cela reste un outil symbolique.**"

> ("Is it scientific? No. Double-blind studies of radiesthesia have not confirmed abilities beyond chance. It remains a symbolic tool.")

**This is the cleanest vendor-side disconfirmation in the corpus** — an AI-pendulum vendor that prints the Munich/Cassel-class null into its own product page. It matches the fr.wikipedia «Radiesthésiste» verification floor (the double-blind nulls, the Rocard ideomotor framing) — but here the null comes from *inside* the AI-oracle mint, not from its critics. The glossary even reproduces the traditional coding (clockwise = yes, anticlockwise = no, lateral swing = neutral, frontal swing = reformulate) — the same convention the French school standardized a century ago, now driving an LLM prompt instead of a hand.

## The holdout: pendule-ouinon.com (AETHER)

The one fr site built specifically to "close the gap" remains the one that hasn't. AETHER's pendulum is physics-adjacent, not LLM: at the click instant the system captures a fluctuation from the **ANU quantum-vacuum random number generator** (Australian National University), with a high-entropy cryptographic algorithm as fallback. The answer is then rendered as **static symbolic prose** (clockwise rotation = OUI, etc.) — no interpretation layer, no model, no reading of the question. Verified again 2026-09-16: no LLM interpreter.

AETHER's framing is the most elaborate in the lane and worth recording for what it claims:

> "AETHER ne crée pas la réponse. Nous créons simplement le chaos parfait et neutre — un support vierge d'intention causale — pour permettre à votre conscience d'imprimer sa propre vérité à travers le mouvement du pendule."

> ("AETHER doesn't create the answer. We simply create perfect, neutral chaos — a support virgin of causal intention — to allow your consciousness to imprint its own truth through the movement of the pendulum.")

The mechanism claim is explicit and idiosyncratic: the physical pendulum works by the ideomotor effect (Chevreul), but the online pendulum *cannot* — "il n'y a pas de fil à tenir, pas de bras qui tremble" — so the operator's role shifts to **choosing the moment of the click**: "vous ne contrôlez pas le résultat... Mais vous choisissez le moment du clic. Et ce moment — votre timing intuitif — est le véhicule de votre réponse." The quantum noise is the "faille du déterminisme" (crack in determinism) through which intention allegedly shows. The page cites "chercheurs en physique de la conscience (tels que P. Guillemant ou R. Leterrier)" for the claim that true randomness is a plenum, not a void.

**A QC find inside the holdout:** AETHER's history section dates the ideomotor discovery to "En 1850... l'abbé Alexis Mermet" — the Prince des Sourciers credited with *discovering* that the pendulum moves by involuntary muscle micro-contractions. The same page, in its reliability section, correctly credits **Michel-Eugène Chevreul, 1833**. The page contradicts itself: Mermet (1866–1937) was not born in 1850, and the ideomotor experiments were Chevreul's. The corpus's own Chevreul dossier (De la baguette divinatoire, 1854) already holds the correct history. AETHER borrowed the French school's founder-pantheon and got the attribution wrong inside a single page — a small, characteristic error: the tradition's historiography is as loose as its metrology.

## What the break actually means

1. **The last major-language holdouts are ru/uk/ja/zh.** The "100% oracle / 0% dowsing" line is now true only there. The fr break was the last one that could be platform-driven (Tarotki has no ru/uk/ja/zh locale — verified across fires 74–76); any further break must come from a *native* surface in those languages. That makes the next breaks genuinely new information, not platform diffusion.

2. **The AI layer lands on the interpretation, not the detection.** Every surface that broke keeps the same structure: a randomizer produces the yes/no (or the configuration), and the LLM interprets it *relative to the user's question*. Nobody claims the AI detects anything — Tarotoui says the AI "structures symbolic correspondances"; Tarotki says it reads "for your concrete question." The divinatory claim has quietly migrated from *detection* (the pendulum senses) to *hermeneutics* (the AI reads) — which is unfalsiable by design, and the vendors increasingly say so themselves.

3. **The honest-vendor pattern is now the norm, not the exception.** Tarotsi prints the double-blind null; Tarotoui states the digital pendulum is "not the same practice"; even AETHER concedes the ideomotor effect for physical pendulums. The lane's 2026 AI-oracle vendors are *more* scientifically candid than the traditional radiesthesia retail layer (compare the Bovis-scale dials, whose vendors print no caveats at all). The disconfirmation, not the claim, is what's being sold.

4. **The French school's vocabulary now runs AI pendulums.** The coding convention (clockwise = yes), the term *radiesthésie*, the Chevreul/Mermet references — the AI pendulums wear the French school's clothes. The tradition's instruments are being absorbed into the AI-oracle mint as prompt furniture: the pendulum is now a randomizer skin, and the century of radiesthesia methodology (calibration, témoins, cadrans) is reduced to a yes/no bit that an LLM decorates.
