Torsion Fields and the Physical Vacuum

"The torsion field" is a physics term originally introduced by Élie Joseph Cartan, a French mathematician, author of important works in mathematical physics, differential geometry, and the general theory of groups, in 1922, in order to designate a hypothetical physical field arising as a result of the rotation (torsion) of space. The name of the torsion field comes from the English word "torsion" — rotation — and from the Latin "torsio" of the same meaning.



General relativity summed up Minkowski spacetime by introducing a variable metric tensor; in the same way, the pseudo-Riemannian manifold of spacetime can be generalized by introducing a variable torsion of connections. The simplest theory introducing torsion is the Einstein–Cartan theory of gravitation. Experimental attempts to detect torsion fields have yielded no results. Contemporary physics regards torsion fields as a hypothetical object contributing nothing to observed physical effects.

The Theory of Torsion Fields developed by Gennady Shipov and Anatoly Akimov, members of the Russian Academy of Natural Sciences, enjoys great popularity.

From the mid-1980s, an experimental research program on torsion fields was conducted on a wide scale in the USSR under the direction of the State Committee of the Council of Ministers of the USSR for Science and Technology. Initially the research was classified (with the participation of the KGB and the Ministry of Defense), and then — from 1989 to 1991 — it was conducted openly. The most important institution involved in the declassified research was at first the Center for Non-Traditional Technologies (from 1989 to 1991), and then the International Scientific-Technical Center "Vent" headed by Anatoly Akimov. In July 1991, soon after the founding of the International Scientific-Technical Center "Vent" and the imposition upon it of the duty of implementing the torsion field research program, at a session of the Committee on Science and Technology of the Supreme Soviet of the USSR, this program was recognized as pseudoscientific and closed down soon after the collapse of the Soviet Union.

The concept of torsion fields was used by Akimov in the phenomenological concept of "EGS states," and by Shipov in his "theory of the physical vacuum." Under Akimov's direction, a group was formed in 1989 that began research as the State Center for Non-Traditional Technologies under the State Committee of the Council of Ministers for Science and Technology.

Gennady Shipov

The Center for Non-Traditional Technologies, and later the "Vent" Center, cooperated with many scientific institutions, including academies of sciences. In 1991, after a scandal initiated by accusations of falsifying research results and misappropriating state finances, an investigation was conducted, which by its resolution of 4 July 1991 definitively characterized the research as pseudoscientific and anti-scientific, and the theories of Akimov and his supporters as "contradictory to notions definitively established by contemporary science, and — regardless of the quasi-scientific terminology employed — unfounded, unjustified logically and scientifically." It was noted that millions of rubles had been spent on supporting torsion field research (according to Akimov's own claims, 500 million), yet at the same time "the scientific community was not acquainted with these discoveries either in an official manner or in 'underground' publications (there exists only one publication, which, moreover, was recognized as refuted)," and in connection with this, the discontinuation of research funding was ordered. Member of the Academy of Sciences Kruglyakov, in a report delivered at the Presidium of the Russian Academy of Sciences, [noted that] the group of scientists was disbanded and Akimov dismissed, after which Akimov "organized a small enterprise with the proud name of the International Institute of Theoretical and Applied Physics under the Russian Academy of Natural Sciences." According to Akimov's version, no one — in connection with the scandal mentioned — dissolved the Center for Non-Traditional Technologies or the "Vent" Center, and the International Institute of Theoretical and Applied Physics, later transformed into the private company "Juvitor," constitutes a direct continuation of that group. Within these institutions Akimov and Shipov continued their independent work on the development of their concepts of torsion fields.

After this brief historical sketch, we present a translation of an interview with Gennady Shipov conducted by Galina Gusyeva, editor-in-chief of the journal "Other Shores" [Innye Berega]. In her texts, Galina Gusyeva makes unexpected comparisons — for example, between Goethe's Faust and quantum genetics, or between Pushkin's Eugene Onegin and the theory of the physical vacuum.

These comparisons bear unexpected fruit and show that the world in which we live is a unity, regardless of the points of view from which we examine it. The concept of the physical vacuum arose at the beginning of the 20th century in connection with the discoveries of theoretical physics. On the one hand, general relativity was created; on the other, quantum field theory.

Galina Gusyeva: That, at least, one can understand.

Gennady Shipov: Without a physics education?

Galina Gusyeva: I think so.


— In any case, we now have the right to assert that the fundamental postulates of religion are a physical fact. The point is that every form of matter, everything we observe around us, including ourselves, was born of the vacuum.


— So people are, in essence, emptiness?

— Indeed. We are emptiness. We are an excited, strictly organized state of the physical vacuum. Of course, I am speaking of the picture of the world as contemporary physics presents it to itself. According to the assumptions of contemporary physics, all present particles were born of emptiness. We now assume that the physical vacuum is the lowest energy state of all forms of matter. From this lowest state arise particles, from which in turn atoms are formed, molecules, organic and inorganic substances — in a word, everything.


— Like a womb from which the entire Universe arises, including the human being?

— Precisely so. In general, the vacuum is neutral. It possesses no charge, no mass, nor other parameters. On average, in every respect, it is zero. Nevertheless, this zero possesses certain properties contained within its interior (the interior of this vacuum), which allow it to give birth, from itself alone, to all kinds of matter.


— What are these properties that allow the vacuum to give birth to matter?

— The point is that the vacuum contains within itself a certain Plan. That is, matter has not yet appeared, but there exists a certain plan according to which this matter is born and takes shape. A kind of matrix of possibilities. Inside the vacuum.


— Where does this plan come from?

— That is the question. It has existed eternally, and for the time being we cannot say where it came from. We can say that it was created by a certain absolute mind, or, if you prefer, a Supermind. But the Supermind possesses such qualities as do not yet lend themselves to our analysis and our logic.


— What does "for the time being" mean? And what does "our logic" mean? Do you have in mind humanity as a whole, or the logic of contemporary science?

— I have in mind the Western type of logic, or the logic of Aristotle. It is very simple, dual, and possesses only two states: yes and no. That is the logic we employ. Science employs it — Western science, which is based on experiments. And on the basis of these experiments, physicists and other scholars build a picture of the world.


— So pure empiricism?

— Yes. That picture of the world which is now published in all the textbooks is a picture based on the logic of Aristotle, that is, on two concepts: "yes and no." In reality there also exists in the world a non-dualistic logic based on many other dimensions. That is why, in this sense, we have the simplest picture of the world. Reality is evidently constructed more complexly, and in the future we will use logics of a higher class. But now let us return to the physical vacuum. The one I am engaged with today leads us to the conclusion that there exist seven levels of reality. Of these, contemporary science studies only four.


— Let us be precise. We are still speaking of physical reality.

— The lowest level is solid bodies. The table, the chair, hard coal. Already in primary school we learn to examine the behavior of solid bodies according to Newtonian mechanics. The next level is the liquid state — liquids. Then come gases. And finally, plasma. The whole end of the 19th century and the whole 20th century, science was occupied with the analysis of plasma. Plasma in the broad sense, that is, elementary particles. Contemporary physics, its avant-garde, studies the theory of elementary particles. At the same time it was understood where these elementary particles come from: they are born of the vacuum, that is, of absolute zero. Of emptiness. And it is precisely the physical vacuum that is the fifth level of reality.


— Which gives birth to the material world.

— You are absolutely right.


— And what is more, this matter does not arise somehow chaotically, but is created according to a strictly defined plan: this form of matter and not another. With such properties, such structure, and such possibilities of development and transformation.

— Exactly. This fifth state of reality is precisely the matrix of possibilities. A certain plan according to which matter arises. It would seem that zero is always equal to zero. It should not contain any information within itself. Yet it turns out to be saturated with information. The question arises: whose plan is this? Who implanted this information? Regardless of whether a person is a physicist or not, this constitutes for them the most important question. But the fifth level is not all. Above the vacuum state there exists a sixth level.


— What is that?

— It is the level of primary torsion fields. That is what we have named them. They are certain primary whirlwinds, which do not constitute matter in the common understanding.


— Matter in the common understanding? Forgive me, but we parted with matter at a lower level. We have already passed through emptiness, the vacuum, and zero containing information — and now matter again? And in some extraordinary sense?

— Yes. It is not matter in the ordinary sense, since it is not capable of carrying energy. It is capable, however, of carrying information. These are informational whirlwinds. Informational fields.


— One could say that this is knowledge as such?

— One could say that this is the knowledge of the Supermind.


— So these whirlwinds are, as it were, a means of transport. The Supermind uses them to saturate the vacuum with information? The vacuum, as it were, receives a concrete assignment?

— Yes. These whirlwinds serve it to shape the Universe and to govern it. These primary torsion fields precede the vacuum state of matter. And that is not all. There exists a seventh level. It is the level of the absolute Nothing. But this "Nothing" must be written in quotation marks. Mathematically it can be expressed by the algebraic identity transformation "zero is identically equal to zero." That is, the "Nothing" is, as it were, absolutely empty from the point of view of our dual logic.


— In other words, we can in no way understand it?

— Of course.


— But after all, it is not emptiness.

— It is another level of emptiness. The highest. It turns out that emptiness in itself contains a certain trinity: the absolute "Nothing," the primary torsion fields, and the vacuum. And those levels, with the help of Aristotle's dual logic, we are able only to distinguish.


— But surely another logic exists. And a human logic — for example, the logic of the East.

— Indeed. And there are people who, with the help of that other, non-dual logic, are able to operate upon that "Nothing." That is, they see certain structures within that "Nothing."


— Are they able to enter into interaction with the seventh level?! Who are these people?

— They are people who possess higher logics.


— Who are they?

— They are people raised within the framework of Eastern philosophy. Tibetan monks, enlightened people — such as, for example, the author of the book "Space, Time, and Knowledge," the Tibetan monk Tarthang Tulku. These people possess the ability to consider this subject far more subtly and deeply. We thought that Western science constitutes the crowning of human thought. In reality it is only one of the possible variants.


— And one of the rather barbaric variants.

— Rather barbaric. In principle there exist two strategic approaches to cognizing reality. The Western, inductive one — when, from the experimental particular, we proceed toward the general picture. The second variant, the Eastern one, is the deductive variant — when, from knowledge of some great truth, some general law, we proceed toward an understanding of the particular.


— In one publication, Valentin Nepomnyashchy (Pushkin's biographer) touched upon this motif. He said that for the person of the East, the world is a single whole, within which that person conducts their operations — dividing and multiplying. Whereas for the person of the West, the world is a series of units, a sum of particulars with which they conduct their operations — adding and subtracting. He also stated that the Russian, Eurasian consciousness, as it were, joins both approaches.

— Exactly. I must absolutely say how extraordinarily symptomatic it is that precisely in Russia there was born a science joining both approaches — the Western and the Eastern way of thinking.


— What science is that?

— We are speaking of it right now. It is called the Theory of the Physical Vacuum.


— When was it born?

— If we speak of the official date of its birth, it was the year 1988 — the millennium of the Baptism of Rus'. It was precisely in that year that the fundamental physical principle upon which this science rests was formulated, and the mathematical equation was derived. For the first time, also, a lecture was delivered in Tomsk at the Russian Conference on General Theories of Gravitational Relativity.


— Who delivered the lecture?

— I did, as its author. And in 1993 I published a book. Perhaps the most important conclusion flowing from this science is that consciousness constitutes a part of the world. Formerly the notion prevailed that matter exists, and consciousness is its product. A product of our brain. Just as bile is a product of the liver. Now we already say: there exists a physical vacuum and a plan for the creation of matter. Matter does not yet exist, but the plan is already there. That is, matter and consciousness interpenetrate one another and cannot be separated. There exists a certain synthesis, but in certain moments matter manifests itself so strongly that consciousness recedes into the background, and we cease to notice its role in these processes. If we speak of the beginning of the creation of matter, then we are dealing with consciousness, or information, which is the representative of some higher factor.


— That is, of an Intention.

— Precisely, an Intention. It is primary and determines everything.


— So the Universe, and that is all. Both the Universe and we, and simultaneously our thought about it.

— Let me try to transfer this onto the plane of aesthetics. We were taught that in art there exist form and content. Two separate beings. They were perceived and analyzed separately. Although no one has ever seen content separately, nor form separately. It is a kind of absurdity.


— Whereas in reality there exists the real work — one being, one artistic gesture, one utterance.

— You have put that well. Now, in science, there has appeared the officially recognized term "psychophysics." Behind it stands the notion that changes in the real world are brought about by consciousness. Contrary to common logic. This confirms the phenomena of telekinesis, levitation, clairvoyance, and so on — that is, the initiator of changes is consciousness. This term has been repeated many times and in many places. But the definition of psychophysics as the action of a developed consciousness — and not of milk-and-water magic — comes from Russia. I worked on this, along with Anatoly Akimov. He is a physicist. Both of us graduated from the physics faculty at Moscow State University. Our consciousness creates, effects changes in the material world without physical actions on our part. As a result of our research, we came to the conclusion that the carriers of this acting consciousness are precisely the primary torsion fields — informational whirlwinds. That sixth level, which we sometimes call subtle matter.


— Does this knowledge carry anything practical for people?

— Of course. This knowledge significantly influences people's lives.


— In what way?

— This knowledge was given to humanity before Western science reached it. I emphasize: it was given. In the form of religion.


— Each of the known religions contains this knowledge within itself.

— Each. It is knowledge of the laws of the Cosmos. Of the laws of cosmic ethics, of the laws regulating interpersonal relations and the relation to one's surroundings.

— The Commandments too. This concerns the domain of consciousness. When we speak of the seven levels, the three upper ones — and especially the sixth, the torsion whirlwinds — constitute the carrier of consciousness.
