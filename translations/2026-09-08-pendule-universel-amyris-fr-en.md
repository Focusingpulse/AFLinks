### Characteristics of the Pendulum

The Universal Pendulum is made of beech wood. Its diameter is 60 mm and its weight is 75 g. It is suspended by a cord that is knotted to a semi-circular non-ferrous handle. The length of the cord is marked by three colored beads — red, green, and blue — representing the three meridians:

- Electric (E)
- Magnetic (M)
- Electromagnetic (EM)
