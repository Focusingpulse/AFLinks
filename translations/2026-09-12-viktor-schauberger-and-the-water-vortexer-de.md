# Viktor Schauberger and the Water Vortexer

Viktor Schauberger was an inventor, natural philosopher, and para-scientist. He lived from 1885 to 1958 in Austria. Through his attentive observations of nature and its regularities, he arrived at hypotheses that contradicted the generally accepted natural scientific laws and conventions. Schauberger first gained fame through the development of innovative log flume systems, which reduced transport costs by 90%.

## Thesis on the Lawfulness of Water Movement

The foundation for his further discoveries was Viktor Schauberger's thesis on the lawfulness of water movement. This proceeds from the premise that nature works with two types of movement. It applies centrifugal forces to break something down and centripetal forces to build something up.

The centrifugal forces correspond to the principle of explosion and are directed from inside to outside. This is a destructive, negative form of energy. The centripetal forces correspond to the principle of implosion and are directed from outside to inside. This type of energy is creative and positive.

Viktor Schauberger discovered that the natural movement of water is spiral-shaped and water vortices move from outside to inside. However, through human influence, water is subject to an unnatural flow from inside to outside. Centrifugal forces arise instead of centripetal forces, which alter the energetic constitution of the water.

Generally, all forms of energy generation by humans are based on the explosion principle rather than the implosion principle.
