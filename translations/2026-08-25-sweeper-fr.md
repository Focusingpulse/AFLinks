## Translated Abstract

This deposit presents two foundational works establishing the theoretical framework of the Wave Universe Theory (TUO), a new unified approach to vacuum physics and gravitation.

### I. Classical Formalism and the Tension Field Tv

The first article (*"Classical Formalisms and Correspondence with TUO: From Maxwell and Einstein to the Vacuum Tension Field Tv"*) establishes the mathematical structure of TUO by introducing the scalar Vacuum Tension field (Tv). The dynamics of Tv are derived from a covariant Lagrangian (L_TUO), allowing field equations to be deduced via the principle of least action. The formal correspondence with classical equations (Maxwell and Einstein) is made explicit, showing that the Vacuum Energy-Momentum Tensor (T_μν(vacuum)) replaces the cosmological constant (Λg_μν) with a dynamic and local term. Enriched mathematical demonstrations (Friedmann-TUO equations, linear perturbations, Noether current) prove the complete integration of TUO within the framework of General Relativity and provide the tools necessary for the cosmological modeling of dark energy.

### II. Conceptual Bridge and Origin of Tv

The second article (*"From Confined Electromagnetic Field to Cosmic Vacuum Tension: A Bridge Between Michaud's Theory and TUO?"*) provides the physical foundation of the Tv field. It proposes that vacuum tension is not an arbitrary fundamental field, but the macroscopic and homogenized result of the internal electromagnetic (EM) energy of elementary particles (Michaud model). This conceptual bridge allows the gravitational field (g) to be derived locally as the negative gradient of vacuum tension (g = −∇Tv). The article thus demonstrates the transition from confined electromagnetism to gravitation, and justifies the physical interpretation of Tv as a geometric property of the state of the medium, fundamental to unification.

## Key Concepts

1. **Vacuum Tension (Tv):** A scalar field replacing the cosmological constant with a dynamic, local term. Derived from a covariant Lagrangian via the principle of least action.

2. **Vacuum Energy-Momentum Tensor (T_μν(vacuum)):** Replaces Λg_μν in Einstein's equations. Makes dark energy a field quantity rather than a constant.

3. **Gravity from vacuum tension:** g = −∇Tv. Gravity is the negative gradient of vacuum tension — a local, derivable quantity rather than a geometric postulate.

4. **Michaud model bridge:** Internal electromagnetic energy of elementary particles is the microscopic source of macroscopic vacuum tension. This connects confined EM (particle interiors) to gravitation (cosmological scale).

5. **Friedmann-TUO equations:** Modified Friedmann equations incorporating the Tv field, enabling cosmological modeling with dynamic dark energy rather than a fixed Λ.

6. **Noether current:** Derived for the TUO Lagrangian, establishing conservation laws within the theory.
