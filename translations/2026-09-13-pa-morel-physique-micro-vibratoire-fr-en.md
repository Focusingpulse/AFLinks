# P. A. Morel (Paul-André Morel) — The Post-Chaumery Consolidator

*Translated dossier (FR→EN) by Forge (translation-qc agent), 2026-09-13. Sources: Editions Servranx "Barre atlante" product page (French); the Avertissement of A. de Bélizal & P. A. Morel, Physique Micro-Vibratoire et Forces Invisibles (Desforges, 1976 ed., via rexresearch1.com PDF — translated passages quoted below); dervy-almora.fr and editions-tredaniel.com book pages for Radiesthésie, vibrations et ondes nocives (Dervy, 2015); the idoc.tips excerpt of Introduction à la physique radiesthésique et micro-vibratoire (Servranx, 2003) incl. the La Radiesthésie pour Tous editorial notes on the Bombe C.30 (Aug 1963 article); books-by-isbn.com author bibliography. Companion to my Chaumery (2026-09-11-leon-chaumery-radiesthesie-fr-en.md), de Belizal (2026-09-12-andre-de-belizal-radiesthesie-fr-en.md), and patent (2026-09-11-chaumery-belizal-patent-fr816132-fr-en.md) dossiers.*

**A note on sources:** unlike Chaumery, de Belizal, Bouly, Mermet, or Viré, no biographical notice of Morel (dates, profession, death) exists in the fetchable record — he has no Wikipedia page, no obituary online, no society notice. What survives is his *work*: his own words in the books, the editorial testimony of the journals that published him, and the apparatus that still carries his corrections. This dossier therefore reconstructs his profile from primary-text fragments and publisher records, and flags clearly where the record is silent.

---

## 1. Who he was — as far as the record goes

**Paul-André Morel**, almost always cited as **"P. A. Morel"**, was a French researcher in physical radiesthesia. No birth or death dates are verifiable from fetchable sources. He appears in the record in one role: the researcher who, after **Léon Chaumery's death on 27 February 1957**, joined **André de Belizal** and carried the Chaumery–de Belizal form-wave research program forward for nearly two more decades.

The Servranx catalogue describes him as "longtemps chercheur aux côtés d'André de Bélizal" — *a long-time researcher at André de Belizal's side* — and as co-author of the tradition's treatises (Servranx, French):

> "La Barre atlante, dans sa forme actuelle, revue et corrigée par le chercheur P. A. Morel, longtemps chercheur aux côtés d'André de Bélizal et tous deux auteurs de Physique radiesthésique et micro-vibratoire (Éditions Servranx, Bruxelles, 2003 – un traité sur les ondes de formes)..."
>
> *"The Barre atlante, in its current form, revised and corrected by the researcher P. A. Morel, a long-time researcher at André de Belizal's side, both authors of Physique radiesthésique et micro-vibratoire (Éditions Servranx, Brussels, 2003 — a treatise on form waves)..."*

The Kook Science wiki entry on de Belizal names him **"Paul-André Morel"** and lists him third, after Chaumery, in the school that developed the *ondes de forme* hypothesis and the claimed discovery of the "alpha ray" or **negative green ray** as a curative force.

## 2. The V+ antidote — Morel's entry into the story (from the Avertissement of Physique Micro-Vibratoire, translated)

The most important primary text for Morel's role is the Avertissement (foreword) of *Physique Micro-Vibratoire et Forces Invisibles*, signed **A. de Belizal**. De Belizal states that the book is the fruit of thirty years of research and the natural continuation of the 1939 volume (reissued 1956 as *Essai de radiesthésie vibratoire*) written with Léon Chaumery, "taken, alas, prematurely from our affection on 27 February 1957" — the famous passage in which de Belizal reports that Chaumery was "victime de ses expériences et complètement déshydraté par notre rayon vert négatif (V-)" (*a victim of his own experiments, completely dehydrated by our negative green ray V-*). Then comes Morel's entry (translated):

> "A cette époque nous n'avions pas encore trouvé la vibration antidote, découverte depuis lors avec M. P. A. Morel qui a bien voulu se pencher sur nos travaux et nous assurer son concours précieux."
>
> *"At that time we had not yet found the antidote vibration — it was discovered afterwards with M. P. A. Morel, who was kind enough to look into our work and give us his precious assistance."*

And further (translated):

> "C'est avec Léon Chaumery, il y a quelque trente ans, que nous avons commencé nos recherches. C'est maintenant avec M. P. A. Morel que nous avons ensemble repensé le problème des forces invisibles et jeté sur le papier ces quelques notes..."
>
> *"It was with Léon Chaumery, some thirty years ago, that we began our research. It is now with M. P. A. Morel that we have together rethought the problem of the invisible forces and put these notes on paper..."*

This is the hinge of the whole school's history: after the negative green ray killed its own co-discoverer, **Morel is the one credited with finding the protective/antidote vibration (V+)** that made the work survivable — and with re-founding the theory in the 1965 book. The Dervy publisher's description of the 2015 reissue (Radiesthésie, vibrations et ondes nocives) credits the pair jointly with the practical applications: "ils ont découvert et isolé le rayon alpha, guérisseur de nombreuses affections" (*they discovered and isolated the alpha ray, healer of numerous ailments*), and stresses that their detection and protection apparatus "sont aujourd'hui encore considérés comme novateurs" (*are still considered innovative today*).

## 3. The books — the consolidation of the school

Morel's bibliography (from books-by-isbn.com, lalibrairie.com, and the publishers):

1. **Physique Micro-Vibratoire et Forces Invisibles** — with A. de Belizal. Paris: Éditions Desforges, first published 1965 (Snippet view on Google Books dates to 1965; the widely-circulated printing is 1976), 217 pp. The main treatise: the sphere spectrum decomposition, the negative green (V-) at point 0–400, the form-wave theory, and the apparatus chapters. The Desforges catalogue page (reproduced in the PDF) lists the full instrument line sold at the Librairie Desforges: Pendule Universel, Pendule Égyptien, Disque électromagnétique à sélecteur (dit **Escargot**), Pyramide de Chéops au 1/1000e, Pendule à cône fictif, Détecteur I.R.-U.V., Biometre spécial, **Rééquilibreur de force (dit LOUKSORK)**, Antiparasiteur magnétique, Aspironde, Micromètre, Règle biométrique, **Bague Atlante**.
2. **Introduction à la physique radiesthésique et micro-vibratoire: ondes de formes, techniques, appareils** — with A. de Belizal (and the Servranx editors). Brussels: Éditions Servranx, 2003 (ISBN 2-87242-101-7 / 978-2-87242-101-5), 132 pp. An accessible re-edition/introduction to the two treatises, compiled from the La Radiesthésie pour Tous magazine articles, for readers who found the main works "hermétiques."
3. **Radiesthésie, vibrations et ondes nocives** — with A. de Belizal. Dervy, April 2015 (ISBN 979-1-02-420090-3, 270 pp.; ebook 2016). The Dervy reissue of the pair's collected reflections — the publisher's blurb frames them as "pionniers dans la recherche des ondes de forme et dans ce que nous appelons aujourd'hui les ondes nocives, qu'ils nommaient, à leur époque, les forces invisibles."
4. **Pendule universel PU-6** — Servranx, 2006 (ISBN 978-2-87242-107-7), with Bernd Schäfer, de Belizal, and Chaumery — the practical manual for the Universal Pendulum, still in the Servranx catalogue.

## 4. The apparatus articles — C.30 Bomb and Escargot-sélecteur (La Radiesthésie pour Tous, Aug 1963)

The Servranx *Introduction* volume preserves the editorial testimony of **La Radiesthésie pour Tous**, the radiesthesia magazine that serialized the pair's work in the early 1960s. The editor's note (N.d.l.r., translated):

> "Les travaux de MM. Chauméry et de Bélizal avaient, dès avant guerre, ouvert un nouveau chapitre de la radiesthésie : l'étude physique des influences de formes et de couleurs... C'est avec M. Morel que M. de Bélizal poursuit ces recherches depuis la mort de M. Chaumery."
>
> *"The work of MM. Chauméry and de Bélizal had, from before the war, opened a new chapter of radiesthesia: the physical study of the influences of forms and colors... It is with M. Morel that M. de Bélizal has pursued this research since the death of M. Chaumery."*

On the **Bombe C.30** (the pair's radionics emitter, dated by the journal to **August 1963**), the editor wrote (translated):

> "La Bombe C.30 est l'une des nombreuses révélations de l'ouvrage Physique micro-vibratoire de MM. A. de Bélizal et P.A. Morel. Dans le domaine des ondes de formes, c'est l'une des réalisations les plus avancées. Et dans celui de la radionique, c'est une conception entièrement originale, qui repose sur l'étude minutieuse et la mise en oeuvre d'énergies exactement calculées pour des effets précis."
>
> *"The C.30 Bomb is one of the many revelations of the work Physique micro-vibratoire by MM. A. de Bélizal and P. A. Morel. In the domain of form waves, it is one of the most advanced realizations. And in radionics, it is an entirely original conception, resting on the minute study and the implementation of energies exactly calculated for precise effects."*

The same apparatus passage in the book itself describes the C.30 as a "séparateur de fréquences vibratoires par sélection" (*a separator of vibratory frequencies by selection*) with a pivoting copper arrow, and its measuring sibling the **Escargot** ("snail") — perfected from Chaumery's initial Escargot, with its neutral pendulum — as the selector apparatus for the emitted spectrum. The journal also credits the pair with a dual-capacity instrument: "il peut tester la valeur physique d'un individu, donc contrôler son état de santé, mais aussi la valeur morale de celui-ci" (*it can test the physical value — the health state — of an individual, but also his moral value*), a claim aimed at employers and personnel managers "de l'échelon le plus élevé jusqu'au simple manoeuvre" (*from the highest level down to the simple laborer*) — a revealing window into the 1963 framing of the instruments.

## 5. The Barre Atlante and the Louksor — Morel's own correction

The Barre Atlante (Atlante Bar) is the protective wooden bar still sold by Editions Servranx (€102, meranti wood, 38.5 × 5.7 cm). Its documented lineage, per Servranx (translated):

> "La barre... vient d'une bague découverte en 1860 par le marquis d'Agrain et qui aurait été transmise à André de Bélizal par son épouse qui était la petite-fille du marquis."
>
> *"The bar... comes from a ring discovered in 1860 by the Marquis d'Agrain, which was said to have been passed to André de Bélizal by his wife, who was the marquis's granddaughter."*

Here Morel's contribution is technical and — by the school's own theory — safety-critical (translated):

> "Si la bague est exceptionnelle dans sa forme propre, P. A. Morel a découvert que sa forme mise à plat et agrandie se saturait au point de produire du vert négatif. Il a donc analysé le problème, modifié les extrémités, jusqu'à lui donner sa forme actuelle qui n'a plus cet inconvénient et représente le nec plus ultra en matière de protection contre les ondes nocives."
>
> *"Exceptional as the ring is in its own form, P. A. Morel discovered that its form, flattened and enlarged, saturated to the point of producing negative green. He therefore analyzed the problem and modified the ends until he gave it its current form, which no longer has this drawback and represents the ne plus ultra in protection against noxious waves."*

Morel named the bar **"Louksor"** — after a model from an Egyptian sarcophagus — and the claimed operating range, quoted by Servranx from the book (translated): "L'action de cet appareil est théoriquement effective dans une zone limite de 20 à 30 mètres autour de son axe, mais pratiquement, il faut seulement retenir une zone efficace de 15 à 20 mètres" (*theoretically effective in a limit zone of 20–30 meters around its axis; practically, retain an effective zone of 15–20 meters*), within which "toutes les 'ruptures de force', qu'elles soient d'ordre tellurique ou magnétique, sont compensées et rééquilibrées" (*all 'force ruptures,' whether telluric or magnetic, are compensated and rebalanced*). The bar was later tested and publicized by Jacques La Maya in *Médecine de l'habitat* — the main channel by which Morel's correction reached the geobiology mainstream.

## 6. The theory Morel inherited and extended

From the Physique Micro-Vibratoire text itself (translated passages from the PDF edition):

- **The spectrum in the sphere:** the negative green (V-) "représente le point 0–400... une vibration mystérieuse, véritable trait d'union entre la vie et la mort. Elle a puissance sur la matière, brûle, détruit... mais peut aussi conserver la Vie en puissance hors de l'espace et le temps. Cette vibration servait aux Egyptiens pour déshydrater leurs morts" (*the mysterious vibration, a true bond between life and death; it has power over matter, burns, destroys... but can also preserve Life in potentiality outside space and time; the Egyptians used this vibration to dehydrate their dead*). The 1936 patent (FR 816,132, with Chaumery) is cited as the school's protected claim on the ray.
- **The measurement problem:** the authors situate form waves, in energy terms, near the magnetic waves accompanying cosmic rays — "de l'ordre de millionièmes et milliardièmes d'électrons-volts" (*on the order of millionths and billionths of electron-volts*) — and concede that no physical instrument then existed sensitive enough to record them: "Le jour où l'on pourrait trouver un tel système de mesures, nous aurions moins besoin de la radiesthésie pour les ondes de forme qui sont davantage du domaine de la physique pure, et deviendraient ainsi partie d'une science exacte. Mais nous n'en sommes pas encore là" (*the day such a measurement system is found, we would need radiesthesia less, for form waves would become part of an exact science. But we are not there yet*). This is the school's own honest boundary: the pendulum stands in for the missing instrument.
- **The biological claim:** vibrations "ayant sur elles une action déséquilibrante certaine pouvant être à la base de diverses formes de cancers" (*having a certain destabilizing action on [cells], possibly at the basis of various forms of cancer*) — the claim that made the detection-and-protection apparatus a matter of health, and the reason the V+ antidote mattered so much to the school's own survival.

## 7. Where the record ends

The last documented Morel imprint is the 2006 Servranx PU-6 manual (posthumous by most reckonings, since de Belizal died in 1975 — the Servranx house re-edited the pair's work for decades). The Dervy 2015/2016 reissues keep both names on the cover. Beyond that: no dates, no portrait, no grave marker in the online record. What Morel left is the **consolidation** — the 1965 book that turned Chaumery and de Belizal's pre-war discoveries into a system, the **antidote** that made the negative green work survivable, and the **corrected forms** (Barre Atlante/Louksor, Escargot-sélecteur, C.30) that the French geobiology and radionics trades still sell and use today.
