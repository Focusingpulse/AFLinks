# Color Vibrations of the Spectrum, According to Chaumery and Bélizal

### Everything in the universe is energy, everything emits a vibration.

The color vibrations of the spectrum, according to Chaumery and Bélizal, are divided into 2 × 12 colors:

- 12 colors in magnetic phase
- and 12 colors in electrical phase

Research has demonstrated that the 12 colors in magnetic phase are pro-biotic — favorable to life and health — and that the 12 colors in electrical phase are anti-biotic — harmful, unfavorable to health.

- Vert – (Negative Green)
- Noir (Black)
- Infra-rouge (Infrared)
- Rouge (Red)
- Orange
- Jaune (Yellow)
- Vert + (Positive Green)
- Bleu (Blue)
- Indigo
- Violet
- Ultra-Violet
- Blanc (White)

To ensure that something (food, drink, place, clothing, art object, device, etc.) is perfectly pro-biotic, one verifies with the pendulum that the spectrum is smooth and in magnetic phase — that is, that all color vibrations are represented in magnetic phase.

### Healing Waves — Direction of Rotation

#### Vert – (Negative Green) → Vert – (Negative Green)
#### Noir (Black) → Noir (Black)
#### Indigo → Indigo
#### Violet → Négatif (Negative)
#### Violet → Ultra-Violet
#### Ultra-Violet → Positif (Positive)

With the pendulum, the basic question is:

"What is the visible or invisible color in electrical or magnetic phase of this object, this person, this place?" — while pendulating over the dial.

One can also query each visible or invisible color in the order given above.

Place your left hand near what you want to test, enumerate the colors (Anti-Green, Black, Infrared... up to White) and observe which direction the pendulum rotates:

- Negative: to the left, counterclockwise
- or Positive: to the right, clockwise
