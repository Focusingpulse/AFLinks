# Energetic Circuit and Study of the Hotie de Viviane - Paimpont - 35

14/10/2016

## Introduction

In September 2016, I conducted a study of part of the geobiological and sacred geobiology phenomena observable at the Hotie de Viviane, in the land of Broceliande, at Paimpont, 40 km southwest of Rennes.

It is a destroyed structure that served as a tomb. It is low and small. It is surrounded by a circle of stones.

I was able to observe numerous phenomena there, and established good communication with the guardian of the site.

The place seems unimpressive when one sees it, especially compared to the great menhirs. But the energies that pass through it are powerful.

## Geobiological Study:

### Hartmann, Curry, and Peyre Currents:

- A positive North/South Hartmann network of level 3 crosses through the center of the hotie.
- Two Curry networks intersect at the center of the hotie, both positive: one of level 2 on the SW-NE axis and one of level 3 on the NW-SE axis. The latter is surely the strongest and densest current at the site.
- A Peyre network of level 3 covers the entire width of the hotie, in the East/West direction. Its capacity to recharge the 4th chakra, the heart chakra, is particularly pleasant.

### Other Networks:

- A Brass line (very negative; it closes all chakras) crosses the stone circle.
- A current of yellow energy from the ground follows a curve near the path.

### Watercourse, CT Chimneys, Vortices:

- A watercourse passes beneath the hotie from east to west.
- A Cosmo-Telluric chimney has an arm that enters the circle from the south side, and the tip of its arm provides a point of negative polarity.
- An arm of a vortex arriving from farther away enters the circle. It rotates clockwise (masculine).
- A vortex has its heart in the center of the hotie and its spiral rotates counterclockwise (feminine). At point "A" on the plan, the two vortex arms touch and create a very pleasant zone where the two energies mingle.

### Etheric Objects:

- I noticed a pinkish dome that followed the stone circle and rises to about 2 meters above the hotie.

### Vibratory Rate:

- The vibratory rate I found there is very high: over 10,000%. It is of course at the center of the hotie that the energy is most powerful.

## Sacred Geobiology:

He spoke to me of presences that position themselves in the stones of the central rectangle, whose hands emerge to heal persons who are in a therapeutic process and in need of care. ### Presences at the Site:

- Dwarves and fairies (etheric) came to see us during our investigations. They frequent the place.
- A strong feminine presence appeared at one point, at the center of the hotie.

## Energetic Circuit:

### Introduction:

- This circuit is a crescendo of energies and pleasantly alternates points of negative polarity (connection with earth energies) and points of positive polarity (connection with the sky).

### Purpose:

- Cleansing of our energy bodies, recharging with energy.

### Duration:

- 10 to 15 minutes

### Description:

- Greet the presences of the place and the place itself, be in consciousness.
- Enter by point "D," walk very slowly to complete in 5 to 10 minutes, according to your feeling, 3 and a half concentric rounds (begin by following the stone circle along the inner edge) counterclockwise (a direction unusual for sacred sites). When you feel the need, stop for a few seconds. The most classic circuits often alternate pauses of one minute on positive points and 30 seconds on negative points. On the last round, walk as close as possible to the stones of the central rectangle.
- Enter the hotie by point "B." You can either remain standing at its center, preferably facing north, or lie down (if possible), preferably with your head to the west. Stay according to your desire between 3 and 15 minutes in this position. Here you take in a considerable amount of energy. Lying down, your astral body undergoes repair.
- Exit by point "C."
- Make one final round, but clockwise, before exiting by point "D." You can at the same time thank the place, the stones, and the gentle presences. Express the wish to carry all this good energy back into your physical body as you leave.
- Thank again and optionally make offerings (flowers, biscuits, seasonal fruits, incense, ...) and enjoy the good energy bath! It restores your energy for 2 to 10 days depending on your sensitivity.

### Effects of the Circuit on Bioenergy:

- Vibratory rates. We are two who tested this circuit (thank you Fred for the help). For one, the vibratory rate went from 75% to 95%; for the other, from 85% to 98%.
- Bio-fields, also called "vital field," measures one of the layers of our aura. In one case it went from 8m to 24m (x3) and in the other case from 9.5m to 18m (x2) between before the circuit and after the circuit. Note that the rates and biofield sizes were already large, surely due to what the site emits and our presence there for several hours already (clearing the site). Nevertheless, upon exit, they had still doubled, even tripled.

Thanks to the presences and the guardian of the site, as well as all those who allow us to preserve this heritage. You can continue to the Val sans Retour for a most lovely walk in Broceliande! The panorama there is magnificent.

### Access:

From Paimpont, take the direction of Ploermel, then a few kilometers further, turn right where indicated "hotie de Viviane, tombeau des geants" after "Val sans Retour." Access from the parking lot: 800m walk, one way. Not accessible to persons with reduced mobility. Once on the trail at the bottom of the parking lot, go straight ahead and climb the rocks. The hotie is on the other side of the hill. Access also possible directly from the Val sans Retour.
