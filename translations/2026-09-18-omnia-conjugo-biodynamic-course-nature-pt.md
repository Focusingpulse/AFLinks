MEETING NATURE

Man consists of a physical body, an etheric body, etc., etc. — this is something anyone can learn by heart, as from a recipe book. Our task, then, is to be guided to distinguish how Nature is and how it works. … But what is an animal? What is the plant world? For plants we must speak of a totality, not of a single plant. We must discover the essential relations between the kingdoms… You may look at animal forms as much as you please; you may even dissect them and come to see their skeletons — yet none of this will lead you to understand what animals really are in the activity of Nature. You will only understand it if you observe what the animal is within the environment with which it is directly and intimately connected.
— R. Steiner, in the Agriculture Course, lecture 7.

How do we relate to Nature?
Is Nature an outer reflection of our own constitution?
Rudolf Steiner tells us that a farm should be conceived in the image of the human being, as an entity, as an organism. But how do we conceive of an organism, and how do we conceive of an entity?

OMNIA CONJUGO – BIODYNAMIC INSTITUTE

The OMNIA CONJUGO – BIODYNAMIC INSTITUTE is organizing, between Michaelmas 2025 and Pentecost 2026, an online course on the relations between the Cosmos, the Human Being, and Nature, based on Rudolf Steiner's Spiritual Science and using phenomenological observation methods.

Over the course of this program we will approach these themes, acquiring knowledge and living experiences that will allow us to establish a broader relationship with the natural world, with the cosmos, and with ourselves — one that will free us from the shackles of a materialistic and reductionist science.

It is open to anyone with an interest in the subject and will be guided by various instructors.

COST: €200 + VAT (23%). Reductions possible in duly justified cases.
Minimum number of participants: 5 people.
LOCATIONS: See program below.
REGISTRATION: E-mail to biodinamicaportugal@gmail.com by October 7th.

• In addition to the presentation of the themes, this course will include artistic exercises, personal development exercises, Goethean observation exercises, assignments and assessments that will contribute to a genuine, lived experience of the relations with Nature.
• There will be a presentation and sale of books and other publications.
• A certificate of attendance and achievement will be issued to those who request it.
