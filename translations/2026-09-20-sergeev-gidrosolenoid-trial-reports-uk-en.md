## Report 1 — Report on the Clinical Trials of the "Gidrosolenoid" Device (NIMIC «NORT» of the Academy of Sciences of Ukraine)

*Original title: «Звіт про проведення клінічних випробувань пристрою "Гідросоленоїд"» · Posted by the Vega program 2026-08-23.*

Report on the clinical trials of the "Gidrosolenoid" device, developed by Prof. G. A. Sergeev, d.sc. (engineering).

**Customer:** the science-and-technology firm "Graviton," director Ya. I. Pryshchenko.
**Executor:** NIMIC «NORT» of the Academy of Sciences of Ukraine.
**Scientific supervisor:** Prof. V. Ya. Berezovsky, d.sc. (medicine).
**Responsible executor:** Dr. L. H. Nazarova.

The purpose of this study was to examine the effect of the electromagnetic-gravitational field on the condition of the cerebral vessels in persons suffering from various forms of vascular pathology.

The device trials were conducted at the outpatient department of NIMIC «NORT» of the Academy of Sciences of Ukraine from 01.12.1993 to 13.02.1994.

**Trial conditions:**

- The device was installed and prepared for operation by representatives of the "Graviton" firm.
- The trials were conducted in a room with unchanging environmental conditions, except for changes in atmospheric pressure over the trial period.
- Each subject was offered 100–150 ml of structured water to drink before and after the procedure.
- The subject remained under the influence of the device for 20 minutes.
- All subjects completed a course of treatment consisting of 10 sessions.
- Before and after the course of treatment, a rheoencephalogram (REG) was recorded.
- Before and after each session, blood pressure was measured by the Korotkoff method with a mercury tonometer on both arms.
- During the treatment period, the taking of medicinal preparations (especially vascular drugs) was not recommended.

**Participants.** 24 persons took part in the trials (10 men, 14 women), aged 36 to 69, with the following conditions:

- Vegetative-vascular dystonia of the hypo- and hypertensive type — 5 persons.
- Hypertensive disease of stage I–II — 13 persons.
- Hypertensive disease of stage II–III with cerebral-vessel atherosclerosis — 6 persons.

**Baseline (REG findings in all subjects):** disturbances of cerebral circulation in the basins of the vertebral and internal carotid arteries, expressed as follows:

- Reduced blood filling in the vertebral-artery basin — 16 persons; in the internal carotid arteries — 8 persons.
- Asymmetry of blood filling in the internal carotid artery basin — 5 persons.
- Increased tone of the arteries (more often small and medium vertebral) — 15 persons; of the internal carotids — 6 persons; atony of the vertebral artery — 2 persons; preserved arterial tone — 3 persons.
- Venous dyscirculation in the form of a presystolic venous wave in the vertebral artery — 7 persons; in the internal carotid arteries — 5 persons.
- Worsened venous outflow in the vertebral arteries — 9 persons; in the internal carotid arteries — 11 persons.

**Results.**

From questioning about subjective sensations during and after exposure to the Gidrosolenoid field, the following reactions can be noted: warmth in the extremities, slight dizziness, a tingling sensation in individual areas of the body, regional hyperthermia, drowsiness, a feeling of lightness throughout the body.

Analysis of the blood-pressure measurements before and after the procedure revealed **no positive effect of the Gidrosolenoid field on arterial-pressure readings.**

According to the analysis of the REG data obtained after the course of treatment, one can assert a positive effect on the hemodynamic parameters of the cerebral vessels, bringing them close to the individual's normal status. Positive dynamics of hemodynamic parameters were noted in **19 of 24 cases**; absence of dynamics in 3 cases; worsening of dynamics in **2 cases**.

The improvement in hemodynamic parameters was expressed as: improved blood filling of the cerebral vessels in 9 cases; disappearance of venous-dyscirculation signs in 4 cases; normalization or improvement of tone of the vertebral or internal carotid arteries in 10 cases. **Worsening of venous outflow in those same vessels was noted in 8 cases.** The presence of positive dynamics of hemodynamic parameters in persons with organic changes of the cerebral vessels deserves special note — 7 cases.

After completing the course of treatment, patients reported the following: absence of their habitual headache through the day — 8 persons; deep night sleep — 10 persons; a state of increased working capacity — 13 persons; improved emotional state — 11 persons.

Thus, the objective data obtained attest to a positive corrective influence of the Gidrosolenoid field on the condition of the cerebral vessels.

In further in-depth studies of the Gidrosolenoid field, it is expedient to **recommend this device as a means of weak-energy physiotherapy in rehabilitation centers.**

Scientific supervisor: Prof. Ya. M. Berezovsky *(original signature)*
Responsible executor: Dr. L. H. Nazarova *(original signature)*

## Report 2 — Report on the Clinical Use of the "Gidrosolenoid" Device (Sevastopol Center for the Protection of Maternal and Child Health, 1994)

*Original title: «Звіт про клінічне використання пристрою "ГІДРОСОЛЕНОЇД"» · Posted by the Vega program 2026-08-23.*

SEVASTOPOL CENTER FOR THE PROTECTION OF MATERNAL AND CHILD HEALTH
CRIMEAN ERGONOMIC ASSOCIATION

Report on the clinical use of the "Gidrosolenoid" device

**Scientific supervisor:** Chair of the Crimean Ergonomic Association, associate professor V. Sotskov, cand.sc. (engineering).
**Executor:** Head of the functional-diagnostics department, Dr. V. Ionov, cand.sc. (medicine).

Report on the clinical use of the "Gidrosolenoid" device, developed by G. O. Sergeev, d.sc. (engineering), and manufactured by the "Graviton LTD" firm, Cherkasy (director Ya. I. Pryshchenko).

The clinical use of the Gidrosolenoid was conducted at the Sevastopol Center for the Protection of Maternal and Child Health under the direction of the "Human–Machine" Modeling and Systems Administration of the R&D Center of the Sevastopol Naval Institute, from 13.06.1994 to 30.12.1994.

The purpose of this study was to examine the influence of the biogravitational effect on the condition of the cerebral vessels in patients with vegetative-vascular dystonia, and on external-respiration function in patients with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease.

**Participants.** 46 patients (19 men, 27 women) with vegetative-vascular dystonia of the hypertensive type, aged 18–26, and 23 patients (18 men, 15 women) with chronic obstructive bronchitis in a stage of prolonged exacerbation, complicated by respiratory insufficiency of grade 1–2 (after A. H. Dembo).

**Monitoring.** Dynamic monitoring of arterial pressure, the electrocardiogram, cardiointervalograms, the rheoencephalogram, echo-encephaloscopy, and spirograms was carried out **before treatment, on the 5th and 10th days, and after completion of the course** of treatment in the Gidrosolenoid. **For the period of Gidrosolenoid treatment, the patients' intake of medicinal preparations was excluded.**

**Protocol.** Gidrosolenoid treatment lasted 20 days and consisted of 10–20-minute sessions conducted every other day. Before each procedure, each patient took 100–150 ml of structured water orally (water passed through Sergeev's Strukturator).

**Results — VSD group.** After treatment of the vegetative-vascular dystonia patients, positive dynamics of psychological status were noted, with decreased scores on the anxiety, hypochondria, and depression scales of the MMPI personal questionnaire. In parallel, a **reduction of systolic arterial pressure by 9.1 ± 3.1 mmHg** relative to baseline values was registered. Diastolic arterial pressure remained without dynamics. On the cardiointervalograms, a tendency toward the establishment of normotonia was noted, with characteristic changes in the amplitude of the mode, the variation range, and the Baevsky tension index.

On the rheoencephalogram, improvement of hemodynamic parameters was found: in 17 cases this was expressed in the disappearance of venous-dyscirculation signs; in 38 cases in the normalization of tone of the vertebral and internal carotid arteries. In 8 patients, no statistically significant dynamics of the rheoencephalography and echo-encephaloscopy parameters were noted; nevertheless, subjectively these patients reported normalization of sleep, increased working capacity, and improved emotional state.

**Results — bronchitis group.** In the chronic-bronchitis group, 13 patients showed a reduction in the number and severity of bronchial-obstruction attacks, without significant dynamics of external-respiration function.

**Conclusion.** Thus, a complex corrective influence on psychological status and vascular tone was noted in the hypertensive-type vegetative-vascular dystonia patients. A positive influence of the weak-energy Gidrosolenoid device on the frequency of the broncho-obstructive syndrome in chronic-bronchitis patients was also noted, and it is recommended for introduction into the physiotherapy treatment complex for this category of patients; **however, the mechanism by which this effect is realized is subject to further study.**

Head of the functional-diagnostics department, Dr. V. Ionov, cand.sc. (medicine)
