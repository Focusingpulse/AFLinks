# Bricage / AFSCET 2025 — Testing Dowsing Practice: A Meta-Analysis Using Artificial Intelligences and an Experimental Testing Methodology of Dowsing PRACTICE

## 1. What this document is

The fr radiesthesia lane's most complete **practitioner-built, fully-documented double-blind test rig since the Observatoire Zététique estrade era**. Two things ride in one paper:

1. **An LLM-assisted meta-analysis** of 328 papers from 113 journals (built by interrogating the free English versions of ChatGPT4 and DeepSeek-V3 back to ≥1 reference per year since 1884), with a factorial correspondence analysis ranking where positive vs. skeptical vs. inconclusive results get published.
2. **An original experiment**: 14 series over 7 half-days = **62 independent tests** (31 predictions made AFTER the draws, 31 BEFORE), run "like a double blind with placebo" against a simultaneously-operating random predictor, with results scored only after all tests ended.

## 2. The rig (translated from the French methods section)

- **Participants:** two locally-reputed radiesthésistes — **RI**, an elder who works bénévolement ("Le don ne se monnaie pas" — the gift is not monetized), chooses when to participate; **RE**, who works for small gifts, her séances a social occasion ("autour d'un thé") — plus two "naïve" non-scientists (incl. random predictor RH) and two "skeptical but open" scientists.
- **Targets:** colored-ball draws (n = 3 colours VJR, 5 VJROB, or 7), drawn by DE in a separate room. Each draw independent, random success probability 1/3 or 1/5.
- **Blinding:** no participant knows any result until the final dépouillement (scoring took an extra half-day). The design "does the same as" double-blind-with-placebo: the random prediction runs simultaneously as the placebo arm.
- **The calibration doctrine (the paper's methodological core):**
  - *"Ce n'est pas le pendule qui est l'instrument de mesure. L'instrument de mesure c'est l'ensemble individu-pendule"* — the measuring instrument is the human-pendule assembly, which must be **étalonné (calibrated) and initialized as a "système perceptif" at the start of each measurement series**.
  - Questions must be **yes/no only, precisely and univocally worded** — the author draws the analogy to LLM query discipline explicitly: the wording conditions the nature of the answers.
  - **Pendulum fatigue:** "un pendule peut se fatiguer" — stop, switch pendulums, and put the tired one **in charge (recharging) on a planche de radiesthésie** before resuming.
  - **RI's n-question cascade:** for n colours he poses n sequential yes/no questions demanding coherence (at most one yes); if incoherent, he re-calibrates or changes pendulums and starts over.
  - **RE's shortcut:** asks until the first yes, then stops (at least 1, at most n questions) — faster, less precise.

## 3. The reported outcomes

| Condition | RI | RE | Random (RH) |
|---|---|---|---|
| AFTER-draw predictions | **100%** | 60–71% (63–66% per footnote) | 0–40% (13–38%) |
| BEFORE-draw predictions | 33–43% (38–50% per footnote; exc. 66%) | 20–40% (27–50%; exc. 60%) | 20–33% (25–27%) |

The author's probability argument: getting a full coherent cascade by chance would be 1/27 (3 draws of 3 colours) down to 1/2187; RI's cascade of n yes/no answers each at 1/2 would be 1/512 — yet he achieves it. Verdict, stated three times: *"Evidently dowsing is a not-by-chance phenomenon."*

The author concedes up front: the study can be criticized for **small sample size (62 tests) and lack of independent replication** — but, he argues, not for lack of controls or blinding.

## 4. QC flags (mine, extending the scout's)

1. **The après-condition is the fragile joint.** "Predictions made after the draw" is a retro-cognition claim: the ball is already drawn in room X, the dowser elsewhere asks yes/no questions about it. The paper asserts (fig. 7) that known vs. unknown information "doesn't affect results" — **asserted, not demonstrated**. A skeptic re-analysis of exactly this condition is the cheapest test in the fr lane.
2. **The cascade has a stopping-rule circularity.** RI controls when answers are "incoherent" and restarts; each restart discards failures and re-rolls the operator's own state. Treating the completed cascade as independent 1/512 evidence while the operator controls stopping is a validation circularity a reviewer would press immediately.
3. **The meta-analysis reference list shows LLM-hallucination hallmarks.** The "50 papers since 2000" bibliography contains duplicate titles under different authors ("Dowsing and the Paranormal: A Reappraisal" appears 3× — Carter 2018, Clark 2019, Ingram 2001; "Dowsing and the Search for Hidden Resources" 3×; "Dowsing and the Art of Detection" 3×), suspiciously patterned page numbers (456-465, 67-78, 89-97 recur), and journal/volume pairings that do not check out against the real literature (e.g. "Journal of Biofield Research", "Journal of Meta-Analysis"). **The AI-collected bibliography is confabulated even if the pre-2020 classic anchors (Sollas 1884, Mermet 1935, Maby & Franklin 1939, Vogt & Hyman 1959, Rocard 1989, Betz 1995, Enright 1995, Schmidt & Walach 2000) are real.** This matters for the corpus: the paper's experiment is a primary source; its meta-analysis layer is NOT a reliable citation base — do not cite Bricage's reference list, cite the classics directly.
4. **The venue analysis (figure 1) inherits the same weakness** — a correspondence analysis over partly-hallucinated references. Its qualitative conclusion (positives cluster in parapsychology journals, critiques in Skeptical Inquirer, real-world-use correlation in water-resources journals) matches what the genuine literature shows, but the 328-paper base is unverifiable.

- **Companion pieces:** INRS controlled dowsing study (institutional side, 2026-09-16 dossier) and the Eau Hexagonale consumer guide (calibrated position, 2026-09-17) — Bricage is the **practitioner side** of the same triangle: the first fr document showing how practitioners themselves keep the instrument calibrated and handle operator fatigue.
- **The calibration-doctrine layer is now documented across three independent fr sub-schools** (Bricage's individu-pendule étalonnage, the two-control-questions school, Le Cher antenna tuning — fire 107's consensus finding). Bricage is the only one with an experimental protocol attached.
- **The fatigue/recharge protocol** (planche de radiesthésie as "charger") is a testable claim about operator state, not about divination — it connects to the receiver-state design rule from the research rounds (record operator state alongside any reading).
