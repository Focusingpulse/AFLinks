# Form Waves: Meaning, Use, and Benefits

We are surrounded by waves. Invisible, yet crucial to our ways of life, waves are at the center of our possessions, our technologies, from our phones to our televisions, from our medical tools to metal detectors.

If we are familiar with Wi-Fi or 4G, microwaves, ultrasound scans, or sound waves, we are generally less conscious that everything around us possesses a vibratory energy. Everything vibrates at a certain frequency. In the same way that we are invisibly affected by the waves emitted by our phones, we are influenced more subtly by all kinds of vibrations in our daily lives.

For radiesthesia, forms emit or amplify waves -- some beneficial, others conversely negative. These are what are called "form waves" (ondes de forme). Unlike Wi-Fi waves, we are speaking here of electromagnetic radiations or vibratory energy.

## Form Waves in Energetic Harmony, Feng Shui, and Sacred Geometry

The idea that the forms surrounding us possess a vibratory frequency is not new. Nevertheless, this concept is not endorsed by current science in the same way as our telephone waves are.

Science does not currently recognize what radiesthesists call "form waves." Whether it is the qi of the Chinese, the prana of India, or the "form waves" of radiesthesists, what is designated belongs to the realm of vital energy, vibratory by essence. This energy has no scientific definition, even though it is the subject of so much work and lies at the center of complex and ancient religious systems. According to our current knowledge, form waves would not be part of the electromagnetic spectrum.

This scientific ambiguity leads us into the realm of mystery. Yet we possess traces of ancient civilizations that attributed paramount importance to forms. One of the best-known examples is that of the pyramids. In Egypt or Latin America, pyramids are sacred places, points of connection with the divine and the beyond. While many mysteries remain regarding their exact function, one thing is certain: these forms were not chosen by chance. It is quite possible that these civilizations possessed knowledge more advanced than our own on this subject.

### Our Sensitivity to Form Waves

Radiesthesia, Feng Shui, and sacred geometry postulate that the vibratory frequencies surrounding us can have an influence on us. We are more or less sensitive to them. From a very down-to-earth perspective, there are spaces in which we feel good and others in which we do not. We generally have the reflex to arrange our homes, offices, or gardens according to certain rules of aesthetic harmony.

### Form Waves in Feng Shui

Feng Shui, originating in ancient China, is concerned with the energy of spaces and their harmonization. The idea is that in a space structured according to a certain energetic harmony, we ourselves feel in harmony.

Feng Shui harmonizes space by neutralizing potential noxious energies that may be present. To do so, it relies on notions of structure and orientation in the organization of our spaces. Feng Shui is therefore interested in the structure of forms, but also in the balance of the five elements as well as the fluid circulation of energy.

According to Feng Shui, just as for radiesthesia, the vibratory energy surrounding us affects us, both in terms of our well-being and our health or mood. Depending on whether the room we are in has a harmonious shape with pleasing proportions, or conversely is cramped or unbalanced, our vibratory energy is affected.

In the same way, all the forms surrounding us emit a certain type of energy. One pays particular attention to the shape of rooms in a house in Feng Shui and in geobiology.

### Form Waves in Sacred Geometry

When we observe the forms produced by nature, it is fascinating to see how living things are structured according to certain rules of mathematical harmony. In the same way, humans have always built temples and sacred places that employ certain harmonious forms.

The harmony of forms in nature and sacred art are intimately linked by a play of mimesis. For some, this harmony is the reflection of divinity. There are several mathematical principles observable in natural forms.

Among them are the golden ratio and the Fibonacci sequence.

#### The Golden Ratio and the Fibonacci Sequence

The golden ratio fascinates because it is found in the structure of all living forms. One need only look at a sunflower to observe it.

In mathematics, it is expressed as (a + b)/a = a/b, where a and b are two lengths, a being the larger and b the smaller. The Fibonacci sequence is a sequence of integers in which each value is the sum of the two preceding values (with the exception of the first two). Thus a Fibonacci sequence reads: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, 34, etc.

The further one advances in the Fibonacci sequence, the closer the ratio between two consecutive values approaches the golden ratio. This sequence corresponds graphically to a spiral.

##### Formulas Aim to Reproduce Harmony

We find this spiral in the structure of a snail shell. It is found in many other structures in nature: it characterizes galaxies as well as pinecones, starfish, or daisies. It has been widely used in Western art, in Greco-Roman architecture, and still is in modern architecture.

Thus many key works of our civilization conform, whether voluntarily or not, to a composition based on the Fibonacci sequence. This is the case, for example, of our famous Mona Lisa. The work of Leonardo da Vinci, more broadly, contributes to the dissemination of the idea of divine proportion, the golden ratio, and the Fibonacci sequence.

These mathematical formulas aim to reproduce the harmony of nature and the universe in human creations. In doing so, one approaches the divine order. Forms that incorporate the golden ratio and the Fibonacci sequence are generally considered beneficial by radiesthesia.

#### Yantras and Mandalas

Sacred geometry predates the life of Leonardo da Vinci by far. It is found across many cultures and religions.

Hinduism, for example, uses Yantras in meditation. The Sri Yantra is a geometric support that symbolizes a value such as love, peace, or truth.

Hinduism, Buddhism, and Jainism use mandalas. A mandala translates literally as "circle." It is a harmonious geometric composition that represents the universe. It is used to create sacred spaces, in meditation, and to enter into contact with the divine. Made of sand, natural elements, pigments... Mandalas are often ephemeral works. The fruit of long hours of concentration, they are erased in a few seconds.

Mandalas are also found in the ancient civilizations of South America, among the Maya, the Aztecs, and many pre-Inca civilizations.

#### Crystals

Geometric forms are of high importance in several domains of spirituality. Among the many examples at our disposal, we find the shapes of crystals. Crystals used in lithotherapy are classified according to their geometric shapes. Cubes, prisms, polyhedra... Each shape corresponds to a particular set of characteristics.

## The Vibratory Field of Forms

For radiesthesia, the wave emitted by a form acts well beyond its aesthetic harmony and its potential significance. The waves emitted by forms affect us on the physical, psychological, and emotional levels.

On the vibratory plane, a form is defined by its structure, its mass, its dimensions, its material, its orientation, as well as its form field -- that is to say, the space in which it is situated. Every body is perceived as both emitter and receiver of energy by radiesthesia.

### Types of Form-Wave-Emitting Shapes

#### Beneficial, Amplifying, or Noxious Forms

Still on the energetic plane, some forms are beneficial in nature while others, conversely, can have a noxious tendency. Other forms can act as energy dynamizers or energy amplifiers. The latter amplify the energies present. Such is the case, for example, of the famous spiral.

In general, rounded forms, or geometrically balanced ones such as squares or rectangles, are beneficial. The more regular and refined a form is, the more beneficial it is. Conversely, forms with broken lines and irregular shapes are noxious in nature.

#### Forms Associated with Meaning

Forms can be associated with concepts, and the nature of these concepts also defines the energetic spectrum of these forms. This is the idea found in mandalas and yantras.

Thus a form bearing a sad or difficult symbolism will have an energy field of the same character. Conversely, forms that symbolize life, truth, or love, also used in sacred art, have a vibratory field connected to these concepts. In the same way, a written message emits a vibration.

Here are some examples of well-known form waves:

#### The Pyramid

We know how much the pyramids of Egypt fascinated French radiesthesists. Bovis, Chaumery, and Belizal, among many others, took a close interest in them. Viewed as spaces of convergence of the energies of heaven and earth, pyramids are powerful forms.

The heart of this Platonic solid would act as an energy dynamizer and would facilitate entry into an altered state of consciousness. Nevertheless, radiesthesists also speak of noxious waves encountered inside pyramids, as at Khufu.

#### The Triskel

The triskel form, of Celtic origin, is a form that amplifies energy. The triskel follows a rotation. To activate energy, or increase vibration, the triskel must be oriented clockwise. In the reverse direction, it is used to decrease energies.

#### The Flower of Life

The flower of life is a universal symbol of life and the interconnection of all things. Its rosette-shaped geometric form recalls cell division. The use of the flower of life is considered not only beneficial but also amplifying.

### Using Form Waves: Radionics

Radionics is a discipline related to radiesthesia that uses form waves to act on energy at a distance. In radionics, form waves are used to transmit vibratory influences. One can thus diffuse energy toward someone or something to modify their energy field. Of course, this tool must be used judiciously by a benevolent practitioner.

One can, for example, diffuse healing energy toward a person. The form waves serve to amplify this intention. Several experiments have shown that patients to whom prayers were addressed without their knowledge saw their condition improve, compared to the control group of patients who did not receive them.[1] This type of intention and directed energy can be accentuated through radionics.
