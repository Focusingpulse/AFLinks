# Enel (Prince Michel Vladimirovich Skariatine, 1883-1963)
## A Life at the Crossroads of Science and Tradition

Guy Thieux, heir to Enel and hieronym of Prince Michel Vladimirovich Skariatine (1883-1963), makes available to us, in three volumes, a body of work by this exceptional man, too little known, but whose oeuvre, associated with a few other corpora equally little known, continues to be fruitfully studied in certain discreet circles.

These three volumes are available from Ibacom Editions (http://www.ibacom.fr/), the publishing house directed by Isabelle de Bremond d'Ars, under the titles: *Le monde astral et l'occultisme* (The Astral World and Occultism), *Science egyptienne et medecine de l'astral* (Egyptian Science and Medicine of the Astral), and *Radiesthesie therapeutique* (Therapeutic Radiesthesia). For Guy Thieux, they form a veritable posthumous spiritual and initiatory testament.

Related to the Romanov family, Prince Michel Vladimirovich Skariatine took an early interest in occultism and esotericism. It was during a stay in Egypt that he was initiated, as was Maspero, then director of the antiquities service. Returning to Russia, he continued his research thanks to exceptional qualifications in ancient languages, philosophies, metaphysics, and traditional sciences. He met Master Philippe and Papus during their stays in Russia.

He also pursued a military career as commander of the Chevaliers-Gardes regiment of Emperor Nicholas II. He attempted to influence the Emperor to implement social reforms and avoid the revolution.

It was during this period that he founded an internal initiatory college that operated in Russia until the October Revolution and had a very singular destiny.

The revolution drove him from Russia and he arrived in France, ruined, where he settled. He began publishing numerous articles and books, some of which have become classics of occultism.

In 1931, he returned to Egypt, reunited with Maspero, and continued his research, notably on the sacred meaning of hieroglyphs, which led him to publish three works: *La langue sacree* (The Sacred Language, 1934), *Les origines de la Genese et l'enseignement des temples de l'ancienne Egypte* (The Origins of Genesis and the Teaching of the Temples of Ancient Egypt, 1935), and *Le Message du Sphinx* (The Message of the Sphinx, 1936). This research echoes that of another exceptional researcher, Louis Boutard, whose legacy Guy Thieux, again, ensures.

He left Egypt in 1953 and developed another field of research: that of EIFS (Emergence Influence Forms), awkwardly termed "form waves" (ondes de forme). This work, which leads us toward coherence fields, the emergence of reference points, the distinction between global and local... opened an immense field of research. It particularly influenced the researchers of the Fondation Ark'all, around, among others, Jacques Ravatin and Guy Thieux. It was during this period that Enel experimented with the Multiple Wave Oscillator, the OLOM of Georges Lakhovsky. He established original protocols and obtained significant therapeutic results.

Over the course of his life, Enel met many important researchers with whom he collaborated, notably Colonel de Rochas and Count Pierre Vincenti Piobb. Among the landmark books, we may cite *Trilogie de la Rota* (1928), *Gnomologie* (1959), and *Un enfant du Nil, la vie secrete de Moise* (A Child of the Nile, the Secret Life of Moses), published more recently, in 1984.

The very eventful life of Enel and his research, at the crossroads of several scientific and traditional disciplines, of several currents as well, partake of a very coherent whole of which Guy Thieux can give account and testimony. He is undoubtedly today the only one who can do so.

In the three volumes that gather previously unpublished works or notes by Enel, the topics include the occult constitution of the human being, egregores, prayers, initiation, the master-disciple relationship, the wisdom of aroma, the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, the sacred language, astrology, phytotherapy, rituals, the liberation of the soul of mummies -- to which Enel devoted himself operatively -- and other practices originating in ancient Egypt. The reader will also discover the devices created by Enel and their therapeutic applications. And many other subjects.

"Enel, Guy Thieux tells us, was in close contact with the founders of schools of thought, study, and experimental metapsychic work, encompassing spiritualism, magic, alchemy, astrology, mythology, symbolism. He reveals the richness of these convergences of thought and this abundance of experimental research around the paranormal. Transcending the real by going beyond the limits of matter, providing answers to the great problems of life, death, eternity -- such are the goals and foundations of Occultism, 'the study of psychic forces and superphysical worlds,' but also a key for apprehending 'the existence of the spiritual world, the primacy of spirit over matter.' The Master here slightly opens the door of Initiation, a new birth in the mysteries and objects of a secret knowledge. (...)

So many messages intended to illuminate and guide the initiate in their esoteric 'quest' to connect yesterday to tomorrow in the presence of Eternity."
