## 1. Who was Ravatin (In Memoriam, Cédric Mannu — translated)

Jacques Ravatin was born on 27 January 1935. He was a doctor of sciences in physics and mathematics (Docteur ès Sciences), an E.S.C.I.L.-trained engineer, and a professor of mathematics at the University of Orsay. He worked in quantum axiomatics and in mathematics in liaison with various research teams, including a group at CERN in Geneva. He is one of the founders of the Fondation Ark'All, and the originator, among other things, of mathematical theories he called "non-Cartesian."

He hated being called by his given name — it reminded him of his grandmother scolding him as a boy — so friends and intimates called him "Vlady," after Vladimir Rosgnik, a character of his invention that was to become the Bourbaki of his unorthodox research. As with the mathematical school (which he knew well, being a specialist of Jordan algebra), he defined Vladimir Rosgnik as a plural character resulting from the whole of the Ark'all then Eurek'all research group he animated.

Vlady took an interest in "forteana" — those strange phenomena of objects relocated in the most improbable places (a 19th-century chair in a salt deposit dating from the Jurassic, for example). Forteana constituted a proper object of study, a census of elements still awaiting a scientific explanation, following Charles Fort's initiatives. This is how Ravatin learned to "hunt" unofficial information from out-of-the-ordinary scientific experiments, and he reflected on form waves, on the localized/delocalized aspects of matter, and so on.

He had begun to develop a great intuition. He taught himself the use of the pendulum during a month off work, and never abandoned that tool afterwards. Thus, knowing nothing of Hebrew, he could verify the quality of a word like *Bereschit* and its exact spelling, as a friend recounted. He had even taken the habit of verifying the feasibility of the experiments and setups he designed by pendulum — which saved considerable time and cost in an entrepreneurial context. In the 1980s, with the help of François Cardinet, he developed the Acti-Z, a machine capable of conferring "actikalisation" on matter, cold — a process he had first found by modifying oils and inserting them hot into molten glass.

He spent the 1990s generalizing form waves, to the point of no longer speaking of waves at all, but of coherence fields and a theory of forms (see his books from Éditions du Cosmogone). That was when I met him for the first time. He was astonished at my ability to understand the whole of his work — which sealed our friendship — having cut himself off from a whole part of the public that could have been interested, by an extremely formalized language: a new algebra.

We then developed the theory of the modulaires — mathematical drawings capable of inducing the physical effects of unknown elements (the philosopher's stone, for example). We could treat physical objects with these properties and observe material modifications.

Jacques Ravatin's other strength, besides his intuition, was collecting references to little-known authors, and to little-known aspects of great authors (I think in particular of Jules Verne — and his pentacle). He made us rediscover MM. Chotard, Boutard, Sprink, Rota, Calligaris, and others. To him we owe the first in-depth enumeration of forgotten researchers and accursed scientists.

He was a discoverer, an opener of ways, a remarkable researcher endowed with a bon vivant's appetite and an exceptional storyteller. Jacques left us on 8 March 2011 — may his memory stay among us a long time, and his researches continue to show us the way.

*Cédric Mannu — member of the Ark'All group, manager of Eurek'all, founder of ESRI. Les Chroniques de Mars, April 2011.*

## 2. The theory of coherence fields (Jacques Bonvin, after Ravatin and Labat — translated)

It is only through the limits man sets for himself that he observes nature and the phenomena around him, and it is only the consequence of his own extremities that he takes for reality. Our present means of investigation drag us more and more toward a failure to understand the most subtle phenomena — the very essence of life. Through a global approach we can rediscover the deep relation that binds us to nature, so as to reach a better understanding of ourselves and our environment.

Ravatin put it sharply: "Our scientific, industrial society has managed to impose its approach and its method on philosophy, linguistics, law, commerce, even morality, biology, medicine. That approach and method breed such confidence in the adopted point of view that no one even thinks of considering another point of view. This is very serious. It is a blockage of research and a drop in the quality of that research."

Only rational coherence — imposed in large part by the officialized scientific milieu — will be recognized. Every other approach is systematically rejected, going so far as to deny results under cover of "principles." As Paul-Émile Victor said: "True scientists are poets and imaginative. The others are accountants and grocers: they discover nothing. And anyway, how sad life would be without imagination."

### The rational coherence field and the Local

The rational Coherence Field allows only a single vision; it possesses only a single "Ray of Coherence" — its own. Ravatin explains: "In the rational world one steers by what we call reference points (repères), units, and the transport of those units. These units and their transport allow us to describe and to evolve in what we name the **Local**."

The Local is essentially *existence with reference points* — and the reference points are of every kind: distance, time, speed, acceleration, power, mass, and so on. The rational coherence field accepts only itself; other ways have been proposed in the scientific milieu, but for military reasons, for the stability of the usual schooling of the masses, and for the safeguarding of physics, they were completely suppressed. One must not forget that with rational thought an economy has set itself in place that muzzles human beings so as to employ them to best effect and make them act within very precise channels and goals.

### The other coherence field and the Global

Conversely, the *other* Coherence Field allows multiple visions; other approaches become possible. It possesses multiple Rays of Coherence, tied to what we call the **Global**.

The Global is *existence without reference points*. The Local emerges from the Global, or immerses back into it. In each Ray of Coherence, the choice of representations increases the multiplicity of "lightings" cast on each story — the creation of other stories.

Postulate: existence without reference points — that is the Global. It allows us to tie to the Global everything that cannot be located, or is only partially locatable. Certain forms are tied to the Global but are not *in* the Global — which would imply a relation of belonging, of container and content, of set and element. The Global is not composed of elements; it IS existence without reference points. It is an evocation — at once the evocation and the existence. The Global expresses itself in that everything not locatable is tied to it.

The Local tends to dissociate in order to grasp and analyze. The Global will often lead us to an impossibility of dissociating the set and the element, which cannot be told apart without breaking the concept or the process. They can stand in dynamic duality in the Yin-Yang sense of the expression: neither set nor element, and at once set and element.

On rational pseudo-objectivity, Ravatin: "In the rational representation, the individual is led to play the game of scientific pseudo-objectivity — that is, one allows oneself to align values, compare results. The pseudo-objectivity lies in this possibility of building multiple scales of comparison, but this is often arbitrary. The R.B.B. (Rationalistes Bêtes et Bornés — Narrow-Minded Bigoted Rationalists) who apply it as hard as they can (it is a justification of their behavior, and a necessity to be recognized as a good scientist) extend it to human relations and to morality. One reaches absurdities. The freedom the researcher gives himself in building and projecting scales of values is a decoy. The human being, in this field, is caught in a straitjacket; the usual field makes him a prisoner. The notion of freedom is then put in place to make him forget the straitjacket. One will say that freedom is a 'dilution.' That freedom, on the other hand, is in the other coherence field — the one in which the obs [observer] projects himself at the same time as he enters it — in which case one no longer needs to speak of freedom: it is implicitly understood."

### EIFS — emissions induced by forms

The other coherence field also allows a valid approach to the Émissions Induites par les Formes (EIFS). Ravatin: "The EIFs are more fundamental than electricity, than electromagnetism; they place themselves at every level: matter, energy, life, thought. One discovers their importance in every domain of usual research and application (physics, chemistry, architecture — housing in particular — biology, allopathic medicine, but also acupuncture, homeopathy, anthroposophy) and further in every domain known to the Ancients — forms — and also the study of thought."

This is the deliberate break with the older French school: where Chaumery and de Belizal spoke of *ondes de formes* (form waves), Ravatin's group stopped speaking of waves altogether — "We no longer speak of form waves but of emissions due to forms. Why? Because we are not dealing with waves — neither those of the electromagnetic spectrum nor those of any other known vibratory system. In short: emissions due to forms do not belong to our physical universe." They are the intermediary between the Global and our limited-Local system.

### The Idathème — the third existence

Once the Local and the Global are in place through the emergence of the one in the other, a new concept appears that must be made explicit: a third type of existence called the **Idathème**. Its peculiarity is that it cannot be represented by a human mind. To summarize: existence with reference points is the Local; existence without reference points is the Global; and we must consider another existence, which Ravatin called the Idathème.

Certain forms, places, acts, and intentions are tied to the Global, or to the Idathème, or to both simultaneously. Examples as Ravatin and others perceived them — these are tied to the Idathème:

- Certain constellations.
- The characters of the writing of Easter Island.
- Certain mathematical curves, like the tractrix or the cycloid (the right cycloid, also called Aristotle's wheel or Pascal's roulette).
- The set of the statues of Easter Island.
- Fog is tied to the Idathème; certain clouds may be.
- The mandrake is tied to the Idathème — but if one dips it in cold or hot water, that state disappears.
- The philosopher's stone is tied to the Idathème.
- The Black Virgins, while tied to the Idathème, emit that information only if one prays before them.
- The magic squares, the Sator square, are tied to the Idathème.
- The castle of Montségur *was* tied to the Idathème. It is so no longer.
- The Ark of the Covenant was tied to the Idathème.
- The double helix of DNA is tied to the Idathème.
- Egg white and other colloids are tied to the Idathème.
- Dolmens, the alignments of Carnac, cromlechs are tied to the Idathème. But a lone menhir is not.
- A very large animal or human figure drawn on the ground is tied to the Idathème — in particular the chalk figures of the English countryside, like the Cerne Abbas giant in Dorset.
- The animals drawn on the Nazca lines are tied to the Idathème.
- The néoVie products are tied at once to the Global and to the Idathème.

The Idathème is an existence not tied to the Global; it is characterized as *being-being* (l'être étant) — which, to be understood, must be thought all of a piece, without being decomposed.

### The symbolic correspondence

If one brings the Local, the Global, and the Idathème alongside the symbolic correspondence Body–Soul–Spirit, there are strictly speaking no real equivalences of their characters. The relation happens through symbolic felt-sense: the soul is tied to the Idathème (it is the manifestation of being-being); the spirit is associated with the Global (it is totally delocalized); the body is attached to the Local (the localized body).

From these concepts one can approach, for example, Yin-Yang, Feng-Shui, homeopathy, acupuncture, geobiology — all the phenomena usually rejected because incomprehensible in rational thought. This approach is not frozen; it is in perpetual movement. Questioning is permanent; no form is rejected for incomprehension — it is the form of thought that is modified so as to view the form under another aspect.

### Practitioner's note (Luc Labat)

Luc Labat: "This global approach has allowed me, for more than ten years, to intervene on sites where the rational approach could neither explain nor resolve the various phenomena encountered. Orchards and vines that no longer produced; animals with diseases or incomprehensible behaviors (cannibalism, aggressiveness, tumors, cysts); people with unexplained physical and psychological health problems — and this with a very high success rate." When Luc Labat introduced the néoVie products to Ravatin, the latter understood very quickly their exceptional relations to the Global and the Idathème. After the tests he ran, he added: "I do not know a single product that works for the plant, the mineral, and the animal that cannot work for the human being."

### The observer footnote

"Obs." for observer — but the word may have a more general sense than the one used in physics. The rational coherence field allows only a single vision. The universe is only an echo to a Coherence Field launched by the Obs, and in which the Obs dilutes. This operation of plunging-dilution repeats as an echo in other acts attached to that Obs. One then realizes that the observer of the world is locked in a prison built by his brain and by a society that seems external to him because it was presented to him as external by one of the echoes of the plunging-dilution in the Coherence Field. The Obs must embrace his creation. (JR)

## 3. ALPHYSIQUE and the new physics (projet-lapasserelle.com — translated)

Present humanity expresses itself through rational thought, though a few scraps of belief remain, unfortunately relegated to superstition; moreover, certain attitudes, being unorthodox, are quickly forgotten, not adapting easily to the intense rationalization — which leads to what Ravatin calls an **overlocalization** (surlocalisation).

What is fundamental, the author teaches, is the concept of coherence. It is not defined — nor are the other concepts presented in the course of the reading, for we are in the presence of a *dynamic* thought: everything is in perpetual evolution, unlike static rational thought, which is why the latter can bear the definitions and laws that express its stability. Thus the Ext, the Dynamic Duality, the Equimsey, the Cumulaires, the Décalaires… will exist only as tied to the Global — existence without reference points — and to the Idathème, the Being-Being.

The Ancients, for example, will not be approached as in usual thought; they are tied to Representations which, for some, engender worlds other than the one we are usually plunged into; those worlds correspond to other Rays of Coherence belonging to a Coherence Field different from the one established by rational thought.

The past then becomes "stretched" instant: depending on the representation chosen by the observer, different elements become possible. A new physics is needed, with unstable and variable reference points, through which the concept of form takes its full amplitude. It is then that **ALPHYSIQUE** emerges.

**A warning to readers** (from the publisher's page): we are not in the presence of an explanatory book but of a series of suggestive works of a complex and falsely hermetic thought. "You will understand little on the first reading. But reread these books tirelessly, to let yourself be rocked by this form of thought. One day it will emerge from you, and everything will have changed."

## 4. Cumulo-decal arithmetic and the Ark'all program (Aetherforce interview material, noted)

The researchers of the Ark'all Foundation propose to replace symbols, rites, rituals, and indecipherable recipes with mathematical formulas. These are *cumulo-decal* arithmetics — arithmetic, because they deal with numbers; cumulo-decal, because they allow one to cross the passages, the "cumulo-decals." As in the past wizards and magi did through rites and symbols, it is today thanks to formulas of this kind that they try to access the Global, to immerse the landmarks, and to bring archetypes and enels into emergence. Ravatin's division of the universe: visible and invisible; measurable and non-measurable; a limited universe where man, to locate himself, has created barriers and set benchmarks — the realm of rationalist thought and its product, the technical object — and an unlimited universe where the usual parameters are abolished, where any notion of reference point disappears. To our local physical universe he opposes the Global: the realm of symbols, archetypes, and *enels*.

The name Ark'all: "Because… Ark of the Covenant," explains Roger de Lafforest in *The Magic Reality* — because its founders carried out studies on the Ark of the Covenant, mysteriously appeared and lost; because the Ark is supposed to contain the sum of knowledge; because the Ark throws a bridge between old thought and thought to come, between beings — those of past times, those of present times, those of the distant future.

Ravatin's group emerged from **Totaris** (research on non-Cartesian systems); he was a master of research at the University of Amiens (in 1995). The foundation's journal was *Ark'all Communications*; the book that sets out the main axes of the global science is *L'Émergence de l'Enel ou l'Immergence des repères* — the emergence of the Enel, or the immergence of the landmarks.

## 5. Position in the French form tradition (Forge's note)

The library already holds the chain: Chaumery/de Belizal (*ondes de formes*, negative green) → Enel (EIFs, Omega ray — his 1953 departure from Egypt opened "the research field of EIFs, awkwardly termed form waves, which leads toward coherence fields" and "particularly influenced the researchers of the Fondation Ark'all, around Jacques Ravatin and Guy Thieux"). Ravatin is the third link: the professional mathematician who took the dowser's and the form-researcher's material and rebuilt it as a formal theory — Local/Global/Idathème, coherence fields, cumulo-decal arithmetic, ALPHYSIQUE. Where the older school measured emissions with the Universal Pendulum, Ravatin's group replaced the instrument with an algebra, and the "wave" with the "emission due to form" — deliberately outside the electromagnetic spectrum, outside "our physical universe" altogether. His In Memoriam notes he verified his experimental setups by pendulum until the end: the two tools, algebra and pendulum, stayed in one hand. His works (Cosmogone editions, the *Développements* in four volumes) are out of print and not fetchable; this dossier is the fetchable record.
