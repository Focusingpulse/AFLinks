# The Kunnen Holographic Body-Scan: A Geobiologist's DU-Ethnologie Mémoire (FR→EN)

**Source:** "Géobiologie Scientifique et Quantique — Extrait de mon mémoire pour l'obtention d'un DU d'Ethnologie," londedisis.fr, published 2025-06-08 by Francis (Noyon), L'Onde d'Isis – Azimut 77 (Saint-Hubert, Belgium). Translation by Forge (translation-qc), 2026-09-18. Companion sources: archibio.it (EN/IT doctrine pages), the ULB doctoral thesis record (Ledent 2025). Claim FAL-fr-107-5.

## Context

This is an excerpt from a practitioner's mémoire (thesis) written for a **Diplôme Universitaire d'Ethnologie** — a real university diploma, which makes this a university-adjacent primary source: a working geobiologist's own account of his method, in the academic register of ethnology rather than the commercial register of a product page. The author, Francis Noyon, is a Belgian geobiology practitioner ("L'Onde d'Isis – Azimut 77," Saint-Hubert) who obtained a DU d'Ethnomédecine in 2013 and has taught his method since; the site now (since 2023, on return to Belgium) shares knowledge via blog and conferences rather than commercial practice.

The method it documents descends from **Prof. Walter Kunnen** (Antwerp 1921–2011), founder of **Archibo Biologica** (Antwerp, 1960 — the first institute specialized in biosphere and bioenergetics research), and **Dr. Nestor Driscart** (b. 1935-06-06), whose teachers included Pr. Maisin (oncologist), Pr. de Duve (Nobel Chemistry 1974), Dr. Borsarello (Paris), Dr. Nogier (Lyon, the auricular-therapy pioneer), Dr. Boudriol (Paris neurophysiologist), and Dr. Voll (German electro-acupuncture).

## The Kunnen doctrine, from Archibo Biologica's own pages (EN/IT, translated/condensed)

The institute's English and Italian pages state the doctrine more precisely than the mémoire:

- **Carrier vs. carried waves (onde portanti / portate).** Carrier waves are balanced across the two magnetic polarities — natural and artificial EM fields belong to this group. **Carried waves are generally circularly polarized**: their levogyre (left-handed) spiraling has an intensity different from the dextrogyre, and they are conveyed by the carrier wave. Kunnen verified that the frequencies that resonate with each organ of the body belong to the carried class — each organ "receives and emits on its own frequency" — and he established those biological frequencies.
- **Qualitative measurement.** With the Lecher antenna, "precisely calibrated and perfected by his researches," one measures the EM field intensity of biological interest **separately on the two polarities**. It is the *difference* of intensity across the two polarities that makes a frequency "charged with information relevant to the living cells with which it enters into resonance." The measurement is qualitative, not just quantitative.
- **Beyond the two-dimensional view.** Kunnen's claim is that both radiesthesia and the scientific community study natural and artificial EM phenomena "bi-dimensionally"; his calibrated antenna opened "a new vision in the field of energetic information and bio-resonance."
- **Bioremediation.** His research also produced a methodology for the **electromagnetic bioremediation of sites and homes**, "based on the laws of electromagnetic resonance, to raise their energetic quality and protect the inhabitants."
- **The instrument's lineage.** The Lecher antenna was elaborated by the German physicist R. Schneider (1950s–60s) on the discoveries of the Austrian engineer Ernst Lecher (1856–1926, parallel-wire wave-speed demonstration 1890). The retail/service layer around it (BIOLECHER, Archibio.it courses at €400 + €390–415 antenna, the Belgian and Italian teaching lines) is documented in the corpus's antenne-lecher dossier (2026-09-07) and the scout's it-fire-109 Kunnen-history row.

**Biographical anchor (independent of the hagiographic sources):** the Flemish biographical encyclopedia (Nieuwe Encyclopedie van de Vlaamse Beweging, tekst.devb.be/nevb/002522.pdf) records Walter Kunnen (Antwerp, 29 May 1921) as a law graduate (KU Leuven) who founded the Algemeen Bouwbedrijf Kunnen construction company after WWII, handed it over in 1980, and thereafter led Archibo-biologica. The encyclopedia entry covers his Flemish-nationalist and European-federalist political activity in detail (BVSE, the journal *Europa Eén*, the European Eresenaat) — and says nothing about geobiology or any professorship. The "Prof." honorific used by the geobiology sources is not corroborated by the independent record; the Italian pages call him "dott. Kunnen" with "laurea in legge" and 'liances' in physics and biology. Treat the title as the tradition's own.

## The counter-row: the ULB doctoral thesis (Ledent, 2025)

The same week the scout banked the mémoire, the verification side of the geobiology coin surfaced in the same lane:

**Maryse Ledent**, *Explorations observationnelles et expérimentales de l'intolérance environnementale idiopathique avec attribution aux champs électromagnétiques : Vers une objectivation ?* — doctoral thesis, Université libre de Bruxelles, École de Santé publique, defended 2025-09-03 (promoter Véronique Feipel; co-promoter Catherine Bouland; jury president Marc Mayer). Record: difusion.ulb.ac.be, ULB-DIPOT:oai:dipot.ulb.ac.be:2013/393603.

Key findings (from the abstract):

- **Co-constructed double-blind provocation protocol** — built *with* IEI-CEM (electrohypersensitivity) participants themselves, to strengthen relevance and acceptability (this answers the standard practitioner objection that lab provocation studies don't reflect real conditions).
- 27 volunteers completed a first double-blind exposure session to an EMF cocktail; 26 then 16 participated in three sessions (collective analyses) and 12 sessions (individual analyses).
- **No systematic link between actual exposure status, perceived exposure, and number of reported symptoms** — with a single exception: one volunteer showed coherence between status, perception, and symptoms.
- **For half the volunteers, reported symptoms correlated significantly with *perceived* exposure** — supporting the nocebo hypothesis.
- Exploratory measures (cognition, behavior, heart-rate variability) showed no consistent exposure-related effects.
- The inclusion questionnaire showed marked psychological distress, high perceived risk from EMF, and a **highly sensitive personality trait** — suggesting a possible relation between that trait and IEI-CEM.
- Companion surveys (ExpoComm, ENVI-EHS, 2020–21, n=382): higher perceived sensitivity associated with greater modern-health worries and avoidance strategies; avoidance ineffective.
