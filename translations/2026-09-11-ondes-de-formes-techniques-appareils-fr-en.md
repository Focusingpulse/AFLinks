## MENTAL RADIESTHESIA — PHYSICAL RADIESTHESIA

### by A. de BÉLIZAL

A question much discussed, much debated, yet never resolved. So I would like here to shed a little light — and a little union — on this formidable conflict that sets mental radiesthesia against physical radiesthesia.

It is not possible, indeed, that we should go on tearing each other apart until the end of time without seeking to resolve intelligently a problem that should long since have found its natural solution. Why this sterile conflict, this "war of religion," as my friend Auscher calls it? Ah, how right he is to raise the alarm like that. And that is why I too would like to try, loyally, to make my own contribution to the search for truth.

In my view, this discord, this incomprehension among us, stems from a defect of form, whose origin must be sought at the base. Without realizing it, people have confused two absolutely different phenomena and generically named them both "Radiesthesia." Without realizing it, still, they have gathered under a single name an art that will always remain an art, and an embryo of science destined to develop and one day become an exact science.

The problem, therefore, was badly posed: psychic phenomena (pure mental radiesthesia) have been confused with physical phenomena due to the vibrations of form waves, in which the human being plays no part.

When the men of our generation began to take an interest in what Abbé Bouly named "radiesthesia," they were truly practicing radiesthesia. And I am in full agreement with all the adepts of mental radiesthesia in affirming that this radiesthesia comes from "the Spirit," that it is a resonance of the hidden forces of the cosmos, that it is "breathed" into us by an immaterial power that everyone may name according to their heart and according to their faith.

For more than thirty years that I have practiced radiesthesia, I have very often had recourse to this great reservoir of the astral, from which I draw the energy needed to set my detector in motion. And when I restore the equilibrium of forces to neutralize harmful waves, it is again to "the Spirit" that I make my appeal, and never has it failed my supplications.

I therefore fully understand that there exists a pure mental radiesthesia, having nothing in common with the theory of quanta, wave mechanics, or form waves, which possess an energy of their own — particles, electromagnetic vibrations? The future will perhaps teach us one day.

And there is where the gulf opened between us: researchers interested in purely mental phenomena, but whose solution was given by a physical instrument (the gyration and swinging of a pendulum), thought to use this instrument to scrutinize, analyze, dissect, and finally catalogue the prodigious universe of vibrations.

From that moment on, they were no longer, strictly speaking, practicing pure radiesthesia, but "physical radiesthesia" (*physique radiesthésique*), which by the very logic of reasoning and discovery gave birth to "micro-vibratory physics" (*physique micro-vibratoire*), of which I would like to speak briefly to readers.

Radiesthesia is a very simple thing, like everything that is great in nature. The essential is to see clearly and not to deliberately entangle truth in a cesspool of worthless words where everyone wants to be right.

Three phenomena, absolutely distinct from one another and yet together forming a harmonious whole — such is radiesthesia, if one is willing to understand it and study it in its fullness.

**First phenomenon: a pure mental radiesthesia.** A kind of clairvoyance, of direct knowing, allowing the operator-radar to plug into the astral and, by means of a support (any pendulum, a ring, a watch, it hardly matters), to capture mental images whose exact or false solution will depend on the sensitivity of the practitioner. Hence spectacular success or lamentable failure. Here the space-time factor no longer exists; but it is a fragile and unstable art, at once the easiest and the most difficult.

**Second phenomenon: physical radiesthesia** (*physique radiesthésique*). The pendulum is no longer a mere support, but a precise detector, calibrated on all the vibrations of the spectrum, visible and invisible alike, with this particularity: it enters into action only on the vibration for which it has been previously tuned — the law of similars. Moreover it has the property of being indifferently a receiver or an emitter, according to the work demanded of it.

**Third phenomenon: micro-vibratory physics** (*physique micro-vibratoire*). This third phenomenon is the consequence and the logical corollary of the second; it is the active and creative part of radiesthesia. The human fluid plays no part in it, the vibration found by the physical method being automatically transferred to emitter apparatus whose energy is liberated by the form waves alone. The three phenomena are interdependent and form a harmonious vibratory whole. In the first two cases, the contribution of the human vital fluid was indispensable; in the third, the energy is liberated without the help of that fluid, which was nevertheless needed to calculate the exact length of the vibration to be emitted.

Thus, when I mummify a beefsteak in less than five days, from Brittany to Paris, by simply presenting a witness of it to the radiations of my high-intensity shock waves, I am not, strictly speaking, practicing radiesthesia, and it is not "the spirit" that mummifies the organic cell at 500 kilometers' distance. It is "micro-vibratory physics."

When, at more than 1,500 kilometers, still on a witness, I sterilize a tumor with my apparatus, without any human intervention on my part, I am no longer practicing pure radiesthesia but always "micro-vibratory physics."

When my friend and collaborator Chaumery, in the course of last winter, completely burned his digestive tract from throat to intestines, and was two fingers' breadth from death for having had the imprudence to place his witness on one of his new vibrating apparatus tuned to the 8th octave, he too was no longer practicing radiesthesia, but "micro-vibratory physics." For the 8th octave certainly exceeds ultrasound, and its dangerous vibration is perceptible to no human sense — it is pure physics. Would it not then be urgent — indispensable, even — that an amicable divorce separate the "mentalists" from the "physicists," and that we find for our nascent science the technical term that would allow us never again to confuse Spirit and Matter?

Each of us, moreover, "mentalists" and "physicists," would have only good to say of this rupture. Never, in fact, will official, orthodox science admit the radiesthesia of "the Spirit" as an exact science. This is not possible. But if we present it with apparatus based on "micro-vibratory physics," and with this apparatus the irrefutable proofs of its action, then loyally, science will be obliged to rethink the problem. I offer these few lines to the meditation of true researchers, hoping that one of them will find the qualified term under which our work could continue.

### Radiesthesia for Everyone

Men have reached such a degree of knowledge in every domain that they are now permitted to think they have become the masters of the world. On land, on sea, beneath the oceans as in the air, the perfection and speed of their machines have abolished the notion of space-time. Radio waves circle the globe in a fraction of a second; they are even reflected by the cosmic plasma, by the Moon or by Venus, then recaptured as echo by radar; and television screens finally allow us to take part, thanks to world-broadcast and Telstar, in the most distant events of the planet, even at the antipodes. It seems, indeed, that the earth no longer holds any secrets for man, and tomorrow his rockets and piloted satellites will permit the most audacious explorations in the Cosmos. Living beings, men, have already been projected into space, and they have returned in perfect physical condition, after circling the globe forty-eight, then sixty-four times, in capsules satelliteized on orbits rigorously calculated by electronic brains. Four days in space, subject to the phenomena of weightlessness, more than two and a half million kilometers traveled! One seems to dream — and yet these performances are only the point of departure of a still-unknown science that tomorrow will free man from terrestrial attraction and make him a true cosmic explorer. Biology, chemistry, and surgery will soon overturn all the old conceptions of the origins of life, and the atom, with its inexhaustible reservoir of energy, will bring to the humanity of tomorrow — if it knows how to deserve it — that golden age the most authorized prophecies have announced to men of good will.

Yes, Homo sapiens is truly a superior intelligence, the fruit of a millennial evolution, and no problem seems foreign to him. He is one with the matter he has disciplined; but his spirit is still free enough to break loose from it when necessary, and he then perceives another Universe, how different from that of forms — a world apart, without consistency, and yet real, on the scale of his human intelligence. Only here: he lacks the key that would restore to these invisible forces their creative power. The secret has been lost, although in ancient times this power was widely used. Whether it be the generation of Mu whose flying machines circled the planet without stopover, or the colossal and mysterious statues of Easter Island and Peru, whose positive action was certainly more than a symbol, or again that Egypt of the Pharaohs, heiress of the civilization of the Atlanteans, which knew the "Eternal Lamp" — that disintegration of the atom and its transformation into luminous waves as resplendent as the Sun — everything leads us to believe that the men of past generations held secrets on the mutation of matter.

What remains to us of these marvels? The fairy wand that enchants our grandchildren's evenings... A legend, perhaps? Not at all: the reality of yesterday, and why not the reality of tomorrow... In the Indies, in Tibet, where certain secrets have been preserved, the masters and sages of those distant lands still have the ability to travel through the ether, over considerable distances, not only in spirit but in body-matter. For this they use force-currents conferring on the human form, along with invisibility, fluidity and the complete absence of weight. For us Westerners, such phenomena make us smile; they exceed us and escape the scientific data of our modern civilization. And yet the invisible forces are not an illusion; they surround us, they press upon us from every side; man is at the center of these latent energies that ask only to be used. They evolve on three planes: (a) thought-force; (b) form waves; (c) the fission and fusion of the atom (of which we shall not speak, for it is not our subject here).

**A) THOUGHT-FORCE.** The energy emanating from thought-force, from intelligence, is an abstract force that needs no material support to liberate vibrations of often considerable power, outside the space-time factor. It is with this energy that hypnotists put their subjects to sleep, that magnetizers relieve suffering, that certain radiesthesists obtain positive results that are incontestable and uncontested. The thought-wave is a reality it would be truly vain to deny, unless one were in bad faith; but what is its mechanism, and why do certain beings possess this active faculty (we would be tempted to write "creative faculty"), while in others it seems dormant or nonexistent?

Would it be too bold to put forward the hypothesis that this is perhaps a logical consequence of evolution? For spirit, like matter, is subject to the laws of cosmic perfection. Here below, everything evolves and must evolve toward a finality whose objective is the Absolute. Many souls therefore possess a degree of evolution that gives them possibilities ranging from simple intuition to supranatural gifts, while others are still in a state of infancy. Among these ancient souls we find the mediums, the seers, and most radiesthesists, whose sixth sense is a door left ajar onto the astral.

**B) FORM WAVES.** The second energy placed at man's disposal is included in matter and liberated — not by the fusion or fission of the atom, but by form waves. It is a purely magnetic energy whose order of magnitude lies in the millionth or billionth of an electron-volt, whereas classical, known radioactivity liberates energies measured in millions of electron-volts. For mesons and hyperons, one must count in billions and tens of billions. Thus, in a nuclear reaction, the magnetic forces exerted between two magnetic moments of two colliding particles are considered perfectly negligible with respect to nuclear power itself. This is perfectly exact; but it is nonetheless true that pure magnetic force, taking into account its minute energetic potential, nevertheless exerts an influence on energy phenomena of formidable power. And it is this natural force that certain apparatus capture — apparatus based on the energy liberated by form waves, even though no instrument of physics currently existing can measure its concrete reality, and whose effects nevertheless make themselves felt on the living cell, either by re-equilibrating it or by disequilibrating it, according to the frequency emitted by the form wave, a frequency that is very easy to calculate rigorously. This is "micro-vibratory physics," which could be the subject of a deeper study in a book. (*Physique micro-vibratoire et forces invisibles*, published 1965 by Desforges. This work is unfortunately out of print.)

## THE UNIVERSAL PENDULUM

### by A. de BÉLIZAL and P. A. MOREL

Since rigorous experiments will often be in question — experiments that can only be conducted or checked with an apparatus based on the laws of physics — we shall now describe our Universal Pendulum, CHAUMERY & DE BÉLIZAL. It was to meet precise standards, and to serve at once as receiver and emitter of exactly calculated vibrations, that this instrument was perfected in 1936. In the view of many specialists, the creation of this detector marked an important date, and the approach of a sure technique that may perhaps one day transform our art into a veritable science.

The Universal Pendulum takes the form of a rigorously balanced sphere, of approximately 60 mm diameter and 125 grams weight. This sphere carries two meridians — one electric, the other magnetic — and an equator sensitized to electromagnetic vibrations. A handle of non-ferrous metal joins the two poles (+ and −), and its mobility allows an exploration of every point of the sphere. On this handle is fixed the suspension thread, which itself can slide from one pole to the other. Moreover, to give the detector great stability, it is equipped with a battery (*pile*) of four elements, whose role is to reinforce the polarities while preventing them from being inverted under the action of a more powerful form wave.

The electric meridian carries, in its upper hemisphere, seven color-vibrations — that is, the totality of the visible spectrum: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet. The magnetic meridian likewise carries seven vibrations, of which four are visible and three invisible: green, blue, indigo, violet, ultraviolet, white, negative green. In the lower hemisphere, the electric meridian emits five invisible color-vibrations: ultraviolet, white, negative green, black, infrared. The magnetic meridian likewise emits five color-vibrations, of which three are visible: yellow, orange, red; and two invisible: infrared, black. Only the lower half of the "electric" meridian therefore carries the totality of the vibrations of the invisible, and particularly the one that was named, by convention, Negative Green (V−) — so named because it sits opposite the green of the spectrum.

It is a mysterious vibration radiating exactly between white and black, whose property is to make a neutral detector subjected to its action gyrate negatively. It is the shortest and most powerful vibration existing in the universe, and its radiating power is such that it crosses thicknesses of lead considered effective screens against X-rays. Its discovery goes back to 1934, and in 1936 it was the object of a patent protecting at once both the apparatus and the method of the "Decomposition of the spectrum in the sphere."

If we now pass to the study of the equator, we note that its characteristic is to vibrate on the whole electromagnetic beam, and that it presents 12 divisions — seven visible and five invisible — with this particularity, that the V+ of the equator coincides with the V+ of the "magnetic" meridian.

These three radioactive planes of the Universal Pendulum — meridians and equator — constitute a harmonious equilibrium and a perfect synchronization in the distribution of the color rays, each of the 12 vibrations being rigorously equidistant.

The Universal Pendulum, as it was designed, is an ideal receiver-emitter, both for research in the field and for laboratory studies; and it has, moreover, the great quality of presenting a particular sensitivity throughout the sector touching on radioactivity, whether natural or artificial.

This sector of radioactivity lies on either side of the V−, and here are the vibrations we have identified: between the V− and Black, the rays V−, Alpha, Theta, X, Nu, Dzeta, Black; between the V− and White, the rays V−, Omega, Rho, Psi, Lambda, Khi, Epsilon, White. This factor for determining the rate of radioactivity is very important to know, especially in our day, in these times of folly when man, become a sorcerer's apprentice, contaminates the terrestrial atmosphere at will — going so far, with his repeated nuclear explosions at high altitude, as to break the magnetic belt that protects the earth from the terrible solar radiations.

## THE C.30 BOMB

*[Editor's note]* The C.30 Bomb is one of the many revelations of the work *Physique micro-vibratoire* by MM. A. de Belizal and P. A. Morel. In the domain of form waves, it is one of the most advanced realizations. With the C.30 Bomb, we therefore arrive at the chapter of the instruments of study and of action of the new "micro-vibratory physics."

### by A. de BÉLIZAL and P. A. MOREL

The idea goes back to Léon Chaumery, and it is one of the last apparatus to come out of the brain of that man of genius. Since his disappearance in February 1957, we have rethought the problem and brought important modifications to the original instrument.

Originally, the C. Bomb consisted of a simple sphere formed of two half-spheres rigorously joined. Each half-sphere presented a cavity of 3 to 4 mm, at the center of which was fixed a bar of positive-index metal and one of negative-index metal, forming a cross when the two parts were fitted one into the other. The equator of the C. Bomb, divided into 400 grades (color-vibrations), was encircled by a mobile copper marker fitted with a lug, on which rested an index, also of copper, having its fixed point at the North pole, and intended to explore the perimeter of the sphere. The cavity was meant to receive the biological witness of the patient to be treated. This witness then found itself between the two metals of different polarity, forming a battery, and the index was placed on the grade corresponding to the beneficial vibration previously located with the help of the Snail-Selector (we shall speak of this instrument later).

To function correctly, the Bomb had to be set facing the magnetic flux — that is, V− pointing toward the North.

This Bomb, as it was originally conceived by Chaumery, has today been completely modified. It is now the C.30 Bomb. Why C.30? Because it now has a diameter of 30 centimeters — more than four times the intensity of the original apparatus. Moreover it now consists of six half-spheres, that is, three complete spheres fitted one inside the other, each polarity having been carefully marked.

The innermost, smallest sphere contains the exposure chamber with interchangeable positive and negative metals. It is there that are placed the witness of the patient and the drawing of the organ to be treated. The outermost, largest sphere carries two meridians, one electric, the other magnetic, and likewise an electromagnetic equator. A copper index, called the "selector," axed at the North pole, can explore the perimeter of the equator, divided into 400 grades, where the color-vibrations of the spectrum, visible and invisible, are read. A mobile marker of non-magnetic metal encircles this equator, a marker fitted with a lug on which the said "selector" rests.

Further, since the energy emitted by the Bomb is unstable for the whole set of color-vibrations — with the exception of the V− beam, whose loss of power is infinitely smaller — a compensator and a magnetic corrector have been provided, whose role is to select and to balance the magnetic flux. The corrector is a mobile silver blade axed like the selector and placed below the latter, at the North pole; and the compensator is a disc, likewise of silver, divided into twelve markers representing the lunar months and their quadratures. This compensator overhangs the whole assembly on the North-pole axis of the sphere.

Finally, to increase still further the vibratory intensity of the Bomb, the latter is connected to the cosmic and terrestrial magnetic forces by means of two batteries whose description was given in the book *Essai de radiesthésie vibratoire*. Thus there is a vertical cosmic pile and a horizontal magnetic pile, the latter oriented on the terrestrial North/South axis.

What are the positive properties obtainable with the C.30 Bomb? First and foremost, the faculty of charging with vibratory oscillations everything subjected to its action. To charge a liquid, it suffices to remove the small inner sphere so as to have a cavity of sufficient size. Water or physiological serum thus treated with the radiations of various metals and radioactive products, combined with the vibratory frequencies emitted, acquires a great intensity of curative power. If one wishes to act directly on a patient's witness, the inner sphere must be put back in place and equipped with the metal blades suiting the case to be treated; then the patient's biological witness is placed in the exposure chamber while the selector is set on the grade emitting the frequency of health, the silver magnetic corrector placed on grade 200, V+, and the compensator always on the phase of the lunar month, facing grade 200.

To exercise a direct call on the cosmic forces, the pile overhanging the Bomb is fitted at its summit with a multi-strand antenna forming a tulip. The vibratory energy is conveyed through the pile to the very heart of the Bomb. One can also capture the cosmic planetary radio-waves by equipping the C.30 Bomb with an antenna-captor formed of two solenoids of opposite polarities, added to the multi-strand antenna. These solenoids are fitted with a ferrite rod to influence the preferred range of wavelengths, and connected to the central axis of the pile on the one hand, and to the magnetic corrector on the other. An orientable magnetic battery, formed of two cylindrical magnets incorporated inside the sphere, plays the role of call-selector, in a manner analogous to the built-in frames of radio receivers.

Such, summarily described, is this C.30 Bomb, whose practical applications authorize the greatest hopes. The probatory experiments already obtained with it exceed our most optimistic forecasts. It cannot, however, be placed in all hands, and its use demands of its users a competence beyond the ordinary, reserved in principle for the supervision of the medical corps, the only body officially entitled. Vibrated liquids, moreover, can in no case constitute remedies, these being likewise unable to be officially recognized.

## THE DISC EMITTER

### by A. de BÉLIZAL and P. A. MOREL

Before undertaking the description of our apparatus — the Disc Emitter, originally designated under the name "Shock-Wave Emitter" — it seems appropriate to initiate the reader into the very special vibratory properties of angular refraction. The layman who opens a book of geometry does not suspect that he is in the presence of the most formidable network of vibrations it is possible to condense within a small volume. Man has complicated everything, entangled everything; he has certainly obtained, through his science, incontestable positive results — but at what price? Take an example. It is time we returned to the simple laws that presided over creation, and used the invisible forces that God has scattered in profusion throughout the Universe.

Life is at once one and multiple; it is also energy, and the great scholar Einstein perfectly defined, in a now-celebrated formula, the equivalence of matter with this energy, and inversely. It was by widening our thoughts, by meditating on the power of form and of the sign, that we arrived at the certainty of finding ourselves before the debris of a lost science, and before invisible forces that it might perhaps one day be possible to liberate anew and to use.

Every craftsman who works wood uses a set square; yet all alike are unaware that they hold there an emitter of waves whose vibration is on the order of the gamma ray. Indeed the set square forms an angle of 90°, and when you thus trace two perpendicular lines having a common point, you obtain the shortest and most powerful vibration in the Universe: negative green (V−). It is form that engenders the vibration, and the emission always shoots forth along the horizontal line of the 90° angle when that line is oriented on the terrestrial North/South axis.

If a modification is made to the vertical line of the set square — displacing it to the right or to the left — one obtains different angles that are no longer 90°: acute or obtuse angles. The vibration changes likewise, and from 0 to 180° it is possible to detect the whole range of visible and invisible vibrations. And it is this property of angular refraction that allowed us to perfect an emitting apparatus whose power is a function of the number of line-elements entering into play, each angle bringing its vibratory energy. Now this energy can be considerably amplified by connecting it, as we shall indicate below, to the cosmo-magnetic forces.

Here, then, are the basic data described, which will allow the reader to understand more easily the characteristics and the process of using our apparatus. Imagine a mobile disc, pivoting on itself and shaped with 21 grooves, hollows and reliefs, the latter painted black. This disc is axed on a plate that bears inscribed all the visible and invisible vibrations of the spectrum, and one side of which supports a fixed wooden rule, likewise painted black, itself terminated by a barrel (*canon*) of the same color. According to the angular refraction of the grooves of the disc relative to the fixed rule, we obtain all the vibrations of the spectrum — and the 90° angle, the set square, gives negative green (V−), as we have just seen. The acute angles emit white, ultraviolet, violet, indigo, blue, and green+. The obtuse angles emit black, infrared, red, orange, yellow, and green−. One observation imposes itself: the parallel of the grooves relative to the fixed rule always gives positive green.

An index fixed at the base of the median groove (11th line) allows identification of the vibrations inscribed on the plate over a half-circumference of 180°. The vibratory emission at the exit of the barrel can take two aspects: first, a magnetic phase; second, an electric phase — according to whether one inverts or does not invert the lines of the disc relative to the fixed rule. One can thus obtain: either a V−negative or a V−positive, or again a V+ negative or a V+ positive.

From the therapeutic point of view, there is an advantage in emitting a magnetic phase, which lengthens the wavelength of the living organism, rather than an electric phase, which shortens that wavelength. Whence it follows that the magnetic phase is favorable and curative, while the electric phase is unfavorable and noxious. In conclusion: to obtain a V−, place the index of the disc on the green− position (magnetic). To obtain a V−positive, pivot the index through 180° so as to invert the sense of the lines relative to the fixed rule (electric phase). The same holds for the V+: one obtains, at an angle open to 180° and closed at 0°, either the V+ negative (magnetic phase) or the V+ positive (electric phase).

Such an emission of waves, due simply to angular refraction, is already very interesting in itself; now, it is possible to amplify this emission in proportions that can be considerable by equipping the disc emitter with two piles — one cosmic, the other magnetic. These piles, composed of a number X of elements that may comprise from 4 to 15 half-spheres, are surmounted by a captor antenna. The cosmic pile overhangs the mobile disc, while the magnetic pile is placed horizontally in prolongation of the North of the fixed rule of the plate.

Thus equipped, the Disc Emitter delivers a vibratory emission of very great intensity, which, as indicated above, can be either magnetic or electric.

To conclude, would it be permitted to us to formulate a hypothesis — namely, that the form waves of geometry issuing from the depths of time, contemporary with Creation, hold in their essence the two aspects of one same figure: Good and Evil. The one created by God, the other by an inverse, reciprocal and mysterious force, born of human nature and of the abstract, consequence of the bad use of liberty, which has corrupted the original energetic force.

This hypothesis can seem logical when we observe that to every action corresponds a reaction, as mechanics teaches us. To every affirmation corresponds its inverse, and to the exponent its reciprocal — this at least is established in pure physics as in biology. The hypothesis of geometric forms capturing the energy radiated by all the physical and abstract forms of energy in nature — assimilated in consequence to the action of Good and Evil — would seem to conform to deductive logic. The Chinese adopted, several millennia ago, a universal philosophy based in part on this principle: the Yin and the Yang, each of the faces of truth being in principle impregnated with the other. Would there not lie the explanation of those two values we have designated by pure convention: magnetic phase and electric phase? The magnetic phase, expression of the favorable forces assimilated to Good. The electric phase, impregnation of the inverse, unfavorable forces in principle, and thus assimilable to Evil.

Studied from this angle, in this light, would it not then be easier for us to better understand the "why of things" and the dualist principle residing in the expression of geometric forms? We leave the reader to judge the merit of this hypothesis.

## GEOMETRIC FORMS

### by A. de BÉLIZAL and P. A. MOREL

### Definition of Form Waves

Geometric forms in their three dimensions — linear, plane, and volume — are influenced by the terrestrial magnetic current. Through the composition of their forms, they capture total energy, in a very small part resulting from their combination. Saturated with the energy captured, they radiate it anew, and so on. The same happens, it would seem, for terrestrial radiation. This is what we call form waves (*ondes de forme*).

The waves emitted by symmetrical forms are beneficial. Those emitted by irregular, uncompensated polygons are maleficent and noxious.
