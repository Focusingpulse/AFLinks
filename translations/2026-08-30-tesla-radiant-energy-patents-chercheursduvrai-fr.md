In his work Tesla called "method of conversion" the conversion of "normal electricity" into "longitudinal waves of electrostatic force," known as radiant energy. As I show in my DVD (Ndt: DVD of a 2006 conference on this theme), Tesla's radiant energy patents clearly show that radiant energy is defined as an electrostatic charge CARRIED by radiant matter. Radiant matter consists of neutral particles of mass at least 100 times smaller than that of electrons. The "method of conversion" is generally to charge an inductor with intermittent direct current, and to collect the inductive discharges in a capacitor. Then, the capacitor must be discharged into another inductor of very high inductance and low impedance to increase the voltage even further for use in lighting or motor systems. In studying Tesla's "method of conversion" it becomes evident that he was speaking of a vast category of phenomena. The original diagram shows 6 different processes, all variants of the production method.

The term "radiant energy" came from Tesla's friendship and admiration for Sir William Crookes who invented the radiometer, a device that Tesla called the most elegant motor ever built. Crookes was studying the effects related to emissions from incandescent matter. The question was, when a piece of matter is heated to the point that it emits light, what EXACTLY comes from the matter that carries these properties (heat and light) into the surrounding space? The radiometer showed that these emissions had mass and could carry a PHYSICAL FORCE to another object. Tesla came to believe that ANY source of light and heat, such as the Sun for example, emitted "that which" is emitted when MATTER RADIATES! Whatever it was, it was called "radiant matter." Since it could pass through the glass of the radiometer without causing the vacuum contained in the enclosure to cease, it was concluded that the particle size was INFINITESIMALLY SMALL. Tesla's subsequent experiments determined that these radiant matter particles were the primary CARRIERS of electrostatic charge.

This represents one of Tesla's many patents called "electrical circuit controller." This patent is very interesting because it consists of two electric motors, each rotating in the opposite direction to the other, with a spark gap between them. Tesla was clearly trying to achieve faster speeds than he could obtain by rotating only one motor. 2) Static spark gaps:

A magnetetically quenched spark gap, from Tesla's patents. This one is an electromagnet and not a permanent magnet as Vassilatos reported. It is part of his equipment intended to "blow" or quench the discharge sparks.

Direct current spark discharges do not start easily. The presence of adjustable handles on each side allows the two rods of the spark system to be brought close enough together to trigger the initial spark. Then the two rods must be separated from each other to bring them back to the intended distance.

Magnetically quenched spark mechanism

For Lindemann, this is proof that Tesla indeed worked with direct current, since DC spark arcs do not start easily. Whereas an AC spark gap would have had a fixed distance, allowing it to trigger at a preset voltage, with direct current, the same voltage does not trigger the arc. The electrodes must first be brought together to trigger the arc and then brought back to the desired distance corresponding to the intended voltage.

(NdT: personal note, if it was an electromagnet, perhaps he also applied alternating or pulsed current to power it, which would have caused the "blowing" of the electric arc at a frequency determined by the power supply of this electromagnet. In any case, Vassilatos speaks of a permanent magnet to blow the arc. This may be true or it may be an imprecision.)

In this spark gap, Tesla blows hot air through the spark gap, and as indicated in the text accompanying the drawing in the patent, there is also a magnetic field present. Tesla thus used both hot air and a magnetic field in his spark apparatus, which clearly shows that he was seeking numerous possibilities to have more control over the discharge of sparks.

His oil circuit (through the control of the oil flow rate) allows Tesla to control the recovery period of the current (the pulse duration) in addition to the frequency at which it is emitted by the gap distance.
