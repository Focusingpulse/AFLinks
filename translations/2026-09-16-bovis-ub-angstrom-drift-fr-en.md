# The Bovis Scale Drift: When the Ruler Changes, the Pendulum Follows

*English translation and dossier · sources: afis.org (Jacques Bolard, "1 bovis = 1 angström ?", *Science et pseudo-sciences*, 24 March 2016); naturolistique.fr "Le biomètre de Bovis" (Andrea Monin Galdi, 11 July 2025) and "Le taux vibratoire" (29 July 2025); bretagne-namaste.com "Élever son taux vibratoire"; energie-denis-sanchez.fr "Le taux vibratoire" (Denis Sanchez, 11 April 2017); stage-magnetisme.fr "Taux vibratoire et unité Bovis"; scout fire fr-79 (FAL-fr-79-2) · translated/compiled by Forge (translation-qc), 2026-09-16*

**The essentials:** The Bovis scale — the unit the entire "taux vibratoire" world runs on — has been silently rescaled. The human health baseline that Alfred Bovis anchored at 6,500 in the 1930s was officially moved to 12,500 UB "since 2014" (+92%), with new dials running to 60,000 and 120,000 UB, sacred sites to millions, and at least one "convertible biometer" scaling to billions — all with no change to the instrument, only to the narrative ("the Earth's vibrational elevation") and the printed graduations. This dossier assembles the primary critique (Bolard/AFIS), the practitioner-side sources teaching the new baseline, and the mechanism named in the critique itself: *re-label the ruler, and the pendulum adapts by itself.*

## 1. The unit's origin — Bolard's foundational critique (AFIS, 2016)

[Translated from Jacques Bolard, "1 bovis = 1 angström ?", *Science et pseudo-sciences*, 24 March 2016 — the primary critique, by a CNRS biophysicist]

> The bovis is the name of a unit of energy, which readers of *Science et pseudo-sciences* certainly do not know, but which the partisans of bio-energetics, radiesthesia and geobiology commonly use to designate the "vibratory rate" of the constituents of the universe, or the "cosmo-telluric energy" of a place or a body — a very poorly defined notion that varies from author to author…
>
> The purpose of these few lines is not to discuss the merit of these approaches, but the merit of the *measure* used.
>
> Everything comes from what Alfred Bovis, around 1935, proposed the "biometer" that bears his name. This "instrument" consisted of a rule graduated from 0 to 100 over 20 cm, graduation 100 corresponding to 6,500 angströms¹ — "the longest wavelength of the visible spectrum for man." Bovis moved his pendulum along the rule placed next to the object whose vibratory energy had to be determined, until the pendulum's reaction at a graduation allowed the determination of that energy. He found, for example, 120° for a grain of maize, 75° for a nickel coin.
>
> Subsequently, radiesthésistes used a 30 cm rule graduated directly in angströms, from 0 to 10,000 (thus 6,666 angströms at 20 cm, no longer 6,500). By reference to Alfred Bovis, they replaced the degrees he used with "bovis," equivalent to angströms. They find values on the order of 10,000 for man — but an impressive increase, in just a few years, of the "vibratory rates" of the universe is described by many, those of man having passed from 6,500 to 15,000 bovis… Some measure 4×10⁶ bovis at the center of Chartres cathedral (4×10⁶ Å = infrared), up to 6×10¹² bovis for the Luxor pyramid (6×10¹² Å = radio waves).
>
> How can one measure such high values when the 30 cm rule is limited to 10,000 bovis? For a radiesthésiste this is not difficult: one need only change the graduations of the 30 cm rule — moving, for example, from a scale of 0 to 10,000 to a scale of 0 to 1,000,000 — and the trick is done: **the pendulum will adapt all by itself!**
>
> Recently, all these approximations have nevertheless led certain radiesthésistes to question the soundness of this scale, and to point out that if the energy of electromagnetic waves can indeed be linked to their wavelength, the "vibratory rate," being of an unknown nature, can only be estimated according to its own laws, which are not physical. In other words, they do not quite see the relation between the color of an object, a place, a living being, and its "vibratory rate." Finally, according to them, the bovis can only be arbitrary and relative. These remarks lead them to wonder whether it would not be better to abandon the bovis… But then what are all the previously described results worth? If the radiesthésistes' principal accessory, the pendulum, gives fanciful measurements that have been, and still are, blindly considered reliable for some fifty years, this is fit to cast a certain discredit on the method itself and the conclusions it has developed from these data. One cannot accept the foundations of an approach whose results depend on the operator's feelings.

¹ The angström (symbol Å) is a unit of *length* equal to 10⁻¹⁰ m, used for atomic radii (0.25 to 3 Å) and the wavelengths of light (roughly 4,000–7,500 Å for the visible).

**The three facts Bolard fixes, which everything else in this dossier presupposes:**

1. **The anchor is a length.** 100 on the original 20 cm rule = 6,500 Å = deep red light. The "vibratory rate" was from the beginning a *wavelength metaphor* — the longest visible wavelength, not a measured property of any body or place.
2. **The conversion already drifted at birth.** Moving from Bovis's 0–100/20 cm rule to the 0–10,000 Å/30 cm rule silently changed the anchor from 6,500 Å to ~6,666 Å at the same physical position. The unit was inconsistent before it was ever a "unit."
3. **The scale-inflation mechanism is internal.** Values above 10,000 are only reachable by re-graduating the rule — and Bolard's sentence "le pendule s'adaptera tout seul" (the pendulum will adapt all by itself) is the whole critique in one line: the instrument reads whatever the operator expects the rule to say. Bolard also records that practitioners *themselves* began asking whether the bovis should be abandoned — the tradition's own internal audit failed.

## 2. The drift, quantified — the practitioner sources

### 2.1 The official-looking rescale: "since 2014, 12,500 UB"

[Translated from naturolistique.fr, "Le biomètre de Bovis : comment mesurer les unités Bovis ?", Andrea Monin Galdi, 11 July 2025]

> For a long time, the zone of good health for a human was considered to correspond to an average vibratory rate between 6,500 and 9,000 Bovis units.
>
> For some decades — especially in recent years — one observes that the average rate tends to rise toward 10,000 UB, even 12,500 UB, especially since 2014. The average rate has thus passed from 6,500 to 12,500 UB and continues to increase, reaching 15,000 UB and beyond.
>
> One should therefore remember that generally, the new base reference is currently around 12,500 Bovis units. It would be necessary to strive to maintain this global level at all costs to remain in good health and inwardly balanced. Consequently, any lower measurement will indicate a loss of energy; any higher measurement, on the contrary, a gain, an increase and a reserve of energy that our organism and mind need.
>
> In principle, one will take the new value of 12,500 Bovis units as neutral for the health of an average individual. As explained above, the average (and neutral point) of 6,500 UB has been exceeded since 2014. But most scales and representations of Bovis units still always retain 6,500 UB as the biotic equilibrium point.

And the two-zone structure of the new dial:

> [The scale] is divided into two zones: the first goes from 0 to 12,500 UB and is used to measure the material world (ascending force); the second goes from 12,500 to 120,000 UB and is destined to measure the descending cosmic force — thus energetic and spiritual.

And the table the same source prints, showing the rescale as a *coordinated re-mapping of every band*:

| | Conventional scale | After 2014 ("Earth's vibratory elevations") |
|---|---|---|
| Physical plane | 0 – 10,000 UB | 0 – 20,000 UB |
| Energetic plane | 10,000 – 13,500 UB | 20,000 – 36,000 (or 40,000) UB |
| Spiritual plane | 13,500 – 18,000 UB | 36,000 (or 40,000) – 930,000,000,000,000 UB |

Note the top of the new spiritual band: **9.3×10¹⁴ UB** — on a unit nominally equal to an angström, that value corresponds to a wavelength of 93 meters: medium-wave radio. The "spiritual plane" of the rescaled Bovis dial is, in its own ostensible units, a broadcast band.

### 2.2 The narrative that justifies it: "élévation vibratoire de la Terre"

[Translated from energie-denis-sanchez.fr, "Le taux vibratoire", Denis Sanchez, 11 April 2017]

> Many radiesthésistes and geobiologists have noticed that the human vibratory frequency has doubled in three years. It has passed from 6,500 to 12,500, and now in 2014 it is 15,000 units on the Bovis scale. The reason is at once simple and fantastic: the vibratory rate of the terrestrial globe is rising at great speed. Many humans, for lack of spiritual structuring, will not entirely pass through the four years to come, for this frequency is going to triple again during this period.
>
> The new base reference in 2014 is 12,500 units — the average measurement of a healthy human being. Any lower measurement indicates a loss of energy; any higher measurement indicates a gain, a reserve of energy.

This is the drift's *mechanism of spread*: the rescale is not presented as a correction of the instrument but as a property of the world — the Earth itself is rising, so yesterday's healthy person (6,500) is today's sick one (anything below 12,500), and the healer's dial must be re-read accordingly. The same page supplies the pastoral values: cattle "vibrate" around 4,000 UB; wild carnivores 12,000–16,000; horses 18,000–27,000.

[Translated from bretagne-namaste.com, "Élever son taux vibratoire"]

> If the measurements of Bovis's era fixed the vibratory-rate threshold of a healthy human being between 6,500 and 9,000, this rate would have increased and was re-evaluated in 2014 at 10,000 to 15,000 UB. The new average reference would thus be 12,500 UB. This evolution gave birth to the new Bovis dial, which integrates a wider spectrum.
>
> The new Bovis dial, updated in 2014, is graduated up to 60,000 UB. Another version of this dial goes up to 120,000 UB — just as the original dial had one version at 18,000 UB and another at 32,000 UB.
>
> With this new dial, the references for the three vibratory levels are as follows: Physical plane 0 to 20,000 UB (instead of 0 to 10,000 on the classic scale); Energetic plane 20,000 to 36,000 UB (instead of 10,000 to 14,000); Spiritual plane 36,000 to 60,000 UB (instead of 14,000 to 18,000).
>
> The new dial was proposed following the realization that the vibratory rates measured increase with time. It thus adapts to current measurements and to the new energies.

The last paragraph is the drift stated as a *design feature*: the dial "adapts to current measurements." In metrology this is backwards — the instrument is fixed and the world is measured against it — but here the world is fixed (rising, per the narrative) and the instrument is updated to track it. Bolard's "the pendulum will adapt all by itself" is the same fact seen from the two sides of the rule.

## 3. What the drift does to the lineage — the corpus connection

The Bovis scale is the corpus's best-documented subtle-energy unit, and the drift lands on every generation of its lineage:

- **Bovis (1930s):** the 6,500 Å anchor — deep red light, the longest visible wavelength. The one physical referent the unit ever had.
- **Simoneton (1949, *Radiovitalité*):** extended the scale to foods and human bodies, still in angströms — superior foods 6,500–10,000 Å, the healthy human "around 6,500–7,000" (per the corpus dossier, after Lesaffre 2025). Simoneton's food *orderings* are the testable residue; his angströms inherit the anchor.
- **Merz (1980s–2001):** rated places in Bovis units — Chartres 11,000, mosque niches 12,000, neutral ground 6,500. Her values sit *inside* the old dial and are now, by the rescaled standard, "low": a Chartres crossing at 11,000 UB is below the 2014 human baseline. The rescale quietly re-grades the tradition's own canon.
- **The 2014+ layer:** the baseline doubles, the dial stretches to 60,000/120,000, sites inflate to millions and (in Bolard's inventory) to 6×10¹² for the Luxor pyramid — radio-wave territory in the unit's own ostensible terms.

The pattern across all four generations is the one the bench notes have been assembling: **the number is never measured; it is re-labelled.** Bovis anchored a metaphor (red light) to a rule; Simoneton kept the metaphor and dropped the measurement; Merz kept the number and dropped the angström; the 2014 layer keeps the pendulum and moves the number to wherever the narrative needs it. Each step is documented in the tradition's own materials, and each step makes the previous generation's published values wrong by decree rather than by detection.

**The sharpest single fact:** the rescale has a date (2014), a named cause (the Earth's vibratory elevation), a new neutral point (12,500 UB), and new dials (60,000/120,000) — and *no instrument change, no recalibration procedure, and no before/after measurement*. A unit whose baseline moves +92% on a narrative, while the physical rule and the pendulum stay identical, is Bolard's sentence demonstrated at population scale: change the graduations, and the pendulum adapts all by itself.

## 4. Testable residue

Consistent with the bench-note series (Simoneton/biophotonics, Merz/inter-rater, Lecher/bench-tunable), the honest tests this dossier leaves are:

1. **Inter-rater, old-dial vs. new-dial** (extends the Merz bench note): run the same blinded dowser panel with both the 0–18,000 classic dial and the 0–120,000 new dial. If readings scale with the dial (a site read ~9,000 on the classic reads ~18,000 on the new), the dial is producing the number, not the site. If readings are stable across dials, something else is going on — either result is informative, and the experiment costs nothing but two printed cards.
2. **The pre/post-2014 canon check:** Merz's published values (Chartres 11,000, neutral 6,500) are pre-rescale artifacts. Any dowser trained on the new dial should systematically read Merz's sites "low." Surveying practitioners on what they think Chartres reads today, against her 11,000, measures the drift's penetration directly — a questionnaire, not a field trip.
3. **The angström absurdity row:** the rescaled tradition's own numbers, converted at its own claimed exchange rate (1 UB = 1 Å), put the spiritual plane at 9.3×10¹⁴ Å (93 m — medium-wave radio) and Luxor at 6×10¹² Å (600 m — long-wave radio). No further critique is needed than the unit's own arithmetic; this row belongs in every dossier that quotes a UB value above 10,000.
