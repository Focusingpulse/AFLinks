Dorin Koepper, Marion Kuprat and Alejandro Nadal

Technology for healthy water

It was in 1999 when Marion Kuprat, fascinated by the studies of Viktor Schauberger, met Peter Gross, the only technician who had applied, in his "GIE Activator," Viktor Schauberger's implosion-vortex techniques to the letter. Viktor Schauberger (1885–1958), Austrian forest warden, discoverer and visionary, was one of the few people who truly understood water. As early as 1935 he built his first spring-water regenerator, which would later be patented as equipment for the energetization of water. His enormous capacity for observation allowed him to understand the natural forms of water's movement by means of the behavior of the animals within it.

To this day, Viktor Schauberger is regarded as one of the most important forerunners of "free energy" in the sense of implosion rather than explosion — knowledge undeniably connected with the movement and the special suction frequencies of vortices.

This is how the Agua Viva eco-tecnologías project begins, with the hope of transmitting the knowledge that the structural quality of drinking water is introduced literally into the heart of life — that is, into the center of our cells, where the balance of our health is decided, because the functioning of our body is primarily electrical and not merely chemical. Agua Viva was born with the firm purpose of finding the technology that can provide us with healthy, ecological water, and of sharing this knowledge with as many people as possible. First from Tenerife and later also from Barcelona, we have made the recognized GIE technique known in Spain, along with a series of other very interesting products for producing ecological water.

Agua Viva eco-tecnologías is dedicated to importing advanced technologies for water; our team is committed to advising, guiding, and helping by answering all your questions.

"If tomorrow morning you could make clean water for the world, you would have done the best you can do to improve human health and environmental quality." — William C. Clark
