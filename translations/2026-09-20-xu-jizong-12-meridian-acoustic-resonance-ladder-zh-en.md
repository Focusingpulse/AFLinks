# Xu Jizong's 12-Meridian Acoustic Resonance Ladder — The Measured Sound-Geometry Protocol of Chinese Meridian Acoustics

**A ZH→EN dossier for the sound-geometry / meridian-resonance rail. Claim FAL-zh-129-2 (scout fire 129). Translator: Forge.**

This dossier compiles and translates the key content of Xu Jizong's (许继宗) 2014–15 publication series — the most complete *measured* "amplify-and-direct" sound protocol in the Chinese lane — together with the mechanism doctrine that anchors it (Pan Liang et al. 2016) and the 2025–26 randomized evidence shelf that now sits on top of it. It is the companion piece to the Temple of Heaven acoustics program (FAL-zh-129-1): there, geometry directs sound at the architectural scale; here, "geometry" (the meridian system) is claimed to *receive* sound at the physiological scale, with instruments.

---

## 1. The Series and Its Method

Between 2014 and 2015, Xu Jizong and colleagues published a series of studies applying somatosensory low-frequency sound (16–160 Hz, played in sequence) to named acupoints while measuring local microcirculation with **laser Doppler flowmetry**. Each study used **N = 30 healthy subjects**; statistics are reported with P < 0.01 significance thresholds.

The methodological signature across the series:

- **Stimulus:** software-synthesized musical tones (电脑软件合成乐音) in the equal-temperament scale, delivered as somatosensory low-frequency sound in the 16–160 Hz band, played in sequence at acupoint families.
- **Measurement:** laser Doppler flowmetry — a real physiological instrument measuring local blood-flow (microcirculation) at the stimulated acupoint.
- **Design:** each meridian tested across its five-shu point family (井/荥/输/经/合 — well/spring/stream/jing/sea points), N=30 per run.

## 2. The Full 12-Meridian Frequency Ladder

The central result: each meridian has a characteristic sound frequency at which its acupoints show maximal microcirculation response, and **the response is invariant across the meridian's five-shu point family** (井/荥/输/经/合 all give the same frequency per meridian).

| Meridian | Test points | Note label | Frequency (Hz) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spleen (脾经) | Taibai/Dadu/Yinlingquan | A0# | **29.14** |
| Stomach (胃经) | Xiangu/Neiting/Zusanli | A1# | **58.27** |
| Liver (肝经) | Taichong/Xingjian/Ququan | D1 | **36.71** |
| Gallbladder (胆经) | Zulinqi/Xiaxi/Yanglingquan | D2 | **73.42** |
| Lung (肺经) | Taiyuan/Yuji/Chize | C1 | **32.70** |
| Large Intestine (大肠经) | Sanjian/Erjian/Quchi | C2 | **65.41** |
| Kidney (肾经) | Taixi/Rangu/Yingu | G1 | **49.00** |
| Bladder (膀胱经) | Shugu/Zutonggu/Weizhong | G2 | **98.00** |
| Heart (心经) | Shenmen/Shaofu/Shaohai | F1 | **43.65** |
| Small Intestine (小肠经) | Houxi/Qiangu/Xiaohai | F2 | **87.31** |
| Pericardium (心包经) | Daling/Laogong/Quze | A1 | **55.00** |
| Triple Burner (三焦经) | Zhongzhu/Yemen/Tianjing | A2 | **110.00** |

Two structural findings:

1. **The twelve frequencies are octave-paired.** Every yang/yin same-element pair is exactly ×2: Stomach 58.27 = 2 × Spleen 29.14; Gallbladder 73.42 = 2 × Liver 36.71; Large Intestine 65.41 = 2 × Lung 32.70; Bladder 98 = 2 × Kidney 49; Small Intestine 87.31 = 2 × Heart 43.65; Triple Burner 110 = 2 × Pericardium 55. The ladder is a 1:2 structure across complementary meridians — the same octave-doubling logic as the classical Chinese pitch-arithmetic tradition (九三相生 / 黄钟, the 3^12 cycle).
2. **The note labels are literally Western equal-temperament pitches** (A0#, A1#, D1, D2, C1, G1, F1, A1, A2...). The papers label them as such. The octave-pair "discovery" is therefore at least partly an artifact of stimulus construction: the tones were *generated* as equal-temperament octaves, so octave pairs were built into the stimulus set before any meridian responded. This is the series' most important honest caveat — the octave structure is arithmetic, not an independent physiological finding.

## 3. The Amplify-and-DIRECT Grammar: Three Independent Controls

What elevates the series above a frequency table is that it reports **three separate direction controls**, all measured:

### 3.1 Frequency → organ selectivity
Each meridian's characteristic frequency produces the most significant microcirculation response *at that meridian's acupoints* — e.g., Kidney meridian G1 49.00 Hz (P < 0.01), Bladder meridian G2 98.00 Hz (P < 0.01). The claim: selective absorption per meridian/acupoint, i.e., the frequency "addresses" the organ channel.

### 3.2 Modulation direction → qi movement (气机升降出入)
The stomach-meridian study (CQVIP 665700684) takes the same 58.27 Hz base tone and applies software-processed modulation variants:

- Frequency *rising* → stomach-channel qi-blood **declining**
- Frequency *falling* → qi-blood **rising**
- Modulation small→large → **dispersal outward** (发散, 出)
- Modulation large→small → **convergence inward** (汇聚, 入)

This is the sharpest "direction" claim in the series: the *trajectory* of the tone, not just its carrier frequency, is claimed to control the direction of the physiological effect. The 2015 clinical paper (治验3则) then applies the full stack to three case reports, using the classical *Fuxing Jue* (辅行诀) five-element generation-and-restraint rules as the tonification/drainage (补泻) decision rule.

### 3.3 Timbre → five-element effect (Weizhong BL40 study, CQVIP 662548797)
Same 98 Hz carrier, five simulated timbres:

- **Bell, drum, flute** *raise* microcirculation and transcutaneous O2 — drum > bell > flute (P < 0.01)
- **Ocarina and guqin** *lower* it (ocarina > guqin)

The paper's own reading: "不同音色的五行属性不同, 频谱成分不同" — different timbres carry different five-element properties because their spectral components differ. Timbre, not just pitch, is the operative variable.

## 4. The Mechanism Doctrine: Pan Liang's 2016 Sound-Wave Resonance Paper

The citation-accumulating bridge between the ladder and classical five-tone theory (五音) is:

**Pan Liang, Fan Di, Hu Hui (潘良/樊笛/胡慧), "Exploring the Mechanism of Five-Tone Therapy Based on Sound-Wave Resonance Principles" (基于声波共振原理探讨五音治病的作用机制), *Journal of Beijing University of Chinese Medicine* 2016, 39(9):731–733 (DOI 10.3969/j.issn.1006-2157.2016.09.006).**

The doctrine, translated from its core claims:

- The five zang organs each possess an **intrinsic frequency** (五脏有固有频率)
- Frequency vibration generates energy (频率震动产生能量)
- Disease is a change in that energy state (病变 = 能量改变)
- The five tones stimulate resonance with their corresponding organs, **"reducing the excess and supplementing the insufficiency"** (损有余而补不足) — the classical resonance-therapy formula from the *Huangdi Neijing* tradition

This is a *stated hypothesis*, not a measurement — no organ frequency is directly measured in the paper. But it has become the standard citation bridge: the August 2026 *Liaoning Journal of TCM University* five-tone review (NSFC 8157579 + Shandong NSF ZR2025MS1079 funding) cites it as the mechanism reference while candidly listing the field's open items: **no standard for matching qi-mechanism to music selection (辨气机施乐标准缺失), high intervention heterogeneity (干预方案异质性大), and weak mechanism research on the tone–qi–organ chain (音-气-脏腑机制研究薄弱)**. PMC10128547 (a personalized-music review) also cites it.

## 5. The 2025–26 Evidence Shelf: Five-Tone Therapy Goes RCT-Grade

The amplification claims now sit on a randomized, instrumented evidence shelf — the largest single upgrade to the Chinese sound lane since the equal-temperament meridian map:

| Study | Design | Key result |
|---|---|---|
| **Dong Yanping et al. 2025**, *Beijing Journal of TCM* 44(4):472–475 (DOI 10.16025/j.1674-1307.2025.04.016) | RCT, 64 post-mastectomy breast-cancer patients (Jiangsu Provincial TCM Hospital), five-element music listening vs control, HRV via multi-parameter vital-signs monitor | Post-op day 1/7: SAS/SDS anxiety-depression scores and LF/HF ratio **lower**, SDNN/PNN50 **higher** in the music arm (P < 0.05) — "correcting sympathetic/parasympathetic balance, raising vagal tone" |
| **Armed Police Medical Journal 2025.07.011** (DOI 10.3969/j.issn.1004-3594.2025.07.011) | RCT, 64 male MDD patients, drug ± five-tone therapy (5×/week, 30–40 min, 4 weeks) | HAMD 6.29±4.13 vs 9.77±7.31; PHQ-9 3.32 vs 5.87; HRV: SDNN 81.32±31.64 vs 66.72±28.58, RMSSD 104.14±68.19 vs 57.63±34.26, LF/HF 3.25±2.33 vs 7.97±6.41 (all P < 0.05) |
| **ChiCTR2400085665** (Guangzhou TCM-affiliated hospital, *Complementary Therapies in Medicine* 2025) | Three-arm assessor-blinded RCT, 87 maintenance-hemodialysis patients: five-tone (FEM) vs Western music vs control, 3×/week 60 min, 8 weeks, GEE analysis, sealed-envelope allocation | HADS-A 10.93→8.03 stepwise (p < 0.05); PANAS dual modulation (+3.00 positive, −3.38 negative vs Western music, p = 0.014/0.005); **FEM > Western music on mood — the cultural-specificity finding, the strongest RCT support yet for the tone–qi–organ claim** |
| **Psychiatry Research 2025** (Guangzhou Brain Hospital, 201 moderate MDD) | Three-arm, five-element vs Western music vs control, 30 min/day × 14 days, assessor-blinded | HAMD MD −1.94 (95%CI −3.77~−0.11, P = 0.034) over Western music; faster HAMA onset (day 7, P = 0.021); sleep-scale ongoing decline only in the five-element arm (B = −3.119, P = 0.001) |
| **Frontiers in Psychology 2026 meta-analysis** (DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1750786) | 24 RCTs, 1,295 participants, 13 countries, PROSPERO CRD420261283257 | Music → HFnu **+7.05** (95%CI 1.00–13.10, p = 0.02), LFnu **−4.94** (−9.13~−0.76, p = 0.02); largest gains in stress/anxiety/sleep; ≤30-min interventions best; participant-selected > standardized; moderate evidence quality |

**The honest magnitude anchor:** sound-frequency intervention on HRV is a moderate-quality, meta-analytically confirmed *small* effect. That is the honest upper bound for the five-tone organ-resonance claims. Named instruments (HRV monitors, SDNN/RMSSD/LF/HF), randomization, assessor blinding, and trial registration now exist — but no study has yet tested the Xu Jizong ladder's specific claim (tone-frequency → *organ-specific* HRV response) directly, and no subtle-force step appears anywhere in the chain.

The registered fringe tail: a college-anxiety RCT (guzheng + singing-bowl five-tone, n=311 survey, SAS 52.3→40.1 vs no-change control, P < 0.01) — the singing-bowl retail claim with a paper attached.

## 6. Honest Read (QC assessment)

**What is solid:** laser Doppler flowmetry is a real physiological measurement; N=30 per study with reported P-values; three independent direction controls (frequency, modulation trajectory, timbre) — a genuinely more complete experimental grammar than most sound-therapy literature; the 2025–26 RCT shelf has real randomization, blinding, registration, and a meta-analytic magnitude.

**What is not:** no blinding or expectation control in the ladder series; all rows from the same lab/author; no independent replication (the standing open item from the equal-temperament map); the octave-pair structure is arithmetic — the stimuli were generated as equal-temperament octaves, so the ×2 pairs were built in before measurement; Pan Liang's "intrinsic organ frequencies" remain a stated hypothesis with no direct organ-frequency measurement; and the RCT shelf measures *autonomic* effects (HRV, mood), not organ-specific resonance.

**Register value:** the Chinese lane's most complete measured sound-geometry protocol — the direct zh answer to the "amplify-DIRECT pair" question, and a cross-lane bridge both to the classical octave/3^12 pitch arithmetic and to the 2025–26 RCT stack. As with the Temple of Heaven row: shape/sound effects are real, measured, and *ordinary physiology and acoustics* — the instruments that confirm the effects are the same instruments that find no subtle-force step.

**Watch items:** whether any RCT attempts the ladder's claims directly (tone-frequency → organ-specific HRV); whether the ChiCTR MHD trial publishes full data; whether the Xu series gets independent replication outside the original lab.
