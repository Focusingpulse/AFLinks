# Goethe's Scientific Works

## A Course of Study on Rudolf Steiner's "Goethe's Scientific Works"
### (Fratelli Melita Editori, Genova 1988)

**Note**

Our friend Roberto Marcelli, to whom we already owe the transcription of the course of study on *The Philosophy of Freedom* published on this site, has now kindly sent us the transcription of another course, also held by Lucio Russo (from September 2000 to November 2001), on Rudolf Steiner's *Goethe's Scientific Works* (Fratelli Melita Editori, Genova 1988).

These are fifty-two meetings which, hoping to be pleasing to the friends of our site, we publish after having appropriately reworked them (with the author's help).
