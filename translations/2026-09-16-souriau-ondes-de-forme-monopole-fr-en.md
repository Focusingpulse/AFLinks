## The book

**Les ondes de forme vues par un physicien** ("Form Waves Seen by a Physicist"), Dr. Laurent Souriau. Editions Servranx, first issued 26 June 2013 (paper, 86–88 pp., ISBN 9782872421206); ePub 29 March 2017 (76 pp., ISBN 9782872421459, DRM-free, €7.99; paper €15.00, at times "en réimpression"/indisponible at retail). Catalog-tagged under Radionique at Servranx. Subtitle on the digital edition: *Les ondes de forme ont une action bien réelle* ("Form waves have a very real action").

**The author.** Doctor of Physical Sciences (PhD from the faculty of Marseille, late 1980s — his electromagnetism teacher there was Roger Petit), specialized in electromagnetic waves; independent researcher since 2003; teacher in Île-de-France; long interest in Feng Shui and geobiology, which he says is what sensitized him to the form-emission problem. His own site (physiqueodf.free.fr, last updated July 2013) is dedicated to the *physical interpretation* of ondes de forme.

**The publisher's framing** (Servranx/Eyrolles blurb, my translation):

> Do the objects and shapes around us emit waves or energy into their environment? The problem of the existence and nature of form waves leaves no one indifferent. Most radiesthesists and geobiologists use these form waves in their daily practice, without however knowing the real nature of the phenomenon they deal with daily. The official scientific world does not recognize their existence. Numerous more-or-less far-fetched hypotheses have been put forward on this subject without ever having been demonstrated. For the first time, Laurent Souriau, a qualified physicist, sets out to define and explore with rigor the nature of the form-emission phenomenon. The author gives his physicist's point of view and provides very precise answers to this question. [...] The author presents in this book several experiments allowing one to affirm that form emissions have a very real action in the domains of **protection against radioactivity, chemistry, the transmutation of chemical elements, and health**.

The blurb's last line is the book's own claim of scope: reader-reproducible experiments that "prove the existence of form waves."

## The thesis: form emissions are light leptonic magnetic monopoles

This is what makes the book more than a curiosity, and it is stated plainly on Souriau's site (my translation):

> I have shown in the book *Les ondes de forme vues par un physicien* and in the two videos above that **form emissions are magnetic monopoles, that is, magnetically charged neutrinos**. In the same work, I present an experiment proving that one of the possible uses of these magnetic monopoles is the **REDUCTION OF AMBIENT RADIOACTIVITY in a contaminated zone**. The underlying principle of this action is the weak interaction (one of the four fundamental interactions of the universe).

The chain Souriau plugs into is **Georges Lochak's leptonic monopole theory** — a real, published, heterodox physics program:

- **The theory.** Lochak (Fondation Louis de Broglie; 1930–2021, heir to de Broglie's school) proposed from 1983 onward that the *massless* Dirac equation admits a **second electromagnetic coupling** (a pseudo-scalar gauge invariance), yielding an equation for a **light leptonic magnetic monopole** with the symmetry laws Pierre Curie predicted in 1894. Key papers: *Wave equation for a magnetic monopole* (Int. J. Theor. Phys. 24, 1985); *The Equation of a Light Leptonic Magnetic Monopole and its Experimental Aspects* (Z. Naturforsch. 62a, 231–246, 2007; arXiv 0801.2752); book: *Theory of the Leptonic Monopole* (Advances in Imaging and Electron Physics 189, 2015).
- **The particle.** Not the heavy GUT monopole everybody hunts — a **fermion with weak interactions**, effectively **"a magnetically excited neutrino"**: in the Weyl representation the equation splits into P-conjugated monopole/antimonopole equations with opposite chiralities, and it reduces to a pair of ordinary neutrino equations when the magnetic charge g = 0. Lochak's derivation also refines Dirac's electric/magnetic charge relation (Dirac 1931: magnetic charge = integer multiple of ~68.5 electron charges) and gives a quantum form of Poincaré's first integral; the classical limit is Poincaré's monopole equation and satisfies the Birkeland effect (Lochak's own favorite indirect confirmation).
- **Predicted production channel.** Monopoles should be produced by **electromagnetic pulses or electric arcs**, leading to nuclear transmutations and — for beta-radioactive elements — **shortened lifetimes and emission of monopoles instead of neutrinos in a magnetic field**. This is the weak-interaction lever Souriau invokes for his radioactivity-reduction claim.
- **The experimental flank (Russian).** Since 1998, Leonid Urutskoev's group at RECOM (Kurchatov Institute) studied electric explosions of titanium foil in water (50 kJ, 5 kV, ~0.1 ms) and reported **distortion of the natural titanium isotope ratios** in the product powder. The search for the monopole then produced the experiment Lochak called "one of the most brilliant": Urutskoev & Ivoilov irradiated **⁵⁷Fe foil** near the presumed monopole source, with one pole of a long linear magnet behind the foil. By the **Mössbauer effect** they found a distinct **shift of the characteristic γ line** (ΔH = 500 ± 70 Gs — a shift, not the broadening a trapped monopole would give; they read it as a collective effect), and **the shift reversed direction when the magnet pole was reversed**. Nikolai Ivoilov (Kazan) later imaged **"monopole tracks"** on two-sided X-ray plates, with a mirror (glass, Si, or Ge) showing the track and its mirror image — the claimed chiral signature. Related: "strange radiation" reports (ICCF-11 Marseille 2004), and a 2023 extended-Standard-Model theoretical treatment (Schild, INSPIRE 2695928) arguing discharge symmetry-breaking can produce such monopoles from modified neutrino states.
- **Souriau's addition.** He takes Lochak's particle and identifies it as **what de Belizal and Chaumery's instruments were detecting all along** — the "real nature" the founders "never made explicit, though they had an exceptional practical and theoretical mastery of it." His site frames the lineage: the Egyptians as "masters of form waves" (architecture, medicine, distance communication, mummy protection, navigation), then Chaumery & de Belizal re-theorizing it from the 1950s, now Souriau supplying the particle.

## The flagship home experiment: magnetizing aluminum

The two videos on Souriau's site demonstrate the book's central reader-reproducible experiment (my translation of his description):

> These videos show that one can **magnetize a piece of aluminum (a non-magnetic metal) by exposing it for 3 days to a form emission of the Vm+ type**. This piece of aluminum is then attracted by both faces of a classical magnet. When one studies the theory of magnetic monopoles, one sees that this experiment shows that the South part of the form-wave emitter does indeed emit magnetic monopoles that lodge themselves in the piece of aluminum.

Why this matters for the corpus: **aluminum is paramagnetic but non-ferromagnetic — a 3-day exposure to a Vm+ emitter followed by attraction to *both* faces of a magnet is a binary, cheap, home-testable claim.** If monopoles lodge in the aluminum, both faces of a dipole magnet should attract it (unlike ordinary induced magnetism, which would give attraction to one face/repulsion from the other depending on orientation). This is the sharpest falsifiable seam in the whole French form-wave apparatus tradition: a ~€5 experiment (aluminum sample, a de Belizal-type Vm+ emitter — or any claimed form emitter, e.g. a pyramid or a half-sphere — and a fridge magnet) with an unambiguous outcome. Note the Vm+ designation: Souriau uses the de Belizal/Chaumery emission vocabulary (V+ / V− / Vm — magnetic green, the "vertical negative green" family), which is exactly the emission taxonomy the corpus already documents from *Physique Micro-Vibratoire* and the 1936 patent.

## The other reader experiments (from the author's introduction and blurb)

From the Everand preview of the book's introduction (my translation of key passages):

> I have always been passionate about waves, from the moment I understood that they were in a way the weft of our universe. [...] At the end of the 1980s, during courses at the faculty of Marseille, I drank in the words of my electromagnetism professor, **Roger Petit** [...] The other course that passionate me was quantum physics [...] a marvelous world where quasi-paranormal phenomena at the human scale could occur at the atomic or subatomic scale. [...] Under Einstein's famous formula E = mc² hides **Louis de Broglie's formula E = hc/λ**: every particle is associated with a wave. [...] I was also interested in authors with more spiritual approaches to physics. **Jean Charon's World of Eons** fascinated me — this man had the audacity to claim that matter has consciousness. **Fritjof Capra's Tao of Physics** was one of my bedside books. [...] I went through a few years of interruption due to military service, a first job, and a period of unemployment. During the latter, I had already begun to take an interest in the problem of form waves and **built pyramids with which I amused myself mummifying pieces of meat, treating headaches, and changing the taste of wine**. But at that time, 1991, the origin of the form-wave phenomenon entirely escaped me.

The blurb adds the book's experiment domains: **radioactivity protection, chemistry, transmutation of chemical elements, health** — with the pyramid experiments (mummification, wine-taste, headache) as the reader-doable tier, and the radioactivity-reduction claim as the boldest tier (presented in the book as an experiment, on his site as a "proven" application via the weak interaction).

## Where this sits in the corpus map

- **French form-wave lane.** Souriau is the *physicist-legitimizer* generation of the Chaumery/de Belizal school — after the founders (corpus: Bouly, Mermet, Viré, Henry de France, Chaumery, de Belizal, Morel, Servranx, Turenne, Bovis, Enel, Ravatin, la Foye, Thieux, Marquette, Frandeau) — he keeps their emission taxonomy (V+/V−/Vm) but replaces their phenomenology with a named particle and a named interaction. The corpus's *Physique Micro-Vibratoire* dossier documents the founders' own admission that they mastered the phenomenon without knowing its nature; Souriau claims to close exactly that gap.
- **Russian bridge.** The Lochak chain is the first hard particle-physics bridge between the French form-wave tradition and the Russian corpus lane (Urutskoev/Ivoilov at Kurchatov — "strange radiation," titanium isotope anomalies — already adjacent to the corpus's LENR/torsion material). Souriau is the French-language popularizer standing on that bridge.
- **Falsifiability audit.** The aluminum-magnetization experiment is the bench-note seam (see the corpus's bench-note template: Simoneton vs biophotonics, Merz inter-rater, Lecher calibration). Protocol sketch: ≥3 aluminum samples (e.g. 99% Al strips), one exposed 72h to a Vm+ emitter, one sham-exposed (same room, emitter absent or neutralized), one unexposed control; test each blind against both faces of a strong NdFeB magnet with a torsion balance or hanging-thread attraction test; the monopole-lodging hypothesis predicts face-independent attraction for the exposed sample only. Cheap, binary, and it directly tests the book's central claim rather than a dowser's judgment.

## Status and caution

The Lochak monopole is not accepted physics — mainstream monopole searches (MoEDAL etc.) target heavy GUT monopoles, and the Kurchatov "strange radiation"/isotope-anomaly results are contested. Lochak's theory is published (Z. Naturforsch., IJTP, Elsevier book) but heterodox; Souriau's identification of form emissions with Lochak monopoles is his own synthesis, published by Servranx in the radionics catalog, not in a physics journal. The dossier records the claim chain faithfully and flags its evidentiary tiers: published-heterodox-theory (Lochak) → contested-experiment (Urutskoev/Ivoilov) → self-published synthesis + home experiments (Souriau). The aluminum experiment is the one row anyone can grade.
