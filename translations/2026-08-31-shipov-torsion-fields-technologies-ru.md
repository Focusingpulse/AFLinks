However, between those who call and those who already have achievements surpassing the world level, there stands the "scientific bureaucrat" who prefers not to take risks and therefore condemns Russia to trail behind the achievements of Western science and technology. They believe that a breakthrough in physics should be expected in elementary particle theory or in astrophysics. These fields of physics use expensive scientific programs and instruments that individual states are not even able to implement. Few pay attention to the already existing torsion experiments and technologies, obtained by groups of people or individuals, in ordinary laboratory conditions and at relatively low material costs. Recently, the number of torsion experiments and technologies ("anomalous" from the standpoint of the modern scientific paradigm) has been increasing, and their silencing by official science causes bewilderment and regret.
