## AVERTISSEMENT (Authors' Foreword)

This work is the fruit of thirty years of study, of silent and patient research — some of it disappointing, some of it exhilarating. It is also the natural continuation of the volume already published in 1939, then reissued in 1956 under a different title: *Essai de radiesthésie vibratoire* (Essay on Vibratory Radiesthesia), written in collaboration with Léon Chaumery, who was taken from us, alas, prematurely, on 27 February 1957.

Negative Green (V–) represents point 0–400; it is a mysterious vibration, a veritable connecting link between life and death. It has power over matter; it burns, destroys... but it can also preserve Life in potency, outside space and time.

The Egyptians used this vibration to dehydrate their dead and thereby withdraw the body from decomposition. We rediscovered it and patented it in April 1936. It can certainly be disputed by some, but this could in no way cast doubt on its authenticity.

It was with Léon Chaumery, some thirty years ago, that we began our research.

It is now with M. P. A. Morel that we have together rethought the problem of the invisible forces and put on paper these few notes which, we hope, will not disappoint the reader.

## INTRODUCTION

Before undertaking the reduction of this book, it seemed necessary to us — indispensable, even — to put the reader into the climate of this work, which employs a very new vocabulary, in a domain not yet explored by official science.

We have therefore had to create from scratch a terminology whose technical value corresponds to the data of the problems set out.

The terms we use are in no way a distortion of those recognized by classical physics; they are terms proper to our conception of the invisible world of vibrations, whose mysteries we have tried to penetrate.

The scientists have inventoried all the laws that govern the phenomena of the visible world, whether the chemical reactions of bodies or the physical reactions of matter; but they have never turned to the problem of the invisible world of vibrations.

Yet it is recognized that matter is not inert, but in continual movement — from the grain of sand or the pin's head to the block of granite. And this matter, which can be compared to miniature universes, is composed of atoms, themselves made up of a nucleus of protons and neutrons, along with electrons.

The different combinations of these first elements give the whole gamut of the living world, with its particular characteristics and its intrinsic values.

All movement is life, and therefore expenditure of energy; now this energy can be captured in the form of radiation whose frequencies will be a function of the constituents of the matter.

And not only does matter have a radiation of its own, by virtue of its constituents, but it also possesses a secondary radiation due to its profile — angular refraction being the basis of every vibratory emission known under the name "form-wave emission" (émission d'onde de forme). This is a phenomenon very little studied to this day, and it is the object of a very thorough inventory in *Physique Micro-Vibratoire*.

We have had to put forward hypotheses, establish laws, create new instruments that answer these new laws. All of this has taken us nearly thirty years of effort, of work, of research — sown with deep joys, sometimes also with bitter disappointments.

We submit this vocabulary of the invisible forces to the reader so that he may follow us without difficulty through these pages, which we shall turn together, perhaps with astonishment, certainly with curiosity and sympathy.

It is possible that a few errors have slipped into our exposition; how could it be otherwise? Man is a perfectible being, not born perfection itself. In doing this we shall at least have the certainty of having sown a few very much alive seeds in a field not yet explored, which we shall continue tomorrow to clear and cultivate, with all the friendship that surrounds us and that, from afar, follows our effort.

## VOCABULAIRE (Vocabulary of Micro-Vibratory Physics)

**Bombardment** — A train of sustained vibratory waves: magnetic carrier waves and carried cosmic waves; a sum of energy from the passage of cosmic particles in the "pile" emitters, whose beam, concentrated at one point, bombards a target, witness-relay, or liquid support.

**Compensateur magnétique (magnetic compensator)** — A silver disc interposed between the BC30, to which it belongs, and its cosmic pile; its function, by its form-influence, is to compensate the variations of the lunar magnetic coefficient, with the aim of obtaining a constant average coefficient.

**Combiné métallique (metallic combination)** — A combination of metal plates, in pairs of metals in capacity-agreement with the natural terrain of a living being, which act upon it by radiation on its witness-relay. These plates are placed in the central cavity of the BC30, stacked one upon another and opposed in polarity. The witness-relay sits in the middle.

**Correcteur magnétique (magnetic corrector)** — A phase separator (magnetic/electric) consisting of a silver arrow that pivots on the central axis of the BC30 and explores its region around the equator. Its principal role is to direct the form-emission onto one or the other of the phases, by its magnetic attraction on the meridians.

**Couleurs (Colors)** — Periodic vibratory frequencies of the waves of the colors of the decomposition of the spectrum. They are proportional to and equivalent to those of sound, of light, and of Hertzian waves.

**Couleurs visibles (visible colors)** — The vibratory state of the seven colors of the spectrum, optically visible.

**Couleurs invisibles (invisible colors)** — The zone of the spectrum constituted of the infra and the ultra, and of the vibratory frequencies that cannot be optically visible, their wavelengths being outside those of our field of perception.

**Couleurs de forme (form colors)** — Periodic frequencies of vibration of the waves generated by forms. They are distinguished from the waves of the visible colors by their difference of wavelength.

**Couleurs biométriques (biometric colors)** — Vibratory frequencies of waves expressed by the nature of the organs of living beings, measured in relative value.

**Couleurs dites "onde astrale" (so-called "astral wave" colors)** — A vibratory rhythm expressed by the fundamental nature of a living being from its birth, which constitutes its base for its entire life period. This notion sits on a plane superior to the habitual one.

**Détecteur (detector)** — A radiesthetic instrument, a separator of waves, sensitive principally to the vibratory fields of energies of various origins, which it helps to identify and measure.

**Énergie physique (physical energy)** — The sum of all forms of energy coming from matter and from the cosmos.

**Énergie psychique (psychic energy)** — An abstract form of thought-force which, impregnated into an accumulator such as the "Gemaf," can act upon living beings.

**Énergie fluidique (fluidic energy)** — A magnetic and energetic potential radiated by a living being, which escapes particularly from its hands. This energy can likewise impregnate and accumulate in the "Gemaf."

**Ferrites** — Ferrous compounds excited by the attraction of the magnet, and transparent to electromagnetic waves, of which they are perfect conductors, being insensitive to external fields and emitting no secondary effects.

**Fréquence vibratoire de forme (vibratory frequency of form)** — The number of times per second that a periodic phenomenon of vibration of form-waves reproduces itself.

**G. E. M. A. F.** — Acronym of "générateur de magnétisme humain, accumulateur de fluide" (generator of human magnetism, accumulator of fluid). Constituted of magnetized, excited ferrites, it charges itself with impregnations of human fluid, under the tension of an emitting pendulum, and then releases this accumulated energy of a given capacity, according to a discharge curve roughly constant in time.

**Hauteur d'ondes (H. O.) (wave height)** — Identification of the vertical of a body by the waves it radiates. The simple bodies are distributed into 8 families of wave heights, marked in grades on the equator of the Universal Pendulum.

**Longueur d'ondes (wavelength)** — A scale of vibratory values proportional to the wavelengths of bodies, such as they appear on the scale of a spectroscope, and which can be measured in relative value.

**Méridiens–Équateur (Meridians–Equator)** — Three great circles on the surface of the sphere (BC30 and Universal Pendulum) that intersect at right angles. The vertical circle in N–S is called the "magnetic meridian"; the one in E–W, the "electric meridian"; the horizontal circle, the "equator."

**Micro-vibrations** — Small vibrations (from the Greek *mikros*, small), issuing from form waves, magnetic and cosmic. "Rapid back-and-forth movement of waves of corpuscles around a position of equilibrium, characterized by its duration, or period, and its amplitude, or maximum departure from the position of equilibrium."

**Onde de forme (form wave)** — Vibrations generated by forms, which take their birth in the decomposition of the natural magnetic current that exists at the center of figure of all bodies. The waves of geometric and symmetric forms are favorable to the equilibrium of living beings. Certain others, more or less combined, unbalance it and are unfavorable to it.

**Ondes cosmiques (cosmic waves)** — Magnetic waves accompanying cosmic particles, which unfurl in a spiral around their line of trajectory, creating a magnetic field that travels at the same speed.

**Ondes nocives (noxious waves)** — Carried waves issuing from anomalies of the subsoil, or from telluric water currents, or from various causes, conveyed by carrier waves, likewise propagated by the subsoil. They influence the vibratory rhythm of the cells of the living being, causing it a prejudicial vibratory imbalance to its health.

**Octave** — The interval of repetition of a vibratory phenomenon designated by the same sign, and which is measured in length of the detector's suspension thread.

**Pile cosmique (cosmic pile)** — An accumulation of half-spheres succeeding one another along a line, set upright; a conductor of the waves of cosmic rays, which it transmits to a target (BC30 or disc emitter).

**Pile magnétique (magnetic pile)** — An accumulation of half-spheres succeeding one another along a line, aligned and oriented in the terrestrial magnetic N–S axis. This accumulation of forms produces a progressive amplification of the magnetic waves, principal carriers in form-emission.

**Phases électrique et magnétique (electric and magnetic phases)** — Difference of state and direction of the vibratory current of form. In correlation with the "poles." Term applied to the meridians (perpendicular sense in the BC30), or to the sense of the lines in a disc emitter (senses inverted by 180° relative to one another).

**Polarités des métaux et des formes (polarities of metals and forms)** — The state of a body in which two opposed poles have manifested, which the detectors identify according to the sense of the gyrations that make them react. Normally, bodies that make the detectors react by clockwise gyrations are called "positive." The inverse for bodies then called "negative."

**Principe fonctionnel curatif (curative functional principle)** — A principle (cause) whose function (activity) is favorable to the re-establishment of the equilibrium of a living being. This principle can be contained in waves or in various supports, whose action it directs.

**Radio-activité (R.A.) / Radio-actif** —
1. The property of certain nuclei, or of heavy atoms, of being able to disintegrate spontaneously, modifying their structure.
2. The conventional designation of the vibratory frequencies situated in the zone of the spectra going from infrared to ultraviolet (symbols of calorific radiations). Certain vibratory frequencies are marked by Greek letters which designate, by analogy, the magnetic waves of radioactivity.

**Rayon (ray)** — The precision, in grades, of a vibratory frequency of form.

**Résonance vibratoire (vibratory resonance)** — A vibratory period common to two bodies possessing similar characteristics. "When a body is susceptible to vibrations of period P, one can make it resonate by subjecting it to repeated excitations having the same period, or a period multiple of P."

**Rupture des forces compensées (rupture of the compensated forces)** —
*a) Equilibrium of the forces:* The cosmic radiations that bombard the earth are normally absorbed in depth by the soil, which is dielectric and permeable to waves. The atmospheric air, positive, is in attraction with the R.A. waves emitted by the soil, of negative propensity. The field at ground level is therefore neutral; there is equilibrium, and the forces are compensated.
*b) Disequilibrium of the forces:* A terrain that is "electrically conducting" and impermeable to cosmic waves absorbs them only to a shallow depth; these waves, reflected and diffused superficially, provoke at the surface of the ground a new interfering field of positive radiations (issuing from the reflected cosmic waves), which merges with the normal field, which is no longer either compensated or neutral. There is a rupture of equilibrium; stationary waves form, better known under the term "noxious waves." Indeed, a positive atmosphere facing a negative soil complement each other, by mutual attraction, and form a neutral field. By contrast, a soil become likewise positive creates a repulsion of polarities — hence rupture.

**Rythme vibratoire de base (base vibratory rhythm)** — The regular alternation of the maximum strong beats of vibration of the nature of a living being, the base of its equilibrium.

**Sélecteur (selector)** (from "selective," that which operates a choice) — A separator of vibratory frequencies by selection. A pivoting copper arrow in the BC30, and a secondary, mobile snail-form in the measuring instrument called the "Escargot."

**Solénoïde (solenoid)** — A helical conductor constituted of a certain number of turns and connected to the earth (which is the current justifying its name). It is called "positive" when the turns are wound clockwise, and "negative" in the inverse sense.

**Témoins-relais (witness-relays)** — Bodies or biological fractions, detached from another body, geometrically similar or biologically attached: the relay and its source of origin are reciprocally subject to the law of resonance.

**Témoin-mot (word-witness)** — An abstract value or expression of a form, an object, an organ, or a function, represented by a symbolic word precisely designating them. It acquires the value of a concrete witness for the solution of radiesthetic research problems.

# FIRST PART — The Theories

### 1) The invisible forces

Men have reached such a degree of knowledge in every domain that they are now permitted to think they have become the Masters of the World.

On land, on sea, beneath the oceans as in the air, the perfection and speed of their machines have abolished the notion of Space-Time.

Radio waves circle the globe in fractions of a second; they are even reflected by the cosmic plasma, or by the Moon, by Venus, and recaptured as echoes by radar; finally, television screens allow one, thanks to world-broadcast via "Telstar" and "Syncom," to take part in the most distant events of the planet, even at the antipodes.

Indeed it seems the earth no longer holds any secret from man, and tomorrow his rockets and piloted satellites will permit the most audacious explorations in the Cosmos. Already living beings — men, a woman — have been projected into space, and they returned in perfect physical condition, having circled the globe 81 times for the man and then 48 times for the woman, respectively in 119 hours and 71 hours, in capsules satellite on orbits precisely defined and rigorously computed by electronic brains.

Five days in space, subjected to the phenomena of weightlessness, more than 5,300,000 kilometers traveled for both together! One dreams, it seems — and yet these performances are only the point of departure of a still unknown science which tomorrow will free man from terrestrial attraction and make of him a true cosmic explorer (1).

In the meantime, 60 artificial satellites turn above our heads (June 1963), and in the U.S.A. as in the U.S.S.R. the problems posed by these flights overflow the military problem, and ever greater means are being sought to go still further.

(1) On 31 July 1964 the Americans, with Ranger VII, succeeded in taking 4,000 photographs of the illuminated face of the Moon, in the zone of the Sea of Clouds. The last photos were taken at 300 m above the lunar soil and make it possible to identify an object the size of an automobile. Finally, new exploit: on 12 October 1964, the U.S.S.R. placed in orbit a cosmic vessel, the "Voskhod," inhabited by three men — Komarov, Egorov, Feoktistov — that is, a pilot, a doctor, and a scientist. After flying 24 hours 17 minutes and accomplishing 16 revolutions around the earth, the Voskhod landed in exceptional conditions of safety and gentleness at the predicted spot, on order from the ground.

Does this not constitute an enigmatic menace for the human species, and one is entitled to ask: what will tomorrow be made of? (1)

Biology, chemistry and surgery will soon likewise overturn all the old conceptions of the origins of life, and the atom, with its inexhaustible reservoir of energy, will bring to the humanity of tomorrow — if it knows how to deserve it — that golden age which the most authorized prophets have announced to men of good will.

Yes, *Homo sapiens* is truly a superior intelligence, fruit of a millennial evolution, and no problem seems foreign to him. He is at one with the matter he has learned to discipline, but his spirit is yet free enough to break from it when needed, and he then perceives another universe, how different from that of forms — a world apart, without consistency, and yet real, at the measure of his human intelligence.

Only here... he now lacks the key that would restore to these invisible forces their creative power. The secret has been lost, although in ancient times this power was widely used. The Egypt of the Pharaohs, heiress of the civilization of the Atlanteans, knew the "eternal lamp" — that disintegration of the atom and its transformation, certainly very simple, into light waves as resplendent as the sun.

She also knew the possibility of escaping the laws of gravitation — a modern problem and a topical one if ever there was; the cosmonauts know something of it (2).

What remains to us of these marvels? The fairies' wand that enchants our grandchildren's evenings! Legend? Not at all: reality of yesterday, perhaps reality of tomorrow. In the Indies, in Tibet, where certain secrets have been preserved, the Masters and Sages of these far countries still have the possibility of moving through the ether, over considerable distances — not only in spirit, but in body-matter. ### 2) Theory of the compensated forces

For us Westerners, such phenomena make us smile; they exceed us and escape the scientific data of our modern civilization. And yet the invisible forces are not an illusion; they surround us and press upon us from every side.

First there are the natural cosmo-telluric vibrations that give all life on earth its equilibrium and allow it to develop normally. Indeed, for the living cell — whether human, animal or vegetable — to be able to grow, enlarge, ripen, age, and finally die, but die of old age and wear and not of illness, it must obligatorily, throughout the whole duration of its existence, vibrate in the equilibrium of two forces:

a) The telluric force, which streams from the center of the earth and tends to escape into the stratosphere;

b) The cosmic force, which comes from the cosmos, from our galaxy, from our solar system, and which must constantly bombard the telluric force to neutralize it.

As long as the living cell finds itself in the equilibrium of these two forces, barring other secondary accidental causes, it is sheltered from all the ills that make life fail; but if, for whatever reason, one of these two forces comes to fail — and it is always the cosmic force — disequilibrium follows very rapidly, generating illness in its most diverse forms.

The state of illness would therefore proceed from a vibratory imbalance generated by what we have named "the Rupture of the Compensated Forces." The Chinese, who knew this disequilibrium, designated it by another name: "the veins of the dragon," and they carefully refrained from building their dwellings on these "ruptures of forces." But nowadays in Europe, who cares about these invisible contingencies, so redoubtable nevertheless?

Yes, redoubtable; for indeed the uncompensated telluric vibration is in fact a carrier wave of negative green (V–) upon which a carried wave travels. A veritable support of all the evil forces, it drains the vibrations of fouled water, microbial emanations of cemetery, sewer, badly drained underground river, or again of a dry radioactive fault, forming a pile by reason of the different layers of ore encountered in the soil.

Everywhere in the earth are found bodies that can contribute, by their magnetic field, to destroying the state of equilibrium of the living cell; but we insist on the fact that, for this noxiousness to act, it is indispensable that these disequilibrating vibrations find a support, a carrier wave, that permits them to escape into the atmosphere. Now this carrier wave is precisely the negative green telluric wave, which plays in the soil the same role as the Hertzian wave in the ether relative to the modulated wave.

As long as the telluric wave is compensated by the cosmic wave, its carried wave can commit no damage and it stays in the soil; it is only when the cosmic force is deviated that disequilibrium occurs.

### 3) Vibrations — Colors

Alongside the natural waves there exist other vibrations that are due not to the cosmo-telluric complex, but to the very form of matter.

Indeed everything — absolutely everything that exists in the universe — radiates and emits vibrations; but these vibrations have an oscillatory field so minute that it escapes the most sensitive instruments of physics: these are the form waves.

Hence it is simpler to deny them. Their wavelength is on the order of the Angström; how far we are from the modulated wave of radio, ultra-short as it may be! Yet this microscopic frequency is precisely its strength, for — conveyed by a natural or artificially created carrier wave — this micro-vibration can circle the globe in a few fractions of a second. These form waves sit on the scale of the spectrum that embraces all the vibrations existing in the cosmos.

Everyone knows that this spectrum is divided into visible color-vibrations and invisible color-vibrations. There are seven visible color-vibrations, each with its infra and its ultra, namely:

Violet; Indigo; Blue; Positive Green, or green of the visible spectrum; Yellow; Orange; Red.

Then five invisible color-vibrations, likewise each with their infra and their ultra, namely:

Infrared; Black; Negative Green; White; Ultraviolet.

It must be noted, however, that in the sector between black and white there exist very many other vibratory points revealing considerable energy. It is a zone of intense radioactivity whose center sits at negative green (V–), the latter being exactly at the antipode of the green of the spectrum, or positive green (V+).

It is the shortest and most powerful vibration in the universe: a natural carrier wave in the soil, it is the telluric wave proper, which streams from the center of the earth and tends to escape into the stratosphere, dragging with it all the vibrations found in its path.

It is of negative sign, and it is precisely when it is not compensated by the positive force coming from the cosmos that it produces a vibratory imbalance disorganizing the living cell. This is what we designated by the term "rupture of the compensated forces." Equilibrium is perfect only when the two positive and negative forces oppose and compensate each other.

Now this negative vibration can also be obtained artificially by a rigorously calculated form wave. This discovery was the object of patent no. 816,132, dated 10 April 1936, taken in collaboration with M. Léon Chaumery, since deceased: these are our cosmo-magnetic piles.

Thanks to this discovery we were subsequently able, beyond our very well known universal detector, to perfect emitters of gamma ray whose power is prodigious — and prodigious too their therapeutic possibilities in the medicine of tomorrow, for the healing of numerous organic deficiencies.

### 4) The circumference

Let us examine a plane circumference and study it from the vibratory angle. We observe two lines of force in cross, whose point of intersection constitutes a center of figure around which are distributed other secondary lines, rigorously equidistant and synchronized on all the vibrations of the spectrum, visible and invisible.

The apex of the circumference is of positive sign and vibrates on the color green of the spectrum.

Its antipode is of negative sign, though likewise synchronizing on the green of the spectrum. We are therefore entitled to conclude that every circumference possesses a positive north pole and a negative south pole.

If we now examine the line perpendicular to North-South, passing through the center of figure, we observe that this line of force synchronizes with red at the West and violet at the East.

Between the branches of this fictive cross are detected all the vibrations of the spectrum, visible as well as invisible, each emitted from the center of figure and coming to graze the periphery of the circumference.

All the visible vibrations sit in the northern hemisphere and all the invisible vibrations in the southern hemisphere.

It has two meridians, one electric, the other magnetic, and an electromagnetic equator. Each of these fictive, radioactive circles records all the vibrations of the visible and invisible spectrum, these two meridians separating the electric waves from the magnetic waves, while the equator vibrates on the complete electromagnetic beam.

This is what follows from the theory that allowed us, in 1936, to patent a new detector under the name: Universal Pendulum (Chaumery and A. de Belizal).

This precision instrument was the object of a very thorough description in our two preceding works, *Traité expérimental de physique radiesthésique* and *Essai de radiesthésie vibratoire*. At the end of this volume we will give again a more succinct study of it, but a necessary one, for this detector is at the very base of all our discoveries resulting from the decomposition of the spectrum in the sphere.

The layman who opens a book of geometry does not suspect that he stands in the presence of the most formidable network of vibrations it is possible to condense in a small volume. From every page spring waves of different frequencies; the whole gamut of the visible and invisible spectrum is represented there, for it is angular refraction that creates the vibration — and the Egyptians knew its emitting power perfectly. Their frescoes are composed of drawings whose angles are so rigorously calculated that they radiate, at will, beneficial or malefic waves according to the use for which they were destined. Their hieroglyphs have the same power, and it is precisely by studying Egypt in its architecture and its bas-reliefs that we managed to surprise the astonishing possibilities of form waves.

This pharaonic civilization, descending directly from the Atlanteans, possessed the most prodigious secrets — secrets that gave man power over matter, and that not, as in our twentieth century, by means of hundreds of millions of volts demanding enormous capital and the collaboration of numerous engineers working in teams, but simply by bringing into play the invisible and natural forces that Providence has placed at our disposal.

### 7) The square (équerre)

All the artisans who work wood use a square, but they are ignorant that they hold there, in their hands, a wave emitter whose vibration is on the order of the gamma rays.

If one brings a modification to the vertical line of the square, deporting it either right or left, one obtains different angles that are no longer 90°. ## THE FORM WAVES (Les ondes de forme)

### 8) What is it about?

The question of form waves has remained rather controversial to this day — between partisans, detractors, or those without a precise opinion — and light is far from having been shed on this important subject.

Is it a matter of simple views of the mind, or of concrete realities? We shall endeavor to elucidate this question.

*Essai de radiesthésie vibratoire* traced the theoretical bases that served our work in progressing to the stage we shall now study.

### 9) The great currents of modern physics

We live in a scientific world in evolution, at a rhythm of accelerating growth — fruits of the progressive elevation of knowledge and of the ever-growing technical level; in fact, we live in a passionate epoch.

The conception of waves, so derided in other times, returns very seriously to actuality. Indeed, what do we observe? To the classical waves — Hertzian, electromagnetic, sonic, acoustic, luminous, optical, resonance waves, all vibratory and of measurable frequencies — we now see applied new wave formulas:

The plasma waves: oscillations between charged particles in an ionized, or at least partially ionized, gas, which subdivide into electronic waves and pseudo-sonic waves. The ionosphere is naturally the seat of these waves.

The masers, quantum amplifiers of hyperfrequency waves.

The lasers, quantum amplifiers of light waves, which, used as optical frequency oscillators, furnish light waves whose characteristics have been able to appear extraordinary.

The gas lasers: helium-neon, or neon-oxygen, and argon-oxygen.

The solid crystal lasers (ruby) with discontinuous (pulsed) operation.

The crystal lasers with continuous operation.

The pulsed lasers.

The traveling-wave laser tubes.

The modulated-light receivers.

All these particulars are used in industry, which has commercialized them. Among other industrial applications, firms supply lasers destined for the high-precision machining of hard or refractory metals; for welding delicate parts such as lamp filaments inside vacuum glass envelopes, etc. For communications in space, these transmissions — insensitive to magnetic storms — constitute a clear progress.

An application project is under way for the control of electronic computers, whose wires would be replaced by continuous-light tubes. Tomorrow, perhaps, the interplanetary signals.

Here then is one face of the technical atmosphere in which we are engaged. Waves are the great stars of modern discoveries.

Why, in these conditions, would one persist in an unjustified skepticism toward the waves of radiesthesia, and more particularly toward form waves?

### 10) The terrestrial magnetic current

As everyone knows, the terrestrial magnetic current evolves along a North-South line of emission, corresponding to the poles of our globe. The magnetized needle of the compass is influenced by this current, which radiesthesists use for their various researches. This current is likewise the fundamental base of form waves. Indeed, geometric forms — linear, plane or volumes — capture this current and are charged with it with more or less intensity, according to their orientation relative to the true magnetic North.

But this current is not constant and undergoes variations of intensity due to various causes: the latitude of the place, diurnal variation, vertical component, the variable total force of the field, irregular variations of the rotation of the earth (speed), and finally, of lesser importance, the continuous displacement of the north pole.

Which comes down to saying that one cannot rely on a north fixed once and for all, but that one must constantly verify it with a good precision compass — while admitting, moreover, that the components of the place be magnetically neutral.

Form waves are essentially composed of the energy developed by these different elements.

These waves, under certain conditions, are captured by our form emitters.

### 12) The energy of terrestrial radiation

This form of energy is constituted of several elements which we shall rapidly review:

The radiation of matter, which disintegrates little by little while releasing energy.

The solar radiation, radiation energy arriving at the ground from the confines of the atmosphere, habitually named the "solar constant" for the simplification of the elements of calculation — a value evaluated at nearly 2 calories per minute per cm² at the ground, convertible into watts per cm³, but which is in fact extremely variable, according to the place, the altitude, the relief of the ground, the season, the hour of day or of night, the atmospheric circumstances, the periods of sunspots, the eruptions of the chromosphere, etc.

As one can see, this value can pass through very important differences.

a) Global radiation. For the lands of the globe the daily average is on the order of about 300 calories per cm². When the sky is entirely overcast, the global radiation is constituted only by that of the sky, which is then on average on the order of a quarter of the global radiation under a very clear sky; but this average is again entirely relative, since the solar eruptions and the spots represent enormous differences.

b) Terrestrial radiation. The earth sends a radiation toward space and absorbs a part of the radiations of the sun and of the sky. Its surface behaves like an integral radiator, or "black body." In principle, in the day it receives more energy than it radiates, and at night the inverse occurs — with, however, various variations, as we have seen above.

This notion is interesting to retain, in order to evaluate, at their scale, the value of the energy of the form emissions, diurnal or nocturnal, as well as of the general atmospheric conditions and of the season. This is what the Chinese had understood several thousand years ago, with the conditions most favorable for practicing acupuncture. c) Atmospheric electricity brings into play three sorts of quantities, all variable:

1. THE ELECTRIC FIELD at the ground, expressed in volts per meter, with incessant variations due to meteorological circumstances very dissimilar from one place to another, to the configuration of the terrain, as well as to the vertically developing clouds, having considerable differences between the high atmosphere and the ground.

2. THE ATMOSPHERIC AIR, slightly conducting and ionized by the radioactive substances and the cosmic radiation. The conductivity varies with the altitude and the meteorology.

3. THE CONDUCTION AND CONVECTION CURRENTS, currents of the charges of the atmosphere under the action of the field, and the discharges by precipitation, such as lightning, etc.; the diurnal variation corresponds to that of the storm centers.

Here again, one can note an extreme variability and very important differences, upon which the form waves are tributary, all proportions guarded.

We must still take account of another factor, which lies this time in the utilization: near emission or emission at a distance.

### 13) The Doppler effect

The Doppler effect, very well known in physics, can by analogy be applied to vibratory emissions. It is defined by an apparent modification of the wavelength of a vibration whose source is in movement relative to the observer; this modification is especially perceptible in acoustics: variation of the height of the perceived sound when the source displaces itself relative to the observer — hence the influence of the movement of the source and of the observer on the measured wavelength.

Which can be stated thus: "if a (or a vibration) of given frequency, an immobile listener placed at the foreseen distance perceives a sound of the same frequency. It is not the same if the source and the listener are in movement; the frequency of the perceived sound is different from that of the source."

It is this effect that Fizeau took up for the propagation of light, called the Doppler-Fizeau effect, and which is used in astronomy to compute the distance of the stars and in particular the expansion of our galaxy. Following the traditional figure, "an observer who watches a whistling locomotive pass by perceives a sound that progressively lowers." Hence an apparent modification of frequency.

By analogy, the Doppler effect applies to the vibratory form emissions, and one must take it into account for emissions at a distance, for which one must increase the intensity or the duration of emission, to compensate in a certain measure the "apparent difference" relative to the emission (this compensation is treated further on, in the chapter on form emitters).
