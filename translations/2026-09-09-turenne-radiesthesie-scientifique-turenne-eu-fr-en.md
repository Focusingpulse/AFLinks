# Scientific Radiesthesia — The Turenne Method (Part 1)

Engineer Turenne, like other scientists of his era, had no esteem for the traditional method, because it offers no guarantees: it is far too empirical and entirely random. Scientific radiesthesia was born out of the discoveries of the nineteenth century — electricity, wired and then wireless telegraphy, photography — and then, in the twentieth century, television, the discovery of radioactivity, and nuclear physics. It is easy to foresee that computing and quantum and post-quantum physics will allow radiesthesia, through the work of new researchers, to find its place even more firmly in the domain of the official sciences — to be recognized and used with all the honors it deserves.

The physicists and engineers who investigated radiesthetic phenomena studied them with an experimental methodology, with the same rigor and intellectual honesty as those devoted to other scientific fields. It is not possible, in the little space and time at our disposal, to recount in detail, demonstrate, and prove what is called scientific-experimental radiesthesia. At most we can state some of its laws — a kind of highway code for the radiesthesist — and show, through a few simple experiments, the vast field of research it offers to curious minds, and its great usefulness in daily practice.

Among those who undertook this research from a scientific standpoint, we should mention in particular L. Chaumery and A. de Bélizal, who worked in tandem, and Monsieur Descris, who limited himself to the frequencies of colors. Engineer Turenne also took an interest in this discipline, which he immediately named Scientific Radiesthesia, before defining it more precisely as Radio-désintégration. The TURENNE METHOD also draws on notions of physics proper, electricity, botany, and geology — notions which for many of us are no more than vague school memories. Our readers will understand that one cannot pretend to explain here 65 years of research by a physicist-engineer who specialized to such a degree in the study of radiesthesia, in the light of new knowledge in the field of electromagnetism.

Contrary to what is said, radiesthesia is not a gift: 80 to 90% of people are capable of practicing it with more or less success. Some learn faster than others. Those who absolutely cannot manage it are generally carriers of serious physical handicaps, or suffer from a psychic blockage. Valid results are the fruit of great work and patient application: enthusiasts should therefore know that one does not improvise oneself a radiesthesist in a short time.

Louis Turenne can rightly be considered one of the most lucid and careful researchers of the radiesthetic phenomenon. An engineer graduated from the prestigious École Centrale, with a thesis on electromagnetism and in particular on "wireless transmissions," he was constantly in contact with the water-diviners who frequented his father's house, and he noticed their skill at detecting the presence of water tables, indicating their depth and flow; it was the statistics of the successes achieved by these diviners that pushed the young Turenne to study this phenomenon from a scientific standpoint.

His thesis was relatively simple: water emits a characteristic electromagnetic wave which, once perceived by the muscular structure of the organism and recognized by a trained sympathetic nervous system, produces an induction of low-intensity current within the neuro-muscular system, which in turn gives rise to microscopic oscillations of the musculature, amplified and manifested by an instrument in unstable equilibrium (the diviner's rod or the radiesthesist's pendulum). A signal so weak, and scientific instruments of the era so underperforming, forced Turenne to use the human organism as the revealing instrument. To avoid, or reduce to a minimum, any influence of the brain or the emotions, Turenne then developed a whole series of apparatuses that profoundly modified the theory and practice of radiesthesia. Thanks to these instruments and his solid knowledge of physics (let us recall that he was a university instructor at Fontainebleau), he elaborated, with the illustrious physicians and biologists of his time, a most interesting theory on electromagnetism and its relations with cellular biochemistry. To this day, this theory is one of the few capable of providing a plausible explanation of why electrical currents and magnetic fields, even of low intensity, have potentially devastating effects on the human organism or on any living cell.

## The Waves of Nature — Selection — Saturation

Start from the principle of electricity and its two types, positive and negative, like the magnetism of a magnet: north pole and south pole. Traditional radiesthesia also knows these two types of waves: positive and negative. This is confirmed by the movements of the pendulum: to the right means positive, to the left means negative.

One question: is it possible to stop at this single definition, and would it be justified to think that terrestrial magnetism is composed of only two types of waves, positive and negative?

To verify this opinion, Louis Turenne had the idea of mounting on his detectors (or receivers) magnetized compass needles, which he fixed perpendicular to the thread (thus in the terrestrial plane). Some experiments on electricity and on certain metals gave satisfactory results... but not all — far from it! It was impossible for him, for example, with these magnetized needles placed horizontally, to detect the presence of water — and this even over a vein he had previously located. This led him to think that the magnetic field created by certain bodies might not be solely positive or solely negative, but negative and positive at the same time, and perhaps also horizontal and vertical. He then modified the mounting of these magnetized needles so as to be able to orient them at will relative to the suspension thread, and also relative to one another.

**THIS WAS THE POINT OF DEPARTURE OF DISCOVERIES THAT FOLLOWED ONE ANOTHER** in fascinating fashion, through experiments first simple, then ever more complex, on the "simple bodies," then on minerals, plants, the radioactive substances known at the time, and all the beings of nature, including the human being. It is from here that his discoveries were born.

He was thus able to detect **ten varieties of different waves**, which we will limit ourselves to listing for the moment:

- 3 horizontal waves: Positive +, Negative −, Inverse +−
- 3 vertical waves: Positive +, Negative −, Inverse +−
- The radioactive waves, or radio-disintegration waves, which provoke in the detectors four consecutive movements plus the stop: saturation.
- The infra-waves
- The ultra-waves
- The so-called "Turenne" waves, very particular and complex, which globally emit infra-waves and ultra-waves, positive and negative neutrons, positive and negative void.

Certain simple bodies of the Mendeleev table thus revealed that they emitted positive or negative waves according to their number of electrons — which was well known — but in radiesthetic terms these waves can be vertical or horizontal or both.

The study of this table also allowed Turenne to discover the phenomenon he called: **SATURATION**. Working in the laboratory on metals and simple bodies, he noticed that his detector turned a certain number of times above each body, then stopped and refused any movement until it was temporarily moved away. Turenne noted the number of turns, and repeated experiments led him to conclude that the number of turns corresponded to each body's number in the atomic classification: 29 turns for copper, 15 for phosphorus, 92 for uranium, etc. — and then, no more movement. He called this phenomenon: SATURATION.

Let us recall that Turenne's permanent preoccupation was to seek a law which, in radiesthesia, would allow one body to be isolated with certainty from another, through its waves, in order to limit as much as possible the errors in research. One of his first experimental discoveries thus corresponded perfectly to his objective. He was led to distinguish between horizontal magnetic waves and vertical waves of electric appearance.

## The Law of Similitude and its Corollary: The Témoins (Witnesses)

**Statement:** two similar bodies, whatever their material, oriented north–south, give rise between them to a wave phenomenon — a reflected wave that goes from one to the other without passing beyond them. This wave ceases to exist as soon as one of the two bodies changes position.

**WE TOUCH HERE ON NEWTON'S LAW.** Indeed, Turenne realized that two bodies of equal shape and mass generate between them a primary reflected wave which goes from one to the other and contains positive then negative, as well as a secondary wave perpendicular to the first. The meeting point of these two waves is situated exactly at mid-distance between the two bodies if they are equal; if they are of different size, the point moves closer to the smaller one. Immediately, Newton's law on universal attraction comes to mind. But Turenne pushed the experiment further, and realized that it is not a question of mass, but of surface: indeed, if one hollows out and empties one of the two bodies (lightening its weight but increasing its surface), the secondary wave moves closer to the full body.

This law allows us, if not to draw conclusions, at least to find multiple applications, known empirically by many radiesthesists but often badly used: thus a geographic map follows the law of similitude with the terrain it represents, the plan of a house with the real house, and this allows research on plan before going on site for verification; one can also find a lost person, or find one's own position on a map when one is lost. One indispensable condition: orient the plan or map correctly before beginning the study.

To carry out a correct search, one must also take into account another problem: that of the witness (témoin) of the body being sought (whether a mineral or a person), which must be as pure as possible — that is, not parasitized by other foreign waves. We thus arrive quite naturally at the TÉMOIN.

To show the complexity of the problem well, take the example of the human being. For a long time it was considered that a photo of the person constituted an excellent "semblable" of the person themselves. Indeed, a photo carries the waves of the person it represents, at least from a visual standpoint, since these waves, colored or not, impress our retina; we would conclude that the photo can be used in radiesthesia. Well, NO — because photos are always parasitized by "improper waves," if only those of the people who have touched them and left their fingerprints on them. If in addition we have asked a third person to indicate on the back of the photo, for example, the name, date of birth, and address of the person photographed (who may be ill or missing), this writing constitutes a very disturbing parasitic wave, and it becomes practically impossible to totally isolate the waves of the photographed person.

Turenne developed a personal technique, which he deliberately kept secret, allowing, in the case of a human being, to produce a TÉMOIN from a freshly drawn drop of blood, and to "fix" its waves indelibly. By examining the INDIVIDUAL WITNESS of Monsieur X, one can know whether he is dead or alive, his state of health, the place where he is, without any risk of confusing him with another body. Following the same procedure, Turenne and his collaborators produced TÉMOINS of all the ELEMENTS OF THE MENDELEEV TABLE, of the colors of the spectrum, of numerous plants, numerous microbial strains, of diseases, of compound ores, and of the different organs of the human body. These TÉMOINS are eternal, indelible, and unalterable even if they pass through the hands of several people.

*Note: All these witnesses and others are available in cases from the Laboratoires Turenne Italia – Associazione Louis Turenne.*

Thanks to the WATER witness (H₂O), the radiesthesist can work more easily, since all the parasites (the foreign waves deposited on the witness) are eliminated automatically, and drinking water can be isolated with certainty.

However, to put you on guard against a certain type of error, we must mention here an auxiliary law of radiesthesia: **the Law of Remanences.** On this subject, we recall the numerous failures in the search for hidden treasures. For example, if one holds in one's hand a gold object or the witness of gold, and one moves through a house or an archaeological site, equipped with a pendulum or a rod, it is possible — and even very frequent — to record numerous reactions of the detectors indicating the presence of gold, but a subsequent inspection proves useless. The reason is simple: there may have been gold or precious metals in that place, but they were removed afterwards, and what the detectors pick up are only the residual waves of those bodies. One calls "remanences" these waves which have "impregnated" the place where the gold was. These waves will eventually dissolve, but over very long times. The REMANENCE lasts all the longer as the metal has a high number in the Mendeleev table, and is a function of the time it spent in that place. Likewise, it is very difficult to find a missing person or to locate any person on a geographic map, because their home and the places they habitually frequent carry this remanence phenomenon. This kind of research therefore demands much modesty and moderation. If we hold in hand (as a witness) a colored ribbon, red for example: we will be attracted by red flowers, or by certain minerals or diseases whose waves belong to the family of red and thus induce with that color.

**THE LAW OF SIMILITUDE IS ALWAYS TRUE.**
