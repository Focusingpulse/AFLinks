# Radionics for Action at a Distance: Mystery and Application

## What is radionics?

Radionics is a discipline of radiesthesia. Its principle is simple: radionics uses form waves to act at a distance. Have you ever "sent good vibes" to a loved one in difficulty? It is the same principle.

But radionics goes further than simple intention by proposing to amplify the vibrations sent. This is where either devices or form waves come into play.

There are in fact two types of radionics: one practiced with electrical machines and another that uses only form wave diagrams. We will focus particularly on the second, which requires no special apparatus.

Form waves are geometric shapes with particular properties used to channel or amplify energy. For radiesthesia, all forms possess a vibratory energy. Certain special forms can amplify vibrations present in the environment. Thus radionics starts from an initial intention, which it amplifies through form waves, to transmit it to the person concerned.

Radionics is an integral part of radiesthesia, which sometimes works at a distance with the pendulum and dials. This is teleradiesthesia. According to the principles of radionics, form waves have the capacity to modify an energy field at a distance. As always in radiesthesia, there is a transmitter and a receiver. Added to these are the energy amplifiers that are the form waves.

The idea, then, is to emit vibrations and amplify them with the aim of positively modifying the energy field of a person, a living being, a place, or a situation, at a distance.

### "Matter-waves" and "form waves"

Chaumery and Belizal, authors of a widely referenced work on radiesthesia, distinguish two types of waves: matter-waves and form waves. The former emanate from fundamental energy and the latter are generated by forms. They are analyzed as rays arising from the "decomposition of the natural magnetic current" [1] contained in all things. According to this approach, form waves have the capacity to act upon matter-waves. This is how radionics operates.

#### Radionics

The term radionics comes to us from the United States, from the English word "radionics," a compound of "radiation" and "electronics." The word appears in the 1950s, but radionics was developed in the United States by the physician Albert Abrams somewhat earlier, at the beginning of the century. He published a work on his research in 1916, which inspired many radiesthetists to continue his experiments.

Abrams invented several machines and focused his work on electromagnetic waves for curative purposes. His machines, which met with frank success, were attacked by the medical establishment of the time. There is a link between this historical radionics based on apparatus and the radionics that uses only form waves. Both practices persist, whether combined or not.

#### Radionics with and without apparatus

In an interview, Raphael Dajafée recounts [2] that radionics may have been born in Germany. A health practitioner [3] who had succeeded in diagnosing an illness at distance asked himself whether he could also treat at a distance. He began experimenting with radio waves, a potentiometer, and a pendulum, searching for a healing frequency.

He placed geometric shapes at key points. He was working with several patients when one day he realized his devices were unplugged. Despite this, the patients reported feeling better. Placebo effect or action at a distance? Radionics develops in the furrow of these experiments, questioning the effect of the device itself.

### Radionics, radiesthesia, and teleradiesthesia

For the Servranx brothers, radiesthesia belongs to the domain of distance perception, while radionics concerns itself with distance action [4]. Nevertheless, teleradiesthesia also acts at a distance.

Radionics does not claim scientific validation. It is based on radiesthesia, and its effectiveness is measured by radiesthesia. Chaumery and Belizal report experiments in teleradiesthesia, where waves were detected at 450 km distance by another radiesthetist. Other radiesthetists have repeated these experiments in the lineage of their work.

### Acting at a distance with radionics

Acting upon a vibratory field at a distance is not a practice reserved solely for radionics. Within radiesthesia, teleradiesthesia and medical radiesthesia also work at a distance. More broadly, various energy disciplines also make use of distance action.

The first known vibratory action at a distance is prayer (regardless of the belief involved). Although the reasons remain mysterious, it has been observed that patients for whom people prayed recovered better than control groups of patients. [5]

Distance action proceeds from the principle that the energy environment of a person or group of people can be subtly modified. This idea was widely explored in the 19th century by various movements interested in the occult and telepathy.

## How does a radionics session work?

To practice radionics, one must first be a radiesthetist. Indeed, the tools that radionics employs are entirely based on radiesthesia. The radionics session aims to transmit a vibratory influence to a specific receiver.

### Operator, witness, and instrument

The Servranx brothers describe the process as the "transmission of a signal" [4] which requires three elements: an operator, a witness, and an instrument. The instrument, which may have been electrical in nature, is generally a diagram emitting form waves.

#### Ethics and state of mind of the operator

It goes without saying that these tools, which aim to act upon others, were developed in a spirit of benevolence. The operator must be in a state of "active consciousness." This state of active consciousness serves both to guide the radiesthetist's action and to protect him. It implies a calm, responsible, attentive attitude, as well as mastery of his imagination. The state of mind of the radiesthetist operating during a radionics session is very important. His intention must be clear. He must be concentrated and act with decision.

#### Witness

The witness is an object that represents the receiving person and carries their vibration. This witness can be a photograph, a lock of hair, or a paper bearing their handwriting, for example. The use of the witness is the same as in radiesthesia.

#### Function of the form wave

The radiesthetist chooses the form wave with which to work and places the witness at the center of the form wave. In doing so, he associates it with a precise intention. As long as the witness is positioned on the form wave, the device remains active.

The device is generally left to act for a long time. The radiesthetist may choose to place a remedy whose vibration he sends through the form wave. He will also carefully choose the colors and materials used.

"The form, or the particularities of the device, modulates the aspects of the field that one evokes with its help." [6] The device thus serves to "favor certain aspects of a subject's field" [7] by sending a signal.

For radionics, the form supports the materialization of the vibratory signal. Certain forms are particularly appreciated, such as the circle, the decagon, and the lozenge. They also function in combination. Thus one uses structures of concentric circles or boards of multiple lozenges.

The emitting diagram can also be composed of symbols. It is associated with the particular intention at play. The creation of the support is sometimes part of the process.

### Operating on the vibratory field through radionics

"In radionics, as in all distance action, one awakens, or rather evokes, with the help of a subject's witness, the field of that subject from the very place where one operates, and one manages to activate this field long enough by means of an apparatus that forms a body with the witness." [8]

Radionics allows one to send vibrations at a distance to a person, a group, a place, or even a situation. This also works toward plants or animals. Sending a signal does not guarantee it will be received. This is one of the reasons why the devices are used for extended periods. Thus, one multiplies the chances of the signal being received.

## Radionics and mysteries

While a large part of mystery remains in our understanding of vibratory fields, energies, and the invisible that operates in our world, certain successful radiesthesia experiments raise genuine questions.

The mystery at the heart of radionics is the same as that of radiesthesia, to which it is intimately linked. Perceiving the world as a mesh of vibratory fields, radionics proposes to influence its structure in a beneficial way using potentialization instruments, such as emitting diagrams. It must always be practiced with a laudable intention and accompanied by sufficient knowledge of radiesthesia.

To go further, discover other form waves such as the Tree of Life or the Triskel.
