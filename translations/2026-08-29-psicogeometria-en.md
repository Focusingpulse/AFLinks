# Psychogeometry — Geometry in Matter and Energy

*Sacred Geometry · Article #4.1 — Geometric qualities of matter and energy. Arturo Ponce de León, Psicogeometría México, with Ninón Fregoso. © All rights reserved. Reproduction authorized with source citation and text integrity. Translated from Spanish by Forge; excerpt from "El Poder de la Vida en la Geometría Sagrada y la Arquitectura Biológica".*

## Qualities of Geometry, Matter, and Energy

Sustainable Geometry (formerly called Sacred Geometry) explains the steps that exist between free energy and the consolidation of this same energy into matter. We can understand energy as particles in chaotic motion, while matter is the same energy, only gravitationally trapped. Free energy is associated with rectilinear movements, and the zero point has the powerful characteristic of making those free particles organize around a point of gravity to create matter. Energy and matter are only differences in geometric arrangement. Matter can exist thanks to the geometric matrices that support and promote the suction of energy in the constructive interface of waves; that is, given the organization of certain geometries, an ordered implosion of chaotic particles is created. This same principle is applicable to healing, the arts, and architecture.

Let us think of a universe made of a single substance. What science calls unified field theory, or what religions call a single God. It does not matter how it is defined: we live in a universe made of the same substance but with different geometric arrangements and different functions. It has been said: one God, many names. One Science, many angles. In the unified field, the first movement of consciousness performed to create all matter — literally to create the atoms that constitute the universe — is to fix a zero point, a focus of attention.

Fixing the point of attention is fixing a void, a fractal space around which one will rotate. Accepting that void is accepting the process of passing from energy to matter. From fixing attention — fixing the "non-tension" — the particle or event is subjected to the three mathematical constants that govern the geometric matrix supporting matter: phi, pi, and Euler.

**Phi** (pronounced *fee*) is the harmonic relation of the smaller to the larger, and of the larger to the whole. It defines the spiral movement around which one rotates at the zero point; that spiral unfolds infinitely. The first movement to generate life is spiral movement. Everything in nature — the turning of water, tornadoes, the sexual encounter between two animals — is not linear confrontation but occurs in a spiral dance. However, the phi spiral, or golden spiral, does not extend limitlessly; there comes a moment when it begins to curve to create matter. In scientific terms, phi, the golden number, creates a heterodynamic conjunction of wave phase.

Phi is an irrational number (whose decimal is infinite and non-periodic) with the numerical value 1.618033… and we can obtain it in three ways: through the Fibonacci sequence, through a mathematical formula, or through a geometric construction.

Phi has the shape of a "0" crossed by an "I", symbolizing the crossing of the void, barred by linear masculine energy, to begin the toroidal process of implosion/explosion. The number phi has been known throughout history by different names: in Greece and Rome it was known as the golden number, in the Renaissance as the divine number or number of gold. This number has impressive characteristics, but the greatest of them is that it is the number that structures the torsional movement that interrelates different scales, dimensions, or planes of Reality. It is the first ordering constant of life. Phi, besides being a number, is a proportion. If we draw a straight line and seek to divide it so that it is sustainable — that is, so that the smaller is contained in the larger, as the larger is in the whole — we would find that only one cut is possible: at 0.618033… of the total length of that line. This is the beginning of fractality, what in most philosophies and religions we find symbolized as the Trinity. In Christianity, it is said that the Father is to the Son as the Son is to the Holy Spirit; in the Egyptian tradition, Osiris the creator father, Isis the mother, and Horus the son; among the North American Indians, Safari the father, Ani the son, and Fayo the spirit. Even in the Hindu Brahmanic tradition there is talk of Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva as perfectly interrelated divine forces.

The size of the spiral is determined by **pi**, which allows phi to know the dimension of the curvature — that is, the size of matter. Our arm, which unfolds in phi patterns, does not grow limitlessly but has a limited size. The limitlessness of the universe is not in size but in forms. It is as if we took a spiral and wrapped it in a sphere.

Pi is an irrational number with a value of 3.141592… and it defines the constant value of a circle's radius with respect to its circumference. In psychogeometric terms, it defines the size of the toroid and is used to circumscribe the energy gravitationally trapped by the spiral and by the point of attention, or zero point.

The sphere takes consistency through the mediation of **Euler**. It is the most important number of mathematical analysis; it determines the constant of the area under the curve and gives consistency to the design created by phi in the spiral and pi in the circumference. For example, Euler adjusts to almost all the properties required to describe the strong interactions of elementary particles.

Euler, the last constant, represented by the letter "e", has a value of 2.718281… and is the base of natural logarithms. It is the only number with the property that the area of the region bounded by the hyperbola of the x-axis and the y-axis equals 1. Euler has the property of solidifying the toroid. In biology, one of its main applications is exponential growth. This type of growth arises when there are no factors limiting growth, as in certain bacterial populations, or in the recovery of a forested surface after a fire. This constant is also linked to the golden ratio and to the logarithmic spiral. When a chain or cable is hung by its ends, it tends to adopt a shape related to the number "e". Even in something as mundane as calculating bank interest, Euler's constant is needed.

Let us look at it graphically in the following way. In the chaos of the universe (in our own sexual, emotional, intellectual universe), the first geometric movement performed is the spiral movement, like a tornado; the spiral extends seeking to encompass the greatest possible area, emerging from the chaos of the void and moving toward order, toward the toroid. In this creation process, the spiral extends to the size dictated by a sphere; if it continues growing, the spiral begins to surround the sphere and turns around it to begin rotating in the opposite direction, moving from the outermost to the center. Once it reaches the center, it radiates the energy it captured from the outermost part and solidifies its own structure, giving it rigidity or flexibility.

In Psychogeometry, this whole process is known as the **Pentaflor** process. Depending on the level at which this process is viewed — whether in plane, in 3D, or in 4D — it can be seen as a point in the center (the void), a spiral (the golden spiral), and a circle delimiting the spiral (a sphere). In most indigenous rituals we find a center of power surrounded by a circle of fire that "protects" from the outside, from the "spirits" or chaotic energies of the order contained in that cosmos. Cosmos means 'Order'; in the cosmic process we emerge from the void and return to it, not without first knowing the experience of traversing the entire toroid. The experience of our life is summed up in this metaphor. It is the maxim of "know thyself": it is when the spirit traverses its creation, farthest from itself, in order to experience and, finally, return to itself.

Psychologically, this happens when the Soul lives at the periphery of the Being, in the personality, in order to understand culture, society, family, and integrate interaction with others within itself. From the Pentaflor and the toroidal creation it entails, the pattern of the Flower of Life begins to be created — always after the spirit has traversed itself. The Flower of Life is a symbol used in many religions and beliefs of the world; it is a geometric figure composed of circles duly separated or spaced, superimposed coincidentally, and ordered so as to reflect, in a symmetric polyptic design, a flower resembling a hexagon. The center of each circle lies on the circumference of six more circles that surround themselves, all of the same diameter (we will see them in detail later, in Article 4.2).
