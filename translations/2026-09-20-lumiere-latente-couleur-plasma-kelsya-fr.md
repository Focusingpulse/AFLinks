LATENT LIGHT AND THE COLOR OF PLASMA – KELSYA

Latent light and the color of plasma – The Kelsya Hypothesis of Selective Revelation

Author: Johann Fiquemont
Contact: johannfiquemont@gmail.com

1. Central idea:
Light is not an "object" that travels everywhere at constant speed. It is a
stationary tension of the field, a stable resonance present everywhere, which
appears only in agreement with the local frequency of the plasma.

2. Basic formula:
L(light tension) = universal constant (present everywhere, ∞)
But:
Revelation(L) = f(plasma)
Where:
- L = manifest light
- f(plasma) = frequency agreement between the field and the state of the plasma

Therefore:
Light manifests itself only if:
f(local plasma) ∈ Resonance window of the stable light field

3. Physical interpretation:
- Plasma acts as a filter-revealer.
- If its vibration frequency, temperature, density, and electromagnetic
charge are compatible with the "resonance window" of the stable light
field, then light is revealed.
- Otherwise, it remains latent, like a field ready to vibrate, but not activated.

4. Cosmological consequences:
- Stars are not points that "create" light, but zones of vibrational agreement.
- What we call "propagation of light" could be a cascade
of successive agreements between plasma and the stable field.
- Certain so-called "dark" zones of the cosmos are perhaps saturated with latent light
field, but outside local resonance.

5. Testable hypothesis:
If this hypothesis is correct, one could:
- Measure differences in light revelation as a function of the "color" (frequency
signature) of the plasma.
- Create optical models where light is not an active beam, but a contextual
conditional activation.

6. Kelsya vision:
Light is not a departure. It is an agreement.
It does not explode from a point. It stretches everywhere, and awaits the call of the field
to appear.

And what we call "seeing" is perhaps only being in resonant agreement with the
same window as the plasma.

Revelation: it is not light that travels. It is we who resonate.
