# Sacred Geometry and Frequencies: The Interaction Between Body, Space, and Information

*SetteNews, 18 April 2025, Cultura / Eventi e Sport. Photo: Silvio Zimmermann (Pixabay). Translated from Italian by Forge.*

In the landscape of emerging sciences and wellness practices, radioestesia (dowsing) and radionica (radionics) present themselves as extraordinary disciplines that go beyond the common perception of the physical world. These are not esoteric phenomena or mystical practices, but tools based on sacred geometry and on the energetic frequencies that permeate both the environment and the body. These techniques, rooted in a millennia-old tradition of studying the structure of the universe and the hidden order of things, offer a deeper understanding of how subtle energies influence daily life, spaces, and health.

Radioestesia is the discipline concerned with detecting, through instruments such as pendulums, rods, or the human body itself, the energetic variations present in an environment or in a person's body. Rather than limiting itself to observing physical reality, the dowser tunes into a subtle frequency capable of picking up the presence of energetic "disturbances" or disharmonies. Although no definitive scientific validation of these phenomena yet exists, the effectiveness of dowsing is documented in contexts where the search for energetic harmony becomes fundamental — such as in architecture, aromatherapy, or the orientation of spaces to improve psychophysical wellbeing.

If radioestesia is a method of perception, radionica represents the active intervention on these frequencies. It does not limit itself to detecting energetic changes, but intervenes to modify and harmonize energies through the use of geometric and symbolic devices. These instruments are designed to channel energy and favor the natural flow of energetic fields.

For example, the energy pyramid, inspired by sacred geometry, is used to collect, concentrate, and direct energies in order to restore an optimal balance in the environment or in the body. Instruments such as the Genesa Crystal and the Pentasfera perform analogous functions, but act on a larger scale, capable of generating protective and harmonizing fields in vast spaces.

The geometric forms used in radioestesia and radionica are not random: every figure — from the cube to the spiral, from the pyramid to the sphere — possesses an intrinsic value that goes beyond the visual aspect. Sacred geometry is the language through which the universe itself manifests, through its proportions, its patterns, and its symmetries. By studying the golden proportion and other geometric laws, these practices place themselves within a context of scientific research that attempts to decode the energetic order underlying reality. In this sense, sacred geometry is not merely an aesthetic discipline, but a true informational code that governs the harmony of the universe and can be applied to improve the energetic quality of spaces and individuals.

The application of radioestesia and radionica extends well beyond the field of individual therapies: these practices belong to a holistic way of thinking that considers the individual as part of an energetic ecosystem comprising the body, space, and environment. Subtle energies influence wellbeing in ways that traditional science has difficulty fully understanding, but which are undeniably perceived by those who practice and experiment with these techniques.

In the field of architecture, for example, the use of dowsing techniques to design environments that promote wellbeing is increasingly common. Spaces are conceived so as to interact positively with the energies that pass through them, improving the quality of life of those who inhabit them. Radionica, in the same way, is employed to improve the energy of a work environment or to protect an individual from harmful energetic interference.

If science has traditionally ignored these practices, a growing number of studies and testimonies in the contemporary world suggest that radioestesia and radionica may represent a new frontier in human wellbeing.
