# Francis Marquette and the C.E.R.A.: The Post-Frandeau Generation of French Radionics

## Who he is

Francis Marquette is a contemporary French radionics practitioner and author, founder of the **Cercle d'Études de Radionique Appliquée (C.E.R.A.)** — the Circle for the Study of Applied Radionics — and president of the non-profit association **HOLISTICA 3000**. He is the direct heir of the Frandeau de Marly line: he describes Dr. Francis Frandeau de Marly, creator of the Pendentif Cosmo-Magnétique (patent 84.03970, 13 March 1984), as his **"mentor"**, and his own apparatus work is a continuation of that lineage.

His public profile, assembled from publisher pages and association material:

- He has practised radionics for more than **twenty years**.
- In **2000** he discovered Sacred Geometry and *ondes de forme* (form waves) — the theoretical layer inherited from the Chaumery/de Belizal school.
- He then made the acquaintance of **Frandeau de Marly**, "his mentor, creator of the cosmo-magnetic pendant."
- Those years of study, tests and experiments led him to create **his own emitter** — the C.E.R.A. — and a distance-healing method called **"Chakra-Radionique."**

## La Radionique à la Portée de Tous

His book *La Radionique à la Portée de Tous* ("Radionics Within Everyone's Reach") was published by Éditions Christine Claire (ISBN 9782378500917, 34.00 €, also distributed by Spiritus Corpus). The publisher's presentation reads:

> Enter the "fairy" world of RADIONICS.
>
> Here is a work that will introduce you to the marvellous world of FORM WAVES and their extraordinary possibilities. This art — but one could call it this science — goes back to the mists of time. RADIONICS was used by the Egyptian priests (Pyramid of Cheops). It is also found among the Dogon of Mali, among the Greeks (the Solids of Plato), in Africa, in China, in South America. Subsequently, "modern" researchers created apparatuses ever more sophisticated one than the other: Doctor Abrams (1864-1924), Ruth Drown (1891-1965), Georges de la Warr (1904-1969), Doctors Brown and Richards. In Europe, one cites the Servranx brothers, Georges Lakowski, de Belizal, Chammery [Chaumery], Morel, and closer to us, Dr. Francis Frandeau de Marly.
>
> The work "La Radionique à la Portée de Tous" is addressed to beginners as much as to experienced practitioners. It will make you a RADIONICIAN capable of carrying out radionic actions whose reach you cannot imagine!
>
> The author Francis Marquette here offers you his experience of 20 years of practice. The method and the "toolbox" that accompany it will let you achieve astonishing interventions… sometimes miraculous.
>
> **The marvellous C.ER.A. emitter is included with this book.**

Two things are notable in this lineage claim. First, the genealogy Marquette invokes is the standard French-school chain — Servranx, Lakhovsky, de Belizal, Chaumery, Morel — extended to Frandeau de Marly, and now to himself. Second, the book is sold *bundled with the emitter*: the C.E.R.A. apparatus is not sold separately as an instrument but distributed as the practical companion to the method. This mirrors the older French tradition (Servranx graphiques, Frandeau's octagonal emitter sold through Éditions Servranx) but with a modern retail structure.

## The C.E.R.A. emitter

The **C.E.R.A.** is Marquette's radionic emitter — the name doubles as the acronym of his study circle (Cercle d'Études de Radionique Appliquée). A 2021 Presse Galactique article by Jean-Jacques Gangnant ("assembleur fréquentiel" of the JjG VIBRASONS frequency assemblies) presents the C.E.R.A. alongside its claimed scope of action:

> The action of radionics extends to all domains of life and all situations: spiritual plane: working towards one's evolution; health: preserving one's health, fighting addictions, aiding healing, revitalising, restorative sleep, supporting a pregnancy, slimming, well-being and beauty…; emotional: meeting the soul mate, harmony in a couple…; work: finding a job, getting a raise, social recognition, a promotion, passing a competitive exam, creating one's own business, growing the turnover…; relationships: overcoming obstacles, reconciling with others, putting an end to a conflict…; protection: against adversity, enemies known and unknown, against slander, un-spell-work, neutralising noxious waves and protecting places and habitat, in a lawsuit, against creditors, when travelling… But also: favouring luck, accelerating and clinching any sale, protecting animals… 
>
> In fact, the only limits are those you will impose on yourself!

The article is promotional in register and gives no technical description of the emitter's construction — unlike the Frandeau patent lineage, where dimensions, materials and the Fortuna Major selector geometry are documented to the millimetre. The technical documentation of the C.E.R.A. itself appears to live in the book and in the C.E.R.A. circle's teaching rather than in public patent filings.

## Les Témoins en Radionique

Marquette also wrote the booklet **Les Témoins en Radionique** ("Witnesses in Radionics"), distributed free by ebookesoterique.com (PDF, 389 KB). The site's presentation:

> The text is by Mr. Francis Marquette, who founded the Cercle d'Études de Radionique Appliqué (C.E.R.A.).
>
> Here is an interesting little booklet treating the use of witnesses in radionics. I often receive questions on this subject, and this booklet is an excellent reminder of the proper use of witnesses — to read and re-read whenever you are in doubt.
>
> As you know, Radionics requires the use of witnesses. These represent the person, the business, the animal, the plant, etc., on which the waves must act and the goal to be obtained. In these pages you will find how to make witnesses and how to use them in radionics.
>
> You will also use these recommendations in the same way in Radiesthesia. The subject is well developed, clearly explained and well written.

The *témoin* (witness — the physical sample, photograph or signature standing for the distant target) is the oldest operational layer of the discipline, continuous from Abbé Mermet's téléradiesthésie through the Servranx graphiques to the radionic boxes of Abrams and Drown. Marquette's booklet is the contemporary French statement of that practice.

## Holistica 3000 and the Celtic line

Marquette is also president of the association **HOLISTICA 3000**, a non-profit "independent and belonging to no religious, political, or cultural ideology," receiving no subsidies and funded by member dues and donations, "working above all for a Better World, Humanist and Solidary." Its branches include the G.S.H. (Groupe de Soins Holistiques), which gathers practitioners of Reiki Usui, LaHoChi, Shamballa, aromatherapy, Bach flowers, EFT and psychogenealogy — and a **radionics branch**, in which Marquette trains members.

A member's account (Emmanuel Denis, edenis-soinsceltes.fr) records the circle's current apparatus work:

> Another branch of the association concerns Radionics, a discipline until now unknown to me but which attracts me strongly, and Francis proposes to train me in it. In the meantime, our respective guides seem to have prepared our meeting well, for we are already working on a **Celtic radionic emitter, CELTISTICA 3000**, the first elements of which were transmitted to me in a dream two days after Francis contacted me…

## Where he sits in the French school

The Marquette dossier completes a generational chain that is now documented end to end in the collection:

1. **Chaumery & de Belizal** (1930s): physical radiesthesia, the Universal Pendulum, patent FR 816,132 (1936), the negative green.
2. **Enel, Morel, Servranx, Turenne, Bovis** (mid-century): consolidation and apparatus diffusion.
3. **Frandeau de Marly** (d. 2000s): the cosmo-magnetic pendant, patent 84.03970 (1984), the Fortuna Major selector, *Les nouvelles techniques radioniques de pouvoir*, the octagonal emitter distributed by Éditions Servranx.
4. **Francis Marquette** (2000s–present): mentee of Frandeau; the C.E.R.A. emitter and circle; Chakra-Radionique distance healing; *La Radionique à la Portée de Tous* bundling method and apparatus; Holistica 3000; the CELTISTICA 3000 Celtic-radionics line in development.

What changes across the chain is instructive. The founding generation published apparatus patents with millimetre specifications and framed claims in the language of physics. The Frandeau generation kept the patent form (84.03970) but shifted toward geomantic symbolism (Fortuna Major, the quaternary radiant motif). The Marquette generation drops the patent form entirely: the emitter travels with a book and a training circle, the claims are framed in the language of personal development and holistic care, and the apparatus itself is described in terms of lineage ("my mentor's pendant") rather than construction. The physics vocabulary of 1936 has become, by the 2020s, a craft transmitted master-to-student — the same social shape as the old sourcier lineages, one century on.
