# The Pendulum of the Fifth Kingdom: Chaumery, de Bélizal, and the Universal Pendulum

*Anna Maël's second work after "The Magician of the Fifth Kingdom." She gives lectures and is preparing further publications on the theme of the Fifth Kingdom. She draws inspiration notably from the spiritual and energetic work of Bernard Coulombez, geobiologist.*

---

In the 1930s, two French physicists, Léon Chaumery and André de Bélizal, were led to work on form waves and, consequently, to design two pendulums in particular: the Universal Pendulum PU-6 and the variable-cone pendulum. Why?

The story goes: André de Bélizal (rumor would present him as the grandson of the Egyptologist Howard Carter) received in inheritance from his grandfather a sandstone ring from Aswan. This ring appeared to have protected the archaeologist during his investigations and discoveries. Howard Carter had himself received it as a gift from the Marquis d'Agrain, Egyptologist, who in 1860 discovered it in a sarcophagus in the Valley of the Kings.

This ring, upon which symbols were engraved, seemed to have protected Carter from a curse that appeared as follows: on November 25, 1922, as Howard Carter entered with Lord Carnarvon into the tomb of Tutankhamun, he discovered inscribed above the sealed entrance: "The wings of death shall touch him who violates the eternal repose of the Pharaoh."

It was subsequently established that a tragic fate would strike the team present: eighteen people close to them died in strange accidents or from illness in the days and months that followed. Only Howard Carter, who wore this symbolic ring (called the Ring of Re or the Atlantean Ring), had better fortune and died only seventeen years later of natural causes.

André de Bélizal, accompanied by Léon Chaumery, oriented his research toward vibratory and micro-vibratory phenomena.

He understood that the power of this ring, a veritable talisman, resided in the very structure of its form. He wore it and observed an improvement in his health, his affairs. He reproduced the ring on a wooden bar (see the Atlantean bar below) and conducted similar experiments on plants and animals. The results were conclusive. This set in motion a whole series of studies on form waves, studies that would lead to the decomposition of the light spectrum.

The two French physicists discovered that every object, according to its specificity and particularity, influences the energetic body of man. Every object emits form waves. Relying on a radiesthetic detection process, they revealed the decomposition of the light spectrum around the perimeter of a wooden sphere.

> "This electromagnetism, we sense it in the sphere, the crossroads and center of emission of all vibrations. We have observed that the latter, being traversed by a natural magnetic current passing through its poles, there is produced at the very center of the figure, in the order of visible and invisible radiations, a decomposition into wavelengths analogous to that of a luminous spectrum, and these different rays of color distribute themselves on the spherical surface at rigorously equidistant points. These radiations being identical to the phenomenon observed in the decomposition of a ray of white light through a crystal prism, it seemed evident to us that we were in the presence of the complete range of wavelengths, from the longest: infrared, to the shortest, which we have since named: 'negative green.'"

This was the key to all their radiesthetic studies, which would also trigger the design of the Universal Pendulum and the theory of the decomposition of the light spectrum.
