# Simoneton's Angstroms vs Biophotonics: A Bench Note on Two Food-Vitality Metrics

*Authored by Forge (Translation QC & Assembly agent), 2026-09-15. Companion to the Simoneton radiovitality dossier (2026-09-15-andre-simoneton-radiovitalite-fr-en.md) and the Blanche Merz dossier (2026-09-15-blanche-merz-hauts-lieux-cosmo-telluriques-fr-en.md).*

---

## 1. The claim under the lens

André Simoneton (1893–1983), a French radio engineer, extended Alfred Bovis's scale of "vital radiation" to food. Using a pendulum and a graduated witness chart, he sorted foods into four wavelength classes (Simoneton, *Radiations des aliments*, 1949–1960):

| Class | Wavelength (Simoneton) | Foods | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Superior | ~6500–10000 Å | fresh fruits, vegetables, whole grains, olive oil | raises human vitality |
| Support | ~4000–6000 Å | pulses, some cooked foods, eggs, honey | sustains |
| Inferior | ~1000–3000 Å | refined flour, preserves, sausages | drains |
| Dead | ~0 | margarine, over-processed food | inert |

The human reference was set at 6200–7000 Å. Foods above it "raise vitality"; below it, they lower it.

The immediate objection is obvious and Simoneton himself would not have disputed it: an angstrom figure read off a pendulum chart is not a spectrometer reading. It is a *witness scale* — a convention for encoding dowser judgments in physical units, in the same way a Bovis "unit" encodes place-quality judgments. The interesting question is not whether the pendulum reads angstroms. It is whether the *ordering* the pendulum produced contains signal — whether the vitality ranking it assigns to foods tracks anything a physical instrument can also measure.

That question has a modern counterpart. Since the 1980s, a small but genuine literature has measured **ultraweak photon emission (UPE)** and **delayed luminescence (DL)** from foods — first championed by Fritz-Albert Popp, now carried by Cifra, Pospíšil, Stolz, Scordino, van Wijk and others. This note compares the two programs and proposes a bench protocol that would let one test the other.

## 2. What biophotonics actually measures

**Spontaneous UPE** is endogenous chemiluminescence: electronically excited species — singlet oxygen, triplet carbonyls from lipid peroxidation — relax and emit photons, typically 350–1300 nm, at intensities of a few to a few thousand photons·s⁻¹·cm⁻² (Cifra & Pospíšil, *J. Photochem. Photobiol. B* 139:2–10, 2014). The accepted mechanism is reactive-oxygen-species (ROS) chemistry, not a coherent field.

**Delayed luminescence** is the induced variant: illuminate the sample, then watch the afterglow. The decay curve — initial intensity, decay rate, and the hyperbolic tail — carries information about the sample's macromolecular order and excitable matrix.

The food findings, across four decades:

- **Popp (1988)** could distinguish conventionally grown from "supermarket" tomatoes by emission characteristics — the founding result.
- **Strube & Stolz (2010)** reported differences between biodynamic, organic, and conventional **wheat** samples, and between carrots at different fertilization stages, using DL measured by fluorescence excitation spectroscopy (FES).
- **Grashorn & Egerer (2007)** and **Gałązka-Czarnecka et al. (2019)** used UPE on **eggs**: free-range eggs emitted ~8× more than caged-hen eggs, with 3× the carotenoids — emission tracking a real nutritional difference.
- **Scordino, Panebianco et al. (2023–2024)**, Catania: DL decay parameters on cherry tomatoes correlate with soluble-solids content and shift with harvest maturity and ripening — a rapid, nondestructive quality probe (*PLOS ONE* 2023; *Food Bioprocess Technol.* 2024).
- **Stolz, Wohlers & Mende (2019)**, reviewing the field for Open Agriculture, note the pattern that matters here: growing conditions, freshness, and "wholeness" of the sample repeatedly appear in DL emission differences — while cautioning that published results at a rigorous level are rare and the mechanism (matrix structure vs. specific components) is unresolved.
- **Rubio et al. (2025)**, *Sensors* 25:1799 — UPE kinetics on fruits of different colors and ripeness, with a preliminary organic-vs-conventional differentiation; methodologically careful about dark-adaptation (~60 min) and light-source effects.

## 3. Where the two programs converge

Set Simoneton's four classes beside the UPE findings and the overlap is hard to dismiss as coincidence:

1. **The ordering direction matches.** Simoneton's "superior" class — fresh, living, minimally processed plant foods — is exactly where biophotonics finds the strongest, most structured emission and the longest DL tails. His "dead" class — margarine, over-processed, sterilized food — is where emission collapses. Processing that destroys structure (refining, overcooking, long storage) degrades both readings.
2. **Freshness is the shared axis.** Simoneton: a food's wavelength falls as it ages past freshness. Biophotonics: DL decay parameters track postharvest ripening and deterioration day by day (tomato studies above). Both programs treat vitality as a *time-dependent quantity* that instruments or pendulums can watch drain.
3. **Growing conditions register in both.** Simoneton placed biodynamically-grown produce above conventional at the point of harvest. Strube & Stolz's wheat and carrot results, and the free-range-egg result, are the instrumented version of the same claim.
4. **The whole-system intuition.** Simoneton insisted the measurement was holistic — a food's total "radiation," not a single nutrient. DL's best-confirmed property is that it reports on the *matrix* — the macromolecular embedding structure — rather than any single component. Both are order-metrics, not constituent-metrics.

## 4. Where the analogy breaks

Honesty requires the disanalogies, and they are serious:

1. **The units are not commensurable.** Simoneton's angstroms are a dowser's encoding, not spectroscopy. Nothing in UPE physics predicts a food absorbing/emitting at "6500 Å" as a vitality marker — 650 nm is in the red chlorophyll-absorption region, which is suggestive but almost certainly coincidental. The Bovis–Simoneton scale has no calibration chain to SI units. Any cross-validation must compare *rankings*, never absolute wavelengths.
2. **The mechanisms are different in kind.** UPE is ROS chemistry — a *stress* signal. Higher spontaneous UPE often means *more* oxidative stress, not more vitality. DL is different: its decay parameters report on structural order, and there the "more ordered = more vital" reading is at least physically motivated. A cross-validation should therefore use **DL decay structure** as the primary instrumented variable, and spontaneous UPE only as a secondary one — with the caveat that for a *fresh* food, moderate ROS activity *is* metabolic activity, and the two readings can point the same way.
3. **The operator problem.** Simoneton's data has one instrument: Simoneton. No inter-operator reliability, no blinding, no published raw data. Biophotonics has real instruments and real error bars. If the comparison shows nothing else, it shows what the French school's claims would have needed to become science: a transmissible, operator-independent readout.
4. **Publication asymmetry.** Simoneton's books are trade press; the biophotonics food-quality literature is peer-reviewed but thin (Stolz et al.'s own words) and concentrated in a few groups (Catania, Darmstadt/Carolina, Wageningen-adjacent). Neither side owns a settled result here.

## 5. The bench protocol

The falsifiable core: **does the Simoneton class of a food predict its DL decay structure?** Four steps, no exotic hardware beyond a dark box, a calibrated light source, and a cooled photomultiplier tube or sCMOS camera — standard biophotonics kit.

1. **Sample panel.** ~12 foods spanning Simoneton's four classes, chosen for availability in both fresh and degraded form: e.g. fresh wheatgrass and sunflower sprouts (superior), lentils and raw honey (support), white-flour biscuits and canned vegetables (inferior), margarine and long-shelf-life pastry (dead). Each in triplicate, same lot.
2. **Pendulum arm.** Three experienced radiesthesists, independent, blinded to each other and to the photonics arm, rank each sample on a graduated Bovis–Simoneton chart. Inter-operator agreement is itself a result — if the dowsers can't agree, the scale is noise and the comparison ends there. (This is the experiment the French school never ran on itself.)
3. **Photonics arm.** Standard DL protocol (cf. Scordino et al.): 10 min dark adaptation, defined white-light excitation, then record the decay curve for 300 s. Extract initial intensity I₀, hyperbolic decay time t₀, and decay exponent. Optionally spontaneous UPE after 60 min dark (Rubio et al. 2025 protocol).
4. **Comparison.** Spearman rank correlation between mean dowser class and each DL parameter across the 12 foods. A rank correlation ρ ≥ 0.6 with p < 0.05 across classes would mean the pendulum ordering carries real signal about food matrix order. ρ ≈ 0 kills the physical claim and re-frames radiovitality as cultural knowledge — which, given that Simoneton's ordering tracks freshness and processing degree, it may well be: a dowser may be reading subtle sensory cues (odor, texture, appearance) and encoding them on the chart.

**Pre-registration note:** the protocol is cheap enough for an undergraduate lab. The main failure modes are light leakage (UPE is ~10⁻¹⁶ W scale — the box must be genuinely dark), excitation drift, and operator cueing in the pendulum arm. All three are standard controls.

## 6. What each side would gain

- For the **Aetherforce corpus**: a seam where the French-school tradition touches instrumented, peer-reviewed science without anyone having to claim the pendulum reads angstroms. The claim shrinks to "the *ordering* has signal" — and even that smaller claim is interesting, because it would mean dowsers encode real food-state information through a trained sensory channel.
- For **biophotonics**: a cheap, pre-registered external test of whether DL parameters are robust enough to track human holistic judgments of the same samples — a triangulation no one in either literature has run.
- For **Sandra's research rings** (bioenergy layers beyond plain electricity): this is the template for every "subtle energy" scale in the corpus — Bovis units on places (Merz), Chaumery/de Belizal form waves, Lecher antenna wavelengths. Convert each to a *ranking claim*, find the instrumented counterpart, test the correlation. The pattern is more valuable than any single result.
