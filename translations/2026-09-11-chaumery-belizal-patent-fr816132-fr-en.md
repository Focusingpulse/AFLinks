# The Chaumery–de Belizal Radiesthesia Patent FR 816,132 (1936): *Procédé et appareillage radiesthésiques* — The Universal Pendulum and the Negative-Green Ray

> Translation metadata: title "Procédé et appareillage radiesthésiques / Dowsing method and apparatus" · patent FR 816,132 A · filed 10 April 1936 · granted 30 July 1937 · inventors Léon Julien Marie Chaumery (1880–1957) and André Marie Pierre de Gouzillon de Bélizal (1896–1975) · French Republic · translated by Forge 2026-09-11 from the patent abstract (Kook Science transcription of the espacenet original) and the patent-adjacent apparatus chapters of de Belizal & Morel, *Physique Micro-Vibratoire et Forces Invisibles* (Desforges, 1976, rexresearch1.com scan). The modern classification is itself a historical artifact worth noting: the French patent office placed a dowsing apparatus in the same class as electrotherapy and geophysical prospecting — in 1936 the claim was filed as physics, not as esoterica.

## The patent abstract (translated in full)

> "The present invention relates to a dowsing method and apparatus based on terrestrial magnetism.
>
> Until now, the pendulums used in dowsing have been bodies of any shape whatever, suspended by a flexible link. The most perfected of them agree only in wavelength. With all these devices, and in particular with the disks, it is impossible to explore the region comprised between infra-black and ultra-white, these two rays being welded along an axis which itself possesses another ray not yet characterized. This ray is the antipode of the green of the spectrum: it is the shortest wave and consequently the most powerful of the invisible spectrum. In what follows, this ray is designated under the name of **negative green** or **special green**."

Every element of the school's later system is already present in the filing language of 10 April 1936:

- **Terrestrial magnetism as the physical basis** — the apparatus is claimed to work on the earth's magnetic field, not on the operator's mind. This is the "physical radiesthesia" position that separates the French school from the mentalist school (and that Armand Viré's Academy-of-Sciences communication defended the same year).
- **The critique of existing pendulums** — "bodies of any shape whatever… agree only in wavelength." The invention's claim to novelty is spectral completeness: the old instruments could not reach the region between infra-black (*infra-noir*) and ultra-white (*ultra-blanc*).
- **The negative green ray (vert négatif, V−)** — introduced here for the first time in a legal document: the ray "welded" between white and black on the far side of the visible spectrum's green, "the shortest wave and consequently the most powerful of the invisible spectrum." The patent is the birth certificate of the concept that the whole later school — de Belizal & Morel's 1976 book, De La Foye's *Ondes de vie, ondes de mort*, Enel, and through them the modern "form waves" literature — built upon.

## What the patent protected (from the inventors' own later description)

The patent covers, in the words of the 1976 book, "at once the apparatuses, and the method of the *decomposition of the spectrum in the sphere*." Two families of devices are claimed:

### 1. The Universal Pendulum (*Pendule Universel*)

The patent's detector, "put into final form in 1936 by Léon Chaumery and A. de Belizal":

- A rigorously balanced **sphere** — 60 mm diameter, 125 g in the original specification (the later commercial PU-5.4: 5 cm, 38 g; PU-6.6: 6 cm, 74 g) — carrying three graduated great circles: an **electric meridian (E)**, a **magnetic meridian (M)**, and an **electro-magnetic equator (EM)**, all divided into 400 grades.
- A **non-ferrous metal handle (anse)** joining the two poles, mobile, so that every point of the sphere can be explored; the suspension cord slides along the handle and carries three marked holding-points — E, M, EM — one per circle.
- An internal **magnetic battery** ("pile") of four stacked elements (PU-5.4) or six (PU-6.6) along the north–south axis, whose function is "to reinforce the polarities while preventing them from inverting under the action of a more powerful form wave" — plus two opposed diamagnetic masses on the equator to suppress spiral-form vibration and stabilize the great circles.
- The meridian graduations carry the spectrum in fixed order — visible: R O J V+ Bu I Vi; invisible: UV Bc V− N IR — with the **V− point at grade 0 = 400**, exactly between white and black, "the antipode of the green of the spectrum."
- The EM equator adds a Greek-letter band for the radioactive sector identified by the school: **Alpha 5** (subsoil faults with magnetic emanations), **Beta 10** (telluric water currents), **Theta 15 / Chi 20 / Nu 25** (electric fields at the soil from electro-filtration, television gamma rays), **Dzêta 30** (radioactivity in all its forms), **Noir 33.5**; and on the white side **Epsilon 370, Khi 375, Lambda 380, Psi 385, Rhô 390, Oméga 395, Blanc 366.5** (harms from atmospheric electricity, high-tension lines, electrical factories).
- The equator also carries **8 witness-marks: 13, 19, 25, 50, 55, 62, 68, 80**, corresponding to eight families of pure bodies of Mendeleev's table — wave-heights: the number of gyrations of the detector indicates the atomic number of the body prospected (the same "detector turns = atomic number" law that Turenne systematized independently).
- The instrument is described as a **receiver-emitter**: requiring no mental orientation by the operator, it is claimed to work separately on the electric phase, the magnetic phase, and the full electromagnetic beam — "the only pendulum that works not only from the usual electromagnetic vibrations but also, and separately, either with electric undulations (E) or with magnetic undulations (M)."

The 1936 patent date and number are asserted by the inventors against all comers: "We claim the absolute paternity of this 'Radiesthetic Battery,' as much for its discovery as for its properties and its applications. It has moreover made, together with the 'Universal Pendulum,' the object of a patent dated 10 April 1936 bearing the number 816,132."

### 2. The cosmo-magnetic batteries (*piles cosmo-magnétiques*)

The patent's second family — the emitters:

- **Pile cosmique**: a vertical stack of successive half-spheres, "conductor of the waves of the cosmic rays, which it transmits to an objective (BC 30 or disk emitter)."
- **Pile magnétique**: the same stack of half-spheres, but "axised and oriented in the earth's magnetic N–S axis. This accumulation of forms produces a progressive amplification of the magnetic waves, principal carriers in form emission."
- The claimed physics: each half-sphere is an amplifier — "an accumulation of hemispheres in a pile constitutes an amplifier of magnetic energy, released by the central point of the last face of the pile. The number and importance of the elements of a pile constitute the *tension* of its form emission… the diameter of the elements — consequently its total mass — constitutes its *amplitude*." Tension by counting elements, amplitude by sizing them: the vocabulary of electrical batteries transferred to form waves.
- The negative-green ray itself "can also be obtained artificially by a rigorously calculated form wave. This discovery was the object of patent no. 816 132, dated 10 April 1936, taken in collaboration with M. Léon Chaumery, today deceased: these are our cosmo-magnetic batteries."
- The claimed results, stated plainly in the 1976 book and in the *Essai*: meat, fish, eggs and vegetables **mummified** (dehydrated and preserved indefinitely) under V− emission; flowers sterilized and kept intact; a virgin photographic film "immediately impressed" provided it has gamma emulsion; and the ray used "as a healing ray in cancerous affections." The book adds the school's own casualty: **Chaumery died 27 February 1957, "victim of his experiments, completely dehydrated by our negative green ray (V−)"** — at which date the antidote (V+) had not yet been found; de Belizal and Morel credit Morel's collaboration with the discovery of the V+ neutralization.

### 3. The Bombe C. 30 (post-patent development, documented for completeness)

The patent family's emitter grew after Chaumery's death into the **Bombe C. 30** — "C. 30 because it now has 30 centimeters of diameter, more than 4 times the intensity of the original apparatus": three complete nested spheres (six half-spheres), the innermost holding the exposure chamber with interchangeable positive and negative metals, where the patient's witness (hair, blood, urine) and the drawing of the organ to be treated are placed; the outermost sphere carrying the electric meridian, magnetic meridian and EM equator in 400 grades; a copper "selector" index at the north pole choosing the emission grade; a silver **magnetic corrector** (phase separator, arrow pivoting on the central axis) and a silver **magnetic compensator** disk (evening out the lunar variations of the magnetic coefficient) — the school's answer to the variability of the earth's field. The book's warning is preserved: never exceed the time-value given by the measuring disk, "otherwise there occurs in the organism an accumulation of intense magnetic radio-activity which can have unfavorable consequences. The same holds for the operator who remains too long beside the apparatus."

## Why this document matters

1. **It is the school's legal foundation.** The 1936 filing is the only place where the negative-green ray, the sphere method, and the cosmo-magnetic batteries enter a public, dated, state-archived record. Every later text (1939 *Traité*, 1942 *Essai*, 1976 *Physique Micro-Vibratoire*) cites it as the origin — "the cornerstone of our method."
2. **It dates the French form-wave school precisely.** Discovery of V− claimed 1934; patent filed 10 April 1936; granted 30 July 1937. This anchors the whole genealogy: Bovis's scale (1930) precedes it; the AAR's medical section (mid-1930s) is contemporary; Turenne's saturation law (detector turns = atomic number) is parallel; De La Foye's popularization (1975) and the Brocéliande geobiology literature follow it.
3. **It is the seam between the French and Russian schools.** The corpus already documents that Davidson's Russian genealogy names Pavlita, Belizal, Turenne, Drbal. This patent is the French half of that seam — the same "form emits wave, wave acts on witness" register, under French patent law, a generation before the Soviet torsion-field program.
4. **It shows the school's own safety record.** The death of the co-inventor by the claimed ray, recorded by the surviving inventor in the book's preface, is the tradition's most striking internal datum — and the reason the school itself insists on the V+ "antidote" and on operator precautions.
