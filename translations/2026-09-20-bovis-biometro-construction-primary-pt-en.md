# The Bovis Biometer Construction Primary

## A. Bovis's own founding text (April 1935)

*Source: "Experiências feitas com o Biômetro de Bovis — Abril de 1935," by A. Bovis, President of the Société de Radiesthésie de la Côte d'Azur. Portuguese text as published by casadaradiestesia.com.br (Brazilian radiesthesia house site). Translated PT→EN by Forge, 2026-09-20. Original French text (1935) not located online; the Portuguese is the fetchable witness.*

> It is a truth now admitted by all scientists that all bodies existing in nature emit radiations. These invisible radiations are perceived by all radiesthetists by means of ultrasensitive instruments — the rod and the pendulum. But what is the nature of these radiations, and how can they be measured? It is to answer these two questions that we devised the Biometer — based, from the point of view of its construction — for its conception departs somewhat from known scientific data — based, we say, on the Law below. We ask readers to read it with attention, and to understand it:
>
> **"Law of Bovis. Every body (drawing or outline of a body) of elongated form, whatever its nature — animal, vegetable, or mineral — has the property of capturing telluric waves when it is placed in the North–South direction. If we give this body any geometric form, it will become an emitter and captor of waves, whatever its position."**
>
> To help in understanding this law, every radiesthetist should do the following experiment: draw a straight line with pencil or pen — it does not matter — on white paper. A pendulum placed at either end of the line will give a positive beat along its entire length. Now orient the line in the North–South direction, and we will find:
>
> 1. A positive beat at the North;
> 2. A negative beat at the South;
> 3. A mixed beat at the center.
>
> This simple stroke has become a captor and emitter of waves. If instead of drawing a straight line we draw a triangle, a square, a diamond, etc., and investigate with the pendulum the radiations over the sides and over the angles, we will see that the beats always reproduce identically, whatever the orientation. An important note imposes itself: we find, on the strokes — even when drawn on metal, or on a metal body — beats indicating magnetic currents of opposite, quite distinct natures. The magnetic currents do not short-circuit. Another small experiment leads us to a second observation: if we touch, with a finger of the left hand, either the end of the oriented line or an angle of the geometric drawings, we will see the pendulum's movements change direction, showing us clearly that we have changed the poles of the magnetism emitted by the drawing.
>
> Our Biometer is based on these observations.
>
> 1. It is composed of a diamond (losango) of a form and size studied so that it emits waves equal to the magnetic field of a horseshoe magnet: the positive, the negative, and the mixed.
> 2. And of a metal ruler 20 cm in length. This dimension was likewise determined by the change occurring in the poles of a horseshoe magnet whose extremities were joined by soft iron, when presented at this distance to either the color red or the color gold. (We use the color red because it has the wavelength of 6,500 angstroms, which is the longest wavelength of the colors of the spectrum visible to man.)
>
> To facilitate calculations, we place 100° at the 20 cm point, which makes each degree of our Biometer two millimeters, and each centimeter equal to 5°. Having established our instrument according to these data, we observed that:
>
> - if we present the **positive side** of a horseshoe [magnet], the change of poles on the diamond takes place **from 20 cm — that is, at 100°**;
> - the alteration, when the **negative side** is presented, takes place only **from 10 cm — that is, 50°**;
> - the change of poles when the **top of the magnet** is pointed takes place **at 175 mm — that is, 75°**.
>
> Further, each pole of the magnet acts differently on the extremities of the diamond: the positive side immobilizes the pendulum on the two extremities; the negative side makes these two extremities negative; the top of the magnet makes the pendulum rotate at the two extremities of the diamond. This is what allows us to measure the wavelength of all bodies by comparing them with the color waves of the spectrum, and to identify their quality by comparison with the magnet.

**[Translator's note:** the closing sentence is the whole instrument doctrine in one line — *wavelength* by comparison with spectral colors, *quality* (polarity character) by comparison with the horseshoe magnet. The three pole-flip distances (100°/50°/75°) are the only quantified calibration events in the founding text.**]**

## Why this document matters (Forge's analysis)

This is the **construction primary** behind the entire Bovis-scale ecosystem — the founding text the corpus's other Bovis files describe secondhand:

- **Companion to the 09-10 biography** (2026-09-10-bovis-echelle-biometre-fr-en.md): the biography says "a 20 cm stick graduated from 0 to 100, the value 100 corresponding to 6500 Å" but does not have the *mechanism* — the horseshoe-magnet pole-flip calibration event, the diamond-as-field-emitter, the pole-flip distances. This text supplies it.
- **Companion to the 09-16 UB-drift dossier** (2026-09-16-bovis-ub-angstrom-drift-fr-en.md): the drift analysis treats the 6500 Å anchor as given; here is Bovis's own stated reason — red is "the longest wavelength of the colors of the spectrum visible to man" (true for the deep-red end; the actual longest visible is ~700+ nm, so 6500 Å was already a rounded convention, not a measurement).
- **The one falsifiable anchor:** the 6500 Å calibration ties the instrument to a concrete physical wavelength. Everything else (diamond "studied so that" it matches a magnet field, pole-flip at named distances) is reproducible in the weak sense — anyone with a horseshoe magnet, a metal ruler, a diamond drawing, and a pendulum can repeat the calibration ritual and check whether the pendulum behaves as Bovis prescribes. The reading remains operator-dependent (pendulum response), so it stays below the Mafra bar — but it is a *published, dimensioned, reproducible instrument spec*, the closest the Bovis ecosystem comes to one.

## The host site's own modern commentary (translated PT→EN)

The casadaradiestesia.com.br page appends three commentary sections of independent value:

**1. "Hidden behind an initial" — the first-name question.** Until recently the first name of Bovis was unknown; a French renovation company's geobiology page lists a service provider as "Jacques Bovis, grandson of André Bovis" — the grandson would know the famous grandfather's name. (The corpus's 09-10 biography independently establishes André/Alfred Bovis of Nice, the coppersmith-hardware merchant — not the mythical Egyptologist.)

**2. "Angstrom — what is that?" — the unit's history and problem.** The famous Biometer, in its current versions, is graduated in angstroms. At the time of its creation (c. 1928) it was necessary to choose a recognized, valid standard of measure; the wavelength unit — the angstrom — was aptly chosen. In the 1940s another French engineer, **André Simoneton**, known for his work *Radiations des Aliments — Ondes Humaines et Santé*, redrew the original Biometer with a new dimension in accordance with wavelength and with new graphics; it was then used for 50 years. The site recommends Guido S. Bassler's *Lugares Altamente Energéticos* and Georges Prat's *L'Architecture Cosmique* (values above 130,000 angstroms!) and Stéphane Cardinaux's *Géométries Sacrées* for alternatives.

**3. The frequency-vs-wavelength contradiction — stated in the tradition's own words.** Take a ripe banana and a vial of homeopathic remedy: which has the higher frequency? The homeopathic remedy, of course. It should therefore give *fewer* angstroms on the biometer — higher frequency means shorter wavelength, fewer angstroms — but it gives *more*. "What do we find in the Biometer when measuring? Exactly the contrary." The solution adopted: keep the graphics as they are, and transform the value obtained into an abstract standard — **Bovis Units or Radiesthetic Units**. And a second problem, stated plainly: "the energies we deal with are by nature **logarithmic**, and the biometer's scale and our understanding are **LINEAR!**" — the practitioner is advised to pendulate a great deal, "until the arm cramps," until body/mind performs the conversion automatically.

**4. The "fat arrow" at 6500 — a measurement artifact acknowledged by the tradition.** Beginners practice pendulum exercises following figures; the fat arrow on the graph marks a place of *attraction* — the pendulum is drawn to it. "We have in the old Biometer falsified measurements because of a fat arrow." The site promises a solution in a later post.

**[Forge's note:** sections 3 and 4 are remarkable honesty rows from *inside* the tradition — the unit redefinition (Å → UB) is admitted to be a patch over a sign contradiction in the founding metaphor, the linear/logarithmic mismatch is admitted, and the 6500 fat-arrow attractor is admitted to distort readings. These rows belong beside the corpus's external critiques (Chevreul, Munich, ideomotor) as the tradition's own self-audit.**]**
