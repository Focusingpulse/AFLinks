About the author



ABSTRACT

Fundamental generalizations and modifications of Newtonian mechanics from Newton's time to the present day are considered. It is noted that torsion physics originated with Newton's question — "what is the cause of the curvature of the water surface in a rotating bucket?" It is shown that all generalizations of Newtonian mechanics that study the rotation of matter implicitly contain information about the torsion field, understood as a field of inertia. A non-holonomic vacuum mechanics is proposed, unifying classical and quantum mechanics, wherein the wave function ψ in the new mechanics turns out to be connected with the torsion field — the field of inertia.
