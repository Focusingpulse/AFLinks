# Using the Radiesthesia Board — Printable Board Templates

The divining pendulum can give us "yes" or "no" answers. But to go further, the use of a radiesthesia board allows you to move beyond the classic convention. Radiesthesia boards most often take the form of dials and sometimes of rulers.

The use of boards and dials for divinatory purposes has existed for a very long time. You may have just bought a radiesthesia pendulum and need supports to use it. Let us discover together the main radiesthesia dials and their functions.

## Divination Boards with Alphabet and Numbers

The most widespread dials include the alphabet or numbers. This allows you to compose words or phrases and obtain numerical results. As with the "yes-no" convention with your pendulum, the key is to ask very precise and clear questions.

## The Bovis Dial (or Bovis Biometer)

The best-known and most widely used board is the Bovis scale, or Bovis biometer, invented in 1900. This board can also take the form of a ruler. The purpose of the biometer is to measure the energetic vitality of a living being or organism. It is therefore an interesting tool for the therapist. On a standard Bovis scale, values range from 0 to 18,000 UB (Bovis Units). Vibrational power is divided into three scales:

- The first belongs to the physical world, with values ranging from 0 to 10,000 UB
- The second belongs to the energetic world, with values ranging from 10,000 to 14,000 UB
- The third belongs to the spiritual world, with values above 14,000 UB

Bovis conducted experiments by placing, for example, fruits at the end of the biometer. He would then advance his pendulum over the biometer from the zero point until the pendulum began to move above a graduation mark. He obtained values close to zero for rotten fruit but values around 8,000 to 9,000 UB for a freshly picked fruit. By repeating his observations, he succeeded in establishing a unit of measurement.

With the rise in the Earth's vibrational rate, an extended Bovis board has been created, going up to 120,000 UB.

## Other Medical Radiesthesia Boards

There are also many diagnostic boards used in medical radiesthesia beyond the Bovis board. These boards are composed of elements from homeopathy, phytotherapy, aromatherapy, and dietetics. Added to these are dials inspired by Chinese medicine and acupuncture. There are also radiesthesia boards based on the chakras and Ayurveda.

Some therapists work with anatomical boards. A board can also contain psychological elements, to answer questions such as "why do I have a blockage at…?" One can also inquire about the domain that is the source of psychological unease, for example: emotional, relational, professional, etc.

Another example: in naturopathy, to identify the source of metal toxicity, radiesthesists use a metals dial.

## Geobiology Boards

In the same way, pendulum boards can be used in geobiology, to determine the presence of metals and other geological elements in the ground. Geobiology is also interested in the nature of electromagnetic waves, to determine whether they are harmful.

There are therefore dials ranging from 0 Hz to 10 GHz, graduated from simple electric current up to radioactivity, including the waves from our telephones. Thus the radiesthesist or geobiologist determines which places are favorable for human activity and which places should be avoided, or rearranged.

## Other Radiesthesia Boards

One can also find radiesthesia boards related to all kinds of alternative therapy schools, which use Bach flower remedies or oligo-elements, for example. There, rather than assisting with diagnosis, the pendulum board helps choose a remedy. There are also boards with the planets and the astrological system, for doing readings.

In a more New Age vein, there are radiesthesia dials with Angels and Archangels. These boards allow you to find which elements we are in resonance with, and which elements we have difficulty with. The variations of boards are numerous.

## How to Use a Radiesthesia Board

Place the radiesthesia board in front of you, and while breathing calmly, place the pendulum at the zero point to begin. You must remain concentrated. The pendulum will indicate a direction. When the direction is clear, stop the pendulum.

How is it that the pendulum becomes active in the direction of an element on the dial? There are several interpretations of this mystery:

- Either it is an energetic phenomenon
- or it is suggestion
- or it is a mixture of both.

Tarot reading relies on a similar idea: we unconsciously choose the cards that mirror us. Our hands would be guided by a card with which they come into resonance. In the same way, there would be a resonance between the radiesthesist, the pendulum, the person asking the question, and the answer. One can also think that we give an unconscious impulse to the pendulum when we encounter the answer.

Nevertheless, this psychological explanation does not work when someone else pendulums for you. Something guides their hand nonetheless. There is no universal explanation, and the mystery of the pendulum remains whole.

As with the "yes-no" convention or the use of the pendulum in geobiology, experienced radiesthesists speak of training. It is better to be comfortable with your pendulum before using boards. You can test the boards alone or with other people, and go see a radiesthesist who uses pendulum dials if you need an answer to a specific question.
