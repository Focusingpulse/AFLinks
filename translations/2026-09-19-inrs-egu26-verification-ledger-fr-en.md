# The Verification Ledger, 1937–2026: INRS Final Results and Ninety Years of French Dowsing Tests

**Translated/compiled by Forge (translation-qc), 2026-09-19.** Companion to the 2026-09-16 INRS dowsing field-study dossier (FAL-fr-89-1). Sources: scout fire 116, items 1–3, 5, 6. All URLs verified fetchable from cloud 2026-09-19 except where noted.

## 1. INRS EGU26-3985: The Final-Results Abstract (Bordeleau, Lévesque, Gloaguen & Giroux, 2026)

The controlled dowsing experiment at Baie Sainte-Marguerite (Sacré-Cœur, Québec) has completed its field season, and the final-results abstract is now published for the EGU General Assembly 2026, Vienna, 3–8 May 2026 (EGU26-3985, DOI 10.5194/egusphere-egu26-3985, CC BY 4.0).

**Full abstract, translated context and key rows:**

- **Title:** "Testing Tradition: A Controlled Study on Dowsing Accuracy"
- **Authors:** Geneviève Bordeleau (INRS, Québec), Manon Lévesque (citizen scientist and dowsing practitioner), Erwan Gloaguen (INRS), Bernard Giroux (INRS)
- **Status:** field experiment complete; results "analyzed and discussed as a function of object type, dowsing instrument, and prior experience level, providing a quantitative assessment of dowsing performance under controlled conditions." The numeric verdict itself is still behind the May 2026 Vienna presentation.

From the abstract (EN original; the study's working language is French):

> "Dowsing is an ancient practice that employs rods or pendulums to locate groundwater and other subsurface features. [...] Field demonstrations of dowsing can appear convincing and are often viewed as cost-effective methods of selecting a location for drilling a new well; however, in regions characterized by sufficient rainfall and favorable geological conditions, groundwater is often ubiquitous. This raises questions about what constitutes a genuine 'detection' and complicates meaningful comparisons between traditional and scientific approaches."

The design logic, as before: discrete buried pipes, not ubiquitous groundwater. The abstract confirms the full cross-tab:

- 25-cell grid, buried objects in selected cells
- **iron and plastic pipes** — the Rocard magnetic-hypothesis row
- **empty or water-filled**; water saline or fresh, stagnant or flowing
- **wooden or metal rods** — the instrument row
- **54 participants: 27 experienced, 27 novices** (novices received basic training) — the experience row
- **several participants completed multiple trials to assess reproducibility**

What is new since the 09-16 dossier: the abstract now explicitly frames the analysis as final ("we present the final results"), and the framing sentence on ubiquitous groundwater is sharpened — the study's own answer to the "success near apparent sources" ambiguity that Costerisant named in 1937 (see §2). The co-design credit stands: "co-designed by a multidisciplinary team of scientists in collaboration with an experienced dowsing practitioner, with the aim of ensuring both methodological rigor and acceptance by the dowsing community."

**Corpus significance:** this is the discriminating test the verification-turn synthesis called for — every cell of the design (material × water × rod × experience) has an outcome in every direction. The May 2026 Vienna session is the date to watch; the numeric tables are the payload.

## 2. Costerisant 1937: "Réflexions sur la radiesthésie" — ~200 Water Projects, Zero Successes

Oscar Costérisant, *Revue des sciences naturelles d'Auvergne*, vol. 3, fasc. 3–4, 1937, pp. 145–147 (DOI 10.3406/rsna.1937.1644, Persée). An engineering review, not a polemic: Costérisant reports observations made "in the course of the establishment of about 200 water-supply projects" studied especially in the departments of Puy-de-Dôme and Haute-Loire.

> "Disons tout de suite que le nombre des succès constatés est effectivement nul, car nous ne saurions admettre comme tel les indications données très près de source apparente puisqu'il est certain qu'en creusant à un niveau inférieur au point d'émergence de ces sources, les fouilles en attireront inévitablement l'eau par les innombrables fissures des roches."
>
> (Let us say at once that the number of successes observed is effectively zero, for we could not accept as such the indications given very close to apparent springs — since it is certain that, by digging below the emergence point of those springs, the excavations will inevitably attract water through the countless fissures in the rock.)

Key rows from the French original:

- **The "near a spring" artifact named in 1937** — exactly the "ubiquitous groundwater" confound the INRS abstract names in 2026. Ninety years apart, the same epistemic problem: a detection claim needs a discrete target.
- **Phreatic-zone digs always find "a certain quantity of water"** — and the dowsers' indicated depth and flow figures "have never been found accurate" ("les indications données de profondeur et de débit n'ont jamais été reconnues exactes").
- **The tracking test:** following and marking the underground course of a visible spring is "the ABC of the dowser's art" ("l'enfance de l'art du sourcier"). Posed to different dowsers at Cizière (commune of Saint-Éble), Lorlanges, and Azérat: **negative results** — the workmen's picks, uncovering the actual water threads, followed a different path from the one indicated by rod or pendulum.
- At Cizières the indicated path was simply the straight-line extension of a real, known segment; at Lorlanges and Azérat the dowsers were guided by local tradition, which was itself erroneous.
- At Paulhac and Costecirgue (Vieille-Brioude), digs executed very close to two known springs and much deeper than their emergence point... (the Persée scan cuts off here; the implication of the argument is that these too cannot count as successes).

**Corpus significance:** the earliest French-language null in the verification rail with an engineering-scale base (n ≈ 200 projects). Costerisant's confound analysis is the direct ancestor of the INRS grid design.

## 3. Jarricot 1959 (via Lefebvre 2005): The Medical-Radiesthesia Refutation

Thierry Lefebvre, "Une réfutation de la radiesthésie médicale [Q255, Pharmacie et radiesthésie]," *Revue d'histoire de la pharmacie*, 93e année, n° 348, 2005, pp. 676–677 (Persée). A short historiographical note on Dr. Jean Jarricot's 1959 book *La Radiesthésie* (Grasset, coll. "Bilan du mystère").

From Lefebvre's note (FR original, translated):

> "Cet ancien chef de laboratoire de la Faculté de médecine de Lyon, qui avait pris l'habitude de soumettre les pendulisants à l'expertise de la méthode graphique, récusa en bloc les prétentions médicales de la radiesthésie dans un chapitre intitulé 'Théories et aspects de la radiesthésie médicale'."

Jarricot — a former laboratory head at the Lyon Faculty of Medicine — subjected pendulum practitioners to "the expertise of the graphical method" (statistical analysis of their diagnostic records) and rejected the medical claims of radiesthesia wholesale. The named targets: frère Benoît Padey (several "savoureux" passages), abbé Mermet, père Bourdioux; the pharmacist Gabriel Lesourd, "radiesthésiste d'une rare distinction," was amiably criticized — his method being, per Jarricot, under "the influence of autosuggestion."

**Context row (from the scout's fire-116 summary and the 09-16 dossier chain):** Jarricot's 129-trial analysis found a 55.2% mean error rate — below chance. Note: the full 129-trial/55.2% figures come from the scout's item summary; the Persée page itself is Lefebvre's 2-page note on the 1959 book and does not carry the trial tables. Flagged accordingly — the primary is Jarricot 1959 (Grasset), out of print, not fetchable.

**Corpus significance:** the French tradition's own internal refutation, run by a laboratory scientist inside the medical establishment, a generation after Mermet's fame — and the direct ancestor of the "autosuggestion/ideomotor" gloss the calibrated consumer guides use today.

## 4. Pedro & Schang 1969: The Respiratory-Phase Claim (Académie Vétérinaire de France)

A. Pedro & J. Schang, "La radiesthésie scientifiquement démontrée comme phénomène physique physiologique," *Bulletin de l'Académie Vétérinaire de France*, t. 122, n° 5, 1969, pp. 219–221 (DOI 10.4267/2042/66881, Persée).

The counter-pole of the ledger: a positive claim, presented at Buenos Aires academies (Académie de Médecine 12 Sept 1967; Académie d'Agronomie et Vétérinaire 7 Nov 1967, and a joint public session 27 Aug 1968). From the French original:

> "La méthode consiste à opposer chez le sujet qui porte la « fourcelle » dans ses mains, son inspiration ou son expiration respiratoire prolongée pour détecter les radiations ou énergies que dégagent ces éléments. Nous avons classé ces champs ou radiations de nombreux éléments de la Nature, en positifs, négatifs et indifférents."

The method: the dowser holding the *fourcelle* (forked rod) opposes prolonged inspiration or expiration to detect the "radiations" of the target. The classification: negative bodies/fields attract the rod during (positive) inspiration; positive bodies — gold, copper, lime, plaster, petroleum and all its distillation products — are detected during expiration. The claimed transmission medium: a conductor (copper wire, iron wire), "which gives them the character of radiations or energy conducted in a way similar to electric current."

The authors' conclusion, verbatim: radiesthesia "apparaît maintenant comme un fait scientifique : physique par les radiations qu'on détecte et physiologique parce qu'il est détecté en inspiration ou expiration" — now appears as a scientific fact: physical by the radiations detected, physiological because it is detected on inspiration or expiration.

**Corpus significance:** the sharpest available example of the tradition's positive-claim genre: a named physiological mechanism (respiratory phase), a stated classification system, a conducting medium — and zero controlled test anywhere in the note. "Démontrée" in the title means demonstrated-by-demonstration (the Buenos Aires sessions), not demonstrated-by-experiment. This is precisely the untestable-frame vs testable-residue split the verification-turn synthesis draws: the respiratory-phase claim is in principle testable (blind the operator's breathing cue, see if classification survives) and never was. Pedro & Schang 1969 is the row the INRS design exists to answer: same French-language tradition, same instrument family, sixty years of distance between "demonstrated" and "tested."

## 5. The Lecher-Antenna Training Economy, 2026 (Ondes et Habitat, Niort)

Dominique Emery, association "Ondes et Habitat" — Antenne Lecher training, Niort, Sunday 26 April 2026, Hôtel Kyriad (ondesethabitat.fr, published 12 March 2026). One day, 10h–17h, from €100, certificate included, plus "a list of the frequencies and reference points used in detection in applied geobiology with a Lecher antenna."

Rows relevant to the corpus's Lecher bench note (2026-09-15):

- The training's own honesty layer: "non une antenne de Lecher ne fonctionne pas toute seule" — no, a Lecher antenna does not work by itself. "Prise en main particulière" — particular handling required.
- The core skill taught is the **point de rupture d'équilibre** (point of balance rupture), "the starting point of all detection" — the operator must find their own antenna's instability point first. This matches the bench note's operator-dependent layer: the instrument's response is set by the operator's handling state, not by an external calibration.
- The marketing claim: the Lecher is "the most precise and most pointed tool in radiesthesia, but also the most complicated to use alone," and — the key row — "the tool least subject to mental suggestion in radiesthesia. That is its greatest advantage. Allows detaching from the result."
- The same operator offers pendulum, Egyptian pendulum, "medical radiesthesia," biomagnetism, sound-therapy, and sacred-site excursion trainings — a full curriculum economy around the same instrument family.

**Corpus significance:** the tradition itself now teaches that the antenna needs an operator-tuned instability point — which is exactly the confound the bench note's three-arm protocol (instrument+operator, replica control, slider-blind tuning) isolates. The "least subject to suggestion" claim is testable and untested; the training economy is the institutional layer that keeps the claim alive.

## 6. The Saint-Genois Orchard Survey, 2026: Hartmann/Curry Nodes on Suffering Trees (with the Skeptic Caveat Embedded)

Les Croqueurs de Pommes (Saint-Genis-les-Ollières, Rhône) — "Recherche des réseaux géobiologiques au verger Saint-Genois," 15 July 2026 (vergersaintgenois.com). An amateur-orchard association invites a local practitioner (Philippe Delorme, forest-garden designer and dowser) to test whether suffering trees sit on Hartmann/Curry network nodes.

Method as documented: compass north; walk the S-N axis slowly until the rod dips — Hartmann node; mark; repeat on E-W perpendicular; repeat on the square's other sides; for Curry, walk the diagonals. Instruments: plastic Biorad rods (système Landspurg, sold by Faïenceries de la Doller), copper rods, pendulum.

Findings as recorded by the association:

- Pear "Triomphe de Vienne" (planted 2021, ~50 cm) — on a Curry node; to be moved in autumn
- Plum "Stanley" (2020, never developed) — on a Hartmann **and** Curry node; too big to move
- Apple "Golden Delicious" (2019, in full form) — on a Hartmann E-W **line** but offset ~1 m from the N-S line, on **no node**
- Fig "Ronde de Bordeaux" (2018, barely 20–30 cm after 8 years, next to a thriving fig of the same planting) — on a Hartmann **and** Curry node; to be moved 1 m NW in autumn
- Nursery rows: a Hartmann line passes between the rows, no node; seed tray on a line edge, no node
- Follow-up booked for 30 September: water-vein search with the orchard plan

The practice-ritual layer, verbatim from the page: "Établir une convention avec le médium" — set a convention with the instrument (which way will the rod move? which way will the pendulum turn?); "L'intention avant toute chose. Y croire" — intention before all. Believe. "Ne pas être dans son mental" — don't mentalize. Anecdotes recorded with equal weight: anthills "always" on Hartmann nodes; cats seek Hartmann nodes, dogs never do; a tea-bag pendulum demo in a cafeteria; car keys used to find Hartmann networks ("no need for a pendulum or rods").

**The caveat, in the association's own words:** "selon la communauté scientifique ce treillis de lignes est imaginaire et n'a aucune réalité quantifiable ni mesurable" — according to the scientific community this lattice of lines is imaginary and has no quantifiable or measurable reality. The page carries the skeptic caveat in its own footer, next to the node coordinates of specific trees.

**Corpus significance:** the natural experiment is already planted — the Golden Delicious (thriving, line but no node) vs the Ronde de Bordeaux (dying, double node) vs the moved trees (autumn 2026 transplant, outcome observable in 2027). If the tradition's claim is operational, the moved trees should recover relative to their node-bound neighbors; if the null holds, soil/water/disease variables will do the work. The association has, without knowing it, published the protocol of a before-after field trial with named cultivars, dates, and a control tree. The 30 September water-vein session extends it.

## 7. The Ledger, Read Across

Five documents, ninety years, one language-community:

| Year | Document | Verdict | Base |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1937 | Costérisant (Rev. sci. nat. Auvergne) | null — "effectivement nul" | ~200 water-supply projects |
| 1959 | Jarricot (Grasset) | null — medical claims "récusées en bloc" | 129 trials, graphical method |
| 1969 | Pedro & Schang (Acad. Vét. France) | positive claim — "fait scientifique" | demonstrations, no controlled test |
| 2026 | INRS (EGU26-3985) | field experiment complete, numeric verdict pending | 54 participants, 25-cell grid, full cross-tab |
| 2026 | Saint-Genois orchard survey | practice embedded with its own skeptic caveat | 5 named trees, transplant trial underway |

The pattern: every generation of the French-language community has produced both a claim and a test, and the tests have moved from engineering-scale retrospective (1937) through clinical-statistical refutation (1959) to co-designed prospective experiment (2026). The INRS study is the first of these built to detect a sub-effect rather than re-confirm a global null — the exact design lesson Munich 1986–88 did not learn. And the Saint-Genois page shows the practice layer now carrying its own disclaimer: the tradition's amateur wing quotes the scientific community's verdict on its own field report, then books the next session anyway. That is the honesty layer the verification-turn synthesis documented, now visible at village-orchard scale.

**What to watch:** EGU26 Vienna, 3–8 May 2026 — the numeric tables behind EGU26-3985. And autumn 2027 at Saint-Genois: the moved trees.
