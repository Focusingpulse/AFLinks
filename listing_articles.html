The Soul Calendar

Northern Hemisphere

Week 26

Sun: Nature,
Moon: Your motherly essence,
Mars: I carry it in my will's being;
Mercury: And the mighty fire of my will,
Jupiter: It steels my spirit's drives,
Venus: That they give birth to a self-awareness,
Saturn: So I may carry my Self within myself.

—Tr. Steiner Online Library

Southern Hemisphere

Week 52

Sun: When from soul-depths
Moon: Spirit turns toward World-existence
Mars: And beauty flows from space's wide expanses,
Mercury: Then draw from distant Heavens
Jupiter: Force of Life into human bodies
Venus: And mightily unite
Saturn: Spirit's Being with human existence.

—Tr. Steiner Online Library
