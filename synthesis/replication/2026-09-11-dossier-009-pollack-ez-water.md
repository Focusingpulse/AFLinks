## The Claim

Water adjacent to hydrophilic surfaces forms an exclusion zone (EZ) — a particle-free region extending hundreds of micrometers with distinct physical properties: negative charge, higher viscosity, hexagonal sheet structure, and growth driven by infrared radiation. This "fourth phase" of water may explain biological water structuring and cellular function.

**Claimant:** Gerald Pollack, University of Washington
**Verification status:** Multiple independent replications; structural interpretation debated

## The Protocol

### Materials

1. **Hydrophilic surface**: Nafion sheet, hydrophilic gel, or treated glass
   - Nafion recommended (standardized material)
   - Size: 2-5 cm²

2. **Particle suspension**: Polystyrene microspheres (0.5-2 μm diameter)
   - Concentration: ~0.01% by volume

3. **Water**: Deionized, dechlorinated

4. **Light source**: IR lamp or sunlight (optional for growth test)
   - IR wavelength: 3000-10000 nm optimal

5. **Microscope** (for visual measurement)
   - Standard optical microscope, 100-400× magnification

6. **Ruler or eyepiece graticule** (for measurement)

7. **Voltmeter** (optional, for charge measurement)

### Setup

1. Place hydrophilic surface in water reservoir
2. Add particle suspension
3. Allow particles to settle (10-15 minutes)
4. Observe particle-free zone forming near surface

### Procedure

**Visual Measurement:**
1. Position microscope to view edge of hydrophilic surface
2. Measure distance from surface to particle front
3. Record EZ width at multiple points
4. Average 5-10 measurements

**IR Growth Test:**
1. Record baseline EZ width
2. Illuminate with IR lamp (or expose to sunlight)
3. Wait 10-20 minutes
4. Measure new EZ width
5. Calculate growth rate: μm/min

**Charge Test (optional):**
1. Insert microelectrode into EZ region
2. Measure voltage relative to bulk water
3. Expected: negative potential (EZ negative, bulk positive)

---

## Pass/Fail Criteria

**PASS:**
- EZ width ≥ 100 μm visible
- EZ grows under IR illumination (≥ 20% increase)
- Charge separation detected (if tested)

**FAIL:**
- No particle-free zone forms
- EZ width < 50 μm (within measurement error)
- No response to IR illumination

**INCONCLUSIVE:**
- EZ visible but < 100 μm
- Single measurement only
- Contaminated water or surface
