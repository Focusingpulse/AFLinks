## The claim (as asserted by the source)

The hesychast tradition describes a **warmth in the region of the heart** arising during the Jesus Prayer with the psychosomatic method. The method has three parts (Ware):

1. **Posture** — seated on a low stool, head and shoulders bowed, eyes fixed on the place of the heart. (An extreme variant, head between the knees, is also described and is **excluded from this card** — see Safety.)
2. **Slowed, coordinated breathing** — the first half of the Prayer on the in-breath, the second on the out-breath. The older sources describe **breath retention** with the mouth closed; modern Orthodox practice uses slowed coordination.
3. **Inward attention** — the mind (*nous*) is led down with the breath and "searches" for the place of the heart.

### ⚠ The tradition's own account is deflationary — and that is what makes this testable

This is the unusual part, and the reason the card is worth running. **The hesychast tradition does not claim the warmth is spiritual. Its most careful voices attribute it to physical exertion and forbid pursuing it.**

St Ignatius Brianchaninov:

> *"Every member of the human body that is subjected to friction gets heated. The same thing happens in the case of the heart under long and constant strain. The warmth which appears as a result of vigorous, material exertion is also material. It is a warmth of the flesh and blood in the realm of our fallen nature."*

And the explicit prohibition: *"whoever strives to maintain and increase this warmth for the sake of its sweetness alone, will develop in himself a kind of spiritual hedonism."*

**So the tradition makes a specific, falsifiable, deflationary prediction:** the warmth is produced by the **somatic** components — the bowed posture, the slowed and held breath, the sustained strain — and **not** by the prayer.

**And its own practitioners disagree with that.** The tradition is transmitted on the assumption that the *prayer* is the operative element and the technique is an accessory — Gregory of Sinai is explicit that the physical method is "no more than an optional accessory." So:

| Voice | Prediction |
|---|---|
| **St Ignatius / the ascetic caution** | the warmth is somatic; the prayer adds nothing measurable |
| **The transmitted practice** | the prayer is the operative element; the technique is an accessory |

**This card adjudicates between the tradition's own two voices.** That is a much better test than "is the warmth real."

### Honest status of the claim

**No dedicated physiological study of hesychast prayer measures temperature.** That is a genuine, checkable gap: HRV studies of prayer types exist (*Zygon* 2009, five prayer types); one thermography study found nasal temperature *falling* during the Lord's Prayer and facial temperature *rising* during personal prayer; but **nobody has put a thermometer on a hesychast.**

**Under-promise, stated plainly:** this card cannot show that the warmth is or is not spiritual — that is not a measurable claim. It can show **which component produces a thermal change, and where.** A null on the sternum is the expected result, and it is a complete and valuable finding.

## Why it matters

- **It is the first card in the queue whose discriminator is *which component* of a traditional practice does the work.** Every prior card asks whether an effect exists. This one asks whether the *traditional* part of the traditional practice contributes anything — which is the question the whole convergence lane landed on.
- **The tradition supplies its own deflationary hypothesis**, which means a null result is not a refutation of the tradition; it is a confirmation of the tradition's own most careful voice. **There is no way to lose this test, which is unusual and makes it worth running.**
- **It has a validated instrument design available off the shelf.** Green's Menninger protocol used the **finger-vs-forehead differential** as an autonomic-relaxation index (hands warm, forehead cools as sympathetic tone drops). That is a published, replicated index — so the autonomic control arm of this card is not improvised.
- **It is a different object from Dossier 045.** 045 tests the *palm*, direction-specific, at will. This tests the *sternum*, component-specific, during practice. They share an instrument and nothing else.

## Replicability: `home`

- Build cost: **~$25** (one non-contact IR thermometer with a skin mode and 0.1 °C resolution; matte tape; a fixed standoff; a low stool).
- Safety: **low, with two named exclusions** — see Safety below.
- Accessibility: an afternoon, four arms, three sessions.

## Safety — read before running

**Two components of the historical method carry real physiological risk and are modified here.**

1. **Breath retention is EXCLUDED.** The older sources describe retaining the breath with the mouth closed. That is a Valsalva-like manoeuvre which raises intrathoracic pressure and swings blood pressure and heart rate. **This card uses slowed, coordinated breathing only — no retention.** This is also the *modern* Orthodox practice, so the deviation is toward the tradition's own current form, not away from it.
2. **The "head between the knees" posture is EXCLUDED.** It is a real risk for anyone with neck or back problems and the tradition itself treats it as the extreme variant. **Use the low stool with a gently bowed head only.**

**Also:** no one with cardiovascular conditions, uncontrolled hypertension, glaucoma, or a history of fainting should run the breathing arms. Slowed breathing is mild for most adults but it is not nothing. **If anyone feels lightheaded, the arm stops.** No treatment claims are made by this card, and none should be read into a result.

## Apparatus (Bill of Materials)

- **Non-contact IR thermometer, skin/body mode, 0.1 °C resolution** (~$20–35). Same class as Dossier 045. **Accuracy caveat identical:** a cheap gun may be ±0.2–0.5 °C absolute — irrelevant, because every measurement is a **within-session delta from the same instrument at the same distance.**
- **A fixed standoff** — a phone stand, box, or table mark holding the gun at **the same distance and angle** for every reading. Hand-held readings at varying distance are the largest single noise source.
- **Matte tape targets** — a small square of matte tape on each measurement site gives a consistent emissivity target and a repeatable aim point.
- **Three measurement sites, marked:** **sternum** (the claim site — mid-chest, over the cardiac region), **finger** (index or middle fingertip, palmar side), **forehead** (mid-forehead, above the brow).
- **A low stool** (~20–25 cm) and a **palm/forearm rest**.
- **A sealed instruction set** — four cards (A / B / C / D) in envelopes, shuffled by the referee.
- **A timer**, a **recording sheet**, and a **phone camera**.

## Protocol (four-arm, within-subject, component dissociation)

1. **Validate the instrument first (positive control).** Sit at rest 5 minutes. Measure the finger. **Rub the hands together briskly for 30 seconds and measure again.** The finger must rise **≥ 0.5 °C**. If it does not, the setup or instrument cannot resolve the effect — **VOID the session and fix the setup.** (Same validity gate as Dossier 045.)
2. **Pre-register.** Write the thresholds, the scoring rule, the arm order rule, and who is blind to what, on the sheet **before** the first session.
3. **Roles.** A **practitioner**, a **measurer** (reads and records, **blind to the arm**), and a **referee** (holds the key, shuffles envelopes, fills the arm column after the session). The practitioner must **not** be the measurer.
4. **Baseline.** Practitioner seated normally, hands on the rest, **normal breathing**. Measure sternum, finger, forehead every 30 s for **5 minutes**. The mean of the last 3 readings is the baseline for each site.
5. **Arms** (10 minutes each, randomized order, 5 minutes seated rest between):

| Arm | Posture | Breathing | Prayer | Tests |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Full method** | low stool, head gently bowed | slowed, coordinated | Jesus Prayer recited inwardly | the transmitted practice |
| **B — Somatic only** | low stool, head gently bowed | slowed, coordinated | **none** — count breaths silently | the components St Ignatius credits |
| **C — Prayer only** | ordinary chair, upright | normal | Jesus Prayer recited inwardly | the component the tradition credits |
| **D — Rest** | ordinary chair, upright | normal | none | drift control |

6. **Measure** sternum, finger, forehead every 30 s throughout each arm, without the measurer knowing which arm is running.
7. **Repeat** the full four-arm set on **three separate days**.

## Pass / fail (pre-registered)

Score each site as the change from baseline, per arm. Two discriminators, both pre-registered:

**Discriminator 1 — component specificity (the primary question).**
- **SOMATIC:** **A ≈ B**, and **C ≈ D**. The prayer adds nothing; the warmth follows posture and breath. *This confirms St Ignatius and refutes the transmitted assumption.*
- **COGNITIVE:** **A ≈ C**, and **B ≈ D**. The prayer does the work; the technique is the accessory. *This confirms the transmitted practice.*
- **BOTH:** **A > B > D** and **A > C > D**. Both components contribute. *The most interesting outcome, and the one neither voice predicts.*
- **NEITHER:** all arms ≈ D. No effect from either component.

**Discriminator 2 — site specificity.**
- **SITE-SPECIFIC:** the sternum moves more than the finger. Attention to a region changes that region.
- **GENERAL AUTONOMIC:** sternum and finger move together, forehead cools. This is ordinary relaxation, and the finger/forehead differential is the validated index for it (Green 1970).

**Report the pattern, not a verdict.** ⚠ **Note the asymmetry:** because the tradition's own careful voice predicts SOMATIC, a SOMATIC result is a *confirmation*, not a failure. **State this in the write-up so the result cannot be read as a debunk.**

**VOID:** the rub-hands control did not register ≥ 0.5 °C; OR ambient moved more than 1.5 °C during a session; OR the practitioner left the posture mid-arm; OR the measurer knew the arm.

## Confounds named in advance (each is an explicit ARTIFACT test)

- **Ambient drift** — a warming room warms every site. Record ambient each session; VOID if it moves >1.5 °C; run arms at the same time of day.
- **Clothing and posture change** — the bowed posture alters airflow over the chest and may change sternum skin temperature mechanically, with no autonomic involvement. **This is a real and unremovable confound for the sternum site**, and it is why the finger/forehead differential is the control: if only the sternum moves and the finger does not, suspect airflow, not physiology.
- **Sustained attention alone** — attending to a body region may change its temperature regardless of prayer. **Arm C does not fully control this**, because Arm C has attention *and* prayer. A fifth arm (attention to the chest, no prayer, no posture) would separate them; it is optional and noted as a limitation.
- **Expectation** — the practitioner knows their own arm; unavoidable, because volition is the claim. The blinding is on the **measurer and scorer**. Say this plainly rather than pretending otherwise.
- **Instrument resolution** — a ±0.5 °C gun cannot resolve a 0.3 °C effect. Mitigation: the positive control and thresholds set above the instrument's error.
- **Practice history** — a person with years of hesychast, qigong, or biofeedback training is not a naive subject. Record each participant's history; a difference between trained and untrained is itself informative.
- **Devotional content** — for a believing practitioner, the Jesus Prayer carries meaning that a breath count does not. That is not a confound to be removed; **it is part of the claim.** Record the participant's relationship to the practice.

## What a result would mean

- **SOMATIC** would be the queue's first confirmation of a tradition's **own deflationary account of itself** — a tradition that says "the thing people chase is a byproduct" and is right. That is a rare and clean result, and it generalises: it is the same finding Kozhevnikov reached for tummo in 2013.
- **COGNITIVE** would be the first evidence that the *prayer* — the content, not the mechanics — moves a physiological variable, which is a much stronger claim than anything in the thermal literature.
- **BOTH** would be the most useful outcome, because it would mean the traditional practice is genuinely assembled from parts that each do something, and the interesting question becomes *how they combine*.
- **NEITHER** is the honest null: the warmth is a devotional experience with no measurable thermal correlate at skin level. Also a real result.
