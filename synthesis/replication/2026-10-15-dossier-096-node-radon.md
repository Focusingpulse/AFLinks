## The claim under test

The source is a Greek-language synthesis of the Hartmann-grid tradition, published by a small environmental/air-quality measurement service in Heraklion, Crete. It states the grid's geometry, the node/neutral-zone distinction, and — the part this card tests — a claim about **radon**:

> *"Other observations proved that at the geopathogenic nodes there is an increased concentration of the radon that is so dangerous to health, as well as of gamma (Γ) radiations."*

and, immediately after, the mechanism:

> *"The movement of radon, and of other dangerous volatile gases that come from the subsoil, underground waters and geopathogenic nodes, is facilitated by the existence of tectonic faults and smaller ground fissures, which also creates geopathogenic nodes, just like underground waters."*

The claim is therefore **not** that a field harms you. It is that a dowsed grid crossing marks a place where a **gas physically accumulates**. That is a claim about matter, and matter can be counted.

## The source's own two named regions — which supply the card's control

The source does not leave the contrast to the experimenter. It names both regions itself:

> *"At the points where the lines cross — lines named in honour of the man who studied them as no one else did, the HARTMANN LINES — nodes are created which are extremely dangerous to human health."*

> *"…a serious reduction of the distances and a deformation of the Hartmann lines is observed, with the result that the distances of the neutral zones are also reduced — that is, of the points where one can place one's bed or desk."*

> *"There is, moreover, a greater probability that we will fall ill if we place our bed on a geopathogenic node rather than our desk…"*

So the source asserts two regions inside the same room, about a metre apart: the **node** (dangerous) and the **neutral zone** (safe — where it tells you to sleep). The card's independent variable is therefore **the source's own distinction**, not one the experimenter invented. This is the cleanest control the queue has ever been handed by a source.

## The source's own geometric prediction — a second, sharper control

The source also carries a claim about the **spatial form** of the thing it is describing, attributed to the engineer Cody:

> *"The radiations of the Earth 'rise' vertically to the level of the ground, without lateral propagation."*

If that is true, a node is a **narrow vertical column**: a detector half a metre to the side should read what a detector a metre to the side reads. If instead the reading falls off smoothly with distance from the mark, the "node" is behaving like a room-wide gradient — which is what ventilation and wall proximity produce. The card therefore adds a **lateral-offset arm** (0 m, 0.5 m, 1.0 m from the mark, in two directions) at no extra cost, because the source supplied the prediction.

## The source's own variability admissions — why the design is paired and repeated

The source states plainly that the quantity it is describing moves with the weather and the hour, and the archive's own Reich/Harrer record states the same about radon specifically. The source:

> *"The slightest change in the atmosphere is accompanied by a significant change in the intensity of the terrestrial radiation that comes from the subsoil."*

> *"The intensity of the radiation changes by the same logic according to temperature: it reaches its maximum point in summer, is minimised in the cold and is approximately zeroed in frost. Moreover, the radiations are stronger on sunny days and at sunrise. A prolonged period of rain causes an appreciable fall in their values."*

> *"On a 24-hour basis we have a maximum at 5 in the morning and a minimum at 10 in the morning."*

And the archive, on radon itself:

> *"The background activity of a place at a particular time depends on the outgassing of radon from the soil, on the radioactivity of the subsoil, and on the incoming cosmic radiation. The radon component, in turn, depends on air movement and on mixing with air from higher atmospheric layers, and thus fluctuates…"*

**Consequence for the design:** a sequential measurement (node on Monday, neutral zone on Tuesday) would measure the weather, not the node. The card therefore requires **simultaneous paired deployment** in the same room over the same window, repeated on three separate deployments.

## The source's own hard condition — carried into the card

The source says the grid is **distorted above the 6th–7th floor of a block of flats**, *"because of the combination of cosmic radiations and the metal reinforcement of the concrete"*, with the neutral zones shrinking accordingly. That is a hard condition on where the test may be run, and it happens to agree with the physics of the thing being measured: radon is a soil gas and enters at or below ground level. **The card therefore requires a ground-floor or basement room, and says why.**

## The source's own quantification (recorded as the source's, not as evidence)

> *"Hartmann even quantified his research, coming to believe that 60% of cases of illness are due to the place and space of residence."*

> *"Ten-year studies carried out in the former West Germany on residents of blocks of flats proved that those who lived above the level of the 15th floor fell ill 57% more often than the tenants of apartments below the 15th floor."*

Neither figure is sourced in the text, neither is reproducible from it, and neither is used anywhere in this card's design. They are recorded because the card's honesty depends on showing what the source claims alongside what it can support.

## The tradition's own radon method — and why this card replaces it

The archive's Brazilian radiesthesia manual describes how the tradition actually measures radon:

> *"To measure radioactivity in the form of radon or polonium gas, radiesthetic charts are used with the units cited above, which were tested in places that emit these anomalies, for comparative purposes. The charts must be used by radiesthesist geobiologists who know the effects of radioactivity directly from the places where there is emission."*

The tradition's only quantitative near/far figures are also from that manual, and they are worth reading carefully:

> *"Dr. Wüst (Bueno, 95) carried out numerous radiation measurements, which he called gamma (radon gas), coming from the earth. He measured in a region where three cases of cancer had developed, with results of 11.5 to 13.0 µR/h, whereas on moving away from the place it decreased to 8-8.15 µR/h…"*

Two things follow, and both are stated in the card. **(1)** The tradition has never measured radon with an instrument; it has dowsed it with a chart. The card replaces the chart with a detector, which is the whole point. **(2)** The one figure the tradition reports is a **gamma dose rate** (µR/h), not a radon concentration (Bq/m³) — the source itself writes *"gamma (radon gas)"*, conflating two different quantities. A family running this card will produce the quantity the tradition has never produced.

## The rival explanation — and why the card expects it

The mainstream position is not that radon in dwellings is imaginary. It is that radon is **real, regulated, and geological**:

> *"Jeżeli więc coś faktycznie szkodzi mieszkańcom domów, bardziej prawdopodobne wyjaśnienia to znane czynniki środowiskowe — np. zwiększona wilgotność podłoża skutkująca rozwojem pleśni w domu, emisja radonu z podłoża skalnego czy po prostu złe warunki mieszkaniowe."*
> (proton.edu.pl, 2025-12-14: if something really harms the residents of houses, the more probable explanations are known environmental factors — e.g. increased substrate humidity leading to mould growth in the house, **radon emission from the rock substrate**, or simply poor housing conditions.)

Radon enters buildings from soil gas through cracks, sumps, service penetrations and permeable foundations, and its indoor concentration is driven by the subsoil's uranium/thorium content, the building's pressure differential, ventilation and weather. **None of those are grid crossings.** The source's own mechanism sentence — radon movement *"facilitated by the existence of tectonic faults and smaller ground fissures"* — is a real transport mechanism; the card's question is whether a **dowser's mark** locates one. That is a genuinely two-sided test: a positive would mean dowsing screens for a real hazard, and a negative would close the question with a number the family can act on.

## Protocol (pre-registered)

**Room.** A **ground-floor or basement** room of the family's own dwelling. Not an upper floor — the source's own constraint, and radon's own physics.

**Marking (must happen BEFORE any radon data exists).** One person dowses the room alone with rods or a pendulum and marks, on the floor, (a) every **grid crossing (node)** they find, and (b) every **cell centre (neutral zone)** — the point equidistant from the surrounding crossings, which the source calls the place to put your bed or desk. Marks are numbered and photographed. **No radon number may be seen by the marker before the marks are fixed, and the marks may not be moved afterwards.** If the marks move between deployments, the run is INCONCLUSIVE.

**Instrument check (must pass, or the run is VOID — not null).** Measure the room's radon level first. **If the room's radon is below the instrument's stated detection floor, or below the level at which the instrument's own repeat-measurement spread is smaller than the effect being sought, the test is UNATTEMPTED, not negative** — a difference cannot be measured that the instrument cannot resolve. Report the room's level and stop.

**Design.** **Simultaneous paired deployment.** At each deployment, place one detector on a marked **node** and one on a marked **neutral zone** at the same time, at the same height (the source says the radiation rises vertically, so height is a variable, not a detail), at the same distance from walls and floor, for the same exposure window. Where the family has more than two detectors, deploy node/neutral pairs at two or three marked locations at once.

**Three deployments**, on three separate weeks, spanning at least one weather change. Record for each: the date, the deployment duration, the room's ventilation state (windows/doors open or shut, and for how long), the outdoor temperature and rainfall, and the barometric trend.

**Lateral-offset arm (free, and the source predicted it).** At one node, place detectors at the mark and at **0.5 m** and **1.0 m** lateral offset in two directions, simultaneously. The source's *"without lateral propagation"* claim predicts a **sharp** spatial peak; a smooth gradient implicates ventilation and wall proximity instead.

**Continuous arm (optional, if a monitor is available).** A 24-hour trace at one node and one neutral zone simultaneously. The source predicts a maximum at 05:00 and a minimum at 10:00; the ordinary radon diurnal curve is different (it typically peaks in the early morning and falls through the day as mixing increases). A trace that matches the ordinary curve is informative about the instrument and the room, not about the node.

**Primary endpoint.** **Radon concentration (Bq/m³, or pCi/L) at dowsed nodes versus at the source's own neutral zones**, measured simultaneously in the same room, across three deployments.

**Secondary endpoints.** The lateral-offset profile at one node; the paired node/neutral ratio per deployment; the room's absolute level against the national action level.

## Pass/fail

- **PASS (the claim survives):** the node mean is **≥ 1.5×** the neutral-zone mean in **≥ 2 of 3** deployments, with the paired difference exceeding the instrument's own repeat-measurement spread, and the node/neutral pairs placed within 1.5 m of each other in the same room. A positive would make a dowser's mark a candidate screening tool for a real, regulated hazard.
- **PASS (the rival is sufficient — the expected, ordinary result):** node and neutral-zone means agree within the instrument's spread in all three deployments. **A dowser's grid crossing does not mark a radon pathway**, and the room's radon is what the geology and the building produce (Skeptic's Star). This is the result the card expects and it is a complete result.
- **FAIL:** the pairs differ but **not** in the pre-registered direction — the neutral zones read **higher** than the nodes.
- **INCONCLUSIVE (and this is not a null):** the room's radon is **below the instrument's detection floor** or below the level at which the instrument can resolve the effect sought — the test was **unattempted**; fewer than 3 deployments; the node marks moved between deployments; the exposure windows differed between the paired detectors; the instrument's calibration is unknown or expired.
- **ARTIFACT:** the difference tracks the deployment date, the weather, the ventilation state, the barometric trend, or the detector's height/distance from the wall rather than the mark; the node marks were placed or adjusted after any radon number was seen; the paired detectors had different exposure windows.
