## The claim (as asserted by the source)

Organic matter placed inside a **pyramid enclosure aligned to the cardinal points**, at **1/3 of the pyramid's height**, does not decompose — it **desiccates**. The source attributes this to *"negative green"* (V⁻), a "powerful desiccating wave" concentrated at the King's Chamber position in the Great Pyramid, which Chaumery and de Bélizal inferred from a mummified cat found in the King's Chamber. Antoine Bovis's Paris experiments are the earlier, primary instance of the same claim.

The source's own modern restatement, quoted:

> "Modern tests, inspired by their work, placed pieces of meat under a pyramid model aligned with the cardinal points. Result: the meat located at 1/3 of the height (the position of the King's Chamber) became mummified, while the control sample rotted."

And the source's own disclaimer, quoted, because it belongs on the card and not in a footnote:

> "⚠️ Important: These observations are not scientifically validated. No instrument can measure form waves. The effects remain within the domain of geobiology and energetic belief."

**Provenance note, stated up front.** The "modern tests" sentence carries **no citation** — no author, no date, no journal, no protocol. It is an assertion inside a secondary source. The Bovis experiments are documented as a lineage but not as a protocol. **So this card does not inherit a protocol from the source; it supplies one.** That is the honest position, and it is also the reason the card is worth running: nobody has published the design, so the design is the contribution.

**Honest status of the claim.** Form waves are classified as pseudoscience; the mechanism ("negative green") is not measurable by any instrument and is therefore **not falsifiable as stated**. The *effect* — desiccation vs. decomposition — **is** falsifiable, cheaply, with a kitchen scale. The card tests the effect and says nothing about the mechanism.

**The confound, stated plainly, because it is the whole design.** A pyramid is a partially enclosed volume. **Any** enclosure changes airflow, humidity, and the rate at which a sample dries. A test with only "pyramid vs. open air" cannot distinguish *"this geometry does something"* from *"being under a cardboard roof does something."* That is why the design carries a **sham box** of the same footprint and flat top. Without it, a positive result means nothing.

**Why it is worth testing anyway, stated plainly.** Three reasons:

1. **The endpoint is categorical and countable.** Rot vs. no rot is not a subtle judgement — it is visible, and it is photographable. Mass is a number on a kitchen scale. Most archive claims end in a perception; this one ends in a photograph and a set of grams.
2. **It is the food-preservation arm of the same thesis card 027 probes.** Card 027 (Drbal) tests whether a built enclosure changes the state of *metal*. This tests whether it changes the state of *organic matter*. Same thesis, different material, different lineage (Bovis/Chaumery, Paris 1930s → Drbal, Prague 1949), different endpoint. Neither subsumes the other.
3. **The practical payoff is a real self-reliance skill.** If a shaped, ventilated enclosure dries food faster or cleaner than open air, a family has a food-storage structure. If it does not, the family has learned what a control is and has retired a 90-year-old claim honestly.

**Under-promise, stated plainly.** A household cannot resolve a 5% difference in drying rate; it **can** resolve "one sample molded and the other did not." Arm 2 is designed to give a first reading in **2–3 weeks**. Arm 1 (grapes → raisins) needs 6–8 weeks and will be noisier. Report both arms separately.

## Why it matters

This is the **other half of the pyramid tradition the archive already holds**. Card 027 brought in the Drbal patent — the metallurgical arm. This brings in the Bovis/Chaumery arm — the organic-matter arm, the one the food-preservation folklore actually rests on. Together the two cards make the archive's pyramid holdings testable end to end: metal and matter.

It is also the first card in the queue whose **primary confound is enclosure itself**, and the first to carry a sham *structure* (not just a sham treatment). A family that runs it learns the difference between "the treatment did something" and "the container did something" — which is the single most common error in home experimentation.

And it is real literacy at almost zero cost: pre-registration, a coded key, a sham arm, a daily log, and a categorical endpoint that a child can read.

## Replicability: `home`

- Build cost: **~$5–15** (cardboard, tape, a compass, a kitchen scale if not already owned, and the fruit or meat).
- Safety: **the only hazard is spoiled meat.** Run Arm 2 outdoors or in a sealed container; dispose of the rotting control sample in a bag, not a bin, and do not open it indoors. No mains power, no chemicals, no heat.
- Accessibility: the pyramid is a 30-minute build. Arm 2 pays off in 2–3 weeks — fast enough that the quest gets run.

## Apparatus (Bill of Materials)

- **Cardboard**, stiff (corrugated box stock), plus scissors, tape, a ruler.
- **A compass** (phone compass is fine) for cardinal alignment.
- **Three identical samples**, same mass within ±5%: three apple slices of the same thickness, or three small pieces of the same meat, or three bunches of grapes of equal mass. Weigh and match them **before** starting.
- **Three identical dielectric supports** — a small block of wood or cork, cut so the sample sits at **1/3 of the pyramid's height**. The open and sham arms get an *identical* support at the *same height*.
- **A kitchen scale**, 1 g resolution.
- **A thermometer/hygrometer** (optional but strongly recommended, ~$10–15) — to prove all three arms saw the same conditions.
- **A camera** (phone) for the daily photo.

## Protocol

### Setup

1. **Build the pyramid.** Use the Czech construction numbers from the scout report: **triangles 159 mm high, base 250 mm, edge 238 mm**, with **2–4 holes at the base and 2 at the apex** for ventilation. Tape the four triangles into a square-based cone; leave the bottom open.
2. **Build the sham box.** A plain cardboard box with the **same footprint as the pyramid base** and a **flat top**. Same material, same wall thickness, same ventilation holes. This is the control for "any enclosure."
3. **Build the open station.** An identical support, on the same surface, in the open.
4. **Orient.** Pyramid side edges to NW/SW/SE/NE (the Drbal orientation — record whichever you choose and hold it constant). Put all three stations **the same distance from any wall or metal object**, in the same room, on the same shelf.
5. **Match and code the samples.** Weigh three samples; reject any set whose masses differ by more than 5%. An adult labels them **A, B, C**, seals the key, and hands them to a second person (a child is ideal) who places them without knowing which is which.
6. **Pre-register.** Write down, before day 1: the sample type, starting masses, the pass/fail thresholds below, the weighing schedule, and who holds the key. Photograph the sheet.

### Arm 2 — meat desiccation (2–3 weeks, first reading)

The source's own test.

1. Place sample A in the pyramid, B in the sham box, C in the open — all on identical supports at the same height.
2. **Weigh all three daily** (or every second day) at the same time of day, on the same scale, and photograph all three. Record the number and the photo.
3. Record **categorical state** daily: mold present/absent, smell, colour, texture, visible insects.
4. Run for **21 days**, or until one sample is clearly mummified and another clearly rotted.
5. Unblind only after the final numbers and photos are recorded.

### Arm 1 — grape desiccation (6–8 weeks, second reading)

The scout report's own suggested test ("grapes→raisins in 6–8 weeks").

1. Same three-station setup, three matched bunches of grapes.
2. Weigh and photograph **twice a week** for **8 weeks**.
3. Endpoint: mass-loss curve, plus whether each bunch dries (raisins) or molds.
4. Unblind at the end.

### Optional third arm — sealed chamber

Repeat Arm 2 with all three stations inside **identical sealed jars** (pyramid-shaped vs. box-shaped vs. open jar). This removes airflow as a variable entirely and isolates geometry from ventilation. It is the cleanest version of the test and the natural follow-up dossier.

## Pass / fail criteria (pre-registered)

- **PASS:** by day 21 the pyramid sample has lost **≥20% more mass** than **both** controls **AND** shows **no mold** where at least one control has molded; **and** the direction repeats in a second run with a fresh set. → The desiccation claim shows up at home scale.
- **FAIL:** all three samples within **±10%** on mass loss **and** mold status identical (all mold or none mold). → The claim does not reproduce with household apparatus. **Skeptic's Star** material: this would be the first honest home burial of the Bovis/Chaumery desiccation claim.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** starting masses differ by more than 5%; room temperature or humidity swings materially between stations; a sample is disturbed, eaten, or dropped; the key was opened before the final weighing; or the pyramid sample molders like the controls while the sham sample does not (which would mean the enclosure, not the geometry, is the variable — a real finding, but not this claim's).

Report the arms **separately**. A meat result and a grape result are independent findings, and either can stand alone.

## What would change the verdict

- A **second independent household** running the same protocol with the same result moves this dossier from `protocol` to `replicated` or `refuted`. One kitchen is an anecdote; two is a datum.
- A **humidity/temperature logger** in each station (~$15) would prove the three arms saw the same conditions — the single strongest upgrade to this design, and the cheapest way to kill the enclosure confound.
- The **sealed-chamber arm** isolates geometry from airflow outright. If the effect survives sealing, it is not ventilation.
- A **positive** result would be the first home-scale support for the organic-matter arm of the pyramid tradition. A **negative** would be the first honest burial of it, and would be worth publishing just as loudly.

## Honest caveats (do not skip these)

- **The source is secondary and says so.** The Centre Ganesha post is a 2026 blog summary, not a study; its own text states that no instrument can measure form waves and that the observations are not scientifically validated. The "modern tests" sentence has no citation.
- **The mechanism is unfalsifiable as stated.** "Negative green" is not measurable. This card tests the *effect* only, and does not claim the mechanism.
- **Enclosure is the dominant confound.** Any box changes drying. The sham arm exists because of this, and a result without the sham arm is not a result.
- **Room conditions dominate drying.** Run all arms in the same room, rotate positions daily, and log the conditions. A hot dry week will out-dry any geometry.
- **Suppression is not evidence either.** Nothing here was suppressed; the claim was published and simply never tested. Do not import a suppression narrative into this card.
