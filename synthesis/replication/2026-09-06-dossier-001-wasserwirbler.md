# Replication Dossier 001 — The Schauberger Water Vortexer (Wasserwirbler)

- **Source work(s):** `2026-09-05-scout-a` find #9 (Orpanit article, "Viktor Schauberger und der Wasserwirbler", https://www.orpanit.de/wissenswertes/viktor-schauberger-und-der-wasserwirbler/) · Schauberger 1951 Luxembourg Patent 31,480 (`2026-09-03-schauberger-luxembourg-patent-1951-de.md`) · Compendium of Vortex Physics (`2026-09-05-compendium-vortex-physics-de.md`)

## The claim (as asserted by the source)

A hyperbolic funnel with a narrow, threaded (rifled) outlet generates a centripetal vortex in water that:
1. cools the water,
2. "revitalizes"/densifies it (claimed softer, rounder, silkier mouthfeel — blind taste preference),
3. reduces algae in aquaria,
4. reduces irrigation energy by ~30% (claimed by the Original Martin Wasserwirbler, developed 1972 by Schauberger's son at the Pythagoras-Kepler-Schule, Lauffen).

**Honest framing:** these are *claims*. The dossier exists to test them. We hold no brief that any are true; the test is the point.

## Why it matters

Schauberger's core thesis — centripetal (implosive) motion builds/orders water, centrifugal (explosive) motion degrades it — is the keystone of the whole vortex physics cluster (SAFE Compendium, implosion science, the 1951 patent's molecular build-up claims). A clean home test of even *one* measurable facet (temperature, taste-blind test, algae) would be the first independent data point the archive has on the entire lineage. It also dovetails with his own family's water reality (permaculture, irrigation, the Village water domain).

## Replicability: `home`

- Build cost: **<$30** (funnel + plumbing fittings; or a purpose-made vortexer if bought)
- Safety: **trivial** — water and plastic, nothing pressurized, nothing hot
- Accessibility: hardware store in an afternoon

## Apparatus (Bill of Materials)

- One hyperbolic funnel (a standard kitchen funnel is conical, not hyperbolic — a hyperbolic curve matters to the claim; DIY with a plastic mixing bowl + drilled outlet, or a grain/feed funnel which is closer to hyperbolic)
- A threaded narrow outlet: a PVC or brass pipe nipple with visible rifling/threads (the claimed twist mechanism), e.g. 1/2" threaded nipple in a 3/4→3/8 reducer
- Two identical water containers (source vs treated)
- Calibrated kitchen thermometer (0.1°C resolution if possible)
- Optional: aquarium test strips (pH, GH/KH, nitrate), 2 identical small tanks for algae test, paper cups + labels for blind taste

## Protocol (draft — refine on first build)

### Test A — temperature
1. Fill two identical containers from the SAME source bucket (same temp).
2. Pour container B through the vortexer (full funnel pour, let it spin freely); pour container A through a plain funnel (control, same pour height/rate).
3. Immediately measure both temps; measure again at 5, 15, 60 min.
4. Repeat 5× (N=5). Record all numbers, note ambient temp.

### Test B — blind taste (requires ≥3 people, or one person across 5 blind trials)
1. Prepare 3 cups each session: A, B, and a second B ("B-repeat") — labeled randomly by a helper (not the tester).
2. Tester ranks: which is "softer/rounder", which is "harder/flatter". Record.
3. 5 sessions minimum, helper re-randomizes. Score vs chance (binomial).

### Test C — algae (2-week)
1. Two identical small tanks, same water, same light, same algae source (a spoon of pond water each).
2. Tank A control, Tank B gets vortexed water changes. Photograph daily, rank greenness blind.

### Test D — irrigation (optional, season permitting)
1. Two rows of identical seedlings, same soil; row B irrigated with vortexed water, row A control.
2. Measure: water volume used (the ~30% claim), growth (height), wilting days. Weekly photos.

## Pass / fail criteria (pre-registered)

- **Test A:** consistent, reproducible temperature difference ≥0.3°C between vortexed and control (same direction across ≥4/5 trials) → supports claim facet 1. <0.1°C or random direction → facet 1 not supported.
- **Test B:** testers pick the vortexed cup as "softer/rounder" ≥8/10 times across sessions → taste claim gets a data point. ~5/10 (chance) → not supported.
- **Test C:** blind photos rank tank B less green ≥4/5 sessions → algae claim supported. No difference → not supported.
- **Test D:** ≥20% water volume reduction at equal growth → irrigation claim supported directionally.
