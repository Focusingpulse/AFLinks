## The claim (as asserted by the tradition)

A coil of insulated copper wire laid out with a head band and hand/foot contacts — connected to itself, NO power source — creates a relaxed, "coherent" state when the person lies in the circuit (claimed to reduce tension, ease pain, balance the body's own electrical field).

**Honest framing:** this is a *claim* from a 90-year-old tradition, adjacent to biofield/radiesthesia studies. The dossier exists to test ONE measurable facet cheaply: does lying in the circuit change resting pulse (parasympathetic tone) vs a sham (same coil, disconnected)? We hold no brief — the test is the point.

## Why it matters

The Eeman circuit is the archetype of a whole family of "subtle energy" self-help devices that people actually build at home — and that families will keep asking about. A controlled $10 test (circuit vs sham, blind) would be the first clean data point the archive has on the entire device family. ## Replicability: `home`

- Build cost: **~$10** (a few meters of insulated copper wire + foam/tape)
- Safety: trivial (no power, no heat, no electricity)
- Accessibility: kitchen table in an evening

## Apparatus (Bill of Materials)

- ~5–10 m insulated copper wire (~0.5–1 mm²; hookup wire works)
- One piece of flexible foam/board (~40 × 20 cm) or just tape channels
- Two flat metal/foil contact pads (or bare-wire loops) for the hands
- A 10-minute timer and a way to take resting pulse (fingers + phone timer; or a cheap pulse oximeter / HRV app for a nicer signal)

## Protocol (Test A — pulse/relaxation, blind)

1. Build TWO identical-looking mats: the **circuit** (coil laid in a figure-eight, connections joined, hand contacts wired to the coil ends) and the **sham** (identical layout, but one wire CUT so it is not a closed circuit — visually identical).
2. Recruit one tester + one helper (the helper knows which is which; the tester does not). Label mats X and Y.
3. Tester lies still 10 minutes on mat X, helper measures pulse at 0, 5, 10 min. Then 10 minutes rest off-mat. Then mat Y, same measurement. (Order randomized by helper each session; 5+ sessions.)
4. Also record: self-rated relaxation 0-10 before/after each (tester's own blind rating).
5. Analyze: pulse trend + relaxation delta, circuit vs sham. Test for consistent difference (≥4/5 sessions same direction).

## Pass / fail criteria (pre-registered)

- **Circuit shows consistently lower resting pulse / higher relaxation delta vs sham** (same direction ≥4/5 blind sessions, delta ≥3 bpm or ≥2/10 relaxation points) → the device has a measurable effect; escalate to a homelab follow-up (HRV, EEG if available).
- **No consistent difference** (chance-level across sessions) → the classic $10 result: the tradition's effect does not replicate under blind test; log it honestly (Skeptic's Star material).
- Either outcome is a win for the archive: a data point where there was none.
