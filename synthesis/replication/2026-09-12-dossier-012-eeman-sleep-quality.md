## The Claim

Practitioners of the Eeman biocircuit tradition claim that lying in the self-connecting copper coil circuit for 10-20 minutes before bed promotes deeper relaxation and improves sleep quality. The original dossier (002) tested resting pulse during a 10-minute session. This dossier tests the downstream effect: **does pre-sleep circuit use measurably improve sleep quality?**

**Honest status of the claim:** contested. The Eeman circuit is adjacent to biofield/radiesthesia studies, which lack mainstream acceptance. No peer-reviewed sleep studies exist for this device family. The relaxation effect (dossier-002) is itself unverified. This dossier does NOT assume the claim is true — the protocol is designed so the claim can pass OR fail cleanly.

**Claimant:** L.E. Eeman tradition; Borderland Sciences archive
**Verification status:** unverified; no clinical sleep data

## The Protocol — pre-sleep circuit use vs control nights

The testable core: **if the circuit improves sleep quality, using it for 15 minutes before bed should produce measurable differences vs control nights (no circuit).** Self-reported sleep metrics are the primary outcome.

### Materials (~$10-15)

1. **Eeman circuit** (same as dossier-002): ~5-10m insulated copper wire, head band, hand/foot contacts
2. **Sleep tracking**: Either:
   - Simple: notebook by bed + phone alarm (free)
   - Better: sleep app with duration/wake tracking (free tier)
   - Best: fitness tracker or HRV monitor (if available)
3. **Timer**: phone or kitchen timer

### Setup

One tester (or multiple family members testing independently). 14-night protocol: 7 circuit nights, 7 control nights, randomized order.

### Procedure

1. **Pre-sleep routine**: Same each night (e.g., brush teeth, dim lights, no screens 30 min before bed).
2. **Circuit nights**: Lie in Eeman circuit for 15 minutes in bed, then remove circuit and sleep normally.
3. **Control nights**: Lie in bed for 15 minutes (no circuit), then sleep normally.
4. **Randomization**: Flip coin each night or use pre-generated random sequence (7 circuit, 7 control).
5. **Morning log**: Upon waking, record:
   - Total sleep time (hours, estimated or tracked)
   - Number of wake-ups (0, 1, 2, 3+)
   - Morning alertness (1-10 scale, 1=exhausted, 10=fully refreshed)
   - Dream recall (yes/no, optional)
   - Any notes (unusual restlessness, vivid dreams, etc.)

6. **Complete 14 nights** (or more for better signal).

## Pass/Fail Criteria (pre-registered)

**PASS (supports the claim):**
- Circuit nights show ≥15% better sleep quality score than control nights, AND
- Effect replicates across ≥2 independent testers in the family

Sleep quality score = (alertness × 10) − (wake-ups × 5) + (sleep hours × 3)
Example: 7.5 hrs sleep, 1 wake-up, alertness 7 → score = 70 − 5 + 22.5 = 87.5

**FAIL (refutes, for this tester and protocol):**
- No meaningful difference between circuit and control nights (within 5% on quality score)
- Or control nights score higher

**INCONCLUSIVE:**
- Small difference (5-15%) that doesn't replicate
- Fewer than 14 nights completed
- Only one tester

**Honest note:** A PASS would be a genuinely surprising result given the lack of prior sleep studies — which is exactly why family-scale testing matters. A FAIL is fully publishable (Skeptic's Star) and honestly retires "use the Eeman circuit for better sleep" advice for this family.
