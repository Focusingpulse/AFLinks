## The claim under test

The source record separates the school's claims into two halves, and says they must never be collapsed:

> *"1. **Detection.** Voice spectral analysis identifies consistent, reproducible patterns correlated with physiological state. *Partly testable* — the 2004 'similar issues produce similar biomarkers' tenet is a falsifiable statement, and blind protocols could be designed from it."*
> *"2. **Application.** Presented frequencies cause tissue change and symptom resolution. *The case histories are the evidence; independent replication is the missing piece."*

This card tests **claim 1 only**. The published tenet, dated 2004, is one sentence:

> *"People with similar wellness issues produce similar vocal biomarkers."*

That is a **between-person similarity** claim, and it is falsifiable: if the tenet holds, two people reporting the same state should have voices that are measurably more alike than two people reporting different states. If it does not hold, the ordering is absent or reversed.

**The card tests nothing else.** It does not test Frequency Equivalents™, the Periodic Table of the Frequency Domain™, Mathways, Sonistry, "Math as Medicine", the tone boxes, or any fed-back frequency. It does not test or endorse any treatment, and it does not ask anyone to ingest, apply, or change anything.

## What the source says the method is — and which part the card can actually reproduce

The record states the method's anatomy:

> *"Mathematical analysis of **anomalous vocal acoustics**, coupled with evaluation of **amplitude decay among low-order harmonics**."*

and:

> *"A **listening program** analyses the Voice Print and identifies imbalanced frequencies; a matching set is then 'fed back' to the body — what she calls **Math as Medicine**."*

The first half — **spectral analysis of a recorded voice, with attention to the amplitude relationship among low-order harmonics** — is exactly what free, standard, transparent tools do. The card pre-registers that feature family (see Protocol §4), which means the family computes the same *kind* of quantity the source says the method uses, with software anyone can inspect. **The second half — the "listening program" and the fed-back frequencies — is proprietary and is not reproduced, tested, or recommended.**

The record also carries the school's own mechanism admission, which belongs in the dossier because it sets the honest ceiling on what a positive result would mean:

> *"We don't know why the body responds to the many levels of frequency; we just know that it does."*

A positive result on this card would show that **voices cluster by self-reported state**. It would not show why, and it would not show that any frequency does anything.

A family cannot produce an independent replication of a 40-year clinical corpus. It **can** run the one sentence the school published as a tenet, blinded, with a pre-registered classifier, and report a number. That is the whole ambition of this card, and it is stated as such.

## The confound that decides the design — and the instrument check that gates it

The ordinary explanation of any positive result is not exotic. It is that **voices differ for reasons unrelated to wellness**: sex, age, vocal-tract size, accent, dialect, native language, smoking, recent shouting, time of day, hydration, and — above all — **the recording setup** (microphone, distance, room, gain). If the participants are recruited from one household and its friends, several of those variables move together with everything else.

The card answers this in two ways, and the second is the one that matters:

1. **A pre-registered, matched recording protocol** — one fixed utterance, one fixed distance, one quiet room, one device where possible, and a recorded note of every deviation. Where the device differs, the deviation is logged and the analysis is repeated with device as a covariate.
2. **An instrument check that must pass before any state data is analysed.** Every participant records the utterance **twice in the same session, two minutes apart**. The **within-person distance** between those two recordings is the method's own noise floor. **If the same person's two recordings are as far apart as two different people's, the method cannot resolve anything, and the run is UNATTEMPTED — not null.** This is the same rule the archive applies elsewhere: *a test with no exposure is not a null result.*

## The rival explanation — and why the card expects it

The mainstream position is not that voices carry no information. It is that **voices carry a great deal of information, and most of it is anatomical and environmental rather than physiological-state**. Speaker-identification and speaker-profiling are mature fields; the features this card pre-registers (long-term average spectrum, H1–H2, harmonic-to-noise ratio, formant frequencies) are exactly the features used to tell speakers apart — which means they are **also** the features most likely to encode sex, age and accent.

**That is why the card's primary endpoint is a nearest-neighbour classification rather than a correlation**, and why the pre-registration must record the participants' sex, age band, and native-language status **before** the analysis: if the classifier's hits are fully explained by those covariates, the result is an ARTIFACT, not support. The card says this in advance rather than discovering it afterwards.

## Protocol (pre-registered)

**1. Pre-register, in writing, before any recording.** Photograph a sheet carrying: the fixed utterance; the three-way state scale and its exact wording; the feature set; the distance metric; the classifier; the participant floor; the primary endpoint; the pass/fail thresholds; and the covariate list (sex, age band, native language, smoking, time of day, device, room). **The thresholds may not be changed after any data exists.**

**2. Participants.** **N ≥ 12.** Household plus friends, neighbours or extended family. Each participant is assigned a random code (e.g. `P-7F3A`) written on the sealed key, never on the audio file.

**3. The utterance (fixed for everyone).** In one quiet room, holding the device at a fixed distance (arm's length) and speaking at a comfortable volume:
- **(a)** a sustained vowel — *"ahh"* — for about 5 seconds;
- **(b)** one fixed spoken phrase, chosen in advance and written on the pre-registration sheet (e.g. counting from one to ten).

Each is recorded **twice**, two minutes apart, in the same session. **Both takes are kept** — the second is the instrument check.

**4. Features (pre-registered, computed in free software).** From each recording, using **Praat** (free, standard in phonetics) or any equivalent tool the family can inspect:
- the **long-term average spectrum** (LTAS), sampled at pre-registered frequency bands;
- **H1–H2** — the amplitude difference between the first and second harmonics of the sustained vowel, which is the source's own named feature family (*"amplitude decay among low-order harmonics"*);
- **harmonics-to-noise ratio** (HNR);
- the **first two formant frequencies** (F1, F2) of the sustained vowel.

**5. Blinding.** Audio files are renamed to the random codes. The key linking code → reported state is written on paper, sealed, and opened only after the classifier's output is fixed and photographed.

**6. State report (private, simultaneous).** Immediately after recording, each participant privately writes **one** of three states, using the pre-registered wording:
- **(A) I feel well right now.**
- **(B) I am tired or low on energy right now.**
- **(C) I have an active symptom right now** (a cold, sore throat, headache, or similar — named on the sheet).

**7. Instrument check (must pass, or the run is UNATTEMPTED — not null).** Compute the **within-person distance** between each participant's two takes. Compute the **between-person distance** distribution. **If the median within-person distance is not smaller than the median between-person distance, the method cannot resolve one person from another and the test is UNATTEMPTED.** Report both distributions and stop.

**8. Classification (pre-registered).** Standardise the features. Compute a pairwise distance between every pair of participants (first take of each). For each participant, find the **nearest neighbour** — the most similar other participant — and record whether the two share a reported state. **Hit rate = the fraction of participants whose nearest neighbour shares their state.**

**9. Chance baseline (the permutation null).** Shuffle the state labels across participants **1,000 times** (a spreadsheet or a short script), recomputing the hit rate each time. The null distribution's mean is the chance rate for this exact group-size mix; its 5th and 95th percentiles are the decision band. **The chance rate is not 1/3** — it depends on how many participants chose each state, and the card requires the family to compute it rather than assume it.

**10. Covariate check (mandatory, reported either way).** Re-run the classification **within a single sex** and **within a single age band** where the group sizes allow. If the hits vanish inside those strata, the result is an **ARTIFACT** — the classifier was reading anatomy, not state.

**Optional secondary arm — the vendor's own free tool.** The Sound Health Portal offers a free voice analysis. A family may optionally submit the same recordings and record what it returns, **as a comparison, not as ground truth** — the tool is the vendor's, its method is not published, and its output is not an independent measurement. **Privacy note: submitting a voice recording to a third-party service is a disclosure; do not submit recordings of children, and read the service's terms first.** This arm is optional and its absence does not affect the primary endpoint.

**Optional secondary arm — repeat the whole run on a second day.** If the tenet holds, the clustering should not depend on the day. A second run is the cheapest way to find out whether the first was a coincidence.

## Pass/fail

- **PASS (the source's tenet survives):** the nearest-neighbour hit rate is **at or above the pre-registered threshold** AND **above the 95th percentile of the permutation null** AND the covariate check does not explain the hits. A positive would make the 2004 tenet a candidate for a real, cheap, non-invasive screening signal — and would be the first independent support the record has.
- **PASS (the tenet is not supported — the expected, ordinary result):** the hit rate falls **inside the central band of the permutation null** (between the 5th and 95th percentiles). **People with similar self-reported wellness states do not produce more similar voices than chance in this group.** This is a complete result and earns the **Skeptic's Star**. It is the result the card expects.
- **FAIL:** the hit rate falls **below the 5th percentile of the null** — voices of people reporting the same state were systematically *less* alike than chance. Report it; a reversed ordering is a real finding about the design (most likely a batch or device effect) and must be investigated before it is reported as a result about voices.
- **UNATTEMPTED (this is not a null):** the **within-person distance is not smaller than the between-person distance** (the method cannot resolve a speaker from a speaker); fewer than 12 participants; the state key was not sealed before analysis; the thresholds were changed after data existed; the utterance, distance, room or device differed between participants and was not recorded.
- **VOID:** a participant's state changed between the two takes (e.g. a cold began mid-session); a participant recorded while ill in a way that also changed their voice acoustically in an obvious way (e.g. severe hoarseness) and this was not pre-registered as a state; the recordings are unusable (clipping, background noise, a phone call interrupting).
- **ARTIFACT:** the hits are fully explained by sex, age band, native language, device, room or time of day in the covariate check; the nearest-neighbour pairs are dominated by one participant (a hub); the analysis was run more than once with different feature sets and only the best is reported.

## Safety, privacy and honesty notes (carried into the card)

1. **This is a measurement, not a diagnosis and not a treatment.** No participant is told anything about their body. The card produces a hit rate and a chance baseline, nothing else.
2. **No health claim is made in either direction.** A positive result means *voices clustered by self-reported state in this group*. It does not mean the voice detects disease, and it does not mean any frequency does anything.
3. **Voice recordings are personal data.** Codes replace names on the audio files; the key is paper and sealed; recordings are deleted at the end of the run unless every participant agrees in writing to keep them. **Do not record children without a parent's written consent, and do not submit any recording to a third-party service without the same.**
4. **A participant who reports an active symptom should be doing whatever they would ordinarily do about it.** Nothing in this card is medical advice, and no one should delay care to participate.
5. **The expected result is the ordinary one.** Voices differ enormously for anatomical and environmental reasons. A null is the most likely outcome and is a complete result.
6. **The vendor's free tool is not ground truth.** If the optional arm is run, its output is recorded as one more observation, not as a reference standard.
