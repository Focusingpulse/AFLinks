## The Claim

A hyperbolic funnel can induce stable free-surface water vortices with three distinct flow regimes. The vortex achieves oxygen transfer coefficients (SOTR) that exceed comparable aeration systems, with energy-efficient operation and simple construction.

**Claimant:** Walter Schauberger (original design, 1960s); JoVE publication team (protocol)
**Verification status:** peer-reviewed, experimental protocol with video documentation

## The Protocol

### Materials

1. **Hyperbolic funnel**: Clear acrylic or glass, following Walter Schauberger's hyperbolic profile
   - Upper diameter: 10-15 cm
   - Lower outlet: 1-2 cm
   - Height: 20-30 cm
   - Hyperbolic curve: y = k/x (Walter's specific proportions documented in JoVE)

2. **Water reservoir**: 20-50 L container

3. **Pump**: Submersible, 5-10 W, flow rate adjustable 1-5 L/min

4. **Tubing**: Flexible vinyl, 1 cm diameter

5. **Dissolved oxygen meter** (for measurement)

6. **High-speed camera** (optional, for regime visualization)

7. **Thermometer**

### Setup

1. Position funnel vertically over reservoir
2. Connect pump to funnel inlet (bottom feed) or pour from top
3. Adjust flow rate to establish vortex
4. Document which regime forms (see below)

### Procedure

1. Fill reservoir with dechlorinated water at known temperature
2. Record initial dissolved oxygen (DO) reading
3. Start pump, establish stable vortex
4. Run for 30 minutes
5. Record final DO reading
6. Calculate oxygen transfer rate: SOTR = (DO_final - DO_initial) × volume / time

### Three Stable Regimes (from JoVE paper)

| Regime | Flow Rate | Visual Signature | Notes |
|--------|-----------|------------------|-------|
| Regime A | Low | Smooth surface, gentle rotation | Minimal aeration |
| Regime B | Medium | Visible vortex core, air entrainment begins | Optimal aeration zone |
| Regime C | High | Deep air core, turbulent surface | Maximum aeration, more energy |

## Pass/Fail Criteria

**PASS:**
- SOTR exceeds 2× comparable bubble diffuser at same power input
- Stable vortex forms within 60 seconds
- At least two regimes observable by adjusting flow

**FAIL:**
- No stable vortex forms
- SOTR comparable to or worse than control
- Funnel design does not match hyperbolic profile

**INCONCLUSIVE:**
- SOTR improvement marginal (1.2-1.9×)
- Measurement equipment unavailable
- Single run only
