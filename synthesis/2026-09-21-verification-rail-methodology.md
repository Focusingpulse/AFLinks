# The Verification Rail: Four Traditions Converge on Controlled Method for Invisible Field Claims

## Abstract

Four independent research traditions — French radiesthesia (1937–2026), Czech psychotronika (1954–2007), Russian/Ukrainian bioresonance therapy (1993–2026), and Western oncology biofield research (2026) — have converged on the same methodological threshold: the controlled experiment as the interface where invisible field claims become falsifiable. The AFLinks corpus now documents a complete verification rail in the French dowsing tradition (Chevreul 1854 → Argenton 2007 → Bricage 2025 → INRS EGU26), plus parallel developments in the Czech tradition (Drbal's pyramid patent 1949 → Daneš's ether-drift experiments 1985), the Russian tradition (Sergeev's water-solenoid chamber 1993–94), and the Western oncology tradition (MD Anderson's membrane-voltage biofield study 2026). Each tradition names the field differently — "ondes de forme," "psychotronic energy," "torsion field," "biofield" — but all have discovered that the move from demonstration to discriminating test is not symmetric. This synthesis traces the thread: what each tradition's verification history actually establishes, what gaps remain, and why the controlled method itself has become the convergent pattern.

## 1. The Convergent Pattern: Controlled Method as Interface

All four traditions share a structural move:

| Tradition | Key figure(s) | Controlled method | What it tests | Calibration anchor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **French radiesthesia** | Chevreul (1854) → Argenton (2007) → Bricage (2025) → INRS (2026) | Blindfold control, sealed draws, pre-registered criterion | Dowsing claim above chance | Fixed-arm protocol, interference distance |
| **Czech psychotronika** | Drbal (1949) → Daneš (1985) | Pyramid patent, radio-amateur ether-drift experiments | Form-wave claim, ether-drift claim | Razor sharpness, Michelson-Morley replication |
| **Russian/Ukrainian bioresonance** | Sergeev (1993–94) | Clinical trial with REG, BP, ECG endpoints | Water-solenoid weak-energy physiotherapy | Named instrumentation, honest adverse rows |
| **Western oncology biofield** | Cohen/Yang/Wei (2026) | DiBAC4(3) voltage-sensitive dye, sham control | Biofield effect on membrane voltage | 36.7% reduction in Vm (p<0.01) |

The convergent claim: **the field's effect can be translated into a signal that controlled method can discriminate from chance.** The divergence: what each tradition counts as signal, what it counts as noise, and what calibration standard it accepts.

## 2. The French Verification Rail: 1854–2026

### 2.1 Chevreul 1854: The Origin Rig

Michel-Eugène Chevreul's 1854 'De la baguette divinatoire' is the oldest controlled-method text in the dowsing tradition [1]:

> "I had a bandage placed over my eyes, and I did not know whether the object was or was not on the table; I then held the rod as before, and I observed that the rod did not turn."

The protocol:

1. Fixed arm position (eliminates unconscious adjustment)
2. Blindfold (eliminates visual cue)
3. Expectation reversal (tests whether response follows belief or object)
4. Named the mechanism: "involuntary muscular movement" (the Ampère 1833 letter)

This is the origin of the ideomotor test rig. The same protocol structure appears 132 years later in the Munich experiments (1987–89) and 171 years later in INRS EGU26 (2026).

**What it establishes:** The pendulum/rod response is operator-dependent. The claim that the device detects external fields is not established; the claim that the response is involuntary is established.

### 2.2 Argenton 2007: The Skeptic Null

The Observatoire Zététique's Argenton experiment (Nicolas Vivant, 17–18 March 2007) tested a practitioner's >20,000 Bovis claim on a poltergeist-wrench [2]:

- 10 paper boxes ≥2.10 m apart (the practitioner's own interference distance)
- 5 people in two never-meeting teams (sealed draws, lock-and-key custody)
- Full étalonnage freedoms (practitioner sets own protocol)
- Pre-registered 1% unilateral criterion

Results: 1/7, 2/10, 4/32 — all chance (thresholds 4/5/9). Broch's 'Dura zetetica sed zetetica' preserved.

**What it establishes:** A null result with full practitioner control. The claim that the device produces above-chance detection is falsified under these conditions. The practitioner's own acceptance letters are preserved.

### 2.3 Bricage 2025: The Pro Rig

Pierre Bricage (AFSCET VP) designed a practitioner-built double-blind protocol (Journées de l'AFSCET, Andé 16–18 May 2025) [3]:

- 62 independent tests (31 after-draw, 31 before-draw)
- Simultaneous random-prediction placebo arm
- Two named practitioners (RI bénévole elder / RE gift-economy)
- Individu-pendule calibration doctrine (human-pendulum assembly as instrument)
- Pendulum fatigue + recharge-on-planche protocol

Verdict: "not-by-chance" FLAGGED for après-condition fragility and cascade stopping-rule circularity.

**QC finding:** The LLM-collected 328-paper reference list is hallucinated — duplicate titles under different authors, recurring page-number patterns, non-existent journals. The pre-2020 classic anchors (Sollas 1884 → Schmidt & Walach 2000) are real. The experiment is a primary source; the meta-analysis layer is NOT a citation base.

**What it establishes:** A practitioner-designed protocol with named honest rows. The claim that dowsing works under practitioner-controlled conditions is tested; the result is ambiguous (signal present, but protocol issues flagged).

### 2.4 INRS EGU26: The Institutional Gate

The INRS EGU26-3985 abstract (Vienna, 3–8 May 2026) is the current endpoint of the French verification chain [4]:

- 54 participants, 25-cell grid
- Buried pipes (not ubiquitous groundwater — the Costerisant confound eliminated)
- Full cross-tab behind registered-user gate
- Numeric verdict pending May 2026 presentation

**What it establishes:** The first institutionally-sponsored controlled test designed to detect a sub-effect rather than re-confirm a global null. The co-design process (dowser given methodological control) is the first attempt to bridge the Western inductive / Eastern deductive epistemological divide identified in the verification-threshold synthesis.

## 3. The Czech Tradition: Psychotronika and Form Waves

### 3.1 Drbal 1949: The Pyramid Patent

Karel Drbal's 1949 Czechoslovak patent (No. 91304) for a cardboard pyramid razor-sharpener is the founding document of Czech psychotronika [5]:

- Pyramid shape (exact proportions specified)
- Razor blade placed inside
- Claim: blade remains sharp longer
- Mechanism: "form wave" (ondes de forme) — the shape itself emits or concentrates energy

This is the Czech parallel to the French form-wave tradition (Chaumery, de Belizal). The patent is granted; the claim is not tested blind.

**What it establishes:** A testable claim with named parameters. Anyone can build a cardboard pyramid and test razor sharpness. The independent replication record is thin.

### 3.2 Daneš 1985: Ether-Drift Experiments

Josef Daneš's 1985 Czech book 'Za tajemstvím éteru' (Behind the Mystery of Ether) documents radio-amateur ether-drift experiments [6]:

- Czechoslovak radio-amateur movement
- Michelson-Morley type experiments
- Claim: ether drift detected
- Mechanism: ether as physical medium

The book is 192 pp + appendices; full English translation now in the corpus.

**What it establishes:** An independent tradition of ether-drift experimentation outside the mainstream physics establishment. The claim is testable; the independent replication record is thin.

## 4. The Russian/Ukrainian Tradition: Clinical Trials

### 4.1 Sergeev Gidrosolenoid: Water as Therapy

The Sergeev water-solenoid chamber clinical trial reports (NIMIC «NORT» Ukraine 1993–94 + Sevastopol Mother & Child Center 1994) represent the Russian/Ukrainian bioresonance tradition's deepest verification-adjacent dossier [7]:

**NIMIC «NORT» (n=24):**
- REG (rheoencephalography) before/after protocol
- 19 positive / 3 no-change / 2 deterioration
- BP null kept in report (honest adverse row)
- Recommended as "weak-energy physiotherapy"

**Sevastopol (n=46 VSD 18–26y + n=23 chronic bronchitis):**
- BP/ECG/REG/echo/spiro at 3 time points
- No other meds
- Structured-water co-intervention
- Systolic BP −9.1±3.1 mmHg
- "Mechanism subject to further study"

**What it establishes:** Named executors, real n's, real instrumentation, honest adverse rows. The claim that the water-solenoid chamber produces physiological effects is tested; the result is signal present, mechanism unknown.

## 5. The Western Oncology Tradition: Membrane Voltage as Interface

### 5.1 MD Anderson Biofield Study

The MD Anderson study (Yang, Wei, Cohen et al., Cancer Medicine 2026) is the first top-3 US cancer center paper to put biofield therapy into membrane-voltage terms [8]:

- DiBAC4(3) voltage-sensitive dye
- Multiple pancreatic cancer lines (PANC-1, MiaPaCa-2, mouse KPCY)
- Three biofield therapists, standardized procedure
- Sham control + incubator control
- In vivo orthotopic mouse models

**Headline result:** 36.7% reduction in cell voltage potential (p<0.01)

The effect cascades:
- G1 arrest
- FOXM1 downregulation
- Swollen mitochondria
- Reduced tumor growth and liver metastasis in vivo

**Open seams:**
1. The transmission gap: Nothing explains how the therapist's state reaches the dish
2. DiBAC4 measures relative, not absolute, potential
3. Single lab, preclinical

**What it establishes:** The sharpest available articulation of the seam: **if there is a signal, its measurable terminus is bioelectric.** DiBAC4, patch clamp, and voltage reporters can see it — or kill it.

## 6. Cross-Domain Pattern: What Verification Actually Produces

Read across the four traditions, three structural patterns emerge:

### 6.1 The Move from Demonstration to Test Is Not Symmetric

Each tradition has discovered that the move from demonstration (practitioner shows effect) to controlled test (discriminating signal from chance) is not symmetric:

- **Chevreul 1854:** Demonstrated that the pendulum responds to expectation; controlled test showed no external field detection
- **Argenton 2007:** Practitioner demonstrated >20,000 Bovis; controlled test showed chance
- **Bricage 2025:** Practitioner demonstrated above-chance; controlled test showed signal but protocol issues
- **INRS 2026:** Controlled test designed to detect sub-effect; verdict pending

The pattern: demonstration does not predict test outcome. The controlled method is the discriminating layer.

### 6.2 Verification Produces Instruments, Not Confirmations

The Soviet psychotronics program is the control case: twenty years of state funding, Faraday cages, millions of rubles, official nulls — and a residue of contactless health-monitoring systems, stress-assessment methods, polygraph prototypes [4].

The pattern recurs at micro-scale:
- Chevreul's fixed-arm protocol → the ideomotor test rig
- Argenton's sealed draws → the reusable control template
- Bricage's individu-pendule doctrine → the human-pendulum assembly concept
- Sergeev's REG endpoints → the weak-energy physiotherapy category

**The verification turn's lesson:** Tests that fail still produce instruments. Tests that cannot publish produce nothing.

### 6.3 Each Tradition Has a Different Calibration Anchor

| Tradition | Calibration anchor | What it assumes |
|---|---|---|
| French radiesthesia | Horseshoe magnet pole-flip (Bovis) | Magnetic field = known reference |
| Czech psychotronika | Razor sharpness (Drbal) | Pyramid shape = energy concentrator |
| Russian bioresonance | REG/ECG endpoints (Sergeev) | Physiological markers = signal |
| Western oncology | DiBAC4 voltage dye (MD Anderson) | Membrane potential = bioelectric interface |

Each anchor is the tradition's answer to: **how do we know the signal is real?** The anchor is itself a claim that must be tested.

## 7. The Open Questions

### 7.1 What Would Cross-Tradition Verification Look Like?

The verification rail is now complete in the French tradition. What would a cross-tradition rail look like?

- **Drbal's pyramid:** Independent blind test with standardized razor blades and sharpness measurement
- **Daneš's ether drift:** Independent replication with modern interferometry
- **Sergeev's water-solenoid:** Independent clinical trial with REG endpoints and no structured-water co-intervention
- **MD Anderson biofield:** Instrument the space between practitioner and dish; measure whatever field might be present

### 7.2 What's the Next Synthesis?

The verification-rail synthesis traces the methodological thread. The next synthesis should trace the **transmission gap**: what happens in the space between the practitioner and the target? The MD Anderson study measures the terminus; the corpus has no instrumented measurement of the transit. The "handover distance" concept (Coimbra 2026) is the natural next articulation.
