# The Applied Turn: Daniel Risy's Field Notebooks and the Operational Layer of Form-Wave Science

*Synthesis by Drunvalo — 2026-10-10*

---

## Abstract

Between 2026-10-07 and 2026-10-10 the archive absorbed the largest single-author corpus it has yet received: eighteen notebooks and essays by the French practitioner **Daniel Risy** (danielrisy.free.fr), spanning torsion-field theory, form-wave radiesthesia, truffle ecology, beekeeping, radionic varroa treatment, biodynamics, and human-field studies [1]. The corpus is not another statement of the French–Russian convergence traced in the form-field-continuum synthesis [26] — it is what that convergence looks like **when someone takes it to work**. Risy keeps the Russian vocabulary (right/left/primary torsion fields, remanence) and the French workbench (witnesses, radionic graphs, pendulum protocols) in the same notebook, and applies both to organisms that cannot be argued with: truffle mycelium, honeybee colonies, varroa mites.

This synthesis maps the corpus, traces its three applied threads (soil ecology, the apiary, the cosmic-rhythm claim), sets it against its five cross-language witnesses — Russian form-effect physics, Polish digital radiesthesia, Belgian apparatus craft, Italian torsional radionics, Chinese instrumental torsion experiments — and asks the only question that matters for an applied corpus: **which of its claims are built so they could fail?**

## 1. The Corpus: A Practitioner’s Operations Manual

The Risy documents divide into four layers:

| Layer | Works | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Program statement** | Préambule à l'assemblage des CARNETS [1]; Champ de torsion et onde de forme [2] | Why the notebooks exist: a 2020 encounter with torsion-field medicine opened "research perspectives" that six years of field notes now record; torsion fields (Cartan/Einstein, "a century old") proposed as the physical substrate of the form wave |
| **Theory notebooks** | NOTES 1–11 [3][4][5][6][12][13] | A personal notation system: CTd/CTg (right/left torsion fields), CTp (primary torsion fields), *duellité*, *rémanence*, the Janus and Tel 1 meshes, trefoil witnesses, delocalization |
| **Applied ecology** | Super Réseaux de la truffe [7]; L'écosystème de la truffe [8]; Soutien aux colonies au rucher [9]; Varroa, expériences de radionique [10] | Form-wave work on truffle beds, bee colonies, and mite infestation — the corpus's core |
| **The human field** | Le champ de forme de l'Homme et l'aura; NOTES 3, 10; NOTES 2 (Ts7/Linky) | The human "form field" and "duellity field", the Life Field Transformer, and a radiesthetic reading of the Linky smart meter's field |

What makes the corpus unusual is its **workbench grammar**. Risy does not write "energy was balanced." He writes which witness was used (right-torsion witness, trefoil witness), which field class was read (primary vs. secondary), and what persisted (*rémanence* — remanence, the field that remains after the source is removed [4]). Whatever one makes of the ontology, this is the notation of someone running repeatable procedures and expecting the reader to re-run them:

> « propriétés des champs de torsion — rémanence, duellités, champ d'insertion gravifique »
> *"Properties of torsion fields — remanence, duellities, the gravitational insertion field" (NOTES 4)* [4]

The form-field-continuum synthesis [26] closed by asking what an *applied* form-wave science would even look like. The Risy corpus is the first answer in the archive: it looks like field notes with a failure mode attached.

## 2. Thread One — The Mycelium Reading: "Super Networks" Under the Truffle Bed

The two truffle works [7][8] make the corpus's most consequential claim: that the truffle's underground network — « Les Super Réseaux de la truffe » (*the truffle's super-networks*) — can be **read radiesthetically**, and that the reading guides cultivation decisions.

Hold this against the mainstream finding it rhymes with. Since Simard's 1997 carbon-tracer work, plant and soil ecology accepts that **common mycorrhizal networks** link trees and fungi into resource- and signal-sharing webs — the popularized "wood wide web." The mainstream result and the Risy claim agree on the load-bearing fact: *the network exists, it is extensive, and it carries information-bearing flows between organisms.* They disagree on the carrier and the sensor: isotope tracers and DNA sequencing on one side, the pendulum and the form-wave witness on the other.

This is the same structure the qi-torsion synthesis [25] found at another scale: an anomaly claim stapled to a real, measurable substrate (there: water spectra; here: the mycelial web). The synthesis value is not in adjudicating the pendulum — it is in noting that **the truffle bed is a place where the subtle-energy claim and the institutional claim are looking at the same object.** If form-wave readings of a truffle network describe structure that DNA soil sampling later finds independently, that is a cross-validation protocol waiting to be written. If they don't, the divergence is localizable and falsifiable — which is all the verification rail [27] ever asks.

## 3. Thread Two — The Apiary as Laboratory

The beekeeping works are the corpus's strongest material, because bees make poor metaphysics:

- **Soutien aux colonies** [9] — « l'utilisation des ondes de forme au rucher » (*the use of form waves in the apiary*): form-wave emitters deployed as colony-support measures.
- **Varroa, expériences de radionique** [10] — radionic treatment of varroatosis on **two stationary hives**, with "fall-model observations": one treated, one reference, mite-fall counts as the observable.

The varroa protocol is the most important single structure in the corpus, and the reason is methodological: **it is comparative.** Two hives, a treatment difference, a hard countable endpoint (mite fall), a seasonal confound acknowledged in the write-up ("fall model"). Nobody who has read the verification rail [27] will mistake this for a controlled trial — n=2, no blinding, no randomization, apiary-level confounds everywhere. But it is built in the *shape* of a test: it has a comparison, an endpoint, and a number. That puts it one rung above the entire anecdote layer of the radionics tradition, and it means the protocol can be strengthened rather than merely believed — more hives, blinded counts, pre-registered thresholds. The archive now contains both the skeptical rail (Chevreul 1854 → Argenton 2007 [27]) and a practitioner protocol that could, in principle, be placed on that rail.

## 4. Thread Three — The Cosmic-Rhythm Claim: Biodynamics Meets Kozyrev

NOTES 7 and 11 record what Risy calls « la réponse de la Terre à l'impulsion des étoiles » (*the Earth's response to the impulse of the stars*) — a claimed radiesthetic detection of stellar timing in terrestrial field behavior, "discovery published February 2026" [12][13]. NOTES 8 draws the consequence: « biodynamie, astrologie et champs de torsion » (*biodynamics, astrology and torsion fields*) [11].

This is the archive's third independent arrival at the cosmic-timing claim:

1. **The Steiner line** — the 1924 Koberwitz lectures (GA 327 [20]), founding text of biodynamics: cosmic rhythms as agricultural inputs, operationalized in the planting calendar and the 500/501 preparations; the Spanish scientific-synthesis work [21] documents the farm-as-organism model that carries the claim today.
2. **The Russian cosmism line** — Kozyrev's stellar-influence experiments, and now Kaznacheev & Trofimov's *Distant-Informational Interactions in Kozyrev Space* [23] (landed in the archive the same day as this synthesis): "cosmoplanetary anthropoecology" — the claim that distant astrophysical processes couple to terrestrial biological systems through a non-electromagnetic channel.
3. **The Risy line** — a practitioner claiming to *detect* the coupling radiesthetically, in real time, and to use it the way biodynamics uses its calendar.

Three traditions, three mutually unintelligible vocabularies (spiritual science, torsion physics, form-wave radiesthesia), one structural claim: **terrestrial biology keeps time with the sky through a channel that is not light and not gravity-as-modeled.** Risy's contribution is the only one that comes with a detection protocol attached. That does not make it true; it makes it examinable — the stellar-impulse readings generate dated predictions that a skeptical re-reader could check against the same pendulum protocol, blinded to the claimed impulse times.

## 5. The Five Witnesses: The Convergence Is Not French

The corpus does not stand alone in the archive. Five independent traditions arrived at the same workbench:

| Tradition | Witness work | What it adds to the applied claim |
|---|---|---|
| **Russian form-effect physics** | Kovalenko, *Study of the Mechanism of the Form Effect* (CyberLeninka, physics specialty) [15]; Kovalenko & Shutov on form influencing water structure | The institutional-Russian version: "form effect" as a peer-reviewed research object, with water as the detector — theory for what Risy operationalizes |
| **Polish digital radiesthesia** | Pietrzak, *Wielkości cyfrowe w radiestezji* [16] and the *energia kształtów* (energy-of-shapes) series | The measurement impulse: closing the Bovis scale at 10,000, forcing radiesthetic readings into conventional numerical descriptors — the same move Risy makes with mite counts |
| **Belgian apparatus craft** | Servranx, *Constructions des appareils d'ondes de formes soi-même* [17] | The build-it-yourself emitter tradition: form waves as hardware, sixty years of published devices — the manufacturing layer Risy's apiary emitters inherit |
| **Italian torsional radionics** | *Tavole Radioniche Torsionali* (Radionica Italiana) [18] | The explicit fusion: radionic tables founded on "torsion fields (Cartan, 1913)" as the mechanism of action at a distance — the same franco-italian equation Risy writes in his preamble |
| **Chinese instrumental torsion** | Gao Peng's 2.2-km non-local experiment: LED torsion generator vs. electrochemical impedance spectroscopy [19] | The instrumental control: a *device*, not a person, emitting the field, with an electronic detector — the design that removes the operator-dependence objection |

And standing over all five, the design tradition Chris's own archive centers on: **BioGeometry** — Ibrahim Karim's shape-resonance science [24], which makes the identical applied claim in architecture and design (geometry as a qualitative energy input to biological systems) and ships it with a measurement practice (BioSignatures, BG3). Risy deploying form-wave emitters in an apiary and Karim deploying shape-balancing in a building are the same gesture in different materials.

Add the memory-bearing-medium synthesis's finding [28] — every tradition needs a substrate that holds an imprint — and the applied picture completes itself: Risy's *rémanence* is the persistence claim in notebook notation, Kovalenko's water is the persistence detector, Gao Peng's EIS is the persistence meter.

## 6. Verification Posture: What in This Corpus Could Fail?

The verification rail [27] grades claims by whether they are built to survive a fair test. Applied to the corpus:

- **Gradeable now:** the varroa two-hive protocol [10] — strengthenable to n-hives, blinded mite counts, pre-registered fall thresholds. The truffle-network readings [7][8] — testable against eDNA soil mapping; the claimed super-network either matches the sequenced network or it doesn't.
- **Gradeable with protocol work:** the remanence claims [4] — a witness-based persistence reading is operator-dependent by design, but the *operator-blind* version (second reader, no knowledge of exposure history) is writable; Gao Peng's instrumental design [19] shows where the operator-free version of the whole class lives.
- **Not yet gradeable:** the duellity/CTp ontology (NOTES 3, 9, 10) — the notation is internally consistent but its primitives have no independent detector; the stellar-impulse readings [12][13] need dated, blinded re-readings before they are evidence of anything but the reader's conviction.

The honest summary: the Risy corpus is the archive's best-developed example of the applied layer, and roughly a third of it is built in shapes that could survive contact with the verification rail. That is a higher fraction than most of the corpus it joins.

## 7. Open Questions

1. **The mycorrhizal cross-validation.** Has anyone, anywhere, run pendulum-based network mapping against eDNA soil sequencing on the same truffle bed? If not, that is the single highest-value experiment this corpus implies — cheap, bilateral, decisive.
2. **The varroa protocol, scaled.** Would a beekeeping cooperative run Risy's protocol at n=20 hives with blinded mite counts? The protocol is already written; it needs an institution, not a theory.
3. **The remanence measurement.** Is Risy's *rémanence* the same observable as Kovalenko's water-structure persistence and the qi-tradition's information-water after-effect [25]? Three traditions, one claimed phenomenon class — a shared protocol would tell.
4. **The corpus gap.** The CARNETS assembly preamble [1] references notebooks beyond NOTES 1–11 (and the aodf series continues past its second page). The archive should hold the complete set before the applied layer is synthesized further.
