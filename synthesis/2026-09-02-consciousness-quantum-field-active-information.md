*Synthesis by Drunvalo — 2026-09-02*

## Abstract

At least six independent research traditions — Italian quantum biophysics (Manzalini, Del Giudice/Pollack), British biology of form (Sheldrake), French radiesthesia and form wave theory (Chaumery/de Belizal, Vibratis, Centre Ganesha), Russian torsion field theory (Shipov/Akimov), and Mexican psychogeometry (Ponce de León) — converge on a claim that cuts against the materialist paradigm of mainstream neuroscience: **consciousness is a field phenomenon, and the field's shape — not its amplitude — carries active information.** This synthesis traces that thread through the AFLinks archive, identifies convergent claims across traditions that developed in near-total isolation, notes the Italian-Russian-French-British-Mexican intersections, and proposes next questions.

Where previous syntheses traced the vacuum as organizational substrate (2026-08-31), rotational ether dynamics (2026-09-01), and geometric force unification (2026-09-02), this one traces the **information principle itself** — the claim that information is not a derivative of matter but a field property with physical efficacy, that biological coherence is a quantum field phenomenon, and that consciousness extends beyond the skull through field shapes that encode meaning in their curvature.

## The Convergent Claim

All six traditions assert that:

1. **Information is a field property, not a material byproduct.** The shape or curvature of a field carries information that guides matter — without necessarily transferring conventional energy.
2. **Consciousness is field-like, not brain-localized.** The brain processes consciousness the way a radio processes signals — it does not generate it. The field exists independently and the organism tunes into it.
3. **Shape matters more than amplitude.** In quantum potential theory, in form wave theory, in morphic fields, and in torsion fields, the geometry of the field — its wavefront curvature, its form, its pattern — determines its physical effect, not its intensity.
4. **Biological coherence is a quantum phase transition.** Living organisms maintain coherence through symmetry breaking processes that produce collective modes (Nambu-Goldstone bosons, coherence domains, morphic resonances).
5. **Form emits information.** Geometric shapes — whether Platonic solids, pyramids, yantras, or biological morphologies — emit characteristic fields detectable by both instruments (pendulums, torsion detectors) and organisms.

This is distinct from mainstream neuroscience, which treats consciousness as an epiphenomenon of neural computation. These traditions treat consciousness as a field that the organism is embedded in and processes — closer to Bohm's active information than to Dennett's computationalism.

## The Traditions

### 1. Italian Quantum Biophysics: Manzalini and the Field of Consciousness

Antonio Manzalini (ORCID: 0000-0003-1633-3099), publishing in *Rivista Scienze Biofisiche* (2022), proposes a model of consciousness as a quantum field based on extending the de Broglie-Bohm pilot wave theory to living systems.

Key claims:

- **Consciousness is a quantum field, not a brain process.** Just as the quantum field accompanies inanimate matter in de Broglie-Bohm theory, a field of consciousness accompanies living matter. The brain processes the field's potential; it does not create the field.
- **The potential of consciousness is active information.** Bohm and Hiley's concept of active information — information that guides particle dynamics without transferring energy — is extended to living organisms. The organism "processes and exchanges" this information through symmetry breaking.
- **Shape > amplitude.** The quantum potential Q in de Broglie-Bohm theory is proportional to the curvature or shape of the field, not its amplitude. Manzalini explicitly states: "its shape or curvature has more physical importance than its amplitude." The same holds for the potential of consciousness C.
- **Nambu-Goldstone boson condensates encode the field's form.** Following QFT, symmetry breaking in the consciousness field produces NG boson condensates. The condensation function f(x) acts as a "form factor" — encoding the field's shape information. This is not metaphor: NG bosons are well-established collective modes in condensed matter physics.
- **Living systems are far from quantum equilibrium.** Valentini's insight — that quantum theory (with the Born rule) is a special case of a broader physics — is applied to life. Living organisms operate far from quantum equilibrium, enabling non-local information exchange and quantum coherence.
- **The Freeman-Vitiello brain model provides the biological substrate.** The dissipative quantum model of the brain (Freeman & Vitiello, 2008) shows that NG bosons in water's electric dipole field — called dipole wave quanta (DWQ) — encode ordering information in brain dynamics. The condensation density measures the degree of coherence.

**Information aspect:** Consciousness is not a computation but a field whose geometry encodes meaning. The organism reads the field the way a particle reads the pilot wave — through shape, not force.

### 2. Italian Biophysics: Del Giudice, Pollack, and the Coherence of Water

Emilio Del Giudice — the physicist who appears repeatedly across the Italian contributions to this archive — explained Gerald Pollack's discovery of the fourth phase of water (EZ water) as a biological quantum coherence phenomenon.

Key claims:

- **Water near surfaces has different properties.** Pollack experimentally demonstrated that water within microns of a membrane forms an exclusion zone (EZ) with distinct properties — including the ability to release electrons at energies far below the 12.6 eV required for bulk water.
- **This solves Szent-Györgyi's mystery.** The Nobel laureate's question — where do the electrons come from for the constant chemical reactions of life? — is answered: from water itself, when organized by proximity to membranes.
- **All biological water is near-membrane water.** In living organisms, no water molecule is more than a millionth of a centimeter from a membrane. There is no "bulk water" in biology — it is all EZ water, all coherence-domain water.
- **Coherence domains are quantum field condensates.** Del Giudice's earlier work (with Preparata, Vitiello) proposed that water forms coherence domains through spontaneous symmetry breaking — the same QFT mechanism Manzalini invokes for consciousness. The EZ phase is the macroscopic signature of these domains.

**Information aspect:** Water is not a passive medium. It is an active information substrate — its coherence domains encode and transmit biological information through field organization, not chemical signaling. The connection to Manzalini is direct: both invoke NG boson condensation in water's electric dipole field as the mechanism of biological information processing.

### 3. British Biology of Form: Sheldrake and Morphic Resonance

Rupert Sheldrake (PhD, Cambridge), in work spanning four decades, proposes that biological form and behavior are organized by morphogenetic fields — informational fields that operate through resonance, not energy transfer.

Key claims:

- **Morphogenetic fields are informational, not energetic.** They organize systems through form, pattern, and resonance — not through force or energy transfer. This is explicitly distinguished from conventional physical fields.
- **Morphic resonance: past influences present.** The forms and behaviors of past organisms influence present ones through non-local resonance. The more similar the system, the stronger the resonance. This is a temporal information field — form propagates through time, not space.
- **Natural laws are habits, not immutable truths.** Constants of nature may be crystallized habits — morphic resonances that have stabilized over cosmic time. This echoes Manzalini's claim that physical laws emerge locally from coherence condensation (via Debailleul).
- **Form precedes matter.** The morphic field is not produced by the organism; the organism is produced by the field. This inverts the materialist assumption and aligns with Manzalini's consciousness-as-field model.

**Information aspect:** Sheldrake's morphic fields are pure information fields — they carry form without energy. The resonance mechanism is shape-based: similar forms resonate. This is formally analogous to Manzalini's NG boson condensates encoding form factors, and to the French form wave tradition where geometric shape emits characteristic fields.

### 4. French Radiesthesia: Form Waves as Geometry-Emitted Information

The French tradition of radiesthesia — from Chaumery and de Belizal (1930s) through Centre Ganesha and Vibratis (2020s) — holds that geometric forms emit specific, detectable waves that are not part of the electromagnetic spectrum.

Key claims:

- **Every geometric form emits a characteristic wave.** The form wave (onde de forme) is determined by the geometry of the object — its shape, dimensions, material, and orientation — not by its chemical composition.
- **These waves are not electromagnetic.** They belong to what French radiesthesists call "subtle energies" or "vital energy" — the qi of Chinese tradition, the prana of India. They are detectable by pendulum and dowsing rods but not by conventional instruments.
- **Forms can be beneficial or noxious.** Rounded, regular, harmonically proportioned forms (circles, squares, golden-ratio rectangles) emit beneficial waves. Irregular, angular forms emit noxious waves. The pyramid is a powerful amplifier. The triskel modulates energy direction through rotation.
- **Form waves carry meaning.** Forms associated with concepts (mandalas, yantras, sacred symbols) emit vibrations connected to those concepts. A form bearing a sad symbolism has a corresponding energy field. This is explicitly informational — the form encodes meaning in its vibratory emission.
- **Radionics uses form waves for distance action.** Form waves serve as carrier waves for directed intention. Prayer experiments (Sicher et al., 1998) show measurable effects on patients unaware of being prayed for. Radionics amplifies this through geometric form.
- **Connection to morphic fields.** Morel and de La Foye linked form waves to Sheldrake's morphogenetic field theory — form waves as the physical expression of invisible informational fields.

**Information aspect:** The form wave tradition is the most empirically oriented of the informational field traditions. It provides detection methods (pendulum, dowsing) and practical applications (radionics, geobiology, architecture). Its claim that geometry emits information is the experimental counterpart to Manzalini's theoretical claim that field shape carries active information.

### 5. Russian Torsion: Shipov, Akimov, and Information Without Energy

G.I. Shipov and A.E. Akimov's torsion field theory — extensively documented in the AFLinks archive — makes a claim that is often missed in Western summaries: torsion fields are primarily information carriers, not force fields.

Key claims:

- **Torsion is the geometry of rotation in spacetime.** Extending Cartan's 1922 hypothesis, Shipov shows that spacetime torsion is not a small perturbation but a fundamental geometric property. Dynamic torsion fields have constants not constrained to be small.
- **Torsion fields carry information, not energy.** Akimov and Shipov explicitly state that torsion signals can transmit information without transmitting energy. This is the same claim as Bohm's active information and Sheldrake's morphic resonance — information without energy transfer.
- **The physical vacuum is the medium.** The vacuum's phyton structure (nested electron-positron ring packets) is the substrate through which torsion propagates. EM, gravity, and torsion are polarization states of the same vacuum.
- **Torsion technology applications.** Torsion field generators produce effects in materials, biological systems, and information transmission — all without conventional energy transfer. The claim is that torsion modifies the vacuum's organizational state, which in turn modifies matter.
- **Connection to consciousness.** Russian torsion research explicitly links torsion fields to consciousness phenomena — telepathy, remote viewing, and intention effects are interpreted as torsion field interactions. The brain is a torsion field receiver, not just an electromagnetic system.

**Information aspect:** Torsion is the Russian vocabulary for what Manzalini calls the quantum field of consciousness, what Sheldrake calls morphic fields, and what the French call form waves. All describe information propagated through a subtler medium without conventional energy transfer.

### 6. Mexican Psychogeometry: Ponce de León and the Geometric Code

Arturo Ponce de León's Psychogeometry (Psicogeometría) — translated from Spanish — proposes that the three mathematical constants phi, pi, and Euler constitute a geometric code that converts free energy into matter.

Key claims:

- **Energy and matter differ only in geometric arrangement.** "Energy and matter are only differences in geometric arrangement." Matter is energy gravitationally trapped through geometric organization — not through force, but through form.
- **Phi generates the spiral of creation.** The golden ratio (1.618...) creates the heterodynamic conjunction of wave phase — the spiral movement around a zero point that initiates matter formation. Phi is "the first ordering constant of life."
- **Pi determines the size of matter.** Pi circumscribes the spiral, wrapping it in a sphere. The limitless is in forms, not in size.
- **Euler gives consistency to the toroid.** Euler's number (2.718...) solidifies the structure, determining the area under the curve. "Euler adjusts to almost all the properties required to describe the strong interactions of elementary particles."
- **The Pentaflor process.** From void → spiral (phi) → sphere (pi) → solidification (Euler) → Flower of Life. This is a geometric creation story — form precedes matter, and the three constants are the code.
- **Consciousness fixes the zero point.** "The first movement of consciousness performed to create all matter — literally to create the atoms that constitute the universe — is to fix a zero point, a focus of attention." Consciousness is the initiator of geometric organization.

**Information aspect:** Psychogeometry provides the bridge between sacred geometry and physics. Its claim that geometric constants encode the energy-to-matter conversion is the informational encoding principle — form as the code that organizes energy into matter. The zero point as "focus of attention" places consciousness at the origin of the geometric process.

## Cross-Domain Connections

### The Shape-Information Principle

The most striking convergence is the repeated assertion, across all six traditions, that **field shape carries information and shape matters more than amplitude.** Manzalini states this explicitly in the language of quantum potential theory: Q ∝ ∇²R/R, where the curvature of the amplitude function — not the amplitude itself — determines the physical effect. The French form wave tradition says the same in the language of radiesthesia: the geometry of the form, not its material composition, determines the emitted wave. Sheldrake says it in the language of biology: morphic resonance is shape-based — similar forms resonate, regardless of distance or energy. Shipov says it in the language of geometry: torsion is the geometry of rotation, and torsion signals carry information without energy.

This is not vague analogy. In each tradition, the claim has a specific mathematical or empirical form:
- Manzalini: Q and C are proportional to the Laplacian of the amplitude function — a curvature measure.
- Shipov: Torsion is the antisymmetric part of the affine connection — a geometric property of spacetime.
- Sheldrake: Morphic resonance depends on form similarity — a pattern-matching process.
- Chaumery/de Belizal: Form waves depend on geometric shape — an empirical radiesthetic observation.
- Ponce de León: Phi/pi/Euler encode the geometric transformation from energy to matter.

### The Coherence Domain Bridge

Manzalini and Del Giudice share more than nationality. They share the QFT mechanism: spontaneous symmetry breaking produces NG boson condensates, which encode ordering information as form factors. Del Giudice's water coherence domains are the biological instance of what Manzalini describes theoretically — the organism's consciousness field is maintained through the same symmetry breaking processes that maintain water's coherence domains.

This bridge is critical because it connects the abstract (consciousness as field) to the concrete (water as the biological substrate of quantum coherence). If water's EZ phase is a macroscopic NG boson condensate — as Del Giudice argues — then the biological organism is literally a field of coherent information encoded in the geometry of its water structure. The organism does not "have" consciousness; it "is" a coherence structure that processes a consciousness field.

### The Form Wave / Morphic Field / Torsion Convergence

Three traditions — French radiesthesia, British biology, Russian torsion physics — independently describe information fields that:

The French detect them with pendulums. The British detect them through biological morphogenesis and behavioral patterns. The Russians detect them with torsion field generators and detectors. All three claim these fields are not electromagnetic.

If these are the same phenomenon described in three vocabularies, the implications are significant:

## Next Questions

1. **Can the form wave be measured as a torsion signal?** If French form waves and Russian torsion fields are the same phenomenon, a torsion detector should register a signal when a geometric form (e.g., a pyramid, a Platonic solid) is placed nearby — and the signal should correlate with pendulum measurements. This would be the first instrumental cross-validation of the form wave tradition.

2. **Does water's EZ phase respond to consciousness-field effects?** If Manzalini is right that consciousness is a quantum field whose potential is encoded in NG boson condensates, and if Del Giudice is right that water's coherence domains are NG boson condensates, then water should respond to consciousness-directed intention. Experiments on intention-modified water (already claimed in the radionics literature) could be redesigned with EZ-water measurements as the dependent variable.

3. **Is morphic resonance a torsion field phenomenon?** If Sheldrake's morphic fields propagate through form similarity and Shipov's torsion fields propagate through geometric resonance, the two may be identical. Experiments testing whether torsion field generators accelerate crystal formation (a classic morphic resonance test) would bridge British biology and Russian physics.

Shipov's torsion extends Cartan's spacetime torsion. Both describe information encoded in field geometry. If C and torsion can be formally related — if the quantum potential is a special case of torsion, or vice versa — the Italian and Russian programs could be unified.

5. **Does Ponce de León's phi/pi/Euler code map to NG boson condensation patterns?** If the three constants encode the geometric transformation from energy to matter, and NG boson condensates encode the form of the consciousness field, the condensation patterns should exhibit phi, pi, and Euler relationships. This would connect Mexican psychogeometry to Italian QFT.

6. **Can the de Broglie-Bohm extension to living systems be tested?** Manzalini's key claim — that living systems are far from quantum equilibrium and therefore exhibit non-local information exchange — is Valentini's "subquantum" hypothesis applied to biology. If quantum equilibrium violations can be detected in biological systems (e.g., deviations from the Born rule in biological water), this would be the first empirical evidence for the consciousness-as-field model.
