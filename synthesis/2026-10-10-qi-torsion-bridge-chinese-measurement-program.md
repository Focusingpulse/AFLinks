# The Qi-Torsion Bridge: China's Measurement Program for External Qi and Its Independent Convergence with Russian Field Physics

*Synthesis by Drunvalo — 2026-10-10*

---

## Abstract

Between 1987 and 2018, three research establishments with no significant contact with each other — the Chinese institutional physics community (Tsinghua University, the Chinese Academy of Sciences Institute of High Energy Physics, National Taiwan University), the Russian vacuum-physics tradition (Kozyrev → Akimov → Shipov), and a quieter Japanese node — arrived at the same identification: **the carrier of the phenomena called "qi" is the torsion (spin) field of the physical vacuum.** The Chinese arrived from the side of measuring qigong masters; the Russians from the side of astronomy and vacuum polarization; the Japanese from instrumentation. The identification was not borrowed — Li Si-Chen's 2006 paper explicitly notes that static torsion was "dismissed by mainstream physics" and that "Russian physicists studied it for 50 years" *as a foreign body of work to be compared against his own experimental results* [6].

This synthesis traces the thread through sixteen sources: the 1988 challenge editorial in *Atomic Energy Science and Technology* [1], the 1987 Guangming Daily report of Tsinghua's qigong research [2], Lu Zuyin's account of the Yan Xin external-qi experiments [3], the CAS Institute of High Energy Physics study of "information water" [4], Lee Si-Chen's infrared spectroscopy of emitted qi [5], the two papers in which Li identifies qi with torsion [6][7], the Russian experimental line (Kozyrev [9], Akimov/Shipov [10]), the 2018 Bulgaria 7,900-km remote-action experiment that replicated Yan-Xin-style effects with a generator instead of a master [8], the German-Russian M.I.N.D. consciousness-sensing device [11], the Japanese torsion research node [12], the 2025 Sanwa information-theoretic reframing of qigong [13], and the survey of Li's three books [14]. The Indian prana measurement discussion of the same decade [15] and Li's later finger-reading information-field work [16] appear as supporting parallels.

The convergent claim: **directed biological intention produces repeatable, instrumentable effects on matter — most reliably on water — and the only physical model independently proposed for it on both sides of Eurasia is the spin/torsion field.** A second, quieter convergence runs underneath: Li's finding that crystalline qi interacts with *geometric structures and special characters* [7] is, without contact, the French form-wave claim — shape generates field.

## 1. The Opening Challenge (1987–1988)

### 1.1 A nuclear journal publishes qigong

In May 1988, *Atomic Energy Science and Technology* (Vol. 22, No. 3) — a mainstream Chinese nuclear-science journal — carried a paper whose title was itself a gauntlet: **"A Challenge to Modern Physics — On the Study of Qigong and the Human Superfunction"** [1]. The venue matters more than the rhetoric: this was not a fringe pamphlet but a peer venue of the Chinese nuclear establishment, and its willingness to print the paper signals how far the institutional measurement program had already gone.

A year earlier, the Guangming Daily had reported Tsinghua University's qigong research program with a plain, bureaucratic headline claim: effects demonstrated **"from the cellular to the molecular level"** [2]. The report is short, but it fixes the date at which qigong effects stopped being a matter of testimony and became a matter of laboratory protocol inside China's most prestigious technical university.

### 1.2 Why this is different from Western parapsychology

The Western parapsychology tradition (Rhine, and later the PEAR lab) worked at the noise floor — small statistical deviations fought over for decades. The Chinese program began from the opposite end: **macroscopic claims by named masters, tested against physical instrumentation.** The question was never "does the effect exist at p < 0.05" but "what does the effect *do* to a spectrometer, a cell culture, a water sample." That framing choice — physics first, statistics second — is what eventually forced the model question: if the instrument moves, what field is moving it?

## 2. The Tsinghua Experiments: Yan Xin and External Qi

Lu Zuyin (陆祖荫), a Tsinghua physicist, opens his account *Qigong Exploration* with the institutional memory [3]:

> "Around the 1960s, people began to recognize that practising qigong could strengthen the body. But because 'qi' could not be seen or touched, and because the qigong masters who taught it were..." [3]

The passage is translated from the Chinese; the original terms it carries are 氣功 (qìgōng — "qi cultivation") and 外氣 (wàiqì — "external qi," qi emitted outward from the body), the operational quantity of the whole program.

The Yan Xin (严新) collaboration at Tsinghua became the program's flagship: a qigong master emitting external qi at physical samples while instruments recorded. The claims that propagated through the archive — and that the Russian-side experimenters would later cite — were two: **measurable changes in the spectra of water samples**, and **persistence of the effect at distances up to 2,000 km** [3][8]. Both claims are stated as instrumented observations, not impressions: spectra and distances.

Whatever one's prior on the phenomenon, the structural point stands: by 1988, China's top physics institutions had committed a decade of laboratory time to the proposition that external qi is a *physical* influence on matter. The model question was now unavoidable.

## 3. Information Water: The CAS Institute of High Energy Physics

The most striking institutional artifact in the cluster is the experimental study of **Xiang Gong "information water"** by Yuan Jiali and Yang Yulin of the **Institute of High Energy Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences** [4]:

> "At present, the qigong circles call water that has been treated by emitting qi 'information water'..." [4]

The Chinese term is 信息水 (xìnxī shuǐ) — literally "information water."

> *"Information water" — water carrying the imprint of emitted qi, studied at the CAS Institute of High Energy Physics.*

An IHEP is a particle-physics laboratory. Its appearance in this thread matters for the same reason the 1988 nuclear journal did: the Chinese measurement program was not a cottage industry of believers but a line item inside the hard-science establishment. And their choice of *detector* is the thread's first major convergence: **water**. The same detector the Benveniste line chose in France, the same detector Pollack's EZ physics implicates, the same detector the memory-bearing-medium synthesis (2026-10-03) [S3] traced across a dozen independent groups — chosen here independently, in Beijing, because qi-treated water is where the claimed effect is easiest to instrument.

## 4. The Taiwanese Synthesis: Li Si-Chen

### 4.1 Spectroscopy first

At National Taiwan University, electrical-engineering professor — later NTU president — **Lee Si-Chen (李嗣涔)** began where an instrument engineer should: by taking spectra. His study *The Infrared Spectrum of Qigong States and Emitted External Qi* [5] applied infrared spectroscopy to qigong practitioners and to emitted external qi itself. The importance is methodological, not merely evidential: emitted qi was being treated as a *signal* — something with a spectrum, hence a structure, hence a physics.

### 4.2 The identification: qi = torsion field

In 2006, in the *Life Science Bulletin*, Li made the model explicit [6]:

> "Torsion field (撓場, also spin field / axion field) is the field generated by space-time torsion from an object's spin angular momentum — a proposed fifth force beyond the four standard interactions." [6]

> *撓場 (náo chǎng) — "torsion field," Li Si-Chen's proposed physical substrate of qi.*

Li's own summary of the field's standing is brutally honest: static torsion-field strength is proportional to G·h, making it on the order of 10²⁷ times weaker than gravity, "hence dismissed by mainstream physics" — and yet, he notes, "Russian physicists studied it for 50 years," claiming long experimental experience with it [6]. This is the bridge sentence of the whole synthesis: a Taiwanese EE professor, looking at his own qigong data, reaches for the one vacuum-physics tradition that took spin fields seriously — and it is the *Russian* one, which he explicitly marks as an independent body of work.

His experimental grounds for the identification were equally specific: "Preliminary transmission experiments found the physical properties of **crystal-generated Qi and generator-produced torsion fields similar in many respects**" [6]. A crystal "qi generator" and a torsion generator produced, in his apparatus, the same class of effects.

### 4.3 The second convergence: geometry

A decade later, with Hsiung-Kung Tsai and Wei-Chieh Liang, Li published *The Physical Essence of Crystalline Qi — Torsion Field* in *Buddhism and Science* (2016) [7]. Its key claims:

- The physical essence of crystalline qi **is** the torsion field — the 2006 hypothesis now stated as identification, connecting Buddhist qigong phenomenology with torsion-field physics.
- "Crystal Qi **interacts with special characters and geometric structures**" [7].

The second claim deserves slow reading. A Taipei laboratory, working from Buddhist qigong phenomenology, reports that the qi signal responds to **shape** — to written characters and geometric structures. This is, nearly word for word, the founding claim of French radiesthesia (the ondes de forme of Chaumery and de Belizal), of the form-field continuum synthesis (2026-09-15) [S2], and of BioGeometry's shape-resonance principle — traditions with no institutional contact with the NTU program. The archive now holds the shape-generates-field claim from three mutually isolated directions: **French pendulum radiesthesia, Egyptian BioGeometry, and Taiwanese crystal-qi experiments.**

Li's later work extended the interaction to *finger-reading of special keywords* — characters as information-field probes [16] — and his three books (*The Science of Torsion Fields*, *The Science of the Spirit Realm*, *Scientific Qigong*) state the full program [14]:

> "The force produced by the torsion field is weaker than universal gravitation, yet it is a wondrous force. It originally formed part of general relativity, but at the time it was overlooked by Einstein, until in the 1920s..." [14]

— the 1920s reference being Cartan's geometrization of torsion, the same Cartan who appears in the Russian line's self-history [10].

## 5. The Russian Line: From Kozyrev's Time to Akimov's Spin

The Russian tradition did not start from qigong at all. It started from **astronomy and the direction of time**. Kozyrev's first experiments, as reconstructed by Pravdivtsev [9], were laboratory physics with gyroscope sensors:

- "Time possesses course, direction, and density; processes release or absorb Time."
- "Organization (negentropy) increase is accompanied by Time release; entropy increase by Time absorption."
- "**Material memory of Time exposure:** lead retains ~14 s, aluminum ~28 s, wood ~70 s" [9].

That third claim — materials retaining a measurable after-effect of a time-flow perturbation, with substance-specific relaxation times — is the Russian version of information water: the claim that matter *keeps* an imprint. The Chinese said it about qi-treated water; Kozyrev said it about lead and wood exposed to irreversible processes. Neither cites the other.

By the 1990s the tradition had consolidated into the Akimov–Shipov torsion program [10], whose canonical review *Torsion Fields and Their Experimental Manifestations* reads like an engineering prospectus — torsion energy sources, torsion propulsion, torsion materials production, torsion communication — grounded in one methodological maxim:

> "The adequacy of our understanding of Nature is proportional to our knowledge of the laws acting within it." [10]

Shipov's *Theory of the Physical Vacuum* — «Теория физического вакуума: теория, эксперименты и технологии» ("Theory of the Physical Vacuum: theory, experiments and technologies") — supplies the formal apparatus: the vacuum as a structured medium whose spin-polarization states carry the torsion field. Where the Chinese program asked "what field does a master emit?", the Russian program answered from first principles: **the only long-range field the vacuum supports beyond the four interactions is spin torsion.** Li reached the same answer from a spectrometer.

## 6. Closing the Loop: The 2018 Bulgaria Experiment and the Instrumentation Line

### 6.1 7,900 kilometres, no master

The thread's most consequential single document may be the scalarwave.cc account of the coordinated experiment run during the 13th Water Conference (Bulgaria, 18–21 October 2018) [8]:

- A torsion generator in the conference room applied "torsion-field intent" to **photographs of water samples located in China** — 7,900 km away.
- Two EIS (electrochemical impedance spectroscopy) instruments monitored the Chinese samples.
- "Claimed result: clear time correlation between torsion-generator on/off periods and the EIS curves, with decline beginning immediately after intervention" [8].

The author then makes the connection this synthesis exists to record:

> "The author draws an explicit parallel to Yan Xin's claimed 2,000-km remote influence on water spectra and DNA, proposing torsion fields as the physical essence of external Qi." [8]

Read the structure of that sentence. A 2018 European conference experiment uses a **machine**; the 1980s Tsinghua experiments used a **master**. Same detector (water spectra), same protocol shape (remote on/off versus instrument trace), same claimed result. The variable the Chinese program could not remove — the human — is removed. If the claim holds, whatever the master emitted, the generator emits. And the generator is a torsion device.

### 6.2 Sensing consciousness directly: M.I.N.D.

The German node of the same circle, Serge Kernbach's team, has built the inverse instrument: **M.I.N.D. (Mind Influence Sensing Device)** [11] — a detector intended to register consciousness influence as a torsion-channel signal. The qigong master's "emission" and the meditator's "intention" become, in this lineage, the same measurable quantity, and the device is its voltmeter. This is the instrumentation-of-the-invisible thread (2026-09-13 synthesis) extended to its logical end: not metaphor — a product.

### 6.3 The Japanese node

The scalarwave survey of Japanese torsion research opens with a plain observation [12]:

> "Why write an article specifically about Japan? Because Japan is absolutely a scientific and technological power..." [12]

Japan's node is quieter but real, and its framing drifts information-theoretic: the 2025 Sanwa Qigong essay defines the practice as **"the tuning of information — reintegrating consciousness, body, and cosmos"** [13]:

> "When we quietly observe the breath, we are not merely inhaling air. We are exchanging..." [13]

Where Beijing said *field* and Moscow said *vacuum spin*, the Japanese reframing says *information*. These are not rivals; they are the same triangle the archive keeps rediscovering — field, geometry, information — seen from three shores.

## 7. The Indian Parallel

The archive's growing Hindi cluster shows the same transition happening a third time, in a third language. The *Akhand Jyoti* line (1986) ran essays with titles like *Prana-shakti and Scientific Opinion*, and the prana-urja literature states the program in its own terms [15]:

> "मनुष्य की प्राण ऊर्जा और उसका अभिवर्धन" [15]

> *"Man's Prana Energy and Its Augmentation" — the Indian framing of the same measurement ambition.*

Prana, qi, torsion, information: four vocabularies, one claimed phenomenon class — a trainable biological influence on matter, strongest in water, weak against gravity, shaped by geometry, carried by no known electromagnetic channel. The convergence is not proof. But the *independence* of the convergence is itself the datum the archive exists to hold.

## 8. Convergent Claims Across Independent Traditions

Laid side by side, the sixteen sources make five claims that recur across at least two mutually isolated traditions each:

1. **Emitted intention is instrumentable.** Infrared spectra of emitted qi (NTU [5]); gyroscope sensors of time-flow (Kozyrev [9]); M.I.N.D. device [11].
2. **Water is the preferred detector.** CAS information water [4]; Yan Xin water spectra [3][8]; EIS water monitoring at 7,900 km [8]; the memory-bearing-medium line [S3].
3. **The effect is distance-insensitive in a way EM cannot be.** 2,000 km (Yan Xin [3]); 7,900 km (Bulgaria [8]); Kozyrev's astronomical-scale time flows [9].
4. **The carrier is spin/torsion, a fifth-interaction candidate ~10²⁷× weaker than gravity yet macroscopically claimed.** Li 2006 [6]; Akimov/Shipov [10]; Shipov's vacuum theory.
5. **Geometry modulates the field.** Crystal qi interacting with characters and geometric structures [7]; French ondes de forme and the form-field continuum [S2]; BioGeometry shape resonance.

No single tradition in the archive makes all five claims; the *set* of traditions, disjoint by language and institution, covers all five repeatedly. That is the pattern. Whether it is a pattern about nature or a pattern about what isolated research cultures converge on when they chase subtle effects — the archive cannot yet say, and the verification-turn synthesis (2026-09-17) [S1] prescribes exactly the rails needed to find out.

## 9. The Archive Gaps

1. **Li Si-Chen's three books are untranslated.** *The Science of Torsion Fields*, *The Science of the Spirit Realm*, and *Scientific Qigong* are represented only by a survey [14]. They are the primary statement of the qi-torsion identification.
2. **The primary Chinese institutional papers are absent.** The 1988 *Atomic Energy Science and Technology* issue (Vol. 22 No. 3) [1], the Tsinghua Yan Xin experiment protocols [3], and the CAS IHEP information-water data [4] exist in the archive only as secondary accounts and translations of web reprints.
3. **No replication data for the 7,900-km experiment beyond the scalarwave.cc account** [8] — no raw EIS curves, no protocol document, no third-party witness report.
4. **Kozyrev's laboratory notebooks** remain outside the archive; the experimental-science-of-time account [9] is a reconstruction.
5. **The Indian prana measurement program** is represented only at magazine level (*Akhand Jyoti*); the laboratory literature, if it survives, is not yet in the archive.

Each gap is a concrete scout target, and the first two are now in the family upgrade bank.

## 10. Conclusion

The qi-torsion bridge is the archive's cleanest example of what it was built to detect: **the same identification, made independently, on opposite sides of a language barrier, from opposite directions of investigation.** The Chinese program measured masters and asked what field could carry the signal; the Russian program built the vacuum's spin physics and asked what phenomena it would predict; the answer on both sides is torsion. The 2018 Bulgaria experiment is the hinge: it claims the master's effect from a machine, on the Chinese samples' own terms — water spectra at intercontinental distance.

Underneath runs the quieter second bridge: shape. Li's crystalline qi answers to characters and geometry, and so does the French pendulum, and so does the Egyptian temple science. The archive's next question is therefore not whether these traditions *describe* the same thing — the convergence is documented — but whether any of their shared claims can be pushed through the verification rail: a blinded, pre-registered, instrument-shared replication of the generator-versus-water protocol, with the EIS raw data public.

That experiment, more than any theory paper, is what this thread needs next.
