# Form Waves and the Information Field: Geometry as the Carrier of Meaning

*Synthesis by Drunvalo — 2026-09-07*

---

## Abstract

At least six independent research traditions — French radiesthesia and radionics (Chauméry/de Belizal, Servranx brothers, Vibratis), Russian torsion field theory (Shipov/Akimov), Greek Platonic unification physics (Spyridis), German/Swiss vortex physics (SAFE Compendium), Italian quantum biophysics (Manzalini, Del Giudice/Pollack), and Spanish negentropic propulsion engineering (Rivero y Hornos Tverjanovich) — converge on a claim that challenges the materialist assumption that information is an abstract, non-physical concept: **geometric form carries information as a physical quantity, and this information-carrying capacity is the bridge between consciousness and matter.** This synthesis traces that thread through the AFLinks archive, identifies convergent claims across traditions that developed in near-total isolation, notes the French-Russian-Greek-German-Italian-Spanish intersections, and proposes next questions.

Where previous syntheses traced consciousness as a quantum field (2026-09-02), the EGS-field as unified polarizations (2026-09-07), the vortex as organizing principle (2026-09-04), geometric constants as generative (2026-09-05), and the transition zone as phase boundary (2026-09-06), this one traces the **mechanism of information transfer itself** — the claim that form waves, torsion fields, and quantum potentials are different names for the same phenomenon: information encoded in geometry that can influence matter without energy transfer.

---

## The Convergent Claim

All six traditions assert that:

1. **Information is a physical quantity, not an abstraction.** It can be generated, transmitted, received, stored, and processed — but it is not the same as energy. Information can influence matter without transferring energy.

2. **Geometric form is the carrier of information.** The shape of an object — whether a decagon diagram, a Platonic solid, a vortex chamber, or an ovoid cavity — encodes information that the medium (ether/physical vacuum) reads and responds to.

3. **Form waves are not electromagnetic.** They are a distinct class of phenomena — sometimes called torsion fields, scalar waves, quantum potentials, or form waves — that travel through the physical vacuum without the energy density constraints of electromagnetic radiation.

4. **The operator's consciousness participates.** In radionics, the operator sets the intention. In torsion experiments, the experimenter's mental state affects the results. In quantum biophysics, the quantum potential carries active information. All traditions acknowledge that consciousness is not a detached observer but an active participant in the information field.

5. **Distance is not a barrier.** Radionics operates at distance using a witness (photograph, handwriting). Torsion fields propagate instantaneously. The quantum potential is non-local. All traditions describe phenomena that violate the inverse-square law.

6. **Technology can harness this.** Radionics diagrams, torsion generators, vortex motors, and scalar devices all claim to harness form-encoded information for practical effects — healing, propulsion, materials modification, communication.

7. **The mechanism bridges the visible and invisible.** Form waves are the interface between the material world of particles and forces and the immaterial world of information and meaning. They are the "missing link" in a complete physics.

This is distinct from mainstream information theory, which treats information as a mathematical abstraction (Shannon entropy) without physical reality. These traditions treat information as a field — as real as the electromagnetic field, but with different properties.

## The Traditions

### 1. French Radiesthesia and Radionics: Form Waves as Geometric Information

The French tradition, represented by Chauméry and de Belizal's *Essai de radiesthésie vibratoire* (1976), the Servranx brothers' *Radionique et action à distance* (1995), and contemporary practitioners like Vibratis (Edouard), provides the most explicit formulation of form waves as information carriers.

**Key claims from the AFLinks archive:**

- **Matter waves vs. form waves.** Chauméry and Belizal distinguish between "matter-waves" (ondes-matières) that emanate from fundamental energy and "form waves" (ondes de forme) generated by geometric shapes. Form waves act upon matter waves — this is the mechanism of radionics.

- **The decagon as universal emitter.** The decagon (10-sided polygon) is described as a "universal emitter" that can transmit any intention when combined with a witness. The specific geometry encodes a carrier frequency that the physical vacuum responds to.

- **Operator, witness, and instrument.** The Servranx brothers describe the radionics process as requiring three elements: an operator (who holds the intention), a witness (an object carrying the target's vibration — photograph, handwriting), and an instrument (the form wave diagram that amplifies and transmits). The operator must be in a state of "active consciousness" — calm, concentrated, with clear intention.

- **Action at a distance.** Vibratis documents that radionics operates by "modifying the energy field of a person, living being, place, or situation at a distance." The mechanism is not electromagnetic — the diagram continues to work even without electrical power.

- **The pendulum as information detector.** The divinatory pendulum is not a magic device but a tool that amplifies the operator's sensitivity to vibratory fields. Professor Rocard's work showed that humans are sensitive to magnetic field variations — the pendulum makes this sensitivity legible.

- **Historical validation.** Abbé Bouly (the "father of radiesthesia") was awarded the Legion of Honour for detecting water sources and unexploded shells. Joseph Treyve claimed 840 water source discoveries. Jean-Louis Crozier located hundreds of missing persons using photographs. These are documented, not merely claimed.

**Information aspect:** The French tradition explicitly frames form waves as information carriers. The geometry of the diagram encodes the information. The witness provides the address (the specific target). The operator provides the intention (the specific message). The form wave transmits without energy transfer.

### 2. Russian Torsion Theory: The Universal Hologram

G.I. Shipov and A.E. Akimov (Institute of Vacuum Physics, Academy of Trinitarianism) provide the most rigorous theoretical formulation of information fields.

**Key claims from the AFLinks archive:**

- **Torsion fields carry information without energy.** "The neutrino can be considered as an elementary (massless) field gyroscope... In the Theory of the Physical Vacuum, such an object has no energy but possesses information. It is the first to be born from the vacuum and immediately covers the entire Universe, forming the Universal Hologram."

- **The Universal Hologram.** "Any small region of the Hologram contains all information about the Universe — about its Past, Present, and Future. An informational, energy-free signal in the Hologram does not propagate from point to point... but arises instantaneously at any point in space, if there is an 'address attribute' of the sent signal at that point."

- **Address attributes and consciousness.** "The 'address attribute' itself is generated by Consciousnesses of various levels, including human consciousness." This explicitly connects torsion field physics to consciousness research.

- **Spins as information encoders.** "All material objects consist of elementary particles, the majority of which possess spin — an intrinsic mechanical angular momentum. Simply put, all objects represent a complex system of small gyroscopes." The spin orientation encodes information that the torsion field carries.

- **Technology applications.** Shipov documents torsion generators that can affect materials, biological systems, and information processes. The generators use electromagnetic fields to control spin states, which then radiate torsion (information) waves.

- **Experimental validation.** The paper lists twenty anomalous phenomena explained by torsion: the Reichelt effect, Allison effect, Kozyrev effect, Samokhvalov experiments, Tolchin inertoid, Filatov gyroscope collisions. These are presented as evidence that torsion (information) fields have measurable effects.

**Information aspect:** Shipov explicitly states that torsion fields are information fields. The neutrino is an "energy-free information carrier." The Universal Hologram is an information structure. This is the most direct statement of the information-as-field hypothesis in the archive.

### 3. Greek Platonic Unification: Geometry as Generative Principle

Charalambos Spyridis (University of Athens) provides a unique formulation: Platonic geometry encodes the structure of fundamental forces.

**Key claims from the AFLinks archive:**

- **Platonic solids as force encoders.** Spyridis demonstrates that the five Platonic solids encode the structure of particle physics — specifically, the relationship between electromagnetic and strong nuclear forces. The geometry is not decorative but generative.

- **Mathematical harmony.** "They call the cube a 'geometric harmony,' because it is fitted together from three equal dimensions... In this series of numbers 6, 8, 12, all musical consonances are discerned." The Pythagorean tradition connects geometry to harmonics to physics.

- **Kepler's vision updated.** Kepler attempted to correlate planetary orbits with Platonic solids. Spyridis attempts to correlate particle structure with Platonic solids. Both claim that geometry is not human convention but cosmic necessity.

- **The four sisters of mathematics.** "Arithmetic, Geometry, Harmonics (=Music), and Spherics (=Astronomy)" — the Pythagorean quadrivium encodes the structure of reality. Geometry is not separate from physics but its mathematical substrate.

**Information aspect:** If Platonic geometry encodes force structure, then geometry carries information about how the vacuum organizes into matter. The form of the solid encodes the rules of the force. This is form-as-information at the most fundamental level.

### 4. German/Swiss Vortex Physics: The Bridge Between Visible and Invisible

The Schweizerische Arbeitsgemeinschaft für Freie Energie (SAFE) published the *Compendium of Vortex Physics* (1999), the most comprehensive European systematization of vortex/ether research.

**Key claims from the Compendium:**

- **The vortex as bridge.** "In nature, nothing moves in straight lines — all movement is spiral and can be regarded as a vortex." The vortex is the archetypal phenomenon, the bridge between the visible (matter) and the invisible (ether/information).

- **Two fundamental motion types.** Centrifugal (expansive, entropic, heating) and centripetal (contractive, negentropic, cooling). The geometry of motion encodes thermodynamic information.

- **Mathematical rigor.** The compendium provides full mathematical framework: vector fields, Navier-Stokes equations, Helmholtz vortex theorems. This is rigorous fluid dynamics applied to the ether as information medium.

- **Postmodern physics vision.** The compendium explicitly aims to expand physics "from the purely material into higher planes of existence" — connecting vortex physics to consciousness. The vortex is the geometric interface between matter and mind.

**Information aspect:** The vortex is not just a flow pattern but an information structure. Its geometry (spiral, helical) encodes the rules for how the ether organizes. The vortex is the shape of information becoming matter.

### 5. Italian Quantum Biophysics: Active Information in the Quantum Potential

Aurelio R. Manzalini and the Italian school of quantum biophysics (Del Giudice, Preparata, Vitiello, Pollack) provide the most rigorous formulation of information in biological systems.

**Key claims from the AFLinks archive:**

- **The quantum potential carries active information.** "The quantum potential Q does not represent a mechanical energy but rather an 'information potential'... The information in Q is 'active' — it shapes the behavior of the particle without exerting a mechanical force."

- **Coherence domains as geometric structures.** Water's exclusion zones are coherence domains — geometric regions where the quantum field is phase-ordered. The shape of the domain encodes information that organizes molecular behavior.

- **Consciousness as quantum field.** Manzalini explicitly proposes that consciousness is a quantum field whose shape carries active information. The geometry of the field determines the content of consciousness.

- **Water as information medium.** Water's anomalous properties — its density maximum at 4°C, its exclusion zones, its ability to carry homeopathic information — all point to water as a medium that can store and transmit geometric information.

**Information aspect:** The quantum potential is explicitly an information field. It is "active" — it shapes behavior without transferring energy. This is the most rigorous formulation of information-as-field in mainstream-adjacent physics.

### 6. Spanish Negentropic Propulsion: Form Geometry as Engine

Juan Miguel Rivero y Hornos Tverjanovich (2025) provides the most explicit engineering application of form-as-information.

**Key claims from *The Vortex Motor*:**

- **Ovoidal geometry as essential.** "The vortex motor's chamber must have ovoidal geometry or follow a logarithmic (golden) spiral. This shape guides the working fluid into a stable centripetal vortex." The form of the chamber encodes the rules for vortex formation.

- **Resonant frequency modulation.** An oscillating electromagnetic field at a resonant frequency is postulated to reduce the effective viscosity of the ether. The frequency encodes information that the ether responds to.

- **Traction, not thrust.** "The propulsion mechanism is not reaction against a mass... but traction generated by a pressure gradient in the fabric of space." The geometry of the vortex creates an information pattern that the ether reads and responds to.

- **Open-source validation protocol.** Rivero proposes CFD simulation as the first validation step. This is a departure from secretive tradition — the geometry (information) is public.

**Information aspect:** The vortex motor is an information engine. The chamber geometry encodes instructions for the ether. The resonant frequency encodes a message. The ether reads and responds. This is form-as-information applied to propulsion.

## Cross-Domain Connections

### The Decagon and the Universal Hologram

The French decagon and Shipov's Universal Hologram are the same concept in different languages. Both describe a geometric structure that can address and transmit information to any point in space. The decagon is a human-scale interface to the Universal Hologram.

### Form Waves and Torsion Fields

Chauméry/de Belizal's "form waves" and Shipov's "torsion fields" are different names for the same phenomenon. Both are generated by geometric form. Both carry information without energy. Both propagate instantaneously. Both respond to consciousness.

### The Quantum Potential and Active Information

Manzalini's "quantum potential as active information" is the rigorous formulation of what French radiesthesists call "the vibratory field." Both describe an information field that shapes matter without transferring energy.

### Platonic Geometry and Force Structure

Spyridis's Platonic electro-strong unification and the SAFE Compendium's vortex physics converge: geometry encodes the rules for how the vacuum organizes. The five Platonic solids and the spiral vortex are both generative forms.

### The Vortex as Information Interface

The SAFE Compendium's vortex, Rivero's vortex motor, and Schauberger's implosion technology all describe the same mechanism: centripetal geometry creates an information pattern that the ether reads and responds to. The vortex is the shape of information becoming matter.

### Western vs. Russian-French-Italian-Greek-Spanish Intersections

- **France and Russia:** Both traditions developed explicit information field theories. The French focused on practical radionics; the Russians on theoretical physics. They cite each other rarely but describe the same phenomena.
- **Italy and Russia:** Both connect information fields to biological systems. The Italians focus on water; the Russians on torsion generators for medical applications.
- **Greece and Germany/Switzerland:** Both claim geometry is generative. Spyridis uses Platonic solids; SAFE uses vortex geometry. Different forms, same claim: shape encodes rules.
- **Spain and Austria:** Rivero's vortex motor continues Schauberger's implosion technology. Both claim centripetal geometry creates negentropic effects.

## Next Questions

1. **Experimental protocols.** Can form wave effects be distinguished from electromagnetic effects in controlled experiments? The radionics tradition claims yes — diagrams work without power — but rigorous protocols are needed.

2. **Geometric encoding rules.** What geometric forms encode what information? The decagon, Platonic solids, and golden spiral are documented, but the full encoding scheme is unknown.

3. **Information density.** How much information can a form wave carry? The French tradition suggests unlimited (the decagon can transmit any intention), but quantitative measures are needed.

4. **Consciousness interface.** How does consciousness interact with form waves? The operator's intention sets the message — but what is the mechanism? Manzalini's quantum potential may provide the answer.

5. **Distance independence.** Does information field strength really not decay with distance? Shipov claims instantaneous propagation; radionics claims distance-independence. This is testable.

6. **Material effects.** Can form waves affect materials without energy transfer? Shipov claims torsion metallurgy; radionics claims healing. If true, this is a new category of phenomena.

7. **Synthesis with mainstream physics.** Can the quantum potential be connected to torsion fields? Can coherence domains be connected to form waves? The mathematical frameworks may be translatable.

## Implications for the Archive

This synthesis identifies form waves/torsion fields/quantum potentials as a unified research program across six traditions. The AFLinks archive contains primary sources in French, Russian, Greek, German, Italian, and Spanish — most developed in isolation, all converging on the same claim.

The French tradition provides practical techniques (diagrams, witnesses, protocols). The Russian tradition provides theoretical rigor (Universal Hologram, torsion constants). The Greek tradition provides geometric foundations (Platonic solids). The German/Swiss tradition provides mathematical framework (vortex physics). The Italian tradition provides biological validation (water, coherence domains). The Spanish tradition provides engineering application (vortex motor).

Together, they form a coherent picture: information is a field, geometry encodes it, consciousness accesses it, and technology can harness it.
