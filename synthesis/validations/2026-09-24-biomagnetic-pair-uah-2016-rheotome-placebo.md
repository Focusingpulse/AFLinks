# Validation — The Biomagnetic Pair Under a Rheotome

**Domain:** health / biomagnetism (static-magnet therapy; the Spanish school's diagnostic practice)
**Recorded:** 2026-09-24 (Replication Seeder, run 4)

## What was claimed

The **Par Biomagnético** of Isaac Goiz Durán: that specific paired placements of ordinary static magnets on the body neutralise pathological regions, and — the load-bearing part — that the **kinesiological leg-shortening test** identifies the correct pair, i.e. that a practitioner's hands can *diagnose* the pair with high specificity. The school's strong form claims 100% specificity.

This matters to the Yard because the diagnostic instrument here is a person, not a box. A claim whose instrument is the operator can only be graded by blinding the operator or by moving the endpoint onto a machine.

## Who tested it, when

**de Juan González de Castejón**, doctoral thesis, **Universidad de Alcalá (UAH)**, 2016; supervised by **Bardasano Rubio**.

- Handle: `ebuah.uah.es` **10017/26679** — CC BY-NC-ND, open access, 81 pp.
- Status: a supervised, examined doctoral thesis — **not** a peer-reviewed journal article, and the corpus records it as **unreplicated since 2015/16**.

## Method

- **Instrument:** rheotome **RH32** (Bernstein–Lapicque lineage) — measures **strength–duration curves** of the **external popliteal sciatic nerve**. An objective electrophysiological readout, not a subjective one.
- **Intervention:** a **0.1 T** magnet pair, placed at the tallo/lumbar regions, 20 minutes.
- **Arms and n:** cases **n = 30 → 42** (magnet pair) vs controls **n = 25** (no magnet), then controls **n = 31** (placebo).
- **Analysis:** paired *t*-tests at pulse durations **0.1 / 1 / 3 / 10 ms** and **0.3 / 30 ms**.

## Result

**A duration-specific signature, and a placebo arm that did nothing.**

- Significant at **0.1 / 1 / 3 / 10 ms**; **null at 0.3 / 30 ms** — *in both studies*.
- Placebo arms **flat (p 0.28–0.99)**.
- Reported shape: not "magnets change nerve excitability" but "magnets change it at particular pulse durations and not others."

Which is the interesting kind of result. A general effect is what a placebo produces; a **duration-signature** is what a physical mechanism is supposed to produce — and it is cheap to re-test by anyone with access to an excitable-tissue rig.

## The thesis's own honest anchors

Recorded verbatim because they are the reason this record is worth filing:

- **"no existe ningún estudio hasta la fecha que demuestre su efecto"** — *no study to date demonstrates its effect.*
- Point-localisation is **"demasiado subjetivos"** — too subjective.
- The therapy **"entra en la magnitud del chamanismo… no obstante los imanes estáticos tienen su propia acción"** — it enters the magnitude of shamanism; nevertheless static magnets have their own action.

That is a thesis in a physiology department saying, in its own text, that the school's claim is unproven — and then reporting a controlled measurement anyway. Both halves belong in the record.

## What this record is *not*

- **Not** a validation of the Biomagnetic Pair's diagnostic practice. This thesis measured nerve excitability under a magnet pair; it did not blind the leg-shortening test, and the corpus's own school literature debates whether the kinesiological test is neurology or operator artifact.
- **Not** independent of the school's orbit in the way the label "independent replication" requires — a single centre, a single thesis, no second group.
- **Not** accompanied by the Marbella numbers. The 14–17 May 2009 external test by **Hilu** (>200 patients in 4 days, pre/post 60,000× dark-field blood microscopy; "80%+ parasite confirmation" + MS best-response, but renal/brain no-response, a prostate-cancer case reinterpreted as misdiagnosis) **never published its statistics**. That is a claim, not a validation, and it is filed here as a claim.

## Honest framing

We hold no brief — the test is the point. Recorded as **claimed / attempted / measured / reported**: a national school's central claim was handed to a doctoral candidate with a rheotome and two control arms; the controls behaved (placebo flat), the measurement returned a specific duration-signature rather than a diffuse effect, and the thesis itself concluded that no study demonstrates the effect. Both statements are true at once, and the tension is the teaching artefact.

For the Yard's own ledger, this is the first record in the **health / biomagnetism** family — a domain that previously held nothing. It also belongs to a small and useful category: tests that were run once, in a form the school did not choose, and then left standing. Nobody has to trust this record. The apparatus is a nerve, a stimulator, and a magnet.
