# Validation — Lattice Energy Converter: a multi-lab replication chain, and the control that splits the claim in two

**Domain:** energy / condensed-matter nuclear science (a gas-metal device — the LENR-adjacent "lattice" claim class)
**Recorded:** 2026-10-13 (Replication Seeder run 12)
**Confidence:** **HIGH** that the *phenomenon* (spontaneous voltage plus anomalous gas conduction from an activated hydrogen-host electrode) is real and reproducible across independent laboratories; **LOW** that the mechanism is nuclear, and **MEDIUM-to-LOW** that the spontaneous voltage is the interesting effect at all — see the control result below, which is the whole reason this record is worth reading.

## What was claimed

The **Lattice Energy Converter (LEC)**, introduced by **F. E. Gordon and H. J. Whitehouse** (InovL, Inc.), is a mechanically simple two-terminal device: an inner *working electrode* — a metal tube or plate surface-activated by **co-depositing palladium (or iron) with hydrogen** — coaxially surrounded, at a gap of roughly **0.5–1.1 mm**, by a *counter electrode* of a different metal, with the gap filled by **hydrogen, deuterium, or air**.

The inventors' claim, in their own words, is that the device:

> "Will spontaneously initiate the production of ionizing radiation and electrical energy based only on the thermal energy in its palladium lattice that is occluded with hydrogen or deuterium… Does not require naturally radioactive materials… Easy to replicate." — Gordon & Whitehouse, LENR Workshop in memory of Dr. M. Srinivasan, January 2021

Their reported behaviour: a spontaneous voltage appearing on first assembly (if not, then after the second or third attempt), sustained for months; a voltage that **rises with temperature** (a working electrode at **185 °C** drives ~**10 µA** and ~**1.5 µW** into a matched load, versus ~300 nW at 140 °C); and a cell that **conducts a current across an otherwise insulating gas gap**. Their stated mechanism — that the hydrogen-occluded lattice emits an unidentified ionising radiation which ionises the gas — is explicitly *not* fixed: *"the mechanism that produces the ionization is unknown."*

The archive's own reason to care is not the mechanism but the **testability**: this is a claim whose apparatus fits on a bench, whose control (an identical device *without* the active layer) is cheap, and which — uniquely in this claim class — **other people actually built and measured**.

## Who tested it, and when

This is the part that makes the LEC unusual: the claim has an **independent replication chain**, not a single assertion.

1. **Gordon & Whitehouse (originating, InovL, USA)** — *"Lattice Energy Converter,"* **JCMNS 35(1):30–48 (2022)**, doi **10.70923/001c.72583**; the follow-up *"LEC II: Iron Hydrogen Host Material,"* **JCMNS 36(1):1–24 (2022)**; *"Scaling up the Lattice Energy Converter,"* **JCMNS 38(1):225–234 (2024)**, doi **10.70923/001c.124959**; *"Progress in Understanding and Scaling Up the LEC,"* **JCMNS 39:14–35 (2025)**, doi **10.70923/001c.134029**. The inventors report **over 100 different LECs tested**, several running continuously for months into a DVM at 1–10 MΩ.
2. **Jean-Paul Biberian (Aix-Marseille University, France)** — replication reported in the inventors' own slides, **2 Nov 2020 and 12 Jan 2021**. First attempt (a Pd–Ag wire, hydrogen, heated to several hundred °C): **no voltage**. After consultation, the wire was additionally electroplated with Pd from a PdBr₂/LiBr bath; the cell then produced **300 mV rising to 500 mV**, and a later concentric-cylinder build produced **540 mV → 740 mV**, with a load sweep from 10 MΩ (0.740 V) down to 1 kΩ (0.05 V). Acknowledged by Gordon & Whitehouse as an independent replication.
3. **Andrew Erickson (Senior Leader, Los Alamos National Laboratory)** — replication dated **23 Nov 2020**: the cell produced **0.115 V into a 5 MΩ load in air**, rising to **0.170 V** with hot air blown in.
4. **Antonio Di Stefano (Prysmian Electronics, Italy)** — began **March 2021**; presented at **ICCF-24 Solid State Energy Summit, Mountain View, 27 July 2022** (preprint: https://lenr-canr.org/acrobat/DiStefanoAexperiment.pdf). **This is the replication with the control campaign, and it is the load-bearing one.**
5. **Alan Smith (UK)** — activated the surface by an electrolytic method and reproduced the LEC (cited as an independent replication by Zhang & Chen, 2025).
6. **Hang Zhang & Si Chen (Qiuran Laboratory, China)** — *"Experimental Results of Lattice Energy Converter LEC,"* **JCMNS 39:241–248 (2025)**, doi **10.70923/001c.134009**. Activated the surface by **iron, nickel or copper plating, or by electrolysis**; measured voltage in **hydrogen, deuterium and air**; excluded contact-EMF and thermocouple effects (Fermi-level argument); reported the experiment *"highly reproducible"*; found **no radiation above background** on a Geiger counter; and stated plainly that the generation mechanism *"is still unclear, and it can't be explained by known theories at present."*
7. **Biberian, Michel & Le Roux (Aix-Marseille / VEGATEC / CNRS, France)** — *"High Temperature LEC Experiments,"* **JCMNS 41:19–24 (2026-06-04)**, doi **10.70923/001c.162978**. Extended the LEC to **~900–950 °C** using the Ni/Cu-nanopowder-in-amorphous-alumina material in which they had previously reported excess heat above ~700 °C; the LEC voltage **begins above ~600 °C** and its temperature dependence **mirrors the excess heat**; the voltage is **higher under vacuum** and occurs **only in a hydrogen atmosphere**, which the authors use to rule out thermionic emission. **Low-information for the replication question** — the visible abstract carries no controls — but it is the rung that links the LEC to the archive's existing excess-heat rail.

The inventors also record a collaboration with **IIT-Kanpur** to replicate with additional instrumentation.

## Method, as reported (the control campaign — Di Stefano, ICCF-24 2022)

The reason to record this replication in detail is that it is a rare case of someone running the controls *before* the experiment, and reporting what they found.

**Device.** A "laboratory" LEC designed for modification: two coaxial metal tubes — **6 mm OD outer (counter electrode, CE), 4 mm OD inner (working electrode, WE)**, both 1 mm wall, **0.5 mm gap** — the WE used to evacuate and backfill the device. Three CE materials tested (brass, copper, aluminium), all with the same brass WE.

**Control devices (identical structure, NO plating on the WE).** Measured with a Keithley **DMM6500** at 10 MΩ input impedance, 20 ms integration, 100-sample averaging, in **air and hydrogen, 300–760 Torr**:

- Capacitance: **30 pF ± 10 pF** for all three metal pairs (confirming identical geometry).
- **Open-circuit voltage: 0.01 mV ± 5 µV** — for *all three* metal pairs, including dissimilar metals. *(This is the result that excludes the galvanic/bimetallic explanation: a bimetallic pair by itself generates nothing here.)*
- **Forced current, −60 V to +60 V: 0 µA ± 0.5 nA** (later refined to a noise floor of ~100 pA). *The untreated device does not conduct.*

**Active device (same body; WE electroplated with a thin iron layer).** The WE was sanded, degreased, and plated in a cell of **¼ HCl (20 %) + ¾ tap water** with four 1 mm iron anode wires, current ramped per the inventors' recipe (0.7 mA → 1.7 mA → 16 mA → 25 mA over ~8 h; the electrode turned matte black). Then inserted into the CE **in air, no hydrogen added**:

- Voltage rose in tens of seconds to ~**242 mV**, and to a peak of ~**330 mV** with the brass CE; aluminium CE **223 mV** with **reversed polarity**; copper CE **−234 mV**.
- Load sweep gave an internal resistance of ~**120 kΩ**, matching the inventors' ICCF-23 figure.
- **Forced current at ±10 V: ~136–140 µA** — versus **<100 pA** on the control device: **about six orders of magnitude more conductive**, with the I–V curve **saturating** (the signature of a limited-charge-carrier *gas* conduction, not conduction through metal or electrolyte).
- Charging a **100 µF** capacitor to **309 mV** in ~90 s (stored energy **4.7 µJ**; internal R ~150 kΩ).
- **No radiation detected** on an alpha-sensitive Geiger–Müller tube (LND712); **no** visible fluorescence of a ZnS(Ag) glow strip.

**Di Stefano's own conclusion, in his words:** *"the ability of ionising the gas is the most surprising and interesting characteristic of the LEC."* He attributes the **spontaneous voltage** to a *secondary* effect — a **bimetallic "gas-phase battery"** (the voltage reverses with an aluminium CE, consistent with electrode potentials) — and the **gas ionisation** to a **primary** effect that is not chemical (the ionisation requires tens to hundreds of eV). The device's useful life is short: the iron plating oxidises in about a day in air, after which the voltage falls to ~50 mV or less.

## Result, as reported

The LEC effect — a spontaneous voltage *and* anomalous conduction across an insulating gas gap from an activated hydrogen-host electrode — **has been reproduced independently by at least five groups** (Biberian, Erickson, Di Stefano, Smith, Zhang & Chen), in air and in hydrogen/deuterium, using both palladium and iron plating, on two continents-plus. Two of those groups report it in institutional or peer-reviewed settings; one is at a US national laboratory.

The **control result** is equally clear and is the part most often dropped from the promotion of such devices: an identical device **without the active layer** generates **no** spontaneous voltage and conducts **~10⁻¹⁰ A** where the active device conducts **~10⁻⁴ A**.

## Confidence

**HIGH that the phenomenon is real and cross-lab reproducible.** The evidence class is the strongest the Yard can ask for short of a blind protocol: multiple independent groups, named actors, described methods, a genuine **control** (the same device minus the active layer), and an effect size (six orders of magnitude in conductance) that cannot be explained by instrument noise or wishful measurement.

**LOW that the mechanism is nuclear, and LOW-to-MEDIUM that the spontaneous voltage is even the phenomenon.** The inventors' own account concedes the mechanism is unknown; their paper claims "ionizing radiation" but Di Stefano's GM tube and Zhang & Chen's Geiger counter both found **nothing above background**; and Di Stefano's controls show the *voltage* is a secondary, electrode-potential effect while the *conduction* is the primary anomaly. The two effects have been reported together so long that they are usually spoken of as one; the controls say they are two. **The honest reading is that this record validates a reproducible bench anomaly, not a reactor.**

**One structural caveat.** All the independent replications trace to the same originating claim and, in several cases, to the inventors' direct advice (Biberian's second build, per Gordon's consultation; IIT-Kanpur in collaboration). This is a *replication chain*, not a set of fully independent programs — which is exactly the pattern the archive's paradigm lane has taught to grade carefully (convergence ≠ independence).

## Honest framing

We hold no brief — the test is the point.

**What this record is:** a rare thing in the energy rail — a claim that other people built, measured, and in one case controlled, and that came back positive on the control's own terms. The LEC's control result (an untreated twin of the same device produces nothing and conducts nothing) is the strongest single piece of evidence in the whole LENR-adjacent shelf, because it is the one design element the field's critics most often say is missing.

**What this record is not:** a validated energy source, and not a demonstration of nuclear reactions. The device delivers **microwatts** (1.5 µW at 185 °C); no group has found radiation above background; the mechanism is admitted unknown by everyone including the inventors; and the voltage half of the claim has a conventional (electrode-potential) explanation that fits the control data. **The anomaly worth chasing is the gas ionisation, not the voltage** — and the right instrument for it is the forced-current measurement, not the open-circuit voltmeter.

**The transferable lesson** — the same one the Yard teaches on the Searl card — is that *the control is what turns a device into a measurement.* Gordon & Whitehouse's device would be one more free-energy claim but for the fact that Di Stefano built the unplated twin, measured 0.01 mV across it, and thereby made the active device's 300 mV a fact about the active layer rather than a fact about the metal tubes.

**What would change this record:** a **blind, pre-registered cross-lab protocol** — identical devices, one shipped to a lab that does not know the inventors, with the active/control distinction held by a third party — and a **radiation measurement that either finds the ionising agent or bounds it** below the GM tube's floor. The 2026 high-temperature paper is the rung that would link this anomaly to the excess-heat rail; its controls are the thing to read when the full text is in hand. **A negative would be as valuable: an unplated twin that *does* conduct would collapse the whole effect into ordinary gas physics.**
