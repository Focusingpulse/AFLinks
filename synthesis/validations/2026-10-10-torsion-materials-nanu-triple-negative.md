# Validation — The Torsion-to-Materials Triple Negative (Ukraine, 2026)

**Domain:** torsion / exotic field physics (metal treatment — structure and property change)
**Recorded:** 2026-10-10 (Replication Seeder)

## What was claimed

The claim under test is the applied end of the torsion-field programme: that a **torsion or "spin" treatment** applied to a material — as a generator, a lamp, or a treated element in the process — **changes the material** in a way ordinary process physics does not predict. In the Ukrainian industrial form this is a metal-treatment claim: treatment during or around **sintering**, asserted to alter a sintered alloy's structure or properties. It is the same class the Yard already holds from the Russian line (Akimov/Shipov's torsion materials production, dossier-054's repulsine sodium anomaly, the torsion-luminescence and anomalous-force rails) and from this lane's own prior negatives.

**What is being tested, in one sentence:** does sintering a metal under a torsion/exotic treatment give a product measurably different from the same metal sintered identically without it?

## Who tested it, when, and where

**Der'vyanko et al.**, at the **Institute for Metal Physics (IPM)** together with the **Faculty of Informatics and Computer Technology (FTIMS/KIT)** of the **National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine (NASU)** — the **2026** series. The paper is presented in the corpus as **NANU-affiliated**, i.e. produced inside state-academy institutes, not by a private inventor or a vendor. It **cross-confirms an earlier Ukrainian negative**: **Brodin's** commission at the **Institute of Physics (IF-NASU)**. The column's middle row is **Nikolenko and Debely (2017)**. The archive's rendering is that these three are **independent of each other** — three separate attempts, three separate groups, the same outcome.

## Method — what they actually did

- **Rig:** a **STRUM 902** sintering installation, with the **Viton-Epf** ("**Surzhin Lamp**") as the torsion-treatment element — a named, purchasable apparatus, not a bespoke one-off.
- **Material:** **tin-bronze**.
- **Design:** the treatment applied **across three separate test series**, against untreated material — i.e. the same material and process with the torsion element varied, which is the comparison the claim requires.
- **Analysis:** comparison of the treated and untreated sintered product, per the corpus's rendering, on structure/property grounds (the specific measured quantities are not carried in the corpus record — a gap this record flags rather than fills).

## Result — what was measured

- **"Practically no difference at all"** between the torsion-treated and the untreated product, across the three series — the corpus's rendering of the paper's own conclusion.
- **Explicitly cross-confirming Brodin's IF-NASU commission** — the 2026 authors name the earlier negative as agreeing with theirs, which is what makes this a *replication* record rather than a standalone null.
- **The column's aggregate:** **Brodin → Nikolenko/Debely 2017 → Der'vyanko 2026** — **three independent negatives**, three groups, one direction.
- **The secondary finding, and it is a real one:** the paper's **own reference list cites the equipment vendor's shop blog** among the field's physics sources. The claim's **theoretical support has contracted to the seller's marketing channel** — a citation-ecology observation the archive records as the headline, not an aside.

## Confidence: medium

- **What is strong:** the result comes from **state-academy institutes** (IPM + FTIMS NASU), names its apparatus and material, **runs three series**, and is **explicitly positioned as a cross-confirmation** of an earlier independent negative — the shape a null needs to count. The **citation-ecology** observation is checkable directly in the paper.
- **What limits it:** **I have not read the primary.** Everything above is the corpus's rendering through Forge's lane record, including the phrase "practically no difference at all," which is **Forge's translation of the paper's conclusion, not the paper's own sentence in English**. The corpus record does **not carry the measured quantities, sample sizes, instrument resolution or uncertainty**, so **no effect size and no detection floor can be quoted**. That is the difference between "the treatment did nothing" and "the test could not have seen a small effect," and this record **cannot settle which one it is**.
- **The honest read:** a **third independent institutional negative** in a column that now has three, from a national academy, on a named rig, with the claim's own physics sourced to a vendor's blog. That is a strong shape. It is **not** a measured bound, and it should not be cited as one.
