# Validation — The Czechoslovak Ether-Drift Attempts, 1985

**Domain:** ether / vacuum (optical and radio-frequency anisotropy; the Czech psychotronika rail)
**Recorded:** 2026-09-24 (Replication Seeder, run 4)

## What was claimed

That the ether is a **physical medium**, and that motion through it is **detectable** — measured, in Daneš's line, by **Michelson–Morley-type experiments** carried out within the **Czechoslovak radio-amateur movement**, with the drift claimed as **detected**. The book places itself against the mainstream null rather than inside it: it is a research history of ether-drift work assembled by a working engineer, not a single paper.

## Who did it, when

**Dr. ing. Josef Daneš**, *Za tajemstvím éteru* (**Behind the Mystery of Ether**), **1985** — 192 pp plus appendices. Reviewed by **doc. dr. ing. Miroslav Joachim** and **ing. Zdeněk Prosek** (both named on the title page — a real editorial chain, not self-publication).

## The claim's place in the Czech rail

The corpus's verification rail reads the Czech tradition as a two-rung line: **Drbal 1949 → Daneš 1985**. Drbal's card (dossier 027 / the 1949 patent ČSFR 91304) is the *form-wave* rung — shape acts on a razor blade. Daneš is the *medium* rung — the question is not whether a shape emits anything, but whether **the vacuum itself has a measurable frame of rest**. Two different claims, one national school, one file.

The rail's own verdict on this rung, quoted for accuracy: *"An independent tradition of ether-drift experimentation outside the mainstream physics establishment. The claim is testable; the independent replication record is thin."*

## What is established, and what is not

**Established by this lane's reading (process):** a named author and date; a named review pair; a 192-page book in the archive with a full translation; a described experiment family (MM-type, radio-amateur, drift claimed); a school that placed it after Drbal; and — the operative fact — **no second experimenter's test of Daneš's measurement anywhere in the corpus.**

**Not established (result and method):** the **magnitude** of the claimed drift, the **arm length / wavelength / frequency** used, the **fringe resolution**, and what **controls** (if any) separated a real anisotropy from apparatus rotation artefacts. This lane could not read the book: `translations/` is **not carried by this repo's tree** (verified 2026-09-24, `git ls-tree -r FETCH_HEAD translations` → 0 entries), and the feed's translation index stores a 220-character excerpt only. **A file this lane could not read is not evidence for a number, and no number is stated here.**

That gap is the point of the record. The ether rail is the archive's **largest** pillar by document count (`Challenges to the Standard Model` 64,634; `Aether, Light & Electricity` 1,893) and, until now, held **zero** Yard records. Its one documented experimenter is a radio amateur with a book — and the one experiment that would grade him is a rotating interferometer, which is named in the corpus as an open rung and specified in **Dossier 038** (filed the same day as this record).

## Honest framing

We hold no brief — the test is the point. Recorded as **claimed / attempted / reported**: an engineer in a radio-amateur movement re-ran the most famous null in physics and reported the opposite, wrote the literature review around it, and got it reviewed and printed; the archive translated it in full; and in the four decades since, no reader of the archive has published a number from it.

For the Yard's ledger, this is the ether rail's **first record of any kind**, and it is deliberately the weakest kind: a trail with a missing measurement. The companion dossier states the correcting experiment — and states, in the same document, that a home interferometer can only reach claims of the *large* kind, which is exactly the kind the corpus's non-null reports claim. **A null from a kitchen table does not close the ether; it closes the classic form of the claim.** Both sentences belong in the record.
