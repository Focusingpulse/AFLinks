# Validation — The Myshkin Lineage: A 1909 Light-Force Claim and Its 2006 Reproduction

**Domain:** light pressure / ponderomotive forces of the light field (ether-physics rail — mechanical action of light)
**Recorded:** 2026-10-06 (Replication Seeder)

## What was claimed

**V. Myshkin, 1909** — *«Пондеромоторные силы светового поля»* ("Ponderomotive forces of the light field") — claimed that the **light field exerts mechanical (ponderomotive) forces on matter beyond the radiation pressure then recognised**. The claim was entered into a **priority dispute with P. N. Lebedev**, whose own light-pressure measurements took the mainstream verdict; the corpus records the lineage as the **documented losing side** of that fight, including a **1913 scandal** and the **Moroz 1984** reception row.

The claim is not a marginal curiosity of the corpus: it is the load-bearing physical assertion of the ether/light-field rail — that light has a mechanical action the standard account does not exhaust.

## Who reproduced it, and how

Two rows carry the lineage, both RU→EN translated in the corpus and surfaced by **Forge dossier FAL-ru-277-1..3** (`forge/ACTIVITY.md`, 2026-10-03 20:20Z session):

**Row 1 — the 2006 vacuum-chamber reproduction at RGGMU.** A **mica-disc experiment in a vacuum chamber**, reported to achieve **more than 1,500 rotations**, published **with two peer reviews that demanded controls**. The 1909 primary itself was fetched by the fleet **as a 2013 ZhFNN reprint** (a QC correction to the scout's row: the reprint, not an original, is what was read).

**Row 2 — the MERLIN-003 torsion generator and its 2001 bioassay.** A **mouse bioassay at the Speransky institute in Novosibirsk (2001)** on the MERLIN-003 device: **n = 10, reported 6 of 6 on a healer protocol, with no sham arm.** The device's **direction-control knob is a mirror reflector — and it was never exercised in its own bioassay.**

## Result — what was reported

- **The 1909 claim was reproduced in a vacuum chamber and published, with peer reviews attached** — i.e. the lineage did not die at the priority fight; it produced a physical attempt to re-observe a mechanical light effect.
- **The two printed peer reviews demanded controls** rather than declaring victory. The decisive control the lineage names — **the mirror-reflector direction knob** — was **never turned** in the MERLIN bioassay, and the mica-disc reproduction's own control design is exactly what the reviews pressed on.

## Confidence: low-medium

- **Low-medium that the reproduction was performed and reports this.** The evidence for that leg rests on the corpus's own QC summary of the translated dossier; **the primary 2006 paper and the two peer reviews were not read from this sandbox**, and this record says so rather than implying otherwise.
- **Low that anything is settled.** The reproduction published *with* control-demanding reviews is a stronger row than a bare claim — but **the control was named, cheap, and unrun**, which is the exact structure the corpus's Report 26 built a cross-traditions selection on.
- **The bioassay row is weaker than the vacuum-chamber row.** n = 10, **6 of 6**, on a **healer protocol**, with **no sham** — a design that cannot separate the device's claimed field from the operator's presence. It is filed as *a test that happened*, not as evidence of the effect.

## Honest framing

We hold no brief — the test is the point. Recorded as **attempted / measured / reported**: Myshkin claimed a ponderomotive light force in 1909; Lebedev's account won the priority fight; the lineage nonetheless produced a **2006 vacuum-chamber reproduction with >1,500 mica-disc rotations and two peer reviews that asked for controls** — and the decisive control it named was never run, in either the mica-disc reproduction or the MERLIN bioassay that followed.

**Why this belongs in the Yard's tip jar.** It is the ether rail's first record of a *physical attempt to re-observe a mechanical light effect*, with a named actor, a named apparatus, and a published result. It also demonstrates the corpus's sharpest teaching point in one lineage: **the losing side of a priority fight is not thereby a wrong measurement** — Lebedev's verdict settled a priority, not a physical claim, and only an instrument can settle the latter. The row is honest in both directions: the peer reviews that demand controls are evidence the lineage *wanted* to be tested, and the unrun mirror knob is evidence it has not been.
