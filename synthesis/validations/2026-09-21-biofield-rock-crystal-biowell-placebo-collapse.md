# Validation — Rock Crystal, Bio-Well/GDV, and Human Biophoton Emission

**Domain:** biofield / bio-photonics (also shape power — inert form acting on a living system)
**Recorded:** 2026-09-21 (Replication Watch, run 3)

## What was claimed

That **crystals do something to a human being that an instrument can see** — the shape-power and crystal-therapy tradition's core operational claim: a mineral object, held or worn, measurably alters a person's "biofield" / biophoton emission. The instrument tradition's own claim rides alongside it: that **Bio-Well (Gas Discharge Visualisation, GDV) electrophotonic imaging** — Korotkov's technique, which images the glow of a finger under a high-voltage field and derives "energy" and organ-system parameters — is a valid measurement channel for that change. This card tests both at once, and the archive's Curriculum VII (Bio-field Science / Bio-Photonic Therapy) is where it lands.

## Who tested it, when

**Audronė Ilgevičienė, Violeta Skinderienė, Darius Petronaitis, Šarūnas Jukna, Alfonsas Vainoras** — a Lithuanian research team.

Published **15 June 2026** in the *Journal of Complexity in Health Sciences* (JVE International, Kaunas), doi 10.21595/chs.2026.26701. Pilot study; authors' citation counts on the publisher's own page are 0–12.

## Method

Two pilot experiments using non-invasive Bio-Well/GDV recording:

- **Experiment 1 — n = 120.** Participants measured before and after **holding a natural, untreated rock crystal for 10 minutes**.
- **Experiment 2 — n = 28.** Participants measured across a **baseline → placebo → rock-crystal sequence**, to separate a placebo response from a crystal response.

Analysis by descriptive statistics, **Wilcoxon signed-rank test**, and **Spearman correlation**.

## Result

- Rock crystal exposure was **associated with statistically significant changes in Bio-Well parameters**, especially total recorded energy and indices related to the head, cerebral cortex, and cardiovascular system.
- Responses appeared **age-dependent**; a reduction in stress-related indicators was reported in the older participant group.
- **The placebo experiment showed that glass and rock crystal produced similar changes** — the authors attribute this to their similar chemical composition (both are silica).
- Conclusion stated by the authors: preliminary quantitative data in an area previously empirical/qualitative; "the findings suggest that responses to rock crystal exposure can be objectively recorded using non-invasive biophysical methods and support further investigation."

The study's own placebo arm therefore **fails to separate crystal from glass**. What the experiment establishes is that *holding a small object for ten minutes and being measured changes the measurement*. That is a statement about the instrument and the subject, not about quartz.

## Confidence: low (on the crystal claim); the negative is the useful part

- **Small, pilot-grade, unblinded.** No randomisation, no blinding of the operator to condition, no pre-registered hypotheses or analysis plan visible, n = 28 in the arm that could actually discriminate. Order effects (baseline → placebo → crystal is a fixed sequence) are not controlled: a monotonic drift would produce exactly the pattern seen.
- **The instrument's validity is a prior question, not a result.** Bio-Well/GDV derives many of its "organ-system" parameters from a single finger's gas-discharge image; most of the group's cited support (Korotkov, Yakovleva, Kostyuk, Bhat et al.) comes from the instrument's own developer lineage. A change in a contested instrument's output under an uncontrolled condition is weak evidence about anything.
- **Low-visibility venue.** *JCHS* is a small open-access journal with a short citation record; there is no evidence of independent replication of these specific results.
- The authors themselves flag the composition confound rather than dismissing it, which is to their credit, but the paper's headline ("rock crystal … associated with … changes") is stronger than its own placebo data supports.

## Honest framing

We hold no brief — the test is the point. Recorded as **attempted / measured / reported**: crystal exposure and glass exposure both moved the instrument, and the tradition's actual claim — *the crystal specifically* — did not survive its own control. **This is a first-class negative for shape power**, and it belongs next to the Munich 1986–88 dowsing null for the same reason: the phenomenon's own investigators built the control, and the control ate the effect. The open question this record leaves behind is not about crystals; it is about **Bio-Well/GDV itself** — whether any of its parameters are reproducible enough to carry a claim at all. Anyone using this instrument as an endpoint in a Yard protocol should pre-register that question first.
