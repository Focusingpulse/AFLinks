# Validation — D2 Gas Permeation Through a Pd Complex, Cs → Pr (Osaka University / MHI, 2003)

**Domain:** LENR / transmutation (gas-permeation, not excess heat)
**Recorded:** 2026-09-29 (Replication Seeder)

## What was claimed

Mitsubishi Heavy Industries (Y. Iwamura et al., ICCF-9, Beijing, May 2002) reported that when D2 gas permeates through a "Pd complex" — a thin Pd film over alternating CaO/Pd layers on a bulk Pd substrate — with **caesium deposited on the surface**, the surface Cs decreases and **praseodymium appears**. Conditions reported as necessary: D2 flow above 1 sccm, 343 K (70 °C), 1 atm, about one week. Substituting H2 for D2 showed no Cs decrease and no Pr generation. Analysis was by X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy.

The implied reaction, ¹³³Cs → ¹⁴¹Pr, would be a transmutation increasing mass number by 8 and atomic number by 4.

## Who replicated it, when

**Taichi Higashiyama, Hiroyuki Miyamaru and Akito Takahashi** (Osaka University, Department of Nuclear Engineering) with **Mitsuru Sakano** (Mitsubishi Heavy Industry). Presented at the **Tenth International Conference on Cold Fusion (ICCF-10), 2003, Cambridge, MA**.

## Method

**Samples:** fabricated by MHI — 25 × 25 × 0.1 mm, purity 99.99 % bulk Pd, sputtered first with alternating CaO/Pd layers (1000 Å) by Ar ion beam sputtering and then a 400 Å Pd layer on top; Cs deposited from 1 mM CsNO3 under a weak electric field (1 V negative, 10 s). Pre-cleaning put the sample and a Pt plate in 1 mM HNO3 under an applied field to strip surface hydrocarbons before Cs deposition.

**Rig:** two chambers. Chamber A holds 1 atm D2 against the Cs face; Chamber B is pumped by a turbomolecular pump, so deuterium permeates through the sample to the back face. Sample normally held at 70 °C. Three runs:

1. ~120 h, temperature stepped 70 → 60 → 80 °C. Flow started above 1.5 sccm, fell below 1.0 sccm — MHI's stated critical value — rose again with temperature, then declined. The authors attribute "unnatural fluctuations" between 20 h and 40 h to an ionization vacuum gauge miscontact.
2. ~200 h, temperature raised 70 → 80 °C. Flow stayed **below** 1 sccm from the start throughout; attributed to atmospheric moisture forming on the sample surface and blocking permeation. A data gap near 30 h is declared: the vacuum equipment was stopped for lack of N2.
3. ~120 h at a constant 70 °C, after baking out the chamber and purging with N2 before loading. Flow exceeded 2 sccm initially, then declined because too little D2 remained to hold 1 atm.

**Analysis:** inductively coupled plasma mass spectrometry (ICP-MS), performed **at MHI**, on samples from all three runs. Independently of that, 14 MeV neutron activation analysis (NAA) at the Fusion Neutronics Source (FNS), Japan Atomic Energy Research Institute, with an HPGe detector — a first measurement (11 d irradiation, 25 d cooling, 7 d counting) and a second on the same sample (8 d cooling, 9 d counting), with the sample moved closer to the detector to improve counting statistics.

## Result

- **ICP-MS at MHI detected Pr from all three runs** (their Table 1).
- **NAA detected Pr** via the 145 keV gamma peak of the ¹⁴¹Pr(n,p)¹⁴¹Ce reaction. The two spectra were normalised on the 511 keV background peak. The 145 keV peak **decayed between the first and second measurement** with an apparent half-life of about 50 days, against 32.5 days exact for ¹⁴¹Ce — **with better than 50 % error bars derived from only two data points**. The authors write that the decay "convinced us of the existence of ¹⁴¹Pr," while stating the counting statistics were not good enough to fix the half-life.
- The sample used for the NAA was one whose D2 permeation had been **done by MHI**.
- Their summary: the reaction "was reproduced qualitatively in the present replication experiment."
- Interpretation offered is Takahashi's 8D fusion model — 8 deuterons → ¹⁶O* → ⁸Be + ⁸Be + 95.2 MeV, and ¹³³Cs + ⁸Be (47.6 MeV) → ¹⁴¹Pr* — which predicts ⁴He as a by-product. The authors state they are **planning** a QMAS experiment to look for ⁴He. No ⁴He, neutron or gamma signature is reported in this paper.

## Confidence: medium

**For the record:** the experiment was run three times, Pr was found each time, and two technically dissimilar methods (mass spectrometry and gamma-ray activation) both reported it — that is real internal cross-checking, and it is more than most claims in this lineage have.

**Against treating it as established:**

1. **The independence is partial, and this needs saying plainly.** A co-author is an employee of MHI — the claimant institution. MHI fabricated the samples, supplied them, ran the ICP-MS, and performed the D2 permeation on the sample that went to NAA. The university contributed the rig, the runs and the activation analysis; the **material and one of the two analytical results originate with the claimant**. Vendor-supplied samples plus a vendor co-author is not a third-party replication.
2. **The NAA statistics are marginal** — two data points, > 50 % error bars, an apparent half-life of ~50 d against 32.5 d exact. Consistent with ¹⁴¹Ce; not tight enough to be strong on its own.
3. **The stated flow criterion was not met in run 2** (< 1 sccm throughout), and run 1's data carry a declared instrument-artefact window.
4. **The mechanism's own predicted by-product was not measured.** ⁴He is listed as future work.
