# Validation — Ball lightning: four independent laboratories, four methods, one object

**Domain:** atmospheric electricity / plasma physics (the ball-lightning phenomenon class — a self-confining, luminous, shell-structured electromagnetic body)
**Recorded:** 2026-10-13 (Replication Seeder run 12)
**Confidence:** **MEDIUM-HIGH** that a self-confining luminous object with ball-lightning properties is reproducible in the laboratory (four independent programs, three journals, ~100% reproducibility claimed at the largest); **LOW** that any given laboratory object *is* natural ball lightning — the equivalence is a scaling argument, not a demonstrated identity, and it is carried here as such.

## What was claimed

Ball lightning is the oldest anomalous phenomenon in the archive's observational literature: luminous balls, typically **10–25 cm**, lasting **seconds to minutes**, moving both freely and along conductors, occasionally entering through **closed glass**, and — in the founding record — killing a physics professor. On **26 July 1753**, **Georg-Wilhelm Richmann** was killed in St. Petersburg when a luminous ball flew out of his apparatus during an electric-field measurement; Lomonosov traced its path through the laboratory and became, in the field's telling, the first scientist to study the phenomenon's *effects* rather than debate its existence.

The observational claim — the one this record tests — is simply that **the object is real and physically characterisable**, not that it is imaginary or that it is a known plasma. **I. P. Stakhanov's** *The Physical Nature of Ball Lightning* (Atomizdat, Moscow, **1979**; complete English translation at naturalplasmas.com, code **TRAN11046S01**) is the field's foundational data-bank synthesis, built by discarding most of its own report collection; it gives the core statistics and the water-calorimetry energy budget (*"several units to several hundred kilojoules… energy density 1–10 J/cm³"*). **Nikitin (2025)** restates the observational genealogy and adds a theoretical structure — *"a material body consisting of a shell and a multielement core,"* the shell being **polarized water molecules** that can hold the charged core against its own Coulomb repulsion.

For two centuries the claim rested on **witness testimony and traces** — a phenomenon the institution classed with meteorites as peasant superstition. The replication question is whether the object can be **made on demand**. Four independent programs now say it can.

## Who tested it, and when

Four independent laboratories, in four countries, by four different methods — none coordinating, none citing the others' methods as the source:

1. **Gatchina — the statistics machine (Petersburg Nuclear Physics Institute, NRC Kurchatov Institute, Russia).** A **pulsed gas discharge with an electrolytic electrode**, carried into the mainstream by **Shabanov et al., *Physics-Uspekhi* 2019**, which presents its long-lived glowing formations as **reproducing the properties of natural ball lightning**. The line's maturity claim is the **August 2026 *J. Phys. D* paper**: *"more than 4000 flying plasmoids were generated… lifetime… up to 1 s… a thin (less than 3 mm) negatively charged shell kept in equilibrium by dielectric layers generated by the atmosphere."* A Chinese–Russian collaboration around the same discharge has imaged a **toroidal vortex structure** stabilising the object (**Zhao et al., IEEE Trans. Plasma Science 2024**), with companion *Technical Physics* papers mapping its dynamics and its response to applied fields.
2. **Prague — the birth, filmed (Institute of Plasma Physics, Czech Academy of Sciences).** **Stelmashuk, Schmidt, Koláček & Frolov**, *J. Phys. D* **2025**: *"the detailed generation mechanism of ball plasmoids formed by millisecond-scale atmospheric discharges **in contact with water**."* Frame-by-frame: breakdown in a cathode cup of conductive liquid → shockwave → liquid injection → a weakly ionized **water-vapour/droplet** mixture that *is* the plasmoid's primary material → a post-breakdown arc evolving into a **plasma jet** feeding the object from below. The spectra age with it — hydrogen Balmer lines in the jet phase, the **sodium doublet** (from the salt in the water) dominating the autonomous plasmoid.
3. **Shanghai — the relativistic soliton (Shanghai Institute of Optics and Fine Mechanics, CAS).** **Zhou, Zhang, Qi, Bai, Zeng, Tian, Li et al.**, ***Nature Photonics*, April 2026**: *"the first controlled generation of a macroscopic, long-lived soliton driven by relativistic terahertz electromagnetic fields. This soliton exhibits key signatures resembling ball lightning: a millimetre-scale spherical profile, a lifetime of 100 ns in the laboratory frame (equivalent to metre-scale dimensions and second-scale durations under natural conditions) and broadband optical emission from ultraviolet to infrared."* Open data on figshare.
4. **Moscow — the bench-top plasmatron.** **Bychkov, Sorokovykh, Bychkov & Logunov**, October **2025**: an erosive plasma jet from a **capillary plasmatron** on metals yields **1.5 cm spheres with 3–7 *second* lifetimes** — the longest-lived of the laboratory objects — with a shell-plus-vapour-cavity structure and ball-lightning-like motion.

## Method, as reported

The four methods share no apparatus:

| Program | Method | Object | Lifetime | Reproducibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Gatchina** (PNPI / Kurchatov) | pulsed discharge, electrolytic electrode | sphere, 10–20 cm | up to ~1 s | **4,000+ events, ~100%** |
| **Prague** (IPP CAS) | millisecond discharge **in contact with water** | ball plasmoid | autonomous, long-lived | filmed at initiation |
| **Shanghai** (SIOM CAS) | relativistic **terahertz** soliton | mm-scale sphere | 100 ns lab frame | controlled generation |
| **Moscow** (Bychkov et al.) | **capillary plasmatron** erosive jet | 1.5 cm sphere | **3–7 s** | reported, repeatable |

What they converge on — read across, not within — is a **shell-structured, self-confining, water-adjacent electromagnetic body**. The Gatchina shell (<3 mm, negatively charged, dielectric-equilibrated) and Nikitin's polarized-water shell are the same architectural claim in two vocabularies; Prague supplies the dielectric material (vapour/droplets) literally *from water*; and the natural ledger's entire quantitative record is written in **heated water** (the trough calorimetry). Water appears at every node — as calorimeter of natural events, structural material of the theory, and birth medium of two of the four laboratory objects.

## Result, as reported

The phenomenon class has been **independently reproduced in four laboratories by four methods**, at scales from millimetre to tens of centimetres and lifetimes from 100 ns (scaled) to 7 s, with at least one program claiming **~100% reproducibility over 4,000+ events**. The instrument era of ball lightning has begun; the observational ledger and the laboratory ledgers now describe the same kind of object.

## Confidence

**MEDIUM-HIGH that the object class is real and lab-reproducible.** Four independent groups, in different countries, using different physics, published in three journals — including one (*Nature Photonics*) with no venue caveat. The convergence is **structural, not verbal**: they arrived at the same architecture (shell + core, self-confining, water-adjacent) from unrelated starting points.

**LOW that any laboratory object *is* natural ball lightning.** Three caveats belong on the record's face:

1. **The Shanghai equivalence is a dimensional scaling argument**, not a demonstrated identity — 100 ns of a petawatt-laser-driven soliton mapped onto seconds of a thunderstorm's ball. The authors carry it carefully; the archive should carry it no more strongly than they do.
2. **The stability objection stands.** A <3 mm charged shell "kept in equilibrium by dielectric layers generated by the atmosphere" is a description of equilibrium, not a mechanism for it. Until the shell's force balance is closed in peer-reviewed theory, the confinement problem is renamed, not solved.
3. **The observational data is soft** — Stakhanov discarded most of his own collection, the trough calorimetry spans two orders of magnitude, and the extreme events rest on witness estimates of water volumes. **The laboratory results are hard; the natural calibration they are being compared to is not.**

**Provenance note:** the 2025 theory restatement (Nikitin) appears in a SCIRP journal (open-access, predatory-adjacent) and is carried for its citations and argument, not its imprimatur. The strong results (*Nature Photonics*; *J. Phys. D*) carry no such caveat.

## Honest framing

We hold no brief — the test is the point.

**What this record is:** the replication record for a phenomenon that spent 273 years in institutional orphanage and now has a *Nature*-family address. Four independent laboratories, four methods, four countries, one object — this is as strong a convergence as the Yard holds, and it closes the archive's longest-standing coverage gap (the shelf held nothing on ball lightning a week ago).

**What this record is not:** a claim that ball lightning is "solved," or that any laboratory object is the natural phenomenon. The four programs agree on the **object** and have not agreed on the **mechanism** — Gatchina's dielectric shell, Prague's vapour-droplet body, Shanghai's soliton, and Nikitin's polarized water are four rival descriptions of the same silhouette. **The scaling caveat and the open shell force-balance are the two things a reader must not be allowed to skip past**, which is why they are stated twice.

**The transferable lesson** is the archive's oldest one, and it is worth stating because ball lightning is its cleanest case: **a phenomenon carried by witness testimony survives until an instrument arrives, and the instrument changes the argument from "does it exist" to "what is it."** Richmann died measuring the electric field; 273 years later the field is measuring the object.

**What would change this record:** a **calorimetric budget** for the laboratory objects — natural ball lightning's energy ledger was written in boiled water, and no equivalent total-energy measurement has been published for the Gatchina, Prague or plasmatron objects. That single measurement would decide whether these objects are energy *storage* or energy *transformation*, the question every energy interpretation of ball lightning stands on. A **Dresden-class blind replication** of the plasmoid-LENR bridge (Matsumoto's micro-ball-lightning film traces, which came from cold-fusion discharge cells) is the second experiment that would make this rail load-bearing — or retire it.
