# Validation — Artificial Torsion Field from Vortex-Electromagnetic Fields: A Weakly-Supported 2026 Claim, Filed as a Marker

**Domain:** torsion fields / scalar (longitudinal) electrodynamics
**Recorded:** 2026-10-09 (Replication Watch)

## What was claimed

That a **vortex electromagnetic field** generated by "vacuum scalar waves" produces, in the laboratory, an **artificial torsion field** and a **gravitational-like field** — with the torsion field described as an **acceleration field associated with the tangential rotational motion of local space**, and the gravitational field as the counterpart for **radial curvilinear motion**. The paper's own framing concedes the domain's standing: it says torsion-field applications such as zero-point-energy extraction and therapeutic use "remain in the hypothetical stage," that mainstream physics does not recognise torsion as a fundamental force, and that the field "continues to reside within the realm of fringe science." This is the same claim class the Yard already carries from the opposite direction — the **IGF/Weidner 2001 test** of Meyl's scalar-wave apparatus, which found the transmission effects were within ordinary electrodynamics and saw **no over-unity effect**.

## Who reported it, when, and where

**Jianzhong Jiang**, **Xiang-Qian Zhang** and **Shan Huang**, in the *Journal of Advanced Engineering and Computation* (JAEC), **27 June 2026** — DOI **10.55579/jaec.2026102.532**. (The Jiang/Zhang pair also authored an earlier, related "longitudinal electromagnetic waves" paper in 2024, so this is a continuation of one group's programme, not an independent arrival.)

## Method — as far as it can be read this run

- A **theoretical framework** is derived from "vacuum scalar wave equations and particle propagation mode," linking artificial torsion and gravitational fields to vortex electromagnetic fields.
- **"Preliminary experiments to qualitatively verify the directional characteristics"** of the induced torsion and gravitational fields are reported.
- **Not available this run:** the concrete apparatus, the measured quantities, the controls, and any numerical result. Only the abstract was reached; the experimental section was not read. **This record therefore logs the claim, not the evidence for it.**

## Result — what was reported

- **A qualitative, directional verification is claimed.** No magnitude, no error bars, and no control battery surfaced in the abstract. Whether the observed directionality survives an ordinary-electromagnetic explanation — the exact thing the IGF test of the Meyl apparatus could not sustain — is not established.

## Confidence: low

- **Why low, precisely:** (1) it is **own-group** work built on the group's own prior framework — **not an independent replication**; (2) the verification is described as **qualitative and preliminary**; (3) the **claim is extraordinary** (artificial gravity/torsion) and extraordinary claims require the standard battery — calibrated signal-response, demonstrated uncertainty budget, bounded conventional EM leakage — that was **not shown in the abstract**; (4) it is **single-source and unreplicated**; (5) the experimental section was **not read** this run.
- **What it does establish:** only that the **torsion/scalar rail is still producing new experimental papers in 2026** — the lane's purpose in catching it. It is an activity marker, **not evidence**.
