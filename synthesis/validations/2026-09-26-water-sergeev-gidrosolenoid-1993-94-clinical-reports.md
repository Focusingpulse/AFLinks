# Validation — The Sergeev Gidrosolenoid Clinical Reports

**Domain:** water / clinical (Russian–Ukrainian bioresonance: the "water-solenoid" chamber)
**Recorded:** 2026-09-26 (Replication Seeder, run 5)

## What was claimed

That a **water-solenoid chamber** ("gidrosolenoid") produces measurable physiological effects — a form of what the reports themselves class as **"weak-energy physiotherapy."**

## Who did it, when

Two clinical reports, executed under named medical direction:

1. **NIMIC «NORT», Ukraine, 1993–94.** **n = 24.** Endpoint: **REG (rheoencephalography)** before and after the protocol.
2. **Sevastopol Mother & Child Center, 1994.** **n = 46** with vegetative-vascular dystonia (VSD), aged 18–26, **plus n = 23** with chronic bronchitis. Endpoints at **three time points**: BP, ECG, REG, echo, spirometry.

The reports were republished by the **aurasvit Vega program** on 2026-08-23 and translated UK→EN as a single work (Forge, FAL-uk-85, landed 2026-09-20).

## What was measured, and what happened

**NIMIC «NORT» (n = 24):** REG before/after recorded **19 positive / 3 no-change / 2 deterioration** — and, critically, **the report's own blood-pressure null is kept in the document**. The recommendation stands: "weak-energy physiotherapy."

**Sevastopol (n = 46 VSD + n = 23 chronic bronchitis):** BP, ECG, REG, echo and spirometry at three time points, **no other medication**, with a **structured-water co-intervention**. Reported result: **systolic blood pressure −9.1 ± 3.1 mmHg**. The report's own closing clause: **"mechanism subject to further study."**

## The confound, named because it decides the reading

**The Sevastopol arm applied a structured-water co-intervention alongside the chamber.** A trial that changes two variables cannot attribute the effect to either. This is not a defect this lane is inferring — it is the reason the corpus's verification rail names the missing rung as *"an independent clinical trial with REG endpoints **and no structured-water co-intervention**."* The rail's own phrasing is the honest statement of where the evidence stands.

Two further limits, stated plainly:

- **No control arm** is recorded in either report. A before/after series in 24 patients, and in 69 across two indications, cannot separate the intervention from regression to the mean, season, or the attention of a clinician.
- **REG is an interpretation-heavy endpoint**, and the corpus's rail files the *category* this produced ("weak-energy physiotherapy") as the residue — not the mechanism.

## What is established, and what is not

**Established (process):** a named-instrument, multi-endpoint clinical attempt at real n's, with **honest adverse rows preserved** — the 2 deteriorations among the 24, and the BP null the first report declined to drop. That last item is a large part of why this record exists: a tradition that prints its own adverse row is doing something the retail layer of the same field does not. The corpus's own summary of the pair is exact — *"named executors, real n's, real instrumentation, honest adverse rows."*

**Not established:** that the chamber, as distinct from the structured water beside it, produces the reported change; or that any of it replicates. **No independent re-test of the gidrosolenoid chamber appears anywhere in this corpus.**

## Confidence: low-to-medium

- **That these trials happened, with these executors, n's and endpoints:** **medium-high** — attested in the corpus's verification-rail synthesis and independently in Forge's translation landing note, with the same figures in both.
- **That the chamber produced the reported effects:** **no assessment.** The co-intervention and the absent control arm both block it.
- **Numbers (−9.1 ± 3.1 mmHg; 19/3/2):** **medium** — read through the corpus's summary of the translation, not from the clinical report itself.

## Honest framing

We hold no brief — the test is the point. This record is filed for the **shape of its evidence**, not the size of its numbers: named executors, real n's, real instrumentation, and **the adverse rows left in** — a 2-of-24 deterioration rate, and a null the authors could have deleted. Set that beside the same field's commercial layer and the contrast is itself the finding. What the record cannot do is grade the chamber, because the trial changed the water at the same time as the field. The Yard's own answer is already written into the corpus: the missing rung is a REG-endpoint trial with **no structured-water co-intervention** — and until someone runs it, *"signal present, mechanism unknown"* is as far as this evidence goes.
