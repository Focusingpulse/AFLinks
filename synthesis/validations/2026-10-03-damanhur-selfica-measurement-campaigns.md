# Validation — Damanhur "Selfica" Devices: Three Campaigns, All Operator-Present, No Blinded Trial

**Domain:** orgone / operator-channel devices ("vital force" device family) — Italy
**Recorded:** 2026-10-03 (Replication Seeder)

## What was claimed

The **Damanhur** school (Vidracco, Turin, Italy) produces and sells **"selfica"** devices — spiral-circuit objects sold through **Selet S.r.l.** (selfica.space), each described as **individually "activated."** The physical claim is that the devices act on a person's "energy" field and psychophysical state. The decisive detail is the school's **own product doctrine**, which states the mechanism as copy:

> *"Your attention, intention, and presence amplify how the device functions — it's not passive, but collaborative."*

That sentence is the corpus's cleanest case of an operator-channel doctrine **written down as a feature rather than conceded as a confound.** If the operator is part of the device, then any unblinded test measures the operator, and only a blinded test can measure the device.

## Who measured, when, and how

Three campaigns in about a decade, all traceable in the QC dossier:

1. **Wyrdoscope — 2× quantum-REG study (April 2025).** Run with **Peter Merry / Wyrd Technologies**, an **11-person training** cohort, over the school's devices and sites. Reported peaks ranged from **1:82** (Spiral of Damjl plus menhir) up to **1:140,000** (a personal "Falco–Tarassaco" connection), with **1:18,000** (Spheroself) and **1:14,500** (collective breathing) in between. The school's own caveats are banked with it: *"not a full scientific experiment,"* and the top peak is flagged as **personal / outside the protocol**.
2. **Multilevel HRV / Bio-Well / Sputnik / FT-IR-NES-DNES study (September 2025).** Reported **−43% stress**, **+44% total energy**, **+63% psychophysical coherence** across the measured devices. **No control group. Operator present.**
3. **Marjanovic (2015) — magnetotelluric survey.** A field survey, **25 mHz–50 kHz**, using an S-meter and a **Heliognosis LM3 orgone meter**. Recorded **Or = 78** at the Crystal Spiral, and E-only 10/19 kHz signals read as **longitudinal waves** (per van Vlaenderen / Waser). The report carries its **own honest negative**: **no scalar field registered at most sites** — plus an insufficient-sensitivity escape hatch.

## What happened

- **All three campaigns are operator-present or school-run.** None blinded the operator, none dropped a neutral handler, none used a control group. On the school's own doctrine, that is the design that cannot separate the device from the person operating it.
- **The one campaign with a partial check returned a negative in its own text.** Marjanovic found no scalar field at most sites — the "most sites no signal" row is the only controlled-ish row in the set, and it is not flattering.
- **The strongest numbers are the least protocolised.** The 1:140,000 peak is flagged by the school itself as personal and outside the protocol, so it cannot count as a hit.
- **The device doctrine predicts its own blind-test null.** If attention is required for the effect, then a blinded trial is *designed to fail* — which is why the tradition has not run one, and why running one is the only informative move.

## What was measured vs. what was asserted

- **Measured (as reported):** quantum-REG deviation ratios up to 1:140,000; HRV/energy deltas of −43% / +44% / +63%; an orgone-meter reading of Or = 78 at one site and **no scalar field at most sites**.
- **Asserted, not measured:** that any of these are attributable to the devices rather than to the operator, the setting, the expectation, or the instrument; that "activation" is a real device state; that the effect survives an operator who does not know which device is live.

## Confidence

- **Medium-high** that the three campaigns were run and reported roughly as described — they are named, dated, and carry the school's own numbers and caveats.
- **Low** that any of them validates the device. There is not one blinded cell in the entire set, and the family's own doctrine says blinding is the thing that would erase the effect.
- **Low** on the Marjanovic "longitudinal wave" interpretation (a physics claim built on two E-field frequencies and an interpretation framework the corpus does not test).

## Honest framing

We hold no brief — the test is the point. **Recorded as three measurement campaigns, none blinded, with the family's own doctrine naming the operator as part of the apparatus.** Nothing here proves the devices do nothing; nothing here shows they do anything. What the corpus does show is the exact design that has never been run, and it is cheap:

**A blind-operator Spheroself session.** A neutral handler assigns live-vs-inert devices behind a concealed key; the operator never knows which is which; pre/post HRV (or Bio-Well) is scored by someone blind to assignment; run enough sessions to beat chance. **The school's own doctrine predicts this returns null.** That is what makes it the decisive test rather than a hostile one — a positive under blinding would be the strongest result in the entire orgone family; a null would simply confirm what the school already wrote down.

**What would change this record:** any blinded, controlled measurement of a selfica device — even a null, and especially a null published by the school. Also: a same-session inert-vs-live arm on the Wyrdoscope rig, with the personal-protocol row pre-declared and scored separately, rather than flagged as an exception after the fact.
