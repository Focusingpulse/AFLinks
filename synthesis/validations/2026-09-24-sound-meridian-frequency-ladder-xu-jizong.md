# Validation — The 12-Meridian Frequency Ladder

**Domain:** acoustic resonance / sound therapy (Chinese integrative medicine; the acoustics family the Yard's wood-resonance dossier also sits in)
**Recorded:** 2026-09-24 (Replication Seeder, run 4)

## What was claimed

That each of the twelve meridians has a **characteristic frequency of maximal response** in the somatosensory low-frequency band (**16–160 Hz**), visible as a peak in **microcirculation at the meridian's acupoints** when that tone is played — and that the yang/yin meridian of each element sits at exactly **×2** the other (a 1:2 ladder). Three further directional claims were tested alongside it: frequency selects the organ, the *direction* of frequency modulation governs the direction of qi movement (rising → qi declining; falling → qi rising; small→large → dispersal outward; large→small → convergence inward), and timbre maps to the five-element categories.

## Who tested it, when

**Xu Jizong** and colleagues — a 2014–15 series published in Chinese journals (via **CQVIP**). Translated ZH→EN by the Forge lane on **2026-09-20** (fire FAL-zh-129-2); companion to a Temple of Heaven acoustics row (FAL-zh-129-1) from the same fire.

## Method

- **Stimulus:** low-frequency sound, 16–160 Hz, on an equal-temperament scale.
- **Measurement:** **laser Doppler flowmetry** — microcirculation (blood flow) at acupoints. A numerical instrument; no pendulum, no operator judgement.
- **Design:** **N = 30 healthy subjects per study**, five-shu point families per meridian.

- **Octave pairing:** every yang/yin same-element pair is exactly ×2 (Stomach 58.27 = 2 × Spleen 29.14, and so on).
- **Frequency → organ selectivity:** each meridian responded most to its own frequency, **p < 0.01**.
- **Modulation direction → qi movement**, and **timbre → five-element effect at BL40**, reported as separate controlled findings.
- **Five-shu point-family invariance** held across the family.

## The confound the series states against itself

Recorded because the source states it, not because we found it: **the frequencies are literally Western equal-temperament pitches, and the octave structure was built into the stimulus set before any meridian responded.** The corpus's own reading of the series is that "the discovery that meridians respond at octave-paired frequencies is at least partly an artifact of testing with octave-paired tones." A ladder whose rungs were chosen by the experimenter and then "found" is a selection artefact until an independent group re-runs it with an **unstructured** frequency set — or with the meridian assignment permuted. That test is the whole of the claim's falsifiable core, and it is cheap.

## The independent-trial layer — and what it does *not* cover

The corpus records a **2025–26 five-tone shelf: four RCTs plus a 2026 meta-analysis pooling 24 RCTs / 1,295 participants.** That is real replication activity, and it is the reason this record exists at all.

**But it is not a replication of the ladder.** Five-tone therapy trials test whether musical-mode sound exposure changes physiological or symptom endpoints (HRV, autonomic measures, symptom scales) — they do not re-measure a 29.14 Hz spleen peak by laser Doppler at acupoints, and a positive five-tone result would not confirm the ×2 ladder. The shelf supports the *gentler* claim next door and leaves the specific, instrumented claim where Xu's group left it. No individual trial or the meta-analysis was read by this lane; that distinction is the entire content of this paragraph.

## Honest framing

We hold no brief — the test is the point. Recorded as **claimed / measured / reported**: a tradition handed its theory to a Western instrument, the instrument returned a clean frequency ladder with a real statistical threshold, and the experiment was built in a way that guarantees most of the ladder's elegance in advance. Both of those are findings.

The teachable line, for the Yard's sound rail: **before you replicate a frequency, ask who chose the frequencies.** The meridian ladder is the corpus's cleanest example of an instrumented claim whose structure may be an artefact of its own stimulus design — and the correcting experiment (unstructured frequency set, or permuted meridian labels, N=30, laser Doppler at fixed points) needs no new apparatus at all.
