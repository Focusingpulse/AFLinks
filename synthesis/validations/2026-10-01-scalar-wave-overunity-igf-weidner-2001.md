# Validation — The IGF Instrumented Replication of Meyl's Scalar-Wave Demonstration (2001)

**Domain:** electromedicine / scalar electrodynamics (resonant wireless power transfer — the overunity claim)
**Recorded:** 2026-10-01 (Replication Seeder)

## What was claimed

**Konstantin Meyl**'s claim, sold as a demonstration kit and published in the *Journal of Scientific Exploration*: that a low-voltage replica of Nikola Tesla's wireless energy-transmission arrangement reproduces Tesla's observations, and that these include **the existence of scalar waves**, **an over-unity effect of about 10**, transmission of those waves at **1.5× the speed of light**, and the inadequacy of a Faraday cage to shield them.

The kit is the point of entry for a great many home experimenters, and the vendor's own documentation states *"Efficiencies of approximate 500% are measured"* and cites a control measurement at the Technical University of Clausthal on 6 July 2000 averaging **1000%**, with received power at the middle pancake coil reported as *ten times* the power transmitted. The claim is not subtle and it is not hedged: **more energy comes out than goes in.**

## Who ran it, when

**H. Weidner, E. Zentgraf, T. Senkel, T. Junker and P. Winkels** — **Institut für Gravitationsforschung (IGF)**, Waldaschaff, Germany. September 2001. Title: *"Experimente zum Nachweis von Skalarwellen — Versuche mit einem Tesla-Nachbau von Prof. Konstantin Meyl"* ("Experiments toward the proof of scalar waves — tests on a Tesla replica by Prof. Konstantin Meyl").

The institute is not a skeptic organisation; it was set up to investigate gravitation and non-conventional energy, and it came to the kit **expecting to find something**. That matters for how the result should be weighted.

## Method, as reported

The report's structure is itself the method — it is a three-stage replication audit, and the first stage is a good-faith reproduction:

1. **Exact reproduction.** Meyl's experiments were reproduced as specified, with the kit's own equipment. The report's summary is explicit that this stage **succeeded**: *"Im IGF konnten die von Prof. Meyl beschriebenen Effekte zwar zunächst reproduziert werden"* — the effects described by Prof. Meyl could indeed initially be reproduced at the IGF. The dramatic observations were seen.
2. **Interference- and error-source analysis.** The arrangement was then examined for what could produce those observations without an anomalous energy source, and the authors' own **working hypothesis became the Lecher line** — i.e. that the whole arrangement is a resonant transmission line carrying ordinary transverse electromagnetic waves.
3. **Independent measurements with the authors' own instrumentation.** Two-channel power measurement on the transmitter and receiver, plus a resonance search and an LED-based output comparison.

**Apparatus, as reported:** Meyl's demonstration kit (function generator in place of Tesla's spark gap, low-voltage operation, two pancake coils on plates, sphere capacitors, interconnecting wire); the IGF's own measuring equipment. Total conductor length of the circuit — highest point of one sphere capacitor to the highest point of the other — is **18.8 m**.

**Two details the IGF pinned down and Meyl's account leaves loose:**

- **Resonance frequency: 5.35 ± 0.1 MHz**, corresponding to a vacuum wavelength of **44.4 m** — which would make a stretched half-wave dipole **22.2 m** long, against an actual circuit length of only **18.8 m**. The discrepancy is discussed in the report rather than waved away.
- **Power accounting:** oscillator output ≈ U·I = **1.2 V × 16 mA = 19 mW**; receiver output, by LED comparison against two identical LEDs fed a known **8.5 mW** of direct current, ≈ **8.5 mW**.

## Result, as reported

- **"Ein Overunity-Effekt wurde nicht beobachtet."** No over-unity effect was observed.
- **Measured efficiency: 8.5 mW ÷ 19 mW = 45%.** The report's own closing line on the number: *"Bis η=100% oder gar 500% ist noch ein weiter Weg!"* — it is still a long way to 100%, let alone 500%.
- **The transmission effects are within classical electrodynamics**, explainable by transmission with **transverse electromagnetic waves**.
- **The superluminal claim is disputed on apparatus grounds:** the report challenges Meyl's assertion that the coil length and hence the wavelength is unchanged, pointing out that the sphere capacitances mean there is no simple relation between wire length and wavelength — *"It is not clear at all which wavelength Meyl actually means."*
- The report also notes a **genuine geometric difference** between Tesla's original arrangement and Meyl's replica in terms of geometry and power handling: the replica is not a faithful copy of the thing it claims to reproduce.

## Confidence: high that this measurement was made and reports this; medium-high that it disposes of the overunity claim

**For it.**

1. **It is an instrumented measurement with an explicit power budget**, not a dismissal. Both the input (19 mW) and the output (8.5 mW) are derived, and the output figure is obtained by comparison against a calibrated DC reference rather than by reading a dial.
2. **The first stage reproduces Meyl's effects.** The report does not say the demonstration does nothing — it says the dramatic observations *are* there, and then shows they are the ordinary behaviour of a resonant line. That is a much stronger form of refutation than failing to reproduce.
3. **Two independent hosts carry the document** (the German original and an English translation), the two versions agree on every number quoted here, and the report's own cross-references point to a fuller write-up in the institute's publication series.
4. **The institute had no prior commitment to the negative result.** IGF was founded to investigate exactly this class of claim.

**Against it.**

1. **One kit, one laboratory, one session's numbers.** The decisive measurement the archive keeps asking for — a published two-channel P_in/P_out measurement with the error sources catalogued — is *approached* here but not tabulated as a full sweep; the 45% figure comes from one LED-comparison method, and the LED is a nonlinear element.
2. **The LED comparison is a proxy.** Establishing output power by matching LED brightness against a DC-fed twin is reasonable and cheap, but it is not a calorimetric or a proper RF power measurement.
3. **The report is not peer-reviewed.** It is an institute technical report, self-published on the institute's site and mirrored by third parties. Its rigour is visible in its structure; its standing is not that of a journal article.
4. **The archive's own QC notes that the strongest available *theoretical* refutation is separate and independent of this measurement** — a TU-Darmstadt analysis showing that every solution of Meyl's own fundamental field equation plus his scalar-wave condition is stationary, i.e. that moving scalar waves cannot exist inside his own system. That is a self-refutation *before* any experiment, and it should not be counted as part of this experimental record.

## Honest framing

We hold no brief — the test is the point. **Recorded as measured and reported:** an institute set up to investigate this class of claim reproduced the demonstration, instrumented it, and measured **45%** where the vendor's material claims **500–1000%** — with the transmission accounted for as an ordinary standing wave on a resonant line.

The honest shape of this record is that the *effect* was reproduced and the *claim* was not. Meyl is right that something striking happens on the bench; the IGF is right that a resonant line explains it without an energy gain. Those two statements are not in tension, and the Yard should stop reading "the effect is real" and "the overunity is real" as the same sentence — that conflation is precisely the failure the archive exists to prevent.

**What would change this record:** a published two-channel P_in/P_out measurement of the kit — with the error sources listed, the LED nonlinearity controlled, and the measurement taken across a frequency sweep — reporting η > 1 repeatably. That has not been published in the twenty-five years since, and it is cheap enough that its absence is itself a datum. That absence is what the companion dossier exists to close.
