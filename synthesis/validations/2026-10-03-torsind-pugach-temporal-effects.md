# Validation — Torsind Temporal Effects: A Positive That Cites Its Own Failed Replication

**Domain:** torsion / anomalous temporal effects (time-rate effects read by a suspended sensor) — Ukraine
**Recorded:** 2026-10-03 (Replication Seeder)

## What was claimed

That a **"Torsind" torsion indicator**, suspended at **neutral equilibrium**, detects **anomalous temporal effects** — measurable shifts in the local rate of time correlated with external conditions (a lunar row on **09.02.2009** being the headline). In the tradition's wider framing, the claim is that a suitably suspended body couples to a torsion/time field and that its motion reads out changes the clocks around it do not.

## Who measured, when, and how

- **Actor:** **Pugach** (Ukraine); the paper is untranslated from uk and the QC dossier **FAL-uk-239-1** was built from it **UK→EN**.
- **Instrument:** a torsind suspended so it sits at neutral equilibrium — the design idea being that a body with no restoring force is maximally sensitive to any perturbing field.
- **Method, as reported:** log the indicator's behaviour over time and compare against background days, lunar phase, and co-located reference conditions. The corpus records that **temporal shifts scatter ±hours with no model** — i.e. the deviations are not fitted by any stated law.

## What happened — and where the replication lives

This record's value is its **honesty about its own lineage**, which is exactly what a validation file exists to preserve:

1. **The paper cites a 1992 clock result AND its 1999 replication failure** — *before* reporting its own Rb-based positive. A source that reports a prior failed replication of the same class, unprompted, is worth more than one that does not. It means the tradition's own authors know the effect has failed to replicate once already.
2. **The one strong row is the 09.02.2009 lunar claim:** **five background days** with a **6× amplitude** excursion on the test day — the single quantitative claim in the paper that stands out from the ±-hour scatter.
3. **The instrument's own null-hypothesis hole:** a **silk-versus-nylon suspension A/B was never run.** Without it, ordinary causes of a drift in a neutrally suspended body — electrostatic charge, air currents, humidity, thermal expansion — are not excluded. The "neutral equilibrium" design that is supposed to make the instrument sensitive also makes it sensitive to everything ordinary.
4. **The co-located control (CC) is confounded by construction** — the corpus notes it cannot separate the claimed field from a shared environmental cause.

## What was measured vs. what was asserted

- **Measured (as reported):** temporal deviations scattering ±hours; one 09.02.2009 lunar row with five background days and 6× amplitude; a co-located reference channel that tracked the same disturbances.
- **Asserted, not measured:** that the deviations are *temporal* (time-rate) rather than mechanical/environmental; that the 09.02.2009 row is field-driven; that the effect is distinct from the 1992 result that already failed its 1999 replication.

## Confidence

- **Medium** that the paper reports what the corpus says it reports — the QC layer read the recovered head (17 pp.) and the scout read the tail.
- **Low-medium** that the effect is real. The instrument has an unclosed ordinary-cause hole, the deviations have no model, and the class already carries one documented replication failure.
- **Low** that the 09.02.2009 row would survive the missing control.
