# The Omega-ORANUR Convergence: Destructive Information Fields in French Radiesthesia, German Orgonomy, and Russian Torsion Physics

## Abstract

Three independent research traditions — French micro-vibratory physics (Enel/Chauméry–de Bélizal school, 1930s–1960s), German orgonomy (Wilhelm Reich, 1950s), and Russian torsion field theory (Akimov/Shipov, 1980s–1990s) — converged on a strikingly similar claim: **there exists a destructive variant of the subtle information field, generated or amplified by the interaction of nuclear radiation with the medium itself.** The French tradition identified negative green (V−) as a dehydrating, mummifying ray and named its horizontal component Omega, marking spiritual energy on Egyptian saints' tombs. Reich documented ORANUR (orgone against nuclear radiation) as an overcharged, pathological state of orgone triggered by radium in an accumulator, with atmospheric effects extending hundreds of kilometers. Russian torsion theory distinguishes left-rotating fields as harmful, linked to nuclear installations and carrying information rather than energy. This synthesis traces the convergent claims, identifies the theoretical bridges, and proposes experimental tests.

---

## 1. The Convergent Claim

Across French radiesthesia, German orgonomy, and Russian torsion physics, researchers converge on five fundamental claims:

1. **A destructive variant exists.** Not all subtle fields are beneficial. Each tradition identifies a harmful manifestation: V− (negative green) in France, ORANUR/DOR in Germany, left-rotating torsion in Russia.

2. **Nuclear radiation triggers or amplifies it.** The destructive field emerges from the interaction of nuclear radiation with the medium — not from the radiation itself. The medium (ether, orgone, physical vacuum) becomes pathological.

3. **The effect extends beyond the source.** The destructive field propagates through the medium, affecting the atmosphere and living organisms at a distance — hundreds or thousands of kilometers in some accounts.

4. **The field carries information, not just energy.** The destructive quality is encoded in the field's structure (rotation direction, spectral position, charge state) rather than its amplitude.

5. **Technology can detect and potentially neutralize it.** Each tradition developed instruments (Universal Pendulum, orgone accumulator, torsion generators) and proposed antidotes (V+ for V−, DOR busters, right-rotating torsion).

## 2. French Radiesthesia: The Negative Green Ray and Omega

### 2.1 The Chauméry–de Bélizal Discovery

Léon Chauméry and A. de Bélizal filed patent FR 816,132 on 10 April 1936, claiming a "dowsing method and apparatus based on terrestrial magnetism" [1]. The patent introduced **negative green (vert négatif, V−)** as "the shortest wave and consequently the most powerful of the invisible spectrum," positioned between white and black on their spectral circle, opposite to green.

Key properties attributed to V−:

- **Destructive power over matter.** The patent claims V− "dehydrates" and "mummifies" organic material. The school's founding legend: Chauméry died 27 February 1957, "completely dehydrated by our negative green ray (V−)" [1].

- **Natural carrier wave.** V− is the "natural telluric carrier," present in underground water veins and geological faults — the same features geobiologists link to harmful "geopathic stress."

- **Artificial generation.** The patent claims V− "can also be obtained artificially by a rigorously calculated form wave" — specifically, hemispherical stacks (cosmo-magnetic batteries) and spherical emitters (Bombe C.30).

- **Preserving and destructive.** Paradoxically, V− both preserves (Egyptian pyramid mummification) and destroys (dehydration, cancer links). The school distinguishes vertical V− (destructive) from horizontal V− (carrier, spiritual).

### 2.2 Enel's Omega: The Spiritual-Energy Marker

Prince Michel Vladimirovich Skariatine (1883–1963), pseudonym "Enel," applied the Chauméry–de Bélizal pendulum method to Egyptian tombs between 1931 and 1953 [2]. He discovered that Christian saints' tombs emanated a specific vibration in the **horizontal negative green** zone, which he named **Omega** (grade 395 on the Universal Pendulum's electromagnetic meridian).

Enel's contribution:

- **Omega as spiritual marker.** Unlike vertical V− (destructive), horizontal V− (Omega) indicates the presence of "spiritual energy" — a door onto other dimensions.

- **Link to Egyptian tradition.** Enel spent twenty years in Egypt, collaborating with Howard Carter's team. His *Radiations des formes et cancer* (1951) connects form waves to Egyptian temple science.

- **Therapeutic claims.** Enel's radiesthesia works (*Premiers pas en radiesthésie thérapeutique*, 1949; *Traitement à distance par radiations*, 1959) claim cancer treatment at a distance using form radiations — the school's most contested assertion.

- **Lineage to BioGeometry.** Ibrahim Karim confirms: "Their work and that of Enel are a reference in the world of so-called scientific radiesthesia." Karim's BG3 (horizontal negative green + higher harmonic of ultraviolet + higher harmonic of gold) directly extends Enel's Omega concept [2].

### 2.3 The French School's Internal Safety Record

The school's own casualty — Chauméry's death by V− — serves as both cautionary tale and evidentiary claim:

> "We claim the absolute paternity of this 'Radiesthetic Battery,' as much for its discovery as for its properties and its applications. It has moreover made, together with the 'Universal Pendulum,' the object of a patent dated 10 April 1936 bearing the number 816,132." [1]

The antidote: V+ (positive green), discovered later with P.A. Morel, which "neutralizes stored radioactivity in the organism" [1]. The school claims operator precautions and time limits are essential.

## 3. German Orgonomy: ORANUR and DOR

### 3.1 The ORANUR Experiment (January 1951)

Wilhelm Reich, having discovered orgone energy (1939–1941), hypothesized that "orgone and nuclear radiation are opposites" and that strong orgone could counteract nuclear radiation [3]. Against the backdrop of the Korean War, he tested this hypothesis:

**Setup:**
- Two 1-milligram radium sources
- One shielded control sample
- One placed in a single-ply orgone accumulator inside a 20-ply accumulator inside an orgone room
- Radium exposed for 5 hours initially, then 1 hour daily for 6 days

**Reaction (Day 7):**
- After 30 minutes, violent atmospheric reaction
- Blue-to-purple haze in the orgone room
- Observers 100 meters away experienced nausea, forehead pressure, stomach cramps, balance disturbances, weakness
- Background radiation readings remained elevated for months
- Effects extended 1000 km radius (claimed)
- Participants developed radiation-sickness symptoms plus recurrence of individual chronic conditions

**Reich's interpretation:**
The orgone energy, when excited by nuclear radiation, transforms into **ORANUR** — "orgone against nuclear radiation" — an overcharged, pathological state that is "the real danger," not the nuclear material itself [3].

### 3.2 DOR: Deadly Orgone Energy

Reich distinguished:

- **OR (orgone):** Normal, beneficial life energy, pulsating, expanding
- **ORANUR:** Overexcited, pathological orgone, triggered by nuclear interaction
- **DOR (Deadly Orgone):** Stagnant, immobilized orgone, associated with drought, desertification, atmospheric stagnation

Jerome Eden's continuation of Reich's work documented:

> "An underground atomic explosion is something like punching someone in the solar plexus. In that region a violent, sudden contraction occurs. The atmospheric orgone reacts similarly... It is so dreadful that it triggers a massive atmospheric contraction extending over many kilometers." [3]

Eden linked underground nuclear tests to:
- Atmospheric low-pressure systems persisting over test sites
- "Flu waves" following nuclear tests
- Connection between Soviet tests and Iranian earthquake (1978, 25,000 deaths)

### 3.3 The Atmospheric Effect

Both ORANUR and DOR are claimed to affect the atmosphere at scale:

- **ORANUR:** Chain-reaction propagation through the orgone ocean, hundreds to thousands of kilometers
- **DOR:** Atmospheric contraction, drought, desertification
- **Nuclear tests:** Each test triggers an ORANUR reaction in the planetary orgone envelope

The claim: "Every time a nuclear power plant releases radioactive material, or when an atomic bomb is tested, the orgone ocean in which we live is disturbed and an ORANUR reaction is triggered" [3].

## 4. Russian Torsion Physics: Left-Rotating Fields

### 4.1 The EGS-Field Concept

A.E. Akimov and G.I. Shipov's "Torsion Fields and Their Experimental Manifestations" (1995) introduces the **EGS-field** concept [4]:

- **E (electromagnetic):** Charge polarization of the physical vacuum
- **G (gravitational):** Spin longitudinal polarization
- **S (spin/torsion):** Spin transverse polarization

The physical vacuum is modeled as a **phyton** structure — nested ring-shaped wave packets of electrons and positrons with opposite spins, self-compensated in charge, spin, and magnetic moment.

### 4.2 Left-Rotating Torsion as Harmful

Key claims about torsion field rotation:

- **Right-rotating (S_R):** Beneficial, life-supporting
- **Left-rotating (S_L):** Harmful, pathological

The German study on torsion fields explicitly states:

> "Nuclear power plants... produce very strong left-rotating torsion fields that have a massive harmful effect on humans. Protective measures against radioactive radiation are permeable to torsion fields and thus offer no protection at all." [5]

This mirrors Reich's claim that orgone and nuclear radiation are opposites — and that conventional shielding is ineffective against the subtle field.

### 4.3 Information Over Energy

The German study emphasizes:

> "At this level [primary torsion field], matter does not exist in the form of substance... Information transmission occurs exclusively through the interaction of quantum vortices... pure information exchange that possesses no energetic characteristics whatsoever." [5]

This aligns with:

- French form waves: meaning-carrying, not energy-carrying
- Reich's orgone: "functional" rather than "mechanistic" — meaning and emotion matter
- The holographic universe model: every point contains information about the whole

### 4.4 Superluminal Propagation

Both Russian and German torsion researchers claim:

> "If we take 2 points in the medium of a primary torsion field with a certain distance for which light needs 15 billion years... the interaction between these points will occur instantaneously." [5]

This addresses a cosmological problem: if the universe is 13.8 billion years old, how do distant parts interact to form a coherent structure? Torsion fields, propagating at ~10⁹ × c, could provide the connecting link.

## 6. Western vs. Russian-French-German Intersections

### 6.1 The French-Russian Seam

The Chauméry–de Bélizal patent (1936) predates the Soviet torsion program by decades. Yet the genealogy shows:

- Davidson's Russian genealogy names: Pavlita, Belizal, Turenne, Drbal
- The same "form emits wave, wave acts on witness" register appears in both traditions
- The Universal Pendulum's saturation phenomenon (detector turns = atomic number) parallels Turenne's independent discovery

This suggests either:
1. Independent discovery of the same underlying phenomenon
2. Cross-cultural transmission through channels not yet documented
3. Both traditions tapping into a shared intuitive/empirical substrate

### 6.2 The German-Russian Connection

Reich's ORANUR experiment (1951) predates Akimov/Shipov's torsion work by 30+ years. Yet both:

- Identify nuclear radiation as triggering a pathological state in the medium
- Claim conventional shielding is ineffective
- Describe atmospheric effects at kilometer scale
- Propose technology to neutralize the effect

The German study on torsion fields (post-1995) explicitly cites Russian work while documenting similar phenomena.

### 6.3 The French-German Connection

No direct connection documented between Reich and the French radiesthesia school. Yet both:

- Discovered destructive subtle field variants in the 1930s–1950s
- Linked them to nuclear radiation
- Developed instruments (Universal Pendulum, orgone accumulator) to detect them
- Documented operator safety issues (Chauméry's death, Reich's ORANUR emergency)

Independent discovery of convergent claims strengthens the case for an underlying phenomenon.

## 7. Theoretical Bridges

### 7.1 The Information Field Hypothesis

All three traditions can be unified under a single hypothesis:

**The physical medium (ether/orgone/vacuum) exists in multiple polarization states, one of which (V−/ORANUR/left-rotating) is pathological when excited by nuclear radiation.**

This suggests:
- The medium is real, not merely a theoretical construct
- Nuclear radiation excites a specific polarization state
- The pathological state propagates through the medium
- The effect is information-based (structure) rather than energy-based (amplitude)

### 7.2 The Rotation Direction Hypothesis

Both French and Russian traditions emphasize rotation direction:

- French: V− is "antipode of green" — opposite spectral position implies opposite rotation
- Russian: Left-rotating S_L is harmful; right-rotating S_R is beneficial

This suggests rotation direction (spin chirality) is the fundamental encoding of beneficial vs. harmful qualities.

### 7.3 The Holographic Connection

The German study explicitly connects torsion fields to the holographic universe model:

> "One can assert on a strictly scientific basis... that every point of the universe has the information of the entire universe." [5]

This connects to:
- Shipov's Universal Hologram concept
- Bohm's implicate order
- Sheldrake's morphic resonance
- French form waves as meaning-encoded

If the universe is holographic, then destructive information fields could propagate instantaneously through the holographic substrate.
