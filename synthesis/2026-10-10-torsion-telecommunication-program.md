## Abstract

Of all the claims made for torsion (spin) fields, the most engineering-ambitious is telecommunications: that a spin-polarized channel can carry information **non-locally, through shielding, and faster than light** — and that it can be built from bench hardware. This synthesis traces that claim as an actual engineering program with dated artifacts, not as a rumor: the Russian proposal text (Shkatov & Zamsha's *Torsion Fields and Interstellar Communication*), a Tomsk physicist's instrument memoir describing the Akimov electro-torsion generator arriving in his laboratory [[1]](http://www.vixri.ru/d/Shkatov%20V.T.%20_Detektirovanie%20torsionnyx%20polej.pdf), a Russian patent for a torsion generator [[12]](https://patents.google.com/patent/RU2115965), a mainstream Russian telecom journal's debate over whether torsion communication belongs in engineering at all [[9]](http://trinitas.ru/), and then — independently, a decade later — a one-man Chinese laboratory (Gao Peng, scalarwave.cc) that translated the Russian book, built semiconductor dark-current detectors and five kinds of generators, and published distance records: **2.2 km** (LED generator vs. water EIS sensor, battery-isolated) [[3]](http://www.scalarwave.cc/?p=998), **7,900 km** (Beijing → Stuttgart, Serge Kernbach's EIS instruments, pre-arranged schedule, randomized intervention windows) [[4]](http://www.scalarwave.cc/?p=1092), and **~11,000 km** (Beijing → New York, cross-photo protocol with Dr. Mark Krinker) [[5]](http://www.scalarwave.cc/?page_id=1017).

Underneath the machine program runs a documented human-channel baseline it explicitly cites as precedent: Kaznacheev & Trofimov's two global telepathy experiments — *Polar Circle* (December 1991, Dikson → USSR) and *Banner of Peace* (June 1993, Novosibirsk/Dikson → 12 countries) — run from hypogeomagnetic chambers and Kozyrev mirrors, reporting a "distant-time advance" effect in which percipients logged information **hours or days before it was transmitted** [[7]](https://physics.socionic.info/index.php/physics/article/download/364/321/312). The machine program and the human program make the same claim at the same distances, with the same addressing system — **a photograph** — and the same candidate carrier. Japan converged from a third direction: qi-instrumentation groups that had never heard the word "torsion" measured spiral-motion qi effects on water conductivity and photomultiplier dark current, then adopted torsion terminology after contact [[6]](http://www.scalarwave.cc/?p=1113). The Western node is analytical rather than experimental: the CIA's declassified holographic-mind assessment treats consciousness as an information channel and sits in this archive beside the hardware [[10c]](https://earth-chronicles.ru/news/2026-07-21-193664).

The skeptical record is part of the program's own archive and is included here in full weight: the Elektrosvyaz debate [[9]](http://trinitas.ru/), the Russian Academy of Sciences anti-pseudoscience commission, and the 2025 PulsePen fact-check whose verdict is that under hardened metrology the claimed effects "fell below the detection threshold" [[10]](https://pulsepen.ru/torsionnye-polya-chudo-energiya-ili-effekt-prezentaczii-proveryaem-fakty/). No blind third-party replication of any distance record exists. What the archive documents is a *program* — coherent, international, twenty years deep, with specific testable predictions — not a confirmed technology.

## 1. The Claim: Spin Fields as a Communications Channel

The communications claim is what separates torsion physics from mere anomaly collection. A field that only heals or only disturbs instruments is a curiosity; a field that can be **modulated and addressed** is infrastructure. The claim, as the Chinese lab states it plainly:

> 挠场的特殊属性-局域和非局域作用同时发生，超光速等特性，非常适合用来通信，并且天生具有保密性。
>
> *"The torsion field's special properties — local and non-local action occurring simultaneously, superluminal characteristics — make it very suitable for communication, and it is inherently secure."* [[2]](http://www.scalarwave.cc/?p=1072)

Three properties are being claimed in one sentence: simultaneity of local and non-local action (the signal arrives regardless of distance), superluminal propagation, and inherent secrecy (the channel does not radiate electromagnetically, so it cannot be intercepted by RF means). If any one of the three held under blind conditions, the technology would be revolutionary. The program's history is the history of attempts to move that sentence from a translated Russian book into bench hardware.

## 2. The Russian Proposal and Its Instruments

### 2.1 Shkatov & Zamsha

The fountainhead text of the communication line is V. Shkatov and V. Zamsha's *Torsion Fields and Interstellar Communication* (Торсионные поля и межзвездная связь) — the book whose title already contains the whole ambition: not meter-range anomalies but **interstellar** signaling, on the argument that a spin channel would not attenuate or disperse the way an electromagnetic one does. The full text is not yet in this archive (it is upgrade-234 in the family ledger); what the archive holds is Shkatov's own instrument memoir, *Детектирование торсионных полей* ("Detection of Torsion Fields") [[1]](http://www.vixri.ru/d/Shkatov%20V.T.%20_Detektirovanie%20torsionnyx%20polej.pdf), which is the more valuable document for QC purposes because it is specific about hardware.

### 2.2 A Tomsk physicist's path in

Shkatov's memoir matters because his credentials are conventional: 1979–2000 at the Institute of High-Current Electronics, Siberian Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences, working on electrophysics and high-current energetics "within traditional scientific and engineering views." His path into torsion detection ran through a Chizhevsky chandelier (a negative aeroion generator) he built as a side job in the underfunded 1990s — and then through two anomalies. The first was photographic: a 35-mm Zenit-E camera with a **capped lens**, irradiated by the chandelier, produced an image of a part located *inside the camera body*:

> Получается, что в предложенных условиях фотоаппарат снимал сам себя изнутри.
>
> *"It turned out that under the proposed conditions, the camera was photographing itself from the inside."* [[1]](http://www.vixri.ru/d/Shkatov%20V.T.%20_Detektirovanie%20torsionnyx%20polej.pdf)

The second was horological: mechanical **Slava stopwatches**, pre-calibrated to ±0.2 s over 12 hours, served as detectors of the chandelier's "hidden" emission — their rates shifting in its presence. Both anomaly classes (photographic plate effects, clock-rate effects) recur across the Russian torsion literature; Shkatov's account is unusual only in being written by the instrument-builder himself, with systematic errors stated.

The pivotal scene comes when a professor from the theoretical physics department delivers a device to the institute:

> Я незамедлительно перенёс прибор к себе, сразу вскрыл и оцепенел, так как никогда не видел ничего подобного…. Это был электроторсионный генератор А.Е.Акимова.
>
> *"I immediately carried the device to my lab, opened it at once, and froze — I had never seen anything like it…. It was A. E. Akimov's electro-torsion generator."* [[1]](http://www.vixri.ru/d/Shkatov%20V.T.%20_Detektirovanie%20torsionnyx%20polej.pdf)

Shkatov's own detector — the one Gao Peng would later independently reinvent in Beijing — was semiconductor-based: a varicap in the oscillatory circuit of a measuring autogenerator, the beat frequency against a reference digitized and displayed. A spin-field-induced change in the semiconductor's dielectric behavior shifts the frequency; the instrument is a frequency-counter, which is why "torsion detection" claims in this literature are always, physically, claims about tiny reactive changes in stressed or polarized components.

## 3. The Hardware Line: Generators and a Patent

The Russian generator line is documented in the archive at three levels of formality:

1. **The Akimov generator** — the canonical device of the 1990s Russian program, appearing in Shkatov's memoir [[1]](http://www.vixri.ru/d/Shkatov%20V.T.%20_Detektirovanie%20torsionnyx%20polej.pdf) and throughout the Akimov–Shipov survey literature [[16]](https://www.trinitas.ru/rus/doc/0231/004a/02310004.htm). Shipov's own catalog, *Электроторсионные генераторы в России и за рубежом* ("Electro-torsion generators in Russia and abroad") [[11]](https://trinitas.ru/), enumerates the design family: spinning-mass generators, plasma-discharge generators, and semiconductor spin-polarization emitters.
2. **A state patent** — RF Patent **RU2115965**, Neganov & Salmanov, *Torsion Generator* [[12]](https://patents.google.com/patent/RU2115965) — the claim entering the legal patent record, with the Ukrainian translation in this archive. Whatever one thinks of the physics, the patent office accepted the device description; the archive treats patents as provenance, not as validation.
3. **Samizdat schematics** — the oborud.ru torsion-generator schematics collection [[13]](http://oborud.ogorodguru.ru/), hobbyist-buildable circuits representing the program's diffusion into maker culture.

The hardware line matters to the communication claim because communication requires a *transmitter*; every distance record below uses one of these generator classes (LED pulsed emitter, Tesla-coil scalar-wave generator, rotating-field devices) as the transmitting end.

## 4. The Mainstream Pushback: Elektrosvyaz and the Skeptical Ledger

The program was not ignored by Russian engineering — it was **debated in a mainstream venue**. *Elektrosvyaz* (Электросвязь), the Russian telecommunications journal, hosted a published controversy over torsion communication, preserved at trinitas.ru and indexed in this archive as the *Torsion Communication Debate* [[9]](http://trinitas.ru/) with Akimov and Shipov as the torsion-side authors. The significance is structural: the claim was specific enough — a modulable channel with transmitter and receiver — that actual telecom engineers engaged it on engineering grounds, and the engagement was negative enough that the torsion side felt obliged to answer in print.

The consolidated skeptical verdict, two decades later, is the 2025 PulsePen fact-check (in this archive [[10]](https://pulsepen.ru/torsionnye-polya-chudo-energiya-ili-effekt-prezentaczii-proveryaem-fakty/)), which separates Élie Cartan's 1922 geometric torsion (real mathematics, effects "too small for laboratory experiments") from the Akimov-line "torsion fields," and delivers the metrology verdict:

> Когда эксперименты переводили в «жёсткий режим» с качественной метрологией, заявленные «торсионные» эффекты уходили ниже порога обнаружения.
>
> *"When the experiments were moved into 'hard mode' with quality metrology, the claimed 'torsion' effects fell below the detection threshold."* [[10]](https://pulsepen.ru/torsionnye-polya-chudo-energiya-ili-effekt-prezentaczii-proveryaem-fakty/)

The fact-check also anchors the institutional position: the Russian Academy of Sciences' Commission on Combating Pseudoscience has published materials on "torsion technologies" (ras.ru/antiche). This ledger entry is not decoration — it is half of the evidence. Any honest synthesis of this program must carry both the distance records (§6) and the fact that no record has survived blind third-party metrology.

Zamsha, and using the techniques in the book to experimentally demonstrate that the essence of scalar waves is the torsion field, I moved fully into torsion-related technology research."* [[5]](http://www.scalarwave.cc/?page_id=1017)

He presented that demonstration — *"Attempts to detect the torsion field nature of scalar wave generated by dual Tesla coil"* — at the **2016 Moscow torsion conference** ("Torsion Field and Information Interaction"), whose 270-page proceedings he distributed from his site [[14]](http://www.scalarwave.cc/?p=949). A Chinese experimenter presenting in Moscow on the torsion nature of a German theorist's scalar wave: the program's international wiring, in one conference slot.

### 5.2 The method: generator, detector, photograph

His communication architecture is exactly the Russian one [[2]](http://www.scalarwave.cc/?p=1072): a torsion generator as transmitter, a torsion detector as receiver, and between them a **"synchronicity" component** — two identical photographs, one placed on the generator, one on the detector (or the detector's photograph placed on the generator). His detector is semiconductor dark-current measurement, sensitive enough, he reports, for weak torsion signals, and he ran "a large number of Local and Non-local tests." Crucially, he is honest about where the program stands:

> 这个实验0和1的时间间隔都很长，还不能算是通信。…想想电磁波通信最早期的时候不也是从一个电火花开始的吗？
>
> *"The interval between the 0 and the 1 in these experiments is still very long — it cannot yet be called communication. …But think: didn't electromagnetic communication also begin from a single spark?"* [[2]](http://www.scalarwave.cc/?p=1072)

The spark analogy is doing rhetorical work, but the concession is real: as of 2018 the channel state changes were too slow to carry meaningful bit rates. What he claims is not a modem but a **physical layer** — a repeatable stimulus visible at distance in a detector.

## 6. The Distance Records

### 6.1 2.2 km: LED generator vs. water EIS, dormitory to laboratory (2017)

The cleanest protocol in the cluster [[3]](http://www.scalarwave.cc/?p=998). Transmitter: a pulse-driven **LED torsion generator** — deliberately a *weak* generator — powered from a 12-volt lithium battery so that no mains connection exists. Receiver: a water-based **EIS (electrochemical impedance spectroscopy)** sensor in the laboratory, 2.2 km away, "because water is the most torsion-sensitive substance." Addressing: a photograph of the water sensor, printed on A4, carried home and placed under the LED. Controls: the post records temperature stability better than 0.01 °C, near-zero magnetic variation, and no vibration across the measurement window, and the transmitter is battery-isolated specifically to foreclose the "it's just mains coupling at 2.2 km" objection:

> 这样就能把发射端变成一个独立的系统了，不然，如果用220V交流电供电的话，估计有人会说，才2.2千米，会不会是输电系统耦合过去的啊。。
>
> *"That makes the transmitting end a completely independent system — otherwise, if I powered it from 220 V AC, someone would surely say: it's only 2.2 km, couldn't it be coupled through the power grid?"* [[3]](http://www.scalarwave.cc/?p=998)

The reported result: after the sensor stabilized (4–6 hours), switching on the distant generator produced visible changes in the water's impedance during the intervention windows (marked gray on his charts). He draws the consequence himself: if a photograph mediates an effect on water at 2.2 km, and humans are ~70% water, then the qigong-master practice of healing from a patient's photograph has "a scientific basis" — the human-channel claim, restated as an engineering result.

### 6.2 7,900 km: Beijing → Stuttgart, at the 13th Water Conference (2018)

The flagship experiment [[4]](http://www.scalarwave.cc/?p=1092), already cited in this archive's qi-torsion synthesis for the water side; here the communications side. At the 13th Water Conference in Bulgaria (October 18–21, 2018), Gao Peng met **Dr. Serge Kernbach** (Stuttgart), who was demonstrating EIS devices. They pre-arranged an ultra-long-distance test and ran a successful rehearsal a week before. Protocol: Kernbach ran **two** EIS instruments in Germany and sent Gao photographs of the water samples; Gao printed them; intervention was scheduled for 00:00–04:00 German time, when the sensors are most stable; only Gao held the photographs, and he intervened **at random times within the window** known only to him:

> 实验过程中，保证这台仪器测量的水样照片，只有我这一份，并只有我在这个时间段内随机时间干预。
>
> *"During the experiment it was guaranteed that I held the only copy of the water-sample photograph, and only I intervened, at random times within that period."* [[4]](http://www.scalarwave.cc/?p=1092)

The reported data show time-correlated responses at both instruments, with two different generators (his parts 1 and 2), and — the detail he stresses — the curves inflect *immediately* when the intervention stops:

> 尤其是干预完成之后，曲线马上就开始下降了，随着挠场发生器的关闭，数据实时反映了出来。
>
> *"Especially once the intervention was complete, the curves immediately began to drop — as the torsion generator switched off, the data reflected it in real time."* [[4]](http://www.scalarwave.cc/?p=1092)

And then the identification that ties this synthesis to the previous one:

> 联想到上个世纪气功师严新博士与清华大学2000公里距离下远程影响水的光谱…可以想到，挠场可能就是外气的本质。
>
> *"Recalling Dr. Yan Xin and Tsinghua University's experiments last century, remotely affecting water spectra at 2,000 km… one is led to think: the torsion field may be the essence of external qi."* [[4]](http://www.scalarwave.cc/?p=1092)

A scheduled, cross-continental, single-blind-adjacent protocol with instrument logs is a different evidentiary class from a seance report — while still not being the blinded, third-party-meters replication that §4's skeptics demand. Both things are true, and the synthesis keeps both.

### 6.3 ~11,000 km: the Krinker cross-photo series (2016–2017)

The longest claimed baseline is the least formally documented: a series of **cross-photo** experiments with **Dr. Mark Krinker** in New York [[5]](http://www.scalarwave.cc/?page_id=1017). Each party placed the other's instrument photograph under his own device: Gao's scalar-wave generator in Beijing addressed a photo of Krinker's torsion detector; Krinker's detector in New York sat over a photo of Gao's generator. Gao switched his generator on a pre-agreed schedule and reports correlated changes in Krinker's detector data — repeatable, he says, though "many people thought it could be coincidence or random factors, which at that distance is a very normal thought" [[3]](http://www.scalarwave.cc/?p=998). Krinker's own side of the series is unpublished as far as this archive knows (it is upgrade-235 in the family ledger); the claim currently rests on one participant's report.

## 7. The Human Channel That Preceded the Machines

### 7.1 Two global experiments, 1991 and 1993

The machine program did not invent the distance claim; it inherited it from a human-channel program that was already planetary in scale. Kaznacheev & Trofimov, of the International Research Institute of Cosmic Anthropoecology (Novosibirsk), state it in one sentence:

> Впервые в истории мировой науки осуществлено два глобальных эксперимента: в декабре 1991 г. эксперимент «Полярный круг», когда передача образов (10 сеансов) проводилась из заполярного поселка Диксон, а прием — в различных точках СНГ, и в июне 1993 г. эксперимент «Знамя мира», в котором образная трансляция осуществлялась из Новосибирска и Диксона (4 сеанса), а прием — в различных географических пунктах Европы, Азии и Америки, всего в 12 странах.
>
> *"For the first time in the history of world science, two global experiments were carried out: in December 1991 the 'Polar Circle' experiment, in which image transmission (10 sessions) was conducted from the polar settlement of Dikson with reception at various points across the CIS; and in June 1993 the 'Banner of Peace' experiment, in which image broadcasting was conducted from Novosibirsk and Dikson (4 sessions) with reception at various geographical points in Europe, Asia and America — 12 countries in all."* [[7]](https://physics.socionic.info/index.php/physics/article/download/364/321/312)

The protocol infrastructure: senders inside **hypogeomagnetic chambers** attenuating Earth's magnetic field roughly 600-fold, **Kozyrev mirrors** (the confocal aluminum spiral enclosures), and synchronous scoring of received imagery against transmitted target "intellectual fractals" across a planetary receiver network.

### 7.2 The distant-time advance

The most disturbing claim in the Kaznacheev–Trofimov series is not distance but **direction in time**:

> В ряде экспериментов на Крайнем Севере выявлены феномены «дистанционно-временного опережения» — получения перципиентами передаваемой информации за несколько часов или дней до ее фактической трансляции.
>
> *"In a number of experiments in the Far North, phenomena of 'distant-time advance' were identified — percipients obtaining the transmitted information several hours or days before its actual transmission."* [[7]](https://physics.socionic.info/index.php/physics/article/download/364/321/312)

If the torsion channel's claimed superluminality is real, retrograde timing at small scales is exactly the pathology one would expect; the papers report reception logs timestamped before transmission sessions. The companion paper in the same journal series, *Distant-Informational Interactions in "Kozyrev Space"* [[8]](https://physics.socionic.info/index.php/physics/article/download/395/288), extends the program to GDV (gas-discharge visualization) readouts of subjects inside the mirrors exposed to information holograms — cosmic imagery, Sumerian drawings, encyclopedia contents — with reported correlations between response strength and prenatal geomagnetic conditions at the subjects' brain-development months. The genetics framing is speculative; the operational frame is not: **information presented, organism responds, instrument logs it.**

Between the human and machine programs sits the applied medical claim, carried in the archive by the scalarwave.cc analysis of remote diagnosis and treatment mechanisms [[17]](http://www.scalarwave.cc/) — the practitioner's claim that diagnosis and treatment can be performed through a patient's photograph at arbitrary distance. The third national tradition arrived at the same bench from its own vocabulary [[6]](http://www.scalarwave.cc/?p=1113):

1. **Dr. Sasaki Shigemi**, president of the Japan Ki Energy Research Society, collaborated with the Chinese human-science community (the Qian Xuesen "somatic science" lineage) and built a qi measurement method on **water conductivity**. He reported that left-rotating and right-rotating crystals emit two different kinds of qi and concluded:

> 气的运动形式是螺旋的。
>
> *"The movement form of qi is spiral."* [[6]](http://www.scalarwave.cc/?p=1113)

Spiral motion of a chiral two-state emission from rotating crystals is, in everything but name, the spin-field phenomenology the Russians call torsion — derived in Japan without the Russian vocabulary.

2. **Takashi Aoki's group**, working with **photomultiplier dark current**, found that their "qi-energy generator" altered the dark current — the same reactive-component signature as Shkatov's varicap detector and Gao Peng's photodiode detector. They had tried to publish years earlier, lost the chance when a collaborator fell ill, and only encountered torsion terminology afterward: "later, when they learned the torsion concept, they changed all their former terminology to torsion" [[6]](http://www.scalarwave.cc/?p=1113).

Three instrument traditions — Tomsk, Beijing, Japan — converging on **dark current in a photosensitive semiconductor as the torsion transducer** is the quietest and most checkable convergence in this synthesis, because dark-current metrology is standard physics-lab practice. It is also, for the same reason, the convergence most exposed to §4's verdict: dark current is exquisitely sensitive to temperature, and temperature is the first artifact any hardened protocol removes.

## 10. What Would Settle It — and the Archive Gaps

The program makes three predictions specific enough to kill it cleanly, which is more than most of the archive's contents offer:

1. **The photograph is necessary.** Remove or substitute the photo link (wrong photo, digital image, verbally described target) and the channel should fail. A double-blind photo-swap test is a one-afternoon experiment with existing hardware.
2. **The channel penetrates Faraday shielding.** A receiver in a screened room should keep responding; an RF-coupled artifact cannot. The 2.2 km battery-isolated protocol gestures at this but does not seal it.
3. **Dark-current response survives temperature-stabilized metrology.** §4's verdict is precisely that it does not, so far; the torsion side's answer must be a published temperature-controlled dark-current series, not another distance record.

Archive gaps, for the scouts: the full Shkatov & Zamsha book (upgrade-234); Krinker's side of the 11,000 km series (upgrade-235); the Elektrosvyaz debate's original journal pages beyond the trinitas.ru summary [[9]](http://trinitas.ru/); the 2016 Moscow proceedings volume (270 pp., linked from [[14]](http://www.scalarwave.cc/?p=949)); Kaznacheev & Trofimov's session logs for *Polar Circle* and *Banner of Peace* — the primary data behind §7, which the papers summarize but do not reproduce.
