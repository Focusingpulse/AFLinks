# The Coherence Convergence: How One Concept Bridges French Radiesthesia, Italian QED, Spanish Psychogeometry, and BioGeometry

## Abstract

Between 1936 and 2013, at least five independent research traditions — French micro-vibratory physics (Ravatin's coherence fields), Italian quantum electrodynamics (Del Giudice and Preparata's coherent water domains), Spanish psychogeometry (Ponce de León's geometric matrices), Egyptian-French BioGeometry (Karim's BG3 quality scale), and British biology (Sheldrake's morphogenetic fields) — converged on the same word: **coherence**. Each tradition uses it differently: Ravatin as a mathematical framework for non-localized emissions, Del Giudice as QED ground-state domains in water, Ponce de León as the geometric ordering principle of matter, Karim as a measurable quality of spatial resonance, and Sheldrake as a biological memory field. Yet the convergent claim is striking: organized structure — whether geometric form, water near a surface, or a morphic field — creates a domain where information is stored and transmitted without conventional energy transfer. This synthesis traces the concept through each tradition, identifies the convergent claims, notes the Western–Latin/Russian–French intersections, and proposes what a unified coherence framework would require.

---

## 1. The Word That Crosses Every Boundary

"Coherence" entered physics through optics — the property of light waves maintaining a fixed phase relationship. Lasers are coherent; incandescent bulbs are not. The word then migrated. By the late 20th century, it appeared in:

- **Quantum field theory** (Fröhlich, Umezawa): coherent ground states in biological matter
- **French radiesthesia** (Ravatin): coherence fields as the fundamental organizing principle of reality
- **Italian QED** (Del Giudice, Preparata): coherent domains in liquid water
- **Spanish psychogeometry** (Ponce de León): geometric coherence as the process by which energy becomes matter
- **BioGeometry** (Karim): coherence as a spatial quality measurable through radiesthetic methods
- **Biology** (Sheldrake): morphogenetic fields as coherence across time and form

The migration is not accidental. Each tradition encountered the same empirical boundary — organized structure produces effects that conventional physics cannot model — and reached for the same word to name the organizing principle. What differs is the mathematical apparatus, the experimental method, and the epistemological framework.

## 2. The French Thread: Ravatin's Coherence Fields

### 2.1 From Form Waves to Coherence

Jacques Ravatin (1935–2011), a French doctor of sciences in physics and mathematics who worked in quantum axiomatics at Orsay and collaborated with CERN teams, made the most deliberate conceptual break in the French form-wave tradition. Where Chaumery and de Bélizal spoke of *ondes de formes* (form waves), and Enel spoke of *radiations*, Ravatin's group stopped speaking of waves entirely:

> "We no longer speak of form waves but of emissions due to forms. Why? Because we are not dealing with waves — neither those of the electromagnetic spectrum nor those of any other known vibratory system. In short: emissions due to forms do not belong to our physical universe." [1]

The replacement concept was **coherence fields** — a framework that distinguishes the **Local** (existence with reference points, our observable universe) from the **Global** (existence without reference points, the pre-existing field from which unexplained phenomena emerge), with the **Idathème** as a third category that cannot be represented by human mind [1][2].

### 2.2 The Key Claim

Ravatin's coherence fields are not energy fields. They are information fields:

> "The EIFs are more fundamental than electricity, than electromagnetism; they place themselves at every level: matter, energy, life, thought. One discovers their importance in every domain of usual research and application (physics, chemistry, architecture, biology, allopathic medicine, but also acupuncture, homeopathy, anthroposophy)." [1]

This is the French coherence claim: geometric forms generate fields that carry information across all levels of reality, and these fields are more fundamental than the electromagnetic spectrum. The concept of "coherence" here is mathematical — it refers to the multiplicity of "Rays of Coherence" that the Global field possesses, as opposed to the single ray of the Local (rational) field.

### 2.3 La Foye's Bridge: The Vital Field

Jean de La Foye (1960s–70s), an agricultural engineer in contact with de Bélizal, discovered the **vital field** — the signature of living matter detected through radiesthesia as fractally repeating circles, contrasted with the hexagonal fractals of physical matter [3]. His Reciprocal Circles Board was designed as an emitter of form emissions, using the Hebrew Tetragrammaton as "directing axes" that permanently balance the emitter's field, creating "a true little universe sufficient unto itself: a microcosm" [3].

This is coherence as self-balancing system — a concept that would reappear in Karim's BioGeometry as "centering."

## 3. The Italian Thread: Del Giudice and QED Coherence in Water

### 3.1 The Preparata-Del Giudice Theory

Emilio Del Giudice, a theoretical physicist at INFN Milan, and Giuliano Preparata (1942–2000), professor at the University of Milan, developed a QED framework for liquid water that predicts **coherent domains** — regions where water molecules oscillate in phase with an electromagnetic field confined within the domain [4]. The theory holds:

- Liquid water is not a disordered collection of molecules but a two-phase system: coherent domains (≈100 nm) embedded in incoherent background.
- Within coherent domains, molecules oscillate in unison between ground state and an excited state, trapping an electromagnetic field.
- The coherence is a ground-state property — it requires no external energy input to maintain.
- Coherent domains can store information (frequency patterns) and release electrons at energies far below the 12.6 eV required for isolated water molecules.

### 3.2 The Pollack Connection

Del Giudice explicitly connected his QED framework to Gerald Pollack's experimental discovery of the **fourth phase of water** (Exclusion Zone water) [5]:

> "In the early 2000s, the American physicist Gerald Pollack of the University of Seattle experimentally observed that water near surfaces, to a depth of several microns, is water that has different properties from bulk water and is capable of easily yielding electrons." [5]

Del Giudice's QED theory provides the theoretical explanation: Pollack's EZ water is the macroscopic manifestation of coherent domains. The 12.6 eV barrier for electron release from isolated water molecules drops dramatically when molecules are in a coherent domain near a surface. This solves Szent-Györgyi's puzzle: where do the electrons come from for the chemical reactions that sustain life? They come from coherent water [5].

### 3.3 The Italian Coherence Conference (2013)

The conference at Rome's Casa dell'Aviatore (2013), organized by the CIFA Foundation, brought together the Italian coherence network: Benveniste's legacy (water memory), Montagnier's DNA electromagnetic emissions, Calamai's cyclotron resonance theory of water activation, Grimaldi's CNR experiments on activated water and cells, and the Aharonov-Bohm magnetic potential as a theoretical bridge [6].

The conference's framing is significant: **coherence** is the unifying concept. Water memory, DNA emissions, and biological effects of EM fields are all treated as manifestations of coherent organization in liquid water. The Italian tradition — from Fröhlich's biological coherence through Preparata's QED to Del Giudice's water domains — forms the most mainstream-adjacent formulation of the coherence claim.

## 4. The Spanish Thread: Ponce de León's Psychogeometry

### 4.1 Geometry as Coherence Generator

Arturo Ponce de León (Psicogeometría México) presents a framework where three mathematical constants — **phi** (1.618...), **pi** (3.14159...), and **euler** (2.71828...) — are the generative constants that create coherence from chaos [7]:

> "Free energy is associated with rectilinear movements and the zero point has the powerful characteristic of causing those free particles to be organized around a point of gravity to create matter. Energy and matter are only differences in geometric arrangement. Matter can exist because of the geometric matrices that support and promote the suction of energy in a constructive interface of waves, which means that given the organization of certain geometries, an ordered implosion of chaotic particles is created." [7]

This is coherence as geometric process: phi creates the spiral, pi circumscribes it, euler solidifies it. The **Pentaflor process** — the journey from void through spiral through sphere through toroid — is a coherence-generating sequence that Ponce de León maps onto the Flower of Life, the toroidal field, and the cosmologies of multiple traditions [7].

### 4.2 The Convergent Claim

Ponce de León's claim is structurally identical to Ravatin's, despite no apparent direct influence:

- **Ravatin**: geometric forms generate coherence fields (EIFS) that organize matter at every level.
- **Ponce de León**: geometric matrices create coherent implosion of chaotic particles, producing matter.
- **Both**: geometry is not a description of matter but a generative principle — the form creates the field.

The difference is vocabulary: Ravatin uses non-Cartesian algebra and the Local/Global/Idathème framework; Ponce de León uses phi/pi/euler and the toroidal model. The convergence is at the level of the claim, not the apparatus.

## 5. The BioGeometry Thread: Karim's BG3 as Coherence Quality

### 5.1 BG3 as a Measurement of Coherence

Dr. Ibrahim Karim's BioGeometry, developed from his work with Egyptian temple geometry and the French radiesthesia tradition (he studied with de Bélizal's successors), identifies three "BG3" qualities — **Horizontal Negative Green**, **Vertical Negative Green (Ultra-Green)**, and **Higher Harmonic of Gold** — as the signature of beneficial environmental resonance [8].

BG3 is not a frequency or a wavelength. It is a **quality** — a coherence signature detected through radiesthetic methods. Spaces, objects, and living systems that emit BG3 are described as "centered" — a state where the energy system is self-balancing, resistant to perturbation, and supportive of biological function [8].

### 5.2 The Bridge to Ravatin and La Foye

Karim's "centering" is functionally equivalent to La Foye's "directing axes" that create "a microcosm" — a self-sufficient, self-balancing field [3]. Both concepts describe coherence as a state where a system is isolated from external perturbation and internally harmonized. The difference: La Foye achieved it through Hebrew letter axes on a wooden board; Karim achieved it through geometric shapes (BioSignatures) placed in architectural and environmental contexts.

Karim also explicitly connects to the French form-wave tradition: the Atlantean ring and Egyptian pendulum that Chaumery and de Bélizal studied are central BioGeometry artifacts. The negative green that Chaumery died from is the same quality Karim identifies as BG3's first component [8][9].

## 6. The Biological Thread: Sheldrake and Fröhlich

### 6.1 Morphogenetic Coherence

Rupert Sheldrake's theory of morphogenetic fields — that nature possesses a collective memory that guides the shape and behavior of living structures — is explicitly linked to form waves in the Italian tradition [10]:

> "Scholars such as P.A. Morel and Jean de La Foye attempted to place the phenomenon within a broader scientific context, linking it to the theory of morphogenetic fields proposed by the Cambridge biologist Rupert Sheldrake. According to Sheldrake, nature possesses a kind of collective memory capable of guiding the shape and behavior of living structures. In this light, form waves could represent the physical expression of these invisible informational fields." [10]

Sheldrake's morphic resonance is coherence across time — a species' form is maintained by a field that remembers previous forms. This is structurally parallel to Ravatin's Global (existence without reference points) and to the Italian coherent water domains (ground-state memory that persists without energy input).

### 6.2 Fröhlich's Biological Coherence

Herbert Fröhlich (1905–1991) predicted that biological systems should exhibit coherent longitudinal electric modes at microwave frequencies — a concept that influenced both Del Giudice's QED water theory and the broader Italian coherence school. Fröhlich's insight: living matter is not a disordered thermal bath but a system capable of maintaining coherent excitations that organize biological function.

The chain: Fröhlich → Umezawa (soft modes, Goldstone theorem in biology) → Preparata/Del Giudice (QED coherence in water) → Pollack (experimental EZ water). Each step reformulates "coherence" but preserves the core claim: biological matter is organized by coherent fields, not by chemistry alone.

## 7. The Convergent Claims

Across all five traditions, the following claims converge:

1. **Organization creates fields.** Not just matter or energy — organized structure (geometric form, molecular arrangement, morphic pattern) generates detectable fields. Ravatin: EIFS. Del Giudice: coherent domains. Ponce de León: geometric matrices. Karim: BG3. Sheldrake: morphic fields.

2. **These fields carry information, not energy.** They can be detected by resonance (pendulums, biological response, frequency analysis) but not by conventional power measurement. Ravatin explicitly states EIFS are "not waves." Del Giudice's coherent domains store frequency information. Karim's BG3 is a quality, not a quantity.

3. **Coherence is the organizing principle.** In every tradition, "coherence" names the state where a system is internally harmonized and self-maintaining. The word migrates from optics through QED through radiesthesia through biology, but the semantic core persists: phase-locked organization that persists without external energy.

4. **Water is the primary medium.** Italian QED: coherent water domains. BioGeometry: water as the carrier of BG3. French tradition: the vital field detected through water's response. Pollack: EZ water as the physical evidence. The convergence on water is striking and unexplained by any single tradition.

5. **The effect is scale-invariant.** Ravatin: EIFS operate "at every level: matter, energy, life, thought." Ponce de León: the Pentaflor process from particle to cosmos. Sheldrake: morphic fields from molecules to societies. Del Giudice: coherent domains from nanometers to biological organisms.

## 8. The Intersections: Western, Latin, and Russian-French

The coherence concept has distinct intellectual geographies:

- **Mainstream Western physics**: Fröhlich → Preparata → Del Giudice. This chain stays within QED and quantum field theory, publishes in peer-reviewed journals, and connects to experimental physics (Pollack, Montagnier). It is the most academically legitimate formulation.

- **French radiesthesia tradition**: Chaumery → de Bélizal → Enel → La Foye → Ravatin. This chain operates outside academia, publishes through small presses (Cosmogone, Servranx), and uses radiesthetic instruments (pendulums, emitters) as its primary experimental tools. Ravatin's mathematical formalism (non-Cartesian algebra, cumulo-decal arithmetic) is the most ambitious but least accessible.

- **Spanish-Latin tradition**: Ponce de León's psychogeometry. Independent of both the French and Italian traditions, emerges from Mexican sacred geometry practice. Uses phi/pi/euler as its mathematical language. Connects to BioGeometry through shared sacred geometry vocabulary.

- **Egyptian-French synthesis**: Karim's BioGeometry. Explicitly bridges the French radiesthesia tradition (negative green, Universal Pendulum) with Egyptian temple science and sacred geometry. The most practically applied tradition — BioGeometry is used in architecture, agriculture, and environmental design.

- **Russian intersections**: Kozyrev's causal mechanics and Akimov/Shipov's torsion fields (covered in the Shape-Field Convergence synthesis) use "coherence" in a related but distinct sense — as the spin-polarized vacuum state. The Russian tradition connects to the French through Enel (Russian aristocrat in France) and to the Italian through Montagnier's work in Russia and China.

The key intersection point is **water**: the Italian QED tradition, the French vital field tradition, BioGeometry, and Pollack's experimental work all identify water as the medium where coherence becomes biologically active. No synthesis has yet traced this water-coherence thread across all traditions.

## 9. What a Unified Framework Would Require

The convergent claims suggest a framework that would need to:

1. **Define coherence operationally.** Each tradition uses different detection methods: radiesthetic instruments (French, BioGeometry), QED calculations (Italian), geometric analysis (Spanish), biological morphic resonance (Sheldrake). A unified framework needs a detection protocol that cross-validates across methods.

2. **Bridge QED and radiesthesia.** Del Giudice's coherent domains are predicted by QED and confirmed (indirectly) by Pollack's experiments. Ravatin's coherence fields are detected by pendulum. If both describe the same phenomenon — organized structure generating information fields — the bridge would be: coherent domains in water near geometric surfaces. This is testable: does EZ water near a BioGeometry shape emit BG3?

3. **Account for the information-storage claim.** The Italian tradition (Benveniste, Montagnier) claims water stores and emits electromagnetic information. The French tradition claims forms emit information. BioGeometry claims shapes create beneficial quality. A unified framework needs an information-theoretic model: what is the encoding, what is the medium, what is the readout?

4. **Reconcile the scale-invariance claim.** If coherence operates from molecular to cosmic scales, the mechanism must be scale-independent. QED coherence in water is nanometer-scale. Morphic fields are species-scale. Ravatin's EIFS are claimed at every scale. The bridge may be fractal geometry — Ponce de León's Pentaflor process and La Foye's vital field (fractal circles) both invoke fractal scaling.

## 10. Next Questions

1. **Can Pollack's EZ water experiments be run near BioGeometry shapes?** If coherent water domains respond to geometric form emissions, the EZ zone should expand, contract, or change properties near BG3-emitting shapes. This would be the first cross-tradition experimental test.

2. **What is the relationship between Ravatin's "Global" and Bohm's implicate order?** Both describe a pre-existing field from which localized phenomena emerge. Ravatin's framework is mathematical (non-Cartesian algebra); Bohm's is physical (hidden variables, pilot wave). The connection has not been explored.

3. **Does the Italian QED coherence theory predict form-wave effects?** If geometric shapes create coherent domains in adjacent water (as the French tradition claims), Preparata's QED framework should predict this. The calculation has not been done.

4. **Can Sheldrake's morphic resonance be reformulated in terms of coherent water domains?** If biological memory is stored in water's coherent domains (as Montagnier's DNA emission experiments suggest), morphic resonance could be reformulated as coherent-domain information transfer — making it testable through QED.
