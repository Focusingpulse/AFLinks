# The Shape-Field Convergence: French EIFS, Russian Torsion, and the Mathematics of Form-Generated Fields

## Abstract

Three independent research traditions — French micro-vibratory physics (Ravatin's coherence fields, La Foye's vital field), Russian torsion field theory (Akimov/Shipov EGS-concept), and German extended electromagnetism (shape effects, Barrett's non-Abelian gauge theory) — converged on a strikingly similar claim: **geometric shapes generate fields that carry information without energy transfer, existing outside conventional electromagnetic frameworks.** Jacques Ravatin (1935–2011), a French mathematician and physicist, formalized this as EIFS (émissions induites par les formes, "emissions induced by forms") and developed the Local/Global/Idathème framework to explain why these emissions cannot be located in conventional space-time. Jean de La Foye (1910s–1970s) extended the Chaumery–de Belizal tradition by discovering 24 manifested colors, the vital field, and the role of Hebrew guiding axes. Russian torsion researchers independently identified shape effects (cones, cylinders) as torsion field generators. This synthesis traces the convergent claims, identifies theoretical bridges, and proposes experimental tests.

---

## 1. The Convergent Claim

Across French form-wave research, Russian torsion physics, and German extended electromagnetism, researchers converge on five fundamental claims:

1. **Geometric shapes generate fields.** Not just conductors or magnets — actual geometric forms (cones, spheres, spirals, cylinders) emit detectable fields through their shape alone.

2. **These fields carry information, not energy.** They can be detected by resonance (pendulums, Lecher antennas, torsion sensors) but not by conventional power meters.

3. **The fields are non-electromagnetic.** They propagate through materials that block EM waves, do not diminish with distance according to inverse-square laws, and have no known quanta.

4. **Spin or rotation is the physical mechanism.** French tradition links form emissions to "neutral particles" or "magnetic current." Russian tradition identifies spin as the torsion source. Both connect shape to spin polarization.

5. **Technology can harness these fields.** French researchers built emitters (Bombe C, Reciprocal Circles Board). Russian researchers built torsion generators. Both claim practical applications.

## 2. Jacques Ravatin: The Mathematician of Coherence Fields

### 2.1 Who Was Ravatin?

Jacques "Vlady" Ravatin (1935–2011) was a French doctor of sciences in physics and mathematics, an E.S.C.I.L.-trained engineer, and a professor of mathematics at the University of Orsay. He worked in quantum axiomatics and collaborated with research teams at CERN in Geneva. He was a founder of the Fondation Ark'All and originated "non-Cartesian" mathematical systems.

His nickname "Vlady" came from Vladimir Rosgnik, a fictional character he invented as the "Bourbaki of his unorthodox research" — a plural identity representing the entire Ark'All/Eurek'All research group.

### 2.2 The Break with Form-Wave Tradition

Ravatin's group made a deliberate break with the older French school:

> "We no longer speak of form waves but of emissions due to forms. Why? Because we are not dealing with waves — neither those of the electromagnetic spectrum nor those of any other known vibratory system. In short: emissions due to forms do not belong to our physical universe."

This is the key conceptual move: **EIFS are not waves at all.** They are emergence phenomena — the Local (our observable universe) emerging from the Global (existence without reference points), with the Idathème as a third category of being that cannot be represented by human mind.

### 2.3 The Local/Global/Idathème Framework

Ravatin's framework has three categories:

| Category | Definition | Characteristics |
|----------|------------|-----------------|
| **Local** | Existence with reference points | Measurable, locatable, dissociable into elements |
| **Global** | Existence without reference points | Non-local, holistic, cannot be grasped by analysis |
| **Idathème** | Being-being (l'être étant) | Cannot be represented, tied to certain forms/places/acts |

The Local is the domain of conventional physics: distances, times, masses, charges. The Global is the domain of archetypes, symbols, and "enels" — what Ravatin called the fundamental units of the invisible. The Idathème is a third category: certain forms, places, and acts are tied to it (mandrake, philosopher's stone, DNA double helix, Easter Island statues, Nazca lines).

### 2.4 EIFS — Emissions Induced by Forms

Ravatin's key claims about EIFS:

1. **EIFS are more fundamental than electromagnetism.** They "place themselves at every level: matter, energy, life, thought."

2. **EIFS are the intermediary between Global and Local.** They bridge existence without reference points (Global) and our limited-Local system.

3. **EIFS can be generated by mathematical drawings.** Ravatin's group developed "modulaires" — mathematical drawings capable of inducing physical effects, including the philosopher's stone properties.

4. **EIFS technology is possible.** The Acti-Z machine (developed with François Cardinet) could confer "actikalisation" on matter at cold temperatures.

### 2.5 Cumulo-Decal Arithmetic

Ravatin's group developed a new arithmetic to access the Global:

> "These are cumulo-decal arithmetics — arithmetic, because they deal with numbers; cumulo-decal, because they allow one to cross the passages, the 'cumulo-decals.' As in the past wizards and magi did through rites and symbols, it is today thanks to formulas of this kind that they try to access the Global, to immerse the landmarks, and to bring archetypes and enels into emergence."

This is remarkable: **a mathematical system designed to do what ritual magic once did.** Ravatin explicitly connected his work to the Ark of the Covenant, the philosopher's stone, and "forgotten researchers and accursed scientists."

## 3. Jean de La Foye: The Vital Field and Hebrew Axes

### 3.1 Who Was La Foye?

Jean de La Foye was a French agronomic engineer and amateur radiesthésiste. His professional work — solving concrete problems in agricultural settings — led him into form-wave research. He explicitly named his lineage:

> "To my Master, Monsieur André de BELIZAL, who introduced me to the fascinating universe of form waves and, with admirable patience, guided my first steps"

With thanks also to "Monsieur BARDET (Jean-Gaston Bardet, architect and specialist of the square Hebrew script) for the precious time he made me save by associating me with some of his research, by making me discover the possibilities of Hebrew."

### 3.2 The 24 Manifested Colors

La Foye extended the Chaumery–de Belizal sphere work by moving from the sphere to the circumference of a tree trunk. He discovered:

- The 12 colors appear on the trunk's **diameter**
- The colors **double** to 24 on **twice the trunk's radius** — 12 "Electric" colors and 12 "Magnetic" colors of opposed polarities
- A nail driven into the trunk **selects** the color present at that point and projects it onto the south (terrestrial magnetic) axis

This led to the **equatorial pendulum**, the **equatorial disk**, and finally the **Reciprocal Circles Board** — a selective color emitter that decouples the colors' course from the sun.

### 3.3 The Vital Field

La Foye's key contribution: the **vital field** — the pendulum-detectable signature of the living.

- The living crystallizes in **circles** (fractal, duplicating like cell division)
- Matter crystallizes in **hexagonal fractals**
- Life overlaps matter: "circles contained within the hexagram"

This connects to Del Giudice/Pollack water coherence research: the living state has a different field signature than non-living matter.

### 3.4 The Guiding Axes

La Foye's breakthrough came through Jean-Gaston Bardet's Hebrew work:

- Each letter of the **Tetragrammaton** (YHWH, "I AM") corresponds to a specific color emission when measured radiesthetically
- These letters can be drawn as **axes directeurs** — guiding axes — on a circle
- The axes **permanently balance** the emitter, preventing saturation and isolating it from exterior influence
- The board becomes "a true little universe sufficient to itself: a microcosm"

This is the operationalization of sacred geometry: Hebrew letters as field-stabilizing axes.

### 3.5 The Reciprocal Circles Board

The Board consists of three interlocking circles:

| Circle | Function |
|--------|----------|
| **North circle** | Origin; oriented to magnetic north −5° (355°); a needle through its center selects the color from the 24-color dial |
| **Center circle** | The "microprocessor" or "heart"; guiding axes depart from here and balance the board; amplifiers may be placed here |
| **South circle** | Per the law of compensation, emits perpendicularly at its center the emission selected in the north; place a photo witness for remote treatment, an object to charge, or a carrier substance to copy a vibration onto |

The Board can be connected in series — a copper wire guides emission from one board's south circle to another board's north circle.

### 3.6 Two Hypotheses for Mechanism

Bellovaque records two hypotheses:

## 4. Russian Torsion Physics: Shape Effects

### 4.1 Shape-Generated Torsion Fields

German translation of Russian torsion research documents:

> "A number of authors have repeatedly pointed out the unusual behavior of objects with a certain surface geometry. It has been noted several times that bodies whose geometric dimensions have the ratio of the 'golden section' are a kind of concentration space for certain 'energies.'"

> "Dr. G. Shipov has demonstrated at a strictly scientific level that an object with a certain surface geometry, as a consequence of geometric or topological excitation of the physical vacuum, simultaneously generates right and left torsion fields of a certain spatial configuration."

### 4.2 Cone and Cylinder Effects

The German document specifies:

| Shape | Torsion Field |
|-------|---------------|
| **Cone** | Right-rotating torsion field above; left-rotating below |
| **Short cylinder** (diameter > half height) | Right-rotating from base surfaces; left-rotating from sides |
| **Long cylinder** (diameter < half height) | Left-rotating from base surfaces; right-rotating from sides |

This is the **same shape taxonomy** that appears in French form-wave research: spheres, hemispheres, spirals, cones, cylinders all have specific emissions.

### 4.3 Golden Ratio Connection

Both traditions identify the golden ratio (1:0.618) as significant:

- French tradition: "golden number" proportions in the Reciprocal Circles Board (30 × 18.6 cm)
- Russian tradition: "golden section" ratio concentrates "energies" and affects crystallization

### 4.4 Spin Polarization and Memory

The German document explains torsion field persistence:

> "If we have an object with a spin whose states are stable, the polarization persists even when the physical object disappears. There is a total analogy with a memory state. The physical vacuum is precisely that cosmic substance that can actually remember images."

This parallels Ravatin's Global — the vacuum as a memory substrate that persists after the local object is removed.

The proposed upgrades — EIFS-torsion cross-validation and vital field-water coherence experiments — would provide decisive tests. If validated, the shape-field convergence would represent a new paradigm: **information as a physical quantity carried by geometric field configurations, accessible through both mathematics and instrumentation.**
