# The Negative Green Carrier Thread: From the 1936 Chaumery–de Belizal Patent to BioGeometry's BG3

## Abstract

Of all the concepts in the French micro-vibratory tradition, one has had the longest and strangest career: **negative green (V−, vert négatif)** — the "ray" that the Chaumery–de Belizal school placed at grade 0–400 of their spectral circle, exactly between white and black, "the shortest wave and consequently the most powerful of the invisible spectrum." It entered the public record through French patent FR 816,132 (filed 10 April 1936), was systematized through angular refraction in the school's emitters, killed its own co-discoverer, was carried into modern geobiology as the diagnostic signature of both harm and healing, and was finally re-interpreted by Ibrahim Karim's BioGeometry as one of three components of the BG3 harmonizing quality — where its "carrier" function, not its power, is the point. This synthesis traces that single thread through eight sources in the archive, identifies the convergent claims across four generations of researchers who each transformed the concept, notes the French–Egyptian and French–Russian intersections, and proposes the questions the thread leaves open.

Where previous syntheses covered form waves as information carriers generally (2026-09-07) and the scalar-wave bridge (2026-09-10), this one follows **one spectral position through ninety years** — a case study in how a fringe-physics concept survives by changing what it claims to be.

---

## 1. The Thread in One Line

**A single named vibration — V−, the antipode of visible green — appears successively as (1) a patented spectral discovery, (2) a hazard that killed its discoverer, (3) a mummifying/preserving power over matter, (4) the geobiologist's diagnostic marker for noxious and sacred places alike, and (5) the carrier component of a modern harmonizing science.** Each generation kept the name and changed the claim.

---

## 2. Origin: The Patent (1936)

The negative-green ray enters history in a legal document. Patent FR 816,132, *Procédé et appareillage radiesthésiques*, filed 10 April 1936 by Léon Chaumery and André de Belizal, granted 30 July 1937 [1]:

- **Terrestrial magnetism as the physical basis.** The apparatus is claimed to work on the earth's magnetic field, not on the operator's mind — the "physical radiesthesia" position.
- **Spectral completeness as the novelty claim.** Existing pendulums "agree only in wavelength" and "cannot explore the region comprised between infra-black and ultra-white." The invention's contribution is the region between them.
- **V− defined.** "This ray is the antipode of the green of the spectrum: it is the shortest wave and consequently the most powerful of the invisible spectrum... designated under the name of negative green or special green."

Two details make this more than a curiosity:

1. **The classification is itself a historical artifact.** The French office placed the patent in the same class as electrotherapy and geophysical prospecting (modern IPC: A61N1/16, G01V9/002). In 1936 the claim was filed as physics, not esoterica.
2. **The apparatus embodies the theory.** The Universal Pendulum carries the spectrum in fixed order on a graduated sphere, with V− at grade 0 = 400, "exactly between white and black." The emitters — cosmo-magnetic batteries of stacked hemispheres — are claimed to *generate* V− geometrically: "the negative-green ray can also be obtained artificially by a rigorously calculated form wave."

The school's own casualty anchors the thread in something stranger than speculation: **Chaumery died 27 February 1957, "victim of his experiments, completely dehydrated by our negative green ray (V−)"** — recorded by the surviving inventor in the preface to the 1976 book, with the note that the V+ "antidote" was found only later, through Morel's collaboration [2][3].

## 3. The Mechanism: Angular Refraction and the Disc Emitter

How does a *shape* emit a *spectral ray*? The school's answer — "angular refraction" — is their most transferable idea, and it is stated with unusual clarity in the apparatus articles [3]:

- **The right angle is an emitter.** "The set square forms an angle of 90°, and when you thus trace two perpendicular lines having a common point, you obtain the shortest and most powerful vibration in the Universe: negative green (V−). It is form that engenders the vibration, and the emission always shoots forth along the horizontal line of the 90° angle when that line is oriented on the terrestrial North/South axis."
- **Every angle is a spectral position.** From 0 to 180°, the full range of visible and invisible vibrations can be detected — acute angles emitting "white, ultraviolet, violet, indigo, blue, green+," obtuse angles emitting "black, infrared, red, orange, yellow, green−."
- **Phase matters more than position.** The same ray has a magnetic phase (favorable, "lengthens the wavelength of the living organism") and an electric phase (unfavorable, "shortens that wavelength"). The Disc Emitter's design exists to select between them.

This is the load-bearing bridge of the whole thread: **geometry → spectral position → biological effect, with phase (magnetic/electric) as the moral polarity.** Everything downstream — geobiology, BioGeometry — inherits this structure, whether or not it keeps the vocabulary.

---

## 4. The Safety Record and the Double Face

The tradition's most striking internal datum is that V− is claimed to be **both the most dangerous and the most preserving ray** — and the school documents both on itself:

- **Preservation:** meat, fish, eggs and vegetables "mummified" (dehydrated and preserved indefinitely) under V− emission; flowers sterilized and kept intact; a photographic film "immediately impressed" [2][3]. The mummification claim connects V− directly to the pyramid-preservation folklore (razor blades, small animals) that Karel Drbal's 1959 Czechoslovak patent (ČSFR 91304, "Method for maintaining razor blades") carried into the technical literature — the same claim structure (a geometric form emitting a preserving ray), reached independently.
- **Harm:** Chaumery's death by dehydration; the book's warnings against overexposure ("an accumulation of intense magnetic radio-activity which can have unfavorable consequences. The same holds for the operator who remains too long beside the apparatus") [2][3].
- **The antidote:** the V+ neutralization, credited to Morel after Chaumery's death.

De Belizal and Morel themselves offered the dualist reading: magnetic phase as "Good," electric phase as "Evil," citing the Yin/Yang and asking whether "geometric forms capturing the energy radiated by all the physical and abstract forms of energy in nature" might hold "the two aspects of one same figure" [3]. The hypothesis is unfalsifiable as stated — but the *engineering* conclusion is not: **the same geometry can be tuned between phases, and the tuning is a matter of orientation and inversion, not of substance.**

## 5. The Double Face in Modern Geobiology

A generation later, the same double face reappears — now as a *diagnostic* rather than an engineering claim. The geobiology tradition (Burnet's spectrum article, the Centre Amyris practitioner literature) keeps the Chaumery–Bélizal 2×12 spectrum intact: twelve colors in magnetic phase (pro-biotic), twelve in electric phase (anti-biotic), with V− first in the magnetic list [4][5]:

- **The full-spectrum test.** "To ensure that something is perfectly pro-biotic, one verifies with the pendulum that the spectrum is smooth and in magnetic phase."
- **V− as the place-marker of power.** In practitioner use, V− in *magnetic* phase is the signature of harmonious, high-energy places — sacred sites, healing spaces — while V− in *electric* phase marks the noxious: corner furniture emitting "V(−) E (negative green, electrical)" that causes "headaches and very tiring insomnia," plants withering beside it [5].
- **Cleansing practice.** The Universal Pendulum (PU-6) is used to clear stones and rooms of accumulated V−-electrical emissions and "imbue them with a positive frequency" [5].

The conceptual move here is decisive for the thread's survival: **V− stops being a ray that does something and becomes a quality that a place or object has.** The instrument (pendulum) becomes a spectrometer for the *phase* of an environment. This is the direct ancestor of BioGeometry's measurement practice.

## 6. The Egyptian Turn: BioGeometry and BG3

Dr. Ibrahim Karim — Egyptian architect, trained in the radiesthesia tradition, working from the 1970s onward — inherited this entire apparatus: the color-calibrated pendulum, the spectral circle, the V− concept. His transformation of it is the thread's current endpoint [6][7]:

- **BG3 as a triad.** BioGeometry's cornerstone "energy quality" has three components: the **Higher Harmonic of Ultra-Violet**, the **Higher Harmonic of Gold**, and **Negative Green** — with V− explicitly described as the *carrier* quality, "from radiesthesia." The name survives; the claim changes: V− is no longer a dangerous ray to be tamed but one of three harmonizing components found "at geometric centers of shapes and at the core of natural forming processes."
- **Centering as the mechanism.** BG3 appears at the *center* of shapes — the geometric center of a form, the core of a forming process. This is angular refraction, abstracted: the school's "emission shoots forth along the horizontal line of the 90° angle" becomes "the center of the shape is where the quality concentrates."
- **Phase becomes hierarchy.** Where de Belizal needed magnetic vs. electric phase to distinguish good from harm, Karim distinguishes *centers that manifest BG3* from everything else — a measurement claim (find the BG3 spot) rather than a polarity claim (avoid the electric phase).
- **Applications at scale.** The Swiss town harmonization projects (Hemberg, Hirschberg — reduced epilepsy, asthma, depression, headache reports under cell-tower EMF conditions), the Wageningen agriculture work, the Hepatitis C liver study, the ICU trial in Cairo [6]. Whatever one makes of the outcome claims, the *method* is the thread's endpoint: a modern, applied, quasi-architectural practice built directly on the 1936 spectral system.

Karim's own framing — BioGeometry as a "design language of shape" recovering Ancient Egyptian temple science — closes a loop the French school opened: de Belizal and Morel claimed from the start that the Egyptians "knew perfectly well" the emitting power of angles, that their "frescoes were composed of drawings whose angles are so judiciously calculated that they radiate at will beneficial or maleficent waves," and that it was "by studying Egypt — its architecture and its bas-reliefs — that we managed to surprise the astonishing possibilities of form waves" [3]. The student of the students of Egypt returned to Egypt.

## 7. Convergent Claims Across Independent Traditions

The V− thread's durability rests on claims that recur across traditions with little or no cross-citation:

1. **Geometry emits.** French: angular refraction (1936). Drbal (Czechoslovakia, 1959): pyramid shape preserves razor blades. Karim (Egypt/Switzerland, 1980s–): shape-centers manifest BG3. All three claim that form alone, without power input, produces a measurable physical effect.
2. **One spectral position carries outsized effects.** V− is "the shortest wave and consequently the most powerful" (1936); BG3's V− component is the *carrier* for the other two harmonics (Karim). The claim that a specific, nameable position in a qualitative spectrum does disproportionate work is the thread's signature move.
3. **The same form heals or harms by orientation/phase.** Magnetic vs. electric phase (de Belizal); V−-magnetic at sacred sites vs. V−-electric at noxious corners (geobiology); the demand for "rigorous orientation on the terrestrial magnetic axis" in all the school's instruments [3][5].
4. **Preservation as the physical signature.** Mummification under V− (de Belizal); pyramid preservation (Drbal); the geobiological test of a harmonious place as one where "the spectrum is smooth and in magnetic phase" [4].

The French–Russian seam is documented elsewhere in the archive (Davidson's genealogy names Pavlita, Belizal, Turenne, Drbal as the torsion program's Western ancestors [1]); the V− thread shows *why* the Russian torsion program could cite the French school: both traditions claim that a specific, nameable, geometrically-generable vibration carries information/effects without conventional energy transfer.

## 8. What Changed at Each Generation

| Generation | Claim about V− | Evidence class |
|---|---|---|
| Chaumery–de Belizal (1934–1957) | A spectral ray, most powerful in the spectrum; mummifies, harms, heals by phase | Patent + self-experiment + casualty |
| de Belizal–Morel (1957–1976) | Generable by angular refraction; needs V+ antidote; dualist physics | Apparatus manuals, book |
| Geobiology (1970s–2010s) | Diagnostic marker: V−-magnetic = harmonious place, V−-electric = noxious | Practitioner literature, pendulum practice |
| Karim / BioGeometry (1980s–) | Carrier component of BG3, found at shape-centers; harmonizing, not dangerous | Applied projects, university-adjacent studies |

The concept survived each transition by *shedding testable content and gaining applicability* — from "this ray will mummify a steak at 500 km" to "this place tests harmonious." Whether that trajectory is the mark of a real phenomenon being progressively understood, or of an unfalsifiable idea adapting to survive, is the thread's open question.

## 9. Next Questions

1. **Is there any conventional-physics candidate for V−?** The school's own numbers put form-wave energies at "the millionth or billionth of an electron-volt" [3]. If V− corresponds to anything real, it is not in the EM spectrum at all — which is consistent with BioGeometry's claim that subtle energy is "non-electromagnetic compression waves beyond EM dimension" [6]. What instrument, other than the calibrated pendulum and its human operator, could see it? The Del Giudice/Pollack water-coherence work in the archive [8] is the closest conventional-adjacent candidate for a *medium* — is there a designed experiment coupling a V− emitter to a water-coherence-domain measurement?
2. **The Drbal bridge.** The pyramid-preservation claim (razor blades, mummification) is the one V−-adjacent claim with a patent history and repeated informal replication attempts. The archive holds the de Belizal apparatus descriptions but not Drbal's patent text — a gap worth filling for the thread's documentation.
3. **Phase as falsifiable variable.** The magnetic/electric phase distinction is the school's most engineering-like claim: it predicts that *the same geometry, reoriented, reverses its effect*. A double-blind protocol over the Disc Emitter's own design (magnetic vs. electric phase, operator blind, outcome = pendulum-independent measures like seed germination or water-crystal imaging) would test the tradition's core mechanism, not its interpretation.
4. **BG3's triad as testable structure.** Karim's claim that three components co-occur at shape-centers is a measurement claim that a calibrated instrument (in principle) could check: do the three components vary independently anywhere, or is "BG3" always found as a unit? Independence would falsify the triad; unitary occurrence would be the tradition's strongest result.
5. **Who else kept the thread?** The archive's Italian popularizers (Contino) and Mexican psychogeometry (Ponce de León, Fregoso) both retransmit the form-wave/sacred-geometry synthesis without the V− specifics — the thread thins as it travels. Mapping where V− vocabulary survives vs. where only "form waves" survive would show which parts of the concept carry the load.
