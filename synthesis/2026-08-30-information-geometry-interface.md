# The Information-Geometry Interface in Subtle Field Theories

*Synthesis by Drunvalo — 2026-08-30*

---

## Abstract

At least four independent research traditions—British biology (Sheldrake), Russian physics (Shipov/Akimov), French radiesthesia (Chaumery/de Belizal), and Mexican psychogeometry (Ponce de León)—converge on a striking claim: geometric form encodes informational structure that organizes matter and energy. This synthesis traces that thread through the AFLinks archive, identifies convergent claims, notes Western vs. Russian-French-Italian intersections, and proposes next questions.

---

## The Convergent Claim

All four traditions assert that:

1. **Geometry is not passive.** Shape emits, focuses, or transmits something measurable or detectable.
2. **That "something" is informational, not energetic.** It organizes matter without necessarily transferring energy in the conventional sense.
3. **The vacuum/aether/field is the medium.** Information propagates through a subtler substrate—whether called the physical vacuum, morphogenetic field, form field, or scalar field.
4. **Consciousness can interact with it.** Human intention, attention, or perception can modulate or detect these informational patterns.

This is not identical to quantum information theory, which treats information as abstract and mathematical. These traditions treat information as **ontologically real** and **geometrically encoded**.

## The Traditions

### 1. British Biology: Rupert Sheldrake's Morphic Resonance

Sheldrake's theory of formative causation (1981, 1988) posits morphogenetic fields that organize biological systems. Key claims:

- **Morphic fields are memories of influence.** They are not energy fields but informational structures accumulated through repetition.
- **Morphic resonance is non-local.** Past forms influence present organisms through direct connections across time and space.
- **Natural laws are habits.** Crystallization patterns, animal behavior, and even physical constants may be stabilized by morphic resonance, not fixed laws.

Sheldrake explicitly distinguishes morphic fields from known physical fields: they are "immaterial" and "not recognized by physics." Yet he claims they have measurable effects—crystallization rates, animal learning curves, and telepathic phenomena.

**Geometric aspect:** Morphic fields impose form on indeterminate systems. They act as attractors (in René Thom's catastrophe theory sense), guiding systems toward specific patterns.

### 2. Russian Physics: Shipov and Akimov's Torsion Fields

Shipov's Theory of the Physical Vacuum (1997) extends Einstein-Cartan geometry to include torsion as a fundamental field. Key claims:

- **Torsion is the field of inertia.** All inertial forces are generated by torsion fields arising from rotating matter.
- **The physical vacuum has structure.** It consists of "phytons"—nested electron-positron ring packets—that can be polarized to produce EM, gravitational, or torsion effects.
- **Torsion fields carry information, not energy.** The neutrino is described as a "massless field gyroscope" with spin but no energy—pure information that "covers the entire Universe, forming the Universal Hologram."
- **Consciousness interacts with torsion.** The "address attribute" of a signal in the hologram is "generated by Consciousnesses of various levels, including human consciousness."

Akimov built experimental torsion generators and documented applications in metallurgy, medicine, and propulsion.

**Geometric aspect:** Torsion is literally the twisting of spacetime geometry. The geometry of absolute parallelism (Weitzenböck connection) replaces Riemannian curvature as the fundamental description.

### 3. French Radiesthesia: Chaumery and de Belizal's Form Waves

In the 1930s, Léon Chaumery and André de Belizal developed a systematic theory of "form waves" (ondes de forme). Key claims:

- **Every geometric shape emits a specific wave.** The "spectrum of forms" is analogous to the light spectrum—each shape emits a "vibratory color" detectable by pendulum.
- **Negative green (V-) is a desiccating wave.** Concentrated in the King's Chamber of the Great Pyramid, it mummifies organic matter. Chaumery allegedly died from exposure to this wave.
- **The radiesthetic pile generates polarized form waves.** A stack of spheres or disks acts like an electric battery, emitting + and - polarized waves.
- **Carrier waves transmit information at a distance.** This is the basis of radionics—a form can "transmit" energetic information remotely.

**Geometric aspect:** The shape itself is the emitter. Geometry is not just a container but an active source of specific vibratory signatures.

### 4. Mexican Psychogeometry: Ponce de León's Phi/Pi/Euler Code

Arturo Ponce de León's Psychogeometry (2000s) articulates a geometric code underlying energy-to-matter conversion. Key claims:

- **Energy and matter differ only in geometric arrangement.** Matter is energy "gravitationally trapped" in specific geometric matrices.
- **Three constants govern the code: phi, pi, and Euler.** Phi (golden ratio) defines the spiral movement; pi defines the size (circumscription); Euler gives consistency to the toroid.
- **The Pentaflor process describes creation.** From the void (zero point), a spiral unfolds (phi), is circumscribed by a sphere (pi), and solidifies (Euler). This is the Flower of Life pattern.
- **Sustainable geometry replaces "sacred" geometry.** The term removes religious connotations while preserving the structural claims.

**Geometric aspect:** Geometry is the code itself. The three constants are not arbitrary but necessary for matter to condense from energy.

**Source:** `translations/2026-08-29-psicogeometria-en.md`

### 5. French Scalar Field Theory: Burtin, Toulgoat, Maillot

The Φκ project (2025) proposes a scalar field replacing dark matter and dark energy. Key claims:

- **Vacuum has inertial density.** Measured at 60.3 ± 1.3 × 10⁻³¹ g/cm³ (Planck 2021 data).
- **Scalar field Φ couples to baryonic density.** This modifies effective gravitation without new particles.
- **Vacuum tension Tv is a state property.** Not a force field but a local tension state—acts on the metric, not through it.
- **Gravity is derivable as g = −∇Tv.** This bridges to Michaud's confined EM theory.

**Geometric aspect:** The scalar field is a local modification of spacetime's informational state. Geometry encodes the field's distribution.

## Western vs. Russian-French-Italian Intersections

### Western Mainstream
- Sheldrake is marginalized in British biology despite Cambridge credentials.
- No Western physics program systematically studies torsion (except Hehl's 1970s work, which remains theoretical).
- French radiesthesia is dismissed as pseudoscience in France.

### Russian Program
- Akimov and Shipov received state funding in the 1980s-90s.
- Torsion generators were built and tested in metallurgy, medicine, and space propulsion.
- The "gravitsapa" device flew on the Yubileiny satellite in 2008.

### French-Italian Scalar Program
- Burtin, Toulgoat, and Maillot publish on Zenodo and engage with SPARC data.
- They propose falsifiable alternatives to dark matter—testable on existing astronomical data.
- They bridge to Russian torsion via the vacuum tension concept.

### Key Intersection
Shipov's "Universal Hologram" and Sheldrake's "morphic resonance" describe the same phenomenon from different angles:
- Shipov: neutrino as massless information carrier, holographic universe, consciousness-generated address attributes.
- Sheldrake: morphic fields as memories, non-local resonance, habituated natural laws.

Both claim information propagates non-locally through a subtler medium, and both claim consciousness can address or tune into specific informational patterns.

## What's Missing

1. **Cross-experimental verification.** No published experiments testing Russian torsion claims on Western astronomical data, or French scalar field predictions on Russian metallurgy samples.

2. **Theoretical unification.** No attempt to formally relate torsion fields (Shipov), morphic fields (Sheldrake), and form waves (Chaumery/de Belizal). All use different mathematical frameworks—if any.

3. **Consciousness mechanism.** All traditions claim consciousness interacts with these fields, but none provide a testable mechanism. Shipov's "address attribute" is suggestive but vague.

4. **Mainstream engagement.** No peer-reviewed papers bridging these traditions to quantum information theory, loop quantum gravity, or string theory.
