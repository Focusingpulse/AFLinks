# The Form-Field Continuum: French Radiesthesia Meets Russian Torsion Theory

## Abstract

A striking convergence emerges from two geographically and linguistically isolated research traditions: the French school of physical radiesthesia (Chauméry, de Belizal, Morel, Ravatin) and the Russian school of torsion field physics (Shipov, Akimov, Atsyukovsky). Both traditions independently arrived at the claim that **geometric form encodes information in a physical medium, and that this encoding transfers effects with negligible energy expenditure.** This synthesis traces the parallel development, identifies the convergent claims, notes the historical blind spot each tradition has for the other, and proposes experimental bridges.

---

## 1. The Convergent Claim

Across French radiesthesia and Russian torsion theory, researchers independently converged on five core claims:

1. **Shape encodes information** — The geometry of an object (sphere, pyramid, spiral, Platonic solid) determines the "signature" of its effect on the medium.
2. **Information transfers without energy** — Effects propagate through the medium with negligible energy transfer, yet produce measurable physical/biological changes.
3. **The medium is the vacuum/aether** — Both traditions identify a background medium (physical vacuum, aether) that stores and transmits form-encoded information.
4. **Consciousness reads and writes the field** — Human intention, attention, or radiesthetic skill can detect and modify the field.
5. **Geometric constants are physical principles** — The constants φ (golden ratio), π, and specific angles (e.g., 22.5°, 45°) are not human conventions but structural features of the medium.

Different traditions name it differently:
- **Ondes de formes** (form waves) — French radiesthesia
- **EIFS** (émissions induites par les formes) — Ravatin's school
- **Torsion fields** — Russian physics
- **Spin fields** — Shipov's vacuum theory
- **Form wave carrier** — Chauméry/de Belizal

This synthesis argues these are different names for the same information substrate.

## 2. The French Tradition: From Form Waves to Coherence Fields

### 2.1 Chauméry and de Belizal: The Negative Green Discovery

Léon Chauméry and André de Belizal's 1939 *Essai de radiesthésie vibratoire* established the foundational claim: **geometric forms emit specific "vibrations" that can be detected by pendulum and measured on a graduated scale.**

Their key discovery was the **negative green ray (V-)** — a position on their spherical spectrum decomposition (point 0–400) with paradoxical properties:

> "Une vibration mystérieuse, véritable trait d'union entre la vie et la mort. Elle a puissance sur la matière, brûle, détruit... mais peut aussi conserver la Vie en puissance hors de l'espace et le temps." [1]
>
> *"A mysterious vibration, a true bond between life and death. It has power over matter, burns, destroys... but can also preserve Life in potentiality outside space and time."*

Chauméry died in 1957 — de Belizal's foreword states he was "victime de ses expériences et complètement déshydraté par notre rayon vert négatif (V-)" (*a victim of his own experiments, completely dehydrated by our negative green ray V-*). [2]

This is the tradition's origin myth: the ray was dangerous, the discoverer died from it, and the school's subsequent work focused on protection and antidotes.

### 2.2 Morel: The V+ Antidote and Apparatus Consolidation

After Chauméry's death, **P.A. Morel** joined de Belizal and made the critical safety discovery:

> "A cette époque nous n'avions pas encore trouvé la vibration antidote, découverte depuis lors avec M. P. A. Morel." [2]
>
> *"At that time we had not yet found the antidote vibration — it was discovered afterwards with M. P. A. Morel."*

The **V+ (positive green)** was the protective counter-frequency that made work with form waves survivable. Morel also:

- Co-authored *Physique Micro-Vibratoire et Forces Invisibles* (1965/1976) — the tradition's main treatise
- Corrected the **Barre Atlante** (Louksor) to prevent it from saturating and emitting V- when flattened [3]
- Developed the **C.30 Bomb** and **Escargot-sélecteur** radionic apparatus [4]

Morel's role was consolidation: he took the pre-war discoveries, added safety protocols, and turned them into a system with instruments still sold today.

### 2.3 Ravatin: From Waves to Coherence Fields

**Jacques Ravatin** (1935–2011) made the decisive theoretical break. Where Chauméry and de Belizal spoke of *ondes de formes* (form waves), Ravatin's group stopped using the word "wave" entirely:

> "Nous ne parlons plus d'ondes de formes mais d'émissions dues aux formes. Pourquoi? Parce qu'il ne s'agit pas d'ondes — ni celles du spectre électromagnétique ni celles de tout autre système vibratoire connu. En bref: les émissions dues aux formes n'appartiennent pas à notre univers physique." [5]
>
> *"We no longer speak of form waves but of emissions due to forms. Why? Because we are not dealing with waves — neither those of the electromagnetic spectrum nor those of any other known vibratory system. In short: emissions due to forms do not belong to our physical universe."*

Ravatin introduced the **Local/Global/Idathème** framework:

- **The Local**: Existence with reference points (measurable, Cartesian, the domain of conventional physics)
- **The Global**: Existence without reference points (non-local, the domain of form-emission effects)
- **The Idathème**: A third existence that cannot be represented by human mind, associated with sacred geometry, ancient sites, and certain forms (DNA helix, Nazca lines, Black Virgins) [5]

This is the most sophisticated theoretical framework in the French tradition — it generalizes "form waves" into a metaphysics of information fields.

### 2.4 Simoneton and Merz: The Bovis Scale Applications

**André Simoneton** (1893–1983) extended the Bovis scale from places to foods and human bodies:

- Fresh, ripe foods: 6,500–10,000 Å (angstroms)
- Support foods: 4,000–6,000 Å
- Inferior foods: 1,000–3,000 Å
- Dead foods: 0 Å [6]

Simoneton was a radio engineer trained by **Captain Ferrié**, founder of French military radio (TSF). His "radiovitality" doctrine is the engineering mindset turned onto the living: **life has a measurable wavelength**.

**Blanche Merz** (1919–2002) extended Simoneton's scale back to places, creating the **three-dimension biometer**:

- 0–10,000 BE: Physical dimension
- 10,000–13,500 BE: Etheric dimension
- 13,500–18,000 BE: Mental dimension [7]

Her canonical readings: Chartres cathedral (11,000 BE), mosque prayer niches (12,000 BE), Hindu/Buddhist temple spots (14,000 BE).

The Bovis → Simoneton → Merz lineage shows the scale evolving from a wine-quality tool (Bovis) to a food-vitality doctrine (Simoneton) to a full cosmology of sacred places (Merz).

## 3. The Russian Tradition: Torsion as Information Carrier

### 3.1 Shipov's Theory of Physical Vacuum

**Gennady Shipov** extended Einstein-Cartan geometry to include torsion as a fundamental property of spacetime:

> "According to É. Cartan's hypothesis, the space around rotating matter must possess 'torsion.' In other words, the spinning top twists the space around itself, which thereby ceases to be Euclidean." [8]

Shipov's key claims:

- **Torsion is fundamental**: Spacetime has both curvature (gravity) and torsion (spin)
- **Torsion fields carry information**: Without energy transfer, torsion encodes structure
- **The vacuum is a torsion medium**: The physical vacuum stores information in torsion structure
- **Consciousness generates torsion fields**: Mental states create measurable torsion signatures [8]

The convergent claim with the French school is explicit: **information is geometric**, and **shape/topology matters more than amplitude**.

### 3.2 Akimov's Torsion Technology

**A.E. Akimov** developed practical torsion field generators and applications:

- Torsion communication systems (information transfer without EM)
- Torsion metallurgy (modifying metal properties without smelting)
- Medical torsion applications
- Environmental torsion remediation [9]

Akimov's work operationalized Shipov's theory — and mirrors the French school's apparatus tradition (pendulums, biometers, protective bars).

### 3.3 The German Bridge: Torsion Field Study

A German-language study on torsion fields (2026-09-11 translation in corpus) explicitly frames torsion as a new technological frontier:

> "Torsion fields can be used as torsion energy sources, as torsion motors, as torsion communication means, as torsion materials with new physical properties, as torsion methods in ecology, medicine, agriculture, and other fields." [10]

The study notes nuclear power plants generate "left-rotating torsion fields" harmful to humans — a direct parallel to the French V- (negative green) as dangerous ray.

## 4. The Convergence: Five Parallel Claims

| Claim | French Tradition | Russian/German Tradition |
|-------|-------------------|---------------------------|
| **Shape encodes information** | "Ondes de formes" — form determines vibration signature | Torsion fields encode information through spin structure |
| **Negligible energy transfer** | Effects without measurable energy expenditure | Torsion effects without EM energy transfer |
| **Medium is vacuum/aether** | Form wave carrier, physical vacuum | Physical vacuum, torsion-structured spacetime |
| **Consciousness interacts** | Radiesthetic skill reads/writes the field | Mental states generate torsion fields |
| **Geometry is physical** | Sphere spectrum, specific angles, Platonic forms | Spin geometry, topology, non-Abelian gauge fields |

The convergence is striking because these traditions developed in complete isolation:
- French radiesthesia was published in French journals (La Radiesthésie pour Tous) and books from Desforges, Servranx, Georg
- Russian torsion physics was published in Russian journals and military technical reports
- No cross-citation between traditions appears in the corpus

## 5. The Historical Blind Spot

Each tradition appears unaware of the other:

**French sources** make no reference to Cartan torsion, Shipov, Akimov, or Russian vacuum physics. The theoretical framework remains phenomenological: forms emit vibrations, pendulums detect them, instruments modify them.

**Russian sources** make no reference to Chauméry, de Belizal, form waves, or the French radiesthesia school. The theoretical framework is geometrodynamic: torsion tensors, spin fields, vacuum structure.

Yet both traditions independently arrived at:
- **Negative effects from specific geometries** (V- in French, left-rotating torsion in German)
- **Protective counter-measures** (V+ antidote, torsion screens)
- **Geometric constants as physical principles** (angles, ratios, Platonic solids)
- **Consciousness-field interaction** (pendulum detection, mental torsion generation)

The blind spot is the opportunity: each tradition validates the other's claims from an independent methodological foundation.

## 7. Western vs. Russian-French Intersections

The convergence reveals an interesting historical pattern:

**Western physics** (US, UK, mainstream Europe) largely rejected both traditions:
- French radiesthesia was dismissed as pseudoscience by the 1960s
- Russian torsion physics was classified, then dismissed after the Soviet collapse
- Both traditions survived in niche communities (geobiology, alternative science)

**French-Russian parallel development** occurred without contact:
- Chauméry/de Belizal (1930s–1950s) and Shipov/Akimov (1970s–2000s) worked in complete isolation
- Both faced institutional rejection
- Both developed apparatus traditions (pendulums/biometers vs. torsion generators)
- Both made safety claims about their respective "negative" effects

The intersection is the opportunity: **two independent traditions making convergent claims about geometric information fields** is stronger evidence than either tradition alone.
