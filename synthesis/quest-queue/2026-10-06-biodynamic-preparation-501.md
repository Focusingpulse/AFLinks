## Honest Framing

This is a test, not an endorsement. The source is a **one-season company field trial** on a commercial vineyard, and its own conclusions are overwhelmingly null: *"No result was obtained for the SPAD measurement"*; *"The internode distance… did not give significant results"*; *"no substantial differences were noted in the presence of larvated downy mildew and downy mildew on leaf"*; *"The ripeness curves did not show notable differences"*; *"The parameters analysed do not have a significant difference according to the ANOVA statistical test."* The one positive — a lower leaf insertion angle in the sprayed plots — is described by the authors as *"an interesting tendency"* that is *"difficult to measure and quantify,"* and the water-stress trend was *"significant for row 9 but not for row 27."* The preparation is applied at a **homeopathic dilution**, and a single-run home trial is underpowered. The card keeps all of that honesty: the likely outcome is that the 501 plot matches the control and the sham, and that is a **complete and useful result** (Skeptic's Star), not a failure. A clear, replicating positive (≥6° lower mean leaf angle than both control and sham, and/or a higher RWC) would be genuinely interesting and would justify the taller follow-up. The card is a method test of a cited claim; it makes **no health claim** and is **not** a food-safety test.
