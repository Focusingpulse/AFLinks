## The honest framing (the spine of the card)

Nobody is being asked to prove that misogi works, and nobody is being asked to prove that water remembers. They are asked one narrow question: **does a 30-second cold finish change your post-shower pulse recovery more than the same shower taken warm?**

Four things the card keeps in front of the family:

1. **The source ran no experiment.** Every number in it is second-hand — the Netherlands cold-shower trial, the Tokyo City University bathing work and the 2000 cortisol study are named but not cited. **There is nothing in the source to replicate; there is only a claim to test.**
2. **The mechanism is ordinary, and the source says so.** Cold-water shock → sympathetic activation → parasympathetic rebound is the mainstream account. The source is not claiming a hidden force; it is claiming that an ordinary mechanism is what a traditional practice has been doing all along. **That is why the card can be run at all.**
3. **The source itself rejects the water-memory explanation** and carries the refutation — the 2005 *Nature* result that a structure's "memory" in water is lost within 50 femtoseconds. **So this card is not a water-memory card.** The queue's three water-memory cards test whether water carries information; this one tests the archive's own counter-argument.
4. **The ordinary explanation is complete.** A warm shower also raises and then lowers the pulse; the pulse drifts with time of day, sleep and caffeine; the cold-shock response habituates with repetition. That is why the design is a **randomised within-person crossover** with a **matched warm arm**, a **fixed time of day**, a **daily coin flip**, and a **habituation check** — and why the primary endpoint is a **pulse count**, not a feeling.

**Under-promise, stated plainly:** the likely outcome is that **cold and warm days do not differ beyond the warm-arm scatter** — that in one household a cold finish is not distinguishable from an ordinary shower on this measure — and that the sick-day difference is too small to see in a single family. That is the ordinary prediction and the card is built to reach it cleanly. The one thing the card establishes whatever the result is the household's own **warm-arm scatter** — the number below which no change in that house means anything — which is worth having whether or not the cold does anything.

**Safety, stated plainly:** this is the queue's first card that puts a physiological stressor on the participant's own body. **It is for adults and for teenagers with a guardian present and consenting — not for young children.** No ice baths, no immersion, no breath-holding in water, never alone, and stop the moment shivering is uncontrollable or the skin goes numb. Heart condition, Raynaud's phenomenon, cold urticaria or pregnancy: **do not run the card.**
