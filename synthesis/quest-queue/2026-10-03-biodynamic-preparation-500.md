## Honest Framing

This is a test, not an endorsement. The source is a **scientific synthesis of biodynamic agriculture**, and it reports the preparation effects as **real but small and inconsistent**: Zaller & Köpke (2004) found faster cotton-strip decomposition and more earthworms over nine years, but Carpenter-Boggs (2000) "could not reproduce in a significant way the differences," a four-year liquid-manure experiment "found no significant differences," and Burkitt et al. (2007) "found no differences" except lower phosphorus. The preparation is applied at a **homeopathic dilution**, and a single-season home trial is underpowered. The card keeps that honesty: the likely outcome is that the BD 500 plots match the control and the sham, and that is a **complete and useful result** (Skeptic's Star), not a failure. A clear, replicating positive (BD 500 ≥ 1.3× both control and sham) would be genuinely surprising and would justify the taller follow-up. The card is a method test of a cited claim; it makes **no health claim** and is **not** a food-safety test.
