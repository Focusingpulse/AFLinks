## Honest Framing

This is a test, not an endorsement. The source is a **geobiology blog** in the form-wave tradition, and its claim — that a 3:5:8 space "will always have a greater resonance and harmony" — is **unfalsifiable as written**: "resonance and harmony" are qualities, not numbers. The card's whole job is to make it falsifiable: it fixes a **forced-choice preference** and a **0–10 rating** from **blinded** occupants in two enclosures of **matched volume**, and pre-registers the thresholds before anyone runs. Two honesty notes the card keeps in front of the family: (1) the tradition's "resonance" is **not acoustic** — the source is explicit that the emissions "have no wave character... nothing to do with electromagnetism or with any other known type of energy" — so the card does **not** measure room acoustics, which would answer a different question; and (2) the endpoint is **subjective and underpowered**, so the likely outcome is a null, and a null is a **complete and useful result** (Skeptic's Star), not a failure. A clear, replicating preference (≥ 70 %, ≥ 1.5-point gap) would be genuinely surprising and would justify the taller follow-up. The card is a method test of a cited claim; it makes **no health claim**.
