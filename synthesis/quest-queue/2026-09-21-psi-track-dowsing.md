## Honest Framing

This is a test, not an endorsement. The psi-track is contested parapsychology: the
reported 32/40 result comes from a single Swedish group, has never been independently
replicated, and the Imich Prize citation itself notes the committee split the award
because no entry merited the full prize. The value of the card is that a family can
run the same blind protocol on their own field and get their own answer — including
the honest null. **Never use this in place of a proper search in a real emergency.**
