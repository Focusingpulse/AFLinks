## The honest framing (the spine of the card)

- **The core practice is not contested — and the card says so.** Polyculture, mulch, no-till and no synthetic inputs are ordinary regenerative agriculture, widely practiced and independently documented. **This is the queue's second card whose core practice is mainstream-supported rather than fringe** (the first was the contour swale). Presenting the whole method as doubtful would be dishonest. The value is not that the practice is doubtful; it is that **the numbers are yours** — which plants actually establish on your ground, and whether *when* you plant them beats *how densely* you plant them.
- **The contested part is isolated and carried as the real test.** The source's own distinctive assertion is that *"the critical factor for the establishment and development of a plant is not as much the factor light as the order and timing of its introduction in the natural succession."* That is a specific, falsifiable prediction — and the source's own worked example gives the control: planted **simultaneously**, "the inga and caimito had difficulties establishing themselves." **An identical plot planted all at once is the control for that claim.** If the two plots behave the same, the order claim is refuted at home scale — which is a complete, publishable result.
- **The source is a practitioner's methods paper, not a study.** It publishes no statistical design, no replication, no error bars and no control plots. Its five-year claim is retrospective and site-specific. **The card supplies the controlled comparison the source never published.**
- **The strong claims are out of scope and named as such.** Five-year recovery of completely degraded soil; the mechanism that a maturing dominant consortium *induces senescence in its neighbours* — which the author himself flags as *"the subject of further research"*; and "not labor intensive." **A one-season plot cannot test them, and this card does not pretend to.** A pass here says nothing about soil carbon, about five-year recovery, or about the senescence mechanism.
- **A null is a complete result.** If order and timing change nothing on your ground and the mix alone does the work, that is a real finding and earns the Skeptic's Star.
- **Safety:** none beyond ordinary care. **Verify any shrub or tree you plant is non-invasive in your region** and not on your local extension's noxious-weed list.
