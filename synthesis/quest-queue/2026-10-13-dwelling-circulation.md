## What makes it the queue's first of its kind

- **The first card whose endpoint is the POSITION of a claimed flow's centre inside a room.** Every prior dowsing card asks *whether* something is there (a water vein, a geopathic zone, a form-wave field) or *where along a line* it is; this one asks where the **centre of a rotation** lands on a floor plan, and scores the distance against a measured geometric centre.
- **The first card whose primary endpoint is AGREEMENT between privately-working operators on a geometric quantity** — not whether one operator can find something (blind geopathic mapping), not whether two operators agree on a binary (dossier-059's concordance question), but whether N operators, working alone and blind to each other, put the same point on the same floor plan.
- **The first card built around a NO-TAPE control.** The single most important artifact in the card is the tape marking the geometric centre: if the marks only land in the middle when the tape is there, the "flow" is a response to a visible cue. The control is the experiment.
- **The first card whose subject is the dwelling's INTERIOR FLOW rather than a field the dwelling sits in.** The queue's geobiology cards (Earth-Energy Grid, blind geopathic mapping, bed-shield) are about the earth beneath or the field around a home; this one is about what the guide says happens *inside* it — a circulation that furniture can change.
- **The first card that takes a source's own hedge seriously as data.** The source says "often the geometric centre", not "always". The card scores a loose-but-non-random clustering as a partial result rather than forcing the claim into a binary it did not make.

## The honest framing (the spine of the card)

The card tests a claim, not a tradition. Nobody is being asked to prove that subtle energy exists. They are being asked one narrow question: **if four people read the same empty room privately, do they put the same point on the floor plan — and is it the middle of the room?**

Three things the card keeps in front of the family:

1. **The source names its own confound.** The guide's own paragraph on "mental dowsing" says a pendulum can answer an *internal questioning* rather than an external stimulus. That is the null hypothesis, written by the claimant. The card is that paragraph, given N operators and a blind design.
2. **The tape is the trap.** A room's geometric centre is easy to see once it is marked. The no-tape control is what separates "the room has a flow" from "the operator aimed at the mark" — and it is the first thing a family should run.
3. **The reading is easy to get wrong, and the source says why.** The guide states that the instruments "merely bring to light what is felt" — so the endpoint has to be agreement between people who cannot see each other's work, not one person's confidence.

**Under-promise, stated plainly:** the likely outcome is that **the marks scatter** — that a rod or pendulum in an empty room reads what the holder expects, and that four people put four different points on the plan. That is the honest verdict and the card is built to reach it cleanly. The one thing the card establishes whatever the result is the family's own baseline: a private, chance-scored mapping series on their own floor, which is worth having whether or not a room has a flow.
