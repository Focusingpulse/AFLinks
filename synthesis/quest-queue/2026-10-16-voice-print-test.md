## The honest framing (the spine of the card)

Nobody is being asked to prove that voice profiling works, and nobody is being asked to accept or refuse any frequency. They are asked one narrow question: **in your group, do people who report the same state sound more alike than chance?**

Five things the card keeps in front of the family:

1. **The source published one testable sentence, and this is it.** *"People with similar wellness issues produce similar vocal biomarkers."* Dated 2004. Between-person, directional, falsifiable. Everything else in the school's claim set is case history.
2. **The school's own record separates what can be tested from what cannot.** *"Detection … Partly testable. Application … The case histories are the evidence; independent replication is the missing piece."* The card tests the first and refuses the second by name.
3. **The school's own mechanism admission is the honest ceiling.** *"We don't know why the body responds to the many levels of frequency; we just know that it does."* A positive result would show that voices clustered by self-reported state. It would not show why, and it would not show that any frequency does anything.
4. **The ordinary explanation is the one to beat.** Voices differ for reasons that have nothing to do with wellness — sex, age, accent, smoking, device, room, time of day — and the pre-registered features are the very ones that encode them. That is why the covariate re-run is mandatory and why the pre-registration must be written before the first recording.
5. **This is a measurement, not a diagnosis.** No participant is told anything about their body, and nothing is fed back to anyone. The card's entire output is a hit rate, a chance baseline, and a percentile.
