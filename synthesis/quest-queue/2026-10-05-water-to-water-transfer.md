## What makes it the queue's first of its kind

- **The first card on non-contact water-to-water transfer.** Every prior water card tests something done *to* the water — vortexing (001, 004, 019, 031), a magnet or mineral (061), a thermal history (042), a form (058), an intent or EM field (028), a treatment device (052, 054). - **The first card whose control is a matched sender.** The control sender is the *same plain water*, same volume, same temperature, same jar, same distance — so the only difference between the arms is the sender's **state**, not its material. Most prior cards control the *treatment*; this one controls the *sender*.
- **The first card whose discriminator is direction.** A receiver that merely drifts from its own baseline is not evidence of transfer. The card requires the receiver to move **toward the structured sender's own signature** — a much sharper criterion than "the water changed."
- **The design choice that is the whole card: a no-sender control.** Ordinary drift and evaporation will move *any* drop pattern over 24 h. The no-sender receiver in the same position is what separates "information arrived" from "time passed."
