## Quest

Test the claim that geometric forms emit "form waves" affecting human energy fields. Use dowsing rods to measure your energy field distance before and after holding beneficial forms (Flower of Life, golden spiral, pyramid).

**Title:** Form Waves Space Harmony Test
**Description:** Stand still. Have a partner walk toward you with L-rods. Mark where the rods respond. Now hold a printed Flower of Life or golden spiral at your chest for 3 minutes. Repeat the measurement. Compare distances. Try with a blank paper control. Report the numbers — whether they change or not.
**Subjects:** Science, Geometry, Observation
