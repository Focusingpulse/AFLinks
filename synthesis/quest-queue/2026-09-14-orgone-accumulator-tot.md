## Quest Summary

**Title:** Orgone Accumulator Temperature Test
**Tier:** Straw (home/homelab, ~$150-200, 30-day protocol)
**Domain:** Health (subtle-energy accumulation / vitality)
**Guild Mirror:** Oddball (Aetherforce complement family — suppressed-science re-try candidate)

**The Claim:** A layered enclosure of alternating steel wool and organic material (cotton, wool) — Reich's orgone accumulator — produces a measurable interior temperature elevation above ambient that cannot be explained by convection alone and correlates with atmospheric/weather states.

**The Test:** 30-day triple-blind protocol. Build matched pair: ORAC (layered) and control (solid-wall, same thermal time-constant). Differential thermocouples in both. Log every 5 minutes. Third party blinds sensor identities. Analyze after 30 days.

**Measurable Outcome:** ORAC interior ≥0.1°C warmer than control on average, with correlation to weather states (higher in clear/dry conditions). Second-harmonic component in diurnal cycle.

**Honest Framing:** This is the discriminating experiment the lineage never got. Einstein replicated the temperature difference in 1941 but attributed it to convection. DeMeo claims "definitive" refutation of convection (2009), but no neutral party has run the matched-control test. A PASS would be extraordinary — it would mean Einstein's explanation was incomplete. A FAIL is the expected result and honestly retires the claim for family purposes.
