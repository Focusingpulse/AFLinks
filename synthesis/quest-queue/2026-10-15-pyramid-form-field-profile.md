## The honest framing (the spine of the card)

Nobody is being asked to prove that the form effect is real or that it is not. They are asked one narrow question: **does removing one panel of paper change what the water inside does?**

Four things the card keeps in front of the family:

1. **The mechanism is not mainstream, and this card is not a revival.** The torsion-field carrier has no accepted physical basis and the programme was dismissed. Nothing here reopens that.
2. **The source has no independent replication, and this card is the first attempt at one.** Both papers are from one laboratory; one has a student as corresponding author; both are in the university's own journal. The card is the third-party test that has never been run.
3. **The source's own numbers disagree with each other, and the card says so rather than picking one.** 204% and 148.6% for the same condition is a 37% spread. The card's criterion is therefore *direction and reproducibility*, not a magnitude.
4. **The expected result is the ordinary one, and the ordinary one is a complete result.** If R̄ sits at 1 everywhere, the 204%/170%/92% table becomes an un-controlled drift measurement — and the family will have watched it happen on their own bench, with a control cuvette running beside it.
