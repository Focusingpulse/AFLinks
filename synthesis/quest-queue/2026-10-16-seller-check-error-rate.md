## The honest framing (the spine of the card)

Nobody is being asked to prove that earth radiation exists, or that it does not. Nobody is being asked to accept or refuse any device. They are asked one narrow question: **can the check that a seller uses to demonstrate a device tell the device from an empty room?**

Five things the card keeps in front of the family:

1. **The demonstration is three steps long, and the third step is the whole of the evidence.** Find the spot; install; re-read. **A reading that goes quiet is the evidence.** Nothing else is measured, and the seller's own instrument is the only instrument.
2. **The counter-finding is about the same check, from the same country, thirty years earlier.** The University of Oulu found that the seekers could not find the same points on different occasions, and the 1985 study found that the same operator re-driving the same road marked **different places on the return journey**. **A check whose reading does not reproduce cannot support a before/after conclusion.**
3. **The university group's own word for a device that does not work is "amulet"** — *"objects whose protective effect is based on belief"* — and the seller's own answer to that is that it does not bother him if belief is the mechanism. **Both sentences are in the source, and both belong in the card.**
4. **A patent is not evidence.** The patent office's own engineer says so: *"A patent does not guarantee that the invention works exactly as the inventor presents."*
5. **This is a measurement of a method, not a verdict on a person.** The card's PASS paths include the check surviving. It records no symptom, no sleep, no pain and no wellbeing — **it is not a health card and must not become one.**
