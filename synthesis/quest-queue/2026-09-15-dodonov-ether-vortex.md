## Quest Summary

**Title:** Dodonov Ether Vortex Test
**Tier:** Sand (home-scale, ~$10-20, 90-minute protocol)
**Domain:** Water (ether vortex demonstration)
**Guild Mirror:** Woodland Care (Aetherforce complement family — ether flows related to trees/forests)

**The Claim:** A wooden cylinder with spiral grooves (Dodonov corbio) placed on a jar of water causes ink at the bottom to form a central vortex column extending upward, demonstrating the presence of vertical ether flows entering Earth from space.

**The Test:** Place ink at bottom of still water jar. Position wooden spiral-grooved corrector on top. Photograph ink distribution every 15 minutes for 90 minutes. Remove corrector and photograph 15 minutes later.

**Measurable Outcome:** Ink gathers toward center forming a funnel/column shape within 90 minutes. After corrector removal, vortex dissipates within 15 minutes.

**Honest Framing:** Atsyukovsky claims this demonstrates ether flows from space entering Earth. Alternative explanations: convection currents, temperature gradients, acoustic vibrations, or ink density effects. A PASS would be anomalous — no mainstream physics predicts spontaneous vortex formation from a wooden grooved cylinder. A FAIL is the expected result for skeptics and honestly retires the claim for family purposes.
