## The honest framing (the spine of the card)

The card tests a claim, not a tradition. Nobody is being asked to prove that geopathic stress is real. They are being asked one narrow question: **does a work position the dowser put on the line produce less finished work than a matched position the dowser put off it?**

Four things the card keeps in front of the family:

1. **The source changed five things and compared before with after.** Machines moved off the line — *and* the aisles went from 60 cm to 90 cm, *and* unused machines were removed, *and* piles of fabric were cleared out of the walkways, *and* combs were fitted to the main cable. The source's own photographs show the hall starting crowded, obstructed and cluttered. **Clearing an aisle raises output in any factory on earth.** The study cannot tell you which of the five did it, and the card's matched positions exist precisely so that only one of them varies.
2. **The dowsed line is the input, not a measurement.** The line was found with a pendulum. The card does not re-test whether dowsing works — the queue has carded that four times, and the archive holds controlled dowsing nulls. It takes the dowser's rating as given and asks whether that rating **predicts** something.
3. **The source's performance score was not blind.** Three factory experts rated the workers on a three-point scale, knowing the study was running and knowing which layout was in place. The card's endpoint is a **count**, not a judgement, and the person counting does not know which position is in use.
4. **A rise under observation is the oldest result in industrial psychology.** Twenty workers, told they are in a study, in a hall that has visibly changed, being scored by visiting experts — the Hawthorne effect alone predicts the rise the paper reports.

**Under-promise, stated plainly:** the likely outcome is that **the two positions perform the same**, that the difference sits inside the group's own session-to-session variance, and that a dowsed line predicts nothing about what a group gets done. That is the mainstream prediction and the card is built to reach it cleanly. The one thing the card establishes whatever the result is the group's own **measured noise floor** — the number below which no change in that room means anything — which is worth having whether or not the line does anything.
