## The honest framing (the spine of the card)

The card tests a claim, not a tradition. Nobody is being asked to prove that the Hartmann network is real. They are being asked one narrow question: **does a point a dowser calls a node read differently on a radio than a matched point the dowser calls neutral?**

Four things the card keeps in front of the family:

1. **The source gives the claim nothing to stand on.** No receiver, no station, no distance, no reading, no study — a sentence in a practitioner synthesis, offered as the explanation for what architects supposedly do. There is nothing to replicate in the source; there is only a claim to test.
2. **The network has never been instrument-detected, and the archive holds controlled dowsing nulls** (the ETH Zürich 1946 trials, the Wunder 2003 series). The node's location is the dowser's judgement. The card takes that judgement as given and asks only whether it predicts anything a radio can see.
3. **The source's own mechanism does not reach the claim.** Its node effect is an increase in **gamma radiation** — ionising, ~10¹⁹ Hz — under falling atmospheric pressure. The claim is about **radio, TV and mobile** signals, ~10⁵–10⁹ Hz. Nothing in the source connects the two, and no mechanism is proposed.
4. **The ordinary explanation is local and complete.** Indoors, a signal drops near metal, water, a corner or a wall. The source itself calls metal frames, cars, reservoirs and water pipes **amplifiers** of its effects — so a "node" that reads differently may simply be a place with metal or water in it. That is why the card carries a metal positive control and logs metal and water at every point, and why the primary result is reported **both with and without** the flagged points.

**Under-promise, stated plainly:** the likely outcome is that **nodes and neutral points read the same**, that any difference sits inside the receiver's own noise floor, and that a dowsed node predicts nothing about a radio. That is the mainstream prediction and the card is built to reach it cleanly. The one thing the card establishes whatever the result is the household's own **measured noise floor** — the number below which no change in that house means anything — which is worth having whether or not the node does anything.
