## The honest framing (the spine of the card)

The card tests a claim, not a tradition. The radiesthesia lineage is not being asked to prove anything — it is being asked what it says a flowing-water hose does, and that answer is countable in a garden in an afternoon.

Three things the card keeps in front of the family:

1. **Both accounts predict a dowser with a rod — that is why the blinding is necessary.** "The flowing water produces a field" and "the dowser is reading the hose, the expectation, or their own hand" both put a person with a rod in front of you. Only the **blind flow/no-flow series** separates them.
2. **The expected result is the null, and that is a complete result.** The source's experiments were unblinded field observation; the archive's two controlled dowsing trials both returned nulls. A clean "the marks did not distinguish flowing from still" is a genuine finding — exactly what the "Skeptic's Star" honours.
3. **A positive would be genuinely interesting and would need to repeat.** A blind hit rate significantly above chance, with the zone distances tracking the flow in the source's own direction and the zones vanishing with the hose full but still, would be a real anomaly — and it would justify the taller follow-up the source himself points at (the vacuum/argon mediator test, which is lab-scale and not a home build).

**Under-promise, stated plainly:** the likely outcome is a blind hit rate at chance and zone marks that do not track the flow. That is the honest verdict, and the card is designed to reach it cleanly.
