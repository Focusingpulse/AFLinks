## Quest Summary

**Title:** Group Steigbild Night
**Tier:** Straw (multi-household coordination, ~$20/household, 4 evenings + 1 rating night)
**Domain:** Community (distributed group experiment)
**Guild Mirror:** Community Living (Aetherforce complement family — group coherence / shared experiment)

**The Claim:** Lili Kolisko's capillary dynamolysis (Steigbild) — metal-salt solutions rising through filter paper — produces "rising pictures" shaped by the evening's astronomical configuration. The community-scale test: pictures made the same evening in different homes should resemble each other more than pictures from different evenings, because the timing (not the operator or materials) is what's claimed to shape them.

**The Test:** 2-4 households run identical Steigbild procedures on the same 4 evenings (2 event, 2 control). Photos are blind-rated by ≥3 people who sort them by visual similarity. If same-evening photos cluster at ≥1.5× chance, the timing claim survives; if raters cluster by household instead, materials/operator dominate and the claim fails honestly.

**Measurable Outcome:** Same-evening pair grouping rate vs chance (~25% for 12 photos into 4 groups), averaged across ≥3 blind raters, replicating across a second round.

**Honest Framing:** This is a TEST of a contested claim, not an endorsement. The "Kolisko effect" is rejected by mainstream science and the anthroposophical community's own 2021 replication attempt was inconclusive — which is exactly why a clean multi-household test matters. A FAIL is fully publishable (Skeptic's Star) and honestly retires the advice. Silver nitrate is caustic and stains permanently: adults handle solutions, children observe the pictures.
