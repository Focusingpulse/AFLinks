## Pass/Fail Criteria
- **PASS** (supports claim): Egg vessel reaches target pH faster than cylinder, OR shows more consistent fermentation (fewer off-flavors, no mold), OR blind taste panel consistently prefers egg vessel sample
- **FAIL** (refutes claim): No measurable difference between vessels, OR cylinder vessel performs better on any metric
- **INCONCLUSIVE**: Both vessels fail to ferment properly (ambient temp issue), or contamination in one/both
