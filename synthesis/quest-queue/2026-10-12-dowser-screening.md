## The honest framing (the spine of the card)

The card tests a claim, not a tradition. Nobody is being asked to prove that dowsing is real. They are being asked one narrow question: **does the school's own stated number pick out the people who can actually find the hidden target?**

Four things the card keeps in front of the family:

1. **The source reports no experiment.** The page is a protocol and a scale, full stop. There is no group, no trial count, no result. The "up to 95% clear success" line is a claim about the school attached to no study. The scale is asserted, not measured — and that is exactly what the card fixes.
2. **The page is a training page that sells training.** Its practical function is to tell a student to keep practising and to gate who is ready for paid sample work. That is not evidence of falsity — it is a reason to test the number rather than repeat it.
3. **The archive holds controlled dowsing nulls.** The queue has carded dowsing four times, and the archive carries the controlled literature (ETH Zürich 1946; Wunder 2003; Munich 1987–88 — 500 dowsers screened, 43 selected, 843 trials; Kassel 1990; Iowa 2006; the Scheunen field protocol). The mainstream prediction is that the blind task is at chance — and if the task is at chance, the count cannot predict it.
4. **A null here is a real result.** If the score does not predict the hit rate, then the community's own rule for who is qualified has no predictive content, and a shared survey is no better off screening its participants than picking them at random. That is worth knowing, and it is the most likely outcome.

**Under-promise, stated plainly:** the likely outcome is that **the rotation count and the hit rate are unrelated**, that the high scorers sit at chance alongside everyone else, and that the school's cut-off predicts nothing. That is the mainstream prediction and the card is built to reach it cleanly. Whatever the result, the family ends up with a **scored, blinded, chance-referenced measurement of its own group** — which is worth having whether or not the number does anything.
