## The honest framing (the spine of the card)

- **This card's core mechanism is not contested — and it says so.** That a contour swale reduces runoff and increases infiltration is standard agrohydrology, Keyline practice, and rainwater-harvesting engineering. **This is the queue's first card whose claim is mainstream-supported rather than fringe.** The value is not that the claim is doubtful; it is that **the numbers are yours** — how much rain your ground actually sheds, and whether a weekend's digging changes it. Presenting a known practice as a discovery would be exactly the filler the honesty rule forbids.
- **The contested part is carried as a real arm.** The source insists the retention space must be *shaped* — meandering banks, steep and flat zones, wind-aligned long axis, no liner — while mainstream engineering says a ditch is a ditch and only capacity, infiltration area, and residence time matter. **An equal-volume straight ditch is the control for that claim.** If the swale and the ditch behave identically, the form claim is refuted at home scale — and that is a complete, publishable result.
- **The source is a movement text, not a study.** It publishes no measurements — no runoff figures, no infiltration rates. Its one quantitative claim is retrospective and site-specific ("Lake 1 filled to two-thirds in the first winter"; "in the first year a new seepage spring emerged"). **The card supplies the protocol the source never published.**
- **The strong claims are out of scope and named as such.** "No rainwater or wastewater leaves the terrain"; "all outflowing water should once again be spring water"; a spring re-emerging in year one; water "maturing" underground and "absorbing information." These are whole-catchment, multi-year, and in part metaphysical. **A plot cannot test them.** A pass here says nothing about aquifer recharge or spring re-emergence, and the report must say so.
- **A null is a complete result.** If the earthwork changes nothing measurable on your ground, that is a real finding and earns the Skeptic's Star.
- **Safety:** call 811 / your utility locating service before digging; keep swales away from foundations, roads, and septic fields; do not undercut a slope above a structure.
