## Source Documentation

- **Primary source:** Olof Alexandersson, *Living Water: Viktor Schauberger and the Secrets of Natural Energy* — English edition 1982, Italian edition *Acqua Viva*. Full translation in the Vault: `translations/2026-09-11-acqua-viva-viktor-schauberger-it.md`, passages at lines 3831–3945. The claim is quoted verbatim in the dossier.
- **Corroborating source (independent translation):** `translations/2026-09-09-schauberger-dynamic-hydroelectric-energy-es.md` (lines 136–174) — a Spanish technical analysis of Schauberger's 1930 jet-turbine patent. It states the same 1952 result: "the friction in this pipe decreased with an increase in velocity and, at a certain point, the water flowed with **negative resistance**." Two translations from two different source documents carry the claim — this is not a single-translator artifact.
- **Provenance note, stated up front:** the 1952 experiment is reported as a **chart**, with no numerical table and no error bars, made with 1950s instrumentation. Pöpel's own written verdict on the spiral tube is quoted ("the column of water frees itself from the walls of the pipe and oscillates freely") — an interpretation, not a datum. The claim is extraordinary and has never been independently replicated. That is the point of the test, and it is stated here rather than buried.
- **What the mainstream predicts:** ordinary coiled/spiral pipe generates secondary (Dean) vortices and normally has **more** pressure drop than a straight pipe of equal length. So mainstream fluid mechanics and the source's claim point in *opposite* directions. That is what makes this a discriminating test: a null result is as meaningful as a positive one.
- **Claim status record (context, not evidence):** `synthesis/claim-status-records/schauberger-vortex-repulsine-1951.json` — Schauberger's vortex/implosion lineage, status *died* (suppression, contractual gag, prototype failure).
