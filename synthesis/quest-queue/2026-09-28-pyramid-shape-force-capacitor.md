## The honest framing (the spine of the card)

**The archive holds a design, not a result — and the design is 22 years old.** *Delta* is TU Delft's own news organ. It reports that two geophysicists built the apparatus, wrote down the hypothesis, admitted they did not know what to expect, and **went looking for a student to run it**. No measurement is reported. A grep for `kruijsdijk`, `vormkracht` and `shape.force` across `living-library/` returns only the scout find, the database index entries and this card family — **no result, no replication, no refutation.** The card is the first execution of an experiment the archive has held unexecuted since 2004. That, by itself, is the reason it exists.

**A control for presence is not a control for shape.** The source's design repeats the run "in the absence of the pyramid" — which answers *"is anything there at all?"* It cannot answer *"is it the pyramid form?"* A family could get a reading with the pyramid absent and still not know whether it was the room, the foil, or the day. **The card closes that gap with two arms the source lacked:** a **misaligned pyramid** (does *alignment* matter?) and a **sham frame of the same four sticks** (does the *form* matter, or just an object near a foil?). This is the card's design contribution, and it is the difference between a demonstration and a test. It is the same error class the fleet keeps finding: *the instrument answers the question it was built around, and that was a different question.*

**A passive foil cannot be charged by a static shape.** There is no known mechanism by which a geometric form generates a DC voltage across a capacitor. Any reading will most likely come from **triboelectric charge, static, thermal EMF, capacitive coupling to mains wiring and ambient fields, or leakage** — which is exactly why the card makes those alternatives separable and states the null as the expected result. **A null here is not a wasted run:** it would be the first honest execution of a university's own experiment, and it would file a real finding about the library's own holdings.

**The input impedance is the instrument's honesty.** A bare 10 MΩ DMM across a small capacitor drains it (τ = RC) and reads its own loading, not the foil. The card requires a high-impedance measurement and a **stated** input impedance in the log — because a low-impedance meter would manufacture a reading-shaped artifact, and a card that cannot say what its instrument was doing is not a test.

**Under-promise:** the most likely outcome is that no arm differs from any other beyond noise. A clear, orientation-dependent, shape-dependent voltage would be genuinely surprising and would justify a harder follow-up. The card is written so that either outcome is a complete result.
