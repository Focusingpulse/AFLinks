## The honest framing (the spine of the card)

Nobody is being asked to prove that dowsing works or that it does not. They are asked one narrow question: **does the rod add anything to what the operator can already do?**

Four things the card keeps in front of the family:

1. **The practitioners state the instrument claim and never test it.** The corpus says what the rod is for and how to program it. It reports no trial in which the rod was removed and accuracy compared. The claim is asserted, not demonstrated.
2. **The critics' literature is real — and it does not answer this question.** Kassel, Scheunen, McCarney and the rest test the **operator's discrimination**. They are silent on the rod. The critics' mechanism is consistent with their evidence; it is not demonstrated by it. The card tests the mechanism, not the conclusion.
3. **The design has a limitation and the card says so first.** The operator knows whether they are holding the rod. Expectancy alone could produce a worse no-rod result. That is why the target is randomised, the order counterbalanced, the no-rod condition run first in half the sessions, and confidence recorded every trial — and why a single session proves nothing.
4. **A null is a complete result.** If neither condition beats the shuffled baseline, the rod adds nothing **and** the operator detects nothing at this task — and the manipulation that produced it is the one the sibling lineage's own record says ended its claim: **removing the input.**
