# The Protective Apparatus Lineage: From Chaumery's Death to BioGeometry Centering

## Abstract

On February 27, 1957, Léon Chaumery died from the very discovery that made his name — the negative green ray (V-), a form-wave emission that dehydrates and destroys biological tissue. His research partner, André de Bélizal, survived only because P. A. Morel discovered the **V+ antidote vibration** that neutralizes V-'s destructive effects. This synthesis traces the protective apparatus lineage that emerged from that crisis: Morel's corrected Barre Atlante (Louksor), La Foye's directing axes using the Tetragrammaton, and Karim's BioGeometry centering quality (BG3). Each generation developed protective technology that made form-wave work survivable — transforming a lethal discovery into a therapeutic tradition. The convergent claim across all four stages: certain geometric configurations create self-balancing, self-protecting fields that resist noxious emissions without external power.

---

## 1. The Fatal Discovery: Chaumery and Negative Green (1936–1957)

### 1.1 The Sphere Spectrum Decomposition

In 1936, Chaumery and de Bélizal patented FR 816,132, describing a method for "measuring and utilizing cosmo-magnetic currents" through a hemispherical pendulum with a spectrum of form-wave emissions mapped onto its surface [1]. The key discovery was **negative green** (V-), located at point 0–400 on their sphere spectrum:

> "Le vert négatif représente le point 0–400... une vibration mystérieuse, véritable trait d'union entre la vie et la mort. Elle a puissance sur la matière, brûle, détruit... mais peut aussi conserver la Vie en puissance hors de l'espace et le temps. Cette vibration servait aux Egyptiens pour déshydrater leurs morts."

*Negative green represents point 0–400... a mysterious vibration, a true bond between life and death. It has power over matter, burns, destroys... but can also preserve Life in potentiality outside space and time. The Egyptians used this vibration to dehydrate their dead.* [2]

This was not merely theoretical. The Egyptian pendulum — one of the school's central instruments — was designed to emit V- for mummification-like effects. The same quality that preserved the dead could, in living tissue, produce fatal dehydration.

### 1.2 Chaumery's Death

De Bélizal's Avertissement to *Physique Micro-Vibratoire et Forces Invisibles* (1965) states plainly:

> "C'est avec Léon Chaumery, il y a quelque trente ans, que nous avons commencé nos recherches... victime de ses expériences et complètement déshydraté par notre rayon vert négatif (V-)... décédé le 27 février 1957."

*It was with Léon Chaumery, some thirty years ago, that we began our research... a victim of his own experiments, completely dehydrated by our negative green ray (V-)... died on 27 February 1957.* [2]

Chaumery's death is the founding trauma of the protective apparatus lineage. The discovery that made the school's reputation also killed its co-discoverer. The question after 1957 was not whether V- existed, but whether anyone could work with it safely.

## 2. The Antidote: Morel's V+ Discovery (1957–1965)

### 2.1 The Missing Vibration

De Bélizal's foreword credits Morel directly with the survival of the research program:

> "A cette époque nous n'avions pas encore trouvé la vibration antidote, découverte depuis lors avec M. P. A. Morel qui a bien voulu se pencher sur nos travaux et nous assurer son concours précieux."

*At that time we had not yet found the antidote vibration — it was discovered afterwards with M. P. A. Morel, who was kind enough to look into our work and give us his precious assistance.* [2]

The **V+** vibration — the positive counterpart to V- — is the technical foundation of all subsequent protective apparatus. V+ does not cancel V- in the sense of wave interference; rather, it provides the balancing quality that prevents V-'s destructive dehydration. The school's own theory describes V- as "le trait d'union entre la vie et la mort" (the bond between life and death); V+ is the vitalizing complement that tips the balance toward life.

### 2.2 The Corrected Barre Atlante

The Barre Atlante (Atlante Bar) is a wooden protective bar derived from a ring allegedly discovered by the Marquis d'Agrain in 1860, passed to de Bélizal through family lineage. Servranx describes the problem Morel solved:

> "Si la bague est exceptionnelle dans sa forme propre, P. A. Morel a découvert que sa forme mise à plat et agrandie se saturait au point de produire du vert négatif. Il a donc analysé le problème, modifié les extrémités, jusqu'à lui donner sa forme actuelle qui n'a plus cet inconvénient."

*Exceptional as the ring is in its own form, P. A. Morel discovered that its form, flattened and enlarged, saturated to the point of producing negative green. He therefore analyzed the problem and modified the ends until he gave it its current form, which no longer has this drawback.* [3]

This is the technical pattern: **a protective form that becomes dangerous when scaled, corrected by a researcher who identifies the saturation point and modifies the geometry**. Morel named the corrected bar "Louksor" after an Egyptian sarcophagus model, claiming an effective protective radius of 15–20 meters.

### 2.3 Morel's Position in the Lineage

Morel's contribution is the hinge between the lethal discovery (Chaumery's V-) and the therapeutic tradition (Karim's BG3). Without V+, the French form-wave school would have ended with Chaumery's death. With V+, the school produced protective apparatus still sold and used today.

## 3. The Directing Axes: La Foye's Self-Balancing Emitter (1960s–1970s)

### 3.1 The Reciprocal Circles Board

Jean de La Foye, an agricultural engineer in contact with de Bélizal, developed the **Reciprocal Circles Board** (Tableau des Cercles Réciproques) as an emitter of form emissions. The board uses fractally repeating circles (the signature of the **vital field**, contrasted with the hexagonal fractals of physical matter) and — critically — **directing axes formed by the Hebrew letters of the Tetragrammaton** (יהוה) [4].

La Foye's innovation: the directing axes permanently balance the emitter's field without external adjustment. The board becomes "a true little universe sufficient unto itself: a microcosm" [4].

### 3.2 The Convergent Claim: Self-Balancing

La Foye's "directing axes" and Morel's "corrected ends" serve the same function: **geometric modifications that prevent saturation with noxious emissions**. Both researchers discovered that certain configurations (Hebrew letters, modified terminations) create self-balancing fields that resist perturbation.

This is the protective principle: not shielding (blocking emissions) but centering (creating a field that maintains its own balance). The distinction matters because shielding degrades over time, while centering is self-sustaining.

## 4. The Modern Synthesis: Karim's BG3 and Centering (1990s–present)

### 4.1 BG3 as Protective Quality

Dr. Ibrahim Karim's BioGeometry identifies three qualities — **Horizontal Negative Green**, **Vertical Negative Green (Ultra-Green)**, and **Higher Harmonic of Gold** — as the signature of beneficial environmental resonance [5]. The first component (Horizontal Negative Green) is the same V- that killed Chaumery.

Karim's contribution: V- is not inherently destructive. It is a carrier quality that can transport beneficial or harmful information. When balanced with the other BG3 components, V- becomes part of a protective, centering field rather than a dehydrating emission.

### 4.2 Centering as Technical Term

In BioGeometry, **centering** describes a state where a system is internally harmonized and resistant to external perturbation. Centered spaces, objects, and living systems emit BG3 and maintain their coherence despite environmental noise.

This is functionally equivalent to La Foye's "microcosm" and Morel's "corrected Barre Atlante" — all three describe self-balancing fields that require no external power to maintain protection.

### 4.3 The Egyptian Connection

Karim explicitly connects to the French tradition: the Atlantean ring and Egyptian pendulum that Chaumery and de Bélizal studied are central BioGeometry artifacts. Karim studied with de Bélizal's successors and inherited the form-wave methodology directly [5].

The Egyptian temple geometry that Karim analyzed provides the sacred geometry vocabulary for BioSignature design — geometric patterns that emit BG3 when placed in architectural and environmental contexts.

## 7. What Remains Unexplained

1. **The physical mechanism of V- dehydration.** The school's own theory places V- near magnetic waves accompanying cosmic rays, with energies "on the order of millionths and billionths of electron-volts" — far below conventional ionizing radiation. How does such weak emission produce fatal dehydration? The school admits: "Le jour où l'on pourrait trouver un tel système de mesures, nous aurions moins besoin de la radiesthésie" (*the day such a measurement system is found, we would need radiesthesia less*) [2].

2. **The relationship between V+ and V-.** Is V+ a separate emission, a phase-shifted version of V-, or a geometric modification that prevents V- saturation? The texts are ambiguous.

3. **The reproducibility problem.** All protective apparatus claims rest on radiesthetic detection — a method that requires practitioner skill and lacks instrumental validation. Can V+ protection be verified by non-radiesthetic means?

4. **The Tetragrammaton mechanism.** La Foye claims Hebrew letters function as directing axes, but provides no theory for why these specific graphemes produce self-balancing fields.
