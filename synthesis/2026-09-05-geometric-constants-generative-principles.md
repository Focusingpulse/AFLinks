# Geometric Constants as Generative Principles: Phi, Pi, Euler as the Mathematical Substrate of Matter Formation

*Synthesis by Drunvalo — 2026-09-05*

---

## Abstract

At least five independent research traditions — Mexican psychogeometry (Ponce de León), Greek Platonic particle physics (Spyridis), French vacuum tension theory (Maillot/Michaud), Brazilian scalar wave engineering (Waechter), and Italian LENR technology (Prometheus) — converge on a claim that challenges the conventional understanding of mathematical constants: **phi, pi, and euler are not human abstractions describing nature but generative principles through which nature organizes itself.** These constants are the "code" of the vacuum, the mathematical substrate from which matter crystallizes. This synthesis traces that thread through the AFLinks archive, identifies convergent claims across traditions that developed in near-total isolation, notes the Mexican-Greek-French-Brazilian-Italian intersections, and proposes next questions.

Where previous syntheses traced the vortex as organizing principle (2026-09-04), water as information medium (2026-09-04), scalar fields as dark sector alternative (2026-09-03), and the non-Abelian gauge bridge (2026-09-03), this one traces the **constants themselves** — the claim that phi, pi, and euler are not merely useful numbers but the generative geometry through which the vacuum becomes matter, through which energy becomes form, through which the formless becomes formed.

## The Convergent Claim

All five traditions assert that:

1. **Mathematical constants are discovered, not invented.** Phi, pi, euler exist independently of human mathematics. They are discovered through mathematics but are not created by it. They are properties of reality itself.

2. **Constants are generative, not merely descriptive.** Phi does not describe the spiral after it forms; phi is the principle through which the spiral forms. Pi does not describe the circle after it exists; pi is the principle through which circular motion becomes possible. Euler does not describe exponential growth; euler is the principle through which growth consolidates into structure.

3. **The three constants form a generative triad.** Phi creates the spiral motion (the path). Pi determines the size (the boundary). Euler gives consistency (the solidification). Together they form the complete process: motion → boundary → structure.

4. **Matter is energy organized geometrically.** What distinguishes matter from energy is not substance but geometry. Energy is the same substance as matter, only in chaotic (non-geometric) motion. Matter is energy trapped in geometric matrices.

5. **The constants operate at all scales.** The same phi, pi, euler that structure galaxies also structure atoms, cells, and organisms. Scale changes; the constants remain.

6. **Technology can work with the constants.** Scalar wave generators, LENR reactors, and geometric healing devices all claim to harness these principles — not by imposing geometry but by aligning with it.

This is distinct from conventional physics, which treats mathematical constants as useful descriptors but not as causal agents. These traditions treat constants as the causal geometry itself.

## The Traditions

### 1. Mexican Psychogeometry: Arturo Ponce de León

Arturo Ponce de León, founder of Psicogeometría México, presents the most explicit formulation of the generative constants thesis.

Key claims:

- **Three constants govern matter formation.** Phi, pi, and euler are the "mathematical matrix that supports matter." They are not arbitrary but necessary — the only constants that can create sustainable structure from chaos.

- **Phi creates the spiral.** The golden spiral (φ = 1.618...) is "the first movement to generate life." All natural motion is spiral, not linear. Phi defines the relationship of smaller to larger to whole — the harmonic proportion that allows parts to relate to each other and to the totality.

- **Pi determines the boundary.** The spiral does not extend infinitely; it curves back on itself. Pi (π = 3.141...) defines the size of the toroid — the sphere that circumscribes the spiral. Matter has finite size because pi limits the spiral.

- **Euler gives consistency.** The constant e (2.718...) "solidifies the toroid." It is "the most important number in mathematical analysis" and "fits almost all the properties required to describe the strong interactions of elementary particles." Euler gives the structure rigidity or flexibility.

- **The Pentaflor process.** From chaos (void), the first geometric movement is spiral (phi). The spiral extends until it reaches a boundary (pi). Then it wraps around the sphere and returns to center, radiating captured energy and solidifying structure (euler). This is the universal creation pattern.

- **The constants are trinity.** Phi, pi, euler correspond to the trinity patterns in all religions and philosophies: Father-Son-Spirit, Osiris-Isis-Horus, Brahma-Vishnu-Shiva. The constants are not merely mathematical but archetypal — the generative pattern through which the One becomes Many.

**Generative aspect:** Ponce de León explicitly states that geometry is not descriptive but causal. "Energy and matter are only differences in geometric arrangement." The constants do not describe; they generate.

### 2. Greek Platonic Particle Physics: Charalambos Spyridis

Professor Charalambos Spyridis (University of Athens) provides the most rigorous mathematical formulation of geometric physics in the archive.

Key claims:

- **Platonic solids model fundamental particles.** Tetrahedron → photon, octahedron → electron, cube → positron, icosahedron → proton, dodecahedron → neutron. This is not analogy but structural identity.

- **Geometric harmony encodes physical structure.** The cube has 6 faces, 8 vertices, 12 edges. The harmonic mean 8 of 6 and 12 encodes the musical fourth (8:6), fifth (12:8), and octave (12:6). This "geometric harmony" is physical, not decorative.

- **Kepler was right in principle.** Kepler's failed attempt to model planetary orbits with Platonic solids was wrong in application but right in intuition: the geometry of space determines the structure of matter.

- **The four mathematical sisters.** Arithmetic, Geometry, Harmonics (Music), and Spherics (Astronomy) are not separate disciplines but four aspects of one mathematical reality. Each requires knowledge of all preceding sisters.

- **Stereometry develops imagination.** The study of three-dimensional forms "develops the imagination of man, leading to models for the interpretation and processing of situations in other fields of knowledge." Geometric understanding transfers to all domains.

- **The cube is "geometric harmony."** Nicomachus: "They call the cube a 'geometric harmony,' because it is fitted together from three equal dimensions." The harmonic mean 8 of 6 and 12 reflects the fundamental trinity structure.

**Generative aspect:** Spyridis explicitly claims that Platonic solids are not models of particles but the actual structure of particles. The geometry is not describing the particle; the geometry is the particle. The constants are not arbitrary but necessary — the only values that can produce stable form.

### 3. French Vacuum Tension Theory: François Maillot

François Maillot's Wave Universe Theory (TUO — Théorie de l'Univers Onde) provides the mathematical framework for understanding the vacuum as a medium with geometric tension.

Key claims:

- **Vacuum tension T_v is a scalar field.** The vacuum is not empty but possesses a geometric tension field. This field replaces the cosmological constant Λ with a dynamic, local term.

- **T_v is a geometric state property.** Unlike force fields (electromagnetic, gravitational), T_v is not a gauge field but a state property of the medium itself. It measures "the geometric tension of the vacuum."

- **Gravitation emerges from electromagnetic confinement.** The Michaud model proposes that vacuum tension arises from the internal electromagnetic energy confined within elementary particles. Gravitation is not fundamental but emergent from confined EM.

- **The field equation is geometric.** The TUO Lagrangian yields field equations where T_v couples to the metric. The vacuum tension modifies spacetime geometry directly, not through force mediation.

- **Dynamic replacement for Λ.** Unlike the cosmological constant (which is uniform and constant), T_v varies spatially and temporally. This produces variable expansion H(z) rather than constant acceleration.

**Generative aspect:** TUO explicitly treats the vacuum as having geometric properties that generate gravitational effects. The scalar field T_v is not a force field but a geometric state — the tension of the medium itself. This aligns with the generative constants thesis: geometry is not describing reality but constituting it.

### 4. Brazilian Scalar Wave Technology: Marcelo Waechter

Marcelo Arthur Mendes Waechter's patented scalar energy generator provides practical application of scalar field principles.

Key claims:

- **Scalar waves at 12 Hz.** The device generates "floating waves" at a fixed frequency of 12 Hz through alternating positive/negative exciter circuits. The waves are "interspersed between neutrons and electrons or protons and electrons or positive and negative."

- **Scalar energy has magnitude but no direction.** Unlike electromagnetic waves, scalar waves "manifest in all directions at once." They are "in electrical equilibrium in their normal state" and therefore "not readily apparent to electromagnetism-based measuring instruments."

- **The Bloch Wall is a scalar region.** Research by Davis and Rawis identified the Bloch Wall in magnets — the region between poles where scalar energy is captured. This is where "its components form the characteristic polarities of the magnet."

- **The body is a scalar network.** "Each living cell in the body emits electromagnetic radiation, each of which has a positive and a negative polarity." The confluence of these polarities generates the body's scalar field.

- **Scalar energy facilitates energy flow.** "Scalar energy plays this role by facilitating energy flow and optimizing cellular functions. In the long run, this can slow aging and prevent the onset of disease."

**Generative aspect:** Waechter's device does not impose scalar waves but generates them through alternating positive/negative pulses — mimicking the natural process. The 12 Hz frequency is not arbitrary but aligns with biological rhythms (alpha brain waves, Earth's Schumann resonance). The device works with the constants, not against them.

### 5. Italian LENR Engineering: Prometheus

The Prometheus project represents commercial application of LENR (Low Energy Nuclear Reactions) technology.

Key claims:

- **LENR uses water and electricity.** Unlike fission or fusion, LENR "uses water and electricity, without the need for fuels or radioactive elements." The technology extracts energy from matter through stimulation rather than destruction.

- **Multiple phenomena under one name.** "The acronym LENR defines a series of phenomena — including metamorphosis, transmutation, cavitation, cold fusion, and low-energy fusion — that stimulate matter in order to extract energy."

- **Institutional recognition.** "To date, LENR technologies are the subject of international studies and research aimed at testing and certifying experimental phenomena characterized by excess heat or isotopic changes. Among the main governmental players are the USA, Japan, and the EU."

- **Practical applications.** The technology targets "high energy-intensity industries, residential use for water heaters and boilers, cooling systems, co-generation plants, and mobility and transport."

**Generative aspect:** LENR technology claims to stimulate matter to release energy rather than destroying matter to extract energy. This aligns with the generative constants thesis: matter is energy in geometric arrangement; by stimulating the geometry (through cavitation, resonance, etc.), energy can be released without destroying the substrate.

## The Convergence: Constants as Code

The five traditions converge on a striking claim:

**Phi, pi, and euler are not human abstractions but the generative code through which the vacuum organizes itself into matter.**

This claim has several dimensions:

### 1. Ontological Priority

Conventional physics: Constants are discovered through mathematics but exist in a Platonic realm separate from physical reality.

Generative thesis: Constants are the causal structure of physical reality. They do not describe; they generate.

Ponce de León: "Energy and matter are only differences in geometric arrangement."
Spyridis: Platonic solids "are the actual structure of fundamental particles."
Maillot: Vacuum tension is "a geometric property of the state of the medium."

### 2. The Generative Triad

All three constants are necessary. None is sufficient alone:

- **Phi (1.618...):** Creates the spiral path. Without phi, energy would move linearly and dissipate. Phi creates the self-referential path that returns to itself.

- **Pi (3.141...):** Creates the boundary. Without pi, the spiral would extend infinitely. Pi creates the closure that allows structure to form.

- **Euler (2.718...):** Creates the consistency. Without euler, the structure would be unstable. Euler creates the consolidation that gives matter durability.

The triad mirrors trinity structures across cultures: Father-Son-Spirit, Brahma-Vishnu-Shiva, thesis-antithesis-synthesis. The constants are not merely mathematical but archetypal.

### 3. Scale Independence

The same constants operate at all scales:

- **Atomic:** Platonic solids model particles (Spyridis)
- **Molecular:** Water coherence domains (Del Giudice/Pollack)
- **Biological:** Cell scalar fields (Waechter)
- **Planetary:** Kepler's nested polyhedra (Spyridis)
- **Cosmic:** Vacuum tension field (Maillot)

Scale changes; the constants remain. This suggests they are not emergent properties of specific systems but fundamental to reality itself.

### 4. Technological Access

All five traditions claim that technology can work with the constants:

- **Mexican:** Geometric architecture and healing devices
- **Greek:** Particle physics implications
- **French:** Vacuum engineering through T_v manipulation
- **Brazilian:** Scalar wave generation at 12 Hz

The claim is not that technology imposes geometry but that technology aligns with geometry. The constants are already operating; technology amplifies or redirects their natural action.

## Western vs. Latin Intersection

A notable pattern: The generative constants thesis emerges most strongly in non-Anglophone traditions:

- **Mexican (Spanish):** Explicit generative thesis
- **Greek:** Platonic solids as actual structure
- **French:** Vacuum tension as geometric state
- **Brazilian (Portuguese):** Scalar waves as biological interface
- **Italian:** LENR as matter stimulation

The English-language literature tends toward descriptive mathematics. The generative thesis — that constants are causal — is more explicit in Latin, Greek, and Romance-language traditions.

This may reflect:

1. **Cultural patterns:** Latin cultures retain stronger connections to Platonic and Pythagorean traditions.
2. **Language structures:** Romance languages may facilitate the ontological leap from "is like" to "is."
3. **Scientific marginalization:** Non-mainstream researchers in non-Anglophone contexts may be freer to make ontological claims.

Whatever the cause, the convergence across linguistic boundaries strengthens the claim. These traditions developed largely in isolation; their convergence suggests independent discovery of the same pattern.
