# The Universal Hologram: Convergent Models of Cosmic Information Storage and Access

## Abstract

At least five independent research traditions — Russian torsion physics (Shipov/Akimov), British biology (Sheldrake), French radiesthesia (Chaumery/de Belizal, Vibratis), French cosmology (Benaros), and Italian quantum biophysics (Manzalini) — converge on a claim that challenges the materialist assumption that information is stored in brains, books, and computers: **the cosmos itself stores information in geometric structure, and this information is accessible non-locally through fields whose shape encodes meaning.** This synthesis traces that thread through the AFLinks archive, identifies convergent claims across traditions that developed in near-total isolation, notes the Russian-French-British-Italian intersections, and proposes next questions.

---

## 1. The Convergent Claim

All five traditions assert that:

1. **Information exists independently of matter.** The cosmos stores information in field structure, not in material substrate. This information persists and is accessible.

2. **Geometric form encodes information.** The shape of a field — its topology, curvature, torsion — encodes information content. Different shapes carry different information.

3. **Access is non-local.** Information does not propagate from point to point like electromagnetic waves. It arises simultaneously at all points that share the correct "address attribute" (Shipov's term) or resonance pattern.

4. **Consciousness can read and write.** Human consciousness interacts with the cosmic information field — reading through radiesthesia, pendulum, intuition; writing through intention, radionics, focused attention.

5. **The mechanism is informational, not energetic.** Accessing cosmic information does not require energy transfer in the classical sense. The field's shape encodes meaning; consciousness "reads" the shape.

This is distinct from mainstream information theory, which treats information as abstract (Shannon entropy) or substrate-dependent (neural codes). These traditions treat information as a field — as real as the electromagnetic field, but with different properties.

## 2. Shipov's Universal Hologram

### 2.1 The Core Claim

G.I. Shipov's Theory of the Physical Vacuum proposes that the neutrino — a massless, chargeless particle carrying only spin — is the first birth from the vacuum and immediately covers the entire Universe, forming a **Universal Hologram**:

> "Any small region of the Hologram contains all information about the Universe — about its Past, Present, and Future. An informational, energy-free signal in the Hologram does not propagate from point to point... but arises instantaneously at any point in space, if there is an 'address attribute' of the sent signal at that point."

This is the most explicit formulation of cosmic information storage in the archive.

### 2.2 Address Attributes and Consciousness

Crucially, Shipov states:

> "The 'address attribute' itself is generated by Consciousnesses of various levels, including human consciousness."

This explicitly connects torsion physics to consciousness research. The cosmic information field is not passive — it responds to consciousness. Intention sets the address; the field delivers the information.

### 2.3 Torsion as Information Carrier

Shipov identifies torsion fields as the physical mechanism:

- Torsion fields carry information without energy
- They propagate instantaneously (no retardation)
- They are generated by spin angular momentum
- They do not diminish with distance (non-local)

The neutrino is an "energy-free information carrier" — the physical substrate of the Universal Hologram.

## 3. Sheldrake's Morphic Fields

### 3.1 Memory in Fields, Not Genes

Rupert Sheldrake's morphogenetic field theory proposes that biological form is guided by fields that carry memory:

> "Morphogenetic fields are a field that until now has not been recognized or taken into consideration by physics... Organisms evolve. They have a history and, thanks to a process called morphic resonance, they contain a memory within themselves."

The key claim: **memory is stored in fields, not in molecules.** Each individual "draws on the collective memory of the species and tunes in to its past members."

### 3.2 Morphic Resonance as Non-Local Access

Sheldrake's morphic resonance is precisely Shipov's "address attribute" concept:

- Past forms influence present forms non-locally
- Similarity of form creates resonance (the "address")
- Information transfer does not require energy transfer
- The field connects across time and space

This is the biological formulation of the Universal Hologram.

### 3.3 Habits, Not Laws

Sheldrake's radical claim: natural regularities are habits, not immutable laws:

> "The regularities of nature are more similar to habits than to immutable laws."

This implies cosmic memory — the universe remembers its own patterns and tends to repeat them. The cosmos has a memory field.

## 4. Form Waves: Geometry as Information

### 4.1 Chaumery and de Belizal

The French radiesthesia tradition, founded by Léon Chauméry and André de Belizal in the 1930s, developed the concept of **ondes de forme** (form waves):

- Every geometric shape emits a specific wave
- The wave's character depends on geometry, not material
- Certain shapes (spheres, cones, spirals) produce specific effects
- The wave can be detected by pendulum and measured on scales (Bovis, Universal)

They invented instruments to detect and amplify form waves: the Universal Pendulum, the Egyptian Pendulum, the radiesthetic pile.

### 4.2 The Carrier Wave Concept

Chauméry and de Belizal discovered that certain geometric forms can "transmit at a distance" an energetic information:

> "Forms capture terrestrial magnetism, become saturated with it, and re-emit the captured energy as a form wave."

This is the basis of radionics: **geometry encodes the message; the field transmits it.**

### 4.3 Vibratis and Modern Form Wave Theory

Contemporary French practitioners (Vibratis, Servranx) extend form wave theory:

- Form waves carry information without energy
- The body detects form waves through magnetite receptors (matching Rocard's findings)
- Consciousness interacts with form waves
- Form waves can be beneficial (harmonizing) or harmful (geopathic stress)

## 5. Benaros: Memory in Cohesion

### 5.1 Matter and Ether as Two Regimes

Pierre Benaros' Silentium model proposes that matter and "Æther D" are two regimes of the same energetic reality — one concentrated (matter), one distributed (ether). Both converge toward a fundamental state E of maximal cohesion.

### 5.2 The Memorial Reading

Benaros introduces a crucial concept: **memory in cohesion.** The cosmic information is not just energy — it is structural information about the history of the universe:

> "The factor D would not translate only an expansion or an energetic dilution, but also the progressive dispersion of the cosmic history initially contained in the Nucleus."

This is Sheldrake's "cosmic memory" in geometric-mathematical form. The cohesion state encodes history; the dilution factor D measures how far the cosmos has moved from its original information density.

### 5.3 The Cosmic Nucleus as Archive

Benaros proposes that the Cosmic Nucleus — the final state of maximal cohesion — stores structural information about cosmic history. The universe remembers its own past in the geometry of cohesion.

## 6. Manzalini: Consciousness as Quantum Field

### 6.1 The Field Model

Antonio Manzalini proposes consciousness is a quantum field C, analogous to the de Broglie-Bohm pilot wave:

$$C = \hbar \frac{\nabla^2 R}{R}$$

The consciousness field guides living matter just as the quantum potential guides inanimate matter.

### 6.2 Active Information

Manzalini explicitly uses Bohm and Hiley's "active information" concept:

- The field encodes information about the environment
- Living systems "read" this information
- The field guides without transferring energy
- Shape/curvature matters more than amplitude

This is the rigorous formulation of the form wave concept.

### 6.3 Nambu-Goldstone Bosons

Manzalini proposes that the consciousness field's curvature is associated with Nambu-Goldstone boson condensates — massless excitations arising from symmetry breaking:

- They carry ordering information
- They require negligible energy
- They encode form/structure

This provides a quantum mechanical basis for form waves.

## 10. Next Questions

1. **Experimental protocols.** Can form wave effects be distinguished from electromagnetic effects in controlled experiments? The radiesthesia tradition claims yes — diagrams work without power — but rigorous protocols are needed.

2. **Mathematical unification.** Can the Universal Hologram, morphic field, form wave function, and consciousness field be derived from a single Lagrangian? Shipov's torsion tensor and Manzalini's quantum potential may be translatable.

3. **Address mechanism.** How does consciousness generate the "address attribute"? Shipov states it happens; the mechanism is unknown. Manzalini's NG boson model may provide the answer.

4. **Memory density.** How much information does the cosmic field store? Benaros' dilution factor D suggests the cosmos has moved away from original information density — but how much remains accessible?

5. **Distance independence.** Does cosmic information field strength really not decay with distance? Shipov claims instantaneous propagation; Sheldrake claims non-local correlation. This is testable.

6. **Technology applications.** Can the Universal Hologram be accessed technologically, not just through consciousness? Shipov's torsion generators claim to do this; independent replication is needed.
