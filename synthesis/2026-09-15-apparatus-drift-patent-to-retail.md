# The Apparatus Drift: From Patent Physics to Retail Craft in the French Form-Wave Tradition (1936–2026)

## Abstract

The French form-wave tradition is now documented in this collection across four generations of apparatus, from the 1936 Universal Pendulum patent to the 2026 retail radionics shelf. Read as a single record, the apparatus itself tells a story its authors never state: over ninety years, the tradition's *technical register* — patents with millimetre specifications, electrical formalism, calibration references — has drained away, while its *commercial register* — books bundled with emitters, training circles, range claims in kilometres — has expanded. The founding generation patented devices and wrote "tension" and "intensité" of a radiesthetic battery; the 1984 generation kept the patent form but filled it with geomantic symbolism; the 2026 generation sells nine-device catalogs at €290–€2,190 with no patents, no construction specifications, and range claims ("200 to 1,500 km depending on weather"; "minimum range of 8,000 km") that no instrument has ever recorded. Yet the same record shows the tradition repeatedly *recruiting* engineers — a Ferrié-trained radio specialist (Simoneton), an imperial Russian officer turned hieroglyph scholar (Enel), an electro-mechanical engineer and pharmacist (Frandeau) — whose technical formation functions as credential rather than as method. This synthesis traces the drift through the newly translated dossiers, identifies what survives as instrumentable (the falsifiable range claims, the Lecher-antenna tuning seam, the biophotonics comparison Simoneton's publishers themselves propose), and asks the question the drift poses: what exactly is being transmitted, if not the physics?

## 1. Generation One: The Patent as Physics (1936–1965)

### 1.1 Millimetres and the Language of the Pile

The founding document of the tradition, Chaumery and de Belizal's patent FR 816,132 (10 April 1936), defined an *apparatus* — the Universal Pendulum and cosmo-magnetic batteries — in the language of measurement [1]. The school's treatise continued the register. *Physique Micro-Vibratoire et Forces Invisibles* (de Belizal & Morel, 1965) states the battery's "physics" in explicitly electrical terms:

> "Tension = fonction du nombre d'hémisphères superposées. Intensité = fonction du diamètre identique de chaque hémisphère."
>
> *Voltage = a function of the number of superposed hemispheres. Current = a function of the identical diameter of each hemisphere.* [2]

And crucially, the same book names its own measurement gap — form waves "de l'ordre de millionièmes et milliardièmes d'électrons-volts" (*on the order of millionths and billionths of electron-volts*), with the pendulum standing in for the missing instrument [2].

This is the baseline register: **patents, dimensions, electrical metaphors, a stated falsification condition**. Whatever one thinks of the physics, the founding generation wrote *as though* the claims could be checked — and one of them, Chaumery, died of the checking (27 February 1957, dehydrated by the V- ray his own apparatus concentrated) [2][3].

### 1.2 The Apparatus Chain of the Founders

The Desforges catalogue preserved in the Morel dossier lists the full instrument line of the founding era: Pendule Universel, Pendule Égyptien, Escargot-sélecteur, Bombe C.30, Barre Atlante/Louksor — each with construction parameters, each sold through a physics-styled retail channel (the Librairie Desforges) [2]. The apparatus was the argument: if the forms emitted, the emissions could be selected, corrected (Morel's modified ends on the Barre Atlante), and dosed (the Heckmann AMC calibration: "4 hémisphères de diamètre 12 mm = tension optimale pour la cellule vivante") [4].

## 2. Generation Two: The Patent as Symbol (1984)

### 2.1 Fortuna Major in a Battery

Dr Francis Frandeau de Marly's patent FR 84.03970 (13 March 1984) is the hinge generation — and the newly translated technical sheet shows the drift in a single object [4]. The filing *is still a patent*, with dimensions to the millimetre (three 12 mm elements, one 11 × 20 mm element, brass gilded with fine gold, 22 g). But its core innovation is the **Fortuna Major selector** — a harmonization generator based on a medieval geomantic figure, chosen because the figure "possède autant de Yin que de Yang" (*possesses as much Yin as Yang*), with the claimed correspondences being traditional divinatory meanings: "Forte Résistance Physique - Grande Chance en amour – Triomphe absolu aux jeux de hasard" (*Strong Physical Resistance — Great Luck in Love — Absolute Triumph in Games of Chance*) [4].

The layering is the tell: the 12 mm base element is explicitly cited as "brevet n° 816.132/10.04.1936 – auteur : Léon CHAUMERY" — the 1936 physics patent *inside* the 1984 symbolic one [4]. The electrical formalism survives (Green+/Green− poles, voltage scaling with stacked hemispheres), but the *selective component* is no longer a spectrum position; it is a divination figure.

### 2.2 The Book-Apparatus Bundle

Frandeau's generation also changes the distribution channel: the treatise *Traité des Influences Cosmo-Magnétiques* is "sold new with a pendant" ("AVEC PENDULE CADEAU"), and the doctrine shifts with it — the pendant "transforms into a veritable dynamo of fluid energy... you then need not have the GIFT for it to work" [4]. The claim to be *apparatus-dependent rather than operator-dependent* is the founding school's physical-radiesthesia line; but in the 1984 packaging it has become a retail promise: the device works for anyone, and it comes with the book.

## 3. Generation Three: The Retail Shelf Without the Patent (2000s–2026)

### 3.1 Marquette: Lineage Instead of Construction

Francis Marquette — Frandeau's self-described mentee — completes the drift, and his dossier states it cleanly [5]. The C.E.R.A. emitter is not patented; it is *bundled with his book* (*La Radionique à la Portée de Tous*, "The marvellous C.ER.A. emitter is included with this book") and transmitted through his study circle (Cercle d'Études de Radionique Appliquée) and association (Holistica 3000). The publisher's lineage claim is the standard chain — "Abrams, Ruth Drown, de la Warr... Servranx, Lakowski, de Belizal, Chammery, Morel, and closer to us, Dr. Francis Frandeau de Marly" — extended to himself. The apparatus is described "in terms of lineage ('my mentor's pendant') rather than construction" [5]. A member's account even records the next emitter (CELTISTICA 3000) as "transmitted to me in a dream" — the physics vocabulary of 1936 fully become craft, transmitted master-to-student "the same social shape as the old sourcier lineages, one century on" [5].

### 3.2 The 2026 Machine Shelf: Numbers Without Measurement

The current retail face — newly translated in full — is the nine-device radioniquepourtous.fr catalog (€290–€2,190), the Belgian BIOLECHER® autonomous emitter, and the Burgundian Vibrasaï box [6]. Two features define the generation:

**The falsifiable numbers.** The Double Decagon claims a range "from 200 to 1,500 km depending on weather conditions" — for a 9V-battery printed-circuit box. The Tedir (€2,190) claims "a minimum range of 8,000 km" plus diffusion "via the EDF electrical network." No radionics field has ever been instrumentally measured at any of these distances; no seller publishes a measurement row. These are the tradition's clearest falsification targets — specific, checkable, attached to ordinary retail objects [6].

**The autonomy paradox.** The shelf markets devices that *require* mains power and night-only operation to "avoid electromagnetic disturbances" (Brocéliande) and devices whose selling point is *total* electrical independence (BIOLECHER, Anneaux de Vie) — both postures presented as advantages over the same imagined rival [6].

The regulatory posture is uniform: every device carries the compliance formula ("complement to traditional medicine," "use remains the responsibility of each person") matching the Miviludes surveillance environment — the disclaimers standardized, the claims (disease search, remedy transmission) not [6].

### 3.3 The One Honest Actor

The BIOLECHER house is the shelf's internal exception, and the exception is instrumentable: its device is tuned by **Lecher antenna** — the graduated-rod instrument the French physical-radiesthesia tradition treats as its precision tool — meaning its tuning knob "could plausibly produce a number on a bench: a Lecher-antenna read before/after tuning would emit a *measurement*, where every other device emits a story" [6]. The same house publishes the lane's only insider boundary-statement: radionics applies "primarily — even exclusively — to beings endowed with consciousness"; the Servranx brothers "never claimed" reproducible influence on insects or plant diseases [6].

## 4. The Engineer's Migration: The Recurring Recruitment Pattern

Running alongside the apparatus drift is a pattern the new Simoneton dossier makes newly visible: **the tradition repeatedly recruits genuine technical formation, which then functions as credential rather than as method** [7].

- **André Simoneton** (1893–1983) was recruited in August 1914 by **Captain Gustave Ferrié** — the founder of French military radio — and trained in wireless telegraphy at the birth of T.S.F. He then spent his career in agri-food, and in the 1930s turned the radio engineer's wavelength onto food: human cells "vibrate at 6,200 to 7,000 Å," four food categories by wavelength, "dead foods" at 0 Å. As the dossier puts it: "the same man who learned to measure electromagnetic waves at the birth of French radio later claimed to measure the waves of foods" [7].
- **Enel** (Prince Michel Vladimirovich Skariatine, 1883–1963) — Russian imperial officer, exile to Egypt, hieroglyph scholar — became the most-cited primary of French therapeutic radiesthesia; his apparatus chapter (*chercheur d'ondes*, *projecteur à aimant*, *Yin-Yang*, *horloge solaire*) is now back in retail print through the Energeia 2025–26 reprint program [8].
- **Frandeau de Marly** — "engineer in electro-mechanics, pharmacist of the University of Bordeaux, doctor honoris causa" — is introduced by his publisher with the credentials first, the pendant second [4].
- The Vibrasaï box's maker introduces herself as "daughter and granddaughter of electronics engineers," with a "retired-engineer father" guaranteeing quality [6].

The convergent move is identical each time: **technical training is cited as warrant for claims made in a non-technical register**. Simoneton's angstroms have no physical definition (the "measurement" is the dowser's declared number); Enel's apparatus is described in recipe form; Frandeau's electrical metaphors describe a battery with no measurable output. The engineering biography authenticates; it does not instrument.

The one place the migration *could* have produced a real experiment is named by Simoneton's own publisher: Trédaniel notes he "anticipated measurement methods since developed by... biophotonics and photobiology" — and the dossier states the natural test: "the controlled comparison — Simoneton's angstrom readings versus, say, delayed-luminescence biophoton measurements on the same foods. That experiment is the natural next claim in this lane" [7]. It has never been run.

---

## 5. The Counter-Current: The Reprint Program as Re-Anchoring

The drift is not the whole story. The Energeia reprint program (2025–2026) — newly documented [8] — runs *against* the retail drift: it puts the primary corpus (Enel's *Radiations des formes et cancer*, *Traitement à distance par radiations*, *Gnomologie*, *Post Mortem*) back in stocked retail print, with Guy Thieux — geophysicist, 34 years in oil-prospect geophysics, Enel's literary legatee — supplying prefaces that re-anchor the corpus to his coherence-field framing ("champs de cohérence, émergence des repères, distinction du global et du local"). The publication chain is now bibliographically complete: Al-Maaref (Cairo) → Dangles (1959) → eBookEsoterique (2022) → Energeia (2025–26) [8]. And the reprints restore the primary's own modesty beside its descendants' claims: Enel's introduction cites Dr Van der Sype and Dr Drown as official-medicine echoes, then concedes "I do not claim to be able to cure all illnesses" — a sentence the 2026 shelf's 8,000-km range claims do not repeat [6][8].

So the 2026 record contains both drifts at once: **the apparatus tradition moving from patent to retail craft, and the textual tradition moving from print-on-demand back to stocked primary editions.** The apparatus drifts; the corpus consolidates.

## 6. Convergent Claims Across the Record

1. **Apparatus replaces operator, then lineage replaces apparatus.** The founding school's claim that the device works without "the GIFT" [4] becomes, two generations later, the claim that the device works because of who taught you [5]. The warrant migrates from construction to credential to lineage.
2. **Every generation claims the founding lineage — none repeats the founding method.** Marquette's publisher invokes Abrams through Morel [5]; the Dajafée house invokes the Servranx brothers [6]; Frandeau's sheet cites the 1936 patent as a component [4]. No generation after Morel publishes a new calibration, a new measured spectrum, or a new safety protocol.
3. **The falsifiable layer survives only as marketing.** The founding generation's falsification condition (an instrument sensitive enough to record form waves [2]) was never met; the current generation's falsifiable content (range in kilometres [6]) is published by sellers who will never test it. The tradition has never closed its own named gap.
4. **The engineer's biography is the constant credential.** From Ferrié's recruit to the electronics-engineer grandfathers of the Vibrasaï box, technical formation is cited as authentication in every generation — while the technical register (dimensions, calibrations, measurements) shrinks toward zero.

---

## 7. What the Drift Poses

1. **What is actually transmitted?** If not construction specifications (the 2026 shelf has none), then the transmission is method-as-practice: witness-handling, tuning by pendulum, doctrine. This is the sociology of craft apprenticeship — which is why the training circle (C.E.R.A., Holistica 3000) is now the functional unit, not the patent office.
2. **Why does the retail shelf make *stronger* claims than the primaries?** Enel's modest introduction vs. the Tedir's 8,000 km. The plausible mechanism is market competition without any external check: claims inflate precisely because nothing can falsify them in commerce — the inverse of the founding generation, whose claims were disciplined by Chaumery's death.
3. **Is the one bench-testable seam worth testing?** A Lecher-antenna read before/after BIOLECHER tuning [6] and a Simoneton-vs-biophotonics comparison on the same foods [7] are both cheap, specific, and would produce numbers either way. They are the two experiments this synthesis proposes as replication targets (see `synthesis/replication/`).
