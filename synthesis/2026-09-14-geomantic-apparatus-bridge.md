# The Geomantic-Apparatus Bridge: How Medieval Divination Figures Became Physical Emitter Designs

## Abstract

A striking feature of the French radionics tradition is its use of medieval geomantic figures as physical apparatus components. Dr. Francis Frandeau de Marly's 1984 patent (FR 84.03970) introduced the **Fortuna Major selector** — a harmonization generator based on one of the sixteen figures of traditional geomancy. This synthesis traces the thread from medieval Islamic divination practice through European transmission to its unexpected materialization in 20th-century radionic apparatus, identifies convergent claims across the French and Russian form-wave traditions, and proposes experimental tests for the core claim: that divination symbols encode detectable field signatures.

## 1. The Geomantic Tradition: Origins and Structure

### 1.1 What Is Geomancy?

Geomancy (from Greek *geōmanteia*, "earth divination") is a method of divination that originated in the medieval Islamic world, likely in 9th-century North Africa or Arabia. The Arabic name is *ʿilm al-raml* ("science of the sand") — the original method involved drawing random points in sand and interpreting the patterns.

**Fortuna Major** (al-Wasl) — the figure at the center of this synthesis — has the structure: one dot, two dots, one dot, two dots (● ●● ● ●●). Its traditional meaning: "greater fortune," triumph, protection, physical resistance.

### 1.2 Transmission to Europe

The method entered Latin Europe through Spain in the 12th century, transmitted by figures such as Hugh of Santalla and Gerard of Cremona [1]. By the Renaissance, geomancy had become one of the most widely practiced divination methods in Europe, taught alongside astrology in university curricula.

The figures were not merely divination tools. In the Hermetic and Cabalistic traditions that absorbed them, the geomantic figures were understood as **symbolic encodings of force relationships** — each figure a "signature" of a particular configuration of cosmic forces. This symbolic interpretation is the bridge to later apparatus use.

## 2. The French Form-Wave School: Geometry as Emission

### 2.1 Chaumery and de Belizal's Discovery

In the 1930s, Léon Chauméry and André de Belizal discovered that geometric forms emit detectable "vibrations" — what they called **ondes de forme** (form waves). A wooden sphere, they found, produced a spectrum of emissions arranged on spiral paths from a positive to a negative pole, with colors assigned by mental convention but detected by pendulum resonance [2].

The key discovery was **Negative Green (V−)** — a "color" with no optical counterpart, located at the point 0–400 on their spectrum. This vibration was:
- Destructive to living tissue (dehydrates matter)
- Preservative of organic material (mummifies by dehydration)
- The natural carrier wave of telluric currents
- The basis of Egyptian pyramid preservation effects

Chaumery died in 1957, reportedly "completely dehydrated by our negative green ray" — the tradition's founding cautionary legend [3].

### 2.2 The Universal Pendulum Patent

Chaumery and de Belizal patented their apparatus in April 1936 (FR 816,132) — the **Pendule Universel**, a spherical wooden pendulum graduated with:
- Color spectrum positions on spiral paths
- Magnetic and electric phase indicators
- Height-of-wave (H.O.) scale for elemental families

This was not divination apparatus but **detection apparatus** — a physical instrument calibrated to resonate with specific vibratory signatures [4].

### 2.3 Morel's Consolidation

After Chaumery's death, P.A. Morel joined de Belizal and co-authored *Physique Micro-Vibratoire et Forces Invisibles* (1965/1976). Morel is credited with discovering the **V+ antidote** — the protective vibration that made negative green work survivable [5].

The book's apparatus catalog shows the direct lineage: Universal Pendulum, Egyptian Pendulum, Electromagnetic Disk with selector (called **Escargot** — "Snail"), Pyramid of Cheops at 1/1000 scale, and the **Barre Atlante** (Atlante Bar) — later corrected by Morel to prevent saturation with negative green.

## 3. Frandeau de Marly: Geomancy as Apparatus

### 3.1 The 1984 Patent

Dr. Francis Frandeau de Marly filed patent FR 84.03970 on 13 March 1984. The patent's core innovation: a **harmonization generator (selector) of the Fortuna Major type** [6].

The technical sheet from the Porte des Étoiles CD-ROM specifies:

> "Système d'intégration du motif radiant : quaternaire (tétravalence de notre atome de base - carbone) - Groupe de sélecteur : Binaire (air.terre = violet.rouge = équilibre cosmo tellurique) - Modèle du sélecteur : segmentaire (2X1 segment brisé Yin & 2X1 segment entier Yang) - Type de sélecteur : Fortuna Major"
>
> "Integration system of the radiant motif: quaternary (the tetravalence of our base atom — carbon). Selector group: binary (air·earth = violet·red = cosmic-telluric equilibrium). Selector model: segmentary (2×1 broken segment Yin & 2×1 whole segment Yang). Selector type: Fortuna Major."

This is remarkable: a **medieval divination figure rendered as physical geometry** — two broken segments (Yin) and two whole segments (Yang) arranged in the Fortuna Major pattern.

### 3.2 The Claim

Frandeau's reading of Fortuna Major:

> "Fortuna Major = la meilleure figure géomantique complètement équilibrante (2X1 pair & 2X1 impair) qui possède autant de Yin que de Yang avec le Yin au dessus du Yang signifiant ainsi Forte Résistance Physique - Grande Chance en amour – Triomphe absolu aux jeux de hasard."
>
> "Fortuna Major = the best completely balancing geomantic figure (2×1 even & 2×1 odd), which possesses as much Yin as Yang with the Yin above the Yang, signifying thereby: Strong Physical Resistance — Great Luck in Love — Absolute Triumph in Games of Chance."

The claim is explicit: the figure's **balance structure** (equal Yin and Yang, Yin above Yang) encodes a harmonizing quality that can be physically implemented as an emitter.

### 3.3 The Radiesthetic Battery

Frandeau's apparatus is a **radiesthetic battery** — a stack of four elements:
- Three elements of 12 mm diameter and 6 mm height
- One element of 11 mm diameter and 20 mm height, fitted with the Fortuna Major selector

The technical sheet explicitly cites Chaumery's 1936 patent as a component: "un diamètre de 12 millimètres (brevet n° 816.132/10.04.1936 – auteur : Léon CHAUMERY)" — the base element is the patented form [6].

The battery emits **Green+** from one pole and **Green−** from the other, with polarity determined by orientation (vertical: top/bottom; horizontal: north/south).

## 4. Marquette: The Post-Frandeau Generation

### 4.1 The C.E.R.A. Lineage

Francis Marquette, founder of the **Cercle d'Études de Radionique Appliquée (C.E.R.A.)** and president of **Holistica 3000**, describes Frandeau de Marly as his "mentor" — the direct heir of the Frandeau line [7].

Marquette's book *La Radionique à la Portée de Tous* is sold **bundled with the C.E.R.A. emitter** — the apparatus travels with the method, not as separate hardware. This mirrors the older French tradition (Servranx graphiques, Frandeau's octagonal emitter sold through Éditions Servranx) but with modern retail structure.

### 4.2 The Generational Shift

What changes across the chain is instructive:

| Generation | Documentation Style | Claims Frame |
|------------|---------------------|--------------|
| Chaumery/de Belizal (1930s) | Patents with millimeter specifications | Physics vocabulary |
| Frandeau (1980s) | Patent (84.03970) + symbolism | Geomantic symbolism, quaternary grammar |
| Marquette (2000s–present) | Book + training circle | Personal development, holistic care |

The physics vocabulary of 1936 has become, by the 2020s, a craft transmitted master-to-student — the same social shape as the old sourcier lineages, one century on [7].

## 5. Cross-Tradition Connections

### 5.1 Russian Torsion Field Research

The Russian torsion field tradition (Akimov, Shipov) independently developed a **geometry-field relationship**:
- Rotation generates torsion fields
- Geometric forms with specific symmetries produce characteristic torsion signatures
- The torus is the universal organizing structure

Shipov's torsion field theory explicitly treats spacetime geometry as causative — the **torsion of spacetime** generates physical effects [8].

The French and Russian traditions converge on a striking claim: **geometry is not merely descriptive but generative**. Where the French tradition uses pendulum-detection and medieval symbolic frameworks, the Russian tradition uses tensor mathematics and mainstream physics vocabulary.

### 5.2 The Symbol-as-Technology Question

Both traditions raise the same question: **why should divination symbols produce physical effects?**

Three possible answers:

1. **Operator effect**: The symbols work through the operator's belief/intention — a placebo effect amplified by apparatus. The pendulum moves because the operator expects it to.

2. **Information field**: The symbols encode information that couples to a non-local field (morphic resonance, torsion field, form-wave field). The geometry is a "signature" that resonates with the field.

3. **Physical emission**: The geometry itself emits a physical field (electromagnetic, torsion, scalar) detectable without operator mediation.

The French tradition's honesty about its own measurement problem is notable. De Belizal and Morel wrote:

> "Le jour où l'on pourrait trouver un tel système de mesures, nous aurions moins besoin de la radiesthésie pour les ondes de forme qui sont davantage du domaine de la physique pure, et deviendraient ainsi partie d'une science exacte. Mais nous n'en sommes pas encore là."
>
> "The day such a measurement system is found, we would need radiesthesia less, for form waves would become part of an exact science. But we are not there yet." [5]

This is the tradition's own boundary: **the pendulum stands in for the missing instrument**.

## 6. Experimental Tests

The core claim is falsifiable. Proposed tests:

### 6.1 Blind Geomantic Figure Emission Test

**Setup**: Construct physical emitters based on the 16 geomantic figures (using Frandeau's segmentary grammar: broken segments for Yin, whole segments for Yang). Each emitter is a blind-coded sample.

**Detection**: Use Lecher antenna (French tradition) or torsion field detector (Russian tradition) to measure emission signatures. The operator does not know which figure is which.

**Prediction**: If the figures encode distinct field signatures, the detector should show consistent differences between figures, regardless of operator knowledge.

**Null result**: No difference between figures → operator effect or null hypothesis.

### 6.2 Fortuna Major Harmonization Test

**Setup**: Construct a Frandeau-type radiesthetic battery with the Fortuna Major selector. Use a second, identical battery with a non-harmonizing figure (e.g., Tristitia — "sorrow").

**Protocol**: Place each battery near a biological sample (seedling, cell culture) under stress conditions. Measure growth, vitality, or stress markers.

**Prediction**: If Fortuna Major encodes a harmonizing quality, the FM battery should show measurably better outcomes than the control.

**Null result**: No difference → the figure's traditional meaning does not translate to physical effect.

### 6.3 Cross-Tradition Detector Calibration

**Setup**: Use both French (Lecher antenna, Universal Pendulum) and Russian (torsion field detector) instruments on the same geometric forms.

**Protocol**: Measure emissions from spheres, hemispheres, pyramids, and Frandeau-type selectors.

**Prediction**: If both traditions detect the same underlying phenomenon, their measurements should correlate — even if vocabulary differs.

**Null result**: No correlation → the traditions may be detecting different phenomena or operator artifacts.
