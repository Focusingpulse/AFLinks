# The Torsion-Vortex Convergence: Russian and German Models of Rotational Field Physics

## Abstract

Russian torsion field theory and German vortex physics represent two independent research traditions that converged on a strikingly similar claim: **rotation generates fields beyond conventional electromagnetism and gravity, and these fields carry information rather than energy.** Akimov and Shipov's EGS-field concept (electromagnetic-gravitational-spin) and the SAFE Vortex Physics Working Group's implosion/negentropy framework share foundational assumptions about rotational dynamics, vacuum structure, and information transfer. This synthesis traces the convergent claims, identifies the theoretical differences, and proposes experimental tests that could validate or falsify the torsion-vortex connection.

---

## 1. The Convergent Claim

Across Russian torsion physics and German vortex physics, researchers converge on four fundamental claims:

1. **Rotation generates a distinct field type.** Not electromagnetic, not gravitational — a third polarization state of the physical vacuum.

2. **The field carries information, not energy.** Detection requires resonance matching, not power measurement. The signal-to-noise ratio is defined differently than in conventional physics.

3. **The vacuum has structure.** Both traditions reject the empty-space vacuum of conventional physics. The vacuum is a material medium with measurable properties.

4. **Technology can harness rotational fields.** Energy extraction, propulsion, materials modification, and information transfer are claimed applications.

These convergent claims emerged independently in Russia (1980s) and German-speaking Europe (1930s–1990s), with limited cross-pollination until recent decades.

## 2. Russian Torsion Field Theory: The EGS-Field Concept

### 2.1 The Theoretical Foundation

G.I. Shipov's theory of the physical vacuum [1] introduces torsion fields through geometry:

- **Absolute parallelism space:** A 4-dimensional space with rotational coordinates, extending Einstein's general relativity.
- **Geometrized field equations:** Einstein, Yang-Mills, and Heisenberg equations written in spinor form and fully geometrized.
- **Torsion as geometry:** All inertial forces (Coriolis, centrifugal, Euler) are generated by torsion fields.

The key theoretical innovation: **torsion is not a force added to physics, but the geometric substrate from which forces emerge.**

### 2.2 The Phyton Model

A.E. Akimov's phyton model [2] describes the physical vacuum as:

- **Nested ring packets:** Electron-positron pairs nested within each other, forming ring-shaped wave packets.
- **Spin compensation:** Opposite spins create a self-compensated system — neutral in charge, spin, and magnetic moment.
- **Perturbation states:** External sources (charge, mass, spin) polarize the phyton structure, creating:
  - **E-field:** Charge polarization (conventional electromagnetism)
  - **G-field:** Longitudinal spin polarization (gravity)
  - **S-field:** Transverse spin polarization (torsion)

The phyton model provides a unified picture: EM, gravity, and torsion are different polarization states of the same vacuum medium.

### 2.3 Dynamic vs. Static Torsion

A crucial distinction in Russian theory:

| Property | Static Torsion | Dynamic Torsion |
|----------|---------------|-----------------|
| Source | Spinning mass without radiation | Spinning mass with radiation |
| Constant | Proportional to G × ℏ (~10⁻³⁰ weaker than gravity) | Not constrained — can be large |
| Detection | Extremely difficult | Practical applications claimed |

The dynamic torsion claim is the key to technology: **spinning sources that radiate can generate strong torsion fields, unlike static spinning masses.**

### 2.4 Experimental Claims

Akimov and Shipov report [2]:

- **Torsion generators:** World's first torsion field generators built in Russia (1980s)
- **Materials modification:** Torsion exposure changes crystal structure, hardness, conductivity
- **Information transfer:** Torsion communication at speeds claimed to exceed c
- **Astrophysical detection:** Torsion processing of astronomical images reveals internal structure of stars

## 3. German Vortex Physics: The Implosion Principle

### 3.1 The Schauberger Foundation

Viktor Schauberger (1885–1958) developed the implosion principle through direct observation:

- **Water as living organism:** Rivers, springs, and natural water systems exhibit intelligent behavior
- **Temperature gradients:** Cold water at the center of vortices; temperature differences drive dynamics
- **Trout and salmon:** Fish navigate upstream without visible effort by exploiting vortex dynamics
- **Timber floating:** Log flumes that followed natural water principles succeeded where conventional engineering failed

Schauberger's key insight: **nature uses centripetal (implosive) vortex motion, not centrifugal (explosive) motion.**

### 3.2 The Implosion Principle

The SAFE Vortex Physics Working Group [3] systematized Schauberger's insights:

- **Negentropy:** Implosion creates order from chaos, the reverse of entropy
- **Temperature gradients:** Cold at vortex center (centripetal), warm at periphery (centrifugal)
- **Water as information medium:** Vortex structure encodes and transmits information
- **Biological implications:** Living systems use implosion to maintain order

The implosion principle is the German equivalent of torsion: **rotation that creates order rather than disorder.**

### 3.3 The Huber-Meyl Field Equation

Jakob Huber and Konstantin Meyl developed a fundamental field equation [3]:

```
∂²E/∂t² × c² = ∆E + σ/ε × ∂E/∂t + κ/µ × ∂E/∂t + σκ/εµ × E
```

Where:
- σ = electrical conductivity
- κ = "magnetic conductivity" or "hydrotic conductivity" (new term)
- The equation reduces to Schrödinger's equation under certain boundary conditions

This equation claims to unify electromagnetism with vortex dynamics through the additional κ term.

### 3.4 The Torus Ring Vortex

Both Huber and Hanspeter Seiler [3] identified the torus ring vortex as the fundamental structure:

- **Smoke ring geometry:** A toroidal vortex that propagates by rolling through itself
- **Self-sustaining:** The vortex maintains coherence without external energy input
- **Information carrier:** The vortex structure encodes information that persists during propagation

The torus ring vortex is the German equivalent of the phyton: **a self-compensated rotational structure that carries information.**

## 7. Testable Claims

### Claim 1: Torsion Field Generation by Rotating Systems

**Claim:** Spinning systems with radiation generate detectable fields beyond EM.

**Test:**
1. Construct a rotating system with controlled spin rate and radiation
2. Measure EM fields with conventional instruments
3. Use torsion detectors (claimed by Russian researchers) or Lecher antenna (French tradition)
4. Compare detection patterns with spin parameters

**Prediction:** Detection at specific wavelengths/settings correlates with spin, not EM power.

### Claim 2: Vortex-Induced Temperature Gradients

**Claim:** Centripetal vortices create cold centers; centrifugal vortices create warm peripheries.

**Test:**
1. Create controlled vortices in water (centripetal vs centrifugal)
2. Measure temperature distribution with high-precision thermometers
3. Compare with theoretical predictions from vortex physics

**Prediction:** Temperature gradients exceed what adiabatic expansion predicts.

### Claim 3: Information Transfer Without Energy

**Claim:** Torsion/vortex fields transmit information without detectable energy transfer.

**Test:**
1. Create a torsion/vortex source with encoded information
2. Shield all EM pathways (Faraday cage)
3. Use trained operators with Lecher antenna or torsion detector
4. Test information transfer rate vs. chance

**Prediction:** Information transfer rate exceeds chance, with no EM signal detected.

### Claim 4: Materials Modification

**Claim:** Torsion/vortex exposure changes material properties.

**Test:**
1. Expose identical material samples to torsion/vortex fields
2. Use blinded protocol (samples labeled but exposure unknown to testers)
3. Measure hardness, conductivity, crystal structure
4. Compare with control samples

**Prediction:** Exposed samples show statistically significant differences from controls.

## 9. Conclusion

Russian torsion field theory and German vortex physics represent two independent research traditions that converged on strikingly similar claims. Both assert that:

1. Rotation generates non-EM fields
2. These fields carry information rather than energy
3. The vacuum has measurable structure
4. Technology can harness rotational dynamics

The convergence is remarkable given the different historical paths: Russian theory emerged from mathematical physics (Shipov's geometrized field equations), while German theory emerged from natural observation (Schauberger's water studies).

Whether these claims represent genuine physical phenomena or shared misconceptions remains an open question. But unlike many alternative physics frameworks, the torsion-vortex convergence makes **testable predictions**:

- Torsion detectors should register above spinning systems
- Vortices should create anomalous temperature gradients
- Information should transfer without EM energy
- Materials should change under torsion/vortex exposure

The proposed upgrades — detector comparison and energy extraction experiments — would provide decisive tests. If validated, the torsion-vortex convergence would represent a new paradigm in physics. If falsified, the convergence would remain an interesting case study in how independent traditions can converge on similar errors.

Either outcome advances understanding.
