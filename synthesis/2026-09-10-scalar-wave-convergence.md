# The Scalar Wave Convergence: French Form Waves, Brazilian Technology, and Tesla's Radiant Energy

## Abstract

Three independent research traditions — French micro-vibratory physics (Chauméry/de Belizal school, 1930s–1970s), Brazilian scalar energy technology (Waechter patent, 2013), and Tesla's radiant energy discoveries (1890s–1900s) — converged on a strikingly similar claim: **there exists a class of longitudinal, information-carrying waves that conventional electromagnetic instruments cannot detect.** These waves manifest as form-encoded perturbations in a material medium, carry meaning rather than energy, and can be harnessed for therapeutic and technological applications. This synthesis traces the convergent claims, identifies the theoretical bridges, and proposes experimental tests that could validate or falsify the scalar-form-wave connection.

---

## 1. The Convergent Claim

Across French radiesthesia, Brazilian scalar technology, and Tesla's radiant energy work, researchers converge on five fundamental claims:

1. **Longitudinal waves exist below the EM spectrum.** Not transverse Hertzian waves, but pressure waves in a material medium — electrostatic field perturbations that propagate without electron movement.

2. **Form encodes information, not energy.** Geometric shape (cone, sphere, spiral, pyramid) determines wave characteristics. Amplitude matters less than shape.

3. **Detection requires resonance, not power measurement.** Conventional EM instruments (voltmeters, gaussmeters, spectrum analyzers) cannot detect these waves. Detection requires tuned operators or specialized instruments (pendulums, Lecher antennas, torsion sensors).

4. **The medium is material but subtle.** French tradition calls it the "natural magnetic current" or "cosmic waves." Tesla called it "radiant matter" or "neutral particles." Brazilian patent calls it "scalar energy." All describe a material medium finer than electrons.

5. **Technology can harness these waves.** French researchers built spherical emitters (Bombe C), re-equilibrators (Louksor), and form-wave generators. Tesla built radiant energy transmitters. Brazilian patent describes a 12 Hz scalar generator for therapeutic use.

## 2. French Micro-Vibratory Physics: The Form-Wave Tradition

### 2.1 The Chauméry–de Belizal School

Léon Chauméry and A. de Belizal published *Essai de radiesthésie vibratoire* in 1939, formalizing a systematic approach to detecting and measuring "ondes de forme" (form waves) — vibrations generated by geometric shapes interacting with the terrestrial magnetic current.

Chauméry died in 1957, "completely dehydrated by our negative green ray (V–)" [1]. This cautionary tale became the founding legend of French micro-vibratory physics: form waves are real, powerful, and dangerous if mishandled.

### 2.2 The Spectrum Decomposition

De Belizal and Morel's *Physique Micro-Vibratoire et Forces Invisibles* (1976) formalized the school's spectrum system:

| Color Band | Position | Characteristics |
|------------|----------|-----------------|
| Infrared | Below red | Thermal, warming |
| Black | Below visible | Absorbing |
| **Negative Green (V–)** | Point 0–400 | Destructive, dehydrating, power over matter, natural telluric carrier |
| Positive Green | Center | Equilibrating, neutral |
| White | Above green | Preserving, balancing |
| Ultraviolet | Above visible | Disinfecting, energetic |

**Negative Green (V–) is the pivot of the entire system.** It is:
- The natural carrier wave of telluric currents
- Both destructive (dehydrates matter) and preserving (mummifies by dehydration)
- The "connecting link between life and death" [1]
- The basis of Egyptian pyramid preservation effects
- The frequency patented by Chauméry/de Belizal in April 1936 (patent no. 816,132)

The antidote to V– is V+ (positive green), discovered later with P.A. Morel, which neutralizes stored radioactivity in the organism.

### 2.3 Form-Wave Generation

Geometric forms capture the terrestrial magnetic current and re-radiate it as form waves:

- **Sphere:** Most powerful volume-form. Central point possesses "very important centripetal magnetic energy" [1]. The Bombe C uses three concentric spheres.
- **Hemisphere pile:** Each hemisphere amplifies magnetic energy. Number of elements = tension. Diameter = amplitude/intensity.
- **Spiral arrested by straight line (escargot):** Beneficial emission.
- **Circle:** Balanced plane form, superior power of expression.
- **Pyramid:** Preserving effect via V– concentration at 1/3 height (Egyptian tradition).

### 2.4 The Universal Pendulum

The Universal Pendulum (Pendule Universel) is a spherical detector graduated with:

- **Equator:** Wave height (H.O.) — 8 families of elements, marked in grades
- **Magnetic meridian (N–S):** Magnetic phase
- **Electric meridian (E–W):** Electric phase
- **Colors:** Both visible and invisible spectrum positions

The operator holds the pendulum at a specific thread length (octave), positions the cursor at a specific color/height, and the pendulum rotates when above the corresponding substance. This is resonance detection — the pendulum is tuned to the vibratory signature.

### 2.5 The Bovis Scale

André Bovis (1871–1947), a Nice coppersmith/inventor, created a scale for measuring "vibratory rates" (taux vibratoire):

- **Units:** Bovis Units (UB), where 1 UB = 1 angström
- **Neutral point:** 6,500 UB — threshold of human vitality
- **Healthy human:** 8,000–10,000 UB
- **Fresh food:** 8,000–9,000 UB
- **Processed food:** Below 3,000 UB
- **Sacred sites:** 50,000–100,000+ UB

The scale was calibrated with engineer André Simoneton using the red spectrum of sunlight as reference. The measurement is pendulum-based and operator-dependent, but standardized across practitioners.

## 3. Brazilian Scalar Energy Technology: The Waechter Patent

### 3.1 The Patent

WO2013155580A1, filed by Marcelo Arthur Mendes Waechter (2013), describes an "Apparatus for Scalar Energy Generation" [2]. The patent claims:

- **Frequency:** Fixed 12 Hz
- **Voltage:** 0.5–2 volts oscillating
- **Waveform:** Positive and negative waves "not simultaneous but interspersed" — alternating polarity
- **Propagation:** Radius of up to 2 meters from antenna
- **Application:** Therapeutic — immune system, blood circulation

### 3.2 The Circuit

The apparatus consists of:

1. **Microcontroller (10):** Sends alternating pulses to positive (20) and negative (30) exciter circuits at 12 Hz
2. **Power converter (40):** Transformer that modifies voltage/current to scalar waveform
3. **Transmitting antenna (50):** Insulated conductive material, minimum 2 cm from user

The key claim: "Scalar energy is energy that has magnitude but no specific direction, that is, it manifests in all directions simultaneously" [2].

### 3.3 Scalar Energy Characteristics

The patent defines scalar energy as:

- **Equilibrium state:** Electrically balanced, not detectable by EM instruments
- **Brain analogy:** Each brain hemisphere is a scalar generator; their confluence creates measurable EM waves
- **Magnet connection:** Bloch Wall in magnets is where scalar energy is captured and components form magnetic polarities
- **Cellular level:** Each cell emits EM radiation with positive exterior, negative interior; ion movement creates the body's electromagnetic field

### 3.4 Theoretical Background

The patent cites:

## 4. Tesla's Radiant Energy: The Original Discovery

### 4.1 Radiant Matter

Tesla's friendship with Sir William Crookes (inventor of the radiometer) shaped his understanding. Crookes asked: when matter is heated to incandescence, what exactly carries heat and light into surrounding space?

The radiometer showed that emissions have **mass** and can carry **physical force** to another object. Tesla concluded that any light/heat source emits "that which is emitted when matter radiates" — what Crookes called **radiant matter**.

Tesla's key insight: **Radiant matter particles are the primary carriers of electrostatic charge.** They are:
- Neutral (not electrons)
- Extremely small (pass through glass without breaking vacuum)
- Mass ~100× smaller than electrons (Tesla's estimate)
- The substrate for electrostatic field propagation

### 4.2 Radiant Energy

Tesla defined radiant energy as **electrostatic charge carried by radiant matter**. This is not electromagnetic radiation but longitudinal pressure waves in the flux of neutral particles.

The "method of conversion" [3]:
1. Charge an inductor with intermittent direct current
2. Collect inductive discharges in a capacitor
3. Discharge capacitor into high-inductance, low-impedance secondary
4. Result: longitudinal electrostatic wavefronts at high voltage

### 4.3 Spark Gap Technology

Tesla's spark gaps were precision instruments, not crude switches:

- **Magnetically quenched:** Electromagnet or permanent magnet deflects ions, preventing parasitic current before spark
- **Hot air quenched:** Air flow evacuates ions
- **Oil immersed:** Oil medium "cracks" without prior ionization
- **Rotary mechanical:** Early experiments used rotating contacts (abandoned for speed limitations)

The goal: **single unidirectional pulse per discharge** — a "crack" not an arc. This creates the "electrostatic breath by wave" effect [3].

### 4.4 Three Forms of Electricity

Tesla distinguished three forms:

| Form | Description | Electron Movement |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| Direct Current | Electrons + neutral particles circulating together | Unidirectional flow |
| Alternating Current | Electrons + neutral particles oscillating together | Bidirectional oscillation |
| **Radiant Current** | Electrostatic pressure waves through neutral particle flux | Minimal or none |

French: Concentric spheres amplify centripetal magnetic energy.

## 10. Conclusion

French micro-vibratory physics, Brazilian scalar energy technology, and Tesla's radiant energy discoveries represent three independent research traditions that converged on strikingly similar claims. All three assert that:

1. Longitudinal waves exist below the EM spectrum
2. Geometric form determines wave characteristics
3. These waves carry information rather than energy
4. Detection requires resonance, not power measurement
5. Technology can harness these waves for beneficial purposes

The convergence is remarkable given the different historical paths:

- **French tradition:** Emerged from radiesthesia practice, formalized through systematic experimentation with pendulums and form-generated waves
- **Brazilian patent:** Modern therapeutic technology claim, grounded in Tesla's work and contemporary scalar wave research
- **Tesla:** Original scientific discovery through high-voltage experimentation, interpreted through Crookes' radiant matter theory

Whether these claims represent genuine physical phenomena or shared misconceptions remains an open question. But unlike many alternative physics frameworks, the scalar-form-wave convergence makes **testable predictions**:

- Scalar generators should affect water structure
- Form-wave detection should correlate with geometric parameters
- Tesla circuits should produce anomalous distance effects

The proposed upgrades — scalar-water interaction experiment and form-wave calibration experiment — would provide decisive tests. If validated, the scalar-form-wave convergence would represent a new paradigm in physics: **information as a physical quantity carried by longitudinal waves in a material medium.** If falsified, the convergence would remain an interesting case study in how independent traditions can converge on similar claims.

Either outcome advances understanding.
