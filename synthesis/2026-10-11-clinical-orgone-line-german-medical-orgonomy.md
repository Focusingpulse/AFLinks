# The Clinical Orgone Line: Post-War Germany's Medical Orgonomy School and the Three-Program Convergence

*Synthesis by Drunvalo — 2026-10-11*

---

## Abstract

Two of this archive's recent syntheses traced national "life-field" programs that built **instruments**: China's institutional external-qi measurement program (Tsinghua, CAS, Li Si-Chen's torsion identification) and the Russian–Chinese torsion-telecommunication program (Tomsk varicaps, Gao Peng's distance records). A new batch of translations from a single German source — **orgon-archiv.de**, "Texts from 60 years" — documents the third program, which built something different: not instruments but a **clinical practice**. This is the post-war German medical-orgonomy school: the 1980 West Berlin replication group that re-ran Reich's cancer-cell diagnostics with modern videomicroscopy; Heiko Lassek (late director of the Wilhelm Reich Institute, Berlin) treating seventeen terminal cancer patients with the orgone accumulator and publishing the outcomes **including the failures** [[1]](https://orgon-archiv.de/blog/2026/04/28/orgonakkumulator-therapie-bei-schwerkranken-menschen/); Dr. Jorgos Kavouras, a GP and homeopath trained in the US by Reich's own students, logging **over 3,000 orgone-accumulator and DOR-buster applications per year** for more than a decade in an ordinary country practice [[2]](https://orgon-archiv.de/blog/2026/04/28/252/); and Jürgen Fischer's survey of orgonomic medicine from the bion experiments to the orgasm reflex [[3]](https://orgon-archiv.de/blog/2026/04/28/die-orgonomische-medizin-wilhelm-reichs/).

Around the practice sits a memory line — Eva Reich's 1989 West Berlin lecture about her father [[4]](https://orgon-archiv.de/blog/2026/04/30/dr-eva-reich-meine-erinnerungen-an-w-r/), Kuno Kruse's documentary portrait of Myron Sharaf [[5]](https://orgon-archiv.de/blog/2026/04/28/ein-analytiker-sucht-physik-der-liebe/), an NDR radio feature asking whether orgone is "healing power from the cosmos" [[6]](https://orgon-archiv.de/blog/2026/04/28/heilkraft-aus-dem-weltall/), and an anonymous continuation of Reich's *Listen, Little Man* [[7]](https://orgon-archiv.de/blog/2026/04/29/hoer-nochmal-zu-kleiner-mann/) — and a skeptical ledger the school keeps against itself: Bernhard Harrer's critical evaluation of Reich's life-energy research [[8]](https://www.datadiwan.de/harrer/ha_001d_.htm), his re-examination of the Geiger-Müller/ORANUR work, and Peter Nasselstein's year-by-year *Chronicle of Orgonomy* [[9]](https://orgonomie.net/hdoc97-39.pdf) and *Oranur-Physics* introduction.

Read against the archive's other two programs, the German clinical line completes a triangle. Three independent traditions — **German orgone, Chinese qi, Russian torsion** — each claimed a life-coupled field not reducible to electromagnetism, each built a method of concentrating or emitting it, and each hit the same metrology wall. Their points of independent agreement are the most interesting data in the archive: the field concentrates in **layered or geometric structures**, it couples measurably to **water**, the **organism is the most sensitive detector** (Lassek's dose-response observation mirrors the qi program's "sensitive persons" methodology), and no program has produced a blind third-party replication. The Indian prana-science journal line (Akhand Jyoti, 1985–89) [[10]](https://www.awgp.org/en/literature/akhandjyoti/1985/January/v2.26), the French form-wave apparatus tradition, and the Romanian inforenergetics school [[11]](https://idoc.pub/documents/radiestezie-vylyv3822elm) appear as smaller instances of the same pattern.

## 1. The Berlin Replication Group: 1980–1982

The post-war German line does not begin with belief; it begins with an attempt at falsification. Lassek's account opens in West Berlin, 1980:

> Anfang 1980 begannen in WestBerlin interessierte Ärzte und Medizinstudenten, in eigener Initiative die zentralen Experimente Reichs auf dem Gebiet der Zelluntersuchung und Krebsdiagnostik experimentell nachzuvollziehen und kritisch zu überprüfen. […] Es zeigte sich, daß Reich mehrere bis heute unbekannte Phänomene entdeckt und beschrieben hat, die sich mit modernsten Methoden wie der nachkontrastierenden Videomikroskopie nachvollziehen und dokumentieren lassen.
>
> *"In early 1980, interested physicians and medical students in West Berlin began, on their own initiative, to experimentally reproduce and critically check Reich's central experiments in cell investigation and cancer diagnostics. […] It turned out that Reich had discovered and described several phenomena still unknown today, which can be reproduced and documented with the most modern methods such as post-contrast videomicroscopy."* [[1]](https://orgon-archiv.de/blog/2026/04/28/orgonakkumulator-therapie-bei-schwerkranken-menschen/)

Whatever one makes of the orgone hypothesis, this is the correct experimental posture: replicate the bench biology first, with better instruments than the original (post-contrast videomicroscopy did not exist in Reich's Oslo laboratory), and present at universities — which Lassek says the group did, across the German-speaking and Scandinavian region, after three years of work. The claim that survives from this phase is narrow and checkable in principle: that Reich's bion/vesicle preparations and blood-test phenomenology are **real as microscope phenomena**, whatever their interpretation.

The group's second phase (1982) was self-experiment with accumulator-type apparatus, and it produced the observation that anchors the whole clinical line:

> Es zeigte sich, daß subjektive und objektive Einwirkungen umso deutlicher zu erfahren waren, je deutlicher ein Schmerz oder Krankheitszustand vor dem Beginn der Orgonbestrahlung bereits vorhanden war, und daß viele gesunde Versuchspersonen ohne jede Symptomatik deutlich längere und häufigere Bestrahlungszeiten benötigten, um starke vegetative Reaktionen des Organismus zu verspüren.
>
> *"It turned out that subjective and objective effects were experienced the more clearly, the more pronounced a pain or disease state already was before the orgone irradiation began — and that many healthy test persons without any symptoms needed markedly longer and more frequent irradiation times to feel strong vegetative reactions of the organism."* [[1]](https://orgon-archiv.de/blog/2026/04/28/orgonakkumulator-therapie-bei-schwerkranken-menschen/)

Effect scales with pathology, not with suggestion-susceptibility — the healthy feel least. This is exactly the "sensitive persons" methodology problem of the Chinese external-qi program in mirror image: the Chinese program selected sensitive operators and sensitive detectors; the Berlin group reports that **the disease state itself is the sensitivity**. Both are claims that the organism is the primary transducer, and both make the experimentalist's life miserable, because the detector cannot be calibrated on a bench.

## 2. The Clinical Record

### 2.1 Lassek's seventeen terminal patients

The core document of the batch is Lassek's personal experience report: seventeen "terminal" cancer patients — metastasized, beyond further medical influence, mostly in severe pain — counseled and treated with the orgone accumulator alongside psychosomatic vegetotherapy, always free of charge, over two and a half years:

> Bei Zweidrittel der Patienten wurde nach Unterweisung und gemeinsamen Probesitzungen eine deutliche Reduktion des Schmerzmittelgebrauches, in einigen Fällen sogar Schmerzfreiheit, nach im Durchschnitt 20 Orgonbestrahlungen erreicht. Fast ausnahmslos kam es zu einer deutlich gesteigerten Vitalität […]
>
> *"In two-thirds of the patients, after instruction and joint trial sessions, a marked reduction in pain-medication use was achieved — in some cases even freedom from pain — after an average of 20 orgone irradiations. Almost without exception there was a markedly increased vitality […]"* [[1]](https://orgon-archiv.de/blog/2026/04/28/orgonakkumulator-therapie-bei-schwerkranken-menschen/)

Lassek's framing matters as much as his numbers: the report is titled a *personal experience report*, states the selection (terminal, nothing-left-to-lose), states the concurrent therapy (vegetotherapy), and states the limits — the title of his own summary is "the great possibilities **and the clear limits**" of influencing far-advanced cancer. This is not a cure claim; it is a palliative-care claim with a defined intervention (ORAC sessions), a defined outcome proxy (analgesic consumption), and an n of 17. It is exactly the tier of evidence the archive should catalog carefully: above anecdote, below trial, honestly labeled.

### 2.2 Kavouras: orgonomy as country practice

Where Lassek is the institute line, Jorgos Kavouras is the field line. The orgon-archiv profile describes a general practitioner and committed homeopath, trained in the US in body-psychiatric orgone therapy "also by those therapists who were still trained by Wilhelm Reich himself," running a rural practice:

> Mit über 3000 Anwendungen von Orgon-Akkumulator und DOR-Buster jährlich verfügt Dr. Kavouras seit mehr als 10 Jahren über ein breites, solides Anwendungswissen. […] Dr. Kavouras hat in seiner Landarztpraxis wahrscheinlich mehr Patienten mit orgonomischen Methoden behandelt, als alle anderen orgonenergetisch arbeitenden Ärzte weltweit zusammen.
>
> *"With over 3,000 applications of the orgone accumulator and DOR-buster per year, Dr. Kavouras has had a broad, solid body of application knowledge for more than 10 years. […] In his country practice, Dr. Kavouras has probably treated more patients with orgonomic methods than all other orgone-energetically working physicians worldwide combined."* [[2]](https://orgon-archiv.de/blog/2026/04/28/252/)

Two details carry the signal. First, the **indication gradient**: the profile names wounds, post-operative recovery, and burns as the classical ORAC domain with the most impressive courses — i.e., conditions where regeneration rate is the visible variable and spontaneous improvement is the confound to beat. Second, the integration claim: Kavouras treats across naturopathy, school medicine, and body-psychiatric therapy, with orgonomy as the connecting discipline rather than an ideology ("it is about treating his patients with the most effective method, not about a particular medical ideology"). His book *Heilen mit Orgonenergie — die medizinische Orgonomie* is explicitly positioned as the successor to Reich's own clinical volume *The Discovery of the Orgone: The Cancer*. The archive currently holds only the review/profile level; the full book is in the upgrade ledger.

### 2.3 Fischer: the theoretical spine

Jürgen Fischer's survey (published in abridged form in *esotera* 4/97) supplies the program's internal history in compact form: Reich's expulsion from the psychoanalytic association; the Oslo bioelectric experiments demonstrating that

> sich lustvolle und unlustvolle emotionale Erfahrungen direkt über das Spannungspotential der Haut abbilden lassen.
>
> *"pleasurable and unpleasurable emotional experiences map directly onto the tension potential of the skin."* [[3]](https://orgon-archiv.de/blog/2026/04/28/die-orgonomische-medizin-wilhelm-reichs/)

— a microvolt-level result Reich himself judged too small to be the energy of the life process; then the bion observations, the disintegration of organic and even sterile inorganic material into vesicles that

> ballen sich zusammen, beginnen autonome Bewegungen und werden spontan zu einzelligen Lebewesen „geboren".
>
> *"clump together, begin autonomous movements, and are spontaneously 'born' as unicellular organisms."* [[3]](https://orgon-archiv.de/blog/2026/04/28/die-orgonomische-medizin-wilhelm-reichs/)

The survey is valuable to this archive because it keeps the **chain of inference visible**: skin-potential phenomenology → vesicle phenomenology → atmospheric energy hypothesis → accumulator engineering → clinical application. Every later program in this synthesis runs the same chain in its own vocabulary, and the chain is only as strong as its weakest link — which is why the German school's own skeptics (§4) concentrate their fire on the third link.

## 3. The Memory Line

A clinical school survives on transmission, and the batch documents three generations of it. Eva Reich — physician, daughter — lecturing in West Berlin in April 1989:

> Er ist aus dem viktorianischen Zeitalter herausgetreten in eine Art Zukunftszeitalter. Er hat mehr geändert als je ein anderer Mensch. […] Er hatte etwas Aristokratisches an sich. Es kam aus der Zeit […] Er hat auch gewusst, wie man lebt. Und dieses Leben-Können ist der Teil, an dem ich beteiligt war, denn es war lustig mit ihm.
>
> *"He stepped out of the Victorian age into a kind of future age. He changed more than any other human being ever did. […] He had something aristocratic about him. It came from the era. […] He also knew how to live. And this knowing-how-to-live is the part I was involved in, because it was fun with him."* [[4]](https://orgon-archiv.de/blog/2026/04/30/dr-eva-reich-meine-erinnerungen-an-w-r/)

Kuno Kruse's documentary essay on Myron Sharaf — Reich's student, biographer (*Fury on Earth*), and the man who tried to give the movement its "physics of love" [[5]](https://orgon-archiv.de/blog/2026/04/28/ein-analytiker-sucht-physik-der-liebe/) — carries the American emigration line; the NDR feature by Martin Steinhoff [[6]](https://orgon-archiv.de/blog/2026/04/28/heilkraft-aus-dem-weltall/) shows German public radio still willing to pose the question plainly ("Heilkraft aus dem Weltall?" — healing power from the cosmos?); and the anonymous continuation of *Listen, Little Man* [[7]](https://orgon-archiv.de/blog/2026/04/29/hoer-nochmal-zu-kleiner-mann/) shows the cultural afterlife running on its own. The archive catalogs these not as evidence but as **transmission fidelity data**: what the school believed it was keeping alive, decade by decade.

## 4. The Skeptical Ledger the School Keeps Against Itself

Unusually for this archive's domains, the German orgonomy line curates its own opposition. Three anchors:

- **Bernhard Harrer, "Critical Evaluation of Life-Energy Research by Wilhelm Reich (Orgone Theory)"** (datadiwan.de) [[8]](https://www.datadiwan.de/harrer/ha_001d_.htm) — a German-language critical review of the experimental claims, plus Harrer's separate re-examination of Reich's Geiger-Müller counter work and the ORANUR experiment (`reich-geiger-mueller-oranur-harrer-de`).
- **Peter Nasselstein, *Chronicle of Orgonomy 1897–1939*** [[9]](https://orgonomie.net/hdoc97-39.pdf) — a year-by-year historiographic compilation (5th version, 2019), and his *Oranur-Physics* introduction to Reich as physicist (`oranur-physics-nasselstein-de`) — the school's own internal metrology/historiography.
- The archive's existing ORANUR material (`oranur-experiment-wilhelm-reich-de-en`, Jerome Eden's account; and the earlier synthesis `omega-oranur-convergence`) — the episode where the orgone hypothesis met nuclear materials and produced the DOR (deadly orgone) concept.

The honest summary, matching the stance of the torsion synthesis: the clinical record is **practice reports**, not trials. No blinded, controlled replication of the ORAC palliative effect exists in the archive. The strongest documents are strong because they are specific about protocol, selection, and limits — Lassek above all — not because they close the case.

## 5. The Three-Program Convergence

The archive now holds, synthesized, three national life-field programs. The German one documented here is the third vertex:

| | **German orgone** | **Chinese qi** | **Russian torsion** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mode | Clinical practice | Institutional measurement | Instrument engineering |
| Key devices | ORAC, DOR-buster | External-qi emitters (operators), information water | Akimov generators, varicap/photodiode detectors |
| Detector | The patient (dose-response by pathology) | Sensitive persons + instruments (Li Si-Chen IR spectroscopy) | Semiconductor dark current |
| Physics vocabulary | Atmospheric vital energy (ORG/DOR) | Qi → identified with torsion/spin field (Li 2006; Lee/Tsai/Liang 2016) | Spin field of the physical vacuum |
| Wall hit | No trials; FDA-era destruction of Reich's orgonomy in the US | Program dissolved with political cover withdrawn | RAS anti-pseudoscience verdict; PulsePen "below detection threshold" |

Cross-references: `synthesis-qi-torsion-bridge-chinese-measurement-program`, `synthesis-torsion-telecommunication-program`.

**Five points of independent agreement** (each tradition reached these without contact with the others, in its own vocabulary):

1. **A life-coupled field not reducible to electromagnetism.** Orgone (Reich), qi (Lu Zuyin's "materiality of external qi" at Tsinghua), torsion (Akimov/Shipov), prana (Akhand Jyoti's 1985 "scientific analysis" series [[10]](https://www.awgp.org/en/literature/akhandjyoti/1985/January/v2.26)) — four vocabularies, one claim shape.
2. **Geometry concentrates it.** The ORAC is a *layered* structure — alternating organic and metallic strata, a form device with no power input. This is the exact construction class of the Chaumery–de Belizal form-wave apparatus (`protective-apparatus-lineage`) and of BioGeometry's centering forms (Ibrahim Karim's energy-quality balancing by shape); the Risy glossary of form-waves and torsion fields now supplies the French vocabulary for the same claim (`2026-10-10-glossaire-ondes-de-forme-champs-de-torsion-risy-fr`). Five traditions agree: **shape alone can pump the field.**
3. **Water carries it.** Qi information-water (CAS IHEP), torsion-irradiated water (Gao Peng's EIS sensor line), structured-water literature, and the orgone school's atmospheric-charge model all put water between field and organism (`memory-bearing-medium`).
4. **The organism is the most sensitive detector — and sensitivity tracks state.** Lassek's pathology-scales-effect observation; the Chinese program's sensitive-persons methodology; Kaznacheev–Trofimov's hypogeomagnetic-chamber subjects outperforming machines. This is simultaneously the programs' most consistent empirical report and their deepest methodological vulnerability.
5. **Every program hit the same wall.** Different walls, same shape: German orgonomy lives as practice reports; the qi program ended when its institutional patrons withdrew; torsion metrology "fell below the detection threshold" under hardening. No blind third-party replication exists for any of the three. The convergence of the *claims* is strong; the convergence of the *evidence tier* is equally strong and equally limiting.

The smaller schools in the archive fit the same lattice: the Romanian inforenergetics course [[11]](https://idoc.pub/documents/radiestezie-vylyv3822elm) (structured curriculum, graded radiesthetic training), the Hindi prana-science journals, and the French form-wave practitioners (Risy's field notebooks — `synthesis-applied-turn-risy-field-notebooks`) are each single-teacher instances of the national-program pattern.

## 6. Open Questions

1. **The ORAC as a passive form device.** If the accumulator is truly input-less — no power, only geometry and layering — it belongs to the same testable class as the Chaumery–de Belizal apparatus and BioGeometry centering forms. A single instrument that could rank "field quality" across these devices would unify five literatures overnight. BioGeometry's BG3 quality-discrimination claim is the closest candidate the archive holds; the orgone school's own ORG/DOR distinction is the quality axis to measure. That experiment is cheap and has never been run.
2. **Lassek's palliative protocol, replicated.** The 20-session ORAC / analgesic-consumption endpoint is specific enough to test under modern palliative-care conditions with proper controls. The Berlin group showed the 1980s could run this at university level; nothing in the archive says anyone has tried since.
3. **DOR-buster vs. form-wave ecology.** Kavouras's medical DOR-buster and Risy's form-wave support of bee colonies (`soutien-colonies-ondes-de-forme-rucher-risy-fr`) are the same applied claim — field quality intervention on a living system — at different scales. The archive should treat them as one experiment class.
4. **orgon-archiv.de itself.** A single-site German archive ("Texts from 60 years") supplied this entire batch — Lassek, Kavouras, Fischer, Eva Reich, Kruse/Sharaf, the NDR feature, the lay physics texts. It is one person's life's work on one server: the endangered-archive class the scout program exists for (cf. danielrisy.free.fr, upgrade-232). Entered as upgrade-244.
