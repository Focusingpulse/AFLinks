# The Vacuum as Organizational Substrate: Convergent Models of Pre-Metric Reality

*Synthesis by Drunvalo — 2026-08-31*

---

## Abstract

At least six independent research traditions—French cosmology (Chardin, Debailleul), Italian biophysics (Del Giudice/Pollack), Swiss vortex physics (SAFE), Brazilian scalar technology (Waechter), Tesla's radiant energy legacy (Lindemann), and modern scalar wave theory (Meyl/Ondulavi)—converge on a claim that cuts against the mainstream: the vacuum is not empty. It is an organizational substrate from which matter, energy, and biological form emerge. This synthesis traces that thread through the AFLinks archive, identifies convergent claims across traditions that developed in isolation, notes the Western vs. Russian-French-Italian-Brazilian intersections, and proposes next questions.

Where the previous synthesis (2026-08-30) traced geometry as an informational encoding layer, this one traces the medium itself—the vacuum, ether, or configuration space that carries that information and from which physical reality condenses.

---

## The Convergent Claim

All six traditions assert that:

1. **The vacuum has structure.** It is not absence but a medium with measurable or theoretical properties—inertial density, coherence domains, phyton packets, configuration space, scalar potential.
2. **Matter and energy emerge from it.** Physical reality is not placed into the vacuum but arises from it—through condensation, polarization, vortex formation, or coherence phase transitions.
3. **It carries information, not just energy.** The substrate organizes matter informationalistically—through form, frequency, or coherence patterns—without necessarily transferring conventional energy.
4. **It is pre-metric or non-material.** The substrate precedes spacetime geometry. It is not a field on spacetime but the source of spacetime.

This is distinct from quantum field theory's vacuum fluctuations, which are mathematical residuals of fields already defined on spacetime. These traditions describe something more fundamental: a substrate from which spacetime itself arises.

## The Traditions

### 1. French Cosmology: Debailleul's DEBA Framework

Michel Debailleul (geophysicist, Université Libre de Bruxelles) published the DEBA framework in December 2025. Its core move is radical: the primordial vacuum is not spacetime but a **configuration space** (C, μ) that is atemporal, non-metric, and acausal.

Key claims:

- **Bubble-universes emerge by stochastic coherence condensation.** A functional Langevin equation governs an organizational field Φ on configuration space. When global resonance exceeds a threshold, a "organizational flash" occurs—a phase transition where a bubble-universe crystallizes.
- **Physical laws and constants emerge locally.** They are not universal but specific to each bubble, determined by the coherence pattern at the moment of condensation.
- **Dark matter and dark energy are artifacts.** They are not substances but manifestations of inherited organizational coherence—the imprint of the pre-metric substrate on the bubble's subsequent evolution.
- **All predictions are falsifiable.** CMB anomalies, early supermassive black holes, the Hubble tension, and large-scale structures all emerge without ad hoc hypotheses.

The mathematical formalism is rigorous: bistable potential V[Φ], Wiener process noise, large-deviation theory for instanton trajectories, stopping time for the organizational flash. This is not vague metaphysics—it is a stochastic field theory with quantitative predictions.

**Vacuum aspect:** The vacuum is the primary reality. Spacetime, matter, and physical laws are secondary, emergent, local.

### 2. French Cosmology: Chardin's Dirac-Milne Universe

Gabriel Chardin (CNRS Silver Medal 2007) proposes a universe with equal amounts of matter and antimatter, where antimatter possesses **negative gravitational mass**. This eliminates the need for both dark matter and dark energy.

Key claims:

- **Antimatter has negative mass.** No experiment has yet measured the gravitational mass of antimatter. The Dirac-Milne model assumes it is negative.
- **Antimatter fills intergalactic voids.** The "disappearance" of antimatter is not a mystery—it is hidden in cosmic voids, where it repels itself and spreads as a cold gas.
- **Vacuum polarization replaces dark matter.** The coexistence of positive and negative masses polarizes the vacuum, modifying gravity equations in a way that resembles MOND but without modifying Newton's laws directly.
- **Cosmic expansion is explained.** Negative-mass antimatter in voids pushes outward, driving the acceleration of expansion without dark energy.
- **Testable at CERN.** The Gbar, Alpha-g, and AEgIS experiments were designed to measure the gravitational mass of antihydrogen.

**Vacuum aspect:** The vacuum is polarized by the coexistence of positive and negative mass. This polarization is the organizational feature that replaces dark matter and dark energy.

### 3. French/Swiss Unified Theory: Haramein and the Vacuum Catastrophe

The article by Gaetana (Ma vie quantique, 2019) traces the ether concept from ancient traditions (Prakriti, Indra's Net) through classical physics to Nassim Haramein's unified field theory.

Key claims:

- **The vacuum catastrophe is a scale problem, not a physics error.** Quantum vacuum energy density (10⁹³ g/cm³) and the cosmological constant (10⁻²⁹ g/cm³) differ by 122 orders of magnitude. Haramein argues both values are correct—they describe the vacuum at different scales.
- **Light does not travel.** If light does not propagate through a medium, the question of a material ether is moot. Everything is connected through space itself.
- **The immaterial ether of ancient traditions is rehabilitated.** Not the mechanical ether of 19th-century physics, but a subtler medium—space itself as the connecting element across all scales.

**Vacuum aspect:** Space is the ether. The vacuum is not empty but is the connecting medium. The 122-order-of-magnitude discrepancy is not a failure but a feature of scale-dependent vacuum energy.

### 4. Italian Biophysics: Del Giudice and Pollack's Fourth Phase of Water

Emilio Del Giudice, the Italian theoretical physicist, explains Gerald Pollack's experimental discovery of the **exclusion zone** (EZ)—a fourth phase of water that forms near hydrophilic surfaces.

Key claims:

- **Water near membranes is fundamentally different.** Within microns of a hydrophilic surface, water forms an exclusion zone that excludes solutes and readily donates electrons.
- **The EZ resolves Szent-Györgyi's mystery.** Living matter requires enormous quantities of electrons for chemical reactions, but freeing an electron from an isolated water molecule takes 12.6 eV—incompatible with life. In the EZ, water donates electrons freely.
- **Coherence domains give rise to biological morphologies.** The organized regions of water (coherence domains) shape where solutes concentrate, and thus the architectures that form between exclusion zones. "These structures trace the structure of the organized regions of water."
- **The vital force is an EZ phenomenon.** Pollack's current, obtained by placing a wire into EZ water, "is the expression of what was once called the vital force."

**Vacuum aspect:** Water's coherence domains are a local, biological manifestation of the same organizational principle. The vacuum's organizing capacity is not abstract—it is physically present in every cell, in the water that constitutes 99% of our molecules. The EZ is where the vacuum meets biology.

### 5. Swiss Vortex Physics: The SAFE Compendium

The Swiss Association for Free Energy (SAFE) published a 108-chunk Compendium of Vortex Physics in 1999, systematizing 150 years of outsider vortex research.

Key claims:

- **The vortex is the archetypal phenomenon.** "In nature, nothing moves in straight lines—all movement is spiral and can be regarded as a vortex." The vortex bridges the visible and invisible.
- **Elementary particles are vortices in the ether.** The ether vortex hypothesis: particles are not point-like but are rotational structures in a sub-material medium.
- **Natural motion is spiral-shaped.** The motion hypothesis: "We move incorrectly; natural motion is spiral-shaped." Linear motion is an approximation; spiral motion is fundamental.
- **Vortex physics as a key to free energy.** The reanimation of ether and vortex concepts could expand natural science "from the purely material to higher planes of existence."

**Vacuum aspect:** The ether is the medium in which vortices form. Particles are not in the ether—they are the ether, organized into rotational structures. The vacuum is not a backdrop but the substance of matter itself.

### 6. Brazilian Scalar Technology: Waechter's Scalar Energy Generator

Marcelo Waechter patented (WO2013155580A1, 2013) a household apparatus that generates scalar energy at 12 Hz via alternating positive/negative exciter circuits.

Key claims:

- **Scalar energy has magnitude but no direction.** It manifests in all directions simultaneously and is "not readily perceptible to measuring instruments based on electromagnetism."
- **Scalar energy is the informational dimension of matter.** The patent traces a lineage: animal magnetism (Mesmer), odic force (von Reichenbach), orgone (Reich), radiant energy (Tesla), eloptic energy (Hieronymus)—and cross-culturally as Ch'i, Ki, prana.
- **The brain is a scalar generator.** "Each half of the brain is a scalar generator, and the confluence of the two waves generated by each half produces an electromagnetic wave captured by the apparatus."
- **Scalar energy optimizes cellular functions.** It facilitates energy flow, slows aging, and prevents disease by restoring balance to the body's energy matrices.

**Vacuum aspect:** Scalar energy is the non-directional, informational component of the electromagnetic field—the part that conventional instruments miss. It is the vacuum's organizing capacity made technological.

### 7. Tesla's Radiant Energy: Lindemann's Patent Analysis

Peter Lindemann's analysis of Tesla's patents (compiled from his 2000 book and 2006 DVD) reconstructs Tesla's method for generating **radiant energy**—longitudinal electrostatic waves carried by radiant matter.

Key claims:

- **Radiant matter consists of neutral particles <100× electron mass.** Tesla, inspired by Crookes' radiometer, concluded that matter radiates particles so small they pass through glass without disturbing the vacuum.
- **Radiant energy is electrostatic charge carried by radiant matter.** Tesla's "method of conversion" transforms normal electricity into "longitudinal waves of electrostatic force."
- **Three forms of electricity exist.** DC (electrons + neutrals in wire), AC (oscillating), and radiant (electrostatic pressure waves). The third form is the one conventional physics neglects.
- **Spark gaps are the key technology.** Magnetically quenched, rotary, and oil-isolated spark gaps enable the conversion from conventional to radiant energy.

**Vacuum aspect:** Radiant energy is the vacuum's electrostatic component—longitudinal, non-Hertzian, and carried by particles so small they are essentially vacuum excitations. Tesla was technologizing the vacuum decades before quantum field theory.

### 8. Modern Scalar Wave Theory: Meyl and Ondulavi

Konstantin Meyl (German physicist) extends Tesla and Maxwell, arguing that scalar waves are the neglected complement to transverse electromagnetic waves.

Key claims:

- **Every EM wave has a transverse and longitudinal component.** The transverse (Hertzian) component is measurable in 3D space. The longitudinal (scalar) component "generally does not manifest in 3D space but moves along the temporal axis."
- **Scalar waves are superluminal.** They "travel at superluminal speeds, exceeding the speed of light, and are not constrained by the limits of 3D space."
- **Scalar fields constitute the informational dimension of matter.** They are the non-physical or informational complement to the physical electromagnetic field.
- **Maxwell's equations contain scalar waves but they were dropped.** The reduction from Maxwell's original 20 equations to Heaviside's 4 eliminated the scalar potential.

**Vacuum aspect:** Scalar waves are the vacuum's information channel—non-spatial, superluminal, and informational. They complete the electromagnetic picture by adding the dimension that conventional physics discarded.

## Western vs. Russian-French-Italian-Brazilian Intersections

### French Cosmology Program
Chardin (CNRS) and Debailleul (ULB) represent a striking development: credentialed European researchers proposing mathematically rigorous alternatives to the dark matter/dark energy paradigm. Chardin is a CNRS Silver Medalist and president of Very Large Research Infrastructures. Debailleul publishes on Zenodo with full mathematical formalism. Both frame their work as falsifiable. They are not outsiders—they are insiders proposing radical alternatives from within the system.

### Italian Biophysics Program
Del Giudice and Pollack bridge mainstream and alternative science. Pollack is a University of Washington professor. Del Giudice was an Italian theoretical physicist. Their work on water's fourth phase is experimentally grounded and published in peer-reviewed venues. The connection to Szent-Györgyi (Nobel laureate) gives it mainstream lineage. Yet the claim that EZ water "is the expression of the vital force" pushes beyond conventional biophysics.

### Swiss-German Vortex/Scalar Program
SAFE (Swiss) and Meyl (German) represent the engineering tradition—practitioners who build devices and compile empirical knowledge outside academia. The SAFE Compendium is explicitly a research program for "outsider ideas" brought into "readable, logical, and consistent form." Meyl reproduces Tesla's experiments and writes textbooks. This tradition is pragmatic: it does not ask whether the ether exists but whether devices that assume it exists produce anomalous effects.

### Brazilian Scalar Technology
Waechter's patent represents the globalization of scalar energy concepts. The patent explicitly traces the lineage through Tesla, Mesmer, von Reichenbach, Reich, and Hieronymus, and cross-culturally through Ch'i, Ki, and prana. The device is household-scale—scalar energy democratized. The patent's status (Ceased) suggests commercial difficulty, but the technological claim is documented.

### Russian Intersection

Shipov's "physical vacuum" with phyton structure is the same medium that SAFE calls the ether, Debailleul calls configuration space, and Del Giudice sees in water's coherence domains. The Russian contribution is the most systematic state-funded research program on vacuum structure—torsion generators, metallurgy applications, satellite experiments. The most striking convergence is between Debailleul's DEBA framework and Del Giudice's water coherence:

- **Debailleul:** Bubble-universes emerge by "stochastic coherence condensation" from a pre-metric configuration space. A bistable potential allows phase transitions. Coherence is the organizing principle.

The mathematical structure is the same: a field on a substrate, bistable states, phase transitions, and the emergence of form from coherence. This is not a metaphor—the dynamics are formally analogous.

## What's Missing

1. **No experimental bridge between cosmological and biological vacuum models.** Debailleul's stochastic field theory and Del Giudice's water coherence domains describe the same organizational principle at different scales. No experiment tests whether water's coherence domains respond to cosmological-scale vacuum fluctuations, or whether DEBA's organizational field has measurable effects on biological systems.

2. **No mainstream engagement with the vacuum-as-substrate paradigm.** Chardin and Debailleul publish in CNRS channels and Zenodo. Del Giudice and Pollack publish in peer-reviewed journals. But no mainstream physics program connects their work to quantum gravity, loop quantum gravity, or string theory—despite the structural similarities (pre-metric spaces, emergent spacetime, coherence condensation).

3. **The vacuum catastrophe remains unresolved in mainstream physics.** Haramein's scale-dependent resolution is not cited in mainstream literature. The 122-order-of-magnitude discrepancy between quantum vacuum energy and the cosmological constant is acknowledged as "the worst prediction in physics" but no consensus alternative exists.

4. **Scalar wave technology lacks reproducible protocols.** Tesla's radiant energy, Waechter's 12 Hz generator, and Meyl's scalar wave experiments are documented but not independently reproduced under controlled conditions. The technological claim—that the vacuum's scalar component can be generated and used—remains unverified by mainstream standards.

5. **The consciousness-vacuum interface is claimed but unmeasured.** Waechter claims the brain is a scalar generator. SAFE suggests vortex physics reaches "higher planes of existence." Haramein argues everything is connected through space. But no experiment measures whether consciousness interacts with the vacuum substrate in a way that is distinguishable from placebo or expectation.
