# The Quantum Potential Bridge: Scalar Fields in Consciousness Research and Cosmology

## Abstract

Two independent research traditions — Italian quantum biophysics (Manzalini, 2022) and French alternative cosmology (Toulgoat, 2024) — converged on a strikingly similar claim: **scalar fields with information-encoding curvature can explain phenomena that conventional physics treats as either emergent (consciousness) or missing (dark matter).** Both traditions build on de Broglie-Bohm pilot wave theory, both emphasize field shape over amplitude, and both propose that a single field principle operates across scales from particle physics to cosmic structure. This synthesis traces the convergent claims, identifies the theoretical bridge through quantum potential physics, and proposes experimental tests that could validate or falsify the scalar-field-as-unifying-principle hypothesis.

---

## 1. The Convergent Claim

Across Italian consciousness research and French alternative cosmology, researchers converge on five fundamental claims:

1. **Scalar fields carry information, not energy.** The field's shape encodes meaning; amplitude is secondary or irrelevant.

2. **Field curvature determines physical effects.** Both traditions explicitly state that curvature/shape matters more than magnitude.

3. **De Broglie-Bohm theory provides the foundation.** Both build on pilot wave physics and the quantum potential concept.

4. **Conventional physics is incomplete, not wrong.** Both claim their scalar field extends rather than replaces standard physics.

5. **Testable predictions follow.** Both traditions make falsifiable claims about measurable phenomena.

These convergent claims emerged independently in Italy (2022) and France (2024), with limited cross-pollination.

## 2. Italian Tradition: Consciousness as Quantum Field

Key innovations:
- **Consciousness field C:** Analogous to quantum field ψ, with associated potential
- **Active information:** The potential of consciousness is information that guides biological dynamics
- **Shape over amplitude:** Like quantum potential Q, consciousness potential C depends on field curvature, not amplitude

### 2.2 Nambu-Goldstone Bosons

Manzalini proposes that **consciousness field curvature is determined by Nambu-Goldstone boson condensation**:
- Symmetry breaking in the consciousness field creates NG bosons
- These bosons are massless, energy-free information carriers
- **Form factors:** Condensation functions f(x) act as "form factors" of the consciousness field

This connects consciousness to **geometric field structure** — not electromagnetic activity but field curvature encoded by boson condensation.

### 2.3 Valentini's Non-Equilibrium Physics

Manzalini builds on Antony Valentini's claim: **Quantum theory is a special case of broader physics** where systems are in quantum equilibrium.

Living systems are **far from quantum equilibrium**, allowing:
- Non-local information transfer
- Superluminal signal propagation
- Active information processing

Consciousness is the **field that guides systems far from equilibrium**.

### 2.4 Key Equations

Manzalini extends the quantum Hamilton-Jacobi equation:

```

```

Where the quantum potential Q = -(ℏ²/2m)(∇²R/R) depends on field curvature.

**The field's shape encodes physical information; amplitude is secondary.**

## 3. French Tradition: Scalar Field as Dark Matter Alternative

### 3.1 Toulgoat's Galactic Model

Fabien Toulgoat, in "Rigorous Study of a Light Scalar Field Coupled to Matter in Galaxies" (Zenodo, 2024), proposes a scalar field Φ as an alternative to dark matter [2].

Key innovations:
- **Light scalar field:** Mass m ~ 10⁻²⁷ eV/c², coupled to baryonic density
- **Modified gravity:** Field creates additional gravitational force with finite range
- **Testable predictions:** Specific parameter values (m, α) predict galactic rotation curves

### 3.2 The Klein-Gordon Equation

Toulgoat derives field equations from a Lagrangian:

```
ℒ = (1/16πG)R - (1/2)gμν∂μΦ∂νΦ - (1/2)m²Φ² + αΦρ + ℒmatter
```

The modified Klein-Gordon equation:

```
□Φ - m²Φ = -αρ
```

In the quasi-static limit:

```
(∇² - m²)Φ(r) = -αρ(r)
```

This inhomogeneous Helmholtz equation governs the scalar field distribution.

### 3.3 Yukawa Green's Function

The solution uses the Yukawa Green's function:

```
Φ(r) = (α/4π) ∫ [e^(-m|r-r'|) / |r-r'|] ρ(r') d³r'
```

**Key insight:** The exponential decay term e^(-mr) creates finite-range effects, unlike Newtonian gravity's 1/r potential.

### 3.4 Physical Parameters

Toulgoat derives specific parameter values:

| Parameter | Value | Physical Meaning |
|-----------|-------|------------------|
| m | ~10⁻²⁷ eV/c² | Scalar field mass |
| λ = 1/m | ~1 kpc | Compton wavelength (galactic scale) |
| α | ~G | Coupling constant (gravitational order) |

**The field's range matches the scale of observed galactic effects.**

**Hypothesis:** The quantum potential Q and the cosmological scalar field Φ are different manifestations of the same underlying scalar information field:

Both:

Italian consciousness-as-field theory and French scalar field cosmology represent two independent research traditions that converged on strikingly similar claims. Both assert that:

1. Scalar fields carry information without energy transfer
2. Field curvature/shape determines physical effects
3. De Broglie-Bohm theory provides the foundation

The convergence is remarkable given the different domains:

- **Manzalini:** Emerged from quantum biophysics and consciousness studies

- Consciousness field effects should be measurable in water coherence

If validated, the quantum potential bridge would represent a new paradigm in physics: **information as a physical quantity carried by scalar fields across scales from consciousness to cosmology.** If falsified, the convergence would remain an interesting case study in how independent traditions can arrive at similar claims from different premises.

Either outcome advances understanding.
