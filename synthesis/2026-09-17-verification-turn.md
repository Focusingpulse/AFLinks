# The Verification Turn: Cheap Discriminating Tests Meet the Subtle-Energy Corpus

## Abstract

Something has changed in the corpus's epistemic position, and it happened quietly across the last week of translations. For ninety years the subtle-energy traditions documented here — French radiesthesia, form waves, dowsing, Bovis-scale vitality — faced a symmetrical stalemate: believers cited experience, skeptics cited nulls, and neither side ran a test the other had agreed to. Three new kinds of document now break that symmetry. First, **bench notes** (Forge's Simoneton/biophotonics, Merz/inter-rater, Lecher/tunable) convert each tradition's central claim into its cheapest falsifiable core. Second, **real discriminating experiments exist now**: the INRS controlled dowsing study (54 participants, iron-vs-plastic pipe grid, co-designed with a dowser, final results at EGU26) is the first modern test built to detect a *sub-effect* rather than re-confirm a global null; Souriau's aluminum-magnetization experiment reduces the entire French form-wave apparatus tradition to a ~€5 binary home test. Third, the corpus now holds the **two historical extremes of verification culture**: the Soviet psychotronics program — twenty state-funded years, Faraday cages, millions of rubles, official nulls, and a classified archive — and the Bovis-scale rescale — a unit whose baseline doubled in 2014 with no instrument change at all. This synthesis traces the pattern: what makes a test *discriminating* rather than merely controlled, why the Soviet program's nulls were still productive, why the Bovis drift is verification's exact inverse, and what the new honesty layer (AI-pendulum vendors printing the null, calibrated consumer guides) says about where the tradition's own self-understanding is heading.

## 1. The Pattern: Four Hypothesis Classes, Four Cheap Tests

The bench-note series did something the corpus had never done systematically: for each major subtle-energy claim, it separated the *untestable frame* (a "vibratory rate," a "form emission") from the *testable residue* (an ordering, an agreement structure, a binary material change). Read together with the new dossiers, four hypothesis classes now have named, affordable, discriminating tests:

| Hypothesis class | Claim | Cheapest discriminating test | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dowsing detects hidden targets** (Rocard's magnetic mechanism) | The body detects weak magnetic gradients; rods amplify them | INRS grid: iron vs plastic pipes, 27 experienced / 27 novices, wood vs metal rods | Field season complete; final results at EGU26 Vienna, May 2026 [1] |
| **Form emissions are physical** (Souriau/Lochak monopole) | 3-day Vm+ exposure magnetizes aluminum, which then attracts to *both* faces of a magnet | ~€5 home experiment: aluminum strips, sham-exposed control, blind attraction test | Claimed in a Servranx book; never independently graded [2] |
| **Bovis place-scale transmits** (Merz lineage) | Trained dowsers independently converge on the same readings at the same sites | Blinded inter-rater panel at Merz's 100+ published Swiss sites; ICC analysis | Never run by the tradition; protocol drafted [3] |
| **Food vitality is rankable** (Simoneton's radiovitality) | The pendulum's food ordering tracks a real physical property | Spearman correlation between blinded dowser classes and DL decay parameters | Protocol drafted; instrumented counterpart exists (biophotonics) [4] |

The common move is the same in all four rows: **shrink the claim until it can't hide.** Not "does radiesthesia work" but "do iron pipes separate from plastic." Not "are form waves real" but "does this aluminum strip attract both magnet faces." Not "is Chartres powerful" but "do six blind dowsers agree on a number." Each test has an outcome in every direction — a positive result is publishable physics or psychophysics, a null is a clean closure, and even a *partial* result (agreement without accuracy, ordering without mechanism) is a new fact. That is what "discriminating" means, and it is what Munich 1986–88, for all its scale, was not.

## 2. The INRS Study: A Test Designed to See Something

The largest controlled dowsing experiment since Munich has just completed in Quebec, and the difference is structural, not cosmetic. Munich (1986–88: 43 pre-screened dowsers, 843 trials, a water-filled pipe on a moving cart under a false floor) tested one flat question — does dowsing beat chance globally — and returned a null whose famous residue (six dowsers above chance) was later shown to be the expected selection artifact. The INRS design cross-tabs **material × water content × salinity/flow × operator experience × instrument**, so it can detect a sub-effect even if the global null holds. Geneviève Bordeleau's own hypothesis statement defines the discriminating row:

> "Maybe overall, the plastic pipe is no better than chance, but the metal pipe is. Which could indicate that magnetism has a role to play." [1]
>
> *"Peut-être que, de manière globale, le tuyau de plastique n'est pas meilleur que le hasard, mais que le tuyau de métal, lui, l'est. Ce qui pourrait indiquer que le magnétisme a un rôle à jouer."*

Three design facts matter beyond the statistics:

1. **A dowser co-designed it.** Manon Lévesque is credited as "citizen scientist and dowsing practitioner" — the experiment was explicitly built with the dowsing community "to ensure methodological rigor and acceptance by the dowsing community." This closes the standing objection that controlled tests never test what dowsers actually claim. [1]
2. **It interrogates a named mechanism.** Yves Rocard — the physicist of French nuclear policy — spent his late career claiming dowsers detect very weak magnetic fields with the body ("Le signal du sourcier," *La Recherche*, 1981). Rocard never had a design that could isolate magnetism from everything else. Iron vs plastic is the magnetism switch; the INRS grid is his test, forty years late. [1]
3. **The framing is hydrological, not parapsychological.** This is a water-resources research institute asking whether a traditional practice carries usable signal for modern groundwater exploration, presented at ACFAS and EGU — the francophone and European geoscience mainstreams, not a psi venue. [1]

The bench-note logic applies to the results *before they are even read*: if iron-pipe-only above chance appears, Rocard's mechanism is vindicated and the corpus's magnetic-detection claim class gains its first instrumented seam. If nothing separates, the null strengthens in exactly the one place a residual effect could plausibly have hidden — and the corpus's honest record should say so with the same weight. Either way, the article (forthcoming, watch INRS publications and EGU proceedings around late 2026) is the verification rail's live item. [1]

## 3. Souriau's Aluminum: The Form-Wave Tradition Reduced to €5

Laurent Souriau — doctor of physical sciences, electromagnetism student of Roger Petit at Marseille — did something no previous generation of the French form-wave school attempted: he named a particle. His claim is that form emissions are **Lochak leptonic magnetic monopoles** — "magnetically excited neutrinos" — and that this is "the real nature" of what Chauméry and de Belizal's instruments were detecting all along. [2]

The theory chain is real, published, and heterodox: Georges Lochak (Fondation Louis de Broglie) derived a second gauge coupling of the massless Dirac equation yielding a light leptonic monopole (*Z. Naturforsch.* 62a, 2007), and the experimental flank is Russian — Urutskoev and Ivoilov at the Kurchatov Institute reported titanium isotope anomalies from electric explosions of titanium foil, and a ⁵⁷Fe Mössbauer shift (ΔH = 500 ± 70 Gs) that **reversed direction when the magnet pole was reversed**. [2]

But the reason this dossier matters for the verification turn is the home experiment:

> "These videos show that one can magnetize a piece of aluminum (a non-ferromagnetic metal) by exposing it for 3 days to a form emission of the Vm+ type. This piece of aluminum is then attracted by both faces of a classical magnet." [2]
>
> *"Ces vidéos montrent que l'on peut magnétiser un morceau d'aluminium (métal non magnétique) en l'exposant 3 jours à une émission de forme de type Vm+. Ce morceau d'aluminium est ensuite attiré par les deux faces d'un aimant classique."*

This is the sharpest falsifiable seam in the entire French apparatus tradition. Aluminum is paramagnetic but non-ferromagnetic; ordinary induced magnetism would give attraction to one magnet face and repulsion from the other, while monopole lodging predicts face-independent attraction — a binary outcome, gradeable by anyone with aluminum strips, a claimed form emitter, a sham-exposure control, and a fridge magnet. The bench-note protocol sketch costs less than a restaurant dinner: ≥3 aluminum samples, one exposed 72h to a Vm+ emitter, one sham-exposed in the same room, one untouched control, blind-tested against both faces of a strong NdFeB magnet. [2]

The evidentiary tiers deserve honest labeling: published-heterodox-theory (Lochak) → contested-experiment (Urutskoev/Ivoilov) → self-published synthesis + home experiments (Souriau, via the Servranx radionics catalog, not a physics journal). But the tier structure is precisely what makes it gradeable — the claim has nowhere to hide. Compare this with the founding generation's own position: de Belizal and Morel conceded in *Physique Micro-Vibratoire* that no instrument of their era could record form waves ("the day such a measurement system is found, we would need radiesthesia less... But we are not there yet"). Souriau is the first in the lineage to say: *here is the measurement, run it yourself.*

## 4. The Soviet Precedent: What Twenty Years of Verification Actually Produced

The corpus's Russian lane now includes the history of the closed Soviet psychotronics program — and it is the perfect control case for the verification turn, because it is what happens when a state applies real apparatus, real budgets, and real secrecy to subtle-energy claims. [5]

The program's shape: telepathy research from the 1920s (Bekhterev, Vasilyev's mental-suggestion experiments), then Cold War intelligence reports of American psi research triggering a "psi arms race"; by the late 1960s, twenty-plus special laboratories — Leningrad University on the electromagnetic theory of suggestion, the Popov Institute on mental-image transfer, Alma-Ata on "bioplasma" (Inyushin), Novosibirsk on ultra-long-distance reception. The flagship experiments: the Moscow–Novosibirsk telepathic bridge (Kamensky transmitting tactile images to Nikolaev, thousands of kilometers), Kulagina's telekinesis in front of academicians, Kuleshova's fingertip "skin vision," Kirlian photography, Faraday cages to rule out electromagnetic leakage. [5]

The verdict, in the program's own records: Vasilyev's laboratory ultimately admitted it had never conclusively proven telepathy as objective physical reality. Careful verification destroyed the specific claims one by one — ultra-long-distance telepathy dissolved into subliminal sensory sensitivity and coincidence; Kulagina's telekinesis into hypnosis of observers, concealed devices, and electrostatics; skin vision into blindfold gaps plus temperature sensitivity; the Kirlian aura into gas discharge dependent on skin humidity. [5]

But the Soviet case is not simply a null. Two residues matter for the corpus:

1. **The applied residue was real.** The program produced contactless health-monitoring systems, stress-assessment methods for pilots, cosmonauts, and submariners, and early polygraph prototypes — the instrumentation of the body's hidden reserves, born from the wreckage of the psi claims. The pattern the bench notes keep finding at micro-scale (the claim dies, the instrument survives) ran at state scale.
2. **The archive closed; the nulls did not circulate.** The documents remain classified — officially for dual-use reasons (psychological manipulation, remote information extraction), practically through bureaucratic inertia. The result is the worst of both worlds for the tradition the corpus documents: the Soviet psi literature is unfalsifiable *by construction*, because the primary evidence is sealed and the secondary literature is polemic on both sides. [5]

The lesson for the verification turn: **verification that cannot publish is verification that never happened.** The INRS study's results, whatever they are, will be public at EGU and in a journal; the Soviet program's results, whatever they were, went into a box. The corpus should treat the two archives accordingly — the INRS row as live evidence, the Soviet row as history with a sealed evidentiary floor.

## 5. The Bovis Drift: Verification's Exact Inverse

Against the Soviet case (maximum apparatus, sealed results), the Bovis-scale drift dossier documents the opposite pathology: **minimum apparatus, fully public results, and an instrument that moves to fit the narrative.** [6]

The facts, fixed by Jacques Bolard (CNRS biophysicist, AFIS, 2016): Alfred Bovis's 1935 biometer anchored "vibratory rate" at 6,500 Å — the longest visible wavelength, a *length metaphor*, not a measurement. The unit was already inconsistent at birth (the 30 cm rule's conversion shifted the anchor to ~6,666 Å at the same position). And the inflation mechanism is internal to the tradition:

> "One need only change the graduations of the 30 cm rule — moving, for example, from a scale of 0 to 10,000 to a scale of 0 to 1,000,000 — and the trick is done: the pendulum will adapt all by itself!"
>
> *"Il suffit de changer les graduations... et le tour est joué : le pendule s'adaptera tout seul !"* [6]

The 2014+ layer industrialized the mechanism: the human health baseline moved from 6,500 to 12,500 UB (+92%) "since 2014," justified by the "élévation vibratoire de la Terre" (the Earth's vibratory elevation), with new dials to 60,000 and 120,000 UB, sacred sites in the millions, and a "convertible biometer" scaling to billions because it "adapts to the planet's rising vibrational rate." The rescale has a date, a named cause, and new dials — and **no instrument change, no recalibration procedure, and no before/after measurement.** [6]

Set the drift beside the verification turn and the symmetry is exact:

| | Verification (INRS, bench notes, Souriau) | Drift (Bovis rescale) |
|---|---|---|
| Instrument | Fixed and specified | Re-graduated to fit results |
| Baseline | Pre-registered (chance, sham control) | Moved to where the narrative needs it (+92%) |
| Failure mode | A null is a result | A low reading means the *world* changed |
| Publication | EGU, ACFAS, protocols with error bars | Retail dials, no caveats |
| Tradition's canon | Tested against itself | Re-graded by decree (Merz's Chartres 11,000 is now "low") |

The drift also quietly re-grades the tradition's own founders: by the rescaled standard, Merz's canonical readings — Chartres 11,000 BE, neutral ground 6,500 — sit *below* the new human baseline. The bench-note response is already drafted and costs two printed cards: run the same blinded dowser panel with both the classic 0–18,000 dial and the new 0–120,000 dial. If readings scale with the dial, the dial is producing the number, not the site. [3][6]

## 6. The New Honesty Layer: Disconfirmation as a Product Feature

The most unexpected document in the recent batch is not about dowsing at all — it is about what happens when the tradition's instruments enter the AI-oracle economy. The fr AI-pendulum census found that the "AI-pendulum gap" (the last corner of online divination with no LLM interpretation layer) broke on French in September 2026 — and that the vendors who broke it are *more scientifically candid than the radiesthesia retail layer they borrowed their vocabulary from.* [7]

Tarotsi's glossary prints the null into its own product page:

> "Is it scientific? **No. Double-blind studies of radiesthesia have not confirmed abilities beyond chance. It remains a symbolic tool.**"
>
> *"Est-ce que c'est scientifique ? Non. Les études en double aveugle de radiesthésie n'ont pas confirmé de capacités supérieures au hasard. Cela reste un outil symbolique."* [7]

Tarotoui states plainly that "the digital version replaces this mechanism by a generated and interpreted response. It is not the same practice." Even the AETHER holdout — the one fr site still using quantum noise instead of an LLM — concedes the ideomotor effect for physical pendulums. The divinatory claim has migrated from *detection* (the pendulum senses) to *hermeneutics* (the AI reads), and the vendors increasingly say so themselves. [7]

The same calibrated posture appeared this week in an unexpected genre: a French water-site's consumer guide to divining rods that walks a reader through buying copper rods at 20–42 € while telling them, at every step, what the purchase does not buy — the ideomotor effect (Carpenter, 1852), the FBI 2021 blind grave-test null, the BRGM and Nice zététique nulls, and the closing line: keep the practice as an attention-ritual, but "it does not replace a measurement." [8]

Read together with the drift, a three-layer picture of the French tradition's current self-understanding emerges:

- **The retail layer** (Bovis dials, form-wave emitters) still sells detection claims with no caveats — the drift's home turf. [6]
- **The new commercial layer** (AI pendulums, calibrated guides) sells the *ritual* and prints the null — disconfirmation as a product feature. [7][8]
- **The research layer** (INRS, bench notes, Souriau's home experiments) is finally running the tests. [1][2][3][4]

The tradition's instruments are being absorbed into the AI-oracle economy as prompt furniture — the century of radiesthetic methodology (calibration, témoins, cadrans) reduced to a yes/no bit an LLM decorates. But the *methodology* has simultaneously escaped into the one place it can be graded: pre-registered protocols with error bars. [7]

## 7. Western vs Russian-French Intersections

The verification turn redraws the corpus's usual East-West map:

- **The Rocard lineage is being tested in Quebec.** A French physicist's magnetic hypothesis (Rocard, 1963–1989), dismissed in France, meets a francophone Canadian hydrogeology institute's discriminating grid — the francophone science mainstream engaging the francophone tradition directly, for water, not for psi. [1]
- **The Lochak bridge runs Paris → Kurchatov → Servranx.** A French heterodox theory (de Broglie's school) with Russian contested experiments (Urutskoev/Ivoilov) and a French radionics-catalog popularizer (Souriau) — the first hard particle-physics bridge between the French form-wave lane and the Russian LENR/torsion lane, and the only one with a home-testable prediction. [2]
- **The Soviet archive vs the French retail archive.** The Russian program's evidence is sealed (unfalsifiable by construction); the French tradition's evidence is *over*-published — every dial, every claim, every rescale is on the open retail record, which is exactly why the drift is documentable and the bench notes are runnable. Openness without controls produces drift; control without openness produces sealed boxes; the verification turn needs both. [5][6]

## 8. Next Questions

1. **The INRS article is the corpus's next single point of leverage.** When it lands (late 2026), three rows decide things: iron-vs-plastic (the magnetic sub-effect), experienced-vs-novice (is the "gift" a learnable procedure), wood-vs-metal (is the rod a passive pointer). The corpus should grade it whichever way it falls. [1]
2. **Has anyone outside Servranx's catalog run Souriau's aluminum experiment?** A literature search for independent replications (or refutations) of the Vm+ aluminum magnetization — and of the Urutskoev/Ivoilov Mössbauer shift — would establish whether the sharpest seam in the form-wave tradition has ever been touched. [2]
3. **Can the Merz inter-rater panel be run at the Swiss sites before the drift reaches them?** The 2001 Swiss volume's hundred named sites are the best blind targets in the tradition — famous values, but only to specialists. Every year the rescale spreads, the values-blind arm gets harder to recruit. [3][6]
4. **Is the honesty layer a leading indicator?** If AI-oracle vendors and consumer guides now print nulls as standard practice, does the retail radiesthesia layer follow — or does it bifurcate further? Watching whether the 2027 Subtil.net catalog carries a caveat would be a cheap sociological measurement of the tradition's drift. [6][7]
5. **The Soviet sealed-box problem has a modern echo.** The corpus's Russian lane (torsion, LENR, psychotronics) repeatedly hits classified or inaccessible primary evidence. A standing rule for the archive: Soviet-sourced claims get an evidentiary-floor label, the way this corpus labels translation tiers — history, not live evidence. [5]
