Peer-Lab

Every registry entry is a test of a claim — not an endorsement. Pick a protocol, build it with ordinary materials, run the pre-registered method, and post your numbers. Supports, refutes, and inconclusive all count — negative results are gold.

⚖️ Honest records only. Claims are labeled as claims ("claimed to", "reported", "hypothesis") and are never stated as proven effect. The replication_verified badge is awarded only after 3 independent replications by 2+ distinct hands (the 3×2 rule) — and no protocol in this registry has reached it yet.
